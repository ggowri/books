Chapter 9 Toy With the Sneaky Pair

The constant doorbell and delivery calls were getting on Blackie's nerves. He paced restlessly beside Cecilia, who gently patted his head to calm him down.

She grabbed a fried chicken drumstick, peeled off the meat and skin in small pieces, and fed him slowly.

"Don't eat too much. Boiled meat and your regular dog food are better for you. Too much salt will make you lose fur—you'll end up a bald dog, and all the girl dogs will run away from you!" she warned softly.

Blackie ate without lifting his head, only glancing up when he finished, his eyelids drooping as if to say, "More!"

Clearly, he'd ignored every word Cecilia had just said.

Before bed, Cecilia took out her hidden knife and practiced a set of moves she'd learned in her previous life.

Her arms and legs ached fiercely halfway through; her current body was far too weak.

Starting tomorrow morning, she'd run, exercise, and practice until she regained the strength she'd had.

Just as Cecilia was about to fall asleep, a text message from Alexander popped up. Full of affectionate words, it recounted their years together and expressed his excitement about their upcoming engagement.

She'd unblocked Alexander earlier to avoid arousing suspicion, but reading the message now made her feel nauseous.

"Who knows what filthy things he's doing in Rebecca's arms right now!"

Cecilia had finally figured out how she'd been manipulated in her previous life.

Rebecca was the mastermind behind Alexander's schemes.

While Alexander was unscrupulous, he was foolish—nearly all his plans came from Rebecca.

If she hated Alexander, she hated the vicious, two-faced Rebecca even more.

Unwilling to drag things out, Cecilia replied simply.

[Got it. Let's talk in detail another day. I'm tired tonight.]

Alexander sent back a gentle "Good night."

Cecilia rolled her eyes.

Such an actor.

On the other side, Alexander lay in Rebecca's arms, frowning as he read Cecilia's reply.

"Rebecca, what do you think this means? I feel like she's being cold to me."

Rebecca studied the message, unsure.

She reassured Alexander, then got up to call Cecilia.

Seeing Rebecca's name on the screen, Cecilia first felt a surge of anger, then a cold chill of clarity.

Since her rebirth, she'd focused on stockpiling supplies and caring for Blackie, her only feelings for the despicable pair being resentment—lingering malice from her past life.

She'd wanted nothing more than to chop them into pieces, and that hatred had made her impulsive.

Now, she realized how irrational that was.

Taking a deep breath and feigning a sniffle, Cecilia answered the call.

"Cici, I heard you and Alexander had a fight? What happened?" Rebecca's voice, brimming with feigned concern, came through the line.

Cecilia sneered inwardly but kept her voice calm, putting on an act of hurt. "Rebecca, you're my best friend, and you know Alexander. Do you think... do you think he doesn't like me anymore?"

She sensed a brief silence on the other end, as if Rebecca hadn't expected that question. Then Rebecca's scolding voice came.

"Cici, what are you saying? As an orphan, you're lucky enough to have the Barnes family's favor! If Alexander didn't like you, would he agree to the engagement? Do you know what kind of family the Barneses are?"

There it was—emotional manipulation, as always.

Cecilia pretended to sniffle again. "But... but I heard today that if someone likes you, they can't help but touch you. Why does Alexander always avoid physical contact with me?"

Alexander would have loved to touch Cecilia, but Rebecca had forbidden it.

Cecilia had deliberately said this to stab at Rebecca.

Sure enough, Rebecca fell silent for a moment.

When she spoke again, her voice trembled slightly, "Cici, Alexander's just respecting you—saving the perfect moment for your wedding night. Where can you find such a good man these days? How can you be so... vulgar, thinking about such things?"

Cecilia rolled her eyes silently.

More emotional manipulation.

"You're right. It's my fault. I'm overthinking. It's just... sometimes I wish Alexander would hold my hand or kiss me, like other couples do. But he always pulls away from me."

Cecilia was lying.

Alexander had kissed her more than once, and the memory still made her sick. But in Rebecca's eyes, even kissing was forbidden.

Cecilia had said it on purpose, to make Rebecca think both Alexander and she were under her control.

Sure enough, Rebecca's voice brightened.

She comforted Cecilia for a few more minutes before Cecilia hung up, pretending to be sobbing.

As soon as the call ended, Cecilia snorted coldly, "Good. Keep thinking I'm a fool, keep underestimating me. That way, when I get my revenge, it'll be quick and clean."

Turning around, she found Blackie staring at her, his head tilted in confusion.

Her fake crying must have confused him.

Cecilia couldn't help but laugh.

"Human emotions are complicated, buddy. A simple dog like you could never understand."

She reached out and stroked his head gently.

All in all, it had been a successful day. That night, Cecilia wiped Blackie down and let him sleep on the bed with her.

At first, he seemed uncomfortable—unaccustomed to the soft mattress and being held.

Cecilia had already fallen asleep when he quietly climbed down and went to his dog bed.

She didn't want to force him; after all, relationships took time to build. Therefore, Cecilia drifted back to sleep.

To her surprise, when she woke up the next morning, Blackie was sound asleep beside her.

He jolted awake the moment she opened her eyes, his large gaze locking onto hers.

"Good morning, Blackie," Cecilia said, pulling his head close and planting a big kiss on his forehead.

Blackie frowned, his expression turning cold again, like a distant playboy.

However, Cecilia didn't mind. She was in a good mood and could ignore his moodiness.

Her first thought after waking was to check the Space, to see what had grown from the bread roll she'd buried.

To her disappointment, nothing had changed. The bread was still there, untouched.

It seemed the black soil wasn't compatible with everything. She'd need to study it further.

For now, bread was off the list, but maybe future upgrades to the Space would change that.

Putting the bread back, Cecilia exited the Space.

She decided to focus on stockpiling supplies and dealing with Alexander over the next few days.

Once the apocalypse hit, she'd have plenty of time to research the Space and find ways to upgrade it if possible.

After washing up, she took Blackie for a run, combining exercise with his morning walk.

When they returned, she stopped by a nearby breakfast diner to place a large order.

She ordered flaky breakfast croissants, creamy grits, fresh-brewed coffee, crispy corn fritters, soft maple-glazed donuts, savory sausage biscuits, tender boiled sweet corn, smooth oatmeal, rich ham, potato chowder, and so on.
