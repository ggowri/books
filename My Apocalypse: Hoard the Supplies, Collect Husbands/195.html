Chapter 195 The Fall of Grant Cole

"What else do you want? Whatever's still left in my organisation, you can have it all."

Grant Cole was afraid now.

If the power room or boiler house went, they still had stored materials. He could always build them again.

As for firearms, they still had a number left on hand, enough to keep intimidating people for a while.

Since Blaise Hart still had not revealed the locations of the hidden stockpiles, Grant had already started looking for other ways. He had found two men who knew how to make crude guns, and he had even begun contacting local bosses in other districts, planning to barter for more weapons.

But the granary was different.

That was what fed the thousand or so mouths under his control.

More than that, he had been counting on that food surplus to exchange for other supplies with rival groups.

If the granary was lost, then his whole organisation was finished.

"Tell your men not to move. Once Blaise and I have gone a few hundred metres, I'll leave you in the snow. Tell them to come find you after fifteen minutes."

Arthur hauled Grant up from the ground. With Blaise in one arm and Grant in the other, he slowly walked out of the encirclement.

Grant ordered the men around them to lower their guns, then followed Arthur in obvious panic.

Very soon, they had gone several dozen metres.

Seeing Arthur apparently relax a little, Grant quietly slipped one hand into his pocket.

Inside was a tiny pistol, no bigger than half a palm.

He had commissioned it specially. It was exquisitely made, easy to conceal, and surprisingly powerful.

It took a special kind of ammunition. Wherever it hit, the victim would bleed without stopping until they died.

A vicious gleam flashed through Grant's eyes.

Did he really think Grant Cole was so easy to bully? He had blown up so much tonight. There was no way Grant was letting them leave alive.

But the instant Grant drew the gun, he felt someone pinning his hand down.

He stared in disbelief at Blaise, who had seemed unconscious with his eyes closed all this time.

"You… you weren't out cold…"

Blaise smiled, revealing the two little canine teeth at either side.

"Arthur always said I'm young and look harmless. That's my best weapon."

The moment Blaise finished speaking, Grant saw the tall man in front of him flash a sharp dagger across his throat.

Then he was thrown into the snow.

The men who had been following at a distance rushed forward immediately.

But when Grant tried to shout, no sound came out.

All he could feel was a strange tearing hiss at his throat.

He forced himself to look down and realised it was the sound of bright red blood spraying out.

Then he heard a massive explosion.

Several of his men lunged towards him, pressing desperately on his neck to stop the bleeding.

But even then, Grant was frantic. Using the last of his strength, he widened his eyes and asked, "The granary… was it blown up?"

The man clutching his neck was already in tears, but after hearing the question, he froze for a second and nodded.

"It was."

Yes.

The moment Arthur dropped Grant in the snow, he pressed the detonator in his hand and blew up the entire granary.

"Bloody hell."

Those were the last two words Grant Cole ever spoke.

Meanwhile, Arthur and Blaise had already run dozens of metres.

Men were chasing them from behind, firing continuously at both of them.

Up on the building, Cecilia watched Arthur and Blaise running towards her in the distance, with dozens of pursuers on their heels. She stepped behind the sniper rifle she had already set up.

With the help of the night vision scope, she fired.

Cici was not an exceptional shot, but she had trained for some time.

So even if she could not hit a head cleanly, she could still strike an arm, a leg, or a chest.

After several shots, the men chasing Arthur and Blaise realised what was happening and immediately started shouting for retreat.

"Sniper! Fall back!"

"There's a sniper!"

At once, the men pursuing Arthur and Blaise stopped in their tracks.

A few of them even returned fire in Cici's direction.

But her position was high up, and most of those men had barely touched a gun before the apocalypse. Their aim was hopeless.

Cici fired several more rounds, scaring them out of their wits, until at last they all fled in a miserable rout.

Only then did she let out a breath.

Even so, just to be safe, she fired a few more shots along the route they were escaping by.

Only when she saw that everyone nearby had scattered, and heard Arthur's footsteps below as he came with Blaise, did she truly relax.

Arthur, carrying Blaise, called out to her, "Move!"

Cici knew perfectly well they could not stay there any longer. She immediately grabbed Blackie and hurried down to the car.

Arthur drove, while Cici took the passenger seat.

Blaise, weakened by his injuries, lay across the back seat.

Blackie was so squeezed for space that he could only lie on the mat between the front and back seats, his face drooping as he stared at Blaise.

"Thank you."

As he drove at speed, Arthur took something from his pocket and handed it to Cici.

She reached out instinctively and caught it, only then realising it was a small pistol.

It was no bigger than half a palm, silver in colour, with an antique pattern engraved across it. It felt unexpectedly heavy in her hand.

She had never seen a pistol like it before. When she opened the chamber, she found that the bullets inside were rounder than normal rounds, almost perfectly spherical, and just as silver as the gun itself.

"I've never seen ammunition like this."

Cici turned to look at Arthur beside her.

Arthur nodded. "No. Use it sparingly. Those bullets are vicious."
