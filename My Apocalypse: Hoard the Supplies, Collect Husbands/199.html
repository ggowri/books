Chapter 199 Jet's First Love

Cecilia saw a figure rush over from the side, moving so quickly it was almost a blur, and in one swift motion Arthur caught Snowy by the scruff of the neck.

Snowy's four little paws flailed wildly in mid air, clearly outraged by the situation.

"This little creature is far too troublesome. Was Blackie like this when he was small as well?"

With a helpless look, Arthur tucked Snowy inside his coat and pulled the zip up, completely restricting her movements.

Only a plump little furry head was left sticking out, with a pair of glossy black eyes darting everywhere. Her tiny paws were still paddling furiously, but stuffed inside his coat as she was, there was very little she could do about it.

The commotion made Blaise Hart frown in his sleep and turn over on the sofa.

"Why don't you let me take Snowy? I can have Blackie look after her for a couple of days. She might settle down a bit," Cici said with a smile.

Arthur nodded at once.

And so Cici brought Snowy home with her.

Jet, who had been shut inside all this time, had been craning his neck in eager anticipation. When he first saw Cici come back carrying something white, he was thrilled.

The moment he realised it was a smelly little dog, however, his head drooped.

Blackie's face drooped as well.

Because the human wanted him to babysit.

"You can think of her as your little future bride, your little future bride," Cici said with a grin, patting Blackie on the head.

To her surprise, Blackie was having none of it. He slapped Snowy straight to the floor with one paw, then bared his teeth at her as if to say, Misbehave and I'll flatten you.

He looked every inch the hopelessly unromantic straight laced male.

Meanwhile, Jet hopped about excitedly. "Human, if we're handing out future brides, get me a dozen!"

Cici flicked him smartly on the forehead. "Dream on."

Who would have thought that when she refused to indulge him, Jet would immediately start performing.

He launched into an endless account of his heroic deeds while guarding the house alone the day before.

"I, Jet, leapt high and low and scratched those two leggers until they howled!"

"Even with an injured wing, I still stood tall and proud and refused to let those two leggers cross the threshold!"

The meaning behind it all could not have been more obvious.

Jet is a hero. Surely it isn't too much to ask for a wife?

Cici was exhausted and starving after the night she had had. At that moment she was holding a bowl of fish stew with pickled greens and eating it with pasta at tremendous speed.

She treated Jet's endless chatter as though he were putting on a comic routine.

Before long, her mind began to drift.

She started thinking about what to do next.

The small laboratory on the sixty fifth floor needed to be set up. Colonel Reed ought to deliver that batch of mutant cockroach corpses today.

Then there was the Barnes family. Jeremy Barnes was dead now, but Nathan Barnes was still alive. And Mabel had to be dealt with as well.

And then there was the further upgrade of the Space…

The more she thought, the further away her mind wandered, until she was barely paying any attention to what Jet was saying at all.

Then she vaguely heard Jet pipe up with, "Right, human?"

Cici answered absent mindedly, "Mm."

"Brilliant! Human's agreed to take me on a blind date!"

Jet gave a delighted shriek.

Cici froze, then turned to look at him, now bouncing up and down in excitement and paying no attention whatsoever to his injured wing.

What exactly had just happened?

Nearby, Blackie, his face drooping as always, dragged Snowy back yet again when she tried to rush off and chew the sofa.

Can nobody let me have a moment's peace?

Cici patiently began explaining to Jet the importance of making a good first impression.

"Look at the state of your wing. You can't even fly properly right now. There's nothing impressive about that. How are you supposed to leave a good impression on Silver?"

Of course, the truth was that Silver had not spared Jet so much as a glance earlier. Her attention had been entirely fixed on Blaise.

Jet blinked. "So the pretty little lady's called Silver?"

Cici twitched at the corners of her mouth.

Were you listening to a single word I said?

She carried on anyway.

"We live next door. That gives you every advantage. And there aren't any other mutant birds around here for the time being. You don't need to worry, and there's no need to rush."

The moment she said that, Jet suddenly began to wail.

"Other people's parrots can lay eggs when they're only one year old! I'm already three, three whole years old, and I'm still a bachelor! Jet is already three and still a bachelor!"

Cici was stunned.

They had been having a perfectly reasonable conversation. How had this turned into a full emotional collapse?

What Cici did not know was that from the day Jet was born until now, he had never once even seen a female parrot.

At most, he had seen the little sparrows outside the window, some of them female.

But those sparrows had taken one look at the chain still attached to his foot and the cage he lived in, and had all promptly looked down on him.

Even sparrows did not fancy Jet.

So now, after finally reaching the grand old age of three, which in the parrot world made him an extremely over age bachelor, the kind people would laugh at, he had at last seen a beautiful little white female bird.

Naturally, he was beside himself with excitement.

He had practically wanted to throw himself at Silver on the spot and declare his love.

Cici spent a very long time soothing him. She even gave him his favourite pine cone before he finally calmed down.

But this time, Jet did not eat the pine cone straight away.

Instead, he tucked it carefully beneath his wing, saying he was saving it as a gift for the pretty lady when they next met.

At that, Cici could only roll her eyes.

Do as you like. Once they were grown, there was no keeping children at home.

Over on the other side, under Blackie's stern supervision, Snowy was being fairly well behaved. She had even learned how to play with dog toys and was now wriggling her little backside furiously on the floor while attacking a plush rabbit with great enthusiasm.

She had quite forgotten about the sofa.
