Chapter 62 Building Manager

Cecilia's hand paused as she closed the window.

She fell silent for a moment, then decided to tell the truth, "Yes. Blackie's grown a lot lately."

Now that she'd agreed to cooperate with Arthur—and he'd just saved her life—they were temporary teammates, after all.

"I've seen plenty of dogs, including many high-quality military dogs, back in the army. But I've never seen a Doberman this big, especially its head—it's disproportionately large."

Cecilia silently praised Arthur's sharp observation.

"Looks like we'll have to watch out for more than just people from now on."

Determination hardening his gaze, Arthur added, "We should seal all the pipe openings first. I found cement in one of the apartments earlier. If you agree, I suggest we seal every pipe opening with it."

Cecilia nodded at his words. "I agree."

They exchanged a glance, both seeing the worry in each other's eyes.

Just then, Cecilia heard a dog bark—Blackie's.

It was alerting her; someone was coming from upstairs.

When she reached the door, Cecilia saw Blackie crouched low, its body tensed in an attack stance, its expression ferocious as it growled low in its throat.

At the same time, she heard another low growl—another dog.

Drawing closer, she spotted a large wolf dog outside the door, growling alongside Blackie.

Judging by its size, it was mutated, too.

Cecilia's eyes widened slightly as her gaze shifted to the man holding the wolf dog's leash.

To her surprise, it was someone she knew—Bryan.

"Hey there. We didn't get properly introduced last time. I'm Bryan Hanks, live on the 15th floor, and I'm the building manager now," Bryan said with a smirk.

"Building manager? Who elected you?" Cecilia looked at the man, her tone dripping with disdain.

Bryan ignored her sarcasm, patting the wolf dog's head. "The neighbors, of course. We all voted me in."

"He's lying! He's a scumbag!" A soft, tearful voice echoed from downstairs.

Cecilia leaned over the railing and saw Phoebe—the beautiful woman who ran the private kitchen on the 65th floor.

She looked disheveled, her clothes askew, a button torn off her dress, and blood on her arm. Clutching a kitchen knife, she stood at the stairwell landing, pointing the blade at Bryan. "You bastard! I'll fight you to the death today!"

Cecilia could guess what Phoebe had been through.

But Bryan didn't care, shoving the wolf dog toward her. "Beauty, what are you doing? Everyone chose me as manager to keep us united through this crisis. Don't cause trouble."

The wolf dog bared its teeth at Phoebe, ready to pounce.

Phoebe collapsed to the floor, sobbing and shouting curses at Bryan.

He paid no mind, his eyes roaming over her body as if savoring a memory.

Then Hailey appeared, helping Phoebe to her feet and pulling her downstairs while comforting her.

Before leaving, Hailey spat at Bryan. "You'll burn in hell!"

Bryan stared at Hailey's curvy figure, his eyes filled with greed. "Sure thing—come drag me there yourself, babe."

It was clear the two girls had fallen victim to Bryan's cruelty.

Cecilia's expression darkened as she looked at the triumphant Bryan. "What you do is your business, but stay away from me. Otherwise..."

Sensing its owner's anger, Blackie suddenly erupted, growling fiercely at the wolf dog through the door.

Its demeanor was completely different from before—Cecilia had originally ordered it to stand guard at the door without attacking, but now it refused to hold back, feeding off her rage.

The wolf dog, which had been growling back at Blackie moments earlier, suddenly cowered.

Its ears drooped, its tail tucked between its legs, and it backed away, pulling Bryan with it.

"General! General, sit!" Bryan struggled to yank the leash.

Emboldened by the wolf dog's fear, Blackie barked louder, even reaching its paws through the railing.

The wolf dog, General, panicked and tried to bolt downstairs, yanking Bryan off his feet and sending him sprawling on his face.

Cecilia laughed out loud.

Bryan let go of the leash, and the dog raced downstairs, disappearing in an instant.

Once she'd finished laughing, Cecilia patted Blackie's head to calm it. "There, there, I know you're tough. That's enough. They're just losers—no need to waste energy on them."

Bryan stood up, wiping blood from his face. Spotting several onlookers downstairs, he flew into a rage, shouting at them, "What are you looking at? I'll set the dog on you!"

Cecilia snorted coldly to herself—a classic case of picking on the weak and fearing the strong. A coward, plain and simple.

Meanwhile, Bryan had lost all interest in flirting with Cecilia. He marched over, his face gloomy with malice. "I'm telling you, I'm the building manager now! So what if you have a big dog? You still have to listen to me! Otherwise, my buddies won't go easy on you!"
