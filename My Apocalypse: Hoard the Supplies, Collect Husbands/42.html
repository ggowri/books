Chapter 42 Scarce Goods

It was clear the boss usually worried about everything.

On the shelves were bottles of herbal tonics infused with things like ginseng, dried berries, and honey, alongside various vitamins, health supplements, and wellness books.

As Cecilia collected these, she thought to herself that running a company this big really must take a toll on the boss.

When she circled around to the desk, she spotted a young bald man holding a large bouquet, a red sash draped over his shoulder reading "Top 10 Entrepreneurs of Heldon City."

His head shone like polished marble, completely hairless. She wondered if all the stress from work had worn him down this way.

There were other items she didn't quite recognize, but when she reached for a stone jar, her Space trembled again.

She hurriedly stored everything inside before investigating further.

There was no time to check the Space for changes yet, so she kept sweeping the office.

On the boss' desk, Cecilia found plenty of luxury items like watches, necklaces, designer handbags, and three safes.

She couldn't crack the safes, so she tucked them into her Space for later.

She also found a cabinet stocked with premium cigars, boxed gourmet chocolates, and gift sets of coffee.

Looking at the coffee selection, she raised an eyebrow. There was literally everything.

Dark roast, medium roast, light roast, espresso blends, and even specialty flavored coffees were all neatly arranged.

Cecilia knew plenty of people had a daily coffee habit.

In the apocalypse, these beans would become hot commodities among the big players in any organization.

The cigars and gourmet chocolates were even rarer.

They were truly scarce goods.

It was obvious these were the boss' go-to gifts for networking and currying favor. She could tell he spent a lot of effort maintaining connections.

Previously, her funds had been limited, so she had passed up buying these luxury items. But now, seeing them, she couldn't let them go.

"The Boss'" office also had a small lounge, probably meant for his occasional rest.

On the wall above the seating area was a sign that simply read "Nap."

Cecilia kicked the door open and saw an enormous, incredibly plush bed.

Without a second thought, she swept it into her Space.

The closet held high-end suits, unopened down comforters, and wool blankets; everything went straight into her Space as well.

Even the desk and the obviously expensive chair didn't escape her.

Everything was stored in her Space, right inside that massive villa.

To her surprise, she also spotted a large glass tank in the boss' bedroom.

Inside floated a white, slightly translucent object.

The tank itself was fancy. It was gold-trimmed, gleaming, and far more luxurious than the cheap ones she had seen outside.

Cecilia figured that if something was kept in such an extravagant container in the inner room, it had to be special.

Whatever it was, she waved her hand, and even the tank joined her Space.

In the boss' lounge, Cecilia wandered around a bit more.

Something about the room felt off.

After exploring several floors, she noticed the layout here was unusual, nothing like the other areas.

As expected, after a few turns, she discovered a hidden door behind a curtain. She had to pull the whole curtain aside to see it.

Inside was a small, dimly lit room. Under the flickering lights, everything glowed a dark red.

In the center of the room was a giant hexagram pattern on the floor, surrounded by a few burnt-out candles.

The scene reminded Cecilia of something out of a movie.

It seemed the boss followed some kind of belief or ritual she didn't understand.

After searching the little room, Cecilia found several solid gold items. Of course, she collected them all into her Space.

Then, to her delight, Cecilia found a replica handgun.

Yes, it was only a fake, but it looked incredibly realistic. It was well-crafted, solid, and heavy in her hands.

However, it had no bullets.

It was obvious the gun was meant for ceremonial purposes, not for actual use.

Still, if she ever ran into someone reckless, it could definitely serve to intimidate.

She had always wanted a real firearm, but local laws forbade it, and she had no connections abroad.

Even with her Space and money, she was just a regular student, a law-abiding citizen. There was no one to introduce her to a weapons dealer.

So she had to wait for the apocalypse, when all the rules fell apart, before figuring out a real solution.

While here, Cecilia also came across two more safes, even fancier than the ones she had collected before. She couldn't open them yet, but they went straight into her Space.

After clearing out the boss' office, Cecilia moved up to the next floor.

It was a research and development department.

There were seeds, hydroponic plants, and all sorts of hydroponic equipment.

Without a second thought, Cecilia stashed everything into her Space.

By the time she was done, the entire floor was cleared.

With all this, she could finally start growing hydroponic vegetables in her Space.

She had plenty of rich soil inside, but growing random weeds or rabbit fodder didn't exactly inspire confidence.

The seventh floor sold exported clothing and bedding.

Here, Cecilia didn't just take piles of clothes; she also hoarded a large stock of fabrics.

After the acid rain, many people's clothes were torn or ruined, and dressing decently to go outside had become a rare luxury.
