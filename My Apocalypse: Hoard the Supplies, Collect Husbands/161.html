Chapter 161 An Unexpected Encounter

"Mmmph… mmmph…"

Blackie immediately moved to block Cecilia.

What Cecilia didn't expect was that a low growl from another dog came from the opposite direction as well.

When the beam of her headlamp shone forward, Cecilia saw a man and a dog that had originally been charging toward her.

First was the dog.

It was a German Shepherd, standing nearly 1.7 to 1.8 meters tall, about the size of a small horse, and extremely thin, practically skin and bones.

Although its fur looked thick, its long dog face looked as flat and bony as a bicycle seat, with almost no flesh on it.

Unlike Blackie, whose face was chubby and full.

Beside the dog stood a man.

In the apocalypse, this man could be considered quite plump.

He was over six feet tall and probably weighed more than 180 pounds. Just from the portion of his face that was exposed, it was obvious his living conditions had been fairly good.

The most eye-catching thing was the red armband on his sleeve, with the words "Sky Slayers" printed on it, arrogant and ostentatious.

At the very least, compared to the last time Cecilia had seen him, he looked much better now.

Yes, Cecilia had seen this man before.

He was the one who had been chatting with his partner on the twenty-third floor and mentioned that he had a dog at home.

And that dog…

Cecilia suddenly remembered that when the apocalypse had just begun, Bryan and his group had once brought a large wolfdog to intimidate her.

It seemed that this man had already been associating with Bryan back then.

Cecilia recalled that after the official base came to pick people up for the first time, she never saw this man again.

Perhaps at that time, the man discovered that his dog had mutated and used that opportunity to enter the official base.

Later, for some reason, he must have left the base again.

"Cecilia?"

The man's hoarse voice revealed that he clearly recognized her.

"Hey, you know this girl? She's the best-looking one we've seen in a while."

"Hahaha, our boss was just saying yesterday that the women we've been running into lately were nothing special. Look at that, and today one just shows up!"

…

Cecilia followed the direction of the voices and discovered that behind the pile of chairs stood four or five men.

They were wearing identical outdoor jackets, each with a red armband on their sleeves. It was obvious they were part of the same group.

Among them, Cecilia noticed another familiar face.

It was the man who had once called the leader "brother" back on the twenty-third floor.

Cecilia had later seen the two of them fighting each other fiercely over a bit of food. She hadn't expected them to end up together again.

When the leading man realized it was Cecilia, he began to grin.

His name was Daniel Brooks, and his dog was called "General."

At first, he had planned to kill General and eat it.

After all, even feeding himself had become difficult, let alone a dog.

Later, Bryan's group saw how imposing General looked and wanted to take the dog out to intimidate people. They borrowed General and even gave Daniel Brooks some benefits for it.

That was the only reason Daniel Brooks didn't kill General.

Later Bryan died, and the members of his group either died or scattered. General eventually returned to Daniel Brooks.

That day, Daniel Brooks had truly sharpened the knife and was ready to kill General.

When General saw the knife, it seemed to understand its fate. It whimpered and lowered its head to look at him.

At that moment, Daniel Brooks had a strange feeling.

General seemed to have become smarter, different from before.

Looking closer, Daniel Brooks realized that General had actually grown a little bigger.

General had always been a large dog, and during the days it had been gone, Daniel Brooks initially assumed that perhaps he himself had been starving and dizzy lately, which made the dog appear larger than before.

Just as he raised the knife, General suddenly broke free and ran down the stairs.

Daniel Brooks tried to chase after it, but how could someone who hadn't eaten properly keep up?

Daniel Brooks cursed General for a long time and returned home, resigned to waiting for death.

Yet less than two hours later, General actually came back.

And it brought a backpack filled with food.

After that, Daniel Brooks repeatedly sent General out to search for food, and that was how they survived through the acid rain period.

He also clearly realized that General had grown bigger and had become smarter.

The word "mutation" appeared in his mind.

He was overjoyed. Because of General, he followed the official boats to the base.

The government confiscated General but compensated him with plenty of supplies and a fairly decent job inside the shelter.

He originally thought that with the dog, he would finally be able to live a comfortable life.

But soon he discovered that the base was chaotic.

The supplies given to him by the government were quickly stolen. The place he lived in was filthy.

Even at work, people constantly made things difficult for him.

Only then did he realize that without General, he was nothing.

So he secretly stole General back from the military. After letting the dog steal some supplies again, he prepared to leave the place.

He had already thought it through.

As long as he could find somewhere without the military around, with General, this mutated dog, he could easily dominate the area.

Later he formed this small group.

Even Jake Torres, his former partner who had once fallen out with him, came to join him.

That man who once had a better background than him, more startup resources, and stronger abilities now looked like he wanted to kneel at General's paws and call it father.

And now, as Daniel Brooks looked at Cecilia standing before him and remembered all the humiliation he had suffered in the apartment before, he laughed and said,

"Heh, Cecilia. I'd say your dog doesn't look as strong as mine. How about you follow me instead? As long as we stay away from those soldiers, the two of us could rule this whole area."
