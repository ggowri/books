Chapter 86 A Different Woman

Cecilia released Hailey and walked over to Kent. Looking down at this pathetic man, she kicked him hard. "Do you know how Ashley died?"

Kent collapsed to the floor, listless, and shook his head slightly.

"Do you know why she died?"

Kent shook his head again.

Cecilia almost laughed. This man knew nothing, yet he came up here to pick a fight.

Before she could say more, the man on the floor leapt up again, jutting his chin out defiantly as he shouted at the group. "No matter how my mom died! They all said you're involved, so it has to be true!"

Cecilia caught the key word. "Who are 'they'?"

"The neighbors who came to get supplies. They all said it's your fault! If you don't give me an answer, I won't let this go. Especially that one named Cecilia! I want her to pay with her life for my mom!" Kent roared.

Cecilia could tell he was a complete idiot.

But those neighbors who came for supplies... to think they'd turn around and smear them. They really thought they were pushovers.

Fine. If they showed no mercy, they'd better not blame her for being ruthless later.

"Your mother, Ashley, ate through other people's supplies, which is like cutting off their lifeline. How could you let her wander around, stealing food everywhere?

"If you're a decent man, you should have saved your own rations to feed her!" Cecilia said coldly.

"Nonsense! If my mom stole their supplies, why didn't they say so? Why did they say you killed her? Besides, if you can't guard your own supplies, the others can take them. They're just more capable than you!"

Like mother, like son. Kent was just as shameless as Ashley.

Kent staggered, trying to stand, but Cecilia kicked him down again.

All that going up and down stairs lately was like training; her leg strength wasn't for nothing.

"You really are stupid. You came over just because the others told you to? Weren't you so filial? Where were you when they were beating Ashley to death?

"Oh, right. Where were you when your mother was stabbed? Her blood was pouring out, and not a single person dared to speak up for her.

"We didn't kill her! If you have the guts, go find the real killer. Stop making a scene."

Cecilia stared at the man, her fierce glare making Kent shrink back in fear.

"Fix the door. Bring three bottles of water up here and apologize to Hailey. Otherwise, I'll ransack your apartment tonight."

Cecilia didn't want to waste more breath on Kent. Her tone was icy.

Hearing this, Kent kept shouting, "If it wasn't you, then who! Who was it?!"

Cecilia slammed him against the wall. "Didn't you always listen to your dear mother? Your mom controlled everything. I'm not your mother. Why should I tell you who killed her? Figure it out yourself!"

A sharp dagger pressed against Kent's neck. "Do as I said, or die. Your choice. I know your son is a real brat. He crossed me before, but I let him go. Should I slaughter him along with you?"

Now Kent was truly terrified. He felt he shouldn't have provoked them.

The anger over his mother's death was now completely replaced by fear. But he didn't want to give up completely. He could only give in for now and agree to Cecilia's demands.

He straightened the door he had broken and gave Hailey the only three bottles of water left in his home before slinking away.

All of this happened to be witnessed by Alexander, who had just come upstairs.

Recalling Cecilia's expression and tone, he shivered. He felt this was no longer the Cecilia he knew.

But seeing Arthur nearby, he gritted his teeth and rushed forward. "Uncle! Uncle!"

Arthur frowned slightly, looking at Alexander outside the door. "Don't call me 'uncle' anymore. I'm not part of your family."

Desperate for a boat to get out, Alexander plucked up his courage. "Uncle, I know you have a boat. Can you lend it to me? I'm really starving. Just... for the sake of our shared blood."

Having just dealt with Kent and now seeing Alexander, Cecilia felt thoroughly annoyed by the day's chaotic events.

She crossed her arms, offering a thin, insincere smile to Arthur. "Your family matter, you handle it. After all, 'shared blood' can always be an excuse. Might as well tie Alexander to your belt later."

They weren't short on boats now. Giving Alexander one wouldn't hurt. But if they give him what he wanted this time, what about next time? Could Alexander always rely on this flimsy "family tie"?

Cecilia had long planned to take care of Alexander and Rebecca, but considering the Ice Age was imminent, and the fifth floor was already corroded by acid rain, those two were living a living hell.

She thought she'd let them suffer a few more days.

But Alexander and Rebecca kept coming, wearing her patience thin.

With that, Cecilia turned and went back inside, washing her hands of it.

Arthur looked at Alexander and said flatly, "I have no boat."

He too turned and went inside, ignoring him.
