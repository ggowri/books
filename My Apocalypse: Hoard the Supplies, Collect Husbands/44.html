Chapter 44 Intruder

Cecilia and Blackie stayed hidden inside the Space. There wasn't much to do, but she didn't dare let her guard down. So she took out a container of barbecue ribs with mashed potatoes and a cup of hot milk, eating slowly while keeping one ear on the sounds outside.

Blackie got a serving too. It was plain ribs with mashed potatoes and no seasoning, plus a bowl of water.

Cecilia had cooked everything earlier at home, especially for him.

One human, one dog—both ate with real appetite.

Only after finishing the meal did Cecilia feel like she'd finally come back to herself.

She'd been so focused on gathering supplies that she hadn't even noticed how close she was to starving.

After eating, Blackie seemed to understand that both it and its owner were safe. It lay down and dozed off.

Cecilia kept listening. Over the next hour or so, the intruder made about three trips, moving supplies at lightning speed.

Judging by the dragging sounds, each haul was no small load.

By around seven o'clock, when daylight had fully broken, the man finally stopped coming back.

Even so, with the sun fully up, Cecilia didn't dare head home.

When that man first arrived, the building had been packed with supplies. Now, it was practically stripped bare.

Worse still, he had followed the sound of Blackie's footsteps to get here.

If her identity were exposed, she would be in serious danger.

So she decided to take advantage of the early hour, when there were still few people around. She would finish clearing this floor, then retreat back into the Space for a full day to properly study the Space's recent upgrades.

Once night fell, she would take a longer route, climb over the walls, and slip back into her residential complex alone.

With that plan in mind, Cecilia stepped out of the Space with Blackie at her side.

That man moved fast and with purpose. He swept up every spicy item on the entire floor with pinpoint accuracy. In one particular room, there had been employee gift packages stacked neatly, clearly labeled on the desk as daily necessities and spicy food gift sets. Now, they were completely gone.

Judging by the number of employees on this floor, Cecilia figured he had taken at least several dozen boxes.

Anything filling or carb-heavy was gone as well. Every cabinet stood wide open. There was even a small private kitchenette, and all the cooking oil, grains, and pantry staples inside had been cleaned out to the last drop.

Even the general manager's office at the very top hadn't been spared; the man had forced it open too.

Interestingly, the premium liquor and cigars inside were left untouched, but the cabinets on either side of the manager's desk were completely empty.

Seeing this, Cecilia raised an eyebrow. That tall guy clearly knew how to survive. And leaving the alcohol alone? It was not a bad habit to have.

She made another round through the rooms and spotted a safe tucked away in a corner.

No hesitation—straight into her Space.

The twelfth floor was mostly open-plan offices. Aside from the small kitchenette, there weren't many supplies left.

After checking a few rooms, Cecilia only found some imported snacks, bottled drinks, and meal-replacement powders meant for dieting office workers.

That said, the desks and chairs were all solid wood. They were perfect firewood for later.

She also took the full projector setup from the conference room. Judging by the brand and model, it was practically new and definitely expensive.

She could set it up in her villa as a home theater.

Whether she'd actually use it or not didn't matter. She'd plan for it first.

The biggest surprise came at the far end of the floor. There stood a decorative stone feature, taller than a person.

As Cecilia approached it, her Space gave a faint tremor. Her eyes lit up. The stone was also kept in the Space.

After wrapping everything up, Cecilia ran through the upper floors twice more. Only when she was sure the entire building had been stripped clean did she finally feel satisfied.

Cecilia wasn't completely unaware that nearby residents might come looking for supplies later.

First of all, once the apocalypse hit, it was survival of the fittest. First come, first served was the only rule that mattered. A moment of softness or hesitation could turn you into prey.

Second, with acid rain on the way, everything left behind here would soon corrode anyway. Better that it ended up in her hands.

After clearing out the entire floor, Cecilia took Blackie back into her Space.

Even though she had been busy all night, she wasn't tired at all.

The haul had simply been too good. She had practically stripped an entire twelve-story building bare.

What really woke her up was what she saw next. The moment she entered the Space, she realized it had upgraded again. Her spirits lifted instantly.

The biggest change was a small floating screen hovering in front of her. On it was the words: [Spirit Space 4.0.]

Cecilia was thrilled. Was this finally a user manual? It almost brought tears to her eyes.

She quickly tapped the screen.

The display shifted, revealing several options: [Happy Farm, Care Ranch, and Recycler.]

Cecilia raised an eyebrow and clicked on "Recycler" first. It was the one she understood the least.

[Recycler]

[Level: 1]

[Function: Place any unwanted item into the machine, and the next day it will randomly produce useful supplies.]

[Limit: Can be used once per day.]

[Note: This machine runs on pure luck.]

Cecilia was stunned. So the machine behind the house, the one that had swallowed her throw pillow, was actually this useful?

This was practically a magic tool that turned trash into treasure.

She couldn't wait to see what her pillow from yesterday had been transformed into.

Cecilia hurried toward the back of the house, only to find that the recycler looked exactly the same as before.
