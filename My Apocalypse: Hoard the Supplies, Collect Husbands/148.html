Chapter 148 Learn His Lesson

Richard's face fell with disappointment.

Cecilia couldn't give Richard the Magic Spring water.

The stuff was too powerful—practically heaven-defying.

Even diluted, it was stronger than regular medicine and could cure almost anything.

Plus, by saying her grandma left it behind before she died, Cecilia was trying to kill any hope he'd ask for more.

But Cecilia smiled. "Actually, I've got something else—another medicine my grandma left me. Works great on injuries. Interested?"

She meant the "Injury Ointment" from the Space.

She'd studied everything the Space produced.

Rabbit meat was just rabbit meat. Socks were normal socks.

Nothing weird in the ingredients.

Just... better quality.

She'd used that injury ointment on Blackie before. Worked well. Strong herbal smell, dark and goopy, decent results—nothing crazy like "regrowing bones" or whatever.

Perfect for doing business with Richard.

Richard perked up, grinning. "Not bad. How much you got?"

Cecilia smiled. "Not a ton. Can't make more without materials, and I need to keep some for emergencies. Maybe five jars I can spare."

Richard didn't hesitate. "Deal. I won't even check the goods. You bring it, I'll make it worth your while."

Cecilia's smile widened. "Perfect."

"So what do you want for these life-saving jars?" Richard asked.

Cecilia had already been thinking about that.

She wasn't hurting for supplies right now. And she had ways to get more soon.

Like those two deals with Logan.

"I want medicinal herbs. The kind you can plant. Any type works." Cecilia said.

She had her reasons.

Plot One and Plot Two in the Space always surprised her.

Medicine was scarce in the apocalypse. If she could grow and produce it later...

"The military does have a research facility preserving and cultivating medicinal plants. That shouldn't be hard to arrange."

Just like that, Cecilia and Richard struck a deal. Both satisfied.

After wrapping up, Cecilia went back to grab the five jars of injury ointment.

She'd actually been storing them in the Space, making batches whenever she had time.

Five jars. Perfect.

Speaking of which—those two ground beetles the garbage processor had spawned? Absolute breeding machines.

In no time flat, they'd filled two entire cabinets in the Space.

And they were still multiplying.

Production on point.

Though the original two beetles were way bigger than their offspring. Easy to spot.

The OGs of this whole cabinet full of beetles.

After finishing with Richard and completing the trade, Cecilia headed out—and found Arthur standing outside.

Weird.

"What's wrong?" she asked.

The big guy was frowning, looking annoyed and... scared?

He didn't say anything. His face was pale as hell.

Cecilia frowned. What's this about?

But she worried something had happened.

"Seriously, what's going on? Did something happen to Blackie?"

Arthur sighed first. "Think Captain Jackman's gonna show up at our door with a gun again?"

The second Arthur finished, Richard bristled behind them.

"What? Logan pointed a gun at you? Has that brat lost his damn mind?"

Inside, Logan—who'd been handling paperwork—suddenly felt a chill run down his spine.

Moments later, a soldier came up to relay a message: Richard wanted to see him.

When Richard exploded and ordered his men to drag Logan over, Cecilia finally realized—Arthur was tattling.

Once Logan entered the room, Cecilia caught Arthur flashing her a grin, his little canines showing.

"Just squeezing a few goodies out of Logan? Not nearly enough. He needs to learn his lesson."

Cecilia gave him a thumbs up. Damn. Respect.

Logan got absolutely reamed. Richard called him rigid.

"We need all the help we can get right now. What's the point of pissing off someone useful? You're too damn stubborn—can't adapt to save your life!"

Richard slammed his cane on the ground.

Logan didn't dare talk back. Truth was, he'd already been reflecting on this.

Even his right-hand man Barry had just talked to him about it.

"Captain, our Blackie doesn't make the wrong call. We raised him—we knew him. If he killed those psychos, he had a damn good reason. It's the apocalypse. Maybe others don't get it, but we've got intel from top experts. This isn't ending anytime soon. So why'd you storm up there waving a gun around?"

So now, all Logan could do was stand there and take Richard's lecture.

After a long silence, he finally managed: "General Caldwell, that guy Arthur? Former special forces, joined a mercenary group after. Skilled fighter, has firearms. I just... didn't want trouble while we're setting up the new shelter."
