Chapter 36 Upgraded

This time, the starlight was more stunning than anything she'd seen before. Mixed in with the blue sparkles were flecks of silver that shimmered as they floated by.

Cecilia scooped up Blackie and brought him inside with her. She held him close as they watched the magical display together.

Blackie looked up with his mouth hanging open and his eyes wide, completely mesmerized by what he was seeing. When some of the starlight drifted close to him, he playfully tried to snap at it with his teeth, but of course, he just bit into the empty air.

This time, the starlight took about ten minutes to fully disperse before it finally faded away.

Cecilia looked around carefully, waiting to see what changes would happen in the Space, when suddenly she felt a massive force push her and Blackie right out. She stood there completely thrown off for a second.

What just happened?

She tried to enter the Space again, but after five or six attempts, nothing worked. It was like the Space had locked itself down on purpose, and that made her seriously anxious. All her supplies were in there.

Cecilia paced around the room nervously, and Blackie started following her in circles like he was copying her movements. That actually made her laugh. "What do you think you're doing, copying me?"

Blackie tilted his head and stared at her with that droopy dog face that basically said, "Who's copying you?"

That night, Cecilia barely slept. But she'd survived the apocalypse before, so she wasn't about to fall apart now.

She pulled herself together, cooked up some food that would keep and stored it in the fridge, and then stuck to her daily routine and exercised with Blackie as usual.

Around midnight, she woke up to vibrations coming from the pendant at her chest. She bolted upright in bed and immediately looked down at her chest.

The pendant had changed color. Besides the original ruby section, there was now a streak of vivid green. The green sat at one corner of the red, making the whole emerald look more alive and dynamic.

Cecilia took a deep breath. She could feel that the pendant was different now.

Blackie woke up too from all the commotion and sat up in bed, staring at her with his usual droopy expression. She scratched his neck to calm him down, and then she tried to enter the Space again with her mind.

This time, it worked.

Once Cecilia got inside, she realized the Space had completely transformed.

First off, the black farmland had expanded again, and it was clearly divided into two separate zones now.

One zone was in the same location as before, but the soil was even darker and richer. When she picked up a handful, she could see tiny blue specks scattered throughout, like it contained some kind of strange power.

The other zone covered about ten acres or so. The soil was still black but not as dramatic, more like regular topsoil, except it felt incredibly soft and fertile when she touched it.

Beyond the farmland, the little cottage had upgraded into a two-story villa.

Cecilia walked inside and found that aside from the original small room that stayed the same, the villa had added about three thousand square feet of space.

Besides the living room between the two floors, there were seven or eight empty rooms that looked pretty practical. The interior was pretty basic though, and aside from the furniture she'd already put in there before, it was completely empty.

She stood there looking at the villa and rubbed her chin. Something about it felt off.

As far as the Space went, this villa didn't really seem that useful. She and Blackie weren't going to live in here permanently or anything. But after walking through the whole place, she couldn't figure out what made it special, so she headed back outside.

When she walked around to the back of the villa, she saw that where the Magic Spring used to start, there was now a small mountain rising up about a hundred feet or so.

The spring water flowed down from the top of the mountain, giving the whole scene this ethereal, almost spiritual quality.

Cecilia looked at the soil on the mountainside and noticed it was just as fertile as the farmland, so she figured she could probably use that too. Then, in the backyard behind the villa, she spotted a massive black machine sitting there.

The machine had a big opening at the back that looked like she could toss things into it, but there was no manual or instructions anywhere, so she had no idea what it was actually for.

The storage areas had turned into rows of brick buildings, and all the supplies were neatly organized by category. The area that used to be hidden under thick fog had expanded even more.

Cecilia spotted a patch of grassland enclosed by fencing that looked exactly like a huge ranch.

Seeing this made her feel excited and frustrated at the same time. She was thrilled because having a ranch meant she could raise livestock now, which meant plenty of meat in the future. But she was also annoyed because she didn't have any actual animals to put in there.

She stood there rubbing her chin, debating whether she should head out while the rain wasn't too heavy yet and find somewhere to buy some live animals.

That was when she suddenly heard a rustling sound behind her, and it made her jump.

The Space had always been dead silent except for her own footsteps. After all, she was the only living person in here aside from Blackie.

When did something else show up?

Cecilia's mind flashed, and the knife appeared in her hand instantly.
