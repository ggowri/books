Chapter 58 Collecting Acid Rain

Isn't Plot One supposed to be where all sorts of strange supplies grow?

I've never seen it sprout anything before!

But Cecilia knew she couldn't rush things. Having a reaction was good enough.

Moreover, these sprouting plants on Plot One were clearly somewhat special. Each one wasn't an ordinary green sprout but rather multi-colored, looking as if they were toxic.

Cecilia couldn't figure out the farmland and could only give up for now, planning to check again in a few more days.

She then looked at Plot Two. The seeds sown there had also sprouted.

However, unlike Plot One, the sprouts here were completely normal, just ordinary little shoots, looking promising.

She squatted on the ground and watched for a while, then happily went to check the Recycler behind the house.

Sure enough, the front cover of the Recycler had opened again.

She looked inside and found it actually contained a down jacket.

It was very thick, the fabric was waterproof, the fill weight was heavy, and it should be able to withstand temperatures of dozens of degrees below zero.

Cecilia already had quite a few down jackets and even things like mink coats.

But this item wasn't useful to her.

She thought for a moment, took the down jacket out, and put it back into the rear entrance.

She wanted to see if items produced by the Space could be treated as waste for secondary processing.

The Recycler swallowed the down jacket again.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow, curious about what would be produced tomorrow.

Her understanding of the Space was still minimal.

And judging from the manual, her Space was currently at a very low-level stage.

Later, I'll still need to find ways to upgrade the Space as much as possible.

Thinking this, Cecilia couldn't help but feel like rolling her eyes.

The Space is useful, alright, but it's a bottomless pit! You have to constantly find energy to supply it.

Of course, the benefits brought by the Space were also considerable.

She went to check on the little animals in the livestock area and found everyone living well. The rabbits were lively and cute, the black-faced goat and the alpaca had become good friends, bleating at Cecilia, and the guinea pigs were happily hopping around in circles.

Only then did she feel reassured and leave the Space.

Once outside, Jet immediately flew over to Cecilia, clinging and nuzzling against her.

Blackie just lifted an eyelid, watching Jet's actions without much reaction.

But judging from Blackie's behavior, it looked down on Jet's methods of currying favor with Cecilia!

After all, Blackie earned Cecilia's affection through capability.

Seeing this, Cecilia suddenly thought of a question—could Jet also mutate and level up?

After being washed with Magic Spring water, Blackie clearly gained improved physical attributes and higher intelligence.

Acid rain also played a very significant role in leveling up animals.

Jet had always been indoors and hadn't been exposed to acid rain, so it might miss this collective animal-level-up opportunity.

Cecilia rubbed her slightly overfull belly and decided to go upstairs for some exercise, also to let Jet get exposed to the acid rain.

She had already placed several buckets for collecting acid rain upstairs.

But those buckets were too big. She couldn't fit many upstairs. In contrast, the rooftop had a large slanted platform where acid rain could collect.

She wanted to use her Space's power to quickly collect the acid rain gathered on the rooftop into buckets within her Space. This would be cleaner, faster, and safer than collecting it downstairs.

"Blackie!" Cecilia called.

Blackie stood up from the floor, came to Cecilia's side, and sat upright, tilting his head to look at her as if to say—"What is it? If it's nothing important, don't disturb my sleep. After all, a dog needs over ten hours of sleep a day!"

Cecilia patted Blackie's head. "I'm taking Jet to the rooftop terrace. Guard the house well. If anyone comes to cause trouble, even the man next door, bite them to death!"

Upon hearing this, Blackie's face immediately drooped, and he gave the gray parrot on Cecilia's shoulder a meaningful, somewhat malicious look.

Cecilia immediately understood—Blackie was jealous!

He thought Cecilia was deliberately taking Jet to the rooftop without him.

"I'm taking it to soak in the acid rain. When we come back, I'll have to wash it with Magic Spring water too," Cecilia quickly explained.

Hearing "soak in acid rain" and "wash with Magic Spring water," Blackie gave the gray parrot another meaningful look, but his sulky mood disappeared.

It was already past 11 PM.

In an apocalypse without water or electricity, everyone went to bed early.

By now, most people were asleep. Arthur was probably asleep too.

To move stealthily, Cecilia first put the somewhat noisy Jet into her Space. Then she put on soft-soled shoes, a hat, carefully passed by Arthur's place, and went up to the rooftop terrace.

This time, she wasn't just giving Jet an acid rain bath.

She calculated the timing. The acid rain had now reached its peak concentration.

This was absolutely the best time for an acid rain bath and collecting acid rain.

For the next few days, the acid rain would continue at this concentration.
