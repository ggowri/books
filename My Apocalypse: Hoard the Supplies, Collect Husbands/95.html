Chapter 95 Rooftop Snow

Cecilia proposed to her fellow residents, Arthur and Hailey, that they work together to clear the snow from the rooftop.

The suggestion quickly gained approval from the other two.

But this task proved far more challenging than anyone had imagined...

The rooftop door opened outward, and the moment they cracked it open, they discovered the entire space beyond was completely buried in snow! The sheer volume was staggering.

With no other option, the group worked from the narrow corridor leading to the rooftop, using shovels to scoop snow bit by bit into a small camping cart, then wheeling it down the hallway to dump out the windows.

Conversation was sparse, especially from Arthur, who kept his head down and worked in silence.

Cecilia even enlisted Blackie's help, tying ropes to the camping cart and harnessing them to the dog's body so he could pull the load.

Blackie hung his head with a resigned expression, wordlessly dragging the cart behind him—and honestly, he was surprisingly good at it! Four legs definitely beat two when it came to hauling cargo.

After an entire afternoon of backbreaking work, they had barely made a dent in the rooftop snow. It was disheartening.

By the end of the day, even Cecilia's stamina was pushed to its limits.

She had planned to rest for the next few days, but then urgent knocking echoed again!

This time, it wasn't just one person—it was a crowd!

They shouted desperately, voices filled with panic and terror.

From their frantic cries, Cecilia pieced together what had happened—mutant rats had invaded the lower floors!

Mabel had brought people up, begging for rescue.

"Help us! There are rats! Huge rats!"

"Cecilia, please, we're begging you—save us!"

The overlapping pleas were deafening.

Cecilia frowned as she emerged from her apartment, finding Arthur and Hailey already in the hallway.

She and Arthur had encountered mutant rats before. They knew exactly how dangerous those creatures were.

Hearing the doors open seemed to amplify the shouting outside.

Mabel's voice rose above the rest—crying and pleading, her words dripping with both desperation and moral manipulation.

"Cecilia, you can't just let us die! You have the power to deal with those rats! You can't stand by and watch us all get torn apart! How can you live with yourself?"

Mabel's words immediately gained support from the others.

Suddenly, Cecilia and her group came under attack—bombarded with accusations and guilt trips!

The way these people saw it, if Cecilia's group didn't save them, they were scum—monsters who didn't deserve to call themselves human!

They even resorted to threats, claiming that when the authorities arrived, they'd make sure to tell them all about Cecilia's "cruelty"—that she and her friends were nothing but monsters!

Cecilia actually laughed.

If being practical and cutting losses made her a monster in this hellscape, then fine—she was a monster. But at least she was still breathing.

"Survival of the fittest," she said, her tone icy.

Her words set off a storm of verbal abuse, most of it aimed squarely at her.

They called her every name in the book, worse than animals, and even brought her parents into their rant.

Fury blazed through Cecilia's veins. She grabbed her knife and stormed outside!

The moment they saw her approaching, the ranting crowd fell silent.

Cecilia scanned the group and noticed two unfamiliar faces—tall men she'd never seen before.

She didn't know they were friends of the Duncans from the neighboring building, who had trudged through the snow last night to discuss traveling to the shelter together. They had no idea how formidable Cecilia and her companions were, which explained their earlier boldness.

From the voices she'd heard cursing her parents, these were the two responsible!

Mabel bit her lip, putting on a pitiful expression as she gazed at Cecilia. "Cecilia, please, I'm begging you—save us... Ah!"

At that moment, a mutant rat charged up the stairs!

Everyone screamed in terror. The two men cursed while backing away, even shoving women from the crowd forward as shields.

When one of the men stumbled toward the door in panic, Cecilia lunged forward, yanked the door open, and seized him!

She locked her arm around his throat, her face wearing a cold smile. "You're the one who was cursing me, weren't you?"

The man immediately began struggling frantically, trying to grab at Cecilia behind him.

But her movements were too precise, giving him no chance to fight back. She even kicked the back of his knee, forcing him to kneel facing forward!

Just then, the mutant rat rushed toward them and sank its teeth into the man's face!

"Ahhh!" The man screamed and thrashed, but Cecilia didn't release her grip. Instead, she tightened her hold, pinning his struggling body in place.

"Insult me, and you pay the price."

Her eyes shifted to the second man who had cursed her.

He turned to flee in terror.

Cecilia freed one hand and hurled the knife from her belt.

The man cried out and collapsed, the blade buried in the back of his skull.
