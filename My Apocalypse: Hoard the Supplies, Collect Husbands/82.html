Chapter 82 Take the Loss

It looked like Arthur's maternal family was finally making their move to destroy Alexander.

Alexander and Rebecca had moved here to get closer to Arthur and keep tabs on what he was up to, but they never expected things to go sideways like this.

Rumors were flying around that Arthur wasn't some college student studying abroad—he was actually military, and he'd been deployed overseas on missions or stuck in some closed-door training program.

Rebecca couldn't sleep that night, and she saw everything. She watched Arthur head downstairs and leave, and when he came back, he had a ton of supplies.

She wanted to take a gamble. She was betting that Arthur really was ex-military and that he'd have some compassion for her. She was betting that he'd see her as a pregnant woman and feel something—anything—because of their blood ties.

But after she'd been kneeling there forever, Arthur just stood there without saying a single word.

And Cecilia? She just stood off to the side with her arms crossed, smirking the whole time.

Rebecca's skin crawled. The entire situation made her want to disappear.

Rebecca crawled forward on her knees, reaching desperately for Arthur's pant leg. "Uncle Arthur, please, I'm begging you..."

Arthur stepped back in disgust, putting distance between them.

Blackie had been watching from the side, and the moment Rebecca got too close, he remembered his orders—no one gets near their door. He bared his teeth and lunged straight at her.

Rebecca scrambled backward and landed hard on her ass.

But Blackie wasn't done. He kept coming, backing her into a corner.

She had no choice but to keep scooting backward across the floor, completely humiliated.

Then the smell hit—sharp and unmistakable.

Rebecca had pissed herself. Again.

Cecilia couldn't help it and burst out laughing. "Rebecca, seriously, just stop embarrassing yourself and go back downstairs to rest. I mean, what kind of pregnant woman climbs sixty floors and still has the energy to put on a show like this? I've never seen anything like it."

Rebecca's face went white.

But she refused to give up. She looked at Arthur with tears pooling in her eyes, her voice trembling. "Uncle Arthur, are you really just going to let us die? Even if you can't help us directly, you could at least connect us with the Barnes family, right? Please, I'm begging you—don't just stand by and watch us suffer!"

What a manipulative bitch.

Arthur had nothing to do with her problems, but now she was twisting it around, making it sound like he was the one refusing to save her life.

Arthur barely glanced at her. "Not my problem."

Cecilia lost it and laughed out loud.

Rebecca's face flushed red, then went pale again.

From the moment she'd laid eyes on Cecilia, jealousy had been eating her alive.

Even in the middle of the apocalypse, Cecilia looked immaculate. Her clothes were clean and pressed, her hair perfectly styled, and she smelled faintly of soap. Her skin was flawless, her features sharp and elegant.

It drove Rebecca absolutely insane.

Seeing Cecilia and Arthur standing together made it worse—they looked perfect, like they belonged side by side.

Rebecca wanted to march over there and rip Cecilia apart with her bare hands.

But she couldn't.

Rebecca clenched her fists so tight her nails dug into her palms, and she bit down hard to keep from screaming. Fine. Let Cecilia gloat now. One day, she'd be the one on top.

But Cecilia caught the hatred burning in Rebecca's eyes, and she didn't miss a thing.

A cold smile flickered through Cecilia's mind. Soon. Very soon, Rebecca would get exactly what was coming to her.

She remembered what happened next in the original timeline—the authorities were about to make their move any day now.

After that, everything would collapse into total chaos, and the apocalypse would begin.

When that happened, these bastards wouldn't be able to run anywhere.

"Everyone, come upstairs for a minute."

The rooftop door suddenly opened, and Mabel stepped out, waving people up.

They all headed upstairs, leaving Blackie downstairs to guard the place.

When they reached the rooftop, they realized the supplies had already been divided up.

Everyone who'd been waiting on the roof was holding a portion.

But the three of them from the 66th floor had nothing.

Mabel walked over to Cecilia and her group, awkwardly holding three packets of compressed biscuits and three bottles of water. "So, um, because of what just happened..."

She glanced at Ashley, whose clothes were torn and whose face was covered in bruises. "Because of some issues, our supplies got damaged, and we didn't have much to begin with. I can see you're the healthiest people in the building right now, and I know you're all capable, so..."

Cecilia raised an eyebrow and let out a cold laugh as she stared at Mabel.

Mabel made it sound so reasonable, smiling like she was doing them a favor. Still, she was really just expecting Cecilia and her friends to take the loss and keep quiet.

Just because Ashley had wasted their supplies, everyone on the 66th floor had to take less and give up their share?

Cecilia didn't need these supplies, and she wouldn't touch those disgusting compressed biscuits anyway.

But why should she take this hit?

Why should she pay for someone else's mistakes?

Before Cecilia could respond, Ashley suddenly spoke up with a mocking tone, even after the beating she'd just taken. "Oh, what's wrong? Don't want it? Maybe the 66th floor is too good for this stuff. After all, you can afford to feed that huge dog, so you must have tons of food and water at home. If you don't want it, we can give it to the rest of us. For us, this is life-saving stuff."

Cecilia looked up and sneered. If Ashley had kept her mouth shut, Cecilia would've dealt with her later, but since she wanted to run her mouth, Cecilia was more than happy to make an example out of her right now.

She strode over to Ashley with sharp, determined steps.

Without a word, Cecilia grabbed the bag of supplies from Ashley's arms and hurled it off the rooftop.
