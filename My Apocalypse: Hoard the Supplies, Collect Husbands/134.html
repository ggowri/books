Chapter 134 Gross

Cecilia stepped forward and shut the silo door.

"This one's toast."

Arthur frowned and nodded. No argument.

Then they bolted for the last silo.

Thank God—the contents were still clean. The rats hadn't gotten to it yet.

They rushed to load everything into the car and boat. Once a new Rat King emerged, they would be in serious danger too.

About half a silo's worth remained. Arthur frowned, clearly reluctant to leave it.

But the car was maxed out.

"Let's haul this back first. We'll see if we can make another trip."

Arthur nodded at Cecilia's words.

While Arthur went to start the car, Cecilia lingered behind, taking her sweet time—and swept the remaining half-silo of sorghum into the Space.

She'd wanted to grab the rat king's corpse too, but it wasn't where they'd left it.

Her eyes darted around nervously. Is someone else here?

Then she spotted it—Arthur had already tossed the rat king's body in the car.

"What do you want that for?" Cecilia asked.

Honestly, she was heartbroken! She'd wanted it!

Arthur clearly had his eye on the rat king's corpse. And since he'd killed it, she couldn't exactly claim it.

"During the fight, I noticed the fur's blade-proof. Probably great insulation too. Figured I'd make a vest." Arthur's tone was casual.

Cecilia nodded. Oh, just the fur. Okay then.

"Can I have the meat then?"

In that moment, the look Arthur gave her was... weird.

"Ever eaten rat meat? It's sour." Arthur's tone dripped with disgust.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. He's eaten rat?

She didn't pry. Just said she wanted to give Blackie an extra meal.

"Fine. I'll skin it and give you the meat." Arthur nodded, then shot Blackie a loaded look—like he was thinking, This dog eats rats? Gross.

Blackie, already strapped into the rigid inflatable boat and ready to go, tilted his head toward the RV. He had no clue that Cecilia had just thrown him under the bus.

By the time Cecilia got back to the boat, Blackie had figured it out.

He stared at her with the saddest puppy face.

She patted his big head. "Alright, alright. I'll cook you a huge pot of meat when we get back!"

Blackie kept up the sad act, but his tail started wagging. Just a little—but enough to betray his excitement.

Cecilia bit back a laugh. Nice try, you big faker.

"Two pots. Deal?"

His tail wagged harder, though his face stayed droopy. He even rolled his eyes up dramatically.

Like he was saying, Not enough. You ruined my reputation. I need WAY more treats to forgive you!

"Four pots. That's it!"

Blackie's mouth dropped open in a grin, tail going full helicopter mode. Pure joy radiated off him.

On the way back, Blackie was covered in scratches, but the four pots of meat dangled like a lure. He ran so fast he nearly passed Arthur's RV.

They had no idea that right after they left, Dyson's SUV rolled up from nearby—clearly planning to haul off more grain.

So when Dyson and his crew reached the warehouse and saw the mess, curses echoed through the night.

******

Cecilia, Arthur, and Blackie rushed to their hidden stash spot under cover of darkness.

After unloading most of the grain, Cecilia and Arthur locked eyes.

They blew the storage room to hell with grenades.

Smoke billowed as tons of grain got buried underneath.

They'd planned this from the start. Hauling everything back would draw too much attention—especially once the shelter opened.

So they stashed the extra grain here, buried in a spot only they knew about. Later, they'd trade it for other supplies.

What they kept in the RV? Enough to feed three people and one dog for two years.

Job done, Cecilia and Arthur glanced at the still-dark sky and rushed back to the apartment.

When they arrived, they had Blackie guard the RV while they hauled supplies up first.

Before heading up, Cecilia waited until Arthur wasn't looking, then pulled out a bowl of spring water from the Space and set it down for Blackie.

Then she grabbed a massive bone and shoved it in his mouth.

"Just hang tight. We'll get you upstairs and patched up soon."

With the bone in his mouth, Blackie wagged his tail like crazy—like he didn't even feel the pain anymore. He chomped away, happy as hell.

Watching his energy return after drinking the spring water, Cecilia finally breathed easier. She patted his head.

But when they reached the 66th floor—each lugging dozens of pounds of corn—a thin figure stood at the door.

"Cecilia, I... I barely made it here following Logan's car. I came to apologize to Hailey. But she won't open the door. I'm freezing out here."

Her voice trembled, wounded.

Phoebe. She'd actually dragged herself to the sixty-sixth floor.
