Chapter 206 The Overturned Car

But Nathan had not expected that the moment he set off, one of his bodyguards would report that something was catching up from behind.

In the rear-view mirror, Nathan saw a woman riding on the back of a huge black dog.

His pupils contracted. It took him a moment to realise that the woman was Cecilia.

And the black dog beneath her was somehow the same one she kept. But since when had that dog been so enormous?

Nathan's mind raced. He knew now that the sense of danger he had felt earlier had been justified.

At the same time, he realised he must have overlooked something before.

Then he saw Cecilia smile into the mirror.

In the reflection of the tail lights, he could even make out her white teeth flashing as she grinned.

Then she raised a hand.

In it was a dark grenade.

Nathan saw at once that the fuse had already been lit.

"Damn it!"

He shouted the words out loud.

With the wind rushing beneath his paws, Blackie seemed almost to be flying as he carried Cecilia forward.

In no time at all, they were within ten metres of the vehicle.

The grenade in Cecilia's hand was already burning. She counted the timing, waited until the instant Nathan's head appeared through the car window, then hurled it.

At that moment, Nathan felt as though everything in front of him had slowed to a crawl.

The burning grenade flying towards them.

The smiling girl on the back of the black dog.

The panic-stricken bodyguard beside him.

In that split second, Nathan lunged for the steering wheel in the bodyguard's hands, trying to wrench the car away and out of the grenade's blast radius.

But it was too late.

Far too late.

The sparking grenade landed beside the car, and with a deafening boom, the whole vehicle was blown over.

Cecilia had already stopped some distance behind. She watched as Nathan's vehicle flipped onto its roof, all four wheels in the air and spinning wildly, every window shattered.

She looked round and saw that they had stopped right beside a residential block.

The noise quickly drew the attention of the surviving residents nearby.

Cecilia took out her gun, deliberately cocked it, and fired a shot into the air as a warning for the onlookers to stay back.

The heads peering out of the windows immediately vanished, and no one dared look again.

Once most of the heat from the explosion had faded and the flames on the ground had gone out, Cecilia rode Blackie slowly towards Nathan's overturned car.

Even so, she kept her gun in hand the whole time, advancing at a measured pace while watching carefully for any movement from the vehicle.

There was none.

Under normal circumstances, with the car on fire and an accident this serious, the people inside should have scrambled out at once.

Yet not a single person emerged.

Had they all died?

Cecilia did not lower her guard. She bent down slightly and whispered a few words into Blackie's ear.

Blackie's icy blue eyes flickered. He understood.

When they were still about two metres from the vehicle, Blackie suddenly leapt into the air.

At the same instant, several gunshots rang out from the car.

The shots were aimed precisely at the spot where Cecilia and Blackie had been about to move.

So, the people inside were not dead at all. They had been waiting for Cecilia and Blackie to come closer.

If Cecilia had not been cautious enough to have Blackie jump onto the roof of the vehicle as they approached, she might well have been hit.

Inside the overturned car, Nathan had his own misery to endure.

The driver had died the instant the vehicle flipped. Only Nathan and two bodyguards had survived.

But one of those bodyguards was gravely injured, and Nathan's own left arm was broken, the bone jutting out while blood poured steadily from the wound.

He had given himself rough first aid, but he could still feel his body growing colder and colder. If he did not receive proper treatment soon, he would probably die here.

The inside of the vehicle was stifling, the metal shell burning hot, but he dared not go outside.

He held a shard from the rear-view mirror and used it to watch Cecilia's movements behind them.

He and the two bodyguards had agreed that once Cecilia came within about a metre of the car, they would open fire. Until then, they would endure the heat and pretend that everyone inside was already dead.

Otherwise, today would end only one way.

But he had not expected Cecilia to be so maddeningly cautious.

At a distance of two metres, she had simply leapt onto the roof of the car, and all their bullets had missed.

Just as Nathan slapped his thigh and cursed her aloud, he suddenly saw something extending down from above, outside the window.

Before he could react, there came a tremendous bang.

After that, he knew nothing.

In the instant before death, he seemed to glimpse the whole of his short life.

And in that moment, only one thought rang through his mind:

Damn it. I died at a woman's hands. Next life, no way I'm underestimating women again.

What he never realized, even in death, was that he'd died so quickly because of his own little tricks.

The shard of rear-view mirror he had kept clutched in his palm had allowed him to track Cecilia's position.

But it had also revealed his own.

Cecilia knew he was in the front passenger seat, but she had not been able to pinpoint exactly where his head was.

From the roof of the car, with Blackie beneath her, it was difficult to judge his precise position.

Then she noticed several small patches glinting under the moonlight.

And when she connected that with the fact that the people inside had stayed put, yet still knew when she was approaching, she realised Nathan had been using a mirror to watch what was happening behind the car.
