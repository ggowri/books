Chapter 125 Don't Play Dumb

Cecilia was stunned to see that Arthur had built a raised platform beside a massive wood-burning stove in the living room.

The fire roared inside the cast-iron belly, heating the whole house.

Arthur climbed onto the platform without ceremony and patted the space beside him. "Sit. We need to talk."

Cecilia: "..."

Sure, Arthur looked dead serious right now, and yeah, in this Ice Stage weather, she couldn't exactly complain.

But she couldn't help thinking of that meme from before the apocalypse. This was... awkward as hell.

At least Arthur had some manners. He poured her hot milk and set out snacks.

Except they were all spicy. Cecilia had zero interest.

"What's going on with the Barnes family?"

Cecilia took a sip of milk and cut straight to the chase.

Arthur raised an eyebrow. "What do you mean?"

"Don't play dumb. You borrowed my car that day—what, just for a joyride?"

Cecilia locked eyes with him.

"Ms. Summers, we both have secrets. You want to know mine? Maybe you should tell me something first."

Arthur stared right back.

"Mr. Barnes, what do you want to know?" Cecilia felt her heart skip a beat, but she kept her breathing steady. No backing down.

"Who else is in your place besides Blackie? First time I knocked on your door—who was I talking to?"

Arthur had been dying to ask this forever.

But in this post-apocalyptic world, their relationship wasn't solid enough yet. He hadn't wanted to pry too much.

Cecilia froze. That's what he wanted to know?!

Jet's goofy bird face flashed through her mind, followed by the memory of that ridiculous conversation between Jet and Arthur at her door.

She... wanted to burst out laughing!

"You're married? Got a kid?" Arthur pressed.

Cecilia was about to say no, but then she remembered—Arthur had hauled off a ton of stuff from the 12th floor across the hall. He'd even chased her and Blackie until they had no choice but to hide in the Space.

Plus, lately he'd been eyeing her with that calculating look, like he was trying to figure out her secrets.

And that car he'd wrecked! She hadn't even gotten to drive it yet.

Sure, Arthur promised to get her another one eventually, but shouldn't she collect some interest?

Cecilia went with it. "Yeah, I do. But not married. I guess you could say... knocked up before the wedding."

She sighed and continued, "Mr. Barnes, the government's setting up a shelter here. We've got a lot coming our way. And we can't just hide on the 66th floor forever—we'll need to go out for supplies. There's only three of us on this floor, and Blackie can pull his weight. I've got a kid and a dog to feed—not easy. So when we divvy up supplies, shouldn't we rework how we split things?

"Like, me and Blackie count as two people. You're one. Hailey's one?"

For a split second, Cecilia caught something unreadable flash across Arthur's face.

She kept buttering him up.

"I mean, when we first met, you said all those high-minded things."

She batted her eyes at him.

Go ahead, keep up the act. Let's see how long you can pull this off.

She'd suspected it all along—Arthur was no retired soldier.

That discharge certificate? Totally fake.

Arthur gave her an amused look, then nodded.

"Blackie counting as a person makes sense. Ms. Summers, raising a kid is tough. We should head out soon and scavenge more supplies."

Cecilia rolled her eyes internally. Arthur really could keep his cool.

But she'd gotten what she wanted. No point pushing further.

"Mr. Barnes, I told you my secret. So can you tell me—where'd you go with my car that day?"

"To kill Miranda." Arthur's answer was blunt.

Cecilia let out a cold laugh. "But Nathan showed up today with the government people to set up a shelter. Mr. Barnes, seems like you didn't finish the job."

Arthur chuckled. "Ms. Summers, I went to kill Miranda. Mission accomplished. No one knows I did it. As for everyone else? Not my problem."

Cecilia nodded slowly, said nothing more. She drained her milk, waved goodbye, and left.

This trip had been productive.

First, she'd secured Blackie's share of the supplies.

Second, she'd confirmed Miranda was actually dead.

Third, she understood Arthur better now.

Cold. Ruthless. And extremely dangerous.

******

Meanwhile, Nathan and his crew were heading back.

Nathan sat in the car smoking, brow furrowed like he was working something out.

After a moment, he kicked Frank beside him.

"Hey, didn't that guy following Cecilia look familiar?"

Frank shot an annoyed look at where Nathan had kicked him, brushing off his pants.

"Isn't he one of your Barnes family people?"

"Barnes family? Since when? I don't know him."

Frank explained what happened last time he and Miranda came here, including what Miranda had told him.

Nathan shot upright. "Holy shit—that's Arthur?! The guy who turned the Barnes family upside down?!"
