Chapter 126 Stop Them

Frank didn't answer. He had zero interest in rich family drama.

Before the apocalypse, he'd been a martial arts instructor.

After everything went to hell, he had no choice but to join up with the Barnes family.

"You're saying no one's figured out how my grandma disappeared? And they still haven't found Alex's body either?"

Frank gave a slight nod.

Back then, Frank had stayed behind with the others to deal with Rebecca. By the time they got back to base, Miranda was already gone.

But after all these days, there was still no trace of Miranda's group.

Even the cars had vanished.

Word was, some abandoned building had collapsed that same day.

But no one could confirm if it was acid rain corrosion or if it had anything to do with Miranda's disappearance.

The only survivor was the kid Miranda had sent ahead to report finding a suitable shelter location.

The rest—two cars following Miranda, multiple bodyguards—all gone without a trace.

"This Cecilia and Arthur... interesting." Nathan finally spoke, propping his feet on the front seat and lighting another cigarette.

After a moment, he chuckled. "Come to think of it, I should thank them. If grandma and Alex hadn't died, I wouldn't be running the Barnes family now."

******

Meanwhile, in Logan's vehicle.

Logan was doing damage control with a panicked woman wrapped head-to-toe in a blanket.

"Relax. The government won't abandon you. The mountain base is pretty solid now. Anyone with two working hands who follows orders can survive there."

The woman nodded weakly, still sobbing. Playing the victim card hard.

"That building... monsters, every last one of them. They killed my husband! Especially those bastards on the 66th floor—that Cecilia bitch! They're demons!"

She lunged forward, clawing at Logan's arm.

Logan's brow furrowed in irritation. But remembering her condition when she'd stumbled out—and her sob story—he felt a twinge of pity. He didn't shake off her skeletal grip.

"Sir, they have guns! I saw them! They're thugs! What if this whole 'shelter' thing is a trap? They'll slaughter everyone and steal your supplies. You have to be careful!"

Her voice rose to a fever pitch. "Better yet—send someone to kill them all! Take back what they stole from us! Those supplies could save so many people!"

She was shaking now, fingers digging deeper into Logan's arm.

The blanket slipped off her head.

A gaunt, hate-twisted face stared up at him.

Mabel.

After burning her husband's corpse, she'd spiraled into total despair.

But she wasn't ready to die.

She'd even considered raiding Building B for supplies.

Then she heard it—the megaphone blaring from below!

Rescue. Finally.

But she didn't rush out.

Recent hell had made her sharp. Cautious.

She bundled up thick and watched from the shadows—Cecilia's group, Evan's crew, all talking with the soldiers.

Then she spotted him. Tall guy in uniform with that easy smile.

Logan. The one calling the shots. A real soldier.

The type who believed in justice.

Perfect.

She stripped off layers, let the freezing wind bite her skin, and stumbled to his vehicle shaking like a leaf.

Then she dropped to her knees right in front of Logan, tears streaming down her face.

"Please... save me. Get me out of here. I know this building's secrets—I don't want more people dying. Take me with you. I can help."

Just like that, Mabel was in Logan's vehicle.

Everyone thought she was dead. None of them knew she'd just hooked a big fish.

Logan finally pried her hand off but kept his tone gentle.

"Easy now. Calm down. I'll handle this."

Then his expression hardened. "You said the 66th floor has guns. Is that true?"

Jackpot. Mabel's heart soared. She knew the government would care about firearms.

Truth was, she had no clue if they had guns. Total bullshit.

The 66th floor had never shown her any weapons.

But when Logan asked, she nodded through her tears and let the lies flow.

"Yes! They put guns to our heads, took everything. They shot my sick husband and laughed—said they were putting him out of his misery."

"They're monsters! And that man... he tried to... he tried to force himself on me..."

The tears came hard and fast.

"Evan's crew? Those 'survivors'? They're with the 66th floor! They lived by killing everyone else!"

"Sir, please—for my husband, for everyone they butchered, for the people you're bringing here—you have to stop them!"

Logan's jaw clenched tight.
