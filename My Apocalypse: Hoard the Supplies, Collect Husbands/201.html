Chapter 201 Elephant-Hide Armour


Sprite sounded utterly drained, as if it were reciting a script by rote.

But Cecilia was thrilled. The elephant backscratcher, that completely useless disaster of a product with flaws from top to bottom, was finally being retired?

Just as Sprite had said, a dialogue box soon appeared in front of her.

Inside were three boxes for her to choose from.

Cecilia knew these were the elephant's three by-products.

She took a deep breath and picked the one in the middle.

The other two boxes slowly faded away, and the middle one opened with a soft click.

Then she saw four bold words appear under the elephant's by-product category:

Elephant-Hide Armour

Beneath it was a line of smaller text:

Elephant-Hide Armour: the ultimate beast among armours. It can block one fatal injury. And by fatal injury, we mean that as long as you are still breathing while wearing it, even a dead man can be hauled back to life. Ridiculous, right? Isn't the Space incredible? So hurry up and offer more Spaces to this one so it can level up! Uses: one only.

Cecilia completely ignored that last bit. It was obviously something Sprite had added on purpose.

She excitedly pressed the Craft button.

Very soon, the Elephant-Hide Armour was produced.

Whenevre a random animal produced something, it didn't suffer any loss. The item itself semmed to come directly from the Space, the animal merely a place holder.

Cecilia picked up the armour, then fell silent.

It was really... hideous.

Ugly on a whole new level.

The surface was rough like old tree bark, and not even remotely neat. As for the cut, it looked so careless it bordered on offensive.

Once she put it on, it was basically a sack pretending to be armor.

It was enormous as well. Cecilia could barely move properly while wearing it, so it would be almost impossible to throw on in the middle of a fight.

Still, the fact that it could practically drag someone back from the brink of death made it extremely useful.

If someone suffered a fatal wound, putting this armour on them ought to save their life.

The only problem was that it could only be used once, so it had to be treasured.

For all its glaring flaws, Cecilia still carefully put away this miraculous life-saving item.

At that moment, she gazed hopefully at the Random Animal Pen in the farm, wondering whether she might get another one tomorrow.

Naturally, Sprite ruined the moment right away.

"Quit staring already. Tomorrow's the elephant's last day here. After that, a new animal will show up."

 

She huffed. "If you want better loot, level up the space."

 

Then she added in a sulky tone, "And I'm starving, you know."

Cecilia rolled her eyes. Every third sentence was about levelling up the Space, and the rest were about food.

Then she remembered the batch of mutant cockroach corpses Colonel Reed was supposed to be sending over today. They ought to have arrived by now.

As for the dinosaur egg she had placed in the Ranch, there was still no by-product, nor had there been any reaction over the past few days.

Cecilia suspected the Space might need even more energy before the egg could be activated.

At last, she took another turn round the Recycler.

Today's output was fairly ordinary: two boxes of instant noodles.

A few days ago, when Sprite had been helping Cecilia collect things inside the Space, it had produced a pair of gloves.

When Cecilia put them on, they felt wonderfully warm, and the material itself seemed quite unusual, as though it might have some special function.

Naturally, the two gloves still did not match.

Compared with the previous pair of socks, which had been an eye-watering clash of red and green, this one was somehow even worse. One glove had SpongeBob on it, and the other...featured a character from some kockoff cartoon.

Once everything in the Space had been sorted out, Cecilia took some Magic Spring water and acid rain, intending to have a soak with Blackie and Jet.

Of course, if Snowy behaved itself, it could join them too.

Thanks to the unforgettable image of Snowy that morning, with its backside covered in filth, Cecilia went straight out and prepared another small tub for it.

Who could have guessed that Snowy had no interest in bathing alone and kept trying to climb into Jet's and Blackie's wooden tubs instead?

Jet flapped its wings furiously, cursing without pause.

Arthur had already cleaned Snowy's fur and body that morning, but the moment it started soaking, all sorts of filthy, foul-smelling mystery substances began floating up again.

The entire little tub turned disgusting.

Snowy kept trying to scramble out of its own tub, only for Blackie to lift a paw and smack it straight back in.

Jet got splashed and immediately started swearing again.

And so the cycle continued. The whole thing was gloriously chaotic.

Cecilia let out a sigh and leaned against the side of her own wooden tub. Watching the three of them carry on like that was strangely entertaining.

Just as she was drifting off, she suddenly heard knocking at the door outside.

After getting dressed, Cecilia went to answer it and found not only Arthur standing there, but also Blaise, wrapped head to toe in bandages.

Arthur looked at her and said, "Colonel Reed is about to wipe out the Barnes family. Are you coming?"

Blaise chimed in, "Didn't expect the Barnes family to get this tough after the apocalypse.A show like this? No way we're missing it."

Cecilia looked at them, surprised. "Colonel Reed's going after the Barnes family? He's not like us—he doesn't even have a score to settle with them.

Isn't he stepping on our turf?"

At the words "like us", the corners of Arthur's mouth lifted slightly.

"Yes. Apparently after Jeremy died, the family went mad. Last night, despite Nathan trying to stop them, they went to General Mercer in tears. After listening to them, General Mercer gave them a proper scolding. No one knows which member of the family lost their head, but that very night they sent people to attack the old general. Colonel Reed learned he'd been injured, and now he's taking men to erase the Barnes family."
