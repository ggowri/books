Chapter 57 A Meal

Alexander hadn't expected that after just a few days, Cecilia had become so beautiful. Her features looked even more refined, and her entire aura was transcendent, making one long to immediately hold her in their arms.

"Alex!" Rebecca was somewhat displeased, but quickly reacted, squeezing out two tears. "Alex, can't you see Cecilia is different from before? She's hooked up with that bastard, the disgrace of your family! She... she must be deliberately getting back at you!"

This last sentence intimidated Alexander.

Bryan chuckled, "Don't worry. Cooperate with me, and you'll definitely get rewards beyond your imagination!"

*****

Cecilia, bringing Blackie, walked next door and found the place kept in perfect order.

The next-door unit was the same layout as hers, with an extremely large area.

In the living room, she could see the exercise equipment previously placed outside, then some simple tables and chairs, but couldn't see the supplies Arthur had collected.

Cecilia didn't ask further. After all, at times like these, supplies are secrets.

To avoid taking advantage, Cecilia brought a chicken over as an exchange.

Cecilia didn't expect that besides the two plates of delicious food earlier, Arthur also brought out thinly sliced mutton rolls, beef rolls, soy products, vegetables, and hotpot base.

It's actually a lavish hotpot meal!

Cecilia hadn't expected Arthur to be so unguarded, showing absolute sincerity.

"Ms. Summers, hotpot is cooked in the same pot, so there's no question of poisoning." Arthur pointed to the pot as he said this.

Cecilia didn't waste words either, handing the chicken she brought to Arthur.

Arthur nodded slightly and took it into the kitchen.

From the sounds, Cecilia guessed he put it in the refrigerator.

This surprised Cecilia even more.

Even now, Arthur has a generator at home. And it can power a refrigerator.

Once the fire was lit, Arthur prepared two dipping sauces. One was bright red, clearly with lots of chili.

The other had a lot of peanut sauce, topped with other seasonings.

Arthur placed the non-spicy one in front of Cecilia, then brought over two bowls containing scallions, cilantro, and chili oil. "Add these according to your taste."

For a moment, Cecilia was truly shocked.

Is this still the apocalypse? Does Arthur also have a Space?

At first, the two ate somewhat reservedly, not talking much.

Finally, Cecilia spoke up, "Didn't you go abroad from a young age? Why are you discharged here as a conscript?"

Arthur glanced at Cecilia, as if surprised she would ask this first.

Then, Arthur briefly told Cecilia about his experiences.

It turned out that the Barnes family's claim that the illegitimate child and the mistress were sent abroad was just a public story.

The truth was, Arthur's mother was also from a respectable family, who had a moment of folly with Hugh, even though there was an age gap of over twenty years.

Later, Arthur's mother returned to her own family and gave birth to Arthur.

Arthur joined the military in his teens, and later, due to certain reasons, spent recent years abroad.

Arthur didn't explain the details in between, and Cecilia didn't ask.

She wasn't particularly interested in gossip anyway.

Just knowing that Arthur wasn't close to the Barnes family and even somewhat disgusted by their ways was enough.

As for Arthur having the surname Barnes, it was his mother's surname, not Hugh's.

After hearing this, Cecilia nodded. "As for my story, it's what you already know. And I have no relationship with Alexander anymore either.

"I hope we can trust each other from now on."

Arthur extended his hand. Cecilia hesitated for a moment, but still shook it.

The meal was fairly pleasant.

Both were the quiet type, and having done physical labor all day, they focused on the food.

They finished four boxes of mutton, two boxes of beef, a basin of greens, and a box of luncheon meat.

What delighted Cecilia was that the plate of pastries and the plate of fried chicken Arthur had shown earlier were incredibly delicious.

Even before the apocalypse, she rarely ate such delicious food.

I wonder where Arthur found such delicacies.

Arthur ate from the spicy pot the whole time, and extremely fast, leaving Cecilia amazed. It was the first time she'd seen someone who could handle so much spice.

Her clear broth pot also cooked quite a bit of meat for Blackie.

When she left Arthur's place, Cecilia was so full she was burping slightly, feeling the chicken was a good deal.

Because of this meal, Cecilia developed some goodwill towards Arthur.

However, her past life's experience in the apocalypse made it impossible for her to truly trust anyone.

She would still be somewhat wary of Arthur, but their fragile cooperative relationship could continue for now.

Upon returning home, Blackie, a bit sleepy, lay down nearby. Jet, clingy, fluttered over, calling out angrily. "Bad girl! Surprisingly, you remember to come back!"

After playing with the little parrot for a bit, Cecilia eagerly entered the Space.

She had been busy all day and hadn't gone into the Space to carefully check the farmland and the Recycler.

Upon entering the Space, Cecilia first ran to Plot One.

To her delight, she found that the places she had sown yesterday had already sprouted!

However, this also left her feeling perplexed.
