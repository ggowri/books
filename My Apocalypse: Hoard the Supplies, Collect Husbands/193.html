Chapter 193 Fire in the Stronghold

Calling Blackie over, Cecilia took a pair of night vision binoculars from the Space and fixed her gaze on the distance.

An hour passed.

Nothing unusual happened.

From where she was, Cici could see that the organisation not far away was practically thriving. The place could only be described as lively and prosperous.

A vast stretch of land belonged to them.

Beyond the villa district, there were several mid rise buildings as well.

At that moment, the entire outer perimeter had been sealed off with iron sheeting more than two metres high.

Before the apocalypse, this sort of blue metal sheeting had usually been used around building sites under construction.

Now it formed a wall.

Outside that barrier, there was an armed guard posted roughly every five or six metres.

Each of them looked tense, every one of them on high alert.

Inside the wall, Cici could even make out two lookout towers built from timber.

Lights swept back and forth from the top of them.

Looking at security like that, she could hardly imagine how Arthur had managed to get inside.

Just as he had said, the place really was dangerous.

Yet in complete contrast to the heavy guard outside, the houses within were ablaze with light. Every single building was lit up.

Cici could even see dancing and singing going on in some of the rooms.

The sound of karaoke rose and fell through the night.

There were also loud shouts from people toasting one another and drinking.

Not far from the compound, she spotted a large boiler house, which was presumably providing heating for the whole area.

She also saw several buildings being used to keep livestock.

As far as her eyes could reach, there was a cattle shed, a pigsty and a sheep pen.

It was obvious that the people here were living very comfortably.

The footprints Arthur had left behind had already been erased by the wind and snow, leaving no trace at all.

A flicker of worry rose in Cici's chest, and she peered even more carefully through the binoculars.

Then suddenly, a deafening bang rang out.

A burst of fire shot into the sky.

Right before her eyes, one of the villas exploded.

The men patrolling near the villa compound all rushed towards the burning building.

But even so, the rest of the guards did not relax their vigilance. They all stayed at their posts.

Those who had been singing and watching the dancing only moments earlier suddenly pulled out guns.

The entire compound fell into chaos.

And yet Arthur still did not appear.

Up to that moment, Cici had not caught the faintest glimpse of him.

The flames consuming the villa grew fiercer and fiercer, and the wind carried a stray tongue of fire elsewhere.

Cici saw with her own eyes as one flicker of flame caught the curtain of a nearby house.

Inside that house lived one of the dancers who had been performing earlier.

The woman cried out loudly and grabbed for something to beat the flames down.

But then, from behind her, a hand appeared, clamped over her mouth, and dragged her away.

Cici saw it clearly.

That hand was gloved.

It had to be Arthur's.

A moment later, the dancer was gone, and Arthur appeared at the window holding a bucket of petrol, sloshing it around the room.

Very quickly, that entire house went up in flames as well.

It looked as though the dancers all lived there. They came pouring out at once, shrieking as they ran.

Watching this, two words popped straight into Cici's head.

Absolutely vicious.

This organisation was disciplined and well trained.

Its rules were strict.

It was obvious their leader was capable, which was why they were not afraid even of going head to head with the military.

But the dancers and singers they kept here were a different matter entirely.

They were timid.

Setting fire to their quarters would only create greater chaos.

And just as expected, the women ran out weeping and screaming, grabbing at people and begging them to put out the fire.

For a time, quite a few of the guards abandoned their posts.

And Arthur vanished again.

But Cici knew he had to be causing trouble somewhere.

The wind and snow grew wilder still.

Not the slightest trace of the moon could be seen that night.

Then suddenly, a deep, booming voice rang out from inside the compound over a loudspeaker.

"Get back to your posts, all of you! First squad, organise the fire response. Everyone else, get back where you belong! Don't give that bastard who broke in a chance to take advantage. Lock this place down!"

As the man bellowed, Cici saw several burly men seize the panicked singers running about and the guards who had strayed from their positions, slapping them hard across the face.

Meanwhile, one squad began tackling the blaze with proper equipment.

In just a few minutes, the chaos was brought under control.

Cici's heart tightened.

Even in those few minutes, she still had not seen Arthur.

But she had seen a tall, thin boy, covered in injuries and dressed far too lightly for the cold.

She could not be certain it was Blaise Hart.

But very quickly, she knew it was.

The voice over the loudspeaker rang out again.

"You little rat, you came here to rescue someone, didn't you? You've broken in several times already. Don't think I haven't noticed. You've even got the nerve to burn my houses. Fine. I'll kill him right now. And when I catch you, I'll make you cough up where your lot are hiding their supplies, you little bastard!"

That person had to be Blaise.

Through the night vision binoculars, Cici saw the man holding Blaise kick him violently to the ground.

Then he drew a gun.

Just as her heart leapt into her throat

there came a heavy metallic crash.

And then everything went dark.

The entire compound lost power.

In an instant, the whole area before her was swallowed by darkness.
