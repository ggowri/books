Chapter 151 A Kitten

Cecilia swept everything into the Space, then followed them in.

A wild wind whipped through the Space.

The serpentine items dissolved into starlight mid-air.

Besides the usual blue dots, there were yellow ones now. And red.

All the light scattered slowly, then rained down on the soil, the buildings, the livestock pens.

In the distance—at the little mountain where the Magic Spring originated—a three-colored rainbow formed.

Yellow, red, blue.

A rainbow bridge arcing through the sky. Gorgeous.

Just as Cecilia was admiring the view, she heard a tiny sound.

"Mew~"

Soft as a baby's cry.

She searched around, then spotted a three-colored leaf floating toward her across the Magic Spring.

On top—a pure white kitten. One blue eye, one gold. Like a lion cat.

Cecilia hurried to the spring's edge and scooped up the kitten.

The little thing rolled in her arms, stretched, and started licking its paws contentedly.

White all over—except for three tails.

Blue, yellow, and red.

The three fluffy tails fanned out behind it, adorable as hell.

Cecilia couldn't put the soft little creature down.

She walked through the Space, cuddling and teasing it.

"You're from the Space too? What can you do?"

The kitten said nothing. Just kept licking its paws in her arms, looking perfectly innocent.

When Cecilia walked up to the villa to check on the upgrade, she froze.

The LCD screen that used to be out front—gone.

"What the hell?" She searched everywhere. Definitely gone.

Just as panic set in, she heard a voice.

A teenage boy's voice—cocky, annoying, with a wicked edge.

"Want answers? Beg me, and maybe I'll tell you."

Cecilia looked down. The voice came from the kitten.

It hopped out of her arms, landed on the ground in front of the villa, and sat primly. Its tone was smug as hell.

"Allow me to introduce myself. I'm the Spirit of the Space. Name's Ari. I'm the butler here. Everything in the Space? I run it. You're Cecilia, right? The lucky human who made a contract with me."

"Looking forward to working together."

"Meow~"

That last meow made Cecilia's eye twitch. This is the Space's real form?

God, she was pissed. She wanted Blackie to bite the thing!

After talking with Ari—the cat the Space had manifested into—Cecilia learned the truth.

The Space had absorbed all that serpentine. But there was no upgrade.

All that energy? Went into creating this. Ari.

"Surprise! Bet you didn't see that coming!"

Cecilia wanted to grab a machete and hack Ari back into the void.

The Space was already annoying enough. Now it had a physical body to argue with.

Still, after more talk, Cecilia realized Ari was useful.

When she couldn't get into the Space to tend the animals and plants, Ari could handle it. Same with the farm.

Even the trash processor—Ari fed it garbage daily and collected the new supplies.

And Ari could read her mind.

So whatever she wanted, the Space would help make it happen—within limits. Like equivalent exchange, but supercharged by the Space's power.

Though... that mountain of pet food from before? Yeah, that felt more like mockery.

As for the other secrets Cecilia wanted to know?

Like where the Space actually came from. What else it could eat besides stones, gems, and mutant creatures. Whether the Space had anything to do with the apocalypse.

Ari's answer to everything: "Host, you're too low-level. Don't be greedy."

Cecilia knew she was still in Phoebe's place. Couldn't stay long. She stopped pestering Ari.

She left the Space, hurried back to her apartment, planning to rest up and introduce Blackie and Jet to Ari tomorrow.

But shortly after she left, someone else entered Phoebe's room.

Hailey.

Knife in hand.

When she saw Phoebe lying in a pool of blood, she jumped.

Then noticed the broken locks. Someone had beaten her to it.

Looking at her former friend, Hailey spat out one word. "Bitch."

******

Next day, Cecilia woke up past noon.

Commotion downstairs. She threw on clothes and went to check.

People hauling stuff everywhere. Logan was leaving.

Barry came up and scratched Blackie's head. He pulled out a sausage from his pocket, grinning.

"The General booted him out yesterday. Honestly? Good riddance. Sitting in this building all day, breaking up fights and dealing with people's affairs? Boring as hell. Time to go bash some mutants. Stretch our legs."
