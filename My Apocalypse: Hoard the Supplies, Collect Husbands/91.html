Chapter 91 Cut Him

But soon, Rebecca clenched her teeth and said, "Alexander... Alexander is dying. His hands and feet are frozen. They're all purplish-blue. Please, I beg you, save him!"

Hearing this, Cecilia raised an eyebrow.

Ignoring Rebecca's pleas, she turned and went back inside.

Rebecca thought Cecilia wouldn't help and began to scream curses, "Cecilia, you heartless bitch! After all you and Alexander had, you'd let him die? You whore! The Barnes family will make you pay!"

But then, she saw Cecilia return. Her heart sank. Was Cecilia changing her mind?

She immediately clamped a hand over her mouth, terrified her words might make Cecilia abandon them again.

As Cecilia approached, Rebecca noticed she carried a bag.

A surge of hope rose in Rebecca's chest. Could it be food? But the bag wasn't big enough for warm clothing.

Especially that fur coat Cecilia wore... she wanted it so badly!

When she excitedly opened the bag, her face turned ashen.

Inside was a large machete, along with some bandages and medication.

"Cecilia, what is this supposed to mean?" Rebecca stammered.

Cecilia looked at the woman before her, her mind flooded with memories of her past life. The scene of this woman sitting calmly by, carefully taking a piece of candy from her own space and popping it into her mouth, while Cecilia was tricked by Alexander, drained of blood, and cannibalized.

"Rebecca, you know the Barnes family's power. You're still with Alexander because you believe the Barnes family will recognize you as their daughter-in-law for standing by him through hardship. Now, your chance to save him and secure your future has arrived.

"Alexander's hands and feet are purplish-blue, you said? That's severe frostbite. If you don't amputate them, the necrosis will spread up his arms and legs. Then the precious heir of the Barnes family will be ruined.

"I'm giving you the tools. You go and amputate his limbs to save him. The choice is yours."

Rebecca stood frozen, completely stunned.

She had suspected it was Cecilia who knocked them out the night before the government evacuation.

But she had no proof. And having known Cecilia for so long, she never took her seriously. She thought Cecilia might have a moment of ruthlessness, but was still that useless Summer family orphan at her core.

But at this moment, Rebecca realized she was wrong.

Terrifyingly wrong.

"Cecilia, are you trying to kill Alexander? Do you even understand the Barnes family's reach? Even if they haven't found us yet, even with the whole country suffering, they're still a dominant force in our city! Once the government rescue comes and I contact them, I'll tell them everything you've done! Let's see how Miranda deals with you!"

Cecilia almost laughed.

She took a step forward. Rebecca stumbled backward in fear.

Cecilia looked at her with mocking amusement. "Alexander is with you. The Barnes family knows that. If he dies, who do you think they'll blame? Instead of threatening me, you should be thinking about how to save him! I've shown you the path. Taking it or not is your choice."

With that, Cecilia ignored Rebecca completely, shutting the stairwell security door in her face.

Rebecca screamed and cursed outside for ten minutes, but no one paid her any attention. Finally, with a heart full of turmoil, she headed downstairs.

She and Alexander were now on the 45th floor. Andy had moved into the neighboring unit.

That morning, she had been woken by Andy's frantic knocking.

Only then did she realize the indoor temperature had plunged.

She hastily put on every piece of warm clothing she could find.

She was okay, but Alexander was unconscious. Andy finally slapped him awake, only to discover the hands and feet on Alexander's side of the bed were purplish-blue and completely numb.

"It's all your fault! You stole all the covers, you vicious woman! You're trying to kill me!" Alexander, seeing the state of his limbs and failing to move them, began hurling abuse at Rebecca.

Rebecca was furious but didn't dare retort.

The government had already evacuated one group; they would be rescued soon.

Then she could surely contact the Barnes family, and her lifelong wealth and happiness would follow.

Nothing could go wrong now.

Rebecca busied herself lighting a fire in the room, urging Alexander to warm himself.

But his hands and feet remained numb, the muscles appearing necrotic.

Alexander moaned, cursed, and vowed that once he contacted his family, he'd make Rebecca pay for doing this to him.

That was why she thought of Cecilia, who had once loved Alexander so desperately. Maybe she had a solution.

And Arthur might help, too.

Even if they couldn't, she might at least get some supplies out of it.

But now, holding the items Cecilia gave her, she walked slowly back down to the 45th floor.

Andy, rubbing his hands for warmth, came out. "Well?"

Seeing Andy, Rebecca started sniffling, complaining that Cecilia refused to help and calling her a heartless bitch.

Andy listened, then fell silent. "If it doesn't work, we just finish off that Alexander guy. He's been moaning and cursing nonstop since you left, driving me crazy."

"No! Absolutely not!" Rebecca cried out, a sudden thought striking her.

She had another secret.

It was she who had coaxed Alexander to come to this apartment building to probe Arthur in the first place. And Miranda knew about it.
