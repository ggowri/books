Chapter 15 Backtrack

"Alex, do I look good like this?" Rebecca's voice trilled through the door.

"You look amazing no matter what, babe," Alexander replied, his tone oozing sleaze.

"Compared to your fiancée? Last night... you even left me to go find her!" Rebecca's voice turned more coquettish, laced with feigned resentment.

"Ugh, what's so great about her? I'd never stick with such a boring woman if it weren't for her money. I went to see her just to smooth things over, keep her from making a scene. She's been in a bad mood lately, you know?" Alexander's voice lost its usual suave warmth, sharpened by a hint of malice.

"So Alex, am I better than her?" Rebecca purred, her voice so soft it could melt butter.

"Babe, you're the only one. Come here, give me a kiss."

The conversation made Cecilia's skin crawl. Glancing sideways, she saw Miranda's face had turned a deep crimson with rage.

Before she could act, though, Sarah, who'd been quiet up until now, suddenly screamed, "Alex! Alex, we're outside!"

That idiotic woman was going to ruin everything!

Cecilia reacted fast, swiping the key card against the lock.

Chaos erupted inside—furniture scraping across the floor, fabric rustling, and startled gasps.

But Cecilia pushed the door open before Alexander and Rebecca could even scramble for their clothes, forcing them to dive under the covers together, naked.

Several vulgar items lay scattered across the floor. They really had been playing quite dirty.

Miranda stormed in, her hands trembling as she pointed at Alexander. "What in God's name are you doing?"

Pale as a sheet, Alexander glanced back and forth between Cecilia and his grandmother, stammering, "We... we're rehearsing a scene. For an event we're in..."

Cecilia let out a snort of laughter. Only someone as brainless as Alexander could come up with such a shoddy excuse.

Sarah rushed to his defense. "Grandma, it's not a big deal! They're just friends, hanging out and talking in a hotel room. Let's not bother them."

However, Cecilia shot Sarah a withering glare, then stepped forward and yanked the covers off Alexander and Rebecca.

Alexander was completely nude, but Rebecca's getup was even more absurd—she wore a tight leather miniskirt and a collar with a bell around her neck.

When the covers fell, she scrambled to cling to Alexander. "Alex!"

Cecilia rolled her eyes. "How affectionate."

Miranda, finally snapping out of her shock, gave Sarah a sharp look.

Then, Sarah immediately spun around and slammed the door shut.

With a forced smile on her face, Miranda turned to Cecilia. "Cici, this matter... it's not as serious as it looks."

Cecilia saw through her act instantly. For all her pretense of siding with Cecilia, Miranda was clearly going to protect her grandson.

Of course she would—blood was thicker than water.

All that earlier posturing had just been to gauge how much evidence Cecilia had.

No one in the Barnes family was good news.

"Madam Barnes, what are you saying? That's not what we agreed on earlier," Cecilia asked coolly.

Miranda sighed, "Alexander isn't married to you yet. Once you two are wed, I promise this will never happen again."

Cecilia understood now.

The Barnes family saw no issue with his behavior because there was no legal bond yet.

To them, Alexander was still "single," free to fool around as he pleased—so long as he settled down after marriage.

"I didn't realize the Barnes family had such loose morals. Loyalty is just a switch you flip on after walking down the aisle," she said dryly.

Miranda tried to play peacemaker, her smile growing strained.

Cecilia stared at her coldly. As soon as her own family was involved, Miranda started fudging things.

She understood—after all, Miranda was Alexander's grandma, not hers.

But Cecilia had come prepared, anticipating the Barneses' underhanded tactics.

She pulled a tiny hidden camera from around her neck, pressed a button, and let the recorded conversation play back.

This time, even Miranda paled. "Cecilia, what are you doing? You recorded this secretly!"

Sarah lunged forward, trying to grab the camera.

But Cecilia kicked her hard in the thigh, sending her sprawling face-first onto the floor. "Don't act tough when you're this weak. Stay out of it."

Sarah glared at her venomously, her jaw clenched tight.

Ignoring her, Cecilia turned back to Miranda. "I want the stocks and funds my parents left me. And I want to call off the engagement. I never want to have anything to do with Alexander—this scumbag, cheater, piece of trash.

"Otherwise, this little device will ruin the Barnes family's reputation for good."

Miranda had only been one part of her plan.

Politeness first, then firmness—that was Cecilia's way.

"You want the stocks, too? That's ours!" Alexander suddenly shouted, forgetting his shame as he lunged for the camera.

But Cecilia had been training for weeks; the weak Alexander stood no chance against her.

She dodged easily, then kicked him hard in the knee. He crumpled to the floor, completely naked in front of Miranda and Sarah.

The sight was almost comical.
