Chapter 8 Another Upgrade

In an instant, a plan formed in Cecilia's mind.

After asking the store owner for the price and comparing it with the various cat litter options online, she decided to buy from the internet.

She placed an order for thousands of bags of different types, averaging around eight dollars per bag.

Just the thought of receiving and moving all that cat litter in the coming days made her arms ache.

Soon, her stomach was growling with hunger.

As she was about to leave, she suddenly felt a warmth in her chest.

Her heart skipped a beat. This was the same feeling she'd had when she found the stone that upgraded the Space.

She quickly glanced around and spotted a stone on the pet store's counter.

It was about the size of her palm, grayish-black, and unremarkable. But Cecilia knew there was definitely something special about it.

Pretending to be interested, she circled the stone a few times before asking the owner, "Where did you get this stone, sir?"

The owner glanced at it casually and smiled, "Oh, this? I bought it at the foot of a mountain while hiking. They sell a lot of these there, I just picked it up because it looked interesting."

Cecilia smiled at him, "Sir, could you sell it to me?"

Hearing that, the owner chuckled, "This thing is probably useless. I didn't pay much for it. If you really like it, since you bought so much stuff, I'll just give it to you."

Cecilia thanked him profusely and tried to pay something, but the owner refused to take any money.

Reluctantly, she bought another pile of items from his store.

Since she'd already bought out all the chicken, beef, venison, and duck-flavored snacks earlier, she had to settle for sardine, cod, and salmon flavors this time.

This made Blackie unhappy, and he kept glaring at Cecilia from the side, as if scolding her for being wasteful.

Once outside the store, Cecilia hurried to comfort Blackie, "Think of it as stocking up for your future girlfriend, okay? What if you meet a nice girl someday who loves these flavors?"

She wasn't sure if Blackie understood, but he still looked displeased.

Putting his mood aside, Cecilia rushed back to the car and took out the stone.

Seeing no one around, she focused her thoughts, and the stone vanished into the Space.

Just like the gold ore before it, the stone crumbled into dust as soon as it entered the Space.

Blue particles floated out, far more than those from the gold ore.

As the particles scattered throughout the Space, the area shook violently.

Cecilia watched in shock as a small house suddenly appeared inside the Space, and she found herself standing right in it.

The Space had upgraded again, and now she could hide inside it!

Amazed, Cecilia explored every corner, touching the walls.

There was no wind, but the climate was cool and comfortable.

It was empty, about 430 square feet in size.

Outside the house was a small plot of land, its black soil looking rich and fertile.

On that fertile soil lay a hemp rope over six feet long this time.

Judging by its position, it was exactly where Cecilia had placed the weeds the previous day.

So, planting a weed had grown a hemp rope?

It seemed plausible, but it made the black soil feel magical and unpredictable.

Suddenly, a thought struck Cecilia. She took a bread roll from the Space, buried it in the soil, and waited to see what would grow.

Not far from the house were her scattered belongings, piled haphazardly.

Looking at them, she realized she needed to buy some shelves to maximize the Space's utility.

After exploring for a little longer, Cecilia felt thrilled, her mind buzzing with ideas for decorating the Space.

It would be her perfect safe haven in times of danger.

She wondered if Blackie could enter, too.

Hurrying out of the Space, she saw Blackie pacing anxiously in the car, whimpering softly—he was clearly worried sick.

But the moment he saw Cecilia, he calmed down instantly. After a brief look of surprise, he returned to his usual cold, indifferent self, his ears drooping.

Cecilia gritted her teeth, murmuring, "Keep pretending! You're clearly worried about me, but you insist on acting like you don't care. We'll see how long you can keep this up!"

A mischievous idea popped into her head.

What if she pulled Blackie into the Space suddenly? Would it scare him?

She grabbed his paw and focused her thoughts.

But she soon realized that only she had entered; Blackie was still outside.

It seemed the Space could only hold one living creature at a time—human or otherwise, but not two together.

She wondered if this was temporary or permanent.

She needed to explore more; maybe she could find another item to upgrade the Space again.

This time, Blackie didn't panic when Cecilia disappeared.

He simply lay down on the seat, barely lifting an eyelid at her sudden comings and goings.

All this excitement had made Cecilia even hungrier.

At that moment, she couldn't help but long for Whisk Bakery, a shop near her home that offered all kinds of delicate savory bites.

Flaky ham and cheese croissants, crispy bacon and egg tarts, truffle-infused mushroom pastries, and mini lobster rolls, among others. Every bite was mouthwatering.

But Blackie couldn't go into the mall, and she didn't feel comfortable leaving him in the car again. So, Cecilia headed home first.

But she made up her mind to visit it the next day and order a mountain of snacks.

After the apocalypse, when she was feeling down or stressed, a plate of those flaky, savory bites—especially the warm, buttery ones—would fill her with happiness.

When she got home, she suddenly remembered she could order takeout.

She quickly placed an order, buying as many snacks as possible and having them delivered.

Cecilia also ordered all her favorite foods from nearby restaurants—anything that could be delivered, and she used an errand service for the rest.

From the moment Cecilia got home until she went to bed, the doorbell kept ringing nonstop.

"Hello, your 60 mini rolls are here, miss," one delivery guy said.

"Hello, your 30 orders of fried chicken are here," another delivery guy called.

"Hello, your 20 servings of spicy chili con carne are here," a third delivery guy said.

Delivery guys kept arriving one after another.
