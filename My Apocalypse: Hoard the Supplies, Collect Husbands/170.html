Chapter 170 The Hidden Smugglers' Stock

Cecilia walked over and shone her flashlight inside. She discovered that the crates actually contained vases, incense burners, scroll paintings, and similar items.

The word "antiques" immediately appeared in her mind.

She even spotted a massive rosewood table, carefully sealed and preserved, as if it were waiting to be shipped somewhere else.

Without thinking further, she stored everything into the Space.

Just like gemstones, these objects carried a certain degree of spiritual essence. They might prove useful later. Even if they did not, once human civilization was rebuilt, the government would inevitably pay attention to these relics again. At that time, they would still have value.

After clearing those two shelves, she moved forward and discovered something unexpected. There were animal corpses ahead.

Cecilia glanced over them briefly and realized that many were rare animals such as Tibetan antelope, pythons, monkeys, and minks. They had all been kept in cages. Now every one of them had starved to death. Their bodies were shriveled and dried, leaving little more than skin and fur, while the flesh inside seemed to have completely decomposed.

Cecilia felt nauseated and quickly passed through that area.

But after walking past it, a question suddenly surfaced in her mind.

Why was there no foul smell coming from these dead animals?

Why had all the flesh on their bodies disappeared, leaving only skin and bones?

Had something eaten them?

The thought instantly made her more cautious.

Beside her, Blackie was also extremely alert, constantly sniffing the air, yet he did not detect anything unusual.

Cecilia could not worry about it for too long. She wanted to finish collecting the supplies here as quickly as possible and leave.

She had already reached the next section and noticed an area covered with a thick rain tarp.

Her instincts told her that something valuable was underneath.

When she lifted the tarp, she saw three or four large crates.

One crate was filled with hunting shotguns, another with ammunition, and the last contained gun parts.

Shotguns were actually difficult to operate. Their firing rate was slow, but their power was far greater than ordinary handguns.

The bullets appeared to be specifically for the shotguns.

As for the crate of gun parts, Cecilia could not identify which handgun model they belonged to, so she simply stored them in the Space for now.

After passing the crates, the next four shelves were filled with videotapes, audio tapes, photographs, and books.

Cecilia flipped through them briefly and discovered that the images depicted a variety of indescribable scenes.

The corner of her mouth twitched. With clear disgust, she turned to leave.

But after walking two steps, she sighed and reluctantly stored part of the collection into the Space anyway.

What if she happened to encounter someone who wanted them someday? Who knew, they might even sell for a high price.

At that moment, Cecilia could not help laughing at herself.

How had her greed reached this level?

Still, leaving them here would only be a waste, and the Space was large enough anyway.

It was obvious that these indescribable materials were the main smuggling goods of that Boss, occupying most of the area.

Cecilia felt extremely disgusted.

Even so, her harvest here was already quite impressive, especially the crates of firearms and ammunition she had just obtained.

As she looked ahead, she was finally about to pass the shelves filled with those materials. At the far end she could already see a table and several elegant antique rosewood display racks, and she began quickening her pace.

At that moment, she suddenly heard an extremely faint scraping sound behind her.

Blackie had already dashed forward.

The sound was so subtle that Cecilia could not even tell where it came from.

"Blackie!"

She could only run after him.

After sprinting more than ten meters, she abruptly stopped.

Because she saw two things in front of her.

They were wriggling, pale white, each about the size of a human forearm.

Maggots.

From Cecilia' position, she could even see the sharp teeth inside their mouthparts.

They were mutated maggots.

Cecilia finally understood why the dead animals earlier had no flesh left on them. These two creatures had eaten everything.

Perhaps sensing the fresh living flesh of Cecilia and Blackie, the two creatures grew restless and began crawling toward them.

However, they moved extremely slowly, making it practically impossible for them to catch up to Cecilia or Blackie.

After running for a short distance and confirming that the creatures were merely disgusting rather than dangerous, Cecilia finally relaxed a little.

Still, she had no idea if there were other creatures hiding here, so she decided it was best not to stay long.

She rushed to the final corner and, without even checking what was inside the table, directly stored it into the Space.

Then she stored the nearby rosewood display racks as well.

Each rack held multiple embroidered boxes, but she did not have time to inspect them closely. She decided to collect everything first and examine them later.

She also stored several tall storage shelves.

Placing these shelves inside the Space would help organize the growing piles of supplies.

However, while collecting the last rosewood rack, Cecilia suddenly felt a sharp sting in her hand.

Looking down, she realized she was bleeding.

Surprised, she raised her head and noticed a palm sized cactus sitting on top of the rack.

But the cactus had turned a strange crimson red.

The spines on it were slowly moving, as if sensing direction and clearing potential threats.

It was a mutated cactus.

However, the degree of mutation was extremely low and posed no real threat to Cecilia.

Without hesitation, she stored the cactus into the Space as well.
