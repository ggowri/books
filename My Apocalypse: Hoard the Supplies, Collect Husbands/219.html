Chapter 219 The Golden Horned Python

Cecilia thought to herself that whatever that thing was, it was bound to be extremely difficult to handle.

So she took Blackie straight into the Space.

Ever since the last upgrade, the Space had allowed her to see what was happening outside while staying inside it.

The very instant Cecilia and Blackie disappeared into the Space, the enormous box was smashed open.

And when she saw what burst out from inside, she was stunned.

"What on earth..."

What came charging out of the box was a Golden Horned Python.

Its body was over a metre wide, and its length rivalled that of a lorry.

On top of its head was a beautiful golden horn over a foot long.

At this moment, however, part of it had snapped off. It must have broken when the creature had rammed itself madly against the box in its attempt to break out.

It was easy to imagine just how terrifying it must have looked, coiled up inside that enormous container.

What shocked Cecilia even more was that the python's body was riddled with syringes.

Some had already emptied their contents fully into its body. Others had only been pushed halfway in.

It was obvious that this python had been experimented on countless times.

She suddenly remembered that during the acid rain, she had once seen another gigantic golden python. At the time, though, that one had not had a horn on its head. She did not know whether this was the same snake or not.

But regardless, the one before her now was clearly far stronger than the one she had seen back then.

The python lunged straight for the corpse of the fat researcher.

In only two or three gulps, it swallowed the man whole.

Cecilia guessed that the researcher must have tormented this python endlessly in ordinary times.

He had probably performed experiment after experiment on it, leaving the great snake with a hatred beyond measure.

Just now, when the fat researcher's blood had spilled out, it had stimulated the python so violently that it had gone mad and forced its way out at any cost, even if that meant breaking its own horn in the process.

After swallowing the man, the snake lifted its head and warily scanned its surroundings, as though searching for something.

From its scarlet eyes and the two enormous venomous fangs at the front of its mouth, Cecilia could tell that it was no ordinary threat.

More than that, the python clearly possessed a certain level of intelligence. There was a disturbingly human quality in its eyes.

The look it cast about the room now was full of vigilance and calculation.

Hidden inside the Space, Cecilia was not afraid of it for the moment.

But just then Sprite suddenly came rushing over and leapt straight onto her shoulder.

"Master, that mutant monster looks delicious!" he cried, then began licking his paws on Cecilia's shoulder, as though imagining they were the Golden Horned Python and trying to satisfy his cravings that way.

"Eat, eat, eat. At this rate I'll chuck you out there and let the python eat you instead."

Cecilia weighed the matter up and decided that, as things stood, she probably could not beat this mutant Golden Horned Python.

Its size alone made that clear. Standing in front of it, she would be nothing more than a snack delivered straight to its mouth.

And mutant creatures were usually powerful, tough-bodied, and often possessed superpowers as well.

Killing this Golden Horned Python would not be easy.

"Master, you can leave the python itself, but that horn on its head is a good thing. Get it for me."

Sprite extended a paw and pointed, through the view the Space gave them, at the broken horn on the python's head.

Even broken, the horn was still over two feet long, thicker towards the base.

The snake had snapped off only the sharp tip, so what remained was still substantial.

At Sprite's words, Cecilia's eyes flickered thoughtfully.

"I do have an idea."

...

Outside, the Golden Horned Python continued to inspect the area, deeply wary.

A moment ago, through the box, it had clearly seen another two-legged creature outside, along with a mutant dog that had superpowers. Yet in the blink of an eye, both had vanished.

It had smashed its horn to get out because it wanted to devour the researcher and avenge itself.

The smell of the researcher's blood had driven it into a frenzy.

At the same time, though, it had also caught the scent of that mutant dog.

What a delicious smell.

It was certain that if it could eat that dog, not only would the broken horn on its head grow back, but its own strength would improve as well.

And yet now it felt uneasy, because both the human and the dog had disappeared.

Then, suddenly, it heard movement behind it and whipped its head round at once.

Cecilia stepped out of the Space in an instant.

The principle of the Space was simple. Wherever she entered it, she would reappear in exactly the same place.

So Cecilia emerged right in front of the box again.

The moment she appeared, the Golden Horned Python snapped its head round.

Its scarlet eyes blazed with excitement, tinged with a vicious streak, as it shot straight towards Cecilia.

But at the exact instant it lunged for her, a black shape dropped down from the air as well.

It was Blackie.

The moment he sprang out, he grew larger.

Just as the mutant Golden Horned Python opened its blood-red maw and charged, Blackie rammed into it with his massive body and knocked it sideways.

At the same time, Cecilia launched herself off the ground and leapt directly onto the python's head.

Its thick tongue was as large as a table. Cecilia gripped the broken python horn tightly and planted her feet on top of its head.

Blackie's enormous body pinned the Golden Horned Python hard against the edge of the warehouse.

No matter how violently it struggled, Blackie did not budge in the slightest.
