Chapter 99 Run Away

Frank glared at Cecilia through clenched teeth, unable to believe this woman could still be so arrogant under these circumstances.

"Cecilia, I came here today for answers! How exactly did my grandson Alexander die?"

Miranda had lost her earlier fury, her words shifting from curses to demanding "answers."

"The answers you want? I actually have them. I know exactly how Alexander died. But why should I tell you?" Cecilia looked at Miranda coolly. "Alexander betrayed me. Now that he's dead, I couldn't be happier. Besides, what do I owe the Barnes family? Why would I give you information you're so desperate for? So you can get revenge for your grandson? What's in it for me?"

Miranda then turned her gaze to Arthur behind her. "Cecilia, I never thought you would be twisted enough to get involved with my family's bastard son."

At the words "bastard son," Arthur's eyes narrowed slightly, but he didn't react.

Cecilia fixed Miranda with a hostile stare. "Miranda, I thought you were smarter than this. Do you really think provoking us right now helps you? At this moment, in this entire building, I'm the only one who knows how Alexander died—and I have proof. Not only that, but wait much longer and you might miss your chance for revenge entirely, because the killer is currently packing to leave."

Miranda glared at Cecilia with pure hatred.

"Give me one of your custom vehicles—everything included—and I'll tell you what happened to Alexander. The proof will speak for itself." Cecilia watched Miranda with cruel amusement.

Miranda hesitated for a moment, then finally nodded. "Fine."

*****

Meanwhile, Rebecca already knew Miranda had arrived and noticed they'd gone to the 66th floor.

She was busy packing with Andy, preparing to flee!

"Andy, let's get out of here while they're upstairs. They have guns!" Rebecca grabbed Andy's arm.

The two had just discussed it—although Miranda had gone to find Cecilia, they hadn't heard any gunshots after all this time, meaning Miranda hadn't immediately killed Cecilia.

That meant Cecilia might reveal what they'd done.

They packed hastily, shouldering two large bags as they prepared to leave.

But just two floors down, they heard footsteps from above and Miranda's furious scream. "Rebecca, you bitch! Alexander loved you so much! I'm going to kill you, tear you apart!"

Rebecca's face went white.

She and Andy rushed downstairs.

But when Frank's group reached Rebecca's floor and broke down the door, they quickly discovered they had packed up and fled.

When Miranda saw the blood splattered across the bedroom, her already exhausted body nearly collapsed.

"Get them! Bring those two bastards back to me!"

Miranda pounded the floor with her fists, screaming in rage.

Frank quickly led his men in pursuit.

In Miranda's hand was a recording device—given to her by Cecilia.

She gripped the recorder tightly, tears streaming down her face.

Inside was evidence Cecilia had captured of Rebecca killing Alexander.

Cecilia had secretly recorded it when she came to investigate a few days ago.

"Andy, what are you doing?"

"Look, didn't you say he can't die? I know his condition—if we don't do something, his whole arm will die and he'll really lose it. We're actually saving him by cutting it off."

...

"Andy, thank you. But you have to back me up—if the Barnes family comes looking later, you have to testify that everything we did was to keep Alexander alive."

...

"Tell me, what's a delicate little thing like you going to do now? How about I take care of you?"

After that came the sounds of Rebecca and Andy's intimate moments.

The more Miranda listened, the more her fury blazed—she wanted to cut these two into pieces!

Rebecca and Andy fled downstairs, but in the bitter cold they were bundled in heavy clothing. These past few days, Andy had been somewhat... overindulgent, making his steps unsteady.

Initially, he'd held tightly to Rebecca's hand.

But when he realized their pursuers were closing in and Rebecca stumbled, he shook her off without hesitation.

"Andy!"

Rebecca scrambled up from the ground and grabbed Andy's arm.

Andy panicked. He'd only gotten with Rebecca for her looks—whatever he'd felt for her vanished the second his life was on the line.

"This is your problem! I didn't have anything to do with this!"

Andy gritted his teeth, shoved Rebecca's hand away, and bolted.

Rebecca was shoved aside, her face smashing directly into the stair railing.

She'd been sweating from the run, and the moment her face pressed against the freezing metal, the sweat froze solid.

Her face was stuck to the railing.

Like a tongue on a frozen flagpole—she needed warm water to get free.

Frank's footsteps echoed closer from above.

Shit. No time for water.

Rebecca gritted her teeth and yanked her head back.
