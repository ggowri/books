Chapter 30 Spend Every Last Cent

All these changes got Cecilia pretty excited. Coming here had absolutely been worth it.

She turned the purple pendant over in her palm, half-tempted for a moment. What would happen if she fed this to the Space too? But she quickly pushed the thought away.

Once the apocalypse hit, there would be countless unpredictable dangers, so it was better to keep the pendant for now. Besides, the Space's current upgrade was already pretty incredible.

Once the Space's upgrade stabilized, she tried bringing Blackie into the Space with her. To her delight, it worked!

That meant the Space could now hold both her and Blackie at the same time, which gave her one more layer of protection against whatever dangers lay ahead.

That evening, Cecilia took Blackie for a couple of walks around the neighborhood and picked up a bunch of local specialties.

People here were obsessed with cured meats, and pretty much every grocery store had entire sections dedicated to them. Besides the sausages, there were smoked ribs, beef jerky, dried fish, and all kinds of other preserved goods.

Cecilia hit up several nearby grocery stores and cleared out their entire stock. After that, she tracked down the biggest frozen food wholesaler in the area.

They'd already closed for the day, but she called and convinced someone to come back and open up for her. Once they got there, she didn't waste any time and loaded up two full rental trucks with all kinds of frozen appetizers and ready-to-cook meals.

Then she hit the food court for some street food, grabbed enough to feed herself and Blackie, and finally headed back to the hotel feeling pretty satisfied with herself. No matter how she looked at it, this trip had been totally worth it.

But then, right before bed, Cecilia checked her bank account and realized she still had over two million dollars left. First thing tomorrow morning, she was going to spend every last cent.

Blackie was curled up next to Cecilia, eyes half-closed as he drifted off to sleep, when he suddenly heard the gasp she let out at the sight of her account balance. His eyes flew open right away.

After checking that there wasn't any danger and seeing that Cecilia was just lying there under the covers, he relaxed.

But even with his boosted intelligence, he still couldn't figure out her emotional rollercoaster. One second she was shocked, and the next she looked like she was gearing up for battle.

Blackie just turned around and pointed his butt at her. He thought humans were impossible to figure out. Whatever—he was gonna hit the hay.

*****

The next day, it was still raining, and it was coming down even harder than before.

Cecilia woke up a little after five in the morning. She didn't have much to pack, so she took Blackie outside for a quick bathroom break, tucked him back into the Space, threw her backpack on, and headed out to another gemstone market.

Her mission was simple—spend every last dollar in her account. Not a penny left over. With that goal in mind, she walked through the doors of the gemstone market.

This market had more finished pieces and high-end merchandise, which was perfect for Cecilia's goal of spending money.

Unlike the last market, the vendors here weren't in any rush to make sales, and there were hardly any livestreams going on.

Most of them just sat at their stalls scrolling through their phones or sipping coffee.

The stalls themselves were much more upscale too. There weren't any gems sitting on the ground. Everything was displayed in beautiful showcases and arranged neatly on the counters.

Cecilia walked from stall to stall but didn't feel any reaction from her pendant. Just when she was about to give up, she suddenly felt that vibration again, and it was even stronger than when she'd found the purple pendant. Her heart jumped.

But there were so many items around her. She was standing in front of a display case packed with bracelets.

Cecilia kept her expression neutral and reached out, slowly moving her hand across the pieces.

Finally, in the section closest to the vendor at the very back, she found it. The bracelet had a beautiful translucent emerald that was at least ice grade, with a vivid green that she absolutely loved.

But just as she reached for it, the vendor gently pushed her hand away. "Sorry, but we don't let people handle that bracelet casually. Why don't you look at some of our other pieces?"

The vendor was a woman in her fifties who had a warm smile and carried herself with an air of quiet elegance.

Cecilia smiled back, knowing the vendor assumed she couldn't afford it. She checked the sensation again and felt absolutely certain this was the right bracelet.

She looked up at the woman. "Ma'am, how much is this bracelet? I'd like to buy it."

What Cecilia didn't expect was that the bracelet cost 400 thousand dollars more than all the money she had.

She spent forever trying to haggle the price down, but in the end, she was still a hundred thousand short.

Left with no choice, Cecilia maxed out every loan app and credit card she could access.

It didn't matter anyway. Once the apocalypse hit, money would become worthless paper.

These online loans would disappear when the power grid and internet collapsed, and people would go back to bartering.

According to her memories, it would be years before currency came back into use.

When that happened, it wouldn't be tied to her old assets anymore and would become completely new currency.

After Cecilia finished spending all her money, she grabbed a bunch of local street food to take with her and finally caught a flight home.
