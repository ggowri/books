Chapter 51 Strengthening Defenses

Arthur had seen many beautiful women, but Cecilia's beauty was unique, especially in an apocalypse like this.

Everyone around was in a state of panic. even those with food at home dared not eat their fill, their nerves stretched taut with fear, leaving most people sallow-faced, emaciated, and listless.

But Cecilia was different: spirited, clear-minded, calm in the face of events, carrying a composure unusual for her age.

"You just said you want to remove the doors from the other units on this floor to fortify our level?"

Cecilia interrupted Arthur's scrutiny.

Arthur nodded and explained his idea, "I used to live on the sixth floor. Yesterday, I saw the first floor get flooded with my own eyes, and the people inside the building all have their own hidden agendas.

"Residents from several floors have already been complaining about having no food and are preparing to break into the doors of unoccupied units."

Arthur's meaning was clear: things haven't completely descended into chaos yet, so this is the chance to reinforce this floor's defenses.

Cecilia gave a cold laugh and got straight to the point. "But how can I trust you? To the outside, it could be called fortification. But internally, it could be a trap for me."

Arthur looked at Cecilia with great appreciation. "Miss..."

"You can call me Ms. Summers."

"Ms. Summers, I greatly admire your frankness and courage. So, to earn your trust, let me introduce myself first."

Arthur looked at Cecilia, rummaged in his pocket, and quickly pulled out a document.

Cecilia naturally took the document and saw it was a booklet with the words "Certificate of Discharge for Compulsory Military Service" written on it.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow.

To think he's a discharged soldier.

"I, Arthur Barnes, can swear to you that under non-essential circumstances, I will absolutely not target you."

Cecilia gave a light laugh. "Non-essential circumstances?"

Arthur nodded slightly, also with a faint smile. "Yes, if you try to kill me, I will certainly fight back."

Cecilia looked at Arthur and saw a sense of "seriousness" in his deep brown eyes.

She knew that every word Arthur said was not a joke.

Cecilia handed the document back to Arthur. "Mr. Barnes, I have no interest in killing you for now, so we can cooperate."

Arthur, just as Cecilia had caught the key point in his words earlier, repeated, "For now?"

Imitating Arthur's earlier posture, Cecilia crossed her arms and said with a faint smile. "Yes, for now. After all, we're not familiar with each other yet."

The two exchanged a smile and didn't say anything more.

At that moment, Blackie pushed the door open with its snout and came out from inside the room.

At that instant, the corner of Arthur's eye twitched slightly, but it was a very subtle twitch, not noticeable to Cecilia.

Cecilia patted Blackie's head. "Do you have tools for prying doors or installing them? I have nothing at home."

Cecilia did not want to reveal her supplies.

"I do," Arthur replied cleanly and decisively.

Cecilia nodded. "Alright, then let's see which unit we should start prying from first? I know there's one on this floor that sells imported snacks, and it even has an extra-wide security door installed. We can try that one first."

Thus, the two found themselves in complete agreement.

They found the doorway of the resident Cecilia, who had long been noted for selling imported snacks, and Arthur efficiently removed the door.

Cecilia assisted nearby, while Blackie kept watch at the stairwell entrance.

In these apocalyptic times, with everyone on edge, any movement on a floor would draw curious, prying eyes from other floors.

Blackie stood at the stairwell entrance, baring its teeth at anyone who approached.

Within a few hours, it had already scared away seven or eight visitors.

In the middle of it all, what disgusted Cecilia the most was that man named Bryan!

Bryan certainly wouldn't miss such a commotion.

Hearing the noise from the top floor, he immediately scampered up.

He hadn't seen the top-floor resident before, but from the voice, he could tell it was a young girl.

Moreover, the voice was cool and carried a hint of arrogance, which made his heart itch with desire.

Yesterday, he heard someone from a lower floor had moved up, and he was also considering moving to the top floor.

However, before he could act, there was a big commotion on the top floor today.

As soon as he reached the top floor, he saw a black dog, Blackie, guarding the stairwell entrance, lowering its body slightly and baring its teeth at him.

Bryan was startled and took a step back.

Blackie now stood almost 5 feet tall when upright and had grown a bit more these past few days, blocking the doorway like a small pony.

"Hey... we're all neighbors, what's the meaning of this?" Bryan, unwilling to back down, shouted towards the interior of the floor.

Cecilia and Arthur continued working, ignoring him completely.

After all, with Blackie there, it was like one warrior holding the pass against ten thousand.

Seeing that no one paid him any attention, Bryan gritted his teeth in frustration.

But just as he was about to leave, he caught a glimpse of Cecilia's figure flashing past in the distance.

Her curvaceous lines and the long legs wrapped in jeans made his eyes glaze over.

Perhaps Bryan's gaze was too blatant, as Cecilia instinctively turned her head back.

When Cecilia turned around, Bryan felt an immediate reaction in his groin.

"What a tempting little vixen!" Bryan couldn't help but murmur.

Blackie growled and stared coldly at Bryan, its large body taking a step forward, clearly ready to launch an attack on Bryan at any moment.
