Chapter 97 Who Killed Him

The burly man at the front carefully helped Mrs. Barnes to her feet.

Mrs. Barnes had regained much of her composure. She closed her eyes and took several deep breaths, tears streaming down her face.

After a moment, she steadied herself and said, "All of you, be careful. Don't... don't break him."

With that, Mrs. Barnes turned and got back into the car.

The team leader waved his hand. "Get the tools. Let's get him out of there!"

"Yes, sir!" the other men replied in unison.

...

Up on the rooftop, Cecilia stood watching the scene below with Arthur and Hailey.

Even Mrs. Barnes's anguished screams had carried up to them—the world had become so eerily quiet that every sound echoed.

"The Barnes are here," Cecilia observed, glancing at Arthur beside her.

Arthur's expression remained impassive. "What does that have to do with me?"

Cecilia looked at him thoughtfully. "Sorry, but I'm afraid today it might actually have quite a bit to do with you."

Arthur paused, turning to study her.

Cecilia flashed him a bright, wide smile. "Help me out here? I'll owe you one."

Something about that smile gave Arthur an uneasy feeling, but without hesitation, he nodded.

Down below, Miranda's men spent roughly half an hour chiseling Alexander's frozen body from the ice.

His body had long since gone rigid, but the freezing temperatures had prevented decay. Still, jumpers rarely looked good, and his face was unrecognizable now.

Miranda held Alexander's body without any sign of revulsion, crying for a long time before finally having the men wrap the corpse in a large bag for transport.

Once that was done, Miranda narrowed her eyes at the towering building before her and marched inside with murderous intent.

Everyone in the building was watching now.

Mabel ordered her husband to bolt the door and under no circumstances open it for anyone.

But within minutes, their door was kicked in!

The team leader threw a young man—one of the few remaining residents who'd been lurking in the stairwell to watch the commotion—into the their apartment.

Mabel recognized him. He lived somewhere in the building, a thin, not-too-bright guy who clearly lacked common sense. Apparently, he'd tried to investigate the newcomers and gotten caught.

The team leader pointed his gun at Mabel's head. "Word is, you're the one running things in this building?"

Mabel wanted to scream! She'd only managed a few situations—she was hardly in charge of anything. But she didn't dare deny it now, nodding reluctantly.

"You know Alexander Barnes?" he asked.

"I... I know him. He used to live on the fifth floor, then moved to the forty-eighth... with his girlfriend. They lived together."

Mabel's voice shook as she answered.

"That bitch got Alexander killed?" Mrs. Barnes asked coldly.

"I... I don't know. Alexander died two days ago. I don't know if he jumped or was thrown from the building. I really don't know anything!"

By the end, Mabel was nearly in tears under the gun's threat.

But Miranda wasn't having any of it. "What else do you know? Tell me everything! And what else is in this building—give me every detail!"

After being kicked, Mabel scrambled to her husband's side.

Her husband, seeing his wife attacked, pulled her into his arms. Finding courage from somewhere, he shouted at Miranda's murderous glare, "Go after the real culprits! If you want revenge for Alexander, you should check the sixty-sixth floor! As far as I know, Alexander had a grudge against someone up there named Cecilia! Not only that, I heard Alexander call one of the men up there 'Uncle Arthur.' Alexander's death definitely has something to do with them."

When Miranda heard "Cecilia," her mind nearly exploded.

"Cecilia is in this building?"

She certainly remembered their last encounter. That girl who'd once seemed so meek and submissive had turned into a wolf with fangs.

And Alexander's Uncle Arthur?

How could she have forgotten about him!

Suddenly, all the pieces clicked into place in Miranda's mind. Every clue screamed the same thing—Alexander's death was definitely connected to Cecilia and Arthur!

Seeing Miranda's expression, Mabel knew she'd hit something important. Gathering her courage, she added, "I've seen them arguing. I even saw Alexander and his girlfriend go up there begging for something, but they were both turned away."

Hearing this, Miranda clenched her fists and struck the air furiously.

She was filled with regret. Why hadn't she come to save her grandson sooner? The Barnes family had lost its most important heir.

And Alexander's horrific condition clearly showed he'd been tortured before his death.

The thought cut through Miranda like a knife.

"Where are Cecilia and Arthur? What floor? I'm going up there right now and tearing them both apart!"

...

Meanwhile, Cecilia returned to her apartment briefly. When she came back out, she was carrying her handgun.

She returned to Arthur's apartment and, with a flick of her wrist, revealed the weapon.
