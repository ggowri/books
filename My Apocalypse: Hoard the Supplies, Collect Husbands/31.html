Chapter 31 The Solar Phone Charger

Cecilia made sure to plan ahead and withdrew 10 thousand dollars in cash with her credit card, just in case she needed it for emergencies.

By the time she got to the airport, the rain was coming down even harder. The flight got delayed because of the weather, so Cecilia ended up stuck at the airport for over an hour.

She kept refreshing the weather forecast on her phone, and with every update, her uneasy feeling grew. If the flight didn't take off tomorrow, she would have to hire a car and drive back herself.

After another hour, the airport finally announced that the flight was cleared for takeoff, and Cecilia let out a huge sigh of relief.

During the long wait, she overheard conversations about the increasingly bad weather. Some passengers mentioned that their homes in low-lying areas were already starting to flood, and the old houses back in their hometowns were leaking all over the place.

Cecilia listened to all this with her brows furrowed, trying to remember the timeline from her previous life.

If the heavy rain before had made her think the apocalypse was coming, then all these reports today were practically a warning that it could be tomorrow.

The plane finally made it back to her city, but because of the awful weather, it had to circle for ages before it could land. As soon as Cecilia got off the plane, she rushed to grab a cab back to her apartment.

But the second she walked through her door, she suddenly remembered that the solar phone charger she'd ordered online was still sitting at the pickup location.

She looked outside at the storm and gritted her teeth. She had to go get it.

But just as she was about to head out, Blackie grabbed his leash in his mouth and stared at her with those big dark eyes.

Cecilia tried to push his head away, but Blackie wouldn't budge. She got it. He wanted to go outside to pee.

Sure, there was a litter box Blackie could use, but he preferred doing his business outside on the grass while walking in circles.

Cecilia figured that once things got really bad, there probably wouldn't be many chances for him to run around outside anymore. So tonight, while it wasn't acid rain yet, she might as well let him enjoy the grass one last time.

Since it was pouring rain outside, Cecilia put a raincoat on Blackie before they left. He clearly wasn't thrilled about wearing it, but he tolerated it.

The second they stepped outside though, the wind nearly knocked both of them off their feet.

The storm had worsened in just a few minutes. The wind had picked up, and the rain was coming down harder than ever.

Cecilia watched as a small tree near the entrance snapped in the wind. Someone walking underneath barely managed to dodge it in time.

Fall had already arrived, and most of the leaves had turned yellow. The storm tore them loose, and they rained down in sheets.

The wind whipped them around like little blades, and if one of those sharp edges caught you across the face, it would definitely draw blood.

She glanced down at Blackie, thinking she should take him back inside first and then grab the solar panel herself. But just as she started leading him back, something massive came flying at her.

Cecilia didn't have time to react. The object hurtled straight at her face, and she'd barely started to raise her arm when something shot past her and knocked it away. She heard a muffled grunt beside her.

Looking down, she saw what had nearly hit her—a yoga studio sign. The fabric cover was ripped, exposing the solid metal frame underneath. If that thing had struck her, she didn't want to think about what would have happened.

And the one who'd saved her was Blackie. He was still standing, but Cecilia could see him holding one leg up. His paw dangled uselessly. He was hurt.

"Oh my God! Blackie!" Her eyes welled up with tears immediately.

She'd survived years in the apocalypse and seen every kind of betrayal. Rebecca and Alexander had both turned on her, and it had cost her everything.

But no one had ever protected her like this. No one had ever put themselves between her and danger without thinking twice.

Before Cecilia knew what she was doing, she scooped Blackie up in her arms.

He weighed well over a hundred pounds now, all solid muscle. He looked confused and clearly wasn't used to being carried like this, but Cecilia held him tight. "Stay still, Blackie. Mommy's taking you home."

The word "Mommy" felt strange coming out of her mouth, but at that moment, she embraced it fully. She was a full-on crazy dog lady, and she owned it.

Blackie seemed to understand what she meant because he went completely still in her arms.

Cecilia carried him through the howling storm back to the apartment building. The moment they entered, the wind and rain were shut out. She finally put Blackie down, and even she felt she'd barely escaped with her life.

She turned back and looked through the glass doors. The world outside had changed.

The wind bent trees sideways, snapping branches that crashed down onto parked cars.

From where she stood, Cecilia could see one guy climb out from under the wreckage, shaken but okay.

Store signs tumbled through the air like deadly frisbees. Some cyclists had already given up and were walking their bikes, looking for shelter from the storm.

But there was no escaping this storm. It was only going to get worse from here. The wind would keep howling, and soon the acid rain would start.

The apocalypse was really coming.

Once Cecilia got home, she checked Blackie's paw.
