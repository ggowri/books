Chapter 187 A Shot for Jet

Colonel Reed hurriedly caught hold of Cecilia. "Miss Summers, Colonel Barnes cannot die here. Even if he's only got one breath left in him, he has to leave my shelter before he dies."

He murmured the second sentence quietly into her ear.

Cecilia frowned and looked at him. Faced with the sincerity in his eyes, she finally gave a small nod.

"I understand."

Thomas had helped her more than once, and he was the sort of man who acted with reason and principle.

On top of that, the old general above him had once known her grandfather.

But Blackie, as he was now...

"Blackie," Cecilia called.

Blackie turned his head stiffly. His chest was still heaving with anger.

Yet the instant he saw it was Cecilia, his tail gave an instinctive wag.

"It's all right now, Blackie."

Cecilia stepped forwards and spoke softly, trying to soothe him.

The moment she reached him and her hand came to rest gently on his head, every taut muscle in Blackie's body loosened at once.

The savagery faded from those ice blue eyes, replaced by a trace of sorrow.

Cecilia could feel him nuzzle faintly into her palm.

His whole body was trembling.

Then Blackie caught her sleeve gently in his teeth, stepped over the bodies at his feet without a second glance, and led her to the injured Jet.

The moment Jet saw Cecilia, he forced himself upright from the floor. Dragging his wounded wing behind him, he shuffled over to her side.

The lower half of that hanging wing was soaked with blood.

Even so, Jet still gave two chattering little cries, though they were weak and breathless.

At the sight of it, Cecilia's temper flared at once.

"Who hurt my Jet?"

Thomas, standing nearby, felt a jolt go through him.

Surely not. Was she about to lose her mind? Had she not just promised him?

He rushed forwards at once, intending to talk her down.

As chance would have it, Colonel Barnes was stirred awake when Cecilia and Blackie stepped past and trod on him.

The moment he opened his eyes, he saw blood everywhere around him, Blackie still not far away, and Cecilia staring at him with a dangerous look on her face.

Then he spotted Thomas running over.

"Thomas, save me! Thomas!"

Colonel Barnes lit up at once.

He knew that if Thomas was here, he might still live.

However bad their relationship was, Thomas was still a measured man. He would never want to bring trouble down on his old general.

Arthur, who had been silent until now, suddenly held out the gun in his hand to Cecilia.

"The wound on Jet's wing was made by a Mauser M2 pistol. Out of everyone here, only that old Barnes bastard is carrying one."

Cecilia took the gun.

Colonel Barnes began shouting even louder. "Thomas, save me!"

Thomas rushed forwards as well, trying to stop her. "Miss Summers, think carefully!"

Cecilia raised the gun and aimed it at Colonel Barnes.

Bang.

The moment Thomas heard the shot, it felt as though something exploded inside his head.

In an instant he could picture the old general thrashing him bloody with a whip.

And yet, at the same time, a deep fierce sense of satisfaction rose in him.

Jeremy Barnes deserved it.

The entire Barnes family deserved it.

Over the years Thomas had suffered plenty of underhanded mistreatment from Jeremy.

His own general had never got on with the general behind Jeremy either. Jeremy was nothing more than that man's dog.

Originally, the Barnes family had not had much to throw their weight around with. Other than Jeremy, they had been a half dead old family in decline.

But at the very start of the apocalypse, no one knew what method Jeremy had used to win over a criminal organisation the military had long been unable to crush.

That gang had even signed an agreement with the military, sparing countless soldiers from being sent to their deaths.

That was how Jeremy and the Barnes family had carved out a place for themselves within the government structure that remained after the apocalypse.

In private, though, the Barnes family threw their weight around and swallowed up supplies for themselves.

Thomas had long since despised them.

It was only because the old general had repeatedly warned him to endure it and avoid direct conflict with Jeremy if at all possible that he had held himself back.

And now, looking at Jeremy sprawled there in such a wretched death, Thomas drew in a slow breath.

A bullet had punched through his head, brain matter leaking from the back. One arm was gone. His shoulder had collapsed inward where the earlier shot had hit. He lay in a pool of blood, his face frozen in terror and desperation, his eyes still wide open even in death.

Inwardly, Thomas thought with savage satisfaction.

Serves you right.

But his mind was already racing.

The Barnes family, and the forces behind Jeremy, would never let this go.

"Who else knows Colonel Barnes came here today?" Thomas turned and asked one of his soldiers.

The young man stepped forwards. "His son and several bodyguards are downstairs..."

"That complicates things," Thomas muttered.

Meanwhile, the three surviving soldiers Jeremy had brought were finally coming back to themselves after the shock.

The old veteran among them gathered his courage and stepped forward, pointing at Thomas.

"Colonel Reed, if you dare shield the killer who murdered our commander, our general will never let you off."

Thomas gave a cold snort. "Rubbish. Have you ever heard of self defence? You barged into someone else's home and into my shelter to throw your weight around, and now you want to talk rules? This is my territory, not the Barnes family's."

The veteran knew better than to push his luck. He quickly stopped the other two soldiers, who were still trying to argue.
