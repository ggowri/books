Chapter 128 Midnight Mission

Blackie's nose twitched like crazy, sniffing hard at the air.

His eyes locked onto the dog loaves in Cecilia's hand like a heat-seeking missile.

Meanwhile, Jet hopped around frantically, putting on his best suck-up act.

"Human, gimme! Human, please~"

Cecilia stared at them, stunned.

Does the Space's food really have that much pull over these two?

She handed out the feed and loaves to Jet and Blackie.

Jet attacked his portion, wolfing it down in seconds.

Blackie ate slowly, savoring every bite like fine wine, clearly wanting to make it last.

So when Jet finished and smacked his beak, eyeing Blackie's half-eaten loaf, he wasn't happy. He turned those pitiful eyes back to Cecilia.

"Boss, I want more, Boss~"

Cecilia froze.

Boss now? Seriously?

What a shameless, opportunistic bird!

Of course, she didn't give him more. The Space had said this stuff could restore pets' evolution abilities.

Last time Blackie hunted that mutant weasel, he'd come back completely drained. Took ages and tons of regular food to bounce back.

Next time she'd give him a loaf—maybe just one would do the trick.

Despite her constant griping about the Space, Cecilia was actually pretty damn satisfied with what it produced.

Feeling content, she headed to bed and slept straight through till late morning.

******

When Hailey and Arthur knocked, Cecilia was curled up inside munching a croissant and binge-watching her show.

"Arthur says we're hitting up a supply run tonight. You in?" Hailey asked with a grin.

Her facial wounds had scabbed over and fallen off, leaving angry pink scars—but honestly, she looked way better now.

Arthur nodded beside her. "Tonight, we go big."

Turns out, Arthur's idea of "going big" meant raiding a grain warehouse in some remote village outside the city.

The place sat high up in the hills. They grew mostly sorghum and corn out there.

And right before the apocalypse hit, it was harvest season—perfect timing for those crops to be dried and stored.

Since the village was far from any population centers, there was a good chance tons of grain were still sitting there untouched.

Honestly, with all the food Cecilia had stockpiled—plus this fresh batch of Space-produced pet food—she could totally skip the supply runs with those two.

But going solo would draw attention. Make people wonder. And wondering led to questions she couldn't answer—like how she had a freaking Space.

Plus, when it came to supplies, more was always better. So she agreed.

Only catch? This trip would take two days, easy.

During the day, they held a quick meeting to plan tonight's run.

Final verdict—most practical option was letting Blackie pull the cart.

But then who stayed behind to guard the place?

"Whoever stays gets a quarter of the haul," Cecilia said.

That quarter included counting Blackie as part of the crew.

After going back and forth, they decided Hailey would stay.

First, the 66th floor was a fortress now. Barely anyone left in the whole building. Unless someone came looking for trouble, the floor wouldn't fall.

Second, Cecilia could control Blackie—she was his owner, after all.

And Arthur? His strength and combat skills were needed out there more.

Settled.

Hailey looked embarrassed. "I'm just watching the door. I can take less."

"No need. If Blackie stayed behind, he'd get the same share. Mr. Barnes and I already agreed."

And just like that, tonight's plan was locked in.

Since she'd slept pretty well last night, Cecilia wasn't tired today.

She pulled out some Magic Spring water from the Space, brewed herself a coffee, grabbed two books, and settled in by the skylight for some quality reading time.

Around seven or eight that evening, she fed Jet and Blackie, then took another lap through the Space.

Thankfully, the Space wasn't acting up today—everything it produced looked normal.

To prep for tonight's mission, she went back to her room for a nap.

When the midnight alarm went off, she got dressed.

This time, she wore the temperature-regulating underwear as a base layer, then pulled on professional-grade expedition gear—the kind people wore to Antarctica. Sleeves and cuffs locked tight. Lightweight but warm as hell.

She'd stockpiled this stuff pre-apocalypse—top-tier Ice Age equipment.

Normally you'd need to layer a ton of heavy clothes underneath, but with the temp-regulating gear? She was set.

Inside her snow boots, she wore Space-made red-and-green socks.

Mismatched and eye-searing, sure. But damn, they kept her feet warm.

Just as she finished getting ready to head out, Cecilia turned around.

Jet stared at her with the most pitiful, accusing look.

"Bastard. Leaving me behind again."

His tone dripped with dramatic longing, voice dragging out each word. It made her skin crawl.

"You're guarding the house! That's a super important job!"

Cecilia tried to sweet-talk him, but Jet just sulked harder.
