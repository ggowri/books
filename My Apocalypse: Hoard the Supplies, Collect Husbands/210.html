Chapter 210 A Taste of Home


"Ah!"

Vivian let out a piercing scream.

But almost at once, a huge snow white rat sprang out from behind and bit straight through her skull.

Then countless rats swarmed into her body, sucking her blood and gnawing at her organs.

Meanwhile, Arthur was already driving away from the warehouse where he and Cecilia had once fought the mutant rats, killed the rat king, and watched a new mutant rat king be born.

No one knew how many generations of rat kings had risen and fallen in that warehouse since then, nor how many people had gone there after Cecilia and Arthur left, hoping to find food, only to end up as meals for the mutant rats.

Still, it was a fitting end for a vicious woman.

Torn to pieces and devoured until not a scrap remained.

...

By the time Cecilia returned to the apartment building and stepped onto the sixty-sixth floor, she had barely reached the corridor when she saw Blaise standing there in an apron, holding a spatula, beaming at her.

"Cici, dinner's ready. We were just waiting for you. Come and eat."

Colonel Reed had changed into ordinary clothes and was gnawing on a chicken leg, grinning as he waved her over.

"Come on, come on, get in here and eat. That lad may be young, but his cooking is the real deal."

He looked completely at ease, as though he had known them for years.

Arthur came out as well. He looked at Cecilia and gave her a faint smile.

"You're back."

For one fleeting moment, Cecilia actually felt...

As though she had come home.

Including the ten years of the apocalypse in her previous life, this was the first time she had truly felt the warmth of home.

It was the bustle, and the little pocket of peace that followed a brutal fight in the middle of the apocalypse.

Since Blaise said there was still one last dish left to finish and plate up, Cecilia first went back to her own place to feed Blackie something.

After all, Blackie had shifted forms today and raced across the snow for so long that he must be starving by now.

But the moment she stepped inside, she heard a mournful cry.

"So you do remember to come back."

Looking down, she saw Jet staring up at her with tear-filled eyes.

Cecilia walked over and gently rubbed Jet's little head, praising him for guarding the home so well yet again.

To her surprise, Jet tilted his head slightly and ignored what she said.

After a long moment of wriggling about awkwardly, he finally looked at her and said, "You lot are going to a dinner party, aren't you? Take me with you."

Cecilia raised a brow. How does Jet know we're having dinner together?

"Blackie told me. He said you're all going round to the pretty lady's place for dinner, so I should come too."

The moment she heard it had come from Blackie, Cecilia strode over and seized the dog by the ear while he was still wolfing down his food.

"Blackie!"

Later, piecing together Jet's version of events, Cecilia managed to work out what Blackie had said.

The rough meaning was that Cecilia was about to go next door for something delicious, and Jet ought to pester his way into coming along, which would also give him a chance to see the lovely Silver.

As for Jet's pursuit of Silver, Blackie was thoroughly supportive.

In Blackie's eyes, Silver was prettier, flew faster than Jet, and on top of that was a specially trained carrier pigeon raised by Blaise, the mercenary, so she was clearly far more reliable than Jet.

Jet had puffed out his chest and repeated all this with the utmost pride.

Cecilia was both furious and exasperated.

"Jet, Silver is so much more capable than you. Even if you did marry her, you'd still just be living off her."

To Cecilia's astonishment, Jet puffed out his chest even more.

"Blackie said it doesn't matter who's living off whom as long as the advantage stays in the family."

Cecilia was so cross she smacked Blackie hard.

"Blackie, what exactly have you been teaching him?"

She spent ages lecturing Jet with things like "a man must rely on himself" and "there's no strength better than your own" before finally calming him down and persuading him to stay at home and recover properly. Once he was healed, he could go visiting next door and think about finding a wife then.

In the end, both of them were shut at home to reflect.

Cecilia took out a large cucumber from the Space, chopped it into finger-thick sticks, then quickly fried up a dipping sauce in a pan.

Carrying a large bowl of cucumber with dip, she went next door.

The place was lively.

Colonel Reed said that once the old general used the wound medicine Cecilia had provided, the bleeding stopped immediately. He was now resting and had passed the critical stage.

Although plenty of the Barnes family were still alive, those left were a useless lot and would never amount to anything.

For all practical purposes, the Barnes family was finished in this apocalypse.

"Hmph. The Barnes family really did get too bold. Fancy provoking the old general. Did they honestly think General Mercer could protect that entire lot of them forever?"

Blaise had cooked a whole table full of dishes.

There were braised pig's trotters, chestnut chicken, spicy fish, spicy sausage and egg skillet, honey-glazed pork ribs, fried peanuts...

But unexpectedly, the most popular thing on the table was the bowl of cucumber and dip Cecilia had brought.

Even Arthur, without a word and with remarkable speed, helped himself to quite a lot.

"These cucumbers are excellent. Were they hydroponic?" Colonel Reed asked between bites.

Cecilia smiled and nodded. "Something like that."

Hydroponic, watered with Magic Spring water?

In truth, by this point even Arthur did not have many fresh vegetables left.

He had his own hydroponic shelves growing greens, but in the cold weather they were growing painfully slowly.

Because Cecilia had previously taken the hydroponic racks given by the old general and had promised Arthur that she would share some of the vegetables once they grew, she had recently sent over some chillies as well.

But looking at the spread on the table, Cecilia could already tell one thing.

Even the rich household was starting to run low.
