Chapter 144 Black Hawk

Barry really did seem to love Blackie. He patted the dog's head and right there in the stairwell, unwrapped a sausage to feed him.

While he fed Blackie, the others headed inside. Barry leaned in close to Cecilia.

"Ms. Summers, when we went downstairs earlier, I talked to the guys with Captain Jackman. They said... you've got guns? And killed someone?"

Cecilia just looked at him. Said nothing.

She could tell Barry was genuine—and really did care about Blackie.

But Logan's attitude had her on edge. She couldn't fully trust this young soldier.

"Look, I'm not a stickler like him. I mean, come on—it's the apocalypse. Who cares if you killed someone? At the villa shelter, people die in fights every single day. He's just worried things will spiral. He's under a lot of pressure."

Barry glanced around, scratching Blackie's chin, a flash of anger crossing his face.

"But you guys? I think you got set up.

"I heard from Noah—one of the guys with Captain Jackman—that last time they came here to scout, they brought back some woman...

"Oh, and there's one named Phoebe. Pretty infamous at our shelter. She..."

Barry laid out the whole story.

It all clicked for Cecilia.

"Thanks, Barry."

She meant it.

"No problem. We can't let bad guys walk free—but we can't frame innocent people either." Barry grinned.

As Cecilia headed inside with Blackie, she caught a figure darting up the stairs.

Well, well. Wasn't that Phoebe?

Speaking of Phoebe, when she found out Logan was heading up to the 66th floor with his men, she was thrilled.

She figured she'd get a front-row seat to the drama, so she snuck into the stairwell on the 65th floor to watch.

But after just a few exchanges, a soldier came up talking about delivering medicine or something, saying the General wanted to see Cecilia.

The moment Phoebe heard that, her stomach dropped.

If Cecilia not only got off scot-free but also earned the officials' favor?

She was screwed.

So when Cecilia and the others headed downstairs to talk with Logan, Phoebe followed.

This time she kept her distance, terrified they'd spot her.

But then—she swore she heard one of the soldiers mention her name.

She crept closer, trying to catch what they were saying.

When she finally heard it clearly, her soul nearly left her body.

She turned to bolt—and her foot slipped.

The noise echoed.

Cecilia's head snapped toward her.

That smile. Half-amused, half-threatening. Terrifying.

Phoebe knew. She was done for.

She bolted back to her room and slammed the door shut, locking it tight.

The officials are here. Cecilia won't try anything.

As long as she stayed locked inside and kept her head down, she'd be fine.

******

Meanwhile, Cecilia glanced at Phoebe but didn't chase after her. Logan's guy was already rushing over, looking frantic.

She thanked Barry again and headed inside.

Inside, one of Logan's soldiers was throwing down with Arthur!

No weapons. Just raw, hand-to-hand combat.

Cecilia moved to intervene, but Hailey blocked her path.

"They're just sparring."

So Cecilia stayed put and watched.

The soldier and Arthur traded blows for a solid ten rounds. Pretty evenly matched.

But Cecilia could tell—Arthur wasn't going full throttle.

When he'd had enough, Arthur suddenly retreated, then vanished behind the soldier in a flash. His hand locked around the guy's throat.

"I lost." The soldier spat the words out, pissed.

"A bet's a bet."

Arthur's voice was cool, but his eyes were on Logan.

Logan took a deep breath. Finally nodded.

Two more soldiers stepped up to test Arthur. Same result.

Then Logan moved forward.

Arthur stopped him. "Sir, if you lose today, you'll lose face."

Logan's brow furrowed.

Arthur grinned. "I only used twenty percent just now."

Logan's frown deepened. Then he burst out laughing and clapped Arthur on the shoulder.

"Haha! Iron Blade Mercenary Corps, huh? We can't compete with that. Can't compete!"

Cecilia shot Arthur a look. What the hell?

Arthur smiled and extended his hand.

"Ms. Summers, let's start over. I lied before. I'm not some retired vet. I'm the leader of the assassin division for the Iron Blade Mercenary Corps on the US border. Codename: Black Hawk."

Logan added, "Mr. Barnes was also a top student in our country's special forces."

Arthur shrugged. "Got kicked out for insubordination during a mission."

Cecilia suddenly regretted wasting time chatting with Barry.

She'd missed one hell of a show.
