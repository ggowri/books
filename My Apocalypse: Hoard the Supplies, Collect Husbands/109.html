Chapter 109 A Desperate Plea

She reached the sixty-sixth floor. The door across opened.

Covered head to toe in filthy blood, she looked at them and smiled.

"Who's Cecilia? I heard you're the most ruthless person in this building..."

"Can you do me one favor? Kill me."

Cecilia eyed this clearly unstable woman, her expression turning cold.

In her past life during the apocalypse, she'd seen plenty of people who'd lost the will to live.

Some had lost all their loved ones. Some were disabled. Others just couldn't handle the apocalypse.

She'd actually killed someone who begged for it once.

That was after the insect plague hit.

She and her squad were scavenging a warehouse for supplies.

They'd stumbled upon a man half-eaten by bugs.

His entire leg was nothing but white bone, his organs pulled out, his face shredded beyond recognition.

He'd begged them to kill him.

Cecilia was closest, so she did it.

One strike. Straight through the heart.

Less than a second, and he was gone.

She still remembered—he died smiling. Relief written all over his face.

But this woman now...

Cecilia looked her up and down, then said coldly, "Want to die? Do it yourself. Or come at me if you've got the guts. If you can't even manage that, how the hell do you expect to die by my blade?"

She was done wasting words. It was the apocalypse, after all.

With that, she slammed the door shut.

"Kill me! Please, anyone, just kill me! Please!" Chloe screamed, pounding on the door.

Why is even dying this hard?

She'd thought about jumping from the building. Ending it all.

But she couldn't work up the courage.

So she'd come to the sixty-sixth floor—these people everyone called monsters.

But they didn't give a damn about her.

She'd gotten close to that bastard for revenge. Seduced him. Killed him in his sleep!

She'd done all that just to leave this world with no regrets.

But now... she couldn't seem to die no matter what.

Back home, she tried slitting her wrists. Couldn't do it.

She tried stripping off her clothes to freeze herself to death, but couldn't even last a minute.

After all that, she finally got it—she still wanted to live.

No matter how hopeless she felt, her survival instinct kicked in.

She wanted to keep going.

"What a pathetic life," Chloe muttered.

So after another day of thinking, she grabbed everything she owned and headed downstairs.

She braved the blizzard, heading toward Building B.

Back when Ashley tried to pimp her out, Building B was the target!

She probably figured it was far enough away—not like Building A, where everyone knew everyone. At least it wouldn't be so humiliating.

Back then, the corridor between A and B was still connected. Ashley dragged her over there by force.

That's where she saw her—this terrifying woman...

That day, Ashley got put in her place. Practically had to grovel on her knees to walk away alive.

And that woman looked at Chloe with contempt.

"Idiots like you? Not worth keeping alive. But if you ever wise up, come find me. People who've been through hell make the best weapons."

Now, Chloe trudged through the blizzard, stumbling downstairs until she finally reached Building B.

She looked up. The woman stood at an upstairs window, looking down with a faint, cruel smile.

Behind her, several men stood in a line—like they answered to her alone.

******

Meanwhile, Cecilia shut the door and went back to finish modding the sidecar motorcycle—minus its engine.

First, she wrapped the wheels with anti-slip chains. Then she added a soft-top she'd ripped off some convertible sports car. Premium quality stuff.

Cecilia tugged on it and grinned. "Hell yeah—this is what you get from a million-dollar ride!"

She welded the soft-top onto the sidecar frame.

Now she had shelter from the wind and snow.

At the front, she cut a hole and sealed it with clear plastic sheeting—so she could see outside.

At the bottom, two more holes—so she could reach out and grab Blackie's reins.

After finishing up, Cecilia inspected everything with satisfaction.

Then she grabbed the rope from her space and tied it to the sidecar.

Blackie circled it a few times, tail wagging, fascinated by this weird contraption.

Totally clueless that once night fell, Cecilia was taking him for a ride through the snow!

Done. Cecilia took a nap.

Around midnight, she pulled both Blackie and Jet into the Space and crept downstairs.

She glanced around. Arthur and Hailey weren't following. Good.

They were teammates now, sure—but some secrets she'd rather keep to herself.

After a week of nonstop snow, eight floors were buried.

Saved her some climbing time, at least.

Days of snowfall had turned everything into a white wasteland.
