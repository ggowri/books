Chapter 89 Three Kills

But he hadn't expected Kent's relentless fury. Even Kent's son, the little brat, joined in, pounding on the man's head.

"You monster! Give back my grandma! You killed her! We're taking everything you have, and you'll be our slave forever to make up for it!"

This was, in fact, a plan Kent and his son had concocted.

These days, Kent knew more than one person had died in the building.

He didn't expect swift legal justice, but he could at least use Ashley's death to extort compensation from the man.

They underestimated their target. The abuser had had enough.

In a burst of rage, he snatched the knife from Kent's hand.

What followed was a brutal, frenzied attack. Kent and his son were each stabbed over a dozen times.

They died on the spot.

"Think I'm some pushover?!" the abuser spat, panting heavily. "I apologized because I didn't want trouble when the government came. You brought this on yourselves! One kill, three kills... makes no difference now!"

He dragged the corpses and threw them over the balcony.

Later, Kent's wife came searching. She saw the blood staining the floor outside the abuser's door.

She was smart enough not to confront him. Covering her mouth to stifle her sobs, she turned and fled back to her own apartment.

A heavy, watchful silence descended over the handful of remaining survivors, each lost in their own thoughts.

Back on the 66th floor, Cecilia had asked Hailey if she wanted to leave.

Hailey offered a sad, soft smile. "I have no family or friends left. There's only you and Arthur, who saved my life. So, there's no point going with them. My supplies are enough for now, and if they run out, we still have the speedboat to gather more."

Cecilia said nothing more.

She had asked Arthur as well.

He offered no explanation, simply shaking his head to indicate his lack of interest.

That evening, Cecilia felt a sudden chill.

She counted the days since the acid rain stopped.

In her past life, the Ice Stage had begun shortly after.

She frowned slightly, turning to Blackie beside her. "Do you feel cold?"

As she stroked him, she realized his fur had grown noticeably thicker.

Animals were highly sensitive to weather changes. Combined with the recent Magic Spring and acid rain baths accelerating his and Jet's mutations, it was a clear sign.

A strong feeling took hold in her heart. The Ice Stage was coming very soon.

Right then, a deafening, earth-shattering roar erupted from outside.

The floor beneath her feet shook violently.

For one heart-stopping moment, the terror of the earthquakes from her past life froze her.

But she quickly calmed down. No way. This was out of sequence.

Earthquakes shouldn't happen yet.

From the corner of her eye, she saw dust clouds billowing outside the window.

Stumbling to the floor-to-ceiling window, she witnessed a scene of apocalyptic horror.

Buildings were collapsing.

It started with one mid-rise residential tower. As it fell, it smashed into its neighbor, which in toppled into the next.

Like a chain of dominoes, one building after another crumbled.

Finally, a smaller building, as it fell, slammed into the side of a thirty-plus-story skyscraper nearby.

The taller tower shuddered with a colossal crunch, but miraculously, it held, stopping the catastrophic cascade.

Even so, in the span of moments, over a dozen buildings within Cecilia's sight were reduced to rubble amidst the lingering acid rain haze.

Countless lives were extinguished in an instant.

The fallen structures were all older, poorly maintained buildings, their foundations already critically weakened by the acid rain.

They were not very tall, all constructed ages ago.

And this was only the beginning. More would fall.

The collapses planted a ticking time bomb of dread in every survivor's heart.

Those living in high-rises now lived with the constant fear that their own building could be next.

Some people, seeing the tower opposite vanish, tried to leap from a window in panic, only to be crushed by falling debris before they could even jump.

Cecilia, Arthur, and Hailey gathered to discuss the situation. To their surprise, Arthur produced three sets of equipment resembling paragliders.

"For emergencies," he stated simply.

Cecilia's eyes gleamed as she examined the gear. It bore no labels, but the materials were exceptionally sturdy, the craftsmanship meticulous.

She could easily believe Arthur had acquired these through special ways before the apocalypse.

She glanced at Arthur. Just how many secrets did this man hold?

She accepted the equipment without argument.

But in her heart, she knew their building would be fine.

Her past-life experience told her all the collapsing structures were old, dilapidated ones.

Even if they had survived the acid rain, they wouldn't withstand the coming Ice Stage and Heat Stage.

But this building, the one she stood in now, she remembered it from her past life. It had remained standing tall in the city center, through it all.
