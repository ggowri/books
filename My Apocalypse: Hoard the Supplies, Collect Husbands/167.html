Chapter 167 Unexpected Harvest

"It's fine. The cat treats are in my hands anyway. Besides treats, I also have catnip, teaser wands, freeze-dried snacks, cat food…"

As Cecilia spoke, she used her mind to control the items, taking them out one by one before putting them back.

She saw Ari on the roof stretch out a paw that froze midair. Its head stiffly turned toward Cecilia, and then an extremely flattering smile suddenly appeared on its face.

"Master~"

It looked as if it was about to jump down and rub itself against Cecilia's legs.

Cecilia rolled her eyes.

The little housekeeper of the Space, even after turning into a cat, was still just as shameless.

…

Ignoring Ari, Cecilia went to check the welcoming pine.

The tree had been properly planted in the soil. Its branches spread outward, its needles a deep green, and its sturdy trunk looked strong and elegant.

Cecilia reached out and touched the pine needles. They were still stiff, like steel needles.

However, with the restraints of the Space, she could feel that the welcoming pine would not cause trouble.

After the last upgrade of the Space, Cecilia had come in to inspect everything carefully. But because she had been eager to collect supplies outside, and Ari was managing things inside the Space, she hadn't paid much attention to it afterward.

Now she walked up to the floating screen and lightly tapped the words "Spirit Space 6.0." Her heart was filled with excitement.

"Mobile Base—

Level: 1

…"

The villa's level had never upgraded after its first introduction. It was still Level 1. Cecilia didn't mind. After all, she didn't need it for now.

"Recycler—

Level: 2

…"

The Recycler also hadn't upgraded and remained Level 2, though that was more than enough for Cecilia at the moment.

Just earlier, she had thrown the damaged bedding sets she used to trap the mutated welcoming pine into the Recycler. She wanted to see what would appear tomorrow.

During the past few days, Cecilia hadn't been closely monitoring the Recycler. Ari had been handling it for her. It had probably thrown in some unwanted cardboard boxes from deliveries earlier, and the output had mostly been common supplies that were automatically stored in the Space.

Today, however, when Cecilia opened the Recycler, she saw two bags of cat treats lying inside.

On the other side was a small box of canned cat food.

Cecilia suddenly remembered that earlier she had briefly thought about something.

She had been thinking that the cat snacks stored in the Space weren't very plentiful. If she wanted to control Ari in the future, she might need more cat treats and canned cat food.

But it had only been a fleeting thought. She hadn't even considered it seriously.

If Ari hadn't secretly nudged things along inside the Space, Cecilia would never believe it.

Annoyed, she stored the cat treats and canned food away, completely ignoring the hopeful gaze Ari was giving her.

Cecilia thought to herself that from now on she absolutely could not think too much about cat treats or canned food. Otherwise the Space might receive her brainwaves and interpret them as wishes, and with a little "assistance" from Ari…

She definitely didn't want Plot One and the Recycler to turn into a cat food factory.

"Master, Master, please have mercy on Ari!"

Nearby, Ari looked like it was about to cry after watching the canned cat food disappear just like that.

Cecilia gently tapped Ari on the forehead.

"Little kitty, be good. Only good cats get cat treats and canned food. If you keep relying on the Space and acting like a troublemaker…"

Cecilia didn't finish the rest of her sentence, but the look in her eyes carried a clear threat.

"Happy Farm—

Level: 3

…"

The farm's level hadn't changed.

Today Cecilia had gone to check Plot One. The batch of screws she had planted earlier hadn't produced anything yet.

Actually, she had been hoping they would grow into a batch of bullets. She had even stood in front of Plot One and prayed for quite a while, wondering whether the Space could hear her wishes.

Since the Space had just upgraded and had gained a mutated tree, its energy should be abundant now.

It should work… right?

Just as she finished inspecting Plot One and was about to leave, she suddenly heard a pfft sound.

It sounded as if someone had been holding a mouthful of water and suddenly spat it out.

Startled, Cecilia looked over.

Plot One had produced something just one second earlier.

The feeling was incredibly strange.

Cecilia had entered the Space many times, but this was the first time she had personally witnessed the moment it produced something.

However, when she looked at the field of Plot One and saw the dense cluster of round objects covering the ground, she froze.

Those were… a large pile of grenades.

Cecilia thought to herself,

Wow. I prayed for bullets and got grenades instead. Did I just profit big or profit big?

The Space must have had abundant energy recently, directly gifting her such a huge reward.

Cecilia was thrilled. She quickly harvested the entire batch of grenades and planted another round of seeds in Plot One.

This time the seeds were not ordinary ones. They were mutated seeds she had obtained from the authorities.

Cecilia wanted to see what mutated seeds would grow into.

Meanwhile, the latest harvest from Plot Two had produced excellent chili peppers.

Each pepper was as long as a forearm. Some were red, some green. Some were slender hot peppers, while others were thick bell peppers.

Looking at the flourishing peppers, a face suddenly appeared in Cecilia's mind.

Arthur.

She wondered whether Arthur would start drooling if he saw these peppers.
