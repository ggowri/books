Chapter 72 Truly Made Of

"Aren't you worried your scar will get infected out there? That's acid rain," Cecilia asked, testing Hailey's resolve.

Hailey's voice was steady. "After almost dying, looks don't matter anymore. And after fainting today, I can feel my strength fading. If I don't go soon, I might not be able to go at all.

"Besides, everyone's let their guard down now—thinking rescue is coming, that the government will hand out supplies. This is the best time to search."

Cecilia considered it quietly.

Her real concern was for Blackie.

To her, the dog was more than a pet; he was her most loyal companion in this ruined world.

Clever and strong as it was, even with the mutations, Blackie was still just a dog—one who couldn't recognize the cruelty humans were capable of.

Cecilia said after a moment's hesitation, "Fine, I'll go with you. Blackie comes, too."

She'd planned to head out tonight anyway. As Hailey had pointed out, the recent lull after the acid rain and the false hope of government aid meant fewer dangers on the streets.

Bringing Hailey along would also let Cecilia gauge her character—after all, actions spoke louder than words.

Hailey's lips trembled. For a long moment, she simply stared at Cecilia, speechless, before finally bowing deeply.

"Don't think I'm doing this for you. I remember a sporting goods store nearby that sells rigid inflatable boats and rafts. I'm getting one of those boats tonight," Cecilia said coolly.

She met Hailey's eyes squarely. "Don't disappoint me. Meet me at midnight."

Without another word, Cecilia closed the door, shutting out the sound of Hailey's muffled sobs.

A part of her pitied Hailey, but what she admired more was Hailey's ruthless clarity.

And there was no way she'd let Blackie go out alone with her.

Midnight arrived swiftly.

The entire building lay wrapped in heavy silence.

After the euphoria following the rescue team's visit, exhaustion had settled over every floor.

Not a single sound drifted from behind any door as Cecilia, Blackie, and Hailey made their way downstairs.

To her surprise, Arthur was already waiting in the lobby, dressed head to toe in black wetsuit gear, clearly prepared for a supply run.

"What a coincidence. Heading out? Want to team up?" he said, looking briefly taken aback.

Hailey stayed silent, glancing at Cecilia as if to say, "It's your call."

After a brief pause, Cecilia nodded. Arthur's mysterious background and capable demeanor these past days made him a useful ally.

Tonight would also be a chance to see what both of them were truly made of. Three people together could keep each other in check.

Only when faced with scarce resources and temptation would real character show itself.

With a pat to Blackie's side and a signal to Hailey, they moved toward the water.

It had already risen past the fourth floor.

The fifth floor, where Alexander stayed, remained above water for now, but Cecilia knew what was coming. When the Ice Stage arrived and the blizzards hit, that floor would be the first buried overnight.

At the thought, a cold smile appeared on Cecilia's face.

Hailey was true to her word—her skill in the water was impressive.

Both Cecilia and Arthur wore basic diving gear, but Hailey moved swiftly through the flooded corridors clad only in a swimsuit.

She reached a ventilation shaft on the fourth floor first, forced the window open, and signaled for the others to follow.

Cecilia and Arthur kept close behind, with Blackie paddling steadily beside them.

Once outside the building, Arthur produced an inflatable raft as if by magic. A portable electric pump quickly filled it with air, and soon the two women, the man, and the dog were gliding across the dark water.

Cecilia's eyes flickered at the sight of Arthur's raft—she hadn't expected him to have one, too.

Under her direction, they soon approached the shopping mall that housed the outdoor supplies store.

Like everywhere else, the mall was partially submerged, but its high ceilings meant only the first two floors were flooded.

The sports retailer, located on the third level, remained untouched.

They entered quickly. When Hailey and Arthur spotted the three rigid inflatable boats stored inside, their faces lit up with relief.

Cecilia, however, fixed her gaze on the large decorative piece resting on the checkout counter. The Space was reacting—this item could upgrade the Space!

While the other two hurried to lift the boats down, Cecilia feigned interest in a cabinet beneath the counter, rummaging through it as a distraction.

The moment their backs were turned, she swept the ornament from the countertop into the Space.

An instant later, she felt a distinct vibration against her chest—and then the pendant vanished.

Her heart nearly stopped.
What now?
Panic shot through her. Was the Space playing tricks again?

Beads of sweat formed on her forehead.

Steadying herself, Cecilia focused inward with her mind. To her immense relief, the Space was still there, now completely internalized.

She let out a quiet breath. So dramatic—it just decided to go hidden without a word. A single tremor was its only warning.

It was a shock—for a heart-stopping moment, she thought the Space was acting up again, or that the ornament was somehow defective, consumed and lost forever the moment it vanished inside.

The scare, however, confirmed one thing: the Space had leveled up once more.
