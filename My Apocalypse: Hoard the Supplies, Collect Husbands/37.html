Chapter 37 A Rabbit?

Cecilia crouched low and moved forward carefully, staying alert. Whatever it was didn't seem too bright because when she found it, the thing was hiding behind the villa with half its fuzzy butt sticking out, head tucked in, shaking like crazy.

Cecilia walked up and grabbed it, lifting it up to get a better look. It was an incredibly fat rabbit.

The whole thing was a creamy beige color, totally different from normal rabbits, and its mouth was this blueberry purple that looked both delicious and kind of creepy.

This rabbit was way bigger and fatter than regular ones, all plump and chunky, and it had some serious weight to it.

Cecilia flipped it over to check and realized it was female. "How did a rabbit get in here?" she muttered.

Then she noticed something stuck to the rabbit's butt—a silica gel packet.

She peeled it off and saw "DO NOT EAT" printed on it. She felt like she'd seen this somewhere before.

The rabbit kept trembling, looking completely terrified and inexperienced with the world.

After staring at it for about ten seconds, Cecilia finally figured out what this rabbit reminded her of.

This thing looked exactly like the bead she'd buried in the black soil earlier!

That creamy beige fur was literally bread color, and that blueberry purple nose and little purple paws were the blueberry filling.

Cecilia felt like her entire brain was breaking. Today's upgrade had thrown way too many curveballs at her.

Her head was spinning, so she just tossed the rabbit into the nearby pasture. The rabbit seemed pretty happy in there and immediately started munching on grass, adapting surprisingly fast.

Cecilia hesitated for ages, then pulled out another bead and planted it in the black soil.

She decided to name the soil that glowed blue "Black-Blue Soil" and keep the regular patch as just "Black Soil" so she could tell them apart.

In the Black-Blue Soil, she planted a piece of bread along with some seeds, a pack of cookies, and a box of chocolates. She even tried burying a shirt in there just to see what would happen. She'd check on everything in a couple of days.

In the regular Black Soil patch, she scattered some spinach seeds to test how well it worked.

Then she walked around to the back of the house where that weird machine sat. After thinking it over for a second, she tossed a throw pillow into it.

The machine whirred to life as soon as it detected the pillow and sucked it right in. And then... nothing. Absolutely nothing happened.

Cecilia circled the machine twice, getting more annoyed by the second. It really wasn't doing anything!

"Stupid machine! Give me back my pillow!" Sure, the pillow wasn't exactly critical, but it was still part of the Space's inventory.

The machine didn't respond. Cecilia rolled her eyes and stepped out of the Space.

Blackie had gotten so used to her popping in and out that he barely lifted his eyelids before dozing off again.

Cecilia felt pretty satisfied knowing the Space still worked and had even upgraded, so she crawled into bed with a smile on her face. She and Blackie both slept in until late morning.

The next day, they were jolted awake by shouting outside. Turned out the power was out.

Cecilia quickly threw on some clothes and grabbed Blackie, who was already standing alert by the door. Together they headed downstairs.

A crowd had gathered in the stairwell, and right in the middle of it was that triangle-eyed guy who'd knocked on her door before, along with a tiny girl.

The girl lived one floor below Cecilia and was currently blocking her own doorway, tears streaming down her face.

A bunch of people stood around watching and whispering. One woman in particular stood out, a tall, striking woman in a denim jacket who had to be at least five-foot-seven with a confident, mature vibe.

She was pointing at the triangle-eyed guy and going off on him. "Bryan, what the hell is wrong with you? You think just because things are crazy right now, nobody's watching? You think I won't call the cops on you right now?"

Cecilia hung back with Blackie, keeping her distance but watching closely. That was when she realized the triangle-eyed guy was Bryan Hanks, the same one who'd commented in the group chat about how pretty Rebecca was. He lived on the fifteenth floor.

Bryan looked a little nervous when she mentioned calling the police and forced out a smile. "Come on, I'm just trying to help everyone out here. The power's down because the acid rain corroded the power lines outside, right? Who knows when it'll come back on? I just figured we should get that generator from her so the whole building can benefit from it."

Cecilia's apartment building was a mixed-use complex with yoga studios, restaurants, a cat cafe, and even a small grocery store on the ground floors. So it made sense that some of the business owners might have generators on hand.

"Bullshit! You just want to steal Phoebe's generator for yourself! We all know what kind of shady stuff you used to pull with your business. We've been neighbors for years, Bryan. You think we don't know what you're really like?"

The woman in the denim jacket apparently hit a nerve because Bryan got aggressive real fast. "What the hell are you trying to say, Hailey Carlson? Don't think I won't hit you just because you're a woman! This is the apocalypse now. Go ahead and call the cops. I dare you to check if your phone even has signal!"

That comment made Cecilia realize something. The internet had gone out yesterday, and the power cut off this morning. Her phone probably didn't have any signal either.

People in the crowd started trying to break things up and calm everyone down. Bryan saw he was outnumbered and backed off, but not before giving Phoebe Walls a long, meaningful look.
