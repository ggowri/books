Chapter 139 Merciless

The men immediately caught on, moving to circle around Cecilia.

But they didn't notice the figure approaching from the apartment building—a man with a cold expression and a knife in his hand.

"Heh, beg us right now, kill that dog and toss it over, then strip and come service us real nice—maybe we'll let you live!" The leader grinned. "Oh, and that RV behind you? Everything in it? All ours now—"

His words cut off. His neck suddenly felt cold.

He tried to keep talking. Nothing came out. Just wind rushing through his throat.

His buddies' faces twisted in horror.

Cecilia had moved. "You talk too much."

But the real terror came next.

He clutched his neck, feeling death closing in. Survival instinct kicked in—he tried to run, tried to grab someone's arm for help.

But then...

Right in front of him, two of his guys dropped.

A shadow moved—too fast to track.

Three down in an instant.

Every strike clean. Throat cut. Not even time to scream.

It was Arthur.

Cecilia caught his eye and nodded.

The supplies here were partly his too. Of course he'd come when trouble started.

"Let's split them—half each," Cecilia said to Arthur.

Arthur gave a slight nod. "Deal."

With Arthur here, she didn't have to worry about Blackie and fight at the same time.

"Blackie, you good?" Cecilia stroked his head.

Blackie bared his teeth, glaring viciously at the remaining thugs like he wanted to rip them apart.

Cecilia slapped his shoulder blade. Together, they charged into the fray.

She struck without mercy—one slash straight to the back of a guy's skull.

He dropped instantly, twitched a few times, then went still.

Blackie had been holding back his rage. Now unleashed, he lunged and tackled someone to the ground.

In seconds, he tore the man's head clean off.

The bloody scene sent the rest scrambling to run.

But Blackie leaped again, taking down another one.

Within moments, all dozen men were dead.

Cecilia wiped the blood splattered across herself, panting hard. She glared at the corpses and kicked their leader's face viciously.

"Bastard! That's for messing with my dog!"

Arthur and Cecilia dragged the bodies to a pile nearby, then hauled up a few more bags of grain.

They hadn't loaded much into the RV yet. With Blackie helping this time, they finished quickly.

Once done, Cecilia told Arthur to drive the RV away and hide it somewhere safe.

That luxury vehicle parked downstairs was way too conspicuous.

Through all of this, they didn't notice the eyes watching from above.

Eyes that narrowed more and more with every passing second.

It was Logan.

After hearing Mabel and Phoebe's story, Logan had his doubts about Cecilia's group. But he'd met Cecilia before.

He'd approved her adopting Blackie because he'd trusted her.

Now? He was watching them kill people and dump bodies.

A chill ran down his spine.

One of his soldiers reported that the dead were local thugs they'd been hunting for weeks.

The soldier's expression said it all. These scumbags had it coming.

But Logan was still shocked by how merciless Cecilia was.

And Blackie—formerly a military dog with strict training. His size hadn't changed much, but he looked more vicious than any of the mutated dogs in their unit.

Then there was that tall guy...

Logan had watched Arthur fight from upstairs. One look, and he knew—even their best fighter wouldn't stand a chance against him.

Just then, a soldier interrupted. Someone wanted to see Logan.

The visitor was a sketchy-looking guy, beat up and bruised.

He grinned and stuck out his hand.

"Captain Jackman, right? I've heard so much about you. Name's Dyson. Just a regular, unfortunate resident from Building B. I'm here to file a complaint."

******

Truth be told, Dyson and his crew had it rough.

After hauling several loads and nearly emptying one silo, the guys were exhausted. They decided to grab some food and catch a quick nap.

They knew two of the silos had mutant rats inside. Figured they'd watch the show after they woke up.

Who could've guessed? After they ate, drank, and rested up, they arrived to find total carnage.

Not wanting to go back empty-handed, they opened the door where the mutant rats were trapped.

Then—countless eyes stared back at them.

A wave of stench hit them. Before them lay a hellscape of corpses and blood.

The newly born Rat King shrieked a command. Like it wanted to make a statement, the attack was vicious.

In the end, they fled in panic—and lost one of their own.

Their car even got a tire shredded!

When they got back, their boss whipped Dyson twice.

Dyson blamed it all on Cecilia and her crew.

Now he stood before Logan, looking utterly wronged.

"Sir, Cecilia and her people cleared out all the silos around here. Left us regular folks with nothing to eat. You've gotta help us out!"
