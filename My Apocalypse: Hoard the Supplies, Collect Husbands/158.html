Chapter 158 The Truth Behind the Child

Cecilia felt extremely annoyed by the noise, and even more than that, she felt angry.

In her previous life, she had seen many people place children at the front just to gain sympathy from others.

Of course, the final result was usually that those children became cannon fodder.

Now Cecilia felt that the person downstairs was probably thinking the same way.

She did not want to show kindness. She knew that if she helped once, someone would definitely come a second time.

She even turned off the intercom, hoping to cut off that idea from the people downstairs.

However, after a while, the child's crying suddenly became louder and more heartbreaking. Even with the intercom turned off, the sound could still be faintly heard coming from downstairs.

Cecilia heard the door across the hall open.

She took a deep breath, picked up her knife, and followed down as well.

Arthur had a cold expression on his face. His hair was still slightly messy. It seemed he had already gone to sleep and had been forcibly woken up by the noise.

As soon as they arrived on the sixty-fourth floor, Cecilia had not even spoken yet when Arthur directly opened the door and raised a pistol.

"You're disturbing my sleep. Hm?"

The gun was pointed at the woman standing outside with the child.

The woman instantly froze.

"I… I… It's my child. My child heard that several brothers and sisters moved to the sixty-sixth floor and wanted to come up to play."

Cecilia looked at the woman. She was dark and thin, but her eyes were sharp and bright.

At first glance, she did not look like an honest person.

When the gun was pointed at her, her whole body trembled, but she kept gripping the child's arm tightly.

The child was still crying and struggling even more violently than before.

But when the child saw that the woman beside him had stopped moving, he struggled even harder and cried loudly.

"She's not my mom. She's not my mom!"

Arthur immediately released the safety on his pistol.

"Your child? Say that again."

The woman in front of them suddenly shrank down onto the ground in fear. She tightly hugged the child in front of her and pulled him directly in front of herself, ignoring the child's struggle as if she wanted to use him to block the gun.

Then she began shouting loudly.

"Murder! Someone here is going to kill people with a gun!"

Bang.

A gunshot rang out.

Cecilia saw that a pit had been blasted into the ground beside the woman.

The woman instantly froze and did not dare move.

"Shout again and the next bullet will go through your head."

Arthur pointed the pistol at the woman's head again.

"Speak. What exactly is going on?"

After being frightened like that, the woman immediately became honest.

Later, the woman explained that the child was not hers at all.

The little boy was an orphan whose parents had died long ago.

The woman had brought the child up to the sixty-sixth floor to cause trouble and try to get some benefits.

Of course, in order to make the boy cooperate, she had already pinched his arms and legs until they were bruised.

When the boy's cotton jacket was lifted, a large patch of bruises could indeed be seen.

"What a piece of trash," Cecilia said coldly.

The woman clenched her teeth. She did not seem very afraid of Cecilia. She muttered toward her,

"At a time like this, what does it matter whether someone is trash or not? The kind ones are all dead."

This was probably the thinking of many people in the apocalypse.

However, targeting an orphan whose parents were both dead was still extremely cruel.

But unexpectedly, the woman looked coldly at Cecilia and Arthur before locking her gaze onto Cecilia.

"Aren't you the same? You're just relying on your youth and looks to cling to a man. You're no different from that famous slut in the shelter, Phoebe. Bah!"

Arthur wanted to make a move, but Cecilia stopped him.

She glanced at the little boy beside them.

"Close your eyes."

The little boy was confused, but under Cecilia' frightening gaze, he really walked to the side, turned his back to them, and tightly covered his eyes with both hands.

The woman, Hannah Cole, suddenly became afraid. She seemed to realize what was about to happen and turned to run.

But it was already too late.

Bang.

A gunshot rang out.

Hannah was instantly shot in the head.

Arthur looked at Cecilia with a smile.

"Nice shooting."

Cecilia said nothing and put the pistol back into her waist.

She had just noticed that the boy's way of walking looked strange.

After observing carefully, she realized that the boy had no toes at all. The front of his shoes was empty.

That was why he limped when he walked.

At that moment, a man came running up from downstairs while cursing.

As he ran, he shouted,

"Hannah, why haven't you come down yet? I still need that kid!"

When the man reached them and saw the scene in front of him, his legs immediately went weak with fear.

Without saying a word, Cecilia rushed forward and grabbed the man's arm.

"Is Hannah your wife?"

The man nodded nervously.

Only then did Cecilia learn that Hannah and the man were husband and wife.

The two had discovered long ago that the little boy had no parents, so they had "rescued" him.

But the only reason was that having the boy counted as another person in the household, which meant an extra share of rations.

However, the two usually beat and scolded the boy and never let him stay warm. His toes had even been frozen off.

But the boy's limp was not caused by his missing toes.

They had actually broken the boy's leg.

They did it deliberately to gain sympathy from the soldiers and obtain more food.

Today Hannah had tried to use the same trick again. She had even pinched the boy until his body was covered in bruises.

But this time, when the boy faced the clean and well-dressed Cecilia and Arthur, he finally gathered the courage to say that Hannah was not his mother.
