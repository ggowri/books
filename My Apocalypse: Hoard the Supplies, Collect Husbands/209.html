Chapter 209 Mabel's End

No one ever knew why Diana had suddenly taken her own life.

By the time she was found, she had already been dead in her flat for several days. Her whole body was decomposing, her clothes were torn, and the corpse was covered in marks where maggots had gnawed at her.

She had not even left behind a note.

Arthur's maternal grandparents, devastated by their daughter's sudden death, declined sharply in health and passed away in the few months before the apocalypse.

At the time, Arthur had been away on a mission. Even his mother's side of the family had been unable to reach him.

By the time he finished the assignment and contacted home, he learned that his mother was dead. When he returned, all that was left of her was a jar of ashes.

It was also because of his mother's sudden death that his mind had been in turmoil. He missed the emergency call ordering him back to base at once.

Later, after returning to duty, one of his comrades was killed during an operation.

Arthur went mad with rage and shot an important suspect dead on the spot, which forced him out of the military. After that, he became a mercenary.

All this time, he had wanted to know the truth about his mother's death.

The mother he knew had been an optimistic woman. She was not the sort to kill herself so easily.

So after leaving the military and gaining more freedom, he began investigating what had really happened back then.

It was only shortly before the apocalypse that his inquiries finally led him to Vivian Barnes.

He came to suspect that Vivian had been the one behind his mother's death.

At the time, the Barnes family had already been at the end of their strength. Diana's family had acquired a large portion of their assets, and the Barnes family had been on the verge of collapse.

And the person leading that campaign against them had been Arthur's mother.

Arthur had also uncovered an old piece of history. Years earlier, Diana Barnes and Vivian Barnes had been close friends.

Both had been socialites in Heldon City, and they were of a similar age.

Diana had come to know Sir Barnes through Vivian.

The more one thought about that, the more chilling it became.

And now Arthur finally knew the truth of his mother's death.

In an instant, Arthur, who had looked lifeless only moments before and seemed on the brink of death, suddenly curved his lips into a faint smile.

His eyes, which had been about to close, flew open.

He lunged forward and seized Vivian's wrist.

"Vivian, I'll make you wish you were dead."

The spot on his chest where Vivian had shot him was not bleeding at all.

The whole time, Arthur had been pressing a hand there only to conceal the fact that he was uninjured.

After all, he was wearing a vest made from mutant rat king hide.

"Kill him!"

Vivian cried out in panic to the bodyguard beside her.

But the moment Arthur came in, he had noticed at a glance that the bodyguard's pistol had not even been cocked.

That was why he had dared to improvise and play this trick on Vivian.

After all, the apocalypse had only just begun. How could men who had barely touched a gun before react that quickly?

Arthur lashed out with one kick, sending the bodyguard crashing to the floor, then immediately put a bullet through his head.

Only then did he turn back to Vivian.

"What my mother suffered while she was alive, I'll make sure you taste every bit of it yourself."

With that, Arthur knocked Vivian unconscious with a punch, slung her over his back, and jumped out through the window.

He could already hear the footsteps of the Barnes family guards rushing towards the door.

But by the time they got inside, the room would be empty.

...

Once outside, Arthur carried Vivian on his back, avoided the Barnes family guards all the way out of the estate, and threw her into a car.

Just as he had tossed her inside and was about to leave, he noticed a figure sneaking out of the Barnes family grounds in the distance.

Arthur frowned slightly, thinking it might be someone coming after him. He shut the car door and quietly followed.

When he got closer, he realised it was Mabel, carrying a large bag and looking utterly panicked as she tried to flee.

She had even stolen one of the Barnes family's cars.

Arthur remembered Cecilia saying earlier that once Nathan was dealt with, she would come back for Mabel. He knew then that this woman was Cecilia's target inside the Barnes family.

The corner of his mouth lifted.

...

By the time Cecilia returned, she had not even entered the Barnes estate yet. She had only reached the place where they had agreed to hide the car when she saw that one of the vehicles had been driven away, and someone had been left lying on the ground.

It was none other than Mabel.

She had already passed out and had clearly been lying in the snow for some time. There was snow on her face, and her eyelashes were rimmed with frost.

The strangest thing was that there was a note tucked into her clothes.

Cecilia bent down and pulled it out.

On it, in bold, sweeping handwriting, were two words:

Don't mention it.

And beneath that was the signature:

Arthur Barnes

Just looking at the note, Cecilia could practically picture the smug expression Arthur must have worn while writing it.

She shook her head lightly, finding his personality rather amusing.

"Well, that does save me a bit of time."

With a small smile, Cecilia pulled out her gun and fired straight at Mabel's head.

Bang.

A crimson flower blossomed across the snow.

Mabel was dead.

Cecilia got into the car, took Blackie with her, left Mabel's corpse where it lay, and drove off.

On the other side, Vivian was roused by the pain in her fingers and toes.

Her first thought on waking was, Thank God. I'm still alive.

But very quickly, she realised she was somewhere unfamiliar, with chattering and squeaking sounds all around her.

She opened her eyes and looked about.

Then she saw countless pairs of blood red eyes.

When she looked down, she found her fingers and toes covered in blood, with several huge rats gnawing at them.
