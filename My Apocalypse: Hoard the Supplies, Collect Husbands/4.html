Chapter 4 Sneaky Scumbag

Cecilia summoned her emotions, squeezing out two tears. "Let me be alone for a couple of days... I've been missing Mom and Dad lately."

Alexander tried to move closer, but Blackie suddenly stood, baring his teeth and positioning himself between them.

Startled, Alexander stumbled back a step.

"Cici, when... when did you get such a big dog?" he asked, voice unsteady.

Cecilia silently praised Blackie. What a good dog. He could already tell she disliked Alexander.

"I just got him. Since I live alone, he makes me feel safer," she said, gently stroking the fur on Blackie's neck.

It was soft and thick, perfect to touch.

"If... if living alone feels too uncomfortable, you could move in with me," Alexander offered.

Disgust rose in Cecilia's throat.

Oh, you wish!

In her last life, Alexander showed up at her door when the apocalypse hit, bringing a pile of supplies and claiming he wanted to weather the storm together.

But that night, he revealed his true colors. He only came to sleep with her.

Naive as she'd been back then, Cecilia didn't agree right away, saying she wanted to take things slow.

Alexander flew into a rage, even trying to force himself on her.

In the chaos, she grabbed something and smashed it over his head.

He snapped, yelling insults that she was just a worthless orphan, acting prude for no reason. And if Cecilia didn't sleep with him now, she'd probably end up with some beggar later.

Furious, she tried to kick him out, but he shoved her to the floor before leaving, their relationship shattered.

He took all the supplies. And even the ring. All because Rebecca mentioned she liked antique jewelry.

Right before Cecilia died in her last life, Alexander leaned in and whispered that Rebecca was a thousand times better than her.

Clenching her fists and biting her lip hard, Cecilia forced herself not to punch him then and there.

"Alexander, this is the house Mom and Dad left me. I feel at peace here," she said firmly.

Alexander opened his mouth to speak, but Cecilia cut him off, raising her voice.

"Aren't we supposed to get engaged soon? Can you return the shares and funds my parents left me? I've been having nightmares lately—they're blaming me... That's why I'm in a bad mood, why I blocked you. I don't want to see you right now."

Sure enough, Alexander's expression shifted instantly.

"Cici, don't overthink it. Your parents would be thrilled to know we're getting engaged. As for the shares and funds, once you marry me, they'll be our joint property. I'll never let you down.

"Uh... I should go. I have something to do. Unblock me, okay? We're getting engaged in a few months, after all."

Cecilia sneered inwardly. That engagement would never happen.

Alexander left, and she watched his retreating figure, cold laughter bubbling in her chest.

Scumbag. Just you wait.

After settling Blackie in, she checked the time. It was already two in the afternoon.

Her stomach rumbled with hunger.

Grabbing her phone, Cecilia prepared to indulge herself.

She ordered five servings each of her favorite foods: barbecued ribs, beef sliders, chili con carne, loaded nachos, fried chicken, and pulled pork sandwiches.

Throughout the afternoon, the doorbell rang constantly, making Blackie perk up his ears and stare alertly at the door every time.

Cecilia stowed all the takeout in the Space, keeping only barbecued ribs with mashed potatoes for herself.

Blackie sat upright beside her, silent, but his large eyes fixed firmly on the ribs in her bowl.

Chuckling, she picked out the biggest piece and held it out. "Want some?"

Blackie tilted his head, looking slightly dumbfounded, as if to say, "Is that even a question?"

He swallowed the entire rib in one bite.

Clearly, he had a huge appetite.

Between takeout deliveries, Cecilia sat in the air-conditioned room, pulling out a notebook to outline her next plans.

Following the apocalypse timeline, acid rain would come first, then floods.

Next, temperatures would plummet to minus 140 degrees Fahrenheit.

By then, a large number of people would die.

After the blizzards and cold, extreme heat would hit, with temperatures soaring to 122 degrees Fahrenheit.

The city would be completely paralyzed by then, with no electricity or running water. People would barely survive in the sweltering heat.

Intermittent earthquakes would follow, chipping away at humanity's will.

She remembered the worst earthquake vividly—tall buildings collapsing, the ground splitting open, countless people falling into the crevasses.

These extreme weather conditions would last for about two years.

But it seemed like the planet's punishment for years of reckless deforestation and environmental destruction wasn't over yet.

Or rather, it had only just begun.

After the natural disasters, many animals and insects would mutate. Some became extremely strong, others grew in size, and some developed rapid reproduction abilities.

Swarms of insects would plague the land after the disasters.

The human population, already decimated by natural calamities, would be powerless against the insect swarms and mutated animal attacks, struggling even more to survive.

These creatures could infiltrate almost anywhere.

That was when the apocalypse would truly show its fangs.

Some animals would even develop high intelligence, becoming "kings" and leading armies of mutated creatures.

With that in mind, Cecilia's resolve to live well in this second life only grew stronger.

Suddenly, she recalled the seeds she'd planted in the black soil of the Space the night before and decided to check on them.

What she saw really left her stunned.

Where Cecilia had sown the seeds, there was now a plush pillow, shaped like a flower.

Using her mind, she pulled it out and squeezed it.

Definitely a plush pillow.

But how did it end up in the Space?

Although she was confused, a thought struck her.

Could the black soil grow all kinds of strange things?

Curious, Cecilia plucked a weed from a potted plant and tossed it into the Space.

*****

Fuming after his encounter with Cecilia, Alexander called Rebecca as soon as he left her house.

"Alex, what's wrong?" Rebecca asked, her voice soft and concerned, picking up on his bad mood.

"Do you think that bitch Cecilia found out about us? She brought up the funds and shares her parents left her today. She's always been so obedient to me before, but now she blocked my number!" Alexander snapped.

Rebecca was putting on makeup—she and Alexander had planned to go out for dinner. His words made her jump, and her eyebrow pencil slipped, ruining the half-drawn eyebrow.

Annoyed, she wiped the end of her brow, but her tone remained gentle when she spoke to Alexander.

"Alex, you're overthinking it. Cecilia's always been stupid. A few days ago, I asked her who would manage the money after you get married, and she let me convince her to give you control of the other half of her funds and shares, too."

In Rebecca's eyes, Cecilia was just a brainless fool. She'd been secretly seeing Alexander all these years, and Cecilia hadn't noticed a thing.

She'd even brainwashed Cecilia into believing that men should manage the household finances, and that, as an orphan, being engaged to Alexander was the greatest luck of her life.

Under this mental manipulation, Cecilia had been utterly loyal to Alexander. She'd given him the money she earned from summer jobs and bought him gifts whenever Cecilia saw something nice.

She'd always been so humble, obeying his every word.

Little did she know that the Barnes family's kindness was all a facade. If it weren't for the remaining half of her funds and shares, they would have dumped her long ago.

Despite appearing prosperous, the Barnes family was actually in debt and desperately needed Cecilia's stocks and funds to save themselves.

That was why Alexander was in such a hurry to get engaged—he wanted to trick her into handing over the other half after the engagement.

However, Cecilia knew nothing about this.

Alexander nodded. "You're right. People don't change that fast. I must be overreacting. How's your makeup coming? I'll be at your place soon. Stay the night tonight, okay? Babe."

"Alex, you're so naughty!" Rebecca cooed.
