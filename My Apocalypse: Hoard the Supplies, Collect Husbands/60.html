Chapter 60 Continuing to Scavenge Supplies

Once Cecilia finally had her feet firmly on the corridor floor and looked back at the door leading to the rooftop, she felt chills all over her body.

Who knows how many mutated creatures are in that water?

In her previous life during the early apocalypse, Cecilia hadn't gone outside and didn't know exactly when animals outside began to mutate. She only knew that after a few months, it was basically impossible to see normal animals anymore.

Especially after the rainwater receded, the land was filled with hideous, mutated creatures, more terrifying than natural disasters.

These things were everywhere, capable of killing people silently.

But before that, not many mutated creatures posed a threat to humans. more were the acid rain, floods, extreme cold, and extreme heat.

Moreover, some pets like cats and dogs that mutated brought great convenience to their owners' lives.

Thinking about the golden python, their confrontation in the dark made Cecilia realize: the python showed no signs of decay, its body had grown tremendously, and its two eyes emitted intense red light in the darkness.

All this indicated the python's evolution was already quite complete.

This made her realize a problem—the fact that such a powerfully mutated creature could appear now meant the acid rain's effect on animal mutation wasn't gradual.

But rather... required a strong, concentrated dose.

The golden python in the wild had been constantly soaked in acid rain, hence its rapid adaptation and mutation.

Although Blackie had been mutating these days, it wasn't enough yet.

Cecilia took a deep breath, deciding to go back and think it over carefully.

She didn't want to harm Blackie's body or make Blackie suffer, but if she could accelerate mutation, it would definitely become an asset in the apocalypse.

As for other creatures, they should have completed mutation by now in theory, but why they would only emerge months later, Cecilia still didn't understand.

She even started to suspect that the insect calamity might arrive early.

After collecting herself, Cecilia changed clothes inside her Space, comforted Jet, and then immediately proceeded cautiously back home.

As soon as she entered, Cecilia saw Blackie squatting guard at the door, his two eyes shining brightly as he looked at her.

Although he was trying to be restrained, his constantly wagging tail betrayed his excitement at seeing Cecilia.

Cecilia crouched down and hugged Blackie's neck. "Blackie, I'm back. Thank you for watching the house by yourself."

Exhausted, Cecilia tidied up a bit after returning home and then fell asleep hugging Blackie.

She didn't wake up until the sun was high in the sky the next day, and Arthur came knocking again.

She sighed somewhat helplessly.

I stockpiled so many supplies just to live comfortably in the apocalypse, so why is there a human alarm clock waking me up every day?

After chatting with Arthur through the door for a bit, Cecilia got up, washed up, and took out a hot breakfast to enjoy.

Jet, after yesterday's scare, seemed much more spirited.

Cecilia gave it another Magic Spring bath. Today it looked even more alert, its eyes showing more intelligence.

Especially when looking at Blackie, Jet's black eyes would dart around, who knows what it was scheming.

Cecilia gave instructions for Jet to guard the house and had Blackie continue guarding the stairwell entrance. Then she joined Arthur to continue moving things.

Arthur pointed to a unit in front of them. "I was just thinking of discussing with you. Besides the units whose doors we removed yesterday, should we also break into the other ones?"

Cecilia glanced at Arthur beside her, thinking he was getting addicted.

"Sure, especially checking people's refrigerators for anything we can take out. It might spoil if we wait much longer."

Arthur fully agreed with Cecilia's suggestion.

And so, their main task for the day was set.

First, they emptied the remaining units from yesterday.

The most pleasant surprise for Cecilia in this round was finding a bracelet that made her Space tremble.

The bracelet seemed to be made of rose quartz, not expensive, but with one hundred and eight crystal-clear beads.

Cecilia traded a pack of instant noodles with Arthur for it.

Arthur initially didn't want the noodles, but Cecilia insisted, "Since we've decided to cooperate, we should distribute fairly. Supplies aren't necessarily just food. Later we might encounter blankets, clothes, warming items. I like this bracelet. To me, it's supplies. Please don't refuse."

Cecilia said this so that later, if she encountered something the Space needed, she wouldn't have to explain further.

Arthur stopped refusing and, somewhat reluctantly, accepted the pack of noodles.

After finishing looting those units, the two discussed and decided to continue breaking into other doors.

Cecilia waved her hand, pointing to a door marked "Pet Supplies Wholesale Company". "Mr. Barnes, if it's convenient, could we start with this one?"

Cecilia knew this pet supplies wholesale company.

Before the apocalypse, she had seen this company making shipments.

This place held more samples. The actual goods were stored in warehouses elsewhere.
