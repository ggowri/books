Chapter 130 The RV

But they didn't expect someone to crash their party. Now they were racing Building B for the grain.

Cecilia eyed the six silos in front of them, wheels turning.

"Let's split up!"

Arthur didn't overthink it. He got to work.

Cecilia bolted to the two farthest silos. Inside—packed full of corn on the cob.

The corn had frozen solid in the cold, hard as rocks to the touch.

She swept both silos' worth of corn into the Space, then grabbed a bag of sorghum and headed back to the rigid inflatable boat.

Arthur had already hauled several bags of sorghum back and forth. Silent. Fast. Efficient.

By comparison, Cecilia looked like she was slacking off.

After countless trips, both boats were stuffed to the brim—except for the spots where people sat. And they'd barely made a dent in one silo.

No time to waste. They commanded Blackie to head back immediately.

Round trip plus loading took three hours total.

Cecilia hadn't thought much of it at first. But compared to Building B's crew with their car? She was pissed.

"Arthur. My SUV. You gonna give it back or what? If we had that thing right now, we'd be three times faster."

They didn't have time to haul everything back to the sixty-sixth floor. Instead, they stashed the grain at a spot they'd scoped out earlier.

An abandoned building, almost completely buried in snow.

Arthur had checked it out during the day—no signs of life nearby.

Plus, they'd broken in through a window. Easy to cover their tracks.

Cecilia had only meant to take a jab at Arthur. But his brow furrowed tight.

"Give me an hour. Lend me Blackie. I'll get us a car."

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. That confident?

"I'm coming with you."

Following Arthur's directions, the two of them plus Blackie reached a flat stretch of ground.

"Here."

When Arthur said "here," Cecilia looked around in disbelief.

Nothing but white snow everywhere. Not a single trace of any vehicle.

Arthur didn't explain. He grabbed an entrenching tool from the boat, paced left and right, occasionally glancing at a tall building a few hundred feet away.

Finally, he drove the first shovel down.

Then the second.

After about ten minutes of digging, Arthur had carved out a hole over three feet deep.

Then Cecilia saw it—a window!

"How did you know this was the edge of the building?" she asked, stunned.

"All these days without a phone or computer—what'd you think I was doing at home?" Arthur shot her a sly look. "I've got the entire city's topography and building layouts memorized."

With that, Arthur kicked the window open and dropped inside.

Then—endless silence.

Aside from Blackie's panting, Cecilia heard nothing.

She had no idea what Arthur was doing down there.

About twenty minutes passed.

Then she heard it—an engine roaring to life below.

Her eyes darted around nervously. How the hell is Arthur getting out?

Blackie suddenly stood up, looking to the right.

Then to the left.

Then straight ahead!

Cecilia's heart dropped. What the hell, Arthur? Why is he going in circles?

"Buzz!"

"BANG!"

"CRASH!"

A series of sounds and tremors.

The second Cecilia sensed trouble, she leaped into the rigid inflatable boat.

Once she was in, Blackie immediately bolted, pulling the boat away.

Cecilia looked back—Arthur came roaring out in a massive RV! Smashing through who knew how many windows.

The ground collapsed beneath it. The RV's gas pedal was floored, barely making it out.

And right behind Arthur? A swarm of giant cockroaches!

Wave after wave, so thick it reminded Cecilia of the insect plague from her past life.

But the roaches only chased them for a few hundred feet before retreating underground.

Blackie sprinted ahead. Arthur followed close behind in the massive RV.

Two people and a dog quickly arrived back at the silos.

Just in time to see Dyson and his crew speeding off in the distance.

Thanks to the angle, they probably hadn't spotted Cecilia and Arthur.

Once they reached the silos, Cecilia got a good look at the vehicle Arthur had found.

Holy shit. Seventy feet long—like a mini tour bus. Fully equipped inside, with tons of space.

Cecilia had stockpiled an RV too, but nothing this flashy.

This thing probably cost tens of millions.

"Think this beast can clear out a whole silo in one trip?" Cecilia asked with a grin.

"One and a half," Arthur corrected.

Cecilia glanced in the direction Dyson's group had left, remembering how smug they'd been.

Sorry, guys. We're not holding back.

No more talk. Cecilia and Arthur got to work.

Even Blackie pitched in, dragging grain bags toward the RV with his teeth.

Though his clumsy tugging looked pretty hilarious.

Soon, they'd emptied an entire silo. Just like Arthur said—the RV could still fit more.
