Chapter 149 Pretty Interesting

"You're way too stubborn. If you used people right, they'd have your back. But you? You went and burned that bridge. Now how the hell are we supposed to deal with them? You're too stuck in your ways—you shouldn't be running this sector."

Logan's heart dropped.

But he didn't dare argue. Just kept his head down while Richard kept going.

Richard sighed. "Since the apocalypse hit, you've been running this shelter—food, supplies, everything covered. You haven't experienced the real apocalypse. There's a swarm of mutant rats causing hell in Lyrae City. Go deal with them. Toughen up. Learn how hard people are struggling to survive out there. When you're done clearing those rats, I'll bring you back."

Logan couldn't refuse. He saluted sharply. "Yes, General!"

After watching Logan head inside, Cecilia and Arthur wandered back upstairs.

They didn't talk much.

But Cecilia could tell Arthur was in a good mood. Probably thrilled about that little "report" he'd just filed.

She'd never been able to read Arthur.

Especially after learning his real background today.

He got kicked out of special forces for insubordination, then joined a merc group.

His mom's family had serious clout—he was a classic rich kid.

But in all their time together, Cecilia had only figured out one thing about him—he liked spicy food.

Everything else? Just... what he wanted her to see.

Like Arthur's every move had a purpose. And he did whatever the hell he wanted.

"Your kid... doing okay?"

Of all things, that's what Arthur opened with.

Cecilia, lost in thought, nearly choked on her own spit.

"Uh, yeah, fine. Place is warm, got plenty of food."

Arthur didn't push it. They kept walking.

After a while, Arthur said, "Need any kids' clothes or formula?"

Cecilia frowned. Since when is Arthur this thoughtful?

She turned back—and caught the smile in his eyes. Looking right at her.

Those two little fangs peeking out again.

Her heart sank. Shit. Arthur knew. He was messing with her.

"It's a parrot, isn't it?" Arthur said, voice light.

Cecilia didn't want to hide it anymore.

When she'd said she had kids at home, she was just messing with Arthur—and trying to score more food for Blackie while she was at it.

"Yeah. Parrot."

"Ms. Summers, you're pretty interesting."

Suddenly Arthur laughed.

She glanced back. He was watching her, shaking his head with that helpless smile.

Cecilia decided to ignore him.

Interesting? She was trying to scam him!

A moment later, Cecilia and Arthur went over the details. The deal with the authorities—hauling grain for mutated pork—was on her mind.

Before they knew it, they'd reached the 66th floor.

These past few days, Cecilia hadn't run into anyone else in the building.

They were probably all hiding in their apartments—either avoiding conflict or waiting for the military to show up and make life easier. No one wanted trouble right now.

When they reached the stairwell, Arthur opened the door. Cecilia glanced downstairs.

Suddenly, she thought of Phoebe.

That woman who'd snitched to Logan and framed their 66th floor.

She had nothing going on tonight—perfect time to pay Phoebe a visit.

While Cecilia was lost in thought, Arthur suddenly said, "Ms. Summers, see you around."

Cecilia replied stiffly. "Yeah."

Usually when they parted ways, it was just a nod.

This was the first time Arthur had actually said goodbye to her.

Whatever. She figured he was just in a good mood after scoring all that food—and screwing over Logan.

Back home, Cecilia fed the two little ones, then pulled out a steaming plate of beef pasta for herself—complete with a grilled sausage and a braised egg.

She slurped it all down.

Blackie's appetite was still massive. And ever since yesterday, the guy had been acting... clingy.

While she ate, he kept resting his head on her leg, staring up at her with puppy-dog eyes.

Cecilia caved and gave him a piece of beef.

He swallowed it in one gulp and immediately wanted more.

Jet joined in the chaos, squawking that if Blackie got some, he needed some too.

The whole meal turned into Cecilia feeding one, then the other, back and forth.

By the end, she'd somehow... eaten two whole plates of beef pasta?

She was stuffed. Had to take a walk in the Space to digest.

The two little ones followed her in, running wild and playing across the open ground.

Jet was big enough now to soar freely through the Space.

Blackie kept playing with his ball. They were having the time of their lives.

Meanwhile, Cecilia wandered around and spotted something she'd grown earlier in Plot One—a giant lollipop.

The thing was bizarre.

On the wrapper, she saw bold letters: HURTS LIKE HELL.

She gave it a couple test swings. Surprisingly light.
