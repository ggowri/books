Chapter 69 Send Someone to Arrest Her

The lighter rain had given Ashley hope that rescue was imminent, that the nightmare was over, and that was why she dared to come to the 66th floor.

Cecilia smiled inwardly but kept a straight face, pulling Blackie back from the door. "Oh? That scares me. If the police arrest me, my life will be ruined—I'll end up in jail, won't I?"

Ashley snickered, hugging her grandson tightly. "I'm not unreasonable. I have a child to feed, and we're out of food and water. Give me some supplies to get us through these days, and I'll put in a good word for you when help arrives. Remember, a clear line separates self-defense from assault."

Her true motives surfaced quickly.

Cecilia sized up the pair—the boy was chubby, clearly unharmed, and Ashley looked energetic, nothing like someone who'd suffered in the apocalypse.

Both were faring well, especially Ashley, a robust woman in her fifties with a loud, strong voice.

Stepping closer to Cecilia, Hailey whispered in her ear, "That old hag is Ashley Lyons. She lives below me. She's terrible—bullies her son's wife constantly, treating her like garbage. These past few days, Ashley has starved her half to death and even tried to sell her to Bryan's group for food and water."

Disgust welled up in Cecilia as she looked at the triumphant Ashley.

The boy, his face twisted with anger, shouted, "Hurry up and give us everything, or my grandma will beat you!"

Clearly spoiled and unreasonable, he'd been warped by Ashley's influence.

Exchanging a glance with Arthur and Hailey, Cecilia opened the stairwell door. "Ma'am, that's a good offer. I'm young, I don't want trouble. Come inside—take whatever you need."

Ashley looked skeptical, eyeing Blackie warily. "Don't try to trick me."

"Blackie, go to Arthur's place," Cecilia said.

Arthur raised an eyebrow, confused but compliant—he led Blackie away.

Ashley then carried her grandson into Cecilia's hallway, following closely behind.

The smell of roasted chicken—Cecilia's leftover dinner—hit them the moment they entered. The boy squirmed excitedly. "Meat! I want meat!"

Ashley's eyes lit up. "I want meat, too—give us all you have."

Cecilia slammed the door shut, sneering, "You wanted to talk about the law? Let's talk. You're in my apartment now—I could claim you're breaking and entering. I can do whatever I want to you."

With that, she kicked Ashley hard in the knee.

This old bully wanted to reason with her?

Cecilia would teach her a lesson.

After a thorough beating, Ashley fled with her grandson, wetting herself in fear as she ran.

The ignorant boy kept shouting insults at Cecilia.

Cecilia pointed her machete at Ashley. "If your grandson makes one more sound, I'll slit his throat right now!"

At her words, Ashley clamped a hand tightly over the boy's mouth.

Spoiled rotten, he bit down hard on her hand, tearing off a piece of flesh.

Once Ashley stumbled out of the 66th floor, she collapsed in the stairwell, wailing about wanting to die.

Just then, a commotion erupted downstairs. "Rescue! I see a rescue team!"

Cecilia looked through the stairwell glass and spotted two boats approaching, with uniformed men on board.

Ashley jumped to her feet instantly, her tears drying. Wiping blood on her face, she glared fiercely at Cecilia. "You little bitch! I'll have the police arrest you! You'll regret messing with me!"

To her shock, the rescue team was gone by the time she reached the ground floor.

Their only purpose was to count survivors, inform everyone that the government was building a safe zone, and announce that acid rain was easing and supplies would be distributed soon.

Starved of official contact for so long, residents swarmed the team, begging for information and food.

With only four men on the boats, any supplies they'd had were already gone.

The team finally broke free from the crowd and departed.

Residents on the stairs were left helpless, watching the boats disappear.

Ashley ran down just in time, screaming at the departing vessels, "Murder! There's a murderer in this building! Send someone to arrest her! Are you abandoning us? You bastards, come back!"

However, the ships had already vanished over the horizon.

Everyone else was shouting, too, their voices mixing into a cacophony that swallowed her complaints whole.
