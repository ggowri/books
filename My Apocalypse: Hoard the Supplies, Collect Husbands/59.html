Chapter 59 Golden Python

By then, most people's homes would have leaking roofs.

Actually, when Cecilia had workers repaint her walls earlier, she also had them repaint the walls of some other residents in the building.

Especially the entire rooftop, which received an anti-corrosion layer using the best materials.

Cecilia did this because she was afraid that damage to other residents' walls would also affect her own.

So, in a way, this building benefited because of Cecilia.

However, Cecilia's walls were coated with anti-corrosion material both inside and out, providing double protection.

This was to prepare for potentially increased acid rain concentration after her rebirth.

But currently, Cecilia hadn't heard anyone mention leaks due to acid rain corrosion. She guessed the acid rain concentration was similar to her previous life.

Yet, looking out from her floor-to-ceiling windows, Cecilia could see many areas of other buildings showing signs of deterioration.

She wanted to fill all those previously prepared buckets with acid rain, so that in the future, people who hadn't been exposed to acid rain, or hadn't had enough exposure to mutate, would have another chance to reshape their bodies.

Thinking this, Cecilia went up to the rooftop terrace and saw that the two buckets she had placed earlier were full.

She collected the buckets and placed two empty ones.

At the same time, she found the entire terrace was covered in water, about one meter deep, just enough to swim in.

Seeing no one around, she dove into the acid rain and swam to a secluded spot.

Then, she used her will to summon Jet out.

Jet, upon appearing, looked clearly confused.

It flapped its wings, circling Cecilia twice. "Go home! Go home."

Cecilia patted her shoulder, signaling Jet to land there first.

Jet was somewhat reluctant but obediently landed on Cecilia's shoulder.

Cecilia ignored Jet and began using her will to fill the water buckets with acid rain.

This process took over half an hour.

Jet grew impatient several times in between, but Cecilia pacified it with snacks.

She fed pine nuts to Jet one by one, finally managing to keep its beak busy.

When Cecilia finished and was about to leave with a sigh of relief, she suddenly saw two red points of light in the distance, at the far corner of the building's rooftop.

The points of light were about the size of ping pong balls, staring intently in this direction.

Cecilia instantly became alert.

The power's been out for ages, and there's nothing in that direction—it's just a stretch of water. How can there be two light bulb-like spots?

Jet on her shoulder flapped its wings uneasily. "Bad thing! Bad thing!"

Moreover, the thing seemed to be moving!

As it got closer, Cecilia's pupils instantly contracted—it was a snake, a massive golden python.

But this thing was extraordinarily large, as thick as a tree!

"It's a mutated animal!"

Cecilia's heart tightened.

I wonder how it climbed up to the rooftop!

In her previous life, she had seen all sorts of mutated creatures. Some even developed superpowers later on.

Being able to get to the terrace means this mutated creature is absolutely not simple.

Cecilia had seen many mutated animals in her past life.

Ants as big as forearms, with front teeth the size of palms—one bite could sever a human's calf.

Cockroaches the size of palms, flying in swarms that blotted out the sun.

Dogs as big as ponies, and butterflies with wings as hard as metal!

But the appearance of mutated animals should have been months away.

So, the moment she saw the mutated golden python, all the hair on Cecilia's body stood on end.

Could it be that the insect calamity of the apocalypse has arrived early? Have mutated animals arrived sooner than anticipated?

But Blackie, although showing signs of mutation, isn't mutating very quickly.

Cecilia's eyelid twitched because she saw the golden python swimming closer.

Jet beside her was restless, repeatedly calling out "bad thing."

With a thought, Cecilia first put Jet back into her Space.

The golden python was mainly drawn here by Jet's scent, wanting to prey on the lively bird.

Sure enough, after Jet disappeared, the golden python stopped in the middle of the water, staring fixedly at Cecilia as if contemplating whether it could swallow Cecilia whole.

Cecilia took out her blade, facing off against the golden python.

She could also choose to retreat into her Space, but snakes are extremely patient creatures. Cecilia couldn't guarantee where the python would go after she entered her Space.

What if... it waits nearby to ambush?

Then, after exiting her Space, she would lose sight of the python, and swimming underwater would make her easier for the python to target.

The golden python looked at the long knife in Cecilia's hand, its red tongue flickering faintly in the weak light, as if thinking and hesitating.

After watching for a long time, the python seemed to judge that this creature was not easy prey, turned, and swam away.

Cecilia watched it crawl down along the edge of the building, moving extremely fast, like a ghost, and soon disappeared.

Only then did Cecilia let out a sigh of relief.

She hurriedly swam quickly toward the rooftop door, then swiftly entered the building level and locked the door tightly.

This door had some steps, higher than the terrace edge, so it wasn't covered by the acid rain.
