Chapter 141 He Wanted More

Especially Blackie, whose abilities and intelligence were off the charts.

And she hadn't known either of them long enough yet. Small animals needed more patience. Training.

But she could tell—these two were good at heart.

She kept tugging Blackie's ear, lecturing nonstop.

She knew Jet was just a lackey. The real mastermind? This sneaky little Blackie.

Blackie always looked stone-faced, expressionless. But inside? Pure scheming.

He was the real troublemaker. The evil strategist.

But he was weirdly obedient today, just sitting there while Cecilia rambled on and on. Though by the end, he looked like he was about to doze off.

Cecilia huffed and bonked him on the head.

"Hey. Pay attention."

What answered her? A loud, rumbling... stomach growl. Long and drawn-out, like a protest.

"Haha! Blackie's hungry! Me too! I'm hungry too!"

Jet bounced around on the bed, practically mocking him.

Blackie's face stayed blank. He raised one paw and smacked Jet down.

Jet shut up instantly.

"Oh crap—I got so caught up lecturing you guys, I forgot to feed you!"

Cecilia rushed to pull food out of the Space.

Blackie had just used his ability and hauled several loads back and forth—he was starving.

The moment Cecilia pulled out food, he shot up from the bed.

Cecilia dumped the food into Blackie's bowl—well, actually a pot. Normal bowls couldn't handle his appetite anymore. She poured it in with a thunderous clatter.

Before she even finished pouring, Blackie shoved his head into the pot and started chomping away.

Jet flapped over, squawking for food too.

Cecilia pulled out a handful of parrot food from the Space and put it in Jet's bowl.

While the two dug in, Cecilia pulled out a steaming plate of braised meat pasta and a glass of grape juice. She ate her pasta while sipping her drink.

She'd figured Blackie would be busy eating for a while. But she'd barely taken two bites when he sidled over, rested his snout on her leg, and wagged his tail hard.

He wanted more.

Cecilia stared in disbelief.

This time, she pulled out Space-produced dog food—surely that would fill him up.

She even gave him Space-produced pet bread that supposedly restored abilities.

Cecilia went back to her meal.

Honestly, she was starving too. She'd been so hungry she'd gone past it.

If Blackie and Jet hadn't pranked her the moment she got home, she'd have eaten immediately.

This time, Cecilia took five bites of pasta.

She counted. Just five bites.

Then Blackie waddled back over, eyes sparkling as he stared at Cecilia, his big face drooping.

He rolled his eyes upward, putting on a pitiful yet haughty look.

He wanted seconds. Again.

And Jet had finished too!

Jet hopped onto the table, bouncing around like crazy.

"Feed me! I want more too!"

Cecilia wanted to swat him off. "Blackie was out there working! You've been napping all day—why waste food stuffing your face?!"

But Jet was hell-bent on competing with Blackie for who could eat more. He circled Cecilia, acting all cute, demanding food.

Cecilia couldn't take it anymore.

This time, she pulled out three massive pots.

One for Blackie's meat, one for grain, one for dog bread.

And Jet? She piled his bowl sky-high too.

"Fine! Eat! Both of you—eat!"

She practically growled the words.

The two were thrilled. Blackie wiggled his butt and wagged his tail. Jet bobbed his head and flapped his wings. Side by side, they dug in.

Cecilia stomped back to her seat and angrily added another dish to her plate.

I'm eating more too! No way am I losing to these two!

And so, Cecilia devoured a huge bowl of braised pork pasta, a drink, a pan-seared steak, and a sandwich.

Then finished it off with a bowl of egg drop soup.

When she finally tried to stand, her stomach felt like an overinflated balloon.

As for Blackie? Cecilia had already refilled his bowl five or six times.

Yeah—after that first pot, every refill was three pots.

Blackie had downed over a dozen pots total.

And he still wasn't done. Looked like he wanted more.

But she noticed he was eating slower than at the start, so he probably wasn't that hungry anymore.

Jet? He hadn't even finished the huge bowl of parrot food Cecilia gave him. Now he sat there, so stuffed he kept hiccupping.

But even while hiccupping, he kept kissing ass.

"Blackie eats so much! Blackie's so amazing!"

Blackie ignored him, focused on his food. But that slightly wagging tail gave him away—he was eating it up.

When Blackie finished another three bowls and stared at Cecilia with those pleading puppy eyes again, her scalp tingled.

"Blackie, I cleared out multiple grain silos today. I was pretty proud of that. But now? Not so much. At this rate, you're going to eat me out of house and home."
