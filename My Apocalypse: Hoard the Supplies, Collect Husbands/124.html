Chapter 124 Negotiations

They'd all helped loot Mabel's place—basically killed her husband too.

So when they heard officials were coming, they panicked and huddled up to figure out what to do.

Now, watching the scene unfold below, it clicked—this was the apocalypse. So many dead already. Why be scared?

After debating for ages, they came out.

Evan stepped forward, eyeing Logan. "You in charge? If you're taking over this place as a shelter, we getting compensated? If so, we're all in!"

Standing opposite Cecilia's group were Evan, Brandon's wife Emily, and a young couple.

The four had clearly banded together.

Logan glanced at Cecilia and the others, then walked over to Evan's group with a smile.

"You've all had it rough lately. Once this becomes an official shelter, the government will run it as a base. We'll assign tasks, provide food, guarantee your safety."

Evan and the others lit up.

"Can we keep living where we are now?"

Logan nodded. "Absolutely. The government won't seize your current homes. You can stay put."

"Then we agree to the shelter takeover."

"Me too."

"Us too."

Evan's group voted yes enthusiastically, fist-bumping each other like they were already picturing the good life ahead.

Cecilia knew fighting the government over this shelter was a losing battle.

But she couldn't just let them destroy her life this easily.

Or let the Barnes family win.

She didn't know how the Barnes family picked this spot as a shelter. In her past life at the shelter, she was never moved here.

So... the only variables were Miranda's recent visit and Alexander's death.

She looked at Logan. "Sixty-third floor and up—sixty-four, sixty-five, sixty-six—off limits. No one lives there."

Her final compromise.

Logan thought for a moment, then nodded. "Deal. We don't want conflict with the original residents anyway. Below sixty-three is more than enough."

During their talk, Cecilia learned something else—when Logan had brought armed men upstairs, it wasn't just about territory. Someone named "Dyson" had ratted her out to Logan, claimed Cecilia and her group had cleaned out the food storage.

Apparently Dyson put on quite the performance. Tears and everything.

Logan brought him up now because he remembered Dyson saying he was from Building B.

Logan had tried sending people to scout Building B before, but failed—all the entrances were blocked.

With everything else going on, he'd let it drop.

Now he wanted to know if Cecilia knew this Dyson. If she had intel on Building B residents.

Cecilia's brow furrowed. Dyson backstabbed me?

She smiled at Logan. Said she barely knew Dyson. Didn't know much about Building B either.

Then she paused, rubbing her chin. "But Logan... have you considered Building B?"

Building A had survived well because Cecilia had coated the walls with anti-corrosion material before the apocalypse.

Building B? No such luck. The walls had already taken some damage.

But the structure was new. Built tall with quality materials.

Among the surrounding buildings, it was still in the best shape.

Logan looked toward Building B. Considering.

Cecilia fanned the flames.

"A and B are close together. You could build a bigger shelter. Easier to manage everything."

She finally got him hooked.

"You're right, Ms. Summers. When the government rescues more people, we'll look into Building B."

Cecilia's lips twitched into a faint smile. She nodded lightly.

One thought consumed her mind—she hoped Logan's people would dig into Building B. Something about those people felt... off.

And Dyson? Playing dirty behind her back? Fine. She could play that game too.

And just like that, the government's plan to establish a new shelter in Building A was set in stone.

After Cecilia and Arthur returned to the top floor, they gathered for a meeting.

The gist: stay alert. Work together. Protect the 66th floor.

Also, Hailey seemed really interested in the work the government was offering.

Cecilia didn't stop her. If it was a good fit, why not let Hailey do something useful?

When the meeting wrapped, Cecilia stopped Arthur before he could leave.

She smiled sweetly. "Want to talk?"

Arthur didn't look surprised. Instead, he opened his door.

"Come in. Let's talk inside."

It'd been a while since Cecilia had been in Arthur's place.

She stepped inside. It felt a few degrees colder than her apartment, but overall? Still pretty comfortable.
