Chapter 102 Total Win

So she couldn't cut them open inside the Space.

Cecilia tied her hair back. Time to work.

An hour later, she'd barely carved a small opening in the first safe.

"What the hell's in here that needs this much protection?"

She wiped the sweat off her face and turned down the fire in the room.

Through the tiny gap, she peeked inside.

Cecilia groaned. Papers. Just stacks of documents.

But she'd already come this far. Might as well finish.

After more work, she finally made an opening big enough to reach through.

She pulled out one of the files—and her heart nearly stopped.

Cargo contracts.

Each document listed locations and what was stored there.

A lot of it was stashed in high places or underground.

Cecilia ran through the locations in her mind. Pretty sure a few hadn't been touched by acid rain yet.

Better yet—two underground warehouses were close. Less than a mile away.

And what was inside? Jackpot.

Heart racing, Cecilia pulled out the contracts and marked every location on a map.

Those two nearby warehouses though...

The blizzard hadn't hit yet. Just light snow for now.

Should she make a run for it?

Cecilia opened her door and spotted Blackie playing outside.

An idea clicked.

You raise a dog, you use a dog, right?

Perfect weather for a dog sled.

Blackie froze mid-play, toy dangling from his mouth, as Cecilia stared at him.

Why's she looking at me like that?

Then someone knocked.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow and answered. Arthur stood there.

He dropped a blood-smeared car key into her palm. "Sorry. Wrecked your ride. I'll get you a new one later, yeah?"

Cecilia eyed the mangled, blood-crusted key and frowned. She didn't take it. Just looked up at him.

Arthur shrugged, pocketed the key. "I'll get you another car."

"Just a car?" Cecilia's eyes narrowed. "What about everything in it? Miranda promised me all of it."

Arthur froze mid-turn. "Fine. A gun too."

"What about the bullets?" she pressed.

"Twelve rounds." His jaw tightened.

"And?"

"Two grenades." Arthur's mouth twitched.

"That's it?" Cecilia crossed her arms.

"Some supplies got blown up. Didn't check what." He sighed.

Cecilia nodded. "Alright."

When Arthur turned to leave, even this rich boy looked like it hurt to pay up.

So when he delivered the gun, twelve bullets, two grenades, and a crate of supplies to her door, he didn't say a word. Just knocked, dropped everything, and left.

Cecilia hauled it all inside.

That deal with Miranda? Total win.

She'd suspected Miranda had real firepower in that car.

Sure, she didn't need supplies right now—but those guns? Such a surprise.

Then she paused, staring at the haul.

What the hell had Arthur done to burn through that many bullets and grenades—and total an entire vehicle?

The blood on that key flashed in her mind. Miranda's face followed.

Cecilia snorted.

She filed it away. Didn't storm across the hall to ask him.

After all—this was his family business.

******

That night, Cecilia slept like a baby.

Rebecca and Alexander were dead. She'd gotten her revenge for her past life.

The thought made her smile in her sleep.

******

Next morning, the temperature had dropped again.

During their outdoor workout, Hailey rubbed her arms. "Why's it so damn cold?"

Cecilia checked the thermometer she'd left outside. Negative thirty degrees Fahrenheit.

"Next few days, let's scavenge this building and Tower B for anything we can burn. Stock up."

Arthur's suggestion made sense.

They'd already hauled up burnable furniture from nearby floors over the past few days. But with the temperature dropping, they'd burn through it faster.

Cecilia had plenty of firewood in her Space, but she wasn't about to let Arthur and Hailey know it existed.

A few days ago, Hailey went down to Phoebe's place and took her generator. Now everyone on the 66th floor had one.

They'd grabbed every space heater and electric blanket from the surrounding floors too.

But none of that electric crap worked as well as a good old-fashioned fire.
