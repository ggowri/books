Chapter 208 Victor Barnes Dies

Once Colonel Reed had made it up, he looked down and realised just how perfect the spot was.

It was a small ledge, and a tree happened to block the line of sight of the two bodyguards standing watch outside.

Arthur remained half crouched the whole time, as though he had already calculated the range of every possible line of sight and hidden himself with absolute precision.

And the ledge itself lay in the blind spot of another guard.

After Reed climbed up, Arthur even pressed a hand down on his shoulder, signalling for him not to raise himself too high if he wanted to stay hidden.

Reed sized it up and realised that even if someone happened to walk past nearby, at Arthur's chosen height it would still be difficult to spot him.

At that moment, Reed could not help thinking that Arthur really did seem far too good at this sort of infiltration.

The next step was getting inside.

Reed had always been a straightforward man. Never in his life had he crept about indoors like a thief the way he was now.

So for most of the way, he was practically dragged along and directed by Arthur, who guided him through the house as if using cheat codes, all the way to Victor's door.

Once Arthur delivered him there, he vanished without another word.

Still, the look he gave Reed before disappearing seemed to say very clearly: Colonel Reed, your skills are lacking. You need more practice.

Reed swore inwardly. I'm a soldier. How am I supposed to be good at assassinations like this?

Still, there was no time to dwell on it. He carefully turned the handle and prepared to enter.

Only then, from inside the room, came a series of utterly revolting sounds of flirtation.

"My lovely, today scared the life out of me. I really need to unwind properly." That was a man's voice. It had to be Victor.

"Oh, Master Barnes, your family is clearly blessed. What is there to be afraid of?" came the soft, cloying voice of a woman.

"Hmph, exactly. The Barnes family is thriving in this apocalypse. What's one old general? One foot in the grave already, and not long for this world. Having him killed was revenge for my brother, and it was also to show General Mercer what the Barnes family can do. If we can even deal with the old general, he'll have to look at us differently. Hmph. And that nephew of mine, Nathan, was actually frightened. He's still too young to shoulder anything."

"Master Barnes, don't talk about killing and fighting. I'm all ready for you," the woman cooed, her voice practically melting.

"Oh, you little darling..."

Colonel Reed could not listen to another second of it. He burst through the door, roaring inwardly, Victor, our old general is lying between life and death, and you're in here enjoying yourself? Let's see how you enjoy this.

A moment later came the very soft sound of a silenced pistol.

Then the woman screamed.

Only for the scream to cut off halfway.

Reed had knocked her unconscious.

Naturally, he had put on a mask before entering.

Having finished, he glared at Victor's naked body, all that disgusting, wobbling flesh, spat fiercely on him, then slipped back out and leapt from the second floor ledge before making his escape.

This had been arranged with Arthur in advance. Once Reed finished, he was not to wait. He was to leave alone and get as far away as possible. Arthur still had other scores to settle with the Barnes family.

...

Meanwhile, Arthur had already reached Vivian's room.

But he had run into a bit of trouble.

Because during the day, Vivian had seen him among the crowd. She knew what he was capable of and had long since arranged for men to lie in wait inside her room.

Arthur was cautious to the last. He did not enter through the main door, but through the window instead.

Yet even so, the moment he came in, someone pressed a gun to his head.

Vivian stood there with a cigarette in one hand, wearing a thin nightdress in the warmth of the villa, barefoot as she slowly walked over to Arthur.

"As expected, the bastard child of a bitch is doomed to a lowly life. Coming here in the middle of the night just to die."

At the words bastard child, Arthur narrowed his eyes slightly, something dark and unreadable stirring in them.

The woman standing before him was Vivian Barnes, Miranda's youngest daughter.

She was the one who had murdered his mother.

It was only after returning to the country this time that Arthur had uncovered the truth behind his mother's death.

He had wanted to kill Vivian for a long time, but earlier she had been pregnant, and he had stayed his hand.

As an assassin, he killed women.

But not pregnant women.

So he had waited until Vivian gave birth before calculating the right moment to come for her.

He had not expected that this cunning woman would still manage to anticipate him.

Now Vivian lifted the hem of her thin nightdress and drew a small pistol from inside her underwear.

In one smooth motion, she cocked the pistol and levelled it lightly at Arthur's chest.

"Since you're so desperate to see your mother, that bitch, I'll send you to her. Hahahaha!"

She fired at once.

Arthur dropped instantly, clutching his chest as pain twisted across his face.

Slowly, he closed his eyes.

In an extremely weak voice, he asked one final question.

"Did you kill my mother?"

Vivian took a drag from her cigarette and let out a cold laugh. Then she stepped forward and kicked Arthur.

"Arthur, you probably never knew this, but when your mother died she was still calling your name. Yes, Diana Barnes was killed by me. I spent a great deal of effort arranging it to look like suicide. You can die in peace now and go see her below."

Arthur's mother, Diana Barnes, had officially died by suicide many years ago.

At least, that was what the police file said.

It had happened during the years when Arthur was serving in the special forces.
