Chapter 183 A Trap at the Door


Cecilia stepped back.

She watched as Frank dragged Mabel to one side and slapped her hard across the face. "Don't make a scene in front of the boss!"

The boss he meant was, of course, Nathan Barnes.

Mabel knew she had lost her composure. She hurriedly collected herself, apologised to Nathan, then stood obediently beside Frank, fixing Cecilia with a venomous stare while not daring to say another word.

In any case, she had already said what she came to say.

And judging by the state of Cecilia and Blackie, they were indeed living well, better than anyone Mabel had seen in a very long time.

She was convinced Nathan had believed at least part of it.

Cecilia looked at him. "Nathan, are the Barnes family really this stupid? Letting a woman like her lead you around by the nose? And what exactly is this Space supposed to be?"

Nathan gave a lazy yawn. "Miss Summers, let's not play games. Right now, everyone at the top across the country is looking for these so called Spaces. And the people who have them usually fit a few patterns. They live well, they own old gemstone objects, and they always seem able to produce supplies no one expects. Miss Summers, I have to say, you do seem to fit the bill rather nicely. I'd be foolish not to be suspicious."

Then he smiled. "I seem to remember you have a family heirloom, a gemstone ring, don't you? Why not let me have a look? I'll leave as soon as I've seen it. No fuss, no delay."

As he said it, Nathan even put his hands together in an exaggerated pleading gesture, as though trying to show how sincere he was.

Cecilia's expression turned cold. "It broke."

When she had broken off the engagement with the Barnes family, she had already used that excuse, that the pendant had been broken. If Nathan asked around, he would certainly have heard as much.

It was just that he clearly did not believe it now.

Nathan sighed. "Well, in that case, we'll simply have to search."

"Oh, and I nearly forgot to mention. While we've been standing here chatting, my men have already gone upstairs."

Cecilia's heart lurched.

Arthur had already rushed forward.

As he passed Nathan, he drove a punch straight into his face.

Nathan fell to the ground, yet did not seem bothered in the slightest. Watching Cecilia and Arthur race upstairs, he simply threw back his head and laughed.

Colonel Reed had a headache coming on.

Orders had come down from above stating that Nathan and his people had full authority to search the sixty sixth floor where Cecilia was staying.

But Thomas had previously promised the old general that he would protect Cecilia no matter what.

So when Nathan's men arrived, Thomas stopped them on the fortieth floor and refused to let them go any further.

Unfortunately, one of them was particularly difficult to deal with.

That man was Colonel Barnes, the Barnes family's power within the military.

He was in his forties, Alexander's second uncle by generation, and Nathan's father.

In Thomas's eyes, Colonel Barnes was a shameless old bastard.

The trouble was, they held the same rank, which made him difficult to suppress.

As matters stood, Colonel Barnes was pointing a gun directly at Thomas.

Behind him stood five or six armed soldiers, all aiming their weapons at Thomas as well.

Thomas, meanwhile, had a line of soldiers behind him too. The two sides faced each other in a deadlock.

"Thomas, are you trying to rebel? You won't even obey a general's orders?" Colonel Barnes was trembling with rage.

There was, in fact, something few people knew, and it was the real reason Colonel Barnes was so obsessed with this matter of the Space.

The government already knew that the apocalypse had brought the existence of Spaces and mutated creatures to light, and researchers had been organised to study both.

Mutated creatures were no rarity now. They could be found in large numbers all over the country.

But Spaces had only been discovered recently. Even the highest levels of government had managed to gather only a handful, and research into them was still in its earliest stages.

The Barnes family had found one of their own.

After much hesitation, they had chosen not to hand it over.

How could they bear to give up something that valuable?

But the matter had leaked out.

Now the government wanted the Barnes family to surrender that Space, and they had offered extremely generous terms in return.

The problem was, the Barnes family could no longer hand it over.

Because just last night, the Space had vanished.

During the first half of the night, someone had entered the Barnes residence without a sound.

That person had not only taken the dimension ring, but also made off with an entire store of the family's firearms.

The guards on duty had known nothing about it.

When Colonel Barnes learned what had happened, his legs had nearly given way beneath him.

Part of it was fear. If someone could move in and out of the Barnes residence as though they were invisible, then killing him would surely be just as easy.

But there was another reason.

The Barnes family had already concealed the existence of the dimension ring instead of handing it over.

Now that the government knew of it and demanded it be surrendered, what would they think when the Barnes family claimed it had been lost?

This was the apocalypse now.

Order had collapsed. The law had collapsed with it.

Killing a man, wiping out an entire family, that was no longer difficult.

So the moment he heard from his son that Cecilia might possess a spatial artefact, he brought men and came up here without a second thought.

"Colonel Barnes, don't pin that sort of charge on me," Thomas said, wearing an expression of helpless innocence. "I'm only responsible for managing this place and protecting the civilians here. You suddenly burst in with armed men. I'm bound to feel a bit nervous too."

Colonel Barnes stared at him, so furious he stamped his foot.

They were the same rank, and both answered to generals above them. Neither side truly outranked the other.

"Thomas, take your complaints to our general if you like. But if you still refuse to move aside today, I really will open fire."

His teeth clenched as he spoke, and he flicked the safety off the gun in his hand.
