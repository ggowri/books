Chapter 186 Blackie's Fury

Colonel Barnes raised his hand, trying to fire at the huge black dog in front of him.

But the dog was faster.

It launched straight for his head.

"Colonel!"

A panicked shout rang out from nearby.

Barnes felt himself being knocked down to the ground.

The gun in his hand went off in fury, three shots in quick succession.

A sheet of blood came pouring down over him, drenching his entire face red.

Every inch of his body hurt. There was a crushing weight on his chest, so heavy it felt as though a thousand pounds were pressing down on him. For a moment, his whole body seemed numb, and his head swam.

He wiped the blood from his face with his hand and finally saw clearly.

It was his personal guard.

The man had thrown himself over him and saved his life.

And now he was dead, his corpse lying across Barnes's body.

Standing on top of that corpse, as though on a grotesque pile of bodies, was a large black dog with a coat glossy as polished leather and a body strong as a bull.

The dog had its jaws clamped around the guard's head, blood still dripping steadily from it.

And those ice blue eyes were fixed on Barnes as though he were already dead.

Panic surged through him. He tried to reach for his gun, only to discover that one arm was pinned, trapped beneath the body and could not be pulled free.

As for the shots he had just fired, all three had struck the guard shielding him.

The dog itself had not been hurt at all.

Blackie.

His ice blue gaze swept briefly over Jet, who crouched weakly on the ground, injured and barely holding on.

Jet blinked up at him and gave a faint, tiny, feeble cry.

"Blackie..."

Blackie looked at him for several seconds, as though reassuring him.

Then, with the severed head still in his jaws, he suddenly spat it out.

It rolled away across the floor, dragging a long bloody streak behind it.

Blackie bared his blood-slick teeth at Colonel Barnes, a low, menacing growl rumbling in his throat.

Barnes was terrified now. One of his hands was still free, and he hurriedly snatched a knife from his belt and stabbed it towards Blackie.

"Ahhh!"

The blade never met flesh.

Instead, Blackie sank his teeth into Barnes's arm.

Then he jerked his neck hard.

Barnes's entire arm was torn clean off.

Blood sprayed everywhere.

The colonel screamed in agony and passed out on the spot.

"Protect the officer! Protect the officer!"

The soldiers who had been knocked over when Blackie charged them finally came back to themselves and staggered to their feet.

They had been with Colonel Barnes for years, yet none of them had ever seen real combat. Even after the apocalypse, they had remained safely under the protection of the Barnes family.

They had never encountered mutated creatures.

They had never truly suffered.

Faced with such a savage, bloody scene, they were terrified.

Their hands shook uncontrollably around their guns.

After tearing off the arm, Blackie gave his head two hard shakes, flinging blood in all directions.

When he finally went still, he stood there with the severed limb in his jaws, staring fixedly at the remaining soldiers.

Faced with the black muzzles of their guns, he showed no fear at all. If anything, there was a cold, almost mocking calm in his eyes.

With a soft thud, the arm dropped to the floor.

Blackie opened his blood red mouth wide at one of the soldiers.

And that soldier fired.

But the instant he pulled the trigger, he felt someone slammed into him from behind —a brutal sent him stumbling forward.

The shot went wild and struck Colonel Barnes, who was lying on the floor, straight in the shoulder.

The colonel, already unconscious, was jolted awake with a scream.

"Ahhh!"

He screamed, and the moment his eyes flew open, he found himself staring directly at the severed head on the floor.

The dead man's eyes were still open. The whites gleamed horribly, less than two inches from his face.

With remarkable cowardice, Colonel Barnes fainted again almost instantly.

The one who had arrived was Arthur Barnes.

In a few swift movements, Arthur kicked the guns from the soldiers' hands. His expression didn't even change as he took in the blood-soaked scene.

By then, Blackie had already bitten through the shoulder of the soldier who had tried to shoot him and hurled him away.

The man's head smashed against the wall, and he slid down without moving, his fate unclear.

By the time Cecilia reached the floor, this was the scene that greeted her.

The remaining soldiers had drawn knives from their waists and were facing Arthur and Blackie.

They were terrified out of their wits, backing away step by step, their legs trembling violently.

Blackie stood amid the blood, his eyes burned with savage fury, almost maddened in their viciousness.

At that moment, his whole presence was like that of a hellhound that had clawed its way out of the underworld.

Cecilia stood frozen in shock.

What had driven Blackie into such rage?

Close behind her, Colonel Reed arrived with several of his own soldiers.

After thinking it over, Colonel Reed had ultimately decided he could not stay out of itany longer.

"That bastard Jeremy Barnes cannot die in my shelter."

It had taken him a little while to come to terms with it, but once he did, he hurried up after them.

However much of a scoundrel Jeremy Barnes was, however much he deserved to die, the man absolutely could not die inside a shelter under Thomas's command.

If he did, the consequences for Thomas Reed and the old general would be catastrophic.

The moment Thomas understood that, he had rushed up at last to intervene.

Who could have imagined that what he would walk into was a scene straight out of hell.

The great black dog was drenched in blood, facing down the young soldiers before him, while Colonel Barnes lay sprawled on the floor, neither dead nor fully alive.
