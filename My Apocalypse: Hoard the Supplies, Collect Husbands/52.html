Chapter 52 Peaceful Days in a Turbulent Era

Bryan was so terrified that he scrambled away, stumbling and crawling down the stairs. After descending one flight, he turned in the direction of Blackie and spat viciously, "You damned dog, I'll turn you into dog meatballs sooner or later!"

Bryan left, but he had already formed a malicious idea.

All of this was also seen through by Cecilia.

She walked up to Blackie and gently stroked his neck. "You're such a good boy for Mom."

She frowned as she watched the direction where Bryan disappeared—just as she suspected, Bryan was definitely not a good person.

A storm is brewing now!

After working all afternoon, Cecilia admired Arthur more and more.

He was skilled in his movements, had a complete set of tools, and could even lift two or three of those heavy iron doors weighing dozens of pounds by himself.

"If we can't be friends with him, we must kill him first. Otherwise, he'll be a ticking time bomb later."

Cecilia mentally assessed Arthur's combat ability and came to the following conclusion.

She was absolutely certain that even if she and Blackie teamed up, they would definitely be no match for him.

The two of them worked hard the entire afternoon, installing two large iron doors at the stairwell entrance and fitting the largest, sturdiest iron door at the eastern entryway.

On the iron door, they also fastened two heavy-duty padlocks, which were found in other rooms.

This looks much safer now.

Cecilia stood at the doorway, stroking her chin, very satisfied with the afternoon's work.

At the same time, Cecilia heard her stomach let out a low groan.

Immediately after, another stomach groan echoed from behind, even louder than Cecilia's!

Cecilia turned around and saw that it was actually Blackie who made the sound!

Blackie was hanging his head low, his eyes rolled upward, showing crescent-shaped whites, looking at Cecilia with a pitiful expression.

Only then did Cecilia realize—not only had she not eaten, but she had also forgotten to feed Blackie.

My fault, my fault.

"Ms. Summers, if it's convenient, you could come to my place for a meal."

Arthur stood at his doorway, extending an invitation to Cecilia.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. "Mr. Barnes, you should know it's the end of the world now, right? Inviting someone to your home is a very dangerous thing."

Arthur smiled confidently, "I've observed all the residents in this building, and I'm confident no one can harm me."

That is truly immense confidence!

To be honest, after spending the afternoon with Arthur, Cecilia felt Arthur was no ordinary person. At the very least, he wasn't just some regular discharged conscript.

He was incredibly strong, with great explosive power, and after doing so much physical labor all afternoon, he wasn't tired at all.

Moreover, he knew how to use all kinds of tools, and he used them with extreme proficiency.

Once, in an empty room, Cecilia saw a chainsaw.

Arthur picked it up, glanced at the brand, started it right away, and operated it effortlessly.

However, Arthur's discharge papers stated "conscript."

Judging by Arthur's age, Cecilia thought he should be over 25.

The age for conscription in this country is 18 to 22, with a service term of two years.

So based on age, Cecilia felt Arthur couldn't possibly be a discharged conscript either.

But his documents clearly stated just that.

Moreover, Cecilia had just glanced at them. His discharge date was actually this June.

What type of soldier gets discharged in June?

Cecilia kept all these points in mind, planning to learn more later, but she also felt an additional layer of wariness toward Arthur.

Therefore, she declined the invitation to eat at Arthur's place. "Sorry, I'm not in the habit of eating with strangers. Maybe when we're more familiar."

Arthur rather appreciated Cecilia's straightforward nature and didn't press further, just nodding. "Then see you after the meal."

"Okay, see you then."

With the doors installed, it was time to move the supplies from those unoccupied rooms.

After returning home, Cecilia closed the door securely, took out a pork chop, some vegetable soup, and a bowl of pasta. She also got some dog food and a can of dog food for Blackie.

The Space had preservation capabilities.

Cecilia had stored all these items in the Space immediately at the time, so they were still warm when taken out now.

Even the white sesame seeds on the fried pork chop emitted a crispy aroma.

Cecilia took a deep breath, feeling instantly revitalized.

"Eating at someone else's house is never as satisfying as eating at your own, right?"

Arthur clearly seemed like a rough, tough guy. Cecilia didn't believe he could currently produce fragrant steamed pasta, freshly fried pork chops, or well-seasoned vegetable soup at his place.

Especially since Cecilia remembered Arthur's preference when gathering supplies—he loved spicy food to the extreme.

Although Cecilia also liked spicy food, she wasn't that addicted to it, so she decided not to challenge her stomach.

Today, the acid rain was still falling.

Cecilia and Blackie stood by the floor-to-ceiling window. Cecilia held her lunchbox, eating her meal, while Blackie lay on the floor eating from his can.

Jet was also let out, flying around before finally picking up a pinecone Cecilia gave him and going off to nibble on it.

At this moment, amidst the apocalypse, Cecilia actually felt a sense of peace and tranquility. It was as if on a deserted island, she had a warm little room that gave her a full sense of security.

She sat on the floor and hugged Blackie beside her.

Blackie, busy with his canned food, lifted his head somewhat disdainfully, looking at Cecilia as if to say—What do you want?
