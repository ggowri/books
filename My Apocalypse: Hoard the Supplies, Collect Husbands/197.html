Chapter 197 Snowy's Lesson

Taking the opportunity, Cecilia had a closer look at Snowy.

Yes, definitely a little female dog.

Expressionless, Arthur picked up the unfortunate creature, whose backside was still smeared with muck, said goodbye to Cici, and took Blaise Hart back inside with him.

Cici returned to her own flat with Blackie, carefully avoiding the trail of filthy paw prints Snowy had left all along the corridor.

A little while later, though, she came back out carrying a mop and cloths, intending to clean the hallway up properly.

After all, Arthur had been the one who cleaned away all the blood in the corridor yesterday.

By the time she finished and was about to head back in for some sleep, Arthur's door opened again.

Arthur stepped out looking exhausted. Silver was perched on his shoulder, and at his feet was Snowy, who the moment she came out seemed intent on bolting off at full speed.

She did not get far.

Arthur caught her by the scruff at once.

"Cecilia, how did Blackie learn to use the toilet?"

Cici had not expected that to be the very first thing out of his mouth.

Then she looked at Snowy in his hand.

She was stunned.

That wretched little thing, hanging from Arthur's grip by the scruff of her neck, was actually peeing.

Directly onto Arthur's shoe.

Arthur's face turned cold in an instant.

"I've got cat litter at home. She can learn to use that. But she's still very young, so I doubt she can hold it properly yet."

Cici had raised dogs before. Snowy looked to be only about three months old.

Before five months, puppies were rather like very young children. Their bodies had not fully developed yet, so accidents happened without much warning.

But then Cici suddenly thought of something.

She called Blackie over and spent quite a while talking to him, apparently wondering whether he might be able to explain things to Snowy.

Blackie approached Snowy with obvious distaste, bared his teeth at her, and grumbled at length in a low rumble.

And then…

Snowy let out something that sounded remarkably like a wolf howl.

As if she objected.

Blackie's face drooped at once, and he smacked her with one paw.

Snowy became obedient immediately.

Then Blackie turned around and walked back inside, giving every impression of a hero who had done his work and asked for no praise.

Cici cleared her throat. "Er… I think that's sorted."

She handed Arthur two bags of cat litter and told him how to use them.

Arthur nodded, looking noticeably relieved.

As it turned out, he later said that although Snowy still could not always hold it in, she had at least learned to go to the litter on purpose, and the flat was suddenly much cleaner.

Just as Cici thought everything was settled and was about to return to her own place, Arthur called after her.

"Cecilia."

She turned round and saw him lift a hand, lightly moving the dimension ring on his finger.

"I picked up quite a few useful things today. I'd like to exchange some of them for medicine for injuries."

Cici raised an eyebrow.

She had to admit, Arthur really had not disappointed her.

She had thought earlier that a base as large as that one would have made anyone weep if she had gone in there with the Space.

She had not expected Arthur to do exactly that.

According to Arthur, he had taken an entire boiler house, which he could trade to Colonel Reed.

He had also taken a power station, including several large generators. He had no immediate use for them, but intended to keep them.

On top of that, he had emptied an entire armoury, taking every firearm in the organisation.

Of course, blowing up those places had also been a way of concealing the fact that he possessed a dimension ring.

To make it convincing, Arthur had even filled those buildings with petrol beforehand, and in the weapons store he had left behind some ammunition and grenades, just so people would hear the explosions of the weapons going off.

In reality, everything valuable inside had already been taken clean out.

He had even locked the doors tight so nobody could get in and notice anything suspicious.

After hearing all that, Cici could only give him a thumbs up.

Brilliant.

Absolutely brilliant.

The medicine Arthur wanted was for Blaise.

He knew about Cici's arrangement with the old general, and he also knew the wound medicine she had access to was extremely effective.

To trade for it, Arthur laid out exactly what he had obtained and said, "Take whatever you want from it. As long as Blaise recovers. I want him properly well again, full of energy."

"All right. But I need to look at his injuries first."

Arthur nodded, opened the door, and let her in.

When Cici entered, she found that Blaise was already asleep.

He was only seventeen, still slight of build, and lying there on Arthur's sofa, he looked very small.

Still a child, really.

She stepped forward and examined his injuries, finding them severe, with the fever still running high.

Perhaps because he was finally in Arthur's home, Blaise had relaxed completely and fallen into a very deep sleep.

Even with Cici examining him like this, he did not wake.

Silver stood quietly nearby, head tilted, watching with bright little eyes full of worry.

When Cici had come into Arthur's flat earlier, Jet had followed her in.

Now he was perched not far away, staring at Silver.

The look in his eyes was rather difficult to describe.

Cici had the strong feeling that if she had not already told Jet not to speak in front of other people, he would have been over there chatting away to Silver already.

"Wait here a moment. I'm going back to fetch some medicine."

Cici said this to Arthur.

Arthur nodded.

But just as she was about to leave, she discovered that Jet had suddenly become awkward and utterly unwilling to go.

His eyes kept darting towards Silver.

Cici would have had to be blind not to understand at that point.

Jet had taken a fancy to Silver.

Sure enough, when she forcibly dragged him out of the door, the moment he crossed the threshold he started protesting, pretending his injured wing hurt, and let out two loud, pitiful squawks.
