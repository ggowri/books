Chapter 190 Jeremy Barnes Falls

Nathan Barnes sat in the car, smoking, wondering whether the Barnes family might be about to get their hands on another Space.

He already knew that the one they had been debating whether to hand over had been stolen.

He also knew the family was now in very serious trouble.

So he waited below in a state of taut anxiety, hoping for good news.

What he got instead was the sight of several soldiers carrying down his father's corpse.

"Who killed my father?"

The moment Nathan saw the body, he leapt from the car.

His eyes were blood red as he glared at the two soldiers carrying it.

Then he lashed out, kicking one of them hard in the stomach.

The soldier clenched his teeth, tears still gathered at the corners of his eyes, and said bitterly, "It was Cecilia from the sixty sixth floor. She fired the shot. But Colonel Reed allowed it. Brother Nathan, we can't just let this go!"

Nathan dropped to his knees and pulled back the cloth wrapped around his father's body.

Then he saw the full horror of Jeremy Barnes's death.

For a long while, he said nothing.

At last, he lifted his head in fury. His eyes were scarlet, but there was not a single tear in them.

"So. Cecilia. Thomas. You dare cut off the Barnes family's road to survival."

Mabel was stunned when she heard this. She had never imagined that even someone like Colonel Barnes, a figure she had always thought untouchable, could be killed by Cecilia.

But very quickly, another thought occurred to her.

Cecilia had made a catastrophic mistake.

Summoning her courage, Mabel hurried to Nathan's side and said, "Mr. Barnes, Cecilia has gone mad. This is the murder of your father. You can't let it go unavenged."

She had barely finished the sentence before Nathan struck her across the face.

Mabel was sent sprawling to the ground. The thin clothes she was wearing tore further, and cold wind immediately rushed through the opening.

But she did not dare move.

Nathan was truly enraged now.

"Hmph. What, you want me to go upstairs and die as well? My father didn't come back alive. If I go up there now, I'm only offering myself up too. Use your brain."

Mabel hurriedly dropped to her knees and slapped her own face, babbling that she had not thought it through and had only been angry on behalf of the Barnes family and Nathan himself.

Nathan cast one last cold look at the apartment building. Grinding his teeth, he ignored the woman on the ground and called to the others beside him.

"Move. We're going back to the Barnes family."

With Jeremy dead, the family would descend into chaos.

On top of that, they still had not found the spatial gemstone, and they had no idea how they were supposed to explain matters to the people above them.

He had to get back immediately and start dealing with the fallout.

"Revenge can wait."

Nathan clenched his fists tightly, ordered everyone into the vehicles, then turned and left.

Mabel climbed in as well, her fists balled tight, silently cursing Cecilia over and over in her heart.

But beneath the curses, she was afraid.

If someone was only a little stronger than you, you might envy them, even scheme against them.

But if someone was far stronger than you, what remained was only fear.

Jeremy Barnes's death gave Mabel a moment of dreadful clarity.

Had she provoked someone she should never have touched?

After all, even Colonel Barnes had always seemed terrifying to her. Yet Cecilia had killed him just like that.

And Nathan had not even dared go upstairs.

For the first time, Mabel truly regretted everything.

...

Meanwhile, after Colonel Barnes's body had been taken away, Cecilia went back inside first to tend to Jet's wound.

She had lived through gunshot injuries in her previous life, so the sight of one was not unfamiliar.

But after being shot, Jet had turned into the most pitiful, clingy little parrot imaginable.

Cecilia was already moving as gently as she could, intending to use tweezers to remove the bullet first.

Yet the moment the tweezers so much as touched the wound, Jet began shrieking at the top of his lungs.

"It hurts, it hurts, it hurts!"

Cecilia could only make her movements even softer.

But Jet really did seem to be terribly afraid of pain.

Her hands were already feather light, yet he kept crying out, louder and louder, practically tearing his throat raw.

"All right, all right. Eat this instead. Don't look."

In the end, Cecilia grew so flustered she had no choice but to fetch something tasty from the Space and hold it up to Jet's beak.

Since she was sitting on the sofa and the height was awkward, she borrowed Blackie's head as well.

She set a whole plate of parrot treats on top of Blackie's broad forehead and let Jet crane his neck to nibble at them.

Then, while Jet was distracted, she darted the tweezers swiftly into his wing and pulled the bullet free.

"It hurts. Waaaah. Blackie, the human, it hurts so much!"

At last the bullet came out. Jet gave one final enormous shriek, then settled down again.

Naturally, by that point he had also finished every last treat from the plate on Blackie's head.

Cecilia wiped the sweat from her brow and quickly poured Magic Spring water over the injured wing.

The wound began healing at a speed visible to the naked eye.

Only the feathers around the injury had not fully grown back yet.

"Try moving it. See how it feels."

Cecilia gently stroked Jet's head.

Jet hopped down from her lap, still letting one wing droop as he walked a couple of steps across the floor.

Then he lifted his little head and looked at his human.

"It still hurts a bit."

Cecilia supposed the tissues inside had been damaged. The skin and flesh had healed, but the muscles would still need time to recover, so she did not think too much of it.

She comforted him, telling him not to be afraid, that he would definitely get better, and that later he could even have another Magic Spring bath.

But Jet said nothing.

He only looked at Cecilia with mournful, pitiful eyes.

What Cecilia had not expected was that once Jet had finished looking at her, Blackie began looking at her too.

He even stood up.
