Chapter 142 We Need to Talk

Cecilia wanted to cry.

But what could she do? Keep feeding him, that's what.

This time, she prepared three more huge pots.

One of kibble. One of boiled meat. One of pet bread.

Blackie dove right back in, devouring everything with gusto.

Minutes later? Done.

Cecilia refilled.

This time, the moment she finished adding meat to one pot, Blackie planted his paw on it and stared at her, mouth open.

Cecilia got it. Finally relaxed a little. He's almost full.

Sure enough, after finishing that pot, Blackie padded over and flopped down, looking totally content.

But Cecilia? Her heart ached.

He'd just used his ability a bit, pulled the cart a few times, and came back to demolish twenty pots of food!

How was she supposed to afford this?!

And the Space—didn't it say those snacks would restore abilities?

Where was the damn effect?

Suddenly, a horrifying thought hit her.

She stared at Blackie, who was half-asleep and blissed out.

The Space never lied. Its products always delivered.

So if Blackie hadn't eaten Space-produced food... would he have eaten even more?

Cecilia, sitting by the warm stove, shivered.

Then her face crumpled.

"Blackie eats way too much."

"I'm gonna go broke."

"Should've stockpiled more dog food and meat back then."

She'd barely finished complaining when a big black paw reached over.

She froze. Blackie's face was drooping, looking somewhat reluctant.

She felt a flash of irritation. What, I can't even complain now?

But then she noticed—Blackie had a string wrapped around his paw. And that string looked... familiar.

Cecilia flipped his paw over and found the purple dimension pendant dangling from it.

"You... you're giving the pendant back to me?"

Cecilia was floored!

She dripped her blood into the Space and confirmed it—Blackie had severed his connection with the pendant.

For the first time, she saw how much Blackie had hauled in during that mission.

It was packed to the brim. All meat!

Stacked layer upon layer, it nearly filled the entire Space!

All the meat from those four floors—Blackie had stuffed it all in.

Cecilia's heart soared. Perfect. Now she had a real stockpile.

"Blackie, you're amazing!"

She threw her arms around his neck and planted two happy kisses on his face.

Just then, Jet flew over and perched on Blackie's head, twisting his neck to angle his face toward Cecilia.

"I want kisses too! I want kisses too!"

Cecilia was in such a good mood, she scooped up Jet and gave his smug little face two kisses as well.

Jet flew two victory laps around the room like he'd just won the lottery.

Cecilia tucked the pendant away, then looked at Blackie—who was acting like nothing had happened—and her eyes narrowed thoughtfully.

Blackie seems... different today.

Sure, he was still proud. Still messing with her. But his tail was wagging more. And the way he looked at her was noticeably clingier than before.

Plus, he'd given her back the Space!

Cecilia realized something—this trip, she'd saved Blackie's ass multiple times.

And in a fight? Their teamwork had been flawless.

So... did Blackie like her more now?

Or had he actually gotten attached?

Cecilia had always known Blackie wasn't some easy-to-tame dog. He had his own temper, his own ideas.

Their relationship had always been half owner and pet, half partners.

But today felt different.

Today she could feel it. Blackie had truly given her his heart.

Just as Cecilia suspected.

That night, she could feel Blackie sleeping way closer than usual.

The downside? Cecilia woke up with Blackie's ass planted on her face.

"Blackie, get off me."

Half-asleep, she shoved him away.

Blackie blinked groggily, looking just as confused.

But he obediently scooted over to the other side.

Problem was—his sleeping habits were terrible.

Cecilia's bed was huge. Normally, there was plenty of room for her, Blackie, and Jet.

After all, Blackie used to sleep at her feet.

But now that he wanted to be closer to her, especially when he was dreaming and his legs kept twitching, he kept waking her up.

Cecilia was at her wit's end. Was this what they called a sweet burden?

Just to get some decent sleep, she had to shove Blackie further away.

He was not happy. His face drooped, eyes rolling dramatically at her.

You don't want me!

Seeing that pitiful look, Cecilia spent another half hour coaxing him.

Finally, the dramatic dog huffed and trotted back to the foot of the bed.

After a chaotic night, Cecilia slept in until late morning.

Once she woke up, ate breakfast, and fed the two troublemakers, she was about to do some stretches when a loudspeaker blared outside.

"Cecilia. Arthur. Hailey. Come out. We need to talk."
