Chapter 98 The Guns

When Arthur spotted the gun in Cecilia's hand, his eyebrow arched in a silent question—where did you get that?

Cecilia didn't answer, just smiled. "Miranda is definitely coming for us. I'd like you to be my bodyguard for a while."

She turned to Hailey. "You should stay inside and lay low. They're armed."

Hailey simply nodded, understanding the urgency without needing explanation.

"I don't need your gun." Cecilia blinked, caught off guard by Arthur's response.

Before she could ask why, Arthur turned and disappeared into his bedroom. When he emerged, he was carrying a submachine gun that made Cecilia's handgun look like a toy.

"I've got my own firepower. Keep yours."

Cecilia stared at the man in front of her, eyes narrowing with intrigue. What was his deal? Even she didn't have access to weapons like that.

Her gaze drifted to the bedroom door Arthur had just exited. She noticed he'd casually locked it behind him.

Something told her that room held even more "surprises."

Meanwhile, Rebecca was spiraling into panic, clutching Andy's arm. "Andy, we should run! There's no one downstairs right now—no one would notice us leaving. I've got a really bad feeling about this. Something's about to go wrong."

The moment Miranda and her crew entered the building, Cecilia had gone into full preparation mode. Knowing they were armed, she'd slipped into her room and retrieved a bulletproof vest from the Space, strapping it on beneath her clothes.

When Arthur saw her emerge wearing body armor, his eyebrow quirked again. "Ms. Summers, you're far more mysterious than I imagined."

Cecilia ignored him—she could say the exact same thing about him.

Arthur chuckled and went back to his room again, returning with two more vests. He put one on and handed the other to Hailey.

Finally, Cecilia understood why Arthur had left all that gym equipment in the hallway on his first day. His room was clearly packed with far more interesting "inventory."

Cecilia's plan was simple: have Arthur and Hailey help her intimidate whoever came knocking. But she had one more trick up her sleeve.

Grinning mischievously, she grabbed a bucket of water and headed to the stairwell, dumping it down the steps.

In the near-thirty-below temperature, the water froze instantly into a treacherous sheet of ice.

"Miranda," Cecilia murmured with a cold smile, "I hope you enjoy my little gift."

But Miranda's arrival took longer than expected. The trio stayed warm inside, continuing their hot pot feast. After dinner, Cecilia pretended to grab something from her room but actually retrieved snacks from the Space as "dessert."

Despite the storm brewing, they weren't particularly tense. After all, they had Blackie as their early warning system.

Since his evolution, Blackie's hearing had become incredibly sharp, detecting sounds from great distances.

Cecilia couldn't help but smirk thinking about Madam Barnes—nearly eighty years old—having to climb sixty-six flights of stairs. The woman was probably taking breaks every few floors.

After about an hour, Blackie suddenly stood alert, staring intensely at the door.

Then came the sound of chaos from outside—crashes, bangs, and furious cursing.

Someone had clearly hit the ice and taken a tumble.

Cecilia shot meaningful looks at Hailey and Arthur. "They're here."

By the time they reached the door, someone was pounding violently on the other side.

BANG! BANG! BANG!

Then came Miranda' rage-filled voice. "Cecilia, you little bitch! Open this door! Did you kill my grandson?!"

Cecilia glanced at her companions. Arthur held his handgun ready, while Hailey gripped hers—though Cecilia had deliberately left Hailey's unloaded. Firearms required training to avoid accidents.

But their intimidating stance on either side of the door was already plenty menacing.

Miranda's arthritis flared up on the climb, so Frank, her security chief, ended up carrying her.

Frank had been doing fine until they reached the top floor—then he'd slipped on the ice and crashed down, his muscular frame slamming into her and nearly knocking her unconscious.

Her chest still ached from the impact.

Seeing the puddle of frozen water on the stairs, Miranda had nearly fainted with rage. This was clearly a trap!

"Cecilia, you filthy whore! Are you trying to kill me?! The Barnes won't let this slide!"

Cecilia peered through the peephole, locating Miranda's position. Then she yanked the door open and pressed her gun directly against the old woman's forehead.

Miranda's violent coughing stopped instantly. She froze, stunned into silence.

"Madam Barnes," Cecilia said sweetly, "long time no see. You're looking surprisingly spry for your age—plenty of energy for hurling insults."

"Cecilia, you—!"

Several guns immediately swung toward Cecilia.

She faced the armed men fearlessly, especially Frank. "Gentlemen, I wouldn't wave those around if I were you. My hand might slip, and then your boss would be taking a permanent nap."
