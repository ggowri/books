Chapter 191 After the Bloodshed

Those two little creatures were staring at her so intently that it made her skin crawl.

At last, Cici asked, "What is it?"

"Blackie says he wants something to eat."

The moment Jet finished speaking, Cecilia saw Blackie shoot him a vicious glare.

Blackie's face fell at once.

Then he lifted a paw and pointed at Jet as if accusing him, plainly meaning, The one who wants food is him, not me.

But the second Blackie made his accusation, his stomach let out an enormous, miserable growl.

He was hungry.

Taking advantage of the fact that he was injured and knew Blackie would not do anything to him, Jet immediately cried out, "Blackie wants food. I'll just have a bit of soup."

Yet the bright little eyes darting about gave him away completely. They were clearly saying, Human, I did well today, didn't I? Heroes deserve extra food.

Cici rolled her eyes. "Oh, enough. You both just want food."

Whenever these two started begging for food, it gave her a headache.

Blackie was the worst. Every time he went out with her and used his superpower, his stomach turned into a bottomless pit.

Still, Jet needed proper nourishment, and Blackie was obviously starving as well. Without another word, she set down two pots, one large and one small—each get their own share.

While the pair were eating with enormous enthusiasm, Cici heard a knock at the door.

She opened it to find Arthur standing outside, along with a front entrance that was now completely clean and tidy.

After Colonel Reed had arranged for the bodies outside to be removed, blood had still been everywhere: on the door, the floor, even the walls.

Cici had meant to clean it herself after tending to Jet's wound.

But now the entire entrance had been scrubbed spotless.

Not only that, even the wall had been repainted.

Arthur still had a smudge of white plaster dust on his face.

She understood at once.

"Thank you."

Arthur looked at her quietly.

While he had been outside earlier, he had faintly overheard her interacting with Blackie and that parrot.

He could only make out a few vague words here and there. In fairness, he had to admit the soundproofing in Cecilia' flat was rather good.

Still, the lift in her voice, and the parrot's loud, wheedling cries, had struck him as unexpectedly amusing.

He had always thought of Cici as someone serious, guarded, someone who rarely let her voice soften. They had known each other for quite some time now, yet she still liked to keep him at arm's length.

Though, given the state of the world now, that caution made sense.

So hearing her speak in that lighter tone for the first time felt strangely new.

"Be careful when you go out over the next few days. The wall will need a bit of time to fully dry."

That was why Arthur had knocked.

Once he had said his piece, he gave her a small wave and told her to get some rest.

After all, later that night, they still had to go and rescue their teammate, Blaise Hart.

With that, Arthur returned to his own place, leaving Cici standing alone in the corridor, looking at the freshly painted wall and the spotless floor.

The corner of her mouth lifted slightly.

He had cleaned it so thoroughly that it looked brand new.

But when she turned back inside, she marched straight over to Blackie and tugged one of his ears.

"Blackie, there was someone outside the whole time. Why did you not warn me?"

Blackie kept gobbling down his meatloaf without even lifting his eyelids.

In his heart, he was thinking, It was only Arthur. Not a threat.

Naturally, Cici had no idea what was going through his mind. She went into the bathroom to prepare some Magic Spring water.

She was planning a warm soak for the three of them: Blackie, herself and Jet.

It would help Jet's injury heal faster, and it would also restore their strength.

By the time she woke from a nap in the Magic Spring water, it was already eleven o'clock at night.

Since she had placed a heating pad beneath the tub, the water had stayed pleasantly warm the entire time.

The first thing she did after waking was check Jet's wing. When she saw that a soft layer of down had already begun to grow back over it, she finally relaxed.

Feeling completely refreshed, she took out a bowl of braised beef noodles while still sitting in the bath, added two braised eggs, and quickly ate it all down.

Before leaving, she let Blackie have another full meal and tried to soothe Jet, who was also desperate to go out with them.

"Once you've healed properly, I'll take you with me, all right?"

Jet had done well this time, and Cici could see that, at his current level of intelligence, he was perfectly capable of following orders and keeping quiet when necessary.

But she was still worried about the wound on his wing, so she did not dare bring him along on this mission.

Jet wailed for quite some time, even trying to prove that his wing had already healed. When she had lifted him out of the bath earlier, he had still been crying that it "hurt", yet now he insisted on flying a few laps.

Unfortunately for him, the moment he took off, Blackie smacked him straight back down with one paw.

Blackie's face drooped as he fixed Jet with those sharp, icy blue eyes.

Three seconds later, Jet lowered his head in resignation.

"All right. I'll go next time."

Whatever Blackie had threatened him with, it had clearly worked.

"Be good. Stay home. We still need you here."

Cici gently stroked Jet's little head in consolation.

Jet immediately straightened up

"Don't worry. I'll guard the house."

And so Cici left home with Blackie at her side.

Outside, Arthur was already waiting for them.
