Chapter 220 The Broken Python Horn

Blackie had his paws clamped firmly over the python's vital spot.

The Golden Horned Python thrashed in fury, but most of its movements were pinned down by Blackie in his enlarged form.

At that moment, Blackie had grown almost as large as an articulated lorry, looming over the python like a mountain and pressing it hard to the ground.

Perched on the snake's head, Cecilia instantly pulled a huge power drill from the Space and hacked at the horn on top of the python's head with all her strength.

The Golden Horned Python began struggling even more violently. Blackie lowered his head and bit down hard on its body.

But the python's hide was unbelievably tough. Blackie's teeth could not even pierce its scales.

On the other side, Cecilia had already cut halfway through the horn on the python's head.

The python flicked its tongue furiously. Its fangs darkened, and a purple-black venom began to seep from them.

A foul stench spread through the air.

The moment Cecilia saw that, she knew things were going badly and quickened her movements at once.

At the same time, the python's struggles grew even more violent than before.

It fought with all its strength, shoving Blackie from side to side. Mouth gaping wide, it repeatedly tried to raise its head and sink those venomous fangs into the body of the great black dog beside it, but each attempt failed.

But as the struggle dragged on, Blackie was clearly weakening.

Maintaining such a huge form was simply too draining.

Cecilia noticed that Blackie was beginning to run out of strength as well, but fortunately, at that very moment, there came a sharp crack.

The horn on the Golden Horned Python's head broke free.

At the same instant, Cecilia grabbed hold of the horn and reached for Blackie beside her.

"Blackie!"

And just like that, the woman and dog vanished into the Space.

All that remained was the mad Golden Horned Python, darting wildly about the warehouse, searching desperately for the human and dog who had disappeared.

It raged for more than ten minutes without finding even the faintest trace of them.

Instead, its body grew weaker and weaker. It urgently needed to go out and hunt if it was going to recover its strength.

Inside the Space, Cecilia sat on the ground clutching the enormous python horn, breathing hard.

That python had been terrifying.

Its scales were practically indestructible, and it carried venom in its mouth.

Cecilia had tested it just now. Not even the power drill had managed to leave the slightest mark on its scales.

To kill it, she would have to break into the soft flesh inside its mouth.

That would not be easy for her.

Still, all in all, they had escaped without disaster, and she had obtained the horn.

The moment Cecilia entered the Space with it in her arms, Sprite immediately twined himself round her.

Those almond-shaped eyes were fixed on her, and he even fluttered a coquettish glance in her direction.

"Master~"

The voice was unbearably sweet.

His eyes stayed glued to the broken horn in Cecilia's hands, and he licked his pink tongue.

The meaning could not have been clearer.

That looks delicious. I want to eat it.

Cecilia gave Sprite a light tap on the head.

"You can't have this for now, and the Space can't absorb it either. Is there any way round that?"

Sprite instantly flew into a temper. One second he had been winding himself round Cecilia, tail swishing and body twisting, and the next he jumped off her altogether and stood there with his paws on his hips.

"Master! What do you mean by that? Are you actually trying to hide things from this Space?"

"The Space has already swallowed so much recently. If it eats this horn as well, it'll probably level up. And an upgrade right now would not be a good sign."

Cecilia still remembered that previous upgrades had sometimes caused the Space to shut down and seal itself off. The thought made her uneasy.

"Fine..."

Sprite's ears drooped. He hesitated for quite a while before finally gritting his teeth and telling Cecilia the method.

In fact, it was very simple.

All Cecilia had to do was firmly think that the item needed to be preserved for the time being and must not be given to the Space. The Space was still, after all, quite obedient to its owner's wishes.

Of course, if Cecilia gave no instructions at all and simply tossed something in, the Space would automatically assume, The master is feeding me, and gobble it up at once without the slightest hesitation.

Once Sprite had explained that, Cecilia stored the python horn inside the Space temporarily, then took Blackie and stepped back out.

The moment they emerged, she saw that the Golden Horned Python had left behind two things.

One was a few drops of venom on the ground.

The other was several scales that had fallen off during the fight.

Delighted, Cecilia stepped forward carefully and used a bottle to collect the venom before storing it in the Space.

The python's body was so massive that even a single drop of venom had a diameter of over twenty centimetres. Each one formed a sticky, revolting puddle on the ground.

Once collected, it amounted to about two small mineral water bottles' worth.

As for the scales, each one was the size of a washbasin, and there were seven in total.

Cecilia stored all of them in the Space as well, taking care to specify that neither the venom nor the scales was to have its energy absorbed.

After that, she gave the laboratory one final look and stripped it bare of everything worth taking.

At the far end of the laboratory, she found a small partitioned room that seemed to have served as the fat researcher's living quarters, around thirty square metres in size.

Sure enough, just as the man had claimed, there was a great deal of grain stored there.

Not only grain, but also various daily necessities.

Cecilia did not hesitate and stored it all away.

But among the supplies was one thing she did not recognise.

It was a tin with no label at all. After turning it over several times, she finally spotted a tiny character carved into the bottom.

Barnes.

Cecilia froze.

"The Barnes family... do they actually have their own canned food factory?"
