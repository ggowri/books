Chapter 140 Manipulation

He put on this slimy, sniveling act, sniffling mid-sentence like a pathetic baby. Made you want to slap him twice.

Logan was annoyed. He muttered under his breath, "How is it Cecilia and her crew again?"

******

At this moment, Cecilia had no idea about any of this.

She'd already made it back to the 66th floor. Arthur had hidden the vehicle and handed her the keys.

"The car I promised you."

Cecilia took the keys.

Huh. Trading a modified off-road vehicle for a top-tier RV? Pretty solid deal.

Soon, they'd divided up all the grain.

Hailey got her share and was grinning ear to ear.

But knowing Phoebe was still alive gnawed at her.

Cecilia glanced at Hailey and smiled. "There's plenty of time. As long as she's still in this building, you'll get your chance."

Hailey nodded, but secretly made a decision.

Cecilia could tell what she was thinking, but didn't say anything.

The grudge between Phoebe and Hailey? Hailey needed to settle that herself.

Cecilia wasn't some saint—she had no intention of stepping in to help.

After everything was divided up and Cecilia got home, she'd barely closed the door when Jet came rushing over.

"Human! Quick! Blackie's dying!"

Cecilia's heart lurched.

When they came upstairs earlier, he'd been strong as an ox—carrying three bags of grain on his own. How could he be dying now?

She'd just given Blackie another bowl of Magic Spring water!

Was he hurt that badly? Should she not have made him haul supplies?

But she'd clearly seen his wounds stop bleeding after drinking the spring water. They'd even started healing.

Cecilia bolted toward the bedroom where Blackie was.

At the bedroom door, Cecilia froze.

Blackie lay on the bed, eyes shut tight, looking miserable.

Her heart nearly stopped.

She lunged onto the bed, checking his wounds frantically.

She poured Magic Spring water over the stab wound, then dove into the Space, slathering healing ointment all over him like it was free.

Soon, the wounds closed up fast.

But Blackie still looked pitiful. Weak. Lifeless.

Cecilia panicked.

In this freezing Ice Stage weather, she was actually sweating from anxiety.

"Jet, ask Blackie what's wrong! Where does it hurt? I've tried everything I can think of!"

She turned to Jet—the only one who could talk to Blackie.

Right now she felt completely lost.

"Blackie says without six pots of meat, four bowls of kibble, fifteen cans of dog food, and six bags of treats, he's not getting up."

Jet rattled off the list like a menu.

Cecilia stood there, stunned.

Then it hit her. Her whole body shook with rage. She grabbed Blackie's ear and yanked him off the bed.

"BLACKIE! YOU! TRICKED ME! For FOOD?! You know how worried I was?!"

In that moment, she was truly furious.

Playing pranks with Jet, being a little bratty—fine. Whatever.

But faking his own death to scare her?

Maybe sensing Cecilia was actually pissed, Blackie rolled his eyes back and sat up timidly on the bed, then started nudging her with his massive head.

Cecilia ignored him, arms crossed, breathing hard.

Blackie then rested his head on her shoulder and started licking her neck.

It tickled like hell.

"You're hitting my ticklish spot—get off!"

Cecilia shoved his head away.

But Blackie acted like someone had cast a charm spell on him today. Tail wagging nonstop, determined to lick her face no matter what.

He kept hovering around her, trying to win her over.

Seeing Blackie like this—so rare—Cecilia's anger melted away.

But she still grabbed his ear for a lecture. That was non-negotiable.

And then there was Jet!

That little troublemaker went along with everything!

Whatever Blackie told him to say, he said it. Ganging up on her.

"Jet, you too. Get over here and stand right there. I'm teaching you both a lesson today."

Cecilia pointed to the side of the bed.

Jet's beady eyes darted around. He obediently hopped over, folded his wings, and squatted on the bed.

"Oh no, you're mad! So scary! But Blackie just told me if we pull this job off, he'll share some meat broth with me!"

"You two can mess around all you want—be a little rebellious, whatever! I'll take it as entertainment! But safety is not something you joke about, got it? You almost gave me a heart attack!"

Cecilia smacked the bed with each word.

She got it. After mutating, both Blackie and Jet had gotten smarter—more human. Their world had changed completely.

Like lower beings suddenly leveling up, seeing a higher plane of existence. That's when they started getting "ideas," wanting to run little "experiments."
