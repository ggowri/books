Chapter 202 Colonel Reed Mobilises

"The one who wounded him was Captain Palmer. The old general's men have already killed him."

Cecilia finally pieced the whole thing together. It was a lot to take in.

Still, just as Blaise had said, anyone who skipped a scene like this was hardly human. Cecilia was definitely going.

The door on the sixty-fifth floor had already been damaged.

Although Arthur had patched it up a little earlier, it still was not truly secure.

If Cecilia and the others all went out, the sixty-sixth floor would effectively be left empty.

Jet was still injured as well and in no state to be of much use, so in the end they had no choice but to leave Blaise behind.

Blaise was thoroughly unwilling. He kicked up a fuss and even tried acting cute with Arthur.

Cecilia truly got to witness the full force of a teenage boy trying to charm his way through something. He was even more dramatic than Jet.

"Come on, Arthur, let me go. It's only a flesh wound. Look, I don't even have a fever any more."

As he spoke, he grabbed Arthur's hand and tried to press it to his forehead.

Arthur pulled away with a cold expression.

Blaise tried again, reaching for Arthur's sleeve this time, blinking those big eyes like a cat.

But Arthur's face only grew darker the more Blaise pouted and pleaded.

In the end, Blaise tried turning to Cecilia instead.

"Cici, look..."

But before he could so much as lay a finger on her, Arthur shoved him back into the room with a stony face.

"You're injured, so rest properly. Stop making a fuss, or I'll thrash you again."

That finally quietened him down.

Cecilia and Arthur brought Blackie with them, and the two of them, along with the dog, set off fully prepared.

They first went down to the fortieth floor to find Colonel Reed, who was in the middle of arranging his forces.

When Cecilia knocked and entered, she heard him raging inside.

"What is this? Are the military doctors useless now? It's just an ordinary wound, so why hasn't the bleeding stopped yet? A pack of idiots. I'm going to the Barnes family right now and tearing the whole place apart to bury them with the old general."

So the old general still had not stopped bleeding. The wound kept reopening, and even the military doctors could do nothing.

The moment Colonel Reed saw Cecilia at the door, he sprang to his feet and strode towards her.

"Miss Summers, do you still have any of that wound medicine you gave the old general before? The last time he took it, he sent it all to the front line. Now that he's been injured, the bleeding simply won't stop. It's driving us mad. A round trip to the front line would take three or four days. We can't wait that long."

As he spoke, he kept reaching forward, looking very much as if he were about to grab Cecilia's hand.

Arthur stepped forward at once, quietly shielding her behind him as he intercepted Reed's hand.

"I still have some."

He meant the jar Cecilia had given him, the one he had used to treat Blaise's wounds. There was still some left.

The medicine had indeed worked wonders. Once Blaise used it, the bleeding had stopped very quickly.

Arthur had judged from the state of the injury that a few more applications would heal it completely, and the remaining medicine would be more than enough.

If he ever needed more later, he could always trade with Cecilia again.

Stepping in just now had been pure impulse.

When he saw Colonel Reed reaching for Cecilia's hand, he had moved before thinking.

As for why he had not wanted Reed touching her, he did not know himself.

He simply found that the sight of anyone else laying hands on Cecilia made him uncomfortable.

The moment Colonel Reed heard this, he brightened at once and seized Arthur's hand in both of his, shaking it enthusiastically.

"If this saves the old general's life, Thomas will make sure you're properly thanked."

Arthur calmly removed Reed's hand from his own. He did not much care for Reed's habit of grabbing people without warning, especially when it came so naturally every time with Cecilia.

"The wound medicine came from Cecilia. If you want to thank someone, thank her. I only came downstairs because I heard you were going after the Barnes family. I have a grudge against them, and I want to join your operation."

At the mention of the Barnes family, Colonel Reed ground his teeth in fury.

"That damned Barnes family really has got too bold. We've already captured the mercenaries they sent over. The evidence is ironclad. This is a declaration of war. Do they really think I, Thomas, wouldn't dare do anything? Bloody hell, it's the apocalypse already, Jeremy is dead, and they still think I won't touch them? Right, get everything ready. We move now."

Arthur went back upstairs, scraped out some of the medicine, put it into a small bottle, and handed it over to one of Reed's men.

After asking about the old general's wound, he concluded that the amount should be enough.

When Blaise saw Arthur return, he immediately started clamouring to go again.

Arthur did not waste a word. He simply walked to the corner and picked up a stick.

Blaise instantly crouched down with both hands over his head.

"All right, all right, I'm not making trouble. I'll stay home and rest properly. I'll even cook for you and Cici and wait for you to come back. Really, I'm not going."

It was obvious Blaise had been beaten by Arthur more than once before. He was genuinely afraid of him.

Arthur tossed the stick aside and headed downstairs.

Before leaving, he glanced at Blaise and delivered a dry verdict.

"You really are tough."

Once Colonel Reed had the medicine, he arranged for someone to take it to the old general.

Then he loaded up two vehicles with firearms, brought Cecilia and Arthur along, and the group set off in force towards the Barnes family estate.

At present, the entire Barnes family was living together in two large villas on high ground, which had not yet been flooded.

Word was that since the apocalypse, they had managed to ingratiate themselves with the military. The family had now installed electric fencing and built walls around the villas, living in remarkable comfort.

Colonel Reed's intentions could not have been clearer. He was taking a team straight in. Anyone who stirred up trouble would be killed on the spot. The rest would be sent to the rear district for forced labour and re-education.

By the end of the day, the Barnes family would be erased.
