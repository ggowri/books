Chapter 105 Back Down

Five or six boxes of charcoal. They even found a camping cart and loaded it all up.

But when they tried to leave, a group blocked the end of the hallway.

"Drop it. Leave the women. Maybe I'll let you live."

The leader—gun in hand—aimed it straight at them.

Cecilia's hand slid into her pocket, reaching for her own gun.

Then Arthur laughed. Cold and sharp.

Before Cecilia could react, he moved.

"D-don't come closer! I'll shoot!" the leader screamed. His lackeys scrambled into fighting stances.

Didn't matter. Arthur tore through them like a wolf in a sheep pen. Seconds—that's all it took.

The leader? Never fired a single shot.

Arthur walked up, kicked the gun from his hand, and stomped it to pieces.

Cecilia and Hailey caught up and realized—the gun was fake.

"Please, man, please! Just this once, I'm begging you!"

The leader crumbled, already on his knees. His guys trembled behind him.

Cecilia walked over, stared at the shattered prop gun, then looked at Arthur.

"How'd you know it was fake?"

"Obvious."

Cecilia narrowed her eyes but didn't push it.

These guys were all that was left in Tower B.

A bunch of unlucky bastards, really.

Tower B was mostly commercial—offices, cubicles, and conference rooms.

Before the acid rain hit, most companies sent employees home.

But their shitty company? Not a chance. They didn't let anyone leave. They made them work overtime.

That overtime trapped them in Tower B when hell broke loose.

At first, they didn't think much of it.

Sure, the power was out, but the guys were actually pretty happy—munching on company snacks, joking that they'd scored paid vacation.

Then the water stopped. The acid rain flooded the lower floors.

That was when panic set in.

But by then, it was too late to leave.

When the rescue boats came, they heard about it late. By the time they ran over, yelling and waving, the boats were full.

"We'll come back tomorrow," they had promised.

"But that night... two of our guys froze to death." The leader's voice cracked. "We were huddled together for warmth. How the hell did they die?"

The guy had loose, sagging skin—probably used to be a big dude before all this. The apocalypse had clearly melted the weight off him.

Cecilia and the others didn't want to get tangled up with these people.

"We're taking some firewood," Cecilia said flatly.

"No way! Everything in this building is ours! It's all we have left! If you take it, we'll starve—we'll freeze! Absolutely not!"

Arthur shot him a look.

The guy shut up.

In the end, they had no choice but to back down.

After hauling the charcoal away, Cecilia's group stripped the conference room bare—tables, chairs, everything. They piled it all in Tower A, then lugged it up to the 66th floor.

With this haul, they could coast for two or three months without scrounging for firewood again.

Tower B had furniture for days. Food, though? They'd searched two floors and found nothing. Those people had probably hidden it all.

Whatever. They weren't here for food anyway.

"They left all that charcoal sitting out in the open. Means they've got something better for heat." Hailey said it once they'd finished loading up.

Arthur and Cecilia agreed.

Cecilia had noticed—the leader kept bitching about losing the wood and charcoal, but he didn't actually seem upset.

It was a smokescreen.

Just as they were about to haul the last load upstairs, the entire group suddenly appeared at the edge of the walkway connecting Towers A and B.

Cecilia's crew instantly went on alert, forming a line.

But the group didn't approach. Instead, they shoved a massive cabinet toward the collapsed section.

Then two men with ropes tied around their waists climbed onto the cabinet and started jumping. Hard.

Cecilia watched, curious.

After a few jumps—CRACK.

The acid rain-weakened floor gave way.

The cabinet plunged straight down.

The roped men got yanked back to Tower B by the others.

Then they pushed another heavy cabinet to the edge...

Three times.

The bridge between Towers A and B was gone.

A gap of over thirty feet now separated the buildings.

The leader stood on the Tower B side, staring across at Cecilia.

"Ha! Fly over here if you've got the balls!"

Cecilia studied the group. Interesting.

"What's your name?" she called out.

"Dyson! Like the vacuum guy!" he shouted back.
