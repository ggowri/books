Chapter 213 The Black Dragon Helicopter

The woman recoiled at once, stumbling back several steps in fright.

After hauling the girl upright, Cecilia reached straight for the tightly fastened collar at her neck and undid it.

The girl let out a sharp scream.

Cecilia smiled.

No wonder she had not been afraid of the knife earlier.

Under the girl's padded coat, she was actually wearing a layer of steel undershirt.

It was nothing more than a thin sheet of metal fashioned into clothing and worn beneath the coat, completely invisible from the outside.

Even if Cecilia had slashed at her throat with a knife, she probably would not have cut through it unless the girl had been entirely unprotected.

"Blackie, tear open the coats on these madwomen and let's see what exactly they're hiding underneath."

At Cecilia's command, Blackie shot forward, pouncing on the loudest of the older women. In a few swift bites, he ripped open their padded coats.

Sure enough, every one of the women in clean new coats, the ones making the biggest scene, had a steel undershirt hidden beneath.

A chorus of shrieks broke out. Several women tried to run, but Blackie pinned them all to the floor before they got far.

In the blink of an eye, most of the crowd had retreated to the sixty-third floor.

In front of Cecilia, aside from the women sprawled on the ground and the girl still in her grip, an empty ring of space had opened up around them.

The young soldier saw what those women were wearing and seemed to understand at once. He looked at Cecilia.

"Ms. Summers, someone is targeting you."

Cecilia gave a cold snort. "Anyone who can pull off something like this is clearly no ordinary nobody."

Then she lifted her head and called out loudly to the people still lingering below to watch the spectacle.

"You've all seen it now. These people came prepared to cause trouble. Their clothes have been specially altered to protect them against knives from those of us on the sixty-sixth floor. That's why they dared come up here so confidently, trying to frame our boy."

"I don't know what the person behind this is after, but anyone who comes looking for trouble with us on the sixty-sixth floor is not going to enjoy the outcome."

With that, Cecilia kicked each of the women once and sent them sprawling towards the soldier standing on the floor below.

"Comrade, perhaps you could arrange some labour reform in the rear district for them."

The young soldier reacted at once. He snapped to attention, saluted, and called for his comrades below to come up and take the women away.

The girl Cecilia was still holding had long since turned pale with terror. She cried out loudly, "We only took someone else's supplies and did a job for them. We've been used. We're innocent!"

Cecilia seized her by the collar and flung her onto the ground.

"Then talk. Who told you to do this, and what was the point of it?"

The girl bit down hard on her lip, trembling all over as she spoke.

"It was... it was a middle-aged man, dressed all flashy and gaudy..."

As Cecilia listened to the description, she drew in a slow breath.

It sounded very much like Dyson.

And the idea of outfitting everyone with metal armour under their clothes was exactly the sort of ridiculous stunt that man might pull.

But what was he trying to do?

Just then Arthur came up behind her.

"It feels like a delaying tactic."

The moment he finished speaking, a roar of engines came from the direction of Tower B.

Cecilia hurried to the nearest window and looked out.

Above Tower B, a helicopter was descending.

Painted across it was a great black dragon, twisting wildly from the nose of the aircraft all the way to the tail.

The instant Arthur saw the emblem on the helicopter, his face darkened.

"We were careless."

None of them had imagined that this whole chain of events had simply been meant to keep Arthur from noticing the helicopter as it approached.

Cecilia and the others lived on the sixty-sixth floor. In the eerie silence of the apocalypse, they could hear everything. Forget the thunder of an approaching helicopter. Even the sound of a car starting up downstairs could be heard clearly from the top floor.

Because the world now was too quiet.

There were no entertainments, no pedestrians on the roads, no passing traffic, no televisions or computers humming in the background.

At times it felt as though even the sound of falling snow could be heard.

But just moments ago, all the shouting, screaming and commotion from those people had perfectly swallowed up the sound of the helicopter engine.

Looking out of the window, Cecilia saw several people disembark on the rooftop opposite after the helicopter landed.

A few others were already waiting there, as though receiving them.

The distance was too great to make out their faces, but among them she could see a woman with long hair whipping in the wind.

From her build, she looked very much like the leading woman from Tower B.

Those people seemed to notice the disturbance on Tower A's side. After exchanging a few words, they all moved together towards the edge facing Tower A.

Then the long-haired woman and one of the men who had stepped off the helicopter stopped right at the edge of Tower A's side and stood there without moving.

Arthur and Blaise walked to the window and looked up at the people across from them.

Blaise swore under his breath. "Bloody hell. No way. That old bastard from the Black Dragon Syndicate is still alive."

In this apocalypse, anyone who could still drive a car was already a formidable figure.

But the people opposite had a helicopter.

They left soon enough, and from Blaise Cecilia finally learned who the man was.

He belonged to another mercenary group, one hostile to the mercenary corps Arthur had once served with.

"If Arthur had spotted that helicopter earlier, he'd definitely have blasted it out of the sky. Damn it. This whole mess started because I went wandering about like an idiot."

Later, Blaise explained that the reason he had gone out in the first place was because, while cooking, he had heard people downstairs shouting about a mutant pigeon again and again for quite some time, until his curiosity had finally been hooked.
