Chapter 203 At the Barnes Estate

Cecilia rode in the same vehicle as Colonel Reed, with one of the young soldiers from his unit driving.

Colonel Reed sat in the front passenger seat, while Cecilia, Arthur, and Blackie took up the entire back row.

On the way, Colonel Reed kept swearing under his breath and listing all the things the Barnes family had done since the apocalypse began.

By all accounts, Miranda was a ruthless woman.

After the Barnes family rose to prominence through their connections with the military, Miranda gathered a group of people around her. Some were criminals with records, some were mercenaries, and some had worked as martial arts instructors.

She offered them benefits and rewards, turning them into the Barnes family's private enforcers and guards.

That was what had truly laid the foundation for the family's power.

There were even rumours that the firearms currently in the Barnes family's possession had come from Jeremy. Before the apocalypse, the military had been relocating a weapons depot, and he had secretly pocketed part of it. What was reported up the chain was that the shipment had been lost in an accident.

"I've also heard that old Miranda rounded up a batch of girls from an arts school and handed them over to that bastard Grant Cole. Otherwise, how do you think the organisation even the police nearly wiped themselves out trying to deal with was taken over by the Barnes family without them losing a single man? That old woman is rotten to the core."

Colonel Reed was smoking as he spoke, his voice edged with cold fury.

Cecilia knew some of this already.

In her previous life, Miranda had been exactly this kind of person.

For her own interests, there was nothing she would not do.

The organisation Grant belonged to had survived all the way into the Heat Stage in Cecilia's previous life. At the time, one girl had managed to escape and said she had been captured by Miranda and sold to Grant.

Beyond that, Reed said the Barnes family was also in charge of certain affairs in the shelter, and they had clashed with his side more than once.

The family lined their own pockets, took bribes, and filled positions they were wholly unfit for.

"Damn it, the shelter they manage is the one where the most people have died, at least from what I know. But have you seen Jeremy's wife? Fat as a pig, and she still cakes powder on her face. Makes my skin crawl just looking at her. And that fur coat Miranda wore before, I heard she stripped it off an ordinary person in the shelter. Had someone beat the poor bloke to death too."

"And Jeremy himself, he was slick as anything at flattering his superiors. Never did any proper work. He was a disgrace to the military, and I've hated the sight of him for ages."

Whenever the Barnes family came up, Cecilia could clearly see the disgust on Reed's face.

By the end of that stretch of road, having listened to it all for dozens of kilometres, Cecilia was furious.

If the Barnes family were allowed to survive, there really would be no justice left in the world.

But when they finally drove up to the Barnes family estate, they found the whole place surrounded by soldiers.

And standing at the entrance was a middle aged man in uniform, with the insignia of a senior colonel on his shoulders.

He outranked Colonel Reed by one grade.

In the military, one rank higher could crush a man flat.

The moment he saw Reed's vehicle arrive, he stepped forward at once.

"Thomas, are you trying to turn the sky upside down? Get out of the car."

Colonel Reed muttered a curse, but he still got out from the passenger side.

Cecilia and Arthur got out as well, along with the few soldiers Reed had brought.

"Colonel Ross." Reed saluted the man, though the salute was anything but willing. His whole expression had turned ugly.

"Thomas, General Mercer sent me to stand guard over the Barnes family. He knew full well you'd come here looking for trouble. If you want to lay a hand on them, shouldn't you first ask General Mercer what he thinks?"

Colonel Ross kept his face cold as he rebuked him.

Reed forced down his temper and said, "Colonel Ross, our old general is still lying in bed. The bleeding hasn't even stopped. His life is hanging by a thread. Do you understand that?"

Ross pressed him with sheer force of presence. "Wasn't the attacker already caught and executed?"

"The one who carried it out was. The one who ordered it hasn't been caught yet."

Reed lifted his chin and refused to back down.

Colonel Ross let out a sigh, clearly realising the matter would not be settled easily. He waved a hand, and one of his men dragged forward a thin, trembling boy from the back.

Cecilia did not recognise him, but she could see he was only in his teens, probably even younger than Blaise.

"This is the one. Jeremy's second son. After learning his father had been killed, he bribed Captain Palmer and had him go assassinate the old general."

The thin boy was Hayden Barnes, only fifteen this year. He had his lower lip clamped tight between his teeth, his whole body trembling with fear, yet he still forced himself to say to Colonel Reed, "That's right. I told Captain Palmer to kill that important official or whatever he was. And not just him. I'm going to kill Cecilia too, for murdering my father. And everyone else who's turned against the Barnes family. I'll kill every last one of them."

His voice lacked force. It sounded like lines someone else had coached him to say.

"Colonel Ross, do you take me for a fool? You've dragged out a child to take the blame for the Barnes family. Can he even explain clearly who the old general is? Does he understand any of what's going on here?"

Colonel Reed was so furious he stamped his foot.

But Colonel Ross refused to budge. He insisted this boy was the one from the Barnes family who had ordered the assassination attempt on the old general.

He told Reed to take his men and leave at once instead of lingering here making a scene.

By the end of it, Reed had lost patience and reached for his gun.

Ross responded with a single kick that sent him sprawling to the ground.

"Thomas, get a grip. I know the old general is like a father to you. But look at the world we're living in now. Do you think General Mercer favours the Barnes family for nothing? It's because they can get things done for us. If you really wipe out the Barnes family, you'll be chopping off one of General Mercer's arms. And Heldon City will descend into chaos as well."
