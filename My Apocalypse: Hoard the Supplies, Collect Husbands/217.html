Chapter 217 The Hidden Laboratory

The hen, however, was tiny, and looked rather frightened.

Cecilia thought for a moment, then gave her a bowl of Magic Spring water, silently thinking, I found you a wife, so perhaps you'll turn into a proper henpecked husband and stop trying to tear the Space apart.

She watched for a while. Although the rooster was enormous, he was surprisingly gentle with the little hen, fussing round her and doing his best to impress her.

That finally put Cecilia at ease.

Still thinking about the supplies outside, she hurried back out of the Space.

There was one final section left in this warehouse.

When she walked over, she found an entire row of red wine, along with various cigars, cigarettes, and all sorts of delicacies that even before the apocalypse would only have been enjoyed by the wealthy.

There was ginseng, deer antler, matsutake mushrooms, truffles, foie gras, caviar, and all kinds of coffee.

Cecilia even spotted several packages labelled civet coffee, which made the corner of her mouth twitch.

Nathan really had known how to enjoy himself.

In the end, there were more than a dozen large ginseng roots. From Cecilia's point of view, each of them looked to be several hundred years old, perfectly preserved, and highly restorative. Elderly figures in the upper ranks would probably prize them dearly.

Thinking of the old general's recent injuries, she decided that perhaps she could give him one of the roots.

There were roughly five or six boxes of deer antler, and even the boxes themselves looked expensive enough that they would have been much sought after before the apocalypse.

There were two crates each of matsutake, deer antler, and foie gras.

There were three crates of caviar.

Cecilia herself was rather fond of caviar. By her reckoning, however, it had been well over ten years since she had last eaten any. Once she got home today, she could finally open a tin and have a taste.

After storing all of it away, the warehouse was finally cleared out.

Without wasting a moment, Cecilia headed for the last warehouse.

Come to think of it, this warehouse must have been built after the Ice Stage began.

The materials used in its construction were exceptionally thick. Cecilia estimated them to be over forty centimetres deep.

She had no idea where Nathan had managed to find building materials this solid.

Looking at the two warehouses in front of her, she simply could not bear to leave behind structures this large, or materials this sturdy. With a wave of her hand, she stored both warehouses in the Space as well.

Not a single thing was to be wasted.

But as she approached the third warehouse, Blackie suddenly stepped forward and blocked her path.

Cecilia looked at the warehouse ahead. She had already felt that something about it was strange.

Because this one was not locked at all.

In fact, the door was even standing slightly ajar.

Which meant there was someone inside.

And standing at the entrance, Cecilia caught a bizarre smell drifting out from within.

It was like... countless heaps of excrement all mixed together, creating a stench that was both revolting and unnervingly strange.

Blended into it were also chemical smells, alcohol among them.

Cecilia drew a pistol from the Space and slowly pushed open the warehouse door, every muscle taut with caution.

But the person inside had apparently already realised someone was at the entrance.

A shot rang out towards the doorway.

The man's aim, however, was dreadful. Not only did he fail to hit Cecilia, but from the corner of her eye she saw the bullet strike the wall well off to one side.

By then, Cecilia had already stepped inside.

The instant she entered, she saw a figure.

It was a fat middle-aged man, balding, dressed in a white lab coat, clutching a gun in trembling hands as he stared at her.

This warehouse was far warmer than the other two. It was even warmer than the shelter itself, as though the stoves here were burning at full force.

"Y-you... who are you? This is Barnes family territory!"

The balding man stammered the question, his whole body shaking.

Cecilia's eyes swept swiftly across the room.

In front of her were rows of caged animals, as well as many animal corpses floating in huge jars, looking as though they had been dead for quite some time.

There were severed limbs and mutilated remains too, cut into all manner of forms and preserved in bottles.

Across the long laboratory tables, countless animals had been cut open, their four limbs bound, their organs removed.

Cecilia took only a brief look, but it was enough. There were two whole rows of such bodies, and every single one of them was a mutant creature.

Mutant rats, mutant dogs, mutant cats, mutant pigs, mutant cattle.

Almost every type of mutant animal imaginable was here.

Now they were all cold corpses, each having died in unspeakable agony. The sight was so horrific it bordered on inhuman.

So this was what Nathan had been doing.

He had been secretly having someone study mutant animals.

But the direction of that research was unbelievably bloody.

Cecilia even saw a mutant female dog that had been pregnant. The researchers had cut open her belly, removed the pups one by one, and laid them out on the table.

Those pups too had been lined up and dissected in turn.

The mother dog stared blankly at the ceiling, her eyes still open, with what looked almost like tears in them.

It was obvious how unwilling she had been to die.

"This is a laboratory?" Cecilia asked in astonishment.

The man did not fire again. Instead, he clenched his teeth and said, "That's right. A laboratory. It belongs to Nathan, young master of the Barnes family. I suggest you leave at once. The Barnes family will be sending people here soon."

Yet by the end of his threat, his voice had already lost most of its force.

Cecilia knew at once he was lying.

Up to this moment, he probably still had no idea that Nathan was dead, and that the Barnes family had already fallen.

He was likely one of the researchers Nathan had brought in, someone who had been living here for a long time.
