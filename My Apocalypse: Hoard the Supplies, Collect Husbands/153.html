Chapter 153 No Games

Arthur's face darkened. Without a word, he turned and stomped off to fiddle with the generator.

Hailey watched, trying not to laugh. She leaned close to Cecilia's ear.

"I saw Arthur grinding his teeth just now."

Cecilia bit back a smile, refusing to look at him. Such a competitive Mr. Barnes.

A moment later, a tall man in military uniform strode over—stern-faced, rank insignia gleaming on his shoulders.

"What the hell's going on here?"

This was Thomas, the newly appointed commanding officer.

Cecilia stayed quiet, watching to see how he'd handle it.

Before she could blink, the beady-eyed guy started whining.

"Sir, these people shocked us with an electric fence! And they've got power! They have a generator! Why do they get one and we don't? That thing should be government property!"

Then he rubbed his hands together with a greasy smile.

"Colonel Reed, since I'm the one who discovered the generator, shouldn't I get some credit? Maybe a position with the officials...?"

Thomas's frown deepened with every word. Finally, he kicked the guy square in the chest.

"Didn't we tell you? Sixty-fourth floor and above is private territory—nothing to do with the officials! You don't mess with anyone above sixty-four!"

He grabbed a nearby soldier and pointed at the troublemakers.

"These guys—take them for punishment. All of them."

Wails erupted immediately.

But Cecilia was satisfied. Not bad. He's reasonable. Richard sent the right man.

Cecilia smiled and extended her hand. "Hi, I'm Cecilia."

Thomas turned, gave them a crisp military salute, then shook her hand.

"Good to meet you all. I'm Thomas Reed. I'll be managing the shelter behind us. Actually, I was looking for you. Logan mentioned you have food supplies you want to trade?"

Cecilia nodded.

Thomas clapped her hand enthusiastically. "Perfect! We're running low. I've got time today—let's finalize this deal now."

Arthur cut in. "Colonel Reed, we can hand over the food anytime. But the supplies you promised us—are they ready?"

Thomas's excitement froze. "The pork's ready. Some refined flour and rice too. But some of the hydroponic equipment... we haven't secured that yet."

"Then I'm afraid we can't make the trade." Cecilia's tone was apologetic but firm.

Thomas panicked. "Wait, wait! The military won't screw you over, I promise! We just rescued a bunch more people—we're running on fumes here. I'll give you a written guarantee, how's that?"

"What about my two pigs?" Cecilia pressed.

"Already on the way."

Cecilia and the others huddled briefly, then agreed to accept Thomas's IOU.

After all, keeping the food stashed outside was risky. Better to let the military haul it away.

They locked up and followed Thomas out.

Blackie stayed behind to guard the place.

Cecilia had Thomas bring extra soldiers on purpose. But when she saw all that grain buried down there, her heart still ached.

After a few hours of digging, they hauled it all out.

Thanks to good storage, nothing was damaged. A few corn cobs got snapped in half, but they were still edible.

When Thomas saw bag after bag of grain, he grabbed Cecilia's hand, practically shaking with excitement.

"This is incredible! This is our lifeline! Logan said you know where to find machine tools?"

Arthur casually stepped over and gently took Thomas's hand—from Cecilia—and held it himself.

"I know where the machine tools are. I can take you."

Cecilia glanced at Arthur. He knows too?

Then again, with Arthur's computer-like memory, it made sense.

"Perfect! Let's go right now."

Thomas was all action, ready to move immediately.

Cecilia stopped him. "Colonel, we haven't talked terms yet. What's a production machine tool worth to you?"

Thomas hesitated. "Honestly? The military's pretty broke right now."

Cecilia: "..."

Thomas was nothing like Logan.

Decisive, ruthless when needed.

But when it came to negotiating? He played the sympathy card.

Too bad for him, Cecilia had Arthur—the king of playing dirty.

Arthur had literally played the victim just to snitch on Logan.

"Colonel, we're just three people scraping by. We're even more broke." Arthur's tone was innocent.

Thomas looked at Arthur. His expression was complicated.

"Fine. Let's cut the crap. What do you want? Anything but weapons—and from what the General told me, you don't need those anyway. You're all capable. We want long-term cooperation. No games."

Arthur glanced at Cecilia. "Ms. Summers, can I handle this one?"

Cecilia saw a spark in Arthur's eyes. Like he had the perfect idea.
