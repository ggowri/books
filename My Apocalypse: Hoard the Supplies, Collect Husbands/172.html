Chapter 172 Chaos Inside the Space

After the blizzard arrived, in just a few short weeks the snow had piled up high enough to bury ten entire floors.

Starting from the fourteenth floor, the building became even darker. It looked completely devoid of light.

Besides the headlamp she was wearing, Cecilia took out another flashlight.

Blackie stayed close beside her.

Cecilia constantly observed Blackie's expressions.

After bringing Blackie out so many times, she had already learned to roughly understand his changes in mood.

If he sniffed around everywhere or raised his ears, it meant he sensed danger nearby but could not locate its source.

If he stood directly in front of Cecilia with bared teeth and low growls, it usually meant the threat was significant and he was preparing to protect her.

If he roared and charged forward, it meant he thought the enemy was nothing serious and could be killed with a few bites.

At the moment, however, Blackie looked relaxed, which meant there was probably no danger nearby.

What delighted Cecilia was that from the fourteenth floor downward, the goods were no longer products being exported overseas.

Instead, they were imports from abroad.

Among them were many things she loved to eat, such as chocolate, Danish butter cookies, purple toffee candies, and fish sausages.

There were even some foreign candies she had searched everywhere for before the apocalypse, ordering them online but never receiving them because the apocalypse arrived early.

Without hesitation, Cecilia immediately tore open a package.

The candies were soft and sweet. She grabbed two and tossed them into her mouth.

Instantly, a wave of happiness flooded through her.

Including the ten years she had survived in the apocalypse in her previous life, she had not eaten anything like this for over a decade.

There were also cookie biscuits, honey flavored potato chips, freeze dried strawberries, buttery crackers, and many other snacks.

Cecilia collected supplies while eating.

Of course, before eating, she had washed her hands with water from the Space.

Just thinking about what had happened on the top floor earlier made her scalp crawl.

Fortunately, these lovely sweet snacks helped soothe her wounded spirit.

The entire fourteenth floor was filled with imported snacks. After collecting everything, Cecilia was full.

Even Blackie had managed to get a few cookies and several packs of jerky.

Blackie wagged his tail and wanted more, but Cecilia pointed a finger at his nose.

"Dogs that eat too much human food will start losing fur. If that happens, you'll turn into a bald black dog. Even Ari and Jet will laugh at you."

Blackie drooped his face in dissatisfaction. His tail stopped wagging, and he no longer came over to beg for food.

Cecilia was very satisfied.

That threat worked.

After finishing that floor, Cecilia excitedly went down another level.

This time, the room was still pitch black without any light.

She walked forward and soon reached the first room on that floor.

The contents were simple.

The entire room was filled with cotton.

Although the United States produced a considerable amount of cotton, the demand was still huge, so some cotton had to be imported.

Cecilia stored all of it into the Space.

Then she went to the second room.

But just as she was about to push open the door, Cecilia suddenly felt the Space inside her body tremble several times.

It was extremely urgent.

This was different from the vibration that occurred when the Space encountered something valuable for an upgrade. It felt more like Ari was jumping inside the Space and shouting—

Master, come quickly!

Cecilia was startled.

What happened?

She entered the Space together with Blackie.

The moment she stepped inside, she saw a round ball like object flying straight toward her.

Cecilia instinctively reached out and… slapped it away.

"Master!"

She heard a miserable wail from that round object.

Turning her head, she saw Ari curled up nearby, looking extremely pitiful.

The cat looked completely exhausted. Its fur was messy and no longer smooth.

Cecilia asked in confusion, "What happened?"

"That… that dinosaur egg isn't behaving!"

Cecilia wondered how a dinosaur egg could possibly misbehave.

But Ari quickly continued.

"The cactus started fighting with the welcoming pine tree!"

Cecilia fell silent.

The welcoming pine tree had a higher mutation level than the cactus and was much larger. However, it had been buried underground and restricted by the Space, which meant it had no advantage in movement.

Ari had tried to bury the cactus as well, but the moment the cactus saw the welcoming pine tree, it rushed over and started attacking it.

Before Ari could even deal with that problem, the dinosaur egg began jumping wildly around the Space, nearly smashing a hole in the house inside.

After helping Ari bury the cactus, Cecilia had to go chase the dinosaur egg.

Unexpectedly, Blackie suddenly enlarged his body and grabbed the struggling dinosaur egg in his mouth.

Just as he was about to deliver it to Cecilia, the egg struggled again and broke free.

Without hesitation, Blackie dashed after it again, clearly enjoying the chase.

After a while, Blackie returned with the dinosaur egg in his mouth, his eyes bright with excitement. He wagged his tail vigorously at Cecilia as if saying—

Let's play ball!

"This thing has mutated. It can be placed in the ranch!"

Ari was obviously furious with the dinosaur egg. Its almond shaped eyes were fixed on it as if it wanted to crack the egg open with its gaze alone.

"Isn't it a fossil? How can it mutate?" Cecilia asked in disbelief.

"Master, don't you think the Space and mutations themselves are already unbelievable?"

Cecilia found that reasoning quite convincing. She said nothing more and told Blackie to place the dinosaur egg into the Care Ranch.
