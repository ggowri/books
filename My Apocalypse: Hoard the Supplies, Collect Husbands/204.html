Chapter 204 A Plan for Tonight


Colonel Reed fell silent.

At the side, Cecilia was already finding the whole thing hard to watch.

She understood now. Given Reed's position, if no one had stepped in to stop him, he could easily have come here, arrested the people responsible for injuring the old general, and sent the rest off to the rear district for labour reform.

There would certainly have been trouble afterwards, but Reed would have dealt with it all one by one.

But now that General Mercer had decided to shield the Barnes family, Reed could no longer act so openly.

"Arthur, looks like Colonel Reed won't be able to do anything after all."

Cecilia tugged Arthur aside a little and lowered her voice.

Arthur gave a faint nod. He said nothing, only looked at her. There was a spark in her eyes, and he understood at once what she was thinking.

"Tonight, we'll have to handle it ourselves."

Cecilia nodded. "Exactly."

That day, Colonel Reed had been sent away empty-handed.

As for Hayden, the boy not yet eighteen whom the Barnes family had pushed forward as a scapegoat, Reed was thoroughly disgusted by the whole arrangement.

It was painfully obvious that Hayden was not the one who had ordered Captain Palmer to assassinate the old general.

But after making the trip, Hayden was the only result Reed had to show for it.

Even so, the boy was innocent, and not even eighteen. Reed could not quite bring himself to lay hands on him.

In the end, fed up beyond measure, he had the boy sent off to the rear district for labour reform. After that, Hayden's fate would depend on himself.

As for Cecilia and Arthur, they told Reed they had no intention of returning with the convoy.

The moment he heard that, Reed's eyes darted about once, and he immediately perked up.

"You two..."

Neither Arthur nor Cecilia answered him, but the smiles on their faces were enough to tell him exactly what they intended to do that night.

"Oh, brilliant, we're thinking the same thing. Count me in, will you?"

Cecilia saw the rough edge in Reed's grin, and the gleam in his eyes. He meant every word.

"No matter what, I'm going to find out who ordered that bastard to attack the old general, and then I'm going to turn him into a sieve."

Reed ground the words out through his teeth.

Cecilia glanced at Arthur. He nodded beside her.

Her own objective was simple enough: Mabel Duncan.

Mabel had come after her time and time again. Cecilia could not allow that woman to remain alive.

Arthur said nothing of his purpose, but Cecilia knew it must have something to do with his mother.

And so the three of them came to an agreement.

When night fell, Cecilia, Arthur, Colonel Reed in a set of black stealth gear, and Blackie, whose footsteps made almost no sound at all, quietly approached the Barnes family's two villas.

The three of them and the dog stopped beside a decorative stone near the estate.

Looking at the heavy security all around them, Arthur gave Reed and Cecilia a hand signal, telling them to stay put for the moment. Then, with a single swift movement, he vanished from their sight like a flash of lightning.

About ten minutes later, Cecilia saw Arthur re-emerge from the villa grounds in a crouch, carrying something over his shoulder.

Once he came closer, she realised it was a person.

She had seen him before. It was the Barnes family's butler.

In the darkness, Cecilia gave Arthur a silent thumbs-up.

Colonel Reed stepped forward and pressed hard at the butler's philtrum.

The butler jerked awake. He was just about to let out a howl when Arthur clamped a hand over his mouth.

At the same time, Cecilia placed her knife against the man's throat.

"We ask the questions. You answer them."

After some interrogation, they learned that the person who had ordered Captain Palmer to assassinate the old general was Alexander's father, Victor Barnes, who lived in the innermost room on the left side of the second floor.

The whole affair had already thrown the Barnes family into turmoil, and Victor himself was panicking.

As for Mabel, she was still at the Barnes estate, staying in Captain Palmer's old room, the first room on the left on the ground floor.

When it was Arthur's turn to ask, he looked at the butler coldly.

"Where is Vivian?"

Vivian Barnes was Miranda's youngest daughter, only forty this year.

Cecilia had seen her a few times before. She was a well-kept, beautiful unmarried woman.

Before the apocalypse, she had been something of a socialite in Heldon City.

Cecilia vaguely remembered hearing that Vivian had been pregnant before the apocalypse, though no one knew who the father was.

"Vivian... Vivian is in the second room on the right on the second floor."

Cecilia glanced at Arthur, surprised to learn that Vivian was the person he meant to kill.

But there was no time to ask further questions.

"And Nathan? Where does he live?"

This time Cecilia's tone was especially serious.

She knew that among the Barnes family, Nathan was probably the cleverest of them all.

He had methods, and he had brains.

The idea of pushing Hayden out as the scapegoat had almost certainly been Nathan's.

He really was ruthless, willing to sacrifice his own younger brother.

Then again, the logic was plain enough. Hayden was only fifteen, too young and weak to shoulder any real burden. For the Barnes family as they were now, he was little more than a fledgling in need of protection.

Victor, on the other hand, was different. A man in his forties, in the full strength of his prime. If the Barnes family hoped to survive this crisis and rise again, they would have to rely on men like him.

"Nathan... Nathan isn't at the villa. Half an hour ago, he drove to the eastern shelter."

The old butler was terrified out of his wits by now. Whatever they asked, he answered.

Come to think of it, a car really had left the Barnes estate half an hour earlier.

At the time, none of them had been able to make out who was inside.

Cecilia frowned. She had fallen into a difficult position.

If Nathan survived, the Barnes family would inevitably rise from the ashes.

But Mabel...

After only a brief moment's thought, Cecilia decided to split from the group and go after Nathan first.
