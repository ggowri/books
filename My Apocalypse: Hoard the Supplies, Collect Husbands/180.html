Chapter 180 An Awkward Interruption

Derek and Liam immediately shouted in alarm.

But then—bang, bang—two more shots rang out. Derek and Liam collapsed to the ground as well.

For a moment, Cecilia was stunned.

But more than shock, she felt caution—there was someone else nearby.

She did not dare enter the Space. If more people discovered the secret of her Space, it would only bring trouble. Instead, she scanned the surroundings vigilantly, pulling a gun from her pocket.

"Who's there? Who's nearby?!"

Arthur stepped quietly out from behind a tree.

"Don't be afraid. It's me."

At that moment, Cecilia finally let out a breath of relief, but her brows quickly furrowed.

"Thank you for helping me, Mr. Barnes. But following me without saying a word like this still makes me uncomfortable."

Arthur sighed softly.

"I'm sorry… I acted rashly today."

Cecilia said nothing, but the gun she had been pointing forward lowered slightly.

"You said you were coming to Boss Carter's territory to collect supplies. I was worried the guards here might have firearms, so I came to take a look. That's all."

Arthur explained briefly as he walked up to her.

"Are you alright?"

In truth, Arthur had followed her on impulse. Halfway through the journey, he had already begun to feel that his behavior might have been inappropriate.

Over this long period of time, he had realized that Cecilia possessed excellent survival skills in the apocalypse. She was also extremely cautious.

They were neighbors, after all. Several times he had greeted her in the stairwell, and Cecilia would politely call him Mr. Barnes before turning and leaving immediately—quick and efficient.

Whenever they cooperated on something, she would politely clarify the terms beforehand.

When there was no cooperation involved, she rarely spoke with him.

At first, he actually found this way of interacting quite comfortable.

After all, he himself was rather reserved.

But after crossing paths with Cecilia several times, he suddenly found himself wanting to understand her a little better.

So when he ran into her today, he felt it might be an opportunity to learn more about her.

Facing Arthur's question, Cecilia gently shook her head. She steadied herself against Blackie and stood up, not noticing that Arthur had slightly extended a hand to help her.

"I'm fine. Still, thank you for helping."

Since Arthur had acted out of goodwill, Cecilia sincerely thanked him.

The incident just now had truly been a close call.

If Arthur had not been here, she could have hidden inside the Space. But afterward, she would have had to kill everyone who had seen her enter it.

And just now, Blackie might even have been injured.

At that moment, a trace of lingering fear crept into her heart—but even more than that, she felt a need to reflect on herself.

It was true. Recently everything had been going smoothly for her.

No matter what she did, she relied on the Space and on Blackie.

Gradually, her vigilance had weakened.

Today she had even rushed straight into someone else's territory to collect supplies, boldly taking everything and then planning to leave just like that.

All of it happened because she had been living far too comfortably these days.

In her previous life, she had lived under constant tension. She had never relaxed for even a moment—yet she managed to survive ten years.

In this life, however, only a few months had passed. The Ice Stage had not even ended yet, and because she had food every day, plenty of supplies stored in the Space, and so many firearms, she had already begun to grow overconfident.

But this was the apocalypse.

Cecilia took a deep breath.

When she lifted her head again and looked at Arthur, she thanked him once more with complete sincerity.

"Thank you, Arthur. Today I was far too careless. I need to seriously reflect on myself."

The latter words were meant as much for herself as for him.

Now that Arthur had arrived, Cecilia pointed toward the villa on the right.

"Arthur, you saved me. Everything in the villa on the left is mine. Everything in the villa on the right is yours. No need to be polite."

She truly wanted to thank him.

Arthur nodded lightly. Seeing that Cecilia clearly did not want to owe anyone a favor, he said nothing more and walked into the villa on the right.

But who would have expected that only a few seconds after he entered, a burst of girls' screams echoed from inside?

Then Arthur backed out of the villa.

When he stepped out, there was even a faint blush on his face.

"What happened?" Cecilia asked curiously.

"There are several women inside… without clothes," Arthur said, frowning.

Cecilia could clearly see that his gaze shifted away awkwardly, and an unnatural flush lingered on his face.

Suddenly, Cecilia realized something.

Oh no.

Just moments ago, that villa had been in the middle of… a rather unspeakable scene.

How could she have forgotten about that?

And those women—why weren't they wearing any clothes?!

But looking at Arthur, who normally carried the stern composure of a disciplined official, now blushing like a shy young man—

Cecilia could not hold it in any longer.

"Hahaha!"

Arthur watched as Cecilia laughed so hard she nearly doubled over. At first he looked embarrassed, but after a moment, a trace of amusement appeared in his eyes.

This version of Cecilia seemed far more lively—and interesting.

This time Arthur said nothing. He simply turned and walked back into the villa.

From inside, Cecilia soon heard him shout loudly,

"Put your clothes on and stand against the wall. Don't move."

The tone sounded exactly like a vice squad officer raiding a scene.

Meanwhile, Cecilia pretended to head back into the villa on the left. From the Space, she took out some of the supplies she had just stored, packed them into several large bags, and carried them outside one by one.
