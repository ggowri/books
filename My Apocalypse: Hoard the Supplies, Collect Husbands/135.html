Chapter 135 Shameful

Her eyes locked onto the massive bags of grain in their hands. She swallowed hard.

When Phoebe left on that government rescue boat, she'd been so smug. Thought she'd finally found security.

Joke was on her. The shelter was a nightmare.

People crammed into one room, sleeping on shared mats, junk piled everywhere. Packed like sardines didn't even cover it.

The old man she'd come with was already sick. He died that very night.

Now she was screwed—a young, pretty girl with zero supplies. And the government had confiscated the old man's stuff too.

She went to claim his stuff, but they demanded proof of their relationship.

Face burning, Phoebe claimed she was his girlfriend.

But they didn't buy it for a second.

Finally, she snapped. "Officer, if I wasn't his girlfriend, why would I humiliate myself like this? I wouldn't claim some old ugly guy as my boyfriend for no reason!"

The officer gave her a long, knowing look.

"Before the disaster? Sure. But now? People will claim anyone as family for a bite of food."

Phoebe immediately swallowed the backup plan she'd been about to try—calling him her sugar daddy.

She stormed back to her room, face white with rage.

But in just that short time away, everything had changed.

She'd picked a bunk area full of women and kids.

But while she was gone, those women and children were gone.

Now on her bed were several men in their forties, their eyes gleaming with nasty hunger.

"Heard your boyfriend's even older than us?" one grimy guy asked with a smirk, his hair so greasy you could fry food in it.

Phoebe tried to run, but someone grabbed her and dragged her back.

The door slammed shut.

She tried to scream, but a hand clamped over her mouth.

The man behind her chuckled. "Don't bother screaming. Won't do you any good. There's twenty-some villas up here, barely a hundred soldiers total guarding them, and half of 'em are out fixing buildings today. You think anyone's watching this place?"

Tears streamed down Phoebe's face. She stopped struggling.

"Heh, smart girl. We'll be nice to you."

******

What Phoebe didn't expect: it wasn't just the men in this villa. Guys from neighboring villas started showing up too.

Most of the people the government brought to this shelter were old and weak.

There weren't many young women or men.

So Phoebe stood out. Way out.

Especially to those middle-aged losers who had nothing going for them before the apocalypse. Their eyes lit up whenever they saw her.

I mean, before the apocalypse, those guys could never have even touched a girl like Phoebe.

At first, she fought back. Then she started trading her body for supplies.

She learned to manipulate them, squeeze out more food. For a while, she survived okay.

But it didn't last long before she got sick.

She stank. She itched.

And those men who'd swarmed her like flies on meat?

Once they knew she was infected, they made her service them in other ways...

But not one of them would help her get medicine, no matter how much she begged.

Finally, the government rescued another batch.

Among them—a twisted freak who had access to valuable supplies.

By the time Phoebe got what she needed to trade for medicine, she was half-dead.

After she recovered, she latched onto a young, capable man—Jason Miller.

Phoebe used every trick she'd learned from manipulating men before, and Jason fell for it completely.

He had masonry skills—worked construction for the government—so he got better rations. He took care of her well.

And since he'd done manual labor before the apocalypse, he was built strong.

He beat off a few guys who tried messing with Phoebe. After that, nobody dared touch her.

Then the third rescue batch arrived.

And Jason's wife was in it!

By then, the Ice Age had hit.

Phoebe had been holed up in the shelter, snuggled with Jason in the small private room his skills had earned them. Then a big, burly woman stormed in.

Amanda Miller didn't say a word—she just started beating the shit out of Phoebe.

Jason tried to protect her. Amanda slapped him across the face with her shoe.

Phoebe tried to run. Amanda dragged her back and went to town with that same shoe—smacking her ass and between her legs.

Phoebe screamed in pain.

Amanda cursed her out, then kicked her out the door.

Phoebe looked down. Her clothes were torn to shreds. Blood running down her legs.

Just then, the government personnel finally showed up.

After hearing what happened, they didn't lift a finger to help Phoebe.

Because in their eyes? What Phoebe did was unacceptable.

"The shelter provides work and food. Why would you do something so shameful?" A young soldier stared at her coldly.

Phoebe's face burned with humiliation.
