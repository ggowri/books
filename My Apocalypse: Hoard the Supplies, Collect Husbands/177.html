Chapter 177 A Deal for Rescue

Instant noodles seemed to have a kind of magic. When eaten often, they felt boring, but after not having them for a long time, one would actually start to miss them.

This immediately stirred up Cecilia' cravings.

And in this freezing weather, taking a sip of hot noodle soup sounded pretty good.

Cecilia could not help swallowing.

Arthur even knew how to eat well when going out!

But when she noticed the faintly smiling look in Arthur's eyes, she immediately became cautious.

"Mr. Barnes, they say when someone shows kindness for no reason, there must be something behind it. What exactly do you mean by this?"

Arthur stared at Cecilia. After about five seconds, the corner of his lips lifted slightly.

"Ms. Summers, I'd like to ask you for a favor."

"Of course, I won't ask you to help for nothing."

After saying that, Arthur handed the instant noodles to Cecilia, then walked to the side and opened the bundles on his sled.

To Cecilia' surprise, the bundles were filled with firearms.

Besides guns, ammunition, and grenades, she even saw several packs of explosives.

Where on earth had Arthur gotten all this equipment? What was he planning to blow up?

Judging from the amount of explosives, Cecilia felt it would not even be difficult to blow up their entire apartment building.

And these explosives were not homemade. They were properly manufactured military explosives. One could imagine how powerful they were.

"So these past few days… you were out getting all this?"

Cecilia asked, patting the bundle beside Arthur.

Arthur nodded.

"If Colonel Thomas Reed knew that the gunpowder stored on the top floor now exceeded what the military has, he probably wouldn't be able to sit still."

In Cecilia' mind, she could not help picturing Colonel Thomas frowning.

"What are you planning to do with all this?"

"Rescue someone. My teammate, Blake Foster."

Arthur then told Cecilia what had happened and where these weapons came from.

In the mercenary group Arthur belonged to, there was a young member who was only seventeen years old. He was an orphan named Blake.

After Arthur joined the group three years ago, he had been taking care of him.

When Arthur returned to Heldon City, he had asked Blake to receive a shipment of smuggled goods coming in from overseas.

But no one expected the apocalypse to arrive.

Arthur lost contact with Blake.

He went out many times searching for him and eventually found the shipment of weapons at the agreed location. He transported them back little by little, like ants moving food, storing them at home and other safe places.

But Blake himself was nowhere to be found.

"After asking around these past few days, I roughly learned that he was captured by a hostile organization. They want to know the exact location of this shipment of weapons, so they haven't killed Blake yet. But I've infiltrated their base several times, and I know their patience is running out. I have to rescue Blake."

Arthur then explained a bit about that organization.

Before the apocalypse, it had already been a notorious criminal force in the region. Now it had become even more lawless.

The police had once launched a major crackdown against them, but it resulted in heavy losses of police resources.

That was also why the shelters were now managed by the military rather than the police.

Not long after the apocalypse began, the police force in Heldon City had already lost more than half its manpower.

Later, that organization reached an understanding with the military, and the two sides stayed out of each other's affairs for the time being.

However, two nights ago Arthur infiltrated their base late at night and discovered that their supply of weapons was also running low. They were already preparing to make a desperate move.

Because of that, their interrogation of Blake had become even more brutal.

"Blake is smart. He revealed two locations that were neither big nor small, and the supplies there weren't much. So the other side gained something and didn't kill him. But now, if he still refuses to talk, I'm afraid he won't survive."

Arthur said seriously.

"I'm just an ordinary person. How could I possibly help you?" Cecilia looked at him.

Arthur looked at her with a slightly wicked smile.

"Ms. Summers, calling yourself an ordinary person seems like underestimating yourself."

Cecilia simply looked at him and said nothing more.

A smile slowly appeared on Arthur' face.

Cecilia waited for him to continue, feeling a little tense.

After a moment, Arthur said, "I'll go in, kill people, and plant the explosives. When the time comes, you help cover me and take Blake away. You don't have to worry about anything else."

Hearing this, Cecilia finally relaxed a bit.

She had thought Arthur might ask her to infiltrate the base or even plant the explosives herself.

She had already been thinking about how to refuse.

"Ms. Summers may be capable, but I don't want you risking your life."

Arthur said with a light smile, his eyes fixed on her as if he had clearly seen the relief on her face.

Cecilia rolled her eyes inwardly. So you did that on purpose. You made everything sound so serious at first, kept such a straight face, and in the end all you wanted was for me to help escort someone out, just to watch my expression change.

"I want this whole bundle."

Cecilia pointed at the pile of explosives in Arthur' hands.

Arthur nodded.

"Alright."

Cecilia had not expected him to agree without even bargaining.

She did not know whether it was because he had plenty of weapons and did not care, or because this Blake truly meant that much to him.

With that, the two of them settled the arrangement.

Cecilia noticed that the sky was already getting bright. But she remembered that she had just dealt with Boss Carter and his group, and Boss Carter had also told her the location of the hidden supplies.

It would be better to go there sooner.

Cecilia quickly finished the instant noodles Arthur had given her, and she did not hesitate to eat the entire box of luncheon meat as well.

Of course, in return she handed her compressed biscuits to Arthur.
