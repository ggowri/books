Chapter 108 Chloe Harper

That got them to behave.

But Cecilia could tell—Blackie was just excited about his new power. Playing with it like a shiny new toy.

A few days passed like that. She barely talked to Arthur.

Outside, the snow picked up—worse than before, but not quite "bury a floor overnight" bad yet.

Cecilia did the math. In her past life, the blizzard hit about two weeks after the snow started.

Before that happened, she needed to make a trip outside.

She'd kept Miranda's car specifically for this—until Arthur wrecked it.

The other vehicles in the Space weren't modified for snow.

So...

Cecilia glanced at Blackie, sprawled out and snoring beside her.

You're up!

She threw on her clothes and reached for that sidecar part from the Space—the one she'd unboxed earlier—ready to turn it into a dog sled. Then Blackie, who'd been out cold, suddenly shot up.

She followed his gaze toward the door. Someone was at the stairwell.

Sure enough, moments later—knocking.

Cecilia finished dressing and headed out with Blackie.

Arthur and Hailey emerged too.

All three of them armed with knives.

Ever since the Tower B trip, they'd been on high alert.

There'd been chaos downstairs these past few days, but no one had dared come up here.

They didn't care how others survived.

But now? Someone was at their door.

They exchanged glances. Arthur nodded and walked to the entrance.

He opened the inner door—and froze.

A woman stood outside, covered head to toe in blood.

Disheveled, bundled in layers—Cecilia couldn't recognize her at first.

Then it clicked. She frowned. "You're... Ashley's daughter-in-law?"

Yes. It was Chloe Harper—Ashley's daughter-in-law.

After Ashley died, her husband and son died too.

Chloe wasn't exactly someone who deserved sympathy.

Before marriage, she came from an ordinary family, but her parents adored her—spoiled her rotten, really. Made her way too naive and trusting.

Then Ashley's son Kent came along. They met, fell head over heels fast.

Kent was a total mama's boy. And mama's boys? They have no spine of their own.

Around his mother, everything revolved around her.

When mommy wasn't around? He'd revolve around someone else.

So early in their relationship, Kent treated Chloe like a princess—basically her yes-man.

Chloe thought she'd found true love.

But her parents didn't.

They saw the cracks right away. Didn't want their daughter suffering in that family.

What followed was two years of fighting.

Chloe on one side. Her parents on the other.

During those years, her once-loving parents nearly cut ties with her out of sheer frustration.

"Chloe, I just don't want you to suffer!" her father pleaded. "If you walk away now, you're only losing two years. But if you marry him, you'll suffer for twenty years or more! Trust me, I'm doing this for your own good."

But Chloe couldn't see it!

She even thought Ashley—who'd been gloating about landing a daughter-in-law for free—was kind.

At one point, under her parents' pressure, Chloe and Kent were this close to breaking up.

That's when Ashley stepped in.

She came up with a plan—get pregnant before marriage.

And Chloe agreed. Actually went through with it.

Now the Lyons had her trapped. No engagement gifts, nothing.

And after she married in, they made her life a living hell.

Shameless. Worthless. Indecent. That was what they called her.

Chloe had fought tooth and nail to marry Kent.

She'd even kept the baby when her parents told her not to.

After that, there was no going back. Things with her family just... broke.

She'd backed herself into a corner—bullied by the Lyons with no parents to back her up!

When the apocalypse came and food ran scarce, Ashley only got worse. She'd almost sold Chloe to other men for supplies.

So when Ashley died? Chloe was thrilled.

She thought maybe—just maybe—the man who once loved her would come back to her now that his mother was gone.

But that hope didn't even last a night.

She lost her husband. And her son.

The man who'd turned on her alongside his vicious mother?

Fine. Let him rot.

But her son—her baby, the child she'd carried inside her—he died too.

Rage swallowed her whole.

And with communications down, she'd lost all contact with her parents.

It felt like she was the only person left in the world.

There was nothing left to live for.

Except one thing. Revenge.

She knew it was that scruffy wife-beater who'd killed her child.

So these past few days, she'd been plotting her revenge.

And now, she'd done it.

Now there was only one thought left in her mind...
