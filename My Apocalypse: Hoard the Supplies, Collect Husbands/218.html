Chapter 218 The Thing in the Box

Who knew how much animal blood stained that man's hands.

At that moment, Cecilia noticed that his gaze kept darting towards Blackie beside her.

"Heh. You can go if you like, but that fat dog of yours stays here. With eyes that icy blue, it's definitely got a superpower, hasn't it?"

The shameless fat man had clearly set his sights on Blackie.

"Of course, the Barnes family will compensate you. For a dog like that, we can give you a hundred catties of grain. That's more than generous enough, don't you think? Better than a body full of dog meat if what you want is something to fill your stomach."

He forced himself to speak boldly and even pointed towards a corner of the laboratory.

"There. Leave the dog and take your grain from over there. You won't get a better bargain than this."

Just then, Cecilia's eyes landed on the corpse of a mutant dog lying on a table nearby.

Next to the body sat a collar. It was obvious the animal had once been someone's pet.

"So this is how you got hold of all those pets, is it?"

Cecilia's voice was icy.

The man looked pleased with himself, apparently assuming she was tempted. He raised his other hand and pointed proudly at several more corpses on the tables.

Most of them were cats and dogs, though there were also parrots, guinea pigs and hamsters.

"Those too. It's the apocalypse. Who still cares about pets? That dog of yours is a good specimen. A hundred catties of grain is more than fair. Don't get greedy. The Barnes family is not so easy to offend... Ah!"

Bang.

Before he could finish, a gunshot rang out and he collapsed to the ground.

His shoulder had been hit.

"Y-you bitch!"

He jerked his gun up, trying to shoot Cecilia.

But before he could pull the trigger, a black shadow lunged forward and tore clean through his wrist with one savage bite.

Blood sprayed everywhere. Blackie whipped his head hard and spat the severed hand and the pistol onto the floor.

A hundred catties of grain for me? What a joke.

The fat man clutched desperately at his ruined arm, sweat already beading on his forehead.

But he had no time to worry about the hand itself. Looking at the woman and dog before him, he felt as though he were staring at two grim reapers.

Struggling to his feet, he snatched up a scalpel from the table.

"I... I can give you all the grain here. I've even got the keys to the other two warehouses next door. Take whatever you want. Just spare my life!"

Cecilia looked at the corpses spread across the tables, then at the innocent creatures still being electrocuted on the lab benches. A faint smile touched her lips.

"Sorry. You talk too much."

She rushed forward. Before the man could react, she pried open his mouth, took out a single drop of the muting liquid produced by Plot One, shoved it inside, then drove a brutal punch into his stomach while clamping a hand over his mouth.

The man swallowed the liquid in one gulp.

Without another word, a knife appeared in Cecilia's hand.

It was none other than the Space-made blade that hurt like hell, the one she had used before on Phoebe, with excellent results.

Cecilia slashed viciously across the fat man's stomach.

He crashed to the floor and began rolling about in agony. But because he had swallowed the mute-making liquid, no matter how desperately he tried, not a sound came out. He only writhed like a bloated grub, his obese body twisting in a grotesque display, the veins in his neck bulging with pain.

In the end, he died from sheer agony.

Until the moment he died, he never understood what exactly had just happened to him.

Nor did he understand why all those supplies had failed to interest the woman standing before him.

Cecilia gave a cold laugh, walked over, and kicked him hard.

He was so fat that even after death, his flesh quivered twice from the blow.

"Disgusting. As if I'd ever sell Blackie. Dream on. You filthy beast living off the flesh and blood of others."

That, in truth, was one of the rare moments of mercy Cecilia showed.

The horrors of that laboratory, and the innocent animals that had suffered there, had stirred something painful in her heart.

She walked round the lab and stored away all the mutant animal corpses.

There appeared to be no living creatures left. Most had already been dissected and cut apart.

This laboratory seemed to be in its early stages, carrying out large-scale dissections to study changes in the internal organs of mutant creatures.

Then Blackie suddenly sprang away with a sharp leap.

Cecilia ran after him and saw that he had jumped onto a metal box over two metres high and five metres wide. He was pressing down on it with all his weight, baring his teeth viciously at whatever lay beneath.

There were a few air holes on top of the box, and a heavy lock fastened the front shut. Everywhere else was sealed tight.

The whole thing looked extremely thick and heavy, clearly built for the sole purpose of containing whatever was inside.

Cecilia understood at once.

There was something in there.

Something alive.

Something extremely dangerous.

Just as she was wondering how to deal with it, the metal box suddenly began to shake violently. Blackie had to grow several sizes larger before he managed to pin it down again.

But once the creature inside realised it could not shake the box loose, it stopped thrashing and began hurling itself at the front door instead, as though it had gone mad.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

Cecilia saw the thick metal door begin to warp under the impact.

"Blackie, back!"

she shouted.

Blackie instantly retreated to Cecilia's side.
