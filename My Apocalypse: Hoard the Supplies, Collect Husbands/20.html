Chapter 20 Do You Remember Harvey

Once all the reinforcements were done, she stepped back to survey her home.

Short of a superhero, no one would be breaking in now.

That day, Cecilia also headed to the warehouse to pick up the medical supplies she'd ordered from the pharmaceutical representative.

After loading the goods into her car, the representative pulled her aside, his voice dropping to a conspiratorial whisper. "Miss, you're too young to know what really makes money in a pharmacy," he said.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow, feigning interest. "What is it?"

"Contraceptives and health supplements! You bought all these regular medicines, but you skipped the big moneymakers. The profit margins on these are insane!" The representative's tone was eager, almost greedy.

Cecilia almost laughed at his eagerness, but he'd inadvertently given her a useful reminder—she should stock up on contraceptives, especially abortion pills.

Cruel as it sounded, these would be invaluable in the apocalypse.

So, she negotiated with the representative to order a batch of contraceptives, asking him to deliver them directly instead of arranging an in-person pickup.

The order was smaller this time, just over a dozen large boxes; she could pick them up herself and stash them in the Space later.

When the representative kept badgering her about health supplements, Cecilia grew annoyed and finally managed to brush him off.

Tired from the day's errands, Cecilia went home hoping to rest.

To her shock, Rebecca had the nerve to contact her.

About ten days after her falling-out with Alexander, Rebecca called Cecilia from an unknown number—unknown because Cecilia had long since blocked both Rebecca and Alexander's phones.

Besides, she'd moved, so there was no way Rebecca or Alexander could track her down.

"Cici, it's Rebecca. Please don't hang up," Rebecca pleaded, her voice thin and pitiful.

That cloying, innocent tone made Cecilia's skin crawl. She silently cursed her past self—how had she ever been fooled by such a two-faced hypocrite?

"Speak. I'm busy," Cecilia said coldly.

In truth, she was just brushing Blackie's fur.

After weeks of living together, Blackie had fully trusted Cecilia—and he absolutely loved having his fur brushed.

Every time she did it, he'd roll onto his back, exposing his belly, and close his eyes in pure contentment.

When the phone rang, Blackie shifted warily, but Cecilia gently rolled him back onto his side and kept brushing.

"I... I owe you an apology, Cici. I really did think of you as a good friend. It's just... Alex was so perfect, I couldn't help myself. I hope you can forgive me," Rebecca said, her voice cracking into sobs.

She even started crying for real, her sniffles loud over the line.

Cecilia remained unmoved, letting out another cold snort. "Speak plainly if you have something to say. Otherwise, I'm hanging up."

Cecilia thought that Rebecca was truly obsessed with drama—coming to her with this act, asking for trouble.

"Okay, okay! I really do have something important to say. I know you don't like me, but please don't hang up. I'm truly sorry, and I want to make it up to you," Rebecca begged.

Cecilia almost gagged, forcing the feeling down.

She buried her hand in Blackie's soft, smooth fur, drawing comfort from his warmth to suppress her disgust.

"Do you remember Harvey Moore from the Institute of International Relations? The basketball player..."

Of course, Cecilia remembered him.

She'd even crossed paths with Harvey after the apocalypse.

"I heard he's back in the country. I remember you... used to have a crush on him, and he liked you too. As a way to make amends, I can set you up with him, okay?"

Rebecca's voice grew more and more sincere as she spoke, "I know this must seem... weird, but I really don't have any ill intentions. I just want to make things right."

Cecilia laughed—a short, cold sound.

Rebecca's thought process was truly absurd.

But then again, Harvey was cut from the same cloth as Rebecca and Alexander.

After the apocalypse, Cecilia had run into Harvey during a mission and felt a flicker of excitement—until he betrayed her.

She later learned that Harvey and Rebecca had been childhood friends, and they'd even had a fling once.

That betrayal had nearly cost her her life.

Still, Cecilia was curious. What was Rebecca up to, bringing Harvey into this now?

"Sure. To be honest, I do remember Harvey vividly. I've been in a bad mood because of Alexander lately, and I'd actually like to catch up with someone I know," Cecilia agreed.

She quickly arranged a time and place to meet with Harvey—without Rebecca, of course.

Cecilia had made it crystal clear that she never wanted to see Rebecca again.

In fact, Rebecca could have found other ways to get Cecilia and Harvey to meet, but Cecilia saw right through her motives.

Rebecca had always looked down on Cecilia, considering her naive and gullible.

She'd deliberately come to taunt Cecilia, hoping to salvage her own reputation by "making amends."

If she could repair her relationship with the "foolish" Cecilia, she could control Cecilia and stop her from spreading rumors about Rebecca being a homewrecker.

And if Cecilia had refused, she could always try another angle.

Either way, Rebecca had nothing to lose.

It wasn't long before Cecilia and Harvey met.
