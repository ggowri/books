Chapter 55 Cecilia's Revenge

Rebecca's face immediately flushed red. "Cecilia, you're slandering me!"

Cecilia gave a light laugh and raised her phone. "Rebecca, I still have the video of you two caught in bed. Want to show everyone? Seems like everyone has plenty of time!"

Rebecca's face instantly turned white. "Cecilia, didn't you delete it?"

Alexander, who had been silent out of shame, finally spoke up. "Cecilia, you took the money, we agreed you'd delete it!"

After anxiously speaking, both realized something was wrong.

Because Cecilia's phone screen was dark. And the power had been out for ages. There was nowhere to charge it.

Cecilia had deliberately provoked them into admitting there was a video and a catching-in-the-act incident!

Now, everyone around laughed, starting to criticize Rebecca and Alexander.

Rebecca bit her lip and resorted to playing the weakling again. "Cici, we're best friends, I..."

Before Rebecca could finish, she felt the door that had been blocking her suddenly open.

Before she could react, she looked up and saw a flash of white light pass before her eyes, then felt a chill at her neck.

Alexander, beside her, screamed loudly, falling onto his rear.

Screams filled the area. Those who had been cursing vigorously moments ago now retreated several steps.

The old woman who had cursed the worst now sat on the floor, a wet patch spreading at her crotch—she had literally scared herself into wetting her pants.

Cecilia held a fruit knife in her hand. a lock of lustrous hair floated past her face, slowly falling to the ground.

Only then did Rebecca, with her hair cut off, realize what had happened, screaming in fear and desperately covering her head. "My hair, my hair!"

Cecilia sneered.

Is that all the guts you have? You seemed braver than this in my previous life.

In her previous life, Rebecca, possessing Cecilia's Space, had crossed her legs and nibbled on a chicken leg while Cecilia was starving and could barely walk.

She even put the gnawed chicken bone in front of Cecilia and kicked her. "Cici, for old times' sake, if you bark like a dog and lick my foot, maybe I'll give you a bite."

Cecilia, holding the fruit knife, took another step forward, directly pinning Rebecca against the wall.

She twirled the knife and placed it against Rebecca's neck. "Rebecca, for old times' sake, I'll spare you this time. Next time, it'll be your head that comes off!"

"Murder! Call the police, get the police!" The pants-wetting woman scrambled to her feet, pointing at Cecilia and shouting.

Cecilia shot her a sharp glare, her eyes carrying the ferocity from slaughtering mutated monsters in her past life. "Then swim out right now and go to the police station to report me. I'll wait right here."

That single sentence silenced all the surrounding noise.

Only then did these people remember there was no electricity or internet, and no one could go out to call the police.

Right then, Cecilia heard a strange sound, followed by a foul smell spreading.

Cecilia looked toward the sound and smell and found it was actually Rebecca, who had scared herself into soiling her pants.

Rebecca also realized her humiliation, tightly clamping her legs and covering her behind.

But the smell had already spread all around.

"Serves you right," Cecilia spat fiercely at Rebecca, then glared sharply at the onlookers and gossips nearby.

Their eyes were evasive. They dared not say another bad word about Cecilia.

Cecilia stopped looking at this crowd, turned, went back to the top floor, locked the door, shutting out all the noise outside, completely ignoring Rebecca's venomous, resentful eyes.

She could have killed Rebecca with a single knife stroke just now, but that would have been too easy on her.

She wanted Rebecca to slowly suffer torment in the apocalypse.

She wanted that despicable couple to gradually fear her and finally die in terror. Otherwise, how could she repay the humiliation and manipulation from her past life?

What made her even more amused was that when she returned to her room, she found the water supply had been cut off.

Let's see how Rebecca washes her behind and her clothes now!

Just as Cecilia found this amusing, she heard a knock on the door.

Arthur's voice came from outside. "Ms. Summers, can we talk?"

Cecilia expected Arthur to come, but not this quickly.

She opened the door to find Arthur holding a plate of delicate pastries and a plate of cheese-covered fried chicken.

The milky aroma and the sweet, rich pastry scent wafted into Cecilia's nose.

Arthur had deliberately chosen these two items—foods most girls would like—for his visit.

In this resource-scarce apocalypse, the combination Arthur held could be considered top-tier, showing full sincerity.

And Cecilia somehow felt these two items in Arthur's hands smelled particularly delicious.

Having just had a confrontation, she now found her appetite stirred.

But Cecilia wasn't someone easily tempted by food, so she frowned slightly, then smiled at Arthur, "Mr. Barnes, I don't think we're that familiar yet. And I really didn't expect... Alexander's relationship with you..."
