Chapter 164 The Mutated Pine

After a moment, Blackie seemed to confirm that nothing was there and stepped forward up another stair.

Cecilia followed, though she kept a tight grip on the knife in her hand, and the gun at her waist remained ready.

After entering the sixteenth floor, the layout was almost identical to the floor below.

Cecilia opened the first door in front of her.

Most of the rooms here weren't locked, probably to make it easier to move goods in and out.

But the room in front of her had its door locked.

And judging from the lock, it was a fairly high-end one.

That didn't pose any difficulty for Cecilia.

She took out the universal key produced by the Space and inserted it into the lock.

With a click, the lock opened.

When Cecilia stepped inside, she was stunned.

The entire room was filled with furs.

All kinds of furs.

Bear skins, deer hides, even tiger skins. There were also pelts from several rare animals.

"These things are probably illegal," Cecilia muttered as she walked around the room, frowning.

No wonder The Boss had secretly created this illegal "haunted building" used for transferring goods.

There were clearly some shady businesses hidden here.

Now, with this natural disaster sweeping across the world, no one knew how many of these precious animals had already died.

Cecilia sighed.

Her hand gently brushed across the luxurious pelts before she finally stored all of them into the Space.

Especially the bear skin and tiger skin. Cecilia couldn't help rubbing them twice.

They felt incredibly soft.

She decided that when she returned, she would spread them over her bed, and place one on the floor as a dog bed for Blackie.

If Jet liked it, she noticed one of the deer hides matched Jet's coloring quite well and could even cut it into a little nest for the parrot.

Just as Cecilia was about to leave, she noticed a small box on the floor.

It had been placed in the corner of the room. If she hadn't looked carefully, she might not have seen it at all.

Cecilia walked over and saw that the box was about a foot long and around thirty centimeters tall, with a bronze lock attached to it.

She took out the universal key again and unlocked it.

The moment the lid opened, a strong and strange scent rushed out.

She froze for a second, then quickly closed the box again, carefully locked it, and directly stored it into the Space.

It was a box full of musk.

If women were exposed to it too often, it could cause infertility.

But it was also an indispensable raw ingredient for many special medicines.

Cecilia had once smelled it before when she was with her grandmother, and she had never forgotten that scent.

"This is good stuff," Cecilia thought as she rubbed her hands together.

She walked around the room a couple more times but didn't find anything else for the time being.

She was about to leave and head for the other rooms.

But the moment she stepped out the door, Blackie suddenly darted forward and blocked her path.

Cecilia instantly knew something had happened.

She shone her headlamp forward but didn't see any human figure.

The surroundings were strangely silent.

Yet Blackie stared ahead, its entire body tense, as if facing a formidable enemy.

After sweeping the area once with the headlamp, Cecilia quickly swept it back again.

It was a windowsill.

On top of the windowsill stood a lush potted welcoming pine.

It had been carefully pruned into a beautiful shape. The purple clay flowerpot beneath it bore the words "Fortune from All Directions," and the fan-shaped needles were vibrant and full of life.

If this had been before the apocalypse, people would have simply thought it was a well-maintained ornamental plant and praised the gardener's skill.

But after the apocalypse, it made Cecilia feel a chill run down her spine.

And when she had come upstairs earlier, she was certain that this welcoming pine hadn't been sitting on the windowsill.

The words "mutated plant" appeared in Cecilia's mind.

Mutated plants were also divided into different levels.

For example, mutated spinach or tomatoes certainly wouldn't be as aggressive as a mutated cactus.

In her previous life, the mutated plants Cecilia encountered most often were unknown weeds and pothos plants grown indoors.

Weeds had very weak attack power and were easy to deal with.

Pothos plants were somewhat troublesome. Their vines could whip and entangle like lashes.

But the mutated plant that left the deepest impression on Cecilia was a mutated cactus she once encountered.

At that time, she had gone out with a group.

The moment they opened a door, the mutated cactus launched an attack and instantly turned the man beside her into a human pincushion.

Two of the largest spines pierced through his eyes and came out the back of his skull, bringing out red and white brain matter.

Several others went straight through his neck.

The man died instantly, his entire body covered in blood.

That meant the cactus possessed a certain level of intelligence and knew where the human body's vital points were.

Because Cecilia had been standing behind him, she had narrowly escaped.

There had been quite a lot of supplies inside that room. After discussing it for a long time, they finally rushed in using a riot shield they had previously found.

Another woman died during that fight before they eventually managed to destroy the mutated cactus.

And now, the mutated welcoming pine stood at the end of the dark corridor, stretching out its branches.

Under the beam of Cecilia's headlamp, the pine needles on its branches reflected a sharp, metallic glint.

The term "welcoming pine" originally came from the pine trees of Yellow Mountain.

The pine trees there had unique shapes, with branches stretching out to one side as if greeting arriving guests.

Among ordinary potted pine trees, however, the name simply referred to a particular pruning and shaping technique.

When pine trees were still young, people would use wires and other tools to bend the branches into curved shapes while trimming away unnecessary growth.

Over several years of careful cultivation, the pine tree would eventually look like a person bending slightly forward with arms open wide, welcoming arriving guests.
