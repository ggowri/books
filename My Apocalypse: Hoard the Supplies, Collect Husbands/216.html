Chapter 216 The Combat Rooster

Cecilia thought about it and had to admit it was true.

Over the past few days, Jet had been milking his injury for all it was worth, and now that he had fallen for Silver, his mouth never seemed to stop moving unless he was asleep or eating.

More than once, Cecilia had found herself wishing she could just shut him up somehow, like she could magically shut him up.

"Exactly," Sprite said smugly. "Plot One can sense your thoughts at any time and randomly grant your wishes. Go on, give Jet a single drop of this and you'll understand."

Cecilia's mouth twitched.

Surely not.

Sprite looked positively thrilled, his face full of eager anticipation, as though silently urging her not to let him down and to hurry up and try it on Jet.

In the end, Cecilia took pity on Jet and did not test it on him. But with the help of a cat treat, she managed to trade Sprite into revealing what it did.

A single drop could render someone mute for an entire day.

Well then. The next time anyone came to the sixty-sixth floor to start an argument, one drop and they'd be silent for a whole day.

So after storing away the artillery piece and shells, which looked impressive but in practical terms were unlikely to be useful often, Cecilia planted them directly in Plot One, curious to see whether they might grow into something equally bizarre.

At the same time, she made a wish to Plot One.

I planted something this impressive for you, so you'd better grow me something genuinely useful.

The second section of the warehouse contained food supplies.

Cecilia took a closer look and realised that the sacks all belonged to the military. Some even had markings for specific army units still printed on them.

There was no question that Nathan and the Barnes family had ripped these supplies straight out of the mouths of the ordinary shelter residents.

Nathan really had been something else. Cecilia could only imagine how much military property he had siphoned off for himself.

Once she had stored away all the food, she moved on to the third section. This part was far more chaotic, as though it had been stuffed with anything and everything.

Most of it consisted of gemstones and gold, all valuable in themselves. But judging by the wear on so many of the pieces, they had almost certainly been looted from ordinary people through the shelter Nathan controlled.

On one gold necklace, Cecilia even found a family photo of three tucked inside. The chain was rubbed smooth from use, clearly something its owner had worn around their neck every day.

Nathan really was vile.

There were also plenty of jade pieces and old objects mixed in, many of which looked as though they could serve as upgrade materials for the Space.

As for the gold, there was an astonishing amount of it. Two entire crates were filled with gold jewellery of every kind, gleaming brilliantly in the beam of Cecilia's headlamp.

She stored all of it in the Space as well, planning to inspect it more carefully later.

But not long after the items entered the Space, Cecilia suddenly felt it begin to shake violently.

This was not the usual tremor it made when it sensed something valuable and wanted to swallow it immediately.

This was Sprite urgently summoning her inside.

The vibration was so frantic that Cecilia had the strange sense Sprite was on the verge of tears.

With no choice, she entered the Space at once.

The moment she did, chaos erupted before her eyes.

Actual chaos, with feathers and fur flying everywhere.

Because inside the Space there was now a rooster over a metre tall, made entirely of metal, chest thrust out proudly as though it owned the place.

Its comb shone brilliantly, polished and bright, and the creature looked absurdly full of spirit.

"This is..." Cecilia stared in disbelief.

"Master! Plot One, Plot One! A huge amount of energy just flooded into the Space and pushed whatever was planted in Plot One straight into production! Ahhh, hurry up! This damn bird won't behave!"

Sprite was darting up and down in panic. Apparently he had somehow offended the rooster, because it was now chasing him relentlessly, pecking with murderous determination.

His plump little body was bouncing off walls and scrambling everywhere to avoid it.

But the rooster was no weakling either. Flapping its wings, it leapt and darted after him, and every time those wings scraped the walls of the villa inside the Space, they sent out long sprays of sparks.

Cecilia hurriedly brought Blackie into the Space as well, and the three of them, one person, one cat, one dog, started trying to corner the monstrous bird.

After a full-on struggle, Cecilia finally managed a flying tackle and pinned the rooster to the ground.

Its entire body was metallic, and its wings were patterned in camouflage colours. When Cecilia landed on top of it, it let out an outraged crow.

Only then did Cecilia realise she had pinned down its neck.

But what shocked her even more came next.

She suddenly felt something hitting her leg in rapid bursts. Looking down, she could not help blurting out a curse.

The rooster was laying eggs.

Not ordinary eggs, either.

Bullets.

One after another, bullets were dropping out.

At that point Sprite hurriedly told Cecilia to throw the rooster into the Ranch. Only after it had been shut in there did he, looking utterly worn out and full of disgust, bring up the description panel for the new creature.

Cecilia tapped on it and saw the name.

Battle Rooster.

Her mouth twitched twice.

And yet, the moment she read it, she became genuinely excited.

Species: Battle Rooster
Description: Who says roosters can't lay eggs? What this rooster eats is ordinary feed, but what it produces are genuine bullets. It can randomly generate ammunition for all kinds of firearms. From now on, no one needs to fear running out of bullets in the apocalypse ever again. Surprised? Delighted?
Note: This rooster has a fiery temperament and a savage disposition. Do not provoke it lightly. If angered, demolishing this Space would be well within its capabilities.

Cecilia's eyes lit up.

She immediately went to the ordinary chicken coop, picked out the largest hen, and put it in with him.

Sure enough, the moment the hen was placed inside, the great rooster straightened its neck, folded in its wings, and fixed his gaze on the hen.
