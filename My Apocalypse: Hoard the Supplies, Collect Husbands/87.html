Chapter 87 Practice Together

For a moment, only Alexander's wails were left outside the door, unanswered.

It was easy to imagine the mockery he'd face when he went back downstairs.

Cecilia didn't care. She went home and hugged Blackie, catching up on sleep.

When she woke up, it was already 9 PM. She heard clanging and banging outside.

Stepping out, she found Arthur installing a new, heavy iron gate right at the original stairwell gate location.

This way, outsiders couldn't see their situation at all.

Cecilia threw on a jacket, watching Arthur work, and walked over. "What are you doing?"

"Too many visitors; so Annoying. Out of sight, out of mind."

Cecilia looked at Arthur, sleeves rolled up, busy working. The furrow in his brow showed his current irritation.

She chuckled lightly and turned to leave.

Unexpectedly, Arthur called out to her. "Hailey is training with me tomorrow, learning combat techniques. Do you want to join us?"

Cecilia paused.

She knew Arthur meant well. Sparring with more people could help enhance physical reflexes.

But she declined.

Mainly because, after using the Magic Spring, her physical fitness had improved. She had plenty of combat experience from her past life and her own training routine.

There wasn't a strong need for this kind of training.

Plus, she had Blackie.

Arthur was strong, but Blackie wasn't bad either.

Sparring with Blackie daily was enough for her.

This wasn't overconfidence. She simply... trusted an animal like Blackie more than other people.

Although she, Arthur, and Hailey were cooperating now, their relationship was still based on cooperation. They weren't real teammates yet.

Hailey actively wanting to train with Arthur showed she genuinely wanted to grow stronger quickly.

Hailey had been a sports student and later a yoga instructor. She looked delicate but had a good foundation.

After a few days of training, she could perform some difficult moves. Her evasion was decent, though her strength was still lacking.

But compared to ordinary people, she was already quite good.

Especially since she never complained about hardship or fatigue, showing incredible perseverance.

Cecilia often saw her knocked down by Arthur.

But she never uttered any complaint, quickly getting back on her feet.

Cecilia could see a light in Hailey's eyes.

It was a light for strength and the future.

Cecilia felt this was the best reflection and performance of an ordinary girl in the apocalypse.

Moreover, Hailey was learning combat skills on her own.

Her progress these days was remarkable.

Cecilia also taught Hailey some skills occasionally, but these were based on her previous-life experience, not systematic.

Still, they worked quite well in practice.

Once, Hailey excitedly ran to tell Cecilia she had finally managed to land a hit on Arthur using a feint Cecilia taught her.

What Cecilia didn't expect was that because of this feint, Arthur sought her out, insisting on sparring with her.

Reluctantly, Cecilia agreed.

Unexpectedly, once they started, she got a bit hooked.

Arthur had excellent combat skills and clearly had training in various martial arts.

Their fights were exhilarating.

However, Cecilia could sense Arthur wasn't using his full strength.

She hadn't been able to gauge his true capabilities yet.

What she didn't know was that Arthur was equally astonished by Cecilia's performance these days.

He hadn't expected Cecilia, seemingly small and slender, to have such explosive power.

Her fighting style was unorthodox but unique, and her judgment in countering his attacks was extremely accurate.

Strangely, Arthur felt a sense of respect towards Cecilia.

But he also sensed that both he and Cecilia were still probing each other, holding back their full power.

After multiple exchanges with Arthur, Cecilia still felt she hadn't probed his depths.

Once, she suggested bringing Blackie into the fight against Arthur.

Unexpectedly, Arthur could still handle it. She and Blackie only gained a slight advantage.

Arthur smiled, giving Cecilia a thumbs-up, but also challenged, "Ms. Summers, I know neither of us is going all out. How about a real, full-force fight?"

Cecilia raised an eyebrow and accepted the challenge.

After several rounds, Cecilia grew somewhat impatient.

Arthur, a step faster, kicked Blackie aside and landed a punch to Cecilia's stomach.

It wasn't too heavy, but it served as a significant warning.

Only then did Cecilia realize—Oh? So you really have been holding back.

Seems she had underestimated him.

Cecilia got up from the floor, looking up at Arthur and not willing to give in. "Again."

But Arthur's expression was very serious. "Ms. Summers, I could see you were growing impatient earlier. We've been sparring for a while today. But since we agreed to a proper fight, what we're doing isn't just momentary strength, but also endurance. Dragging out an enemy is also a tactic in warfare."

Cecilia agreed with what Arthur said and thanked him.

She had indeed been careless earlier.

Only...

That kick Arthur gave her earlier really hurt.

Cecilia's eyes rolled thoughtfully. "Mr. Barnes, actually, I've been holding back too. How about we have one more match?"
