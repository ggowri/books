Chapter 77 Mobile Base

Once again, they focused on gathering food and water.

But when they passed a store selling fur coats, Cecilia's eyes lit up. She pointed at the display and said to the others, "Hey, we should grab a few of these. There's no way we'll have heat once winter hits, and we can't run the AC either. These things'll keep us warm."

She had a point. The weather had already turned cold, though it hadn't dipped below freezing yet—nothing close to the brutal temperatures that were coming.

But this was the north. Without heating or air conditioning, winter would cut straight through to the bone.

Fur coats would be essential, apocalypse or not.

Arthur and Hailey agreed. Each of them took two coats—full-length styles that fell to the ankle. Before all this, a single jacket like this would've cost tens of thousands of dollars. Now they sat abandoned in the mall, completely worthless to anyone but them.

Earlier, when they'd first arrived at the mall, Cecilia had spotted several bags of gemstones and gold that Bryan's group had just collected.

Arthur and Hailey had shown zero interest in that kind of thing, so she'd kept her thoughts to herself.

But now, as they wrapped up for the night and prepared to leave, Cecilia seized her moment. While the other two weren't looking, she swept everything into the Space.

No point leaving it behind.

Especially the gold. When order was eventually restored and the government established bases around the world, gold would replace paper currency as the universal medium of exchange.

What Cecilia hadn't expected was the violent tremor that shook the Space the moment those packages entered it.

She had no time to investigate. She hurried to catch up with the others, and they returned to the apartment together.

This time, Cecilia didn't ask Hailey to contribute more supplies. She and Arthur had agreed that Hailey's previous contributions had already covered her share. From now on, they would operate as a team, splitting everything they found equally.

Hailey became incoherent with emotion, thanking them over and over as tears streamed down her face.

Cecilia didn't say much. She simply patted Hailey's shoulder.

The truth was, Hailey had earned both her trust and Arthur's through her character and effort.

Even Blackie had taken a liking to her. Just moments ago, Blackie had helped Hailey pick up something she'd dropped, compensating for her stamina not quite matching theirs.

After dividing the supplies, they decided to rest and head out together again the following night.

Most people still hadn't grasped the reality of their situation, dreaming of a rescue that would never come. That gave them a window to clear out the supermarket and the restaurants upstairs, then search for diesel to fuel the motorboat.

Once everyone said their goodbyes and went to rest, Cecilia closed her door and clenched her fists in excitement. She could feel the Space again, the familiar presence blooming in her chest.

The upgrade had finished. The Space had returned. And finally, she had time to explore what had changed.

She had a feeling something significant had transformed inside her Space.

Cecilia slipped into the Space and immediately felt the difference. The entire place had transformed since her last visit.

Before, stepping into the Space felt no different from being outside. But now, the moment she crossed over, comfort washed through her.

Just standing here felt nourishing, as if the Space itself were feeding her energy.

The first thing she noticed was the Magic Spring stream right in front of her.

The water had changed—it wasn't clear anymore, but had turned a pale blue.

She walked closer and saw tiny blue light particles floating on the surface. The energy radiating from it had more than doubled.

Cecilia cupped her hands and scooped up some of the spring water. She brought it to her nose and caught a light, sweet fragrance with hints of something almost sugary. Without thinking, she took a sip.

The exhaustion she'd been carrying vanished instantly. Her whole body felt recharged, like she'd just woken up from the best sleep of her life.

She walked over to the villa and studied it. The structure looked more solid, but the building itself hadn't changed much in size.

In front of the villa, that floating display screen still hovered in the air.

Cecilia stepped up and tapped it. The screen lit up with colorful text reading "Spirit Space 5.0."

So the Space had upgraded after all.

Below the Space label, a new line of text appeared.

[Reminder: The Space upgrade system has changed from Hard Mode to Easy Mode. Think of it like this—before, you were a baby who could only handle milk and mush. Now you're older and can eat real food. Happy about that?]

Cecilia stared at the message and felt absolutely zero happiness.

She muttered under her breath, "Yeah, except when I was stuck on 'milk and mush', you never told me what the hell that actually meant! I had to figure out on my own that you wanted stones with special energy in them!"

And now the Space had upgraded, but it still wasn't telling her what this "real food" was supposed to be.

Was this thing trying to screw her over on purpose?

Stupid Space.

She kept reading and saw that the farm, the livestock area, and the waste processor had all upgraded, too.

But then something new caught her eye—an option labeled "Mobile Base."

Cecilia's eyebrows shot up, and she quickly tapped on it.

[Mobile Base]

[Level: 1]

[Function: A movable apocalypse shelter that can be deployed outside the Space to provide protection for the Space owner and companions.]

[Restrictions: Time allowed outside the Space varies by level.]

[Note: At this level, the base cannot be moved or deployed outside the Space. Surprise! Bet you didn't see that coming!]

Cecilia's emotions went on a complete roller coaster ride.
