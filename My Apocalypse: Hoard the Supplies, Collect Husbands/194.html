Chapter 194 Into the Darkness

Then Cecilia heard gunfire.

And shouts. Confused, frantic shouts.

The man's coarse voice came blaring through the loudspeaker again. "Third squad, get to the power room. Now. Move!"

Then came a scream.

It was the same man's voice, still booming through the loudspeaker, and close enough that it sounded as though whatever had happened was not far from him at all.

"You little bastard, I'll kill you, then I'll kill your teammate too!"

After that came the harsh screech of the loudspeaker hitting the ground.

Cici stood at the window, scanning the darkness in every direction. She could see flashes bursting here and there wherever guns were being fired.

Her heart was lodged in her throat, yet she still could not make out anything clearly.

At that moment, Arthur was in real danger.

Even with darkness all around him, he had still been exposed.

The leader of this organisation was Grant Cole.

He had been entrenched here for years, and all that time he had been at odds with the mercenary group Arthur belonged to.

Heldon City was close to the harbour, and it was also a key overland transport hub linking the northern and southern routes. The two sides often crossed paths here.

Before the apocalypse, Arthur's mercenary group had been more than capable of keeping Grant Cole's organisation firmly under control.

But after the end of the world, things changed.

For one thing, Arthur's group did not have its main base in Heldon City, whereas Grant was local, the true strongman of the area.

For another, Grant had reacted quickly. He had expanded fast, seized more firearms, and turned himself into one of the local overlords.

Naturally, that meant he had also begun eyeing Arthur's stockpile of weapons.

Blaise Hart had always worked under Arthur. After the apocalypse, he had been responsible for watching several weapons caches and had remained in constant contact with Arthur.

But Blaise was still young, and he had not always been careful enough.

Before long, Grant had caught him.

By the time Arthur realised Blaise had gone silent and began searching for him from every possible angle, Blaise had already been in captivity for some time.

In fact, Arthur had first learned the slightest fragment of news about Blaise from Miranda. Only by following that trail had he finally found his way here to Grant.

Arthur already had one hand clenched in Blaise's collar.

But all around them, countless guns had been levelled in his direction.

Grant had just been kicked to the floor by Arthur and was still struggling there.

Arthur had one foot planted on Grant, one hand holding Blaise, and with dozens of guns trained on him from every side, the corner of his mouth lifted slightly.

Then, from somewhere behind them, there came a tremendous boom.

It was followed by a chain of explosions.

Shards of twisted iron flew through the air in a storm of sparks, setting the surrounding buildings alight.

"It's the ammunition depot. The ammunition depot's been blown up!"

Grant writhed on the ground, trying to get up. In that instant, it felt as though a knife had gone straight through his heart.

That was a whole room full of ammunition he had spent ages gathering.

But at that moment, Arthur, who had been pinning him down from above, lifted his foot and stamped down hard.

At once, several ribs in Grant's chest cracked.

"Boss! Boss!"

"Boss!"

The men around them shouted furiously, faces twisted with rage, yet not one of them dared fire.

Because Grant was in Arthur's hands.

And they had seen for themselves just moments before that this man's shooting was terrifyingly precise. He barely needed more than a glance to put a bullet straight through someone's head.

For a moment, not one of them dared step forward.

"Put out the fire! Put it out!"

Grant clutched his chest on the ground and roared the order.

Arthur glanced at Blaise, who was now half conscious.

Blaise was a little over one point seven metres tall, though because he was still young, all long limbs and narrow frame, he would probably grow taller still. His hair had grown rather long and now half covered his face.

In weather this cold, he was wearing only a thin layer of clothing, and his lips had already gone bluish from the freezing air.

Across his sharp collarbones ran lash marks, one after another.

There was also a brutally obvious scar on his face, one that looked as though it had been whipped open again and again until the flesh had begun to rot.

Arthur could feel that Blaise was burning with fever.

His whole body was scorching hot.

And yet, the instant Blaise saw Arthur, he had still smiled.

There had been light in the boy's eyes.

And he had called him "Arthur."

Everything that had been done to Blaise set Arthur's blood boiling.

Arthur lowered his gun to Grant's head. "Next comes the power room and the boiler house."

As he spoke, he pressed the switch in his hand.

The moment the words left his mouth, thunderous blasts erupted from the direction of the power room and the boiler house.

Both had been blown up as well.

"Put out the fire!"

Grant shouted hoarsely.

At that moment, the pain in his heart was unbearable. Worse than death, to him, was watching all this happen.

This base had been built by his own hands, piece by piece.

In some twisted corner of himself, he had even been grateful for the apocalypse. It had given him the kind of power he could only fantasise about before.

It had made him a petty king here, free to burn, kill, plunder, murder whoever he pleased, even challenge military officers head on.

But for Grant, it was still not enough.

He wanted more.

He even feared that all his arrogance rested on nothing more than the weapons now in his possession.

And weapons, sooner or later, would run out.

That was why he had gone after Blaise, hoping to force the locations of those hidden ammunition caches out of him.

Who could have guessed it would bring down a living demon on his head?

From the shadows, Arthur had already killed dozens of Grant's men.

He had been like a beast concealed in darkness, waiting for the confusion to spread before devouring them one by one.

And now he had blown up one of their ammunition depots, along with the boiler house and the power room.

"And next, the granary…"

Arthur went on, as though preparing to press the switch again.

"No, no, no. Hero, please. I'll let you go. I'll let both of you go. Just spare the granary!"

Grant shouted at once.

Arthur looked down at him coldly and did not answer straight away.
