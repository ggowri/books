Chapter 78 Supplies

At first, Cecilia was thrilled with the Space and all its possibilities, but now she wanted to punch the damn thing. The system was being absolutely ridiculous.

She held back, though. No matter how much the Space pissed her off, it was still her precious Space.

She looked around the villa and realized this had to be the "Mobile Base" the system mentioned.

A rush of excitement hit her. This could be huge. She knew that once the apocalypse started, nobody could stay in one place for long. Everyone would be constantly on the move, running from danger.

But with this Mobile Base, she'd have a real shelter. And since it came from the Space, she could keep upgrading it. Perfect.

The Recycler had upgraded, too, and now she could put in two items per day instead of one. The cardboard boxes she'd tossed in yesterday had transformed into something incredible—a handgun.

Cecilia's heart raced as she held the heavy black weapon. This was precisely what she needed for protection and intimidation in the apocalypse.

After everything went to hell, plenty of people got their hands on guns through shady channels. She'd used one herself in her previous life, but that had been a homemade piece of junk with barely any firepower.

This was the real deal.

She checked the magazine. Three bullets. Not many, but she'd only need them for emergencies anyway. It would do for now.

Over in Plot One, the bulging area had finally produced something—a whole crate of firewood.

The wood looked ordinary enough, but Cecilia had a feeling it wasn't.

She thought about the Ice Stage coming up soon and decided she'd try it then.

In Plot Two, all the seeds Cecilia had planted were growing like crazy.

The spinach reached half a meter tall, the cabbage stood as high as a person, the peppers stretched a foot long, and the trees were already loaded with apples the size of bowls.

Cecilia couldn't believe it, but she still harvested all the vegetables and fruits. Then she planted new batches in the farm plots.

But the real surprise was the Ranch upgrade. The Ranch could now produce food automatically. When the rabbits got fat, she could just click "Canned Rabbit Meat". When the alpacas' wool grew long, she could click "Shear Wool".

Each animal only had one option for now, but Cecilia was thrilled. At least she didn't have to shear wool or butcher rabbits herself anymore.

It was so convenient.

She had this weird feeling that the Space was kind of shitty, but also pretty reliable.

When she came out of the Space, Cecilia wasn't tired at all since she'd drunk the Magic Spring water.

She grabbed more Magic Spring water and mixed it with the acid rainwater, then decided to give herself and her two pets another bath.

Jet hadn't seen Cecilia all night and wanted to stick to her like glue. Still, the moment he saw her preparing bathwater, he bolted and disappeared.

When the bath was ready, Blackie somehow tracked down Jet and dragged him back by the scruff, tossing him straight into the water. Then Blackie jumped in, too.

Cecilia rubbed his big head. "Blackie's so smart. You've already figured out how good this water is, haven't you?"

Blackie's face still looked droopy, but his wagging tail showed he was pleased with her praise.

Jet looked like a drowned rat in the water, but he wasn't struggling like last time.

The three of them soaked together, laughing and playing around.

Before they knew it, an hour had passed.

That day, Cecilia slept like a rock during the day. When night came, she headed out with Arthur and Hailey to the mall, and they made several trips back hauling supplies.

They cleared out the small storage room in the mall and emptied several restaurants inside.

Most of the supplies went into Cecilia's Space, but the others got plenty, too.

Arthur even snuck into the underground parking garage. He used tubes and sealed bags to siphon a ton of gasoline and diesel.

Cecilia and Hailey helped out with Blackie, and they all walked away with a solid haul.

At least now they had enough fuel for the rigid inflatable boat for a while.

After spending these past few days together, Cecilia figured Hailey and Arthur were decent people.

They hadn't stabbed anyone in the back or hoarded supplies for themselves. They followed the rules and did things by the book.

Once everyone had gathered enough supplies and stashed them in nearby houses, they finally decided to stop going out for now.

Making trip after trip would eventually catch the residents downstairs' attention.

They'd been careful—only moving at night, keeping quiet, and splitting up to scout ahead so no one would spot them.

The people in the building had been lying low anyway, convinced the government would soon rescue them. Hardly anyone went out searching for supplies, and at night they all stayed hidden indoors.

But they couldn't keep pushing their luck forever. They had enough for now, so there was no point taking more risks.

Cecilia slept well that night. When the sun came up, she heard a commotion outside—people shouting and yelling so loud she could hear it through several layers of doors.

Then someone knocked on her door.

Cecilia quickly threw on her clothes and went to answer it with Blackie at her side.

Cecilia opened the door and found Arthur standing there with a grim expression. "The government dropped supplies on our roof. Now everyone from the lower floors is crowding the stairwell, and they're demanding we let them up to get their share."
