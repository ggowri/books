Chapter 163 The Warehouse in the Ghost Building

Let General kill those people.

Looking at Blackie's icy blue eyes and the spark of intelligence within them, Cecilia felt that her guess was probably correct.

Cecilia followed Blackie and continued on their way.

The screams behind them quickly disappeared.

Cecilia glanced back and saw, in the darkness, a pair of faint green eyes staring straight in the direction where Blackie and Cecilia were leaving.

Although she couldn't see the figure clearly, Cecilia knew that it was probably General.

After a long while, General also disappeared from that spot, heading off somewhere unknown.

Wherever it went, it would likely be better than staying with humans filled with malice.

Cecilia stopped and rubbed Blackie's head.

"When we get back, we're going to have a proper talk."

Of course, that conversation would happen with Jet acting as the interpreter, along with a large supply of… food prepared in advance.

After a while longer, Cecilia and Blackie finally arrived at their destination for the night.

This place was near a freight distribution center.

Nearby stood a building specifically used as a warehouse for the freight hub.

However, most of the warehouses there were relatively low buildings that had long since been buried beneath the acid rain and snow.

Looking at the thick snow beneath her feet, Cecilia felt a little regretful.

But it didn't matter. From what she knew, many of the goods stored below were packed inside shipping containers, which had good sealing and could resist the corrosion of acid rain.

Once the Ice Stage ended and the frozen layers melted, she could come back again.

For now…

She looked toward the tallest building nearby.

It appeared to be an ordinary residential building of more than thirty floors. It had always been treated as an abandoned structure and no one had ever lived there.

Cecilia had heard of this building before.

Online news articles always referred to it as a "haunted building" or an "urn tower."

Rumor had it that the building was filled with funeral urns and that ghosts often appeared there.

But in reality, it existed to hide certain people's illegal businesses.

According to the contracts Cecilia had taken from The Boss's safe, the building was full of valuable goods, items intended for export overseas.

Cecilia looked at the building.

"I really have to thank the people who planted this tree before me."

According to national regulations, high-rise residential buildings were not allowed to be used as storage spaces or warehouses.

Commercial property was meant for commercial use, while residential property was meant for living.

Residential properties usually had lower costs for utilities like water and electricity compared to commercial spaces.

Because of that, some online merchants illegally rented residential apartments for commercial storage.

The Boss had taken it even further.

He had directly rented an entire building to serve his commercial needs.

Since the location was close to the freight station, many large trucks transported goods in shipping containers from here to a nearby coastal city, where the goods were then exported by sea.

The place Cecilia stood now was a freight distribution point.

However, the warehouse rental prices here were extremely high.

The Boss had rented the entire residential building and turned it into storage space for others. It benefited him while also benefiting those who needed the warehouses.

Without wasting time, Cecilia brought Blackie to the front of the building. She took out a large metal rod, smashed a window on an upper floor, and climbed inside.

The building's sealing was quite good.

Since it had been used to store goods and the windows had rarely been opened, it hadn't suffered much damage from the acid rain.

The level she entered was roughly the fifteenth floor of the entire building.

With the recent worsening blizzards, more than ten floors below had already been buried under snow.

Walking inside, Cecilia saw rows of tall storage racks in front of her.

On the racks were countless items stored in either cardboard boxes or plastic containers.

She didn't have time to check what they were and simply stored everything directly into the Space.

One of the boxes had a damaged corner. Cecilia glanced inside and saw that it was a box of pickled vegetables produced by an agricultural research institute.

She assumed that the remaining boxes were probably also various agricultural products.

She then went to the next room.

This one was filled entirely with steel construction materials.

These items might not be useful now, but when the government began rebuilding society in the future, they would mobilize large numbers of people to collect steel and metal materials everywhere.

At that time, even a single screw would not be overlooked.

When that time came, these would become extremely valuable resources.

This building had two elevators and four units per floor, with rooms of different sizes.

The larger ones were over two hundred square meters, while the smaller ones were still more than one hundred square meters.

In the smallest room on this floor, Cecilia found a pile of ceramic goods.

The ceramics were beautifully made, but they didn't have much practical use.

After thinking for a moment, she decided not to store them in the Space.

She walked toward the final room and saw rows of wooden crates filled with packaged electric motors.

Her eyebrows lifted slightly.

She wondered if these could be traded with Colonel Reed for something valuable.

Without hesitation, she stored them in the Space.

After clearing out all four rooms on this floor, Cecilia decided to collect supplies from the upper floors first and check the damage below afterward.

After all, many of the lower levels had likely been corroded by acid rain, and there might not be much worth taking.

Blackie stayed beside Cecilia the entire time, alertly scanning the surroundings. Its nose kept twitching, as if sniffing for something.

Cecilia patted Blackie's head.

"Smell something?"

Blackie suddenly stopped moving, its ears standing upright as if listening carefully.

But from its expression, Cecilia could see confusion, as if it were saying, where did it go?

At that moment, Cecilia and Blackie were standing in the stairwell leading to the sixteenth floor. The door to the next level was right in front of them.

Seeing Blackie's reaction, Cecilia also became a little tense.
