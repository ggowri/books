Chapter 157 The Truth About Ari

Just as Ari had started bristling its fur, Blackie, who had been fooling around with Jet moments earlier, suddenly seemed to sense something. It rushed over in a flash and instantly pinned Ari to the ground.

"I'll talk, I'll talk… I'll tell you everything… This dog… its breath smells terrible. Stay away from me, meow!"

Ari shouted loudly.

Cecilia rolled her eyes from the side. Of course it smelled. Blackie ate so much meat every day, how could his breath not stink? But it also reminded her that it was time to brush Blackie's teeth.

Later, Ari explained that it was actually just the imprisoned steward of the Space.

After so much time had passed, it had basically merged with the Space.

The Space itself had no consciousness. It was only a machine.

It followed the rules of the Space and carried out tasks for it.

However, after it discovered that it could secretly steal the energy of the Space to make itself stronger, it began stealing again and again and again.

In the end, it had originally just been an ordinary cat.

"So without you, the Space itself wouldn't prank me, right?"

Cecilia stroked her chin and asked seriously.

Ari did not answer the question directly.

But Cecilia saw the answer in its evasive eyes.

Cecilia sighed and waved her hand.

Close the door. Release Blackie.

After figuring out the situation of the Space, Cecilia had just planned to check what changes had happened after this upgrade when she suddenly heard knocking coming from outside.

Cecilia was a little surprised. It should already be night. Who would come looking for her?

When she went out, she found that it was Arthur knocking on the door.

And behind Arthur was a pile of miscellaneous items.

Looking carefully, she realized they were hydroponic plant devices. There was also a box of sanitary pads.

Cecilia suddenly remembered that these seemed to be the supplies Hailey had asked for before.

However, both Cecilia and Arthur had discovered that Hailey had already left, and she had even left them a note on the door.

The handwriting was neat and decisive. It only contained two words.

Thank you.

"I can't use this stuff. Do you want it?"

Arthur pointed to the box of sanitary pads.

Cecilia immediately carried it into the room.

"Sure. I'll keep this."

Judging by the days, her period should arrive in a couple of days. Although she didn't lack such things in the Space, the more the better.

Then there were the two racks of hydroponic planting equipment.

Cecilia already had this kind of equipment.

But the next words from Arthur caught her attention.

"The soldier who delivered it brought a message. He said this hydroponic planting equipment was newly developed in our country. It can cultivate mutated plants."

As he spoke, Arthur pointed at two bags among the items.

"The seeds of mutated vegetables are inside."

When Cecilia heard this, her eyes lit up. What a coincidence. She happened to need mutated plants, and now they had been delivered.

She immediately grabbed the hydroponic planting equipment happily.

"I'll take this too."

After she said that, Arthur did not respond as readily as he had when he gave her the sanitary pads.

Cecilia looked up at him and found Arthur staring at her with a half-smile.

"Ms. Summers, don't you think you're being a bit greedy?"

Cecilia smiled.

"I'll give you two plants after they grow."

Arthur did not speak and continued looking at Cecilia.

Was that too little?

"I mean I'll share some with you every time!"

Cecilia rolled her eyes.

"Alright. Even brothers settle accounts clearly."

Arthur looked very satisfied. The smile on his face widened as if he had gained an advantage.

Cecilia complained silently in her heart. She had originally planned to give him two plants anyway. What if he got constipated from not eating enough vegetables? After all, who could handle spicy BBQ every meal?

After Arthur left, Cecilia moved the hydroponic equipment into the house and placed it in the living room beside a stove.

It was also relatively close to the balcony and could receive sunlight.

However, Cecilia still took out a grow light from the Space.

Then she planted the mutated vegetable seeds sent by the military.

After that, Cecilia took out a pile of other seeds from the Space.

There were seeds of various flowers and plants, vegetables, and even grains.

She placed all these seeds into the hydroponic equipment and then poured in some acid rain.

She knew that plant mutation was even more difficult. Most plants could not withstand the corrosion of acid rain and would die directly.

So she adopted a "mass quantity" tactic and put in several hundred seeds at once, hoping that at least a few would manage to sprout.

Watching the seeds sink into the acid rain and produce small bubbles in the water, Cecilia waited with anticipation.

She truly hoped that some would survive tenaciously and add more vitality to the Space.

Just then, Cecilia suddenly heard a voice coming from the intercom.

One intercom was still placed on the sixty-fourth floor. Since it was not yet bedtime, it had not been turned off.

In addition, because of the authority Cecilia had established during the day, no one dared to come up and provoke them anymore.

But now, a child's voice came from the intercom.

"Big brothers and sisters… please… please save me."

The voice sounded young and weak, with a hint of crying in it. It made people feel a little sorry.

However, when Cecilia heard it, her brows could not help but tighten.

What was this? Since force didn't work, now they were trying the soft approach?

Cecilia originally did not want to respond. But about two minutes later, the child outside suddenly began crying and kept calling for his mother.

The sound was extremely irritating.
