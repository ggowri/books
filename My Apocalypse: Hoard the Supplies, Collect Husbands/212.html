Chapter 212 The Steel Undershirts

But the woman paid no attention to the blood on her head at all. She rolled up from the floor in one movement, then let out an even louder wail than before.

"Murder! Murder! They're killing people!"

The girl in front of them began sobbing theatrically as well.

"I can't go on living! Never mind that he slept with me for nothing. I don't care about my reputation. But these unreasonable people have actually hit my mum!"

As she spoke, she kept wiping at her tears.

Quite a few of the onlookers behind her also started chiming in.

They said the people on the sixty-sixth floor had always been unreasonable, and that they had killed people indiscriminately before.

Bang.

A gunshot rang out, and the corridor fell silent at once.

Cecilia had fired into the wall.

Now she stood there, looking at the people in front of her, especially the cluster of women at the front.

Leading them was the girl claiming Blaise had slept with her.

She did look fairly young, even a little younger than Cecilia at first glance. But there was something sly and affected in her expression, and her eyes kept darting about. One look was enough to tell Cecilia she was trouble.

Around the girl stood a group of middle-aged women. Their eyes held a trace of caution, but far more calculation.

What surprised Cecilia most was what they were wearing.

All of their clothes were new.

Every one of them had on a fresh padded winter coat, barely marked with dirt.

Cecilia had been down to the lower shelter levels herself. Even with the army keeping order, those places were still filthy and chaotic. There was no way coats could remain this clean unless they had only been worn for a few days.

There was definitely something suspicious here.

What Cecilia had not expected, however, was that the group all insisted they had seen Blaise enter the girl's room. They even claimed he had warned people not to come in before he went inside.

Looking at them, Cecilia suddenly laughed.

"You're saying our boy slept with your daughter? Stop talking nonsense. With the way your girl looks, even if she stripped naked, Blaise would not spare her a second glance."

The moment Cecilia finished speaking, Blaise shouted, "Exactly!"

Then, after that enthusiastic outburst, he muttered behind her, "What do you mean ten minutes? Absolute rubbish! As if I'd only last ten minutes. In ten minutes I wouldn't even have got my trousers off."

As soon as the words left his mouth, Arthur smacked him on the head.

"Behave yourself."

Blaise gritted his teeth furiously and jabbed a finger at the woman in front of him.

"She said there was a mutant pigeon in her room and wanted to show it to me. That's why I went in with her. I was thinking of finding Silver a companion. Who knew there wasn't any pigeon at all? I got so angry I kicked her, and then she grabbed hold of my leg and started stripping off her clothes while yelling for help. I nearly slit her throat on the spot, but then a whole mob of women came charging in and boxed me in!"

The more he spoke, the more agitated he became. He was practically hopping with rage.

According to Blaise, after that he had wanted to bolt. It was not as if he could slaughter an entire crowd of women.

But what he had not expected was that although he was certain he had moved fast enough that none of them could even catch sight of him, dashing upstairs in a blur, the group had still ended up blocking the entrance on the sixty-fourth floor and shouting that he had ruined the girl.

In summary, Blaise pointed furiously at the crowd.

"They're a bunch of shameless frauds! Arthur and I haven't even dated anyone before. I'm still a virgin!"

The instant Arthur heard Blaise drag him into it, his face went cold and he punched him without ceremony.

"Stick to the point."

The crowd immediately started talking over one another again. They claimed Blaise had taken advantage of the girl, and that he had clearly fancied her, wandering back and forth outside their room several times.

"I was looking for the pigeon call, that's all!" Blaise nearly exploded again.

By now, Cecilia understood everything.

Blaise had been set up.

What she still did not know was who had known that Blaise was raising Silver, and had deliberately used the sound of a pigeon to lure him in.

Just then, a young soldier heard the commotion and came upstairs. He found the entrance by the lift on the sixty-fourth floor blocked by a crowd of people.

Some of the crowd even shoved him forward, demanding that he uphold justice.

Cecilia saw at once that he was an unfamiliar face, so she asked where Colonel Reed had gone. Only then did she learn that he had gone to see the old general and was not currently at this shelter.

Cecilia frowned.

These people had come to make trouble, and Colonel Reed just happened to be away.

Was that really a coincidence?

"I don't care. It's the end of the world, and something like this still happens in a shelter under military management. Officer, hurry up and lock them up. Lock all of them up!"

The woman Cecilia had kicked clutched the young soldier's trouser leg and wailed as she spoke.

The others started clamouring again, but they kept repeating the same few lines.

The girl began crying once more, insisting over and over that Blaise had bullied her.

"Send them to the rear district, or we won't accept it!"

"That's right. Send them to the rear district. They mustn't get special treatment!"

The crowd began shouting in unison.

The soldier looked quite young. He kept trying to calm everyone down, saying the matter had to be investigated first and asking them all to quieten down.

Cecilia knew that with so many people gathered here, using force herself would only make things spiral even further.

Besides, if she killed them all now, Blaise would never clear his name.

So she strode forward and hauled the crying girl up from the floor.

A few of the middle-aged women beside her tried to stop her, but Cecilia shouted, "Blackie!"

Blackie, who had been standing some distance away, suddenly charged forward.

The great black dog fixed the women with his icy blue eyes, then bared his teeth at one of them as she tried to move closer to Cecilia.
