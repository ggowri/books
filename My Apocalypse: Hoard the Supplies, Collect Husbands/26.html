Chapter 26 Folk Remedy

The soldier, whom Logan introduced as Barry Allen, pointed at Blackie's back.

Amusingly, Blackie sat up straight, presenting his back to Logan and Barry.

Logan's eyes lit up. He knelt down, running his hand over Blackie's back as if searching for the old wounds. "They're gone! Really gone?"

Cecilia rubbed her nose, reciting the explanation she'd prepared. "After adopting Blackie, I've tried every remedy I could find to heal his scars. One of the folk remedies must have worked. Dogs have stronger recovery abilities than humans, I guess—I never expected it to work this well."

Logan looked at her as if she were a treasure. "Ms. Summers, do you remember the remedies you used? Many of my friends and comrades have permanent injuries!"

At his words, Cecilia's eyes widened. She'd only made up the folk remedy excuse to get out of trouble. She'd never actually researched any remedies!

But as she heard it was for soldiers injured protecting their country, an idea sparked in her mind.

"Give me a minute," Cecilia said, hurrying to her bedroom and closing the door.

Locking it behind her, she entered the Space, filling two large bottles with spring water.

But she hesitated—the spring water's healing power was intense. If it healed wounds instantly, regrowing flesh and bone, it could scare people.

She grabbed a large purified water bucket, pouring the spring water into it. Then, she added several bottles of mineral water to dilute it.

Now, the spring water made up only about ten percent of the mixture.

The clear, odorless liquid didn't look like a "folk remedy," so Cecilia steeped some medicinal herbs in it, turning the water pale yellow.

It was still before the apocalypse—giving them a "folk remedy" wouldn't arouse suspicion.

Carrying the large bucket out of the bedroom, she found Logan staring in surprise.

"This is the folk remedy?" he asked.

Cecilia nodded, a faint blush on her cheeks. "Well, folk remedies are all sorts of things. I got this one by chance. You can take it and split it into smaller portions—I don't have any other bottles here."

Logan said he didn't need that much, but Cecilia insisted.

"This remedy is supposed to work on fresh wounds, too. Test it out. If it works, your comrades can carry a little with them on missions," she said seriously.

Logan couldn't stop thanking her, while Barry, clearly interested, showed a scar on his arm and said he wanted to try it as soon as he could.

Logan laughed, tapping Barry on the head. "Don't waste this on a small scar like that. Several guys on the team have scars on their faces—it's messing with their love lives."

They chatted about Blackie for a while longer, then it was time for them to leave. As they said their goodbyes, Barry suddenly hugged Blackie's neck and shared his story.

It turned out, Blackie had saved his life during a mission. Barry had nearly died due to his inexperience, but Blackie had tackled him out of the way just in time.

"Ms. Summers, seeing how close Blackie is to you and that his scars are healed... I'm so relieved, and I can rest easy now. Please take good care of him. If you ever need anything, don't hesitate to ask."

Barry saluted Cecilia and Blackie solemnly.

Flustered, Cecilia handed them some homemade pastries.

She'd been practicing baking lately, figuring it would keep her busy once cooped up at home after the apocalypse.

Logan took a bite, praising her skill enthusiastically, making Cecilia blush.

As they left, Logan carried the bucket of diluted spring water, thanking her again.

Cecilia waved them off, telling them not to worry about it.

She'd decided to give them the spring water because it truly had healing properties.

If it could cure Blackie's severe wounds, it was no ordinary water.

However, she couldn't tell Logan about the apocalypse; no explanation would make sense.

But these brave soldiers would be on the front lines when disaster struck, and injuries were inevitable. This water might save their lives.

As they said their final goodbyes, Logan hesitated. "Cecilia, there might be severe weather in the next few days. Be careful, and stock up on supplies."

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. It seemed the authorities had noticed the unusual weather patterns. They likely hadn't predicted the apocalypse, though—only that the bad weather would last a while.

Otherwise, there wouldn't have been such heavy losses in her past life.

She thanked Logan, seeing him and Barry off.

Glancing out the window at the rain, which was growing heavier by the minute, a twinge of worry settled in Cecilia's chest.
