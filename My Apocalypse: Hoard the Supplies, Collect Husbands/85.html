Chapter 85 Revenge

But some people weren't satisfied with government handouts.

A group of young people from upstairs decided to go out and look for supplies.

Alexander and Rebecca were part of the group, and the guy leading them was Andy—the same guy who'd seen Rebecca's photo in the building's group chat and immediately started hitting on her.

Funny thing was, Andy lived right above Rebecca and Alexander.

Rebecca and Andy had been seeing each other privately these past few days.

Every time they met up, Andy brought Rebecca food and drinks, so she hadn't ended up looking as rough as Alexander during the apocalypse.

Andy might've been dumb as a rock, but he was a total horndog.

He'd gotten plenty of action from Rebecca in return.

Alexander had no idea about any of this.

When Rebecca told him that Andy wanted them to come along to search for supplies and asked if he wanted to go, Alexander actually felt touched.

Andy was clearly someone who worked out—supposedly some kind of athlete with crazy stamina who could fight. Going out with a guy like that would definitely feel safer.

But then Andy looked at Rebecca and Alexander with an awkward expression and said, "Do you guys have a washtub or something like that? I only have one big bathtub, and it can only fit me and Becca."

Alexander's mood soured immediately.

Rebecca was his girlfriend, after all, so how could she ride with another guy?

But Rebecca looked at Alexander with those doe eyes. She said, "Alex, Andy only suggested I sit with him because I'm small. Don't read too much into it, okay? But we really don't have anything heavy-duty like a washtub at home, so maybe you should just sit this one out?"

Alexander had been through hell these past few days, so there was no way he wasn't going.

In the end, he put every ounce of strength he had into prying the door off the hinges from the apartment next door.

"I'll just use this to follow you guys," Alexander said.

But the others laughed at him almost immediately.

Alexander looked fit enough, but he was useless when it came to actual physical work. The moment he stepped outside, he lost his balance and fell straight into the water.

His face turned ashen, and his fists clenched tight. He'd never been humiliated like this in his entire life.

Rebecca's eyes gleamed, and she leaned in close to whisper in his ear. "Your uncle has a boat. I saw him go downstairs to look for supplies. Why don't you ask him? Tell him we just need to borrow it for a bit."

Alexander's eyes lit up. Of course! That was his uncle! If he asked nicely, he could probably get the boat. Then he'd show all those people who laughed at him. He'd decide who got on that boat and who didn't.

Feeling confident, Alexander patted his chest and said he'd go borrow the boat. He didn't notice Rebecca and Andy exchanging a knowing glance.

The whole thing was an act designed to manipulate Alexander into getting that boat for them.

Meanwhile, Cecilia had her own problems.

Ashley's son had come looking for her.

Ashley's son was Kent Lyons, thirty-five years old and utterly devoted to his mother—devoted to the point of blind obedience.

Everything good in the house went to Ashley, and whatever she said was law.

That morning, when Ashley declared that no one else could go upstairs to collect supplies, Kent stayed behind without question.

His wife had tried to argue, but Kent slapped her across the face, and she didn't dare say another word.

But then everyone else came back downstairs happy and loaded with supplies, and Ashley didn't return.

When he asked what happened, they told him she was dead, beaten to death.

But when he demanded to know who did it, no one could give him a straight answer.

They also said Ashley's body had already been thrown down the stairwell, and the supplies his family was supposed to get had been tossed downstairs too by that woman, Cecilia, on the sixty-sixth floor.

So the government distributed supplies, and his family got nothing—worse than nothing, because his mother lost her life.

When Kent heard the news, he broke down crying, and then he grabbed a cleaver and ran straight to the sixty-sixth floor despite his wife's attempts to stop him.

He stared at the closed door in front of him and raised the cleaver high, bringing it down hard against the metal. "Cecilia, get out here! I'm going to kill you today!"

The door rattled and clanged under the assault.

Everyone inside the apartment was startled by the noise.

Hailey came out first and saw a maniac hacking at the door with a cleaver, so she kicked the door from her side without hesitation. "You looking for trouble?"

Kent had no idea who Cecilia was—he only knew it was a woman.

When he saw Hailey's fierce face through the peephole, he assumed she was Cecilia and started chopping even harder.

The blade crashed against the door again and again, and maybe it was his grief giving him strength, but the lock actually started to give way under the assault.

Hailey gripped the knife and squared her stance, but Kent charged at her like a wild animal and slammed into her full force.

She hit the ground hard.

"You bitch, I'm going to kill you!" Kent roared and raised his knife, bringing it down toward Hailey.

Arthur and Cecilia burst through the door just in time.

Arthur's foot connected with Kent's ribs and sent him flying across the hallway.

Cecilia pulled Hailey to her feet, her expression ice-cold.

Kent crashed into the wall but scrambled up fast and came at them again with the knife.

Arthur didn't give him a second chance. He caught Kent's wrist in an iron grip and squeezed.

The knife clattered to the floor.

"Ah!" Kent's scream echoed through the entire hallway.

Arthur's face showed nothing as he threw Kent to the ground.

Kent curled up on the floor and sobbed.

"What happened?" Cecilia asked Hailey.

"That's Ashley's son..."

Cecilia understood immediately.

So he came for revenge.

She almost found it amusing.
