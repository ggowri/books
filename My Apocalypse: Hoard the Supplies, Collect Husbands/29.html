Chapter 29 The Dark Earth

With that in mind, Cecilia decided to hold onto the purple pendant for now and figure out later whether to use it for an upgrade.

She kept wandering through the market for a long time after that, but nothing else triggered a reaction from the pendant in her chest. She made a complete circuit of the entire trading market, and the Space stayed silent the whole way through.

Cecilia was starting to panic. What if the Space was mad at her because she hadn't given it that gemstone yet?

Just when she was spiraling into self-doubt, the pendant suddenly vibrated again. She looked up and realized she'd wandered into the raw stone gambling section.

And this time, the pendant was reacting to a massive boulder sitting right next to her, easily three feet tall.

Cecilia's eye twitched.
How the hell am I supposed to walk out with that thing?

"Got your eye on our star piece, huh?" A middle-aged guy noticed her staring and walked over.

Before he could say more, a younger vendor from the next stall burst out laughing. "Star piece? More like a dud. That rock's got nothing inside, man. Don't scam that poor girl."

The older vendor made a move like he was gonna smack the kid, looking pissed. "Hey! Stop messing with my business!"

"It's been sitting here for three years and nobody wants it. How many people have checked it out and said there's nothing in there? What are you even bragging about?"

The young guy, completely unfazed, turned to Cecilia. "Miss, if you want raw stones, check out my stuff. This guy's trying to rip you off. That boulder might look impressive, but it's empty."

Cecilia's mind kicked into gear. Time to play the clueless newbie. She wandered over to the young vendor with a confused expression. "I'm just here for fun, you know? That big rock caught my eye because it looked cool. Do you actually have stones that'll produce gemstones? I was hoping to carve out a bracelet myself."

The middle-aged vendor and the young vendor clearly had bad blood between them. The older guy stomped his foot. "Jake, don't think I won't kick your ass! Always stealing my customers!"

After cursing Jake out, he turned back to Cecilia with a smile. "Let me tell you something, miss. That piece you were looking at? That's our crown jewel!"

The second Cecilia heard "crown jewel," she almost rolled her eyes. Every damn shop had the same pitch. Did they really think people were that gullible?

She ignored the owner for now. The rock had been sitting here for days with zero interest, so it wasn't going anywhere. She actually wanted it, but she sure as hell wasn't going to let them fleece her.

She followed Jake around his shop for a bit, politely declined his sales pitch, and then circled back to the original vendor. "So, I really like this stone. I guess you could say it speaks to me. How about you sell it to me?"

In the end, Cecilia walked away with the massive raw stone for 1.2 million dollars.

The stone was tested in multiple spots—no green flash at all, only milky white quartz patches in a few areas. This kept its price reasonable. Most people thought it was just for show and worthless inside.

She turned down their offer to cut it open on the spot. Instead, she had them transport the stone to a small warehouse she'd scouted earlier and rode along in the truck.

By the time the workers left, it was already past seven in the evening.

Cecilia held up the purple gemstone pendant in her hand, glancing between it and the enormous raw stone before her. She couldn't help but smile. Today had been a good haul.

Still riding that high, she released Blackie from the Space first.

He appeared in the unfamiliar hotel room looking confused for a second, but he quickly pulled himself together and sat there quietly, watching her and eyeing the weird giant rock next to her.

She walked over and scratched behind his ears. "Next time you go back in the Space, you'll understand why this rock is so amazing."

Blackie had figured out that the strange place was called "the Space," but even with his boosted intelligence, he couldn't connect the dots between a big useless boulder and anything important.

He started wandering around the hotel room aimlessly because he really needed to use the bathroom.

There was a litter box in the Space, sure, but he'd assumed his owner would take him outside for a proper break, so he'd been holding it.

Watching him pace around restlessly, Cecilia knew exactly what he needed. She quickly moved the stone into the Space, and this time, she stepped inside with it.

She watched as the massive boulder started breaking apart into smaller chunks, and then those chunks crumbled into fine powder.

Blue particles of light emerged from the disintegrating stone, floating upward like tiny stars before spreading throughout the Space. The Space seemed to breathe them in, absorbing every last bit of light.

At the same time, the glowing spring water became even brighter than before. The house doubled in size, and the rich black farmland expanded outward until it covered a full five acres.

Cecilia grabbed a piece of bread from her bag and placed it on the soil, curious to see what would happen.

To her surprise, the dark soil absorbed the bread completely. She had no idea what might grow from it, but at least now the farmland could take in processed food. That was definitely new.
