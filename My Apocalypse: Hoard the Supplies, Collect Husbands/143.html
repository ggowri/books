Chapter 143 The Standoff

Cecilia frowned when she heard the voice. It was Logan.

When she got dressed and headed out with Blackie, Hailey and Arthur were just stepping out too.

They opened the inner door. Outside the security gate stood Logan in full military uniform.

Flanking him—two soldiers with rifles.

Black muzzles pointed straight at them.

"Sorry, Ms. Summers. I have no choice." Logan sighed.

Cecilia nearly burst out laughing. "Captain Jackman, what's this supposed to be?"

Blackie padded over, baring his teeth at Logan.

Logan looked startled. He knew Blackie's temperament—back at the base, the dog had been a total terror among the military K-9s.

"Apocalypse or not, the government's still here! We've got multiple reports—you've been killing people, threatening civilians, hoarding illegal firearms."

"Oh? So you telling me everyone you've shot was pure evil?" Cecilia's smile was sharp. "Who reported us? Did you check their stories? How do you know we weren't set up?"

"I saw you kill those men downstairs with my own eyes," Logan said flatly.

"What, let them kill Blackie instead? When I adopted him, you told me to take good care of him." Cecilia stroked Blackie's head, her smile ice-cold. "Or does the apocalypse mean we just forget? This decorated war dog saved your buddies, your friends—but his life's worth less than some thug waving a knife around threatening to rape women?"

Logan's jaw tightened. "Ms. Summers, I respect your courage and survival skills. But firearms are prohibited."

"So if I use a gun to defend myself against attackers, versus some guy with a knife breaking into homes, robbing people, slashing whoever he wants—who's the good guy? Who's the bad guy?" Cecilia locked eyes with him.

"I'm not here to debate this. We have orders from above—protect civilians, save as many people as possible."

"So there's no room to talk. Got it." Cecilia's laugh was cold.

Right when things were about to explode, a soldier came sprinting up the stairs.

"Captain Jackman!" he yelled, still running. "Division commander needs you! That medicine you sent saved the General! He wants to meet whoever gave it to you—Blackie's owner. Says he's coming to thank her himself!"

Cecilia recognized this soldier too.

He'd come with Logan during that welfare check on Blackie.

The same guy who'd said Blackie saved his life.

Sure enough, when Blackie saw him, he got a little excited. His tail wagged a few times.

The soldier finished yelling, then noticed the standoff.

He spotted Blackie and Cecilia through the security gate.

He froze. "What the hell's going on?"

Logan looked absolutely mortified.

He knew the medicine Cecilia gave him—that big barrel of water—actually worked.

He'd first used it when someone in the unit got injured and they were low on medical supplies.

Total Hail Mary.

Shockingly, it worked.

Later, when the General got sick and was bitten by a mutated animal, they all scrambled for medicine.

Nothing helped.

That's when Logan gave him the rest of that barrel.

And now, of all times, word came that the General had been recovered—thanks to Cecilia's medicine!

And he wanted to thank her personally.

Fine.

Logan reluctantly gestured for the two soldiers to lower their weapons.

He forced an awkward smile. "Ms. Summers, looks like we need to talk."

Cecilia looked at Logan and mentally rolled her eyes. Now he needed her.

She wasn't one to kick people when they were down, but this debt? Yeah, she was keeping score.

"We definitely need to talk." Cecilia's smile was brilliant.

******

Cecilia and the others followed Logan and a few soldiers to the 40th floor.

Logan's crew was setting it up as the official shelter base.

Cecilia glanced around. Half the units on this floor were already locked up, and electricians were installing doors on the rest.

The military would probably move some supplies here too.

The soldier Blackie had saved was named Barry.

He lit up when he saw Blackie.

He pulled a sausage from his pocket and offered it to Blackie.

In the apocalypse, sausage was precious.

"Barry, Blackie's got food."

Cecilia felt awkward. Barry looked way thinner than before.

Sure, he wore a thick coat, but his face was gaunt, almost skeletal.

"No worries. I've been carrying this for days, waiting to give it to Blackie. He saved my life, after all."
