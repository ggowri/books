Chapter 12 I'll Be Waiting for You

Taking a deep breath, Cecilia gathered the packed items and placed them neatly inside the small house in the Space.

Though the house was cramped, she arranged everything as orderly as possible, even mimicking the layout of her current home.

If she ever got the chance in the future, she hoped to recreate the old house exactly as she remembered it.

Lost in thought as Cecilia stared at the family photo—one that had accompanied her for years in her past life—she was suddenly startled by Blackie pressing his large head against her.

His eyes fixed on her, filled with a hint of concern.

It was then that Cecilia noticed tears streaming down her cheeks.

"Are you worried about me? I'm okay," she whispered, her voice hoarse as she gently stroked his head.

The big dog was truly adorable—and this was the first time he'd nuzzled into her arms in days.

Could this mean Blackie was growing more fond of me?

The thought filled her with joy, and she reached out to hold him tighter.

But just as Cecilia did, Blackie, seeing she was fine, darted out of her arms in a flash, leaving only his large, dark rear end in view.

Cecilia sighed helplessly. The dog was all tough and aloof on the outside, but secretly craving her attention.

Before bed, she didn't expect another text from Alexander.

Once again, his message was sickeningly sweet, filled with musings about their future and nostalgic ramblings about their past.

The thought of him probably being with Rebecca right now made her stomach churn.

What kind of twisted amusement did he get from harassing her like this?

Anger welled up inside her; Cecilia refused to let the pair off easily.

Glancing at the sentimental words on her phone, she smiled faintly.

She typed and sent:[Alex, I've been thinking about what I said the other day—it was too harsh. I haven't been able to eat or sleep well these past few days. Can you come stay with me tonight? We need to talk.]

Minutes passed with no reply.

Cecilia smirked in disdain. Playing the innocent flirt? She could do that, too.

Chances were, Alexander and Rebecca were already having a terrible argument.

Or maybe Rebecca was putting on a show of being understanding and pitiful, manipulating Alexander as usual.

But Cecilia knew Alexander well.

Even if he liked Rebecca, even if he felt nothing for Cecilia, he'd never let go of a "prize" like her.

Now that she'd extended an olive branch like this, he'd jump at the chance to take it.

Yet, ten minutes went by, and still no response. Rebecca must have quite a hold on him.

Yawning, Cecilia felt sleepy and decided to turn in early.

She sent one more text: [Alex, I know this is sudden. But we've been together so long—it's time we took that next step. I'm pushing myself to do this tonight. I'll be waiting for you at the Grand Harbor Hotel on Ocean Drive. Just tell the front desk you're here for room 1203.]

After hitting send, Cecilia turned off her phone and drifted off to sleep—sleep that was undisturbed and peaceful.

She hadn't chosen the Grand Harbor Hotel on Ocean Drive or room 1203 at random.

In her past life, she'd crossed paths with a wealthy playboy who thought he could buy anyone.

Rumor had it that this playboy kept a permanent suite at the Grand Harbor Hotel—room 1203.

Tonight, she wanted Alexander to run into that wall. The thought of him getting humiliated made her want to laugh even in her sleep.

The next morning, Cecilia turned on her phone and was met with dozens of missed call notifications and over a dozen text messages—some from Alexander, others from Rebecca.

Intrigued, she opened her messages and clicked on Alexander's first.

The first few were frantic: [Where are you? I'm at the hotel—why won't you answer?]

Followed by more of the same, then: [I'm at room 1203. Why won't you open the door?]

The next message came twenty minutes later: [Cecilia, what the hell is this? Why is there a man in here? Where are you? Answer me! I got beaten up!]

After that, the messages devolved into angry insults.

It was clear Alexander was frantic—and that he'd definitely been assaulted.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow, her mood lifting considerably.

Then, she exited Alexander's chat and opened Rebecca's, which painted a very different picture.

Rebecca started with feigned concern: [Alexander texted me saying you're going to give yourself to him tonight. Have you really thought this through, Cici?]

[A woman's virginity is the most important thing—you have to be sure! I heard from Alex that you asked him to meet you at a hotel... Cici, this isn't like you. What's wrong? Did something happen?]

Reading that, Cecilia nearly gagged.

What a fake show of sympathy—crocodile tears.

The messages that followed were all under the guise of concern, but in reality, Rebecca was trying to convince Cecilia to back down, to not sleep with Alexander.

By the end of the thread, it was clear Rebecca had even gone to the hotel herself.
