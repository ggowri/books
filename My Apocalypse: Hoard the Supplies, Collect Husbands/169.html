Chapter 169 The Locked Top Floor

Cecilia continued upward, basically collecting anything she saw.

Except for a few things that truly had no use, she even stored away the various coffins of all sizes she found on one floor. After all, they were made of wood. They could always be used as firewood.

Speaking of coffins, there was a county in China that was particularly impressive. Just the coffins produced there every year supported the entire county's economy. Most of those coffins were exported to nearby countries, while others were shipped across the ocean to more distant nations. They even controlled over ninety percent of the coffin market in one particular country.

Cecilia glanced at the coffins she had stored in the Space. Sure enough, they were all produced in that county.

She continued collecting as she moved upward until she reached the twenty fifth floor.

When Cecilia entered the twenty sixth floor, she immediately smelled a strong scent of traditional medicine.

This entire floor was filled with medicinal herbs.

Since they were prepared for overseas shipping, everything had already been carefully packaged. The wrapping was tight enough that the constant rainfall had not caused any dampness.

When she was young, Cecilia had learned some basic traditional medicine knowledge from her grandmother. She took a brief look and realized most of these herbs were quite common medicines. Many were used to treat colds, fevers, and external injuries. However, there were also quite a few precious medicinal materials, such as deer antler and deer whip used for strengthening vitality and boosting yang energy, along with ginseng and reishi mushrooms.

Cecilia stored them all into the Space.

On a table in one of the rooms, Cecilia noticed a small iron box placed neatly on top.

The box seemed specially designed and even had a combination lock attached.

Without hesitation, Cecilia took out an axe and smashed the lock open with several heavy blows.

After opening it, she discovered that the interior was lined with red velvet, inside of which rested several irregular objects roughly the size of bowls.

Cecilia did not recognize these objects.

The one on the left was golden in color and emitted a strange smell.

When Cecilia picked it up, she noticed two characters written underneath it: "Ox Bezoar."

Her eyes immediately lit up.

Ox bezoar generally referred to a stone formed inside cattle and could be used as medicine. The ones usually seen on the market were black and about the size of a fist.

However, there was another type of ox bezoar that formed in the cow's stomach. It only developed under very special circumstances.

Before the apocalypse, the market price for this type could reach tens of thousands per gram.

Because its formation was extremely rare and could not be artificially produced, and because it was considered a valuable medicinal material used for treating many difficult illnesses, its price had been driven extremely high.

Furthermore, only very old cattle had a chance of forming such a bezoar.

The item in the middle was about the size of a fist, light brown in color, and roughly oval shaped.

Underneath it was written: "Dog Bezoar."

The final one was round, gray in color, about twenty centimeters in diameter, and covered with swirling patterns, resembling a strange stone.

There was a note beneath it labeled "Horse Bezoar."

These three items were all extremely rare and valuable medicinal materials that were almost impossible to obtain.

Moreover, each one was unusually large compared to typical specimens.

Before the apocalypse, selling any one of these items would have driven many traditional medicine families into a frenzy.

Cecilia placed the entire box into the Space, thinking it might come in handy someday.

Starting from the twenty seventh floor, the layout of the rooms changed.

From here on, the building had two elevators and five apartments per floor. The rooms were smaller, so the goods inside were mostly packed in smaller boxes.

The twenty seventh and twenty eighth floors mostly contained firewood, tea leaves, and sugar.

Cecilia stored them all into the Space without hesitation.

The twenty ninth floor contained medical equipment, while the thirtieth floor held various medicines.

Especially among those medicines were large quantities of anti inflammatory drugs, fever reducers, and a massive supply of masks.

Everything above the thirtieth floor had been rented by a single company and contained camping supplies.

There were camping lanterns, portable camping stoves, tents, outdoor clothing, outdoor mattresses, folding stools, tables, and many other items.

Cecilia remembered that just before the apocalypse began, camping had suddenly become very popular.

Many people had purchased camping equipment to go outdoors and enjoy fresh air.

She briefly inspected the goods and discovered that they were products manufactured in domestic factories, later shipped abroad to have foreign labels attached before being re imported and sold again. The quality was actually quite good.

She stored all of them into the Space as well.

When she finally reached the very top floor, Cecilia initially assumed it would contain more camping equipment.

However, the moment she stepped up from the staircase, she saw a large lock tightly securing the entrance above.

Beside the door was a yellow warning sign that read: No Entry Without Permission.

Looking at the warning sign and the locked iron door, Cecilia became interested.

There must be something valuable upstairs.

After all, the real treasures were usually kept on the rooftop.

She took out the universal key produced by the Space, unlocked the door without hesitation, and stepped inside.

What surprised her was that the entire top floor was a massive open platform with no individual rooms at all.

The ceiling was also much higher than the floors below.

It appeared that the attic and rooftop levels had been merged together to create a single enormous high ceiling space.

In front of her, within this vast area, stood countless rows of shelves arranged side by side.

The shelves were stacked high with all kinds of goods.

These were illegal items that were not permitted for export and could only be traded on the black market.

Right in front of her were two shelves that were not particularly tall, and the goods on them had all been sealed inside wooden crates.
