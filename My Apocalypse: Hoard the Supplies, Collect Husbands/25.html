Chapter 25 Where Did Blackie's Back Scars Go

Pulling out a toy ball, Cecilia teased Blackie with it.

After roughly an hour of soaking, he went still suddenly.

By then, all traces of his scars had vanished, and thick, black, putrid liquid began oozing from his body—exactly the same substance Cecilia had expelled in the Space earlier.

Blackie stared at the tub water, as if deep in thought, before lifting his head moments later.

He was still the same dog, yet Cecilia sensed a shift in his eyes—they looked more human, as if his intelligence had heightened somehow.

Satisfied with his progress, she lifted him out of the tub joyfully.

Rinsing the black sludge from his fur with clean water, she dried him thoroughly with a pet dryer.

Once dry, Cecilia checked the old scar sites—no trace remained, his skin smooth and unblemished.

Cecilia tested his legs gently, then coaxed him to jump and roll.

Gone was the stiffness from his old injuries—Blackie was now a fully healthy, lively big dog!

Logan had told her once his wounds were severe, damaging his core; he'd never fully recover, hence his retirement.

Cecilia had originally hoped Blackie's injuries would heal post-mutation after the apocalypse.

Originally, even with faded scars, he still licked the old spots occasionally—a sign of lingering discomfort.

While he could run and jump seemingly normally, he was slower than most dogs, his movements slightly sluggish when leaping.

Throwing her arms around Blackie's neck, she cried, "That's amazing, Blackie! You're completely better!"

To her surprise, Blackie leaned in and licked her ear softly.

The tickle made her giggle.

That night, Blackie slept beside her, never straying.

Waking halfway through, Cecilia found herself cuddling him gently—and for the first time, he didn't pull away.

Warmth filled her heart; Blackie was growing closer, and that meant everything.

The rain still hadn't stopped by morning.

Checking the forecast, Cecilia saw it would continue for seven more days—not just locally, but nationwide, even overseas.

Clear signs that the apocalypse was imminent.

After buying the cars, she still had over six million dollars left—money she planned to spend entirely in the coming days.

Today, the grain she'd ordered arrived.

Claiming she was opening a supermarket, Cecilia had purchased an additional 200 tons of whole grains—quinoa, oats, barley, farro, and more—on top of her initial order.

Nutritious and filling, they would add variety to her diet.

Cecilia had the grain delivered to a rented factory on the city's outskirts. Taking Blackie with her, she waited until the coast was clear to store all the grain in the Space.

She also ordered another batch of vegetable and fruit seeds; the small amount she'd bought for testing earlier wasn't enough.

Her Space had a whole field—even if the seeds grew into something unusual, it would be a waste not to use it.

Once the apocalypse came, she'd have plenty of time to farm and study the Space.

With the grain stored, Cecilia spent the rest of the day shopping at various markets, stockpiling fresh produce.

She also bought several thousand food containers, planning to cook and store meals for the early apocalypse days.

Power and water cuts were inevitable; most families would have little food, and cooking openly would only spark jealousy.

She also picked up two large pots, intending to boil corn overnight so she could enjoy sweet, glutinous corn even after the apocalypse.

The day passed in a flurry of activity.

Unexpectedly, Logan called that evening—he wanted to bring some comrades to see Blackie the next day.

Panic fluttered in her chest.
How would I explain the sudden disappearance of Blackie's scars?

There was no avoiding it, though. Logan arrived bright and early the next morning, accompanied by another soldier.

Cecilia recognized him—he'd been there when she adopted Blackie, his affection for the dog obvious.

The two brought Blackie's favorite treats.

At the sight of Logan and the soldier, Blackie wagged his tail vigorously, showing rare friendliness.

"Whoa—Blackie's gotten so affectionate lately, and he's grown!" Logan exclaimed, surprised.

It was true—Blackie had grown a lot.

After learning he needed more food, Cecilia had upped his rations with nutrient-rich meals, and he'd shot up in size over the past few days.

Now sitting beside her, he came up to her chin.

Cecilia had a hunch he'd grow even faster after his bath the previous day—all of it would make him stronger when he mutated.

When the time came, Blackie would be among the most powerful mutated dogs.

"Wait—where did Blackie's back scars go?" A surprised voice came from beside her.
