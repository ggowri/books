Chapter 174 The Man Who Eats Dog Meat

In the man's hand, he was actually dragging a large dog covered in blood.

Looking closer, it was the General.

The General already looked half dead. His head drooped to the side, his eyes barely able to stay open, and his body trembled weakly.

Meanwhile, the man was chewing on a piece of bloody meat.

He was eating raw meat.

And the meat had been sliced directly from the General's body. The man had cut open a patch of skin and taken a living piece of flesh from it.

A knife was still stuck in the General's hind leg.

"Heh heh. I love eating dog meat the most. Little lady, leave that big black dog behind and I might spare your life. The dog I just caught is way too skinny. Not enough to eat."

The fat man spoke with some dissatisfaction, yet he still took a savage bite out of the dog meat in his hand.

But his eyes were fixed tightly on Blackie.

"The dog next to you is fat enough. I like that one."

Cecilia glanced at the man.

He was wearing a full fur coat, made from top quality mink that wrapped his entire body. It looked like two mink coats had been stitched together to make one oversized garment.

Even in the apocalypse he was this fat. And he had managed to subdue the General. It was obvious he was a formidable opponent.

Standing beside him was Jake, smiling constantly in a flattering manner. One of his arms was gone. After being crudely bandaged, he had also been given a thick down jacket.

Clearly he had been rewarded.

And that reward was most likely for "offering up" the General.

Cecilia looked at the group in front of her. Her hand moved toward the backpack on her back, but in reality she took the handgun out of the Space.

The moment the pistol appeared, the men around her immediately stirred in panic.

"This woman has a gun!"

The fat man angrily kicked Jake, clearly blaming him for providing incomplete information.

"Boss Carter, I really didn't know! She never pulled out a gun earlier!"

Jake rolled on the ground from the kick but only dared to defend himself quietly.

This Boss Carter was not from their territory. He was the leader of a group stationed about five kilometers away near a school.

Boss Carter and Jake had known each other before the apocalypse. After the apocalypse, when Jake once followed Luke Wei, they had almost clashed with Boss Carter's group.

However, both sides had many people. Boss Carter had more men, but Luke Wei's group had a mutated dog.

So in the end, they agreed to stay out of each other's affairs.

But Boss Carter had one particular hobby.

He loved dog meat.

And not just casually. He loved it to an extreme degree.

A few days ago, Boss Carter had sent someone to find Jake, saying he wanted to eat Luke Wei's dog and would reward Jake if he helped.

At that time Jake naturally refused.

With the General around, their group lived well and ate well.

Although Luke Wei was a bit sinister, the food and supplies brought back by the dog were always shared with them.

But who could have predicted what would happen only a few days later.

Jake had almost been bitten to death by the General.

He pretended to be dead and happened to be pinned under another man's corpse, which allowed him to barely escape.

After the General left, he was still terrified. Afraid the dog might return to finish him off, he tried to run away.

Yet after running only a few hundred meters, he ran into Boss Carter's men.

As it happened, Boss Carter had been craving dog meat so badly that he had come personally to hunt down the General.

So Jake led Boss Carter's group there and helped them ambush the General and capture it.

Boss Carter was extremely cruel.

He liked eating raw meat. When he saw that the General was too skinny, he decided to fatten it up slowly before eating it.

That was why the current scene existed.

The General had been beaten half to death while Boss Carter gripped its head firmly, peeling back its skin and eating the flesh directly.

Jake had also led Boss Carter and his men to the place where Luke Wei had hidden supplies and offered all of those supplies with both hands.

Boss Carter was very pleased.

He even gave Jake the position of second in command in Luke Wei's former group and promised that from now on Jake would live well and eat well.

At the same time, he carefully asked Jake why the General had gone mad.

Jake then told him about Cecilia.

Who could have expected that when Boss Carter heard that Cecilia had a big, black, fat dog, his eyes immediately lit up.

While gnawing on the General's hind leg, drool even flowed from the corner of his mouth.

"You mean a black dog with a good mix of fat and lean meat, lots of oil, shiny fur, and a big build?"

Jake instantly understood what Boss Carter was thinking.

He nodded quickly and described Blackie as a dog so fat that it was practically impossible to see such a thing in the apocalypse.

"Damn it. I never thought I, Wang Hongfei, would get this kind of blessing!"

Boss Carter bit fiercely into the dog meat, his eyes shining with excitement.

"Come on, Jake. If you help me catch that dog this time, we'll be equals from now on."

Jake did not believe the promise of equality.

But he could clearly see that Boss Carter truly craved dog meat, and he was tempted.

"Then when the time comes, can I have that woman?" Jake asked with a grin.

Boss Carter loved good food but had no interest in women.

So he immediately agreed.

Jake knew Cecilia lived in the apartment nearby. He also knew there was only this one path she would take back and forth.

So he had Boss Carter's men lie in wait along this road.

And just before dawn, they really did see Cecilia returning.

As for Boss Carter, he was extremely satisfied with the big black dog beside her.
