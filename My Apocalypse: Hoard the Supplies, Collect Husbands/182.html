Chapter 182 The Secret of the Space


Cecilia ignored the final saccharine, insinuating remark Mabel had made. Instead, she fixed on the earlier part of it and frowned.

"Mabel, what exactly do you mean by that?"

There was clearly something underneath her words.

It was almost as if she were deliberately telling the others that Cecilia had known all along that the apocalypse was coming.

Cecilia's brow tightened slightly. Out of the corner of her eye, she noticed Nathan Barnes studying her with open interest, his gaze carrying a weighty sort of meaning.

What Cecilia did not know was that the Barnes family was currently searching for something.

They were searching for the Space.

She was not the only person in the world who possessed one.

The Barnes family had already found someone with a spatial artefact. After killing that person, they had obtained a dimension ring containing a storage area as large as a football pitch.

Through the channels the Barnes family had access to, they had also learned that there was a specialised national organisation devoted to researching the apocalypse.

That organisation supposedly had five or six pieces of dimensional jewellery.

All of them shared one trait. They were old objects made of gemstone.

The Barnes family had searched many places, yet apart from the dimension ring taken from that dead person, they had not found a single other one.

After Mabel attached herself to Frank, she naturally learned the basics of all this as well.

Frank had even said to her, "If we had one of those spatial artefacts, we could do as we pleased in this apocalypse."

That single sentence had made her think of Cecilia.

She thought carefully about what she knew of her.

Cecilia had always lived suspiciously well since the apocalypse began. She even kept a dog plump and healthy. She herself looked nothing like someone who had been battered by the end of the world. There was still a brightness and vitality about her.

So Mabel began to wonder whether Cecilia had a Space of her own.

And yet, on second thought, Cecilia had always been following the two people from the sixty sixth floor, working hard to scavenge supplies. She did not exactly look like someone sitting on endless hidden wealth.

Besides, when the government had handed out those meagre relief supplies, Cecilia had fought fiercely over them along with everyone else.

But then Mabel thought again. Whether Cecilia actually had one or not hardly mattered.

As long as the Barnes family believed she did, they would go after her.

And if they went after her, Cecilia's life would become a misery.

So Mabel had quietly planted the idea in Frank's ear, embellishing just how capable Cecilia was and listing one example after another to suggest that she might well have a Space for storing supplies.

Frank had met Cecilia before. After hearing Mabel lay it all out like that, he felt there might be something to it, so he went to Nathan.

And that was how the group had ended up here today, at the apartment shelter.

"Cecilia, don't you think you've been living a little too smoothly in this apocalypse?" Mabel's face twisted with malice for a moment.

But almost at once, remembering that Frank and Nathan were standing beside her, she smoothed it away and replaced it with a sorrowful smile.

"Cecilia, we already know your secret. There's no point hiding it any longer. Hand over the Space you have, and the Barnes family will give you more than enough in return."

At the mention of the word Space, Cecilia's heart gave a sharp thud, though her face remained controlled.

Still, she was absolutely certain of one thing. She had always been exceedingly careful. Mabel could not possibly know. Even Arthur, who had spent far more time with her, had never discovered the secret of the Space.

She looked at Mabel and gave a cold laugh.

"Mabel, when you told Captain Logan last time that I had a gun, you made that up too, didn't you? At that point, I had never even used it. I had not so much as taken it out."

For an instant panic flashed across Mabel's face, but she quickly forced it down.

"Now everyone knows you have a gun, Cecilia. How I found out is none of your business. The point is, you do have one."

"And now you're saying I have some secret?" Cecilia took two steps forward.

Mabel instinctively tried to step back, but Frank caught hold of her with a grim expression and tucked her lightly against himself.

"Cecilia," he said coldly, "Mabel is my woman now."

Cecilia smiled at him. "I've seen you before. I know exactly what you are. One of the Barnes family's dogs."

"You!"

Frank flew into a rage and raised a hand as though to strike her.

Arthur stepped forward at once and came to stand beside Cecilia.

Nathan, meanwhile, lifted a hand as well, signalling Frank not to act rashly.

He stood to one side, watching the whole exchange with evident amusement, stroking his chin, a knowing smile playing at his lips.

"You may not know what happened to Mabel's previous husband," Cecilia said.

Before she could continue, Mabel suddenly shouted, "My husband died because of you!"

Cecilia ignored her. She only looked at Frank and said pleasantly, "She burned her husband as firewood. If you don't believe me, you can go upstairs and ask Colonel Reed. When his men cleared out their flat, some of them nearly threw up."

Cecilia had heard about it from Barry when he had been gossiping.

At the time, she had almost admired Mabel's desperate instinct for survival.

"It was all your fault. All yours. All yours!"

The moment Mabel heard that part of her past dragged into the open, the memory of Charles Duncan crashed over her and she lost all control. She lunged forward like a madwoman, trying to claw at Cecilia's throat.

All these days, she had been enduring it, thinking only of how to take revenge on Cecilia.

In order to do that, she had gone out of her way again and again to make herself useful in front of the Barnes family.

At long last, she had managed to latch on to Frank.

He was not the sort of man who was easily swayed by beauty alone. Mabel had spent no small amount of effort making him acknowledge her usefulness.

But she knew very well that, to Frank, she was only a tool.
