Chapter 122 The Government's Visit

Cecilia couldn't imagine what the cities up north must look like right now, let alone what was happening to the Arctic and Antarctic.

But she knew one thing: this wasn't over.

It was going to get colder.

At least, that's how it went in her past life.

Cecilia poured herself hot coffee, fed the two little ones, and decided to check the Space.

That mutated white cat tail from yesterday had been on her mind.

She headed to the trash processor. The materials had updated.

Excited, she lifted the lid—

Nothing?!

Cecilia blinked. Her heart nearly stopped.

What the hell? Does the damn Space just eat it again?

But yesterday, she'd watched the cat tail go into the recycling system!

She peered inside again, even reached in and felt around.

Then her fingers hit something hard.

Cecilia's heart jumped.

She'd been using this processor long enough to know it was smooth as iron inside. There was never anything in there.

She squinted into the darkness and spotted something about the size of a coin. Black.

Was that... a bug?

Disbelief washed over her as she pulled it out.

Finally, she recognized it—a ground beetle!

One of those round, hard-shelled black bugs she'd seen back in the countryside as a kid.

She'd heard these things had medicinal value and weren't cheap either.

But... why did she feel like crying?

Such a huge cat tail, and all she got was this tiny bug. Talk about getting ripped off.

What made her feel slightly better was when she felt around again and found another ground beetle.

Cecilia held the two bugs, their legs flailing in the air, and let out a long sigh.

Fine. Lost on volume, won on quantity.

After thinking it over, Cecilia tossed both bugs into the livestock farm.

To her surprise, the farm actually took them! It even created a box-shaped breeding area inside.

And above the farm, just like with the other animals, a label appeared showing what these ground beetles could produce—bruise and injury ointment.

Cecilia's heart soared.

Finally! The Space doesn't screw me over this time! I actually catch a break!

Space-produced injury ointment was guaranteed top-tier. And way easier to explain than Magic Spring water.

Magic Spring water was colorless and odorless—suspicious as hell.

But this ointment? She could pass it off as a family herbal remedy!

Before she could investigate further, Blackie started barking like crazy outside.

Cecilia rushed out of the Space. Someone was knocking.

She answered the door. Arthur.

Arthur's brow was furrowed as he looked at her. Cecilia had never seen him this serious before.

"What's wrong?" she couldn't help but ask.

"Government people are here. They're saying they're taking over our building as a new shelter," Arthur said coldly.

"And they claim the Barnes family picked it. The Barnes family sent people too."

When Cecilia heard this, her face instantly darkened.

******

Cecilia, the others, and Blackie headed downstairs together.

Before leaving, they locked every door tight and double-checked—in this short time, no one should be able to breach the 66th floor's defenses.

Once downstairs, they quickly spotted two modified jeeps on the icy snow outside.

The person stepping out of the jeep?

Cecilia recognized him immediately. Lieutenant Jackman.

Well, Captain Jackman now.

After the apocalypse hit, the military had maintained strict order and avoided chaos.

But during rescue operations and shelter management, they'd run into plenty of criminals. Many soldiers had sacrificed their lives.

Logan had distinguished himself multiple times, proven his mettle, and gotten promoted fast.

"Ms. Summers? What a coincidence!"

Logan glanced at Blackie behind Cecilia, surprised to see the dog hadn't changed much—still about the same size as before the apocalypse, just thicker fur.

"All our military dogs mutated. They're big as ponies now. How come Blackie..."

Cecilia looked at Blackie, currently in his normal size, and stroked his head.

"Blackie got a little bigger. Not much though. Any bigger and I couldn't afford to feed him."

Two reasons for the lie.

First, Blackie's abilities were pretty incredible, and she didn't want to show off too much.

Second, Blackie used to be a military dog. Retired now, but who knew if Logan would try to recruit him back once he knew what Blackie could do?

So no way was she exposing Blackie's true power to anyone.

Logan chuckled and nodded, his interest in Blackie visibly fading.

Now he was studying the people in front of him.

Downstairs earlier, he'd had a soldier use a megaphone to call up.

They'd announced the building was being taken over as a shelter and hoped residents would come down to talk about it.

But no one came.

That baffled them.

Every other resident they'd encountered had been thrilled when the government showed up. If not exactly rolling out the red carpet, at least excited.
