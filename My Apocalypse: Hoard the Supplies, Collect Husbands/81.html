Chapter 81 I've Seen Worse

When Cecilia walked in and immediately backed out, everyone knew something was wrong. She told them to go upstairs and check it out themselves.

The building erupted into chaos. The thirty-some survivors still living there stampeded up the stairs, pushing and shoving each other to get there first.

Within seconds, sounds of fighting and cursing echoed down from above.

Cecilia glanced at Arthur standing beside her. "Did you see this coming?"

Arthur snorted, "I've seen worse."

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. She hadn't expected that response.

While everyone else screamed and fought upstairs, Cecilia and Arthur stood side by side in the hallway like two spectators watching a show that had nothing to do with them.

They'd been neighbors for a while now, but they didn't spend much time together. They had an unspoken agreement to stay out of each other's way and respect those boundaries.

Still, Cecilia had to admit she didn't mind Arthur. He was strong, reasonable, and reliable. He didn't talk much, but when he did, he did it right. Nothing like the rest of the Barnes family.

The only problem was that he clearly had secrets, just like she did. That was why she hadn't let him get too close yet.

But when it came to scavenging for supplies or dealing with threats, he made a decent partner.

Arthur was watching her too, though he tried to be subtle about it.

Over these past few days, he'd formed his own opinion of Cecilia. She was independent and strong, and she kept her calm no matter what happened. She had her own way of handling things, her own system.

She was guarded, though. Maybe too guarded. He could tell she still didn't really trust him.

But that was fine. He wasn't in a hurry. Time would prove what kind of person he was.

"Mr. Barnes, are you really a soldier?" Cecilia asked, trying to break the awkward silence between them.

Arthur laughed.

It was the first time Cecilia had seen him laugh like that. His eyes crinkled at the corners, and his whole face seemed to come alive with genuine amusement. His light brown eyes fixed on her with something close to scrutiny.

"You know, some soldiers can't reveal their real identities," he said, his tone measured and deliberate. "Some missions are classified. And sometimes, even the reason for discharge is confidential."

Cecilia raised an eyebrow but didn't push further.

Silence settled between them again.

Above them, the commotion grew louder. Cecilia could hear Ashley's voice cutting through everything else—shrill and desperate. She was screaming about murder and hurling profanities that made Cecilia wince.

The cursing went on and on, each word more vicious than the last.

Upstairs, things had turned into a complete mess, but it wasn't total chaos.

The two brothers who'd been fighting had been pulled apart.

But when everyone saw what Ashley had eaten, they weren't having it anymore.

A few of the younger residents who'd already dealt with Ashley's crap before rushed over and went after her.

Ashley got dragged around by her hair, her clothes got ripped, and someone kicked her a few times.

She even threw up everything she'd just stuffed her face with.

Then there was the woman who'd come upstairs with them—Mabel Duncan, the property manager for the building. She'd always been good to everyone, and whenever people had problems, they'd go to her.

She and her husband were crazy about each other.

Right now, her husband had just seen her being kicked to the ground, clutching her stomach. When he realized the two fighting brothers had hurt her by accident, he started shaking with rage and charged at them.

But some of the calmer people blocked off the government supplies and said they'd wait for everyone to cool down before dividing things up.

Meanwhile, Cecilia was downstairs listening to all the noise from above.

That was when she saw Rebecca coming down the stairs.

Rebecca looked way cleaner than Alexander, and her overall condition was way better, too.

When Cecilia spotted her, a cold smile crossed her face—she wanted to see what kind of drama Rebecca was about to pull now.

But who would've thought Rebecca would walk right past Cecilia and head straight for Arthur instead.

She stood in front of Arthur, tears streaming down her face, and dropped to her knees. "Uncle Arthur, I'm pregnant with Alexander's baby. Please, I'm begging you to help us. For the sake of the Barnes family's bloodline."

Cecilia's eyebrows shot up—wait, that wasn't right. She didn't remember Rebecca being pregnant in her past life at all.

Arthur's cold gaze fixed on Rebecca, and he didn't say a word.

Rebecca trembled under Arthur's stare.

She knew Arthur wasn't just anyone.

She was close to Alexander, so she'd heard plenty of rumors about "Arthur"—things outsiders had no clue about.

She knew Miranda was actually a legitimate heiress, and when she got pregnant with Arthur, she was only nineteen. Hugh had tricked her.

Back then, it caused a huge scandal. Everyone thought Miranda had handled it like a boss—she'd dealt with that homewrecker and put Hugh in his place.

But what people didn't know was that the Barnes family had taken a massive hit. Miranda's family had taken their daughter back home and, to protect her reputation, kept things quiet. But behind the scenes, they'd been crushing the Barnes family ever since.

After all these years, the Barnes family was barely hanging on. They were nothing compared to what they used to be, and it was all because Miranda's family had been sabotaging them from the shadows.

And Arthur himself was beyond successful. He didn't give a damn about Alexander's family fortune. He'd recently shown up out of nowhere and offered to give up all his inheritance rights, cutting ties with the Barnes family for good.
