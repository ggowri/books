Chapter 56 Partners

Cecilia didn't finish what she was about to say, because it felt just too dramatic.

It was all just too absurd.

She had lived through one lifetime already, carefully chosen a shelter for the apocalypse—and still managed to run into the Barneses.

Thinking this, Cecilia felt a bit speechless.

She instinctively still disliked the Barneses, and even her previous slight fondness for Arthur diminished a bit.

Arthur smiled, his expression also carrying a trace of helplessness.

It was the first time in these two days she had seen Arthur genuinely smile. "I can't choose my birth, and I've never blamed my mother. But what Alexander is like, what the Barnes family is like, has nothing to do with me. My surname has no connection to Alexander's surname at all."

Cecilia raised an eyebrow, finding these words interesting.

Is Arthur trying to completely separate himself from the Barnes family?

"You're right. The reason I came back this time was precisely to sever all ties with the Barnes family, including the blood and legal relations I don't even want to acknowledge. I just didn't expect to run into acid rain and the apocalypse."

Cecilia was somewhat interested in this.

Because of the entanglements between families, Cecilia had long heard some things about Arthur.

But she was surprised that Arthur had come back to sever ties with the Barnes family.

After all, legally, even as an illegitimate child, Arthur could inherit a very substantial sum.

Cecilia heard that when Hugh died, he also left Arthur many shares. Those alone would be enough for Arthur to live a wealthy life.

But what Cecilia didn't know was that the Barnes family's massive wealth was already gone. The Barnes family was an empty shell.

And Arthur's mother's family was a more powerful one than the Barnes family. Arthur never cared about the Barnes family's assets anyway.

As Arthur said, he had nothing to do with the Barnes family. He just couldn't choose his birth.

"Ms. Summers, I didn't know about your relationship with Alexander before. Otherwise, I would never have come to the 66th floor." Arthur looked at Cecilia seriously, his eyes carrying a sincere light.

"But after these past few days, we've both gained some understanding of each other. So, I hope we can put aside those prejudices. After all, the apocalypse is the current problem."

To be honest, Cecilia was somewhat moved by Arthur's words.

She took a deep breath, originally intending to steady herself, but ended up inhaling the aroma of the delicious food in Arthur's hands.

Cecilia chuckled inwardly, thinking Arthur was quite strategic, bringing things girls love to eat.

Thinking that what Arthur said made sense, Cecilia nodded. "Alright, then let's talk, also for facing external threats together later."

After all, there were just the two of them on this floor now.

Cecilia beckoned to Blackie, who had just gone to drink water and now stood by her side like a guard again. "If I'm going to eat at your place, I have a few conditions.

"First, I don't drink alcohol. Second, I'm bringing Blackie. Third, you eat all the food first. And, I need to pick which piece on the plate you eat."

Arthur nodded slightly. "Your conditions are completely reasonable."

At this moment, Arthur admired Cecilia.

He hadn't expected Cecilia's thinking to be so sharp and so alert.

Only someone like this can face difficulties like the apocalypse calmly and go far, right?

And so, Cecilia brought Blackie and entered Arthur's home for the first time.

Meanwhile, something Cecilia didn't know about was happening elsewhere.

Alexander and Rebecca had climbed from the 5th floor to the 66th floor without gaining any advantage, and now had to climb back down from the top floor, which was already infuriating.

And Rebecca had just soiled her pants. Now, not only was she blushing with shame, but with every step, she could feel that sticky sensation on her body.

When she walked, she could even feel that disgusting stuff rubbing together.

Though she didn't want to admit it, she knew it was her own excrement.

The smell was slowly spreading, making her hate Cecilia even more.

Alexander had a dark look on his face, completely unlike his usual considerate self. He kept walking ahead without caring whether Rebecca was keeping up.

Suddenly, Rebecca let out a scream, and only then did Alexander turn back, asking impatiently, "What is it?"

"Relax, I mean no harm. I want to talk about cooperation with you."

An unfamiliar voice came from the stairwell corner.

Rebecca had been startled by this suddenly appearing person.

Bryan, with his sharp, weasel-like face, slowly walked over. Not even minding that Rebecca was currently a walking stink bomb, he smiled at Rebecca and Alexander. "You have a grudge against that bitch on the 66th floor, right? Then we can be partners..."

Hearing Bryan say this, Rebecca's eyes also lit up. "How do we cooperate? I'm sure that bitch has a lot of supplies at home! Otherwise, she wouldn't install protective doors at her entrance! And she always loved hoarding things. Her home was like a mini-warehouse."

Bryan licked his lips. "The supplies we can split equally later. But that bitch, you have to give her to me."

"What do you mean?" Alexander frowned.

He had just seen the beautiful Cecilia and was also struck by her beauty. Even on the way down, he had been thinking if there was any possibility of begging Cecilia to get back together.
