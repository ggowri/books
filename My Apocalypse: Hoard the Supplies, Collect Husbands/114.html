Chapter 114 A Weasel

"See a door? Try to twist it open! You can do this!"

Blackie had learned how to open doors ages ago at home. Regular doors were no problem for him.

As for the cold storage door... well, worth a shot.

"If there's danger, or if you can't do it, that's okay. Just come back fast."

Cecilia repeated this several times right into Blackie's ear.

Only after making sure he understood did she let him go.

And so, Blackie shrunk down, pendant in tow, and squeezed into the hole.

Cecilia hugged Jet close and waited.

Jet kept one eye trained upward, alert and tense.

Cecilia knew—the mutant cats might be too scared to approach, but they were still lurking nearby, watching her group like hawks.

She waited.

And waited.

Two hours passed.

No sounds from below. It was like Blackie had vanished into thin air.

During those two hours, Cecilia worried, sure. But thinking about Blackie's usual sharpness and smarts, plus his insane new size-shifting ability and powerful body—he should be fine.

When four o'clock rolled around and Cecilia spotted the morning star brightening through the window, she finally heard something!

She rushed to the hole.

But what she saw made her freeze.

A pitch-black paw was clawing its way out of the hole.

That definitely wasn't Blackie's!

The second that dark paw appeared, the mutant cats lurking behind her went wild.

Clearly, these cats and whatever creature that paw belonged to were mortal enemies.

But Blackie—where the hell was Blackie?

Cecilia pulled her hidden blade from the Space, eyes locked on the hole.

Earlier, she'd dimmed her headlamp to a dull yellow to avoid pissing off those mutant cats.

Now, facing whatever this thing was, she pressed the button. The light shifted from yellow to pale white.

In that instant, she saw the claw clearly.

The base wasn't just dark—it was covered in dark red blood!

Her heart jumped into her throat.

Has something happened to Blackie?

Right then, the creature finally squeezed through the hole.

Triangular head. Tiny crimson eyes. Dark yellow fur all over.

A weasel!

A weasel about a foot and a half long with vicious eyes.

The mutant cats behind her started growling the moment it appeared—threatening, but also afraid.

They paced anxiously behind Cecilia, no longer even scared of the dead mutant cat she was carrying.

Like they were ready to throw down with this weasel.

The weasel wasn't backing down either. It stood at the hole, baring its teeth at the cats and Cecilia, threatening them right back.

Weasels were basically carnivores—they ate chickens, lizards, frogs, fish. Sometimes they'd nibble on roots or sweet potatoes.

Normal weasels would eat newborn kittens, the ones barely able to open their eyes.

But this weasel? A foot and a half wide—and easily six and a half feet long!

Several times bigger than normal!

Compared to the cats behind her, this thing had evolved way more.

And in its eyes? Those cats were food.

Not just that—when it looked at Cecilia, there was zero fear. Almost looked cocky.

Especially when it spotted Jet. The mutant weasel's eyes lit up.

Everyone knew weasels loved poultry best. Back in the day, anyone raising chickens or ducks got raided by these things constantly.

Jet didn't dare make a sound, circling anxiously overhead.

"Meow."

"MEOW!"

The cats behind her were getting more agitated by the second.

Cecilia noticed the black cat seemed to be calling the shots now.

That black cat looked ready to murder the weasel—lunging forward multiple times, itching for a fight.

But each time, it held back, clearly spooked by the scent of that mutated white cat corpse on Cecilia.

Cecilia gripped her knife tight, worried sick about Blackie while keeping eyes on both sides.

She could duck into the Space right now, easy. But Blackie was still out there somewhere. If she bailed, he'd never find her.

When the weasel's gaze finally shifted her way, she knew—it wanted Jet more.

Gotta get Jet into the Space. Now.

She focused her mind and reached for the bird circling above.

But in that exact moment, the weasel moved!

It shot out of that hole like a bullet—so fast Cecilia could barely track it!

Heading straight for Jet.

In that split second, Cecilia spotted another figure emerging from the hole.

Right on the weasel's tail.

The ice on the fourth-floor landing suddenly exploded—like something massive had smashed through from below.

Massive jaws clamped down on the weasel, already over three feet out of the hole.

The thunderous crack of ice. Cats yowling. The weasel's shriek of agony.
