Chapter 66 I Want to Survive

If Hailey failed to live up to expectations, she could still keep Arthur in check—his true intentions remained unclear.

Either way, sharing the floor with Arthur meant Cecilia should consult him first.

She turned to Arthur, who'd stood silently in the background, and asked for his thoughts.

To her surprise, Arthur had no objections to Hailey staying on the 66th floor.

"You make the call. There are plenty of empty rooms anyway," he said casually.

Cecilia then addressed Hailey, "You can stay here temporarily, but don't get any funny ideas. Saving you was just a whim. Whether you can stay long-term depends on your performance."

Hailey nodded frantically, wiping blood from her face with the back of her hand as she clenched her jaw. "I understand. You're my savior. I swear—my life is yours from this day on!"

Cecilia smiled faintly at her determined look, unimpressed by the oath; actions, not words, mattered to her.

However, Hailey meant every word, though. She should have died that night, and Cecilia's intervention had given her a second chance.

No matter what Cecilia asked, she would obey, resolving to spend her remaining life repaying the debt.

"As long as I live, I'll never forget your kindness," she vowed silently.

An even bigger surprise awaited Cecilia the next night. The injured man was found dead in his apartment, killed with a single strike, his eyes wide in terror.

Phoebe, who'd slept beside him, knew nothing of the incident, and there were no signs of forced entry—like the Grim Reaper himself had claimed him.

The moment Cecilia heard the news, she suspected Arthur, but that thought was set aside for later.

Back in the present, Cecilia helped Hailey into an empty room.

After shedding a few tears, Hailey calmed down, pressing a handkerchief Cecilia gave her to her wound.

The gash ran from her right jaw to her brow, narrowly missing her eye but ruining her once-beautiful face completely.

"What are your plans now?" Cecilia asked.

Hailey hesitated, her eyes shifting from confusion to resolve. "I want to survive," she said, adding silently that she'd cherish this life for her savior's sake.

Cecilia approved of her quick recovery—no excessive weeping, just quiet grit.

Instead of agreeing immediately, she fixed Hailey with a cold smile. "I saved your life, so you owe me. Staying here means getting our protection, so what can you offer in return?"

Hailey glanced nervously at Arthur, fearing he might covet her as the other men had.

Seeing his impassive expression, she closed her eyes, took a deep breath, and spoke up, teary-eyed.

"I'll scavenge for supplies and split everything with you and this gentleman. I was a state swimming champion in high school and I'm not afraid of acid rain. If the disaster ends, I'll give you half my income for life, or do whatever you ask."

She laughed bitterly, pressing harder on her wound. "I've already died once. What's the point of clinging to this face if it means surviving?"

Cecilia was satisfied and looked to Arthur—they shared the floor, after all—and he nodded in agreement.

Unexpectedly, Arthur knelt before Hailey. "You're forgetting one thing," he said slowly.

Hailey froze, staring up at the tall, intimidating man.

"Revenge. Make those who betrayed you pay double," he continued, his voice cold with menace.

From Cecilia's angle, his eyes glinted with ruthless resolve.

Cecilia admired this, looking at Hailey and repeating firmly, "That's right. You also need to get revenge—pay them back double. Otherwise, you don't deserve to stay on the 66th floor, and my saving you would be for nothing."

She shot Arthur an appreciative glance—his personality matched hers perfectly.

Afterwards, Cecilia took out medicine and bandaged Hailey's wound gently.

Hailey thanked her repeatedly, well aware of how precious medicine was in the apocalypse.

However, Cecilia just lifted her head slightly, saying, "Pay me back when you're healed."

She settled Hailey in an empty room on the east side of the floor, directly across from Arthur's, maintaining a careful balance.
