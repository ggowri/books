Chapter 90 Sudden Chill

Moreover, Cecilia had applied anti-corrosion coating to this building's exterior before the apocalypse, which should make it even sturdier than in her past life.

Her current concern was something else: the imminent arrival of the Ice Stage.

When their discussion wrapped up, Cecilia deliberately mentioned that the weather seemed colder today, and Blackie's fur felt thicker.

Arthur showed keen interest, reaching out to feel Blackie's coat. His expression turned serious. "Animals are generally more sensitive to environmental changes than humans. Blackie's mutation is likely signaling that this winter will be colder than usual. With no electricity or heating now, I suggest everyone wear extra layers to sleep and place more blankets, furs, and other insulating materials near the windows."

Cecilia was pleased with Arthur's response. She took Blackie back to her apartment for a meal.

Tonight, she planned to have something spicy.

With so few people left in the building, she no longer had to hide the strong smell.

After finishing her meal and collecting the day's output from the Recycler in her space, it was past 10 PM.

Interestingly, today's supplies were also Ice Stage related: two pairs of heavy wool socks.

Farm No.1 had two large bulges again. Nobody knew what strange things would grow next.

That night, Cecilia placed a fur coat beside her bed and kept the stove filled with firewood, ready for the Ice Stage.

She didn't expect to be woken up by Blackie licking her face.

Opening her eyes, she saw Blackie's urgent expression.

She felt freezing cold, her hands and feet like ice.

Grabbing a thermometer, she saw the room temperature was already 13 degrees below zero.

She hastily pulled on the fur coat and rushed to light the stove.

After getting the fire going, she realized Jet was missing.

"Where's Jet?" she asked anxiously, looking at Blackie who remained on the bed.

Blackie shifted slightly, revealing Jet curled beneath his belly.

Jet let out a weak chirp.

Cecilia hurried over and found Jet shivering violently, weakened by the cold.

Jet's mutation had started later, and he couldn't fully adapt to the sudden temperature drop.

"Blackie, thank goodness!"

Blackie had noticed the temperature drop first, picked up the semi-conscious Jet, and kept him warm under his belly. Then he had worked hard to wake Cecilia.

Cecilia considered sending Jet to her space but noticed tiny downy feathers beginning to sprout on Jet's body.

It was a change that hadn't been there before bedtime.

He was rapidly mutating to adapt to the weather.

Cecilia quickly pulled out a pile of tasty food and placed it before Jet. "Eat up, quick! Mutate faster! Once you're done, you'll be fine."

She instructed Blackie to stay put and care for Jet until the mutation was complete.

With the stove burning, Cecilia bundled up tightly and rushed out her door.

She began pounding forcefully on Arthur's and Hailey's doors.

In her past life, it was the same: the temperature plummeted, and many people died in their sleep.

The extreme cold could cause unconsciousness, and without immediate warmth and a heat source, they might never wake up.

Arthur answered his door quickly, wrapped in a fur coat. He visibly relaxed upon seeing Cecilia. "Sudden temperature drop. I was just about to come get you."

Cecilia nodded without elaborating and started banging on Hailey's door.

But after several minutes, there was no response.

"Step aside," Arthur said, gently moving Cecilia away from the door.

He raised his foot and kicked Hailey's door open.

They rushed in to find Hailey already unconscious on her bed.

Cecilia pretended to rummage in her pocket, pulling out a small thermos. She lifted Hailey and fed her warm water, sip by sip.

Hailey soon regained consciousness but was still disoriented.

Cecilia then wrapped her in a nearby fur coat.

Arthur, meanwhile, brought a gas canister and stove from the kitchen, quickly lighting it.

After a while, Hailey fully regained her senses, realizing she had just brushed with death. She looked gratefully at Cecilia and Arthur. "Thank you... I didn't expect the temperature to drop so fast."

Cecilia didn't say much.

Hailey's health had improved lately, but she was still too weak.

Combined with the intense training with Arthur during the day, Cecilia had noticed Hailey seemed drained when they parted earlier and had reminded her to eat more to replenish her energy.

"Well, looks like all three of us made it," Cecilia said with a rare smile.

The Ice Stage was harsh, but the first night was the hardest.

Such sudden, brutal cold was most likely to claim lives.

That night, countless people who thought government rescue meant safety died in their own homes.

But that wasn't the worst of it.

The next day, Cecilia was surprised to hear Rebecca rushing to the 66th floor, banging on the stairwell security door. "Save Alexander! He's dying! Please, I beg you!"

Cecilia snorted coldly. Finally.

She opened the door. Rebecca stood there, face pale with panic. A flash of jealousy and greed crossed her eyes when she saw the fur coat Cecilia wore.
