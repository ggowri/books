Chapter 68 Trouble Found Her Anyway

Fatigue settled over Cecilia by the end of the bath.

Glancing at the time, she knew bedtime was near. Lifting Blackie and Jet out, she dried them thoroughly before cleaning herself up.

The soak had been incredibly relaxing, and the slight murkiness of the water in the barrel confirmed her suspicion—it had boosted her physical fitness significantly.

Midway through the bath, Blackie and Jet had already grown tired.

Cecilia hurried to blow-dry them, tucking both into bed afterward.

Disposing of the used water down the toilet or drain was out of the question; with everyone else without water, her ability to flush would only spark jealousy from other floors.

After a moment's thought, she entered the Space and made her way to the Recycler.

To her mild surprise, it had produced a pack of compressed biscuits—nothing out of the ordinary.

Moving to the machine's back, she poured in all the bathwater, curious to see what it would spit out the next day.

With that task done, Cecilia returned to her apartment and fell into a deep sleep.

By the time she woke the next morning, chaos had erupted throughout the building.

The man who'd slept with Phoebe was dead, and Bryan's group was beating her, accusing her of the murder.

No signs of a struggle marred the room, making his death all the more suspicious.

Stripped naked, Phoebe was being assaulted on the 65th floor.

Her miserable screams and pleas echoed through the building.

"Please, stop beating me! I don't know anything—I didn't kill him! Do whatever you want to me, just stop!"

The cries were sickening to hear.

Once Cecilia learned the cause of the commotion, surprise mixed with indifference settled over her.

She had no desire to intervene—whoever killed the man deserved to die, and whether Phoebe was innocent or not, she got exactly what she deserved.

As she turned to head back to her apartment, Hailey came into view behind her.

"How are you feeling?" Cecilia asked.

A cold laugh escaped Hailey. "Feeling... great."

The wound on Hailey's face looked gruesome. Despite the medicine, it showed little sign of healing, leaving one half of her face beautiful and the other twisted like a monster.

Cecilia said nothing, only patting Hailey's shoulder gently.

Magic Spring water was at her disposal, capable of healing Hailey's wound instantly, but she chose not to use it.

Revealing such a "miracle cure" carried too much risk, and even with Hailey staying with them, Cecilia still wanted to test her character.

Too many times in her previous life had kindness been met with betrayal—she wasn't foolish enough to invite trouble.

However, trouble found her anyway.

After about an hour, the noise downstairs died down.

Standing by the window, Cecilia watched as the men dragged the corpse to the edge and threw it off the building.

The body was swept away by the rain in an instant.

The acid rain was lighter than usual, leading Cecilia to guess it would stop soon.

Cheers erupted from other floors, with people talking excitedly, hoping the disaster would end shortly.

She was playing with Blackie in her apartment when a loud, aggressive knock echoed at the door.

An eyebrow raised on Cecilia's face. She'd made a show of force the day before, yet someone still dared to provoke her.

Grabbing a machete and signaling for Blackie to follow, she opened the door.

Arthur and Hailey were also outside, having been disturbed by the noise.

The three of them walked toward the stairwell, expecting Bryan's group once more.

Instead, they found an old woman, Ashley Lyons, whom they'd met before, wearing a garish floral robe and holding a five or six-year-old boy.

The racket came from the boy, who was kicking the door repeatedly.

"What do you think you're doing?" Cecilia stepped forward, her voice sharp.

The boy froze for a moment at her question, glanced at Ashley, and—encouraged by her gaze—kicked even harder.

A chuckle escaped Cecilia as she patted Blackie's shoulder.

Having soaked in the Magic Spring and acid rain mixture the night before, Blackie had grown even larger.

It lunged forward instantly, growling fiercely through the security door bars.

The boy saw a massive maw closing in, nearly biting his leg, and the deafening roar sent him stumbling backward onto his bottom, where he burst into tears.

"Grandma, Grandma! Waaah!"

Ashley jumped in fright, patting her chest as she lifted the boy.

Pointing at Cecilia, she shouted angrily, "You—you're bullying the elder and a child! The acid rain is stopping, and help will be here soon! I'll call the police, have you arrested for assault, disturbing the peace, and occupying public space!"

Cecilia suddenly understood.
