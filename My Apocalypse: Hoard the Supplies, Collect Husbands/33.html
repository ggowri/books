Chapter 33 A Genuine Girl

Before that happened, once people ran out of food and clean water, once the power grid failed and medicine ran out, a lot of lives would be lost.

That morning, Blackie stared at her with that look that clearly said he wanted to go outside. Cecilia checked his paw from last night and saw it had healed up completely.

It had to be the Magic Spring water. That thought made her relax a little. She scratched Blackie's big head, grabbed his leash, and didn't even bother with an umbrella as they headed outside.

Out on the street, everyone was completely bundled up, like they were terrified of exposing even an inch of skin.

Looked like the whole "acid rain" theory had really spread.

Cecilia and Blackie, walking around without any protection at all, probably looked like weirdos.

But looking around the city, there were plenty of other people like her who didn't buy into the whole acid rain apocalypse thing.

Some were even standing out in the rain on purpose, testing whether it was actually acidic or not.

She couldn't walk him for too long though. She didn't want skin lesions showing up this early.

The plan was to take short walks every day and build up her resistance to whatever was in the rain.

Later on, when everyone was dealing with acid rain and showing corrosive wounds on their skin, having a few injuries herself wouldn't look suspicious at all.

When Cecilia got back, she checked the school group chat. Someone had posted videos from campus showing people flooding the school store and loading up on snacks.

Another person mentioned that the cafeteria had completely sold out of bagels and rolls because students were buying them in bulk to stockpile.

The campus mail room was buried under mountains of food deliveries. Apparently, all those students who'd read apocalypse novels actually had some survival instincts after all.

She opened Instagram and saw that Julia, who barely ever posted anything, had just uploaded a bunch of photos.

The images showed piles and piles of food and warm clothing stacked everywhere. Julia's caption read: [Who bought me all this? Come forward lol.]

Cecilia smiled when she saw that Julia had received everything.

She'd ordered a whole batch of supplies and had them shipped to Julia at school as a way of paying her back for all her help. But seeing the post made her nervous, so she quickly sent Julia a message.

Cecilia: [You should delete that post and hide all that stuff. Don't let anyone know you have it. The whole doomsday thing is getting really popular right now, and who knows what might happen next.]

Julia responded instantly: [Wait, did you buy this?]

Cecilia didn't reply, but when she checked Instagram again, Julia had already deleted the post. A few minutes later, Julia called her.

Cecilia answered, and Julia got straight to the point. "I'm returning everything today. I can't accept all this from you."

Cecilia sighed. Kids at school were still so innocent. In her past life, after years of surviving in the apocalypse and seeing every kind of betrayal and manipulation, she'd rarely met anyone this genuine anymore.

She put on her best surprised voice. "Huh? I never said I bought that stuff. I just saw your post and with all the doomsday talk going around lately, I wanted to warn you. Maybe it was some guy who has a crush on you?"

No matter how much Julia pushed, Cecilia wouldn't crack. Julia finally gave up and hung up, clearly frustrated.

But a few minutes later, she sent another text: [I've hidden everything in different spots. Whether there's an apocalypse or not, you be careful too. And thanks.]

Reading that, Cecilia felt relieved. Honestly, she really liked Julia's personality—straightforward, no nonsense, to the point. She hoped Julia would get through whatever was coming.

Cecilia spent the entire day cooking nonstop. She made a thousand sandwiches in all different varieties and packed them into containers with three sandwiches each. There was turkey and swiss, BLT, chicken salad, tuna melt, and pretty much every other flavor she could think of.

Then she moved on to the main dishes. She started with the heavier, richer stuff like pot roast, baked chicken thighs, meatloaf, and beef stew.

She already had some of these stored in the Space, but she had time to kill at home anyway, so she followed recipes and made fresh batches of everything.

She packed those in containers too, and then she cooked up some pasta and spooned it right on top of the dishes. Each container had the perfect mix of protein and carbs for one full meal.

When Cecilia wasn't cooking, she hung out with Blackie and worked on their training.

She'd noticed that after Blackie drank from the spring water in the Space, he'd gotten seriously strong.

She grabbed a wooden practice sword and they sparred together, but after just a few rounds, she was already losing ground.

By the end, she was sitting on the floor gasping for air while Blackie stood there wagging his tail at her. She shot him an annoyed look. "Could you go a little easier on me?"

Blackie definitely understood her, but he just stood there staring at her with that droopy dog face that screamed, "You're just too weak."

That pissed Cecilia off enough to grab his big fluffy face and squeeze it in frustration.

The next evening, Cecilia saw posts online saying the acid rain was actually dangerous.

Apparently, some people who didn't believe the warnings and went out without proper protection started developing red rashes on their faces after just two days.

Some of them went to dermatologists, and the doctors confirmed their skin showed clear signs of chemical burns.
