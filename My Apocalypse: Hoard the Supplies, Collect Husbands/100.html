Chapter 100 Make Her Suffer

"AHHH!"

Rebecca clutched her face and spun toward the railing.

A chunk of skin hung from the railing—her skin. Raw, bloody meat.

No time to cry about it. She scrambled up and bolted downstairs, but Frank caught her anyway.

Frank stared at the woman with half her face peeled off and frowned slightly.

"That Barnes heir's got shit taste."

Soon after, Andy was dragged up too.

Both cheating bastards got dumped at Miranda's feet.

Staring down the gun barrel, Andy cracked. Told them everything.

He said Rebecca had ordered him to hack up Alexander. Said Rebecca had seduced him even before Alexander died. Said she'd neglected Alexander those last few days, letting him get frostbite before he jumped to his death.

Rebecca wasn't having it.

Hair wild, eyes manic, she shrieked that Andy seduced her. That Andy was the one who chopped off Alexander's hands and feet.

And just like that, they went for each other's throats like starving dogs.

Miranda stood up, trembling.

She clutched the voice recorder tight, her face dark as storm clouds.

She walked up to Frank and took his gun.

Before Rebecca and Andy even noticed—BANG.

The bullet tore through Andy's skull.

White brain matter and crimson blood sprayed across Rebecca's face.

Rebecca's curses died in her throat.

She stared at the old woman who looked like the Grim Reaper herself, then dropped to her knees.

"Mrs. Barnes, Mrs. Barnes! Please! I loved Alex—why would I hurt him? It was Andy! And Cecilia! They killed him!"

Miranda gave a cold snort. She stared down at the pathetic thing groveling at her feet.

"Frank."

Frank stepped forward. "Ma'am."

"My grandson suffered." Her voice was steady. "Make her suffer a hundred times worse. Three days. Here. Send me photos when it's done."

"Yes, ma'am."

Miranda ignored Rebecca's wailing behind her and walked out with the other men.

Frank cracked his knuckles. The sharp pop-pop-pop made Rebecca's skin crawl.

The two goons with him closed in.

Rebecca scrambled backward.

Then she bit her lip and ripped off her shirt.

"Please, please... do whatever you want, just don't kill me! Please!"

She tried to look helpless, posed her legs like she was some kind of seductress.

But the men burst out laughing.

"This ugly bitch thinks she can seduce us? Ha!"

"Someone get her a mirror!"

Rebecca froze.

Ugly?

She had been pretty her whole life. Proud of it. No one had ever called her ugly.

Then it hit her.

Her face. It was ruined.

She caught her reflection in the window—skin peeled back, splattered with Andy's brain matter and blood.

She looked like a demon.

"Oh, we'll play with you," Frank said lightly. "Don't worry."

He drew a knife. The blade caught the light.

Soon, Rebecca's screams echoed through the entire building.

******

Three days later.

On a bed soaked in blood lay what barely resembled a woman.

Not a patch of intact skin remained.

Her hands and feet were gone.

What remained was a mutilated, trembling torso—kept barely alive.

Beside the bed lay several syringes.

The drugs inside had kept her alive through three days of hell. Without them, she'd have died on day one.

But they'd kept her conscious. Kept her feeling everything.

The thing on the bed? Rebecca.

Frank had followed Miranda's orders to the letter. Three days in that building. Three days of making Rebecca suffer.

And when it was over, he photographed every blood-soaked detail.

******

Rewind to the day Miranda left.

After ordering Frank to make Rebecca suffer hell, Miranda was carried downstairs.

Once the rage cooled, her mind cleared.

She kept replaying it—Arthur and Cecilia side by side, armed and ready.

"Hmph. Hugh's bastard kid's got skills, I'll give her that," she muttered through clenched teeth.

Then something clicked.

When Rebecca and Andy were tearing into each other, they'd said Cecilia ordered them to hack off Alexander's hands and feet!

Rage boiled over. Miranda's fists clenched as she cursed Cecilia in her mind.

But she knew better. No one started trouble on someone else's turf.

Right now, Cecilia and Arthur owned the 66th floor. Hell, the whole damn building.

Miranda was pissed, sure. But not stupid.

Downstairs, her men asked if she really meant to leave Cecilia a vehicle.

Miranda glared up at the top floor and sneered. "Leave it. Why wouldn't we? What, the Barnes family can't afford one car now?"

They left behind a modified SUV. Just like they'd promised—didn't touch a thing inside.

Miranda took one last look at the building. Tall. Sturdy. Walls barely touched by the acid rain.

That surprised her.

Then her eyes narrowed. A cold smile twisted her lips.

"Think you're safe hiding up there? Think again."
