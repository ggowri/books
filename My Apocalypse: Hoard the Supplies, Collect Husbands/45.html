Chapter 45 Recycler

She stepped closer and studied the machine again, silently cursing the Space for playing dirty, handing her a so-called user guide that barely explained anything at all.

When she noticed the red and green buttons, she instinctively pressed the green one.

The machine suddenly began to vibrate.

Startled, Cecilia jumped back a step.

Blackie, who had been sticking close to her the whole time, stayed completely calm. It even walked around the machine twice.

After about ten seconds, the shaking stopped, and a panel at the front snapped open.

Cecilia stepped forward and saw a full case of bottled water sitting inside.

She smiled, "Not bad. This random drop is actually pretty great."

At that moment, she finally relaxed.

The Space claimed it could turn junk into random supplies, but she hadn't truly believed it until she saw it with her own eyes.

Water was one of the most critical resources there was. A throw pillow, on the other hand, was worthless trash in the apocalypse. This trade was a steal.

Just as Cecilia happily reached out to lift the case of water, Blackie, who had been circling nearby, noticed the flashing red button.

Its big head tilted back and forth, as if the button were some fascinating new toy.

Then, copying what Cecilia had done earlier, it stretched out a paw and pressed it.

The machine started shaking again.

Cecilia's hands were already inside the opening, but ten years of survival instincts kicked in. She yanked herself back immediately.

At that exact moment, the lid above slammed shut.

Cecilia broke out in a cold sweat. If she hadn't moved fast enough, half her body might have been crushed inside the machine.

Meanwhile, Blackie, the clear culprit, sat off to the side looking completely innocent. It wore that serious, blank dog expression, squatting there as if nothing had happened.

Gritting her teeth, Cecilia marched over and grabbed its ear. "Was this your doing?"

Blackie's face instantly fell. It rolled its eyes at her, flat-out refusing to admit fault.

Cecilia was so mad she could barely stand it, yet she had no real way to deal with this oversized troublemaker.

In the end, all she could do was decide to cut its dog food in half tonight.

While Cecilia and Blackie were bickering, the machine behind them had already settled down.

From the intake side—the end where trash was fed in—the machine made a soft puff, and the throw pillow Cecilia had tossed in yesterday was spat out, completely unharmed.

It hit her square in the face.

Cecilia was stunned.

It took her a moment to process what had just happened.

"So, the green button starts the machine. And the red button..." She turned to Blackie and asked angrily, "You pressed the red one just now, didn't you?"

Blackie turned its head away and ignored her.

Cecilia raised her voice. "Did you or did you not?"

Blackie drooped its face. It walked over, placed a paw next to the red button, and mimed pressing it.

Cecilia finally got it. "The red button returns the item."

With that in mind, she put the pillow back into the machine and pressed the green button again.

This time, nothing happened.

Then she remembered the manual had mentioned it could only be used once a day, and that any junk put in would only turn into supplies the next day.

Even though Blackie's little blunder had technically used up today's chance, Cecilia was still in a good mood.

With this thing around, even the dull days of the apocalypse would come with a daily surprise.

And more importantly, it added another layer of security.

No matter how careful she was, something could always slip through the cracks.

And if the one thing she missed ended up being spat out by this machine, that would be just perfect.

After finishing her inspection of the machine, Cecilia walked back toward the front of the house.

The display screen was still floating in midair, wrapped in an air of mystery.

She reached out and tapped the option labeled "Happy Farm."

Almost immediately, a description popped up on the screen.

[Happy Farm.]

[Level: 2]

[Function: Plot One can grow anything except living flesh. Based on the properties of the item planted, it will randomly produce resources. During Space upgrades, planted items may undergo mutations. Farm Plot Two can only grow plants, but it shortens growth time and increases yield.]

[Limit: Overuse will cause the farm to enter dormancy.]

[Note: Happy Farm—guaranteed to keep you happy.]

Cecilia read the final line and sighed to herself again. This system really had a mischievous streak.

Still, she was genuinely happy—thrilled, even.

Plot One had to be the area she had previously named "Black-Blue Soil."

Now that she had seen the system's explanation, everything finally clicked. No wonder she had once planted grass and ended up with hemp rope and planted bread only to get a rabbit. This Plot One was anything but ordinary.

Planting grass and getting hemp rope meant the output was based on the grass' properties. Although hemp rope wasn't exactly useful, it couldn't be eaten or drunk.

But in the apocalypse, rope could be turned into a weapon, used to tie up gear, or serve a hundred other purposes. It definitely had its uses.

As for the bread, it just so happened to be planted during a Space upgrade, which triggered a mutation. That was how that strange-looking rabbit had come into being.
