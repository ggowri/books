Chapter 63 She-devil

Cecilia had noticed them earlier—besides the onlookers, several tattooed men stood nearby, holding weapons and staring with ill intent.

These must be the "buddies" Bryan had mentioned, likely his unsavory friends.

Word was they'd run businesses together before and all lived in the building.

Cecilia snorted coldly, turned around, and walked back inside without a word.

Bryan, left outside, preened with satisfaction.

He assumed Cecilia was scared, so he shouted after her, "From now on, everyone pays protection fees—two pounds of wheat per person per month, or equivalent food. Otherwise, I won't be nice! Of course, pretty girls like you can pay in other ways. We guys would be happy to..."

His words cut off abruptly.

The "scared" Cecilia he'd watched go inside reappeared, this time wielding a massive cleaver—half a foot long and five inches wide, the kind used for butchering hogs.

Her slender frame clashed sharply with the imposing weapon.

A cold smile played on her lips as she walked to the door and swung it open fully.

Bryan froze under her intense aura, stepping back instinctively. "W-what do you want?" He waved frantically for the other men to join him.

Without a word, before Bryan could react, she kicked him square in the chest.

He stumbled backward, stunned by the sheer strength coming from such a small woman.

She didn't stop there. Lifting the cleaver, she brought it down on Bryan's shoulder without a second thought.

Blood spurted instantly, splattering her face.

The other men moved to rush in but were blocked by Blackie and Arthur, who emerged from the apartment right after.

"Ah! Ahh! Bitch!" Bryan screamed like a stuck pig.

Cecilia walked over and pressed her foot down on his injured shoulder, choking off his further insults.

At that moment, she looked like a grim reaper risen from hell.

"Your name's Bryan, right? I'm Cecilia. Next time you see me, keep your head down.

"Maybe then I'll spare your life.

"Stay out of my way—don't let me see you again. Otherwise, I'll finish you off for good. Scum."

She spat on the ground.

"Don't mistake my kindness for weakness."

Bryan and his goons fled in terror.

As he left, his eyes were wide with shock and disbelief, but his face twisted with hatred.

Cecilia paid no mind, flicking the cleaver to send a spray of blood flying—soaking one of the goons helping Bryan and making him jump.

From that day on, Cecilia's reputation spread like wildfire.

The entire building knew about the crazy woman on the 66th floor—beautiful but ruthless, quick to draw a knife over the smallest slight, a she-devil straight from hell.

Cecilia didn't care; she even relished it.

The more feared she was, the fewer people dared to bother her.

Arthur, watching from behind, stared at her slender back in silence. Admiration and thoughtfulness flickered in his eyes.

Bryan wouldn't be causing trouble anytime soon.

Cecilia had spared his life only because the apocalypse had just begun—she wasn't ready to kill so soon.

The acid rain would stop in a few days, and official rescue teams were supposed to arrive. Killing now might bring unnecessary trouble.

But when people thought the rain was over and normalcy would return, a blizzard would engulf the entire region.

That was when the authorities would be truly powerless, and the long-awaited "rescue" would become a farce.

And men like Bryan? He'd be the first on her list.

After clearing all the rooms, Cecilia and Arthur sealed every pipe on the floor with cement, preventing mutated creatures from crawling up and securing their safety.

Over the past two days, Cecilia had found Arthur trustworthy and methodical.

She was quite satisfied with her neighbor, though she resolved to keep observing him.

Exhausted from another busy day, Cecilia decided to spend the evening and the next day relaxing—no work, no worries.

The day after, she'd stay home to unpack the packages and safes she'd stored in the Space from the opposite building.

As for the Space, she'd noticed the pink crystal beads hadn't triggered an upgrade—their energy was insufficient, only replenishing the Space slightly.

Cecilia wasn't in a hurry.

To her surprise, the Recycler spat out a bulletproof vest that day.

She figured the pink beads' energy had enhanced the unit, yielding this pleasant surprise.

Thrilled, she stashed the vest away and tossed an unused broom into the unit, planning to check the results tomorrow.

She also visited the farm and breeding area—seedlings had grown noticeably, and a large bulge had formed in Plot One's soil, likely another effect of the pink beads. Cecilia touched it but found it wasn't mature yet, needing a few more days.
