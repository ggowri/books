Chapter 192 Arthur's Ring

Arthur looked at Cecilia. "Before we set off, there's something I want to tell you."

As he spoke, he reached into his pocket and took out an old ring with a rather antique design.

Cici's pupils contracted at once.

Because she could feel the Space at her chest beginning to throb violently.

It was a dimension ring.

"This is…"

Doing her best to suppress Sprite, who was fluttering about inside her chest and urging her to swallow the ring whole, Cici forced herself to sound as though she did not recognise it.

"This is probably the item the Barnes family have been searching for," Arthur said.

Cici lifted her eyes to him. "How did you get it?"

Arthur looked at her, a faintly crooked smile tugging at his lips. "Come to think of it, I ought to take responsibility for your parrot getting hurt this time. I stole this ring from the Barnes family. Without it, they went mad trying to find another piece of dimensional jewellery."

Cici was stunned.

In that instant, a thousand thoughts rushed through her mind.

Why was Arthur showing her something as important as the dimension ring?

If he had stolen it from the Barnes family, would they be able to trace it back to him?

Did he know that she also had a space accessory? Was this a test?

But what came next was even harder to believe.

"Cecilia, tonight's operation is dangerous. If I die, remember to find my body, take this, and look after Blaise Hart for me."

Arthur smiled at her, then calmly began explaining exactly how the dimension ring worked.

Over this period of time, he had already worked out how to use it.

A drop of blood would make it recognise an owner.

With a thought, you could store things inside or take them back out.

And with another act of will, you could sever the bond between yourself and the ring.

Earlier, he had already broken his own connection to it, simply so he could show Cici how the ring recognised a new owner.

He took a knife and sliced a small cut into his fingertip, then let a drop of blood fall onto the ring.

Very quickly, the ring absorbed it.

At the same moment, Cici felt Sprite settle down at last.

"See? That's how it binds itself to someone. If I'm right, then if I die, the ring will break its connection to me. When that happens, you can drip your blood onto it and it'll be yours. In exchange, you look after Blaise."

Arthur looked at her as he carefully slid the dimension ring onto his finger. Then he pulled on a glove over it, hiding it from view.

"Arthur, aren't you afraid I might try to take it from you?" Cici asked seriously.

"Blaise is the most important teammate I have. He matters to me as much as family. If I had to choose between this ring and Blaise, I'd choose Blaise. If I trust you with Blaise, then I'm not worried you'll try to steal the ring."

Arthur met her gaze, utterly serious.

In truth, he had been observing this girl for quite some time.

She was clever, independent, powerful, and guided by her own principles.

She always repaid a grudge, and she could be ruthless with her enemies, even cruel.

But when it came to those who had shown her kindness, she never owed them anything for long.

At the same time, she kept everyone at a distance.

After all this time, Arthur had come to feel that Cecilia was entirely capable of looking after herself. Perhaps she did not even need teammates or friends.

She treated the animals around her exceptionally well, better, perhaps, than she treated most people.

It was obvious that she trusted her pets more than she trusted anyone around her.

He did not know what she had experienced to become this sort of person.

Arthur was different. He could doubt people, stay wary, keep his guard up. But once he recognised someone as a teammate, he never questioned them.

In countless battles balanced between life and death, he had survived by entrusting his back to Blaise.

That was why he was determined to bring Blaise back.

Because Blaise was someone he had trained himself, and someone who had saved his life many times over.

At the same time, he wanted to get closer to Cici.

Cici looked at Arthur and gave a faint nod. "I understand. I'll make sure I cover Blaise and bring him back."

The weather was bitterly cold, but neither of them dressed too heavily.

Their sleeves and trouser legs were tightly fastened, and everything they wore was black, making them almost invisible in the unlit city.

Once they had gone downstairs and reached a secluded spot, Arthur produced a modified vehicle from the dimension ring.

Blackie sat in the back seat, while Cici took the passenger seat.

On the way there, she and Arthur chatted now and then, neither saying very much.

Before long, they arrived.

More than a kilometre from the enemy organisation's base, the two of them and the dog got out.

Blackie sniffed around, and once he was sure no one was nearby, he took the lead with his tail swishing behind him.

Cici and Arthur followed close behind.

The two of them and the dog kept to the edges as they moved. Along the way, they did run into a few patrols sweeping the area, but managed to avoid them every time.

The night was pitch black, after all, and the wind and snow made enough noise to cover their movements.

Arthur had clearly scouted the place in advance. He led Cici to a high point nearby.

It was an abandoned unfinished tower.

Truth be told, before the end of the world, plenty of buildings had still been under construction. Once the apocalypse came, they were all left half built, standing dark and hollow in the snow.

"You keep watch. I'll have Blaise make a run for this building. Be careful as well."

With that, Arthur headed downstairs and gradually vanished into the wind and snow.

From this spot to the enemy organisation's villa compound, there were barely two hundred metres between them.
