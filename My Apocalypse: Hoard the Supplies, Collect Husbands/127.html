Chapter 127 Quality Guaranteed

He didn't buy her story. Not completely.

But guns? That he had to investigate.

******

Cecilia left Arthur's house and headed straight home.

Things were about to get messy—she could feel it.

Might as well use this quiet time to make pet food.

Blackie's appetite was insane lately. Sure, dogs were omnivores, but all meat, all the time? Not happening. Even with her massive dog food stash, it wouldn't last at the rate he was eating. Time to bake some homemade loaves.

Then there was Jet. His bird feed was almost gone. Worse, the parrot had shot up in size lately, eating nearly as much as Blackie now.

She'd whip up a batch of parrot food using stuff wild birds actually ate.

Plus, the Space was crawling with animals—big and small. Better to prep extra now than run out later and let the Space's spiritual energy take over. Hell no. She wasn't giving it that win.

With that settled, she got to work.

First up—Blackie's grub.

Cecilia grabbed pumpkin, carrots, chicken breast, beef, fish, broccoli, cornmeal, sweet potato flour—the whole nine yards. She blitzed the chunky bits into mush, mixed it all with flour, shaped it into loaves, and tossed them in the oven.

Blackie watched from nearby, practically glued to her heels.

Jet swooped in to cause chaos, perching on the bowl's edge. He pecked at the meat paste, then the flour, scarfing down quite a bit with each peck.

Cecilia had to add more ingredients.

At first, Blackie was cool with sharing. But when Jet wouldn't quit, the dog lunged and tackled him right off the table.

Jet—instantly KO'd!

The bowl? Also KO'd! It flipped and crashed to the floor.

All that perfectly mixed dough... face-planted on the ground, flattened into a sad, grimy pancake.

"Jet! Blackie! Bedroom! NOW!"

Cecilia finally lost it.

Both troublemakers slunk away. She salvaged what she could—tossed the dirty bits, kneaded the clean parts back together.

By day's end, she'd cranked out over twenty pans of dog loaves.

Blackie demolished six on the spot—half a pan gone just like that.

For Jet's bird food, Cecilia was too tired to shape it into tiny pieces.

She just pinched off little chunks. Jet was big enough now—he should be able to swallow them.

Wrong.

Jet choked on the first one!

He sat there gagging, eyes rolling back, stuck between swallowing and spitting it out.

Before Cecilia could rush over to help, Blackie pounced and pinned Jet under his paw.

The food shot right out of Jet's mouth.

Though by the look of him, Blackie had knocked half the life out of the poor bird.

In the end, Cecilia had to break all the bird food in half again before Jet could actually eat it.

"Good! So good! Really good!"

After a few bites, Jet flapped his wings and squawked, circling the room in pure joy.

Cecilia surveyed everything she'd made today. Pretty satisfying.

She cleaned up her tools, planning to make more tomorrow if she had time.

Then she headed for the Space, eager to check on what she'd planted and see what new loot the trash processor had coughed up.

But the second she stepped into the Space, she froze.

Plot One's massive black soil was completely covered—bags upon bags of pet food everywhere.

Bird food. Dog food. Feed for the herbivores on the farm.

All in clear bags with labels screaming: "Space-Produced Dog Food," "Space-Produced Parrot Food," "Space-Produced Rabbit Food," "Space-Produced Livestock Feed"...

Even the guinea pigs in the barn got their own packages of "Space-Produced Guinea Pig Food"!

Cecilia bent down and walked through Plot One, checking each bag of food before collecting them all.

With mixed feelings churning in her gut, she headed over to the trash processor.

She lifted the lid. The two batches she'd tossed in yesterday? Now transformed into a massive bag labeled "Space-Produced Dog Loaves" and another labeled "Space-Produced Parrot Feed."

These weren't like the mass-produced stuff from the farm.

Below the bold "Space-Produced" logo was fine print.

"Space-Produced—Quality Guaranteed. This pet food is healthy, filling, and replenishes energy used during ability evolution! Surprised? Shocked?"

In that moment, Cecilia could practically see a smug clown doing a victory dance right in her face.

Pushing every last button she had.

She gripped the bags tight, jaw clenched.

"Damn Space! You're doing this on purpose, aren't you?"

Right then, Cecilia was convinced her Space had some kind of smartass soul living in it.

It knew she'd spent all day making pet food. So of course it had to mock her by producing this stuff—and rub it in her face that Space-Produced beat homemade. Like it was saying: Give up already and bow down to me!

Cecilia gritted her teeth but carefully packed away both bags.

Though not before grabbing a loaf for Blackie and a handful of feed for Jet.

The second she left the Space, both animals charged at her before she could even move.
