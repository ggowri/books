Chapter 35 The Internet Went Out

Almost immediately, a bunch of young guys in the group started popping up.

Andy, Building A, 6F: [Wow, gorgeous! What floor are you on? Since we're all stuck inside anyway, want to chat and get to know each other?]

Bryan, Building B, 15F: [Do you have a boyfriend? I'm single, financially stable, and would love to get to know you better.]

Rebecca didn't respond to any of them, but Cecilia could already imagine how many guys were probably sliding into her DMs right now.

Two minutes later, a notification popped up in the group: [Alexander has been invited to join the chat by Rebecca.]

Cecilia raised an eyebrow.

Alexander is moving in too?

Rebecca quickly tagged Alexander and then made a big show of their relationship in the group chat: [Hey everyone, this is my fiancé, Alexander. We just moved in together on the fifth floor of Building A. Hope we can all be good neighbors!]

Soon after, both Rebecca and Alexander changed their display names in the group to "Rebecca, Building A, 5F" and "Alexander, Building A, 5F."

Seeing their floor made Cecilia smirk. This was going to be interesting. How long did it take for the fifth floor to get completely flooded again?

She couldn't remember exactly, but it shouldn't be too long. And this apocalypse was clearly hitting harder and faster than the last one. There was definitely going to be some drama ahead.

The day after Rebecca and Alexander moved in, the internet went out in their apartment complex.

Suddenly, Cecilia could hear people coming out of their apartments and knocking on neighbors' doors looking for information.

People were so used to having the internet and being able to share news online. Now they couldn't leave their homes because of the dangerous acid rain outside, and without any way to get information from the internet, everyone was starting to panic.

Then someone knocked on her door too.

Before the person even reached the door, Blackie was already on alert. He jumped up and ran over, his whole body tensed up and ready to charge forward at any moment.

What was even more impressive was that Blackie covered the fifteen or twenty feet from his bed to the door in just a few seconds, barely making a sound. His ears stood straight up, showing how focused he was.

Cecilia walked over to the door and gently scratched Blackie's head as a reward and to calm him down. The knocking kept going, sounding pretty urgent.

She opened the inner door but left the security screen door locked. Standing outside was a young guy who looked pretty sketchy.

"Hey, is your internet still working? I've been going around asking everyone, and they're all saying it's down."

He sounded polite enough, but his eyes kept darting around, trying to see inside her apartment. Blackie must have picked up on the guy's bad intentions because he started making this low growling sound, like a warning.

That finally got the guy's attention. "Holy crap, you've got a huge dog! How come I've never seen him before?"

Cecilia figured she'd been living here for a while now, but she hadn't met everyone in the building yet.

She just smiled politely, not really wanting to chat with him. "Our internet's out too. I think everyone's connection is down."

"Oh, well, there's been a lot of talk online about the world ending and all that. Have you stocked up on supplies? If you haven't, you should probably go out and get some stuff now." While he was saying this, his eyes were still wandering around, trying to scope out what was inside her apartment.

Cecilia had set up a massive cabinet right by the entrance, so there was no way he could see what was inside. She'd bought the cabinet to hide the camp stove in her living room, since most of her supplies were stashed in the Space anyway.

"Is there anything else you need?" Cecilia asked flatly.

Maybe Blackie picked up on the coldness in her voice, because he started letting out a low, threatening growl. The man took one look at Blackie's hostile glare and made a quick exit.

Cecilia frowned slightly as she watched him go. Something told her this guy was going to cause trouble once things got worse, and she'd need to keep an eye out for him.

After he left, she bent down and scratched Blackie behind the ears. "Good boy."

With a dog that size in the house, even if the guy had bad intentions, he'd think twice about messing with her, at least in the beginning. Blackie was the perfect deterrent.

That day, Cecilia ignored whatever chaos was happening outside and spent her time cooking while the gas still worked.

After several days of nonstop meal prep, she'd finally filled up all the disposable plastic containers in the Space. They were stacked on the shelves, packed with every kind of food she could think of.

When evening came, Cecilia decided to experiment with the black farmland in the Space and see what else she could figure out.

She also still hadn't put that emerald bracelet into the Space yet. She'd been holding off because the Space usually upgraded every few days, never two days in a row, and she didn't want to risk something going wrong.

But enough time had passed now, so it should be fine.

Cecilia pulled out the emerald bracelet and turned it over in her hands for a bit. Honestly, she loved this bracelet. It was gorgeous.

But the Space seemed to love it too, because it kept vibrating like it was begging her to give the emerald bracelet to itself.

She rolled her eyes and tossed the bracelet into the Space without hesitation. Then she stepped inside with it.

The emerald bracelet dissolved into specks of glowing blue light, drifting through the air like tiny stars. It was absolutely beautiful.
