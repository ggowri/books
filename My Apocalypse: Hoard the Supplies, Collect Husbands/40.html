Chapter 40 Valais Blacknose Sheep

Cecilia couldn't see his face clearly, but just from his build alone, he'd be easy to recognize if she ever ran into him again.

At the same time, her eyebrows drew together. This person was dangerous, and she'd need to be careful if their paths crossed.

First off, he was tall with serious strength. If it came down to a fight, even with Blackie on her side, this person would be a real threat.

Second, he could move freely through the acid rain and was bold enough to actually swim in it, which meant he had guts.

And finally, the fact that he was out scavenging supplies right now meant he had vision. He'd already figured out the apocalypse was coming and that society was about to collapse.

What Cecilia didn't expect was for the person to swim directly across the street and then enter her building. They went straight into Building A, the same one she was in.

Watching this unfold, Cecilia felt her eye twitch. Looked like they were neighbors after all.

Whatever, she'd deal with it. She'd stay out of his way as long as he stayed out of hers. As long as he didn't mess with her, she didn't really care. But she'd definitely need to stay more alert from now on.

Once the person had fully entered the building, Cecilia let out a breath. She waited a little longer and didn't see him come back out, but she was still on edge.

She walked over to Blackie and pointed at the window. "Blackie, how about you keep watch here? If you see that person come out again, come get me right away."

She had the Space, so, worst-case scenario, she and Blackie could just hide inside it and stay completely safe. What she was really worried about was that person sneaking up behind her and catching her off guard.

Blackie's face drooped as he sat down without a word, his big round dog eyes locked on the window like a little soldier standing guard.

Now that she thought about it, Blackie seemed to have grown even taller over the past few days. When Cecilia sat down now, Blackie's head reached her shoulder.

Seeing Blackie so focused made her feel better. She checked the time and saw it was almost two thirty. She planned to leave by six thirty at the latest, which gave her four more hours, but she still had ten whole floors to search. She needed to pick up the pace.

On the third floor, Cecilia got lucky. In one of the small rooms, she found a whole stash of propane tanks, at least a dozen of them.

This building didn't have natural gas lines and still used propane for cooking. There was a small communal kitchen on the third floor that served lunch to the whole building.

Cecilia moved all the propane tanks into the Space.

Once the apocalypse hit and natural gas stopped working, these would be critical supplies.

She didn't stop there either. She took the big industrial fridge from the kitchen, all the meat inside it, boxes of potatoes stacked in the corner, and all the other shelf-stable food like sweet potatoes, squash, dinner rolls, and bread. Everything went straight into the Space.

Before leaving, Cecilia swept through the kitchen and grabbed everything in sight. The big metal tables, chairs, prep stations, chef's knives—she stuffed it all into the Space without a second thought.

In her past life, by the time she'd gotten here, all the vegetables had rotted and people had already looted the bread and flour, so she'd missed out on everything.

The propane tanks had been washed away by floodwater too. At least this time around, she'd arrived early enough to claim it all.

She moved on to check out the nearby live-streaming studios and found that someone had already been through most of them.

The food sellers had been hit especially hard. Stuff like crackers, cookies, Cup Noodles, and microwaveable dinners were mostly gone. Based on what was left behind, she figured it had to be that guy from before.

From the mess he'd left, she could tell he was incredibly picky about what he took. Maybe he didn't have a dimensional space like hers and could only carry so much, so he had to be selective.

He clearly loved spicy food because all the spicy microwaveable meals were completely gone, but he'd left all the chicken noodle soup and mild beef pasta totally untouched.

As Cecilia swept through the remaining studios, her eye twitched. The apocalypse was literally happening, and this guy was still being a picky eater?

She wasn't picky at all. Just like before, she took absolutely everything, including the shelves and chairs. Nothing got left behind.

When she reached a studio that sold hoodies and down jackets, she noticed all the clothes had been rifled through. The largest sizes were clearly missing. That confirmed her earlier estimate about the guy's height. He had to be at least six-one, maybe taller.

Cecilia eventually found several studios selling skincare and makeup products.

These places were stocked with high-end stuff she'd never been able to afford before.

Skincare sets, luxury gold face masks, professional-grade beauty devices that cost thousands of dollars—the kind of equipment fancy spas used.

She didn't hesitate for a second and swept it all into the Space. Even if the world was ending, she was going to maintain some standards and keep up with her skincare routine.

On the third floor at the very end of the hallway, Cecilia discovered something completely unexpected in one of the studios. There was a sheep inside.

When she walked in, the poor thing was barely hanging on. It had finished off all its feed and hay days ago and could only manage a weak bleat when it saw her.

Cecilia took a closer look and realized she recognized this breed. It had a round black face where you could barely make out its eyes, and it was way bigger than a normal sheep with soft, fluffy curls covering its entire body.

She remembered seeing these on social media before. They were called Valais Blacknose sheep.

She walked over and gently patted its head. The sheep seemed pretty docile despite everything.
