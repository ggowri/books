Chapter 152 Electric Fence

From Barry, Cecilia learned that most of the military's forces were actually hunting down mutated animals.

During the acid rain, tons of mutated creatures and bugs had already appeared.

They just hadn't reached critical mass yet—the military was keeping them in check.

Cecilia finally understood why in her past life, stuck in the shelter during the early apocalypse, she'd never encountered these mutants.

Turns out, soldiers had been protecting them the whole time.

"Stay safe out there," Cecilia said with a smile.

"Don't worry! They're just mindless beasts. Before all this, I fought off armed criminals. The most dangerous thing in this world? Still people." Barry grinned.

After Logan left, Richard sent over a new major—Thomas Reed.

Cecilia hadn't met Thomas yet. Word was he was busy at the villa district.

She'd also heard that a few people were going to start moving in this afternoon.

Sure enough, by afternoon, chaos erupted as they moved in.

Apparently they tried to put people in Building B too, but the lower floors connecting to ground level were sealed shut. No way in.

The military was down there now, shouting up at Building B.

But the people inside acted deaf. Completely ignored them.

The military tried forcing the doors. Didn't work.

So for now, everyone got crammed into Building A.

The place turned into a madhouse.

A few hundred people—some freshly rescued, others from the villa shelter.

Cecilia, Arthur, and Hailey stood in Arthur's place, looking down through the windows as truck after truck unloaded people.

Cecilia sighed. "I was hoping to enjoy some peace during the apocalypse. Guess that's not happening."

"Let's rig up an electric fence on the 64th floor," Arthur suggested suddenly.

The idea got instant approval from Cecilia and the others.

They moved fast, before everyone finished moving in.

Calling it a "fence" was generous—more like an electric gate.

They had generators to spare. They set up two by the 64th floor stairwell, installed a pair of doors, and wrapped them in wire.

Of course, they also put up a sign: DANGER - DO NOT TOUCH.

That's when Electrician Arthur came out to play.

Cecilia watched him work—clean, practical, and he dialed the voltage to just the right level.

One touch? Thirty percent chance you're dead. Just shy of taking half your life.

Who would've guessed? The gate had barely been up an hour when a scream echoed from downstairs, followed by crackling sounds.

They'd left a walkie-talkie behind the gate.

Basically, if anyone showed up, they'd hear it.

Not that they kept it on all the time—Cecilia and the others could turn it off when they didn't feel like listening.

At first, no one wanted to go down.

They figured someone got zapped, learned their lesson, and that'd be the end of it.

But then the walkie crackled with a stream of curses.

"What bastard set this up?! Nearly fried me! Ah, there's electricity on this thing! Hey everyone, this thing's live!"

Footsteps scrambled. More cursing followed.

"Let's find a way to break through this door. There's gotta be a generator inside! We snag that, we can trade it for serious cash." Someone was already scheming.

"The government told us not to mess with anyone on the 64th floor or above."

"Screw that. I got a relative who used to live here. Says there's only three people up there. We get a few guys together, how hard can they be to handle?"

That exchange made Cecilia laugh out loud.

"See? I knew it. The second they build this damn shelter, nothing but trouble."

Cecilia called Blackie over, grabbed the knife, and waved for Hailey and Arthur.

They headed downstairs together.

Time to show these newcomers who ran the 66th floor.

On the way down, they heard one of the electricians had found insulated gloves and was about to cut the live wire causing all the trouble.

Cecilia's face went cold. She threw open the door, grabbed the insulated gloves without a word, picked up a live wire, and pressed it straight to the guy's shoulder.

He was fat—rare sight in the apocalypse.

The electricity hit. His whole body shook like jelly.

Seconds later, he collapsed, foaming at the mouth.

His hair stood straight up.

Cecilia stared down the crowd, one corner of her mouth curling up.

"Anyone else wanna try?"

The seven or eight men there froze solid.

Arthur and Hailey stepped up beside her.

"We—we're going to Colonel Reed! We're getting the military!"

The first guy who'd gotten shocked stood at the front. Realizing they were outmatched, he immediately suggested getting backup.

Sure enough, everyone agreed. They scattered downstairs like rats.

The fat guy who'd been shocked first was coming to now, getting carried down by the others.

Cecilia glanced at Arthur. "Mr. Barnes, didn't you say your electric grid was precise enough to take out half someone's life? How come these two seem... pretty fine?"
