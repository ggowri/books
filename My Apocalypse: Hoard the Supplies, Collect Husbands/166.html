Chapter 166 The Price of Cat Treats

"Um… you worked hard," Cecilia said, roughly guessing what had happened.

"Waaah… no way! Master has to compensate me, or I won't do anything anymore! I'm injured, I can't get up!"

Ari, who had just been standing there shouting at Cecilia, suddenly collapsed to the ground with a loud thump, looking weak and helpless.

Once it fell, it refused to get up.

Cecilia glanced at Blackie beside her.

Did you teach it this?

Blackie: "..."

Then Blackie bared its teeth at Ari.

But this time Ari clearly believed it had the moral high ground. Even facing Blackie's threat, it refused to get up, lying there with the attitude of someone completely unafraid of consequences.

Blackie felt its authority had been challenged. Step by step, it walked toward Ari, raising a paw as if preparing to teach it a lesson.

Cecilia quickly stepped forward and stopped Blackie. "Alright, it did do some work after all."

Besides, Ari was technically injured.

All those pine needles were still stuck across its back.

"Come here, Ari. Let me pull the pine needles out first," Cecilia said, beckoning to it.

But Ari remained sprawled on the ground, stubbornly refusing to get up. It kept throwing a tantrum, insisting on benefits and compensation.

Cecilia was so annoyed her molars hurt.

Do those spikes in its back not hurt at all?

Then she remembered that Ari was still a cat. An idea suddenly came to her, and she took out a pack of cat treats from the Space.

The moment the treat appeared, Ari shot to its feet with a whoosh.

Two shiny strings of drool hung from the corners of its mouth.

Its pair of heterochromatic eyes spun rapidly, staring intently at the cat treat in Cecilia's hand.

What Cecilia didn't expect was that Blackie also sat down beside her, no longer thinking about slapping Ari. Its dog eyes stared unblinkingly at the cat treat as well.

Cecilia was puzzled.

How good could this thing possibly taste?

She glanced down at the package in her hand and saw the words printed on it: "90% real meat content. Incredibly delicious."

She tore open one pack, and neither pair of eyes left her hand.

Cecilia opened another pack and fed one with each hand.

Blackie and Ari immediately began licking the treats from her hands.

While licking, they kept staring at each other. The more they stared, the faster they licked, as if afraid the other would finish first and come steal theirs.

Very quickly, Blackie won the first round.

After all, it had a bigger mouth.

Ari switched from licking to biting fiercely, practically shoving the treat straight into its mouth in an attempt to speed up.

But Blackie was already edging closer and closer.

Cecilia clearly saw the fighting spirit in Ari's eyes. Its mouth moved faster and faster, as if determined to finish the treat before Blackie got there.

Its entire expression practically screamed: not a single drop for that big black guy!

Cecilia nearly burst out laughing.

Just as Blackie's huge head was about to lean in, Cecilia pushed it aside with her hand. "Alright. I'll open another pack for you later."

Unexpectedly, Ari finished its treat and immediately protested when it heard that.

"This cat wants another one too!"

In the end, one cat and one dog each devoured two packs of cat treats.

Blackie narrowed its eyes slightly, looking very satisfied.

Cecilia was baffled.

What kind of magic did these cat treats contain that both cats and dogs loved them?

Maybe they were packed with some kind of high-tech additives.

Thinking about that, Cecilia stored the remaining cat treats back into the Space, deciding they definitely shouldn't be eaten too often.

After feeding them, she saw Ari sitting comfortably on the ground licking its paws, its face full of satisfaction.

However, the pile of pine needles still stuck across its back bounced slightly every time it licked its fur, which looked quite amusing.

"Come here, Ari. Let me pull the needles out and see if you're hurt," Cecilia said, waving it over.

But after finishing its grooming, Ari spun once in midair.

All the pine needles instantly disappeared.

Cecilia saw it clearly.

The pine needles had been absorbed directly into Ari's body, not shaken off.

Suddenly Cecilia realized something.

Ari had previously been able to absorb the corpses of mutated animals.

So the materials from mutated plants…

"Hehe, that was delicious!"

Ari stuck out its pink tongue and licked its lips.

Cecilia instantly understood.

That entire performance earlier had been nothing more than a scheme to trick her into giving up her cat treats.

"Ari!"

Cecilia roared.

Blackie also realized something was wrong and immediately lunged forward, ready to tackle Ari and settle both old and new grudges at once.

But Ari had been prepared. It dashed away in a flash and leapt onto the roof.

Standing on the roof, Ari strutted back and forth with elegant cat steps, looking slyly at Cecilia.

"Next time you throw a mutated plant into the Space, remember to plant it yourself. Otherwise this cat will charge interest. Hehehe~"

However, Cecilia also noticed something.

Although the Space contained all kinds of items, Ari could not freely access them.

If something thrown into the Space could be absorbed or utilized by the Space itself, the Space would take it away directly, and that portion of energy had nothing to do with Ari.

Ari could only occasionally sneak a tiny bit for itself.

As for the supplies stored in the Space, Ari couldn't touch them at all without Cecilia's permission.

The only things it could do were help collect by-products produced inside the Space and assist with the normal operation of the farms, livestock areas, and the Recycler.

In other words, it functioned more like a housekeeper.
