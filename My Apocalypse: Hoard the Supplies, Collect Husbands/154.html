Chapter 154 Dead Weight

From what Cecilia had learned about Arthur, he wouldn't let them get screwed over.

And if he said he knew where the machine tools were, he could find a few himself.

So yeah. He could handle this.

"We want one percent of everything the machine tools produce. Like shares."

Cecilia's eyes lit up. Smart.

Thomas thought it over. "I need to run this by General Caldwell."

Once a food production line got going, it'd pump out countless cans and products daily.

Even with the military running low on supplies, Cecilia knew about the mutant pigs. She also knew that later on, more mutant animals and plants would emerge—and the government would develop all kinds of mutant products to deal with the apocalypse.

Those products would become the main food supply.

It'd take time before the military could mass-produce them.

But even small-scale production? More than enough to feed Cecilia and her crew.

"Fine. Once you've talked it over, we'll hand over the production line." Arthur nodded.

Thomas was clearly impatient. He tried to get them to give up one or two machines now.

He even offered more mutant pork. But they just refused.

No choice. Thomas said they'd have to wait while he got approval from above.

When Cecilia got home, she found the two frozen, shivering piglets already delivered to the 64th floor!

Seeing them made her heart feel warm.

They were round and chubby with pink skin. She poked one with her finger and it squealed.

She kept poking. Eventually, the little things got annoyed and huddled together, trembling and watching her warily.

Amused, Cecilia picked them up and brought them inside.

But before she could put them in the Space, Hailey knocked.

Cecilia opened the door. Hailey looked at her.

"Cecilia... I want to move out of the 66th floor."

Cecilia froze. Then it clicked.

Hailey felt useless around them.

Cecilia chatted with her briefly and learned she'd spotted a high school classmate among the new arrivals.

The guy had some pull in the military—a squad leader. They'd dated back in the day.

Today, when he saw her ruined face, he didn't flinch. Said he'd take care of her.

Hailey knew what kind of guy he was. Felt like she could trust him.

"Living on the sixty-sixth floor is great, but all I do is house-sit. I'm useless. Hell, sometimes I'm not even as helpful as Blackie. Don't wanna be dead weight anymore."

Cecilia didn't try to stop her.

Before leaving, Hailey pulled out a set of jewelry, looking embarrassed as she handed it to Cecilia.

"My grandmother left me this. Before all this, it was worth a fortune. Now... probably nothing. But I don't have anything better. Most of what I own, you already have. So I'm giving you this—it's the best thing I've got."

Cecilia's heart raced. The Space inside her was thrumming, reacting violently—like it wanted to devour the gems.

Cecilia took the jewelry and said goodbye.

Then, Hailey packed up and headed downstairs.

Cecilia didn't judge her choice. Everyone had their own path.

Hailey hadn't seen Cecilia kill anyone, but she suspected Cecilia had killed Phoebe.

These past few days with Cecilia and Arthur made her realize just how useless she was.

Next to them, she felt like dead weight.

Truth was, she'd lied to Cecilia.

She hadn't found some great connection through an old high school friend.

Today, she had run into someone from high school—a squad leader in the military.

He led a team that hunted mutant animals.

He told Hailey they were short on people. Specifically looking for smaller, slimmer women who could squeeze into tight spaces for missions.

Hailey signed up on the spot.

As a former yoga instructor, she had the flexibility and strength. She'd survived the apocalypse better than most—hadn't lost too much muscle mass.

He agreed immediately.

Hailey stashed her supplies in her old place on the 65th floor, packed a few essentials, and got ready to leave with him.

She clenched her fists tight, repeating it like a mantra in her head.

Get stronger. Get stronger. I have to get stronger.

Meanwhile, after saying goodbye to Hailey, Cecilia returned home and put the two piglets into the Space's livestock pen.

The second she entered, Ari wrapped around her.

"I want it! That thing was so good—give me more!"

It coiled straight around Cecilia's neck.

But the moment Ari latched on, two dark shapes lunged from behind Cecilia.

Ari shot off like a bullet.
