Chapter 24 Spring

A powerful energy stirred within her, spreading slowly through her limbs.

The pain was unbearable—she rolled on the ground, sweat dripping from her forehead.

When it slid into her eyes, Cecilia wiped it away, only to find her hand coated in black, stinking muck.

It wasn't just her forehead; her arms, chest, and face oozed the foul black sweat, making her gag.

The agony intensified until everything went black—she'd passed out from the pain.

When Cecilia woke up, she lay in a puddle of the black muck, her body weak and limp.

She lifted a hand to wipe herself clean and froze.

Her skin was translucent and smooth as silk.

Leaning over the stream to check her reflection, she gasped.

Beautiful!

She'd always been attractive, inheriting her parents' best features, but now her skin was even smoother, and her aura was ethereal—as if she'd become more delicate and elegant.

Staring at the stream, Cecilia knew she'd struck gold again—the Space had upgraded once more.

She didn't understand why, but it didn't matter.

The benefits were undeniable; her physical condition had improved drastically.

She tested this by throwing a few punches and kicking at wooden planks, which shattered instantly.

Her past-life strength and constitution were back and even stronger than before.

She felt faster, more powerful, more agile.

Cecilia followed the stream to its source behind the cottage, where a clear spring bubbled up.

The blue specks were denser here, swirling like tiny stars.

She grabbed two buckets, filled them with spring water, and carried them out of the Space.

Outside, Blackie lay quietly by the spot where she'd disappeared, only standing when he saw her.

Cecilia rolled her eyes mentally. He pretended not to care, but he'd been waiting the whole time.
Typical Blackie.

She set the buckets down beside him. "Blackie, wanna take a bath?"

He tilted his head in confusion, then jumped back abruptly at the sight of the buckets.

So he hated baths.

Too bad—she wasn't giving him a choice.

Preparing for the apocalypse, Cecilia had already bought an extra-large tub specifically for Blackie's baths.

She made several trips to the Space, filling the tub with spring water and adding enough hot water to make the temperature comfortable.

Cecilia had considered letting him drink the water, but after the pain she'd endured, she couldn't bear to make him go through it. A bath was a safer test.

Blackie resisted fiercely, almost having to be dragged into the bathroom.

Lifting him into the tub took every ounce of Cecilia's strength, leaving her soaked to the bone.

"It's just a bath, Blackie. What are you scared of?"

He turned his back on her, tail drooping—a clear sign of anger and betrayal.

Sighing, Cecilia grabbed a pet washcloth, splashed water on his back, and started scrubbing.

When her hand brushed his old scars, Blackie trembled violently, letting out a soft whimper.

His wounds had healed long ago, never bothering him even when wet. Why did the spring water hurt him now?

A thought struck her. Cecilia turned his big head to face her, voice serious. "Blackie, this isn't ordinary water. It's good for you, but it might hurt when it touches your scars. I need you to soak your whole body in the tub. Can you do that?"

Blackie stared at her silently.

Pressing down on his hindquarters gently but firmly, Cecilia said, "Lay down, Blackie. Lay in the tub."

He struggled for a moment before obeying, water overflowing and soaking her clothes completely.

However, Cecilia didn't care, smiling at his grumpy face.

He was still trembling—likely from the pain of the water on his scars.

Cecilia stroked his head softly, comforting him, and pulled out a bag of dog treats she'd prepared earlier to distract him.

Sure enough, after half the bag was gone, Blackie relaxed, his attention fully on the snacks.

In that short time, Cecilia noticed something amazing—black fur was growing on his scars.

Before, the scarred areas had been completely bald, as if the injuries were too severe to fully heal. Now, a fine layer of fuzz sprouted, soft and dark.

"Blackie! Your scars are healing! You have to hold on," she urged.

By then, he seemed to have grown used to the water, eating his snacks quietly without resisting anymore.
