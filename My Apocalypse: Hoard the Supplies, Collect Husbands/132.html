Chapter 132 The Rat King

The Rat King was testing their strength!

Too bad for it. The three rats in this wave got taken down just as easily by them.

A mournful wail rose from the rat swarm.

They stared at the corpses at Cecilia's feet—restless, seething with hatred.

Cecilia gripped her hidden blade tight. Couldn't let her guard down.

Dyson's crew hadn't killed these rats, hadn't gotten hurt by them either—which meant these things were at least evenly matched with Dyson's group.

"Squeak!"

The Rat King let out another sharp cry, more urgent and furious than before.

But nothing happened.

No rats stepped forward. No movement at all.

Then the wind picked up.

It howled past, roaring in their ears.

The stench from the rats in the silo hit them full force.

Cecilia glanced at Blackie—body tense, ears standing straight up, nose twitching nonstop.

She knew his hearing and sense of smell were razor-sharp. But right now, the wind and overwhelming stink made it impossible to pick out subtle changes. He was nervous. Anxious.

Cecilia's mind raced. Something's wrong. Something's definitely happening.

Suddenly, Blackie jumped.

He launched straight up from the ground, face twisted in pain.

"Watch your feet!" Arthur shouted.

That's when Cecilia saw them—a swarm of newborn rats, peanut-sized, pale pink with near-transparent white fuzz. In the snow, these little rats were practically invisible.

And they were tiny. Silent. No footsteps at all.

In the howling wind and choking stench, even Blackie hadn't noticed them emerge.

Now they were scrambling up Cecilia and Arthur's legs. Mouths open, revealing needle-sharp teeth—lunging for Blackie's leg.

One bite. Blood.

A swarm of newborn mice—hundreds of them, crawling everywhere.

Mice hit breeding age at around two months. Each litter? Five or six babies minimum. Sometimes up to twenty.

And they bred fast—multiple times a year. Hell, sometimes the next litter was already on the way before the current one was even weaned.

The mice in front of them must've had great living conditions near the silos.

They'd evolved early breeding capabilities. Or maybe their entire evolution path focused on reproduction.

Cecilia's clothes were high quality, but she still winced seeing two huge holes torn in her pants.

Thank god her pant cuffs were tight. If those little bastards had gotten inside...

Arthur was swatting mice off himself nonstop too.

When he realized they were too fast, he dove to the side and rolled hard across the ground, flinging them off.

At least the two of them had clothes. Blackie wasn't so lucky. The mice bit straight through to his skin.

He scraped them off with his paws, then grabbed those parasite-like things in its mouth and flung them away.

But the mice figured it out. Blackie had no defenses. He was the easy target.

More than half the swarm charged straight at him.

Blackie's massive body was quickly buried under the horde.

"Blackie!" Cecilia screamed, rushing toward him.

But in that moment, she didn't notice—the Rat King was moving again.

It let out low, rumbling squeaks—quiet, but loud enough for the others to hear.

Then five or six rats slipped out of the warehouse, creeping toward them.

A huge rat lunged at Cecilia's throat. She reacted instantly—headshot.

Another circled behind her.

Cecilia spun, blade flashing. Done.

But two more rats latched onto Blackie's neck.

Blackie roared, then copied Arthur's move—rolling on the ground.

Big mistake. He exposed his soft belly.

From the shadows, another rat shot forward, aiming straight for Blackie's vulnerable gut.

Cecilia's boot connected just in time, sending it flying.

Mid-air, the rat twisted and clamped down on her ankle!

Pain shot through her foot, but she could tell it wasn't broken. Those Space-made socks were tough as hell.

She already knew how to deal with these things.

Fire.

She reached into her pocket—actually pulling oil and a lighter from the Space.

Arthur wiped blood from his face, eyes blazing with fury.

He tossed something her way.

Cecilia caught it. A grenade.

"Ten-foot radius. Don't blow yourself up."

She nodded.

Then Arthur moved like lightning, stomping over mutant rat bodies straight toward the warehouse.

SLAM. He yanked the door shut.

Closing the rats in like fish in a barrel.
