Chapter 137 Her Enemy

What if she hadn't stuck with Bryan's crew from the start?

What if she hadn't pissed off Hailey?

What if she'd earned Cecilia's approval like Hailey had?

Would she be living in that warm room right now? With dignity? With more food than she could eat?

But right now, Cecilia looked her up and down, then just snorted. She and Arthur headed for the door, completely ignoring Phoebe as they went inside.

Panic flared. Phoebe reached out to grab Cecilia.

But mid-air, her hand changed direction—latching onto Arthur's arm instead.

"Um... could I come in and warm up? I've been standing out here all night. My legs are frozen solid. Please?"

As she spoke, Phoebe bit her lower lip, putting on her most pitiful expression.

Her go-to move.

At the villa shelter, it worked like a charm on those old creeps.

But the man in front of her? His brow furrowed deeper.

Then he hoisted all those bags of grain onto one shoulder.

Phoebe's jaw dropped. She'd never seen a man that strong.

Arthur shook his now-free arm hard, staring at the sleeve she'd touched.

The whole move—plus that look of pure disgust—said everything.

Filthy.

Phoebe couldn't believe it. He thinks I'm filthy?

Before she could process it, Arthur walked inside.

Then Cecilia came back and locked the stairwell door.

The entire time, neither of them even looked at her.

Phoebe pounded on the door, sobbing and screaming that she wanted to see Hailey.

She said she needed to apologize face-to-face. That she'd been wrong. That Hailey should forgive her for old times' sake.

After two or three minutes of this, the door finally opened.

Hailey's voice cut through.

"Phoebe, shut the hell up!"

Phoebe's lips curved into a smile.

Of course Hailey's still soft. She finally came to see me.

She took a deep breath, mentally rehearsing the "sincere apology" she'd practiced a thousand times.

But when the door swung open, she saw a twisted face filled with murderous rage.

"Scream again and I'll kill you!"

A knife flashed toward her.

Phoebe's face drained of color. She stumbled backward.

But Hailey didn't let up. She threw both doors open and charged forward, blade aimed straight at Phoebe.

"You've got some nerve coming here! Did you forget what you did to me?"

The first stab came down—straight through Phoebe's shoulder.

Phoebe screamed in agony, begging Hailey to stop.

But Hailey looked ice-cold, staring at her like she was already dead.

Truth was, Hailey was nervous too.

This was only her second time killing someone.

And this time, it was her former best friend.

Her enemy.

"Hailey, please! Please stop!"

Tears streamed down Phoebe's face.

She regretted it now. Regretted messing with the people on the 66th floor.

She'd seen how ruthless they were when she left the apartment before.

So why the hell did she think learning a few tricks to manipulate men would let her play them?

Hailey stared at Phoebe's tear-streaked face. Her gut twisted.

But her mind was set.

She raised her hand again—this time aiming for Phoebe's throat.

Before the blade could fall, a massive force kicked her aside.

"Stop!"

A commanding voice boomed.

Hailey turned. Several men in military coats approached, the leader's face stern and hard.

"Even in the apocalypse, you can't just kill people. This building's about to become an official shelter under military control."

It was Logan.

He'd agreed to Phoebe's proposal but hadn't let her come alone.

From the start, he'd stationed people downstairs—worried the 66th floor actually had guns and might do something reckless.

He'd just woken up, came to check things out, and walked right into an attempted murder.

"Captain Jackman, please! Save me!"

Phoebe threw herself into Logan's arms.

Just like that, Logan whisked her away.

He even lectured Hailey about it.

Hailey stayed silent the whole time. Only when someone called Logan away did she speak, her voice ice-cold.

"Captain, if a lecture could stop evil—if everyone could be reasoned with—you wouldn't be busting your ass every single day. Phoebe's gonna pay for what she did. You can save her once. You can't save her forever."

Logan's brow furrowed, but he said nothing.

He had too much on his plate already. Put out one fire at a time—he couldn't handle everything personally.

Hailey watched Phoebe disappear, gripping her knife tight.

We've got time.

Once she went back inside, Cecilia finally emerged from the apartment.
