Chapter 215 Nathan's Warehouses


"Ms. Summers, there are a few things I want to tell you. I'll be staying here for the night. When you get back, could you come and find me on the fortieth floor?"

Hearing Barry say that, Cecilia found herself rather curious.

"What is it about?"

"It's about Captain Jackman, but it's a long story. I can't explain it properly in a sentence or two. We'll talk when you get back. I've still got a sausage with me as well. I'll give it to Blackie then."

Barry was smiling as he spoke, but Cecilia still caught a trace of heaviness in his expression.

So the two of them agreed to meet again after she returned, and Cecilia headed downstairs with Blackie.

Once she reached the lower levels, she realised that the wind and snow were mixed with bits of hail.

It was colder than before too.

She checked the thermometer she was carrying. It was already nearing minus sixty degrees.

The Ice Stage was finally reaching its coldest point.

With her skates on and Blackie beside her, Cecilia shot away from the area like an arrow.

Once she was sure there was no one around, she slapped Blackie lightly on the shoulder.

"Blackie."

He understood at once and grew to the size of a small pony.

Then they set off towards their destination.

The place where Nathan had hidden his supplies was a little way off, about thirty kilometres away.

Cecilia and Blackie raced through the wind and snow for a long while before finally arriving at three warehouses that had clearly been built not long ago.

From a distance, the three warehouses looked like low hills, each over five metres high and stretching for dozens of metres, all made from sturdy metal.

In the apocalypse, building warehouses of that size was no easy feat. Building three of them was even more telling. It was obvious how much power both Nathan and the Barnes family still held after the end of the world.

Now that Nathan was dead, no one had come to clear the snow around the warehouses over the past few days, and half of each structure had already been buried beneath it.

On either side of the warehouses, Cecilia spotted two modified lorries.

Before the apocalypse, she had never stocked up on lorries. They were huge, took up too much room, and had never seemed especially useful.

But now that the Space had levelled up, taking in two lorries was effortless.

Her brows lifted slightly, and she stored them away at once.

When she reached the first warehouse, she saw a massive lock on the door. She took the universal key from the Space and opened it.

The moment the main doors swung open, a strong smell of petrol rushed out at her.

Delighted, Cecilia entered with Blackie and found the warehouse packed to the brim with barrels of fuel.

This was perfect timing, because petrol was one of the few things she had not managed to stock much of before the apocalypse. It had never been easy to get hold of in large quantities.

Her original plan had been to wait until the Ice Stage ended, the ice melted, and the floodwaters receded, then raid a few petrol stations for free.

She had not expected to find fuel here first.

Walking further inside, she saw not only ordinary petrol, but also vast quantities of diesel. Practically every grade of fuel available on the market was here.

And it filled an entire warehouse.

Nathan clearly knew how to stockpile properly.

In times like these, diesel and petrol were even more valuable than food.

As for how he had managed to obtain all of it, Cecilia did not much care.

It all belonged to her now.

She worked her way through the warehouse, collecting barrel after barrel until her hands almost seemed to ache from the sheer repetition, and finally emptied the entire place into the Space.

Once the first warehouse was cleared out, Cecilia headed for the second.

She prised open the lock in the same way. The moment the doors opened, she was hit by the strong smell of rust.

Unlike the first warehouse, this one was not filled with just one type of thing. It could roughly be divided into four sections.

At the very front were weapons.

Rifles, pistols, grenades, and other individual firearms filled about two crates, all accompanied by various kinds of ammunition.

At the very back sat another crate, carefully covered with a camouflage oilcloth, as though it were especially prized.

Cecilia walked over and pulled the cover away, only to find that inside was an artillery piece.

She did not know much about that particular model, but it was about the size of a car. At the front were three barrels. The central one was the largest and thickest, while the two on either side were shorter, though they hardly looked weak.

Beside it was a crate of shells. There were not many, only a little over twenty.

Cecilia stored both the artillery piece and the shells.

She probably would not have much use for something like that herself, but on Plot One...

Could she plant it?

The batch of bullets she had planted a few days earlier had finally matured that very morning.

When she stepped into the Space, she found Plot One covered in a whole field of flowers.

At that moment, she hardly knew what to feel. The only thought in her mind was that perhaps the Space was about to cheat her again.

When she went closer, however, she realised that atop each flower sat a single crystal-clear drop of liquid.

Puzzled, Cecilia grabbed Sprite, who was still yawning endlessly from lack of sleep, by the back of the neck and hauled him over.

"Sprite, what is this? Is Plot One becoming more unreliable by the day?"

The moment Sprite heard Cecilia insulting the Space, all drowsiness vanished. He sprang upright in a flash and landed on her shoulder.

"Who's unreliable? How dare you slander our glorious Space!"

Cecilia twitched at the corner of her mouth.

Was he praising himself?

After quite a lot of fuss, Sprite finally asked her whether, over the past few days, she had found Jet unbearably noisy, chattering in her ear until it felt as though her ears were growing calluses.
