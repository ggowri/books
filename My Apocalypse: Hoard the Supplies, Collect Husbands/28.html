Chapter 28 A Dimension Pendant

After getting everything ready, Cecilia stepped out of the Space and crouched down in front of Blackie. "Blackie, I need to put you in the Space for a couple of days. You'll be okay in there, right? Don't worry, I'm not abandoning you. I'll be right here the whole time, and whenever I get a chance, I'll let you out to play for a bit. Deal?"

Blackie stared at her for a long moment, then actually nodded his head like he understood.

Cecilia nearly jumped up and cheered right then and there. The spring water had definitely made him smarter!

Once Blackie was settled in, she checked on him through the Space and noticed he seemed way less freaked out than the first time. He sniffed around a bit, grabbed his favorite toy in his mouth, and curled up in his dog bed to play.

Seeing that, Cecilia finally relaxed. She threw on a backpack, grabbed her rolling suitcase, and headed for the airport.

When the plane touched down in Destony City, rain was pouring there too. Actually, it was coming down even harder than back home. The second Cecilia stepped off the plane, the wind nearly ripped her umbrella right out of her hands.

Her stomach dropped. She really hoped this trip would go smoothly. If the apocalypse suddenly hit and all the flights got canceled, getting back home would be a nightmare.

Still, based on her past life experience, this rain would keep up for at least a few more days, so she wasn't panicking yet.

She called a cab to the gemstone trading market and found it way busier than she'd expected. The whole place buzzed with people haggling over prices, and plenty of vendors were doing livestreams on their phones.

Cecilia walked into the market wearing her raincoat, one hand pressed lightly against the pendant at her chest, paying close attention to any reaction from it.

What made her heart race was that she'd barely walked a few yards before the pendant started vibrating against her chest. And this time, the sensation was stronger than it had ever been before.

Through the vibrations, Cecilia could actually feel the Space's excitement.

She tested it a few times, moving back and forth, and quickly narrowed it down to one of several stalls clustered together.

The vendor was livestreaming to his phone camera while a bunch of shills standing nearby hyped up the gemstones in his collection.

Cecilia knew enough about this stuff to recognize a scam when she saw one. This guy clearly didn't have anything worth buying. She crouched down anyway and shuffled through his display for a moment before spotting a particular gemstone.

It was a purple pendant shaped like a teardrop, set in 14-karat gold with tiny diamonds scattered around the edge. Honestly, it looked pretty nice.

Cecilia turned it over in her hand a few times. Something about it felt different, special even.

Before she could ask about the price, the vendor streaming nearby practically lunged over to her, grinning as he snatched the pendant from her hand. "Miss, you've got great taste! This pendant is the crown jewel of my entire shop!"

Cecilia's mouth twitched. She'd literally just heard him saying the exact same thing to his livestream viewers about three other pieces. The guy was full of it.

She didn't bother calling him out though. This was just the start, and the market up ahead was massive.

"How much?" she asked.

The vendor held the pendant up to his phone camera, going on and on about the pendant's clarity and color for his livestream audience. He completely ignored Cecilia's question about the price.

"Oh wow, Ms. Harrington, you're interested in this piece too? No problem, we'll definitely save it for you!" the vendor said.

Cecilia's eyes narrowed. The vendor was obviously trying to jack up the price, and the sketchy-looking crew standing around pretending to be customers made it crystal clear this gemstone pendant wasn't going to be easy to walk away with.

But Cecilia had survived the apocalypse. A small-time vendor's tricks didn't faze her in the slightest.

Without a word, she turned and walked away.

One of the shill women even grabbed her arm. "Wait, you're leaving already? That pendant's gorgeous! If you don't want it, I'm gonna buy it."

Cecilia let out a cold laugh. "Go ahead. This market's huge, and I just got here. Plenty more to see."

The vendor's expression shifted the second he realized she wasn't bluffing. He actually put down his phone and turned to face her properly. "Hey, hold on! We can work something out here!"

In the end, Cecilia walked away with the purple pendant for five thousand dollars. The stone itself was about thumb-sized with decent color but nothing special in terms of clarity.

The vendor had tried to push the price to 20 thousand dollars by hyping up the 14K gold setting and diamond accents, but Cecilia shut that down fast by saying she only wanted the raw stone and he could keep all the metalwork. That killed his pitch real quick.

Once Cecilia had the pendant, she didn't immediately toss it into the Space. She had a theory she needed to test.

Finding a quiet corner, she pulled out a brooch from her bag and pricked her finger. Blood welled up and dripped onto the purple stone.

The pendant absorbed it instantly.

"Knew it!"

Excitement surged through Cecilia's chest. She focused on the purple pendant the same way she connected with her Space, and suddenly, her mind filled with the image of a space roughly a few hundred square feet in size.

This was a dimension pendant!

No wonder the pendant in her chest had reacted so strongly. This purple pendant definitely wasn't just some ordinary gemstone.

But now, Cecilia hesitated. If she let her Space absorb this pendant, it would trigger a major upgrade for sure. But the spatial energy contained in this purple pendant might have other uses too.

In her previous life, she'd seen people fight tooth and nail over spatial treasures like this. They could be traded for unimaginable resources and connections.
