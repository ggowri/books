Chapter 200 The Elephant's Favour

After a whole night of activity, Cecilia was feeling rather tired and wanted nothing more than to get some rest.

But then she remembered the fresh batch of alfalfa she had just planted in the Space and decided she ought to go and check on it. She took out a bowl of Magic Spring water from the Space, drank it down, and prepared to head inside for a proper tidy up.

The moment she entered, however, a little white blur came flying straight at her.

The first time might have caught her off guard, but by now she knew perfectly well what it was. With a sigh, Cici reached up and plucked Sprite off her shoulder.

"Why didn't you eat that space? Why didn't you eat it? Why didn't you eat it?"

Sprite bounced up and down, muttering the same grievance over and over.

Cici knew it had wanted to devour another space for a long time.

But things like that were simply too precious. The one she had found herself had only come after tramping through the biggest jade market in the country from end to end.

And the one Arthur had found had required the entire Barnes family to mobilise their resources and search through several nearby cities before they located a single one.

"All you ever think about is eating," Cici said, lightly tapping Sprite on the head.

Sprite looked put out, but clearly understood that there was no point making a fuss anymore. It jumped down from her shoulder, lifted its head in wounded dignity, and stalked off with the deliberate tread of a cat pretending not to care.

Cici ignored it and first went over to the two mutant plants she had put beside Plot One.

Once planted in the soil of the Space, the mutant plants had behaved themselves perfectly well, and in fact looked as though they were thriving.

The welcoming pine was lush and green, with another fresh cluster of buds already visible.

The cactus looked rather adorable. Cici prodded at its spines a few times and found them extremely hard.

This time, in Plot One, Cici had planted a batch of bullets.

That was because Plot One had performed so brilliantly not long ago. Some seeds planted there had actually produced a crop of grenades.

That had greatly improved her stock of firearms and explosives.

Naturally, Cici wanted to take another gamble.

Plot One, like the Recycler, had always been a matter of luck, but she simply could not suppress her eager little sense of anticipation.

What if something extraordinary grew?

For the moment, however, Plot One looked utterly calm, with no visible change at all. It probably still needed time.

After leaving Plot One, Cici wandered over to Plot Two.

Sprite had already lost interest and gone off to lounge on a nearby roof.

From where Cici stood, she could clearly see Sprite's round little belly turned up towards the sky, plump and soft.

Perhaps it was her imagination, but Cici felt that Sprite had grown a little fatter lately. Even the two soft pouches along its lower belly were starting to droop.

She wondered whether that had anything to do with the set of jewellery Hailey Carlson had given it to eat.

This time, Plot Two had not been planted with fruit or vegetables.

Instead, Cici had filled it with an expanse of timothy hay and alfalfa.

Those were both favourites of the herbivores in the Ranch.

But the moment she walked up to Plot Two, she stopped short in surprise.

Spread before her was a whole sea of purple.

"Ah. The alfalfa's flowered!"

And the blossoms were enormous, each one the size of a palm.

The alfalfa itself had grown more than two metres tall, thick and green in great swathes. It ought to keep all the little herbivores in the Ranch fed for quite a long time.

With a single thought, Cici harvested the alfalfa and timothy hay.

At the same time, she happily picked some of the alfalfa flowers.

They were delicious mixed with cornmeal and steamed, and also excellent stir fried with meat.

Cici had eaten them once as a child, when her grandmother was still alive and had made them for her. She decided that when she had time, she would make a large bowlful and savour the taste of childhood again.

Once Plot Two had been harvested, Cici went over to the Ranch nearby. She topped up all the animals' feed and checked through the various by products.

Rabbit meat cans, fresh cow's milk, fresh goat's milk, alpaca wool…

The guinea pigs she had raised now numbered in the dozens.

She had kept them as pets in her previous life. They made the cutest noises, and the moment they heard the fridge door open, they would start shrieking at the top of their lungs to remind the human to feed them.

And yet now…

Cici stared in silence at the large words roast guinea pig cans and selected them.

No matter how small they were, they were still meat.

In an instant, seven or eight guinea pigs vanished from that section, and seven or eight cans appeared in the Space.

She had heard that in some places overseas, and in Yunnan too, plenty of people ate them and that they were quite tasty.

She wondered whether Blackie would like them.

Then Cici moved to the section where the weekly random animal appeared, and there she found the elephant, looking faintly depressed.

It seemed to be an Asian elephant, not especially large by elephant standards.

Perhaps because it had not seen another of its kind for so long, it was obviously unhappy.

Cici took some pumpkins and apples from the Space and went over to feed it.

The moment the elephant saw her approaching with food, it happily swayed its trunk.

After eating the apples and pumpkin, it even stamped its feet twice on the spot, its eyes suddenly full of life again.

Cici took out another apple and gently stroked the elephant's trunk. The apocalypse had come now, and she could not help wondering whether creatures like elephants had already gone extinct, whether she would ever see one again in the future.

Just as her hand was gently moving over the elephant's trunk, a clear ding sounded beside her ear.

Then Sprite came sauntering over at its own leisurely pace, still looking faintly annoyed that she had refused to let it devour another space.

"Master, you have gained the affection of the randomly appearing elephant and triggered the Space's favour towards you. You may now choose a different by product from the elephant."
