Chapter 165 Subduing the Mutated Pine

That meant this kind of plant had once been mistreated by humans.

And it was very likely to harbor hostility toward people.

All these thoughts flashed through Cecilia's mind in an instant. She immediately released the steel plates she had just stored in the Space, stacking them in front of her to form a solid barrier.

Several loud bang bang bang sounds echoed through the silent, empty corridor.

Cecilia noticed that one of the plates was relatively thin and had been pierced straight through by the welcoming pine's needles. A short section of pine needle protruded right in front of her eyes.

Beside her, Blackie bared its teeth and retreated together with her.

Cecilia recalled how the mutated cactus had been dealt with in her previous life.

Fire.

But she couldn't do that here.

This was the first mutated plant she had encountered.

And it was extremely powerful. It could provide the Space with a far greater upgrade and energy replenishment.

Her thoughts raced, and soon she came up with a plan.

For her, there was actually no need to defeat the welcoming pine. She only needed to store it inside the Space.

With that in mind, Cecilia quickly settled on an idea.

Perhaps realizing that its pine needles could not pierce through the steel plates, the welcoming pine stopped wasting them after launching a single round of attacks.

Cecilia took a deep breath and gently touched the steel plates with her hand. In an instant, they disappeared back into the Space.

Across from her, the welcoming pine seemed to freeze for a moment. Then suddenly, countless pine needles shot forward.

At the critical moment, Cecilia activated her mind and threw out countless bedsheets toward the welcoming pine.

Red ones, green ones, cartoon ones—bedsheets of every pattern imaginable. They were part of the bedding sets Cecilia had collected earlier from the twelve-story apartment building across from her own building.

All of them slammed down heavily.

The bedsheets formed a dense, airtight net. Layer after layer slowed the speed of the incoming pine needles. As long as there were enough layers, they could completely pin the mutated welcoming pine underneath.

At the same time, Cecilia grabbed Blackie and retreated.

One enormous pine needle pierced through countless layers of bedsheets. At last, moving slowly as if using its final bit of strength, it reached Cecilia.

But then it curved downward and dropped to the ground.

The welcoming pine, crushed beneath hundreds of layers of bedsheets, could only be seen as a bulging shape underneath the pile.

Without hesitation, Cecilia stepped forward and stored both the bedsheets and the tightly wrapped welcoming pine into the Space.

After the whole operation was finished, she looked ahead and found the space in front of her empty. Checking inside the Space with her consciousness, she indeed saw the welcoming pine there. Only then did she finally relax.

By the time the battle ended, half an hour had already passed.

Time at night was precious, so Cecilia hurried to continue her work.

She entered the second room on the floor and discovered it contained exported clothing—mostly hoodies, windbreakers, and similar items.

Without thinking much, she stored them all into the Space.

Then she moved on to the next room.

Inside were various electronic components.

Most of them had no brand labels. They were probably meant to be exported overseas, labeled with brand tags to make them look premium, and then shipped back to be sold domestically.

Cecilia glanced through the parts and roughly identified them as components used in computers, phones, televisions, and similar electronics.

Thinking that technology would continue developing in the future, and that these items could likely be exchanged with the government for significant benefits, she stored them all without hesitation.

The final room turned out to contain automobile parts.

They were neatly categorized in small boxes, with large quantities of each type.

Basically, if a vehicle broke down, these parts would be enough to fix it.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow.

Not bad. In the apocalypse, these would definitely become scarce resources.

After finishing the floor, Cecilia felt a bit tired and decided to drink some water and eat something.

She also planned to check on that welcoming pine inside the Space.

Blackie beside her looked somewhat listless. Cecilia suddenly had an idea and took out a bag of dog treats.

She watched as Blackie, who had been lying flat on the ground, heard the crinkling of the snack bag. Its large dog head slowly "rose up" from the floor.

She gave Blackie some dog food mixed with the treats.

For herself, Cecilia took out a serving of spicy hot pot and a bowl of steaming hot coffee and finished them quickly.

Her period was approaching, and she had been experiencing some stomach pain recently, so she figured drinking something warm in advance would help.

After all, the weather had become extremely cold lately.

The outdoor temperature had already dropped close to minus sixty degrees, approaching the coldest point of the Ice Stage.

After finishing her meal, Cecilia brought Blackie with her and prepared to check inside the Space.

But the moment she entered, she saw a white… hedgehog-like cat with a drooping face.

Ari's entire face radiated killing intent, and its almond-shaped cat eyes had widened until they were nearly perfectly round.

Its fur had puffed up in anger, yet bright pine needles were stuck all over its body, covering its entire back.

"And you still remember to come into the Space? You heartless traitor! Completely irresponsible! Waaaah… my smooth, beautiful fur!"

The furious screaming echoed throughout the entire Space.

Cecilia was momentarily stunned. What just happened?

Beside Plot One, a small new patch of land had already been cleared.

The welcoming pine had been quietly planted there.

The soil looked freshly turned.

And on top of the soil were adorable plum-shaped cat paw prints.

In Cecilia's mind, an image suddenly appeared—Ari digging the soil with its tiny cat paws, planting the welcoming pine into the ground, and then patting the soil down little by little with those same paws.
