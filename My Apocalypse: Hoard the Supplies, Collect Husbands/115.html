Chapter 115 Blackie's Strength

And heavy, ragged breathing.

When everything finally settled, Cecilia realized all the mutant cats had scattered.

And in front of her was a massive dog head.

The head belonged to Blackie, panting hard, now about ten feet tall and completely filling the stairwell.

His jaws were clamped tight around the mutant weasel.

His huge eyes locked onto Cecilia, still burning with post-battle intensity.

The weasel wasn't dead yet—making awful squeaking sounds, writhing and clawing at Blackie's mouth.

A flash of annoyance crossed Blackie's eyes. He bit down hard.

Crack. The sound of breaking bones.

The weasel went still instantly.

Blood poured from its body, running down Blackie's fur.

In the freezing cold, the warm blood quickly froze into icy chunks.

Jet excitedly hopped onto Blackie's head, bouncing around.

Since Cecilia wouldn't let him talk, he pecked at Blackie's skull with his beak instead.

Blackie shook his head in annoyance, trying to shake him off.

The scene was almost comical.

Only then did Cecilia feel like she could breathe again.

"Blackie, thank god you're okay." Cecilia exhaled in relief.

Glancing at the shattered ice, Cecilia had no idea what Blackie had gone through down there.

But through the broken surface, she could see a large hollow area below—dug out by that mutant weasel.

The thing had made a den down there.

She could see several animal skeletons scattered around.

From their shape, they looked like cats.

And there was a half-eaten mutant cat carcass.

Pure black fur, smaller build—looked exactly like that mutant black cat.

The weasel's claws were stained dark red, probably from eating this cat just moments ago.

No wonder those cats hated the weasel so much.

Cecilia stroked Blackie's fur, relieved they'd made it through that nightmare.

But it proved Blackie's strength.

After comforting both Blackie and Jet, they started heading up.

The sky outside was turning pale—sunrise was coming.

Cecilia needed to get back fast, or someone might spot them.

With that thought, she led Blackie and Jet quickly back to the top floor.

What surprised her—as they climbed, the mutant cats seemed terrified of them now. They kept their distance, watching from the shadows instead.

Countless glowing green eyes on every floor, staring silently.

Cecilia looked around. There was still plenty of rotting meat here—stuff humans wouldn't touch, but vital food for these mutant cats.

If she was right, these cats would stick around until every scrap was gone.

As for her? She wouldn't be coming back.

Even if Blackie hadn't completed his mission.

By the time Cecilia reached the top floor, she turned to Blackie with a puzzled look.

"Blackie, did you collect anything down there?"

Blackie tilted his head at her, looking confused.

Cecilia asked several more times. No response.

"I just taught you how to collect stuff and take things out. Show me."

Still nothing.

Cecilia frowned. Wait, what? Why does he look dumber?

But no time to dig into that now.

She scrambled out the window.

After this whole night, wind and snow had nearly buried the opening.

Any later and she'd be digging her way out.

She pulled out the sidecar, stashed Jet in the Space, strapped Blackie in, and headed back to the apartment.

When she got close, she found a hidden spot and put Blackie in the Space too.

Then she ducked inside the Space to change clothes—couldn't risk anyone spotting her outfit.

Finally, she crept along the walls, moving carefully, and made it up to the 66th floor and through her door.

The second she got inside, Cecilia sucked in a deep breath and let it out.

Close call. Way too close.

Plenty to do now that she was back, but first priority—give Blackie a bath!

She rushed into the Space.

The moment she stepped in, Jet swarmed her, chattering nonstop.

"Human! Human! I thought I was done for back there!"

"Human! Did I do good today? Can I get extra treats?"

"Human! Blackie stinks! His nasty blood got all over my feathers!"

Cecilia's head throbbed.

Jet was making up for every word he couldn't say earlier, spilling everything at once.

After covering the highlights, he dove into the details.
