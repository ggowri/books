Chapter 101 Another Experiment

She turned to a burly man beside her.

"Isn't the government looking for new shelters? This building would be perfect. Go to General Warren. Tell him the Barnes family has secured an ideal location."

The man nodded and drove off in an SUV.

"Cecilia, you bitch. And that bastard Arthur—hmph!"

Miranda spat the words out, then climbed into their last remaining SUV with the others and left.

******

On the 66th-floor rooftop, Cecilia watched Miranda leave with a cold smile.

Just as she turned to head back inside, she spotted Arthur downstairs.

He jumped into the SUV Miranda had left behind and followed them.

Cecilia frowned. What the hell is Arthur up to?

******

After Miranda left, Cecilia headed back inside.

All that chaos had eaten up the whole day—it was already dark.

First, she gathered up the fully charged solar panels.

Then she checked on the generator.

It had snowed today. The snow would only get heavier from now on. Sunny days would become rare.

Good thing she'd hoarded hundreds of charging panels.

She'd been charging them all for days now—every single one was maxed out.

Should be enough to keep her tablets, phones, and other small devices running through the deep freeze.

As for the big stuff—fridges and whatnot—the generator would handle those.

After feeding Blackie and Jet, Cecilia slipped into the Space.

First stop—Plot One.

After that last firewood harvest, Cecilia felt a little mischievous.

Time for another experiment.

This time, she buried a mutant creature's carcass in the farm.

She'd found it on the rooftop—a mutant magpie. Been frozen dead for days. Big as a pheasant, with feathers that had mutated into wild, brilliant colors.

She'd buried plenty of things in Plot One before.

The farm produced all kinds of weird things—like that purple rabbit stuffed with blueberry filling.

Cecilia had checked on it yesterday. The thing had grown huge—basically dog-sized now.

And the babies it popped out? A wild mix of mom and dad. Colorful little weirdos. Cute as hell.

She stood in front of Plot One now, staring at where she'd buried the mutant magpie yesterday.

The ground should've been raised. But there was nothing.

Cecilia crouched down and dug around with a shovel.

Empty.

The farm hadn't produced a damn thing.

"Did the farm just... eat the mutant carcass?"

Cecilia said it out loud, stunned by her own conclusion.

She'd have to find more mutant animal bodies. Test it again.

Since Plot One gave her nothing, she grabbed a huge cabbage from Plot Two and planted it in Plot One instead.

Another experiment.

She wanted to see what would happen when Space-grown vegetables got a second power-up.

******

The ranch animals were thriving.

Cecilia checked on them daily now, harvesting whatever they produced.

She'd noticed something weird—animals in the farm grew way faster than normal.

Like the alpacas. She'd sheared them two days ago. Today? Ready to shear again.

She stockpiled the wool for now.

The Space hadn't unlocked a feature to turn wool into clothes or blankets yet. But someday it would. She was sure of it.

Finally, Cecilia headed to the trash processor.

Things had been on a roll lately, spitting out decent stuff.

She opened it up, grinning, ready for today's "treasure."

What she found: a single glove. And on the other side? A single sock.

Yep. One glove. One sock.

Cecilia wanted to throw something.

Who the hell gives you one of a pair? Is this a joke?

She could feel the Space mocking her.

If the Space could take human form right now, Cecilia would've punched it in the face without hesitation.

Are you kidding me right now?

******

After tidying up the Space, Cecilia decided to go all in.

That day she'd cracked open a few safes from the Boss's office—but two were left. Both from his secret room.

Those two were high-security models. She hadn't been able to cut them open before.

Today, she was finishing the job.

Cecilia grabbed her power saw and tools, hauled out the two waist-high safes, and left the Space.

Outside, Jet was perched on Blackie's head, half-asleep.

They were used to her popping in and out by now. Didn't even bother opening their eyes.

Cecilia dragged the safes into an empty room and shut the door—no point torturing the animals' sensitive ears with that noise.

Weird thing about the Space: any electrical equipment stopped working inside.
