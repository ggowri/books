Chapter 53 Uncle

Cecilia threw a piece of pork chop into the dog bowl, and Blackie instantly wagged his tail.

Cecilia couldn't help but knock on Blackie's head. "You little guy, you're really such a snob!"

Blackie didn't care at all. After finishing one piece, he raised his head again, wagging his tail, his eyes shining brightly as he looked at Cecilia.

Cecilia and Blackie took turns like this, sharing bites, and finished the day's meal.

Jet also finished the pinecone, flying down to make his presence known in front of Cecilia.

The acid rain was heavier than before. Cecilia took out binoculars and looked at the building opposite, discovering that the water had already reached the second floor.

From a high vantage point, the entire city was submerged in a vast sea.

Trees were mostly gone, with only a few occasional tall trees showing their tiny crowns.

From Cecilia's perspective, she could also see that many buildings had already suffered acid rain corrosion, beginning to show problems like deteriorating walls.

But still, as the saying goes, all of this is just the beginning.

After finishing her meal, Cecilia comfortably rubbed her belly.

She checked the time. Over half an hour had passed.

She locked Jet up again and went out with Blackie.

Once outside, she found Arthur already busy working outside the door, probably having eaten quickly and come straight out to work.

I didn't expect him to be so diligent.

Cecilia thought to herself.

"This is the supplies I just cleared out from unit 6612."

Arthur pointed to the items in front of him.

Cecilia walked over and looked, finding that this unit didn't have many supplies. Most were snacks, pasta, and such, along with a few blankets.

Since arriving here, Cecilia had never seen the residents of unit 6612. They were likely on a business trip, or this place was just an occasional stop for them.

"Can I have these? You can take all the other supplies."

Arthur pointed to two bottles of chili sauce and three packs of spicy-flavored snacks among them.

Cecilia tried hard to hold back a laugh.

Aside from those, there were three packs of pasta, one pack of rice, two packs of potato chips, and one pack of candy.

To be honest, with this distribution, Cecilia had the advantage.

But Cecilia didn't want to take advantage either. She tossed two packs of pasta to Arthur. "Since we're cooperating, the distribution should be fair. I'm not really interested in overly spicy things anyway."

Arthur was very satisfied, nodding slightly, his expression even more cheerful than before.

Because of Cecilia's action, Arthur seemed to feel closer to Cecilia afterwards, helping her move things several times.

If the occasional assistance before was out of gentlemanly habit, now it was special consideration for Cecilia.

This made Cecilia a bit uncomfortable.

But she didn't know that Arthur, though solitary in nature, was someone who had a very clear sense of propriety.

Under such circumstances, Cecilia's fair distribution of supplies and her agreement to his request made him view her more favorably.

The two continued their scavenging. The most rewarding location was that imported snack shop.

It had all kinds of snacks, many already packed in delivery boxes ready for shipment.

Perhaps because the apocalypse arrived suddenly, the deliveries never went out.

The owner of this shop was originally a cute girl. Cecilia still didn't know where she was now or if she was doing okay.

Cecilia and Arthur split the shop's contents equally, each taking away five or six boxes of snacks.

Arthur didn't seem very interested in these snacks. His brows constantly furrowed, even showing some disdain, and he was very casual when picking.

Cecilia rolled her eyes inwardly.

You have no idea how precious these things will become once the apocalypse truly sets in!

Especially the high-end candies and chocolates inside. They can quickly replenish energy, are easy to carry, and will become highly sought-after commodities.

Cecilia moved the goods to her doorway, not letting Arthur help, and carried them in herself.

When Arthur was out of sight, she stored everything in the Space.

After moving things from three places, Cecilia checked the time. It was already past 8 PM.

Cecilia proactively suggested, "Let's continue tomorrow. We've sealed off this floor. No one will come up for now."

It was indeed because she was somewhat tired.

She had plenty of supplies in her Space, not worth working herself to the bone moving things like this.

Arthur nodded slightly without saying much.

Suddenly, Blackie, who had been lying on the floor, stood up, arching his back into an attack posture, making a low growling sound in his throat.

That was a warning signal when facing danger.

And the direction he was facing was precisely the sealed stairwell entrance on the 66th floor.

"Uncle Arthur... are you up there?" A familiar voice appeared here, most inopportunely.

Cecilia took two steps forward and saw that, behind the iron door, was actually Alexander.

No wonder Blackie's reaction was so intense just now.

Blackie had met Alexander before and knew Alexander was no good.

But just now, who was Alexander calling Uncle Arthur?

"What are you doing here?" Arthur suddenly replied, his voice extremely cold.

Cecilia abruptly took a step back from beside Arthur.

Arthur is Alexander's uncle?

Before Cecilia could say anything, she heard an extremely affected and particularly sharp voice coming from the stairwell entrance. "Oh my god! I was just wondering why Cici suddenly said she was breaking up with Alex. So it turns out... it turns out she's already living with another man!"
