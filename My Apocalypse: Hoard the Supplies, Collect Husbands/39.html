Chapter 39 There's Someone Else?

The water was full of floating debris, and being completely submerged in the acid rain made her skin sting. Cecilia could feel it burning slightly, the acid eating away at her skin. But it was fine. She knew the acid rain would come in handy later.

From where she was, Blackie actually looked pretty cool swimming. He paddled along with his front paws in that serious, determined way dogs do, and it was kind of adorable.

The streetlights had been dead for days now, and trash floated everywhere in the murky water, but the distance wasn't far. She and Blackie reached the other side quickly enough.

The water had already flooded into the first floor of the small building here.

Without hesitating, Cecilia pulled out her tools and picked the lock on the front door.

Even though it was already one in the morning, she didn't dare use a bright light with the whole city gone dark and silent.

She'd brought a dim yellow flashlight she'd prepared earlier. That way, even if light leaked out from inside, people in the distant apartment buildings wouldn't notice.

She waded into the building. It was an open warehouse space with a desk by the entrance covered in paperwork, a calculator, and a computer, probably where they normally processed deliveries and tracked packages.

The rest of the Space was packed wall to wall with delivery boxes. Some were wrapped in plastic bags, others sat on metal shelving units.

Whoever ran this place must have raised everything up on moisture-proof mats before evacuating, worried about flooding. Right now, none of the packages had gotten wet yet, which was a relief.

Time was tight, so Cecilia didn't bother opening packages to see what was inside. She just went straight down the line and grabbed everything.

She even took the desks, chairs, electronic scale, moisture-proof mats, and computers. When the extreme cold hit, the wooden furniture and mats could be burned as firewood.

As she kept adding more stuff, the Space was filling up fast. With the recent expansion, she was running out of shelving in there.

Those computers and electronics might seem useless now, but who knew if they'd come in handy later? Better to take them than let them get ruined by the floodwater.

After clearing out the entire first floor, she headed upstairs to keep collecting supplies.

If she remembered right, the second floor was also a package distribution center. Cecilia worked her way up and grabbed everything in sight, shoving it all into the Space first. She'd sort through the packages later when she got home.

What really surprised her was finding a small storage room on the second floor packed with winter gear that belonged to the delivery workers. There were puffy coats, heavy-duty jackets, boots, and insulated vests everywhere.

The stuff wasn't exactly fashionable, but it was warm, durable, and windproof, which made it perfect for surviving the apocalypse.

The company must have ordered new uniforms for the season change but hadn't handed them out yet.

Cecilia did a quick scan and saw most of it was men's clothing in all different sizes. She didn't hesitate and swept the entire stash into the Space.

After clearing out the first and second floors, she knew the upper levels held livestream companies, food suppliers, and other businesses.

Time was running out, so she headed straight for the third floor with Blackie right behind her.

Blackie had been perfectly behaved the whole time, going exactly where she pointed without barking or wandering off.

She'd been to the third floor before and knew it was mostly small livestream companies, each one set up in its own little office space with all kinds of products inside.

But just as she reached the top of the stairs, Blackie suddenly clamped his teeth onto her pant leg.

Cecilia jerked in surprise and looked down at him. His eyes were wide open and locked on something ahead of them, and his whole body had shifted into an attack stance.

She got it immediately. Someone else was up here.

She quickly switched off her flashlight and tugged Blackie backward, slipping into a tiny side room.

It was a janitor's closet with barely any space and nothing worth taking, which also meant nobody would bother checking it.

She pulled Blackie deeper inside and pressed against the wall, straining to hear what was happening outside.

Sure enough, within 30 seconds she caught the sound of footsteps. There was also the scraping noise of something being dragged and the beam of a flashlight sweeping across the hallway.

Someone else had the same idea she did and was stockpiling supplies for the end of the world.

Her knife materialized in her hand with just a thought. She tensed up, ready to fight if this person tried anything.

But thankfully, whoever it was had already finished loading up and headed straight for the stairs without stopping.

She listened to the footsteps fade away, and when Blackie finally relaxed beside her, she let out a slow breath.

The thing was, once that person got down to the first and second floors and saw that everything had been completely cleaned out, they were going to freak out.

She just hoped they'd assume a whole crew had come through and decide to bail before things got messy.

After waiting a bit longer, Cecilia crept over to the window in the next room and watched as a tall figure carrying a large pack walked out the front door on the first floor. She finally let herself relax.

From what she could see, the person was tall and built, probably around six foot one or even six foot three.

He had a seriously muscular frame, and even wearing loose black clothes, she could make out his broad shoulders and thick back.

Judging by how easily he moved through the water while carrying that huge bag of supplies, the guy clearly had some serious skills.
