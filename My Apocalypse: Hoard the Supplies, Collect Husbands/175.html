Chapter 175 A Desperate Counterattack

He truly had not expected that Cecilia had a gun.

Boss Carter kicked Jake and then looked coldly at Cecilia.

"Well now, little girl, are you planning to fight us to the death? All this over a dog? Is it really worth it?"

Cecilia said nothing. Her face was cold as she fired a shot.

With a loud bang, the bullet struck Boss Carter.

However, the distance was a bit far. Cecilia did not aim accurately. It seemed to hit his shoulder instead of his heart.

"Ah!" Boss Carter cried out and fell to the ground.

Something happened that Cecilia had not expected.

Several men beside Boss Carter suddenly pulled out three guns.

All of them pointed directly at Cecilia.

Boss Carter actually had guns as well.

Yes, the reason Boss Carter dared to come looking for Luke Wei tonight was because he had obtained firearms.

And the reason Boss Carter had been able to capture the General was also because they had guns.

Boss Carter was helped to his feet by Jake. Covering his shoulder with one hand, he gritted his teeth and pulled a handgun from his pocket, shakily pointing it at Cecilia.

"Shoot her! And kill that dog too!" Boss Carter shouted loudly.

Those men immediately lowered their heads and began fiddling with their guns.

It was their first time handling firearms, so they were not very familiar with them.

Cecilia clenched her teeth and took a deep breath. Without thinking further, she pretended to put her hand deep into her pocket, but in reality she took out two grenades from the Space.

Without hesitation, she pulled the pins and threw them out in one swift motion.

Two thunderous explosions rang out.

Most of the people were knocked down.

Two of them, in their panic, managed to fire their guns.

But both shots went wide. No one knew where the bullets went.

The grenades blasted two craters into the ground and instantly killed three people.

Two others had their legs blown apart and were crawling forward on the ground.

Cecilia rolled across the ground, closing the distance with those scum. She quickly moved to higher ground and arrived beside Boss Carter.

Boss Carter was still holding that piece of dog meat.

When he saw Cecilia approach, he shouted loudly, "Don't… don't kill me! I have a lot of supplies. I can give them all to you."

Cecilia smiled and asked, "Where did you hide them?"

Boss Carter's eyes rolled around once.

"Let me go and I'll take you there. But you have to swear to the heavens. Swear you won't kill me."

Cecilia took out her pistol and pointed it at Boss Carter's forehead.

"Tell me, or I'll kill you right now."

Boss Carter trembled all over with fright.

Even in the apocalypse this man was still so fat. It was obvious he had been eating well.

Seeing his hesitation, Cecilia kicked him hard again.

"Are you going to talk or not?"

"I'll talk, I'll talk! I'll tell you right now! But don't kill me, alright? Just leave me alive."

Cecilia looked at the terrified fat man whose flesh was shaking all over his face and smiled lightly.

"Alright. I won't kill you. I'll leave you alive."

Boss Carter's eyes lit up immediately, and he told Cecilia where he had hidden his supplies.

Cecilia nodded slightly. Then she pretended to reach into her bag, but in fact she took out that lollipop that hurts like hell from the Space.

She swung it straight at Boss Carter's head.

"Ahhhh!"

Boss Carter screamed loudly, twisting his body violently on the ground as his whole body convulsed.

Cecilia kept a cold expression and struck his back again.

Boss Carter screamed even more miserably than before, trembling all over the ground as if electricity were running through his body.

Thinking that the lollipop had a limited number of uses, she decided she could not use it again.

Otherwise, Cecilia really would have liked to hit Boss Carter a few more times.

"Scum."

Cecilia kicked Boss Carter hard, then looked at the nearly dying General beside him and sighed softly.

She had thought that after the General left, perhaps it could live a free life.

Who would have expected it to be captured like this and even suffer the torment of having its flesh cut open and eaten alive?

At that moment, Blackie walked over.

It let out two low whines toward Cecilia, its eyes fixed on the General.

The General also looked at Blackie and responded with a very faint and weak sound.

After a moment, Blackie looked at Cecilia without blinking, its eyes full of pleading.

Cecilia understood. Blackie wanted to save the General.

She gently touched the General's neck, as if comforting it.

She thought that the General was a good dog. Even under such harsh conditions it had still obeyed Luke Wei's orders.

Later, when it met Blackie, it followed Blackie's command, realized its mistake, and took revenge for itself.

Originally it could finally live freely according to its own will.

But at this critical moment it encountered Boss Carter and was once again schemed against by humans.

"I hope that in the future you can be a free and happy little dog."

Cecilia murmured softly.

At the same time, a stream of Magic Spring water appeared in her hand and she slowly fed it into the General's mouth.

The General's eyes, which had been dimming, gradually regained their brightness. The wounds on its body also began to heal slowly.

The General suddenly widened its eyes and looked at Cecilia beside it.

Blackie let out several soft whines nearby, as if communicating with the General.

After a while, seeing that the General should be mostly fine, Cecilia withdrew her hand.

She did not completely heal the General, because they were still outside. She was worried about attracting attention. Although the surroundings looked empty, who knew if someone might be secretly watching from some hidden corner nearby.
