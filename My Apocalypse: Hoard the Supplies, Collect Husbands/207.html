Chapter 207 Nathan's Secrets

That settled it.

Using the reflections from the mirror shard on the front passenger side where Nathan was sitting, Cecilia worked out roughly where his head had to be.

One shot.

Straight through the head.

Once Nathan was dead, Cecilia jumped down from the overturned vehicle with Blackie.

She knew there were still people in the back seat, most likely bodyguards hired by the Barnes family.

"Out of the car."

She deliberately cocked her pistol, letting the sharp metallic sound ring out.

The two remaining bodyguards inside were terrified by now.

The moment they heard her, they scrambled out of the back seat in a panic.

One of the younger guards had a large burn across his chest. The bleeding had stopped, but he was clearly struggling to move properly.

"Please, don't kill us. We only took the Barnes family's money and did jobs for them. We've got nothing to do with the family itself."

The older one was trembling so badly his voice shook.

He was still carrying a case in his hand.

"I've got something important here, something Nathan always kept with him wherever he went. I'll give it to you. If I do, will you let me go?"

As he spoke, he threw the silver case to the ground and nudged it towards Cecilia with his foot.

Cecilia looked at it. It was fairly large, about half a metre long and thirty centimetres wide, impossible to tell what was inside.

It was secured with a combination lock.

"What's the code?" she asked the older man.

In truth, she had not intended to kill the two of them anyway.

She knew what they were: hired muscle, mercenaries, or petty thugs with a bit of skill, people who had taken the Barnes family's money and worked for them. They had no personal grudge against her.

From tonight onwards, the Barnes family would never recover. They would no longer be in any position to trouble her again.

And these men, just trying to survive by taking paid work, posed no real threat to her.

"The code... I don't know the code. I was only responsible for carrying it. But I do know this case was extremely important to Nathan. There's bound to be something valuable inside. I was his close bodyguard these past few days, and even then he never once let me see what was in it."

The older man wiped the sweat from his forehead.

The younger one, seeing the other man already offering something up in exchange for his life, hurried to speak as well.

"I know some of Nathan's secrets. The places where he hid supplies, and his ties with several other organisations. I'll tell you everything. If I do, will you let me go too?"

Cecilia lifted a brow. She had not expected this little windfall.

About ten minutes later, the two bodyguards hobbled away, supporting one another.

Cecilia and Blackie remained standing still in the snow for quite some time.

Cecilia was excited.

Because from the younger guard, she had learned something extremely important.

Nathan, or perhaps the Barnes family as a whole, had really gone far beyond what anyone would expect. While the military was still studying how to induce mutation in animals, they had already set up a laboratory of their own, full of experimental animals and even human test subjects.

Their goal was to trigger mutant traits in both animals and humans so they could be used as tools.

Apparently Nathan had already made some progress, though the work was still unfinished because there had not been enough time.

According to the bodyguard, the most impressive thing in the laboratory at present was a mutant tree.

Originally it had only stood a little over three metres tall. Now it had grown to more than five metres, and had even developed a degree of intelligence. It could already respond to a small number of commands.

Cecilia had no interest whatsoever in whether the mutant tree was intelligent or obedient.

What interested her was one thing only.

It could be used to level up the Space.

And according to the bodyguard, the laboratory also contained a great many other mutant plants.

They had also captured some mutant animals and were in the middle of experimenting on them.

Cecilia decided she absolutely had to visit this laboratory, so she made sure to ask for its location.

On top of that, Nathan had a separate private cache of supplies, and she now had that address too.

Then there was the case currently in her hand.

Cecilia looked at the combination lock and did not bother with it. She pulled out an axe and hacked at it several times, but it did not budge.

After that, she tried various other tools to break the lock open, yet nothing worked at all.

She studied the case. The material was clearly unusual, some kind of metal she could not identify.

That alone told her it was no ordinary box.

Deciding she would look into it later, she tossed it into the Space.

Checking the time, she saw that more than half an hour had already passed. She needed to get back quickly and still had Mabel to deal with.

Thinking that Blackie had spent a good deal of energy using his ability, Cecilia first took a basin of dog food out of the Space and let him eat.

Blackie devoured the whole thing in a whirlwind, finishing in ten seconds flat.

She rubbed his neck, then gave him a pat on the forehead.

"Let's go."

...

Meanwhile, Colonel Reed had already dealt with Victor.

To his surprise, things had gone far more smoothly than he had expected.

He and Arthur had slipped quietly into the Barnes estate.

At first, Reed had thought it would be difficult. After all, the family's security was now extremely tight.

But Arthur only took a few quick looks before devising a flawless infiltration plan.

And once they reached a blind spot in the surveillance, the way Arthur disabled the electric fence was clean and efficient.

The moment the power went out, Arthur vaulted straight over and into the estate.

Colonel Reed was deeply impressed by his skill.

After entering the grounds, Arthur led Reed to a drainage pipe and climbed up to the second floor in a matter of seconds, then reached down and hauled up Reed, who had never been much good at climbing even as a child.
