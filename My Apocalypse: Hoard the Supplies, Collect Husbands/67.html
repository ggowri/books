Chapter 67 Relax

Cecilia yawned, thinking she was cursed to be busy—she'd just planned to rest for the night, only to be dragged into another mess.

After a quick wash, she headed to bed, and no one disturbed her this time, letting her sleep until midday.

Blackie lay beside her, while Jet rustled around the living room, making faint noises now and then.

Cecilia patted Blackie's large head. "Good morning, Blackie."

For once, Blackie deigned to rub its head against her palm.

The contact made her notice Blackie's head seemed bigger than before, and she suddenly thought of the mutated snake she'd found a few days earlier.

Cecilia wondered if she should let Blackie and Jet evolve soon, but worried about the pain—acid rain was harsh enough.

Still, mixing it with Magic Spring water might yield unexpected results.

Determined to relax that day—no scavenging, no unpacking the Space's packages—Cecilia decided to explore the mixture's reaction once she'd rested.

She stretched, washed up briefly, fed the clingy Jet a treat, then pulled a bowl of creamy vanilla pudding and lobster rolls from the Space, settling in to watch a drama while eating.

Jet loved dramas as much as she did; as soon as the tablet turned on, it perched on her shoulder and stayed motionless.

The lobster rolls, bought in Destony City, were stuffed with rich meat and buttery sauce—one bite filled her with satisfaction, and she finished two servings before realizing her stomach was full.

Blackie got two rolls too, eating until its mouth was smothered in sauce. It then lay lazily on the floor, wrestling with a small alligator toy and nipping at it occasionally—looking surprisingly cute.

Once she'd finished eating and the drama ended, a thought hit her: the blood in the stairwell would reek if left unattended.

Sighing, Cecilia fetched two packs of wet wipes from the Space and headed to clean up.

To her surprise, Hailey was squatting there, wiping the surfaces with a tattered sheet.

The blood on the floor, walls, and door was almost gone.

"I thought it might start to smell, so I came to clean," Hailey explained with a smile when she saw Cecilia.

Cecilia glanced at Hailey's festering hands—she'd soaked the sheet in acid rain to make a cloth—and her respect for Hailey grew.

She said nothing, just turned and walked back to her apartment.

For the rest of the day, Cecilia indulged in pure relaxation: training briefly, playing with Blackie, eating, sleeping, and watching dramas—a thoroughly lazy routine.

That night, she grew tired of lazing around and poured Magic Spring water and acid rain into a large wooden barrel.

Relief washed over her when the barrel showed no corrosion, and she waved Blackie over.

Seeing the barrel, Blackie's ears drooped—its hatred of baths was obvious, every muscle tensing in resistance.

Cecilia had to bribe it with snacks to get it inside.

Jet flew nearby, chattering mockingly, "Bath time! Bath time for the grumpy one!"

When it hovered over Blackie's head, Blackie suddenly stood and swatted it into the barrel.

Cecilia froze, panicking briefly before seeing the parrot turn into a soggy mess, swimming pitifully.

Blackie was not to be messed with, and the incident meant both pets could bathe together.

Jet tried several times to climb out, but Blackie swatted it back each time.

Even the timid parrot lost its temper, yelling insults, "Bad dog! You son of a..."

Blackie ignored it, swatting Jet back whenever it tried to escape.

Cecilia fetched another barrel for herself, filled it with the Magic Spring and acid rain mixture, and climbed in.

She patted Blackie's head and Jet's small one. "Come on, let's all bathe together!"

Earlier, she'd heated the Magic Spring water beforehand, and the wooden barrel retained the warmth well—relaxing and beneficial.

Cecilia grabbed her tablet, fetched a fruit salad and a bowl of nuts from the Space, and settled in to watch a drama while bathing with her two pets.

Jet finally quieted down once the drama started, floating in the water next to Blackie and occasionally eating the nuts Cecilia handed it.

The three of them soaked for over two hours.
