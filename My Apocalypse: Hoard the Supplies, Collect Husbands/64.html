Chapter 64 Trouble Downstairs

The animals in the breeding area were all well-fed, and the earth-colored rabbit from the farm was pregnant!

Cecilia raised an eyebrow in amazement.
Truly extraordinary.

Emerging from the Space, she collapsed onto the sofa in a lazy slouch and pulled out a feast of pre-portioned food: chicken and mushroom pot pie, crab cakes, baked chicken thighs, grilled lobster tails, and spicy mussels.

It was a luxurious spread, complemented by a plate of fragrant pasta.

The pasta was made with premium durum wheat flour, cooked in an electric cooker until al dente, tender yet firm to the bite.

She sprinkled a pinch of black sesame seeds on top, boosting both nutrition and aroma.

With a tablet in hand, Cecilia half-reclined on the sofa, eating and watching a drama—mindless one, but utterly addictive.

Blackie showed no interest in the drama, gnawing vigorously on a toy Cecilia had given it before drifting off to sleep.

Jet, however, was fascinated. It perched on Cecilia's shoulder, watching intently.

Whenever the villainous female characters tormented the protagonist, it flapped its wings and squawked angrily, as if it truly understood the plot.

Cecilia discovered that after being exposed to acid rain and the Magic Spring water, Jet had gained control of its bladder and bowels.

Its diaper had stayed clean all day, and when she removed it, Jet hurried to its cage to relieve itself.

Overjoyed, Cecilia left the diaper off, laid a pee pad in the cage, and emphasized twice that it must use it.

She patted Jet's head in praise. "Good boy, Jet! You're probably the only parrot who can learn to use a pee pad. Such a good baby!"

Jet fluttered its wings happily, glancing at Blackie with pride—as if saying, "See? I'll never poop on your butt again!"

She binged dramas all night, finishing two bags of potato chips before dozing off on the sofa.

The sound of screams from downstairs jolted her awake in the middle of the night.

"Ah! I'll kill you! You bastard!"

Blackie and Jet stood guard at the door, staring nervously outside.

Cecilia frowned, wanting nothing to do with the commotion.

She rolled over, ready to go back to sleep, but the noise grew louder—shouting, crashing objects, clattering sounds that made rest impossible.

Annoyed by the disturbance, she pulled on a coat, grabbed a dagger from the Space, slipped on thick-soled Martin boots, and headed out with Blackie.

Arthur was already standing at the stairwell door, looking down.

Cecilia asked impatiently, "What's going on? Can't a person sleep at night?"

"Trouble downstairs."

As soon as Arthur spoke, Cecilia saw someone rushing up—it was Hailey.

She wore only minimal undergarments, her body covered in wounds, hair disheveled.

The worst injury was a deep, bloody gash on her thigh, oozing blood.

Yet she seemed oblivious to the pain, clutching a knife and inching backward, as if fending off a monster.

Soon, a group of people followed—including Phoebe, who'd been close to Hailey.

Phoebe was surrounded by several of Bryan's goons, wearing a sheer lace nightgown and red lipstick.

Two of the men were half-dressed, with minor wounds, cursing under their breath.

It was clear what had transpired downstairs.

Teary-eyed, Phoebe bit her lip and begged Hailey weakly, "Hailey... let's give in. I know you have no water or food—you'll die!"

"Like you did? Spreading your legs for them to get food? I'd rather die!" Hailey screamed, her voice breaking. "I'm disgusted by you, Phoebe! And you betrayed me—I trusted you so much! I saved you countless times, and you stabbed me in the back!"

Phoebe's face paled further.

Cecilia, now wide awake, frowned as she watched the scene unfold.

This Hailey was tougher than she looked, and she'd misjudged Phoebe completely.

Clearly, a lot had happened that day.

"Bitch, stop being stubborn!"

The injured man glared at Hailey fiercely, advancing threateningly.

Hailey raised her knife at him. "Come closer, and I'll kill you!"

"Kill me? I'll have my way with you right here and see if you still talk big!" Greed flashed in the man's eyes.

Hailey was stunning, even in her disheveled state—she owned a yoga studio, and her figure was impeccable.

Cecilia had seen her in yoga wear before, a confident bombshell.
