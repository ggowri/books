Chapter 176 A Quiet Escape

Her series of actions just now looked as if she were gently stroking the dog.

"General, wait until we leave before you get up."

Cecilia whispered softly beside the General's ear.

The General's eyes had already regained their brightness. It looked at Cecilia with gratitude and obediently continued lying on the ground without moving.

Cecilia stopped paying attention to the General and walked over to collect the guns from the men.

After checking them, she saw that they were all just old hunting rifles. No wonder they had been so slow when trying to fire earlier.

Only the one in Boss Carter's hand was somewhat smaller, but after a closer look she realized it was a crude homemade pistol and not very reliable either.

Still, something was better than nothing. Cecilia stored them all in the Space.

She checked the bodies again. After confirming they were truly dead, Cecilia finally let out a breath of relief.

After a while, Boss Carter had already fainted from the pain and lay on the ground like a heap of flesh.

At that moment, Cecilia heard the sound of a car approaching from behind.

Standing on higher ground, she looked into the distance and discovered it was a military vehicle.

The explosions and gunshots from earlier must have alerted the nearby military.

Cecilia looked at Blackie beside her.

"Blackie, just leave this fat guy alive. He was just talking about eating you. Oh right, bite through his throat so he can't speak. Tear his tendons too so he can't write."

After saying that, Cecilia put on her ice skates and left first.

Behind her, Blackie lunged at the fat man and bit down.

Although the military already knew Cecilia had a handgun, she still did not want them to know that she had done all of this.

After all, the military did not know that she had grenades.

Meanwhile, she headed straight for the place where Boss Carter had said he hid his supplies.

According to Boss Carter, besides food and drinks, there was also a "good thing" there.

But who could have expected that not long after Cecilia left, she ran into Arthur, who happened to be skating over.

Arthur said he was nearby and had also come to check after hearing the noise.

Seeing the blood on Cecilia, Arthur asked with concern, "Are you alright?"

Cecilia looked at Arthur in front of her and noticed that his clothes were not very thick. Through his windbreaker she could see white fur underneath.

If Cecilia guessed correctly, that should be the fur of the mutated rat king.

Behind her, she could hear Blackie running over.

And the military vehicle had already stopped. Soldiers were likely getting out to investigate the scene.

Cecilia knew this was not a good place to talk.

"Let's go first. This isn't a good place to talk," she said to Arthur.

Arthur nodded slightly. Wearing ice skates as well, he followed Cecilia as they skated toward the distance.

Cecilia noticed that Arthur was carrying an extremely large backpack on his back. Behind him he was dragging a huge sled loaded with many bundles.

Although she did not know what was inside, Cecilia guessed they were probably supplies.

After the two people and the dog traveled a bit farther, they arrived at a residential area.

Cecilia noticed that some people still lived there, but when they saw the two of them with a mutated dog, they immediately pulled their curtains shut.

Cecilia and Arthur found an empty house. They removed their ice skates, changed into regular cotton shoes, and walked inside.

Because someone was still living in the house, they both moved very carefully. After entering the room, they quickly shut the door and blocked it with several objects.

Once everything was done, Arthur sat on a stool and looked at Cecilia.

"You also came out to look for supplies?"

Cecilia nodded lightly.

Blackie looked a little tired. After transforming several times today, it was probably already starving.

Cecilia pretended to search through her backpack, but in reality she took out a small bag of dog food from the Space.

The moment Blackie heard the rustling of the dog food bag, its ears immediately perked up. The entire big black dog sprang up from the ground in an instant.

That reaction speed made Cecilia feel that its weak and exhausted appearance earlier might have been completely fake.

However, with Arthur beside her, Cecilia could not joke with Blackie, so she simply opened the dog food bag silently.

Blackie pushed its big head forward and buried its mouth into the dog food bag, devouring it eagerly.

After finishing, its ice-blue eyes looked at Cecilia as if saying, "I want more."

Cecilia glanced at Blackie helplessly. Eat, eat, eat. All you think about is eating. Didn't you see there's someone else here? You can have more when we get back.

Cecilia herself was also a bit hungry after everything that had happened.

So she reached into her bag and took out a compressed biscuit.

After all, Arthur was sitting beside her. She could not pull out big meals. She would just make do with the biscuit for now.

But when she took out the biscuit, she noticed Arthur looking at her with a faint smile.

His gaze made Cecilia feel uneasy.

At that moment, Arthur spoke.

"Ms. Summers, we're not exactly starving yet. We still have plenty of supplies. Did you really prepare so little before coming out?"

While Cecilia was still feeling confused, she saw Arthur pull his bag over and rummage through it for a while.

He took out a box of braised beef instant noodles, then pulled out a thermos and poured hot water into the noodles.

After that, he took out a jar of chili sauce and a box of luncheon meat. Then he turned to Cecilia and said, "You can eat mine. It tastes better than compressed biscuits."

Cecilia chewed her biscuit and suddenly felt that it was not nearly as delicious anymore.

Thinking about it, she really had not eaten instant noodles in quite a while.

Recently she had been eating things such as burrito bowls, honey-glazed pork ribs, crawfish boil, loaded chili bowls, fondue, and savory meat pies.
