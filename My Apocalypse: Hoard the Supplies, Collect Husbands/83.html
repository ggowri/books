Chapter 83 She Was Dead

Ashley didn't care that Cecilia was about to fight back, but Cecilia stepped back and kicked Ashley's knee again.

Ashley went down hard and ate dirt.

"Whoever starts trouble deals with the consequences, and I don't play nice," Cecilia said. "You think you can screw over the 66th floor? Better make sure you can handle what comes next."

She turned her sharp gaze to Mabel, the woman who'd approached her earlier. "Whose brilliant idea was it to short us on supplies? Hm?"

Dead silence fell over the crowd, and no one dared to speak.

It was apparent these people had pushed Mabel forward to handle things.

Cecilia glanced at Mabel's husband. She noticed the bag he was holding was slightly bigger than everyone else's—not by much, but enough for two extra bottles of water.

Mabel must have pulled some stunt in front of everyone to get them to accept her as their leader, but she wasn't stupid enough to do it for free. She'd definitely demanded a little something extra for dealing with this mess.

Cecilia almost laughed. So Mabel took her cut and then tried to short-change on the 66th floor? What did she think they were, pushovers?

"Oh my god, she's trying to kill me! You fucking bitch, you're trying to kill my whole family! You threw away our supplies—I'm going to tear you apart!"

Ashley struggled to her feet, and even with her injured leg, she lunged for Cecilia's hair.

Ashley had come up here alone to grab supplies—her precious son and grandson were all still downstairs.

Ashley's original plan was simple—grab the supplies, eat her fill first, and then share what was left with her son and his family. She'd even planned to hide a little extra for her precious grandson.

But she'd miscalculated. If she'd known it would go this badly, she would've at least brought her son along to back her up. Now she was losing face in front of Cecilia, and there was nothing she could do about it.

When Ashley lunged forward, Cecilia moved fast and caught Ashley's arm, twisting it hard.

She was done with this. Ashley had come after her one too many times.

Cecilia's hand moved to the small of her back, reaching for her knife.

But then Ashley stumbled from Cecilia's shove and crashed into someone nearby.

Ashley looked up and saw a pair of hands clutching a bag of supplies.

She didn't care who those hands belonged to. All she saw was that bag.

She immediately grabbed for it and started yelling. "Give it to me! I've got kids and elderly at home! If you don't give it to me, you're heartless!"

"Crazy old hag, what the hell are you talking about? You looking to get hit?" The person holding the supplies wasn't backing down.

Ashley screamed louder, her grip tight on the bag. "You're all trying to starve an old woman to death! Black-hearted bastards, every one of you!"

Everyone around them just watched. No one stepped in to break it up. They all clung to their own supplies and kept their distance.

Some people who didn't want any trouble had already slipped out the door while no one was looking.

"Ahhhhh!"

A piercing scream cut through the air.

The man Ashley had tried to rob finally snapped. His eyes went red, and he started hitting her.

Unlike the younger guys who'd just shoved Ashley around and kicked her a few times, this man's punches landed hard and deliberate. He went straight for her head, fist connecting over and over.

The guy seemed addicted to it, cursing non-stop as he beat her without holding back.

Cecilia frowned as she watched him, and then she caught his accent. Her blood ran cold. This was the domestic abuser who'd moved from the first floor to the 58th.

Ashley had picked the wrong person to mess with.

Hailey pressed closer to Cecilia's side, trembling. Cecilia took her hand and leaned in close. "Watch carefully. You'll see plenty more of this. Get used to it now."

Hailey froze for a second, then her voice shook as she whispered back, "Don't worry. I won't slow you down."

More people slipped away from the rooftop, and Mabel grabbed her husband's arm with a pale face.

Ashley let out one final scream and collapsed. She hit the ground hard and didn't move again.

She was dead.

The man who'd beaten her stumbled back two steps when he realized what he'd done. He looked around at the few people left, and panic filled his voice. "I didn't mean to! She tried to steal my stuff! I didn't mean to kill her!"

Then he ran.

Only Cecilia's group and Mabel's couple remained.

Cecilia turned to Mabel. "Did you see this coming?"

Mabel flinched under Cecilia's stare. "What do you mean?"

Cecilia let out a cold laugh and grabbed Mabel by the collar.

Mabel stumbled forward as Cecilia dragged her, screaming and struggling, but it didn't matter.

Her husband tried to intervene, but Arthur and Hailey held him back.

Cecilia dragged Mabel over to Ashley's body and shoved her down hard. Mabel screamed and tried to pull away, but Cecilia forced her face down until it was inches from Ashley's dead face.

"Look at her," Cecilia said coldly. "This is what happens when you mess with me. Take a good look, and stay out of our way from now on."

"Please, I'm sorry! I won't do it again, I swear!" Mabel screamed.

Her screams turned into sobs, and her whole body shook.

Cecilia finally let her go.

Earlier, when Cecilia had asked who came up with the idea to cut their floor's supplies, no one had said a word.

But she'd noticed something—most of the residents kept glancing at Mabel and her husband.
