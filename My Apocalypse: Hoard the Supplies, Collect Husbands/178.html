Chapter 178 The Villa of Stolen Supplies

Arthur did not mind at all and finished the compressed biscuits in just a few bites.

After the two of them finished eating, they stood up and packed their things, preparing to leave.

When they reached the door, Cecilia originally intended to say goodbye to Arthur. But after thinking for a moment, she turned back and looked at him.

"Mr. Barnes, I'm only helping to escort someone. Taking all those weapons from you feels like too much. I know a place with supplies. Want to go together?"

Cecilia said this for two reasons.

First, she was only helping to escort someone, so taking that many weapons really felt excessive. She did not want to owe Arthur a favor.

Second, when she killed those people earlier and spoke with Boss Carter, Arthur had probably already seen it.

It was very likely he had noticed that she had asked about the supply location.

Cecilia thought it might be better to go together. It would also give her one last chance to test Arthur' character.

Unexpectedly, Arthur did not answer her question. Instead he said, "Ms. Summers, we've known each other for a few months now. You can just call me Arthur from now on."

Cecilia thought that made sense and nodded.

"Then I'll call you Cecilia as well," Arthur added.

Cecilia had no objection.

"You killed those people. The supplies have nothing to do with me, so I won't go."

With that, Cecilia and Arthur went their separate ways.

Before parting, Arthur told Cecilia that the operation would take place tomorrow night.

As for the bundle of explosives he had promised her, Arthur said he could deliver it to the apartment first.

Cecilia had no objections.

She also appreciated that Arthur knew his place and did not insist on going with her to the supply location.

However, Cecilia did not know that when she turned toward the supply location following the direction Boss Carter had given, Arthur quietly followed behind her.

Arthur looked at the slender figure ahead and the large black dog running beside her. Thinking about the blood on Cecilia' body and the weapons Boss Carter's group had earlier, he frowned slightly.

In the end, he still felt somewhat uneasy.

Cecilia knew nothing about this.

She skated forward with Blackie. Taking advantage of the fact that the sky had not completely brightened yet, she traveled through the wind and snow for about an hour.

Finally, she arrived at the place Boss Carter had mentioned.

It was a villa on a mountain.

Unlike the villas the government used as shelters, there were only two villas here, both occupied by Boss Carter's group.

She carefully removed her ice skates and changed into ordinary snow boots. Taking Blackie with her, she quietly circled around to the back of the villa.

It was clear that someone regularly cleared the snow here. The snow around the villas had been cleaned fairly well, so stepping on it made little noise.

But Cecilia noticed that both villas were occupied.

Boss Carter had said that all their supplies were stored on the first floor of the villas, while the second floor was used for living.

But now the first floors of both villas had lights on, which meant someone was guarding them.

Just as Cecilia was considering what to do, she saw a man walk out from the villa on the left.

The man was about as fat as Boss Carter, with a face full of flesh. He was holding a chicken drumstick in his hand.

However, in his right hand he was dragging along a thin young girl.

The girl looked about twenty years old and had been crying and struggling the entire time.

Because she struggled too much, the fat man kicked her hard in the stomach.

"Behave yourself!"

The girl did not dare cry anymore. She stood up and followed the fat man with a wronged expression.

Seeing that she had quieted down, the fat man began to grin and grope her body with his hands while speaking filthy teasing words.

At that moment, a man suddenly came out of the other villa, with a seductive-looking woman beside him.

"Derek, why do you think Boss Carter and the others have been out so long and still haven't come back?"

The man called Derek Carter squeezed the young girl's waist and laughed.

"Maybe they're outside enjoying dog meat hot pot! They didn't want us two to guard the place and share it, so they're coming back late."

Derek laughed as he dragged the girl back into the villa on the right.

The man standing at the door of the other villa also laughed and went inside with the seductive woman.

Soon, cries and shouting could be heard from inside the house.

Even Cecilia felt that the noise was disgusting to hear.

"Today I'll take all your supplies away. Let's see how you keep committing evil after that."

Cecilia said coldly.

She pushed open the door and entered the villa. Inside she saw that the place was packed with food, canned goods, and various items used for surviving the winter.

From the styles and sizes of the items, it was clear that they had been looted from many households. Many of the clothes and quilts were not new, and quite a few even had traces of blood on them.

Cecilia stored all the food and canned goods in the Space.

As for the winter supplies, she selected the clean ones and stored them.

The dirty ones she tied together, stored in the Space, and threw into the Recycler.

The first floor was quickly emptied.

She then went upstairs to the second floor and found it extremely messy.

Men's clothes were scattered all over the ground. There were also bits of food and drinks everywhere, making the place look like a pigsty.

Cecilia looked around briefly but found nothing useful.

Only one room looked more luxurious than the others. It was most likely Boss Carter's room.

Inside, Cecilia found some gold and stored it in the Space.

After finishing this, Cecilia prepared to leave with Blackie.

She had no intention of going to the other villa to take a look.
