Chapter 131 The Warehouse Rats

On the way, Cecilia asked Arthur what happened when he went for the car.

Arthur told her the parking garage was packed with luxury vehicles, but most were dead in this Ice Stage cold.

Regular gasoline wouldn't ignite in freezing temps like this.

Only the RV had cold-resistant diesel, which was why he'd managed to drive it out.

Problem was, starting the engine woke up the mutant cockroaches hiding in that mall. They swarmed him instantly.

"Those mutant creatures are way tougher than us. They hide in the deepest corners—normal noise doesn't wake them."

Cecilia nodded slowly.

Those things had their own survival tactics. Numbers were still low for now. But later, once their mutations ramped up, they'd breed like crazy. Insect apocalypse.

Not wanting to waste space, Cecilia loaded grain into the inflatable boats too. No point hauling them back empty.

As they approached another warehouse, Blackie suddenly bolted over.

He bared his teeth at the door, expression twisted and vicious.

Cecilia had spent enough time with Blackie to read him perfectly.

"Something's inside."

Suddenly she remembered Dyson's parting words—that smirking "watch yourself."

Sure enough, the moment Cecilia reached the warehouse door, something slammed against it from inside.

Then came the bone-chilling sound of grinding teeth and high-pitched squeaking.

"Rats. More than one," Arthur said.

They weren't about to take stupid risks. Time to check out the other warehouses.

So far, Cecilia had cleared two warehouses, they'd just emptied one together, and Dyson's crew had already looted another before that.

Out of the original six warehouses, only two remained.

When they'd scouted the two Cecilia had emptied earlier, Arthur's face stayed blank.

Made sense—Cecilia had banked on that. Arthur had no clue how many trips Dyson's group had made, so he'd naturally assume they'd cleaned those places out.

But the last two warehouses? Both crawling with rats.

Cecilia and Arthur locked eyes. Both their faces went grim.

"We doing this?" Arthur asked bluntly.

"Hell yeah."

No way were they leaving good grain for those mutated bastards to gorge on. What a waste.

Blackie pressed tight against Cecilia's leg, body tense, ready to protect her.

Cecilia noticed—Blackie was fiercely protective. Loved messing with her when things were chill, but the second danger hit? Always the first one in front of her.

Arthur stepped forward, massive machete in hand, pistol on his hip. He swung hard and shattered the heavy iron lock.

The lock looked brand new—nothing like the corroded ones on the other warehouses. Clearly acid rain hadn't touched it.

Dyson's crew must've found the mutant rats earlier, fought them off, then locked the survivors inside.

The door swung open—three black shapes lunged out.

Blackie launched himself up, jaws clamping down on one.

Arthur's blade flashed. A rabbit-sized mutant rat split clean in half.

Cecilia's hidden knife traced a beautiful arc through the air. Another rat's head went flying.

Then—silence.

That's when it clicked for Cecilia:

Three rats had charged out—each going after one of them.

They were testing their strength.

Her headlamp swept the warehouse. Countless red eyes stared back. Dozens of them.

And in the center stood one massive rat—way bigger than the rest. White fur, dog-sized, standing upright on two legs like a person.

It stared at Cecilia and Arthur without a hint of fear.

Cecilia noticed its eyes were blue. Just like Blackie's after eating that blue orb in the Space.

This rat's different. It's evolved something.

Then the whole granary came into view.

Layout-wise, it looked like any other warehouse.

Rows of white sacks stuffed with corn and sorghum.

Weirdly, the mutant beasts seemed to understand how precious food was now. They hadn't trashed everything—just grabbed a few bags from the edges to munch on, and even piled their garbage in the far corner.

Everything screamed one thing: these rats—at least the Rat King—had intelligence.

"Squeak."

A low, rumbling sound. The Rat King.

Three more shadows burst forward, again splitting targets—Cecilia, Arthur, Blackie.

And these three? Way bigger than the first wave.
