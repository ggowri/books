Chapter 27 Trip to Destony City

She called several acquaintances, warning them she'd heard through a friend that severe weather might be imminent and urging them to stock up.

Rumors of unusual weather were already swirling online, making her alert feel natural, not overbearing.

Her northern hometown hadn't seen drastic changes yet, but reports from the south spoke of far more extreme climate oddities.

Unsurprisingly, hoarding food had become a hot topic—posts on the subject dominated trending pages that day.

After making the calls and passing along her warnings, Cecilia headed to the reservoir again. This time, she used large buckets to collect water for post-apocalyptic daily use.

Unlike before, the Space didn't absorb the water—this batch stayed put.

With that task done, she began to ponder the optimal use of her remaining millions of dollars.

There were still supplies to buy, but she had post-apocalypse plans and didn't need to spend every penny now.

Her moment to act would come when everyone cowered indoors, afraid of acid rain—especially since Blackie would likely have mutated by then.

Upgrading the Space felt more critical than anything else.

She'd noticed a vast expanse beyond the white mist in the Space, with curiosity piqued about the secrets it held.

If it already boasted the Magic Spring, could other incredible abilities be far behind?

The apocalypse was only days away; she had to spend the money soon.

After a long moment of thought, a perfect plan took shape—hoarding stones to keep upgrading the Space.

Cecilia now knew special stones triggered the advancements—first the strange ore, then the stone from the pet store owner.

Her target was the country's largest raw stone market—the gemstone trading hub in Destony City.

It teemed with raw stones.

As Cecilia glanced at Blackie beside her, hesitation flickered.

The fastest route was by plane, but she couldn't bring him along.

Sensing her gaze, Blackie stared back, his large head tilted with a faint, human-like curiosity.

"Wait a minute!"

A thought struck her, untested but promising.

The Magic Spring's appearance meant the Space had upgraded again—maybe from energy-rich stones at the reservoir's bottom, or perhaps the water itself held the Space's needed energy.

After the last upgrade, it could accommodate one living creature.

Has this advancement made two possible? Could Blackie enter too?

Raising an eyebrow at Blackie, Cecilia focused her will.

In an instant, he vanished from the room.

Cecilia checked the Space immediately—and there he was, standing confused and alert in the unfamiliar surroundings.

She tried to step into the Space herself, but nothing happened.

The upgrade hadn't unlocked dual occupancy; the Space still wasn't advanced enough.

Yet Cecilia refused to dismiss the possibility entirely—the Space had surprised her too many times before.

Her only wish was simple—to enter alongside Blackie.

Putting the thought aside for now, she quickly pulled the agitated Blackie back out.

Realizing he was suddenly home again, with Cecilia grinning mischievously in front of him, Blackie froze for a beat before understanding—he'd been tricked.

He hung his head, turning his back to her in a clear show of anger, as if saying, "I'm mad at you!"

Cecilia laughed until her sides ached. The spring water had made this dog even more personality-filled.

With Blackie's transportation issue solved, she hurried to book the earliest flight to Destony City.

After booking the ticket, she packed a small bag and prepared to head to the airport.

With an hour's drive ahead, a flight departing in two hours, and a roughly three-and-a-half-hour journey, she'd land in Destony City by 3 p.m.—just in time to take a taxi to the gemstone market for stocking up.

Then Cecilia could spend the evening hunting down local food, many of which were her favorites: assorted pastries, desserts, and egg tarts, all of which she planned to buy as much of as she could carry.

The next day would follow a similar rhythm—another trip to the gemstone market in the morning, more food shopping in the afternoon, and an evening flight back home.

In just two days, she'd blow through the remaining millions of dollars—a true spending spree.

Before leaving, Cecilia stepped into the Space to prepare for Blackie, stocking up on his dog food, treats, meat, and water for the two days ahead, along with his toys, dog bed, and litter box.

She'd been training him to use the latter lately, and he'd already mastered the skill.

Unsure if she'd get a chance to let him out for walks during their trip, Cecilia knew it would be a minor inconvenience for him, but unavoidable.
