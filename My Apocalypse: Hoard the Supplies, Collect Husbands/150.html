Chapter 150 Pure Torture

She pocketed the lollipop for now.

In Plot Two, the veggies she'd planted were ready. This time—a huge crop of tomatoes, each one pumpkin-sized.

Cecilia grabbed one and took a bite. Juicy, sweet, amazing.

At the garbage processor, she'd tossed in those busted safes from the Boss's office yesterday.

She opened the back pocket. A knife and a key.

The knife on the left was thin and sharp, with an engraving: Hurts Like Hell.

The key on the right was simple and blunt, labeled: Master Key.

Cecilia was very interested in that master key.

Could it really open any lock?

But then something hit her—tonight, she'd already decided to pay Phoebe a visit and teach her a lesson. And the Space just... handed her these three things?

Like it was saying—I know what you're planning. Here, I got you covered.

This wasn't the first time either.

Cecilia stared at the Space's foggy boundaries. For a second, she felt like there were eyes in there, watching her.

Or maybe... the Space inside her had eyes of its own.

But without materials to upgrade the Space further, she couldn't figure out what was really going on.

******

Late that night, when everything was quiet.

Cecilia slipped into her black outfit and crept out, moving silently to the 65th floor.

Phoebe's door had several new locks. She was clearly terrified.

But people who ran their mouths needed to pay.

Cecilia pulled out the master key.

To her delight, one gentle turn and the lock clicked open.

A real "master key" that opened everything.

But when she got to the third lock, Phoebe's trembling voice came from inside.

"Who—who's out there? I'll scream if you don't answer."

Turns out, after seeing Cecilia's expression earlier, Phoebe had been too scared to sleep. She'd been sitting in the living room clutching a knife, and only now worked up the nerve to come to the door.

Cecilia's hands flew. She'd already opened the fourth lock—the last one.

She yanked open the security gate, then the heavy inner door.

"Phoebe, if you think someone's coming to save you, go ahead and scream."

Cecilia didn't do much.

She just went in and tested that knife and the Space-produced lollipop on Phoebe.

Punishment for her big mouth.

One cut—Cecilia didn't even see blood.

But Phoebe collapsed, whole body convulsing.

She couldn't even scream anymore, just whimpering quietly, begging.

Cecilia was shocked. The cut wasn't even deep, but Phoebe looked like she was in absolute agony.

Then came the lollipop.

Phoebe went completely insane. She started running into walls, headfirst. Finally she grabbed Cecilia's arm in a death grip.

"Kill me. Please kill me."

Seeing the knife in Cecilia's hand, Phoebe lunged forward, grabbed her wrist, and drove the blade into her own chest.

One strike. Dead.

And in that final moment, Cecilia swore she saw relief flash across her face.

Yeah. Those two items from the Space? They hurt like hell.

And just like that, Phoebe was dead.

She didn't even understand what had happened to her in the end.

In that final instant, her whole life flashed before her eyes.

She'd been born into a happy, loving family.

She'd been pretty since she was a kid, and she had a good education too.

After graduation, she'd opened a small restaurant—easy work, didn't need to make tons of money.

She'd even had a rich boyfriend. Their relationship was solid. They'd talked about getting married next year.

Then the disaster hit. Everything changed.

She changed.

Sometimes she felt filthy. But to keep herself from falling apart, she'd tell herself—there was no other choice.

She didn't want to sleep around. She just had no way out.

Deep down, she envied Hailey.

Why could Hailey be so strong, so unwavering? While she fell apart?

She'd only ever had one boyfriend. Only ever slept with one man!

So why... why did she feel so dirty now?

The moment Phoebe died, Cecilia glanced at the knife and the lollipop-hammer lying nearby.

These two... yeah, they're pure torture.

But they had a downside. After using them, numbers appeared on each item.

The lollipop showed a 9.

Both knives showed an 8.

Limited uses, apparently.

She didn't spare Phoebe another look. The woman got what she deserved.

As Cecilia turned to leave, something in Phoebe's room made the Space inside her body vibrate violently.

She searched frantically.

Then she stopped by the cabinet she'd passed earlier.

She opened the cabinet. Inside—a serpentine ornament, plus serpentine bracelets, seals, pendants, the works.
