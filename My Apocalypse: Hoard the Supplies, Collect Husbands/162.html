Chapter 162 The Reversal

At this moment, Daniel Brooks didn't take the dog beside Cecilia seriously at all.

Blackie, however, slightly lowered its body, clearly provoked by Daniel Brooks's words.

Cecilia sighed.

She then glanced around and realized that nearby were two unfinished buildings.

The tables and chairs placed in front of her were probably moved from the office building not far away.

Cecilia thought that it was no wonder they chose this place for an ambush. It really was a good location.

There was no one on either side, not even a witness, and the road stretched long both in front and behind. Anyone stopped here would have no way to escape.

On both sides of the road were paths leading to two commercial districts in the city, both filled with shops.

Some residents who hadn't yet entered the shelter probably came to these two areas occasionally to search for food.

Out of the corner of her eye, Cecilia noticed something on the ground nearby.

There were actually two corpses.

It was a naked woman holding a young man in her arms.

Both of them had twisted expressions. The young man's neck had been bitten through, blood covering the ground. One of his eyeballs had even fallen out. The scene was horrifying.

The woman knelt on the ground while tightly holding him. Her body was also covered in blood, and there were claw marks around her waist.

Just looking at the two bodies, it was easy to imagine what they had gone through before they died.

Snow had already buried half of their bodies, but it was clear they hadn't been dead long.

It had probably happened just a few hours earlier today.

Looking further to the side, Cecilia saw even more corpses buried beneath the snow.

There were teenagers, elderly people, and women in their fifties.

What was even more unbelievable was that even some of the elderly women were naked.

These people were worse than beasts.

"You killed them?"

Cecilia asked Daniel Brooks.

Holding General by the leash, Daniel Brooks stepped forward a little and looked Cecilia up and down.

"Cecilia, you don't want to end up like them, do you?"

Cecilia sighed again. She raised her wrist and checked the time on her watch, realizing that she had already been wasting ten minutes here.

She didn't have time to continue this standoff.

"Blackie, go."

Cecilia looked at the dog beside Daniel Brooks and felt a trace of pity.

Most animals didn't have a concept of good or evil.

Their intelligence mainly gave them the instinct to hunt, their primitive impulses, and their loyalty toward humans or companions.

People always said a dog had turned bad, but in most cases it was actually the owner who had failed to raise the pet properly.

Blackie had already been eager to fight, just waiting for Cecilia's command.

The people nearby burst into mocking laughter.

They shouted things like "General is unbeatable," "General, tear it apart," and "Such a tiny dog daring to challenge General."

Blackie's blue eyes grew colder and colder amid the shouting as it slowly stepped forward.

General approached proudly, baring its teeth. Blood stains could still be seen on them, along with bits of flesh stuck between the gaps.

But when it met Blackie's eyes, it suddenly froze.

General let out a whimper, its tail tucking under its body as it kept retreating.

Seeing this, Daniel Brooks frowned in confusion.

"General, attack!"

But General became even more fearful. Its legs even started trembling.

When Blackie saw this, it bared its sharp white teeth toward General.

Daniel Brooks grew angry. He pulled out the whip from his waist and lashed it across General's backside.

"You useless beast! I told you to charge and bite it to death! Didn't you hear me? It's such a small dog. What are you afraid of?"

The people around them also began shouting, telling General to obey or it would be beaten again.

It was obvious that Daniel Brooks often whipped General in order to control it.

These people were worse than animals.

General clearly possessed far greater strength than them, yet because of its loyalty, it had stayed by their side all this time.

It had even followed their orders to hunt other people.

And yet they whipped it and oppressed it.

From General's gaunt body, it was clear that it probably didn't even get enough to eat most of the time.

"Wuff… hmm."

Blackie let out a short sound.

The trembling General suddenly froze.

"Hm hm."

Blackie barked twice more.

Even as Daniel Brooks continued whipping it, General showed no reaction, as if it were thinking about something.

Then Blackie's voice grew more urgent, as though urging it.

Finally, General slowly lifted its head and looked at Daniel Brooks and the other men around him.

Its eyes turned red.

Under Daniel Brooks's whipping, a line of tears flowed from its eyes, slowly freezing into small icy crystals in the frigid air.

"General, you're not listening now? I'll beat you to dea—ah!"

Daniel Brooks hadn't even finished speaking when General suddenly turned and lunged at him, biting off his head in a single snap. Blood sprayed everywhere.

The other men who had been shouting just moments ago were stunned. When they realized what had happened, they turned to run.

But General smashed through the tables and chairs blocking the way, rushed over, and knocked them to the ground. Almost one bite at a time, it tore off their heads.

Blood splattered everywhere.

Cecilia stared at the scene in shock.

Blackie, however, looked slightly smug. Calmly lifting a paw, it walked through the path General had opened by knocking aside the tables and chairs.

Then it turned its head to look at Cecilia, as if asking whether she was leaving.

Cecilia glanced at Daniel Brooks's corpse, then at the men still being chased down by General, and finally at the smug Blackie.

In that instant, she understood.

Just now, Blackie had been threatening General.

Or rather, controlling it.
