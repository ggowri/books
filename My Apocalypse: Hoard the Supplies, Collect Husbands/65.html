Chapter 65 Save Hailey

"Hailey... Please, please be sensible! I don't want to watch you die!"

Phoebe begged, teary-eyed and delicate—her appearance stirred compassion, but her words made one want to slap her.

At the very least, Cecilia loathed women like her.

In her previous life, she'd seen many women trading their bodies for supplies in the apocalypse. Most thrived in the early days, some even bullying others like Phoebe did.

But in the later stages, when people awakened superpowers, women without such abilities became fragile as vases—easily shattered.

Beauty meant nothing in the face of strength.

Worse still, Phoebe had betrayed her own friend.

Disgusting, through and through.

"Tch, such an ungrateful bitch!"

Patience worn thin, the injured man snapped.

He snatched a stick from a comrade and stormed toward Hailey, fury blazing. "We'll see how long you keep acting tough! I'll knock you out first, then teach you a lesson!"

Hailey gripped her dagger, resolve hardening her features.

She shot Phoebe one last resentful glance, then pressed the blade to her neck. "Even as a ghost, I'll never let you get away with this!"

The man rushed to stop her, but Hailey moved faster.

At the critical moment, a dark object whipped toward her neck, knocking the dagger aside.

The blade still grazed her cheek, slashing open a long gash across her once-pretty face.

Blood gushed forth.

Phoebe screamed hysterically until a man beside her slapped her silent.

Stepping out from behind the door, Cecilia held a knife, her gaze cold as she stared at the injured man halfway up the stairs. She pulled Hailey to her feet.

Fear flashed across the man's face at the sight of Cecilia; he stumbled back, flashbacks of Bryan's gash that morning haunting him.

With medicine scarce, Bryan had lain bedridden all night with a high fever—his survival uncertain.

"Scram." Cecilia's voice was calm, sharp as a blade.

One of the men glowered at her, indignant. "Bitch, who asked you to meddle?"

Cecilia's cold stare silenced him instantly. She nodded toward her security door, splattered with Hailey's blood.

"You dirtied my door. This isn't meddling. Keep talking, and you'll learn what real meddling feels like."

Blackie emerged from behind the door, too, growling low—a menacing sound that froze the group. Its lips curled back, as if ready to tear the men apart.

The injured man stepped back twice, gritting his teeth as he glared at the disfigured Hailey before retreating.

Only when they reached the stairwell landing did they dare shout back, "Little girl, don't think a dog and a tough act make you untouchable! Push us too far, and we'll kill your mutt—and you!"

Blackie lunged downward in a flash, sinking its teeth into the lead man's leg.

He screamed in agony as Blackie shook him violently before releasing its grip.

The others fled downstairs in terror, while the injured man crawled away—his leg clearly broken.

When Blackie returned, Cecilia patted its head, wiping blood from its fur with her sleeve. "Good boy, Blackie. You even know how to protect me now, huh?"

Blackie wagged its tail, eyes glinting with disdain, as if the men were barely a snack.

"Thank you..." Hailey's voice wavered, her earlier resolve crumbling.

Cecilia had held back until the last second to test Hailey's character.

If Hailey had surrendered, it would have proven her weak—even if Cecilia saved her this time, she'd likely fail to survive future crises. Such a person wasn't worth rescuing.

If Hailey had begged Cecilia for help, it would have revealed her lack of self-reliance. Save her once, and she might come begging again when trouble struck. Rescuing someone like that would be a waste of effort.

But when Hailey raised the dagger to her own neck, a thought crossed Cecilia's mind. This woman was tough, decisive, and resilient—she didn't deserve to die like this.

She could be a valuable asset in the apocalypse.

Right now, Cecilia lived on the 66th floor in an uneasy truce with Arthur. She still hadn't fully figured out his background. Would adding a third person to form a checks-and-balances triangle stabilize the situation on the floor?

That was why she stepped in, even considering taking Hailey in temporarily.

Besides, Hailey posed no threat—Cecilia could easily have Blackie take Hailey down if she wanted to.

Nurtured properly, Hailey could become a capable subordinate and teammate.
