Chapter 179 A Sudden Gunshot

After all, something unspeakable was happening inside, and Cecilia felt disgusted.

Since there were still people in the other villa, Cecilia led Blackie out from the villa on the left and carefully closed the door.

Then she held the gun and slowly walked outward.

At that moment, the door of the other villa suddenly opened not far away.

Blackie instantly turned his head and took a protective stance.

The seductive woman who came out also did not expect to see a stranger here.

The seductive woman was named Jenny Shaw. She was twenty four years old and used to be a minor internet influencer.

After the apocalypse began, she quickly attached herself to Boss Carter. However, when Boss Carter was not around, she was involved with Liam Barnes here.

So during this period of time she had been living quite comfortably, with people serving her.

She had just finished her business with Liam and came outside to smoke a cigarette.

Her face carried a faint flush. She was wrapped in a fur coat, and underneath she wore a light blue qipao.

Then she saw Cecilia.

The girl carried an energetic aura. There was a dog beside her, and an extremely large backpack on her back.

She instantly understood.

Someone had come to steal from them.

At some point she had already completely regarded herself as a member of this gang rather than just someone's woman.

After all, here she received tremendous respect, endless food, and never had to suffer the cold.

The younger members of the gang all called her Sister Jiang when they saw her.

"Derek, Liam, stop what you're doing. There's a thief here… ah!"

She shouted loudly.

Then she felt pain in her left shoulder.

When Cecilia saw someone appear, she immediately pulled out her gun and fired a shot straight into the woman's shoulder.

Cecilia did not want trouble. After firing the shot, she turned around and was about to run.

But unexpectedly, after being shot, Jenny remained calm. She took out a gun and fired directly at the road beside Cecilia.

"Hehe, hurry up Derek and Liam. There's also an especially fat black dog. Boss Carter will definitely like it."

Cecilia clenched her fists.

Jenny actually had a gun.

"Oh, little girl, I advise you not to move. I intentionally missed just now. I used to be a shooting enthusiast. If you move again, next time I'll shoot your head."

Jenny was not lying.

After saying that, she raised the gun again and fired at the trunk of the tree beside Cecilia.

The bullet brushed past Cecilia' face and even cut off a strand of her hair.

It had to be said that the marksmanship was excellent.

"See? I'm not trying to scare you."

Jenny said with a flirtatious tone.

However, her shoulder truly hurt terribly. She could even see her clothes being dyed red with blood.

But at this moment she was too excited to care about it.

Looking at Blackie beside Cecilia, she thought to herself that when Boss Carter returned, if he saw such a large black dog he would definitely be delighted.

Right now Cecilia had her back to Jenny, while Blackie stood in front of her.

Cecilia also had a gun and was pointing it at Jenny.

But Jenny's reaction just now had been fast, and her shooting had been extremely precise.

Cecilia knew she was not faster than Jenny.

Now there was only one method.

Enter the Space.

However, if she did that, it would expose the fact that she possessed the Space. And before nightfall she probably would not be able to leave the Space again.

Moreover, everyone who witnessed her entering the Space would have to be killed.

At that moment, Derek and Liam came out from inside.

When they saw Blackie and Cecilia outside, Derek grinned.

"Wow, this dog is really nice. Our brothers could eat it for several meals. As for that little girl, turn around and let your big brother take a look."

Derek said with a smiling face.

He looked at Cecilia. Her figure could not be clearly seen because of the thick clothes she wore, but her long black silky hair was impressive.

Her face was covered up, yet those eyes were truly beautiful.

Recently the women he had found all looked malnourished. Not only were they thin and weak, their hair was dry and dull.

This smooth hair alone was enough to stir wild thoughts.

At that moment, Derek and Liam both pulled out their guns and aimed them at Cecilia.

"Little girl, there are three of us and only one of you. You can't be faster than us," Liam warned.

Cecilia began thinking of a plan.

She raised her hands above her head, making a gesture of surrender, trying to make the people in front relax their guard.

But she slightly bent her body, preparing to move closer to Blackie so she could take Blackie into the Space at any moment.

At that moment Liam spoke again.

"You can keep the girl, but kill the dog first. Otherwise it might bite someone later."

The moment Liam said that, Cecilia felt a sudden jolt in her heart.

She saw Jenny already raising her gun.

Cecilia cursed inwardly and quickly reached out to grab Blackie, intending to take Blackie into the Space with her.

But in that split second, before her hand could even touch Blackie, a gunshot rang out.

Everything in front of her seemed to move in slow motion.

Cecilia saw the woman named Jenny curl her lips slightly, her gun pointing toward Blackie.

However, before the muzzle could lower, her beautiful face suddenly burst open.

A bullet struck directly between her eyebrows.

Blood immediately flowed down.
