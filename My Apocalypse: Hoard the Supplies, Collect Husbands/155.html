Chapter 155 Secrets

Two black shapes lunged—and missed.

No surprise. It was Blackie and Jet.

Cecilia had let them in on purpose. Time to knock Ari down a peg.

"Stupid dog and parrot!" Ari hissed at them.

Jet? That bird could trash-talk for a full day without an audience.

So when someone insulted it? Game on.

"Go fuck yourself...!"

It didn't stop for a full minute. Not a single repeat insult.

Even Cecilia, busy putting the piglets in the Space, stood there stunned.

Jesus. Who the hell is Jet's previous owner? And why did they teach it to curse like a sailor?

Ari wasn't backing down. It leaped at Jet, ready to brawl.

Blackie, silent until now, saw its chance. One paw slammed Ari to the ground. Then it sat on the cat.

"Ahhh! Animal abuse! Get off me, you dumb dog! ABUSE!"

Blackie looked down at Ari, droopy-faced and silent. But its eyes said everything: Listen to Cecilia. Stop being a brat.

Cecilia ignored the chaos. She set the piglets down in the farm, dumped in feed and fresh grass, and filled their water.

Then she checked what the pig pen could produce.

Pork sausages!

Her mood lifted. Nice. Having my own pigs is definitely worth it.

After tidying up, she grabbed the two animals and said goodbye to a sulking Ari, ready to leave the Space.

Then Ari stopped her.

"Hey... so... according to the contract, I'm supposed to call you Boss. Hmph. Fine. Boss it is."

"Boss, I've got a secret about the Space. I'll tell you—but only if you give me that thing you got today. Oh, and your other dimension pendant too. I want to eat them both!"

"This secret's super useful. Might even save your life someday."

Ari licked its paw, grinning like a little devil.

Cecilia narrowed her eyes at Ari.

She had to be extremely careful.

The Space had screwed her over too many times. She couldn't afford to trust it.

But looking at Ari now, a theory formed.

Wait—did the jewelry set Hailey gave her also have a Space?

Cecilia didn't answer right away. "Let me think about it."

Before Ari could protest, she left the Space with Jet and Blackie.

She pricked her finger and dripped blood on the gemstone set. No reaction.

She examined it closely. Definitely antique—the metal chains showed oxidation. Pearl accents dotted the pieces.

It was a complete jewelry set: necklace, bracelet, ring, earrings. Gorgeous.

The aesthetic was eclectic—multiple gemstone types, vintage feel, thick materials, exquisite craftsmanship.

After testing it every way she could think of, she confirmed—no spatial reaction whatsoever.

She relaxed.

So... maybe she could let Ari eat it?

But she wasn't going to make this easy.

She grabbed the jewelry and reentered the Space.

Ari saw what she was holding and lunged forward excitedly.

Blackie and Jet immediately flanked Cecilia like bodyguards, blocking Ari's path.

"The purple pendant? Not happening. This I might consider." Cecilia's tone was firm.

Ari looked annoyed at first, then its eyes rolled cunningly.

"Fine! Then I'll tell you half the secret!"

Cecilia didn't hesitate. She turned to leave with Blackie and Jet.

Ari panicked immediately. "Boss, let's talk this out!"

Cecilia looked at Ari, holding up the gemstones, dead serious.

"This set has two earrings, one necklace, and one bracelet. Four pieces total. So I want four secrets about the Space in exchange. You don't agree? We're done here."

"Three!"

Ari huffed, practically rolling on the ground in frustration.

But Cecilia didn't budge.

She'd figured it out—Ari was mischievous and a total scammer, but not that smart. Mentally? About ten years old.

And it was desperate.

Desperation makes you sloppy.

Cecilia had lived a whole extra life. She could handle one ten-year-old, no problem.

In the end, she traded one earring for the real method to upgrade the Space:

1. Spiritual stones of all kinds—gems, diamonds, even certain special rocks.

2. Mutant creature corpses. Even partial remains work.

3. Mutant plants. Plant them in the Space, and they'll fuel its internal energy cycle.

Cecilia hadn't expected mutant plants to be part of it. They were still in the Ice Stage—she hadn't run into any yet.

But then an idea hit her...

She had acid rain! And she had plant seeds!

What if she cultivated mutant plants outside the Space first, then transplanted them in?

Only problem? Plants mutated way slower than animals. Who knew how long it'd take to grow even one mutant plant.
