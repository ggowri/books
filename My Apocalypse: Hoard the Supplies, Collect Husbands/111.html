Chapter 111 A Cat?

Jet was way too chatty at home. Even though Cecilia had drilled it into him a hundred times—no talking outside—and he'd promised, he was still a problem.

After circling once, Jet finally perched on Blackie's head, eyes darting around nervously.

At least he kept his beak shut, following Cecilia's orders to stay silent outside.

"Jet, you're fast and nimble. Fly down and scout what's below."

That was Cecilia's plan.

Jet could move freely in the air, flew quietly, had perfect night vision—ideal for recon.

Time to put that bird to work.

Jet hesitated, staring at the stairwell like it might eat him.

But finally, he spread his wings and flew off.

Cecilia and Blackie waited in the darkness, watching where Jet disappeared.

One minute. Two minutes...

In the silence, Cecilia thought she heard something below.

A soft, light landing.

Then—a quiet meow.

Cecilia's blood ran cold. A cat?

Blackie's spine arched, eyes locked forward, ready to attack.

Then Cecilia saw it—a black cat, nearly invisible in the darkness, slowly climbing up from the stairwell.

Suddenly, a black shape shot up from below.

Cecilia jumped—then realized it was Jet.

She'd never seen him fly that fast!

Jet circled frantically above Cecilia's head, opening his beak several times but staying silent—still following her "no talking outside" rule.

"Report," Cecilia said quickly, eyes locked on the cat.

The black cat tilted its head, looking curious. It didn't climb higher, just lifted one paw playfully.

It looked harmless—like those neighborhood cats sunbathing in the grass, ready to roll over for belly rubs.

Except... its size.

This black cat was leopard-sized. Over three feet long, tail curled upward, paws the size of dinner plates.

She could only imagine how massive and razor-sharp those retracted claws were.

Big cats had always ruled the top of the food chain.

Tigers, kings of the forest. Lions, rulers of the savanna.

Even leopards, cheetahs, snow leopards—all apex predators.

Someone once said housecats only acted sweet and cuddly because they were small.

Well... the thing in front of Cecilia was anything but sweet and cuddly. It made her skin crawl.

But what came next was even worse.

The moment Cecilia gave permission, Jet shrieked, "Cats! So many cats! SO MANY CATS!"

His voice trembled. Terrified.

Then he dove onto Blackie's back, shaking as he stared ahead.

And Blackie? He lunged in front of Cecilia, shielding her completely.

Then Cecilia noticed movement below.

Soft footsteps. Almost silent.

But there were so many that they created an eerie rhythm.

Pad. Pad. Pad...

Then she saw them—countless pairs of glowing green eyes emerging behind the black cat.

"Meow."

"Meow."

"Meow."

The chorus of meows in the darkness formed a spine-chilling symphony.

Cecilia did a quick count. Over twenty cats!

All of them massive, mutated beasts!

Her mind raced.

In her past life, after the insect plague hit, mutated creatures had appeared everywhere.

She'd faced desperate situations before, but always with a team.

The key to dealing with mutated animals? Use their instincts.

No matter how much they mutated, at their core, they were still the same animal.

Like Blackie—smarter now, could change size, way stronger—but still a dog at heart.

Still loved toys and treats.

So these cats...

Cecilia pulled a catnip ball from the Space—one she'd grabbed from the pet sanctuary.

The moment it appeared, the cats went wild!

Before they could charge, Cecilia hurled the catnip ball through the gap in the stairwell railing—the opening that went straight down to the first floor!

The big cats that had been focused on her instantly scrambled downward, meowing frantically, disappearing from sight.

Cecilia bolted back to the top floor with Blackie and Jet, slamming and locking the stairwell door behind them.
