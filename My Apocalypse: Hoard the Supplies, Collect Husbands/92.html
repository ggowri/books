Chapter 92 Suicide

Back then, all Rebecca could think about was how Alexander was easy to control and that the Barnes family fortune must not fall into others' hands. She never imagined they'd encounter acid rain and all this.

If Alexander died now... Rebecca clenched her fists.

She knew Miranda's methods.

But looking at the knife and bandages in her hand, she hesitated again.

"What's this?" Andy asked, eyeing the items curiously.

After Rebecca explained Cecilia's suggestion, Andy fell silent for a moment. Then he picked up the tools and walked into the room.

"What are you doing?" Rebecca asked.

Andy snorted. "Hmph, you said he can't die, right? Then amputation shouldn't be a problem. I know his condition. If we do nothing, his whole arm will rot, and he'll really be gone. We're saving him."

Rebecca knew Andy was right. Gritting her teeth, she stomped her foot and ran to the next room.

It was self-deception, a way to distance herself from the act for when Miranda questioned her later.

Soon, Alexander's agonized screams echoed through the walls.

About two hours later, Andy walked to Rebecca, holding the bloodied knife. "Done. I bandaged him up. With the anti-inflammatory meds, he won't die. But his hand and foot are gone. He's a cripple now."

Hearing this, Rebecca felt a complex mix of emotions.

But under Andy's intense gaze, she shyly lowered her head. "Andy, thank you. And you must testify for me. When the Barnes family comes, you have to say everything we did was to keep Alexander alive."

"Of course. In this damn weather, surviving is thanks to us. That pretty boy would've been dead otherwise."

Rebecca nodded quickly. She was determined now to brainwash Alexander, to make him feel indebted to her.

He wasn't bright anyway.

"You know, a delicate girl like you, what can you rely on? Let me take care of you, okay?"

Andy suddenly moved closer to Rebecca.

Rebecca feigned shyness, lowering her head with a slight nod.

Andy's heart itched. He pulled her into an embrace...

Actually, not long after Rebecca went downstairs, Cecilia followed.

She listened to Rebecca and Andy's plan, heard Alexander's screams, watched Andy lead Rebecca into a bedroom...

Then, she quietly slipped into Alexander's apartment.

A fire was burning inside, but the temperature was still low.

Alexander lay on the bed like a pile of ruined flesh. His right hand and foot were gone.

Though crudely bandaged, blood still seeped out.

The area around the bed was splattered with spray patterns of blood, a gruesome sight.

Cecilia noticed Andy had forgotten to give Alexander the anti-inflammatory pills. Smiling, she stepped forward, took a bottle of water from her space, forced a pill into Alexander's mouth, and washed it down.

"You can't die from infection so easily. That would ruin the fun later."

After doing this, Cecilia patted Alexander's unconscious cheek. Amidst the indecent sounds from Rebecca and Andy's room, she turned and went upstairs.

The medication and bandages Cecilia gave Rebecca weren't meant for just one hand and one foot.

There was enough for two hands and two feet.

Sure enough, a few days later, due to poor care, Alexander's other hand and foot also developed frostbite.

Having done it once, Andy felt no fear this time. With a swift motion, he amputated Alexander's remaining limbs.

Rebecca put on a show, hugging Alexander and weeping, saying it was Cecilia's idea, that they had no choice.

She swore she would love Alexander no matter what he became.

Alexander, utterly haggard, just stared coldly at Rebecca.

Later, while Rebecca and Andy were entangled in the other room, Alexander used all his remaining strength to drag himself to the window and jump.

When Rebecca returned to the room and saw the open window and Alexander gone, her face drained of color.

Before his death, Alexander had torn open his bandaged stumps and written a message in blood on the floor: [I WILL HAUNT YOU FROM THE GRAVE! THE BARNES FAMILY WILL NEVER FORGIVE YOU!]

What Rebecca never expected was that the Barnes family would arrive very soon.

Cecilia was by her window, sipping tea by the stove while looking down. Then, she saw Alexander's body after the fall.

The acid rain below had frozen. Alexander had landed, becoming a mangled mess of flesh on the ice.

Cecilia recognized him by his clothes.

A slight smile touched her lips. Rebecca must be having sleepless nights now.

But the more interesting part was yet to come.

She remembered from her past life that the Barnes family held significant power for a period, having aligned themselves with some influential figure.

Back then, when she nearly froze to death during the Ice Stage, the Barnes family had "kindly" come to her rescue.

Their true intention was to see if Cecilia's family had any other spatial items among their heirloom jewelry.

But she remembered clearly: in her past life, Alexander lived just fine. He must have reunited with the Barnes family soon after reaching the shelters.

In this life, Alexander never made it to the shelters.

So, wouldn't the Barnes family, who held considerable power during that time, come looking soon?

Cecilia wondered how they would react when they arrived and saw the frozen, mutilated corpse of their precious heir.
