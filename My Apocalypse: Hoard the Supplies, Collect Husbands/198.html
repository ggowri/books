Chapter 198 Jet's Pretty Lady

To help Jet's wing heal properly, Cecilia had fitted it with a splint and wrapped it with two turns of bandage.

That meant Jet could only hop about. Flying was completely out of the question.

Only when Cici shot him an angry glare did he shuffle a couple of steps across the floor, twist himself about a little, and mutter in a low voice, "That pretty little lady is really lovely."

Where on earth had Jet even learned the phrase pretty little lady?

At that moment, Cici seriously felt like cutting off the power to his IPD.

She looked at him in utter exasperation, like a hopeless little rake, and flicked him hard on the head.

"Jet, the two of you are completely unsuitable. She's a pigeon, you're a parrot. She's tiny, and you…"

Cici rolled her eyes.

Jet, however, was utterly unconcerned.

"Breed isn't the problem. Size isn't the problem. Even gender isn't the problem."

Cici was so annoyed she dragged him straight back into the flat without another word.

She did not go to fetch the medicine immediately. Instead, she went over to the IPD, determined to see exactly what Jet had been watching by himself at home lately.

The moment she checked, she was appalled.

Jet had been watching some influencer style animated shows Cici had downloaded from a video site, the kind that came with scrolling live comments.

There was nothing wrong with the cartoons themselves.

But those comments…

Those comments could lead anyone astray.

Face darkening, Cici immediately put the IPD away.

She could not let Jet learn bad habits.

The moment Jet saw his usual little toy confiscated, he let out an indignant squawk and tried to hop onto the sofa so he could wheedle and pout at Cici.

Unfortunately, he jumped too quickly. His injured wing struck the edge of the sofa, and with a heavy thump he toppled over backwards onto the floor.

The whole thing looked ridiculous.

Blackie watched from the side and, expressionless, merely lifted his eyelids slightly, as if to say, Idiot.

Ignoring Jet's theatrics, Cici took out the medicine produced by the Space.

It was the same dark ointment she traded with the old general, the very medicine Arthur had been asking for.

A bruise and wound ointment made from purely herbal ingredients, straight from the Space.

Blaise Hart's injuries were all external, so this would be more than enough.

As for fever medicine and the rest, Cici trusted Arthur would already have that sort of thing.

She brought the ointment to Arthur and explained how to use it.

In truth, it was simple. Just apply it directly to the wounds.

Cici had already tested this medicine herself. It was simply much more effective than ordinary wound ointments, worked faster, did not leave scars, and felt cool and soothing on the skin. It was nowhere near as miraculous as the Magic Spring water.

That was why she had dared trade it with the old general, and why she dared give it to Arthur now.

Arthur thanked her, then tossed the dimension ring in his hand straight to her.

"Take whatever you want."

The instant the ring landed in her palm, Cici felt her chest begin vibrating again.

Which meant Arthur had severed the bond between himself and the ring.

She paused for a moment, forcing down Sprite's agitation inside her chest, then asked with a teasing smile, "Arthur, aren't you worried I might take your dimension ring and disappear?"

Arthur did not even lift his head. He was carefully applying medicine to Blaise's wounds, and from the side, the slight furrow of his brow made him look uncannily like an overworked father tending a son.

"Would you?" Arthur asked in return.

Cici only smiled and said nothing. She cut her fingertip with a knife, let the blood fall, and entered Arthur's dimension ring.

The space inside was not large.

Compared with her purple pendant, it was smaller, let alone compared with the Space inside her chest.

It covered perhaps three hundred square metres, though it was over five metres high.

All sorts of things were piled inside.

Most of it was food, weapons, boilers, generators, and other supplies, which must have come from Grant Cole's organisation.

It was obvious that before going to Grant's base, Arthur had already emptied out the ring to make room.

Things like the boilers and generators were not useful in the small rooms they lived in now, just as Arthur had said. They could be traded to Colonel Reed for other resources.

As for the weapons, Cici selected some of them, about one whole bagful.

She did not lack food for the time being, so she left the supplies untouched.

Just as she was about to leave Arthur's space, Cici spotted an old chest sitting in one corner.

It was tightly secured with a brass lock.

She had a feeling there was something good inside it.

In the end, Cici took out the bag of firearms and the old chest, then severed her own connection to the ring and handed it back to Arthur.

Arthur glanced at the two things she had chosen, accepted the ring, and asked with a smile, "Just those? When you bargain with Colonel Reed and the old general, you go in at full force."

In other words, she was being rather merciful with him.

Cici laughed.

"I charge fair prices. It's only one jar of ointment. I'm hardly going to fleece you."

Arthur looked at the smile on her face, and something bright flickered in his eyes.

He still had to take care of Blaise, so after exchanging a few more words with Cici, they were ready to part.

Cici herself was feeling hungry and more than a little tired, and wanted nothing more than to go back, eat something, and sleep.

She picked up her things and turned to leave.

But to her surprise, Snowy suddenly shot out.

Cici saw nothing but a white blur racing straight at her.

"Snowy!"

Arthur called out sharply from behind.
