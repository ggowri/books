Chapter 159 The Deal for Mutated Creatures

This man was also completely heartless.

Looking at his wife's corpse now, he immediately realized he had run into someone he could not provoke.

He hurriedly took everything out of his pockets and kept saying that he would never dare to do it again.

He did not show even the slightest bit of sympathy for his wife.

Aside from the very beginning, he never looked at his wife's body again.

At that moment, footsteps suddenly came from downstairs.

The two gunshots earlier had probably attracted people.

Several soldiers arrived. When they saw the man in front of them, one of the soldiers immediately frowned and kicked him.

"Harvey Webb, what are you causing trouble for again?"

It was obvious that this Harvey was already notorious with the military.

When Harvey saw the soldiers arrive, he immediately knelt down and began kowtowing. He pointed at Cecilia and Arthur, claiming that they had killed someone and asking the military to deal with them.

"They saved me! Harvey and Hannah are the bad ones!"

The little boy who had been covering his eyes against the wall suddenly spoke.

He had already turned around. When he saw Hannah lying dead in front of him, fear first appeared in his eyes, but it quickly turned into overwhelming hatred.

When the boy saw the soldiers arrive, he immediately ran over and began crying while explaining everything that had just happened.

"Soldier brothers, it really wasn't those two brother and sister. It was those two… they've been abusing me all this time. I didn't dare say anything. My parents are already dead. They said if I told you, they would throw me outside to freeze to death. Soldier brothers, please believe me."

The soldier looked at the woman lying dead at the stairway and then glanced at the injuries on the boy's arms after he rolled up his sleeves.

He immediately kicked Harvey.

"You animal. Do you have any shame left? Come with me to the back area."

He did not mention anything about Cecilia and Arthur killing someone or carrying guns. It was probably because Colonel Reed had given instructions earlier.

Only then did Cecilia learn that after Colonel Reed arrived, he had directly arranged a back area.

The back area was a place for the dirtiest and hardest labor.

Only people with bad behavior or those who had made mistakes were sent there.

Although it had only been implemented for two days, its reputation had already spread, and many people knew how harsh the back area was.

It was also said that the place was in the lower levels buried under the snow where daylight never reached.

Harvey shouted loudly, but he was still dragged away by the soldiers.

One soldier stayed behind and gave Cecilia a military salute before preparing to take the crying boy downstairs.

Before leaving, the little boy bowed to Cecilia to express his gratitude.

Cecilia only nodded lightly and said nothing more.

Cecilia was very interested in this back area, so she stopped the soldier and asked more about it.

"Actually, we don't have enough manpower right now. There's a lot of work that can't be handled. You can't expect those people to keep things clean. Often a group of grown men are crammed together, with filth everywhere. But poor sanitation easily leads to illness. So some people in the back area are sent to the dirtiest places to clean. Others are sent outside to carry supplies."

At the same time, Cecilia learned that the military had also organized some people to go out under military protection to search for resources.

Most of the resources they found would be handed over, but each person could receive some food as a reward.

After asking around for a while, Cecilia finally asked the question she most wanted to know.

"If you encounter mutated creatures and eliminate them, how are their bodies handled?"

The soldier was slightly surprised before replying.

"If some parts are edible, they're taken back to the unit. The ones that can't be eaten are thrown away."

Cecilia smiled.

"Could you give me some of the ones that aren't needed? I can exchange them with some other things."

The "other things" Cecilia mentioned were the locations of the two warehouses she had promised to provide to Captain Hayes earlier.

She had been thinking about what she could exchange with the military using the supplies in those two warehouses.

Now that she understood the true method for upgrading the Space, Cecilia had an idea.

As for why she wanted the bodies of mutated creatures, she had already prepared an explanation.

Research, medicine production, and food manufacturing.

As far as she knew, most mutated creatures were valuable from head to toe.

However, the military only discovered this during the Heat Stage.

At that time, food had almost completely disappeared and had become extremely precious.

The military then ground the bodies of mutated creatures into powder for people to eat.

Some powders were made from bones, some from skin, and some from entire creatures that could be processed.

Although the powder made from mutated creatures did not taste good, it was far more filling and could even slightly strengthen the body's resistance.

Later, it actually became a common food and even a staple.

It saved countless people from starving to death.

Only several years later, when mutated crops like mutated rice and mutated wheat could be mass-produced, did the era of eating powdered mutated creatures as staple food finally end.

Even so, some people still consumed mutated creature powder as a "nutritional supplement."

Cecilia planned to reveal this discovery about mutated creatures being edible ahead of time.

At the same time, it would help upgrade her Space.

This could also be connected with the next task where she would take Colonel Reed to find the machine tools.

When the time came, she could bring back two sets of equipment together and place them on the sixty-fifth floor.
