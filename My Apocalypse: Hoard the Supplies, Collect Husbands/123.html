Chapter 123 Next Move

They had no idea what the hell had happened in this building.

Then someone from the Barnes family in another car grabbed the soldier's megaphone and started barking orders at the people upstairs—real arrogant.

Said the government had picked this shelter, that the Barnes family had chosen it, and everyone better fall in line.

Who would've thought—the person who came down was Cecilia again!

And Logan couldn't believe what he was seeing.

She was spotless. Head to toe. Wearing thick, expensive fur. Her eyes sharp—wary, suspicious.

She was totally different from the baby-faced little girl he'd seen before.

Logan glanced at the other two with her.

The woman with the scarred face looked plain, nothing remarkable about her—but she was clearly doing alright.

Then there was the man beside them...

Logan felt it immediately. Danger. Couldn't pinpoint why, but this guy radiated it.

Like a sharp blade hidden in its sheath.

"So I hear our building's been seized as a shelter? By the Barnes family?"

Logan caught the mockery in Cecilia's voice.

"That's right—"

"Who the hell do you think you are? What's with that tone? Got a problem with the Barnes family?"

Logan didn't even finish before someone cut him off—voice blaring from the other jeep.

They all turned as a man climbed out, bundled in a fur coat under a parka.

The dude was bundled up like a bear, with only his eyes visible.

Cecilia's brow furrowed. She squinted at him.

The man swaggered over, casual as hell, eyeing Cecilia and Arthur.

Once he got a good look, he snorted. "Well, well, if it isn't Alex's pretty bargain wife."

The guy who just stepped out of the car? Nathan Barnes. Alexander's cousin.

Cecilia had met him before.

Back when she and Alexander were dating, Nathan always looked at her with that half-smirking expression.

She remembered her past life—she'd almost broken up with Alexander once because of Nathan.

She'd gone to a Barnes family banquet, had a bit too much to drink, and ended up lying down in the back of Alexander's car.

But she wasn't asleep.

By chance, she overheard Alexander talking to Nathan in the passenger seat.

Nathan said Cecilia was just an orphan—no matter how pretty she was, she wasn't good enough for the Barnes family.

Alexander replied that girls like her were easy to control.

Later, when Cecilia tried to break up with him, Alexander sweet-talked her for ages, claiming he'd meant she was sweet and well-behaved. Bought her all kinds of gifts to win her back.

Cecilia had been such an idiot back then and got back together with him.

Looking back now, she realized how incredibly stupid she'd been in her past life.

As for Nathan...

From what Cecilia knew, he was smarter than Alexander, more arrogant than Alexander, and had way more brains than Alexander.

Like in her past life—Cecilia later found out Nathan had known she wasn't asleep in the backseat. He'd deliberately stirred up trouble between her and Alexander.

And for what? No reason at all. Just thought it was fun, entertaining. Liked causing problems for people.

Total piece of shit, basically.

So yeah, Cecilia had never liked Nathan. Not in her past life, anyway.

This time, hearing Nathan's words, her brow furrowed. Her hand moved toward the knife in her pocket.

The moment Nathan looked down to light his cigarette, Cecilia lunged.

"Holy shit!"

Nathan yelped and stumbled backward.

Logan moved to intervene, but Arthur blocked him.

Several soldiers rushed forward.

Hailey and Blackie cut them off—no one was getting through.

Just as Cecilia reached Nathan, the jeep doors flew open.

Several massive guys formed a wall, protecting Nathan.

They deflected her knife.

Cecilia recognized them.

The crew Miranda had brought last time. Including that guy Frank.

She glanced at Arthur, confused.

Arthur watched the scene unfold, totally expressionless. Like none of this had anything to do with him.

"Ce—Cecilia, let's just talk this out, okay?"

Nathan peeked out from behind Frank, grinning nervously.

"Nathan, Alexander and I have nothing to do with each other. Before the apocalypse, I broke off the engagement with the Barnes family."

Cecilia crossed her arms, staring at Nathan's pathetic display.

"Okay, okay, fine. My unlucky cousin's dead anyway. So I'll just call you Ms. Summers from now on, like Logan does." Nathan backed down instantly.

Cecilia glanced at Hailey and Arthur, who'd been blocking Logan. She nodded slightly.

Then they walked over and stood beside her.

Logan approached, his face cold, sighing.

"Ms. Summers. Didn't expect we'd meet again like this."

Cecilia smiled without warmth.

"Logan, if you want to requisition this place as a shelter, shouldn't you ask the actual residents first?"

Just then, she heard footsteps behind her.

She turned. The other survivors from the building were coming down.

And leading them? That kid Evan.

The reason they'd taken so long? They'd been huddled together plotting their next move.
