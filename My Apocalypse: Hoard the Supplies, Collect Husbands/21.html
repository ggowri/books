Chapter 21 Sold the Remaining Half

Faced with this man, whose smile masked a dagger, Cecilia clenched her fists tightly, forcing herself to hold back from swinging.

The dinner date itself went smoothly enough.

Harvey had a natural knack for charming women, the kind who could win over most young girls with just one meal.

But Cecilia knew better; the handsomer his face, the darker his heart.

In her past life, he'd thrown her straight into a pit of disgusting, mutated centipedes, mocking her scarred face as ugly.

He'd even taunted her to strip, saying he'd decide whether to save her based on her figure.

In the end, others had rescued Cecilia, and she'd clung to life by a thread.

Harvey said suddenly, feigning distress as they walked back after dinner, "Cici, back in school, everyone said you were a rich kid. Rebecca even mentioned you had stocks, funds, tons of money. Honestly, a girl like you... I'd be too scared to pursue. I don't think I'm good enough."

A wave of relief washed over Cecilia—she finally saw through Rebecca's game.

She had sent Harvey to swindle the remaining half of her fund shares! And that 20 million dollars from the Barnes family? Alexander must be kicking himself now, regretting letting it slip away.

Now that their motives were clear, the next step was straightforward.

A cold glint flickered in Cecilia's eyes—they'd come after her first, so they'd have to pay the price.

After their first date, Cecilia insisted on meeting in spacious, crowded spots for subsequent outings—always with Blackie in tow.

She'd quickly noticed his wandering hands; on their first dinner, he'd tried to slip an arm around her waist.

Later, during a phone call, he'd even suggested getting a hotel room.

The urge to slap him had been strong, but Cecilia had held back for the sake of her plan.

Instead, she'd used "Blackie has separation anxiety and clings to me" as an excuse to bring him along every time.

Ever perceptive, Blackie would bare his teeth and growl the moment Harvey got too close, keeping the man at least three feet away.

By their fourth or fifth meeting, Cecilia could tell Harvey was growing impatient. That was when she sighed, putting on a helpless act. "Actually, I do have some fund shares. I've been wanting to sell them at a low price lately, but I don't know how to go about it."

Harvey's eyes lit up instantly. "I spent two years abroad—picked up a lot about finance. Maybe I can help you out."

Suppressing a smile, Cecilia put on a grateful expression. "That would be amazing! I just want to get rid of them quickly. I'd take two-thirds of the market price, as long as I find a reliable buyer."

And just like that, she sold the remaining half of her shares to the Barnes family for another 20 million dollars.

Whether the money came directly from the Barnes family or included a cut for Harvey, Cecilia didn't care.

Soon enough, all that cash would be worthless anyway.

Selling all her stocks and funds through official channels would have taken months, and Cecilia would likely face malicious price undercutting. In the end, she'd waste precious time and might not even get as much as she did from the Barnes family.

Asking for a higher price would have led to endless haggling, delaying her even more.

This way, Cecilia got the money and bought herself time. The Barnes family thought they'd scored a huge bargain, handing over the cash eagerly.

Little did they know, they'd be forced to sell off their businesses and supply chains—many of which were food and restaurant networks—to raise the funds quickly.

Cecilia was certain; this would cost them a crucial lifeline when the apocalypse hit.

Let them savor their little victory for a few more days.

When Harvey asked why she was in such a hurry to sell, Cecilia made up a bunch of nonsense—she wanted to buy a house, move abroad, anything to paint herself as a naive, emotionally fragile rich girl with no real plans for the future.

With the money in hand and her mission accomplished, Cecilia planned to stock up on more supplies.

But Harvey wasn't ready to let go.

One day, he tricked her into meeting him in a wooded area of a park, using the name of a mutual friend.

Cecilia had found it suspicious, but knowing the friend was close to Harvey, she'd seen through the ruse immediately.
What a fool, walking right into my trap.

When she arrived, it was indeed Harvey waiting for her.

He grinned, glancing around cautiously. The moment he saw Blackie wasn't with her, his smile widened into a lecherous grin.

He reached for her as he spoke, "Hey, Cici... we've known each other a while now. Wanna be my girlfriend?"

As she scanned her surroundings, Cecilia's voice remained calm. "Are there any security cameras nearby?"

Harvey tried to touch her face. "No need to be shy, baby. Don't worry, no cameras here. And I didn't tell anyone I was coming—just you and me."

Cecilia smiled, a cold smile on her face. "That's perfect."

Before Harvey could react, she waved her hand and vanished him into the Space.

Turning on her heel, Cecilia left the park and took a taxi to a remote mountain forest on the city's outskirts.
