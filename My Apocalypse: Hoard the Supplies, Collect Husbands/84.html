Chapter 84 Jet Can Talk

The message was clear. This was Mabel's idea.

Cecilia knew the property manager well enough.

Mabel was all smiles to your face, but vicious behind your back.

Now that Bryan was dead, Mabel was the biggest threat in this building.

Today, Cecilia was making a statement. She wanted everyone here to know—don't touch us, or you'll regret it.

"Throw the body out," Cecilia said. "Then you can all leave."

Cecilia shot Arthur and Hailey a look, and the two of them let go of Mabel's husband, Charles Duncan.

They watched as Mabel and Charles hauled Ashley's body up to the roof and threw it over the edge, then slunk away like beaten dogs.

Cecilia and the others headed back downstairs.

The supplies had been slim to begin with, so they split what was left without much fuss.

By the time all the chaos died down, it was already noon, and Cecilia was starving but weirdly not tired.

Probably had something to do with drinking that Magic Spring water last night.

Back at her apartment, she grabbed a container of pasta and dug in, washing it down with an ice-cold orange juice. Damn, that hit the spot.

Jet was pissed and wouldn't shut up, chirping non-stop in her ear about how she never took him anywhere anymore.

Jet had changed a lot over the past couple of days.

First off, he'd figured out how to use the bathroom on his own, which was a huge relief.

On top of that, he'd grown bigger—way bigger—about the size of a hawk now, and when he perched on her shoulder, she could feel the weight.

His talons and beak had gotten sharper, too. When Cecilia ran her fingers over them, she could tell they were serious weapons now.

His voice had changed the most, though—he didn't stutter anymore and could handle way more complex words, and his tone had gotten deeper.

If she closed her eyes and just listened, Cecilia would've sworn she was talking to some burly dude instead of a parrot.

"Alright, alright, I'll introduce you to my friends tonight." Cecilia pulled out a nut and handed it to Jet.

She'd figured out that nuts were Jet's absolute favorite.

Jet flapped his wings happily.

Cecilia added, "But before that, we need to talk about the rules. If you can't behave, I'm not taking you anywhere!"

Cecilia stared at Jet thoughtfully and tried to gauge whether he was smart enough to understand what she was saying.

After all, he could get in and out of the Space and knew her deepest secret.

Jet never stopped talking and loved to run his mouth!

If she let him out like this, he'd be a walking disaster waiting to happen!

Jet tilted his head and looked back at her.

Then he said something Cecilia never expected. "Blackie told me I have to listen and follow orders when we go out. He said I can't just say whatever I want, and I especially can't tell anyone about our secret base!"

Jet's eyes lit up, and he circled around Cecilia in excited loops.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. Not bad, Blackie. That dog really was hers through and through. He even knew the Space was their "secret base."

But then something clicked. Wait. What did Jet say?

"You and Blackie can talk to each other?"

Cecilia grabbed Jet mid-flight and pulled him down, staring into his beady little eyes with growing excitement.

"Yeah, we can!"

Cecilia's heart jumped. Ever since she'd brought Blackie home, she'd never been able to figure out what was going on in that dog's head.

They were close now, sure, but Blackie still had a way of acting distant sometimes, and it made her wonder if he actually liked her or just tolerated her because she fed him.

"So what else did Blackie say?" Cecilia stroked Jet's head and smiled sweetly. Then she added, "I'll give you an all-you-can-eat nut buffet today. You can eat until you're stuffed."

For food, Jet only hesitated for one second before he sold Blackie out completely.

"Blackie said he loves deer and rabbit meat canned food!"

Cecilia already knew that.

"Blackie said he wants to bite anyone who tries to mess with you!"

Cecilia couldn't help but smile. See? She hadn't spoiled him for nothing.

"Blackie also said he likes you the most—"

Before Jet could finish, a black blur shot across the room and clamped down on Jet's wing, dragging him away.

"Help! Blackie's gonna kill me!" Jet wailed as Blackie hauled him off.

Cecilia stood there, warmth spreading through her chest. Blackie had said he liked her best. He really had.

*****

After they played around for a bit and finished eating, Cecilia went into the Space and pulled out a cup of fresh cow's milk and a cup of fresh goat's milk.

The ranch in her Space had grown pretty efficient now, and each type of animal produced something useful every day.

The cows gave fresh milk, and the goats did too, and both restocked daily.

She kept the cow's milk for herself and split the goat's milk between Blackie and Jet.

She honestly hadn't expected Jet to drink goat's milk at all.

She didn't know much about parrots, so she couldn't tell if this was because Jet had mutated or if parrots just naturally drank milk.

Today, the Recycler spat out two pairs of down-lined cotton pants.

Cecilia checked the sizes and saw they were one man's and one woman's, so she tucked them away for now.

Then she tossed two more useless items into the Recycler and planned to check back tomorrow.

Cecilia was starting to think the Space could predict what was coming next in the apocalypse.

Based on everything she'd seen so far, the Ice Stage was definitely coming up next.

And recently, the Space had been producing precisely the kind of stuff they'd need to survive freezing temperatures.

By afternoon, the weather outside had completely cleared up, and all those people who'd grabbed government supplies started getting hopeful again and thinking everything would go back to normal soon.
