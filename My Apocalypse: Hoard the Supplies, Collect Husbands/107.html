Chapter 107 Evolution

It was negative thirty outside. Cecilia had a fire going, but the room barely hit freezing.

Even so, Cecilia was sweating bullets.

She didn't dare give Blackie meds or Magic Spring water—just held him tight.

After checking him over and over, her gut told her: Blackie was evolving.

Soon his body started burning up.

She checked his temp. One-forty Fahrenheit.

He passed out cold.

Jet stopped messing around. Just stood there, staring at Blackie.

Three days. Blackie didn't wake up.

Cecilia checked his temp every few hours, tried to get him to drink water.

He wouldn't eat a thing.

On the fourth night, Cecilia woke to something wet on her face.

A massive dog head. Bright eyes staring at her.

Except—those eyes glowed blue in the dark, filled with tiny sparks of light.

Just like... the light specks she'd seen in the Magic Spring and Black Soil.

Wait. Blackie had eaten one of those blue specks in the Space.

That's what did this?

All her drowsiness vanished. Cecilia shot awake.

She sat up and noticed something else—Blackie had shrunk.

Before, it'd been the size of a small pony. Moving around the apartment was a nightmare—like having a mobile wall in the way.

But now? Blackie was back to its pre-mutation size. Except for those blue eyes, it looked completely normal again.

Cecilia stared, gently stroking its head, murmuring to herself.

"What the hell happened to you?"

Before Blackie could answer—not that it could—Jet started flapping and screeching.

"Hungry! Blackie hungry! Starving!"

That's when it hit Cecilia—Blackie woke her up in the middle of the night to say: "Hey, human! Feed me!"

And then she witnessed what could only be described as a bottomless pit.

Two pounds of dog food? Gone in seconds.

Blackie sat there afterward, staring at her with those big, dreamy blue eyes. Tail wagging. Waiting.

Those eyes made her feel like she was being gazed at by every beautiful thing in the universe.

"More! More food!" Jet chimed in helpfully.

Cecilia sighed and pulled out a few cans of dog food from the Space.

Blackie devoured them. Gone in seconds.

Maybe regular stuff wasn't cutting it. She tried the Space-produced rabbit meat instead.

Same result. Gone in seconds.

Alright. She was done playing around.

She grabbed a laundry basin, dumped in forty pounds of kibble, and added an entire bag of freeze-dried treats on top.

This time Blackie didn't inhale it in three bites. He actually took his time—chomping away for a solid ten minutes until the bowl was spotless.

Then he sat back down. Staring at her. Waiting.

Cecilia froze. No way.

"At this rate... I can't afford to feed you!"

She was about to cry.

But what could she do? He was her dog. She had to feed him.

She pulled out another twenty pounds of dog food and a huge pot of boiled chicken breast. Mixed it all together.

This time, Blackie ate slower. Toward the end, he looked like he might actually be full.

But true to form, he powered through. Finished every last bite.

Then he flopped over and passed out.

Cecilia stood there, staring at him in disbelief.

What am I supposed to do? He's eating me out of house and home!

What kind of bullshit evolution was this?

Terrifying.

The next few days, she watched Blackie like a hawk, anxiety gnawing at her.

Even with a mountain of supplies, if Blackie kept this up? He'd clean it out.

Thankfully, after that first insane meal, his appetite went back to normal.

And after a few days, Cecilia figured out his mutation—he could shrink and grow at will.

His intelligence had leveled up too. Especially when it came to screwing with her.

Before, he'd just turn on the AC or jump-scare her in the bathroom.

Now? She'd open a door and—BAM—giant furry black wall. Wouldn't budge. And unless she bribed him with treats, he wasn't moving.

Straight-up highway robbery.

Cecilia was ready to lose it.

Worse, Jet was in on it.

Blackie had no clue when she'd leave. Jet kept feeding him intel.

That was it. She grabbed both troublemakers and laid down the law.

"Misbehave again? No more treats. Blackie—snacks and toys, gone. Jet—every nut you own, confiscated. Try me."
