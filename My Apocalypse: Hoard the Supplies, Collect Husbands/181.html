Chapter 181 The White Puppy

Everything had been piled up outside the door.

Meanwhile, Arthur Barnes had been inside for quite a long time without coming out. Cecilia waited by the entrance.

After about ten minutes, she finally saw him emerge. He seemed to be carrying something in his arms, wrapped in a piece of cotton cloth.

She walked over. "What is that?"

Arthur's face remained cold. He shifted slightly, not letting her get a proper look at whatever he was holding, then let out a sigh. "A baby."

Cecilia frowned, unsure what to say.

Of course, the baby was heartbreaking, but at a time like this... how were they supposed to take it home and raise it?

For one dreadful moment, she was afraid Arthur was about to say, "Cecilia, I need your help with something."

Just then, she noticed that Blackie, who was lying nearby, had suddenly become unusually agitated, as though he wanted to get up and sniff the bundle in Arthur's arms. He kept making urgent little whimpering noises, eyes wide and round.

Cecilia blinked in confusion. What was wrong with Blackie?

Arthur crouched down and lowered the bundle in front of him.

Only then did Cecilia finally get a clear look.

It was not a baby at all.

It was a tiny snow white puppy.

It looked barely three months old, small and fluffy, ridiculously adorable.

"Why on earth have you wrapped a dog up like a swaddled infant?" Cecilia snapped through gritted teeth.

The moment she met Arthur's half amused gaze, she understood.

He had done it on purpose. He was getting revenge on her for laughing at him earlier.

Though, in fairness, she had laughed rather too loudly.

"I'm taking this little thing home to raise," Arthur said, setting the puppy down in front of Blackie.

Blackie immediately licked it several times, tail wagging furiously.

From the way the puppy let out soft little yips and wriggles, it seemed rather fond of Blackie too. One big, one small, the pair looked as though they were trying to talk to each other.

"Probably thinks it's too tiny to eat yet. Wants to fatten it up a bit first."

Arthur pulled off the cloth wrapped around the puppy, tossed it aside, and set the little creature on the ground. Then he went back into the villa and dragged out another batch of supplies.

"Leave the rest for the women inside."

Cecilia watched as he brought out bags of rice, flour and cooking oil, along with two boxes of roasted chilli sauce.

There were also several firearms and some ammunition.

She pointed at the pile she had left by the door of the villa on the left. "This is all I'm taking too."

With that settled, the two of them drove Boss Carter's modified vehicle round from the back of the villa, loaded everything onto it, took the puppy with them, and prepared to leave.

Before getting in, Cecilia glanced back at the villa.

Several heads had appeared at the windows of the villa on the right. They were all young women, seven or eight of them by the look of it.

Now that Boss Carter and his men were dead, those girls had gone from prisoners to the new owners of the place.

Whether they would be able to hold on to it, or whether someone else would take it from them, was another matter entirely.

How they would survive from here on out was no longer Cecilia's concern.

She and Arthur drove back to the apartment building.

On the way, Arthur got out and loaded the explosives he had hidden earlier into the vehicle as well.

There was not much conversation between them during the drive.

Only Blackie and the puppy kept up a lively commotion in the back seat, one licking, the other nipping, then swapping roles a second later. They were having a marvellous time.

Cecilia glanced back several times. Arthur's little stray was genuinely very pretty, though its face was rather pointed and its tail a touch too thick, which made it look slightly odd.

"What are you going to call it?" she asked.

"Snowy."

Arthur answered almost at once.

"Snowy?" Cecilia glanced at Blackie beside him. "Snowy and Blackie. That actually suits them."

When Cecilia was not looking, something flickered in Arthur's eyes.

"Yes," he said quietly. "It does."

By the time they got back to the apartment block, the sky was fully bright.

Just as they were about to unload everything, a smug clicking sound came from ahead, followed by an infuriating drawl.

"Well, well. Looks like you two have been living rather nicely."

At that moment, Cecilia and Arthur were just about to lift down the bag containing the guns.

The instant he heard the voice, Arthur quickly pressed a hand over Cecilia's.

The back of her hand went warm beneath his palm.

Without changing expression, she nudged the bag further inward, then turned to look towards the speaker.

Standing there was Nathan Barnes, dressed in a riot of gaudy colours, looking thoroughly improper.

Beside him stood a heavily built man.

Cecilia recognised him at once. It was none other than Frank, the team captain who had been following Miranda before.

What she had not expected was the tall woman standing at Frank's side.

In weather this cold, the woman was still wearing far less than anyone else, clearly determined to show off her curving figure. She was even wearing lipstick.

When she saw Cecilia, she smiled lightly. "Cecilia. It's been a while."

It was Mabel Duncan.

The three of them had obviously not come with good intentions.

Cecilia turned to Mabel, mockery plain on her face. "Mabel, only a few days apart and you've already found yourself a new husband?"

By now, Mabel had moved up to Frank and slipped a hand lightly around his arm.

She did not seem bothered at all by the sarcasm. Instead, she leaned her head gently against Frank's arm.

"Cecilia, not every woman is as lucky as you. You were lucky enough to prepare in advance, lucky enough to store supplies before everything fell apart, and now, even in the apocalypse, you're still living in comfort. I heard it's been months already, and your place still has meat and electricity. For ordinary women like us, finding someone to rely on is hard enough. There's no need to keep pressing us so cruelly, is there?"
