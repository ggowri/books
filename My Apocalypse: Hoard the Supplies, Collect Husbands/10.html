Chapter 10 Spree

Anything that caught Cecilia's eye, she bought without hesitation—fifty servings at a time, claiming it was a corporate order.

She visited several more diners and food stalls, drawing curious glances at her age more than once.

Instead of arguing, she simply pulled out her wallet, and the owners, impressed by her decisiveness, hurried to prepare her food.

When Cecilia mentioned she'd be picking up fifty breakfasts from each stall every morning for the next two weeks, their eyes lit up with excitement.

Her car was always parked nearby; after collecting each order, she'd load the food into the vehicle before tucking it away in the Space.

A neighborhood bakery specializing in sweet, fluffy dinner rolls with generous fillings was a particular favorite of hers.

Calculating she could eat three per meal, Cecilia placed a monthly order for two hundred rolls of various flavors.

Blackie loved them, too.

Cecilia planned to visit the night market that evening.

First, though, she needed to wrap up the house sale during the day, then buy dozens of large shelves and daily necessities: laundry detergent, towels, cups, kitchenware, disposable containers, and more.

Most importantly, she needed to stock up on grain.

She'd already ordered a batch online, and with fresh funds now available, she headed straight to a wholesale supplier to buy it by the ton.

Cecilia rented a warehouse and had the goods delivered there, pretending to store them away before secretly moving them into the Space once the delivery crew left.

Watching the Space fill up steadily brought a smile to her face.

Cecilia had a weakness for tropical fruits—jackfruit, durian, sugar apples, and the like.

In the northern city where she lived, these were prohibitively expensive.

That night, she contacted the customer service of a live-streaming fruit seller, inquiring about buying a large shipment.

She requested full truckloads for delivery, and the seller initially thought it was a prank.

Undeterred, Cecilia asked for the seller's phone number and called directly.

After an hour of negotiations and a deposit transfer, the deal was sealed.

A week later, the seller's trucks arrived.

She had them unload near the warehouse and paid the balance.

Once they left, she pulled out a sugar apple, broke it open, and took a bite. It was sweet and creamy, just as she'd hoped.

This spree continued for nearly two weeks.

One morning, after picking up her usual breakfast and heading home with Blackie, Cecilia's phone rang.

The caller was Julia Quinn, a college classmate she'd never gotten along with.

As soon as she answered, Julia launched into a sarcastic tirade. "All you do is brag about your perfect boyfriend and your upcoming engagement at school, but your boyfriend's out cheating on you!"

Cecilia's mind jolted. This had happened in her previous life, too.

Back then, her relationship with Julia was already strained, and she'd been so infatuated with Alexander that she'd snapped at Julia, then complained to Alexander about her classmate's jealousy.

Now, she cringed at her own foolishness.

"Julia, do you have time to meet today?" she asked calmly.

It was summer vacation, and neither of them had classes.

Julia was shocked by her composure.

Cecilia offered, "I'll treat you to your favorite spicy BBQ. Don't worry, I mean no harm. We've never been close, but if I remember correctly, your ex-boyfriend cheated on you, too...

"Think of this as helping me get revenge on a player?" Cecilia added.

Her words struck a chord.

Julia agreed to meet and explained how she'd found out.

Her family owned a hotel, and she'd been covering the front desk that morning when she saw Alexander checking out with another woman.

She hadn't been entirely sure it was him; she only saw his photo before. Still, she decided to tell Cecilia to annoy her.

In her past life, Cecilia had never bothered to ask for details, never realizing the accusation was so solid.

Julia, she realized, could be the perfect ally.

She'd been wondering how to break things off with Alexander, and now Julia had fallen right into her plan.

"Please don't tell anyone about this yet," Cecilia begged.

Julia fell silent, seemingly surprised by her humility, before finally nodding in agreement.

Cecilia spent the rest of the day finalizing the house sale with the agent, who worked at lightning speed, anxious to lock in the deal before she changed her mind.

The bank would disburse the funds in about two weeks, a timing that couldn't have been better, as her current savings were running low.

Her parents had left her a substantial inheritance, but half of it had been spent on tuition, living expenses, and her recent stockpiling spree.

She was certain she'd blow through the remaining half in the next two weeks.

Cecilia hadn't touched her stocks and funds yet; she had another plan for those—to trick the Barnes family one last time.

Getting back all the stocks and funds she'd given the Barnes family would be nearly impossible, but she hoped to recover at least part of them.

Combined with the ones she still held, selling them to the Barnes family would guarantee her a profit.

The proceeds from the house sale would let her stockpile even more supplies. Simple as that, just keep buying.

With time to spare before she met with Julia, Cecilia visited a nearby market and ordered over eighty large shelves, each 16.4 feet tall, 9.8 feet long, and 3.3 feet wide.

The shelves came with varying spacing, perfect for organizing her diverse supplies.

After she paid a deposit, the owner told her to pick them up in two weeks.

As Cecilia was leaving, she noticed large plastic water storage tanks in the owner's factory—each capable of holding 13,208 gallons, designed for industrial use in storing liquid food before canning.

Cecilia immediately ordered a hundred of them, knowing water would be invaluable after the apocalypse.

She planned to fill them all at the first opportunity, ensuring she'd never run short.

After all, using bottled water for tasks like laundry or bathing Blackie would be a waste.

The tanks were also scheduled for pickup in two weeks.

With everything sorted, Cecilia checked the time and drove to the mall where she'd agreed to meet Julia.

Julia loved spicy food, so they'd settled on a spicy BBQ joint in the mall.

When they met, Julia rolled her eyes at Cecilia before slamming down a printout from the hotel's internal system.

It clearly showed Alexander had checked in. And his companion was none other than Rebecca, Cecilia's childhood best friend.
