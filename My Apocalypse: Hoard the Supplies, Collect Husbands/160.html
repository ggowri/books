Chapter 160 A Trade for Mutated Creatures

Producing some edible mutated creature powder.

The young soldier froze for a moment before finally replying, "I'll report this to Colonel Reed. Please wait for a while. Actually, there's already a batch of mutated cockroach corpses today…"

Cecilia nodded lightly.

Creatures like cockroaches could indeed be eaten… She had eaten them in her previous life as well, though their price had always been very low.

They were far less popular than bone powder made from mutated animals, or powders ground from mutated creatures like silkworm pupae.

After chatting with the soldier for a few more moments, he said somewhat awkwardly, "Although Colonel Reed instructed us earlier to avoid interfering with matters on the sixty-sixth floor whenever possible, and honestly, when you shot Hannah just now we all felt pretty satisfied! That couple had been doing evil for a long time, and we always suspected they were abusing that child. But there are simply too many people for us to manage everything, and we have orders—unless absolutely necessary, we can't open fire on civilians. Still… it would be best not to fire guns inside the shelter in the future. Some of the civilians may panic."

Cecilia understood that these soldiers had a difficult job managing so many people, so she nodded gently to show she would keep it in mind.

But just now, when she saw the way Hannah behaved—and the pitiful state of that little boy—she had felt a trace of compassion.

In her previous life, she had seen countless suffering children.

She had also been deceived by children who looked innocent and harmless on the surface, but whose souls had already been worn down by the apocalypse.

All those experiences had led her to one conclusion—

A bad person pretending to be good was easy and hard to detect. But a good person pretending to be bad was far more difficult.

All she needed to do was determine who the bad people were.

That was why she would not blindly try to save someone out of kindness, because she feared making the wrong judgment.

However, she could be certain of one thing.

Hannah was absolutely a bad person.

So she had no problem killing a bad person.

Hannah's body was soon taken away by the military. They were afraid leaving a corpse there would cause panic.

However, the news that Hannah had been shot dead on the sixty-sixth floor quickly spread throughout the shelter.

Many people said that Hannah had been ruthless and vicious, and that she deserved to die.

Others claimed that the people on the sixty-sixth floor were even more ruthless than the wicked themselves, since they dared to accept and deal with criminals.

Because of that, no one dared to come up to the sixty-sixth floor anymore.

Arthur didn't say much. He simply went back to sleep.

Cecilia could tell that he was truly exhausted.

That made her somewhat curious—why was this guy so tired?

She suddenly realized that recently Arthur often looked drained, as if he hadn't had a proper night's sleep for several days.

After a while, Cecilia decided that tonight she would go collect the supplies from the closer warehouse first, just in case something unexpected happened later.

After all, the military wasn't far from that warehouse now. If they discovered it first, she would be devastated.

But when she stepped out of her room, something strange came over her, and she walked to Arthur's door.

She pressed her ear against the door and listened for quite a while, but heard absolutely nothing.

Then she released Blackie and whispered softly in his ear, "Listen carefully. See if there's anyone inside. If there is someone, lie down. If there isn't, spin in place."

Blackie didn't even put his ear against the door. Instead, he tilted his head and listened quietly in the air for a moment.

Then Blackie spun around in place.

Arthur was not in the room.

After confirming that Arthur wasn't inside, Cecilia thought for a moment and finally decided to leave Jet at home.

Although they had already established their authority twice today and it was unlikely anyone downstairs would come looking for trouble, it was still better to be cautious.

"Jet, if someone comes looking for trouble, don't talk. If they dare enter the room, hide in the dark and go straight for their face with your claws!"

Cecilia instructed seriously.

Jet's eyes lit up. "Don't worry, Master of the Litter Box! Your warrior will faithfully complete the mission!"

Cecilia thought for a moment. It seemed that the cartoons she had shown Jet these past two days were all overly dramatic action anime.

Now that there were more people around, Cecilia was far more careful when going downstairs.

Along the way, she still encountered several patrol soldiers.

However, this also made it clear that the shelter's management at night was very strict.

Other than the patrol soldiers, there wasn't a single person in the corridor. When she asked one of the soldiers, she learned that after ten o'clock at night, civilians were not allowed to go outside for safety reasons.

"Ms. Summers, there are mutated creatures outside. Please be careful."

The soldiers already knew the names of the people living on the sixty-sixth floor, so when they saw Cecilia heading out, they didn't stop her and only offered a reminder.

Cecilia smiled and patted Blackie's head beside her.

"Don't worry. I've got Blackie with me."

With that, she went downstairs. Taking out the ice skates she had prepared earlier in her backpack, she put them on, fastened a headlamp onto her forehead, and then disappeared into the night together with Blackie.

The reason she didn't let Blackie pull the sled today was because there were simply too many people living nearby now. It was hard to avoid drawing attention.

However, what Cecilia didn't expect was that after traveling only a few hundred meters and leaving the residential complex, she suddenly found the road ahead blocked.

Cecilia immediately grabbed Blackie and looked around cautiously.

It was a pile of useless tables and chairs, all made of metal, stacked on the ice about half a meter high, completely blocking the five-meter-wide road.

Cecilia frowned, realizing that someone must be nearby. She quickly prepared to retreat.

But just as she turned around, a figure suddenly rushed out from the side.
