Chapter 117 Addicted

It turned into a regular tree.

But if she left it in Plot One, it'd only fruit three times before dying.

Bottom line—the Space was a mixed bag. Sometimes it surprised her. Sometimes it pissed her off.

Exhausting.

Cecilia checked on the breeding area next. The rabbit population had exploded.

That blueberry-stuffed rabbit from the Space? Insanely fertile.

This time, she'd hooked up with a black rabbit—kicked the white buck to the curb ages ago.

The two lovebirds were all cozy together.

Meanwhile, the white buck sat off to the side, munching grass miserably.

Blueberry had just popped out four babies.

One had yellow and black stripes—looked like a road sign.

Another was blue with black spots—straight-up ladybug vibes.

The wildest one? All yellow except for its black eyes, paws, ears, and tail. Looked like a messed-up raccoon.

Cecilia didn't get rabbit romance. Or rabbit genetics, apparently.

She gave the mini horses a few pats and left the Space.

Once outside, Cecilia decided it was time to grill Blackie.

That was four whole floors of meat down there!

His main food supply going forward.

If he'd actually grabbed it, she'd up his portions every day.

Hell, if he couldn't figure this out, maybe she should just teach him to unlock that purple pendant space and drip her own blood in there instead.

But Blackie—who used to pick things up lightning-fast—now acted like a total idiot.

No matter what she said, he just stared at her with those big dumb eyes.

Finally, exhausted, Cecilia tapped his head.

"Just think about breaking the contract with this Space! Just think it! Say it in your head! That's all, Blackie! Got it?"

Blackie flopped onto the ground and closed his eyes.

Wouldn't even look at her anymore!

Furious, Cecilia turned to Jet instead.

"Jet, did Blackie say anything just now?"

Jet hopped around on the ground, beady eyes darting everywhere. Silent.

Cecilia's suspicion grew.

Wait—why isn't Jet talking either?

She pulled out Jet's favorite pine nuts from the Space, shot Blackie one last glare, then lured Jet aside with the treats.

"Tell me what Blackie said. All these pine nuts are yours!"

Jet hesitated for a long moment, then looked at Cecilia with something almost like... amusement.

"Blackie said you're really dumb!"

Cecilia exploded.

Finally, she got it. Blackie wasn't confused. He hadn't suffered brain damage. He wasn't slow from internal injuries.

He was doing this on purpose!

He just didn't want to give her the pendant.

And now she noticed—Blackie was treating the pendant like a toy, having the time of his life. Putting his ball in, taking it out. Over and over.

He'd even gotten creative.

He'd jump in the air and store the ball mid-leap.

Then use his mind to make it pop out while airborne.

So he could headbutt it. Creating his own little interactive game!

Cecilia marched over and gave him an earful.

She crossed her arms, fuming. Fine. She was done with him.

Then Blackie turned around and started trying to make up with her.

He rested his giant head on her leg, wagging his tail like crazy—like he was saying come on, don't be mad.

Then he blinked those puppy eyes and pulled several hundred pounds of meat out of the Space, pushing it toward her with his paw—Here, here! Take it! Don't be upset! Just let me play with it a few more days, okay? This pendant thing is SO fun!

The moment Cecilia saw the meat, relief and frustration hit her all at once.

Relief—there was meat in the Space. Blackie's risky trip paid off, and the pendant wasn't wasted on him. And there was plenty of it too—four whole layers!

Frustration—Blackie was treating the pendant like a damn toy. And he was addicted.

But then it clicked.

Blackie had evolved. And he was still evolving.

His intelligence had shot up. Everything around him fascinated him now. He was thinking in ways he never had before, feeling things he'd never understood. The world looked completely different through his eyes.

It was like hitting puberty—sudden growth spurts, getting smarter, a little rebellious.

That's where Blackie was at.

Realizing this, Cecilia patted his head.

"Fine. Play with it for a bit. Just give it back when you're done."

Blackie wagged his tail hard, eyes sparkling like stars.

You're the best! I promise I'll give it back!

Over the next two days, Blackie kept conjuring up huge chunks of meat, dropping them at Cecilia's feet, demanding she cook them for him.

Like he was saying—This is MY meat. I'll eat as much as I want. Now cook it.

Cecilia didn't know whether to laugh or cry.

Even worse, Jet started whining nonstop.

"I want my own snack stash too."

"I want extra meals too."
