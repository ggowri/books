Chapter 93 Freeze to Death

And she had a special gift in store for the Barnes—something about Rebecca.

In the week since Ice Stage arrived, the group on the 66th floor hadn't ventured downstairs once, barely even leaving their apartments.

Mabel came up to find Cecilia once.

The government never sent anyone to pick them up the next day as promised.

Now everyone was panicking.

Mabel frowned as she spoke to Cecilia: "We've decided to break into every apartment in this building and search for clothes and anything edible."

As she spoke, she was shivering violently from the cold, her lips turning pale.

Cecilia looked at the woman and the two young people trailing behind her, then said coldly, "Do whatever you want. None of it concerns the 66th floor."

Just as Cecilia was about to close the door, Mabel shouted from outside: "Do you know people froze to death last night?"

The sudden temperature drop overnight made it easy for people to freeze in their sleep. Even with warm clothes available, when you're groggy and half-asleep, you can't get up to find clothing or heat sources.

That was the terrifying reality of apocalyptic freezing.

Cecilia raised an eyebrow. "And that's my problem how?"

Mabel stared at the stubborn woman before her, gritted her teeth, and finally turned away with her group.

Cecilia had zero interest in this woman.

The 66th floor had enough supplies, and they didn't want to get dragged into this mess.

As long as no one bothered them, they planned to lie low for a month or two during Ice Stage.

Of course, they weren't just going to sit around.

They'd agreed to spar with each other regularly, run practice drills, and build their physical strength and abilities to handle future crises.

Every morning, Cecilia would wake up, make herself a hot coffee, and start her morning workout routine bundled up in heavy clothes.

She'd added this exercise regimen on her own to maintain good endurance.

Then came breakfast and playtime with her dog and bird.

Ever since Jet's last mutation, the bird had completely transformed!

Jet was now over a foot and a half long—too big to perch on Cecilia's shoulder anymore. Instead, he had to stand on Blackie's back.

The sight of the bird and dog together was quite amusing.

His claws were razor-sharp, and his beak curved and elongated.

His feathers had kept their original gray but now showed hints of blue, giving him an imposing appearance.

On top of his head, a blue-purple plume had appeared that bobbed every time he spoke—quite entertaining to watch.

His feathers had also grown much thicker. If you ran your fingers through them, you'd discover a dense layer of down beneath the outer plumage.

Ever since growing this down layer, Jet was no longer afraid of the cold.

Unfortunately, Jet remained as chattery as ever, constantly babbling in Cecilia's ear.

And he was always tattling on Blackie!

"Black is bullying me! Save me, human!"

"Black says he wants to eat roasted bird! Save me, human!"

"Black wants to throw me in the furnace! Save me, human!"

It gave Cecilia a splitting headache.

She'd originally wanted to introduce Jet to Hailey and Arthur, but she worried about his unstable personality and excessive chatter.

What if someone with ulterior motives took advantage of him?

She'd been wrestling with this problem recently. Jet absolutely could not speak in front of people.

But would Jet actually listen?

Cecilia felt another headache coming on.

She needed to find the perfect way to keep Jet quiet before introducing him to Hailey and Arthur.

Blackie had evolved too.

He now stood as tall as Cecilia, over two meters in body length, with a thick coat of black fur. His ears, head, and nose were noticeably larger than before.

His broad paws had retractable claws like a cat's, so despite his massive size, he could walk completely silently.

Blackie was quite pleased with his evolution. Several times now, he'd deliberately walked into the bathroom while Cecilia was using it, then hung his huge face there staring at her.

Cecilia would jumped out of her skin every time.

But this seemed to have become Blackie's favorite game.

Even Jet would watch Cecilia get startled and flap his wings, squawking with laughter.

It made Cecilia want to throttle them both!

Cecilia had also burned one of the wooden logs produced by the Space.

To her surprise, this firewood was extraordinary!

Not only did it light easily, but one small log could burn for an entire day—and it was smokeless.

This single small piece of wood could warm her entire apartment. It was truly a heating miracle, essential for home and travel during the Ice Stage.

Over the past few days, Cecilia's Space had collected two more boxes of this firewood, plus a small box of bullets that perfectly matched the handgun she'd retrieved from the garbage disposal earlier.

This gave her upgraded firepower.

Additionally, Cecilia had finished opening all the remaining packages in her Space.

She'd found food, clothing, pet supplies, toys, and daily necessities.

Most amusing of all... Cecilia had actually unpacked several boxes of condoms.

Several other boxes were empty, but judging by the packing slips inside, they'd originally contained jewelry and gemstones.

Cecilia had also managed to cut and break open the safes she'd taken from The Boss's office.
