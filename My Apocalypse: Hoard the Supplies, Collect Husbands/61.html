Chapter 61 A Mutated Rat!

Even so, Cecilia had caught a glimpse of rows of shelves inside, stocked full of various pet supplies.

With an entire farm to tend to now, she'd had her eye on this place for a while.

Arthur glanced at her and nodded. "I don't have any pets. You can take most of the things inside if you need them."

Cecilia hadn't expected Arthur to be so generous.

She didn't decline outright, but she'd already made up her mind—if she found other useful items later, she'd repay him with supplies of equal value.

After prying open the door, the first thing that greeted her was a row of dog food, cat food, rabbit food, and guinea pig food from different brands. Beyond that stood an entire wall stacked with timothy hay and alfalfa hay.

"Wow!" Cecilia was even happier than when she'd found supplies for herself.

Arthur, a man of few words, simply carried the items to Cecilia's doorstep.

Once her excitement faded, a calm settled over her.

She looked at the cat and dog food in front of her, then up at Arthur. "Do you want some of these? Especially the cat food."

Arthur shook his head. "I'm not that desperate yet."

His meaning was clear—he still had other food; no need to resort to this.

Amused, Cecilia decided to tease him, her tone serious, "Dog and cat food are like protein bars, you know? Cat food especially has a high meat content—filling and packed with protein.

"In the apocalypse, this is more valuable than grain. Are you sure you don't want to keep a couple of bags?"

Arthur looked up at her, his brows furrowing deeper, as if saying, "Are you joking?"

Cecilia snickered to herself.

To her surprise, Arthur walked over, picked up half of the cat and dog food, and said, "Fine, we'll split them evenly then."

This left Cecilia dumbfounded—hey, that was for her Blackie!

It wasn't until she noticed Arthur watching her with a smirk that she realized she'd been played.

The guy had seen how reluctant she was and done it on purpose!

After moving the supplies, Cecilia checked the time and saw it was noon. She suggested continuing in the afternoon, then headed home to enjoy a nice lunch with Blackie.

For lunch, she heated up a takeout container of loaded nachos—crispy tortilla chips smothered in melted cheese, salsa, and a drizzle of spicy ranch, paired with a cold glass of cherry limeade. Pure bliss.

That afternoon, Cecilia and Arthur cleared out the remaining rooms with edible supplies.

To repay Arthur for giving up the pet food, Cecilia barely took any supplies for herself.

Arthur didn't hesitate to claim them.

As they entered the last room, a putrid stench hit Cecilia's nose. She stepped back instantly.

Arthur, right behind her, sensed something was wrong. "What's the matter?"

"Something's in there."

No sooner had the words left her mouth than a large black figure lunged out of the room. It moved so fast that Cecilia couldn't make out its shape.

She'd never expected anything to be in the abandoned room, so she hadn't grabbed any weapons.

Cecilia relied solely on her agility to dodge sideways.

But the creature was eerie, twisting mid-air to charge straight at her face.

Cecilia raised her arm to block, and as it drew near, she finally saw what it was—a rat, as big as a rabbit. Its fur, over four inches long, was black, stiff, and spiky like quills. Its eyes were bloodshot, and two large front teeth glinted coldly.

It was a mutated rat!

Gritting her teeth, Cecilia summoned her hidden dagger with a thought.

Before she could strike, a dark blur flashed past her.

The rat, mere inches from her face, was kicked flying.

It sailed several feet away and slammed into the wall, letting out a pitiful squeak.

"Are you okay?" Arthur stood in front of her, his broad back shielding her completely.

Cecilia froze for a moment before responding. "I'm fine."

"Don't stand there dumbfounded next time—call me!" Anxiety laced Arthur's voice.

The mutated rat was tough; it quickly jumped to its feet and charged again.

Arthur had no time to say more, rushing forward with a steel knife in hand.

While Arthur was distracted, Cecilia slipped the dagger back into its sheath.

She took a deep breath, itching to explain she hadn't been dumbfounded—she'd been about to strike with the dagger if Arthur had been a second late.

Still, the image of him standing guard over her stirred a flicker of warmth in her chest.

After ten years in the apocalypse, she'd never dared to trust anyone with her back.

Today, though, a man had put his own back on the line for her.

Once the mutated rat was dead, Cecilia and Arthur stood silent beside its body.

Cecilia's mind raced—how had this thing gotten to the 66th floor?

She inspected the room and noticed the window wasn't fully closed.

It must have climbed up the pipes and slipped in through the window.

But the 66th floor? It was unsettling.

After a moment of silence, Arthur spoke up, "Is your dog mutated, too?"
