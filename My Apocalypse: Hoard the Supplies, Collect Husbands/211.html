Chapter 211 Shameless Accusation

The table was absolutely loaded with meat.

Colonel Reed had not come upstairs empty-handed either. He had brought a chunk of meat with him and asked Blaise to turn it into honey-glazed pork ribs.

But the moment he saw the cucumbers Cecilia had brought, he lit up so much that, in his own words, it was as though he'd just struck gold. He crunched through one after another without stopping.

For a while, apart from the odd bit of conversation, the room was filled with nothing but the sharp crunch of people chewing cucumber. Anyone who did not know better might have thought it sounded like a room full of rabbits crunching away.

Cecilia, on the other hand, had no particular craving for vegetables. She had more than enough of those in the Space. So she focused happily on the full spread of meat dishes in front of her.

Blaise was genuinely talented in the kitchen. At only seventeen, he had a very bright future ahead of him.

Still, by the time the meal was over, an almost comical scene had emerged. Aside from the portions Cecilia herself had eaten, hardly anyone had touched the meat dishes after those first few bites before the cucumbers appeared. The only thing truly polished off on the entire table was the large bowl of cucumbers.

When Blaise saw it, he looked close to tears. "What is this? I slaved away all evening, and in the end I still lost to a few cucumbers Cici brought over!"

Cecilia said nothing. She only laughed to herself and gave his shoulder a sympathetic pat.

Well...losing to the Space didn't really count, did it?

After the meal, everyone was finally full and satisfied, and one by one they drifted off to catch up on sleep.

Blaise had nothing in particular to do, so he said he wanted to wander round the shelter for a bit, just to stretch his legs.

His fever had long since gone, his wounds had been properly bandaged, and the healing medicine had done its work. He was full of restless energy. Perhaps it was because he was still so young, or perhaps because he had spent too many days locked up by that criminal organisation, but now he seemed utterly incapable of sitting still for even a moment.

Arthur knew Blaise could handle himself, so he was not overly worried. He merely told him not to move about too much in case he tore his wounds open again, then went off to sleep himself.

When Cecilia returned home, Jet came trotting over at once.

He wanted to ask whether Silver "looked pretty today".

"Yes, yes, very pretty," Cecilia said, nearly in tears.

What kind of hopeless romantic behaviour was this?

Ignoring the two little creatures in the house, she washed up, drew the curtains in her bedroom, and lay down, planning to get some proper rest.

To her surprise, she only managed to sleep for two hours.

Then the commotion outside her door woke her.

To be fair, that really was unusual.

Since the shelter had first been established and people had come all the way up to the sixty-sixth floor looking for trouble, it had been a very long time since anyone had dared come up to make a nuisance of themselves.

Blackie appeared at the bedroom door with his great black face hanging low. He looked as though he had just been woken as well, and he was clearly irritable.

Jet was yelling in the sitting room, "Shameless! Shameless people!"

Cecilia threw on some clothes, picked up her knife and gun, wrapped herself in her fur coat, and stepped outside.

The moment she opened the door, she heard Blaise shouting furiously, so worked up he was nearly stamping his feet.

"Stop talking rubbish! I'm only seventeen! Why would I ever fancy an old woman like you? Have some shame!"

No wonder Jet had been shrieking about shameless people indoors. That was exactly what Blaise had been calling them outside.

Arthur stood in the corridor with a cold expression, brows drawn together.

Only after asking him did Cecilia learn what had happened. Blaise had gone out for a walk, and somehow a girl in her early twenties had suddenly accused him of molesting her.

Worse still, she had brought along a whole crowd of so-called aunts and uncles, and now they were all standing outside the door on the sixty-sixth floor, demanding justice.

"My daughter was pure and innocent. How did she end up shut away in a room with your boy? It was only ten minutes, and now her honour is ruined!"

The woman speaking looked to be in her forties. She was the girl's mother.

At that moment she had one arm wrapped round her daughter, crying at the top of her voice outside the door.

She howled and wailed so loudly it made the walls ring.

Blaise was furious, hopping mad, but he could not explain himself clearly, and his whole face had turned red.

He was already prepared to pull out his gun, but with so many people gathered nearby to watch the spectacle, he knew that if this was not settled properly today, he would choke on the injustice of it.

Arthur stood to one side, watching coldly. When he saw Cecilia arrive, he seemed to relax at once.

"This is more your thing."

The meaning could not have been clearer.

Cecilia, you're up.

She was speechless.

So that was it. The moment they had become a little more familiar, he was already putting her to work.

Arthur took one look at her face and clearly knew exactly what she was thinking. The corner of his mouth lifted slightly.

"You liked Blaise's chestnut chicken today. Later, I'll have him make you two more plates as thanks."

Cecilia had to admit, the chestnut chicken had been excellent. Soft, fragrant, and rich.

Without another word, she strode forward. With one flick of her hand, a silver knife flashed out and came to rest against the crying girl's throat.

"Tell the truth," Cecilia said coolly. "Otherwise there will be blood today. My brother is only seventeen. He is still a child. If you're going to blackmail someone, at least pick a better target."

What Cecilia had not expected was that the moment she finished speaking, the girl's eyes shifted slightly and slid towards Arthur at the side.

As if to say, he would make quite a suitable target too.

More importantly, the girl was not afraid of the knife in Cecilia's hand at all.

"Oh, so now you're being unreasonable! Your lot slept with my daughter, and now you want to kill someone! Go on then, kill me if you dare! Here, slash my throat!"

The middle-aged woman who had been standing nearby suddenly lunged forward, clearly trying to knock Cecilia aside.

Unfortunately for her, Cecilia's strength had improved enormously during this period, and her reflexes had already returned to the level of her previous life. There was no chance she would be rammed by some half-starved older woman.

She lifted one foot and, at the very instant the woman charged at her, kicked her away.

There was a heavy bang as the woman crashed into the stair rail behind her, splitting her head open on impact.
