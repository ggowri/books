Chapter 16 Goodbye Forever

Sarah screamed, covering her face and fleeing the room.

Watching the farce unfold, Cecilia thought Alexander was pathetic—brainless and spineless, a total embarrassment.

Miranda's hands shook with rage as she pointed at Cecilia. "Cecilia, what do you want?"

Cecilia shrugged. "I already told you. Were you not listening?"

She repeated her demands, her tone flat.

Miranda's face burned with anger, but Cecilia could see the wheels turning in her head—she was calculating her next move.

"Madam Barnes, this isn't going away quietly. You saw it yourself—your grandson has no feelings for me. Forcing this marriage would only make us all miserable.

"And by the way, the footage is already uploaded to my cloud storage. Even if you take this camera and destroy it, it won't matter. You'd only make an enemy of me," Cecilia said.

She'd had enough; it was time to lay all her cards on the table.

Alexander scrambled to his feet, fumbling for his clothes, his embarrassment plain to see.

Staring at Cecilia, Miranda said, her voice tight, "We can't give you all the stocks and funds. The Barnes family has invested a lot of time and money into managing them over the years."

"I know. Give me 20 million dollars, and we'll call it even."

The stocks and funds were worth at least 30 million dollars.

By asking for twenty, she was letting the Barnes family save face—though she knew they'd never hand over the full amount anyway.

Cash in hand would be far more useful for stockpiling supplies before the apocalypse.

Sure enough, relief washed over Miranda and Alexander's faces.

"Transfer it to me now—immediately. Don't make me regret this. I'd hope the Barnes family isn't so poor you can't come up with 20 million dollars." Cecilia's voice was cold, every word sharp as a knife.

Miranda's eyes turned icy. "What if I pay you, and you still leak the video?"

"We can sign a legal agreement. I'll even record a video stating that I'll take full responsibility—including jail time—if I release the footage. That should satisfy you."

With that, Cecilia's engagement to the Barnes family was officially over.

Miranda made a few quick calls, and within minutes, 20 million dollars appeared in Cecilia's bank account.

She signed the agreement, then destroyed the camera in front of Alexander and Sarah and deleted the cloud backup.

"Goodbye forever. Don't ever come near me again." She glared at the group, then turned and walked out.

Before turning around, Cecilia had noticed the joyful expressions on their faces.

The Barneses looked relieved, as if they'd gotten away with a steal.

Little did they know, in a few short months, their precious money would be worthless paper.

No one mentioned the ring—the one that had originally belonged to the Summers family, something they'd dismissed as worthless.

Cecilia didn't care; she was just glad to be free of the Barneses for good.

Outside, she spotted Julia peeking around the corner. She walked over and gave her a nod. "It's done."

Julia rolled her eyes. "You owe me one."

Cecilia nodded. When the apocalypse drew near, she'd warn Julia—repaying the debt in the only way that would matter then.

With the money secured and the Barnes mess behind her, Cecilia felt a surge of excitement. She wasted no time calling a pharmaceutical rep she knew.

Medicine was expensive, but with 20 million dollars at her disposal, she could finally stock up without holding back.

Cecilia put in an order for over two million dollars' worth of supplies: cold medicine, fever reducers, vitamins, burn cream, heatstroke medicine, intestinal meds, antibiotics, blood pressure medication, blood sugar medication, wound dressings, painkillers, calcium supplements—everything she could think of for common illnesses.

She also stocked up on meds for the elderly and infants, which would be invaluable after the apocalypse, along with pet meds: flea and tick treatments, probiotics, and more.

Her excuse? She was planning to open a chain of pharmacies.

Some medications required special permits, but the rep—eager to land such a large order—offered to handle the paperwork for her, promising to deliver everything to her specified location in a few days.

Cecilia paid a deposit and hung up, satisfied.

With that done, she hurried home—Blackie would be waiting for her.

To her surprise, the air conditioning was blaring when she walked in, sending a chill down her spine after the humid heat outside.

She'd definitely turned it off before leaving.

And where is Blackie? He usually greets me at the door.

As Cecilia stepped further into the house, she found him lying directly under the AC vent, looking perfectly content.

When he saw her, he shot her a resentful look that seemed to say, "Where the hell have you been?"

Wait… did Blackie turn on the AC himself?

At that moment, Cecilia could've worshiped him. "You really are something else, buddy."
