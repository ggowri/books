Chapter 6 Decorated Hero

After signing the contract, Cecilia grew to like the agent during their conversations, and an idea struck her. She could ask him to sell her current house.

"Price it 200 thousand dollars below market value. Only one requirement, sell it fast," she told the agent seriously.

The agent's eyes lit up right away. "Girl, you gotta know—houses in your neighborhood are total hot sellers! Usually, as soon as one goes up online, tons of buyers ask about it. If you're gonna cut the price this much too..."

He rubbed his hands together, eagerness shining in his eyes. "Wait a minute, I need to go discuss this with my boss."

While the agent made the call, Cecilia hurried downstairs.

Blackie had been in the car for two hours, and she worried about him.

To her horror, a crowd had gathered around her car, taking photos through the window.

Cold sweat broke out on her forehead as she rushed over.

In the middle of the crowd, a young man was livestreaming with his phone pointed at Blackie inside the car.

He kept tapping the window, trying to get Blackie's attention.

From Cecilia's angle, she could see Blackie baring his teeth at the window, his muscles tensed. He was furious, but holding back.

Anger flared in Cecilia.

"What are you doing?"

She rushed over and snatched the phone from his hand.

The young man, with dyed hair, scowled at her sudden action. "Do you know the rules? You're supposed to have a muzzle on a dog like this! Scared now? Don't want people to see you breaking the law?"

He snatched the phone back, raised his voice, and kept telling the crowd that Cecilia wasn't following the regulations. He even threatened to call the police and have Blackie taken away.

Several people stepped closer, peering into the car window. They saw Blackie standing on the back seat, almost filling the entire rear compartment.

"Wow, that's a huge dog! How can you keep such a dog in the city? What if it bites someone?" The man wore a triumphant grin, spouting obscenities.

"My dog is none of your business! And you're livestreaming this for clout? Twisting the truth?"

As Cecilia stepped forward to argue, he suddenly reached out to push her.

Thanks to her apocalypse-honed reflexes, she dodged in time, but his hand still brushed her collar. Rage boiled inside her.

Cecilia had wanted to avoid trouble since Blackie was in the car, but this man had gone too far.

She glared at him, ready to argue, when she heard a "click."

Her car had an automatic unlock function. When she was within three feet of it with the key, the doors unlocked.

Cecilia froze, still processing what had happened, when a black figure leaped out of the back seat and tackled the man to the ground.

The man screamed, but it was useless.

After knocking him down, Blackie hopped back and forth on top of him, preventing him from getting up.

The onlookers were stunned, stepping back more than ten feet in unison.

Cecilia stood up, watching Blackie hop around, baring his teeth at the man.

An indescribable feeling welled up in her chest. Was Blackie protecting her?

"What's going on here?" A stern voice suddenly came from behind the crowd.

A police officer walked over.

The man lay on the ground, pale-faced and trembling. A dark puddle formed beneath him, and a faint smell of urine filled the air. He'd wet himself from fear.

"Officer! Officer, help me! This dog is going to eat me! And she's breaking the rules—no muzzle! Get her!" he cried.

Cecilia hadn't expected Blackie to act like this, let alone unlock the car door. She felt a little awkward.

Stepping forward, she grabbed Blackie's leash. "Blackie, come here."

Blackie obeyed instantly, walking to Cecilia's side and sitting close to her. His large eyes, however, remained fixed on the man, baring his sharp teeth in a silent threat.

"Is this a police dog? Retired?" the officer asked, looking at Cecilia.

Cecilia nodded quickly and fetched the relevant documents from the car.

She'd known Blackie would attract attention, so she carried his identification everywhere she went.

The officer said, smiling as he took the documents, "I knew it when I saw it tackle him and hop around like that. That's a military K9 technique to subdue criminals—hopping keeps them from getting up until backup arrives.

"We have a police dog at the station too, super smart."

After checking Blackie's papers, the officer handed them back.

His sharp gaze then turned to the man. "So, what did he do to make a retired military K9 this alert?"

At that moment, the onlookers, like fence-sitters, began speaking up for Cecilia.

They said the man had falsely accused her of breaking the dog restraint rules and pushed her. Blackie had jumped out to protect his owner after seeing her attacked.

The man hadn't expected this turn of events, but he refused to back down. He insisted that even if it was a retired military dog, it was too big to be in a downtown area.

"A lot of these military dogs have PTSD! What if it bites someone? Can you just get away with saying it's retired? I was doing a good deed, trying to make you see reason!" he stubbornly argued.

The officer's frown deepened.

Blackie stared intently at the man, as if waiting for Cecilia's order to pounce.

"Is... is this military dog named Blackie?" A timid voice suddenly came from the crowd.

Cecilia looked over and saw a young woman around twenty years old.

She nodded. "Do you know Blackie?"

At her words, the young woman's eyes lit up.

She stepped out of the crowd and approached Blackie slowly.

Blackie, sensing her kindness, stayed still, watching her quietly.

Gradually, the young woman reached out and touched Blackie's head.

Though he seemed reluctant, he didn't pull away, letting her pat him once before standing up and hiding behind Cecilia—like a shy child who didn't want to interact with strangers.

Even that single pat made the young woman overjoyed.

She stood up and told the crowd excitedly, "I can't believe I got to touch a decorated hero! This is Blackie, the military dog that fought criminals in Sovener City and saved an entire team a while back! Haven't you seen the news?"

The police officer suddenly realized. "No wonder the name Blackie sounded familiar! This is the one from the news a while ago. So he's retired now!"
