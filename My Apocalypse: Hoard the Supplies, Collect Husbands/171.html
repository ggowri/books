Chapter 171 The Swarm Above

But she did not expect that suddenly the entire floor began to tremble.

It felt as if a massive number of things were moving.

Blackie quickly retreated to Cecilia' side, placing himself in front of her in a protective stance.

Cecilia nervously looked around, yet she could not see where the movement was coming from.

She searched quickly in every direction.

Until… her headlamp shone upward and revealed countless wriggling white worms.

In an instant, her scalp went numb.

Only then did she realize that the ceiling of the room had never been white.

Those mutated maggots had connected together, forming that entire pale surface.

No wonder when Blackie first entered earlier, he had kept looking around as if he sensed something but could not find the direction.

Because those things had been hibernating.

And just now her hand had been punctured and started bleeding.

The scent of blood had awakened them.

"Let's go, Blackie. We need to get out of here!" Cecilia said in alarm.

It was simply too disgusting. Just standing underneath them made her whole body uncomfortable.

Unfortunately, she had already run to the far end of the room. To reach the exit again she would have to cross dozens of meters.

But some mutated maggots had already begun dropping from the ceiling without warning.

With a heavy thud, something landed beside her.

The creature rolled slightly, then quickly identified its direction and began wriggling toward Cecilia.

The thick layers of fat inside their bodies meant they were not afraid of falling from such heights.

Thud. Thud. Thud.

More and more maggots fell from the ceiling one after another. One nearly dropped onto Cecilia' head.

Yet above her head there were still hundreds, even thousands of mutated maggots.

The door was only several dozen meters away, but to Cecilia it felt impossibly far.

In her previous life she had seen mountains of corpses and seas of blood, yet she had never felt something this disgusting.

These things were still wriggling.

And they were piled everywhere underfoot. Cecilia nearly stepped on them several times.

Suddenly, she had an idea.

"Blackie!"

Blackie looked at her.

Cecilia grabbed a handful of fur at the back of his neck and climbed onto his back. "Carry me out."

Blackie immediately understood. His body expanded, becoming large enough to carry Cecilia.

Blackie was far more agile than Cecilia. Even though he had four legs, he avoided touching the maggots completely.

Within moments he had leapt all the way to the doorway.

But just as they were about to exit, Cecilia' peripheral vision caught sight of a glowing object.

It was in a corner of the room, originally covered with cloth, so she had not noticed it before.

Now the wriggling maggots had shifted the cloth aside.

Cecilia immediately pointed in that direction. "Blackie, go over there. I need to grab something. We'll come right back."

The maggots were disgusting, but they were not lethal. Supplies were still more important.

However, after those creatures dropped from the ceiling and began crawling on the ground, the entire room had filled with a nauseating rotting stench.

Cecilia quickly took a mask out of the Space and put it on.

Within a few strides, Blackie ran to the corner, and Cecilia finally saw the glowing object.

It was actually a dinosaur egg.

This kind of item counted as a fossil and was also one of the illegally smuggled goods. On the black market, a single dinosaur egg could sell for anywhere from five hundred thousand to several million.

But why was this one glowing?

Without thinking further, Cecilia stored the dinosaur egg into the Space.

Blackie did not stop moving. Stepping carefully between the wriggling maggots, he dashed out of the room on that floor.

As soon as they exited, Cecilia hurried to close the door.

But one maggot tried to crawl out with them. Cecilia kicked it back inside.

"Disgusting thing!"

She shut the door and locked it.

The creatures inside did not possess much strength, nor much intelligence.

Cecilia listened as the rustling noises continued chaotically for a while inside before gradually falling silent.

Most likely the last trace of blood scent from her hand had dispersed, leaving the creatures unable to locate anything, and they eventually lost their ability to move.

Cecilia finally let out a breath and checked the time. She discovered she had spent over an hour inside.

It was now already past three in the morning.

She needed to move faster.

She patted Blackie on the head. "Blackie, you were amazing."

Somehow she had accidentally unlocked Blackie's mount ability.

Thinking about it, maybe Blackie did not need to pull carts anymore in the future. Riding him like a horse would work just fine.

However, after being praised, Blackie did not look happy at all. Instead, he simply stared at Cecilia with wide eyes.

Inside those eyes was pure longing.

Cecilia burst out laughing. She took out a bag of dog treats from the Space and stuffed it directly into Blackie's mouth.

"Alright. Since Blackie did so well, of course you deserve a reward."

After the close call, Cecilia felt a little hungry. However, remembering what she had just experienced, she had no appetite.

She glanced at Blackie.

Such a carefree dog. He could still eat after all that.

Now that all the supplies from the fifteenth floor up to the rooftop had been collected, Cecilia quickly headed downstairs with Blackie.

They soon arrived at the fourteenth floor.

Strictly speaking, this level had already been buried by snow.
