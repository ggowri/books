Chapter 156 The Secret of Plot One

After Ari got the earrings, it really hooked one of them with its claw and swallowed it directly!

After finishing it, it let out a loud burp, then looked at Cecilia with a greedy expression.

As for the other earring, Cecilia exchanged it for the small LCD screen from earlier. It was the screen that displayed the levels and ability descriptions of the different sections inside the Space. She also made an agreement with Ari that it would never have the right to hide this screen privately again. Cecilia could check it at any time.

With a bracelet, Cecilia exchanged for one question.

"The Space… what is it?"

Unexpectedly, the paw Ari had just stretched out suddenly withdrew.

"No. I can't answer that question. You don't have the permission to unlock it."

Seeing the rare serious expression on Ari's face, Cecilia' lips twitched slightly. In the end, she could only change the question.

"The real use of Plot One."

This was something Cecilia had wanted to ask for a long time.

For now, the animals in the Care Ranch could expand as long as food was sufficient, and as the level increased they could also produce more by-products. There was nothing to worry about there.

The Recycler produced materials randomly and could not be controlled.

As for Plot Two, it was obvious that it provided a kind of "enhancement," allowing the vegetables and fruits grown there to become stronger and more nutritious.

Only Plot One remained unclear. Cecilia had planted many things there, but she still had not found any pattern.

Ari suddenly began licking its fur on the spot and did not answer Cecilia' question.

While grooming itself, it tilted its head and looked at Cecilia. Its beautiful almond eyes carried a hint of ill intention.

"Master, you can choose to ask this question together with your final question. But that necklace…" Ari looked at Cecilia and dragged out the last words slyly.

Cecilia looked at Ari and thought that this fellow was trying to trick her again.

She frowned. "What trick are you trying to play?"

"Master, you've misunderstood Ari. I really mean well."

Ari jumped onto Cecilia' shoulder and greedily stared at the necklace and bracelet in her hands.

These two items looked far more delicious than the earrings it had just eaten.

"You mean the secret you mentioned about the Space, the one that would be very useful to me, is related to Plot One?"

Cecilia asked.

Ari jumped onto Cecilia' arm and reached for the necklace in her hand with its paw.

But Blackie suddenly stood up and slapped Ari away with one paw.

A smile curved at the corner of Cecilia' mouth.

"Alright then. I won't ask."

Cecilia suddenly closed the box containing the necklace and bracelet in front of her.

Ari, which had just been slapped away, immediately became anxious.

"You!"

"If I'm not mistaken, the secret you mentioned should be this… If I make a wish to Plot One, or if I desperately need certain supplies and ask you for them, as long as I can provide enough energy or pay a sufficient price, Plot One can grow whatever I want, right?"

"In fact, it's not only Plot One. The Recycler probably has part of this function too, but it isn't as powerful as Plot One in this regard."

Cecilia looked at the stunned Ari and said with a smile.

Actually, it was Ari that had reminded her.

Of course, part of it was also Cecilia' own guess.

After all, when the Ice Stage was about to arrive, the Space had produced warm socks and clothes.

After Cecilia obtained a handgun and complained that there were too few bullets, the seeds she planted later all grew into bullets.

There was also the time when pet food suddenly grew out of nowhere. It was obvious that Ari had been playing tricks on her.

Now that Ari mentioned it again, Cecilia immediately connected everything together.

Ari stared at her in shock. "You… how did you know?"

Cecilia walked up to Ari and gently tapped its forehead with her finger.

"Because I'm a smart human. And you're just a cat from another planet."

After saying that, Cecilia opened the box in her hand again. But this time, she grabbed the necklace and bracelet inside and threw them directly into the air.

Ari tried to snatch them, but it was already too late.

The necklace and bracelet instantly flew into the air and transformed into countless colorful points of light that slowly drifted downward.

Ari immediately leaped up to chase those light points, trying to swallow them into its mouth.

Jet and Blackie followed suit and tried to eat them as well.

But the countless light points had already split into thousands upon thousands. There was no way the few of them could eat them all.

Most of them were absorbed by the Space.

At that moment, the entire Space began to tremble violently again. It seemed that it was about to upgrade once more.

Cecilia looked at Ari rolling on the ground in frustration and walked over to it.

"You're not the true body of the Space, are you? Ari, what exactly are you?"

Because just now, Cecilia had asked Ari to display the LCD screen of the Space again. She checked the level shown on it and found that the Space had not upgraded.

If Ari and the Space were truly one entity, then the appearance of Ari must have been caused by the upgrade of the Space.

But the Space's level had not changed. That made the situation worth thinking about.

Moreover, Cecilia had just watched Ari swallow both earrings.

Yet the Space still showed no reaction at all.

That meant Ari's upgrades and the upgrades of the Space were completely separate.

So what exactly was Ari?

At that moment, Ari stood up again, its fur bristling as it took an attacking stance.

"Woman, so you finally discovered it!"
