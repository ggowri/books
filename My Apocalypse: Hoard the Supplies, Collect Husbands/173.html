Chapter 173 The Thing Beneath the Ice

Even Ari could not say what kind of by-product the dinosaur egg would produce in the Care Ranch.

Cecilia ran over to the ranch, wanting to see what the display screen would show.

What she did not expect was that under the dinosaur egg's by-product section, the screen read:

Calculating. Please wait.

Cecilia asked Ari about it.

Ari said angrily, "Neither this thing nor the Space has ever seen something like it before. It needs a few days to observe it."

With that, the chaos inside the Space finally settled down.

Cecilia reminded Ari to keep watch. She also rewarded it for calling her in time and preventing the dinosaur egg and the mutated cactus from tearing the Space apart. She fed it a cat treat before leaving with Blackie.

After all that commotion, more than half an hour had passed.

Cecilia could only speed up even more.

For the remaining floors, she practically collected things with her eyes closed.

No matter what it was, she simply stored it in the Space first.

One floor was entirely filled with imported beef. However, because so much time had passed, it had already rotted before the Ice Stage arrived. Cecilia smelled the stench even before entering. After taking a quick look, she once again saw those rolling maggots.

Fortunately, these maggots were hibernating, and the wound on her hand had already healed with Magic Spring water, so they were not awakened.

Cecilia and Blackie quietly withdrew from that floor.

On the next floor down, she discovered a room full of imported soybeans. One of the beans had even sprouted a faint bluish shoot, clearly showing signs of mutation.

Cecilia happily stored the thousands of pounds of soybeans into the Space. As for the mutated soybean, she handed it directly to Ari and asked it to plant it.

There were also various precision components such as computer parts, display panels, and instruments she could not recognize. Cecilia stored all of them into the Space as well.

When she reached the eighth floor, she discovered that everything below was already frozen solid.

The entire stairwell beneath was blocked by thick ice.

She immediately understood that everything below that level was likely buried under acid rain and frozen solid. No matter how valuable the supplies were, they were currently unreachable.

Just as Cecilia was about to look further down to confirm whether the lower floors were completely sealed in ice, she suddenly felt Blackie tugging at her sleeve.

Turning around, she saw that Blackie's eyes were wide with tension.

At that moment, Cecilia heard a splashing sound coming from below.

It sounded as if something… an animal perhaps… was moving beneath the ice.

Cecilia immediately stepped backward up the stairs.

When she disappeared around the corner of the stairwell, she saw that beneath the thick ice layer, a massive creature was swimming closer.

She could not see it clearly.

All she could make out was a huge fish tail.

It was grayish blue with faint silver specks shimmering across it.

At that moment, Cecilia felt the Space inside her tremble.

It was the familiar vibration that appeared when the Space sensed something delicious.

She could almost imagine Ari jumping around inside, shouting—

Eat it! Eat it! Eat it!

Cecilia calmly moved a little farther away from Blackie before tapping her chest twice.

"Eat, eat, eat. All you ever think about is eating. Even Blackie is scared of that thing, and you still want to eat it? You'll choke to death!"

It was most likely a mutated fish.

Beneath the thick ice, the acid rain had not frozen completely. Just like beneath Antarctic glaciers, there were still large amounts of flowing water.

Cecilia knew that the degree of mutation in animals was related to how long they had been soaked in acid rain.

Of course, there were still other factors she had not fully understood yet.

But if a fish had remained submerged in acid rain twenty four hours a day…

Cecilia could hardly imagine how far that fish had evolved.

Now that the supplies here had all been collected, Cecilia decided not to stay any longer. Who knew whether the mutated fish below might suddenly leap out of the water one day, perhaps even evolving the ability to move on land.

Cecilia returned with Blackie to the fifteenth floor and climbed out through the window back onto the ground outside.

After thinking for a moment, she took out a large backpack from the Space.

Inside the bag she placed some of the supplies she had collected.

These were meant to show the soldiers and Arthur when she returned.

After all, if she came back empty handed after being out all night, it would seem rather suspicious.

At that moment, the morning star had already appeared in the sky, although the sun had not yet risen.

Cecilia put on her ice skates and prepared to head back to the apartment with Blackie.

When she passed by the place where Luke Wei had blocked her path earlier, the area had already become a chaotic mess.

Corpses and broken human limbs were scattered across the ground. They were so mixed together that it was almost impossible to tell who belonged to whom.

Still, Cecilia carefully searched through the bodies.

Before she left earlier, she had counted them. Including Luke Wei, there had been six people.

She examined the remains carefully.

There were enough arms for about five and a half people, but there were only five complete bodies.

That meant one person had lost an arm when the General attacked them but had managed to escape.

With that thought, Cecilia carefully examined the heads of the corpses.

Sure enough, one of them was missing.

The man who had been doing business with Luke Wei was not among the dead.

Which meant Jake had escaped.

As Cecilia frowned and thought about this, Blackie moved closer to her, making a low growling sound.

At the same time, chaotic footsteps echoed from all directions.

"Boss Carter, I told you she would come back! Her dog is really fierce. You need to be careful!"

Cecilia turned around and saw a fat man with a round face and large ears staring at her.
