Chapter 94 Thermal Wear

The first safe was refreshingly straightforward—cash, gold bars, and checks. Nothing fancy, just practical wealth storage. The gold bars felt satisfyingly heavy in Cecilia's hands, each one a solid brick of security.

The second safe had two compartments. The upper level held four property deeds, each with keys tucked inside. The lower level was empty. What caught her attention were the small papers clipped beneath each key, labeled with cryptic color codes: "Red, Orange, Yellow, Green."

Given this guy's obsession with naming and categorizing everything, Cecilia couldn't immediately decipher what "Red, Orange, Yellow, Green" meant. The addresses on the deeds were all suburban—villas and mid-rise buildings scattered across the outskirts. Too far to investigate right now, but her instincts told her these properties were special. Worth checking out when she had the chance.

The empty lower compartment showed clear signs that something had been stored there previously. Cecilia strongly suspected the Space had "consumed" whatever had been inside.

After its recent upgrade, the Space had mentioned its evolution method had changed. Previously, it could only feed on natural gemstones and crystals with spiritual energy. Now, apparently, any gemstone, raw crystal, or precious stone could fuel its growth.

The thought made Cecilia genuinely happy. This was actually much more convenient.

The third safe contained mystical artifacts she couldn't make heads or tails of, plus diagrams covered in incomprehensible symbols about creating magnetic fields and gathering wealth—typical wealthy eccentric superstitions. What surprised her more were the religious amulets and crystals mixed in with everything else. This guy really had been into all sorts of New Age stuff.

Since she didn't understand any of it, Cecilia packed it all away for later.

The most intriguing discovery, however, was the pale, smooth object she'd retrieved from the water tank in the rest room. She poked it experimentally—it had a bouncy, almost jelly-like texture that was oddly fascinating.

After studying it for ages without success, a sudden realization struck her: Could this be some kind of rare medicinal mushrooms?

She'd heard stories about exotic mushrooms and fungi that wealthy health nuts paid fortunes for—things that supposedly had incredible anti-aging and healing properties. The Boss had clearly been obsessed with health and longevity, so it wouldn't surprise her if he'd gotten his hands on something rare and expensive.

Cecilia had to admire his dedication, but she wasn't brave enough to actually eat the thing.

Instead, she split it in half and planted both pieces in Farm No. 1, curious to see what would grow.

After a while, she reached into Farm No. 1 and pulled out a thermal shirt. Then came thermal pants.

She nearly cried with frustration. She already had thermal underwear! She would've kept the weird mushroom—at least that might have had beauty and health benefits.

But once she actually tried on the Space-produced thermal wear, she understood its true value.

She'd been wearing heavy fur coats for two days, but they were cumbersome and impractical. When she went looking through the Space for better cold-weather gear, she found this set.

The moment she put it on, the fabric molded perfectly to her skin with incredible elasticity and freedom of movement. More importantly, it was genuinely warm. Despite being no thicker than regular thermal underwear, it provided more comfort and warmth than her bulky fur coat.

Even more remarkably, Cecilia noticed she felt less thirsty during physical activity while wearing it. It reminded her of the stillsuits from Dune—designed to maintain body temperature in extreme environments while reducing water loss through a self-regulating thermal and moisture system.

Pretty incredible, actually.

Unlike the market knockoffs that claimed to be "temperature-regulating," this was the real deal.

The snow had been falling heavier each day. Since they were all living on the top floor, Cecilia was starting to worry about a serious problem.

The building's rooftop had a raised edge about three feet high, topped with additional railings extending another foot and a half. These safety measures had been installed after several rooftop suicides and accidental falls in Heldon City years ago.

With the increasingly heavy snowfall, Cecilia had checked the roof once and found massive snow accumulation. Unlike the previous acid rain that could drain through the gutters and drainage systems (the earlier flooding had only occurred because the acid rain fell too fast for the drains to handle), this snow was just piling up.

If it reached a certain weight threshold, it could create devastating pressure on the roof structure—potentially enough to cause a complete collapse.
