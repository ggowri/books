Chapter 54 Foul Language

Cecilia shot a sharp glare over, and Rebecca took two steps back in fright.

That startling, irritating voice came from none other than Rebecca, that woman who wanted to have her cake and eat it too.

"Cici, you... you're actually hooking up with my uncle?" Alexander asked in disbelief.

Looking at Alexander like this, Cecilia wished she could go back to her previous life and slap herself twice.

Just how did you ever fall for such a foolish man?

"You're Sir Barnes' child from abroad?"

Cecilia suddenly remembered Hugh's once-sensational scandal.

Hugh had a mistress. His wife exposed the mistress, and later the mistress took the illegitimate child and went abroad.

"You're the one..." Arthur didn't answer, but instead asked Cecilia.

Both Cecilia and Arthur fell silent for a moment.

They each knew some of the other's past, but only from rumors heard from others, and none of it was particularly positive.

After all, in the rumors, Cecilia was an orphan, a pitiful figure.

And what Cecilia knew about Arthur was that he was an illegitimate child with a not-so-great reputation.

But both trusted their own judgment. Their interactions over the past few days showed that the other was a decent person.

But Rebecca refused to let it go.

She saw that Cecilia actually looked even more beautiful than before, and Alexander's eyes practically popped out when he looked over.

When she and Alexander moved into this building, it was very rushed, or perhaps they only intended to stay temporarily, so they didn't bring much.

Initially, they thought the Barnes family was wealthy and powerful, and Alexander was their precious heir, so even at the end of the world, they wouldn't abandon Alexander.

But within days, the power went out. They tried to contact the Barnes family but couldn't. Their food at home was running out, the rainwater kept rising, and they couldn't go out even if they wanted to.

They could only come up to beg Arthur to see if he had any food or ask if he had any way to contact the Barnes family.

But they never expected to run into Cecilia, looking fresh and clean, more elegant than before, completely unaffected by the apocalypse.

Seeing the commotion had attracted neighbors in the building, Rebecca's eyes shifted, and a stream of tears flowed down.

"Cici... you... you abandoned Alex, then schemed to make Grandma Lin think Alex and I had that kind of relationship. Was it because you had already hooked up with Alex's uncle?"

Cecilia instantly got goosebumps all over.

What a scheming bitch, talking absolute nonsense!

Many neighbors watching the spectacle had gathered not far behind the door.

Many were people who had tried to come over to check things out yesterday when the 66th floor was installing doors, only to be blocked by Blackie.

Now seeing the drama, they joined in, adding their own comments and gossip.

An old woman snorted coldly, "Such a spirited-looking young girl, how could she do such man-seducing things? So shameless!"

Rebecca, outside the door, knew Cecilia wasn't good at arguing and thought Cecilia was cowed by her act, so she performed even more vigorously. "Cici, this engagement between you and Alex was arranged by your grandfather before he died. How can you ignore your own relative's dying wish for your own selfish gain?"

Hearing this, the watching neighbors also chimed in, "What a piece of work!"

"Exactly, yesterday they installed security doors and even set the dog on people. I knew that young girl was trouble, turns out she's loose too!"

"We're all neighbors, yet she guards against us like thieves. Just how many supplies have you hoarded to make such a big show of installing doors?"

"I bet those supplies were probably stocked by spreading her legs."

These chattering voices brought out all the anxiety these people had felt over the past few days, and their hostility grew.

People who once had respectable jobs and smiled to do business now became gossiping, foul-mouthed harpies.

Rebecca lowered her head to hide a smile, only thinking of how to shame Cecilia and trample her into the mud.

Cecilia looked around at the eager faces and gave a cold laugh.

She smoothed the hair at her temples and meticulously re-tied her slightly loosened braid, all while looking at the smug woman before her. "Rebecca, did your mouth eat shit this morning? Nothing but nonsense."

Several people nearby suddenly laughed.

Because Rebecca happened to be wearing a mustard-yellow padded jacket today, looking like she'd crawled out of a cesspool. Those watching the show didn't care about right or wrong.

Rebecca's face turned pale as she looked at Cecilia, but she continued acting delicate. "Cici, we're both educated people, how can you speak such... such foul language?"

Cecilia laughed coldly, "Well, I'm still much better than you, preaching morality while privately sleeping with your best friend's fiancé, and getting caught in bed by his parents!"

The onlookers turned to Rebecca again, starting to point and comment. "Such a pretty girl, how can she be so immoral?"

"She and that dog owner are cut from the same cloth."

"A dog-eat-dog fight. Neither is any good."
