Chapter 49 Visit

Back inside, Cecilia unfolded the note. It simply read: [I'm visiting tomorrow.]

A spark of curiosity flickered. Her new neighbor seemed polite enough, but why did it feel like trouble was lurking behind that courtesy?

With someone now living across the hall, Cecilia became extra cautious. She double-checked the locks before letting Blackie and the gray parrot out.

Whatever had happened in the Space, both animals seemed off. The usually brazen gray parrot now drooped its head, eyeing Blackie with an almost timid glance.

After flying out, the gray parrot perched on Cecilia's shoulder and stayed quiet for once.

"What's wrong? Did you two fight?" Cecilia teased the parrot, but it just shrank its neck further instead of squawking.

Blackie wore a sullen expression, clearly in a foul mood.

Before Cecilia could figure out what was happening, it spun around, presenting its big, pudgy rear toward her.

And then Cecilia spotted a splat of bird droppings right on Blackie's backside.

She couldn't help but burst out laughing. "Blackie, you got hit by bird poop!"

Birds, parrots included, were notorious for their lack of control over their waste. Wherever they went, they went.

Cecilia realized at once that having this big gray parrot around could be a nightmare.

She wasn't about to spend her post-apocalyptic days scrubbing up endless bird droppings. It would drive her mad.

Suddenly, she remembered the stash of pet supplies she had collected earlier. One box had been labeled, in bold marker: [poop catcher.]

With a thought, a small box appeared before her.

She tore it open with scissors and found an assortment of colorful, waterproof parrot diapers.

They were tiny outfits that could be worn by the parrot and were designed to catch both its droppings and urine.

Alongside the little clothes were plenty of matching parrot-sized diapers.

Without hesitation, Cecilia grabbed a blue outfit from the box and, along with a diaper, fitted it onto the gray parrot.

The parrot didn't resist at all, as if it had grown used to this kind of thing. Though it still drooped its head, clearly not thrilled about it.

Cecilia reached out and gently patted its head. "Hey, what's your name?"

The parrot lifted its head, its black, grape-like eyes darting around, tilting its head this way and that in a surprisingly charming way.

Just as Cecilia was about to assume the little creature wouldn't answer, it spoke, "Jet. Jet."

At first, Cecilia thought she'd misheard, so she repeated, "Jet?"

The gray parrot flapped its wings excitedly, hopping up and down, nodding repeatedly. "Jet! Jet!"

Cecilia shook her head, figuring its owner had a sense of humor to name a parrot that way.

But on second thought, it actually fit.

It was a gray parrot anyway.

"Alright, Jet, this will be your home from now on. I'm Cecilia, and this is Blackie." Cecilia pointed to herself, then to Blackie.

Jet chirped with excitement when Cecilia introduced herself, but the moment Cecilia pointed to Blackie, the parrot immediately wilted.

Its wings folded, its head drooped, and it tucked its neck in a terrified little ball.

Cecilia couldn't help but laugh at the sight.

She patted Blackie, who was sulking after getting hit by the parrot's droppings, and said, "Come on, let's get cleaned up first."

Having both swum through the acid rain, Cecilia and Blackie had varying degrees of skin irritation.

She mixed some Magic Spring water with hot water in the Space, cleaning and healing at the same time.

After the bath, Cecilia looked at her skin, once raw and bleeding, now smooth and flawless, her face even more radiant than before. She couldn't help but marvel that the Space-made products really were something else.

Once she and Blackie had enjoyed a warm bath, they lay down, and it was already six in the morning, with the first light of dawn creeping in.

Cecilia brought out Jet's cage but didn't close it. She just hung it in the living room and curled up with Blackie, drifting off in comfort.

Later, she was woken by a ruckus.

When she opened her eyes, she saw Blackie standing at the bedroom doorway, tense and alert, staring out.

Outside, a crisp, clear voice rang out. "No one's home! No one's home!"

Then a somewhat deep male voice, tinged with impatience, replied, "I'm not a bad guy. I'm your neighbor."

"No one's home! No one's home!"

"I left a note yesterday. I wanted to talk about something."

"No one's home! No one's home!"

The man was speechless.

Cecilia walked to the door and finally realized what was going on.

It was already noon, and the neighbor who had mentioned visiting in yesterday's note had come knocking.

And the one holding the conversation wasn't anyone else but Jet!

Seeing Cecilia awake, the parrot flapped its wings excitedly, shouting, "No one's home! No one's home!"

Cecilia, still half-drowsy, couldn't help but burst out laughing.

After taking the mischievous Jet back into the bedroom, she finally went to the door to answer.
