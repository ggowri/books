Chapter 196 Silver and the Pistol

Arthur then went on to explain just how formidable those bullets really were.

By the time he finished, Cecilia was practically speechless. She had only ever come across weapons like that in fiction.

Thinking back to the scene just now, with Arthur dragging Blaise Hart out from the middle of that crowd, Cici felt that although she had used her rifle to kill a few people, she had not really helped all that much. Yet she had walked away with a bag of Arthur's weapons, learned that he possessed a dimension ring, and now received an outrageously powerful pistol as well.

She had made out like a bandit.

Cici liked the pistol enormously and spent a long while turning it over in her hands.

She did not notice that Blaise, resting in the back, had woken up and was now watching Arthur and Cici talk.

One eyebrow lifted.

Well now. Arthur was actually smiling at a woman. That did not happen often.

Just as he was preparing to watch a little longer, Blackie, who had been lying on the floor mat, sprang upright in a flash and planted his enormous black face directly in front of Blaise.

"What the hell!"

Blaise jumped so violently he nearly threw himself backwards.

"What on earth is that?"

Once he realised it was only a large black dog, he patted his chest and let out a long breath of relief.

At that moment, Cici spotted something outside the car window.

A tiny white bird, no bigger than a palm, was actually peering into the car.

The car was going well over a hundred, yet the bird was somehow keeping pace.

Cici saw that it had a red beak, delicate and rather lovely to look at.

"Arthur, there's a…"

She had not even finished speaking when Blaise suddenly shouted from the back, "Silver!"

At once, the white bird slowed slightly and came alongside the rear seat window.

Blaise opened the window and let it in.

The little bird chirped away excitedly, fluttered around Blaise several times, then settled on his shoulder and affectionately pecked at his face with its beak.

"All right, all right, I'm fine, Silver. Did you miss me?"

Blaise gently stroked the bird's head with one finger.

The two of them fussed over one another shamelessly.

Blackie tilted his head and stared at the tiny white creature in front of him. His great dark head kept turning from side to side, and he sniffed hard, as though trying to fix Silver's scent firmly in his memory.

"That's Blaise's carrier pigeon. After the apocalypse, it mutated and became much smaller, but it's incredibly fast, perfect for delivering messages and scouting."

Seeing the puzzled look in Cici's eyes, Arthur explained.

Cici looked at the delicate little bird and could not help comparing it to Jet.

If this bird stood in front of Jet, it would look like a doll.

For some reason, the question slipped out before she could stop herself.

"Is that pigeon boy or girl?"

A laugh burst from the back seat.

"Hah. Cici, it's make or female, not ‘boy or girl'"

The moment he laughed, the wounds on his body pulled and he winced with pain, quickly shutting his mouth again.

"Our Silver's female."

Blackie was still staring at Silver with that huge solemn face of his.

Silver, perched on Blaise's shoulder, was studying Blackie in return.

One huge and one tiny, the two animals seemed to become strangely absorbed in each other, as if some silent exchange had begun between them.

Cici thought to herself that Jet, if she remembered correctly, was male.

"Cici, I'm Blaise. Arthur took me in as his younger brother. You can call me Blaise from now on."

From the back seat, Blaise held out one hand.

He was wearing Arthur's coat, but even so, the fabric covering his arms looked thin.

Cici could see the pale skin of his arm, covered in lash marks.

And yet despite all that, the smile on the boy's face was still dazzling. When he smiled, it was like his whole face lit up.

"Hello. I'm Cecilia Summers. You can call me Cici."

She reached back and lightly shook his hand.

Before long, they arrived at the apartment building.

Blaise was so weak by then that Arthur had to half carry him upstairs.

Cici could see plainly how much Arthur cared for him. When he spoke to Blaise, he sounded less like a teammate and more like an elder brother speaking to family.

By the time they got home and confirmed that everything inside was safe, it was only about four in the morning.

Arthur was supporting Blaise, clearly about to say goodbye to Cici.

But the instant the door opened, a white blur shot out.

It came with an overwhelming smell as well.

Lowering her eyes, Cici saw a tiny white dog.

Round as a ball, covered in fluffy fur, except for the patch around its backside, which was smeared yellow.

Was that really Snowy?

Only now Snowy was thoroughly unappealing.

It was covered in its own filth and seemed entirely unaware of the fact. The moment it got out, it began scampering wildly up and down the corridor.

Cici watched, speechless, as pale yellow paw prints bloomed all over the floor like little blossoms.

Then Snowy vanished down the hall in a flash.

"Arthur, since when did you keep such a messy little dog?"

Blaise clicked his tongue.

Silver, perched on his shoulder, gave two chirps that sounded unmistakably disapproving.

Blackie had already run off to catch the little menace.

For a moment, the sixty sixth floor was lively indeed.

Cici let out a laugh.

Arthur's face had gone completely dark.

Very soon, Blackie returned with Snowy dangling from the scruff of its neck.

The little thing looked thoroughly unconvinced by the whole arrangement, kicking all four paws in the air and wriggling in protest.

Blackie, his face drooping as always, carried Snowy over to Arthur and set it beside him. Then he lifted his eyes and looked straight at Arthur.

The meaning could not have been clearer.

Your dog, your problem.
