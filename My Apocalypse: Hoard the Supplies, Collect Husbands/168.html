Chapter 168 The Random Animal

"Care Ranch—

Level: 3

Function: In Care Ranch, all animals can coexist peacefully and will automatically be fed with the Space's energy. Fully automated management is provided. Of course, the owner of the Space may also feed them manually. Each type of animal can now produce two by-products.

Restriction: When the Space's energy is insufficient, it will draw energy from the Magic Spring, so remember to feed them.

Note: After all, they are living beings. Show a little care.

New feature added in this upgrade: Each week, one random animal will appear in the ranch. It will produce either valuable or extremely frustrating random by-products, so cherish it well!"

Cecilia stared at the final line labeled "New feature added in this upgrade." She read it three times, confirming that the words "extremely frustrating" were really written there.

Taking a deep breath, she directly called out, "Ari!"

Ari still looked a little displeased. It sat to the side, walking with slow, dignified cat steps and holding its head high like an arrogant feline lord.

Cecilia pointed at the words on the screen.

"I've always wanted to ask you. Did you write all these feature descriptions yourself? Can you modify the text on this screen?"

Ari didn't answer. Instead, it tilted its head, its almond-shaped eyes blinking twice. Two sparks of intelligence seemed to flash within them.

Cecilia thought she detected a trace of cunning in those bright cat eyes.

Then Ari stretched out a paw and swiped forward.

Immediately, Cecilia saw the text on the screen change.

"Stinky Animal Gathering Ground—

Level: 3

Function: In this stinky animal gathering ground, all animals do nothing but eat, poop, and reproduce all day. A bunch of brainless idiots. Automatic feeding is available, or you can shovel poop manually. The only use of each stinky animal is producing two by-products. It would be even better if there were something edible for cats. Unfortunately, there isn't.

Restriction: They eat too much and poop too much, and the Space still has to deal with it, which consumes energy and is extremely annoying. If there are too many animals and energy becomes insufficient, the Space will be drained, meaning the Magic Spring water will be used.

Note: They're all smelly idiots. If it were up to me, I wouldn't even let them enter the Space.

New feature added in this upgrade: Each week the stinky animal gathering ground will randomly produce one animal depending on mood, which can generate some strange by-products. If it happens to be a dinosaur, remember to call me to take a look."

Cecilia was momentarily dumbfounded.

Beside her, Ari yawned.

"You asked me. I don't have permission to change it normally, but since you asked, I simply followed your wishes, Master."

Cecilia's mouth twitched. "Change it back."

Ari looked at her pitifully.

"Master, modifying this consumes energy. Until the next Space upgrade, I'm afraid it can't be changed back…"

Cecilia rolled her eyes and decided to ignore Ari.

Cats were simply unreasonable creatures.

At that moment, Blackie seemed to sense her mood and wagged its tail as it approached.

Cecilia patted the big dog's head, and her mood immediately improved a little.

Blackie was far more considerate.

Ari stared at Blackie, its tail sticking straight up. "Stupid big dog."

The next second, Ari was slapped flat onto the ground by Blackie's paw.

Cecilia instantly felt much better.

From this Space upgrade, Cecilia realized something.

Future upgrades would probably become more difficult, and each time only one attribute might improve. Just like this time, the ranch had been upgraded.

Ignoring the cat and dog already fighting behind her, Cecilia turned and walked toward the ranch to see what "random animal" had appeared after the upgrade.

Then she heard a deep, booming call.

For a moment, Cecilia felt that she had heard this sound somewhere before.

When she walked closer and saw the enormous figure, she remembered.

Hadn't she heard this at the zoo before?

Standing before Cecilia was a huge elephant.

She took a deep breath and opened the elephant's by-product section.

After reading it, the corners of her mouth began twitching again.

"Seriously… that ‘extremely frustrating' description was absolutely accurate."

Under the by-product section, it clearly said:

Elephant Brand Back Scratcher.

The description was even more exaggerated, claiming that this back scratcher had miraculous effects. No matter where you felt itchy or how unbearable the itch was, scratching just twice would immediately relieve it.

Cecilia's mouth twitched, but she still chose to start production.

After all, free items were still free.

Even if it seemed useless right now, it might come in handy someday.

One could be produced every day.

Cecilia told Ari to keep an eye on it and collect the back scratcher daily. After seven days, she would come back and see what new animal appeared in the ranch.

After finishing everything in the Space, Cecilia checked the time.

More than half an hour had already passed.

She didn't dare delay any longer. Having rested enough, she brought Blackie out of the Space and prepared to continue sweeping through the building.

They had just finished clearing the sixteenth floor, so Cecilia immediately led Blackie toward the seventeenth floor.

The seventeenth and eighteenth floors appeared to be entirely occupied by a single company. The contents inside were very similar, mostly paper products.

There were A4 sheets, rice paper, and various colored papers.

Cecilia stored them all directly into the Space.

After the disaster, the number of trees around the world would drastically decline, and paper would become a scarce resource with many future uses.

Continuing upward, Cecilia basically moved forward nonstop, collecting whatever she saw.

As a major export country, Auroria exported a vast variety of goods.

These included agricultural products, communication equipment, steel, ceramics, clothing, metallurgical materials, construction raw materials, and many other products.
