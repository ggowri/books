Chapter 205 Nathan Must Die

"Nathan has to die tonight."

Once Nathan was dealt with, they could come back and handle Mabel, whose fighting ability was much lower.

After settling the plan, the group split up.

As for the old butler, Arthur simply knocked him unconscious and tossed him into the snow.

The old bastard had no direct grudge with any of them, but he was still one of the Barnes family's dogs. Who knew how many vile things he had done behind the scenes. Left out here in this frozen wilderness, his fate would depend on his own luck.

Once Arthur and Colonel Reed disappeared into the darkness and slipped into the Barnes estate, Cecilia turned and gave Blackie a sharp pat on the head.

"Blackie, unlock mount mode."

Blackie's eyes lit up at once. Brilliant. At last, he could run wild across the snow.

At Cecilia's command, his body quickly grew to twice its usual size, becoming about as large as a small pony. Then he lowered himself, waiting for her to climb on.

Cecilia swung a leg over his back, settled herself in place, and patted his neck.

"Go. Catch that car."

The snow was slick, and the vehicle had only been gone for about half an hour. At most, it could not have travelled more than twenty kilometres.

And once Blackie started sprinting over the snow, he was far faster than an ordinary vehicle.

Blackie shook out his fur.

Off they went.

This was Cecilia's first time truly racing through the open air on Blackie's back.

The last time, they had done no more than a short run indoors.

Now she felt the wind howling past her so fiercely that she could hardly keep her eyes open. Bloody hell, this speed had to be close to a hundred kilometres an hour.

She hurriedly pulled a pair of goggles out of the Space and put them on.

Once she had them in place, she saw the endless white snow all around them streaming backwards at astonishing speed. Against the vast expanse of white, she and Blackie were nothing more than a tiny black streak hurtling across it.

Her arms tightened round Blackie's neck as she lowered herself close against him.

After travelling a while, Cecilia spotted tyre tracks pressed into the snow.

She had Blackie follow them forward.

Sure enough, it was only a short while later that she actually caught sight of the car ahead.

It was a black modified off roader, its wheels fitted with snow chains, struggling forward through the snow.

Without hesitation, Cecilia took a grenade from the Space and leaned close to Blackie's ear.

"Get closer. Steady now."

Blackie instantly accelerated, faster still than before, his four paws kicking up sprays of snow as he ran.

And at that moment, Nathan had already noticed someone coming up behind him.

The truth was, he was not heading for the eastern shelter at all.

He was running away.

He had already decided to abandon the Barnes family.

For a long time, he had believed that sheltering under the Barnes family tree would make life easy. When Miranda died, he had not felt the slightest grief. On the contrary, he had been glad, because it meant the Barnes family fortune would finally fall into his and his father's hands.

After all, his father held power within the military, and he himself had the brains.

Things had indeed gone as he expected. After Miranda died, it was he and his father who had taken charge of the family.

Even his uncles, and that aunt who had always looked down her nose at everyone, had begun treating him differently.

He had long intended to carve out a real empire for himself in this apocalypse.

Nathan was not like the rest of the fools in his family.

He was the one most like Miranda. Ruthless, clever, and patient.

That was why, when his father died, he had not rushed forward in blind fury to go after Cecilia.

He knew that if he made a move then, he would never make it back alive.

He had already planned everything. He would lean on the Barnes family as a shield while quietly growing stronger.

But he could not do as his father had done and curry favour with the higher ups through flattery.

Nor could he do as his grandmother had done and strengthen the family by trafficking people.

At a time like this, he needed results. He needed to make himself valuable enough that General Mercer would not cast the Barnes family aside.

And he needed blood to pave the road ahead.

So he had pushed his own younger brother out as the scapegoat in this matter. It gave Colonel Reed's side a way to save face, and at the same time sent a warning to everyone left in the Barnes family.

The family was facing disaster. If Nathan was in charge, he could sacrifice even his own brother.

If the rest of them did not hurry up and prove their usefulness, he could just as easily sacrifice them too.

At the same time, he had already decided that once this storm had passed, he would deepen his cooperation with organisations like Grant's and profit from both sides of the law in this ruined world.

He already had a plan.

He knew labour was dirt cheap now.

He meant to gather a group of people, lock up their families as leverage, feed them barely enough to survive, and force them to dive beneath the ice into the acid rain below to salvage supplies.

At present, only the authorities were carrying out underwater salvage operations beneath the ice. Breaking through the ice was difficult, acid rain was still dangerous, and most people had no medicine.

But if he seized their families, those people would have no choice but to risk their lives.

Then whatever they hauled up could be used as bargaining chips with the military and with the criminal organisations.

That was how he would restore the Barnes family to power.

Only...

Today he had seen the look Arthur, that bastard son, had lifted towards him.

And after speaking with his uncles again, he had found that the two old fools actually believed that once Colonel Reed left, they were safe.

Utterly idiotic.

So Nathan had taken a few trusted men and a batch of supplies and fled.

He intended to seek refuge with a criminal organisation he had been contacting in secret, where he could set himself up as a petty king and abandon the Barnes family entirely.
