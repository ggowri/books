Chapter 75 In a Matter of Seconds

Cecilia stood to the side, holding the restless Blackie in her arms.

Hailey sidled up to her, pressing close.

Although Cecilia could feel the girl trembling, Hailey kept silent, gripping tightly the golf club she'd picked up from the sports store.

As Cecilia marveled at how quickly Hailey was adapting to the post - apocalyptic world, a chorus of howls erupted ahead.

Arthur darted forward, unarmed, moving as swiftly as a jungle predator.

He hunched slightly and landed two quick punches in each of the twins' ribs before they could even react.

Then, with two sweeping kicks, he sent the burly men crashing to the ground.

"Damn it!"

"I'll kill you!"

A look of disdain flashed across Arthur's face as he caught the baseball bat swung at him with one hand and grabbed the wrist of a thug with the other.

"Ahhhh!"

Ignoring the man's wails, Arthur raised the bat and brought it down hard on the thug's head.

The man crumpled into unconsciousness.

Seeing his brother go down, the other twin lunged from behind, wrapping his arms around Arthur's waist.

Arthur braced his legs, pushed off the ground, and with a powerful leap, used his legs and core to flip the man over. He snatched the bat and delivered a similar blow to the second man's head.

It all happened in a matter of seconds.

Cecilia hadn't even managed to follow Arthur's movements clearly before it was over.

For a moment, her eyes darkened, and she regarded Arthur with palpable caution.

This man was stronger than she'd thought, stronger than anyone she'd met in the post - apocalyptic world.

In her past life, if they were enemies, Cecilia was certain she wouldn't have survived the ten - year apocalypse.

Even after many people awakened superpowers later, Arthur's performance right now made her believe he could take down most superpowered individuals, even without such abilities himself.

He was incredibly powerful.

Bryan and his lackey, witnessing this display, backed away in terror.

"Stay back!" Bryan cried, filled with remorse.

He regretted underestimating the tall man and regretted provoking Cecilia once again.

"A-All the stuff we've collected is yours! Take it!"

Bryan dropped his large backpack on the ground.

It hit with a loud metallic thud, and the zipper burst open, revealing a stash of gold bars.

"I-I've never done you any harm. Just let me go. I won't bother you again," the shorter man stammered, his face ashen.

He too dropped his backpack and gently unzipped it.

"There are gold bars and gemstones in here. It's all yours. Please, just let me leave."

Inside the backpack were more gold bars and red jewelry boxes.

Arthur said nothing, simply arching a brow and glancing back at Cecilia as if awaiting her decision.

Cecilia found his silent inquiry rather intriguing.

She spun the dagger in her hand with a practiced flick. "One each?"

Arthur didn't reply, but his body was already in motion.

The shorter man tried to bolt, but Arthur lunged forward, kicking his legs out from under him.

Before the man could hit the ground, Arthur's knife flashed—a clean slash across the throat.

It was over in an instant.

Bryan turned and fled without a word.

Cecilia reacted a beat slower, but showed no panic. She hurled her dagger, and it sank deep into Bryan's thigh.

He stumbled and crashed face-first onto the asphalt, scrambling desperately to rise.

By then, Cecilia was upon him. She pressed close, a fresh blade already at his neck.

"W-What are you doing? The rescue teams are here! Police could be patrolling right now! Everything will go back to normal soon—you'll be murderers!" Bryan stammered, sweat pouring down his face.

Cecilia let out a cold laugh. "Sorry. The apocalypse has only just begun. Your police and rescuers aren't coming."

With a sharp twist of her wrist, she drew the blade across his throat. Blood sprayed.

Cecilia leaped back, avoiding the crimson arc.

Truth be told, seeing Bryan again had suddenly reminded her of something from her past life—or rather, it was the twin brutes who had triggered the memory.

In that previous existence, Cecilia hadn't lived in this apartment building, though her home hadn't been far away.

After the Ice Stage passed, order collapsed completely. The death toll reached staggering numbers.

Supplies stocked during acid rain and the Ice Stage were mostly depleted.

It was then that various "groups" began to form.

Some were composed of ordinary people with legitimate jobs before the collapse, who now embraced the law of the jungle, shedding morality and humanity for their own gain.

Others had never been decent to begin with. In a stable, orderly society, their cruelty had been restrained. But the apocalypse unleashed their true natures—they committed every atrocity, finding pleasure in killing and tormenting others.
