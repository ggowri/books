Chapter 147 Toxic Haze 9

“Doesn’t matter. You held Naomi and pulled her up. Since you laid a hand on her, you have to take responsibility.”

The man speaking lived over by the Administration Hall. The others standing beside him, helping to pressure Calvin into taking responsibility, were all hangers-on he had gathered around himself.

It had started three days earlier.

Calvin had been out digging coal when he heard someone calling for help. He found a young woman, the very same girl now crying in the corner, Naomi Blake. She had fallen into a pit three or four metres deep and could not climb out. Calvin was not the sort to leave someone there to die, so naturally he helped pull her out. He had even used a rope.

But by the time the story passed through Nathaniel, it had somehow turned into embracing her and taking her by the hand.

“Nathaniel, are you picking a fight on purpose?”

The survivors living near the Administration Hall were now under Nathaniel’s control. He and Calvin had dealt with one another before, and there had been more than a few clashes between them. Calvin suspected Nathaniel was using this filthy little trick to settle old scores. He was so angry his face had gone green.

“Why would I need to pick a fight? You’re a widower in your thirties with a child in tow. My sister’s only twenty. I’m telling you to take responsibility for her, and you’ve still got the nerve to complain.”

“Who exactly are you calling a burden?” Calvin snatched up his shovel and looked ready to bring it down on Nathaniel’s head. The people nearby hurried to restrain him. Nathaniel, unwilling to back down, started shouting that Calvin had had the best of it and was still acting hard done by.

At that moment, Mayor Whitmore and the other two arrived. Once they had heard the whole story, Whitmore looked thoroughly exasperated.

“Nathaniel, Calvin helped her out of kindness. He saved her, and now you’re trying to force your sister on him. Have you lost your mind?” Whitmore rarely lost his temper, but this time he did.

The instant Nathaniel heard that, his fury flared.

“Whitmore, you’re covering for Calvin. You’re being unreasonable. My sister’s a young woman, she’s the one who’s suffered here. Whether Calvin meant well or not, he touched her, so he has to take responsibility.”

Nathaniel kept on arguing in circles, and everyone around them looked utterly fed up. The trouble was, he was the sort of man who did not understand reason. If he did not get his way, he would likely keep causing trouble for quite some time.

Evelyn and Adrian stood among the crowd. Evelyn was just as speechless as everyone else, but this was the sort of thing Calvin had to deal with himself. Outside help would only make it worse.

That said, if the other side tried to lay a hand on him again, she would step in at once.

Calvin shoved Nathaniel. Then he shoved the others as well. While everyone was still reeling, he let out a cold laugh.

“Well then. I’ve touched all six of you now. Do I have to take responsibility for you as well?”

Nathaniel was struck dumb.

So was everyone watching.

“No, Daddy, I don’t want to be family with that ugly man.” Wendy suddenly burst into tears, and Calvin bent down and picked her up.

“Don’t worry. Dad will throw them out in a minute.”

Nathaniel was so furious he nearly coughed up blood. He was a grown man. Had he just been flirted with?

“Calvin Shaw, are you looking for death?”

Calvin ignored him. Nathaniel glanced at the men behind him, and several of them picked up their tools, ready to start fighting again. Just then, Nathan stepped forward with a homemade crossbow in hand.

Everyone recoiled at once. Even Nathaniel became rather more well-behaved.

“First rule of the Safe Zone. No fighting. Are you all blind, or merely deaf?” Nathan’s gaze landed on Nathaniel. “Out of all the men here, you had to pick Calvin. Frankly, if he doesn’t kill you, that’s more mercy than you deserve. And you.”

Nathan had Naomi dragged over. “Do you actually want to marry Calvin?”

Naomi shook her head at once. “No. My brother’s forcing me.”

Well now. That was news.

All eyes turned to Nathaniel, but he still looked as stubborn and shameless as ever.

“What do you mean, forcing you? He touched your hand. Of course he should take responsibility.”

“Then why don’t you marry him? You were all over him in that fight just now.”

The second Naomi shouted back, everyone’s expressions turned even stranger as they looked at Nathaniel.

“You little traitor. I’m helping you, and this is how you repay me?” Nathaniel raised a hand to strike her, but Nathan stepped in and stopped him.

“Nathaniel, are you trying to get yourself thrown out of the Safe Zone?”

That finally shut him up. Once Nathaniel and his lot had been taken away by the security guards, Nathan let out a weary sigh and looked at Calvin.

“Maybe you ought to find someone new after all. Someone who could help look after Wendy. How many times has this happened just this month? All the widows and young women in the Safe Zone have their eyes on you.”

And it was hardly surprising.

Calvin was simply too capable.

He was tall, strong, hardworking, and could do just about everything. He could grow vegetables, dig coal, hunt, fix things. There was almost nothing he could not turn his hand to. Next to him, every other unmarried man in the Safe Zone looked somewhat lacking.

In times like these, quite a few women wanted someone dependable to lean on. A man like Calvin was exactly the sort they would choose.

And the way he looked after his daughter only made him seem more ideal.

Calvin looked helpless. “I’ve said it before. I’ll never find anyone else. Not in this life. I’d sooner die. There’s only ever been one person in my heart.”

Even if she was already gone.

“All right, all right. No one’s forcing you,” Nathan said. “No point trying to twist something that won’t bend. But the rest of you should stop staring only at Calvin. There are other men in the Safe Zone. We encourage people to pair up and make a life together, but no one is to cause trouble over it. Anyone who does, my crossbow won’t care who they are.”

Nathan swept his gaze over the crowd, making himself perfectly clear. It was a warning as much as anything else, and quite a few men and women quietly dropped their little schemes.

Naomi cast Calvin a timid glance, murmured an apology, and ran off.

“When am I ever going to have a bit of luck in love?” Eddie groaned, clutching his chest in exaggerated misery.

“If you had a normal haircut, you might stand a chance,” Evelyn said.

She gave him another up-and-down look. “Honestly, as you are now, you look less like a romantic prospect and more like an escaped convict.”

“Evelyn, why are you always so vicious with me?” Eddie pressed a hand to his heart, looking utterly wounded.

“Because you’re basically my brother.”

“Well, there goes my dream of finding a beautiful rich widow. Thirty already. Honestly, how many thirties does a man get?”

Evelyn glanced over at Calvin. “Calvin’s nearly forty, and he’s still in demand.”

One sentence, two casualties. Calvin looked at them all with a helpless smile.

“Right, off you go. I can handle something this minor.”

Evelyn glanced at Wendy. “Your daughter said you were about to be beaten to death. Naturally we ran over.”

“Wendy, you can’t go around passing on false alarms.” Calvin rubbed her little head. “Didn’t I tell you? I could deal with them myself. I told you to stay out of the way.”

The noble, upright Calvin teaching his daughter like that was enough to broaden everyone’s horizons.

On the way back, Eddie was still moaning without pause.

“Why does no one ever fancy me? I don’t even get the ones trying it on for convenience.”

Evelyn was just about to offer a few words of comfort when someone stepped forward from the side and looked at Eddie with bright, eager eyes.

“Eddie, what about me? I’ve noticed you for ages.”

Eddie stared at her, then asked with painful effort, “How old are you?”

“I’ve just turned forty. I’ve also got two sons. They could start calling you Dad straight away.”

Eddie “died” on the spot.
