Chapter 87 Surviving Alone: Part 11

Evelyn noticed the stone ledges were coated in black bat droppings, scattered like tiny pellets. Guano. In some old remedies, it had even been used as medicine.

She had no interest in collecting them. She needed to keep moving.

Some of the tunnels were so narrow that her kayak barely squeezed through.

"Hey…"

At every new cavern entrance, she shouted once, using the echo to estimate its size.

She soon spotted another stone platform and paddled over quickly. This one was much smaller, only three or four square meters. She carefully inspected the rock wall and wiped it down with alcohol again.

Nothing appeared.

"Did I already use up all my luck?"

She pulled off her mask for a breath of fresh air. The stalactites and stone columns here were different from the ones outside. Much smaller, almost coral like. Delicate and beautiful.

"I'll rest for a bit. There's no way I'm going home empty handed today."

She took a chair out of her space and set it on the platform, planning to sit for a while. But she had not used the bathroom since morning, and now it was becoming uncomfortable.

She glanced around awkwardly.

Better hold it.

Just then, she noticed two narrow seams in the rock wall.

She sprang up from the chair and rushed over with her flashlight, staring at the wall.

"This is a stone door, right? You have got to be kidding me. I searched everywhere and it's been right here. But how do I open it?"

She shoved it hard.

It did not budge.

Right. She was not some martial arts master with inner power from a TV series. There had to be a mechanism.

She lay flat on the ground and started searching for a trigger. Thirty minutes passed.

Nothing.

Maybe she was overthinking it. What if those were just ordinary cracks?

"Guess I'm not blessed by God today. Maybe I'm cursed instead."

Creak…

The stone door suddenly began to move.

She froze, staring down at her feet.

Only then did she notice a trigram symbol carved into the floor. Her boots were positioned perfectly on either side of it.

She smacked her forehead.

"Don't tell me I'm actually the chosen one."

The stone door fully opened. She shone her flashlight inside and saw a long staircase descending into darkness.

She stored the kayak in her space and stepped through the doorway with her flashlight and trekking pole.

The moment she entered, the stone door creaked shut behind her.

She lowered her light and, as expected, found another trigram symbol on the ground.

So as long as both feet pressed down evenly on the symbol, the door would open. There had to be a weight triggered mechanism underneath. Balanced pressure activated it, moving the stone slab.

She started down the steps.

The staircase seemed carved directly from the natural rock, fused seamlessly with the walls. It felt less like a cave now and more like a hidden chamber.

She hesitated.

Should she keep going?

What if there were more dangerous mechanisms ahead?

But turning back now did not feel like an option either.

She switched to a flashlight with a fuller battery and continued downward.

After about ten minutes, she reached another stone door.

Standing on the trigram symbol again, she triggered it open.

Inside, the beam of her flashlight revealed something that made her breath catch.

It looked like an underground tomb.

Rows of stone coffins hung suspended in midair, bound tightly with iron chains.

Behind them stood a massive stone stele. The inscription carved into it was wild and flowing, almost illegible.

She could not make out a single word.

Evelyn crouched low and edged along the wall, moving carefully. She half expected hidden weapons to fire out of nowhere and turn her into a pincushion.

But after circling past the suspended stone coffins, she found no traps. No arrows. No blades. Nothing.

Her courage grew little by little. She slowly straightened up, though she still kept close to the wall as she moved.

The walls inside were covered with intricate murals.

But something felt… off.

Every figure in the paintings was completely naked. The poses were explicit and anything but elegant.

A second later, realization hit her.

These were not ordinary murals.

They were ancient erotic illustrations.

She walked the length of the passage and discovered that the entire tomb corridor was covered in them.

Good grief. Just what kind of person was buried here?

And more importantly, where were the treasures? Not a single piece of gold or silver in sight.

She began searching for another exit. Behind the stone stele, she discovered another stone door. After triggering it and stepping inside, she found three more coffins suspended in midair.

Behind them stood another stele.

This time, the inscription was written in a more orderly script. She could read it clearly.

"…You've got to be kidding me."

After finishing the text, her whole expression twisted.

It told the story of a powerful city lord from a thousand years ago. He kept hundreds of wives. His body had been weak, ruined by excess and indulgence, and he died young.

Before his death, he ordered his people to find this hidden paradise and place his coffin here to prevent grave robbers from finding it.

His hundreds of wives were executed to accompany him in death.

The coffins hanging outside contained the women who had borne him children.

As for the ones who had not, the inscription did not mention them.

Evelyn could only guess their fate had been even less dignified.

She stood there in silence.

So this city lord had been that broke? He kept hundreds of women, yet did not leave behind a single burial treasure?

She circled his coffin once. No mechanisms. No hidden compartments. And she had zero interest in opening it.

Staring at the murals lining the walls, she felt speechless. Earlier she had admired the artistry, even praised the lifelike detail.

Turns out she had been appreciating ancient adult content.

There was yet another stone door ahead, like progressing through levels in a game. She opened it and stepped into another chamber.

This time, she saw several large chests stacked against the wall.

Her heart leaped.

Finally. The gold and silver she had been waiting for?

Pulling on her gloves, she approached and prepared to open one.

There was no lock.

A bad feeling crept over her.

Had someone beaten her to it?

She lifted the lid.

Inside was a chest packed full of yellowed books.

Excitement surged through her. She grabbed one and flipped it open.

"…You've got to be kidding me. Again?"

More erotic illustrations.

The style was bold, the brushwork crude, the pages aged and brittle. But she could tell immediately. They were identical to the murals outside.

"This city lord was something else. No wonder he died young."

She opened the other chests.

Every single one was filled with the same material.

Despair washed over her.

It felt like the universe was mocking her. Other people went treasure hunting and found priceless artifacts. She risked her life and found… this.

She checked the time.

It was already afternoon.

Time to head home.

She turned to retrace her steps.

But when she stepped on the trigram symbol again, nothing happened.

The stone door did not move.

The entire passage had only this one exit.

Now it would not open.

Was she going to die trapped here?

Had the tomb's owner designed this place so that anyone who entered would never leave?

Evelyn forced herself to calm down.

There had to be another way out.

There always was.
