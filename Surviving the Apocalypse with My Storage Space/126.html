Chapter 126 Letting the Wolf In

Security at the base had become stricter than ever. On the way back to the apartment block, Evelyn and Adrian were stopped and checked twice.

What Evelyn had not expected was to find Clara back from the Medical Division as well, and with her was Sophie.

The moment Sophie saw Evelyn, she became wildly emotional.

“Evelyn, Iris really wasn’t lying. It is you. I never thought I’d see you again. Iris is dead, Evelyn. I’m so scared. Thank God I found you again.”

Seeing the cool look on Evelyn’s face, Clara awkwardly scratched at her hair.

“Evelyn, she told me she was your best friend. You disappeared yesterday and never came back, and she said she was worried about you, so I brought her here. I’m sorry, I...”

Clara clearly knew she had meant well and made a mess of it. She looked deeply annoyed with herself.

“It’s all right. Go back inside,” Evelyn said. “Elliot’s been worried sick about you these past few days.”

“All right. If you need anything, just call me.”

Once Clara had gone, Evelyn turned to Sophie, her voice cool and flat.

“Well, now you’ve seen me. Can you leave?”

“Evelyn, are you still angry with me? Back then Iris and Francesca forced me to call you. I’m sorry. It was all my fault. But at the time, apart from you, I really didn’t know who else to turn to.”

She meant the night the torrential rain had flooded the dormitory, when she had rung Evelyn and asked her to come and fetch the three of them, then take them in as well.

“So you do remember,” Evelyn said, her tone openly mocking. “You called me, offered me a one-thousand-pound tip, and expected me to come out in a storm to collect the three of you.”

Sophie shook her head as she cried.

“But you were the only one from Elaris. You know that. My family’s in Altaris. Iris and Francesca both came from better-off families, and I didn’t dare go against them. You had already suspended your studies. I was on my own at university. I was afraid they’d make life difficult for me, so I had to stay close to them.”

“Yes, of course,” Evelyn said coolly. “And now that Iris and Francesca are both dead, you can say whatever you like.”

She had no interest in going round in circles with her and signalled to Adrian to open the door.

“Evelyn, could you let me stay here for a while? Iris and I were living over in the single rooms, but it’s awful there. Iris died because she drank the water there. I don’t want to die. We were good friends. Please help me.”

“You’re still using that same old trick a year later?” Evelyn said. “Sophie, this is the base, not university. Emotional blackmail won’t get you anywhere with me.”

“How can you be this cold-blooded?”

Evelyn gave a small shrug. “Simple. I am cold-blooded.”

Adrian had already unlocked the door. Evelyn ignored Sophie and turned to go in, but Sophie suddenly rushed forward and grabbed her.

“Evelyn, you’re actually living with a man? Have you forgotten Nathaniel Blake? I saw him at the base a few days ago. He liked you so much. If he knew you were with someone else, what would he think?”

Evelyn stared at her.

Who on earth was Nathaniel Blake?

“I don’t know the person you’re talking about. Let go. Don’t make me hit you.”

Sophie looked at her through tearful eyes. “You really don’t even remember Nathaniel? In your first year, you entered a competition together and won a prize. I even took photos for you both. Weren’t you in love? How have you moved on so quickly, and started living with someone else?”

That was where Evelyn finally lost patience.

She reached out, caught Sophie by the throat, and dragged her away.

“Evelyn... let go... I can’t breathe...”

There was a hidden corner by the turn in the corridor. Evelyn hauled her over there and pinned her against the wall with one hand at her neck.

“Say that again.”

“Evelyn, let go... I can’t breathe... please let go...”

Evelyn did not loosen her grip. If anything, she tightened it.

“How ridiculous. I used to think you were at least a decent person. Turns out the most false-hearted one of all was you.”

Sophie’s eyes filled with pure terror.

Now she was truly frightened.

“I made it up. Please let me go. Evelyn, I was angry, that’s all. I was talking nonsense. We’re friends.”

“We have never been friends.”

Evelyn flung her to the ground. Sophie lay there gasping for breath, staring up at her in fear.

“Even if I said the wrong thing, I don’t deserve to die,” she shouted. “Evelyn, you’re vicious. You’re completely cold-blooded.”

With that, she stumbled to her feet and fled down the stairs.

Evelyn looked at her own hand and gave a cold little laugh.

She had never been particularly kind-hearted.

And as for letting the wolf into the house, she had already learned that lesson once through Claire Brooks.

When she returned to the sixth floor, she found Adrian still standing outside the door. The moment he saw her, he stepped forward, caught hold of her wrist, and pulled her inside.

Evelyn let Pip and Lark out again. Thankfully, she had left a gap in the rucksack and the bedding had not been packed down too tightly, so the two little things had stayed tucked away quietly.

“Even you two have to hide yourselves now. Poor little souls.”

She poured out some clean water and scattered sunflower seeds for them so they could eat.

“Who is Nathaniel?”

Evelyn turned to look at Adrian, baffled. “Just some old classmate. I’d completely forgotten he existed, and now somehow he’s been turned into the hero of some ridiculous melodrama. Since when did you become this nosy?”

Something flickered in Adrian’s brow. “The woman just now. Shall I kill her?”

Evelyn stared at him.

Adrian lifted his eyes to hers. “She isn’t a good person.”

“I know.”

Seeing she had no wish to continue the subject, he let it drop.

Evelyn sat in the chair in silence, staring into space. Just then the broadcast outside crackled to life.

“Attention, attention. Any residents still holding unopened tins of meat should bring them to the Administration Hall to exchange for other supplies. The tins are out of date and unfit for consumption. Please go to the Administration Hall as soon as possible to exchange them.”

So the base had already realised the tins contained mutated nematodes.

But they did not dare tell the residents the truth. Instead, they were using the excuse that the tins had expired in order to collect every last one that had been sold.

Evelyn took out the base-issued tins she still had in storage. She intended to go and have a look at the situation for herself.

Naturally, if she was going out, Adrian was coming too.

“Evelyn, are you going to exchange supplies as well?” Clara asked. “How many tins have you got?”

Evelyn counted them. Including the ones distributed while she had been trapped in the Medical Division, she had thirty-five in total.

“We’ve only got ten,” Clara said. “Elliot wants to swap all of ours for compressed biscuits. What about you?”

“I haven’t decided yet. I’ll go over and see what they’re offering first.”

The four of them went downstairs and headed for the Administration Hall. Quite a few people were on their way there with tins in hand.

“Clara, how did you get back?” someone asked.

“The Medical Division’s been shut down by the base now. They’re using blood tests to check whether people have worms in their system. If someone tests positive, the patrol takes them away directly. I don’t know where. Dr. Hargreaves told us not to ask questions.”

Evelyn nodded. She already had a fair idea.

“Evelyn, I’m sorry about earlier,” Clara said quietly, meaning Sophie.

Evelyn shook her head.

“It wasn’t your fault. She really was my university classmate.”

Clara sighed. “Elliot and I were in a dreadful state before. We ran into some old classmates and lent them a hand. They ended up robbing us for it.”
