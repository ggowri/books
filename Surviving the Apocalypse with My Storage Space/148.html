Chapter 148 Toxic Haze 10

Evelyn had to summon every ounce of self-control she possessed to stop herself from bursting into laughter.

Eddie’s face was a masterpiece of shifting colours. Evelyn grabbed Adrian and made a swift escape, leaving Ray and Evan behind. They were not so lucky, and endured quite a long spell of threats from Eddie.

By the time she got home, Evelyn was so hungry her vision had started to blur. She hurriedly took out their food, and the two of them ate like people returned from the grave, clearing the table in no time at all.

“I really think the Safe Zone ought to set up some sort of residents’ committee. Get a few older women to mediate these things. Yesterday someone lost eggs, today someone’s short on coal, and then there’s Calvin, with women throwing themselves at him left and right. There may not be many people here, but there’s no shortage of trouble.”

“I know why Nathaniel wanted his sister to marry Calvin.”

Evelyn stared at Adrian. “You know? Why?”

“He thinks Calvin is the sort of man a woman could entrust her life to.”

Evelyn was speechless.

“So he wasn’t just trying to make trouble?”

“Probably not. I saw him coughing up blood. He may have thought he was dying, so that was the plan he came up with.”

“Was he actually dying, then?” Evelyn had not expected a twist like that. It was a wretched way of going about things, but looked at from that angle, Nathaniel perhaps was not completely beyond redemption.

“Probably not. I think he had stagnant blood in his system. Once he coughed it up, he felt better.” Adrian looked at Evelyn with complete seriousness. “Wasn’t Eddie saying he wanted a woman? Why did he refuse that one just now?”

“He wants a beautiful rich widow. Clearly, that woman didn’t fit the bill.”

Adrian nodded. “Eddie’s too fussy.”

Evelyn bit back a laugh. “So what you’re saying is, he should have accepted her and become a father on the spot?”

“Wouldn’t he want that?”

“Judging by his face, no, not especially. What about you? Would you?”

Adrian’s eyes widened. “Of course not. I only want to live with you. You’ve got food.”

Evelyn burst out laughing. “You’re brutally practical. So as long as there’s food, anyone could take you away?”

Adrian said nothing. He just looked at her in that long, quiet way of his.

The people who had joined the Safe Zone not long ago had settled in steadily enough. Old Walter, too, passed away one rainy day.

Mayor Whitmore personally took people to cremate the body, then let the ashes scatter on the wind in the direction of Seabourne.

Whenever Evelyn passed the house where the old people lived, she thought of him.

And there were many more like him in the Safe Zone.

What she had not expected was for Nathaniel to come asking her for treatment.

Remembering what Adrian had said, Evelyn studied his complexion carefully. There was nothing visibly wrong with him.

“Doctor Evelyn, I need you to tell me how much time I’ve got left.”

Since being put in his place by Nathan last time, Nathaniel had been rather more subdued.

“You mean you think you’re dying?”

Nathaniel glared at her. “Do you always have to be so blunt? Couldn’t you be a bit more tactful?”

Evelyn was unimpressed.

“What sort of phrase would you prefer? Passing on peacefully? Kicking the bucket? Breathing your last?”

“Enough, enough, stop talking. Just examine me and tell me whether I’m about to die.”

Evelyn gave a small cough, then took his pulse. “You’re perfectly healthy. Unless something unexpected happens, you should live a good while yet.”

Nathaniel did not believe her. “But I keep coughing up blood. Every morning, the moment I get up, I cough up blood.”

“Your eyes are bright, your colour is good, your pulse is normal, your circulation’s fine. If you don’t trust me, you’re welcome to lie down and let me cut you open to inspect your organs.”

Nathaniel looked dazed. “So you’re saying I’m not dying?”

“I’m not guaranteeing anything. If you walk out and fall over dead, that has nothing to do with my diagnosis.”

Nathaniel ground his teeth. “Everyone connected to Calvin is insufferable.”

He turned to leave, but Evelyn stopped him.

“What about my fee?”

“Put it on account.”

It was the first time anyone had ever tried to run up a tab with her, and Evelyn found it oddly novel. “I don’t do credit.”

“All right, all right, I’ll go and fetch it. You’re a menace.”

Just then Adrian came downstairs and fixed Nathaniel with a distinctly unpleasant look. Nathaniel knew very well Adrian was not someone to provoke, and fled at once.

“Adrian, let’s go and collect the eggs.”

Evelyn put away her medical case, but Adrian’s expression was still dark.

“Nathaniel doesn’t need to go on living. We can kill him tonight.”

Evelyn could only stare. Adrian really did think like a judge of the underworld.

“Let him bring the fee first. Come on, we’ll collect the eggs.”

Behind the villa, Adrian had built a shed especially for the chickens. The ground floor of the house was used for vegetables and medicinal herbs, while the second and third floors were for living. That was how most people in the Safe Zone arranged things now.

They had not collected eggs for several days. Evelyn took a basket, Adrian lifted Lark from his shoulder and set her down, and the two of them went into the shed together.

“The shed’s getting too crowded. We should probably slaughter half of them and free up some space.”

To Evelyn’s surprise, Adrian had turned out to be rather gifted at animal husbandry. What had once been a few dozen chicks had now grown into well over a hundred birds.

“All right. We’ll butcher the older ones first.”

“Let’s collect the eggs before anything else.” Evelyn beamed at the basket after basket of eggs. “They’re incredible. Two eggs a day.”

These days, almost everyone in the Safe Zone was growing vegetables or raising animals. After the toxic haze arrived, everyone had expected life to become even harder. Instead, somehow, things were slowly getting better.

Even the newcomers had exchanged for eggs. They were struggling more for now, but with the chicks growing as quickly as they did, they ought to have meat to eat within a month or so.

What the Safe Zone lacked most now was still water, clothes and blankets.

Apart from Evelyn and Adrian, nearly everyone’s clothes had been scavenged.

When the apartment block had collapsed earlier, many people had died. The clothes and quilts dug out from the wreckage had been fought over at once.

Evelyn and Adrian dressed poorly as well. Everyone else was in rags, so if the two of them turned up in clean clothes, they would only be asking for trouble.

The orphans, too, had been settled properly under Nathan’s care. By now, people inside the Safe Zone were more or less able to eat their fill.

By the time Evelyn and Adrian had finished collecting, they had gathered well over a thousand eggs. Both the flock and the number of eggs had exploded, and the shed could no longer hold them all, so the pair had no choice but to boil water and continue slaughtering chickens.

Nathaniel soon came back. Evelyn saw the two cabbages in his arms and took them without ceremony.

“Doctor Evelyn, one thing. Could you pass a message on to Calvin? Tell him to forget what happened before. My sister doesn’t like him, so he needn’t feel any burden over it.”

Evelyn nodded. “All right. I’ll tell him.”

“And don’t go changing my words. You tell him exactly what I said.”

Evelyn looked at him helplessly. “Don’t worry, I’ve an excellent memory. I’ll repeat it word for word. But if you don’t leave now, I think you may well die here.”

Nathaniel felt a chill crawl down his back. He turned and saw Adrian watching him expressionlessly.

“Wh what are you trying to do?”

“Can’t you tell?” Evelyn said, thoroughly enjoying herself. “He wants to kill you.”

Nathaniel gave a terrified yelp and bolted. “Help! Adrian’s going to murder me!”
