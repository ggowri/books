Chapter 75 Sudden Upheaval, Bear Attack, Earthquake

At two thirty in the morning, Calvin and the others finally returned. Evelyn noticed dirt smeared all over their clothes. Thankfully, no one was hurt.

"What happened?"

Eddie took a moment to catch his breath before lowering his voice. "We ran into wild buffalo on the way down. We fell trying to avoid them. Luckily, nothing serious happened."

Calvin poured a cup of water for Wendy and pulled her into his arms, soothing her for a long while.

"It's a shame the gun isn't silenced. There were four buffalo. Even if we managed to kill one, we wouldn't be able to carry it back. And if we failed and they chased us instead, that would've been the end."

So they had run into the remaining four buffalo. Evelyn briefly told them what had happened earlier.

"That Hayden already sent his men up to deal with the two black bears. They'll probably take the bears back to the base."

Eddie snorted. "So those people died for nothing?"

No one disagreed.

Around three in the morning, one of the black bears was carried down the mountain. It took eight men to haul it. Some people were drowning in grief over lost family members. Others were already hoping Hayden might share some of the bear meat.

The bear was carried in front of the RV. There was a refrigerator inside. The men quickly skinned and butchered it on the spot. Even the hide was cleaned thoroughly.

The four people who had been bitten to death by the bear received a portion of the organs. Everyone else received nothing.

The metallic scent of blood spread through the entire convoy. Some rejoiced. Others despaired.

Then another roar echoed from the dark mountains.

The ground began to tremble faintly beneath their feet.

A group of black bears burst out of the forest.

Perhaps they had come to avenge the one that was killed. They charged straight toward the source of the blood.

Chaos exploded through the convoy. People screamed and cried. Engines roared as drivers slammed into cars in front of and behind them. With over a thousand vehicles crammed together, no one could move forward or backward.

Evelyn's hand tightened around the steering wheel. A small gap opened ahead. She immediately started the engine and surged forward.

Everyone's faces were pale. No one had expected Shattered Ridge to hide so many black bears. Gunshots rang out ahead, but they failed to hit the charging bears. Instead, two fleeing civilians were struck and fell.

The mistake ignited fury. Faced with a bear attack, instead of uniting against the threat, those with weapons had harmed the unarmed.

"What do we do? The car can't get out. Should we abandon it and run?" someone shouted.

Evelyn reached into her bag and pulled out several small packets. Inside were medicines she had prepared, a hand drawn map, a lighter, night vision goggles, and a compass. She shoved one into each person's arms.

"If we get separated, head north. But stay in pairs if you can. Don't split up alone. Remember, the Drakepool Base is to the north. If you can't reach it, go southwest or northwest."

"Evie, I don't want to be separated from you." Ray's hands trembled violently. As he watched the black bears attacking people indiscriminately ahead, he looked on the verge of collapse.

"I don't want to be separated either," Evelyn said quickly. "I'm talking about if it happens. Don't panic. Stay calm. There's a lighter and night vision goggles in each pack. Don't lose them. Now everyone, put your backpacks on."

"Listen to Evelyn," Calvin added firmly. "Carry everything on you. Stay in pairs if you can. If you get separated, don't waste time searching. Leave immediately and head north."

He secured Wendy against his chest and slung the most essential supplies across his back.

Outside, vehicles were jammed together in a solid mass. People were being torn apart by charging black bears.

Evelyn did not hesitate. She shoved open the car door and jumped out.

The world suddenly spun.

Her face went pale.

"Run. It's an earthquake."

A violent tremor ripped through the ground.

For one irrational second, she wanted to slap Eddie for tempting fate earlier. Now the mountain truly was shaking.

Shattered Ridge was nothing but jagged peaks and steep cliffs. Strange boulders above, ravines below. No matter which way they turned, danger waited.

They had to force themselves to stay calm.

Evelyn dragged the others out of the vehicle.

"Run east. There's a small plain that way. Go!"

Around them, the survivors finally realized what was happening. Under the double terror of bear attacks and an earthquake, everyone erupted with desperate survival instinct. They ran like mad.

Evelyn stopped looking at anyone else.

She kicked aside a car door blocking her path. When she fell, she scrambled back up and kept running. The earth split open beneath her feet. A person beside her suddenly vanished into a crack.

Her ankle was seized.

For a split second, she was almost dragged down.

It was a child, maybe ten years old, hanging in the fissure with both legs dangling. The child's eyes were wide with terror.

Evelyn hesitated for one heartbeat.

Then she hauled the child up with a sharp pull. Their eyes met briefly, shock and disbelief flashing across the young face.

Behind them, massive boulders thundered down from the mountains, crushing people where they stood.

Evelyn turned and ran.

"Help me!"

"My leg, it hurts!"

"Dad! Mom!"

"Where's my son? Leo! Leo!"

The violent shaking knocked her down again and again. Her forehead split open on the rocks. Blood streamed down her face and into her mouth.

She was dizzy.

Just squat down for a second. Just sit for a second.

A strong man shoved her hard. He stepped on her back as he ran past. Pain exploded through her chest and abdomen. Her limbs trembled, weak and unsteady. She staggered upright and forced herself forward.

A rock came hurtling from behind and smashed into the back of her knee.

She screamed. When she fell, she got up again. And again.

One thought burned in her mind.

I can't die. I absolutely cannot die.

Ahead, a man tripped. Before he could rise, the crowd trampled him. He struggled to push himself up, only to be crushed by a rolling boulder moments later.

Thunder cracked across the sky.

Within seconds, rain poured down in sheets. Hailstones the size of eggs pelted the fleeing crowd. When one struck her head, her vision swam.

Sprig cried anxiously inside the canvas bag, but Evelyn had no time to soothe it. Her body was covered in wounds. People shoved and crushed one another. Someone swung a knife wildly to clear a path. The blade sliced her arm.

Instinct took over.

She drew her military knife and struck.

The man fell.

The surrounding people stared at her like she was insane.

Evelyn gripped the knife and kept moving, dragging her battered body forward.

Screams and sobs tore at her ears. The bears were still roaring behind them.

She caught sight of one of Hayden's bodyguards, his clothes soaked in blood as he sprinted past her.

A heartbeat later, a falling rock smashed him beyond recognition.

He had been less than two meters away.

Warm blood sprayed across her face.

Evelyn glanced back at Shattered Ridge.

The eighteen peaks loomed like eighteen monstrous jaws, opening wide, ready to swallow every last human alive.
