Chapter 20 Rainstorm, Venom Moths: Part 16

Piercing screams rose from the fourth floor and carried all the way up to the tenth.

Evelyn stood with her eyes closed, one hand gripping the doorknob.

She was like a lion poised to strike, waiting for the perfect moment.

"Don't kill me. I know who has food. I can tell you."

A pair of filthy, reeking hands clamped around Maya Wren's throat and lifted her into the air. The man's eyes roamed over her body without restraint as he burst into ugly laughter.

"Oh? Really? I'm not interested in food right now. I'm more interested in you."

Maya trembled violently. Her face turned red, her eyes rolled back as she struggled to breathe.

"I'm sick. I have AIDS," she blurted out in desperation. "But there are clean, pretty girls in this building. There's one in 902. Everyone in her family is old except her. And in 1001, there's another one. She's an orphan. She was the top SAT scorer of her year. She has lots of food. I saw her bring back so much stuff. They're both prettier than me.

"I can take you to them. I really am sick. Please, sir, spare me. I'm begging you."

"Bitch, trying to trick me?"

A slap cracked across her face.

Her head snapped to the side. Blood spilled from her mouth, and two teeth fell out.

Dizzy and in pain, Maya barely stayed conscious as the man tore open her clothes. A knife pressed against her throat.

Venom moths fluttered into the room.

Her entire body shook uncontrollably.

"Behave if you don't want to die."

"I really am sick," she sobbed. "My medical records are in the cabinet. You can check. I'm not lying."

Her father and younger brother had already had their throats cut. Her mother's screams came from the other room.

Her hands trembled as she forced herself to stay calm.

The man opened the cabinet and found a medical report inside. It was full of technical terms he couldn't understand, but he didn't doubt her anymore.

Staring at her delicate face, he cursed under his breath.

Then, without hesitation, he slashed the knife across her neck.

"So fast?" one of his companions laughed when he came out. "That didn't take long."

"Fast my ass. She was diseased trash."

He glanced toward the room where screams were still echoing, then pointed upward.

"Heard there are two more young, pretty ones in this building."

On the ninth floor, Claire's door was smashed open with an axe and kicked in.

Her grandparents were locked inside a bedroom. She and her parents stood in front, gripping kitchen knives as they faced the raiders.

Claire remembered Evelyn's words.

Only by staying alive could she protect her family.

Her hand was steady on the blade.

At that moment, she clearly realized that the world had changed.

And so had she.

"There really is a young one here," one man sneered. "Frank, kill these two old ones first. We'll take this girl back and have some fun later."

Claire's father stepped in front of Claire and her mother.

Hearing those words, he raised his knife and rushed forward.

A long blade flashed, then his arm was severed.

Before he could even scream, another strike pierced his abdomen.

Claire's eyes turned bloodshot.

She watched her parents fall one after another.

Her weak resistance was nothing in front of several powerful men.

Then, suddenly, she remembered what Evelyn had given her.

She grabbed a glass bottle from the table and hurled its contents straight at the man in front of her.

A scream tore through the air.

The liquid splashed over his body, and his flesh began to burn and rot almost instantly.

Seeing this, Claire threw another bottle at the next attacker.

He reacted quickly, but it still splattered across his face.

Within seconds, his entire face corroded and peeled away.

No wonder Evelyn had called this stuff a life-saver. But there wasn't much of it, and you had to land a fatal hit in one move. Claire wasn't the best shot. She missed their necks and hearts.

As the screams of the two men echoed through the room, Claire turned to look at her parents. Then she raised the knife. Stroke after stroke, she hacked at the two raiders until they stopped moving completely. Only then did she come back to herself. Fighting through the pain, she dragged her parents into another room. She picked up their long knives and left Unit 902.

The second Evelyn heard footsteps outside the door, she threw it open and fired the first crossbow bolt. It was laced with poison.

She didn't hear the cries anymore. Didn't register the screaming. It was like something had snapped. Her mind had room for only one thought—kill them. Kill them all.

Calvin moved with her in perfect sync. They swept the tenth floor. Then the eleventh. Then the twelfth. Not a single civilian was injured, but every raider in Block D was wiped out. However, many of the residents had already died.

They took a motorized raft and sped toward Block E. When they arrived and saw the carnage, Calvin nearly lost his footing. His hand trembled around the military knife. Evelyn had seen the cruelty of the apocalypse before. She didn't have time to be angry.

A poisoned bolt flew from her crossbow. A raider charged with a blade, but the shot hit him square in the chest. He tumbled down the stairs without a sound.

There had been eighteen of them in total. The raiders split into three groups and stormed three different buildings to kill and loot. By the time Evelyn and Calvin cleared the last of them, two full hours had passed.

Evelyn's protective suit was shredded. Blood soaked through to the assault jacket underneath. But she was unscathed, thanks to the crossbow and her distance in combat.

Calvin wasn't so lucky. There were cuts on his back, waist, stomach, and leg. By the time they made it back to Block D, he was barely conscious. Blood poured from his wounds. He was calling out for his daughter, choking on mouthfuls of blood.

Evelyn dragged him up to the twelfth floor, only to find all the survivors crammed together there.

No one thanked her. No one looked at her with gratitude—only fear. When they saw Calvin dying, they shrank back in horror.

Evelyn found Claire on the seventh floor, soaked in blood, hacking away at a raider's corpse with a knife. Evelyn yanked her up and gave her shoulder a shake. Together, they dragged the bodies to Unit 601 to feed the venom moths.

"You had weapons. Why didn't you show up sooner? If you had, my husband would still be alive!"

A middle-aged woman lunged at Evelyn. In an instant, the two of them became scapegoats. The heroes had become the accused.

Evelyn ignored her. She turned toward Jessica instead, her voice firm. "Calvin isn't doing well. We need to treat his wounds now."

Then she looked at Claire. "You should go home."

"Evelyn, go check on my son. He's still alive, I know he is. Please, save him."

"My son... those monsters... they weren't even human."

"Evelyn, why didn't you save my wife? You could've helped her."

"Calvin Shaw, how can you live with yourself? You're a cop. Why didn't you come sooner? Why didn't you save us?"

They shouted, accused, and cried.

Not one of them stepped up to defend Calvin. Not one of them mentioned his elderly parents or his little daughter. No one defended Evelyn either.

She was just a nineteen-year-old girl.

"Enough," Claire said coldly. Her gaze swept across the crowd. "If it weren't for Evelyn and Calvin, all of you would be dead right now. You did nothing. You knelt on the floor, begged for your lives, and watched your families die without lifting a finger. And you think you have the right to blame them?"

She stared them down, her words sharp and slow.

"I'm telling you now. The world has changed. Killing and looting are the new normal. You want protection? Keep dreaming."
