Chapter 44 Heatwave, Snake Infestation: Part 8

The next day, Evelyn continued clearing the snake corpses on the ninth floor. The door to 902 was wide open. From the doorway all the way inside, the floor was covered in dead snakes. Evelyn already knew the answer before stepping in. She quickened her pace and finished clearing the ninth floor, then entered 902. When she saw a skeleton lying in the bedroom, Evelyn closed her eyes.

As for Natalie and Margaret, their remains were found in the kitchen.

The stove, the range hood, the refrigerator, everywhere was draped with snake corpses. Just as Evelyn was about to leave, a hissing sound came from a wooden rack in the corner. She raised her shovel and slowly moved toward the sound.

She saw a small snake struggling to break out of an egg.

Evelyn let out a breath, but there was no hesitation in her movements. The shovel came down cleanly. The snake, not even fully hatched, was instantly cut in half.

So they were not completely gone yet.

To be safe, Evelyn decided to conduct a thorough sweep, leaving no corner unchecked.

It took her an entire day to barely finish clearing the ninth and eighth floors. Before calling it a day, she went up to the twelfth floor to check on Calvin. He still had not woken up.

She fed Wendy, checked the apartment inside and out once more, and only left after confirming there were no snakes.

Back home, Evelyn took a cold shower. After a full day of labor, she was starving. After dinner, she took out a watermelon and ate half of it with a spoon. Full and exhausted, she collapsed onto the bed. The moment she closed her eyes, she fell asleep.

The next day, Evelyn did not wake up until after nine.

She ate breakfast, put on her protective suit and water boots, and went to the twelfth floor first to check on Wendy and Calvin. Wendy was awake now. She sat motionless on the floor, clutching a filthy stuffed rabbit. Calvin's condition had improved slightly.

Evelyn fed Wendy, then found a clean set of clothes for her. While changing her, Evelyn realized Wendy had soiled herself. She hesitated for a few seconds, then clenched her teeth and cleaned her up.

Taking care of a child was really not easy.

Evelyn was doing everything she could to save Calvin. It was not just because he had helped her before. There was another reason. She hoped Wendy would still have family left. Even if only one person remained.

Back on the seventh floor, Evelyn continued clearing snake corpses with her shovel. As for the human remains, she left them undisturbed.

Cleaning an entire building was no easy task, especially in extreme heat, with the constant risk of heatstroke. She could only manage one or two floors a day. It took five full days to clear most of the building. The two floors below the third were buried under sand and silt. After clearing an exit, Evelyn built a simple makeshift door, then returned to her home on the tenth floor.

On the sixth day, Calvin finally woke up.

He was still very weak, but his mind was much clearer. The canned food Evelyn had brought was down to one can. There was only half a bottle of water left. All the glucose had already been fed to him.

When Calvin saw Wendy safe and sound, he was so emotional that he pulled her tightly into his arms. He knew it was Evelyn who had saved them. Struggling to sit up, he tried to kneel to her. Evelyn hurriedly stopped him.

"Your body is still very weak. It's best if you stay lying down."

"Evie, thank you for saving me and Wendy, and for bringing us food. It hasn't been easy for you either. I will never forget this."

"If Wendy hadn't made some noise, I might not have come in to check," Evelyn said calmly.

Calvin's eyes reddened. "It's my fault. I was useless and let Wendy suffer. By the way… my parents, and my wife. Their bodies?"

"They're still in that bedroom."

Calvin staggered to his feet at once. He pushed open the bedroom door, and when he saw the bodies of his parents and his wife, he collapsed to his knees and broke down, sobbing.

Evelyn did not know what they had endured, and she did not want to know.

After leaving the Shaw family's apartment, Evelyn stood in the stairwell. Every person in this building, every face, flickered through her mind like passing scenery.

Outside, the snake corpses had completely rotted. Red headed blowflies swarmed over the decaying flesh, their buzzing drilling straight into her ears.

Evelyn tore down the red brick wall on the balcony, storing the bricks back in her space and throwing the chunks of cement out the window. She repositioned the solar panels and the outdoor AC unit back onto the balcony.

The temperature kept climbing. At forty six degrees, Evelyn felt like the ground itself was smoking. Fans were no longer enough. She had no choice but to turn on the generator and run the air conditioner.

Calvin had mostly recovered and went out to look for food. The water downstairs had dried up, and the mud flats had cracked under the relentless heat. He checked several neighboring buildings and actually managed to bring back some supplies.

To thank Evelyn, he gave her half of everything he found.

"There should still be survivors in the neighboring building," he said.

Evelyn glanced at him but did not respond.

"I didn't get too close," Calvin continued. "I heard some… bad sounds. Evelyn, we need to stay alert. You really shouldn't go out."

Evelyn nodded. She already had a guess forming in her mind.

They returned to their own apartments. Evelyn went to the balcony, took out her binoculars, and began observing the nearby buildings.

Sure enough, on the balcony of Block E, eleventh floor, she spotted a man. He even had a cigarette between his fingers. He looked like he was living quite comfortably.

That night, a sudden shout came from downstairs. Evelyn rolled out of bed and rushed to the balcony. She saw a snake suddenly dart out and bite a man. The group below was beating the snake to death, but the bitten man was already beyond saving.

Evelyn grabbed her crossbow and left her apartment. In the stairwell, she ran into Calvin. The two of them went downstairs together, one in front, one behind.

There were five people in total that night, including the man who had been bitten to death.

After killing the snake, they moved quietly toward Block D. Just as they were about to push open the security door, a crossbow bolt shot out of the darkness and pierced straight through the chest of the man in front.

Evelyn raised an eyebrow in satisfaction. Her aim was getting better and better.

"Who's there? Who's hiding? Come out," someone shouted.

When the first man fell, the three behind him panicked, looking around wildly. The night was pitch black. They were exposed, while Evelyn and Calvin were hidden.

The outcome was already decided.

Evelyn fired three more bolts in quick succession. The last one went a little off, striking a man in the abdomen. Evelyn and Calvin pushed the door open and stepped out. She pulled her bolt free and kicked the only survivor hard.

"From that far away, I could already smell the human stench on you, you animal."

The man doubled over, wailing. Calvin stepped forward and dragged him back into Block D. They did not bother with the other bodies.

"Spare me. Please. I didn't do anything. I was forced," the man begged.

Evelyn slapped him hard across the face. His head snapped to the side, several teeth flying out of his mouth.

Calvin looked at Evelyn, his eyes full of awe.

"Eating people was forced too? Talk. How many of you are there? And the people you took… how many are still alive?"
