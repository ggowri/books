Chapter 94 Can I Inherit Your Estate?

Evelyn curled up on the bed, the twisting pain in her abdomen forcing a groan from her throat. It felt like a knife was stabbing her over and over again.

She clenched her teeth and let the cold sweat roll down her forehead. After an entire day and night of agony, she honestly thought this might be the end for her.

When the pain finally faded, Evelyn stared at her reflection in the mirror. Her complexion looked flushed and healthy, like she had just downed some miracle tonic. She could not help but tap her head in disbelief.

After drinking that bowl of biochemical weapon, even though it had hurt for a full day and night, she actually felt a lot better now.

Her abdomen no longer hurt at all, and the color had returned to her face.

"So you're still alive." The moment Evelyn stepped out of the bedroom, 0018 walked out of the kitchen, looking mildly surprised.

Was that even something a person would say?

The instant Sprig and Fallon saw her, they rushed over and rubbed against her legs. Evelyn picked them up.

"Sprig, Fallon, did someone abuse you while I was gone?"

"Are you talking about me?" The someone in question had absolutely no self awareness, yet still asked knowingly.

"What do you think?"

"If you died, could I inherit your estate?"

Evelyn took a deep breath, forcing herself to calm down. There was no need to argue with an idiot.

"Unfortunately for you, I can't die. I'm doing just fine now."

He nodded. "I can see that. So you should thank me."

"I'm hungry." Evelyn ignored his comment.

"I didn't make any food for you." He sat down and began enjoying his lunch. Evelyn glanced at the stir fried meat he had made. It was charred black and gave off a burnt smell. The fact that he could eat it only proved he was not human.

"I wouldn't eat your food even if you begged me to."

Evelyn went into the bathroom to boil some water. She needed a shower. Her body felt awful.

Looking at the clothes 0018 was wearing, she realized he had not changed in a very long time. After returning to the bedroom, she pulled out a sack of clothes from her storage space. One thing was one thing. This counted as repayment for that bowl of blood, even though the thought of it still made her a little nauseous.

"For you."

He looked at her in confusion, his mismatched eyes making him seem strangely blank.

"Clothes. Change into them."

"Is this my inheritance?" He immediately opened the sack to check. Evelyn smiled.

“Think of it as your lord and savior being generous.”

He stared at Evelyn thoughtfully. “Then I’d like more generosity.”

Evelyn said nothing.

"The wolves didn't come?"

"They did." He paused, then added seriously, "A lot of them. The meat is outside. Go collect it."

Evelyn opened the door and saw piles of meat buried in the snow. The poles nearby were covered with wolf pelts.

How brutal.

She did not bother hiding anything from him now. Their secrets were already out in the open. There was no need to pretend anymore, especially since she could not beat him in a fight.

Evelyn counted them. Twelve wolves. It looked like he had wiped out every wolf on Shattered Ridge.

After washing up, Evelyn made herself a bowl of egg noodles. The moment it was done, he took it away.

"That's not for you."

"You got the meat. I get this." He sounded completely justified, with no sense of shame at all. He did not even let the scalding hot broth stop him, finishing everything in a few quick bites.

"You're a bandit."

"Yeah. Want to fight?" After changing into a new set of clothes, the white sweater gave him a youthful look. He looked perfectly human, yet acted nothing like one.

Evelyn had no interest in arguing with a dog, so she made another bowl.

"I saw the karst cave you mentioned."

She turned to look at him, not understanding what he meant.

"Do you want to go see it?"

Evelyn did not hesitate. "No."

"Fine. I'll go by myself."

That afternoon, he really did leave. By nightfall, he still had not returned. Evelyn fed Sprig and Fallon, then went back to her room and went to sleep. Whether he came back or not had nothing to do with her.

The next morning, the moment she stepped out of her room, she saw 0018 sitting by the fireplace. On the table beside him was a lump of yellow metal.

Evelyn picked it up and examined it. "Is this gold?"

"Do you want it?"

It felt like a trap. She did not answer.

"I found a lot. If you want it, I can take you to get more."

"What's the condition?"

"I want six dishes every meal."

Evelyn almost smashed the gold over his head.

"What a shame. I don't want it. Not even a little."

She took out a ball of yarn and prepared to crochet another sweater.

He said nothing else. He went back to flipping through the book from before. When the firewood in the fireplace burned low, he added a couple of logs.

Hearing movement outside, he stood and went out.

A while later, he came back carrying a pheasant.

"You set traps out there?"

He nodded.

"We already have enough meat."

He shook his head. "Not enough. I eat a lot."

Evelyn did not argue. It was not like she had to do the work. He could do whatever he wanted.

"I'm making a new bed."

"Why?" She glanced at the folding cot. It was not broken.

"It's too short."

That was when she realized. It was not the bed. It was him.

She handed him a saw, a planer, and some nails. He went back outside to get to work.

By evening, his new bed was finished. It looked surprisingly decent. He had even built a wardrobe beside it and neatly folded that whole sack of clothes inside.

"Do you still need to drink blood?" he suddenly asked before bed.

"No." Evelyn stared at him warily, afraid he might try to force more down her throat.

"I forgot to ask. What's your name?"

She crossed her arms and looked down at him. "You first. What's your real name?"

He fell silent. So did she.

Maybe his real name was his biggest secret.

Evelyn was not that interested. She yawned and turned to leave.

"My name is Rowan Kane."

She turned back to look at him. From a few steps away, those emotionless mismatched eyes were almost… beautiful.

"I'm Evelyn Foster."

"This is for you." He handed her the piece of gold. She took it.

"Thank you. But you're still not getting six dishes."

She had not expected to make peace with 0018. No, Rowan. She had not expected to reach any kind of understanding with someone like him.

It counted as making peace. Probably.

Outside, the blizzard still howled and swept across the world. Evelyn had already fallen asleep. Rowan stood by the window, watching the snow for a long time. Then he picked up a knife, sliced open his left arm, and removed a tiny chip.

From that moment on, there was only Rowan Kane in this world. 0018 no longer existed.

He walked to the door of Evelyn's bedroom and listened to her steady breathing inside. Then he opened the cabin door and stepped out into the night.

Evelyn opened her eyes. She picked up her pocket watch and checked the time. It was one in the morning.

Sprig and Fallon were pressed together, fast asleep.

What was he doing out there?

She was curious, but not stupid enough to follow. If Rowan caught her, she would not gain anything from it.

Besides, it was freezing outside. She had no intention of suffering through that.

January was almost over. When spring came, the snow would melt. Wouldn't it?
