Chapter 58

Lila took the rice out of the pot and served herself a bowl.

When she lifted the lid of the clay pot, white steam rose at once, carrying the rich savoury aroma of braised pork belly.

She picked out a slightly caramelised piece and put it into her mouth.

It was so hot she had to suck in a breath, but she did not spit it out.

Salty, fragrant, faintly sweet, and melting the moment it touched her tongue.

Lila sighed with satisfaction. Honestly, she was a little genius in the kitchen.

The pork skin had been braised until it was almost translucent. Lila placed a piece of pork belly on top of the rice and pressed it gently with her chopsticks. It was so soft it nearly melted into the grains.

It was simply irresistible.

Together with a mouthful of rice, Lila put the whole large piece of pork belly into her mouth.

The rice seemed to bring out the fragrance of the pork even more. That bite was pure satisfaction.

After eating half a bowl of rice, Lila touched the steamed egg custard.

It was no longer hot at all, just warm.

She found a clean shallow plate in the Vault, added half a spoonful of rice, then scooped in two spoonfuls of the unsalted egg custard and mixed it carefully. She placed the plate at the edge of the table.

Into the remaining egg custard, she added two spoonfuls of braised pork sauce and mixed it with rice for herself.

The sauce from the braised pork belly was even richer and more fragrant than soy sauce. Paired with the egg custard, it was delicious.

Once she had eaten her fill, she put the plate of egg custard rice on the floor.

The two puppies had never eaten proper food before, so they had no idea that food placed on the floor meant they were supposed to come over and eat.

Lila got up from her chair, picked them up specially, and placed them beside the plate.

The moment they smelled the egg custard, they plunged straight in.

Their tiny mouths opened surprisingly wide as they ate mouthful after mouthful of rice mixed with custard.

Puppies made little smacking noises when they ate, but it was not unpleasant at all. The soft little sounds only made them seem even cuter.

"Quick interview with you two. What are your thoughts on your first ever meal?"

Lila held up her phone to record them and spoke to the two puppies.

The two little things, of course, understood none of it. Their entire minds were occupied by one thought. This was far better than goat's milk.

Patches became so excited while eating that both front paws ended up in the plate, little feet covered in grains of rice.

"Right, right. I've received your answer. It's the finest thing in the world."

With that, Lila quickly stopped recording and pulled Patches's paws out of the plate.

Before long, the two little ones had finished the entire plate of egg custard rice.

Lila pulled a wet wipe from the dining table and cleaned their messy little mouths, along with Patches's two rice-covered paws.

Only then did she stand up and clear the bowls and chopsticks.

As Lila washed up, she thought that life now was actually rather good.

When the Collapse arrived, stockpiling and hiding away had been something she had done out of necessity. But now that she was truly living like this, she suddenly felt astonishingly free.

Heartless as it sounded, she rather liked the Long Freeze.

She had just finished washing the dishes when she heard the wind howling outside the kitchen's rear window. It seemed the wind had picked up.

Lila dried her hands and pressed two more lumps of coal into the kitchen range.

Coal burned slowly. Once placed in the range, it would keep the heated hearth bench warm through the night.

Now that the puppies had started eating proper food, they would start relieving themselves too. She needed to solve the issue of their toilet training.

During those last frantic days of stockpiling, she had not bought a litter tray or any litter.

So she would have to make one herself.

Lila had stored plenty of shoes in the Vault, and almost all of them came with shoeboxes.

She chose a shallower shoebox, climbed down into the cellar, and once again entered Rock Hollow.

Using a shovel, she dug up some loose soil and filled half the shoebox, then placed it into the Vault.

After returning upstairs, she set the shoebox of soil beside the dog bed.

She was going to begin training the two puppies to use the toilet.

While they were still small, they could use the shoebox. Once they were bigger, she could take them outside.

Lila took a bit of tissue she had used to wipe up puppy urine and placed it in the shoebox. Puppies followed scent, and they would learn to relieve themselves in a fixed spot based on the smell.

Snowy and Patches had just eaten their egg custard rice, and now they were lying in the dog bed with round, bulging bellies, sound asleep.

Lila was not worried about disturbing them. She simply lifted both little ones up and placed them in the shoebox.

The two puppies opened their eyes drowsily. When they saw the soft loose soil beneath their paws, they grew curious and began pawing at it.

As they played, they caught the scent on the tissue. Then, as naturally as could be, they relieved themselves in the shoebox.

"Oh, you clever little things!"

Lila was genuinely surprised. She had not even started trying yet, and none of the tutorials she had found had been needed.

After using the shoebox, the two puppies climbed out by themselves. Snowy even tumbled over, then staggered back to the dog bed.

Once there, they lay on their backs again with their round bellies up and fell asleep.

The two puppies were surprisingly well behaved. For Lila, they were very little trouble.

After all that fuss, Lila was thoroughly tired.

It was time for her daily nap.

Although it was already four in the afternoon, that did not stop it from being a nap.

Lila curled up on the sofa in her usual position and pulled the blanket over herself.

Listening to the crackle of the fire in the inglenook fireplace mingling with the howl of wind outside the window, she gradually fell asleep.

At five in the afternoon, it was time for food distribution in the estate again.

Anna was still nursing her anger and refused to go collect the meal.

But Maggie was hungry.

She had not eaten a single grain of rice since the night before. If she had to give up this evening's meal to Anna as well, she truly felt she might starve.

"Anna, don't lower yourself to their level. They're beneath you. How could they compare with my little princess? But we still have to eat. If you don't go and collect your food, you're letting them win!"

Maggie had nearly worn her mouth out trying to persuade Anna to go. Food was about to be served, and if they did not leave soon, it would be too late.

"I'm not going! Who cares about their food? Haven't we got instant noodles? Besides, we haven't finished that old man's things yet."

Anna folded her arms and refused to move no matter what Maggie said.

Edward came over from inside with his bowl and kicked Anna in the back.

"How did I end up with such a fool for a daughter? You don't want to eat, fine. What about the rest of us? Bring the food back. If you don't want it, we'll share it."

His voice was full of anger.

Anna, who had just been kicked, felt utterly wronged. Tears gathered in her eyes. She straightened stubbornly, turned her head and said bitterly, "Dad! How can you treat me like this? I was humiliated at lunch and you didn't even care. Now you want me to lose face over half a bowl of watery porridge?"

"You're going whether you like it or not. Daniel, drag her there."

Edward glared fiercely at Anna and left first.

Daniel knew exactly who he should listen to at a time like this. Without a word, he grabbed Anna's arm hard and pulled her out.

"What are you doing? I'm not going!"

Anna struggled with all her strength, but Daniel still dragged her outside.

This time, Maggie did not stop him. Instead, she quietly let out a breath of relief.

At least tonight, she would finally get to eat.

Daniel dragged Anna to the doorway, where they happened to run into Sophie coming out of the room next door.
