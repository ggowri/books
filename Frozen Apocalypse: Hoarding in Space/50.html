Chapter 49

Early in the morning, the thing everyone in the estate cared about most was whether the heating had come back.

Anyone with a thermometer at home went to check it the moment they woke up.

The result, clearly, was disappointing.

The indoor temperature was between three and five degrees, several degrees lower than yesterday's ten.

Households with air conditioning switched it on again. Those without it crawled back under their quilts after breakfast and used electric blankets to keep warm.

As for Maggie, Edward and the others, they had neither air conditioning nor electric blankets. All they could do was wrap themselves in thick quilts on the sofa and not dare move.

The residents' group chat was full of complaints.

"What's going on? Is it still not fixed? Why is it getting colder indoors?"

"I called the district-heating supplier earlier. They said several more pipes burst, so our side can't get enough heat. Apparently we're lucky, though. In some places, the radiators have gone completely cold, and the indoor temperature is minus forty."

Everyone who saw that message shivered in fear. They could not even imagine how terrifying it would be to have a room at minus forty.

Anna grew more and more irritated as she read. She threw her phone onto the sofa and simply stopped looking.

She buried her head under the quilt to warm up. Once her aching cheeks and nose felt a little better, she lifted her head again.

Then she suddenly said, "If Lila hasn't come home, where could she be hiding?"

The question stunned everyone.

Exactly.

With temperatures plunging across the world, and hotels no longer taking guests, where could Lila have gone?

"I'll ask her again." Maggie picked up her phone and sent Lila a message.

"Lila, why have you disappeared? Where on earth have you gone? We're all so worried about you."

Maggie put on a convincing act, her tone full of concern.

Lila was reading through messages on online forums, where all sorts of experienced posters were sharing survival advice for the Long Freeze.

When Maggie's message suddenly came through, Lila read it, then opened the monitoring app.

In the camera feed, after sending the message, Maggie kept staring at her phone.

"That ungrateful girl. She really isn't replying at all."

Maggie spoke angrily.

Anna clenched her teeth so tightly that each word seemed to come out one by one.

"If I find out she's hiding somewhere warm, eating and drinking well, I won't let her get away with it."

Edward, who had been silent all this time, suddenly spoke.

"Don't you think Lila has changed? She used to treat us like family. She gave us everything she had. When did she become like this?"

"I think she planned it from the moment she told us to go on holiday. She said she'd reimburse us, but we haven't seen a penny, and now she's disappeared too." The more Anna thought about it, the more wrong it felt.

"Only just realised something was wrong? Too late."

Lila took a packet of crispy noodle snacks from the snack cabinet and crunched through them loudly, feeling thoroughly pleased.

"We still need to keep contacting Lila. It would be best if we could coax information out of her and find out where she's staying. Then we can make her take us in, and we won't have to suffer here any more."

Edward understood Lila. She valued family more than anything. As long as they softened their tone and coaxed her a little, there should still be a chance.

"All right. I'll message her whenever I have time."

Maggie nodded firmly, placing her last hope on Lila again.

Lila sighed softly, partly at how foolish these people were, and partly in regret for the person she had been in her previous life.

The dough had risen well.

The pale, tender mass had expanded several times over and carried the distinct warm scent of fermented dough.

Lila put on her padded jacket and carried the basin into the kitchen.

She scrubbed the large iron pot clean, filled it with water, then set the steamer basket on top.

She added firewood to the kitchen range and lit it, keeping the water in the big pot heating steadily.

Then she restarted the drama she had been watching. After washing her hands, Lila began making the buns.

She kneaded the dough into a smooth ball, placed it on the worktop, cut it into even portions, then rolled each one into a thick wrapper.

Using a spoon, she scooped a large heap of pure meat filling and placed it in the centre of the wrapper.

The mound of filling was so large that if she tried to wrap it directly, the dough would barely close around it.

Lila pinched up one edge of the wrapper, gently pulled it outwards and folded it in. After several pulls and pinches, a beautiful bun with thin skin and plenty of filling appeared in her palm.

By the time she had used up the whole basin of meat filling, the water in the pot had just come to the boil.

Lila placed the buns one by one into the steamer basket. They filled one large basket exactly.

She put on the lid and let the pork buns steam over high heat. Then she turned round and began wrapping the vegetable buns.

By the time all the buns were finished, the first batch of large pork buns was ready.

The moment she lifted the lid, fragrant steam filled the entire kitchen.

Lila waited for the white vapour to disperse before using cloths to lift out the whole steamer basket.

The big buns were pale, plump and beautifully pleated.

A few had a little meat juice seeping from the sides, which only made them look even more tempting.

She put on heatproof gloves and carefully moved the buns onto plates.

There were twenty-eight meat buns, each as big as a fist.

Lila kept two that had leaked a little juice and stored the rest in the Vault.

When she was too lazy to make breakfast in future, she could simply take them out and eat them.

Next, she placed the remaining vegetable buns into the steamer basket.

After putting the lid back on, Lila did not stay in the kitchen to wait. Instead, she carried her two pork buns back to the east parlour.

The buns were still very hot, but she could hardly wait.

She tore open a small gap where the meat juice had seeped out. Inside was a large, tempting ball of meat, with glossy juice gathered at the bottom.

It smelled incredible.

Lila swallowed and suddenly wanted to record the moment.

She took out her phone and snapped a photo of the intact bun beside the opened one.

The photo showed both the bun's lovely shape and the generous meat filling inside. Just looking at it was enough to make someone hungry.

Lila put her phone away, blew on the opened bun, and cautiously took a bite.

The meat filling had formed into a tender, juicy centre. The spices and pork fragrance had melded together, rich and savoury with every chew.

Paired with the soft, fluffy bun skin, it was indescribably delicious.

After working on them for so long, finally getting that first bite brought her an unprecedented sense of satisfaction.
