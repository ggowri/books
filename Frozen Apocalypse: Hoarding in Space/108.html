Chapter 107

Lila had no intention of interrupting the interview process, so she quietly leaned against the wall in the corner and waited.

The turnout was far larger than anyone had expected.

There was no way they could continue interviewing people on the spot.

William rose from his chair.

"Ryan, Evan, register everyone who actually meets the requirements. Once you've finished, bring the applications to my office. Captain Walker and I will review them this afternoon before scheduling interviews."

Without another word, he left the increasingly chaotic cafeteria in the hands of his two assistants.

Then he turned to Captain Walker.

"You should get some rest as well. We'll sit down this afternoon and go through everything properly."

Although William was considerably older, he never pulled rank because of his age.

Despite Captain Walker being much younger, he always treated him as an equal.

"Sounds good."

Walker rubbed the bridge of his nose.

The constant noise had already given him a headache.

He stood, ready to leave.

"Captain Walker! Captain Walker!"

Annabel finally seized her chance.

"My name's Annabel Spencer. I have a postgraduate degree from one of the country's top universities. I'm applying for the Legal Officer position. Could you interview me first?"

She had been waiting for him all morning.

Now he was about to leave.

She hurriedly delivered the speech she'd prepared.

It didn't matter whether any of it could be verified.

The old world was gone.

No one could check academic records anymore.

If she claimed she'd graduated from an elite university, then as far as anyone knew, she had.

"Please register over there. Qualified applicants will be interviewed this afternoon."

Walker barely slowed his pace.

He had already said he was leaving, yet she still insisted on blocking his path.

His patience was beginning to wear thin.

"Oh, come on. It'll only take a minute."

She flashed what she believed was an irresistible smile.

"I'm smart, I'm great with people, and if you let me skip the line..."

She lowered her voice into an overly sweet whisper.

"...I'll make it worth your while."

She batted her eyelashes.

"I promise I can be... very helpful."

Walker's lips pressed into a thin line.

Every word made him more uncomfortable.

Could this woman not speak like a normal person?

"What did you say your name was?"

"My name's Annabel."

She smiled even wider.

He was asking her name.

That had to mean he'd taken the bait.

Her voice became even softer.

Walker looked past her.

"Ryan."

"Yes, Captain?"

"Remove Annabel Spencer from the Legal Officer candidate list."

Ryan blinked.

"...Understood."

Annabel froze.

Then she laughed as if it were all part of some playful joke.

"Oh, you're terrible."

She giggled.

"You almost scared me."

Walker didn't respond.

He simply frowned and walked away.

Annabel stood there for a second before letting out an irritated huff.

"Fine."

"If legal's off the table, I'll apply for everything else."

She remained convinced Walker had only been teasing her.

Surely he'd come looking for her tonight.

Meanwhile, there was no reason not to cast a wider net.

If she landed several jobs at once, she'd be swimming in Survival Credits.

The entire Spencer family was busy filling out application forms, squeezed into the middle of the crowd.

Had they stepped away for even a moment, they would have come face to face with the person they'd been obsessing over.

Lila.

Walker had barely reached the cafeteria entrance when a clear voice stopped him.

"Captain Walker."

He turned.

It was the young woman from the mountain house.

They had only exchanged a few words earlier.

His eyes settled briefly on her fair, composed face.

Her skin was unusually healthy.

Her equipment was first rate.

Nothing about her suggested someone who had spent years living as a rural farmer.

She looked more like someone raised comfortably in the city.

Yet beneath that calm appearance was unmistakable determination.

And confidence.

The kind that came from someone who always had a plan.

"What is it?"

"I'd like to discuss a trade."

Lila stepped closer and lowered her voice.

Even though she was fairly tall, Walker still stood nearly a head above her.

As she moved closer, the scent of shampoo drifted toward him.

Fresh blueberries.

His throat tightened unconsciously.

It had been so long since he'd even thought about blueberries.

"What kind of trade?"

Realising he'd been distracted for a split second, he quickly refocused.

"Could we talk somewhere private?"

"There are too many people here."

Walker nodded.

"Come with me."

He headed outside without another question.

Lila immediately followed.

The moment they stepped outdoors, she pulled her hood and helmet back into place.

Together they walked toward the Military Quarter.

Soldiers constantly moved in and out of the barracks.

Seeing Walker accompanied by someone dressed in top quality survival gear attracted more than a few curious glances.

Neither of them paid any attention.

Walker led her to the room at the very end of the corridor.

His quarters mirrored William's office almost exactly.

The only difference was that one belonged to the Military Quarter while the other sat inside the Administrative Quarter, separated by a single wall.

"Have a seat."

The room was simple but functional.

Lila didn't waste time looking around.

She sat neatly on the sofa.

Walker poured her a mug of hot water before sitting opposite her.

"So."

"What kind of trade are we talking about?"

Lila nodded.

"I saw you're recruiting people with crop cultivation experience."

"What I actually wanted to ask is whether the refuge is short on growing equipment."

"I happen to have several unused Full Spectrum Grow Lights."

She paused.

"If I donate them, could I earn a permanent place on your recovery missions into the city?"

Then she added,

"A refuge this size will eventually need to grow its own food."

"I'm guessing equipment like this would help."

Walker's eyes lit up.

During the Long Freeze, sunlight was painfully scarce.

The sky stayed dim almost every day.

Plants barely grew.

The refuge had only recently been established.

Its agricultural programme was practically nonexistent.

They hadn't even managed to build a proper Indoor Grow Room yet.

He had already submitted multiple requests for cultivation equipment.

Every request had come back with the same answer.

Other refuges had already claimed the available stock.

Nothing more could be issued for the foreseeable future.

That was exactly why he'd begun recruiting Crop Cultivation Biologists.

If they couldn't improve the environment, perhaps skilled people could compensate.

If the woman sitting across from him truly possessed professional grow lights...

It would be a significant boost to the entire project.

"How many lights are you willing to contribute?"

Lila quickly did the maths.

She had originally purchased two complete lighting systems.

Eight lamps per set.

Sixteen in total.

Four were already installed for her own crops.

That left twelve in storage.

She couldn't hand them all over.

She still needed replacements.

"I can spare four."

That was enough to illuminate an entire small grow room.

Walker nodded.

"Fair enough."

"What do you want in return?"

Lila smiled.

"Unlimited opportunities."

"Whenever I want to join one of your recovery missions into the city, I get a seat."

She knew it was an ambitious request.

Maybe even shameless.

But if you never asked, the answer was always no.

Walker chuckled quietly.

The young woman certainly knew how to negotiate.

Still...

Every recovery convoy already took civilians from the refuge.

Saving one seat for her wasn't exactly impossible.

On the other hand, Captain Walker had never been the type to accept an opening offer without bargaining.
