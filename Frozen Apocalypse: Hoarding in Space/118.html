Chapter 117

A knock sounded at the door.

Since Lila was closest, she walked over and opened it.

The young soldier standing outside froze for a moment.

A woman?

Inside the captain's quarters?

And not just any woman.

She had fair skin, delicate features, and looked nothing like anyone he remembered seeing around the refuge.

"What is it?"

Ethan's voice came from inside, snapping the young soldier out of his daze.

"Oh. Right."

"Captain Walker, is it time to move out?"

"Should we assemble the team now?"

Ethan tipped back the last of his porridge, swallowed, and answered in his usual crisp military tone.

The warmth he'd shown while speaking with Lila vanished instantly.

"Form up."

"We're leaving."

"Yes, sir."

The soldier spun around and hurried away.

Moments later, a whistle shrieked through the refuge.

"Assembly!"

"City recovery team, assemble!"

"So... should we head over too?"

Lila asked.

"Let's go."

Ethan finished putting on his outer gear.

The two of them stepped outside together.

Naturally, Lila walked a few paces behind him.

When they reached the assembly yard, everyone was already there.

Besides the thirty members selected for the mission, a large crowd of survivors had gathered nearby to watch.

Some were simply curious about how the expedition would leave.

Others wanted to memorise the faces of those heading into the city.

Making friends with supply teams could pay off later.

Anna stood among the spectators.

Hidden beneath her thick hat and scarf, envy and resentment burned behind her bloodshot eyes as she stared at the gathering team.

Then Ethan appeared.

And walking behind him...

Was a woman.

She wore professional grade winter gear.

A charcoal grey expedition suit paired with reindeer hide boots that reached above her knees.

It looked warm.

Expensive.

Elegant.

Anna clenched both fists.

Why did that woman get to dress like that?

Who was she?

What gave her the right to stand beside Ethan?

Anna strained to see her face.

But the woman wore a thick hood, a specialised extreme cold face mask, and protective goggles.

Only her eyes were visible.

Even those were partly hidden behind the lenses.

Still...

Those eyes looked strangely familiar.

Holding the roster, Ethan personally checked attendance one final time.

Satisfied everyone was present, he gave the order to depart.

Two military lorries waited outside.

The expedition members climbed into the covered cargo beds one by one.

Blankets had been spread across the floor inside to help keep everyone warm during the journey.

Lila watched everyone climb aboard and started walking toward the cargo bed herself.

"Miss Spencer."

"Come with me."

Ethan stopped her.

Then he turned and climbed into the cab.

Besides the driver, the front compartment seated four more people.

Lila didn't hesitate.

She followed him straight inside.

Who would willingly sit freezing in the back when there was a warm cab available?

The step into the lorry sat surprisingly high.

Lila practically had to haul herself up with all her strength.

Watching Ethan climb in so effortlessly earlier had given her the completely wrong impression.

She had seriously underestimated that step.

Leo and Sophie naturally rode in the cab as well.

As the team's doctor and Legal Officer, both needed to remain close to Ethan.

The four of them shared the same vehicle.

From the crowd, Anna watched every second.

She stared at Lila climbing into the cab wearing premium winter gear and carrying a brand new backpack.

That wasn't ordinary treatment.

Only people with real status rode in the front.

How could someone without connections possibly receive that privilege?

There was only one explanation.

That shameless woman had seduced Ethan.

Anna stamped her foot furiously.

He'd been interested in her first.

Now this woman had stolen all of his attention.

That seat should have belonged to her.

The chance to earn huge amounts of Survival Credits in the city should have belonged to her too.

If hatred alone could kill, Lila would have died ten thousand times beneath Anna's glare.

Lila settled into her seat.

Leo and Sophie climbed in behind her.

Unable to see Lila anymore, Anna redirected all her resentment toward Sophie.

That woman was no better.

She was trying to steal Ethan too.

"Just wait."

"Captain Walker's going to be mine one day."

"You pathetic women can all drop dead."

She glared so fiercely at the front of the first lorry that it seemed she wanted to burn a hole straight through it.

"Anna."

"The woman who got into Captain Walker's truck..."

"Isn't she the same girl wearing the white coat on supply distribution day?"

Maggie thought she looked vaguely familiar.

She remembered seeing someone dressed differently from everyone else during the refuge's first meeting.

Realisation flashed across Anna's face.

No wonder this mysterious woman had appeared out of nowhere.

It really was the same person.

She had stood out back then too.

So she really was wealthy enough to own more than one expensive set of extreme cold gear.

In that case...

Maybe Ethan wasn't actually interested in her.

Maybe he was simply using her.

Surely she was still the one he truly liked.

"She doesn't even live in the refuge."

"She's from one of the villages nearby."

A man standing beside them casually spoke up.

He had personally watched Lila walk down from the mountains that morning.

"What?"

"She's not even a refuge resident?"

"Then why is she involved?"

"What gives her the right to join the city expedition?"

Anna's frustration boiled over again.

So many people inside the refuge hadn't been selected.

Even she hadn't made the list.

Why should an outsider be allowed to go instead?

Too angry to watch the convoy leave, Anna spun around and stomped home.

Maggie looked completely confused.

Unsure what had upset her daughter again, she hurried after her.

Inside the lorry cab, the heater was running at full blast.

It wasn't cold at all.

Lila's thick face mask was making it difficult to breathe, so she pulled it off.

Sophie settled comfortably into the back seat and loosened the scarf wrapped tightly around her neck.

The moment Lila saw her clearly, she recognised the woman who had helped her during the first refuge meeting.

"You're the one who helped me that day, aren't you?"

Lila smiled first.

Sophie didn't recognise her at all.

Back then, Lila had been wearing an oversized helmet that hid practically everything.

After studying Lila's winter gear for a moment, Sophie noticed something familiar.

The colour was different, but the quality and brand matched the outfit worn by the mysterious helmeted girl.

Recognition finally dawned.

"Oh."

"You're the girl with the helmet."

"That's right."

"What a coincidence."

"We even ended up riding in the same truck today."

Lila nodded with a smile.

Ever since the Collapse, she had trusted almost no one.

Yet for some reason, Sophie immediately felt different.

Perhaps it was simply because Sophie had helped her before.

"You two know each other?"

Ethan usually spoke very little.

Even Sophie looked surprised that he'd joined the conversation.

"We met during that first meeting."

"She helped me out."

"Someone tried to steal my coat."

"Captain Walker, you really should tighten security around here."

Lila joked.

"Then you should be telling the person sitting next to you."

Ethan didn't even turn around.

"She's the refuge's newly appointed Legal Officer."

"Maintaining order and enforcing the rules is part of her job."

He introduced Sophie in full.
