Chapter 2-2

Everyone had changed.

As they beat and humiliated her, they told her many things she had never known.

It turned out her parents had died because of them.

It turned out Daniel had colluded with Edward's family from the beginning and had deliberately pursued her.

It turned out every kindness they had ever shown her was for the sake of her family's property.

It turned out the people Lila had treated as family had long been plotting an accidental death for her in secret.

Even without the Collapse, Edward and Maggie's family would have killed her sooner or later.

The memories ended there.

Lila clenched her hands so tightly that the wound on her wrist split open again. Blood began to flow.

Heaven had opened its eyes and given her another life.

This time, she would love only herself.

And she would make sure the wicked received exactly what they deserved.

Lila used a wet wipe to clean the blood from her hands, bit by bit. Her gaze gradually turned cold.

In a lawful society, there were people she could not deal with.

But once the Collapse came, they would be hers to handle.

For now, though, revenge was not the most important thing.

Survival was.

Lila had just graduated from university. She was 23.

During university, she had started an online shop and saved a decent amount of money.

Together with what her parents had left her, she had $930,000.

But $930,000 was nowhere near enough.

Aside from that money, Lila also owned a property. It was the very home she currently shared with Edward and Maggie.

Her parents had left it to her.

Over the years, it was not so much that Edward and Maggie had taken Lila in. It was more accurate to say that their entire family had been living in Lila's house.

But Lila had been young and alone.

They had forgotten who the real owner of the house was.

Lila glanced at the time.

It was now 20 May.

There were less than two months left before the Collapse on 10 July.

The torrential rain would begin at the end of June. Strictly speaking, there was only about one month and ten days left before the end truly began.

She forced herself to stay calm.

It took her an hour to sort through her thoughts and lay out everything she needed to do before the Collapse.

First, raise money. Mortgage the property and secure as much cash as possible.

Second, stockpile supplies.

Third, find a safehouse and reinforce it.

Fourth, settle comfortably into her stronghold and watch those people suffer.

All of this had to be done within thirty to forty days.

Lila did not want to waste even a second.

She washed her face in the bathroom, grabbed the title deeds and all her documents, slung a bag over her shoulder and got ready to leave.

"Lila, where are you off to?" Maggie was making dinner in an apron. Seeing Lila hurry out, she could not help asking.

"I'm meeting a friend. I won't be home for dinner."

Lila did not want anything to do with them.

But there was little time left to prepare before the Collapse. She could not afford to waste it tangling with rubbish people. It was better to act as if nothing had happened.

"What friend are you eating with? A man or a woman?" Maggie asked, sounding casual.

Inside, she was already panicking.

If Lila became involved with another man and abandoned Daniel, then would she not slip out of their control?

"A woman," Lila said.

Then she opened the door and left.

Nervous, Maggie picked up her phone and called Daniel.

"Have you and Lila had an argument?"

Daniel sounded baffled. "No."

"She seems off. Hurry up and smooth things over. Don't let her run off with some other man."

Afraid Lila might not have gone far, Maggie lowered her voice as she warned him.

Daniel had one arm around Annabel's shoulders. His hand kept stroking her smooth skin as he answered carelessly.

"I know, I know. She's completely wrapped around my finger. She won't run off with anyone."

Then he hung up.

Annabel looked up and cupped Daniel's face with one hand.

"I don't want you mentioning that awful woman in front of me."

Daniel lowered his head at once.

"All right. I won't mention her. She can't compare to you, Anna."

In the dim room, the two of them quickly became lost in each other.

At the same time, Lila stood outside the bank, frowning.

She had been too tense today.

In her panic, she had grabbed the title deeds and her documents and rushed out without even checking the time.

It was 6:53 in the evening.

The bank staff had long since gone home.

It seemed nothing could be done today.

Lila turned back to her car and drove off to get something to eat.

Over those months in the Collapse, she had truly been starving.
