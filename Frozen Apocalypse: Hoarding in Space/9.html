Chapter 8

Lila was not sleepy yet.

After briefly noting down her stockpiling plan on her phone, she opened a shopping app and began buying supplies online.

The first things she needed were winter survival gear and bedding.

She would buy two professional, life saving pieces first. This was not an area where she could afford to be cheap.

She ordered two expedition down parkas. They were windproof, waterproof and able to withstand temperatures of minus fifty degrees Celsius. Together, they cost $4,000.

The rest would be ordinary cold weather clothing.

For those, Lila focused on saving money. Her main shopping platform was BargainBasket.

She bought ten of each of the following: long down coats, thick padded coats, old fashioned quilted jackets, army greatcoats, thick padded jackets and padded trousers.

She also bought twenty sets or pieces each of thermal underwear, jumpers, outdoor shell jackets and tracksuit bottoms.

Three hundred pairs of socks.

One hundred sets of underwear.

Ten padded hats and ten scarves.

One hundred cotton face masks.

Fifty pairs of padded gloves.

Twenty pairs each of snow boots, old fashioned padded shoes, padded slippers, summer slippers and trainers.

Five soy fibre duvets and five thick cotton quilts.

Thirty duvet cover sets. She bought all of these from the same shop.

That shop was reliable. Back when she was at university, all her bedding had come from there.

The fabric was soft, did not bobble, and the colours were fresh and pretty.

Lila bought ten sets in pale yellow, five in pink, five in light green, five in dark green and five in dark grey.

After bargaining with the seller, she got them for $50 a set.

She also bought five memory foam pillows and five buckwheat husk pillows.

Then Lila looked at the price of down duvets and found them far too expensive.

There were still two unused down duvets at home, gifts that other relatives had once given Maggie.

Lila decided not to place an order. When the time came, she would simply take those two down duvets away.

For every item, Lila haggled with the sellers. In the end, all of them were bought at discounted prices.

Including the $4,000 spent on the expedition down parkas, everything came to $20,000 in total.

Every product Lila chose came with free return postage. When they arrived, she would inspect them one by one.

If anything failed to meet her standards, she would return it directly and buy more of the items that passed.

By the time she finished buying all the winter survival gear, Lila was exhausted.

She put down her phone, lay back, and fell asleep at once.

On 24 May, Lila woke naturally.

When she picked up her phone, she saw messages from Anna and Markus.

Lila automatically ignored Anna and, feeling particularly tense, opened Markus's message first.

"Ms. Spencer, my team has calculated the cost. Based on your requirements, the original price would be $960,000. As agreed, using the clearance price for the materials, it will be $660,000."

$660,000.

That was all right. She could accept it.

But according to the principle of saving wherever possible when stockpiling for the Collapse, Lila still bargained as a matter of routine.

"Could it be any cheaper? Money is a little tight for me at the moment."

It seemed Markus had been waiting for her reply for some time, because he answered instantly.

"Ms Spencer, this really is the lowest we can go. It is already a very large discount. However, if you can accept this price, we also have an off-grid utility system here. It is a newly developed advanced system, and we can install it for you free of charge."

An off-grid utility system?

It sounded incredibly high end.

And he was giving it to her for free?

Lila found it hard to believe.

"Can we discuss it by phone?"

She sent the message.

About half a minute later, Markus called.

"Good morning, Ms. Spencer."

He greeted her politely first.

"Good morning, Markus. Could you explain the utility system in detail?"

Lila went straight to the point.

"It's like this. When I surveyed the property the other day, I noticed there was a well in the courtyard. This utility system can connect directly to the well and purify the water source. That means you won't need to draw water from the well manually. You'll have clean running groundwater inside the house."

Markus paused. Hearing Lila make a soft sound of acknowledgement, he continued.

"Secondly, the Lower Vale Mansion is quite far from the city, so I imagine the power supply is not always stable. The electrical part of this system can generate power through solar energy, wind energy and thermoelectric generation. Even during power cuts, it can maintain the basic electricity needed in the house."

After explaining the water and power functions, Markus fell silent and waited for Lila's response.

Lila was already secretly stunned.

She had originally planned to spend money later on solar panels and stored water. If she had this system, she would not need to do those things at all.

"What is the thermoelectric generation like?" she asked.

During the Long Freeze, Lila would certainly heat the place heavily. She wanted to know whether that could be linked to the thermoelectric system.

"Aren't you planning to build an inglenook fireplace?" Markus said. "When the time comes, I'll install the power system into the fireplace. If you burn smokeless coal there, the system can use the waste heat to generate electricity.

"If you're interested in this, Ms. Spencer, stockpile more coal in future and you'll even save on electricity. And please don't worry. It won't affect the heating function of the fireplace."

Markus was trying hard to sell the idea.

Inside, Lila was already delighted.

"I can accept that."

Markus chuckled softly on the other end of the call.

"Ms. Spencer, you can rest assured. It is absolutely good value. We previously quoted $300,000 for this utility system. It is simply left over from a luxury property project. Once we finish your project, we'll be returning to Germany immediately, and we don't want the trouble of taking it back with us. So we may as well install it for you.

"The only thing is, could you ask your friend Charles Mason to help promote us a little?"

Lila knew perfectly well that she was benefiting from Charles's connection and getting an excellent deal along the way.

"Of course. Thank you so much. When can you start work? And roughly how long will it take?"

Lila agreed readily to the promotion.

"We're also in a hurry to go home. If you agree, we can start tomorrow. The project should take around one month."

Markus had clearly calculated the construction schedule in advance.

Next, the two of them agreed that work would begin the following day. Lila would pay a deposit at the start, and the balance after completion.

As soon as she ended the call with Markus, Anna's video call came through.

Lila hesitated for a moment, then answered.

"Lila, why didn't you reply to my messages?"

Anna's saccharine voice came through again.

Lila pressed down the disgust in her heart and raised a faint smile.

"You're all away, so no one woke me up. I've only just got out of bed."

"Then hurry and look at our photos. We went on a yacht last night and had dinner on board. It wasn't cheap, though. It cost more than $4,000."

Anna began by introducing it all excitedly, then put on a pitiful expression at the end.

"That's not too expensive," Lila said. "It isn't often you get to go, so try everything and enjoy it. I'll reimburse you when you're back."

$4,000.

That was enough for Lila to buy two expedition down parkas.

She wondered how much it would hurt them when they realised she had no intention of paying them back.

"Lila, you're the best. We want to try diving today. Is that all right?"

Anna was testing for Lila's approval again.

She was afraid that if Lila became unhappy later, she would refuse to reimburse them.

"Of course. Diving sounds fun. Just be careful. Tell me how much it costs afterwards."

Lila still sounded entirely supportive.

"I know. Then we'll go out and have fun now. We'll talk when we have time. Bye, Lila."

Anna sounded perfectly sweet and obedient. The moment she heard Lila agree, she quickly ended the call.
