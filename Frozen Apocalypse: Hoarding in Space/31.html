Chapter 30

After putting the 4x4 away, Lila quickly slipped into her own small car, turned the heater up to full, and drove towards home.

It was getting colder and colder outside. The display in the car showed the outside temperature was only three degrees. She had to get back to the mansion as soon as possible to guarantee her safety.

At this point, all of Lila's preparations were complete.

Her stockpiling journey was finally over.

Now she only wanted to drive home at once and truly begin her new chapter of riding out the Collapse in comfort.

Inside the upmarket French restaurant, Annabel and Daniel were taking charge of the ordering.

"What are your signature dishes?"

Annabel lifted her chin, putting on the airs of a wealthy young lady as she asked with deliberate nonchalance.

"These first five dishes are our house specialities," the server said with excellent manners.

That attitude greatly satisfied Annabel's vanity.

"Two of each. And one each of this, this and this. Also, two bottles of this red wine."

Since Lila was already on her way, Annabel could order boldly without worrying.

"Anna, that's enough."

Edward frowned and stopped her from continuing.

He had not seen Lila yet, so he could not feel at ease. What if Lila could not come? How much would this meal cost?

"Can we each order a dessert as well?"

Annabel secretly pouted, but with Daniel there, she had to maintain her image. She forced herself to discuss it patiently with Edward.

Edward nodded and said nothing.

"Bring us four portions of your best dessert."

As for Lila's portion, she could order it herself when she arrived.

Twenty minutes later, the dishes began arriving one after another.

"Don't eat yet! Let me take photos first."

Annabel held her hands over the table, terrified that someone might pick up their cutlery and ruin the presentation of the food, spoiling the photos.

Although Edward looked stern on the surface, he still indulged his daughter in his heart. He did not stop her and silently put down his cutlery.

Annabel struck all kinds of exaggerated poses while Daniel held up his phone and took photo after photo of her.

The people dining nearby were not only drawn by Annabel's dramatic posing, but some even lowered their heads and whispered among themselves.

Annabel checked the photos Daniel had taken. Not a single one achieved the effect she wanted.

She shoved the phone back into Daniel's hand.

"Do I really look that ugly? Daniel, take them again."

Daniel had no choice but to pick up the phone and carefully photograph Annabel all over again.

The temperature inside the restaurant gradually fell. Some of the more cold-sensitive diners even began rubbing their arms to keep warm.

Seeing this, the manager turned the restaurant's central heating up to the highest setting.

Even so, customers began leaving the restaurant one after another before finishing their meals.

Of course, Edward's table did not leave.

After another twenty minutes of photos, even Annabel could no longer withstand the cold.

"Isn't this supposed to be a high-end French restaurant? Why is it so cold? I'm freezing just trying to take pictures."

Annabel complained irritably, completely unaware that danger was creeping closer.

Maggie had waited patiently for a long time, but Annabel still was not finished. Finally, she had no choice but to stand up.

"You warm yourself up for a bit. Let me take a couple first."

Maggie turned on a heavy beauty filter and began taking photos of herself and the food on the table.

Even though her shoulders were trembling from the cold, the smile on her face did not fade in the slightest.

By the time the two of them were finally done fussing, there was not a trace of warmth left in the food.

Annabel hugged her arms and shivered violently.

"It's too cold. It's too cold. Are we still eating? Why don't we go home?"

Maggie gave her daughter a reproachful look.

"What a waste. How could we not eat food this expensive? Besides, we still have to wait for Lila to come and pay."

The mention of Lila made everyone pause.

That was right.

Why had Lila still not arrived?

Edward and Maggie exchanged a look. Maggie immediately picked up her phone and called Lila.

"Sorry, the number you have dialled is switched off."

Maggie's face filled with confusion.

The others froze as well.

Why was her phone switched off?

"Call again," Edward said coldly.

Maggie dialled again and received the same message.

"Sorry, the number you have dialled is switched off."

"Has her phone run out of battery? What if she can't find us?"

Annabel was very worried.

"Again."

Edward's voice was squeezed out from between clenched teeth.

Maggie did not dare delay. She took Annabel's phone and tried calling again, but the result was still the same switched-off message.

Even Daniel grew nervous.

If Lila really did not come, who was going to pay for this meal?

Surely it would not be him.

Annabel and Maggie lost all their excitement. They sat shivering at the table, all their attention fixed on the phone being used to call Lila.

Another twenty minutes passed.

The other customers had all left because it was simply too cold. Only their table remained.

"Sorry to disturb you, madam."

The server stood in front of Maggie with the bill folder and gave a deep bow.

"Due to the weather, and in order to ensure the safety of both our guests and staff, the restaurant has decided to close early. We're very sorry for affecting your dining experience. All dishes today will be settled with a thirty per cent discount."

They had not managed to wait for Lila.

Instead, they had waited for the server urging them to pay.

Annabel's anger flared. She finally could not maintain her ladylike image any longer.

"What is wrong with you? You're just an employee, and you dare rush us to settle the bill early? Are you saying you're throwing us out before we've even finished eating?"

Being rushed was one thing. Being made to pay was the real problem.

Annabel had ordered a lot of dishes today. If they had to settle the bill themselves, it would cost a fortune.

The server looked apologetic.

"Please look outside."

Following the direction the server indicated, Annabel and the others finally looked out of the window.

At some point, the rain outside had turned to snow.

The cold wind howled. Great sheets of snow fell from the sky, less like snowfall than masses of goose feathers pouring down from above.

There were very few cars left on the road. Only a handful of pedestrians hurried past, all wearing trench coats or thick jackets.

The sight was frightening.

"This… How is it snowing in the middle of summer?"

Maggie's face was full of shock.

Although the weather had been abnormal these last few days, none of them had expected snow in high summer.

"We're very sorry. This is also because we're worried it may become unsafe for you to travel home if you leave too late and the snow builds up."

Silently, the server added another sentence in her heart.

It would be unsafe for us to go home too.

"Then… if you're forcing people to leave, this meal should be on the house."

Annabel's eyes darted as she came up with what she thought was a good excuse.

"I'm afraid that won't be possible. With such a large table of food, the cost is not low."

If they had only ordered a salad and some pasta, spending a few hundred, perhaps the manager might have been generous enough to waive it.

But this was a full table of food, plus two bottles of expensive red wine. The bill showed $84,000. Even the restaurant manager did not have the authority to simply wipe that away.
