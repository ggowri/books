Chapter 1

Day 104 of the Freeze

Lila Spencer was tied so tightly with steel cable that she could not move. The chair beneath her was sticky and reeked of filth.

She barely noticed any more.

She had been bound there for sixty-seven days.

Steel cable pinned her body to the chair, while her right wrist had been hauled up separately and lashed with wire to the bars of the security grille.

She had tried to break free countless times. Even after the skin around her wrist had been rubbed raw and bloody, the restraints still would not give.

The brutal cold had stiffened her entire body. Now, escape was even more impossible than before.

"Hurry up. Get food out of the Vault!"

Her cousin, Annabel Spencer, gripped a length of steel pipe and brought it down hard across Lila's back.

Pain shot through Lila, and a low groan escaped her throat.

She knew there was no point resisting. Obediently, she pressed her thumb against the Crimson Mark on her forefinger. With a flicker of will, she took out a bread roll.

Annabel snatched it up, turned away, and went to lean against her mother.

Annabel's mother was Lila's aunt, Maggie Wells.

"Mum, can't we move the Vault onto one of us?" Annabel said as she chewed. "She stinks. We might as well get rid of her."

"Oh, my poor darling," Maggie said, patting Annabel's hand, her eyes full of pity. "Just put up with it a little longer. What if she dies and the Vault disappears? All the food is inside her Vault."

Lila stared at the white roll and swallowed.

She was desperately hungry.

Those people had dragged her right hand far away and tied it down. Everything from the Vault appeared only beside her right hand, which meant she could not even bring it to her mouth.

All she could do was let them use her and cling to the last miserable scraps of life.

Her fiancé, Daniel Carter, and her uncle, Edward Spencer, came up from the service tunnels carrying several rats in their hands.

"Not a bad catch," Annabel said, eyes lighting up at the sight of the fat rats.

"Don't eat them all at once. Keep them alive," Edward said, handing the rats to Maggie.

"Keep them alive? With what?" Maggie said. Right now, she wanted to cook every last one.

"There's food ready-made over there." Edward pointed at Lila, tied up in the corner.

Maggie understood at once.

She put the rats into a cage, then, with a look of disgust, ripped away the fabric covering Lila's legs and set the iron cage on her lap.

The starving rats smelled flesh. They lowered their heads and began gnawing through the wire mesh.

The scrape of those tiny teeth brought a sharp, sickening pain that seemed to drill straight into Lila's bones.

But she no longer had the strength to scream.

She could not even twitch her leg.

Annabel laughed. "Look at her. She's revolting. I can barely stand it."

She pulled a face as if she were disgusted, yet her eyes lingered greedily on the pain twisting Lila's features.

"Don't overdo it. We still need her alive," Daniel said. Then he picked up his own small share of the bread from the table and stuffed it into his mouth.

That night, the monsters slept soundly.

Lila stared at the rats still gnawing at her thigh and felt the deep, stabbing pain pulse through her.

At last, she understood.

It was not only the Collapse that devoured people.

People devoured people too.

There was no point living like this.

Slowly, Lila slipped out of consciousness.

Half an hour earlier, Lila had returned to her second life.

Even after she steadied herself, part of her still wondered whether the Collapse had only been a nightmare.

She raised her hand and looked at it.

There was no Crimson Mark on her fingertip.

And she did not have the Vault.

So it had been a dream?

No. Something was still wrong.

In a panic, Lila got to her feet and rushed to the bookcase, tearing through the shelves.

She remembered.

She remembered it clearly.

The bookmark had been inside one of the books on this shelf. A red book.

Which one was it?

Where was it?

Fighting down the pain in her head, Lila searched through every red book she could find.

At last, she found it.

By then, half the bookcase had been turned upside down, with books scattered in untidy heaps across the floor. But she had found it.

Lila sat down right there on the pile of books, holding the Gilded Bookmark in her hands.

She had no idea what kind of metal it was made from. It gave off a dazzling golden light, and its surface was carved with intricate openwork patterns.

There was no time to admire it.

She bit her finger and squeezed a drop of blood onto the bookmark.

Dark red blood splashed across the hollowed pattern, creating a strange, unsettling beauty.

Lila even forgot to breathe. She stared at the bookmark for a long while.

Nothing happened.

In the nightmare, she had cut her finger on this bookmark by accident while reading, and the bookmark had created a vast space.

So why was there no reaction now?

Had it really been only a dream?

Panic surged through her. She needed proof. She had no attention left for pain or injury. Gripping the bookmark, she dragged it hard across her wrist.

Blood welled up.

A lot of it ran over the bookmark, making it look even stranger than before.

Then the golden light on the bookmark grew brighter and brighter, shifting from gold to white.

The glowing parts slowly melted away. The bookmark shrank little by little until it vanished completely.

So that was it.

The bookmark only reacted when it was the thing that drew blood.

Last time, the mark had formed on her finger.

This time, it formed on her wrist.

The bookmark became a mole, settling into her skin as the Crimson Mark.

Lila closed her eyes. With a flicker of will, her consciousness entered the Vault as naturally as if she had done it a thousand times.

Yes.

It was exactly the same as the Vault from the nightmare.

The bookmark really could become the Vault.

Which meant...

The Collapse in her nightmare had been real.

The inhuman torment she had suffered had been real.

None of it had been false.

And now, she had returned to her second life.

"Lila? Are you awake? Come out and have some fruit."

Maggie's gentle voice sounded from outside the door.

The moment Lila heard it, she was violently pulled out of the Vault.

A chill swept through her as if she had been dropped into an ice pit. Her stomach churned again and again.

Scene after scene of torture flashed wildly through her mind.

Lila squeezed her eyes shut in pain, then forced her voice to stay steady.

"I don't feel well. I'm going to sleep a bit longer. Don't worry about me."

Maggie's anxious voice immediately came from outside.

"Oh dear, what's wrong? Let me take you to hospital."

Once, that voice had seemed so warm.

Now, all Lila heard in it was coldness and nausea.

Clenching her teeth, Lila said, "I'll be fine after some sleep. Really."

"All right, then. If you still feel unwell, call me. I'll take you to hospital."

After that, the footsteps outside the door gradually moved away.

Lila drew in great gulps of air. Cold sweat slid down her temples.

The memories from her previous life were becoming clearer than ever.
