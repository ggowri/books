Chapter 5

Dusk was falling. It was time for Lila to go home.

If she left it any later, the roads would be too dark for comfortable driving.

She locked the Lower Vale Mansion and the courtyard gate properly, then drove back towards Ashton.

By the time she reached the city, it was still not too late. Half past seven in the evening.

Before going home, Lila went to the collection point she had used for her online order and picked up the imitation title deeds she had ordered the day before.

She had not sent them to the parcel depot near home. If Maggie or Anna picked up the parcel for her, everything would be exposed.

She had just parked the car when her phone rang.

It was Anna.

"Lila, why aren't you home yet? We're all waiting for you to have dinner."

Anna's sweet, affectionate voice came through the phone.

Lila frowned.

It sounded nothing like the voice Anna had used in her previous life when she struck Lila with a steel pipe.

"I'm downstairs already."

With that, Lila hung up and went upstairs.

The moment she opened the door, the smell of dinner drifted out.

"Lila's back. Come and eat. I've only just finished cooking."

Maggie's voice was gentle.

For one brief moment, Lila felt dazed.

The warm light, the familiar smell of food, and Maggie's tender manner had once been among Lila's warmest memories.

But after the Collapse, every humiliation and every ounce of pain had also come from these people.

Lila lowered her head to change her shoes. A trace of mockery flickered through her eyes before she quickly hid it.

Still, she really was hungry.

She had not had time to eat on the way back.

She had been afraid that returning too late would make Edward and Maggie suspicious.

There was still quite a while before the Collapse. If they noticed something wrong before then, it would interfere with her preparations and stockpiling.

Perhaps she should send them away.

The idea came to Lila in an instant.

Edward, Maggie, Anna and Lila sat around the dining table.

There were four dishes and a soup.

Braised ribs with dried beans, red-braised pork, hot and sour shredded potato, tomato and scrambled eggs, and clam and tofu soup.

"Wow, these are all my favourites. Thank you, Maggie."

Lila put on a delighted expression, behaving exactly as she had before around them.

"Have more if you like it."

Maggie was just about to put food into Lila's bowl when Lila looked down, frowned, and stood up with her bowl and chopsticks.

She went into the kitchen and rinsed them under the tap.

"What's wrong, Lila?" Maggie asked, puzzled by the odd behaviour.

"There was something black and dirty in the bowl. It hadn't been washed properly. I'll just rinse it off."

Lila's voice came through the sound of running water.

"Did I miss something when washing it?"

Maggie sounded doubtful.

At the sink, Lila scrubbed the bowl and chopsticks hard under the water until she was certain they were completely clean.

The food was shared by everyone, so poisoning it was unlikely. The only possible target would be her own cutlery.

Although the chances of them poisoning her at this stage were not high, she still had to stay on guard.

Tomorrow, she would have to avoid eating with them.

Lila silently made a note of it.

After sitting back down at the table, she finally began eating with peace of mind.

"Lila," Maggie said after a little cough, "I was cleaning your room today. Why is your drawer locked?"

She had spent the whole day worrying about it.

Yesterday, when she cleaned the room, she had not seen the title deeds. Today, the drawer where the title deeds were usually kept had been locked.

It made her uneasy.

"Oh?" Lila said. "Didn't you tell me yesterday to look after the title deeds properly? I was afraid a burglar might get in, so I locked the drawer."

Of course, Lila had prepared that answer in advance.

What she had not expected was how closely Maggie had been watching her.

The first time Maggie noticed the title deeds were gone, it could have been a coincidence. But now she had noticed the drawer was locked as well.

That meant Maggie must have been checking her room every day.

How had she been so slow in her previous life? How had she never noticed?

Lila felt secretly relieved that she had locked the drawer. Otherwise, the truth might already have come out.

"Oh, look at my memory. I completely forgot I said that." Maggie smiled. "Lila is such a good girl. I tell you once, and you lock it away properly. Anna, you should learn from her and listen to me more often."

Hearing Lila's explanation, Maggie found it reasonable enough and turned the conversation towards Anna.

"Oh, Lila has always been sensible. I could never be like her. As long as Lila likes me, that's enough."

Anna pouted, eating the ribs in her bowl in small bites while acting spoiled.

In the past, Lila would have found this adorable, as if Anna were still a child who had never grown up.

Now, hearing it only made goosebumps rise all over her skin.

"Maggie, Anna, would you like to go travelling?"

Lila felt the timing was right, so she brought up the plan she had thought of earlier.

"What? A holiday? I want to go."

Anna loved going out and having fun. If there was a chance to travel, nothing could be better.

"What holiday?" Maggie shot Anna a glance and scolded her lightly. "You don't work, you don't have a job. Where would you get the money to waste on that?"

Lila smiled faintly and spoke again.

"After graduation, Anna only travelled around the neighbouring county. She hasn't had the chance to go south yet. Why not make it a graduation trip?"

Maggie immediately understood what Lila meant.

Lila rarely brought things up on her own. If she did, then she would almost certainly be the one paying.

"Wouldn't it be expensive?" Maggie asked, testing her.

"Not really. I saw a tourism advert for Seabourne today. Flights are on special offer. If you go now, return tickets are only thirty per cent of the usual price."

That was true.

The economy had not been doing well that year, so in order to encourage tourism, flights to holiday cities had been heavily discounted.

The moment Anna heard it, she was so excited she almost jumped out of her seat.

"Oh my goodness, I want to go. I really want to go."

Maggie could barely keep the smile off her face. Clearly, she was tempted too.

"Then... when would we go?"

"Edward, you and Anna should go together. I asked a university friend to help me find work, and I'll need to attend interviews and start soon, so I won't go."

Naturally, Lila had no intention of going.

But as soon as Lila said she was not going, Maggie panicked.

If Lila was not going, who would pay?

"If you're not going, then we won't go either. It wouldn't be much fun without you."

If Lila did not go, Maggie had no interest in paying for it herself. That would be a complete waste.

"You and Edward raised me all these years, and I've never had the chance to properly repay you. Anna has just graduated from university, so the three of you should go and enjoy yourselves. Otherwise, once Anna starts work, she'll have even less time. Keep the records for the flights, hotel and other expenses. When you come back, I'll reimburse everything."

Lila spoke at length, her tone sincere and heartfelt.

This was not something she had invented out of nowhere. In her previous life, Lila really had planned to do this.

Only later, the Collapse had arrived so suddenly that the plan had never been mentioned again.

"Well, would the trip cost a lot?" Maggie asked carefully. "I'm worried that if we pay upfront, we might not have enough on us."

The cunning old fox was afraid Lila would not actually reimburse them.

Anna grew anxious.

"Mum, I really want to go."

Lila felt as if her heart were bleeding from the expense, but she still said, "The money my parents left me was in a fixed term account. I'll be able to withdraw it in two months. I can give you $20,000 first. You and Edward can cover the rest for now, and once I can take the money out, I'll pay you back. How does that sound?"

Once Maggie heard that Lila was willing to give them $20,000 upfront, she felt that the rest would most likely be reimbursed too.
