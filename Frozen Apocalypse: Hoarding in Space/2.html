Chapter 2-1

28 May 2030.

Lila remembered the date clearly. It was her birthday.

That day, while reading in her room, she had stumbled upon the Gilded Bookmark, which had been missing for years.

It had been a present from her father for her tenth birthday.

Her father used to give her a birthday present every year. Then, when she was 14, both her parents took their own lives for reasons no one had ever properly explained.

Lila had been sitting there, remembering the warm little moments she had shared with them, turning the Gilded Bookmark over in her fingers.

Then she slipped.

The edge of the bookmark was shockingly sharp. It cut straight across her finger.

In an instant, the Gilded Bookmark burst into dazzling light. It slowly melted, flowing into her fingertip, until a small Crimson Mark formed on her skin.

Lila was stunned by the impossible sight.

After studying it for a while, she discovered that if she closed her eyes, pressed the Crimson Mark and sensed its presence, she could enter another space.

The Vault was vast, though her physical body could not go inside. Only her consciousness could enter.

It looked like an enormous warehouse, roughly four or five hundred square metres.

The interior was brightly lit. Beyond the warehouse lay thick white mist.

When Lila approached the mist, she could not make out what was beyond it. If she reached out, her hand was stopped by an invisible barrier.

Later, after testing the Vault again and again, she discovered that she could store and retrieve objects whenever she wished.

Living things could not be placed inside. Anything else that entered the Vault remained in stasis.

A steaming bread roll put inside one day would still be steaming hot when taken out the next.

After Lila obtained the Vault, she thought it was fantastical, but it did not seem to affect her daily life much. She told no one. At most, whenever she came across something delicious, she would casually tuck some of it into the Vault.

The Collapse began on 10 July 2030.

At the end of June, there had first been several days of torrential rain.

Lila had worried the rain might not stop for a while and would make it difficult to go out for groceries, so she stocked quite a lot of supplies in the Vault.

No one expected the rain to keep falling as the temperature dropped lower and lower, until it finally turned into snow.

In just one day, thick snow covered the ground.

Schools suspended classes. Companies sent their staff home. Transport collapsed.

The temperature kept falling, gradually becoming a brutal deep freeze.

After Lila's parents died, she had lived with Edward and Maggie's family.

They had always looked after her, or so she thought. Lila had treated them almost like her own parents.

When the Long Freeze arrived, there was still plenty of food in the Vault.

Without the slightest hesitation, she told them about it.

The food she had stocked inside the Vault was shared by the whole family.

Later, Lila even brought her fiancé, Daniel, to the house.

They gathered enough padded clothes and bedding, then hid in the Basement together, ready to endure that terrible period as a family.

But the peace did not last.

Lila caught Daniel and Annabel together.

For a time, she was crushed by grief and fury.

Edward and Maggie acted as though they were ashamed of their daughter. They slapped Annabel several times and forced her to apologise to Lila. Afterwards, they sat with Lila and drank with her.

Lila became drunk.

When she woke again, she was tied to a chair.
