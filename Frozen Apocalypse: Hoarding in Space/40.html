Chapter 39

After arranging the furniture in the east parlour, Lila still had the other rooms to deal with.

She strode over to the wardrobe, took out one of the padded granny jackets and wrapped it around herself. Then she opened the door and went into the main hall.

The main hall was empty, apart from a set of rosewood furniture placed in the centre.

Lila took the shoe cabinet from her old flat out of the Vault and set it against the wall by the entrance.

When she opened it, she pulled out all the foul old shoes belonging to Edward, Maggie and Annabel.

She had originally planned to burn the lot, but when she saw the uppers on some of the better leather shoes, she felt a little reluctant.

She took a woven sack from the Vault, tipped every pair of shoes into it, and threw the whole thing back inside.

If she ever needed materials for handiwork later, perhaps she could dig them out.

Lila found a bottle of rubbing alcohol in the Vault, poured it into a spray bottle and thoroughly sprayed the shoe cabinet from top to bottom, determined to clean away every trace of bacteria.

Then she wiped the whole cabinet down with a cloth. Only then did she feel a little more comfortable.

By the time Lila had found her own shoes in the Vault, the alcohol inside the cabinet had already evaporated.

She neatly placed two pairs of padded slippers, one expensive pair of reindeer-hide boots and two pairs of padded snow boots into the shoe cabinet.

During the Long Freeze, unless something unexpected happened, Lila would not be going out.

These few pairs of shoes would be enough for a very long time.

As she put the last pair of snow boots into the cabinet, her hand suddenly froze.

A thought had occurred to her.

No living thing could survive in the Vault. So when she put the shoe cabinet into the Vault, did that mean all living bacteria and viruses had been shut out of it?

By that logic, the moment the shoe cabinet entered the Vault, it had already been disinfected once.

As though she had discovered a whole new world, Lila could not help laughing.

"So that loophole can be used like this too."

The temperature in the main hall was not especially high, and it was not somewhere Lila would linger for long.

Apart from the shoe cabinet, she only placed a broom and mop by the door leading into the east parlour.

Next came the kitchen.

The kitchen would be much trickier to arrange.

Fortunately, the renovation crew had cleaned everything before leaving, saving Lila a great deal of effort.

The kitchen was large and spacious, but it was also very empty.

Apart from the kitchen range, there was only a two-metre-wide counter opposite it.

On the right side of the counter were the vegetable sink and washing-up sink. On the left was another metre or so of free worktop.

Beneath the counter were two separate cupboards. Under the right side were the drainage pipes for the sink, so it was not very convenient for storage.

Only the cupboard on the left could really be used.

Lila stood up and looked around the kitchen.

It seemed that the whole kitchen only had that one usable cupboard for storage.

She had overlooked something. She had forgotten to buy kitchen cabinets.

In her flat at Oakwood Estate, the kitchen cabinets had all been fixed to the walls and could not be taken away.

So there were no proper kitchen cabinets in the Vault.

Lila held her breath and carefully searched through the furniture inside the Vault.

In the end, she chose Annabel's doll display cabinet.

It was made from a wooden frame with glass doors, and had once held all kinds of delicate dolls.

When Edward had bought the display cabinet for Annabel, it had cost a fair amount. The quality was definitely reliable.

Lila gently placed the cabinet on the right side of the sink.

It was wide, about one and a half metres across, and stood roughly one metre eighty high.

There were five shelves in total, each divided down the middle into left and right sections, making ten large compartments.

Each shelf had plenty of height and could hold all kinds of kitchenware.

Even though anything that had passed through the Vault could count as disinfected, Lila still felt uneasy.

She sprayed it thoroughly with rubbing alcohol first, then began placing the items she used most often.

On the first shelf, the very top one, she put several packets of dried vegetables, dried noodles, vermicelli and instant staples.

The second shelf sat just about level with Lila's line of sight, making it the prime spot.

She put a set of tableware in the right-hand compartment. The left-hand compartment would be used for leftovers in future.

The third shelf held the kitchen appliances and tools she used most often: a rice cooker, electric griddle, air fryer and frying pan.

The fourth shelf was for appliances used less frequently: a small milk pan, casserole pot, steamer and non-stick pan.

Some of the kitchenware had been bought during her stockpiling. Some had been brought directly from the flat.

For instance, the enamelled casserole pot had been given to Maggie by the same relative who had sent the down duvet. Now it had become Lila's private pot.

The fifth shelf sat close to the floor, so weight was no concern. She shoved in a bag of rice and a bag of flour.

Although the kitchen cabinets from the flat could not be taken, there had been a spice rack at home, and that was now in the Vault too.

Lila took it out and placed it on the counter beside the sink, keeping it in plain sight for easy use.

Beside the spice rack went a rotating knife block, used to hold different kinds of knives, along with chopsticks, forks, spoons and other cutlery.

On the empty wall above the counter, Lila stuck up several hooks.

She hung a spatula, slotted spoon, pan brush and grater there, ready to grab whenever she needed them.

The chopping board went just outside the spice rack and knife block. The counter was spacious enough for it with room to spare.

Seen from a distance, the pretty display cabinet and the spice rack on the counter made the kitchen look refined and charming.

Still, it felt as though something was missing.

After thinking for a moment, Lila placed a storage rack beside the kitchen range.

It was one of the dozens of racks she had bought wholesale to organise supplies in the Vault.

In the end, she had not used all of them. Now one fit perfectly beside the range.

She tossed several stainless steel basins and a water scoop onto the third shelf of the rack.

In future, any vegetables, fruit or tools could be placed there directly, making them easy to take and use.

Next came the bathroom.

Yesterday, she had been too tired and had rushed through her shower, so she had not had time to arrange it properly.

Lila placed cleanser, toothbrush, toothpaste and soap on the small shelf beside the washbasin.

Shower gel, shampoo and conditioner went into the storage niche inside the shower cubicle.

On the floor outside the shower cubicle, she laid an absorbent diatomaceous bath mat.

There was a small rail for clothes outside the shower cubicle, so Lila hung a bath towel and hair-drying turban there. It would be more convenient later.

The space between the bathtub and the door was just big enough for a washing machine.

Lila patted the lid of the washing machine in satisfaction. The layout of the bathroom had been designed brilliantly. Every toiletry and appliance found its place without anything feeling awkward.

Only the west room was left.

Lila planned to turn it into a chicken room, but the chicks were still too small. There was no hurry to sort it out just yet.

She washed her hands clean.

The first round of furnishing her new home was complete.

For now, this was enough. Anything else she needed could be added gradually as she lived here.

Lila checked the temperature in each room.

The thermometer in the east parlour showed 25℃. The main hall was only eighteen. The kitchen was slightly warmer at 20℃.

The bathroom was 15℃.

The west room was the most ridiculous, at only 10℃.
