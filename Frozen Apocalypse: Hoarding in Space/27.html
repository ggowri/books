Chapter 26

The inglenook fireplace was framed by two rings of red brick, while the whole wall behind it had been inlaid with arched patterns made from red brick and stone.

It looked wonderfully artistic, almost as if she were sitting in a detached cottage somewhere in a small European country in winter, reading the paper and drinking coffee.

Lila was extremely satisfied with everything in the east parlour. She turned and went to inspect the kitchen and bathroom next.

The kitchen counter had been built at exactly the right height, and everything had been done according to her requirements.

She could stew something in one pot, stir-fry in another, and still have a separate hob free for boiling water. It was more than enough.

The bathroom had been fully tiled. The washbasin was simple and generous in design, and it looked sturdy and durable at first glance.

There were even two spare shower heads inside the cabinet beneath the sink.

Lila could not help admiring the work inwardly. The site manager really had been both thoughtful and meticulous.

Now the entire Lower Vale Mansion was practically unbeatable in terms of hardware. The only issue was that the rooms were still a little empty.

During the renovation, Lila had allowed the manager to throw away all the old, battered items inside the house. Only the rosewood table and chairs in the main hall had been left exactly where they were.

It was not that she had no feelings for Agnes and George.

But in the face of the coming Collapse, survival came first.

Rather than clinging to broken old cupboards, she was better off thinking about what furniture and household items would last longer and be more practical.

At present, the place lacked appliances and furniture. Even the basic daily necessities were still inside the Vault and had not been taken out.

But there was no hurry.

Once the Long Freeze arrived and she was shut indoors, she would have plenty of time to sort everything out.

Reluctantly, Lila locked the mansion door and drove back to the hotel.

The mansion was already liveable now, but she still wanted to spend a few more days stockpiling in the city. The place was too far from the city centre, and driving back and forth would be exhausting. It was better to keep staying at the hotel for now.

The next day, Lila went to a medical equipment shop and chose five sets of the best biohazard coveralls. They cost $25,000.

Although the only disaster Lila knew about was the Long Freeze, she had not survived long after the Collapse in her previous life before being tortured to death. She had no idea what might come after the extreme cold.

That meant she had to prepare for several possibilities.

If there were an insect infestation or an infectious outbreak later on, biohazard coveralls could be enormously useful.

Lila carried the coveralls back to the car. In her Bluetooth earphones, the audio from the pinhole cameras at home was still playing.

She had only just put the coveralls into the Vault when she froze where she stood.

The sound in her earphones was not loud, but it was sharp enough to pierce.

"When Lila comes back, we'll start giving her a slow-acting poison."

Edward's low voice came through the earphones.

Immediately after that came Maggie's approving voice.

"Fine. We've put this off long enough. We should have made up our minds ages ago. If that girl works for too long, she won't be so easy to control.

"In a couple of months, once Lila is diagnosed with a serious illness, we'll coax her a little. She'll definitely be willing to leave all her savings to us."

Lila frowned.

So that was their plan. They wanted her to become terminally ill, then make a will and transfer all her assets to them.

Shameless.

She would never leave her property to them.

Heartless lot.

Lila clenched her fists tightly.

After the torment she had endured in her previous life, and now hearing this with her own ears, her hatred burned even deeper.

Furious, she exited the surveillance feed.

By chance, she saw a message in the Oakwood residents' group chat tagging everyone.

"The weather has been strange lately. Extreme weather may be on the way.

"As it happens, I have a batch of Yakutsk goods in hand. If any neighbours want them, I'll give you priority.

"I have reindeer-hide boots and mink hats."

Below the text were two product photos and descriptions taken by the seller himself.

Handmade reindeer-hide boots, described as the warmest boots on earth. The boot body was made from reindeer-leg hide, taken from the lower leg of the reindeer. It stayed tough and did not crack even at minus fifty degrees, and could withstand extremely low temperatures. $5,000 a pair.

Mink hats, $3,000 each. Made entirely from mink fur and able to withstand the extreme cold of minus fifty degrees.

Lila had seen these handmade reindeer-hide boots in a documentary before. They reached above the knee and were made entirely from reindeer hide. They were indeed sturdy and warm.

She had thought about buying a couple of pairs before, but she could not find genuine ones online. The photos posted in the residents' group, however, genuinely looked the part.

Lila immediately searched for information on reindeer-hide boots and found that they really did look almost exactly like the ones in the photos.

The outside of the boots was decorated with traditional beaded patterns and ornaments, giving them a strong regional character.

Lila opened the seller's profile.

His profile picture was a mink coat, and his username was Fine Furs for Sale.

She tapped to add him as a friend.

Within five seconds, the request was accepted.

"Hello, neighbour. Are you interested in the reindeer-hide boots?"

The person on the other side was very enthusiastic.

"I'm not at the estate at the moment. Can you bring the items over so I can inspect them in person?" Lila asked directly.

She could not go back to Oakwood Estate yet. It would be best if she could buy the items outside the estate.

"Of course. Where are you? Let me know in advance what you need, and I'll bring everything together."

Callum was so excited that his hands trembled as he typed.

This batch of imported goods had been stuck with him for ages. Although local winters were cold, ordinary padded shoes and leather boots were enough. No one was willing to spend a fortune on boots decorated so extravagantly.

With the recent strange weather, he had only thought to try his luck by posting in the group chat. He had not expected anyone to actually want them.

"Three pairs of reindeer-hide boots and three hats."

If the boots were genuine, then of course she had to stockpile a few more pairs.

"Lovely. By the way, do you need a fur coat?" Callum agreed at once, naturally hoping to sell one more piece of fur while he was at it.

"No, thank you."

After refusing, Lila sent him the location of a cafe beside the hotel.

An hour later, Callum and Lila appeared in the cafe at the same time.

Callum was wearing a checked shirt and holding a huge black leather bag in his arms.

Lila wore black-framed glasses and a black face mask. She walked straight over and sat opposite him.

"Hello, neighbour. I brought what you wanted. Take a look."

Callum had thought about it for ages and could only address Lila as neighbour. Then he passed the large leather bag to her.

He had obtained the reindeer-hide boots through private channels, so the transaction naturally had to be kept discreet.

That suited Lila perfectly. She had no desire to draw attention either. She unzipped the leather bag and began inspecting the contents carefully.

The reindeer-hide boots were exquisitely made. From the inside, she could tell each boot had been made from a whole piece of hide. The exterior was inlaid with beautiful patterns and ornaments, giving them a strong exotic style.

Following the guide she had looked up online beforehand, Lila checked them from several angles and confirmed that they were genuine reindeer-hide boots.

She then carefully inspected the mink hats. They were indeed authentic too.

Only then did she zip the bag back up.

"Well?" Callum leaned closer and asked.

"They'll do. Give me a better price," Lila said with a nod.

"I can't go any lower. I need to make my money back. I'll give you two pairs of leather mittens instead. They're Yakutsk goods too. Locals wear them in winter. Even at more than minus fifty degrees, your hands won't freeze."

Callum glanced around and lowered his voice.
