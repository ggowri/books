Chapter 50

"Lila, where are you? I'm so cold, and I'm so hungry. Can I come and find you?"

Anna sent the message in a sickly sweet tone, but her mouth did not stop moving either.

"Awful woman. Just wait until I find you. I'll take everything you have and leave you to freeze out there."

Lila saw the message and did not reply.

"Oh! I nearly forgot!"

She swallowed the bun in her mouth, put down her phone, quickly pulled on her padded jacket, and hurried to the kitchen.

She lifted the pot lid. The vegetable buns inside were perfectly steamed too.

Like the pork buns, some of the vegetable buns had juices seeping onto the surface of the dough.

The green filling showed faintly through, fresh and inviting.

Lila took one cabbage bun and one aubergine bun, then placed the rest onto plates and stored them in the Vault.

Four large buns were pretty much her limit.

Carrying the two buns back to the east parlour, she found that eleven new messages had already arrived on her phone.

All of them were various expressions of concern.

Lila had no intention of replying. She simply picked up a vegetable bun and ate.

The cabbage filling was fresh and crisp. The aubergine filling was soft, rich and fragrant. Each one was delicious in its own way.

Meanwhile, the family opposite were cold, hungry and bored enough to stare at their phones, waiting for Lila to reply.

Every message disappeared like a stone sinking into the sea. Not a single reply came back.

"Why aren't you answering? You can see these messages, can't you? Why are you ignoring us? We're family."

Anna finally could not hold back and sent a whole string of questioning messages in one go.

Lila still did not answer. She only watched the monitor.

She watched the family fall apart in frustration.

"Why won't she reply to me? How dare she ignore me? She used to do everything I said. She never talked back to me. Why is she acting so vile now?"

Anna's temper exploded, and she shouted at her phone.

Maggie and Edward were both silent, frowning in thought.

Lila had not replied at all. Could something have happened to her? Could she have died?

Impossible.

The flat had been cleared out. She must have hidden somewhere to enjoy herself.

But how could they make Lila help them?

After thinking for a long time, Maggie picked up her phone.

"Lila, are you angry with me? If I've done something wrong, tell me, all right? We're family. There's nothing we can't get past."

Her words sounded so sincere that if Lila had not known the truth, and if she had not been watching the monitor, she might almost have been moved to tears.

Lila did not keep watching. She tossed the phone aside.

After finishing the buns, her mouth felt dry and a little greasy, so she found a bottle of honey citron tea in the Vault.

She took hot water from the fireplace, spooned some honey citron tea into a cup, and stirred it.

After blowing on it gently, she took a sip. Sweet and tangy, it cut through the richness and quenched her thirst.

She propped her tablet on the coffee table, put on a comforting film, and lay on the sofa to watch.

Maggie and the others waited for ages, but Lila still did not reply. In the end, they could only sigh and resign themselves to boiling dried noodles again.

They had not bought many supplies, and the government had not given much either. Every time they cooked, they only dared make a little.

At four in the afternoon, the three people living in the Basement gradually woke up.

It was not especially warm down there either. When Sophie woke, the tip of her nose felt cold.

She pulled on a thick fluffy set of pyjamas and used an electric pot to cook herself a bowl of porridge and one egg.

After gulping it down, her body warmed up a good deal.

She decided to modify her bed so that the sleeping area would be warmer.

Using wooden poles and steel pipes from the basement storage room, she built a tent frame over the bed.

She had brought down all the fabric she had at home.

She picked out some worn old sheets and duvet covers and draped them over the frame as makeshift tent cloth.

After briefly tidying the storage room, she took toilet paper and her stun baton and left.

The temperature across basement level one was about the same, so it did not feel much colder after she stepped out.

She went straight to the toilets on basement level one.

On her way back, she ran into Leo and Callum, who were carrying waste bags out to dump.

Leo had already crossed paths with her the night before. Although Sophie was still wary of him, she could more or less judge that he posed no real threat.

Through the plastic bags, Sophie could vaguely guess what was inside.

"You two..."

She pointed at the bags, hesitating.

"We dump waste over there. You can also use it later if you need to..." Leo began, but Sophie cut him off.

"There are toilets on basement level one. They're over there."

After saying that, Sophie held her breath and ran back to her storage room.

Whether the bags smelled or not, she did not want to breathe the air around them.

Leo and Callum stood where they were and looked at each other, both their eyes full of surprise.

What?

There were toilets on basement level one?

Neither of them said another word. In perfect, silent agreement, they headed in the direction Sophie had pointed.

When Sophie returned to her storage room, the thermometer showed five degrees above freezing.

She checked the online forums. Apparently, the outdoor temperature had already reached minus sixty-two.

It seemed the Basement really was a good place to shelter from the extreme cold.

Sophie locked the door and climbed back into the tent bed she had made.

The tent blocked out the cold air outside, and the electric blanket beneath the bedding was warm. Somehow, it no longer felt quite so cold.

Before long, Leo and Callum returned as well.

Callum's room needed no explanation. The heating pipe was still warm, so the room itself remained cosy.

Leo also put in a great deal of effort and completely separated the area where his mattress lay from the rest of the storage room.

He even built a square tent over the mattress to block out the cold air outside.
