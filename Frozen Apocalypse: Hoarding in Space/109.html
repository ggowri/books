Chapter 108

"Deal."

Walker leaned back comfortably against the sofa.

"I'll take you on every recovery mission."

He paused before adding another condition.

"But every time we come back, you'll need to contribute useful supplies to the refuge, just like everyone else."

"They won't be taken for free."

"You'll receive Survival Credits at the standard rate, and you can exchange those Credits for anything available in the refuge."

What a sly bastard.

Lila cursed him silently.

Of course he wasn't going to let her enjoy unlimited trips without expecting something in return.

Still, none of that showed on her face.

She simply smiled and nodded.

"That's fair, Captain Walker. I'll do my best."

As long as she did her best.

If she couldn't find anything worthwhile, that wasn't exactly her fault.

"When can you bring the grow lights?"

He wanted them as soon as possible.

The Indoor Grow Room couldn't wait.

"Any time."

Lila thought for a second.

"Actually... why don't I bring them on the day we head into the city?"

She wasn't naive enough to hand them over before the agreement was actually honoured.

What if he changed his mind afterward?

Walker understood exactly what she was thinking but didn't call her out on it.

"Fine."

"We leave in three days."

"And dress warmly."

That would be the next scheduled recovery mission.

Lila had no objections.

She nodded.

"Looking forward to working with you."

Walker straightened up and extended his hand.

"Likewise."

Lila shook it briefly.

She stood.

"Then I won't keep you any longer. I'll head back."

She turned toward the door.

"One more thing."

Walker's deep voice stopped her.

Lila froze.

"...Yes?"

Please don't tell me he's adding another condition.

"My name is Ethan Walker."

He said it with a faint smile.

Lila let out a silent breath of relief.

That was all?

For a second she'd thought he was about to ask for her vegetable grow pots too.

"I'm Lila."

"It's nice to meet you."

She introduced herself properly.

"I know."

A hint of amusement flickered across his face.

He had already learned her name during the investigation at the mountain house.

"Right."

"Well... I'll see you later, Captain."

She wisely decided not to linger.

Who knew whether he'd suddenly think of another demand?

Before he had the chance, she slipped out the door.

A fierce wind had picked up outside.

Combined with the brutal cold, every gust felt like invisible blades slicing through the air.

Lila tightened her hood, pressed her helmet firmly into place, shut her eyes, and pushed into the freezing wind.

She couldn't wait to get home.

She wanted to peel off every layer of heavy gear, collapse onto the sofa, binge a few episodes of something mindless, and snack on a bag of Spicy Snack Sticks.

Unfortunately...

She didn't get very far.

"Hold it."

A wooden club slammed across her path.

"You don't belong to this refuge."

"You grabbed our supplies and now you're just walking off?"

"Leave the grain here."

"Otherwise you're not going anywhere."

Lila looked up.

Three men stood in front of her.

Their patched clothing barely held together.

Their faces were blue from the cold.

"Seriously?"

She rolled her eyes.

"Get lost."

She stepped forward.

The man blocked her again.

"I said stop."

"Take that coat off."

"Why the hell should everyone else freeze while you're dressed like royalty?"

His jealousy had completely taken over.

All he wanted was to rip the expensive winter gear off her body and claim it for himself.

People nearby had already begun stopping to watch.

That made things inconvenient.

Lila didn't want to expose herself.

If these idiots actually tried grabbing her...

She would quietly release one of the iron skewers hidden inside the Vault.

With her current level of mental control, driving one straight through a kneecap would be effortless.

The moment the two filthy men stepped toward her, she subtly raised her palm.

Her target never left their knees.

Then a sharp voice cut through the tension.

"What the hell do you think you're doing?"

Sophie Hale charged in without hesitation.

Her kick slammed into one man's chest, sending him crashing backward.

She still held her application forms in one hand while spinning toward the second attacker.

Another kick.

Another man hit the ground.

Sophie had trained in kickboxing for years.

She ate well, stayed fit, and these half frozen thugs, shivering like newborn chicks, weren't even close to a challenge.

Since someone had already stepped in, Lila no longer needed to expose her own abilities.

She quietly lowered her hand.

The man with the club pointed at Sophie.

"What the hell is your problem?"

"She isn't even from our refuge."

"Whose side are you on?"

Sophie laughed.

"You expect me to take your side?"

She took two slow steps toward him.

"I've seen sewer rats with more dignity than you."

"And they're a hell of a lot easier to look at."

She lifted one boot.

The man's courage evaporated instantly.

"You..."

"You just wait!"

He stumbled backward before turning and sprinting away.

The other two scrambled to their feet and bolted after him.

Normally, intimidating lone women earned them an easy meal.

Today they'd picked the wrong target.

Sophie watched them disappear before turning back to Lila.

"You alright?"

"Those idiots have been causing trouble for ages."

"Now that the refuge has real rules, their days are numbered."

Lila nodded.

"I'm fine."

"Thank you."

It wasn't often people risked themselves to help strangers anymore.

Especially after the Collapse.

Sophie smiled.

"It was nothing."

"I just can't stand watching grown men gang up on a woman."

She almost reached out to pat Lila's shoulder.

Then she noticed the snow covering her gloves and stopped herself.

Looking at Sophie's eyes, Lila felt a strange sense of familiarity.

She was almost certain they'd crossed paths before in the Underground Car Park.

Another resident from Oakwood Estate.

"Either way..."

"Thank you."

"If you ever need help, find me."

"I owe you one."

Lila never liked leaving debts unpaid.

The freezing wind made long conversations impossible.

After exchanging a few more words, Lila left the refuge and began the climb back toward the Lower Vale Mansion.

The snow on the mountain had grown even deeper.

Going uphill proved far harder than the trip down.

By the time she reached halfway, she was breathing heavily.

"I seriously don't get why Grandma and Grandpa built a house halfway up a mountain."

"This climb is trying to kill me."

She blamed the location.

It never occurred to her that someone with stronger stamina wouldn't find the slope difficult at all.

Meanwhile, Sophie walked home in an unusually good mood, humming quietly to herself.

She had never been the type to fear trouble.

If nobody bothered her, she was perfectly happy living her own life.

But if trouble came knocking...

She had absolutely no problem knocking back.

Watching several grown men bully a young woman had crossed a line she simply couldn't ignore.

Now that the refuge had established proper laws, she had even fewer reasons to hold herself back.

If she passed the Legal Officer interview...

She would have the authority to deal with people like that properly.

By noon, Ryan and Evan had finally finished registering every applicant.

William called Walker over, and together they began reviewing the files.

Fortunately, the brothers had already filtered out most of the completely unsuitable candidates during registration.

The remaining stack looked far more manageable than the crowd they had faced that morning.

The two men carefully compared qualifications.

Applicants who met the standards stayed.

Everyone else was crossed off.

Two hours later, the interview shortlist was complete.

That afternoon, the refuge's loudspeaker broadcast the names one by one.

When Sophie heard her own name, she wasn't surprised in the slightest.

She genuinely had the qualifications.

Annabel, on the other hand, stood there completely stunned.

Wasn't Captain Walker supposed to come looking for her tonight?

Why was the interview happening this afternoon instead?
