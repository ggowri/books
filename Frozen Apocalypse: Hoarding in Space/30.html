Chapter 29

In the surveillance footage, Annabel was trying on clothes in the living room.

She was unhappy with every single outfit.

They were going to a high-end restaurant that evening to take photos, and she was terrified of wasting such a precious opportunity by failing to get pictures worth showing off.

In the end, she chose a pink strappy dress.

"That's too thin. I don't know what's going on with the weather, but the temperature's dropped suddenly. You can't wear that."

Edward was looking at the weather report on his phone and clearly did not approve.

"No. I'm wearing it. I'm not afraid of the cold. Looking good is what matters."

Annabel stamped her foot in frustration, then took the pink dress back to her room.

Seeing that he could not control her, Edward said nothing more.

It was summer, after all. How cold could it possibly get? If she wanted to wear it, let her wear it.

Maggie chose a deep burgundy dress with a plunging neckline. It was her first time going to such an expensive restaurant too, and she was determined to take beautiful photos to show off to their relatives.

When the time came, she would say Anna had earned money and taken her there.

Watching this, Lila's smile grew deeper and deeper.

Good. The less they wore, the better.

Just then, a message from Daniel arrived on her phone.

"Lila, I heard you're coming back tonight. Why didn't you tell me? I'll come and pick you up."

Annabel had probably told him.

"No need. I'm treating Edward, Maggie and Anna to dinner tonight. Do you want to come?"

Lila could even guess why Daniel had messaged her.

First, he wanted to maintain their relationship. Second, he wanted to sponge off this expensive dinner.

"I… would that be all right? Can I really come? Wouldn't it be a burden for you?"

Daniel absolutely wanted to go. As for him saying he was afraid of burdening Lila, that was nothing but a careful way of asking in advance whether she would be paying.

How could Lila not know what he was thinking?

"My treat. Order whatever you want. Of course it's all right. You're my boyfriend. Treating you to something nice is only natural."

Since she was lying anyway, Lila's current approach was simply to say whatever suited her.

She put away her phone and went to the patisserie next door, buying every dessert in the shop.

The shop manager was so pleased that she gave Lila two sets of the shop's finest dessert tableware.

Cream-yellow ceramic side plates, one steel knife, two steel forks and two delicate spoons.

She also threw in a thick stack of baking paper and plenty of beautifully packaged little boxes.

Who would not want to keep such a generous customer happy?

With the help of the patisserie staff, piles of desserts were carried into Lila's car.

Lila checked her balance.

After a full day of heavy spending, only $18,000 remained in her account.

She would leave that untouched, just in case.

Lila opened the surveillance feed and happened to see Maggie, Edward and Annabel leaving the flat in their finest clothes.

She immediately drove home.

As she took the lift upstairs, she carefully checked the feed again to make sure they really were no longer at home before daring to go in.

Once inside, everything looked exactly the same.

So familiar.

This was her flat. The home she had lived in for more than twenty years.

Now she had to harden her heart and say goodbye.

Lila went first to Maggie's bedroom and took the down duvets she had been eyeing for ages into the Vault before anything else.

The two duvets were of excellent quality. Not only were they filled with down, the outer fabric was silk.

Soft, smooth, warm and wonderfully comfortable.

They had been given to Maggie by the only successful relative in her family.

Maggie had never even been willing to open them and look, keeping them instead as part of Annabel's future dowry.

Lila was deeply grateful that Maggie had never used them. Now they had fallen smoothly into her hands.

Apart from the duvets, Lila stored everything within sight in the bedroom.

That included Maggie and Edward's clothes, wardrobes, bed and bedding.

Of course, Lila would never wear their clothes. But she could take them back and use them to make dog beds. She was not leaving them so much as a scrap that could keep them warm.

The drawers of Maggie's dressing table were locked. Inside were all the gold pieces she had saved over the years.

Lila could not open them, but that did not matter. She stored the entire dressing table in the Vault.

Once she got home, she could take her time figuring out how to open it.

Annabel's room was treated the same way.

As for Annabel's shabby little collection of cosmetics, Lila had no interest in them.

With a sweep of her hand, she knocked all the cosmetics to the floor.

Everything else was packed up whole and stored in the Vault.

When it came to her own room, Lila was much gentler.

She carefully put away her bed, quilt, dressing table, clothes together with the entire wardrobe, her bookshelves and books, and the carpet.

By the end, nothing remained in the room.

Naturally, she did not let the living room or kitchen escape either.

The sofa, coffee table, television, dining table, chairs, washing machine, microwave, oven, rice cooker, everything went into the Vault. Not even a pair of chopsticks was left behind.

By the time Lila had stripped the flat bare, there was nothing left except the marble floor tiles. It looked like a new flat that had not yet been furnished.

Lila checked inside and out to make sure nothing had slipped through the net, then clapped her hands in satisfaction.

She went to the sink, carefully washed the dust from her hands, closed the door, and left with effortless confidence.

It had not taken long to clear everything out.

Only half an hour.

It was exactly six o'clock now, the time she had agreed to meet them for dinner.

Of course, Lila had no intention of going.

She drove to the woods near the Ashton outskirts, then ordered a cab to take her to the car-hire office.

With the rain, it was difficult to get a ride. The app searched for around ten minutes before finally finding a driver.

She covered the chick and puppy cages with blankets, leaving only a small breathing gap, then locked the car door with peace of mind and got into the cab.

Half an hour later, Lila walked out of the car-hire office with a set of keys in her hand.

She had rented the best rugged 4x4 they had.

It was a new vehicle with excellent four-wheel drive and a high chassis. The rent was $600 a day, and she had booked it for three days.

Including the deposit, she paid $2,800 altogether.

By then, Lila's phone had already been bombarded with calls. Row after row of missed call notifications showed nothing but Annabel, Maggie and Daniel.

Looking at the long list of missed calls, Lila called Maggie back.

"Maggie, you go ahead and order first. The rain outside is heavy, and I'm stuck in traffic. I'll be there in about half an hour."

"Hurry up, then."

Lila's call finally allowed Maggie to breathe a sigh of relief.

Everyone waiting at the table also relaxed.

After hanging up, Lila rubbed her freezing hands together, switched off her phone, and drove the newly rented 4x4 back to where she had parked her old car.

There were no cameras in the woods, and there was barely anyone around.

She reached out, and the 4x4 was swallowed into the Vault.

Rental cars all had trackers fitted. Trackers went dead inside the Vault.

The Collapse had not truly begun yet. To be safe, keeping it inside the Vault was the best option.
