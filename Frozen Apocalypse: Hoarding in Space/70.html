Chapter 69

Afraid the milk in the jar would boil over, Lila quickly lifted it off the hob.
On the table sat a large, chubby egg roll and a cup of hot milk. Her breakfast.

The roll was packed with fillings. The beef sauce was fragrant. Lila finished the whole big roll, washing it down with the large cup of sweet hot milk.
Warm and satisfying.

At the bottom of the plate was one plain egg pancake, no salt. That was for the two puppies.
The pancake had cooled down. Lila tore it into small pieces with her hands and put them into the puppies' bowl.
They smelled it and came running. They ate greedily, snorting and grunting with enjoyment.

Lila finished the last of her hot milk and cleared away the breakfast plates.

The outdoor temperature had dropped to minus eighty. The house was a bit cold in the morning too.
When Lila had seen the two puppies curled up together sleeping, her heart had softened. She decided to add some extra warmth for them.

She planned to start by adding a roof to the dog bed. That would keep the dogs warmer when they slept.
Lila didn't need to look up any instructions. She took some wire from the Vault and bent it into an arched frame the size of the dog bed, covering about two thirds of it.

Next, she found a dress that Anna didn't wear much anymore. She cut the fabric into a single large piece, laid it over the wire frame, and stitched it tight.
The main part of the roof was done.

To make the roof more flexible, she sewed a row of buttons along the outer edge of the dog bed.
The wire loop at the bottom of the roof could clip right into them. When it was cold, she could put the roof on for warmth. When it was warm, she could take it off for ventilation. Easy to attach and remove anytime.

Once it was fitted, the dog bed looked a size bigger than before. The arched roof covered most of the bed, making it warm and cosy, giving it a real sense of security.

While Lila was working, the two puppies had been messing around and getting in the way. Now that the bed was finished, they were over the moon.
They scrambled to be the first inside, then lay there obediently, refusing to come out.

"Looks like you like it a lot."

The puppies' approval was the greatest compliment.
Lila then went to Edward's wardrobe and found a brand new cashmere jumper. Soft, thick, good quality.
She took scissors and cut the jumper apart into several large pieces of fabric.

Using the puppies' measurements, she made a cashmere sweater for each of them.
Her sewing skills were limited. For the legs, she simply cut two holes in the fabric that they could poke their feet through.

Puppies grew fast. Lila didn't plan to make more. She put the leftover fabric back in Edward's wardrobe. When the dogs got bigger, she could make new sweaters.

The two puppies didn't want to come out of their newly upgraded bed. Lila had to reach in and pull them out to put on their new sweaters.
It had to be said, the two little chubby round things looked even cuter in their light grey cashmere sweaters.

Best of all, neither Snowy nor Patches minded wearing clothes. They gave a hard shake when the sweaters first went on, but after that, they moved around freely.
Lila was very pleased with her work. Every time she made something with her own hands, she felt an immense sense of satisfaction.

The public toilet in the basement had broken. Whether the pipes had frozen from the extreme cold outside or the pressure of too many people using it, the waste just wouldn't flush down anymore.

Even though the basement wasn't warm, an indescribable stench hung in the air around the toilets.
Leo and Callum pinched their noses as they came out of the bathroom.

Only after they had walked a good distance away did Callum dare to gag. "Ugh. These people have no decency. If the toilets aren't working, stop using them. Now it's everywhere. After seeing that, I won't be able to eat for three days."

Leo wasn't much better. The back of his tongue was sour.

Mrs Harris hurried out of the toilet, her mind racing for a solution. She bumped straight into Callum, who was still retching.
"Ugh." The bump made Callum let out a loud retch.

"Are you alright, love? Did I make you sick?"

Callum's reaction frightened Mrs Harris.
Callum waved his hands quickly. "No, no. The toilets are absolutely disgusting. Made me feel ill."

Mrs Harris had just looked at the women's toilet. The situation there was indeed dire, but she couldn't imagine what the men's toilet must look like.
"Let me go back and think of something. Don't panic."

Mrs Harris hurried back and called a meeting with the estate staff.
They discussed for over half an hour before finally reaching a compromise.

The staff went upstairs and brought back massive rubbish bins.
From now on, each family would be given a chamber pot. Every morning, they would empty their pots into the large bins. The basement residents would take turns dumping the bins in the green areas of the estate.

When the announcement came out, people complained bitterly. No one wanted to empty human waste. But there was no better solution, so they had to accept it.

Now, more people were needed for above ground duties. In addition to cooking and boiling water, they also had to empty rubbish and waste.
Today, it was Edward's family's turn.

Mrs Harris, accompanied by two estate staff members, knocked on the Spencers' basement door.
Mrs Harris had notified so many families by now that she was completely numb. No small talk needed. She got straight to the point. "It's your family's turn for duty."

"Oh, right. And my daughter?"

Edward asked. Anna had been confined for a long time. He wasn't sure if she was allowed out.

"Yes, she's confined, but she still eats the shelter's food, doesn't she?"

If being confined meant no work, that would be a reward, not a punishment.
"You and Maggie will do the cooking. Your daughter will empty the waste bins."

According to the roster, that was the assignment.
Anna, handcuffed inside the room, vaguely heard Mrs Harris and Edward talking. She even heard her name mentioned, but she couldn't make out the details.

After Mrs Harris left, Edward unlocked Anna's handcuffs without changing his expression.
"Dad! I don't have to be tied up anymore?"

Anna was thrilled. She was finally getting her freedom back.
Her arms had been kept in the same position for so long. Now that they were free, they actually felt sore.

"It's our family's turn for duty. Your mother and I will cook. You'll empty the rubbish and the waste bins."

Edward stated the facts calmly.
"What?! You want me to empty waste bins? Do I have that right? Me, the prettiest girl in my class, pursued by countless rich boys, and now I have to empty waste bins?!"

Anna pointed at herself, her eyes full of disbelief.
Of course, the bit about being pursued by rich boys was entirely in her own head.
If a rich boy smiled at her or said a single word to her, in her mind, that meant he was pursuing her.

"Stop shouting. If you want to live, do as you're told."

Edward's eyes, full of murderous intent, bore into Anna. He was genuinely fed up with this idiot.
Anna flinched at her father's glare. She dared not speak another word.
