Chapter 78

People gradually woke up, many rising to grope through the darkness, trying to locate the source of smoke.

A sudden scream pierced the basement. "It's upstairs! The building's on fire!"

Mrs Harris, still half-dazed, scrambled into her clothes. Tears streamed freely as smoke stung her eyes. She grabbed her megaphone and shouted, "Fire! Fire! Quickly, put on your clothes and grab what you can...get out now!"

Her voice grew hoarse, choking on smoke as she coughed and shouted repeatedly, desperate to wake anyone who might be asleep.

Prompted by her warnings, more people quickly dressed, gathered their belongings, and rushed for the exit.

Sophie had already woken when the thunder first struck. When smoke began to pour in, she was ready. She donned her heavy coat, slung a large pack over her shoulder, and ran straight for the basement exit. Flames roared on the ground outside, the burning building standing like giant torches in the snow.

Sophie reached a hundred metres onto an open patch of ground, gasping for air, watching others escape the smoke-filled basement. Though the air was freezing, the surrounding fires made it feel slightly warmer. Yet the thunder overhead continued, threatening with each flash.

Leo and Cedric also made it outside, carrying their essential supplies, coughing and gasping through the blackened smoke and tears.

"This smoke is unbearable! I can't breathe," Cedric said, overwhelmed by the chaos.

"Getting out alive is luck enough," Leo replied, catching his breath.

Maggie and Annabel had been unconscious from smoke inhalation, only waking after Edward kicked them. By now, the basement was largely empty. Mrs Harris prepared to retreat. Flames licked the doorframe, which began to sway precariously.

"Mrs Harris! Get out! The doorframe's collapsing!" Sophie yelled, unable to stay silent at the sight of imminent danger.

Mrs Harris hesitated at the threshold, glancing inside. Coughing came in sharp bursts from within...Annabel and Maggie. Edward had already escaped; only Maggie was helping Annabel out slowly.

The lack of oxygen made their movements painfully slow. Mrs Harris gritted her teeth, dashed back inside, and dragged the two women out just as the heavy iron door collapsed. Annabel's leg was struck by the falling frame.

"Ah!" Annabel screamed, her cry mingling with the roar of the flames.

"Help her!" Mrs Harris bellowed, voice raw. People finally rushed to assist, pulling Annabel to safety.

Another group of survivors emerged, battered but alive. The thunderstorm still rumbled, though distant. People wept as they faced the fire, the homes they had built with years of labour now reduced to rubble.

Annabel screamed, her leg injured. Maggie, face blackened from soot and tears, cradled her protectively. Mrs Harris stood, stunned, wondering how she could safeguard the survivors with the basement gone.

Fortunately, the surrounding flames provided heat, preventing immediate freezing.

Elsewhere, Lila had been sleeping in her warm bed when the deafening thunder woke her. Heart racing, she hurried into her coat and looked outside. A massive bolt struck just beyond her yard. The iron gate remained unmoved, but the flash blinded her.

She bent to scoop up her two dogs, intending to retreat to the basement, but another bolt struck closer. Panic surged,there was no time to run. Lila froze, instinctively raising her hands as if to shield herself.

The lightning vanished as mysteriously as it appeared, sparing her. Relieved, she hugged her dogs and ran to the nearby cave. She switched on her torch, settling the pups inside.

Snowy and Patches explored the large, unfamiliar space with delight, sniffing everywhere. Lila exhaled, appreciating their ignorance of danger...they were happy.

Nervous, she looked up at the storm, hoping it would pass soon. She slumped to the ground, reaching into her Vault for a bottle of soda to calm herself. Only then did she notice it...a massive, thigh-thick bolt of lightning had appeared within her Vault, forming a new section on the right side. It crackled and shimmered menacingly.

Lila shivered, clutching the soda. "Big brother… how did you get in?" she whispered to the lightning, though it, of course, did not answer.

She retreated from the Vault, sipping her soda, contemplating the possibility that she had inadvertently drawn the lightning into her space when she had raised her hand earlier.
