Chapter 75

With supplies reduced, Mrs Harris had to reallocate ingredients for every meal. Less food meant the porridge had to be thinner, though it was still enough to keep them alive.

From the sticky porridge at the start of the Collapse, to the slightly varied mixture, it had now become a watery porridge dotted with whatever ingredients were left. No one could get full, but survival was assured.

Those least affected by the changes were Callum, Leo, and Sophie. Their stockpiles were sufficient to last months, even without the communal pots. Annabel had little, but she remained confident that Cedric would eventually grant her control. In the meantime, she relied on the basement stores, and gradually Edward and Maggie trusted in her certainty.

Within a few days, they had eaten through every last reserve in the basement. When the personal stock was gone, they had no choice but to rely on the communal meals.

"Absolutely disgusting! Once I get control, I'll dump all this swill," Annabel muttered, swallowing a mouthful of the watery porridge. Edward and Maggie stayed silent, anticipating the day when she would take charge.

Meanwhile, Lila's life couldn't have been more comfortable in comparison. Lying in her giant dog bed, she counted the portions of hotpot in her Vault: 117 bowls in total.

She couldn't understand why she hadn't stockpiled more, enough for a lifetime. She was tempted, yet restrained, afraid to eat the last portion and regret it later.

Resolute, she sat up, determined to make her own hotpot.

Dressing warmly, she stepped out of the east parlour. With temperatures outside at minus eighty degrees, the kitchen and main hall were chilled. Extra layers were essential.

Snowy and Patches had grown and were more persistent than ever. Seeing Lila move out, they wriggled through the door to follow.

Lila fetched a small clean basin and selected a handful of enoki mushrooms, five slices of lotus root, half a potato, half a pack of duck blood, various hotpot balls, a small portion of wide noodles, a handful of greens, and some coriander. She washed everything carefully and filled a small pot halfway with water, placing it on the little hob.

Squatting beside it, she lit the fire. The kitchen air was cold, and her cheeks tingled, but the red flames warmed her quickly. She stretched her hands, letting the heat reach them.

Suddenly, something pressed against her feet. She looked down: Snowy and Patches, plump little bodies pressed close, warming themselves by the fire.

"You two really like the attention," Lila said, laughing. She stood and began preparing the broth.

The two pups watched her, tails wagging. Lila added a small portion of hotpot base from the Vault and a carton of milk. Pausing as she poured, she noticed the pups watching. The carton wasn't empty, so she poured the remaining milk into their bowls.

Snowy and Patches lapped it up eagerly. Their shiny eyes then returned to Lila, silently begging for more.

"All gone now," she said, shaking her head and waiting for the water to boil.

When the pot bubbled, red oil swirled with the milk, forming a rich, fragrant base. She added the hardy ingredients first, then turned to prepare the dipping sauce: minced garlic, cumin, Sichuan pepper oil, sesame oil, and vinegar, mixed together.

With the sauce ready, she added the quicker-cooking ingredients to the pot. Once cooked, she poured the sauce over everything, filling the air with an appetising aroma.

Lila inhaled deeply. "This is as good as any shop-bought hotpot," she said, pleasantly surprised.

The pups barked in excitement. Lila ladled a bowl of water and briefly soaked four hotpot balls, rinsing off excess sauce. She carried the hotpot back to the east parlour and called the pups to join her. Two balls each, just enough to satisfy their cravings.

Patches shivered with excitement as he nibbled, while Snowy calmly devoured his portion.

"Patches is the greediest of the two," Lila observed, nudging him lightly with her shoe. She sat back at her little table and began eating, enjoying the fragrant, spicy hotpot.

She even thought that once the Collapse ended, she could open her own hotpot shop.

After the meal, Lila played with the pups again. Today's training was "lie down" and "stand up." Naturally, Snowy mastered it first. After half an hour, Patches finally learned to lie down. Lila abandoned teaching him to stand, exhausted.

She stood, brushing imaginary fur off her hands. "Go play!" she called, putting on her coat to continue training her Vault shooting skills.

Ten minutes of practice each day had already improved her precision. Anything within sight could be struck, though she needed more force. At present, she could guarantee a lethal shot only within a metre.

"I need more practice," Lila muttered. Weapons would complement her abilities. She recalled a cabinet full of round barbecue skewers during her scavenging.

Closing her eyes, she retrieved a small handful from the Vault...at least 200 iron skewers. She shot one at the practice wooden board. It pierced over two centimetres deep, the force impressive enough to startle her.

With the efficiency of the skewers, she realised her Vault abilities could indeed be deadly.
