Chapter 110

Anna replayed Ethan's words from earlier that day.

Anyone who started a fight would be expelled from the refuge immediately.

Maggie had been outside for ages already, yet now she expected Anna to lie and claim she'd only just stepped out.

No chance.

Why should Anna risk getting dragged into it?

She shrank back, lowered her head, and slipped into the room without another word.

"Anna! Anna! Tell them! Come on, say something!"

Panic crept into Maggie's voice as she shouted toward the doorway.

No one came back out.

"Enough yelling. Get the Legal Officer over here."

The patrol leader turned to one of his men.

A few minutes later, Oliver Prescott arrived.

Seeing Sophie already standing there, he frowned.

"Officer Hale's here already. Why did you call me in such a rush?"

"I'm involved in the case."

Sophie's voice carried a trace of helplessness after remaining silent for so long.

The patrol leader scratched the back of his head.

"So... who's supposed to make the ruling?"

"You are," Sophie said with a quiet sigh.

By refuge regulations, anyone who deliberately attacked another resident was to be expelled.

Out in this weather, though, expulsion was practically a death sentence.

Oliver had only started the job today. His thinking still belonged to the old world.

To him, assault was serious, but not something deserving certain death.

"Officer Hale... what about this?"

He stood there thinking for quite a while before finally settling on a compromise.

"Maggie Spencer intentionally assaulted another resident and provoked violence. Since this is her first offence, the sentence is one day of expulsion from the refuge. After returning, she will perform ten days of unpaid street-clearing duty. Any repeat offence will result in permanent expulsion."

That was the whole point of having Legal Officers.

If every mistake automatically meant execution, there would be no reason for the position to exist.

Sophie frowned.

Personally, she'd have preferred to see Maggie disappear for good.

Still, if that was Oliver's judgment, she could live with it.

"What? One day outside?"

Maggie's face drained of colour.

"That's sending me out there to die! Officer Prescott, you've got to help me! I didn't hurt anybody. You can't seriously throw me out there to freeze to death!"

"Take her out."

Oliver waved a dismissive hand.

He had porridge simmering back home and wasn't about to let it burn.

The patrol escorted Maggie straight through the refuge gates and tossed her outside.

With the excitement over, everyone scattered back indoors before the cold could steal any more body heat.

Sophie headed home as well.

She lit the kitchen range and poured that day's ration of mixed grains into a pot.

She needed to eat.

The stronger she became, the better chance she'd have of fighting back the next time someone came after her.

Then the anger returned.

She slammed another piece of firewood onto the floor.

She hated Anna's entire family.

Every last one of them.

Hopefully Maggie froze solid before morning.

Even if she survived, one miserable day outside ought to teach her something.

Using the warm water she'd set aside earlier, Sophie gave herself a quick wash.

After finishing a steaming bowl of porridge, she carefully lay down on the heated bed.

Her back still ached from the attack.

She couldn't afford to let herself get blindsided like that ever again.

She'd had a knife hidden inside her sleeve the whole time.

She simply hadn't drawn it.

The moment she pulled a blade, it would've become mutual combat.

Maggie might have escaped punishment entirely, and Sophie could easily have lost her own job.

Turning those thoughts over in her mind, she gradually drifted to sleep.

Anna, meanwhile, remained wide awake.

Wrapped tightly in her quilt, she sat at the edge of the heated bed, fidgeting nervously.

"Mom completely lost her mind."

"Sophie's a Legal Officer now. How could she actually lay hands on her?"

"This isn't going to come back on me, is it?"

"I still want to compete for one of those refuge jobs. Sooner or later people are going to freeze to death, and somebody will have to fill the vacancies."

She rambled anxiously from one worry to the next.

Edward stared at her as though she had finally snapped.

"Stop overthinking it."

"It won't affect you."

"She made her own stupid decision. She can deal with the consequences herself."

With that, he climbed fully dressed beneath the blankets.

The heated bed helped, but the room itself remained bitterly cold.

"What if something happens to Mom?"

Only then did Anna remember to worry about Maggie's safety.

"If she freezes to death, that's on her."

Edward had completely run out of patience with his wife.

Anna slumped with a miserable expression.

"If Mom dies, who's going to cook for me or keep the heated bed going?"

"I was counting on her to earn Survival Credits too."

There was no way she was hiking into the mountains on an injured leg just to gather firewood.

Outside the refuge, Maggie was already shaking uncontrollably.

She'd been hiding in the corner waiting for Sophie long enough to become chilled to the bone.

Now it was even worse.

She looked around.

Every direction was nothing but endless snowfields.

Where was she supposed to survive for an entire day?

Then, through the darkness, she spotted what looked like a farmhouse higher up the mountain.

If someone lived there, she might still have a chance.

Ignoring her numb legs, Maggie staggered uphill.

The distance wasn't far.

About ten minutes later, she reached the property.

Looking at the several-metre-high walls surrounding the place, she felt hope return.

Someone had to be living here.

Bang. Bang. Bang.

"Hello? Please! Somebody help me!"

She screamed until her throat felt ready to tear apart.

Lila had fallen asleep halfway through a film.

The sudden shouting jolted her awake.

Snowy and Patches let out low warning growls, both staring straight toward the front gate.

"So annoying."

Lila shifted on the sofa, rolled over, and closed her eyes again.

She didn't even have to guess.

Whoever was outside had obviously been expelled from the refuge.

Ethan had made it perfectly clear during today's meeting.

Anyone caught fighting would be thrown out.

Now, only a few hours later, someone was already pounding on her gate.

It had to be one of the people the refuge had kicked out.

If it had been wandering survivors, they'd head straight for the massive government refuge instead of some isolated farmhouse.

Lila had no intention of getting involved.

Anyone expelled by the refuge was almost certainly trouble.

She wasn't about to invite that into her home.

The desperate cries continued.

Eventually, she put on her headphones, turned on her bedtime audiobook, and went right back to sleep.

After waiting for ages without any response, Maggie pressed her face against the crack in the iron gate.

Everything inside was pitch black.

So nobody lived here after all.

She glanced at the towering wall.

There was no way she could climb over it.

Forget it.

By now, her hands and feet had gone completely numb.

Looking around once more, she noticed another farmhouse on the eastern slope overlooking the refuge.

A faint light glowed through one of its windows.

Clinging to the last shred of her survival instinct, Maggie stumbled toward it.

Half an hour later, she could no longer feel her legs.

Her face had gone completely numb as well.

At last, she reached the gate.

With stiff, frozen arms, she pounded against it with all the strength she had left.

"Please... help me..."

Her voice was no longer the desperate roar it had been earlier.

Even so, it was still loud enough for whoever was inside to hear.
