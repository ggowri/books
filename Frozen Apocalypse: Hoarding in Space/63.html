Chapter 62

"I didn't mean it! I didn't mean it!"

Anna was only jolted back to reality when the security guard grabbed her arms. She shook her head, muttering to herself.

"You, take over the serving!" Mrs Harris could see how severe the wound was. She handed her ladle to another staff member and hurried over. "Get the medical kit! Find some hemostatic!"

Mrs Harris leaned in for a look. She couldn't bring herself to look a second time.
The gash on Maggie's face was so deep she could see the teeth underneath.
Whether she lived or died was up to fate now.

"Is there a doctor? Anyone a doctor?"

Mrs Harris called out to the crowd twice. No one answered.
Maggie was in so much pain she passed out on the spot.

There were no doctors among the onlookers. Mrs Harris could only disinfect the wound as best she could, then dust it with haemostatic powder. At last, the bleeding stopped on that half of the face.
She wrapped the wound in thick gauze. "That's all I can do. Medical supplies are limited, and there's no ambulance coming."

Edward had finished his meal back in their basement and noticed that neither Maggie, Anna, nor Daniel had returned. He went out to look for them.
He found a crowd gathered around the serving area. Anna was pinned by the arm by a guard.
He pushed forward and saw Maggie lying on the floor, a huge pool of blood beside her.

"What happened?"

Edward sounded a bit anxious, but instead of rushing in, he asked the onlookers at the edge of the crowd.

"Oh dear, her own daughter stabbed her right through the face. From the eye to the ear. Cut the whole cheek open."

The man speaking winced as he described it.
"Nasty business. You're lucky you came late. If you'd seen it, you wouldn't be able to eat."

Another onlooker shook his head.

"Family! Is the family here?" Mrs Harris shouted into the crowd.

"Here." Edward stepped forward calmly. "Help me carry her back."

Several security staff came forward. Two of them took one arm each and dragged Maggie back to their basement.

"Dad! Help me, help me, Dad!"

Anna saw Edward and cried out, hope flashing across her face.
Edward was furious. He slapped Anna hard across the face, once, twice. "You animal!"

Anna was already upset. Being slapped made her burst into tears. "Wahhh! Why are you hitting me? She's the one who ran out blindly! She walked into my knife, so how is it my fault? Everyone's blaming me, and even you think I'm wrong!"

Anna was beside herself with rage. She genuinely didn't understand. Her mother had run out on her own. Why was everyone blaming her?

Daniel saw all of this. He knew it was his fault.
Shaking, he went back to the basement, grabbed his bedding, emptied his box of biscuits and instant noodles, wrapped everything in his quilt, and fled to the farthest corner of the underground car park.
From now on, he would live here alone. He would never go back to that basement again.
He just hoped no one would come looking for trouble.

Sophie hadn't left the scene yet. She frowned at the fool in front of her and shook her head.
"She was trying to attack me. I dodged. Mrs Harris, what are you going to do about this? If it's not sorted, she'll come after me again."

Sophie needed this threat eliminated.

"Lock her up. She's not allowed to leave your basement again. If she comes out one more time, I'll ask my superiors to evict your whole family."

Mrs Harris didn't have any better solution. She brought out the handcuffs her superiors had given her and handed them to the security guard.
Ignoring Anna's crying and screaming, the guard dragged her back to their basement and cuffed her securely to the steel bars of the window security grille.
Just like in the previous life, when they had cuffed Lila.

"Let me go! I didn't do anything wrong! Why are you cuffing me? That cow was coming on to Daniel. She deserved to die!"

Anna was screaming like a madwoman. She didn't even glance at Maggie, still lying unconscious on the floor.

"Sorry for the trouble."

Edward managed a polite word as the staff were leaving.

"Keep your daughter under control. If she disrupts the basement order again, I really will throw you all out."

Mrs Harris wasn't her usual agreeable self. She gave him a stern warning and left.
Edward slammed the basement door shut. The loud bang made Anna flinch into silence.

"Dad..." Anna said timidly.

Without a word, Edward picked up a stick and started beating Anna hard across the body.

"Ow!"

The pain made Anna shriek.
As if screaming loudly enough would make him stop.
Edward looked around, grabbed a towel, rolled it up, and stuffed it into Anna's mouth.
Then he raised the stick again and kept beating her viciously.

He only stopped when Anna had no strength left and her head began to droop.
Anna was wearing several layers of thermal underwear. Even though he had beaten her so hard, no blood seeped through.
No external injuries. All internal.

Edward put the stick back in its place. Then he noticed that the bed nearest the door was gone.
"Bloody hell. That ungrateful rat."

He kicked the wall in anger.
Why did he have to end up with all the idiots?

The people who had been listening by the door had a great time.
"What a family."
"The dad's violent, the mum's an idiot, and the daughter's a nutter."
"Lucky me. I live right next to them."

The Spencers' reputation was thoroughly ruined in the basement.

Two hours later, Maggie woke up in agony.
It was a true knife wound, raw and burning as if scrubbed with coarse sandpaper. The piercing pain pulled her back to consciousness.
She opened her eyes, trembling, and realised she was lying in the basement.

"Awake?"

Edward's voice was cold.
Maggie looked at him and saw the anger on his face.
It hurt too much to speak. Edward cursed her again: "Idiot!"

"Mum! You're awake! Please help me! They've locked me up!"

Anna rattled the arm cuffed to the metal bars, crying with snot and tears streaming down her face.
Maggie's heart broke at the sight. She tugged weakly at her husband's trouser leg. "Ah... ah..."

Edward jerked his leg back. "You've spoiled her rotten. One more mess and we'll all be thrown out."

Hearing this, Maggie let go.
Her daughter was still a child. Wasn't it normal to spoil her a bit?
But the pain on her face was terrible. Once the wound healed in a few days, she would have to go raid someone else's basement for medicine.
Stock up, in case she got hurt again.

"Daniel's run off. That bastard."

Edward's breathing was sharp with anger. He slapped his thigh.

"No way! Daniel loves me. He wouldn't just leave."

Anna couldn't believe it.

"From now on, you'd better behave. If you drag us down again, I'll kill you myself!"

Edward's teeth were clenched. Anna was too frightened to say another word.
