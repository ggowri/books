Chapter 119

Only after confirming the apartment was completely empty did Lila finally relax.

She took her time looking around.

The place was almost unnaturally spotless.

It looked as though no one had ever lived here.

There wasn't a speck of dust.

No trace of daily life.

No signs anyone had ever cooked a meal.

Either the owners had never moved in after finishing the renovation...

Or some wealthy family owned so many properties that this one had simply sat vacant.

To test her theory, she headed straight for the kitchen.

Half the room was wrapped in custom Italian cabinetry.

Elegant.

Minimalist.

Expensive.

Unfortunately, those cabinets were permanently fixed to the walls.

No matter how much she liked them, they weren't going anywhere.

Near the window, however, stood three freestanding Italian display cabinets.

One large cabinet in the centre.

Two smaller ones on either side.

Wearing gloves, Lila gave one a gentle push.

It was sturdy but not bolted down.

Perfect.

The cabinet bodies were crafted from waterproof hardwood.

The upper sections featured crystal clear tempered glass doors, displaying everything inside at a glance.

The lower sections held deep soft closing drawers fitted with premium German stainless steel hardware.

Behind the glass sat an immaculate collection of fine bone china.

Dinner plates.

Soup bowls.

Side plates.

Tea cups.

Coffee cups.

Every piece gleamed with an ivory translucence almost like polished jade.

Each rim carried a subtle band of gold.

Everything was absolutely brand new.

Matching premium stainless steel knives, forks, and spoons had been carefully arranged beside them.

Lila picked up one spoon.

It felt satisfyingly heavy.

A complete luxury set that had clearly never been used.

Her mouth slowly fell open.

"I just hit the jackpot..."

Only then did she remember why she'd entered the kitchen in the first place.

She hurried over to the built in refrigerator.

It was a massive double door model finished in brushed silver.

Its exterior remained perfectly clean, with nothing but the manufacturer's discreet logo in one corner.

She opened the doors.

The interior was completely empty.

Every shelf looked factory fresh.

Not even a single water mark remained.

It had obviously never been switched on.

Under the circumstances, an empty refrigerator excited her even more than a full one.

An unused fridge.

Brand new dinnerware.

That confirmed it.

Nobody had ever lived here.

Anything she took would effectively be untouched.

And honestly...

Who wouldn't want a luxury double door refrigerator?

Without another thought, she waved her hand.

The entire refrigerator disappeared into the Vault.

Next went the display cabinets.

Complete with every single piece of fine china inside.

Opening the remaining cupboards, she found even more treasures.

A brand new air fryer.

A built in oven.

A microwave.

A soy milk maker.

A rice cooker.

An electric griddle.

Two unused nonstick frying pans.

Two premium ceramic casserole pots.

One by one, everything vanished into the Vault.

Lila couldn't help smiling.

She'd intentionally avoided buying too many kitchen appliances before the Collapse.

Finding them for free now felt even better.

An entire matching knife block sat neatly arranged on the countertop.

She didn't even hesitate.

Whether expensive or ordinary, every knife disappeared into the Vault with a flick of her hand.

Once the kitchen had been completely emptied, she stepped into the dining area.

In the centre stood a long luxury stone dining table finished in soft grey.

Its natural patterns flowed like drifting clouds.

The legs combined solid brass with black walnut wood, creating a piece that looked both elegant and immovable.

Lila tried lifting one end.

It didn't budge.

"Now that's quality."

She grinned.

Anything that heavy automatically qualified as premium in her eyes.

Six matching dining chairs surrounded the table.

Each featured black walnut frames with thick oil treated buffalo leather seats.

She pressed a finger into one cushion.

The leather remained supple despite the brutal cold.

Even the protective plastic film had never been peeled away.

There wasn't much left to think about.

The six chairs disappeared.

Then the dining table followed.

Nearby stood a custom built wine cabinet.

It looked just as luxurious.

Several expensive bottles rested inside.

Unfortunately, some of the red wine and champagne bottles had burst as the frozen liquid expanded.

Dark wine had frozen into deep crimson blocks filled with shards of ice.

Some remained inside the cabinet.

Others had spilled onto the floor.

In one corner, however, several bottles of single malt whisky and stronger spirits remained perfectly intact.

Their freezing points were much lower.

Among the destruction, they looked especially valuable.

Lila removed the shattered bottles and broken glass.

Then she stored the entire wine cabinet together with the surviving bottles.

It still had stains and frozen residue clinging to it.

She didn't care.

She had plenty of time to clean it once she got home.

Furniture built to this standard would have been far beyond her budget before the Collapse.

Now that it was free, there was no way she was leaving it behind.

Leaving the kitchen and dining area, she entered the living room.

A minimalist Italian three seat sofa dominated the centre of the space.

Its frame had been carved from solid North American black walnut with no visible joints.

The wood grain shimmered like polished black jade.

The cushions were wrapped in thick full grain buffalo leather treated specifically for cold climates.

Even temperatures below minus eighty degrees hadn't caused the leather to crack or stiffen.

Lila absolutely loved it.

In front of the sofa sat a coffee table carved from a single slab of natural blue stone.

Its polished surface looked almost translucent.

The matte black titanium base was formed as one seamless piece.

Cold.

Luxurious.

Perfectly scratch free.

"This would be a crime to leave behind."

Her hands almost trembled with excitement.

Before the Long Freeze, she'd never have spent this kind of money on furniture.

Now she could own it without paying a penny.

The sofa.

The coffee table.

Even the rug beneath them.

Everything disappeared into the Vault.

Only the bedrooms remained.

The master bedroom featured another solid black walnut bed that clearly matched the rest of the furniture.

The headboard was upholstered in matching buffalo leather.

Thick.

Pristine.

Built to withstand extreme cold.

The seven zone pocket spring mattress still wore its factory plastic wrapping.

No one had ever slept on it.

The matching bedside tables were equally untouched.

Lila collected the entire bedroom set in one sweep.

The wardrobe had been built into the wall.

It stood mostly empty.

Inside, however, she found two unopened silk quilts and two sets of brand new silk pyjamas.

Into the Vault they went.

The curtains felt exceptionally high quality as well.

She stored those too.

Only the bathroom remained.

Even the washing machine was still wrapped in its original protective plastic.

Unused.

Naturally, it joined everything else inside the Vault.

Within minutes, the once luxurious apartment had become completely empty.

Lila shifted her awareness into the Vault, looking over the brand new collection of top tier furniture now waiting there.

The corners of her lips curved upward ever so slightly.

Who could have imagined it?

After the end of the world, she'd somehow furnished her home with a complete set of luxury designer furniture without spending a cent.

She glanced around the apartment one last time.

Nothing worthwhile remained.

Since nobody had ever moved in, there were no personal belongings or decorative items.

Those things only appeared after a house became a home.

She had already gained far more than she'd expected.

There was nothing left to regret.
