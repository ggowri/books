Chapter 43

Early the next morning, Lila woke naturally again in her wonderfully warm bed.

She glanced at the thermometer.

Nineteen degrees indoors.

The fire in the inglenook fireplace had gone out, so it was normal for the room to feel slightly cool.

Lila pulled on her padded jacket and trousers. The first thing she did was take firewood from the Vault and relight the fireplace.

The second thing was to light the kitchen range connected to the heated hearth bench.

Barring any surprises, during the Long Freeze, the first thing she would have to do every time she woke up would be to light the fireplace and the range.

Lila sighed helplessly.

"If only I could go inside the Vault. Then I'd never have to deal with firewood and coal again."

Even as she said it, her hands did not stop moving.

Lighting both fires and sweeping the floor clean counted as quite a lot of physical effort for her.

After that brief burst of work, her stomach was already beginning to feel empty.

She went to the bathroom for a quick wash, then decided to make herself noodles for breakfast.

She filled a saucepan with the right amount of water and set it on the range.

Once the water boiled, she added a packet of tomato and egg instant noodles, then cracked in an egg to poach.

A simple, delicious breakfast was ready.

After finishing the whole bowl of noodles, Lila felt warm and comfortable all over. A thin sheen of sweat had even appeared on her skin.

The indoor temperature had reached twenty-four degrees.

She washed two cloths in warm water and began carefully wiping down the heated hearth bench and the furniture.

Meanwhile, people in the city were waking up too.

With the government heating running, everyone had slept well that night.

The extreme cold had only just arrived, so every household still had food.

Staying in warm rooms with heating, eating what they still had at home, life felt comfortable enough.

Most people were not even bothering to ration their food. They were all waiting eagerly for the free supplies to be distributed that afternoon.

Of course, deciding who would go downstairs to collect them was also a major matter.

At Anna and Maggie's flat, after everyone discussed it, the person chosen was still Maggie.

Edward was the head of the family and had no desire to suffer through it. Anna was their only daughter, and no one could bear to let her endure hardship. As for Daniel, Anna could not bear to let him go, so Maggie had no choice but to go along with it.

At three o'clock in the afternoon, a heavy-duty lorry with excellent performance drove into the estate from outside.

Oakwood Estate Management tagged everyone in the group chat, saying distribution would begin with Building One.

Each building would have only fifteen minutes to collect supplies. Anyone who missed their slot would get nothing that day.

Everyone rushed to put on clothes, piling every thick garment in the house onto the person going outside.

Maggie was no exception.

Two layers of thermal underwear, five layers of long johns and thermal tops, then two heavy padded coats on top.

Anna kept her eyes fixed on the messages from Estate Management. The moment their building was called, Maggie shot out of the flat.

When she reached the ground floor of the block, residents were already waiting there. Maggie was still near the back of the queue.

Each household received one leek, one frozen cabbage, one kilo of rice and three packets of instant noodles.

That was the free ration given to residents for three days.

Maggie looked at the meagre supplies in her hands and said in disbelief, "Is this it? Who's supposed to live on this?"

"Every household gets the same. Move along. Don't make trouble. Take it and go."

The worker distributing supplies was wearing a thick expedition down parka. He waved her away, clearly unwilling to get dragged into an argument.

When the others heard Maggie speak, they joined in.

"Mate, this really is too little. There are five people in my family. How is this meant to last three days?"

"This is a difficult period. The point isn't to fill you up. It's to keep you alive. Anyone who keeps causing trouble won't be getting supplies next time."

The worker was thoroughly fed up.

Other people could hide in their warm homes and wait out the extreme cold. He had left his wife and children behind and was risking his life to come out and work.

Not only were these people ungrateful, they were complaining.

He truly had no patience left.

Seeing his attitude, the residents retreated resentfully.

The main issue was not anything else. It was simply too cold outside. Every extra second made their whole bodies ache with cold.

After collecting their supplies, people began complaining in the group chat again.

"Why is there so little each time they distribute supplies? In weather this cold, we risk our lives going outside, and what we get isn't even worth the freezing."

"Is this all the government has stored? Who can survive on this?"

"If they're still giving out this little in three days, I'm not going down to collect it."

The same people who had thanked the country yesterday had completely forgotten themselves. Their words were harsher than the last.

Just then, Estate Management received an official notice from above and forwarded it to the group chat.

Explanation on Supply Distribution During the Extreme Cold Period and Proposal for Collective Response

The duration of this extreme cold event remains highly uncertain.

Based on the most cautious long-term planning, the Council Emergency Reserves Department is distributing limited supplies with careful precision. The core goal is to ensure stable support throughout the entire extreme cold period and avoid supply interruptions later on.

This is to help everyone make it through the full duration of the emergency. We hope all residents will actively cooperate so that we can overcome this difficult time together.

"That sounds nice, but I bet most of the supplies have gone to the rich and powerful."

"Don't say things like that. You'll get yourself arrested."

"Everyone, stop complaining. It's free. Just eat sparingly. It's better than nothing."

Although the group chat was noisy and full of all kinds of opinions, once this notice appeared, everyone understood one thing.

The extreme cold period would not be short.

Perhaps the Long Freeze really had arrived.

For a while, panic spread through people's hearts.

Just then, a new notice appeared across major social platforms, and it made everyone's already suspended hearts drop like stones.

"Due to dangerously low outdoor temperatures, all large supermarkets and shops will close from today."

The news landed like a thunderclap, completely shattering people's psychological defences.

All at once, claims that the Collapse had arrived spread wildly online. The previous posts arguing against it vanished without a trace.

When Maggie carried the supplies back inside, her hands were already frozen stiff.

She slowly put the supplies down. Anna immediately rushed over.

"What good food did they give us? Can we make braised pork? Daniel said he wants braised pork."

Anna crouched down and rummaged through the bag. Apart from three packets of instant noodles, there was only a rock-hard frozen cabbage and some rice.

"Why is there nothing decent? And instant noodles are the thing I hate most."

Anna stood up, kicked the bag angrily, then sat back down on the sofa and continued playing on her phone.

Edward glanced towards the doorway. The supplies were more or less what the group chat had said. There really was very little.

He frowned, withdrew his gaze, and continued searching for news online.

Maggie stood alone by the doorway.

After the worst of the cold faded from her body, she silently bent down, picked up the bag, and placed it on the kitchen cupboard.

Not a single person cared about her.

If this had been before, back when Lila was still there, Lila would definitely have come over to ask whether she was frozen through. She would also have taken the bag from her without being asked.

In previous winters, whenever Maggie came home, that was exactly what Lila used to do.
