Chapter 59

The Long Freeze had worn people down until they barely looked like themselves.

Take Anna, for example. Before the extreme cold arrived, she had loved keeping clean and dressing up. Now her hair was a mess, her face was grimy, and there was an unpleasant smell about her that no one wanted to mention.

Sophie was the complete opposite. Even in the Collapse, she had looked after herself properly.

Because she ate well every day, Sophie's complexion was healthy and rosy. She looked alert, and her clothes were thick, clean and tidy.

When Sophie stepped out, she heard the commotion from Daniel and Anna and turned back instinctively. She happened to see Daniel dragging Anna outside.

Sophie lived alone and had no companions. She did not want to invite trouble, so after one glance, she turned away and went to queue for food.

Daniel, however, lost focus the moment he saw Sophie and let go of Anna's hand.

In his eyes, she was beautiful. Her brows were softly curved and dark, and her black almond-shaped eyes were bright and deep. Even without make-up, her skin was pale and rosy.

Her clothes were thick and clean, yet they did not make her look bulky at all. Instead, they made her seem tall and slender.

Most importantly, she looked well. One glance was enough to tell she was not short of supplies.

"Daniel! You're hurting me!"

Only when Anna's syrupy, affected voice rang out did Daniel finally come back to himself and withdraw his gaze.

"Sorry."

He left that single word behind, then hurried towards the food queue with his lunchbox.

Anna stood frozen in place.

Daniel had been so forceful just a moment ago. Why had he suddenly stopped caring about her?

Daniel did not care about her any more.

The thought left Anna feeling strangely lost.

Daniel reached the food queue, and sure enough, he saw Sophie.

There had been only a few seconds between them. Sophie was standing at the very end of the line.

Daniel walked straight up behind her.

"Hello. I live next door to you. Are you living by yourself?"

Daniel glanced behind him. Once he was sure Anna had not followed, he quickly tried to start a conversation.

Sophie looked at him warily and frowned, but said nothing. She turned back around and did not look at him again.

Daniel knew that under these circumstances, any girl living alone would be highly cautious, so he tried to make himself seem as helpful as possible.

He spoke again.

"I only mean that since we live close by, if you're on your own and need anything, you can come to me any time."

This time, Sophie did not even turn her head. She gave no response at all.

Daniel assumed she simply had not seen his face properly.

He looked decent enough, and he had always been confident in his appearance.

He smoothed the slightly overgrown hair at his forehead, licked his cracked lips, cleared his throat and stepped round in front of Sophie.

"It's good to be cautious during the Collapse, but you shouldn't shut out kind people completely."

He was a little taller than Sophie, so he looked down at her slightly, putting on a tender, indulgent expression.

Sophie might have looked young, but she had been a lawyer for several years. She could usually tell at a glance whether someone was decent or not.

She decided at once that Daniel was a hypocrite.

"I suggest you stay away from me. Otherwise, don't blame me for what happens."

Sophie lowered her voice, speaking quietly enough that only the two of them could hear.

"You..." Daniel grew irritated.

Why was this woman refusing to play along?

All he wanted was to find a single woman with plenty of supplies, live off her, and get away from Edward's family.

"Daniel! What are you doing?"

Anna's sharp voice cut Daniel off.

Only then did Daniel realise he was standing a little too close to Sophie, and he quickly stepped back.

"Anna, I was just saying something. Let's queue properly."

Daniel stood beside Anna, afraid she would cause another scene.

But of course Anna was not going to let it go.

She stormed up to Sophie.

"So it was you trying to lure Daniel away. With a face like that, I bet you're used to going after other people's men."

Sophie folded her arms and looked Anna up and down, making Anna feel uneasy all over.

"What are you looking at?" Anna snapped, growing even angrier at her expression.

Sophie gave a crooked little smile.

"Honestly, only you would want someone like him."

"What do you mean, someone like him? How dare you talk like that? Angry because you couldn't get him?"

Anna's voice grew louder and louder, drawing the attention of the people queuing nearby.

"Isn't that the woman who wasted food yesterday?"

"Why is she making trouble again?"

"Is there something wrong with her?"

The murmurs around her reached Anna's ears, each word grating painfully.

"It's all your fault."

As she spoke, Anna lunged forward, hands raised as if she meant to hit Sophie.

"I'd think carefully if I were you."

Sophie tugged the dagger slightly from her sleeve. It was a specialised meat-cutting knife, extremely sharp.

The cold gleam of the blade beneath the light looked especially chilling.

Anna was so frightened she took a step back and fell silent.

Edward was at the front of the queue and had already received his food. Hearing the disturbance behind him, he glanced over casually. Sure enough, it was Anna again.

But Edward did not stop.

He carried his bowl and went straight back to the basement room.

Maggie had only just come out and did not know what had happened. She only saw Anna's furious face.

"What's wrong, sweetheart? Did someone upset you again?"

Maggie asked in a low voice.

"It's all your fault!"

Anna shoved Maggie hard, but she did not leave. She stayed in the queue and waited for her food.

"Why are the lights on here, but there's no power in our basement room?"

When it was Anna's turn to receive food, she asked.

"The battery banks issued by the government are for public resources. They're not provided for personal use."

Mrs. Harris answered with obvious impatience.

"Then just say that. What's with the attitude?"

Anna truly could not understand why everything had gone so badly for her since the extreme cold arrived.

It felt as though every single person she met was against her.

"Move along."

Mrs. Harris tapped the ladle and had no desire to waste another word on her.

"You!"

Anna still wanted to argue, but Maggie half pulled, half dragged her away.

Back in the basement room, Anna drank her porridge in a huff. She did not dare mention what had happened between Daniel and Sophie, afraid Edward would grow angry and stop allowing Daniel to stay with them.

Daniel could only pretend nothing had happened as he sipped his porridge, while secretly considering how to make Sophie interested in him.

After the meal, everyone lay down to sleep, but Anna still could not rest.

"That woman. Trying to take Daniel from me. One day, I'll make her pay."

After what had happened with Old Mr. Finch, Anna felt that killing someone during the Collapse was no longer such a shocking thing.

Right now, all she wanted was to get rid of Sophie.

Next door, Sophie had not gone to sleep either.

She had wanted to keep a low profile and get through this period quietly. She had not expected to be targeted by the man next door after only a few days.

Judging from the way that woman had lost control afterwards, the trouble probably was not over.

Sophie switched on her hand-cranked rechargeable lamp and sewed a long narrow pocket into each of her sleeves.

The left sleeve would hold her dagger. The right sleeve would hold a screwdriver.

From now on, whenever she went out, she had to be more alert.

She had to protect herself at all times.
