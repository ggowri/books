Chapter 38

Even with everyone around her criticising her, Maggie's expression did not change.

As long as she got what suited her, why should she care what anyone else thought?

Snatching things had been difficult. Paying for them was even worse. After queuing for more than an hour, the two of them finally managed to get their purchases out of the superstore.

Carrying two large bags of supplies, they said nothing. First, each of them pulled on five pairs of thermal leggings. Then they wrapped several layers of bedsheets around their waists before taking the rest of the supplies outside.

It was minus twenty-six outdoors.

With more layers on, the temperature suddenly felt just about bearable.

But snow had begun to fall again.

"What miserable weather. Snowing again! The government still hasn't cleared the roads properly. In a few days, we won't even be able to drive."

Maggie dragged her heavy steps forward, complaining as she went.

At first, she had thought the extra layers made her warm enough. But after walking through the snow for a while, even those layers of thermal leggings could no longer keep out the cold.

Dragging their numb legs behind them, the couple finally made it home.

But the moment they stepped through the door, they froze in shock.

Daniel and Annabel were asleep in each other's arms.

They were cuddled together beneath three thick duvets, sleeping soundly. There was even a faint, satisfied flush on their faces.

Anyone could tell what had just happened.

Edward's anger surged.

It was one thing for his own daughter not to want to suffer, but Daniel had already run off and now had the nerve to come back, waiting to be looked after.

"Get up! Why are you back here? Get out."

With his frozen hand, Edward yanked away the duvet covering Daniel.

Daniel woke blearily. When he saw Edward had returned, he instinctively glanced around the room. Sure enough, he spotted a large bag of supplies on the floor.

His eyes lit up, and he quickly sat upright.

"You're back. Anna and I were just worrying about you. Are you all right? When it's a bit warmer tomorrow, I'll go out and find supplies. I won't let any of you go hungry."

"You're not having any of what we brought back. Leave now."

Edward could hear the nonsense in every word and only wanted to drive him out as quickly as possible.

"Please, let me stay. I'm begging you. It's too cold outside, I'm not wearing enough, and my car won't move. If I go out there, I'll die. If you save my life today, I'll repay your whole family one day."

Daniel's tears came instantly. He dropped to his knees with a thud, begging and crying at the same time.

Annabel's heart softened at once.

"Dad, please. Let Daniel stay."

"All right, all right. Your dad's only angry. He can't really send Daniel out there to freeze. Hurry up and put on the clothes we bought."

As Maggie spoke, she took the thermal base layers from the bag and handed them to Annabel and Daniel.

There were only three sets of thermal base layers in total. Daniel took one, and Annabel took two.

"It's too cold. It's too cold. I need to wear two. Once I have them on, I won't be so freezing."

With that, she went back to her empty bedroom to change.

"But..."

Maggie looked at her own empty hands. In the end, she did not say that she and Edward had not worn any yet.

The remaining thermal tops and leggings were divided among them and layered on.

Oakwood Estate still had electricity, so Maggie quickly boiled a kettle of water. Since the sudden temperature plunge, it was the only real source of heat they had seen.

After eating a simple meal of boiled dried noodles, they finally warmed up a little.

Then the group returned to the sofa, wrapped themselves in thick duvets and conserved their strength.

For now, preserving energy and body heat was vital.

Two hours earlier, at the Lower Vale Mansion.

Lila swallowed the last bite of her breakfast bun. Her stomach was warm, and the inglenook fireplace in the room was burning hotter and hotter. It was wonderfully cosy.

This was just when Edward and Maggie had gone out looking for supplies. Finding the camera feed dull, Lila casually switched it off.

She had plenty to do today.

The point where the kitchen range connected to the heated hearth bench was right in the centre, and that was the hottest part. Nothing could be laid there, or the hearth-bench matting might be scorched.

Lila pulled her bedding to the far west side of the heated hearth bench. It was warm there, but not burning hot. From now on, that would be her fixed sleeping spot.

Barefoot, she climbed onto the heated hearth bench and walked to its far east end. Closing her eyes, she took her wardrobe from the Vault.

It stood directly against the eastern wall, the height just right.

She opened the wardrobe, and the familiar scent inside rushed out.

Since her second life began, Lila had barely taken a proper look at her wardrobe. Seeing the inside now felt both familiar and strange.

It was filled with memories from before the Collapse in her previous life.

Inside was the dress her university classmates had given her at graduation, a dress from Daniel, and the tracksuit Maggie had chosen for her.

Lila did not feel repulsed by any of it. It did not matter who had given the clothes to her. If they could be worn and used, that was enough.

After looking through those memories, Lila carefully sorted the wardrobe.

All the pretty but impractical dresses and anything difficult to move in went neatly back into the Vault.

She kept only three sets of loose, comfortable pyjamas, along with several sets of underwear and socks. These had already been in the wardrobe.

Then Lila took two padded granny jackets and two pairs of thick padded trousers from the Vault.

In the east parlour, ordinary pyjamas would be enough. But if she needed to spend a long time in the kitchen, she would have to wear something thicker.

Apart from the heated hearth bench, the floor space in the east parlour was completely empty.

Lila placed a three-seater sofa two metres from the fireplace, directly facing the fire. Beside it, she put down a small coffee table.

Both had come from her old home. They had once been the sofa and coffee table in her living room.

But the sofa was not the whole set. The full set had been made up of three smaller sofas, and this was only one of them.

The other two could wait in the Vault and be used as replacements once this one wore out.

Lila sat on the sofa and tried it. It felt even more comfortable than it had at home.

In future, she could sit here by the fire, reading and drinking tea. It would be wonderfully pleasant.

The thought alone made her mood brighten.

On the floor of the east parlour, against the western wall, she placed her sturdy writing desk and chair.

Her diary and a pen pot were still sitting on top.

Lila had always kept a diary, and she also liked collecting blank notebooks and pens.

She pulled open the drawer beneath the desk. It was full of beautiful notebooks and pens in all sorts of shapes and styles.

There was enough to last a very long time.

The writing desk was large and took up quite a bit of space in the east parlour.

Apart from the notebook and pen pot, it looked a little empty.

After thinking for a moment, Lila took out her dressing table.

She used storage racks to arrange the half-used skincare products and face masks from the dressing table in one corner of the writing desk.

"That looks much better. From now on, it can be both writing desk and dressing table."

Lila was very pleased with her storage idea.

With a casual stretch of her hand, she put the dressing table, still holding the rest of the makeup and accessories, back into the Vault.

For now, she had no use for those things.

Her old home had once had a wooden bookcase that covered an entire wall. She placed it against the empty wall between the writing desk and the fireplace.

It added a touch of old-fashioned charm to the room.

Since every piece of furniture in the east parlour had come from her original home, the style was consistent. It echoed the simple elegance of the inglenook fireplace.

The arrangement was simple, but it had a quiet beauty all its own.
