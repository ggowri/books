Chapter 6

Maggie agreed at once.

"That sounds lovely. Lila really is thoughtful. Far more sensible than Anna."

"I won't go. You two go," Edward said.

He was not particularly interested in travelling.

"Oh, Edward, you should go with them," Lila said. "Think of it as my way of showing gratitude. I heard Seabourne has a jade market. Buy a piece you like, and I'll reimburse you when you're back."

Lila knew exactly what Edward liked, so of course she aimed straight for it.

The moment Edward heard the word jade, his eyes lit up.

"Go on, come with us," Maggie urged, already impatient. "Lila is trying to do something kind for us. Don't waste her goodwill."

Lila did not wait for Edward to agree. She took out her phone and transferred $20,000 to Maggie directly.

Maggie looked at the transfer and smiled so widely she could hardly close her mouth.

A free holiday. Why would she not go?

She had known Lila for so many years. Of course she knew what Lila was like. Once Lila gave her word, she rarely went back on it.

If Lila was willing to give them $20,000 first, then the rest of the travel costs would definitely be reimbursed later.

After dinner, Anna was in such a good mood that she volunteered to do the washing up.

Lila returned to her room first.

"I haven't seen the title deeds for two days," Edward said quietly to Maggie. "Go and have another look later. Now she suddenly wants us to go on holiday. It feels odd. If I don't see the title deeds, I'm not going."

Maggie nodded without speaking.

In her room, Lila had just placed the imitation title deeds into the drawer when Maggie knocked on the door.

"Lila, I washed some fruit for you."

"Come in, Maggie."

Lila sat at her desk and turned with a smile as Maggie entered.

Maggie put the fruit on the table.

"I feel a bit bad that you can't come with us."

"It's all right. When you come back, you can keep cooking nice things for me."

Lila pretended to comfort her.

"Why is the drawer unlocked?"

Maggie finally steered the conversation towards the title deeds.

"Oh, I was looking through a diary I wrote when I was little."

Lila pulled the drawer open openly, took out the diary inside, and waved it lightly in her hand.

Maggie gave the diary a distracted glance. Her eyes drifted straight into the drawer.

She saw the title deeds lying inside.

Lila noticed where Maggie was looking and took the document out.

"I'll keep the title deeds safe. Don't worry."

As she spoke, she opened them.

Maggie saw Lila's name and the name of Oakwood Estate. Only then did she relax completely.

Edward had worried for nothing. How could Lila possibly have done anything with this house?

Once Maggie knew the title deeds were still there, she chatted for a few more minutes and left.

At last, Lila relaxed.

After a quick wash, she lay on the large bed and began scrolling through her phone.

Next, she needed to think carefully about how to find a reliable construction team from outside the county.

Then she thought of Charles Mason.

Charles had been one of her university classmates. He was a homebody and an apocalypse enthusiast.

After graduation, he had devoted himself to researching survival strategies and apocalypse strongholds.

He studied them while making short videos online, and by now he was an influencer with five million followers.

Lila had seen one of his videos not long ago. He had built a very high end underground bunker and mentioned some sort of team at the time.

That meant he must know a trustworthy, high end construction crew.

Lila opened What's app.

"Are you there? Do you know any high end construction teams?"

She and Charles had always been on good terms, so there was no need for small talk. She went straight to the point.

He replied almost instantly.

"I do. Did you see the video I posted a while ago? That team was pretty good."

"Roughly how much do they charge?"

Lila was not worried about quality. If Charles approved of them, they must be good. Price was the key issue.

"Mine was expensive. It covered a large area and it was underground, so it cost more than $3 million in total. But I heard from their manager that they recently finished a villa stronghold project for a wealthy client and had quite a lot of materials left over. The team is German, and they're in a hurry to go home, so they're trying to offload the remaining stock quickly. Do you need them? I can ask for you."

Charles had sent a voice message and said the whole thing in one breath.

When Lila heard more than $3 million, her vision almost went dark.

But then she heard they were trying to clear the leftover materials quickly, and hope returned.

"Please ask for me, then."

As long as it did not exceed $1 million, Lila could accept it.

Twenty minutes later, Charles sent over someone's What's app contact.

"I asked for you. They're still taking small jobs. I told them you're my friend. Add him and ask directly."

Lila thanked him and added the contact at once.

The man's profile picture was the logo of a construction company. Lila specifically searched for it online and found that it was a high end international firm.

After exchanging greetings, the other side introduced himself.

"We're a private German security systems team. We came over to install security for a wealthy client's villa. All the materials were shipped in from overseas. We have some left over now, and shipping them back would be costly, so we're prepared to take on one more smaller project and use the remaining stock directly."

He explained the situation clearly, and Lila understood.

Along with the voice message, he sent several image files showing small projects the team had completed before, so Lila could use them as references.

Lila looked through them roughly. After getting his permission, she made a voice call.

Once she confirmed that, apart from structural reinforcement, they could also handle building work such as heated hearth benches and an inglenook fireplace, they agreed on a time. Lila would visit them the next day.

That was enough for today.

Lila put down her phone and soon fell into a deep sleep.

The next morning, when Lila left her room, Edward, Maggie and Anna were already packing and planning their route.

They had booked flights for 23 May, which meant tomorrow.

Using a job interview as her excuse, Lila went out again.

Two hours later, she stood in a high end building materials storage district in the neighbouring city.

A short man wearing black framed glasses and a black suit stood at the entrance.

"Ms. Spencer?"

The man asked politely.

"Yes, that's me. Good morning."

Now that they had confirmed each other, they both knew they had found the right person.

"I'm Markus Weber, head of the RK Security team. Let me show you our materials."

Markus held a tablet in one hand and gestured ahead with the other, indicating that Lila should follow him.

They entered a separate room filled with all kinds of building materials.

Markus introduced them one by one, and Lila listened.

In truth, she did not understand much of it.

But on the way there, she had done a hurried bit of research online. Following what the articles suggested, she touched the materials, knocked on them, and could tell that the quality really was excellent.

When Markus finished explaining, Lila asked about the price.

"The exact cost will depend on the renovation plan. Why don't I visit your property tomorrow? I can carry out an on site inspection, discuss your requirements, and then calculate the final price."

Markus answered responsibly.

Lila nodded in agreement.

That was reasonable. He would have to see her old, run down house before knowing how much it would cost.

They agreed to meet at the Lower Vale Mansion at noon the next day.

Lila did not dare delay. She took the high speed train back at once.

Tomorrow, the three of them would be leaving for Seabourne. Today, she had to get home early so they would not become suspicious.

She needed to send them away smoothly.

By the time Lila arrived home, they had already packed three suitcases.

All they had to do was leave at eight the next morning.
