Chapter 71

At night, Annabel finally freed her hands and lay in bed, relishing the long-lost comfort.

Seeing Edward and Maggie asleep, she reached into the pocket of her worn coat and pulled out a tube of lip gloss.

She had brought it with her to the upmarket French restaurant that day for touch-ups, and it had remained in her pocket even after moving to the basement.

Annabel clenched the gloss tightly, hoping everything would go according to plan tomorrow.

The next morning, the breakfast time for the basement residents was moved an hour earlier. Today wasn't just a day for ration distribution...an inspector would accompany the delivery.

The crowd grumbled at the morning portions.

"Why is it even less than usual?"
"Not as many sweet potatoes this time."
"Oh, I'll starve at this rate…"

Mrs Harris, wielding her large iron ladle, slammed it on the basin twice to command attention.

"Quiet! Everyone, be quiet! There's a visitor today, so behave yourselves. But make sure the inspector notices that the rations aren't enough...that way, we might get more supplies. Stick together, understood?"

Her booming voice silenced the room instantly. Complaints faded; everyone wanted to eat, and if Mrs Harris said so, they would play along.

"How important is this official? It's the Collapse, and now they come showing off while giving out rations," Callum muttered into Leo's ear.

"Shh," Leo said, unconcerned. All that mattered was getting food.

After eating, Annabel washed Maggie's tray and then attended to herself in the small mirror. She cleansed her face, dampened her hair, and combed it twice more.

She used a spent matchstick to darken her eyebrows slightly and applied a soft pink to her lips. Weeks in the basement had left her pale, and now the subtle makeup made her look delicately feminine.

"My daughter looks lovely when she dresses up," Maggie remarked, pleased to see her daughter neat and presentable after so long in confinement.

Annabel glanced at Maggie. Her mother's flushed face was beginning to subside, but the scar was stark and unyielding, more pronounced than ever. Maggie shifted uncomfortably and returned silently to her bed.

The room fell silent.

"Out you go! Everyone, move! The inspector's arriving, quick, quick!" Mrs Harris's rasping shout split the quiet, startling Maggie. Annabel's eyes lit up.

She took a last look in the tiny mirror, folding down her coat collar to reveal the red jumper Mrs Harris had given her. Though small, the neckline was loose enough to show a glimpse when she bent over.

"Move!" Mrs Harris's large hands thumped on the doors.

The steel doors opened one by one. Annabel slipped out eagerly, Maggie quickly following in a fluster.

The central basement area was already crowded. Men and women looked grey and grim, packed like a neat pile of potatoes. Annabel clicked her tongue in distaste and pushed forward to the front row, standing on tiptoe to see the doorway.

Mrs Harris stood ahead, instructing, "Everyone, show some enthusiasm."

A sound came from the entrance.

"They're here," murmured the estate security officer.

Mrs Harris hurried to the door.

The party entered. At the front was a short, portly middle-aged man in a black mink coat and a thick fur hat, obscuring his features. Annabel stared for a couple of seconds but couldn't make out his face.

Once inside, he removed his hat and mask, revealing a shiny, reddened face chapped by the cold.

"Greetings, Inspector," Mrs Harris said quickly. "Thank you for coming. Please, have a seat."

The man sat, and those behind him began removing their hats. They wore uniforms, and a female officer took notes in a notebook.

"Welcome, Inspector!" Mrs Harris led the applause. It was sparse but polite.

The portly man raised his hand, smiling kindly. "Comrades, thank you for your efforts. I am Cedric Dawson."

His eyes swept the crowd, sticky as if glued to each person's head. Annabel straightened instinctively when they landed on her. The gaze paused, lingering.

The basement doors opened again, and more supplies arrived, slightly more than the previous delivery.

"Thank you for the provisions, Inspector. We depend on these to survive," Mrs Harris said, clasping her hands nervously.

"Oh, it's nothing. These are for you," Cedric waved, indifferent to her gratitude.

Mrs Harris continued, introducing the basement and the residents living in the garage. They then headed further into the basement.

Cedric rose slowly, hands on his knees, and followed. Annabel watched his back...the fur coat gleamed under the fluorescent lights, hair at the nape of his neck perfectly combed, almost like it had been licked smooth.

The inspection party moved through the crowd, deeper into the basement. People stayed put, not daring to stray.

"This fat man remembers nothing, it's just a show," Callum whispered to Leo.

"Let it be, it doesn't concern us," Leo replied.

"Alright, you may disperse," murmured the estate officer.

People returned to their bunks or basement spaces.

"Let's go back," Maggie tugged Annabel's arm.

Annabel pulled free. "You go on. I've been trapped down here so long...I need some air."

Guilt crossed Maggie's face. "Alright, but don't get into trouble."

Annabel ignored her, focusing on the visiting party. About ten minutes later, Mrs Harris and the officers returned with Cedric, heading toward the centre.

Annabel stepped aside, pretending to return to her room. As she passed, she ‘accidentally' tripped over a loose brick.

"Ah..." she gasped softly, tilting to one side.

Cedric caught her arm just in time. "Be careful."

Annabel looked up, her eyes glistening under the fluorescent light. "Thank you, Inspector."
