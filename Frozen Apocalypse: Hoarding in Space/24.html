Chapter 23

"Paying good money to suffer."

Lila rubbed her aching shoulders and muttered under her breath.

That had only been one vanload.

One single load had taken half a day and nearly worn her into the ground.

But whether she liked it or not, she had to endure it. If she spared herself the hardship now, she would only end up freezing to death after the Collapse.

Before driving away with the first load of wood offcuts, Lila called out to the old gatekeeper.

He was lying in his booth asleep. Hearing her, he only nodded drowsily.

Lila drove the van back to Lockridge Storage Unit, transferred the whole load of wood offcuts into the Vault, and was completely drained. There was no way she could manage a second trip.

"Tomorrow. I'll do it tomorrow."

She muttered weakly to herself, then drove home.

By the time she got back, she was covered in sawdust and stale sweat. She forced herself to take a shower, then collapsed into bed. In less than five minutes, she was fast asleep.

For the next few days, Lila kept going back and forth stockpiling wood offcuts.

It was not just the wood. On every drive there and back, she also stocked up on petrol.

Five days later, Lila had stored away ten vanloads of wood offcuts and sixty jerrycans of petrol.

Looking at herself in the mirror, she felt as if she had lost a whole dress size in a single week.

She was just about to give herself a day off and have a proper rest when the parcel depot called again.

The seeds and farm tools she had bought earlier had arrived.

Dragging her exhausted body along, Lila collected the seeds and tools, put them into the Vault, and finally earned herself a little time to rest.

At that moment, she did not want to do anything at all. She only wanted to lie down quietly and sleep.

She fell asleep at ten in the morning and did not wake until four in the afternoon.

Those last few days had been too much. After sleeping so deeply, she woke to find much of the heaviness in her body had eased.

Now there was only one feeling left.

Hunger.

Terrible, gnawing hunger.

It was time for a proper meal.

Lila washed and freshened up, then went into the kitchen and searched around. She took all ten plates in the house, large and small, into the Vault and arranged them one by one on the storage racking.

Only then did she drive to a high-quality Japanese buffet.

This buffet was one of Lila's favourites.

It was not cheap, costing $47.90 per person.

In her previous life, she had never been able to bear coming here often. She had only eaten here on holidays or when there was something worth celebrating.

She picked up the menu and began ordering in bulk.

Two hundred slices of salmon sashimi. Two hundred sweet prawns. One hundred slices of surf clam. One hundred portions of sea urchin sashimi.

Five portions of red wine foie gras. Five portions of sizzling Angus beef. Eight portions of chargrilled lamb cutlets. Ten cheese-baked crab shells. Twenty cheese-baked prawns.

Ten portions of grilled eel. Five fillet steaks. Eight portions of chargrilled ox tongue. Twenty salt-grilled giant prawns. Five portions of tempura. Three pork cutlets.

Lila looked over the amount she had ordered.

That was about enough for the first round.

Any more would be too outrageous.

She stopped there and handed the menu to the server.

As expected, the server politely reminded her, "Sorry, miss, let me just confirm this with you. Are you sure you want all of these? It is quite a lot, and you might not be able to finish everything. You can always order less first and add more later."

Her tone was pleasant. It sounded like a genuine, well-meant reminder.

Lila smiled and shook her head.

"Don't worry. I can finish it."

Once the server had her confirmation, she said no more.

"Of course. Please wait a moment."

Before long, the dishes began arriving one after another.

Every dish was freshly prepared, beautifully presented, and smelled incredible.

Lila could not help swallowing. She picked up her chopsticks and began to eat.

This was the most luxurious meal she had eaten since her second life began.

She dipped a slice of salmon into a little wasabi and soy sauce and placed it in her mouth. The flesh was delicate and buttery, lifted by the sharpness of the wasabi and soy. There was a faint sweetness in the aftertaste, rich but not cloying.

After eating more than a dozen pieces in one go, Lila began to feel a little overwhelmed by the richness.

Still, she picked up another piece of salmon and lifted it towards her mouth.

As it reached her lips, she covered her mouth with her other hand. With a flicker of will, the salmon on her chopsticks was transferred into the Vault.

The remaining hundred-plus slices of salmon were slowly sent into the Vault in the same way, landing neatly on the plates she had prepared on the storage racking.

Lila had her own order when eating buffet food. She finished one thing before moving on to the next.

The sweet prawns and other sashimi were handled just like the salmon. First, she ate until she was satisfied, then covered her mouth and sent the rest into the Vault.

This buffet had no time limit, so Lila did not need to rush.

She put a film on her phone and ate slowly while watching it.

After more than forty minutes, the first round of dishes was finally almost gone.

Lila ordered a second round.

She left out the items from the first round that had only tasted average, then reordered everything else.

She also added twenty pieces of assorted sushi.

When the server took the menu and saw what had been ordered, surprise flashed through her eyes.

Then she looked at the table.

And the floor.

The dishes on the table had actually been cleared completely.

When Lila had placed the first order, the server had thought there was no way one person could finish that much food. Yet now, Lila had ordered so much again.

The restaurant had really encountered a champion eater today.

Of course, online big eaters had come here to do challenges before. The dishes in the first round were something those people could also manage.

Now they could only wait and see whether this pretty young woman could finish the entire second round too.

The second round of dishes arrived one after another.

Lila started eating.

She still picked up the salmon delicately, then covered her mouth with her other hand just before it entered.

The movement would not make anyone suspicious. It only made her look well-mannered, as if covering her mouth while eating was a polite personal habit.

Besides, it was summer. Lila was wearing short sleeves. Even if she covered her mouth with her hand, the food could not be hidden anywhere else.

The staff in the restaurant were trained. It would be rude to stare at customers while they ate.

But the server assigned to Lila was simply too curious. From time to time, she could not help glancing over secretly.

And so, right before her eyes, she watched this slim, well-proportioned young woman finish table after table of dishes.

Once the second round had been successfully cleared, Lila did not order anything else.

She picked up her phone from the table, turned, and left with effortless confidence.

Inside the Vault, all ten empty plates on the storage racking were now full.

They were filled entirely with precious foods she would never normally have been willing to buy for herself.

Those two rounds had more than paid for the meal. Strictly speaking, she had even come out ahead.

There could not be a third round.

Ordering any more really would be wrong, and with an even larger amount, she would absolutely draw suspicion from the other diners.

Lila patted her round stomach.

Although she had been busy sending food into the Vault, she had genuinely eaten plenty herself. Today's meal had been satisfying, filling, and delicious.

To digest it, Lila drove to the Lower Vale Mansion and had a walk around.

The renovation was basically taking shape. It would not be long before everything was fully completed.

She reminded the site manager once more to handle the drainage in the courtyard properly, and only then did she feel reassured enough to go home.
