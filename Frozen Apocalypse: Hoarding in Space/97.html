Chapter 96

There were meatballs for hot pot, sliced meats of every kind, seafood, marinated meats, leafy greens, mushrooms, wide rice noodles, and rice vermicelli.

Lila prepared more than thirty different ingredients.

She nearly ran out of bowls and basins in the kitchen.

Fortunately, she still had stacks of disposable takeaway containers she'd stored in the Vault. She lined them up across the counter one by one.

While ingredients continued simmering in the pot, she portioned her homemade spicy sauce into each container.

Lila liked bold flavors.

She always worried restaurant food wasn't spicy enough, so every container received an extremely generous amount of sauce.

One batch produced ten portions.

Once the ingredients were fully cooked, she drained them with a strainer and divided them among the containers.

The hot ingredients mixed with the sauce below, releasing an aroma unlike anything she'd smelled before.

It was unmistakably Loaded Spicy Hot Pot Bowl.

More specifically, it was Lila's version.

Thirty portions later, all of it disappeared into the Vault.

Next came the noodle dishes.

Those were much easier.

She simply emptied large quantities of seasoning packets into a stockpot.

Once the broth reached a boil, she added the packaged noodles along with mushrooms, shredded seaweed, shredded tofu, bean sprouts, beef slices, and meatballs.

The fragrance immediately filled the kitchen.

This particular brand tasted remarkably close to restaurant quality.

Lila approved.

The Spicy Noodle Soup, Hot & Sour Noodle Soup, and other varieties followed nearly identical steps.

Thirty portions of each.

By the time she finished everything, dusk was approaching.

Down at the refuge, work was wrapping up for the day.

The crews were conducting final seal tests on the doors and windows while moving furniture into the completed buildings.

The soldiers breathed in wave after wave of spicy aromas drifting down the mountainside.

Their mouths watered constantly.

One moment it smelled like Loaded Spicy Hot Pot Bowl.

The next it was noodle soup.

A little later, the unmistakable scent of Spicy Noodle Soup floated through the air.

"The folks up there must be loaded."

"I'm starving. Think they've got a daughter? I'd happily marry into that family."

"You? No chance. You eat more than a farm animal. You'd bankrupt them in a month."

"Oh please. You eat even more than I do."

The soldiers laughed and traded jokes while they worked.

"Focus."

Captain Walker stepped down from a pickup truck.

"I hope you're all finished clowning around. The civilians arrive tomorrow."

He had just returned with another load of furniture and immediately found his younger troops fooling around.

Nobody wanted to deal with Captain Walker's stern side.

The chatter died instantly.

A rich aroma of spicy rice noodles still lingered in the air.

Mixed into it was the scent of smoked bacon and pickled vegetables.

The smell reminded him of home.

Walker frowned and looked toward the farmhouse halfway up the mountain.

So it was her.

The survivors would arrive any day now.

He'd already warned her to keep a lower profile.

Apparently she had ignored that advice completely.

Lila lifted the final bowl of Smoked Bacon Noodle Soup from the stove.

The smell alone was enough to make her dizzy with anticipation.

Who would've guessed?

The smoked bacon she'd ordered from BargainBasket was actually excellent quality.

The translucent slices glistened in the broth, coating the noodles with a rich sheen of oil.

One look and her stomach growled.

She stored the final batch in the Vault, then tackled the mountain of dishes she'd accumulated throughout the day.

Every bowl.

Every tray.

Every pot.

The floor had become a muddy mess as well.

Water had splashed everywhere while she carried vegetables back and forth, and she'd tracked it across the kitchen all day.

After putting away the dishes, scrubbing the cookware, and wiping down the counters, she mopped the floor twice.

The kitchen looked brand new again.

Exactly as clean as it had been that morning.

"Whew."

Lila let out a long breath.

The day's work had nearly worn her out.

She smelled like spicy broth, chili oil, and noodles.

At this point she felt marinated.

Skipping dinner was no longer an option.

She switched on the kitchen ventilator, grabbed clean pajamas, and headed for the bathroom.

The first thing she did was strip off her clothes and toss everything into the washing machine.

Then she treated herself to a long, steaming hot shower.

When she finally emerged, she felt completely refreshed.

The laundry was already finished.

She hung the clothes to dry, locked the doors and windows, and returned to the east parlour.

The moment she sat down on the heated bed, pain shot through her thighs and heels.

"Oh man... oh man..."

She sounded like a little old lady as she slowly climbed onto the bed.

After standing all day, her body wasn't thrilled about resting either.

Of everything she'd cooked, what she wanted most was the Loaded Spicy Hot Pot Bowl.

Or maybe the spicy rice noodles.

Or the smoked bacon noodles.

Or the Spicy Noodle Soup.

Lila shook her head firmly.

If she kept thinking like that, she'd end up eating one of everything.

She had just changed into clean pajamas and didn't want them smelling like chili oil.

After some consideration, she chose the least offensive option.

Hot & Sour Noodle Soup.

A steaming bowl appeared on the table.

Bright red chili oil floated across the surface, covering a generous layer of minced meat.

It looked incredible.

Hot & Sour Noodle Soup always tasted better with something on the side.

Lila pulled a Pulled Pork Sandwich from the Vault.

Perfect.

Her appetite surged immediately.

She stirred the noodles with her chopsticks.

Only when you cooked for yourself could you get away with this much excess.

The bowl was packed.

Pickled radishes.

Pickled green beans.

A mountain of minced meat.

Seaweed.

Bean sprouts.

Mushrooms.

Leafy greens.

She lifted a huge bundle of noodles.

The translucent strands glistened in the light, mixed with vegetables and seaweed.

Several pieces of pickled green bean perched on top.

Lila almost laughed.

She couldn't imagine a happier girl than the one about to take this bite.

She opened her mouth wide and dug in.

Amazing.

Absolutely amazing.

The noodles were silky yet pleasantly chewy.

The mushrooms and bean sprouts added crunch.

Every bite of pickled vegetables brought another burst of tangy flavor.

It was ridiculously good.

She blew across the broth and took a sip.

The chili oil, sourness, and rich seasonings combined perfectly.

Even better.

By the time she finished the entire bowl, the Pulled Pork Sandwich was gone too.

She was completely stuffed.

Too comfortable to get off the heated bed, she simply waved a hand.

The trash vanished into the Vault.

A bottle of Pepsi appeared in its place.

Her movie still wasn't finished.

She sipped the soda while watching, slowly washing away the richness of the meal.

This was the life.

If only days like this could last forever.

Day Five.

Lila woke up craving buns.

Then she realized she'd already eaten every last batch she'd steamed before.

Well.

Time to make more.

This time she went all out.

Five huge bowls of dough rose simultaneously in the warm kitchen.

For the fillings, she prepared every variety she could think of.

Beef-Filled Buns.

Chicken-Filled Buns.

Pork Buns.

Sauerkraut Buns.

Eggplant-Filled Buns.

Cabbage-Filled Buns.

Vegetable Buns.

Once the dough was ready, she wrapped the fillings and arranged the buns inside steaming baskets.

The wrappers were thin.

The fillings were enormous.

When the first batch emerged, each bun was nearly the size of her palm.

Lila transferred them into large basins and stored them in the Vault.

Then she started the second batch.

She steamed continuously until nearly noon before finally finishing every filling.

Only enough dough remained for one last basket.

Half became Dinner Rolls.

The rest received brown sugar, honey, and white sugar before being turned into sweet Sugar Rolls.

She had been busy steaming buns all morning and still hadn't eaten breakfast.

While the final batch cooked, she could no longer resist.

Reaching into the Vault, she pulled out one Chicken-Filled Bun and one Beef-Filled Bun.

Fresh from storage and still piping hot.
