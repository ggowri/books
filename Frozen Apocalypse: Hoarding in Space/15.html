Chapter 14

Couriers had no obligation to help load parcels into anyone's van.

So Lila helped as well.

She picked up the cardboard boxes from the ground and passed them into the back of the van.

It took a long while. By the time every parcel was finally loaded, sweat was dripping from Lila's forehead.

The van bed was packed full, with the boxes piled even higher than the sides.

The courier gave her some hemp rope for free and helped tie the entire load down tightly.

Lila thanked him, then drove towards Lockridge Storage Unit.

There was still a little space left inside. The van could just about squeeze in.

After parking, she locked the roller shutter behind her and began moving the food into the Vault.

The van had to be driven home, and she had no desire to unload all the food into the storage unit first. So she gritted her teeth and moved everything into the Vault in one go.

She had only just transferred two hundred and fifty tonnes of coal the day before, and her body had not fully recovered. Now, after taking in so much food as well, the exhaustion hit her hard.

She decided not to do anything else today and drove straight home.

While taking the lift, Lila quickly ordered takeaway.

By the time she had washed and freshened up, the food had already arrived at her door.

After eating, her body regained a little strength, but her mind still felt sluggish.

She lay down on the bed and fell asleep not long after.

When she woke again, it was six in the morning.

Her body felt light, and the fatigue had disappeared.

Wrapped in a thick duvet, she did not get up.

Instead, she entered the Vault with her awareness and prepared to sort the huge amount of food she had moved in the day before.

The moment she entered, Lila sensed the difference.

The Vault had grown again.

It was half as large again as it had been last time.

The change left her both astonished and delighted.

With this much space, it could basically hold all the supplies she needed.

Before, she had been thinking that if the Vault ran out of room later, she could unload some of the coal and leave it in the yard.

Now, it seemed there was absolutely no need.

Lila sorted the food according to the categories she had used when buying it and placed everything onto the storage racking one by one.

Many shelves were filled completely. Seeing them packed so neatly gave her a deep sense of security.

Lila left the Vault and decided to take the day off.

She had used too much physical strength over the past two days. She needed to keep herself healthy before continuing to stockpile.

Once the decision was made, she wanted to move even less.

Still lying in bed, she picked up her phone and began buying household essentials.

One thousand lighters. Five hundred boxes of matches. Fifty firesteel magnesium rods. Five hundred candles. Fifty torches.

Two thousand packs of toilet paper. One thousand packs of sanitary pads. Five hundred packs of night-time sanitary pants. Fifty winter face masks. Two hundred protective respirators.

Five hundred bags of washing powder. Three hundred bars of soap. Two hundred bottles of shampoo. One hundred bottles of face wash. One hundred bottles of shower gel. Two hundred bottles of washing-up liquid.

Three hundred toothbrushes and five hundred tubes of toothpaste.

Two hundred towels and twenty bath towels.

After thinking it over, Lila also ordered one hundred boxes of Crack-Care Balm and three hundred packets of heavy-duty barrier balm.

Crack-Care Balm could prevent skin from drying and splitting. During the Long Freeze, the air would certainly become extremely dry.

Lila would also need to light fires and cook for herself every day. Keeping extra Crack-Care Balm for her hands and face would be an effective way to protect her skin from cracking.

As for the heavy-duty barrier balm, it was an extremely oily, highly moisturising skincare product.

It was cheap and effective. As an end-of-the-world skincare item, there was nothing with better value for money.

Then Lila thought of the empty Lower Vale Mansion.

Reluctantly, she bought kitchenware and tableware as well.

Two iron woks, two frying pans, five clay pots, two non-stick pans, two rice cookers and one steamer.

For pots, bowls, ladles and basins, she bought two full stainless-steel sets of everything.

She also chose a few pieces of tableware and cups she liked.

One six-piece set of pale butter-yellow tableware, several simple, understated coffee cups, five milk tea cups and five pretty salad bowls.

That would be enough.

The kitchenware already at home could be used too. There was no need to buy too much.

All of this cost forty-five thousand dollars in total.

Her current balance was nine hundred and five thousand dollars.

By the time she finished shopping, it was already noon.

She spent the whole afternoon doing nothing, either playing on her phone or sleeping. It was, in the truest sense, a full day of rest.

The next day, Lila was back to full strength.

Just as she was about to plan what to do that day, her phone nearly exploded with calls from the courier.

"Miss Spencer, please come quickly. Your parcels are here again. The depot needs rescuing."

The courier sounded extremely urgent.

Lila agreed at once, washed up quickly, and hurried out.

Although the courier sounded desperate, this batch of medical supplies took up much less space than the food had.

Loading the van was much faster this time.

Lila drove the vanload of medicine to Lockridge Storage Unit and moved everything into the Vault in one go without any effort at all.

Perhaps it was because she had rested for an entire day, but even after taking everything into the Vault, she did not feel tired in the slightest.

Lila sorted the medical supplies by category and placed them properly on the storage racking.

Another day full of security.

Once the medicine was organised, Lila drove to Lower Vale.

She had not been there for several days, and she needed to see how things were progressing.

When she pulled up outside the Lower Vale Mansion, the sound of machinery and renovation work could be heard from inside.

Lila walked into the yard and saw several workers straddling the high boundary wall, welding steel reinforcement bars.

The site manager was there too. He was looking at a tablet, his brows tightly furrowed, as if dealing with some difficult problem.

Perhaps sensing that someone had arrived, he looked up. When he saw Lila, he put down the tablet and walked over with a smile.

"Miss Spencer, you're here."

"You carry on. I'll just have a look around."

Seeing that he was busy, Lila did not ask him to accompany her.

She walked around the whole place herself, inside and out.

The heated hearth bench in the east room had already been built. It was the same size as before, but it was still damp and had not dried properly yet.

As for the rest, Lila could not really understand much, so she could only pretend to inspect it with a professional eye.

To show that she cared, and to make sure the workers did not slack off, Lila forced herself to stay at the mansion for the entire afternoon. She only hoped the house would be transformed as well as possible.

That evening, lying in bed, Lila picked up her phone and placed another order.

This time, it was not for bits and pieces.

She ordered three hundred jerrycans in one go.

Each had a capacity of twenty litres.

The next day, before the household essentials and jerrycans arrived, Lila still had many other things to stockpile.

The most important was meat.

There was a small meatworks near the outskirts of Ashton that specialised in supplying meat to restaurants and food businesses.

Lila felt that this place would be perfect for her stockpiling.

The prices would be low, and buying in bulk would be convenient.

After breakfast, she drove there.

Following the navigation, Lila parked at her destination.

It was a small factory compound. To the right of the iron gate hung a signboard, black with gold lettering, that read Rosie's Meatworks.

Lila was still looking it over when the gate suddenly opened, and a refrigerated lorry drove out from inside.

The gatekeeper came over to close the gate and happened to see a young woman standing outside in a daze.
