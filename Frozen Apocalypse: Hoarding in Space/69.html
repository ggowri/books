Chapter 68

When it was time to eat, a pot of rice porridge with sweet potato and cabbage was brought to the serving table.
"What is this rubbish?"
"It looks even worse than before."

Before it had been plain rice porridge. Now they had thrown in sweet potatoes and cabbage. It looked like a mess.
"Eat what you're given. The supplies were small this time. We need to ration."

Mrs Harris's ladle began to shake, giving out even smaller portions than before.
At her words, the crowd fell silent.

The people living in the underground car park took their food back to their beds and grumbled quietly to their neighbours.
"If she keeps cutting our rations, we should report her. Get her replaced."
"Exactly. They're supposed to be serving us. Why is she acting like this?"
"Maybe the government is sending more supplies than this, and she's hiding them before they reach the basement."

Sophie finished her porridge and, just as the clock struck seven, fetched a bottle of hot water.
The hot water supply was limited each day. Mrs Harris had ruled that each family could take only one bottle.

After all these days in the basement, Sophie hadn't had a wash. The smell of her own body was starting to make her feel sick.
She poured half the bottle into a basin and tucked the other half under her duvet to keep warm.
She took off her underwear and put on a loose, thick set of lounge clothes.

Then she poured the scalding water into the basin, added some cold water, tested it with her hand. Just right.
She dipped her towel in and wrung it until it was half dry.
Without taking off her thick clothes, she slipped the warm, damp towel inside her collar and wiped her skin.
From her neck to her back, then her arms and stomach. She went over everything twice.

Her thick clothes stayed on the whole time, blocking the cold air from outside. Wiping down didn't feel too cold at all.
When she finished, she changed out of her slightly damp clothes and put on her dry, thick pyjamas.

She tidied up the washing area, double checked that the basement door was locked, and hurried back under the duvet.
The centre of the duvet had already been warmed by the hot water bottle. It was toasty warm inside.
Her skin was slightly cool from the washing, but now, tucked into the warm bed, her whole body relaxed.

Sophie sighed with pleasure, curled up in the warm duvet, and fell asleep.

Leo and Callum were also struggling with hygiene.
"Bloody hell, the stink of my own feet is giving me a headache. I've had it. I'm washing them today no matter what."

Callum's voice crackled through the two way radio. The radio itself seemed to smell.
But Leo wasn't in a position to judge. His own feet weren't exactly fragrant.

"If you have something to say, say it in person. Stop wasting the battery on that thing."

Leo put down the radio, grabbed his keys, a collapsible baton, and a water bottle, and went out. He knocked directly on Callum's door.
Callum opened the door a crack, showing only half his face.

"Come on, let's go get hot water." Leo shook the bottle in his hand.

"Should I wash my feet or not? I don't want to waste the water. I need it to warm my bed. I also want to drink some hot water, warm myself up."

Callum talked as he walked with Leo toward the hot water station.
"Wash your whole body. I'm doing the same tonight. Just this once." Leo pressed his lips together. With so many people catching colds in the basement lately, a bit of personal hygiene might reduce the risk of getting sick.

"If you're washing, then I'm definitely washing."

Callum stopped agonising.
But two careless men washing themselves were not as thorough as Sophie had been.

They poured the whole bottle of hot water into a basin. First they washed their faces, then their bodies, then their feet.
The water left behind was dark and murky. You couldn't even see the bottom of the basin.
Callum swallowed his disgust and rinsed his crusty socks in the same water.

Still, after the wash, he felt much cleaner. He got into bed cold and shivering, but he didn't regret it.
"Ah, being clean feels so good. So comfortable."

Callum spoke into the radio to Leo.
Leo frowned. He had said not to use the radio unless it was important.

Maggie had also brought back a bottle of hot water. She poured half of it into a cup and handed it to Anna.
"Drink some hot water, Anna."

Her heart ached for her daughter, but there was no way she could undo the handcuffs.
Anna looked at Maggie's face, swollen like half a pig's head, rolled her eyes, but took the cup anyway.
"You're getting uglier every day. You make me sick."

That red, swollen, weeping face made Anna feel genuinely nauseous.
"Oh, you..."

Hearing those words from her own daughter felt like a hundred ants chewing on Maggie's heart.
But she was partly responsible for Anna's situation. If only she hadn't run into the path of that knife, Anna wouldn't be chained up here.
The harsh words died on her lips. She couldn't bring herself to say them.

The next morning, Lila woke up again in her warm bed.
Her routine was so regular now. Early to bed, early to rise, every day. Her body felt good, her energy was great.

She had snacked a bit too much last night and skipped dinner, so she woke up with her stomach growling.
She got out of bed and pulled on her fluffy thick pyjamas. The room was 17 degrees. A bit cold.

Snowy and Patches were curled up in their dog bed, two little round balls. They were feeling the cold too.
Lila checked the outdoor temperature. Minus 83 degrees. That surprised her.
She had thought the Long Freeze would bottom out at minus seventy. She hadn't expected it to drop this low.

But she had to admit, after Maggie and her family had locked her away in her previous life, she had stopped paying attention to the outside temperature.
No point dwelling on it. Lila took some kindling out of the Vault and quickly lit the inglenook fireplace.
The flames caught. Warmth spread through the room. Soon it was cosy again.

She hurried to the kitchen and lit both kitchen ranges.
After all the tedious chores were done – feeding the chickens, watering the vegetables and flowers – she finally had time to eat.

Lila found a clean ceramic bowl in the kitchen, added a spoonful of flour, cracked two eggs into it, and mixed everything together. Then she sprinkled in some fresh chopped spring onions.
She put a little oil in a frying pan and poured in the egg and flour batter, spreading it flat.
The smell of egg and spring onions burst into the air.

Just before the egg pancake came out of the pan, she sprinkled a thin layer of sesame seeds over it. It looked even more delicious.
One bowl of batter made two golden, fragrant egg pancakes.

She set the frying pan aside, took a ceramic jar she had bought from the supermarket, and placed it on the hob.
She poured in a carton of milk, added a spoonful of honey, and waited for it to boil.

While waiting, she took a head of lettuce, a few sprigs of coriander, a jar of beef sauce, and a sausage out of the Vault.
She washed the lettuce and coriander, and sliced the sausage.

She spread the beef sauce evenly over one egg pancake, piled on the lettuce, coriander, and sausage slices, and rolled it up.
A chubby egg roll. And the milk in the jar was just coming to a boil.
