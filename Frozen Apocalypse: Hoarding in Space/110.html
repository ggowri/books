Chapter 109

By the time Lila finally made it home, every inch of her body felt frozen.

She peeled off her layers of winter gear, and her hands and feet were numb with cold.

After changing into comfortable clothes, she settled onto the sofa and unpacked her backpack.

Inside was the small bag of mixed grains she'd spent half the day collecting.

She let out a sigh.

"Well... you definitely weren't free."

Carefully, she stored the grain inside the Vault before scooting closer to the inglenook fireplace.

The warmth slowly chased the chill from her fingers and toes.

Thankfully, she'd been bundled up well enough.

Even after slipping several times in the deep snow, she hadn't suffered so much as a bruise.

The moment she walked through the door, Snowy and Patches had raced over.

The two puppies hated being separated from her for long.

Lila laughed as she rubbed their heads and scratched behind their ears.

Before long, feeling had returned to her hands.

She leaned back into the sofa.

Leaving the house was such a hassle.

Nothing compared to staying warm indoors.

She propped her tablet on the coffee table and picked out a movie she'd been meaning to watch.

A good film deserved good snacks.

After rummaging through the Vault, she found a tub of Garlic Chicken Bites.

Only then did she realise she hadn't eaten any since the Collapse began.

She emptied them into a bowl and paired them with the remaining half of a watermelon she'd been saving.

Cold, refreshing, and packed with flavour.

The frustrations she'd picked up at the refuge quickly melted away.

...

Back at the Lower Vale Refuge, everyone whose name had been called over the loudspeaker gathered once again inside the cafeteria.

Applicants queued patiently for their interviews.

Mrs. Harris was waiting to interview for a serving position in the cafeteria.

Lauren Hart was hoping to secure a cleaning job in the Administrative Quarter.

Meanwhile, Annabel was back home throwing a full blown tantrum.

Maggie hovered beside her, desperately trying to calm her down.

"It's alright, Anna."

"There are plenty of other ways to earn Survival Credits."

"We'll go collect firewood together."

That only made Annabel angrier.

"It's all your fault!"

"I didn't even get an interview."

"My leg's injured."

"What's your excuse?"

"How come you couldn't land a single job either?"

Maggie had no answer.

She honestly didn't understand it herself.

Every position she'd applied for had rejected her before the interview stage.

Edward hadn't fared any better.

He'd tried applying for Legal Officer, arguing that his previous work had been related to legal matters.

Apparently the review panel hadn't agreed.

The entire family sat around complaining, each more pessimistic than the last.

None of them could see a promising future.

The following morning, the official appointment list was posted.

Successful applicants reported for their first day of work.

Mrs. Harris officially became one of the cafeteria servers.

Leo Turner began work in the Medical Unit as a doctor.

Sophie Hale was appointed as a Legal Officer.

She received her own centrally heated office, although she wasn't required to remain there all day.

She only needed to report whenever legal disputes or criminal cases arose.

Lauren wasn't selected.

She would have to earn Survival Credits through other means.

Annabel hurried to read the announcement herself.

The moment her eyes reached the Legal Officer section, she froze.

There it was.

Sophie's name.

"What?"

"Why her?"

"What makes Sophie qualified to be a Legal Officer?"

She completely lost control.

Her shout startled everyone reading the noticeboard.

People instinctively stepped away from her.

Coincidentally, today's civilian patrol happened to be the first official patrol shift.

Their sense of responsibility was at an all time high.

Seeing someone causing a disturbance, two patrol members immediately hurried over.

"What's going on here?"

"Why are you making a scene?"

The instant Annabel noticed the red armbands on their sleeves, she swallowed every complaint.

Without another word, she turned and hurried home.

"It had to be Sophie."

"She must've slept her way into that job."

"Just wait."

"I'll make her regret this."

Annabel ground her teeth so hard her jaw ached.

Maggie's expression darkened.

"Why does that woman keep getting in your way?"

"My daughter's twice as capable."

"She had to use dirty tricks."

In Maggie's mind, there was no other explanation.

She silently added another name to the list of people she intended to settle scores with.

At ten o'clock that morning, everyone holding an important position attended a staff meeting with William, Ethan, and Cedric.

The meeting focused on dividing responsibilities.

Each employee was informed which senior official they reported to and who would oversee their work.

All engineers and technical specialists answered directly to William.

Patrols, sanitation, cafeteria operations, and similar departments fell under Cedric's authority.

He also supervised routine supply distribution and registration.

Major security matters, along with final authority over warehouse grain reserves and cafeteria food supplies, remained in Ethan's hands.

Sophie's day to day coordination would be handled through Ryan and Evan.

Leo needed very little supervision.

His job was simply to save lives.

If anything significant arose, he was free to report directly to any of the three senior officials.

Mrs. Harris also reported to Cedric, who assigned all of her daily tasks.

After the meeting, everyone returned home.

William asked Sophie and the other newly appointed Legal Officer to stay behind for a few extra minutes.

By the time Sophie finally left, darkness had already settled over the refuge.

She had no interest in lingering inside the office.

It was time to head home, heat the heated bed, and make sure the house was warm before bedtime.

The streets were nearly empty.

As she rounded the corner leading toward her row of cottages, a crushing impact slammed into her back.

Her legs gave way instantly.

She collapsed to the snowy ground.

Her vision blurred.

She bit down hard on her tongue, forcing herself to stay conscious.

She wanted to know exactly who had attacked her.

The footsteps approached quietly.

Someone crouched beside her.

Sophie cracked one eye open just enough to see.

Maggie.

A knife glittered in her hand as she lined up a strike toward Sophie's throat.

Not happening.

Sophie exploded into motion.

She drove her foot straight into Maggie's chest with every ounce of strength she had.

Maggie cried out as she crashed backward.

The knife never left her hand.

She scrambled upright and lunged again.

"Help!"

"Someone help!"

Sophie shouted at the top of her lungs while grabbing Maggie's wrist, fighting to keep the knife away.

Maggie's eyes had already turned bloodshot.

She'd been caught.

If Sophie survived, she was finished.

There was only one way out now.

Kill the witness.

The nearest patrol was still too far away to hear Sophie's first cries.

Someone else heard them instead.

Callum.

He recognised Sophie's voice immediately.

Throwing on his coat, he burst outside.

The instant he saw the knife, he rushed forward and tore it from Maggie's grasp.

Only then did the patrol arrive.

A crowd of curious residents quickly gathered behind them.

Sophie rubbed her aching back.

Pain shot through her spine.

"I was walking through here."

"She hit me from behind."

"Then she tried to slit my throat."

"If I hadn't stayed conscious..."

She didn't finish the sentence.

She didn't need to.

The patrol officers had witnessed enough themselves.

There was no need for additional witnesses.

The evidence spoke for itself.

"Come with us."

"We're taking you to the Legal Office."

The two patrol members grabbed Maggie by both arms.

"I'm innocent!"

"I'm an old woman."

"Why would I attack her?"

"She's framing me!"

Maggie denied everything without hesitation.

Just then, Annabel stepped outside after hearing the commotion.

One glance at her mother in custody and another at Sophie told her exactly what had happened.

Maggie looked up and spotted her daughter standing in the doorway.

She immediately shouted,

"My daughter can prove it!"

"I didn't do anything!"

"I only just came outside!"
