Chapter 84

Back at Oakwood Estate, residents were beginning to prepare lunch.

Unfortunately, a new problem had emerged.

Fuel.

Or rather, the lack of it.

Without wood to burn, nobody could cook. Living on frozen food alone wasn't a realistic option.

As a result, Mrs. Harris was forced to organize an additional Work Detail dedicated entirely to gathering firewood and scavenging supplies.

It was dangerous work.

To keep things fair, she announced a new rule.

Anyone who joined a scavenging team only had to turn in the firewood they collected. Any food or useful supplies they found could be kept for themselves.

The moment the rule was announced, interest skyrocketed.

Mrs. Harris repeated it to make sure everyone understood.

"Anyone going out scavenging will be issued one of the expedition parkas. Bring back firewood for the community, and anything else you find is yours to keep."

The room immediately buzzed with conversation.

Callum and Leo weren't interested.

Their personal supplies were still sufficient for the time being.

Sophie, however, paid close attention.

Her stockpile was holding up for now, but she knew it wouldn't last forever.

She volunteered.

So did Chloe.

So did Nathan.

Several other survivors who were healthy enough to travel signed up as well.

After reviewing the list, Mrs. Harris created a Duty Roster.

Each Foraging Team would consist of four people.

Everyone had to return before two o'clock in the afternoon.

Participants could form their own groups.

Sophie teamed up with Lauren, Chloe, and Nathan.

All four were relatively healthy adults, making them the first team scheduled to head out.

The group geared up with the expedition parkas supplied by Oakwood Estate Management.

They added their own hats, gloves, and winter layers.

Sophie was especially grateful she'd bought a mink hat from Callum earlier.

Today it proved worth every penny.

The four women quickly agreed on a plan.

Their target would be the nearest apartment block that hadn't been damaged by fire.

Sophie and Lauren would search one side of the building.

Chloe and Nathan would take the other.

Simple.

Efficient.

And hopefully safe.

Entering through the ground floor, they climbed the stairwell and began working apartment by apartment.

Most doors remained locked.

Each woman carried a hammer.

A few solid blows usually took care of the lock.

Then the search began.

When the Long Freeze first arrived, most residents had carried their food reserves into the shelter.

Still, people had left things behind in their panic.

Those forgotten supplies now became valuable discoveries.

Of course, firewood remained the highest priority.

The communal kitchen depended on it.

Without fuel, nobody ate.

That responsibility came first.

Eventually, Sophie and Lauren entered an apartment successfully.

Before beginning the search, Sophie turned toward her companion.

"If we find food, we split it fifty fifty. Doesn't matter who finds it."

She preferred settling potential arguments before they happened.

Especially in a dangerous environment.

Lauren nodded immediately.

"Works for me."

Her daughter was waiting back in the shelter.

Staying healthy mattered more than fighting over supplies.

The two women started in the kitchen.

The rice and flour had already been removed.

However, several bottles of soy sauce remained intact, and two unopened bags of salt were tucked into a cabinet.

Those went into the communal supply sack.

One reason the communal ration meals tasted so awful was the lack of seasonings.

A little flavor would make a huge difference.

They searched every cabinet and drawer.

The only food they uncovered was half a packet of moldy dried noodles.

Sophie had no interest in it.

She let Lauren keep it.

Since food was scarce, they turned their attention to fuel.

Four wooden dining chairs sat in the living room.

Using an axe, they broke them apart and packed the wood into their Utility Sack.

Good quality firewood.

Heavy, but valuable.

To conserve energy, they dragged the sack with them as they climbed to the third floor.

The next apartment door had already been broken open.

Inside, a body lay in the middle of the living room.

Nearby were the remains of burned furniture and old firewood.

Most of the furnishings were gone.

The scene told its own story.

The occupant had tried to stay warm.

And failed.

Both women froze.

Instinctively, they reached for each other's hand.

Lauren took a breath.

"It's okay."

Her voice was soft.

"We've seen too much of this already."

She wasn't sure whether she was comforting Sophie or herself.

They carefully moved past the body and continued searching.

This apartment turned out to be far more rewarding.

In the kitchen alone they found half a sack of rice.

Half a sack of flour.

Three packets of pasta.

Three packets of instant noodles.

Both women stared in disbelief.

Then immediately split everything evenly.

The problem now was carrying it all.

Neither had expected to find this much food.

After searching further, they discovered two large clothing bags and repurposed them for their haul.

Time slipped away.

Before they realized it, nearly two hours had passed.

The building had been fully searched.

Both women were exhausted.

Dragging two stuffed sacks of firewood and carrying bags full of food, they finally headed downstairs.

Outside, they ran into Chloe and Nathan, who had just finished their own search.

The entire team returned safely to Basement Level One.

When Mrs. Harris saw the four bulging sacks of firewood, her face lit up.

"This is wonderful."

"Really wonderful."

"We'll be able to cook for days."

The scavengers had completed their mission.

Everyone returned to their rooms carrying their personal finds.

The other survivors watched with obvious envy.

Sophie quickly locked her basement door.

Only then did she inspect her haul properly.

Inside her bag were two frozen cans of Red Bull.

Five pounds of rice and flour.

Two packets of pasta.

One packet of instant noodles.

And half a box of biscuits.

A successful trip.

One afternoon of risk had earned her a substantial amount of food.

Knock. Knock. Knock.

Someone tapped on the door.

Sophie immediately hid the supplies.

"Who's there?"

"It's me. Mrs. Harris."

"You all worked hard today. I brought some hot water."

Her voice sounded cheerful through the door.

"Thank you."

Sophie cracked the door open just enough to accept the bottle.

Then she locked it again immediately.

Looking down at her reddened hands, she had to admit the trip had been brutal.

Without the motivation of finding food, she never would have endured the cold.

Clutching the Vacuum Flask, she crawled beneath her blankets and hoped her body temperature would recover quickly.

Before leaving that morning, Lauren had asked a neighboring resident to watch her four year old daughter.

The moment she returned, a tiny figure launched herself forward.

"Mommy!"

Lauren caught her daughter in a hug.

Then she turned toward the neighbor.

"Thank you so much."

"I really appreciate it."

She offered a packet of instant noodles as thanks.

The woman accepted it with bright eyes.

"Oh, it was nothing."

"Your daughter was an angel. She practically looked after herself."

Lauren smiled.

Then she led her daughter back to their room.

Setting down the bags, she grinned.

"Want to see what Mommy brought back?"

The little girl immediately rushed over and started digging through the sack.

Lauren reached inside.

Then she carefully pulled out three pieces of Milk Candy.

Her eyes softened.

Those candies had originally been found by Sophie.

But Sophie had quietly handed them over.

She'd said only one thing.

"Take these home to your daughter."

"The Long Freeze is hard enough."

"Don't let her entire childhood become a nightmare."

Lauren had accepted the candies with tears in her eyes.

And she hadn't forgotten those words.

Now she placed the sweets into her daughter's tiny hands.

The candies were frozen solid from the cold.

The little girl worked at them for several moments before finally tasting the sweetness.

Her face immediately lit up.

"So good!"

She threw her arms around her mother and squeezed tightly.

For a brief moment, surrounded by endless snow and hardship, the world felt a little warmer.
