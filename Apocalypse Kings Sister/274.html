Chapter 274

"The whole road ahead is packed with zombies. There's no way through.

No way forward, no way back. We'll be surrounded and picked off one by one if we just stand here waiting to die!"

"The only way any of us walks out of here alive is if we fight together, side by side, and cut a path through."

Her voice was calm, hard, impossible to ignore, and it silenced the crowd instantly.

The survivors who'd been screaming and raging a moment ago fell quiet under the force of her presence, their anger draining away.

Only now did they see clearly what was blocking the road ahead.

The middle-aged ringleader who'd been sent sprawling didn't dare say another word. He clutched his chest and slunk off to the side.

A tense hush settled over the crowd. Just as the horde looked ready to break through the front line, someone finally stepped forward, snatching up a loose length of pipe, and joined the soldiers in cutting down the low-level zombies pressing closest.

"Let's finish them."

Once one person moved, more followed. The rest of the survivors surged forward to fight.

Soldiers and civilians fought side by side.

Paisley was the first to notice something was wrong.

An ordinary horde moved with no pattern at all, lashing out on pure instinct, chaotic and reckless.

But this horde attacked with eerie precision. Fearless, relentless, coordinated — they blocked escape routes and flanked from the sides, nothing like mindless, shambling corpses.

******

Stranger still, no matter how fast the group cleared them, more zombies kept sliding into place with uncanny precision, sealing off every path, as if some unseen eye were directing the entire swarm from the shadows.

It was exactly like the horde they'd run into before.

Paisley's eyes went sharp. She stilled her breathing and let her psychic senses flare outward, searching for whatever was pulling the strings.

Five hundred yards out, on a hilltop, a humanoid figure stood motionless on the branch of an old tree.

Mind control had range limits. Get too far, and the effect weakened fast.

The figure stood tall, its skin nearly translucent white, its pupils an unsettling dark red, faint black psychic energy curling around it. It carried none of the rot-stench of a normal zombie, only a cold, bone-deep pressure that set nerves on edge.

It didn't join the fight up close. It simply let its psychic waves ripple out, controlling every zombie across the battlefield — far smarter than most mutated ones.

Level Five — a Psychic Affinity mutant zombie.

They had to take it down first to win.

That thing's intelligence put it well above most low- and mid-tier mutants, rivaling even human Affinity users.

On the hilltop, its dark red pupils narrowed, and an invisible ripple of psychic energy spread out silently over the battlefield.

Fine threads of psychic attack slipped unnoticed into everyone's minds.

A number of soldiers froze mid-motion, heads swimming, grips weakening on their rifles, their formation falling apart.

The civilians took the brunt of it, dizzy and disoriented, hands going still where they stood.

"It's psychic interference!"

Paisley's chest tightened.

This high-tier zombie didn't just direct the horde. It could unleash a wide-area psychic attack, far more dangerous than any brute-force mutant, its methods cold and calculated.

It stayed safely out of reach, watching the slaughter below with detached eyes.

Elijah felt the invisible pressure too, his voice dropping low and cold.

"High-tier psychic zombie. It's controlling everything from the hilltop."

Neither of them needed to say more. They moved in sync.

Elijah quickly briefed the others.

"We're going to kill the one in charge."

Orion nodded, glancing at Paisley. "Pais, be careful. Getting back safe comes first."

Elijah released his Lightning Affinity to clear the way.

Paisley followed close behind, a biting cold spreading from her, freezing every zombie that blocked their path.

The Level Five zombie on the hilltop sensed them closing in. Its dark red pupils flared with murderous intent as it poured everything into its psychic assault.

Countless slivers of psychic force condensed out of nothing, stabbing toward the pair, while it drove half the horde below to converge on their position without restraint.

The surge of psychic force hit like a wave. Any ordinary person's mind would have shattered on the spot, but Paisley's psychic strength held firm, a mental barrier snapping up to deflect it all.

"Not going to work."

She said it quietly, raising a hand to shape clear, sharp ice blades, wind whipping around them. Her ice power swept the whole hilltop edge in frost, freezing solid every zombie that charged.
