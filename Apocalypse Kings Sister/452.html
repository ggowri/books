Chapter 452

Most telling of all, the whole district showed old marks and almost no fresh ones, no new tire tracks, no trampled footprints. Clearly Affinity users rarely came here to loot. It was nearly a forgotten, intact treasure trove.

The SUV finally stopped at the entrance of the top commercial district in the city center.

A giant shopping mall stood before them. Its glass curtain walls were thick with dust, but not shattered on any large scale, and its huge bulk dwarfed the buildings around it, commanding the whole block.

It held a large supermarket, brand stores, warehouses, and cold storage underground, all in one. It was the biggest concentration of supplies in Larkspur.

The electric shutter at the main entrance hung half down. The gap beneath it was pitch black, with no sign of anyone forcing it open. The ground outside was clean and bare of zombie remains.

Everything pointed to one thing. Since the apocalypse, almost nobody had been here, and the place had been kept in remarkable shape.

Ashley leaned against the car window, staring at the grand, intact building, and murmured in amazement.

"Oh my gosh. I've never seen a mall this well preserved since the apocalypse. It's unbelievable."

Ordinary shopping districts in the ruins had been picked clean dozens of times. Anything edible or useful had been carried off, and the buildings were wrecks.

But this giant mall seemed sealed in time, a treasure chest standing quietly in the chaos, waiting to be opened.

Orion opened the door and got out. He looked around, his eyes sharp and steady, scanning the surroundings.

"No signs of a big fight outside, no signs of a beast horde, no camps. Why is that? Is there something here the zombies are afraid of?"

******

Which was exactly why so many of the supplies had survived to this day.

Paisley stepped out and looked up at the towering mall.

Five floors. The ground floor was a big fresh-food supermarket and housewares. The second and third floors had clothing brands, daily goods, and baby and hygiene sections. The fourth held snacks, drinks, and instant foods. The fifth was a large equipment and warehouse area, with two levels of cold storage and a supply depot underground.

With resources drying up everywhere, a complete mall like this was a gold mine.

The wind swept across the empty commercial street and lifted fine dust. It was silent all around, so quiet it was unsettling.

Elijah walked up beside Paisley and gave the team its orders. "Time to go in and search for supplies."

Everyone gripped their weapons, slipped carefully through the gap under the half-lowered shutter, and entered the huge mall one by one.

The moment they stepped in, the sounds of the outside world vanished.

Dead silence.

No wind, no insects, no zombie groans. The vast enclosed space was frighteningly quiet, so still they could hear their own heartbeats and breathing.

Dust floated in the dim daylight. Beams of light slipped through gaps in the broken skylights and cut the air like gray blades, lighting the ruined interior.

The huge supermarket that had once blazed with lights and filled with people was an empty shell.

The ground-floor fresh-food section was eerily orderly.

Rows of refrigerated cases, shelves, and checkout counters stood intact, with shopping carts scattered across the main aisle.

The shelves still held snacks, daily goods, and packaged foods that nobody had managed to grab in the early days, covered in thick dust.

The apocalypse had been going for a month.

The floor was unnaturally clean.

No blood, no corpses, no clutter, not even the bone scraps and debris you usually found around zombies.

It was too clean, so clean it gave a chill. It was just like the world before the apocalypse.

"Something's off," Orion said under his breath, his fingers tense, Metal Affinity quietly building in his palm.

"The cleaner and more intact an abandoned mall is, the more likely a high-level beast is living here. It's cleared everything out and made this its lair."

Madison frowned slightly and spread her senses. "There's a faint, sweet, metallic smell in the air. Not zombie rot. Human blood. It's very subtle and well hidden."

"If my nose hadn't gotten sharper after I awakened, I might not have caught it."

The air in the mall was cold and damp, the interior dim and deep. The long aisles looked like the throat of a crouching beast, pitch black with no visible end.
