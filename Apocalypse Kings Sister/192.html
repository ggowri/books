Chapter 192

When the head of the base heard that, he finally relaxed and quickly answered with a smile.

"Good, that's great to hear!"

Elijah's eyes flickered, and his expression turned serious.

"We were all at death's door yesterday, and someone at this base saved us, which is a debt we can't take lightly. We'd like to thank them in person, so could you tell us who it was?"

Orion chimed in, looking curious and a little puzzled.

"After the poison got into me yesterday, my body was shutting down, my Affinity was falling apart, and I was about to lose consciousness.

"But today, all that deathly pain is gone. My body and my Affinity are back to how they were, and the poison's gone too."

He looked around at everyone, sounding amazed.

"I had no idea Rivergate Base had a healer this incredible hiding here."

The other three soldiers nodded along, equally puzzled and grateful. They'd been unconscious, facing death, with no idea who'd pulled them back. Naturally, they were curious too.

The head of the base looked at Orion and Elijah in surprise.

He never would've guessed that even though they were teammates who spent every day together, none of them knew it was Paisley.

Paisley was clearly sharp and careful, and she kept a low profile, hiding her abilities well.

He remembered yesterday's promise of secrecy, so he pushed his surprise down and gave away nothing, steering the conversation elsewhere instead.

"It was a very powerful, high-level Plant Affinity user. Now that you're all recovered, should tomorrow's aquarium mission go ahead as planned?"

He looked at Elijah seriously, waiting for his decision.

At the mention of the mission, Elijah's easy expression dropped, his face turning grim.

"We need to be fully prepared," he said in a low, serious voice.

"Yesterday, we were only surveying the outside of the aquarium, and we still ran into some unidentified toxic contaminant that took out the whole team. Nobody knows yet what kind of terrifying mutant beasts or high-level contamination sources are hiding inside. It's definitely way more dangerous than we thought."

He thought it through for a moment, then laid out exactly what they'd need.

"This mission is extremely risky, so we need to add two key people to the team. At the very least, we need a Level Five Psychic Affinity user to scout ahead and spot traps, and a Level Five Plant Affinity user to come along the whole way for healing and purification."

He sounded certain. "Ideally, the Plant Affinity user who saved us would come with us. With that healer around, I'd know everyone was safe."

The head of the base nodded right away. "No problem. I'll go work it out right now and do everything I can to get that Affinity user on board, so the mission goes off without a hitch."

Just as he finished, light, brisk footsteps came from the end of the hallway, and Paisley arrived at the door with Ashley and the rest of the group.

The head of the base looked up without thinking, and his eyes brightened the moment he saw Paisley at the front.

It was the healer from yesterday.

Before anyone in the room could react, he walked over and gave Paisley a respectful nod.

"Ms. Lennox, could I have a word with you in private? There's something I'd like to discuss with you alone."

Paisley's eyes flickered, and she gave a calm nod.

The two of them stepped into a private office, leaving everyone else watching with curiosity.

Once he'd made sure no one else was around, the head of the base dropped the pretense and got straight to the point.

"Ms. Lennox, tomorrow's high-risk aquarium mission is extremely dangerous. You already know our top fighters all got badly hurt yesterday. We've talked it over, and we'd like you to come along and handle all the purification and healing for the mission."

Afraid she might turn him down, he pressed on before she could answer.

"I know you like to keep a low profile and stay out of the spotlight. If you're willing to help with this mission, the base will give you an extra 10,000 contribution points, on top of what you earn from the mission itself.

"I promise everyone will keep their mouths shut, so your secret is safe. I'll also have people assigned to protect you."

The moment he finished, Trovie's sweet voice chimed in her head.

[Trading System main quest triggered: Help complete the high-risk aquarium mission successfully.]

Paisley's eyes quietly lit up.
