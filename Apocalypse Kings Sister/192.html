Chapter 192

At that, the last of the Director's worry let go, and he answered with a quick, relieved smile.

“Good, that's good to hear!”

Elijah's eyes shifted slightly, his tone turning serious.

“Thank you for your concern. We were all close to dying yesterday, and someone at this base saved us. That's a debt too heavy to leave unpaid—we'd like to thank them in person. Could you tell us who it was?”

Orion added, curiosity and a little puzzlement in his eyes.

“After the poison took hold yesterday, my meridians were failing, my Affinity falling apart, my mind going with it. But today, that dead feeling is gone. My body, my Affinity, my meridians—all back to normal, and the poison's gone too.”

He looked around the room, something like wonder in his voice.

“I didn't realize Rivergate had a healer like that hiding here.”

The other three soldiers nodded along, equally puzzled and grateful. They'd been unconscious, facing death, with no idea who'd pulled them back. Naturally, they were curious too.

The Director gave the two of them a startled look.

He hadn't expected that their own teammate, someone they saw every day, could have pulled this off without a single one of them realizing it.

It said something about how carefully she kept herself hidden, how sharp she really was under that unassuming surface.

Remembering yesterday's promise of secrecy, he pushed his surprise down and gave away nothing, steering the conversation elsewhere instead.

“It was a very powerful high-tier Plant Affinity user. Now that you're all recovered, should tomorrow's aquarium mission go ahead as planned?”

His gaze settled on Elijah, waiting for a decision.

At the mention of the mission, Elijah's easy expression dropped, his face turning grim.

“We need to prepare for everything.”

His voice was low and serious.

“Yesterday we were only surveying the aquarium's perimeter, and already we ran into an unidentified source of severe toxic contamination that took the whole team down. We still don't know what's waiting inside—what kind of mutant beasts, what level of contamination. Whatever's in there is far beyond what we estimated.”

He thought it through for a moment, then laid out exactly what they'd need.

“The risk on this one is too high as is. We need to add two more key people—at minimum, a Level Five Psychic Affinity user to scout for danger and dodge traps, and a Level Five Plant Affinity user to stay with us the whole time for healing and purification.”

His tone turned certain.

“Ideally, the one who saved us comes along. With her there, I'd trust everyone's safety.”

The Director nodded at once.

“Not a problem. I'll go arrange it myself and do everything I can to bring her on board. We'll get this mission done right the first time.”

Just as he said it, a string of light, unhurried footsteps came down the hall, and Paisley arrived at the door with Ashley and the rest of the group.

The Director glanced up on reflex, and his eyes brightened the moment he recognized the slender figure leading the way.

The healer from yesterday.

Without waiting for anyone else in the room to react, he stepped forward and inclined his head toward her, his manner respectful.

“Ms. Lennox, could I borrow a moment of your time? There's something I'd like to discuss, privately.”

Something flickered in her eyes. She gave a calm nod.

The two of them stepped into a private office together, leaving the rest of the group watching with open curiosity.

Once he'd checked there was no one else around, the Director dropped the pretense and got straight to the point.

“Ms. Lennox, tomorrow's mission to the aquarium is extremely dangerous, as you already know from yesterday, when our top fighters all went down. We'd like to ask you to come along—to handle purification and healing for the whole operation.”

Afraid she might turn him down, he pressed on before she could answer.

“I know you prefer to stay out of the spotlight. If you agree to help, on top of your mission earnings, the base will give you an extra ten thousand contribution points, separately.”

“And I guarantee, everyone will keep quiet. No one will breathe a word of your secret.”

“I'll also assign personnel specifically to protect you.”

The moment he finished, Trovie's voice chimed in her head.

[Main Trading System quest triggered: help complete the high-risk aquarium mission successfully.] [Reward: 5,000 Trading System points, one lucky draw.]

Her eyes lit, quietly.
