Chapter 359

"I'm sure."

Three words, plain and solid, settling the whole room's uncertainty at once.

"But let me examine him first."

She looked at Mrs. Blackwood, her tone serious.

"Mrs. Blackwood, I wouldn't joke about something like his life."

Mrs. Blackwood's fingers trembled, the weight she'd been carrying for days loosening ever so slightly, her eyes reddening.

She'd heard nothing but helpless excuses from every doctor for weeks, grown so used to despair that this plain, certain answer left her almost dazed.

"Really...?"

she asked again, voice shaking, still unable to fully believe this sudden turn.

Paisley nodded gently, unhurried.

"Don't worry. I'll examine him in a moment. Anything I say before that won't convince you anyway."

Ashley chimed in right away, her eyes curving with pride and certainty.

"Don't worry, Mrs. Blackwood. With Pais here, he's going to be fine."

Hearing them, her mother's doubts eased further, a breath of relief escaping, hope filling her eyes.

Just then, the study door at the end of the hall creaked open.

Elijah stepped out, carefully supporting his father.

The poison had worn this once-formidable pillar of the base down to almost nothing.

His face was paper-white, his lips bloodless, exhaustion hanging over every feature, leaning heavily on his son's shoulder, each breath labored, his whole frame so weak it was painful just to watch.

Elijah kept him upright, steady despite the worry plain on his face, his gaze finding Paisley at once, hope and urgency churning in his eyes.

******

He understood Paisley's real abilities better than anyone else in the room.

Twice already, in that danger-riddled lab, he'd survived only because she'd stepped in. Without her, he'd never have made it this far.

He looked at her intently, his voice rough with held-back urgency.

"Pais, can you save my father?"

Mrs. Blackwood blinked, caught off guard.

She knew her son too well. Proud, composed, never one to ask for help, always grinding through things alone. He'd never lower himself to beg a young girl for help in public unless he had nowhere else to turn — or unless he was completely certain of what she could do.

In that moment, the last of Mrs. Blackwood's doubt vanished entirely.

Her son's plea alone was all the proof she needed — Paisley's abilities had to be real.

Watching her husband, weak and barely standing, finally broke through the last of her composure.

Her eyes went red, pleading, almost desperate as she looked at Paisley, her voice breaking.

"Pais, please. Save him."

Paisley stayed calm, offered no extra words, and moved quickly to help Elijah settle his father onto the nearby couch.

She laid her fingers lightly over his wrist, focusing in to assess his condition carefully.

His pulse was faint and erratic under her fingers, the poison having worked its way deep into his meridians and organs.

She calculated quickly — he'd been poisoned for over twenty days.

Which meant someone had poisoned him right at the moment the apocalypse began — a deliberate, vicious act.

She drew her hand back, her voice certain.

"This can be cured."

She looked at Nathaniel.

"It's going to hurt a bit. Please bear with me."

At that, his downcast eyes snapped open, shock flooding through them.

For days he'd been eaten alive by the poison, agony every single day, and every expert he'd called in had told him the same thing — incurable, nothing left but to wait for death.

He'd already made peace with the worst outcome.

He'd never expected to hear anything different.

Was this young girl really capable, or just saying it lightly?

But knowing his son the way he did, whatever the outcome, he was willing to try.
