Chapter 516

Awed whispers exploded through the crowd.

"No wonder Captain Blackwood said ordinary containers couldn't hold it. It's true."

"What is Paisley? A monster? Her Storage Affinity can store flowing river water!"

"With all this clean water, the base's water crisis is finally solved!"

The river kept pouring into the huge reservoir, its roar ringing in everyone's ears.

The crowd was stunned. The whispering stopped abruptly, and only gasps could be heard, again and again.

Some people stood rooted to the spot, forgetting to move their legs, eyes locked on the river pouring out of nothing, pupils shrinking. They muttered to themselves.

"Impossible… how could they find this much clean water?"

An old scavenger who'd been going out for years had horror all over his face. He rubbed his eyes, afraid his exhaustion was causing hallucinations.

"Today a lot of Affinity users went out looking, and not one drop of clean water. And she hauls back a whole river like this…

This is beyond anything I understood about Storage Affinity. Nobody knows how she did it. Can her space even hold living things? How big is Paisley's storage dimension?"

Two young Affinity users who'd been grumbling about exaggeration were pale now, standing blank-faced at the back of the crowd.

Their earlier skepticism had been wiped away by the sight in front of them. Only deep awe was left.

The registrar's hand shook around his tester. He looked from the pouring river to Paisley, a storm raging in his chest.

He'd seen countless Affinity users turn in supplies, barrels of water, bottled water, small collections. But never anyone who simply let loose an entire living river.

The news spread fast, and more and more people crowded around the reservoir.

Base staff, Affinity teams waiting to turn in missions, and even plenty of ordinary residents who'd heard came to watch the amazing scene. Their eyes all turned to Paisley, burning hotter and hotter.

"It's Paisley! Thanks to her! We don't have to worry about clean water anymore!"

"Thank you, Falcon Squad! You took a huge risk going out there and brought back this precious water!"

"Great, we have water! Compared to other bases, we're so lucky. Thank you, Falcon Squad, thank you, Paisley!"

…

Heartfelt thanks rose around them, one after another.

After the Crimson Rain, clean water was life. This pool of clean water had saved countless people at the base.

The water kept pouring into the reservoir, the level rising little by little until the huge pool was nearly full.

Then Paisley thought a command, and the rushing flow stopped. It went quiet, the only sound the soft ripples on the surface.

The registrar checked off all the supplies, confirmed that every mission objective was complete, and finalized the results in the mission system.

The result prompt popped up on the screen.

[Level Seven high-risk search mission complete!]

[Mission rewards: 30,000 contribution points, 800 Level Four crystal cores.]

[Mission exceeded expectations. Bonus reward: 10,000 contribution points.]

The registrar looked up at Paisley and smiled.

"The rewards have been sent to the Falcon Squad captain's account. You can split them however you like."

Elijah pulled up his panel and divided the rewards in front of everyone.

There were five on the team. The 40,000 contribution points came to 8,000 each, and the 800 Level Four crystal cores came to 160 each.

******

Transfer prompts popped up on the panel one after another. Points and crystal cores went to Paisley,

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, and finally, what was left was Elijah's own share.

Paisley looked down at the numbers jumping on her ID card.

Her total contribution points now stood at 270,640.

A hefty fortune. Even across all of Aetheris Base, that was probably enough to rank among the top.

The 160 Level Four crystal cores weren't much use to her now that she was Level Seven, but she could save them for Diane to train with and level up.
