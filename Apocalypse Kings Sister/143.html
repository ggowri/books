Chapter 143

Everyone felt their own breakthroughs at once, and they were stunned and thrilled.

Ashley's eyes went wide, and she practically bounced in her seat.

"I broke through! I'm so full of energy! This one pot of stew did more for me than half a month of hunting mutant beasts!"

Thomas looked amazed and deeply moved.

"Normally, leveling up takes a ton of crystal cores and a lot of dangerous hunting. We just ate one meal and broke through like it was nothing. That's insane."

Spencer nodded over and over, feeling lucky.

"This just saved us at least a thousand high-level crystal cores, plus all the risk of fighting for them. Totally worth it!"

Madison took slow, deep breaths, feeling the energy filling her up. Her face softened in quiet awe.

With Paisley around, getting stronger was like playing with cheat codes. They'd skipped countless detours and saved more resources and time than they could even count.

Orion looked at Paisley with warm affection and gratitude.

Our girl's been quietly helping everyone get stronger this whole time.

In this brutal apocalypse, she'd paved a safe, smooth road for all of them to get stronger.

Elijah looked serious, but there was love hidden in his eyes that nobody else noticed. He was more sure than ever that with Paisley on board, their chances of pulling off the top-secret underground lab mission had gone up several times over, and so had everyone's odds of survival.

Everyone looked at Paisley gratefully. They all knew every one of their breakthroughs was thanks to her.

Without the rare fungus she'd saved, the energy-packed mutant chicken, and her generosity, there was no way they could've broken through so easily.

One wall away, it was a whole different world.

The four Lennoxes were still crammed into their cold, bare room, choking down dry hardtack. They were stressing over a single hot meal and a single crystal core, eaten up by jealousy and resentment.

What they didn't know was that everyone next door had just leveled up. The team had gotten even stronger, and the gap between the two groups kept getting wider.

The warm stew dinner came to an end, but the rich smell still hung in the room.

Everyone pitched in without being asked. They split up the work, cleared the dishes, and wiped down the table until the room was neat and clean again.

Once everything was cleaned up, the room went quiet. Elijah stepped forward, his face serious, and started handing out tomorrow's assignments.

"Tomorrow, Orion and I have something else to take care of, so we can't go with you.

"The rest of you just broke through today, so your new levels aren't stable yet. Tomorrow, all of you should go to the mission hall and take some hunting missions. That way, you can get stronger and stock up on crystal cores at the same time."

Then, Elijah's tone changed, and he sounded serious and sincere.

"Also, I think everyone here should chip in 200 crystal cores and give them to Paisley as payment for tonight.

"Just saying thanks isn't enough. We should give her something real.

"If it weren't for her, none of us would have broken through so easily, and we've saved a ton of resources and life-or-death risks. We owe her big, and we need to show it."

The moment he said it, nobody hesitated. Everyone nodded, and they all looked more than happy to do it.

"I agree! Paisley totally deserves it!" Ashley raised her hand first, her eyes sincere.

"Exactly. Two hundred cores is nothing compared to a breakthrough like this, and if I keep eating for free, I won't have the nerve to show up for dinner next time..." Spencer nodded firmly.

Thomas, Madison, and Marcus agreed too. Nobody objected, and they all truly wanted to pay Paisley back.

Paisley was a little stunned. She was about to turn it down and tell them they didn't need to do that.

But when she looked up, every face was full of sincerity. Nobody looked the least bit reluctant, and they all wanted to give it to her from the heart.

If she said no now, she'd only be brushing off how much they meant it.

After a brief pause, her face softened, and she nodded. "Okay, I'll take it.

"I know you're all using your cores to level up, so there's no rush. Just give them to me whenever you've got some to spare."

After chatting a little longer, everyone got up and said goodnight one by one.

Once she'd seen everyone out and locked the door, the room went completely quiet.

After so many days on edge, she could finally relax. Paisley slipped straight into her Storage Dimension.
