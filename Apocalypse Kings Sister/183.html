Chapter 183

The moment Paisley stepped out through the hall's main doors, the cool evening breeze hit her face. Suddenly, there was a commotion over by the base's main gate, and the mood turned grim.

Five or six military SUVs slowly pulled up, covered in scratches and dark bloodstains, all of them in rough shape.

The doors opened, and uniformed figures climbed out one after another.

Soldiers who were usually so formidable looked like they'd been through hell.

Their combat uniforms were torn and soaked with dark red blood, with new wounds crisscrossing old ones. Their faces were white as paper, their lips colorless, and their breathing weak and ragged. Some could barely stay on their feet, worn down by exhaustion and serious injuries.

Every single one of them was hurt. Some had arms twisted and swollen, and others had shoulders torn into bloody pulp, all in terrible shape.

Medical staff who'd been standing by rushed in immediately, stretchers lined up in neat rows, their faces tense as they checked each soldier's injuries and began emergency treatment in practiced order.

A few guards on duty quickly stepped in to keep order and hold back the crowd, and the mood was tense and heavy.

The attending doctor quickly checked their injuries, frowning deeply, his voice grave and urgent.

"These injuries are really serious. Some unknown toxin is still eating away at their tissue, and regular medicine can't suppress or clear it. They need emergency specialized treatment right now!"

Even as he spoke, they were already wheeling the stretchers into the makeshift hospital.

The crowd at the gate kept growing, and hushed whispers rose all around, full of shock and worry.

"How did they get hurt this badly? They're the strongest elite fighters in our base!"

"Did the scouting mission go wrong? Did they run into a high-level beast horde?"

"Their faces look gray. They must've been hit with some nasty poison! Unknown toxins like that are the worst, and regular meds can't clear them out!"

"This is bad. With toxin damage this severe, they need a high-level healer to purge it, or they'll be dealing with the side effects for the rest of their lives!"

"But Rivergate Base doesn't have any high-level healers."

"If the strongest, most unbeatable team in the base got hurt this badly, things out there must've gotten even more dangerous..."

The whispers kept piling up, and panic and unease spread quietly through the crowd, making the whole area by the gate feel heavy.

Paisley had been planning to turn and leave.

This was Rivergate's internal affair, a matter for its own garrison. It had little to do with her, and she saw no reason to get involved.

But just then, her eyes happened to land on the last stretcher and stopped there.

Whoever lay on it wore a bloodstained uniform. Even though he was badly hurt and weak, his back stayed straight out of instinct, a soldier's toughness that ran bone-deep.

It was Orion.

He's hurt, and it's bad!

Orion, who'd always been so steady and sure of himself, who'd always stood in front of her to protect her, now lay still on the stretcher. His face was pale as frost, his lips cracked and tinged blue, and his breathing was faint and shallow. Blood had spread across his chest in layers, a horrifying sight.

All at once, every bit of her cool detachment crumbled, and her heart clenched.

Paisley couldn't stay out of it anymore. Worry filled her eyes, and without thinking, she hurried after them into the makeshift hospital.

Just then, the head of the base rushed over with a group of senior officials, looking tense, and started firing off questions the moment he arrived.

"What happened? How did the whole team end up this badly hurt?

"How are the wounded? Do everything you can to save them!"

The tension was unbearable. The attending doctor held the full scan report that had just come out, his fingers shaking slightly and his face deadly serious, and reported in a heavy voice.

"Sir! All five unconscious soldiers have severe poisoning, and their radiation contamination is way over the limit!

"The toxin is extremely corrosive, and it's already spread through their blood into their organs and nervous systems. Regular healing meds and basic purification don't work at all. Not only can't they suppress the toxin, they actually make it spread faster!"

He drew a breath and gave the worst of it, his voice hoarse and heavy.

"If we can't fully purge the toxins and contamination from their bodies in time, the poison will eat away at their nervous systems and permanently damage their minds. They'll never wake up again, and they'll end up in a permanent vegetative state!

"Worst case, their organs will fail, and they won't make it through the night!"

"Sir, every second counts right now!"
