Chapter 499

"The base's latest weather alert says at least three more days of heavy snow. The temperature's going to keep dropping."

"So everyone should grab extra warm clothes."

They each picked out clothes that fit and layered up.

In Paisley's mind, Trovie chimed and marked a hidden treasure spot.

She kept her face calm and told the others she was going to check the next section for more supplies. Then she walked toward the spot Trovie had marked.

At the far end of the shelves stood a wall plastered with ad posters. It looked ordinary. Without Trovie's scan, nobody would have given it a second look.

A rusty padlock hung in a corner.

Paisley flicked a finger. A thin plant vine wrapped around the lock without a sound and gave one twist. With a click, the rusted lock snapped and dropped to the floor.

She pushed open the heavy sheet-metal door. A dry, freezing draft rolled out.

It was a hidden warehouse of five thousand square feet, wide and open. Crates were stacked in neat rows nearly to the ceiling.

The boxes were clearly sorted. Canned staples, ration bars, dehydrated vegetables, bottled water, medical gauze, antiseptic, candles, fuel blocks, spare batteries,

and piles of intact comforters, blankets, oil heaters, and electric heaters. Food, clothes, tools, all of it, in perfect condition, barely touched.

No effort at all. Free shopping is the best.

Paisley thought a command and opened her storage dimension. Crate after crate vanished into it.

When she finished, she noticed a small alloy door deeper inside.

She opened it. Inside was a hidden small vault. Gold bars and coins sat neatly in boxes, along with whole sets of platinum jewelry and gemstone necklaces and bracelets that gleamed softly in the dim light. She guessed it came to about 3,300 pounds.

Trovie spoke.

[Converting gold to system points…]

[Gold converted automatically. You received 30,000 system points!]

[Congratulations, Master! You've stockpiled a massive amount of strategic supplies and triggered a large stockpile bonus. +5,000 points!]

[Current total: 3,560,360 points!]

Pale blue text flickered across her mind, and 3,300 pounds of gold turned into points in an instant.

Not much. Still almost 1.5 million short of the 5 million I need to make Level Five trader. At this rate, who knows how much more I'll have to loot.

Deep down, she hoped that someday she'd run into an entire mountain of gold.

Paisley left the vault and went back to the third-floor clothing section to rejoin the group.

Ashley and Jasper had already picked out their own clothes and were waiting.

Jasper saw her and hurried over, eager. "Paisley, find anything over there? We're all set!"

"I found a storeroom and collected a lot of supplies," Paisley said with a nod.

Jasper didn't know she had a Storage Affinity. He peered past her toward the storeroom, puzzled.

"Where is it? Do you need help? We can carry it together."

Paisley didn't explain. She just thought a command, in front of everyone.

All the clothes, comforters, and bedding piled across the floor vanished in five seconds. The entire floor emptied out, leaving only toppled, snow-covered shelves.

Jasper's jaw dropped and his eyes went wide. He stood frozen.

"What… what just happened?"

"Where did it all go? It's gone?"

One teammate rubbed his eyes hard and muttered in disbelief. "Am I seeing things? There was a huge pile of supplies just now! Where did it go?"

The other pointed at Paisley, his voice full of awe.

"I heard last night that Paisley had awakened Storage Affinity, and that her space was insanely big. I figured it was just base gossip. Now that I've seen it myself, I'm completely sold."

"So it's true! Paisley is amazing!"

"I wonder how many affinities she's actually awakened. I already know of three!"

Ashley lifted her chin, a little proud, and teased.

"You've never seen anything, have you?"
