Chapter 519

Twenty-odd years of raising her wasn't something she could shake off just because she wanted to.

Rachel went back to her own room too, shut the door, and blocked out all the rambling.

She frowned, deeply irritated.

Her mom had become more and more of a nag. She wasn't the mother who used to favor Rachel in everything.

In Rachel's mind, the root of every misfortune was Paisley.

She dug her nails into her palm and swore in her heart.

One day, she would grind Paisley under her feet.

She opened the base's public forum. Post after post kept refreshing, every one of them hot and about Paisley.

[#PaisleyLennox is our goddess]

[#Falcon Squad is unstoppable, everyone's heroes]

[#Paisley brought back a whole living river today and solved the base's water problem. She's amazing!]

[#How crazy is Paisley's Storage Affinity? It can hold living things?]

[#Does anyone know how big Paisley's storage dimension is?]

…

Rachel's eyes grew darker as the posts rolled by.

A storm surged through her. In her last life, that bracelet space had never been this terrifying. It could only hold a small number of lifeless things.

Had Paisley been reborn, and the bracelet space with her, upgraded and transformed?

Raw greed gripped her.

That bracelet belonged to me in my last life.

It can only belong to me in this one.

If she took back that space bracelet, everything Paisley had now, all that glory, would be hers.

Then the one everyone worshipped, the one the base leaders valued, would be Rachel Lennox.

At that thought, a thick, cruel greed flashed in her eyes, and a cold smile curved her lips.

She lightly rubbed the plain trinket on her wrist and ran through plans in her head, scheming for the right moment to take Paisley's bracelet back.

******

Meanwhile, in the main Lennox family villa,

Vivian sat before the living room terminal, scrolling through forum posts praising Paisley and Falcon Squad.

The screen was full of worship. Paisley's reputation grew by the day. More and more ordinary people and Affinity users backed her, and supporters of General Blackwood's side kept multiplying with them.

Vivian's expression darkened bit by bit, and anger hid between her brows.

"If this keeps up, fewer and fewer people will support the Lennox family."

"This Paisley dares to take the spotlight from me.

She's just an adopted daughter. What does she have to compare with me?"

"We can't let them keep stealing the spotlight."

Harold Lennox sat in the seat at the head and glanced at his agitated daughter, steady, with a note of reproach.

"When did you learn to shout? No matter what the situation, keep your composure."

"Paisley is nothing. She was never worthy of being compared to you. But now she's grabbed all the attention."

Harold's gaze fell on the terminal posts and slowly turned grave.

"I never expected her Storage Affinity to be this powerful. Put together every Storage Affinity user in the main family, and their space still wouldn't match hers."

"She's become an obstacle to our plans."

His finger tapped lightly on the table, a glint of killing intent in his eyes.

"If she can't be used by the Lennox family, then she has to be removed.

Earlier I thought the Quinns were overreacting. I planned to watch from the sidelines.

Now it looks like we have to move to take her out, and fast."

"If we let her keep growing, she'll become our biggest threat."

"Think of something. Come at it through the minor branch. Aren't they Paisley's adoptive parents? Years of raising her aren't so easy to cut off."

"You can start with this severance notice. Use it to show everyone her cold side and tarnish her name. It has no downside."

Harold paused and looked at Vivian, his tone turning to advice.

"And Vivian, you've been too quiet lately. That's bad for the family's future.

After the Crimson Rain, the base's scarcest resource is purifiers."

"You're a Level Six purifier. Right now is the best time to step forward and win people over.

Show everyone at the base who's truly indispensable."

"She can never compare to you on that."
