Chapter 425

The whole room went silent as everyone waited for orders.

Nathaniel stopped at the podium. His deep voice carried through the solemn room, every word strong and clear.

"Today's grain depot mission is complicated and dangerous. High-level mutant beast packs, unknown natural traps, and enemy forces lying in ambush. The layers of danger make this harder than any field mission you've ever done."

He named the lethal risks plainly, then his tone shifted, his eyes steady and burning, full of hope and respect.

"But you are Falcon Squad. You're the sharpest blade on the base and the hope and confidence of thousands of people."

"On this trip, you carry the burden and take the risk to protect the base's lifeline and thousands of lives.

You are, without question, the base's heroes."

He raised a hand in salute, grave and solemn, full of an elder's concern and a commander's trust.

"I don't ask you to bring back all of it. I only ask that you all come home safe.

Everyone on the base is waiting for you, with a celebration feast ready, waiting for the falcons to return to the nest!"

"Everyone comes back safe!"

The last order was slow and heavy, carrying a thousand pounds of hope.

"Understood, Chief! We'll complete the mission or die trying! Everyone comes back!"

Falcon Squad answered together, their voices roaring through the room, full of fight.

The troops were set. The will to fight was lit.

When the meeting ended, they left in order to collect the supplies they'd need from the base's war storehouse.

The team members worked in pairs, drawing supplies in an orderly way, quick and practiced.

High-power rifles and armor-piercing pistols were loaded and checked. Sharp, tough combat knives went on belts, their blades cold, ready for close combat.

Plenty of high-energy ration bars, nutrient packs, and portable water purification packs went into tactical bags to keep everyone's strength up.

Special healing spray, Affinity recovery vials, and antitoxin serum were handed out for emergency injuries and poison.

After Elijah collected his own high-end weapons and supplies, he quietly requested a light protective gear set and an antidote and passed them to Paisley, his voice low and steady.

"Wear this close to your body. If anything happens, look after yourself first."

Paisley nodded, took them, and stowed them in her tactical bag, quick and clean.

The others had also finished, geared up, bags on, weapons in place, fully armed from head to toe, grim and cold.

In just over ten minutes, everyone had drawn their supplies and climbed into the armored vehicles the base had modified for them.

Elijah looked toward the heavy horizon. "Everyone, move out! Target, the grain depot in the Exclusion Zone!"

"Move out!"

Mason took the driver's seat, hands on the wheel, calm and experienced.

He was the squad's oldest driver. He'd been going on missions since before the apocalypse and had spent years on dangerous roads, so he knew every kind of terrain. He tapped the gas, and the heavy armored vehicle rolled steadily out of the base gate toward the Exclusion Zone.

******

Inside, the mood was quiet and relaxed, with tension underneath.

Paisley turned her head, leaned lightly on Ashley's shoulder, closed her eyes, and slept.

Ashley kept her body stiff and still, afraid that the slightest movement would wake her sister.

She quietly served as the most stable pillow, guarding her.

At the front of the vehicle, Elijah spread out a high-resolution digital map of the Exclusion Zone, its glow lighting his cold face.

Orion, Thomas, and Spencer gathered around. The four of them stared at the dense route markings, scrolling with their fingertips, reviewing and comparing the roads, zombie distribution, and suspected ambush sites, trying to find the safest, fastest route.

Elijah pointed deep into the map and analyzed in a low voice.

"At normal speed, we should reach the depot around four in the afternoon.

Zombies go berserk in the Exclusion Zone at night and Affinities get unstable, so driving back after dark would double the risk. It's too dangerous."

He moved his finger to an abandoned district near the edge of the map. "The route runs near the abandoned area of Ridgemont. According to reliable information, there are lots of supplies left there.

We can reach the depot first and finish the mission, then move into Ridgemont before dark, rest, scavenge supplies, stay a night, and head back to base tomorrow."

He counted the team's gear, his tone getting more serious.
