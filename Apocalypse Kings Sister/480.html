Chapter 480

Their plan had looked perfect.

Falcon would run into a broken bridge and a high-tier shark. They'd burn through time, strength, and power, and wear themselves out dealing with it.

Crossing a frozen river would be impossible, leaving them in no shape to fight.

Reality was the opposite!

Paisley had handled all of it herself. She had awakened a terrifying Ice-Water Affinity, froze the river, killed the shark, and held up the bridge, and the whole team was unhurt and confident.

They were all rattled.

Paisley's strength had already made her famous across the base. After a few days apart, she'd grown even stronger and had pulled out Ice-Water Affinity as a new card. Her power had jumped again.

On one side was Elijah's peak Level Six Lightning. On the other was Paisley, with new cards every fight, getting stronger as she went.

Against these two, a ragtag group with no real confidence stood no chance.

It would be suicide!

The will to fight collapsed. Fear took over. Even when they forced themselves into fighting stances, their bodies already admitted defeat. Their legs were stiff, their power sluggish, and even calling on their Affinities felt uncertain.

The fight that looked ready to explode was decided from the start.

There was no tense clash and no battle of equals.

These people were afraid and didn't want to fight. When their nerve cracked, they couldn't hold on, and one after another they dropped to their knees and begged.

"We give up! We're not fighting!"

"Grant forced us to come! We didn't mean to block Falcon Squad!"

"Spare us! We'll retreat right now, we swear we won't do it again!"

...

Begging rose again and again. A battlefield full of killing intent turned absurd.

At the front, Grant gritted his teeth, forcing himself to stand against Elijah's lightning pressure.

He powered up the alloy prosthetic arm he'd just fitted, trying to form metal blades and strike back, but in the next instant, Elijah's eyes flashed cold.

Crackle!

A violent, freezing violet bolt of lightning lashed down and struck Grant's prosthetic arm dead on!

Peak Level Six lightning was merciless and perfectly suited to wreck metal. The current punched through the alloy's surface and destroyed the power structure inside.

With a sharp snap, the hard metal arm cracked and broke, ruined!

The roaring current ran through Grant's whole body, locking his veins and shattering his power. He didn't last five seconds. His body stiffened, his eyes rolled up, and he dropped to the ground, out cold, all fight gone.

On the other side, Chloe faced Ashley. They hadn't even traded a blow. One cold look from Ashley and the sense of her heavy Earth power was enough to make Chloe panic.

She was only a healer and no match for Ashley. If they fought, she'd end up hurt and humiliated.

To avoid the beating, she rolled her eyes, went limp, and dropped to the ground, pretending to faint.

The whole battle became a farce.

Ashley looked down at Chloe on the ground, then at the enemies begging and scattering, and couldn't help raising an eyebrow, half laughing, with a dry, teasing tone.

"That's it? Did they come here to do stand-up?"

"I haven't even thrown a punch and it's over already?"

All of Falcon Squad was speechless.

Nobody had expected that Grant and his group, after planning an ambush and blocking the bridge like they'd fight to the death, would end the battle like this.

Nobody went easy on them. They dragged every fainted and kneeling enemy together, tied their hands and feet, locked down every movement, and strapped them to the roof of the SUV.

When that was done, everyone got back in the car and set off again, racing toward Aetheris Base.

The car was quiet inside, only the wind from the speed hissing past.

Elijah looked down at the map in his hand and tapped a spot ahead.

"In another half hour there's an abandoned gas station. We can stop there to rest and recover."

He looked up at everyone, his voice heavier.

"The Great Freeze is coming. The road home may get even more dangerous. Everyone stay alert for the rest of the trip."
