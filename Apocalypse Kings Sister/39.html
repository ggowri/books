Chapter 39

Now that Elijah could breathe easy, he turned to Orion. "Put the lock back the way it was. Don't leave any sign that someone's been in here."

Then, he looked back at Paisley, his voice gentle. "Paisley, we still need to stock up on a lot of medicine. Any idea where the hospital keeps it?"

Paisley looked up at him, surprised and a little amused.

He really didn't hold back, did he? Ever since he'd seen what her senses could do, he'd been treating her like his personal radar, and he wasn't even shy about it.

She pointed toward the west building. "If I'm right, the hospital's pharmacy storeroom is in the basement of the west building. All the specialty meds, antibiotics, emergency supplies, sealed vaccines, and rare medical stuff should be stockpiled down there."

Then, her face turned serious. "But it's really dangerous down there, way more dangerous than the main building.

"The basement's sealed and dark, and high-level mutant zombies have taken over down there. There are at least two Level Three zombies."

By now, none of them doubted Paisley's calls, and their faces turned grim.

Elijah's eyes sharpened. "Medicine is priceless in the apocalypse, and this stuff could save countless lives. No matter how dangerous it is, we have to get it."

Orion turned to Paisley right away, sounding worried. "Pais, that basement's way too dangerous.

"With Level Three zombies down there, I can't promise I'll be able to keep you safe.

"Maybe I should take you back to the car, and you can wait for us there."

Elijah nodded. "I agree. There are only a few low-level zombies near the car, so you'll be safer there.

"The three of us will make this quick. I'm worried I won't be able to watch your back in a fight, so the car's the safer bet."

But Paisley knew exactly what she could do. Her Level Three Plant Affinity was great for attacking and defending, she could heal herself fast, and her senses were way sharper than anyone else's.

If she went along, she could predict where the high-level zombies would move and sense any hidden danger before it hit. She could also use her Plant Affinity to heal people and hold the enemies back. If anything, she'd make it safer for everyone.

Besides, there was more down there than high-level zombies. Something else was lurking in that basement, and even she didn't know what it was.

These people were doing everything they could to protect her, and she was going to protect them too.

Paisley gently shook her head, soft but firm. "Being alone in the car would scare me even more. I'd rather come with you guys, since I know you'll keep me safe."

She'd clearly made up her mind, so they didn't push it. They just kept her behind them and stayed alert.

Orion quickly fixed the lab's lock and made sure they hadn't left a trace. Then, they all hurried back down to the first-floor lobby.

They'd barely reached the lobby when a bloodcurdling scream came from the west building. It ripped through the silent hospital, full of terror and despair.

The screaming mixed with raspy zombie growls and the sound of claws ripping through cloth and flesh. It made their skin crawl.

Elijah's face went cold. "That's the west building. Something's wrong over there.

"Everyone stay sharp. Let's move."

Before he'd even finished, he was sprinting out of the lobby with blue lightning crackling around him.

Orion and Mason ran on either side of Paisley with their machetes out, right on Elijah's heels. The four of them kept formation the whole way to the west building.

The west building was way more wrecked and darker than the east building. Claw marks and dried black blood covered the walls, and the floor was littered with broken glass, rotting garbage, and old bloodstains. The stench of rot was strong enough to choke on.

Zombie growls kept echoing from deep inside, one after another, and it set everyone on edge.

Paisley could sense the two Level Two mutants rampaging inside. They'd stirred up all the Level One zombies, and now the whole pack was crowded around the stairwell exit.

The four of them quickly reached the west building's first-floor entrance.

Before they could even get to the stairwell, two people came stumbling out of the pitch-black hallway, scrambling for their lives.
