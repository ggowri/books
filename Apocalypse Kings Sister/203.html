Chapter 203

Rachel's so-called foresight was nothing but scraps of blurry memory from her past life. She had no clue where anything actually was.

She vaguely remembered that the aquarium hid something valuable, but she couldn't remember which room it was in or where exactly.

But with everyone staring at her and waiting for an answer, she had no room to back down.

And if she showed any fear now, she'd never get her hands on anything.

She quickly pushed down her panic and put on a timid, fragile look, biting her lip as she pointed toward the dark tunnel at the very back of the aquarium.

"I... I just sensed it," she said. "The equipment and the files are in the deep-sea performance hall, the one all the way in the back. It's the darkest, creepiest room in here, so that's got to be where they are."

They're all going to die here anyway. Right or wrong, no one will ever know the truth.

Even as she finished, a vicious glint hid in her eyes.

Chase played along right away. He looked up lazily, put on a serious act, and sent out a thread of psychic power like he was really checking.

"I just swept the area," he chimed in. "The deepest part has the most activity by far, with danger and energy all tangled up back there. That's our target, and she's right."

With Chase, a Level Five psychic, backing her up, a lot of the regular team members let go of their doubts right away. Nobody objected.

But at the very front, Paisley gave a cold, mocking smile.

The aquarium's strange magnetic field blocked her psychic power and kept her from scanning for details, but she could still clearly read the flow of the air and energy.

The deep-sea performance hall at the back gave off none of the steady energy that stored equipment would. Instead, it was thick with mutant-beast rage and the smell of blood.

This wasn't foresight. Rachel was just guessing, or worse, deliberately leading everyone down the deadliest path.

Elijah and Orion saw through it right away too. They frowned slightly, and their faces turned cold.

The scanner sent back its data at the same moment, and the screen showed it clearly. The deepest hall was crawling with high-risk life signals, and there was no mechanical trace of any equipment or files.

That settled it for Elijah and Orion: Rachel was lying, and Chase was covering for her. They hadn't come to help at all. They'd come to sabotage the mission.

But with the mission underway and the whole team gathered here, now wasn't the time to start a fight among themselves.

Elijah pushed down the cold fury rising inside him and kept a straight face as he gave the order in a deep voice. "Everyone, check your gear. We're splitting into three teams.

"One team per vehicle. Each team moves on its own, so scout carefully along the way and stay on guard.

"My team will take the left corridor into the building. Chase, you lead your people down the main passage in the middle. Everyone else will go around through the side passage on the right."

He'd thought it all through. Right now, the team wasn't united, and nobody could tell who was really there for the mission and who was up to something. Forcing everyone to stick together would only leave them open to being sabotaged at every turn.

Splitting up was the safest move they had right now.

As soon as he gave the order, everyone moved fast and checked their weapons.

Chase took the assignment without hesitation, hiding a sneer. "Fine by me."

It was exactly what he wanted. Once they split up, he'd finally be out from under the main team's eyes, free to sabotage them and stage accidents however he liked. This time, they'd all die in there.

He made a show of pulling Rachel close to his side, sweet and fragile as she looked.

Under her sleeve, Rachel's fingers curled slightly as she held back a secret, satisfied smile. This time, Paisley has to die. That pendant is going to be mine.

Paisley gave Rachel a cold look and saw right through her. Rachel was definitely up to something again. We'll see who ends up dead today.

Elijah looked over the whole group and gave one last stern warning. "Everyone, keep in touch. Don't do anything reckless, and don't break away from your team.

"If you find any leads on the classified equipment or files, report it on the team channel right away and stay where you are. Do not touch or damage anything yourself!"

Chase let out an impatient scoff and waved a hand. "Yeah, I get it. Enough with the lectures."

With that, he led Rachel and four others straight into the pitch-black main passage, strutting the whole way, and the dark swallowed them within seconds.

On the other side, Paisley followed Elijah and headed for the left corridor with Orion and the others.

The moment they stepped into the cold, dead-silent building, a damp, rotting sea wind hit them in the face. The thick walls even blocked out the sound of the wind, and the silence was so complete it made their hearts pound.
