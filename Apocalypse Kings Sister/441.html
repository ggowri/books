Chapter 441

Hum!

A freezing psychic pressure exploded outward and covered the whole sky, heavier and more savage than yesterday.

Black shadows churned and rolled, blotting out the thin morning light.

The human-headed, snake-bodied Level Seven mutant appeared.

Its upper body was a cold, corpse-pale human torso. The face was handsome but lifeless, and its narrow, jet-black slit pupils locked on Elijah, full of bitter hatred and a hunger to kill.

The wound at its neck from yesterday had healed completely. The skin was smooth and unmarked. Its recovery was just as absurd as before.

Its thirty-three-foot tail coiled and slid, black scales glinting like cold metal in the faint light. Dark venom seeped between the scales and dripped to the ground, eating little curls of white smoke out of the earth.

After a night of recovery it wasn't weaker at all. Its presence was wilder and heavier than ever. Yesterday it had been mid Level Seven. Now it was peak Level Seven.

It got that strong overnight.

It must have devoured countless zombies or Affinity users yesterday to evolve that much.

If it kept growing, killing it would only get harder.

And it clearly still held a grudge against Elijah.

A cold, rasping voice, laced with hissing, rolled over the ruins.

"You insect... walking right into my hands. Today you die. Consider it an honor to become my meal."

The words weren't finished when its huge body shot forward.

Boom!

The air tore apart under the force, and the shriek of wind crushed the ruins.

Peak Level Seven pressure slammed down. The ground cracked and sank in layers, and small stones turned to powder before they could leave the ground.

All the serpent mutant wanted was Elijah dead, to wipe out yesterday's humiliation.

The thirty-three-foot black tail whipped out like a ghost, trailing a cloud of corrosive poison mist, and swung down toward Elijah's chest with crushing force.

This strike was far wilder and more terrifying than the one that nearly killed him yesterday.

Elijah's heart lurched.

He felt the gap in realms immediately. Peak Level Seven pressure came down like a mountain, choking off his blood.

He didn't dare underestimate it. He burned through his Level Six Affinity, poured all of it into his arms, and blocked head-on.

Clang!

The noise rattled his eardrums, and a massive shock rebounded up through the rifle.

His palms split and bled. His arms went numb and weak, and he was thrown backward like he'd been hit by a speeding train, slamming into a broken wall.

He coughed up a mouthful of hot blood. His old wounds tore open, and pain shot through his whole body.

"Now!" Paisley shouted.

All the hidden teammates burst out of cover, no hesitation. The planned encirclement went off at exactly the right moment.

From the high ground on the west side, Spencer's Fire Affinity roared. A giant wave of red flame swept across the sky and formed a wall of fire across the battlefield. The heat burned off the poison mist and blocked the serpent from dodging left or right.

Orion's Metal Affinity formed countless golden blades that rained down, driving into every gap between its scales and slowing its charge.

On the east side, Thomas poured out his Plant Affinity.

Vines tore out of the ground, thick and knotted and covered in thorns, wrapping over and over around the tail and torso, trying to lock it in place.

But how could they stop a peak Level Seven's body?

Rip!

The black scales flared with cold light as the serpent shook violently. The tough vines tore apart and snapped, splintering into pieces, green sap flying.

******

Thomas grunted. The backlash made his blood surge, and he stumbled back several steps, a thin line of blood at the corner of his mouth.

The serpent's slit pupils flared with fury. Nobody was going to stop it from tearing Elijah apart.

It twisted around and swung its tail in a wide sweep, wrapped in concentrated black venom, and lashed at Elijah where he lay half-propped on the ground. The attack was savage and final.

In the rear, Ashley went pale and threw out her Earth Affinity.

Thick walls of rock stacked up in layers and rose in front of Elijah, several feet thick.
