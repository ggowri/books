Chapter 240

Paisley froze for a second when Elijah suddenly blocked her view. Just as she looked up to see what was going on, Trovie's voice suddenly popped up in her head.

[Master! I'm detecting a huge energy strawberry patch about 550 yards to the left! It's a rare mutant plant from the apocalypse, packed with energy, and it can be traded for tons of points!]

Paisley spoke up right away to let everyone know. "Captain Blackwood, Orion, there's a strawberry patch to the left. They're rare mutant plants that can quickly restore and boost powers, so let's hurry over and check it out."

After making it through several hopeless situations, everyone trusted Paisley without question by now. Nobody doubted anything she said, and they believed in her one hundred percent.

They followed her a few hundred yards to the left, cutting through woods overgrown with weeds, and suddenly, the sight in front of them made their eyes light up.

A strawberry patch of about 5,000 square feet appeared in front of them. The mutant strawberries were growing like crazy, each one plump and huge, as big as an apple. They were bright red and glossy, and they looked ready to burst with juice.

Even from a distance, they could clearly feel the warm, rich energy swirling around the fruit, and waves of power washed over them nonstop.

Thomas's eyes went wide, and he couldn't help gasping. "I can't believe there are actually strawberries out here in the middle of nowhere!

"These strawberries are as big as apples! I really want to take a bite!"

As soon as he finished, Thomas took a few steps forward and actually reached out to pick the closest plump strawberry.

But just as his fingertips were about to touch the fruit, the ground suddenly shook violently, and the bushes burst apart.

A huge bear charged out from deep in the strawberry patch and reared up a full 26 feet tall. Its whole body was covered in thick, coarse, dark fur, and its sharp fangs were bared. Sticky drool kept dripping from its mouth, and its savage eyes were locked on the people who'd invaded its territory.

A wave of fierce, beastly pressure swept over everyone.

It was actually a Level Six mutant bear!

Everyone's hearts lurched, and their whole bodies went tense. A Level Six mutant beast was incredibly fierce and way stronger than ordinary mutant beasts, definitely not something they could take on easily!

The giant bear had already crouched down. With its huge, bloody mouth open, teeth bared and drool dripping, it lunged at Thomas in front of it with overwhelming power, moving shockingly fast.

In a split second, Paisley's eyes turned fierce, and power burst out of her. Countless green vines shot out of the ground like lightning, wrapped precisely around Thomas's waist, and yanked him back hard.

With a whoosh, the strong, flexible vines pulled Thomas back into the middle of the group before he knew what was happening. He barely escaped the bear's deadly pounce.

With a thunderous boom, the huge, heavy paw slammed into the ground right where Thomas had just been standing. The hard dirt cracked into a spiderweb of lines, and rocks and soil flew everywhere. It was terrifying.

Thomas stood frozen in place, his back soaked with cold sweat and his heart racing. A wave of fear swept through his whole body as it sank in how close that had been.

He looked at the huge, vicious paw just inches away, and his throat went tight and his legs went weak.

A split second had almost turned him into the Level Six bear's lunch. He'd brushed right past death.

He swallowed hard, still shaken, and blurted out, "Holy shit, what the hell is that thing?!"

He looked up at the giant beast towering over them, horrified. "That thing's almost as tall as a two-story building!"

Next to him, Mason went pale and confirmed it in a shaky voice. "It's a Level Six mutant bear. Its aura is terrifying!"

Thomas didn't care about the strawberries anymore. Terrified, he quickly turned to Paisley and urged her to run.

"Paisley, forget the strawberries! That big guy looks way too vicious, and we can't handle it, so let's get out of here! If we can't win, we run, because staying alive comes first!"

But before he could finish, heavy footsteps suddenly sounded, thudding over and over.

The 26-foot black bear slowly stepped closer. Every step made the ground tremble, and every plant in the strawberry patch shook with it. Its massive aura bore down on everyone, making it hard to breathe.

The giant bear's blood-red eyes were locked on the intruders. Hot breath blew from its nose and mouth, its sharp fangs glinted coldly, and it radiated overwhelming bloodlust, giving them no chance to retreat.

There was nowhere to hide and nowhere to run!

"Everyone, attack together! We can't back down!" Elijah shouted sharply. His eyes turned fierce, and he was the first to call up his power.
