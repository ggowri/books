Chapter 240

The sudden shadow falling over her made Paisley blink, and just as she looked up to figure out what had happened, Trovie's voice chimed in her head.

[Host! Five hundred yards to the left, detecting a massive patch of energy strawberries, a rare mutated plant species from this era, packed with Affinity energy and worth a huge number of points!]

Paisley spoke up right away to alert the group.

"Captain, Orion, there's a strawberry patch off to the left, rare mutated plants that can boost and restore Affinity fast. Let's go check it out."

After everything they'd been through, the group trusted Paisley completely by now. Not a single person questioned her. Her word was good enough for all of them.

They followed her lead a few hundred yards to the left, cutting through overgrown woodland, and the sight that opened up ahead stopped them cold.

A strawberry patch, roughly five thousand square feet, spread out before them, the mutated strawberries growing to incredible size, each one as big as an apple, deep red and glistening, flesh plump and full.

Even from a distance, a thick, warm pulse of Affinity energy radiated off the fruit, washing over them in steady waves.

Thomas's eyes went wide with shock, and he couldn't help blurting out loud.

"I can't believe there's actually a strawberry patch out in the middle of nowhere like this!

These things are the size of apples, I want to bite into one right now!"

He stepped forward as he spoke, reaching out to pluck the nearest plump strawberry.

But just as his fingers brushed the fruit, the ground shook violently, thick soil trembling, and the surrounding brush exploded apart!

A massive bear tore out from deep within the strawberry patch, rearing up to a full twenty-six feet tall, its body covered in thick, coarse, pitch-black fur, fangs bared and razor-sharp, thick drool dripping from its jaws, eyes blazing with murderous intent as it locked onto the group of intruders.

A crushing wave of predatory pressure rolled out, slamming over everyone at once.

It was a Level Six mutant bear.

Everyone's blood went cold instantly, every muscle locking tight. A Level Six mutant was an entirely different class of threat, far beyond anything ordinary, absolutely not something to take lightly.

The massive bear had already crouched low, jaws wide, teeth bared, drool dripping, radiating overwhelming power as it lunged straight for Thomas standing nearby, moving with terrifying speed, all in the span of a heartbeat.

Paisley's eyes went sharp, her energy surging instantly. Countless green-black vines tore up from the ground, shooting out like lightning, wrapping tight around Thomas's waist and yanking him back with brutal force.

SHWA—!

The taut, powerful vines hauled Thomas back into the middle of the group before he could react, pulling him clear of the bear's lethal strike by a hair's breadth.

BOOM!

The massive paw slammed down exactly where Thomas had been standing a second earlier, the hard-packed earth splitting into spiderweb cracks, dirt and debris flying in every direction, the force of it staggering.

Thomas stood frozen in place, his back drenched in cold sweat, his heart pounding wildly, the full weight of how close that had been crashing over him.

Staring at the massive, snarling paw just inches from where he'd been standing, his throat went tight, his legs shaking beneath him.

That split second had nearly made him a meal for a Level Six bear. He'd brushed past death itself.

He swallowed hard, voice still unsteady with shock, and let out a stunned curse.

"What in the world is that thing?!"

He craned his neck up at the towering beast looming over them, eyes wide with disbelief.

"That thing's almost two stories tall!"

Beside him, Mason had gone pale, his voice shaking as he confirmed it.

"That's a Level Six mutant bear. This kind of pressure isn't normal at all!"

Thomas had completely lost all interest in strawberries by now, fear taking over entirely. He turned fast toward Paisley, urging her, voice tight with panic.

"Forget the strawberries! That thing looks way too dangerous, we can't take it on, let's get out of here!"

But before he could finish, heavy footsteps thundered through the ground.

THUD. THUD.

The twenty-six-foot-tall bear advanced slowly, each step shaking the ground beneath their feet, the entire strawberry patch trembling with every stride, the crushing weight of its presence making it hard to even breathe.

Its blood-red eyes locked onto the intruders, hot breath steaming from its nostrils, fangs gleaming cold, murderous intent radiating off it with no sign of backing down.

There was no running. No retreating.

"Everyone, fight! Hold your ground!"

Elijah's voice cut through sharp and commanding, his gaze turning razor-edged as he moved first, power already surging to life in his hands.
