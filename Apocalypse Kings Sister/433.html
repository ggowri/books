Chapter 433

The moment that green power entered him, his clenched, spasming body loosened.

Ribbons of jade light ran along his veins, wrapping around the black venom. Under that pure life force, the poison that had been spreading and corroding began to break apart and fade.

******

Paisley frowned and kept feeding in power, patiently mending his torn veins and rattled organs, smoothing the damage away.

The hard part was the mental contamination lodged in his mind. It had no body, and it dug in deep.

While her Plant Affinity healed his body, she sent out a soft purifying force to sweep the cold, vicious taint out of his mind, bit by bit.

Time ticked by.

The ugly blue-black drained from Elijah's face. Color crept back into his skin. His breathing evened out, and his pulse steadied.

The internal injuries closed under that steady flow. The blood on his body dimmed, and the shadow of death around him lifted.

At last his thick lashes fluttered.

His eyes opened. For a moment they were hazy, his vision still blurred by leftover poison. Then he saw the girl bent over him, working with total focus, and those steady, battle-hardened eyes rippled.

His mind cleared. The pain and numbness were gone, replaced by a soothing warmth that ran through his limbs and fed his body and his mind.

He remembered everything. The tail smashing through his shield, the agony, the poison in his lungs, his mind collapsing. He'd been sure he was dead.

Yet here he was, alive.

Paisley had burned through her own power to drag him back from death's door.

"Pais..."

His voice was rough and weak. He said her name softly. Tenderly.

He looked up. Her face was cool and clear, her hair a little messy, her skin pale, a thin sheen of sweat at her temples. She'd spent a lot of power on him.

She was always distant, never bothered by anything. Yet now her eyes were full of worry for him as she checked him over.

Something hard and cold inside him gave way and melted.

The trust of fighting side by side, the lifeline she'd handed him when things were darkest, the choices made at the edge of death, all of it piled up and burned hotter.

It wasn't just gratitude between teammates anymore. The feelings he'd kept buried for so long broke loose, stronger than ever.

He gazed at her face, so close. His composure fell away, leaving a tenderness too big to hold, mixed with fear of how close it had been, and thanks.

"Thank you, Pais."

A few words, but heavy, carrying the relief of surviving and a love he hadn't dared say out loud.

"If you hadn't turned back... I'd be dead right now."

Paisley drew back her hand and straightened up, her face lighting with relief.

"You're finally awake. How do you feel? Does anything still hurt?"

Elijah shook his head and pushed himself up to sit. He felt strong and clear. The poison was gone, and even his wounds had healed without a trace.

"I'm fine. Everything's healed."

"I had no idea your Plant Affinity was this powerful. Thank you..."

He kept his eyes on her, unable to look away, his feelings spilling over.

He truly hadn't known. Her Affinity could heal and purify like this?

She was a treasure. Strong in a fight, and a healer on top of that. If anyone found out, they'd guard her like something priceless. Healing like hers was impossibly rare, and she'd pulled him back from the brink in minutes.
