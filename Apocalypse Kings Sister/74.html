Chapter 74

Jasper sighed and turned to the two of them, his voice suddenly sharp and cold.

"You two, apologize to Paisley right now!

"Do whatever it takes to get her to forgive you. Otherwise, don't fucking blame me when I stop being nice."

Paisley kept her head down, her eyes rimmed with red, looking like the most wronged person in the world.

She didn't say a word the whole time. She just cried quietly.

Rachel and Owen both went stiff, more humiliated and furious than ever.

Their whole lives, they'd lorded it over Paisley, pushing her around and humiliating her whenever they felt like it.

As far as they were concerned, Paisley was born to be beneath them, someone for them to kick around however they wanted.

Now, they were being forced to grovel in front of everyone to the "loser" they'd bullied for years.

It was the most humiliating thing that had ever happened to them.

They both clenched their fists. Neither of them had any intention of apologizing.

Owen shot Paisley a threatening look.

He wanted her to just say she forgave them and be done with it.

But Paisley just kept crying softly and ignored him completely.

With Elijah backing her up, she could keep messing with them for a while longer and get a little more payback.

Jasper saw that the two of them still wouldn't back down, and he could tell Elijah's patience was running out. With a dark scowl, he strode over and shoved them both to their knees in front of Paisley.

"You won't apologize? Fine. Then get on your knees and do it!"

Their knees hit the ground with two dull thuds, and there they were, kneeling at Paisley's feet.

Rachel had no choice. She clenched her jaw and forced the words out, stiff and grudging.

"I'm... sorry."

Owen's face was livid, and he was seething with resentment. He barely managed to squeeze out a halfhearted apology.

"We were wrong, okay? Just let it go."

Neither apology was even close to sincere.

Paisley was still crying quietly. Out of the corner of her eye, she saw how pissed off and humiliated they looked, and how they didn't dare say a word about it. It made her feel a whole lot better.

But it still wasn't enough.

She dabbed at her eyes like she was wiping away tears, then patted the little white cat on the head.

The cat got the message right away, and a sly, vicious look flashed in its eyes.

The next second, it sprang lightly out of Paisley's arms and landed right on Rachel's shoulder.

Before Rachel could react, the cat swiped its sharp claws hard across her cheek!

Its paw whooshed through the air and left five distinct scratches on Rachel's cheek. Blood welled up right away, and a stinging pain spread across her whole face.

And it wasn't done yet.

In one smooth leap, the little white cat jumped onto Owen's head and pissed all over him without a care in the world.

In the blink of an eye, Rachel's face was covered in bloody scratches, and she looked pathetic.

Owen wasn't any better off. His head was soaked in cat pee, and he reeked.

They both looked ridiculous, and every last shred of their dignity was gone.

Both of them clenched their fists, burning with hatred.

That little cat had humiliated them, and they hated it with everything they had. But they hated its owner, Paisley, even more!

Rachel clutched her burning, stinging cheek, and her fingertips came away sticky with blood. She stared at the blood on her hand and thought about her ruined face, shaking with rage. Then, she gritted her teeth and pointed at the kitten.

"Why, you little..."

With its work done, the little white cat hopped nimbly back into Paisley's arms. It snuggled up against her neck and kept mewing softly, like it was waiting for a reward.

Paisley stroked its fur like she couldn't help it and said in an innocent voice, "Bad kitty."

She looked up at the pale, humiliated pair and offered them a perfectly polite apology, playing the bigger person.

"Oh no, I'm so sorry! It's all my cat's fault. The little rascal's so naughty, and it didn't mean to hurt you."

Then, she waved her hand like she was being incredibly generous and letting bygones be bygones.

"But it's fine. My cat already paid you back for breaking my pendant, so let's call it even. I accept your apologies."

To Owen and Rachel, her whole fake little performance was the ultimate humiliation!

Owen had never been this disgraced in his entire life. He'd always been so proud and cocky. When had anyone ever humiliated him like this?

His rage completely took over.

His hostility exploded, and Fire Affinity energy flared wildly around him. Two blazing fireballs formed in his hands.

Being played with and humiliated in front of everyone like this had driven him insane with hatred!

He glared at Paisley with murder in his eyes and hurled both fireballs straight at her as hard as he could!
