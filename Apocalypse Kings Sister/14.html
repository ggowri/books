Chapter 14

Three years of fighting for her life in her past life had made Paisley a hardened fighter. Low-level zombies like these were nothing to her.

Just then, seven or eight more zombies came pouring around a street corner and charged straight at them, with more coming every second.

"Come on, Madison, get out," Paisley said, turning to her. "We're sitting ducks in here. Let's go straight into the mall."

Madison took a deep breath and fought down her fear. She gripped her knife tighter, gritted her teeth, and pushed the door open, sticking close behind Paisley.

For some reason, having Paisley next to her made her feel safe.

Ashley and Kelly climbed out of their car too. Huddled close together, the group crept toward the mall entrance.

Paisley took the lead without a hint of fear. Any straggler that dared to come at her went down with one strike, as easy as slicing fruit.

The others watched with their jaws on the floor. Since when was killing zombies this easy?

Thomas and Spencer were completely speechless. Even Orion, who'd already awakened his ability, started to doubt himself.

His ability had made him stronger and faster than any ordinary person. By all rights, he should've been the better fighter.

But every time he fought a zombie, he had to circle it and wait for an opening, which took time and energy.

Paisley, on the other hand, struck clean and fast, and every blow was a kill. When it came to fighting, he couldn't hold a candle to her.

Ashley's eyes were shining as she stared at Paisley up ahead, full of admiration.

Paisley is so freaking amazing!

Paisley had already become her role model. Ashley gripped her knife tighter, and most of her leftover fear melted away. She worked up the courage to keep up and pitched in to help the others fight.

The rest of the group closed in too, and together, they quickly cleared out the last few zombies near the entrance.

They seized the moment and hurried into the mall, slamming the heavy doors shut behind them to keep out the endless stream of zombies.

Once they were safe for the moment, Elijah quickly handed out orders. "Orion, take Paisley and gather supplies.

"Mason, you're with me. We'll hold the entrance and take out anything that gets near the door.

"Everyone else, watch each other's backs. Be careful, and don't wander off on your own."

Paisley grabbed Orion's hand and hurried toward the supermarket on the first floor. Whenever a zombie got in their way, Orion took it down.

Meanwhile, Paisley's eyes swept the shelves. With a single thought, item after item vanished into her dimension, fast and steady, without a pause.

Inside her head, Trovie, her system, was practically bouncing with excitement, its squeaky little voice egging her on.

[More, Master, more! Grab everything you can! The more, the better!]

Paisley worked her way through the store, clearing the shelves one by one.

She took soft bread, cases of instant ramen, vacuum-sealed snacks, and ready-to-eat meals. If it would keep and travel well, it went in.

She moved down the aisles and cleared out the flour, cooking oil, ration bars, canned goods, and dry goods. Then, she grabbed the bottled water, energy drinks, and milk stacked in the corners, case after case.

Up ahead, Orion finished off a few zombies hiding between the shelves. When he turned around, he stopped dead in his tracks.

Rows of shelves that had been packed full a minute ago were completely bare, as if the place had been looted top to bottom.

He'd been right next to her the whole time, barely blinking, and he still hadn't seen how she'd done it so fast.

"Pais, that dimension of yours... It's insanely fast."

Orion was amazed. He was more grateful than ever that Paisley had awakened such an overpowered ability. With her around, the team would never have to worry about supplies again.

Paisley smiled. "I've still got a little under half the space left.

"Besides food, I made sure to grab a bunch of practical stuff too. I cleared out a few whole cabinets of meds. Bandages, stuff to stop bleeding, anti-inflammatories, cold and fever medicine.

"I also packed up toiletries, tissues, wet wipes, trash bags, lighters, and candles. That's all the everyday stuff people get desperate for in the apocalypse."
