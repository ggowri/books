Chapter 208

Just then, a ruthless look crossed Rachel's face. She grabbed Chase, and the two of them crouched low and pressed against the wall along the side of the passage. They held their breath, hunched over, and stepped lightly as they scrambled toward the exit.

The moment they ran, Paisley's eyes went cold, and green light surged at her fingertips.

Countless thick, tough, dark-green vines covered in fine patterns shot up from under the flooded floor. They crisscrossed and tied up the thrashing Level Six mutant octopus in seconds.

The dense vines wrapped around its huge body and every flailing tentacle, squeezing so tight it couldn't move, and cut off any chance for it to escape or attack.

The chili powder dissolved into the water and spread fast, making it burn even worse. The whole pool turned into a fiery hell, and the trapped octopus had nowhere to run.

The Level Six mutant octopus had thick skin and didn't fear physical attacks or burning powers, but chili powder was the one thing it couldn't handle.

The searing burn forced its way in through every breathing pore and every gap in its membranes, scorching its flesh and organs. The pain was agonizing, and there was no relief from it.

It twisted its huge body like crazy, but the cage of vines held it tight. The harder it struggled, the tighter the vines squeezed, and the faster and deeper the burning water soaked in.

The pain swallowed whatever sense the Level Six mutant octopus had left.

It couldn't live and couldn't die, and the torment drove it out of its mind. It started tearing itself apart.

Its hard tentacles whipped its own body, its sharp suckers ripped at its own flesh, and black venom mixed with dark red blood kept swirling through the water.

The monster that had ruled this place was now ripping itself to pieces, on the verge of falling apart.

The surviving team members stared in shock. They'd never seen anything like it.

An unstoppable Level Six beast had been brought to its knees by one bag of ordinary chili powder.

Elijah and Orion stood off to one side, watching Paisley with total faith. She could always be trusted, and she never stopped surprising them.

When everyone else was lost in despair with no idea what to do, Paisley was the only one who could come up with such a clever, sneaky fix in a hopeless situation.

She was way too smart and scary calm. Even in danger, she never lost her cool, and every move she made was precise and ruthless, without a trace of panic.

At the same moment, they both felt a wave of relief. Thank God she's on our side.

In the pool, the agony kept eating away at the mutant octopus's mind.

It had gone berserk and couldn't tell friend from foe anymore. Its huge tentacles kept lashing at its own body, and it bit off and tore away one thick tentacle after another. Ink and blood gushed from the wounds and stained the murky seawater red.

When its body was torn to shreds and its struggling grew weak, Paisley walked forward slowly with the Frostbloom Sword.

The blade gleamed with an icy light. She zeroed in on the softest, weakest spot on the octopus's head, drove the sword in hard, and carved out its Level Six Water crystal core in one clean move.

Without the core holding it together, the huge body went stiff, and all its struggling stopped dead.

The Level Six mutant octopus that had been so terrifying a moment ago was dead. It slumped into the water and never moved again.

Dead silence spread for a moment. A second later, stifled gasps broke out one after another, and the team members shaking in the corner stared in disbelief.

"It's... it's dead?! The Level Six mutant octopus is really gone, just like that!"

"She killed that Level Six mutant octopus with just a bag of chili powder... That's insane!"

Someone spoke in awe, still reeling. "This isn't a dream. That girl pretty much killed that Level Six mutant octopus all by herself."

"If it weren't for her, we'd probably all be dead down here today."

Elijah and Orion looked at Paisley, standing there cool and tall, and they were deeply moved and full of admiration.

Paisley had turned everything they thought they knew upside down again. How many more surprises did she have for them?!

She'd taken down a Level Six mutant octopus on her own. She was the one who'd protected everyone here. Paisley was even stronger and more mysterious than they'd ever imagined.

Once everything was quiet and the danger was finally gone, everyone could relax at last.

Only then did they look around and realize that Chase and Rachel had slipped away in all the chaos.

Paisley and Rachel were both young women. One had risked her life and turned the tide to protect everyone.
