Chapter 208

Just then, something sharp flickered in Rachel's eyes. She grabbed Chase, and the two of them, moving in perfect sync, hugged the corridor wall low, holding their breath, backs bent, feet light, and fled toward the exit.

The instant they bolted, Paisley's eyes went cold, green light flaring at her fingertips.

Thick vines burst out from under the flooded floor, crisscrossing in an instant, binding the thrashing octopus fast.

Vine after vine wrapped its massive body and every flailing tentacle, cinching down until it couldn't move at all, sealing off any chance to flee or fight back.

The chili powder dissolved into the water and spread fast, the whole pool turning burning hot.

It became a furnace with no way out, and the trapped creature had nowhere left to go.

The octopus could shrug off physical blows and Affinity burns both, but this—this it couldn't take.

The burn tore into it through every pore, every membrane, scorching flesh and organ alike, a pain with no end and no relief.

It thrashed against its own size, but the vines only tightened with every struggle, driving the burning deeper and faster.

Whatever intelligence it had drowned under the pain.

Unable to live, unable to die, it slipped into total frenzy, tearing at itself.

Its own tentacles lashed its body, suckers ripping into its own flesh, black venom and dark blood churning together through the flooded corridor.

The terror that had ruled this place was tearing itself apart, undone by a bag of chili powder.

The surviving team members watched, stunned, the whole scene breaking everything they'd thought they knew.

A Level Six mutant that nothing could stop, brought to pieces by a bag of ordinary chili powder.

Elijah and Orion stood off to the side, pure conviction filling their eyes as they watched her.

She was always worth trusting. Always worth watching for what came next.

While everyone else had been drowning in despair, only she had found a solution this sharp, this unexpected, in the middle of it.

She was terrifyingly smart. Terrifyingly calm.

Not a trace of panic, even now. Every move precise, every move final.

Both of them felt the same quiet relief, all at once: Thank god she was on their side.

In the water, the pain kept eating through what was left of the octopus's mind.

Lost entirely to frenzy, it couldn't tell friend from foe anymore, lashing and biting its own body, tearing off its own tentacles one by one, ink and blood spraying out to stain the murky water red.

Once its body was in ruins, its struggle fading, Paisley's eyes went cold, and she stepped forward with the Frostbloom Sword.

The blade gleamed, cold and sharp. She found the softest, most vulnerable point in its head and drove the sword in, carving out its Level Six Water Affinity core in one clean motion.

Without the core, its enormous body went rigid, every last struggle stopping dead.

The mutant that had ruled this stretch of water minutes ago sank, lifeless, without another twitch.

Silence swallowed the room, and then, all at once, strangled gasps broke out.

In the corners, the shaking team members stared, shock written across their faces.

“It's—it's dead?! A Level Six mutant, just like that?!”

“She beat a Level Six mutant with a bag of chili powder… that shouldn't even be possible.”

Someone else spoke, awe and disbelief tangled together.

“I'm not dreaming. She basically took that thing down single-handed.”

“If it weren't for her, we'd all be dead down here right now.”

Elijah and Orion looked at her standing there, cool and unbending, nothing in their eyes but sheer admiration.

She'd upended everything they thought they knew, again.

How many more surprises could one person possibly have?

She'd one-woman'd a Level Six mutant.

She was the reason everyone here was still breathing.

Paisley was stronger, deeper, more unknowable, than anything they'd imagined.

With the danger finally cleared and the area gone quiet, everyone's nerves finally let go.

Only then did anyone think to look around, and realize that in all the chaos, Chase and Rachel had vanished.

Two young women, the same age, and one had thrown herself into danger to protect everyone here;
