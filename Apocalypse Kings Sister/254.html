Chapter 254

"Granted."

"You lot, go kill three more Level Four zombies for him."

His eyes turned vicious as he pointed to the other wall of cages, full of kidnapped survivors, men and women and children, every face bloodless with fear.

"These ones are used up. Drag them over, feed them to the zombies!"

His men obeyed at once, dragging the cages out roughly.

The people inside broke down, beating against the bars, tears and blood streaming together, begging.

"No! Help! Please, I'm begging you, let us go!"

"Please, have mercy! I don't want to die!"

"You animals, you'll get what's coming to you."

...

The screams echoed through the basement, raw and unbearable.

Watching from the shadows, Paisley felt a wave of disgust and fury twist through her, her stomach churning hard enough to make her sick.

Her eyes went flat and cold, every word deliberate.

"They deserve to die."

Orion's fists clenched tight, fury burning in his eyes, his presence turning ice cold.

"Animals. All of them."

"How dare they! Feeding people to zombies just to fuel their own power! There's nothing human left in them!"

Elijah's fingers curled into fists, knuckles white, lightning humming dangerously around him, killing intent rising in his eyes.

Enough.

Cold light flashed in Paisley's eyes as she abandoned all pretense of hiding and stepped out of the dark.

"Stop."

Her voice cut through the basement, sharp and clear.

Two more figures stepped out right behind her.

Her white hem stirred in the stale air, and to every man watching, she looked like a blade cutting through ice.

Elijah's lightning erupted in full.

Crackling silver-blue bolts filled the space around him, the pressure rolling through the basement hard enough to rattle the lights overhead.

Orion's Metal Affinity formed heavy golden armor around him, fists charged and ready, his fury undimmed.

Fat Wagner's grin froze solid, his whole body tensing as he stared at the three intruders.

Their arrival silenced the room for a moment.

"WHO?! Who dares come into my territory?!"

After a flash of panic, Fat Wagner remembered he was Level Five now, and tried to bluster his way through it.

His men were terrified, dropping their knives, frozen in place where they stood.

"You've got some nerve, barging into my den and wrecking my business!"

Fresh off his breakthrough, Fat Wagner's ego was at its peak, and he didn't rate these three strangers as any kind of threat.

His eyes landed on Paisley, greed lighting up his face.

"Now that's a looker! Better than every woman I've got upstairs!"

"Lucky me, she walked right in! Girl, come here if you don't want to die. You're different. I'll even marry you..."

Orion said nothing but:

"Please. A toad dreaming of swan meat."

"You think you get to touch Paisley?"

His Metal Affinity conjured a storm of blades, hurtling toward them.

Fat Wagner threw up an Earth Affinity wall to shield himself and his men, shouting orders.

"Everyone, move! Tear the men apart and feed them to the zombies! Take the girl alive, I want her for myself!"

More than twenty men snapped into action, fireballs and wind blades and earth spikes all converging on the three of them at once.

None of the three so much as flinched.

"Trash. Not worth a second thought."

Ice settled into Paisley's eyes as she unleashed her Level Six peak Plant Affinity in full.
