Chapter 232

Intense jealousy spread through Rachel's heart. Her hands clenched tight at her sides, and her bitterness and resentment piled up layer by layer.

She had to get her hands on that pendant. It was mine in my past life, and it's going to be mine in this life too!

The front rows of the auditorium, on the other hand, were buzzing with excitement.

Elijah's eyes stayed fixed on Paisley, so calm and composed on the stage. His gaze burned with admiration and emotion he couldn't hide.

With the smoke of battle gone and everyone gathered around her, Paisley was so dazzling that no one could look away.

Orion stood up straight, unable to hide his pride, and grinned as he said quietly to Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer beside him, "See that? That's my sister up on that stage. Isn't she gorgeous, and even more badass?"

Ashley, the youngest, had her bright, sparkling eyes glued to Paisley on the stage, and she cheered softly, glowing with excitement. "Paisley, you're incredible! I'm so proud of you!

"I'm gonna train hard too, so I can be just as strong and brave as you!"

Thomas looked so proud. "She's amazing. She turned the whole fight around on her own and saved everyone at the base."

Spencer was deeply moved. "She's unbelievable. If Mom and Dad knew what Paisley did, they'd be so proud."

In a quiet corner on the other side of the auditorium, the three Lennox brothers, Owen, Reid, and Ryan, saw Paisley up on the stage holding her First-Class Merit medal. Their feelings were all over the place, and all that was left was endless regret and emptiness.

During today's dangerous beast horde, the defense line had nearly broken several times, and countless high-level beasts had crashed against it. Even giving it everything they had, the three of them had only managed to kill a few low-level mutant beasts together.

The tiny number of contribution points they'd earned from the whole fight was barely enough for a few pieces of hardtack and a few bottles of water. They could hardly even get by.

But Paisley had become a legend overnight. Her reward alone was 10,000 contribution points and First-Class Merit, a level the three of them might never reach even after a lifetime of fighting.

The huge gap hit them hard, and regret flooded over them like a tide.

If they hadn't been so blind back then, always taking Rachel's side and blaming, ignoring, and hurting Paisley again and again, and if they hadn't agreed so easily to cut ties with her, they wouldn't have ended up in such a sorry state today.

It finally hit them. Maybe all those fights and arguments over who was right hadn't been about Paisley being unreasonable and bullying Rachel.

Rachel had just been too good at playing weak and acting pitiful, twisting the truth to win sympathy. That was why they'd gotten it wrong again and again, blamed the one who truly cared, and pushed away someone as amazing as Paisley with their own hands.

How ridiculous. They'd been so obsessed with blood ties, thinking that staying close to fragile Rachel meant protecting their family. They'd pushed away the one person truly worth cherishing and treated a fake, vicious act as the real thing.

Only now did they really understand. They'd been wrong. They were the ones who'd pushed away the sweet, thoughtful sister who'd always put them first.

Endless regret wrapped around their hearts. The three of them stood stiff, too bitter to say a word.

If they hadn't broken off from her, a share of today's glory, an honor everyone envied, would've been theirs too.

At the very least, Paisley wouldn't have just stood by and watched them go hungry.

When the awards ended, Paisley took her medal and slowly walked down from the stage.

She stood tall, her expression cool and calm, and the medal on her chest sparkled, making her shine even brighter.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan's hearts suddenly clenched. They took half a step forward without thinking, a faint hope rising inside them, and wanted to go talk to her. She was so soft-hearted, and she'd cared about them so much once, so maybe she'd forgive them.

But the next second, they watched Paisley walk right off in another direction, toward the Thompsons.

The Thompsons gathered around her, openly loving and proud. They surrounded her with laughter and cheer, keeping her at the warm center of it all.

The three of them, on the other hand, sat on the edge where nobody noticed them. They didn't fit in, and it was humiliating.

The Paisley they'd once thrown away like trash now stood at a height they could never reach, and she had a family again who adored her and truly loved her.

They wanted to get closer, but the people who truly cared about her had already crowded in around her. There was no room left for them at all.

All they had now were growling stomachs.

The celebration ended, and the crowd slowly scattered as the noise died down.

Paisley went back alone to the private bedroom she'd been assigned.
