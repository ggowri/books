Chapter 268

What's there to celebrate?

All this work, all these points, and I'm still not even halfway there.

At this rate, I'd need to stumble onto an actual mountain of gold to speed this up.

Right as she was grumbling to herself, Trovie's voice piped up again, sweet and chipper.

[Host, a trader from the Ancient Realm wants to connect.]

Something sparked in her eyes.

[Connect.]

******

A pale blue hologram unfolded, visible only to her.

The princess appeared in elegant period robes, her face bright with excitement, her voice almost buzzing.

[Hello! The wheat, sweet potatoes, and corn are all in the ground!] [With the methods you taught us, and good soil, every single seed has sprouted!] [I don't even know why, but I trust you completely. Thank you, truly. With crops like these, I know this year will be bountiful, enough for everyone to eat and stay warm.]

Paisley waved a hand, calm as ever.

[Don't mention it. We're just trading evenly, and helping you turn out well makes me happy too.]

She moved on.

[You reached out for something specific, didn't you?]

Caught out, the princess blinked, cleared her throat, and turned serious.

[There is, actually. I was hoping you could help.] [The crops are in now, but farming everything by hand, plowing, weeding, it's slow, and the yield suffers for it.]

Her eyes turned hopeful.

[Is there anything you have that could help our farming efficiency?]

Paisley understood immediately.

She wanted tools that could revolutionize traditional farming.

Paisley named her price plainly.

[A full set of modern farming tools, samples and blueprints both. Could cut your labor dramatically.] [Standard price: 3,300 pounds of gold.]

The princess's eyes lit up, relief flooding her face as she agreed without a second's hesitation.

[Deal!]

3,300 pounds of gold was a lot, but in her eyes, tools that could save this much labor were worth far more.

Right now, farming relied entirely on hand labor and oxen, backbreaking in spring and meager in autumn, fields left fallow every year for lack of hands.

With new tools and the high-yield seeds Paisley had already given her, this year's harvest could finally be a real one.

The princess pressed further, eager.

[What exactly is in this set? How do they work? Can our craftsmen build from the blueprints?]

Paisley laid it out calmly.

[The set includes a deep-plow, a light harrow, a hand-seeder, a weeding hoe, and improved sickles, all adapted for your conditions. Anyone can use them with basic training.] [The blueprints specify every dimension, material, forging step, and assembly detail. Your blacksmiths and carpenters can replicate them directly. Low barrier, easy to mass-produce, works across all your farmland.]

Simple designs, built for her world, stripped of anything too modern but keeping the efficiency, exactly what her kingdom needed.

The princess nodded eagerly, practically glowing.

[That's more than enough! It'll completely outpace what we have now! I'll have the gold counted immediately.]

Seconds later, the system chimed in Paisley's head.

[Cross-dimensional trade detected: 3,300 pounds of gold!] [Converting to points... conversion successful!] [Points earned this trade: 30,000!] [Large trade bonus triggered! Bonus points: 3,000!]
