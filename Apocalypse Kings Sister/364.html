Chapter 364

Her own Level Five Plant Affinity had always been a point of pride.

Her squad, once the base's crown jewel, had just been crushed flat against the sheer brute force of a full Level Six roster.

******

But pride ran deep in her, and losing face this publicly only made her dig in harder, forcing out a bitter laugh, her voice turning even sharper.

"So your level's a little higher — that doesn't translate to actual combat ability! Field missions come down to experience, coordination, firepower. Not some cold number on a sheet!"

She clenched her fists tight, glaring straight at Ashley, her voice hard.

"You want to bet on it?

If you're all so strong, prove it where it counts!"

Ashley glanced at Paisley once. As long as her sister was there, she wasn't scared at all.

She didn't flinch, answering at once.

"Fine, let's bet. You think I'm scared of you?"

"What's the bet, then? Terms?"

Chloe's eyes swept the mission board on the platform, landing precisely on the newest Level Eight high-risk field mission.

"This. The Level Eight mission just posted."

Already rattled, the crowd held its breath again at that, shock written on every face.

Level Eight missions were the hardest the base currently offered publicly, with casualty rates past fifty percent historically. Very few squads dared take one on.

"Mission brief: eliminate a Level Seven mutant morning glory, rescue trapped survivors."

Chloe went on, her tone smug.

"There's a rare Level Seven mutant plant out there — a morning glory.

Extremely aggressive, releases a mind-fogging toxin. Most high-level users can't even handle it.

We bet on whichever squad finds and kills it first."

Clear terms.

Ashley glanced at Paisley again, standing right beside her. With her there, nothing scared her.

"Fine. I'm in."

Chloe's sneer deepened, her tone sharp and domineering.

"I'm dying to see how a bunch of rookies with no record and no field experience think they can compete with a seasoned elite squad.

"If you lose, Squad Falcon disbands, publicly."

"Fine."

Seeing that neither Elijah nor Orion stepped in to stop her, Ashley accepted the terms with gritted teeth, honestly surprised the stakes had gotten this high.

This wasn't just a bet between the two of them anymore.

She couldn't back down now. She met Chloe's eyes squarely and set the terms even.

"If you lose, your squad disbands the same way. In public."

Grant nodded haughtily beside her, utterly certain of his victory.

"Deal. You started this — don't come crying for mercy or making excuses when you lose."

Elijah, silent throughout, stood watching, unbothered, not a flicker of intent to intervene — if anything, a faint smile crossed his face.

He understood Squad Falcon's strength better than anyone. A full roster of Level Six couldn't be matched by an ordinary squad.

And besides, the rift between the Thompson houses ran deep. Even if they sidestepped this bet today, Chloe's side would find some other excuse to cause trouble later.

With the Quinns and Blackwoods already circling each other openly, this bet might actually crack things open and hand him the initiative.

They'd started this fight. Whatever came of it was on them.

Just then, Paisley, who'd stayed quiet and composed through all of it, finally lifted her gaze.

Her expression stayed cool and distant, her clear eyes sweeping calmly over the arrogant pair across from her, her voice cold but leaving no room for argument.

"A verbal bet doesn't mean anything. Let's put it in writing. Two signed copies."

She added one more line, landing precisely where it would sting the most, cutting off any chance of backing out later.

"I'd hate for you to be sore losers."

The hall went still at that.

Grant's face went livid, feeling thoroughly humiliated in front of everyone.

As one of the base's celebrated prodigies, a longtime squad captain used to being admired, when had some young girl ever dared question his word like this?

The air around him turned cold, fury boiling over as he snarled through gritted teeth.
