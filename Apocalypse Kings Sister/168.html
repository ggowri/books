Chapter 168

The noise of their cars had stirred up the stray zombies lurking all over the park.

From the ragged bushes along the paths, from under the abandoned Ferris wheel's supports, and from the shadows of crumbling walls, countless low-level zombies slowly rose. Their rotting heads turned stiffly, and their cloudy red eyes locked onto the living people, hungry for blood.

Within seconds, a dense mob of Level Two and Three zombies closed in, more than 100 of them. They packed the park entrance and the path ahead in a dark, crushing mass.

"Everyone, on guard! Let's clear them out, and stay safe!" Spencer called out in a low, steady voice. Red flames leaped around him as his Fire Affinity flared to life, and he was ready to fight at full strength.

Nobody needed to be told twice. They'd fought side by side for so long that teamwork was second nature, and they immediately spread out into a tight formation, each taking their role without a hint of panic.

They were all about the same level, and against mindless low-level zombies like these, it was a total beatdown.

Thomas struck first, unleashing his Plant Affinity.

Countless thick, dark green vines burst out of the ground, crisscrossing like a giant jungle net that spread across the whole battlefield in seconds. They wrapped around and stabbed through the charging zombies, pinning a huge chunk of the horde in place so they couldn't move.

Spencer immediately followed up with his Fire Affinity.

Flame raced along the vines and burst outward, engulfing the trapped zombies. The crackle of fire and the stench of burning flesh filled the air as bodies dropped, charred, one after another.

On the other side, Ashley threw everything into her Earth Affinity.

Thick, hard stone walls rose from the ground to shield them from the zombies, while sharp rock spikes shot up and skewered zombies clean through. She'd gotten amazing at both offense and defense.

Mason launched a fierce attack with his Wind Affinity.

Howling winds spun into sharp blades of air that swept across the field, mowing down the oncoming zombies from a distance. Body parts flew wherever the wind passed, and he carved a wide, open path through the horde, keeping the whole front line in check.

At the same time, Marcus called on his Earth Affinity.

A thick stone shield rose from the ground to protect the back of their formation against sneak attacks. Meanwhile, small stone spikes formed and shot out with deadly precision, picking off every zombie that slipped through.

Madison released her Water Affinity.

A flexible water shield protected the group from flying blood and debris, while sharp water blades took down the scattered zombies at range.

Everyone's affinities exploded at once, and fire, green vines, stone, wind blades, and water swirled together in a stunning display.

In no time, more than 100 low-level zombies were cut down, their bodies piling up across the field.

Paisley walked at the front of the group, cool and graceful. The Frostbloom Sword in her hand gleamed with cold light, a faint, icy aura swirling around the blade. Its chill spread through the air, pushing back the lingering stench of rot.

Suddenly, a sharp, clear yowl split the air.

The little white cat's fur puffed up as pure Ice Affinity burst out of its body. A layer of clear frost covered its snowy coat, glowing with a faint blue light as icy mist swirled around it.

Its body swelled fast, and in moments, it had become a giant ice spirit cat 16 feet tall. Its legs were strong and powerful, sharp ice blades formed at the tips of its claws, and its eyes were glazed with a thin layer of ice, cold and sharp.

Paisley and the cat moved in perfect sync toward the Level Six carnivorous flower.

Along the way, a few leftover zombies hiding nearby heard them and lunged out to attack.

The cat struck first, swiping out with a giant paw.

A wave of bitter cold swept out, and frost raced across the ground. Any zombie it touched froze stiff on the spot, its flesh crusting over with ice until it turned into a brittle ice sculpture.

Paisley followed right behind with her sword. With a flick of her wrist, the Frostbloom Sword sent out several thin, sharp arcs of icy energy, each strike precise and clean.

The cat didn't stop for a second. It weaved nimbly through the fallen bodies, its keen ice senses pinpointing every zombie's crystal core.
