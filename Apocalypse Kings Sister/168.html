Chapter 168

The noise from their arrival had stirred up every stray zombie in the park.

From the broken tree lines along the road, from under the collapsed Ferris wheel supports, from the shadows of crumbling walls — low-tier zombies rose slowly, rotted heads turning in unison, red eyes locking onto the group with hungry intent.

In seconds, a swarm of Level Two and Three zombies closed in, over a hundred strong, choking off the park entrance and every path forward, a wall of dead flesh pressing in.

"Everyone, hold position! Clear them out! Stay sharp!"

Spencer barked the order, fire already crackling to life around him, Fire Affinity surging as he dropped into full combat stance.

No one needed telling twice. Months of fighting side by side had worn the coordination into instinct. They spread out, took positions, locked into a tight formation without a flicker of panic.

Everyone here was close in level — against low-tier zombies like this, it was a complete mismatch.

Thomas struck first, Plant Affinity erupting.

Thick vines tore up from the ground, weaving into a dense net, sweeping across the battlefield, wrapping and piercing the incoming zombies in one motion, pinning a whole section of the swarm in place, helpless.

Spencer followed instantly with Fire Affinity.

Flame raced along the vines and burst outward, engulfing the trapped zombies. The crackle of fire and the stench of burning flesh filled the air as bodies dropped, charred, one after another.

On the other side, Ashley threw everything into her Earth Affinity.

Thick stone walls rose from the ground, blocking incoming attacks, while sharp rock spikes shot up and skewered zombies clean through — offense and defense in perfect sync.

Mason unleashed his Wind Affinity.

Spinning gusts condensed into razor blades of wind, sweeping the field, cutting down anything in range at range, carving an open stretch through the horde, suppressing the whole front line.

Marcus matched it with Earth Affinity of his own.

Stone shields rose to guard their flank, while fine rock spikes flew out in bursts, picking off every straggler that slipped through, nothing getting past.

Madison backed it all with Water Affinity.

A flexible water shield protected the group from flying blood and debris, while sharp water blades took down the scattered zombies at range.

Fire, vines, stone, wind, water — all six affinities crashed together, a blur of light and force.

In a blink, the hundred-strong horde was wiped clean, bodies dropping across the field.

Paisley walked at the front of the group, cool and composed, the Frostbloom Sword glowing with a thin edge of frost, the air around it carrying a faint chill.

A sharp, clear cat cry split the air.

Kitty's fur stood on end as pure Ice Affinity burst out of her, white fur dusted with frost, blue light flickering, mist curling around her.

Her body surged, growing in an instant into a sixteen-foot-tall ice spirit cat, limbs powerful and sure, claws tipped with frost, amber eyes glazed with a thin layer of ice, cold and sharp.

Cat and owner moved as one, heading for the carnivorous flower.

Along the way, stragglers tried to ambush them.

Kitty struck first, a massive paw sweeping out.

Frost swept the ground in every direction, and any zombie caught in its path froze instantly, movement locking up, flesh turning to brittle ice sculptures.

Paisley followed right behind, sword sweeping out, thin blades of frosted energy cutting through them with precise, angled strikes.

Kitty moved without pause, weaving through the fallen, her ice-sharpened senses picking out every crystal core location with perfect accuracy.
