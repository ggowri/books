Chapter 390

Jasper didn't seem to mind her cold tone at all. He shamelessly pulled up his contacts list, grinning like an obedient dog.

"Big shot, add me as a friend!"

"You do freelance jobs? With your strength now, there's no high-tier mission, no dangerous task you couldn't handle! Name your price, I won't haggle!"

If the base ever imposed mandatory mission quotas on Affinity users, he wouldn't want to risk his life doing them himself.

She's the strongest around, with her protection, he'd definitely be fine.

Paisley's gaze flickered slightly as she weighed it in her mind.

Jasper, the base's top playboy, had wide connections, sharp ears, plenty of resources. Keeping him around might actually prove useful down the line, one more avenue to work with.

She added him as a contact.

"Good dogs don't block the road."

Jasper immediately stepped aside, bowing and scraping, waving her off with fawning enthusiasm.

His whole group of lackeys went silent at once, filing the terrifying name away in their minds.

Paisley, someone even their boss didn't dare cross. Best to steer clear of her from now on.

Meanwhile, back at the villa.

Diane was already sitting cross-legged on the bed, posture upright, the air around her calm and focused.

In front of her sat hundreds of low-tier Level Two and Three crystal cores, each one crystal-clear, faint traces of spirit light flickering within.

Following Paisley's instructions, she held the cores steady in her palm, closed her eyes, and focused, drawing in the pure energy stored inside.

Thin threads of warm, pure Affinity energy seeped into her body through her palm and meridians, gentle and unhurried, perfectly suited to her freshly awakened, still-fragile Metal Affinity.

As the energy kept flowing in, the Metal Affinity force in her core was steadily nourished, refined, and strengthened.

The once faint, scattered golden light gradually grew denser, brighter, and sharper, wrapping around her body in a faint golden glow.

Metal Affinity specialized in sharpness, hardness, and balanced offense and defense, the stronger the foundation, the greater the combat strength later on.

The massive energy stored in hundreds of low-tier cores poured relentlessly through Diane's meridians, reshaping her body's structure, clearing out accumulated impurities, and solidifying her brand-new Affinity foundation, inch by inch.

Pure, concentrated energy flowed unendingly into her core, and she could clearly feel her body's power surging, transforming by leaps and bounds.

Starting from the barest beginnings of a fresh awakening, she broke through the barriers without the slightest resistance, climbing rapidly, Level Two, Level Three, Level Four!

Finally, her Affinity settled at the peak of Level Four.

The overwhelming, sharp force of Metal Affinity surged through her entire body. Her once-frail frame had been thoroughly refined and rebuilt, filled with a dense, solid strength she'd never felt before.

Diane slowly opened her eyes, the mildness in her gaze replaced by a sharp edge unique to Metal Affinity.

She rose and walked to the center of the courtyard, looking up at a thick ornamental tree by the garden's edge, raising a hand to gather her Affinity.

A sharp golden blade of light condensed in her palm in an instant. A flash of cold light, and she struck cleanly!

Crack!

A crisp snapping sound rang out as the bowl-thick trunk of the tree severed clean in one stroke, the cut smooth and precise, showcasing the full sharp, overwhelming power of Metal Affinity.

Diane stared at her own hand, murmuring softly to herself.

"So this is what Affinity power feels like..."

Powerful. Decisive. Thoroughly intimidating.

In that moment, all the resentment and suppression she'd been carrying for so long vanished completely.

From now on, she was no longer the helpless, defenseless ordinary person the apocalypse had made her, she had her own power to protect herself, the confidence to stand up to anyone who wronged her.

Just then, the courtyard gate creaked open. Paisley had finished pulling the surveillance footage and was walking back inside.

The moment she stepped through the gate, she saw the fallen tree trunk on the ground and her mother, wrapped in a dazzling golden aura, her whole bearing completely transformed. A genuine smile bloomed across Paisley's face.

Diane spotted her right away and turned, eyes lit up with excitement, hurrying over.

"Pais! You're back! Mom's Metal Affinity has reached the peak of Level Four!"

With that, she raised her hand to demonstrate again.

Golden light blazed in her palm, fine metallic blades spinning and shifting at her fingertips, sometimes condensing into a thick, solid shield, sometimes sharpening into a lethal blade, switching between offense and defense with practiced ease, her control remarkably skilled for someone who'd only just awakened.

Looking at her mother, completely transformed, radiating newfound confidence, Paisley offered a genuine compliment.

"Mom, that's amazing. Reaching peak Level Four on your very first attempt, you've got real talent."
