Chapter 287

Every stone on the ground, every piece of ore buried just beneath the topsoil, had its energy purity, grade, and point value laid out clearly on the system interface, plain as day.

******

Trovie worked fast, scanning eagerly.

This one, that one, all delicious!

As Trovie locked onto target after target, Paisley gave a mental nudge and opened her storage's collection range.

In an instant, every piece of raw jade, scrap ore, and energy-bearing stone across the factory grounds vanished into her storage.

Debris shifted, the soil rippled faintly, and stone after stone disappeared into her storage space.

In barely two minutes, every scrap of jade-bearing rock in the entire abandoned factory had been collected.

In her storage, Trovie's cheerful announcements rang out one after another.

[Consumption successful!]

[Converting resources in bulk! Points +72,000!]

[Current total points: 698,060!]

Watching the points climb rapidly on the display, Paisley's eyes lit up, barely able to contain her excitement.

She'd only ever known gold could be traded for points. She hadn't realized raw jade and ore could bring in this much too.

This town had always been famous for its jade, veins of it running everywhere underground. If she swept the whole region clean, she'd likely hit Level Four trader status in no time.

Trovie, buzzing with excitement, offered a suggestion.

Host! There's probably tons more jade hidden around the surrounding blocks, abandoned mine shafts, old workshops, farmhouses. Should we sweep it all?

Paisley answered without hesitation.

Trovie, scan everything.

The order given, Trovie's scanning range expanded instantly, covering the entire town.

The abandoned open-pit mine on the outskirts was the first to light up.

Next came the town's derelict jade workshops, old shops, and collapsed houses.

Even small pockets of jade buried beneath the roadside, hidden under overgrown weeds, got pulled up precisely, nothing left behind.

Batch after batch of raw stone vanished into her storage, Trovie's notification chimes coming nonstop, flashing across the interface.

[Large jade deposit detected! Points +55,000!]

[Collected large batch of crafted jade pieces and half-finished jewelry! Points +38,000!]

[Scattered ore batch converted! Points +42,000!]

The point count kept climbing fast, shattering records with every tick.

[Current total points: 833,060!]

Within minutes, the town's scattered jade resources, discarded ore, and workshop inventory had been swept nearly bare.

Trovie reported, buzzing with excitement.

Host! Level Four trader privileges are getting closer and closer!

Paisley felt the anticipation building too.

Trovie, do a full scan. See if there's a major jade vein nearby. Find one vein and we can level up straight to Level Four trader.

Trovie began the scan.

A minute later, it chimed in, thrilled.

Host! Massive energy concentration detected three miles to the west! Something big is definitely buried there!

"Let's go check it out."

Paisley didn't linger, heading straight for the marked location.

Three miles vanished in moments, and a massive abandoned mountain mine came into view.

This vein had already been half-excavated before the apocalypse, the rock face exposed and gouged with old dig marks, rusted mining equipment lying abandoned at the base of the slope, weeds overtaking everything, the whole place worn and desolate.

But Trovie's scanner had never been wrong before.

Paisley asked.

Trovie, this is it?

Trovie nodded eagerly in her mind.

Host, hurry in. My stomach's getting hungry again.

Paisley walked in, picked up an unremarkable rough stone along the way, and with a sharp flick of a thin blade of energy, cut it clean open.

The outer shell fell away, and a brilliant, saturated red flared to life inside.

It was a massive, flawless red gem, deep and radiant, glowing with an inner light, polished and gleaming, breathtakingly beautiful.

"That's a gorgeous ruby."

Paisley's eyes went wide with delight, fingertips brushing lightly over the gem's smooth surface, thoroughly pleased.

If a single rough stone picked up at random could hold a gem this fine, this vein had to be something extraordinary.
