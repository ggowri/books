Chapter 47

Inside, heavy gold bars were stacked in neat rows, gleaming. Paisley figured there had to be at least 55 pounds of them.

She smirked coldly.

There was no way a regular hospital director could save up this much gold. Not even if he worked his whole life and never spent a cent.

Obviously, it was dirty money. He'd gotten rich off bribes and kickbacks, bleeding ordinary people dry.

Now, the apocalypse had come, and he'd ended up a zombie. For a crook like him, that was karma.

And now, it was all hers.

Paisley didn't hesitate. With a thought, she pulled the whole safe of gold into her dimension.

Mine, all mine!

Seeing all that gold gleaming in her dimension made her feel rich, and her mood got a whole lot better. Gold was exactly what she needed.

She didn't waste any time. [Trovie, convert all this gold into points and see if it's enough for a space upgrade.]

Trovie answered right away. [Got it, Master! Converting now!]

A second later, her dimension's display lit up with a soft white glow, and system messages popped up one after another.

[Rare valuable resource detected: 55 pounds of gold.

[Calculating cross-dimensional exchange rate... Conversion successful!

[Points earned: 5,000! Total points: 11,730

[Congratulations, Master! You've reached the point threshold! You're now a Level Two cross-dimensional trader!

[Trader Level: Two]

The moment the points came in, rich golden light flooded the whole space. Everything shook violently as her dimension stretched out in every direction.

The cramped storage area suddenly opened up into a huge, airy space.

[Space upgrade complete! Current storage area: 100,000 square feet.

[Premium feature unlocked: permanent freshness and frozen time.

[Exclusive bonus unlocked: your very own two-story villa!]

Paisley blinked in surprise. With a thought, she was standing right in front of it.

It was a beautiful villa, and it was an exact copy of the one she'd paid for in cash right before the apocalypse hit. The outside, the layout, and the decor were all exactly the same.

The yard, the floor-to-ceiling windows, and the neat little patio matched down to the last detail.

Another message popped up. [The villa is fully equipped for sleeping, bathing, and cooking! It comes with a fridge, washing machine, tablet, running water, and electricity!]

It felt warm and cozy, like home. There was no blood or wreckage here and no need to be on guard every second. It was just peaceful.

For a second, Paisley almost felt like the apocalypse had never happened, and she was still living her old, normal life.

With a smile, she walked inside and sank into the soft couch. After days of nonstop fighting, her whole body finally relaxed.

Just then, Trovie piped up again.

[New cross-dimensional channel unlocked: the Ancient Realm!

[Now that you're a Level Two trader, Master, you can open a second trade connection! Do you want to connect with an Ancient Realm trader now?]

Paisley stretched out on the couch and opened the fridge. It was packed with cold drinks and snacks, so she grabbed two popsicles and handed one to Trovie.

Inside the dimension, Trovie could touch food just fine. It zipped over, grabbed the popsicle, and started nibbling happily.

It zoomed in happy circles around Paisley. [Master, what is this? It's cold and sweet, and it's sooo good!]

Paisley bit into her own popsicle and smiled. [It's a popsicle. Be good and work hard, and you'll get all the treats you want.]

Trovie spun in excited circles, over the moon. [Uh-huh! I'll be good and stick with you forever, Master!]

Paisley finished her popsicle. [Trovie, open the connection to the Ancient Realm trader.]

Trovie answered right away. [Got it! Connecting to the Ancient Realm... Connected!]

In an instant, an elegant trading screen with an old-world look unfolded in front of Paisley.

A stunning woman stood on the other side, wearing an ornate royal gown, with jeweled gold pins in her hair. She looked dignified and elegant, warm but unmistakably royal. She was gorgeous!
