Chapter 307

He set a few pieces of tender greens into her bowl, his voice careful.

"Pais, did something happen?"

"The Blackwoods and the Thompsons go back generations. We're family too. You can tell me anything."

Paisley looked down, eating quietly, her fingers tight around her bowl and fork.

Elijah didn't push.

"I just talked to my grandfather. He's going to help smooth things over with this base. As for the lab mission, it's dangerous. You don't have to go. Nobody's going to make you."

At that, she finally set her fork down, looking up. Whatever had been weighing on her was gone, replaced with something firm.

"I want to go."

"I need to be there myself. I need to find out what's really going on."

Orion watched her, something soft and protective in his expression, and piled her bowl high with the pork ribs she loved.

"Alright. Whatever Pais wants."

"Don't let it get you down. I'll be there. Whatever happens, I'll keep you safe."

Thomas and Spencer chimed in, both sincere.

"I've got your back too."

"I'll always be behind you."

Ashley leaned in, eyes bright.

"Me too, Paisley! I'll protect you!"

Madison cut in, dry.

"You all need to cool it. Paisley could probably take all of you at once. If anything, we should be asking who's protecting who."

Elijah laid out the plan for the next few days.

"Until the mission's finalized, everyone's free to rest. If you get bored, head to the mission hall and pick up something low-risk, keep yourselves sharp."

"Remember — don't go looking for trouble. But don't back down from it either."

"We've got three days at most before the base hands down the mission. Five days after that, and we're out of here, back to Aetheris."

At that, Ashley's eyes lit up, and she looped her arm through Paisley's.

"Let's go shopping after lunch! Stillwater looks way nicer than Rivergate — it's gotta be good!"

"It's been forever since I got to actually shop for anything."

"I want new clothes, a bag, maybe get my nails done…"

Madison jumped in.

"Count me in. We're heading home soon, I want to check out the specialty shops, grab something for my family."

Kelly chimed in too.

"Can I come? I want to pick something up for my family while we've got the time."

Before Paisley could say anything, Orion pulled up the base's virtual banking panel and transferred ten thousand contribution points to her account without a word.

His tone was easy, indulgent.

"Go spend the afternoon however you want. Buy whatever catches your eye."

"I can earn more. Don't hold back."

Paisley stared at the sudden ten thousand points on her screen, frozen for a second, something warm and aching settling low in her chest.

In two lifetimes, this was the first time anyone had given her something that large without wanting anything back.

Ten thousand points could carry an average family through a year, easy.

In her last life, under the Lennox roof, she'd always been the one getting drained, not given to. They'd taken everything — resources, crystal cores — like she existed to be bled dry.

Even without an Affinity, she'd gone out on runs every day, risking her life against the dead just to scrape together enough to keep the family fed. And when she had nothing left, not even a scrap of bread had come her way.

She'd never once, in that life, been looked after. Never had anyone actually in her corner.

Before she could get her bearings, Spencer and Thomas transferred three thousand points each.

Spencer smiled, warm.

"Spend it however you want, little sister. I can always make more."

Thomas nodded, certain.

"Same here. Don't hold back."

Then Elijah transferred thirty thousand, a number big enough to make her blink.

She stared at the total blinking on her screen, completely thrown, and looked up at him.

"Why would you give me this too?"

Elijah's eyes crinkled, warm and easy.

"Don't you call me your brother?"
