Chapter 494

Grant still said nothing, as if no one stood beside him.

He had taken the blame for her in that room and gambled three years of his freedom and his life. What he got back was her instant betrayal.

That burning love had gone cold in the interrogation room. Nothing was left but wasteland.

He should have seen it sooner. He'd just been too slow to see clearly.

He would never fall for her again, and he would never raise a hand to protect her.

Chloe stared at his cold, distant profile, and shame and fear washed through her.

On the dangerous frontline, without Grant's protection, she'd have a hard time lasting a year.

No matter how low she bent or how sweetly she begged, Grant ignored her. He never answered a word.

The assembly whistle blew. The squad shouldered their gear and walked into the howling snow, toward the contaminated zone and whatever waited there.

******

At the same moment, Paisley's eyelashes fluttered.

She slid one pale hand out of the soft blankets. Her fingertips felt the warmth the room had kept before her eyes slowly opened.

She'd stayed up late last night trading across realms with the modern-world trader, the Galactic trader, and the Beast Realm's Matriarch. Supplies and energy crystals had poured in nonstop.

The system panel showed her total.

[3,525,360 points!]

Trovie's bright voice chimed in.

[Side quest triggered: The Freeze has arrived and warm supplies are running short. Help the base solve this problem.]

[Reward: 10,000 points and one Affinity Awakening Pill.]

Paisley's eyes brightened and the corners of her mouth curved up.

Awakening Pills were precious. She hadn't expected a bonus like this.

She got up, washed, and went to the dining room.

The huge house was quiet. Only a warm breakfast waited on the table.

Gerald and Diane were out collecting winter supplies. Orion, Thomas, and the others had gone off to work. She was alone.

Paisley picked up her glass and finished the last of her warm milk. She pulled on her down jacket, wrapped herself in a fleece-lined hat, a thick scarf, and insulated gloves, and stepped out into the yard.

The Freeze raged on. Snow was still coming down.

Big flakes drifted down without stopping. The grape trellis sagged under heavy snow, white piled on every vine. The whole yard lay under a sheet of ice, and the wind threw stinging snow into her face.

Looking at the storm, she felt playful.

She raised a hand. Her Ice-Water Affinity stirred, and moisture and snow swirled together. In seconds, a round snowman stood in the yard.

With a thought she pulled a small knitted hat and a red scarf from her storage dimension and dressed him. She snapped a photo of him under the snow-covered grapevines.

Looking at it, she realized the snowman looked a lot like Elijah.

Paisley rubbed her nose, thought nothing of it, and walked out the gate.

A message popped up in the Falcon Squad group chat.

[Elijah: Report on today's assignments.]

[Spencer: Shoveling snow outside. It's so deep the roads have frozen solid. Twenty contribution points an hour. My hands are numb.]

[Thomas: Same here. If the main roads don't get cleared, the convoys can't get through.]

[Orion: Still training. Conditioning and shooting. The cold burns through your stamina.]

[Madison: I'm with Orion.]

[Mason: Took an escort job, hauling supplies to another base.]

[Ashley: I'm still at the mission hall. I want to go out on a mission, but I can't find a good team. Does anyone want to come with me? Heavy snow out there, and going alone is too risky.]

Paisley tapped her screen.

[Just got up. Heading to the mission hall now.]

Ashley answered almost instantly, practically bouncing through the screen.

[Ashley: Paisley, are you coming to find me? I'm the luckiest girl ever! I'll wait right outside the mission hall!]

Ashley dashed to the entrance, hunched against the wind. She stood on tiptoe to scan the road again and again, hopeful.

The gale whipped huge flakes around, and the road was glazed with a hard sheet of ice that creaked underfoot.
