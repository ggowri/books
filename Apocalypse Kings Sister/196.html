Chapter 196

“When did high-risk missions start letting just any girl walk in for a quick payday?”

“This isn't some game, sweetheart, it's a nine-deaths-one-life kind of mission. Soft little thing like you, we'll all be stuck babysitting you in there. Pure dead weight.”

His eyes ran over her, slow and unashamed, something greasy and crude in the look, his tone dropping even cheaper.

“Though, I guess you're pretty enough that I could be talked into making an exception.”

“Say yes to being my girlfriend, be good and keep me happy, and I'll make sure extra attention goes toward keeping you safe this time. Sound good?”

The whole thing dripped with contempt, flirtation with no shame behind it, and the room went rigid.

The next instant, a sharp-edged figure stepped forward, putting Paisley behind him, blocking that leering look entirely.

Every trace of warmth drained from Elijah's face. Frost settled in his eyes, and the gentle air around him turned to biting cold.

He didn't waste breath on words. His hand shot out, the force of it sharp and crushing, driving straight at Chase.

Chase had been ready. He sneered, cold light flaring in his eyes.

“What, you want to fight?”

“A Lightning Affinity user, running his mouth in front of my Level Five psychic power?”

The words had barely landed when an invisible storm of psychic force exploded outward.

Pressure like a tidal wave, unseen but crushing, swept through the whole office, aimed straight at Elijah.

A psychic attack was the ugliest kind—it struck the mind directly, rattling the soul itself, with nothing physical to block it.

Elijah's whole body jolted. His brow clenched, his face went pale in an instant, his breathing turned ragged, and his feet stumbled half a step.

******

Chase hadn't moved an inch, his smugness only growing, his taunt open.

“Know your place. Who do you think you are? Let's see what you've actually got.”

Everyone's face changed at once. No one dared step into a standoff between Level Five psychics—they could only edge back and watch, hearts pounding.

Behind him, Paisley's eyes turned cold.

She could feel exactly how hard the attack was slamming into Elijah's mind, and anger rose in her at once.

This wasn't a game. Sustained enough, an attack like that could leave a person a hollowed-out husk.

Who would have thought a base this small would even have a Level Five psychic.

The next moment, Paisley's eyes darkened. She opened her mind fully, and her own dormant, high-tier psychic power came crashing out without reservation.

It was the same invisible, formless kind of force, but purer, denser, far more overwhelming—like a silent deep sea suddenly surging, meeting his attack head-on.

Her counterstrike met his pressure dead center.

Two silent collisions detonated somewhere deep in the mind, no sound at all, and yet the air in the entire office froze solid.

No one could see the invisible flood of force, but everyone felt the room's atmosphere shift all the same.

Chase's Level Five pressure, which had steamrolled the whole room a moment ago, hit hers and simply came apart—like a storm crashing into a silent sea, cut off, torn apart, crushed in an instant.

No one knew that Paisley carried three rare Affinities at once—Plant, Water, and Psychic—each feeding the others until her mental strength had been honed to an extreme few could match.

Level Five like his, yes, but hers ran deeper, purer, harder to break—in raw depth and burst strength alone, she outclassed him a hundred times over without even trying.

His power was a wild, chaotic storm. Hers was a vast, bottomless sea. They weren't even playing the same game.

The arrogance on Chase's face drained out in an instant, leaving it bloodless.

A violent shockwave tore through his mind, like countless needles of ice driving straight into the core of his psyche, the pain spreading through every inch of him.

“Agh—!”

He couldn't hold back a strangled groan, his body jerking out of control. His lazy slouch snapped rigid, his knees buckled, and he staggered back three full steps.

The look on his face, arrogant a second ago, now held nothing but raw terror.

The Level Five psychic power he'd ruled this base with had just shattered like glass against a girl who looked like she couldn't hurt a fly.

Paisley stood exactly where she'd been, perfectly composed, not so much as a finger twitching.
