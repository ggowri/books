Chapter 196

"Since when did high-risk missions get so easy to join?" Chase sneered. "Now any little girl can tag along to rack up contribution points?"

"This is a life-or-death mission, not some game. A delicate little thing like you will need all of us protecting you the whole time. You'll just drag us down."

His eyes roamed shamelessly over her face and body, slimy and lecherous, and his tone turned even sleazier.

"Then again, you're pretty enough that I could make an exception.

"If you agree to be my girlfriend and keep me happy like a good girl, I'll put a little more effort into keeping you safe on this mission. How about it?"

His words were sleazy and arrogant, full of open harassment and contempt, and the whole room froze.

In the next second, someone stepped forward, cold and fierce, and pulled Paisley behind him, blocking that slimy, disgusting stare.

Every trace of warmth drained from Elijah's face. Frost settled in his eyes, and the gentle air around him turned to biting cold.

He didn't bother with words. He attacked right away, his strike sharp and crushing as it drove straight at Chase.

Chase had seen it coming. He gave a scornful sneer, his eyes turning icy.

"What, you want to fight?

"You're just a Lightning Affinity user, and you think you can mess with my Level Five psychic power?"

The moment he said it, an invisible psychic storm exploded.

An unseen wave of psychic pressure crashed through the whole office like a tidal wave, aimed squarely at Elijah.

Psychic attacks were the most vicious kind. They struck straight at the mind and rattled the soul, and there was no way to block them.

Elijah's body jolted. His brow furrowed hard, his face went pale, his breathing turned ragged, and he even stumbled half a step.

Chase stood there without moving an inch, more smug and cocky than ever, and mocked him openly.

"You don't know your place! Who the hell do you think you are? Let's see what you've really got!"

Everyone's faces changed. Nobody dared to get involved in a standoff with a Level Five psychic, so they backed away nervously to watch.

Behind Elijah, Paisley's eyes turned cold.

She could feel exactly how hard the attack was slamming into Elijah's mind, and anger rose in her at once.

This was no joke. If someone's mind took a beating like that for too long, they could end up crippled for life.

Who would've thought a small base like Rivergate would have a Level Five psychic?

The next second, Paisley opened her mind all the way, and the high-level psychic power she'd kept hidden exploded out with everything she had.

It was the same invisible, formless kind of power, but hers was purer, denser, and far more overpowering. Like a calm, deep sea suddenly surging, it rushed up to meet his attack head-on.

Her counterstrike met his pressure dead center.

Two silent psychic blasts collided deep in their minds. There was no earth-shaking sound, but the air in the whole office froze.

No one could see the invisible flood of force, but everyone felt the room's atmosphere shift all the same.

Chase's Level Five psychic pressure, which had been crushing everyone in the room, hit Paisley's power and was cut off, ripped apart, and crushed, like a raging storm slamming into a silent sea.

Nobody knew that Paisley was one of the rare people with three affinities, Plant, Water, and Psychic. They fed into each other, and her psychic power had been sharpened to the extreme.

She was Level Five too, but her psychic power was deeper, purer, tougher, and more overpowering. In raw depth and explosive force, she easily outclassed Chase a hundred times over.

His power was a wild, chaotic storm. Hers was a vast, bottomless sea. They weren't even playing the same game.

Chase, who'd been so cocky a second ago, went white as a sheet.

A violent shockwave tore through his mind, like countless needles of ice driving straight into the core of his psyche, the pain spreading through every inch of him.

"Agh!" He couldn't hold back the pain and let out a strangled groan, his body jerking out of control. His lazy slouch snapped stiff, his legs went weak, and he staggered back three steps.

The arrogance in his eyes had turned into pure horror.

The Level Five psychic power he'd been so proud of, the power that had let him rule the base, had actually crumbled against this delicate-looking girl.

Paisley stood calmly where she was, cool and composed, without moving so much as a finger.
