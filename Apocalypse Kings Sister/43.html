Chapter 43

Rachel didn't have an ability, and she'd only survived this long by clinging to other people. Coming that close to death had scarred her for good.

Worst of all, Jasper had used her as a human shield twice without a second thought. Whatever hope she'd had in him was gone.

She wasn't going to risk her life like that again. The apocalypse was way too dangerous, and she'd barely made it this far. She wasn't going to die here.

She had to get Paisley's pendant, and she couldn't wait any longer.

Once Jasper and Rachel were gone, Elijah turned to the other three, his face grave.

"The basement is sealed off, dark, and really damp. It's extremely dangerous down there, so everyone be careful."

"Got it!" Orion and Mason said together. They went on high alert right away, keeping Paisley in the middle.

Paisley's eyes narrowed as she reached out with her senses.

Deep in the basement, she could feel a massive, dark, toxic plant energy churning wildly. It reeked of death and rot, and it felt way more sinister than any high-level zombie.

It was a Level Three mutant plant.

"There's more than Level Three zombies down there. There's a Level Three mutant plant too, and it's really aggressive. It likes to hide, ambush people, and wrap them up, so watch your step and the cracks in the walls."

She gripped her dagger as she said it, her voice steady.

Elijah's stomach dropped. This was turning out to be even worse than he'd thought.

But he was a soldier. When his country was in trouble, he had to step up.

He just hated dragging Paisley into this. Still, as long as he was here, he'd do everything he could to keep her safe.

"Stay sharp, everyone," he said, and Orion and Mason nodded grimly.

Once they were ready, the four of them slipped through a hidden entrance on the side of the west building. Slowly, they headed down into the pitch-black basement.

The second they stepped into the tunnel, cold, damp air hit them in the face. It stank of rotting leaves and blood, and it made their chests feel tight.

The tunnel was long and dim, and its walls were covered in wet moss and mildew. Puddles dotted the floor, slick and sticky underfoot, and every wet footstep sounded way too loud in the silence.

The tunnel ended at the hospital's pharmacy storeroom. Its heavy iron door hung half-open, and it was pitch-black inside, except for faint flickers of eerie green light leaking through the gap. Something about it felt seriously wrong.

Carefully, they eased the iron door open.

It creaked on its hinges, and countless thin, whip-like vines glowing a creepy green burst out of the darkness!

They were packed in thick tangles and covered in tiny black thorns, with palm-sized leaves that had saw-toothed edges. A sickly green, poisonous glow pulsed through their veins as they whipped through the air, lunging to wrap up all four of them!

"Watch out! Mutant vines!" Elijah barked.

He slashed hard at the vines coming at him, but his blade only cut through the outer layer. Thick, dark green venom oozed from the cut ends. It was so corrosive that it burned little black holes in the floor wherever it splattered.

Paisley's eyes sharpened, and she spotted the real monster hiding in the dark. In the middle of the storeroom, a giant, dark green Level Three mutant pothos had taken root on a concrete platform.

It didn't look anything like a houseplant anymore. Its stem was as thick as a man's arm and rooted deep in the muddy floor. Its vines had spread through the whole room, wrapping around the shelves, covering the walls, and blanketing the ceiling in one massive, deadly green net.

The huge leaves were layered so thick they blocked out every bit of light. In the center of each one was a pitch-black flower that kept giving off a faint nerve toxin, and the air around it was poison.

It was a Level Three Venom-Devouring Pothos, one of the rarest high-level mutant plants in the apocalypse.

The plant didn't have eyes or ears, but it could sense living people and their body heat with scary precision. It was great at hiding and ambushing, and its vines were as sharp as blades and loaded with poison that could burn and paralyze.

Anyone its vines caught and cut would go stiff and numb fast, until they couldn't move at all. Then, the vines would suck them dry and turn them into plant food.

Worse still, it had been rooted down here for years and had basically become part of the basement. Its vines could be anywhere and attack from any direction, so there was no way to see them coming.

"Everyone, back up! They're poisonous! Don't touch the vines!" Paisley yelled, hacking at the vines nonstop with her dagger.
