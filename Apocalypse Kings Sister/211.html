Chapter 211

No one knew why anyone would build a research lab under an aquarium.

Maybe after soaking down here for years in toxins and that magnetic field, it had been infected and taken over by the beasts' poison, until it finally turned into this creepy half-human, half-beast thing.

As if it sensed living people, the mutant slowly raised its head. Its murky black eyes locked onto them, empty, eerie, and stone cold.

Long, messy black hair clung wetly to both sides of its pale, swollen face. Its torn lips curled back, showing rows of fine, sharp white fangs that gleamed coldly.

Its mutated fingers were long and sharp, their tips glinting a dark, poisonous blue. They could rip through flesh with no effort at all.

With it staring at them like that, everyone's skin crawled, chills ran down their spines, and every hair on their bodies stood on end.

Fear and panic swept through the whole group. One team member who couldn't handle pressure well lost it. On instinct, he raised his hand and fired off his power, attacking first.

But the second his power left his hand, countless dark blue-green tentacles burst up from below, moving so fast they were just a blur.

The flexible, venomous tentacles shot toward him, wrapped tight around his waist, and yanked him off the ground with brutal force.

The moment the tentacles touched his skin, a faint black bruise of poison spread fast up his neck and arms. A stinging numbness came right behind it, and the venom seeped into every part of his body.

His whole body went rigid, then limp, as the pain and paralysis crushed him. He struggled uselessly, screaming in a shaky, desperate voice. "Help me... Somebody help me!"

When they saw their teammate in danger, everyone attacked at once. They called up their powers, and layer after layer of fire, lightning, and wind blades flew straight at the mutant.

Just then, the humanoid mutant suddenly threw its head back, its chest heaving, and let out a shrill, ear-splitting shriek.

With a deafening boom, an invisible shockwave exploded outward. It turned into a violent blast of air that was almost impossible to see and rolled across the whole area.

Everyone felt a sudden, splitting pain in their heads. Their temples pounded, the pressure in their skulls felt like it would burst, and their minds went fuzzy and scrambled.

The sound wave smothered every power they'd thrown, and they all fell apart in midair without doing the mutant any harm at all.

Overwhelming fear swallowed everyone. Some went white as paper, gritting their teeth against the pain in their skulls as cold sweat covered their foreheads. Others couldn't hold back a groan and clutched their heads, swaying on their feet with the hopeless look of people about to die.

The narrow underground tunnel left them nowhere to retreat. The mutant in front of them was strange and powerful, and it clearly had no intention of letting them go. Everyone was sinking into helpless despair.

Even though they were going through the same splitting pain in their minds, Elijah and Orion's first instinct was still to do everything they could to protect Paisley.

Elijah forced down the explosive pain in his skull and fought off the dizziness. On instinct, he turned sideways and shielded Paisley, using his own body to block the aftershocks for her.

His eyes were tense and grim, locked on the shrieking humanoid mutant ahead, and he asked urgently, "Does anyone know how to deal with this? What the hell is that thing, and what level is its power?"

His voice turned heavy with worry. "It's probably stronger than all of us put together. If we keep dragging this out, we're all going to die down here."

Orion ignored the stabbing pain in his own mind and pressed both hands tightly over Paisley's ears to block out as much of the shrill sound as he could. "Paisley, do you have a way to stop it?" he asked anxiously, a faint tremor in his voice.

His heart clenched as he glanced at their poisoned teammate, who was black all over and twitching in the tentacles' grip. "That thing's attacks are too freaky. He's got five minutes, tops."

Even with everyone on the edge of death and full of despair, Paisley stayed cool and calm, not shaken in the slightest by the panic around her.

A faint light flashed at her fingertips, and a sleek energy gun glowing with a cold blue light appeared in her palm.

Her eyes narrowed slightly, and she said casually, "Maybe we can give this a try."

Elijah's eyes widened in surprise. He stared at the brand-new energy gun, confused, and almost asked why she hadn't pulled it out earlier if she'd had another one all along.

He had a million things to say, but they all stuck in his throat, and in the end, he said nothing. The last thing he wanted was to look like a fool in front of her.

Orion was just as surprised. His hands paused for a moment over her ears, and in the end, he couldn't help asking what they were both wondering.
