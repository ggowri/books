Chapter 70

She turned to Paisley, shocked and thrilled, her eyes shining with gratitude and admiration.

"Paisley, I awakened an ability!"

Ashley's eyes finally landed on her empty cup.

She knew it was the water that had awakened her ability.

It's all thanks to Paisley.

Madison stared at her in shock. Before she could even congratulate Ashley, power surged through her own body too.

She didn't even have time to process it. A cool, refreshing energy rose inside her, and the moisture in the air drifted toward her and formed sparkling droplets at her fingertips.

Madison's breath caught, and her face lit up with pure joy.

"I awakened one too! It's Water Affinity!"

The gentle Water Affinity energy flowed through her and soothed away all her restlessness and weakness. Her frail body felt stronger right away, and she seemed calmer and brighter.

Once the initial shock wore off, Ashley rushed over and threw her arms around Paisley.

"Thank you, Paisley! I know you're the reason I awakened!

"I never thought I'd get an ability of my own! I don't even know how to thank you. Without you, I never would've had this chance!"

Madison was just as overwhelmed.

She stepped forward and gently took Paisley's hand. Her eyes were red, and her voice was serious.

"I'll never forget what you did for me, not for as long as I live.

"From now on, as long as I'm around, I'll protect you. I won't let anyone hurt you, ever!"

Just then, Thomas and Spencer came out of their rooms and headed over. They'd both awakened abilities too.

The brothers couldn't hide how excited they were, and energy swirled faintly around them.

Thomas thanked her first, practically bursting.

"Pais, I awakened a Plant Affinity! Thank you. You're our good luck charm!

"If it weren't for you, we'd probably be stuck as regular people for the rest of our lives, unable to even protect ourselves. You gave us hope."

Spencer kept nodding, overflowing with gratitude.

"Paisley, I love you so much! I actually awakened a Fire Affinity, and I'm so freaking happy right now!

"I really thought I'd be a regular guy my whole life. Pais, you gave me the chance to be strong.

"From now on, my life is yours. I'll do whatever it takes to keep you safe."

In no time at all, four more members of the team had awakened abilities.

Paisley looked at their happy faces and spoke gently but seriously.

"I need all of you to keep this a secret. Nobody outside this group can know."

Madison's smile faded at once, and she nodded solemnly.

"She's right. This can never get out.

"Something this powerful is way too big a deal. If anyone else found out, they'd come after it, and Paisley would be in serious danger. We won't breathe a word."

Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer nodded seriously too. They were all in agreement.

Deep down, they all knew just how incredible Paisley's spring water was.

In a brutal, dog-eat-dog apocalypse, if word ever got out, it would bring nothing but snooping, scheming, and people out for blood.

Paisley had helped them awaken because she trusted them.

They'd never forget what she'd done for them.

And they'd never let her get hurt because of it.

Without another word, they all went back to their rooms and acted like none of this had ever happened.

Sure enough, it poured for two straight days. On the sixth day of the apocalypse, the rain finally stopped.

By then, Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, and Madison had all awakened abilities.

Kelly looked seriously sour about it.

She'd been the only woman on the team with an ability. Now, she had no edge at all.

During those two days, Paisley had snuck out to another big mall in Southspire and cleaned it out completely.

She'd also made another trade with the Beast Realm's Matriarch and gotten a bunch of animals and crystal gems.

Thanks to all that, her Plant Affinity had climbed to early Level Five.

Her total points were up to 56,900.

She was still over 40,000 points away from becoming a Level Three trader, but Paisley was more than happy with that.

Everyone had just finished breakfast.
