Chapter 215

With that, Paisley pulled the supplies she'd prepared out of her Storage Dimension.

She handed everyone a bottle of crystal-clear spirit water. It was cool and pure, and it held gentle, concentrated energy.

Everyone quickly took a bottle and drank in small sips.

The warm energy flowed down their throats and spread through their whole bodies right away. The exhaustion and drain from the fight, the poison, and the sound waves faded fast, and their drained powers and stamina slowly came back.

Everyone was shocked all over again.

Ordinary water could only quench thirst, but this spirit water could restore energy and heal the body fast. It was a rare treasure most people could only dream of.

Before they could calm down, six steaming boxed lunches appeared on the ground one after another.

Without thinking, everyone looked down at the supplies in their hands and quietly put their stale hardtack back in their pockets. Once again, they could hardly believe their eyes.

The boxed lunches gave off warm steam. Each one came with two kinds of meat, a side of veggies, and a scoop of creamy mashed potatoes, and the savory smell slowly spread through the tunnel.

In the apocalypse, where supplies were scarce and people spent every day running and fighting, just getting enough to eat was a dream. A hot, balanced boxed lunch like this was an incredible luxury.

Some of them felt their throats go tight, so moved they almost cried.

After a whole morning of high-pressure fighting and struggling to survive, they were worn out, body and mind. Right now, one hot meal and one bottle of spirit water were enough to wipe away all their exhaustion.

While everyone rested and ate, Elijah opened the mission group chat and sent their exact location, telling the other six team members to come meet up.

Before long, faint footsteps came from deep in the tunnel, and the six team members who'd fallen behind hurried over.

They were covered in dust, their clothes were torn, and each of them had minor injuries. They couldn't hide how exhausted and beat up they were.

All the way here, they'd kept running into beast ambushes and magnetic interference. Every step had been a struggle, they'd barely escaped death, and they were in terrible shape.

But when they saw the others, rested and looking great, all six of them stopped short, hit with a mix of feelings.

They'd faced the same danger. The six of them had ended up hurt and fighting just to survive, but the others didn't have a scratch on them and even got to eat hot boxed lunches.

It left them speechless.

Paisley took out more supplies and handed each of the six a bottle of spirit water and a steaming boxed lunch with two kinds of meat and a side of veggies.

Right then, warm food and water felt especially precious.

The six of them were starving and worn out, so after saying thanks, they didn't hold back and started eating right away. The warmth spread down to their stomachs and slowly chased away their exhaustion.

After a quick meal, they took out the medicine they carried and helped each other treat their scrapes, bites, and torn wounds. The sharp smell of medicine quickly filled the air.

During the break, the two team members who'd fought their way out and run their weapons dry couldn't hold back any longer. They stepped up and looked right at Elijah, cautious and hopeful.

They'd been fighting hard the whole way, and if they hadn't bought energy guns, they never would've made it to the meeting point alive.

After seeing with their own eyes how the energy guns could kill a high-level mutant in one shot and rip through a sound-wave barrier, everyone was dying to get one.

Someone finally asked, "Captain Blackwood, do you have any energy guns left... Could you sell us two?"

"My energy gun's out of power. Could you sell me just an energy core?"

The other team members looked over too, openly envious. Top-tier weapons this powerful were almost impossible to buy at any price, and ordinary people never even got near one.

Ten thousand contribution points was an astronomical number for ordinary fighters like them.

They didn't even dare think about buying one.

Elijah didn't say anything, so Paisley spoke up and named the price straight out. "Energy guns are 10,000 contribution points each, and the matching energy cores are 1,000 apiece."

The two team members who needed weapons the most didn't hesitate at all.

In a place this dangerous, going without a strong weapon meant being a sitting duck. No matter how expensive it was, it was worth it.

Both of them borrowed contribution points from the base on the spot, came up with the full price, paid it all at once, and got their energy guns.

The two who'd owed her from that morning found out they could take out loans too, so they borrowed right away and paid off what they owed. On top of that, they each bought an energy core.

Elijah and Orion bought two energy cores as well.

After the deals were done, Paisley's contribution points came to 105,690 in total.
