Chapter 215

The words had barely left her when she reached into her Storage Dimension and pulled out supplies she'd prepared ahead of time.

A bottle of clear, shimmering spirit water went to each of them, cool and pure, humming with concentrated, gentle energy.

They each took a bottle and drank in small, careful sips.

The warmth spread down their throats and raced through their limbs. The exhaustion from the fight, the poison, the sonic blast, all of it drained away fast, their depleted Affinities and stamina climbing back.

Shock hit them all over again, written plain on every face.

Ordinary water only quenched thirst. This spirit water restored energy and healed the body in the same breath. It was the kind of treasure most people never even heard of, let alone touched.

Before anyone could settle, six steaming lunch boxes appeared on the ground one by one.

Everyone looked down at the supplies in their hands and quietly pocketed the hardtack they'd been about to eat. Disbelief rolled through the group again.

The lunch boxes gave off warm, fragrant steam, each one packed with two meat dishes, a vegetable, and soft, full-grained rice, the smell spreading rich through the tunnel.

In a world this starved of resources, where everyone was fighting and running just to survive, a warm, balanced meal like this was close to unthinkable luxury.

Someone's eyes went damp. A few of them looked close to tears.

A morning of brutal fighting, of fighting to survive, had left them all wrung out. One hot meal, one bottle of spirit water, was enough to smooth all of it over.

While the others ate and recovered, Elijah opened the mission's group chat and sent out their exact coordinates, calling the other six missing teammates in to regroup.

******

Before long, soft footsteps echoed from deeper in the tunnel. Six stragglers hurried into view.

They were covered in dust, clothes torn, each carrying cuts and bruises of various depths, exhaustion and misery written across every face.

They'd spent the whole stretch dodging beast ambushes and magnetic interference, barely scraping by at every turn, in the worst shape imaginable.

Then they saw the rest of the group, well-fed, clean, and fully recovered, and their footsteps stuttered, a mess of emotions rising in them.

Same danger, and they'd ended up battered and half-dead, while the others sat there untouched and eating hot food.

This...

Paisley pulled out more supplies, handing each of the six a bottle of spirit water and a hot lunch box with two meats and a vegetable.

Warm food and water had never felt so precious.

Starving and worn down, the six didn't bother with pleasantries. They said their thanks and dug in immediately, warmth spreading through them, pushing back the fatigue bit by bit.

Once they'd finished eating, they dug out their own medical kits and started treating each other's scrapes, bite marks, and gashes, the sharp smell of antiseptic filling the air.

During the break, the two who'd fought their way through with weapons already dry of charge couldn't hold back any longer. They approached Elijah, careful and hopeful.

They'd only made it to the rendezvous point because they'd bought energy guns. Without them, they wouldn't have survived the trip.

Having seen one of those guns kill a high-tier mutant and shatter a sound barrier in a single shot, the hunger in the room was impossible to miss.

Someone finally spoke up:

"Captain, you've still got one of those energy guns left, don't you? Would you sell us two more?"

"My gun's out of charge. Could I just buy a crystal core for it?"

The rest of the group glanced over too, eyes thick with envy. Weapons that could tilt a fight like that were never for sale on the open market, not for people like them.

Ten thousand contribution points was an astronomical number for an ordinary operator.

They hadn't even considered asking.

Elijah said nothing.

Paisley answered instead, naming the price flat out:

"Ten thousand points for the gun, one thousand for a matching crystal core."

The two who needed weapons the most didn't hesitate for a second.

Out here, without real firepower, they were nothing but easy kills. No price was too high.

Both of them took out loans against the base's credit on the spot, paid in full, and walked away with energy guns.

The other two, who already owed the base from the morning, learned they could do the same, took out loans, and cleared their debts.

They each bought a crystal core too.

Elijah and Orion bought two crystal cores of their own.

A few transactions later, Paisley's total contribution points stood at 105,690.
