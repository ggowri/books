Chapter 506

In the past, Ryan, Owen, and Reid would have joined Rachel in blaming Paisley.

But today, after the brush with death outside the city and the sight of Paisley's calm indifference, the three felt a mess of conflicting things.

They knew it was their own relentless pressure, again and again, that had broken Paisley's heart and brought them to this.

Ryan kept his head down and said nothing. He didn't join in the smears.

"Bang!"

Helen slammed her palm on the wooden table so hard it shuddered. Her face was red with rage and her voice was shrill.

"That ungrateful wretch Paisley! She actually stood there and watched you die out there!"

"Not our own flesh and blood, of course it's different! Eighteen years and we still couldn't win her over!"

"Heartless, rotten thing!"

"She ate our food for eighteen years. Does she really think one severance agreement wipes out every debt?"

"I'll settle this account with her myself, sooner or later!"

Gordon's face was dark and his brows were knitted. His voice was cold.

"She acted so quiet and harmless at home. I thought she was just well-behaved. All of it was an act!"

"I knew she'd be this cold and let people die. We should have strangled her back then, so she couldn't hurt our Rachel now."

"Don't worry, Rachel. You're our real, precious daughter. Everything she took, the glory and the resources, I'll get it all back for you."

"Don't think running to the Thompsons makes her safe. The main Lennox family has deep roots. They're not people to be crossed. One day I'll make her kneel in front of you and beg."

The thought of Paisley on her knees, begging, put a sick look of anticipation in Gordon and Helen's eyes, as if they could already see it.

A flash of longing crossed Rachel's face and she spoke softly.

"Dad, the reason we got back alive this time was Miss Vivian."

"She's so beautiful. She's calm and graceful, and she shines just by standing there. People can't help wanting to be near her."

"I wish I could work by her side."

Gordon's face eased a little, and calculation crept into his eyes. He nodded slowly.

"That's not hard. I've still got some old connections in the main Lennox family. If you want a place near Miss Vivian, a little smoothing over and it's done."

He looked over at Owen, Ryan, and Reid, lowered his voice, and went on, hungry with ambition.

"And you three."

"Miss Vivian isn't engaged yet. If you've got the talent to catch her eye and marry her, our whole family makes it to the top overnight! We'd get supplies, status, everything handed to us!"

"Starting tomorrow, the three of you find a way to win Miss Vivian's heart."

The room was swept up in the idea. They forgot the pain in their wounds and began to daydream about an easy, glorious life.

Only Ryan kept his head down, a bitter, mocking smile twisting his mouth.

They really had some nerve. It was a pipe dream.

******

At the same time, in the study of the Quinn villa, it was heavy and oppressive. Thick floor-to-ceiling curtains were drawn tight, and a single dim wall lamp threw a yellow glow. Cold seeped in through the gap under the door, and the room felt grim and quiet.

Behind the wide ebony desk, the head of the Quinn family sat in the seat of power, face as hard as cold iron.

On the sofa nearby, Damon lounged, wrapped in layers of gauze. Much of it was seeping red.

The private doctor had just finished dressing all of his wounds, and the smell of medicine hung in the air.

If he hadn't reacted quickly and escaped from that fall using his powers as a cushion, there was no way he'd have made it back to the Quinn villa alive.

Whenever he recalled the bullet that had come out of nowhere in midair and blown up the plane, and Paisley standing calm and cold in the storm, hatred churned in his chest. He could never swallow this near-death grudge.

The doctor packed his bag, bowed, and left. Only the father and his sons remained in the study.

Russell Quinn spoke, voice low and heavy.

"What exactly happened? You set up a perfect kill. How did it fail?"

Damon clenched his fist, knuckles white, and said nothing.

"Falcon Squad finished their mission on schedule and brought back a huge haul. Now the Blackwood family is at the height of its power."

"More and more people are backing the Blackwoods."
