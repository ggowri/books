Chapter 492

"Look at what they got. Thick coats, plenty of coal, real food. And look at what we got. These ration bars are like chewing gravel. This little bit of fuel won't last us through the Freeze."

Her eyes darted, and an idea took shape. She turned to Dennis, urgent.

"We have to get Chloe out on bail. Only if she's free can our family get back to how things were."

Dennis's face was dark, his brows knotted. After a long silence, he nodded slowly.

"Fine. I'll figure something out. I'll talk to some old friends."

But he knew Chloe's charges were serious. Bail wouldn't be simple.

Not unless she found a new patron.

Meanwhile, the heavy iron door of the Security Office banged open, rust screeching on the hinges.

The interrogation room was dim, lit only by a few bare bulbs that threw down a pale glare. Wind lashed the window and howled, and the room felt grim.

Today's interrogator came from the base's neutral faction. He was known for sticking to evidence and ignoring favors.

Chloe, Grant, and the rest of the captured ambushers were taken into separate rooms, one by one.

Chloe went first. She sat in a cold iron chair, hands cuffed to the table. There was no remorse on her face. The first thing she did was try to clear herself.

"Officer, this was all Grant's plan! He pressured me and tricked me. I had no choice but to go along."

The words poured out. She forced a helpless, wounded look and shoved all the blame onto him.

"The planning, the ambush, stopping Falcon Squad on the road, releasing the high-tier zombies, that was all him. I just did what I was told. I never wanted to hurt anyone from the base!"

She worked hard to paint herself as a coerced victim. She said nothing about attacking first, or mocking Paisley, or wanting Falcon Squad dead.

Soon it was Grant's turn in the next room. The interrogator relayed Chloe's statement.

Grant froze for a moment. Then he slowly raised his eyes, and there was nothing in them but cold hurt and disappointment.

He'd thought Chloe truly loved him. Even in failure, he'd believed they would face the consequences together.

He had taken this mission to avenge her.

And she was this cold.

Now, the first thing she'd done was sell him out and dump everything on him.

He was silent for a long time. He closed his eyes briefly and stopped defending himself.

"That's right. I planned it all. The ambush, the zombies, the kill squads, all my idea. Chloe was misled by me. The fault is mine."

He took full responsibility as the principal offender, flat and clean, without a single excuse.

The interrogator checked every piece of physical evidence and testimony and compared the two statements again and again.

Chloe sat in her chair, secretly pleased.

If Grant took everything, she'd be cleared. She was a rare talent, and the leadership would go easy on her. They'd let her walk.

She was already imagining it. Once she got out, she'd find a new patron and get her comfortable life back. She never noticed the disappointed look Grant had given her.

When the questioning ended, everyone was gathered in the hall to hear the verdict.

The interrogator held the finished file. His voice was steady and clear in the empty room.

"After reviewing all evidence, Grant Quinn is found to be the principal offender in this ambush. He planned multiple kill attempts and used high-tier zombies to try to murder members of this base. His crimes are severe."

"Sentence: three years of unpaid hazard duty in the contaminated zones outside the base."

Grant's hand clenched slightly at his side. He didn't argue.

"Chloe Thompson. You actively took part in the encirclement of Falcon Squad and attacked fellow base members with your abilities. You were not the mastermind, but you cooperated in the ambush. Your guilt cannot be waived."

"Sentence: assignment to a high-risk frontline squad for one year of unpaid hazard duty."

The thin smile on Chloe's face froze. Her pupils shrank, and she looked up in disbelief.

Shouldn't she have been released?

She'd put everything on Grant!
