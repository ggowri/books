Chapter 199

Paisley couldn't help wondering. Did Rachel really have no clue how weak she was?

Rachel knew how dangerous the aquarium was and signed up anyway, so Paisley had to admit she had guts.

But that was fine. If Rachel was so desperate to show off and grab the credit, Paisley would give her the chance. In this life, Rachel didn't get to call all the shots anymore.

Out of the corner of her eye, Paisley saw Elijah and Orion frowning, clearly about to object to Rachel joining the team. She raised a hand to stop them, silently signaling them to go along with it.

Since Paisley didn't object, Elijah and Orion had no choice but to push down their concerns and nod in silence.

With the team settled, the head of the base called out an order.

"Everyone's in place! Gear up and move out now for the aquarium mission!"

At his order, the 14 team members lined up and filed out of the office to gather at the base motor pool.

Three military SUVs had been assigned for the trip. They were heavily armored, blast-proof, and crash-resistant, built specifically for high-risk scouting missions in the apocalypse.

Paisley, Elijah, and Orion rode in the first car, leading the way.

Rachel, who was desperate to latch onto Chase, got her wish and was put in the second car with him and three other team members, five in all.

The rest of the team rode in the third car, and they all set out together.

The cars rolled smoothly out through the base gates and sped toward the abandoned coastline on the outskirts of the city.

In the first car, Paisley sat in the back seat. With a thought, she spent 1,000 points in the system shop to buy two Poison Immunity Pills.

Each pill gave 24 hours of total immunity to poison, protecting against toxic gas, contamination, and mutant venom.

The aquarium's toxins were deadly and unpredictable, and they'd nearly died there yesterday, so she wasn't taking any chances.

A thousand points wasn't that expensive, and she could easily afford it. Using it to protect the people who mattered to her was well worth it.

Trovie's sweet voice chimed.

[1,000 points deducted. Two Poison Immunity Pills purchased.

[Remaining points: 298,060.]

With a flick of her fingers, two milky-white pills with a faint herbal scent appeared in her palm.

Before, she'd only thought of Elijah and Orion as powerful allies she could lean on.

But after these past few days together, they'd become the people who always protected her, favored her, and put her first.

Now, they were the most important people in her life.

Of course she'd look out for them too.

She reached out and handed a pill each to Elijah and Orion.

"The toxins in the aquarium are tricky and hard to defend against. These are Poison Immunity Pills, and they last for 24 hours. Once you take one, no contamination or toxin can touch you."

They trusted her without a second thought, so they took the pills and swallowed them.

The pills melted in their mouths, and a warm, sweet energy spread from their throats through their whole bodies. A faint protective barrier formed around them, and they felt great.

Before either of them could react, Paisley raised her hand again, and two finely made, matte black rings appeared in her palm.

"And these. They're storage rings, each with 100 square feet of storage space, which should be enough for whatever you collect today."

She handed one to each of them. Elijah and Orion took the rings at the same time, and the moment their fingers touched the cool, smooth metal, their eyes went wide with shock.

They both looked at Paisley, and the shock slowly turned into gratitude.

These were actually storage items, and they definitely didn't come from this era.

In the resource-starved apocalypse, storage items were priceless treasures money couldn't buy. Even a basic 100-square-foot storage ring was something everyone wanted but nobody could get.

They'd never imagined Paisley would have something this precious, let alone hand one to each of them without a second thought.

Elijah closed his fingers around the ring, his eyes shining with warmth, and his gaze stayed fixed on Paisley. He was so moved he couldn't speak.

She seemed cool and distant, but deep down, she was incredibly soft. She never said sweet things out loud, but in her own way, she gave them everything good she had.

This looks just like a ring. Someday, I'll put a real ring on her finger, one that's just for the two of us!
