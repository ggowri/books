Chapter 164

Paisley stood off to the side, watching them laugh, something soft in her eyes. Kitty curled in her arms, nuzzling lazily against her.

Once everyone had eaten and played their fill, Spencer spoke up first.

"Time's up. We should get to the last mission."

"Right!"

The group agreed and headed down the stairs.

Once the footsteps faded and the floor emptied out, Paisley's gaze flickered, and her storage opened.

Take it!

The milkshake equipment, coffee machine, every bit of mix and syrup — gone.

Take it!

The kettle corn stand, hot dog roller, every pre-cooked item, every sealed ingredient — gone.

Take it!

Every claw machine, dance game, arcade cabinet, theater seat — all of it, gone.

In seconds, the food court and arcade stood completely empty, nothing left but bare counters and machine stands.

Trovie spun happily through the air.

[Host, I want a milkshake, a hot dog, and kettle corn!]

Kitty meowed too, rubbing her head against Paisley's sleeve, eyes wide and hopeful, clearly asking for the same thing.

Paisley's expression softened.

[Fine, deal. Kitty, head into storage, you two can eat in there.]

She pulled the fresh milkshake, kettle corn, and hot dogs back out and set them up neatly for the two of them.

With that handled, she turned to head downstairs and catch up with the others — when her senses caught something. Several people, moving carefully, trying not to be noticed.

Someone's close.

Paisley's eyes sharpened, and her Level Five Psychic Affinity spread out, locking onto a hidden corner outside the mall.

Behind a crumbling wall, hiding in the shadows, were Rachel, the three Lennox brothers, and Kelly — all five of them.

They'd tailed her all this way, hiding, watching. Clearly up to something.

Paisley's mouth curved, something cold and amused in her eyes.

They want to play this game — fine.

She let her Psychic Affinity reach further, locking onto a Level Five mutant zombie wandering nearby, hunting for food.

Her will pressed down on it, forcing compliance, delivering one precise command.

Go.

Kill the five in hiding.

******

The Level Five zombie, lazy a second ago, snapped into a frenzy, turning its massive body toward Rachel's hiding spot, charging with murderous intent.

A Level Five mutant with that kind of ferocity would give Rachel's group more than they could handle — a real taste of despair.

Paisley's group loaded into the SUVs, engines roaring to life, and drove off toward the amusement park.

The dust from their cars hadn't even settled before Rachel's group crept out of hiding, ready to follow — only to freeze at the sound of heavy footsteps closing in fast.

A roar tore through the air, shaking dust loose from the rubble around them.

A massive, hulking Level Five mutant zombie burst through the ruins and planted itself directly in their path.

Rotted flesh hung off its frame, claws gleaming white, and a wave of pure menace locked down the entire area.

The sight of it froze all five of them, faces drained of color.

"A — a Level Five zombie!"

"Weren't they all supposed to be dead?"

"How is something this strong even here?"

"Why did it just appear?!"

Their pupils shrank, pure terror flooding every vein. Legs went weak. Bodies locked up. Even breathing felt impossible.

They'd never imagined their little ambush attempt would run them straight into something this dangerous.

The zombie's target was unmistakable — its blood-red eyes locked on Rachel, dead center of the group, and it lunged, tearing the air apart with the force of the charge.
