Chapter 164

Paisley stood quietly off to the side, watching them laugh and have fun, her eyes warm and soft. The little white cat snuggled in her arms, lazily rubbing against her.

Once everyone had eaten, played to their hearts' content, and rested up, Spencer spoke up first. "It's about time. We should go take care of the last mission."

"Let's go!" the others said, and they headed down the stairs together.

Once everyone had left the fifth floor and their footsteps faded away, the place went quiet again. Paisley's eyes flickered, and with a thought, she opened her Storage Dimension.

First, she took the milkshake equipment, the coffee machine, and every last topping and ingredient. Then, the kettle corn machine, the hot dog roller, and all the prepped food and sealed ingredients went in. Finally, every claw machine, dance game, arcade cabinet, theater setup, and lounge booth disappeared too.

In just a few seconds, the food court and arcade were bare, with nothing left but empty stalls and machine bases.

Trovie spun happily through the air, its sweet voice full of joy. [Master, I want a milkshake, a hot dog, and some kettle corn!]

The little white cat chimed in with two soft meows, rubbing its head against Paisley's sleeve. It stared up at her with big, shiny eyes, clearly begging for its share.

Paisley's face softened. [Fine, you two win. Kitty, go into the dimension, and you can both eat in there.]

With a thought, she took out some of the fresh milkshakes, crunchy kettle corn, and juicy hot dogs she'd just stored and set them out neatly in the dimension's living room for the two greedy little things.

With everything taken care of, she was about to head downstairs to meet up with the others when her psychic senses suddenly picked up several people sneaking around.

Someone's coming!

Paisley's eyes narrowed. She spread out her Level Five psychic power and zeroed in on a hidden corner outside the mall.

Rachel, the three Lennox brothers, and Kelly were hiding in the shadows behind the crumbling walls.

They'd secretly followed her all the way here and were lying low, watching. They were obviously up to no good.

Paisley gave a faint, cold smile, clearly amused.

They went to all this trouble to follow me? Fine, let's play.

She released her vast, pure Level Five psychic power and locked onto a Level Five mutant zombie prowling for food about 500 yards away.

Her powerful psychic energy flooded the beast's mind, forcing it under her control and giving it a clear order.

Go. Kill the five hiding over there.

A second ago, the Level Five mutant zombie had been lazily wandering around. Now, under her psychic control, it went wild. It swung its massive body around and charged toward the spot where Rachel and the others were hiding, giving off a terrifying, savage aura.

With a vicious Level Five on their tails, Rachel and the others were in for a world of hurt and a real taste of despair.

Meanwhile, Paisley and the others got into the SUVs. The engines roared, and they quickly pulled away from the abandoned mall, heading down the desolate road toward the amusement park.

The dust behind their cars had barely settled when Rachel's group, still crouching low, got ready to follow quietly in their own car. But before they could take a step, they heard heavy, rapid footsteps pounding toward them.

A vicious roar exploded through the air, shaking dust loose from the rubble all around.

A massive, hulking Level Five mutant zombie burst through the ruins and planted itself directly in their path.

Its blackened, rotting flesh was peeling open, its sharp claws and teeth gleamed white, and its savage presence froze the whole area.

The terrifying beast had come out of nowhere. All five of them went pale, and their blood ran cold.

"A-a Level Five mutant zombie!"

"Weren't all the zombies killed?"

"How is there still a zombie this strong here?"

"Where did it even come from?!"

Sheer terror washed over them. Their legs went weak, their bodies locked up, and they could barely breathe.

They'd never imagined their little ambush attempt would run them straight into something this dangerous.

The zombie knew exactly who it was after. Its bloodthirsty red eyes locked onto Rachel in the middle of the group, and it coiled its massive body before lunging at her with a force that ripped through the air.
