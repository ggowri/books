Brute force had failed.

In that desperate moment, an idea flashed through Paisley's mind.

Its outside is invincible. So I'll break it from the inside.

Her eyes darkened, a cold, eerie light crossing them.

She moved like a ghost. Before the enraged beast could react, she laid her slim white hand on the thick, hard scales on top of its head.

"Parasitize."

A faint, secret thread of dark green life, a single seed, slipped from her palm. It drifted through the pores on the beast's scalp like dust and sank into its flesh, bones, and organs without a sound.

It was the cruelest, most vicious card her Plant Affinity had. It was also the first time she had ever used it.

The seed could take root inside a living body.

The Lizard King felt nothing at first. It kept thrashing against the vines, contempt in its eyes, unaware that anything was wrong.

Two breaths later, the mountain of a beast froze.

It shuddered violently and whipped its head from side to side, a low, panicked groan rumbling from its throat. Beneath its hard skin, countless living things were taking root and splitting through it, and the pain tore through every bone.

Its armor was perfect and untouched. Inside, its flesh, veins, and organs had already been overrun.

A strange pulse of life burst out from within.

Under the swollen black hide, bumps pushed up. Shoots spread. Buds formed.

Then roses bloomed. Blood-red, glossy, unnatural roses forced their way out from between the scales, from the flesh, from the eye sockets, from the nose and mouth.

Red petals soaked in warm blood. Roots wound around its bones and pierced its organs. Countless flowers covered the black body until the Lizard King was a single strange, beautiful tree of blossoms.

It was lovely and horrifying at once.

Elijah's pupils shrank to pinpoints. His whole body went rigid, shock and disbelief in his eyes.

Cole stood slack-jawed, his pain and exhaustion wiped out by the sight. His mind went blank.

They had seen Affinity control, firestorms, and psychic crushes. They had never seen anything like this.

She didn't break the armor. She didn't touch the flesh. She planted herself inside her enemy and let flowers eat its body and ruin it from the core.

When the last rose finished blooming, the cold light in Paisley's eyes sharpened. She snapped her fingers.

"Burst."

A thunderous explosion shook the whole mall.

Blood-red petals detonated inside the giant body. The unbreakable scales cracked, shattered, and flew apart under the force from within.

The Level Seven Lizard King, immune to blades and fire, was torn to pieces in an instant.

Shredded flesh, broken scales, and fallen red petals filled the air and drifted to the floor. One second invincible, the next ash.

It was gorgeous and gruesome and utterly strange.

With the Lizard King dead, the swarm of black-scale lizards lost its control and its life force all at once.

The head was gone, and the horde fell apart.

Paisley picked up the Lizard King's crystal core and stored it in her Storage Dimension. She didn't pause. She raised her hand again, and the remaining seeds spread out with her Plant Affinity.

Every ordinary lizard on the floor had been seeded too. A moment later, flowers burst from all of them.

Explosions rolled one after another. Beasts dropped in heaps, dead.

In a few breaths, every black-scale lizard in the corridor was gone.
