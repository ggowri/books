Chapter 350

******

A flicker of weary regret crossed Gerald's face as he explained, his tone heavy.

"Your cousin took up with a boyfriend from a side branch of the Quinn family and threw her lot in with them completely. Once the outbreak hit and everyone panicked, she kept pushing your uncle, telling him the Quinns were a far safer bet than the Blackwoods."

"The real turning point was when Mr. Blackwood was poisoned and confined to his bed. Someone leaked that news deliberately. Every faction in the city started assuming the Blackwoods were finished, and most of the families that used to follow them jumped ship to the Quinns or the Lennoxes."

The rumors of the Blackwoods' collapse were exactly why her uncle's family had cut ties and gone over to the Quinns.

Orion's expression steadied, and he spoke to reassure him.

"Don't worry, Dad. Elijah's back safe, and with him at the helm, the Blackwoods aren't going anywhere. Things are going to turn around from here."

He'd grown up alongside Elijah, and this whole journey had shown him firsthand just how sharp, capable, and steady he was — hardly someone content to stay small. Whatever trouble the Blackwoods were in, it wouldn't last long.

After a moment's thought, Orion made a suggestion.

"Why don't we host a dinner tonight? Formally invite Mr. Blackwood and the whole family over."

"It'd thank them for everything Elijah's done for us and for Pais, and it'd give us a chance to talk through where the Thompsons stand going forward, get a clear read on our situation, and settle what needs doing first."

Her father gestured at the stack of files on his desk.

"These are the latest base regulations, faction assignments, and resource allocations. Go through them first and sort out what needs doing."

"I'll call Mr. Blackwood now to set up the dinner, and let your mother know so she can start on the food."

Now that they were home, everything was about to change.

Meanwhile, in another standalone villa in the core district, something darker was stirring.

Rachel hadn't expected her own parents to be living this well at the base either.

The moment she stepped through the door, every pretense dropped, and she threw herself into her mother's arms, sobbing out every hardship and injustice of the whole journey.

She painted the whole ordeal as unbearable, burying every bit of her own scheming and selfishness, playing up only her own suffering, laying every misfortune at Paisley's feet.

Reid and Ryan stood off to the side, grim and silent, dark resentment simmering behind their eyes.

In a bedroom off the living room, Owen, the eldest brother they'd brought back, remained deep in his coma, his breath faint.

In their telling, the truth flipped entirely.

Paisley was selfish, cold, refusing to do a single thing for them, insisting on cutting ties entirely.

Every hardship they'd endured, even Owen's coma, somehow became her fault.

None of the three siblings looked inward at all. They'd settled on one story — blame Paisley for everything.

Twisted thinking and petty self-interest spread through the villa unchecked, all of it rooted in buried resentment toward her.

Rachel kept sobbing into her mother's arms, shoulders shaking, her voice bitter and broken.

"Mom, now that Paisley's found her real parents and climbed into the Thompson family, she doesn't think twice about any of us anymore."

"She's living it up while we get nothing, not even the scraps.

We nearly died out there — zombies, ambushes, every kind of danger. If we weren't just plain lucky, we'd never have made it back to see you again!"

Every word of it twisted the truth, casting herself as the victim, smearing Paisley's name.

Her mother's whole face darkened with hatred as she listened, her voice turning sharp and cruel.

"I knew it. An ungrateful wretch, through and through.

We took her in out of kindness, gave her a roof over her head, and this is what we get — raised ourselves an ingrate.

If I'd known she'd turn out this selfish, I should've smothered her the day we took her in."

She patted Rachel's back gently, her tone turning suddenly bold.

"Don't be scared, sweetheart. You don't have to fear anyone.

The main Lennox family is one of the three top powers in this whole base, real power behind it. With them backing us, there's nothing for you to worry about."
