Chapter 434

If not for her, I'd be dead.

After a day like this, he finally understood something. He'd always assumed there was plenty of time, but some things had to be said early. Some people don't wait forever.

He opened his mouth to tell her how he felt, and then hurried footsteps, the click of weapons, and surging Affinity pulses came from outside the warehouse.

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, Madison, and Mason had finished clearing the zombies outside. Sensing that something was off in the warehouse, they'd rushed over at full speed.

They burst through the doors and saw the wreckage. Cracked floor, rubble everywhere, thin wisps of black poison mist still hanging in the air, the smell of blood and rot. Dried blood, and equipment torn apart by brute force.

Elijah was just sitting up, his chest soaked dark red. Healed or not, he looked a mess, nothing like his usual neat self.

Paisley's face was white, and she was wiping sweat from her forehead.

One look told everyone that a vicious fight had just happened here.

They hurried over, anxious.

Orion's eyes landed on Elijah's bloodied shirt, and he frowned. "You're hurt? How bad is it?"

Thomas scanned the scene, then turned to Paisley and asked gently, "Pais, are you okay? That's a lot of blood. What did you run into?"

Ashley rushed up, her soft voice shaky. "Paisley, are you guys hurt? What happened?"

Everyone's eyes were on Paisley and Elijah.

Paisley glanced around at their tense faces and told them, calm but heavy, what had happened.

"We ran into a Level Seven artificial mutant. Human head, snake body. It wasn't born from the apocalypse. A lab spliced it together."

She paused and stressed the key danger.

"It heals absurdly fast. Normal attacks can't kill it. You have to smash its crystal core. It's also fully conscious, smart, cruel, and cunning, and it carries deadly venom and mental contamination. It got away in the middle of the fight."

"It's probably hiding somewhere around these ruins, and it'll almost certainly come back. Everyone stay alert. Don't wander into unfamiliar areas, or you might step into its territory."

"It's terrifyingly strong. Ordinary people can't touch it. Nobody goes off alone, and nobody gets lucky."

The mood in the warehouse sank.

Ashley got goosebumps. She glanced at the dark corners as if the snake might lunge out any second. Her face went pale, and she pressed against Paisley's side, clutching her sleeve. Her voice shook.

"No way... Even Captain Blackwood got wrecked? That thing is insane! Paisley, I'm scared."

Paisley looked down at the timid girl, and her cool expression softened. She patted her head.

"Don't be scared. I'm here. I'll protect you."

Thomas stepped up right away and clutched his chest in mock terror, grinning. "Pais, I'm a scaredy-cat too. I'm terrified of snakes. Protect me too!"

Spencer wasn't about to be left out. "And me, Pais! The best-looking guy on the squad needs a personal bodyguard. Can't let some monster mess up this face!"

The two clowns lightened the mood a little.

Orion rubbed his forehead, half laughing. "Seriously? Things are this dangerous and you two are fighting for attention?"
