Chapter 22

Someone kicked in the mall's heavy glass doors, and shards of glass sprayed across the floor.

A big group of people stormed in like they owned the place.

The young man in front had a cocky swagger and a cruel, shifty look in his eyes, with a smirk on his face. He twirled a black pistol in one hand, carelessly sweeping its barrel across everyone in the room. He was obviously bad news.

"Well, well, small world," he sneered, his eyes locked on Elijah. "Never thought I'd run into you here."

He had a dozen or so lackeys behind him, all of them hanging on his every word.

He was Jasper Quinn, the younger son of the Quinns, one of the Four Great Families of Aetheris. Back in Aetheris, everyone knew him as a spoiled, depraved playboy who did nothing but cause trouble.

He'd always used his family name to get away with anything, and he'd ruined the lives of countless women.

He held grudges like nobody else, and he especially hated straight-arrow guys like Elijah, who actually lived by their principles.

Before the apocalypse, Jasper had pushed his luck one time too many and gotten into serious trouble. Elijah was the one who'd shipped him off to Southspire as punishment, and Jasper had hated him for it ever since.

But no one had seen the apocalypse coming. Overnight, law and order fell apart, and every old rule went out the window.

If anything, it was a gift to a ruthless, reckless guy like Jasper. Nothing could hold him back now, and in a world this lawless, he could do whatever he wanted.

The moment Paisley saw his face, all the warmth drained out of her eyes, leaving nothing but ice.

She knew him all too well.

In her past life, Jasper's older brother, Damon Quinn, had risen to power in the later years of the apocalypse. He'd built a massive base in the south and become one of the biggest names around.

With his brother behind him, Jasper had only gotten worse. He was rotten to the core.

In those three years, Jasper had used his power to prey on countless innocent women. He had blood on his hands and more crimes to his name than anyone could count.

In her past life, the three Lennox brothers had sucked up to whoever was on top and joined Damon's camp to secure their place in the chaos.

To get ahead, they hadn't even hesitated to sell her out. They'd tried to hand her over to that pervert Jasper like a gift.

She'd fought back with everything she had and refused to give in, even if it killed her. Looking back now, that was probably a big part of why they'd shoved her into that horde of zombies in the end.

All her old hatred came flooding back at once, and a bone-deep chill spread through her body.

Paisley's eyes went icy as she looked past Jasper. Sure enough, she spotted a few familiar faces.

The four Lennox siblings were standing right there among his followers.

So this time, they'd latched onto Jasper even earlier.

Rachel spotted Paisley right away, safe in the middle of Elijah's team. Shock flashed across her face, then something vicious.

She'd been looking for Paisley all morning, trying to figure out how to get her hands on that pendant.

She'd searched high and low, and now Paisley had just dropped right into her lap, here of all places.

Rachel quickly hid the greed and malice on her face. She already had a plan.

Being reborn gave her one huge advantage. She knew what was coming.

She knew better than anyone that the pendant Paisley wore was priceless.

Once she got her hands on it, she could unlock the Storage Dimension and awaken the rare Plant Affinity. That came with healing powers and a gift for growing plants.

In the apocalypse, healing and growing food were two of the most valuable gifts a person could have. She could save lives and win people over, and she could grow vegetables and crops to stock up on supplies.

If she showed Jasper how valuable she was, he'd definitely take notice. Then, she could coast along until the world got back on its feet.

Rachel took a deep breath and forced down her greed.

When she looked up again, her eyes were brimming with fragile, innocent tears. She took a few timid steps forward, playing the poor girl who'd been through hell and missed her family.
