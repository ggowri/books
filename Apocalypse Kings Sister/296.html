Chapter 296

If this had happened before, she probably would have considered saving something this valuable for someone she loved most.

But now, there was no need to think it over.

Use the Affinity Awakening Pill immediately.

A rich, warm medicinal power dissolved instantly in her throat, spreading through her limbs, gently washing through every channel and her core.

Trovie's chime rang out.

Pill fully absorbed! Host is free to choose any Affinity to awaken!

Paisley's gaze sharpened, and she locked onto her target instantly — Lightning Affinity.

The next instant, a burst of blue lightning cracked to life deep in her core.

Crackling bolts of lightning spread across her body instantly, fine, sharp sparks dancing across her skin, the fierce, commanding weight of Lightning Affinity sweeping through the entire space, its presence shaking the air.

The new ability took shape fast, rooting itself deep within her core.

Congratulations, Host, on successfully awakening — mutant Lightning Affinity!

Trovie's voice came through full of shock.

That's one of the rarest, most elite mutant lightning affinities out there! Top-tier powerhouse material! Explosive power, corruption-breaking strength, battlefield control, instant-kill potential, all maxed out! Absolutely top-tier against zombies, dark mutants, and shadow affinities alike!

Paisley raised a finger, and a thread of blue lightning leapt out, crackling sharp and fast, instantly shattering a floating piece of rubble in the space into dust.

She stared at her fingertip, hardly believing it.

The power was staggering.

She now held five Affinities — Ice-Water, Plant, Fire, Psychic, and Lightning, working together, her overall strength now firmly among the absolute peak of this world, unmatched by anyone.

After a stretch of back-to-back leveling, draws, trades, and a brand-new Affinity awakening, her mind and energy had both been pushed hard, and now that the tension finally eased, exhaustion crashed over her all at once.

She stepped out of the storage space, returned to her room, lay down on the bed, closed her eyes, and drifted off to sleep.

Outside, the night sat deep and quiet, the courtyard lights soft in the dark.

Beside her, the little red fox curled up at her side, quietly watching over its master.

A dreamless, peaceful night.

...

The next morning, dawn broke, soft light spilling through the window into the quiet room, gently chasing away the last of the night.

The room sat calm and still, nothing but the soft rhythm of breathing.

Paisley woke to a warm, soft weight pressed against her palm.

She slowly opened her eyes, still heavy with sleep, lingering warmth from the morning lull.

The moment she moved, she felt the small, warm body nestled against her hand.

The little red fox, who'd clung to her so anxiously the night before, had completely let its guard down, sprawled out in her palm, its little belly rising and falling evenly, sleeping soundly and peacefully, its red fur catching a warm gold glow in the morning light.

Meanwhile, in the corner of the storage space, the little white cat still sat curled up and listless, knowing it had done something wrong, too ashamed to come out.

Paisley stroked the fox's smooth fur gently, feeling the full, harmonious power of her four Affinities settled within her, solid and grounding.

A night's rest had left her refreshed and sharp.

******

From down the quiet villa hallway came a soft knock, and Ashley's bright, cheerful voice carried through the door, full of youthful energy.

"Pais, breakfast's ready! We're leaving for Stillwater Base as soon as we're done eating!"

On the bed, Paisley's lashes fluttered, and she slowly came back to herself, answering softly.

"Got it. Coming right down."

She sat up, dressed quickly, washed up, and scooped up the freshly-woken fox before heading downstairs with Ashley.

Downstairs, the living room was warm and inviting, the smell of breakfast drifting through the air.

Ashley bounced along beside her the whole way down, eyes sparkling, clearly fishing for praise.

"Pais, my Earth Affinity just hit the peak of Level Five! I feel like I've got so much energy now!"

Paisley looked at the girl's eager, hopeful expression, warmth spreading in her eyes, and gave her an enthusiastic thumbs-up.

"Great work, Ashley. You're improving so fast. Already really impressive."

Beside her, Thomas smiled too, his eyes crinkling with pride.

"Pais, my Plant Affinity hit the peak of Level Five too. Just need one more push to break through to Level Six."

Spencer chimed in right after, his tone light.

"Pais, my Fire Affinity's at the peak of Level Five as well. My strength's really come up."

Paisley praised each of them in turn.
