Chapter 345

"Mr. Thompson, Mr. Coleman, Ms. Hughes, Ms. Walker — welcome back, all of you. If you don't mind, I'll see you home myself."

"Since the outbreak, survivors from everywhere have gathered at the Aetheris base. It's the most secure stronghold in the city."

His gaze landed on the unfamiliar Marcus, a flicker of confusion crossing his face.

"And you are?"

Marcus answered calmly, introducing himself.

"I traveled with them. I'm heading to the Aetheris base too."

Elijah nodded, unconcerned.

"In that case, let's all head back together."

They all agreed, climbing into the cars in order.

Elijah pulled Paisley directly into his own car, the two of them riding together.

Vance caught the whole scene, astonishment and curiosity written across his face, though he didn't dare say a word.

Everyone in Aetheris knew the young Blackwood heir as cold and distant, never letting a woman near him, let alone sharing his private car with one.

And yet this girl, a face he didn't recognize, clearly not part of any of Aetheris's prominent families, had earned exactly that treatment. He couldn't help wondering who she really was.

The convoy pulled out, leaving the airfield in orderly procession.

Chase, Rachel, and the Lennox siblings followed in a separate convoy behind them, heading for the same base.

Half an hour later, the convoy arrived at the main gate of the Aetheris base.

Security at the gate was tight, lined with the most advanced detection equipment available, able to precisely screen out anyone carrying the zombie virus.

Vance's presence alone announced the Blackwood name, and with it, status and power.

People stepped aside at once to clear the way.

The gate guards saluted the moment they recognized him.

Once they recognized Elijah too, they became even more deferential handling the paperwork.

"That's him? The Blackwood one?"

People only dared whisper about it behind his back.

They proceeded one by one through registration, screening, and filing into the base's records.

Out of every hundred people, at most one awakened an Affinity.

When it was Paisley's turn, her panel displayed the data: Level Six Plant Affinity.

Right after her, Orion, Elijah, and Ashley went through registration too — Fire, Lightning, Metal, Earth, all of them Level Six!

The staff processing the registrations stared at the numbers flashing across their screens, eyes wide with disbelief, their minds reeling.

Every single one of them was a high-level user.

******

In a base this size, even a Level Five was a rare, prized asset that every faction wanted to recruit. Level Six was rarer still — the absolute top tier.

No one had expected this handful of young people to turn out like this — every single one of them Level Six.

Were they here to upend the whole base?

Staff and users alike in line turned to stare, murmuring in shock. Everyone understood — Aetheris's power structure was about to be reshuffled.

While the hall was still buzzing, every eye on Paisley's group, a figure broke through the crowd, rushing forward and pulling Paisley into a crushing, desperate embrace.

The hold was warm and fierce, the kind only family gave.

The woman trembled all over, years of longing finally breaking loose, tears soaking into Paisley's shoulder, her voice cracking with emotion.

"My Pais... my baby girl... Mom finally found you. My precious girl, after all these years..."

The sudden embrace, the raw emotion behind it, froze Paisley solid.

Across two lifetimes, no one had ever held her this openly, this completely.

The weight of it — all that longing, all that love — left her stiff and lost, not even sure how to breathe right, let alone respond.

Is this what a mother's arms feel like?

Back at the Lennoxes', watching Rachel's mother dote on her, she used to feel a quiet envy.

Because no one had ever loved her like that — unconditionally, with everything they had.

That warmth had taken years to arrive, but here it finally was.
