Chapter 342

The little fox, curled obediently at her feet and soaking up the leftover energy the whole time, had grown even glossier and fluffier.

The instant Paisley broke through, red light flared around the fox too. Its own power surged and settled at mid-Level Seven, matching its master step for step.

Just then, Trovie's chipper little voice chimed in.

[Host, the fourth trader reached out first! Connect the cross-dimensional link now?]

Paisley's eyes opened, a flicker of interest in them.

[Connect.]

An instant later, light rippled in the air and a clear projection formed before her.

On the other end stood a sharply dressed, impeccably put-together woman, three enormous warehouses stretching behind her, their doors thrown open to reveal mountains of supplies — food, medicine, clothing, equipment, stacked floor to ceiling.

She spoke crisply, no preamble.

[Three full warehouses — food, daily necessities, medicine, emergency supplies. Worth five billion. Ready to trade whenever you are.]

Something lit up in Paisley's eyes, a rush of satisfaction warming her chest.

Leave it to someone from the twenty-first century to hustle this hard.

Just three days, and she'd already pulled together this much. Impressive.

With this haul, she wouldn't have to worry about supplies for a long time.

Paisley sealed the deal without hesitation.

[Deal.]

Then she noticed the bruise on the woman's face and asked, out of nowhere:

[Can I ask — what happened to your face? Looks like someone hit you.]

[Is someone giving you trouble?]

******

The woman sighed, her voice weary.

[I've been moving too much cash buying up supplies lately, and it caught the wrong attention. Got jumped and beaten on the way home. Money just draws thieves out of the woodwork. I called the police, but they never found a lead.]

A flicker of longing crossed her face.

[I wish I could get strong somehow. Stop being an easy target. Protect myself for once.]

Paisley raised an eyebrow, curious.

[Doesn't your system shop carry anything that strengthens the body? Pills, tonics, that kind of thing?]

Her own shop stocked everything like that — just pricey.

The woman looked blank and shook her head.

[Nothing like that. My system is just for stockpiling, liquidating, and matching trades. It's a path to financial freedom, not physical strength — nothing for training or Affinities at all.]

Paisley understood at once.

Every cross-dimensional trader's system worked completely differently — different permissions, different categories entirely.

Each system ran its own lane, with its own strengths and its own blind spots.

An idea struck her, and she seized the opening, offering up a bargaining chip of her own.

[I happen to have some spirit water, infused with energy. Drink it long enough and it strengthens the body, rebuilds you from the ground up. With luck, it might even awaken an Affinity.]

[Want to make a trade?]

The woman's eyes went wide instantly, disbelief and excitement flooding her face.

[Yes! I want it! Something that actually works like that really exists?!]

[What do you need in exchange? Supplies? Name any amount!]

Paisley didn't hold back, going straight for a bold ask.

[Put together another full set of supplies, the same scale as before, and we'll trade that for the spirit water.]

Silence stretched on the other end.

Five billion in goods was no small figure. Matching that again would be a serious strain.

After a long moment, the woman set her jaw and nodded firmly.

[Fine. Deal.]

[I'll need a week to pull it together, but I'll deliver on time. I won't go back on this, I promise.]

A faint smile crossed Paisley's face, pleased by how readily she'd agreed.

She transferred the remaining forty billion yuan on the spot, along with five bottles of especially pure spirit water, rich with energy.

[Here's the rest of the payment, settled in full. Try the spirit water first — see the effect for yourself before you decide whether the trade's worth it.]

The woman stared at the cash and the five bottles that had materialized out of thin air on the warehouse floor, astonished.

[You trust me that much? Not worried I'll just drink it and go back on the deal?]
