Chapter 379

"You know exactly how ordinary people survive in this apocalypse."

The thought of the lives she'd seen other ordinary people living drained the color from Brenda's face completely, every trace of her stubborn pride gone in an instant.

Before the apocalypse, she'd grown used to a life of comfort and respect, she couldn't possibly accept going back to the bottom, to grinding, exhausting labor.

Dennis sat in silence for a long while, his expression a tangle of complicated feelings, before finally letting out a heavy sigh.

He had to admit it, his daughter was speaking nothing but cold, hard truth.

In the apocalypse, power, resources, and backing were the only things that mattered for survival. So-called pride wasn't worth a thing.

"So we should go pay them a visit and congratulate them ourselves."

Chloe's gaze was steady now, the plan fully set in her mind.

On the surface, she'd play humble. Underneath, her calculations were running at full speed, scheme after scheme.

"If I can get close to Paisley, get close to Falcon Squad, I'll have a shot at getting close to Elijah."

"And if I can land Elijah, good days are coming for all of us, even better than before."

The warm yellow light spread across the table, falling on Chloe's beautiful but cold, indifferent face, sketching out a portrait of pure calculation, every trace of ambition laid bare.

Dennis, fully convinced by his daughter, let go of the old grudges without another thought. He picked up his phone, planning to call Paisley's biological father directly, to extend an olive branch and request a visit.

The screen lit up. The dial pad appeared. A second later, a cold, automated system message played.

[The number you have dialed has been blocked by the recipient. Call failed.]

Dennis's face froze solid. His fingers tightened around the phone, and he tried twice more, disbelieving, only to get the same result every time.

His stomach dropped. He said, voice low and hoarse. "We've been blocked."

Brenda quickly grabbed her own phone to try. The screen refreshed, same blocked message.

Still unwilling to accept it, Chloe pulled up her own contacts and dialed. Same cold, automatic failure.

Not just Dennis, every number belonging to all three of them had been blocked by Paisley's father. Every channel of contact, completely severed.

In an instant, the air in the villa went rigid.

The three of them, who moments ago had been full of schemes and certainty, now wore matching expressions of pure dismay.

Awkwardness and humiliation piled up, one on top of the other, a bucket of cold water thrown over every plan and hope they'd had.

Only now, belatedly, did it occur to them, all those years leaning on the Quinn family's power, looking down on and mocking the younger branch without a second thought, hadn't gone unnoticed. It had all been remembered.

Now, they had no connection left at all.

The first step toward making amends, toward gratitude and reconciliation, had been slammed shut before it even began.

What were they supposed to do now?

Meanwhile, in another top-tier standalone villa district at the base, the main Lennox family's residence blazed with light, its décor elegant and opulent, a world apart from the Thompson household's mood.

The Lennox family's core relatives had all gathered in the living room for an emergency family meeting.

Every topic of conversation, from start to finish, circled back to the same person: Paisley, the branch family's foster daughter.

The living room sat in dead silence, every face carrying shock and dread.

No one had expected that this daughter, abandoned to live out in the world, raised by an ordinary family, once ignored by everyone, would turn out to be this powerful.

A Level Six fighter, crushing a Level Seven mutant flower bare-handed, not even using her Affinity, killing a high-tier mutant creature with sword skill alone, singlehandedly turning the entire fight around and carrying Falcon Squad to legendary status, becoming the single strongest Affinity user among the base's new generation.

This kind of talent, this kind of combat strength, no one among the base's younger generation could match it. Across the entire apocalypse, there probably weren't more than a handful who could.

At the head of the room, the Lennox family patriarch sat with a grave expression, his gaze sweeping slowly over everyone present before he broke the silence, his voice solemn.

"We were careless before. We never paid attention to her growth, and we underestimated her talent and ability."

"Now that she's shown her true strength, she's the single most powerful force among the base's new generation, with limitless potential ahead of her."

"Right now, every resource, every connection, every bit of momentum at this base is flowing toward her and Falcon Squad. And she has several brothers behind her who guard her fiercely. Whoever manages to win her over will hold the absolute advantage in this base's future power structure."

Everyone nodded along, their eyes full of certainty and greed.

Paisley's value now was something no ordinary prodigy could match. On her own, she was worth half the weight of a top-tier family's entire legacy.
