Chapter 205

“Left, right teams—get to the center, NOW!”

The panic in his voice came through even over the static.

Elijah and Orion's faces went cold the instant they heard it.

A pack of idiots who'd walked themselves into their own grave, and now the whole team had to bail them out.

Chase's group hadn't come here to do a job. They'd come here to get everyone killed.

The three of them traded a quick glance.

Elijah spoke first, his voice heavy.

“What do you two think?”

Orion didn't answer right away. He turned to Paisley instead.

“Paisley, what do you think?”

Both sets of eyes settled on her at once.

She looked up.

Honestly, she had zero interest in saving them.

She'd be happy to see Rachel and Chase die right there.

They'd earned every bit of it themselves.

But she couldn't ignore what it would mean for Orion and Elijah.

A team had its own rules, and she wasn't about to let her own grudge saddle them with a reputation for letting people die.

She said it, half resigned.

“Why's everyone looking at me? Like I'm going to say let them die and that's that?”

She tilted her head slightly, handing the decision off.

“Captain Blackwood should call it.”

Elijah's voice came low.

“The mission matters. People matter more.”

“We have the strength to help. We're not leaving them.”

“That's five lives. They're part of this team, whatever else they are. We don't just let them die.”

Orion went quiet for a moment, something shifting behind his cool eyes, then nodded slowly.

“Fine. Your call.”

Every eye came back to her again.

She let the irritation go, her voice flat.

“Fine. Save them first.”

With that, the three of them changed course without hesitation, picking up speed toward the central danger zone.

The closer they got, the thicker the reek of foul water grew, the shrieks and crashes and screams sharpening in their ears.

In the dark passage, water splashed everywhere, pooling past their ankles. Mutant fish-beasts crowded in layers, blood-red eyes fixed on the five trapped people, slamming in to bite and tear.

Chase's barrier was already failing, cracked through, shedding bits of itself with every hit. Sweat soaked his forehead, his face bloodless, breath ragged—his power was nearly spent.

The others were wounded, their Affinity drained dry, huddled inside what was left of the shield.

Rachel had completely fallen apart, legs useless, hunched in the middle of the group with both hands clamped over her ears, sobbing so hard her whole body shook. Not a trace left of the gentle, clever front she'd put on.

Just as the barrier was about to shatter completely, three figures appeared at the mouth of the passage.

Paisley stood at the front, her gaze cool, green light flaring at her fingertips as thick vines burst from the ground, spreading fast across the flooded floor.

They wove together in an instant, a massive net catching every mutant that lunged.

Elijah and Orion raised their guns together, blue light humming as their fingers hit the triggers.

Two sharp bolts of blue tore through the air, punching clean through the skulls of the two lead Level Six mutants.

Two muffled cracks, and the high-tier mutants dropped dead, their bodies slamming into the water, sending up a wall of spray.

Their arrival turned the whole fight around at once.

The central team's nerves, stretched to the breaking point, finally slackened. Relief swept through them, and someone called out, voice cracking with joy.

“We're saved! We're actually saved!”

Blue light kept tearing through the water, and the Level Five mutants had no answer for it, bodies floating up pale and dead one after another.

If they'd known these guns were this good, they'd have bought one to protect themselves from the start.

With one of these, they'd never have come this close to dying.

Captain Blackwood hadn't been exaggerating about these guns at all.

They really were that good.

A Level Five mutant sea beast, and one shot took its head clean off.

******

That alone told them everything about what these guns could do.

No question—they needed to buy one of these the first chance they got.

Paisley's vines crisscrossed the room, sealing off every angle of attack, binding the remaining mutants fast until not one of them could move.
