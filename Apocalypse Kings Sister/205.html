Chapter 205

"Left team, right team, get to the middle and back us up, now!" Chase shouted. Even over the comm channel, the panic in his voice was impossible to miss.

Elijah and Orion's expressions turned ice cold as they listened.

What a bunch of idiots. They'd pointed everyone the wrong way and walked right into a death trap, and now the whole team had to go save their asses.

Chase and his people hadn't come to do the mission at all. They'd only come to get themselves killed and drag everyone else down with them.

The three of them exchanged a quick look.

Elijah spoke first, his voice heavy. "What do you two think?"

Orion didn't answer right away. He turned to Paisley instead. "Paisley, what do you think?"

Both of them waited for her answer, and she glanced up.

Deep down, she didn't want to save them at all. She'd love nothing more than for Rachel and Chase to die in there. They'd brought it all on themselves.

But she had to think about Orion and Elijah.

A team had its rules, and she couldn't let her personal grudge give them a reputation for being cold enough to let people die.

Paisley sighed, a little helpless. "Why are you all looking at me? If I say we don't save them, are we really just going to leave them to die?"

She tilted her head slightly, handing the decision over. "Captain Blackwood should make the call."

Elijah's voice was low and steady. "The mission's important, but saving people matters more.

"We've got the power to help them, so we can't abandon them. Those are five lives, and they're on our team. We're not leaving them to die."

Orion was silent for a couple of seconds, then slowly nodded, his cool expression softening a little. "Fine. Your call."

All eyes were back on Paisley.

She pushed down her irritation and said flatly, "Fine. We save them first."

Without another word, the three of them changed course and raced toward the danger zone in the middle.

The closer they got, the stronger the fishy stench of seawater became, and the howls, crashes, and screams grew louder and sharper.

Water splashed everywhere in the dark passage, and it was ankle-deep on the floor. Black mutant fish-beasts were packed in layers, their blood-red eyes locked on the five trapped people as they rammed and tore at them like crazy.

Chase's psychic barrier was about to give out. Cracks covered every inch of it, and tiny bits of psychic energy kept crumbling away. His forehead was covered in cold sweat, his face was ghost white, and he was gasping for breath, clearly almost out of psychic power.

The other team members were all hurt and out of power, and all they could do was huddle inside the barrier.

Rachel was a complete wreck. Her legs had gone weak, and she hid in the middle of the group with both hands clamped over her ears, sobbing so hard she shook all over. Where was the sweet, clever girl from before?

Just as the barrier was about to shatter, three tall figures suddenly appeared at the entrance to the passage.

Paisley stood at the front, her eyes cool. Green light surged at her fingertips, and countless thick, tough vines burst out of the ground and spread wildly across the flooded floor.

The vines slipped and wove between each other, and within seconds, they formed a huge net that stopped the charging mutant beasts in their tracks.

Elijah and Orion raised their hands at the same time. Their energy guns glowed blue, and they pulled the triggers.

Two sharp beams of blue light tore through the air and pierced the heads of the two Level Six mutant fish-beasts leading the charge.

With two soft cracks, the high-level beasts died on the spot. Their bodies crashed into the water and sent up huge splashes.

Their arrival turned the whole fight around.

The middle team's nerves had been stretched to the breaking point, and now they finally relaxed as the thrill of surviving swept over them.

"We're saved! We're actually saved!" someone shouted.

Blue beams kept flashing through the passage. The Level Five mutant fish-beasts couldn't stand up to the crushing power of those special weapons, and their dead bodies floated belly-up all over the water.

If the middle team had known how good these weapons were, they would've bought one to protect themselves. With one of those guns, they never would've come so close to dying.

Elijah hadn't been exaggerating at all. These were Level Five mutant sea beasts, and one shot was enough to blow their heads off.

That showed just how powerful the energy guns really were. That settles it. I'm buying one the first chance I get!

Paisley's vines crisscrossed the passage and locked down every line of attack. They tied up the rest of the fish-beasts and pinned every one of those Level Five mutant sea beasts in place.
