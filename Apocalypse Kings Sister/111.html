Chapter 111

The 16-foot white cat padded along behind her, light on its feet. Every time a zombie dropped, it hurried over, neatly plucked out the crystal core, and tucked it away.

Girl, cat, and sword worked together perfectly, clearing the way at an incredible speed.

The front doors of the office building were shut but not locked. One light push, and they swung open.

Paisley glanced around, walked over to an elevator that was still working, and pressed the button.

The doors slid open with no trouble, and she pressed the button for the top floor, the 28th.

The elevator rose steadily and took her straight up to the executive offices.

The 28th floor was the building's top executive floor. It was beautifully decorated, with rows of private offices and big windows that let in tons of light.

There was only a thin layer of dust on the floor, and every desk, chair, file, and piece of furniture was still in perfect shape.

Paisley swept all of it into her Storage Dimension.

It would come in handy when it was time to rebuild, and she could trade it for contribution points later.

As she went deeper in, a plant hiding in a dark corner suddenly lashed out at her. Its vines reeked of damp, rotten greenery!

It was a Level Four mutant pothos!

Thick, tangled green vines shot out wildly in every direction and wrapped toward Paisley, fast and vicious.

Paisley actually smiled a little.

Out of all the crystal cores out there, Plant Affinity and Psychic Affinity cores were the rarest and the ones she wanted most.

She didn't even flinch. With a quick flick of her wrist, she drew the Frostbloom Sword, and a burst of icy energy exploded out of it.

The blade flashed back and forth, sharp and precise. In the blink of an eye, it cut through every whipping vine. They withered and fell to the floor in pieces.

Paisley reached into the center of the plant's roots and pulled out a clear, emerald-green Level Four Plant Affinity crystal core.

When she held it, her Plant Affinity stirred a little, and a thin thread of warmth ran through her. But it still wasn't quite enough to push her through to Level Six.

Paisley raised an eyebrow, but she wasn't worried.

She kept following Trovie's directions deeper into the floor until she reached the office at the very end. It belonged to the chairman.

It was huge and grand, with a classy layout and seriously luxurious furniture.

Trovie's excited voice chimed in her head.

[Master! Go in! There's a huge private lounge hidden in this chairman's office!

[And inside the lounge, there's a giant safe packed with guns, ammo, stacks of cash, and solid gold bars. It's all good stuff!]

Paisley's eyes gleamed, and she pushed straight through the heavy wooden door.

The office was even bigger and fancier on the inside. There was a high-end wooden desk, a leather couch, a classy coffee table, and floor-to-ceiling windows that looked out over the whole city.

This chairman sure knew how to live it up.

Except for a thin layer of dust, the room was spotless. There was barely any sign of the apocalypse, and every piece of furniture was still in perfect shape.

Paisley quickly scanned the room, and her eyes landed on a hidden soundproof door in the wall. It was painted the exact same color as the wall, so anyone who wasn't looking closely would never notice it.

She walked over and gave it a light push. The hidden door swung open, revealing a huge, comfy private lounge.

It had a luxury bed, a private bathroom, a little bar, and storage cabinets. It was obviously a private hideaway for the bigwigs.

In one corner against the wall sat a heavy black metal safe with a combination lock. It was thick, solid, and built to keep thieves out.

[That's it! I can smell the gold! Master, that safe is full of treasure!]

Trovie bounced with excitement and cracked the old digital lock all on its own.

The latch popped open with a soft click.

Paisley pulled out a pistol and shot the lock anyway, and it shattered.

She reached out and pulled the safe open, and her eyes lit up at the gold, silver, and jewels inside.

The top row held dozens of solid, pure gold bars, stacked neatly. Every one of them gleamed and looked heavy, practically screaming money.

Next to them were thick stacks of brand-new cash, still sealed and bundled up. Cash was useless in the apocalypse, but it was great for trading in the Trading Plaza.

Next to that were piles of sparkling gemstones in every color imaginable, every one of them top quality.

Trovie let out an excited squeal.

[Ahhh! Master, we're rich! With all this gold and these jewels, I promise you're going to make Level Three trader this time!]
