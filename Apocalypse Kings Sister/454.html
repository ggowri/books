Chapter 454

A moment later the lizard's claws slammed into the wall.

With a deafening crash, half the earthen wall exploded!

The shock was savage. Ashley reeled back, her blood churning, her hands numb. The blast scraped her arm, and fine beads of blood welled up.

"Watch your back!"

Thomas shouted, his Plant Affinity surging. Several thick vines tore out of the floor and snared the limbs of the two lizards circling behind.

But the beasts had huge strength and scales hard as iron. They strained the vines until they creaked, and cracks spread along them.

The pressure on the back line spiked!

Mason gripped his heavy weapon and took on a flanking lizard head-on, swinging hard enough to knock it back. But another lizard leaped over it, and its claws raked across his shoulder!

Rip!

His combat suit tore. The flesh on his shoulder split open, and thin black lines of poison raced out from the wound, numbing half his body in an instant.

"Poison!"

Mason grunted, and his body locked up.

At that moment, rifle fire rattled out. Elijah fired precise single shots. The bullets pierced the lizards' outer scales but only left flesh wounds, nowhere near a kill.

The mutant black-scale lizards had thick hides and strong resistance. Ordinary firearms could only hold them off for a moment. They couldn't break through.

In the chaos, only Paisley stayed as calm as ever.

She saw their purpose at a glance.

The eight mutant black-scale lizards had clear roles. They were there to pin the squad down. Their goal wasn't a quick kill but to wear them out! Once everyone was split up and out of Affinity, the next wave of attack would finish them.

"Kitty, fox, clear the flanks! Everyone, pull in tight, don't spread out!"

She shot forward as she spoke.

The little white cat's light flared. Its small body burst with extreme power, leaping to slash its claws across a lizard's soft belly scales and tear open a bloody gash.

The golden fox's tails whirled. Sheets of fierce wind blades swept out and forced two circling lizards back, protecting Madison in the rear.

At the same moment, violet flame burst to life in Paisley's palm!

It was the annihilating fire that burned poison and scale alike, and it spread across the room.

Reptiles feared fire above all else.

Violet flame rolled down the aisle, scorching the air with its heat, and wrapped every diving, crawling lizard in fire.

Shrill screams rose all around!

Their proud armor scales and venom-filled bodies charred, cracked, and melted away under the annihilating flame.

While the lizard pack was stiff with pain, Paisley drew the Frostbloom Sword. Cold light flashed in a string of cuts, her afterimages crossing, every stroke aimed at a lizard's neck.

Squelch, squelch, squelch!

The sound of blades cutting flesh came again and again.

The deadly, inescapable encirclement was broken in a few breaths. The lizards' assault was cut off, their formation torn apart.

Elijah seized the one chance and pressed in with his rifle, finishing off wounded beasts. Spencer's fire swept through the survivors. Thomas's vines locked down the ones trying to flee, and Madison shot one in the tail to cut it off.

Even with the tide turned, everyone's backs ran cold, and their hearts raced.

If anyone had hesitated even a moment just now, the whole squad would have been wiped out.

In the silent mall, blood and the stench of beast poison drifted through the air, and the mood was still tight as a wire.

Paisley spread out her psychic sense, and her eyes suddenly changed. Her face went cold.

Her senses cut through floors and walls, and she saw it plainly. Under the ground, on the first floor in the corners, on the second-floor escalator, inside the ceiling, even in the streets outside the mall, packs of mutant black-scale lizards were rushing toward them from every direction!

Not a dozen. Over a hundred!

The eight they'd killed were only the outer sentries, the bait.

This abandoned mall was the core nest of the entire Larkspur lizard colony!

The floor shook faintly. The delicate sound of scales scraping and the rustle of beasts crawling piled up layer upon layer, drawing closer and closer like a tide. It made their scalps crawl.

"We're out of time! Everyone, run!"

Paisley gave the retreat order at once.

"Up! We go up! There are people on the fifth floor. It's safer there!"

No one hesitated. They knew how serious it was. They followed her and sprinted for the inner fire stairs.
