Chapter 401

Just as her legs gave out, someone came rushing toward her.

It was Caleb Brooks, a young man from her squad, one she had helped once.

He hadn't been able to leave Chloe alone. He had followed her from the Security Office to the villa and waited outside, and he had heard every burst of laughter and jeering from inside. Anger had been building in him for hours.

Now he saw her soaked in wine, hollow and red-eyed, and his own eyes stung.

He caught her before she fell, as gently as if she might break.

"Chloe! Are you okay? Hang on, I'm getting you out of here."

He glared up at the glittering villa. "What happened in there? Did they do this to you? I swear, someday I'll make every one of them pay."

He was young, hotheaded, and completely sincere. On a mission once, he had been bleeding out and half dead, and Chloe had burned through her power to save him. To him, she was the one light in this dark world, and he owed her his life.

Seeing her humiliated like this made his chest clench. He wanted to storm in and tear apart the spoiled brats who had mocked her.

Chloe barely lifted her head. Her eyes were hazy and tired, and there was nothing left in them. No tears, no accusations.

"Take me home," she said, her voice hoarse and thin as a breath.

He didn't push. He helped her into the car and buckled her seat belt with careful hands, eyes fixed straight ahead the whole time.

The streetlights slid across her pale face as they pulled away from the villas.

After a long silence, he said, "Captain Grant picked a great time to be unconscious. Couldn't do a thing for you."

"Don't be scared, Chloe. I'll remember everyone who laid a hand on you tonight."

His eyes were steady. "You saved my life. I'll protect you for the rest of mine, and I'll get you your revenge."

He had seen the armed elite guards at the villa gate. Alone, he stood no chance. Charging in would only get him killed and make things worse for her.

So he swallowed his rage and memorized every face, and he waited.

They reached the Thompsons' senior-branch villa fast. He helped her out, waited until she was safely inside, and only then drove off.

Chloe dragged herself through the empty, silent house. The lights were bright, but the place felt dead.

She stumbled into her room.

Closing her eyes in the car had been an act, just a way to hold herself together. The moment the door shut behind her, they snapped open.

The drunkenness had mostly drained away. What remained was exhaustion, humiliation, and despair. Her eyes burned, and tears she had held back all night finally swam to the surface.
