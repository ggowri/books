Chapter 354

"How is this possible?!"

"This whole base, built up over years, has fewer than five registered Level Six users total! These are a dozen kids who went out on one trip, and almost all of them came back Level Six!"

"Are these people even human? That speed, that power — it's not normal, none of it!"

"The Blackwoods already had a solid base, and now they've got this kind of firepower on top of it. Their strength just multiplied several times over. What do we do about this?"

"Elijah especially — he was already a formidable soldier. His presence alone is going to shift things, no question."

...

Panicked, anxious muttering filled the room, every voice tight with dread.

Everyone understood — Elijah's return was the single biggest obstacle standing between the Quinns and their ambition to rule all of Aetheris.

At the head of the table, the Quinn patriarch sat immovable, radiating authority, his expression dark as water, his gaze sweeping the noisy room in total silence, the pressure of his presence almost suffocating.

The chamber gradually fell quiet, no one daring to speak out of turn.

Then one official, dressed in black, his eyes sharp and calculating, rose abruptly, breaking the silence with something dark in his voice.

"Patriarch, I have a plan. We can profit off this without lifting a finger — let it tear the Blackwoods and the Lennoxes apart ourselves."

Every eye in the room snapped to him.

He pressed on.

"Through a private channel, I found something very interesting — the Thompsons' long-lost daughter spent years as an adopted daughter in a branch of the Lennox family."

"Reliable sources say that family treated her terribly, no warmth at all. There's deep, longstanding bad blood between them."

"That's our opening."

A cold, cunning gleam crossed his eyes, his voice certain.

"All we need is to stoke that resentment quietly, drive a wedge between the Blackwoods and the main Lennox family, and let the two of them tear each other apart."

"We stay hidden, keep stirring the pot, and wait for our moment. Let them wear each other down, and we swoop in at the end to absorb both powers whole."

No sooner had he finished than another man stood, his tone fervent and vicious.

"There's more! Every core position at the base's top research facility, every researcher — we've already swapped them all for our own people!"

"Research on a zombie virus cure has already started. Once we bring that cure to market, every voice in this world listens to us, every survivor's life is in our hands. We'll be running the whole show."

His cold, grandiose words settled, and the room fell into total silence.

After a long moment, the patriarch, silent until now, finally lifted his gaze, something bloodthirsty churning in his dark eyes, his voice low and utterly cold.

"It was your idea."

"You're in full charge of it. If it goes wrong, if it costs us the plan — it costs you your head."

******

The sudden weight of that threat sent a shudder through the man who'd made the proposal — a flash of triumph first, then a cold sweat down his spine. He bowed low at once, his voice taut.

"I will not fail you, Patriarch. I'll see this through or die trying."

No sooner had he finished than someone else spoke up, a note of hope in his voice.

"Any word on whether the young master found the complete data from the Stillwater lab? When's he coming back to Aetheris?"

"With his judgment steering things, we wouldn't be on the back foot like this. We'd have a lot more confidence."

As murmurs passed through the room, another senior official rose, his expression serious.

"Patriarch, word is your grandson Chase came back to Aetheris along with the others.

He's a Level Five Psychic Affinity user — exceedingly rare, even across the whole base. Hardly anyone has that kind of power."

"I hear a Level Five Psychic can control over a dozen high-level zombies remotely — devastating. With him in our ranks, our overall strength would jump considerably."

The patriarch closed his eyes briefly and sighed, a flicker of regret crossing his face, his tone heavy.

"He's still in the hospital. He was poisoned on a mission and his foundation took serious damage — dropped all the way from the peak of Level Five down to Level One."
