Chapter 40

Jasper was in front, and his lazy swagger was long gone. His nice black jacket had been slashed open in several places, and a deep claw wound ran down his arm. Blood dripped down his forearm and soaked half his sleeve.

His hair was plastered to his forehead with cold sweat, his face was pale, and he was breathing hard. He still looked terrified, and he'd clearly gotten his ass kicked by the zombies.

Right behind him, Rachel looked even worse.

Her clothes were torn to shreds. Dust and blood caked her tangled hair, and her makeup was smeared, with dark smudges of dirt on her pale cheeks. That sweet, delicate act of hers was nowhere to be seen.

Scratches covered her arms and neck, and some of her skin was scraped raw and bleeding. Her legs were shaking so hard she could barely stand, so she stuck close behind Jasper.

Tears of terror filled her eyes, and she couldn't stop shaking.

A huge mob of zombies was right on their heels. Several ordinary zombies came pouring out, snarling, and two deformed Level Two mutants followed close behind, moving way too fast.

Their stiff bodies were a sickly blue-black and covered in oozing sores. Their black claws gleamed, and they growled viciously as they closed in.

With a roar, the mutants suddenly sped up and lunged, their claws slashing straight for Jasper's and Rachel's backs.

Jasper sensed it coming. He gritted his teeth, called up what little was left of his ability, and yanked Rachel in front of him like a human shield. That was the only reason he dodged the deadly blow.

Rachel couldn't believe Jasper would stoop this low. When it was life or death, he'd actually shoved her in front of him to take the hit!

Luckily, she'd been reborn, and she knew exactly how to handle a Level Two zombie.

At the last second, she twisted away and barely dodged the attack.

They both slammed onto the ground at the bottom of the stairs, and the dust they kicked up hit them right in the face, and they started coughing.

Jasper pushed himself up into a sitting position with his aching arms. His back was soaked with sweat and blood, and he still looked shaken.

He'd always been cocky about his ability. He'd never taken ordinary zombies seriously, and he'd charged into the west building without thinking. He hadn't been ready for the two Level Two mutants hiding around the corner of the stairwell.

They'd ambushed him, working together and moving insanely fast, and caught him off guard. After a few rounds, he'd burned through most of his ability and gotten cut up. If he hadn't been quick, he'd be dead by now.

Rachel had nothing left in her. She lay limp on the ground, her skirt caked with dust and blood. Scratches crisscrossed her skin, and blood kept seeping out, gluing her clothes to her body.

Her legs wouldn't stop shaking. Dodging that zombie had used up every ounce of strength she had, and she couldn't even push herself off the ground.

She was still terrified, and tears cut two dirty tracks down her dusty cheeks.

She stared at the Level Two mutants closing in, frozen with despair.

A dozen or so ordinary zombies came pouring out behind them and blocked any way out. They shuffled closer, their cloudy eyes locked on the two of them.

Death was staring them in the face.

Jasper gritted his teeth and forced himself to his feet, wiping the dirt off his mouth. The wound on his back made him wince, and he swayed for a second.

His ability flickered weakly in his palm. He had nothing left to fight with.

He'd never been so scared of zombies in his life.

Then, he spotted Elijah and the others, and relief flooded through him. "Help... Help me!" he shouted.

Just as the mutant gathered itself and lunged to rip them apart, a bolt of blue lightning split the air and came crashing down!

It exploded with a deafening crack, and blinding blue light lit up the dark ground in front of the building. The sheer power behind it was overwhelming.

The lead Level Two mutant had no time to dodge. The lightning went right through it, and it convulsed violently as its rotting flesh charred black. Its snarl cut off, and it crashed to the ground, dead.

The rest of the horde froze.

Elijah stood a short distance away with lightning coiling around him. The wind stirred his hair, and his eyes were ice-cold. Nobody would want to cross him right now.

"Take care of the rest," he said flatly, like one-shotting a Level Two mutant was no big deal.

Orion and Mason jumped into the fight right away.
