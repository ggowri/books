Chapter 226

The room erupted the second he said it, excitement and disbelief breaking out on every face.

Thomas's eyes went wide, and he burst out laughing.

"So we could actually be back in Aetheris by this afternoon?"

He looked at Elijah with real admiration.

"Elijah really comes through. Fuel alone is nearly impossible to get these days with how broken down everything is, and you managed to pull a private plane out of nowhere?"

The rest of the squad lit up too, practically giddy at the thought of going home.

"We're finally heading back. I honestly thought I'd never get the chance again."

"With the world like this, who knows how Mom and Dad are doing, how they're handling the danger out there."

...

No grueling overland trip, no dodging threats mile after mile. Flying straight back to Aetheris felt like an unexpected gift.

In the middle of all that joy, only Paisley sat quiet, fingers tightening slightly, a knot of anxiety she couldn't shake.

Leaving this base meant heading back to Aetheris, which meant finally meeting her birth parents.

Unease churned in her chest.

Would two people she'd never met actually accept her, truly, with open arms?

A string of quiet worries wound through her mind, leaving nothing but unease behind.

Elijah gave the order.

"Alright, everyone finish up, go pack, convert whatever supplies you need, and meet back here in half an hour. We leave for Aetheris."

Everyone acknowledged him and dug into their food fast, eager to get moving.

That was when it happened.

—BZZZT!!!

A sharp, piercing red alarm tore through the entire camp.

Every face in the room shifted at once, the joy draining out and tension rushing in to replace it.

An urgent announcement blared over the base's speaker system, the broadcaster's voice tight with panic.

"Emergency alert!

Emergency alert!

The eastern coastal defense line has fallen! A massive wave of sea-mutant creatures is converging on Rivergate!"

"Threat tier unknown, numbers unknown, the assault is heavy. All personnel move to First Combat Alert immediately. Repeat, all personnel to First Combat Alert!"

A few short sentences, and the whole room went dead still, the air itself going rigid.

Everyone's stomach dropped, faces turning grim.

Sea mutants had always been the most terrifying, most unpredictable threat this world had to offer.

Bred under the crushing pressure of the deep ocean, they came out tougher, better defended, more strange in their gifts than anything on land, and they hunted in packs.

Nothing like a coordinated assault on this scale had ever happened before.

"That's it... the sea mutants are making a full landing!"

"The coastal line was supposed to be our strongest defense. It's already fallen?"

"We're in real trouble now. Are we even getting out of here?"

Outside, wind howled, and in the distance came the deep roar of monsters and crashing tides, the ground itself trembling faintly.

Dark clouds rolled over the base, carrying a bitter, briny wind, a thick, choking sense of dread settling over the entire camp.

A full-scale battle was about to break out, and the stakes couldn't be higher.

Elijah's expression dropped instantly. He stood, authority radiating off him, and gave the order without a second's delay.

"Everyone, move to the eastern district, now."

His eyes went sharp, his words clipped and fast.

"If the sea mutants breach the base, tens of thousands of civilians inside won't stand a chance. Everyone, prepare for battle."

******

Orion answered first, voice steady.

"I'm ready."

The rest of the squad rose as one, resolve hardening in every face as they answered together:

"We're ready too. Whenever you need us."

Without another word, Elijah led the way out of the room, heading straight for the eastern front.

The rest of the group fell in right behind him.

On the high wall of the eastern district, the garrison commander stood tall, face set and grim, directing the chaos of battle from the command post with steady precision, coordinating the front line without missing a beat.

Beyond the reinforced alloy wall, the scene had already turned into something out of a nightmare.

Countless grotesque sea mutants surged forward, a dark tide crashing against the defense line wave after wave.

Giant crab-like creatures with shells like hardened steel slammed their massive claws into the alloy wall, sparks flying with every brutal impact.

Endless streams of mutant sea serpents and tentacled horrors wove through the chaos, agile and vicious, climbing over the dead to strike at the defenders;
