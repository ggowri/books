Chapter 226

The moment he said it, the whole room erupted, and everyone was thrilled.

Thomas's face lit up, and he burst out laughing. "So that means we'll be back in Aetheris this afternoon?"

He looked at Elijah, impressed. "Elijah, you're amazing! With everything falling apart and supplies so tight, even fuel is hard to find, and you still got us a private plane!"

The other team members were all smiles too, excited to go home.

"We're finally going back. I thought I'd never get the chance again as long as I lived."

"Now that the apocalypse is here, I don't even know how my mom and dad are doing, or how they've been dealing with the danger."

They wouldn't have to make the long trek or dodge danger the whole way. Flying back to Aetheris was a nice surprise.

In the middle of all the excitement, only Paisley's mind was a mess. Her fingers tightened slightly, and she was hiding how nervous and anxious she felt.

Leaving Rivergate and going back to Aetheris meant she was going to meet her birth parents.

She was really nervous. Would the birth parents she'd never met really accept her?

Countless little worries swirled in her mind, and all she felt was uneasy.

Elijah gave the order. "Okay, everyone, finish breakfast, then go pack and trade for some supplies to bring back. We'll meet in half an hour and head back to Aetheris."

Everyone answered and quickly dug into their food, excited to set off.

But just then, a shrill, piercing red-alert siren suddenly blared through the whole base.

Everyone's expression changed at once. The joy on their faces vanished, replaced by tension and dread.

The base's emergency broadcast came on, and the announcer sounded rushed and panicked. "Emergency alert! Emergency alert!

"The eastern coastal defense line has fallen! A huge number of ocean mutants are closing in on Rivergate Base!

"Mutation levels unknown, numbers impossible to count, and the attack is fierce. All personnel, go to Level One combat readiness now! I repeat, all personnel to Level One combat readiness!"

Those few short lines left the whole private room dead silent, and the air seemed to freeze.

Everyone's heart sank, and their faces turned grim.

Ocean mutants had always been the most terrifying and mysterious things in the apocalypse!

These mutant creatures were born in the crushing pressure of the deep sea. They were incredibly strong, with insane defenses and strange powers, way beyond land beasts. They also liked to live and move in groups.

Nothing like this massive group attack had ever happened before!

"It's over... The ocean mutants are coming ashore in huge numbers!"

"The coastal defense line was our strongest barrier, and it fell this fast?!"

"Now we're really in trouble! Can we even get out of here?"

The wind howled outside the window. In the distance, they could already hear earth-shaking roars and the thunder of churning tides, and the ground was even trembling slightly.

Dark, heavy clouds hung over the whole base, and a fierce wind carried a bone-chilling salty sea breeze. A crushing sense of despair settled over everyone.

A huge battle was about to break out, and the situation was critical.

Elijah's face went grim. He jumped to his feet, radiating fierce authority, and gave the order on the spot. "Everyone, head to the eastern district and fight, now!"

His eyes were sharp, and he spoke fast. "If the ocean mutants break into the base, not one of the tens of thousands of survivors inside will make it! Everyone, get ready to fight!"

Orion answered in a steady voice. "I'm ready."

The other team members got to their feet, nothing but determination in their eyes, and answered together, "We're ready too, whenever you need us!"

Without any hesitation, Elijah walked out of the private room first and headed straight for the eastern battlefield.

The others followed right behind him.

On top of the high wall in the eastern district, the base's commanding officer stood tall at the defense command post, his face extremely grim. He calmly directed the whole battle and kept the front-line fighters in order against the enemy.

Outside the thick alloy defense wall, things had already turned into hell on earth.

Countless hideous ocean mutants attacked in dense swarms, a black tide crashing against the defense line wave after wave.

Giant mutant crabs with shells as hard as steel slammed their sharp, bloodthirsty claws into the alloy wall, making sparks fly and the wall shake nonstop.

Countless mutant sea snakes and tentacled monsters darted around, quick and sneaky, climbing over piles of bodies to lunge at the defenders.
