Chapter 23

Rachel made her voice soft and sweet, with just the right little catch in it, and looked over at Paisley.

"Paisley... it's really you! Ever since you left last night, my brothers and I have been worried sick.

"We spent all morning looking for you. We were so scared something had happened to you."

But behind the tears, she was already calculating.

She knew Paisley inside and out.

Paisley was soft-hearted, sentimental, and desperate for love. She fell for the family guilt trip every single time.

All Rachel had to do was play the victim, and Paisley would come running back.

Even if Paisley didn't want to, she'd never turn her down in front of all these people.

One way or another, that pendant was going to be hers.

And with Jasper backing her, if Paisley still refused to play along, Rachel could always just kill her and take it.

Off to the side, Jasper watched with interest, and the pistol stopped spinning in his hand.

He'd always had a thing for soft, fragile girls who looked like they needed saving. Then, his eyes landed on Paisley in the middle of her group, and a slimy, filthy hunger filled his gaze.

The girl in front of him was gorgeous, with a sweet, delicate face, a cute little nose, and soft pink lips. Her skin had a warm, flawless glow that seemed to light up the dim, wrecked mall. She looked like she didn't belong there at all.

She had a toned, perfectly proportioned figure and clear, cool eyes. Even in the middle of all this chaos, there was something fresh and innocent about her, and it made men want her.

Heat shot straight to Jasper's groin, and his eyes lit up with greed. He wanted to drag her off somewhere private right then and there and fuck her.

The Lennoxes hadn't been lying. Paisley was a knockout, a hundred times hotter than the cheap, ordinary girls he usually saw.

Paisley could see right through Rachel's smug little scheme. She'd also noticed the filthy way Jasper's eyes were crawling all over her.

She felt nothing but cold disgust, and she scoffed to herself.

Same old fake family act. It's pathetic.

In her past life, she'd fallen for this exact act, Rachel's sweet, harmless, loving-sister routine.

Over and over, she'd let her guard down, given in, and poured her heart out to Rachel. And in the end, Rachel had betrayed her and shoved her into a horde of zombies to die.

Now that she'd been reborn, she knew exactly what Rachel was. She was a selfish, venomous snake.

Did Rachel really think a cheap family guilt trip would work on her again? That she'd just let Rachel steal everything and climb over her dead body?

Paisley's face went cold, and every trace of warmth disappeared.

This time, she was going to rip Rachel's mask off with her own hands. Every one of her schemes would blow up in her face, and Rachel would get exactly what she deserved.

As for Jasper, the way he was leering at her made her want to kill him. She wanted to take out that piece of trash right then and there.

But before she could make a move, Orion caught Jasper's slimy look too, and he couldn't hold back.

How dare that bastard look at my sister like that!

Orion's face turned ice-cold. His Metal Affinity exploded around him, and a thin, razor-sharp metal blade formed at his fingertips.

With a sharp whoosh, the blade shot past Jasper's cheek, so fast he never even saw it coming.

A second later, a thin cut opened up on his cheek, and blood slowly trickled down his face.

Orion glared at him, his eyes cold as ice. "Where the hell do you think you're looking? Look at her like that one more time, and the next one goes through your throat."

Jasper froze. Once the shock wore off, he was seething with rage.

He touched his cheek and felt warm, sticky blood on his fingers. The smirk vanished from his face, and pure fury took its place.

Nobody had ever dared to lay a finger on him, and now some random guy had humiliated him in front of everyone!

"Do you have any fucking idea who I am?" he snarled. "You think some worthless nobody like you can cut me?"
