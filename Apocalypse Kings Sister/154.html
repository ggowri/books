Chapter 154

"Let's head out too," Paisley said.

Everyone moved out fast, driving off in two SUVs.

Thomas drove the lead car, with Paisley lounging in the passenger seat. She had a sweet fruit lollipop in her mouth and the little white cat cuddled in her arms.

The little white cat copied its owner with a tiny lollipop of its own. Its cheeks puffed out as it snuggled in her arms, sweet and well-behaved.

When Ashley saw that, she leaned forward from the back seat and whined sweetly, "Paisley, I want a lollipop too!"

After so many days together, her early shyness was long gone, and she'd figured Paisley out. Paisley seemed cool and distant, but she had a soft heart and was easy to talk to, so Ashley felt freer and freer to cuddle up to her.

Paisley lowered her eyes with a faint smile. With her bag as cover, she quietly pulled three lollipops in different flavors from her Storage Dimension and handed them to Ashley, Thomas, and Madison.

"Thanks, Paisley!" Ashley beamed, thrilled.

Madison took hers and thanked her gently.

Thomas kept his eyes on the road and his hands on the wheel. "Paisley, can you unwrap mine for me?" he asked casually.

After days on the road together, facing death side by side again and again, things had changed between them. They'd grown close and comfortable, and there was no awkwardness left.

In the second car, Mason was driving, with Spencer in the passenger seat and Marcus alone in the back.

Marcus kept his eyes locked on the SUV ahead.

Inside it sat Paisley, the woman who'd saved his life and the one he secretly loved.

These past few days, he'd tried more than once to get close and talk to her. But someone always cut in, whether they meant to or not, and he never got a moment alone with her.

Just this morning, he'd meant to take the seat beside her in the first car. The others had laughed and dragged him into the second one instead, so he'd missed his chance again.

But Marcus didn't let it get to him.

As long as Paisley was still single, he still had a chance. Time was on his side, and one day, he'd be the one by her side.

The two SUVs rolled steadily down the empty, ruined streets toward the abandoned mall on the eastern outskirts.

None of them noticed the plain black car trailing far behind them, quietly keeping a safe distance.

Inside it sat the four Lennox siblings and Kelly.

Every one of them had the same vicious plan. The moment Paisley died, they'd grab the pendant.

The road ahead was uncertain, and death was quietly tailing them.

The cars left the main road and the base far behind, heading into the deserted ruins on the eastern outskirts.

The pavement here was cracked and full of potholes, and weeds ran wild along the shoulders. It was bleak and lifeless, a world away from the safe, orderly streets inside Rivergate Base.

Crumbling walls lined the road in long rows, and abandoned cars sat tilted and piled on top of each other. Dust filled the air, and everything was dead quiet.

Every so often, a slow, low-level zombie wandered aimlessly along the road. When it sensed the cars, it jerked its head around and stumbled after them, a grim reminder of how ruined the world had become.

The SUVs plowed right through them without slowing down.

Paisley focused and released her pure Level Five psychic power. It spread out like an invisible net, covering 1,000 yards in every direction.

Every blade of grass, every tree, and every movement in that range showed up clearly in her mind.

Clusters of red dots lit up in her senses.

The Level Five wolf, seven or eight Level Four mutant zombies, and a huge crowd of Level Two and Three zombies were lurking all over the mall, in the stairwells, on the escalators, and in the corners of the stores.

Perfect. Great practice for everyone.

Even better, the whole mall was practically untouched. The shelves were still stocked with supplies, everyday goods, and food, and there was barely any sign that scavengers had ever picked through the place.

A fully stocked mall this late into the apocalypse was almost unheard of.

That must have been thanks to the Level Five mutant wolf.

The timing couldn't have been better. She'd been planning to stock up big lately, and this untouched mall had shown up at exactly the right moment.

Ten minutes later, both SUVs rolled to a stop outside the mall's ruined front entrance.

The doors swung open, and everyone climbed out. The moment their feet hit the ground, a cold, deathly chill washed over them. The crumbling walls were covered in dark cracks, every window was shattered, and trash was everywhere, while the wind whistled through the building carrying the rotten stench only zombies gave off.
