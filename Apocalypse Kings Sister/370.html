Chapter 370

One teammate's face drained of color, letting out a startled cry, utterly thrown.

Grant's arrogance froze solid on his face. He sat bolt upright, eyes full of shock and dread.

"Something's wrong. Why would they suddenly switch targets?"

Outside, the zombies kept coming, layer after layer of them, crowding in, biting and slamming against the vehicle, packing the entrance to the tree line within seconds, surrounding the vehicle completely.

At this rate, the vehicle wouldn't hold together much longer.

Chloe's cold smile shattered completely, replaced by pure panic and venom. She cursed through gritted teeth.

"Damn it! Why are they coming for us?!"

She'd been so sure they'd sit back and watch from a distance, reaping the rewards. She never imagined they'd end up surrounded, dragged into the fight against their will.

"No more hiding! Get out and fight, now!"

Grant's face went ashen, his composed front collapsing entirely as he snarled the order.

Staying in the vehicle any longer meant taking hits with no way to strike back, a slow death, ending in a wrecked vehicle and a body count.

They had no choice. They shoved the doors open in a panic and were forced to join the slaughter.

In an instant, wind, fire, water, and earth Affinities burst from the Scarlet Squad all at once.

Chloe forced down her panic. As a Healing-support Affinity user and the team's darling, she'd always been the one who stayed out of the fighting.

She turned to Grant beside her, voice sharp, giving an outright order.

"That mutant trumpet flower, it's yours to handle."

The words froze Grant in place. He stared at her, utterly disbelieving, a bone-deep chill flooding through his chest.

He was only Level Six. Among the base's younger generation, that might count for something. But the trumpet flower on top of the silo was Level Seven!

Massive, viciously strong, wreathed in an invisible, undetectable, lethal haze. Sending him up there alone was practically the same as sending him to die.

He'd always thought Chloe cherished him, depended on him, trusted him, always sweet-talking him, always considering his needs, always shielding him.

But right now, with one throwaway sentence, she'd pushed him straight toward certain death.

Had all that tenderness, all those declarations of love, been lies all along? Had she only ever loved herself, had he only ever been something to use?

The crushing gap between then and now swept through him like ice water. Grant's fingers trembled; he stood frozen, unable to move.

Seeing him still sitting in the vehicle, Chloe's anxiety spiked, terrified of losing their window and the wager along with it. She snapped at him, sharp and urgent.

"What's wrong with you?"

"What are you still standing around for?!"

"Falcon Squad already has someone moving to kill that flower! If you don't go now, we're going to lose!"

Before she'd even finished speaking, a cool, distant figure had already shot straight up into the air.

Paisley's toes barely grazed the ground before she went airborne, her form light and sharp, riding the momentum of her leap clear out of the zombie horde.

Just as her body reached the peak of its arc, countless thick, thorned vines burst up from the ground, catching her steadily and lifting her the rest of the way to the top of the thirty-three-foot silo.

Frost coated the Frostbloom Sword entirely, its edge cutting through the air with an overwhelming pressure that swept across the whole airspace, locking directly onto the massive, eerie Level Seven mutant trumpet flower coiled at the top.

The sight froze everyone below in the Scarlet Squad. Every movement stopped at once, every face a mask of disbelief.

"How is she even doing that?! Conjuring vines out of thin air, lifting herself through the sky?"

"Thirty-three feet up! Her Plant Affinity is that strong? We're both Plant Affinity users, how can the gap be this massive?!"

"That whole area's thick with hallucinogenic toxin, and she's right next to it, completely unaffected?!"

"Is she out of her mind? Is she seriously planning to solo that Level Seven mutant flower by herself?"

"She's insane! That's a Level Seven mutant plant!"

"It takes at least three Level Seven Affinity users working in perfect sync just to have a sliver of a chance at killing it!"

"And she's just a Level Six user, thinking she can kill it alone? That's not bravery, that's suicide."

A wave of shocked murmurs rippled through the Scarlet Squad, every face twisted with confusion, disbelief, and mockery, certain Paisley's move was nothing but reckless, suicidal overreach.
