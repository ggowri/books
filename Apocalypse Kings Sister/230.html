Chapter 230

The hooded man was a high-level mind controller trained by the people at the top. He controlled mutant beasts and slaughtered Affinity users, and he'd never met his match. He never imagined a girl this young would overpower him.

His shock faded fast and turned into pure viciousness.

Hostility surged in his eyes, and his hands moved faster and faster as he wove his power. The black mist around him swelled wildly and turned into vicious psychic claws that swept down on Paisley's head from every direction. He wanted to rip her mind apart and cripple her for good.

"Since you know the secret and have this kind of talent, I definitely can't let you live!"

A fierce wind swept the black mist across the hollow in the reefs. The seawater around them churned violently, the rocks shook, and the whole hidden spot was swallowed by a deadly aura.

Paisley swung the Frostbloom Sword, and its blade flashed. A freezing sword aura full of pure energy split the mass of black mist claws.

The silver-white light cut through the heavy darkness, and every sinister psychic attack it touched fell apart and melted away.

She pushed off the reef with her toes, darted out of the shadows, and went on the attack.

Her cool voice rang out clearly through the sea wind, chilling to the bone. "You're the one who deserves to die."

The little white cat shot forward beside her like a white lightning bolt. Its sharp claws, full of fierce power, went straight for the hooded man's neck.

His face changed. He leaned back and twisted aside in a panic, barely dodging the deadly swipe. The strong wind tore his hood off, revealing a pale, eerie face covered in dark, sinister lines.

Before he could steady himself and strike back, power surged at Paisley's fingertips, and the ground trembled slightly. Several thick, tough, dark-green vines burst out of the earth and wrapped around him with incredible speed. They tied his arms, legs, and body tight, squeezing layer by layer until he was pinned to the rock, unable to move.

Terrified and furious, the hooded man forced out his black mist and psychic power and struggled like crazy. But the vines were thick with power and held him tight, and he couldn't break free at all.

The tight vines dug deep into his flesh, and the black mist and energy flowing through his body jammed up, close to falling apart.

Overwhelming terror filled his heart, and his blood nearly froze.

She actually had two powers! No wonder her psychic power was so strong.

Massive panic swallowed all his arrogance and cruelty. His cold, vicious attitude was gone, and all that was left was fear and dread.

He couldn't keep up his icy act anymore, and his voice shook as he screamed in shock and fear. "You've got two powers! Psychic and Plant!

"That's impossible! Who the hell are you?!"

He'd been beaten so badly today. Bitterness and fear churned inside him, and a vicious look crossed his face as he tried to threaten her.

"You think you can kill me?! They'll never let you get away with it! The people behind me are everywhere, and they control countless experiment bases and mutant beast hordes!

"If you let me go, I promise I'll never come after any of you again."

When she didn't flinch at the threat and stayed unmoved, the fear of death took over. His tone suddenly flipped from vicious threats to humble begging, and his voice was hoarse and shaking.

"I was wrong! I shouldn't have gone after you, and I shouldn't have sent the beast horde to attack the base!

"I can tell you who's behind all of this, the whole truth, and where every experiment base is! Just spare my life, please!"

Paisley's eyes were cold and still. She stepped closer across the cool rocks, the Frostbloom Sword gleaming with a freezing aura, and her voice had no warmth at all.

"Oh? That depends on whether what you tell me is actually useful."

A glimmer of hope sparked in the hooded man's eyes, and he couldn't wait to talk.

But the moment he opened his mouth, his face twisted in pain, and every muscle in his body started jerking and spasming out of control.

A broken, eerie choking sound squeezed out of his throat. His body went limp, and he crashed onto the cold rocks. Foam kept pouring from his mouth, and his stiff arms and legs jerked wildly.

In just two seconds, all his movement stopped dead. His body went stiff, his eyes bulged wide open, and thin streams of dark red blood seeped from his eyes, ears, nose, and mouth. His pupils were blown, and every trace of life was gone.

Just like that, he'd died a strange, sudden death.

Paisley pulled back her sword aura, and her face turned cold.

Whoever was behind him was really careful. They'd been using poison to control him all along. Deadly poison had been planted in his body ahead of time, and if he was ever beaten, captured, or at risk of spilling secrets, it would go off and kill him on the spot, making sure no secret ever got out.
