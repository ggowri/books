Chapter 230

He'd been trained by the upper echelon as a premier mental controller, dominating beasts and killing Affinity users without ever meeting resistance. He'd never once imagined losing ground to a girl this young.

The shock didn't last. Pure malice replaced it fast.

Hostility surged in his eyes, his hands moving through seals faster now, the black mist around him swelling, twisting into monstrous claws, sweeping toward Paisley's head, trying to tear straight into her mind and shut her down for good.

"You know too much, and you've got too much talent. I can't let you live."

Wind howled through the reef, carrying the black mist across the hollow, seawater churning violently, rock trembling beneath the force of it, the entire hidden platform swallowed in deadly intent.

Paisley raised the Frostbloom Sword, its edge flaring to life, cold sword-energy wrapped in pure power cutting through the wall of black claws.

Silver-white light split the darkness clean, and every strand of psychic attack it touched dissolved into nothing.

She stepped lightly off the rock, body surging out of the shadow, launching her own counterattack.

Her cold voice cut through the sea wind, sharp with certainty.

"You're the one who needs to die."

The little white cat shot forward beside her, a streak of white lightning, claws slicing through the air thick with menace, aimed straight for the hooded man's throat.

His face twisted in shock. He threw himself backward in a panic, barely dodging the killing blow, his hood tearing loose in the wind to reveal a face scarred with dark, twisting marks, pale and grotesque.

Before he could steady himself and strike back, Paisley's Plant Affinity surged, the ground trembling slightly as thick, dark-green vines tore up from the earth, lashing around him with brutal force, binding his limbs and torso, pinning him to the rock, holding him completely still.

Fear and fury flooded the hooded man's eyes as he thrashed, channeling every ounce of black mist and psychic force he had left to break free, but the vines held firm, dense and unyielding, crushing in tighter with every second.

The vines bit deep into his flesh, his chest heaving, the black mist churning through his body stalling, close to collapse.

His pupils shrank, dread flooding through him, blood running cold.

She was a dual-Affinity user.

No wonder her psychic power had been this overwhelming.

Terror swallowed every shred of his arrogance and malice whole. Nothing was left of his earlier coldness, just raw fear.

He couldn't hold his composure anymore. His voice cracked, trembling with fear and shock as he shouted.

"You're a dual Affinity! Psychic and Plant!

That's impossible! What are you?!"

Having fallen this completely, desperation and fear tangled together, and something vicious flickered in his eyes as he tried to threaten her.

"You think you can kill me?!

They'll never let you get away with this!

My people are everywhere, controlling research bases and mutant hordes across the entire country!"

"Let me go, and I swear I'll never come after any of you again."

Seeing she stayed unmoved, her resolve unshaken, fear of death finally broke him. His tone flipped completely, from vicious threats to pathetic begging, his voice cracking and raw.

"I was wrong!

I shouldn't have gone after you, shouldn't have sent that horde against the base!

I'll tell you everything, every name, every truth, every location of every lab!

Just spare my life!"

A flicker of hope lit in the hooded man's eyes, desperate to get the words out.

But the second he opened his mouth, his face twisted in agony, his whole body seizing in violent, uncontrollable convulsions.

"Uhhh—!"

A broken, garbled sound tore from his throat. He collapsed hard onto the cold rock, foam spilling from his mouth, limbs locked rigid, thrashing in helpless spasms.

Within two seconds, every movement stopped cold. His body went completely rigid, eyes wide open, thin trails of dark red blood seeping from every opening in his face, pupils blown and lifeless.

He was dead in an instant, in the strangest, most violent way.

Paisley's gaze dimmed slightly, the edge of her sword stilling, something cold passing through her eyes.

Careful, right to the end.

Poisoned, preemptively.

Someone had planted that toxin in his body long before now, a failsafe set to trigger the moment he was captured or at risk of talking, killing him instantly and erasing any chance of the secret getting out.
