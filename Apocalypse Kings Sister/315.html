Chapter 315

Gabriel Sterling turned his head slowly toward the slight figure standing beside his bed, confusion and surprise flickering in his half-focused eyes, his voice rough from disuse.

"You saved me?"

Paisley drew her Affinity back, the faint glow at her fingertips fading, and nodded.

"The poison's gone. Rest and you'll recover fully. It won't affect your Affinity foundation at all."

Outside the room, the escort had been pacing nervously, already resigned to another failure, already preparing himself for the fallout.

Then he heard the man's voice, clear and steady, through the door.

He froze, his pupils blowing wide, his mind blanking out entirely.

Awake?!

The deputy director — trapped by an incurable poison for days, treated by the base's entire medical team without a shred of progress — was awake?!

He didn't waste a second. He shoved the door open and ran in. The sight of Gabriel sitting up, alert, breathing easy, left him utterly speechless.

Word tore through the hospital like wildfire.

The doctors and healers who'd failed him before came running, crowding into the room, checking him over again and again.

After a thorough examination, every one of them stood there in total shock.

"The poison's completely gone! No trace of it left!"

"His channels are clear, his Affinity foundation intact — if anything, more stable than before he got sick!"

"How is this possible? The toxin none of us could touch, and she cleared it in a matter of minutes? How did she even do that?"

The doctors' expressions shifted entirely as they looked at Paisley — from dismissal to something close to reverence.

Down in the lobby, Ryan, still mopping away, heard the commotion ripple down from above, snatches of excited conversation drifting through the air.

"He's awake! The deputy director's actually awake!"

"It was that new girl — cured an incurable poison in a few minutes! Saved the director!"

"She's not just his savior now, she's getting thirty thousand points for this mission."

"That girl's luck is unreal."

"Who even is she? I've never seen her around before."

Everyone was talking about Paisley.

The mop clattered out of Ryan's hands, hitting the floor.

He stood frozen, back stiff, staring up toward the third floor, his expression a mix of shock and disbelief.

The adopted daughter their family had thrown away, treated like nothing — now holding a level of power the entire base looked up to.

And here he was, stuck in a worn-out cleaning uniform, scraping by at the bottom.

The weight of it hit him hard, a bitter taste rising in his throat, regret twisting through him. He thought about how carelessly their family had treated her, how differently things might have gone if they'd shown her even a sliver of real kindness.

In the room, the doctors continued their examination of Gabriel, full of praise and disbelief, every eye in the room fixed on Paisley with something close to awe.

This girl was not ordinary.

The escort snapped back into motion, pulling out the base terminal and transferring the mission reward, respectful and quick. Thirty thousand points landed in her account instantly.

Paisley wasn't one for crowds or praise. Once the reward notification popped up, she slipped quietly out of the crowded room and left the hospital without a word.

Outside, the noise faded behind her. She walked the base streets, pulling up her terminal as she went.

The treatment mission had just netted her thirty thousand points. After the twenty thousand she'd spent earlier on gifts, that left her up ten thousand, net.

On top of that, Orion had sent ten thousand, Elijah thirty thousand, and Thomas and Spencer three thousand each — fifty-six thousand points total added today.
