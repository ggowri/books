Chapter 464

Blood-red petals floated down, mixed with black scale chips and gore, and covered the dark corridor.

When the last trace of her power faded, the mall went silent. No roars. No claws scraping.

The whole horde that had held Larkspur's mall was dead. Two days of siege were finally over.

That parasite kill had been too strange and too shocking.

Armor no one could break couldn't stop a seed. Blood-red roses had bloomed from a beast's bones, veins, and organs, fed on its flesh, rooted in its core, and then exploded. It was beautiful, savage, gentle, and deadly.

Elijah and Cole finally steadied themselves, but the shock still gripped them. They stared at the petals and remains scattered around them, unable to speak.

Paisley stood quietly. The killing chill had left her face, and only a pale, tired look remained.

The psychic pressure, the Oblivion Flame, and the high-level parasite kill had drained her power almost dry. She swayed slightly.

Elijah snapped out of it and crossed to her in a few quick steps. He lifted her into his arms, so gently, as if the slightest pressure might hurt her. Warmth and tenderness filled his eyes.

He carried her carefully out of the mall.

"Pais... thank you..."

"...You've been through so much..."

There were too many things to say, and he didn't know how to start.

This girl kept creating miracles in dead ends. Every time everyone was out of options, she carved out a way through.

She had saved his life again. Without her, he would be dead right now.

Cole followed behind them, looking at Paisley with awe and gratitude, and said nothing.

Outside, the night wind was cool and swept away the smell of blood and smoke.

The others waiting by the SUV spotted three figures coming and felt their hearts drop back into place after more than ten minutes in the air.

Orion hurried to meet them. The tension drained from his shoulders, and the anxiety faded from his face. He let out a long breath.

"If you'd taken any longer, we were about to go back in for you."

His eyes landed on Paisley's pale face and the worry came rushing back. "How's Pais? Is she hurt?"

The Falcon and Lone Wolf members crowded around, all eyes on Paisley. They knew the two men couldn't have come out without paying a heavy price.

Elijah set her gently in the back seat, adjusted the backrest for her, and draped his combat jacket over her.

Then he sat beside her and rested her head on his shoulder so she could lean more comfortably. He turned to reassure everyone.

"She's fine. Don't worry. She burned through too much power, that's all. She's exhausted, not hurt."

Everyone let out a long breath.

As long as she was all right, nothing else mattered.

Orion's face turned serious. "There's a five-star hotel not far ahead. We'll rest there tonight."

"One more thing. The base's meteorologists issued a warning. The day after tomorrow, the Great Freeze hits. Temperatures will plunge, blizzards will close the roads, and frostbite will be everywhere. Nobody can move outside."

"We leave at first light tomorrow and push straight back to base. We have to be home before the Freeze lands."

"Understood." The answer came in unison. Nobody argued.

After a deadly fight and a desperate breakout, they were exhausted. They were low on power and carried injuries, and their nerves were frayed. Everyone needed rest.

In the back seat, Paisley fell into a deep sleep.

Elijah sat beside her, gently straightening the jacket on her shoulders, moving as lightly as he could so nothing would wake her.

Ashley eyed the seat that had been taken from her and didn't dare say a word.

My sister got stolen. I'm so annoyed.

Orion and his brothers glared like they had fire in their eyes. If looks could kill, Elijah would be a corpse.

He's gone too far.

But one look at Paisley's tired face made it clear this was no time to settle scores.
