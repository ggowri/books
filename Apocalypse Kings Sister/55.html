Chapter 55

But the Level Four monster's power was just too much. A few small icicles still broke through and shot straight at them!

Paisley stayed calm. Dark green energy pulsed around her, and countless vines burst from the ground. In a heartbeat, they twisted and wove together into a huge, airtight net.

The rest of the icicles pounded into the net one after another and shattered on impact, bursting into clouds of icy mist. At the same time, the poison in the vines surged back toward the creature.

The monster's huge body went rigid. It was clearly wary of the powerful poison, and a low, raspy growl rumbled out of it. Its eyes blazed ice-blue, and the cold around it surged several times stronger as it tried to freeze every last vine solid.

But Paisley's Plant Affinity had already evolved into something toxic. Her life force was laced with poison, and it was the perfect counter to extreme cold. Instead of freezing, the vines grew wildly, wrapping around the creature layer after layer!

"Everyone, get back. Don't let the cold get you." Paisley waved them back.

Her face didn't change one bit.

To the others, a high-tier Level Four monster was an unbeatable threat.

For me, it's loot! Perfect for a little practice.

The second she finished speaking, she darted forward in a blur, dodged its attack, and appeared right in front of the frost giant.

The 16-foot frost giant towered over her, its presence crushing. Paisley clenched her fist, packed it with pure, concentrated plant energy, and brought it down lightly but precisely on the creature's hard, icy head.

Each punch landed with a dull, solid thunk, again and again.

The wild ice energy inside it was thrown into chaos. Layer after layer of thick ice cracked and fell off its body, and its massive frame shrank right before their eyes!

It went from 16 feet to 13, then 10, then just six and a half...

Her venomous plant energy forced its overwhelming ice power down. Its murderous rage faded fast as its huge body got smaller and smaller.

The cat was still restless, so Paisley bopped it on top of the head again with a soft thunk. Then, she gave it one more for good measure.

In just a few seconds, the 16-foot Ice Affinity mutant cat had turned into a fluffy, snow-white house cat about the length of her forearm!

A faint, cool mist drifted from its fur, and its eyes were still a clear, icy blue. It looked soft and adorable, without a trace of its earlier viciousness.

The terrifying Level Four Ice Affinity mutant cat was now ridiculously tame.

It seemed to finally get how outmatched it was. Its ears drooped, and it curled up at Paisley's feet and kept rubbing its little head against her shoe, sucking up to her shamelessly.

It let out soft little mews, almost like it was begging for mercy. It had given up completely.

The cat even nudged a deep blue, frost-cold Level Four Plant Affinity core over to Paisley's feet as a sign of surrender.

Paisley smiled. She bent down to pick up the core and scooped the kitten into her arms.

The kitten was super clingy. The second she picked it up, it curled into a little ball.

It nuzzled its cool, fluffy head against Paisley's neck. Its ice-blue eyes had gone clear and gentle, and it clung to her with total trust.

Paisley ran her fingers through its fluffy white fur. It felt cool and soft, so nice that she couldn't stop petting it.

She petted it like a pro, and her touch was incredibly gentle. She seemed like a whole different person from a minute ago, when she'd been pummeling the monster into submission.

Honestly, Paisley had thought this frost cat was ridiculously cute from the moment she first saw it.

It was way smarter than a typical mutant beast, with a pure, simple nature and top-tier fighting power. On top of that, Ice Affinity was incredibly rare.

Most importantly, it was seriously adorable! It was snow-white all over, with bright blue eyes, and it checked every single box for her.

That was exactly why she'd held back. She'd never planned to kill it. She just hadn't expected it to surrender on its own.

Meanwhile, Elijah, Orion, and Mason stood frozen in place. Their minds had gone completely blank, and they were too stunned to say a word.

A moment ago, that 16-foot frost cat could freeze everything around it with a wave of its paw. It had been so strong that every one of them had been ready to fight to the death.

And then what?
