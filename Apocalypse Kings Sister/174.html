Chapter 174

Paisley flicked her wrist and unleashed the sword energy she'd been building, and it sliced through the air with a sharp hiss.

A crisp, cutting sound rang out through the field as the Frostbloom Sword's innate ice-breaking power sliced clean through the stem's last hardened shell.

The thick main stem split open with a crack, and fine cracks raced across the entire stem. The last faint spark of life froze under the icy sword energy and shattered.

She drove the tip of her sword into the soil and dug along the withered roots, zeroing in on the core deep underground. With a light flick of her wrist, she pried out a dark green Level Six crystal core, perfectly intact and still giving off a faint, dying trace of poison.

The moment the core was out, the last trace of life drained from the stem, and it dried up, cracked, and crumbled into dirt.

The mighty Level Six mutant carnivorous flower that had ruled the whole amusement park was finally dead.

With the source of its power gone, the deadly purple-black mist slowly cleared, and the sharp, rotten stench faded from the air. The cracked ground gradually settled, and the poisoned weeds and flowers lost their eerie colors, turning back into ordinary plants.

The oppressive, deathly weight over the park lifted entirely, clean air rolling back in, the whole field settling into quiet calm.

Nearby, the all-purpose ward shimmered and faded, the barrier protecting the group finally dissolving.

The group slowly came back to themselves, staring at the transformed, harmless field in front of them, at the heavy Level Six crystal core in Paisley's hand, still too stunned to speak.

After a few seconds of silence, the pent-up emotion broke loose, and everyone erupted into cheers at once, relief and joy flooding through them.

"It's dead! That Level Six flower is really dead!" Mason let out a long breath, his voice shaking with excitement as the tension finally drained out of him.

"We're not going to die, and it's all thanks to Paisley!" Madison's eyes shone, all her despair replaced by pure joy.

Thomas looked at the plants, back to normal now, and thought about the unstoppable monster they'd just faced. He still couldn't believe it.

"Who would've thought a bag of spicy jerky could take down that terrifying Level Six boss? Paisley's a genius for figuring that out!

"I'm going to brag about today for the rest of my life."

Spencer looked at Paisley, who stood there calm and composed as ever, his face full of admiration.

"Pais, you're incredible. If it weren't for you, we'd all be dead today. You keep pulling off miracles and getting us out alive."

Ashley ran to Paisley's side, gazing at her in total awe.

"Paisley, you're amazing! Only you could come up with such a weird way to turn things around!"

Every word they said came straight from the heart, full of shock and admiration.

They'd all just stared death in the face, so they knew better than anyone how terrifying that Level Six carnivorous flower was. That was exactly why Paisley seemed so amazing to them.

In that moment, their faith in Paisley reached its peak.

Paisley looked cool and slight, but she'd single-handedly broken them out of a death trap in the middle of the apocalypse. She'd carried them all and saved every one of their lives.

In the few seconds while they marveled, the mutant willow devoured every last bit of the lesser carnivorous flowers' roots, venom, crystal cores, and energy.

Thick, pure energy from the beasts poured into the willow's core, feeding every branch and vein.

With a soft hum, a ripple of energy spread out, and pure, vivid green light burst from the tree.

The willow's power shot up, climbing level after level. It broke straight through its peak Level Four bottleneck and reached mid Level Six.

The moment it leveled up, the giant tree's massive branches shrank and condensed fast. Its countless willow strands drew in, and it visibly got smaller and more delicate.

In moments, the giant tree that had blotted out the sky turned into a dainty little willow with glossy green leaves. Its branches were slender and flexible, its leaves fresh and dewy, and it glowed with a faint, pure Plant Affinity aura.

It leapt lightly, riding a gust of wind, and landed neatly on Paisley's shoulder, its thin branches trailing down, brushing against her hair, gentle and affectionate.

Paisley tilted her head, eyes soft, and gently stroked the tender new leaves resting on her shoulder.
