Chapter 36

Jasper didn't give Rachel a second look, like he hadn't just forced her onto her knees. His lazy smirk was back, but behind it, he was already scheming.

As long as he got his hands on that virus analysis equipment, who cared about Rachel's pride?

"If you're coming with us, you follow my orders," Elijah said, his face darkening.

Without waiting for an answer, he gave the order. "Gear up. We're heading to Southspire General Hospital downtown."

Everyone jumped into action, and the convoy was ready to roll in no time.

Elijah, Orion, Mason, and Paisley rode together in one car.

Jasper, Rachel, and eight of Jasper's men piled into two more cars and followed close behind.

The three cars pulled out of the gated community one after another and turned onto the city's empty, silent main road.

The streets that used to be so busy were a wasteland now. The pavement was cracked and full of potholes, abandoned cars and junk were everywhere, and dark, dried blood stained the road.

Every shop on both sides had smashed windows and doors, and the walls were covered in claw marks and bullet holes. There wasn't a sign of life anywhere.

The apocalypse was only a few days old, and the whole city had already turned into hell on earth.

Wind swept through the empty streets, kicking up dust and scraps of paper. It carried a faint stench of rot and blood in through the cracks around the windows, and the air in the car felt heavy.

Every so often, a stray zombie shuffled along the side of the road, stiff and rotting. Its cloudy eyes locked onto the passing cars, and it let out low, raspy growls.

The cars were moving way too fast for them, and the zombies were left in the dust.

Inside the lead car, nobody said a word.

Mason gripped the wheel, his eyes glued to the road. "Elijah, Southspire General's three blocks up.

"That's the heart of old downtown, and it was always packed. It had to get hit hard when everything went down, so there's gonna be a ton of zombies there. Maybe even some high-level mutants."

In the passenger seat, Elijah stayed cool and calm.

"I figured. Hospitals mean supplies and crowds, so it's high-risk. But we still have to go."

He turned to Paisley in the back seat, and his voice softened without him even noticing.

"Once we're inside, Orion and I will take the front and clear a path. Mason will cover our sides and keep our exit clear. You stay right in the middle, and don't wander off.

"I'll keep you safe.

"Do you still have room in your space?"

Paisley nodded. "About 200 square feet."

That was enough to put Elijah at ease. "If we take out a high-level mutant and get a crystal core, it's yours. You use it to level up first."

Paisley really wanted to say she'd rather have a ton of gold to level up with. But she didn't want Orion getting suspicious, so she kept her mouth shut.

With her Plant Affinity at mid Level Three, she could sense everything within 100 yards. She could feel every lurking zombie, every faint stir of life in the plants, and every little movement in the shadows.

Up ahead, the hospital was swarming with zombies. She could sense a huge mass of them packed in layer after layer, and it was seriously dangerous.

Deep in the horde, she also picked up a few stronger, more vicious presences. They were almost definitely high-level zombies.

Everything they needed was in the hospital's main building. That included the virus analysis equipment, scarce meds, surgical supplies, antibiotics, blood-clotting drugs, and disinfectant. There was also all kinds of medical equipment that would be priceless in the apocalypse.

It looked like they had a tough fight ahead.

******

In the car behind them, Jasper was leaning back with his eyes half-closed. He looked relaxed, but his mind was razor-sharp.

Next to him, Rachel hadn't said a word the whole ride.

Paisley wasn't the same person anymore. She wasn't easy to fool now, so Rachel kept racking her brain for a way to snatch that pendant.

******

The cars sped on, and before long, they pulled up outside the hospital's front gate.

The battered hospital stood alone in the ruins, its outer walls stained black with blood and grime.

Every window in the building was smashed, and the iron front gate was twisted and hanging crooked off its frame.
