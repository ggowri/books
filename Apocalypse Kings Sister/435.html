Then he turned serious.

"Still, Pais is right. A high-level mutant like that is a huge risk if we let it live. It'll cause more deaths. There'll be no end of trouble."

Elijah got to his feet, mostly recovered by now.

"It just got hurt and ran. It won't go far. Odds are it's hiding around the Ridgemont ruins, nursing its wounds. We can set a trap, lure it out of its den, and kill it for good."

Paisley nodded. "Good idea, but there's no rush. Let's finish the main mission first and clear out all the grain here."

Everyone agreed. Pushing down their unease, they followed her deeper into the depot.

Five huge grain depots stood in this area, the country's most important strategic reserve before the apocalypse. They were enormous, with staggering stores.

They pushed open the doors of one long-sealed depot. Inside, supplies were stacked to the ceiling.

It was dry and well ventilated, untouched by weather or zombies. Neat stacks of bagged oats, wheat, and flour rose in layers all the way up and covered the floor.

Besides the staples, there were dehydrated vegetables, compressed whole grains, cured meats, vacuum-sealed cooking oil, and all kinds of beans, whole grains, and seeds. Everything anyone could want, in huge quantities.

All five depots together held enough to feed several thousand people at Aetheris Base for at least a year.

Paisley thought it, and it all went into her space.

Towering stacks of grain, crates of oil, dry goods, and seeds vanished into thin air, one after another, at astonishing speed.

First depot, second, third...

Within moments all five depots were empty, nothing left but cold steel shelving and bare floor. The mountain of supplies was in her space.

It was the first time the Falcon Squad had seen her store goods on this scale. They'd known she had a Storage Dimension, but never anything like this.

They froze where they stood, mouths hanging open.

Thomas gasped. "Holy... Paisley, how big is that space? You swallowed all of that in one go?"

Spencer nodded. "Pais, that space is amazing. And your luck is insane. You're like a main character with cheat codes. Every good thing finds you!"

Ashley bounced over with sparkling eyes and fired off questions.

"Paisley, you're incredible! That was such a neat trick! Did everything really go into your space? You're a Storage user, right? Do you have lots of good stuff in there? Any fresh fruit?"

Even steady Orion lost his cool. He stepped closer, curious and worried.

"Pais, can it hold more? There's no way we could have hauled this much grain out in one trip without you. What's the limit? Doesn't it wear you out, moving all this at once? Any side effects, like power drain or mental strain?"

Paisley took an apple from her space and put it in Ashley's hand. "There's fruit. Come find me whenever you want some."

Then she answered Orion. "The space is big, and it can hold more. It's one of the Affinities I awakened. Storing this stuff doesn't hurt. It's fused with my body, like part of me. I move things with a thought, no strain."

While everyone was still gasping, Trovie's soft, excited voice chimed in her head.

Master! There's a hidden basement under the warehouse! It's packed with gold and national treasures. Super valuable!
