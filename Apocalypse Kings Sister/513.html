Chapter 513

The other team's leader was a tall man with a cocky look. It was Chase.

Beside him stood Rachel, along with Owen, Reid, and the rest of the Lennox siblings.

The shameless Rachel and her crew.

Rachel stood by Chase, a smug sneer on her lips, looking down at the opposing team.

Chase stepped forward, his Metal Affinity churning in his palm. Metal shards floated beside him, glinting. His tone was arrogant and bullying.

"Be smart. Hand over the fruit and potatoes in that basket!

After the Crimson Rain, edible crops are hard to find. A bunch of nobodies like you can't hold on to this."

"Do you know who I am? Do you know who's behind me?"

"Hand over the food now, or don't blame us when…"

******

The ordinary team's leader clenched his teeth and his fists.

"We risked our lives for this!

I don't care who you are or who's behind you. Why should we give it to you?

Base rules say whoever finds the supplies keeps them!"

"Are you ignoring the base rules? You're just going to rob us?"

"Rules?"

Chase sneered, his eyes vicious.

"Out here in the wilds, strength is the rule.

Our team is stronger, so the supplies make more sense in our hands.

Don't make this hard. If we fight, none of you will make it back to base alive."

Rachel fanned the flames from the side, her voice sharp.

"Chase, don't waste your breath on them.

With contamination everywhere, clean food is rare. It's a waste in their hands.

Just take it!"

"I'll tell you something. I'm Paisley's sister. You've heard who she is, right? If you want revenge, go find her."

Owen stood beside her, an uneasy look on his face. His eyes happened to flick toward the cave exit and froze.

He saw Paisley's group.

His body went stiff. He tugged at Rachel's sleeve and whispered, "Rachel… look over there."

Rachel shook him off, annoyed, and followed his gaze. When she saw Paisley standing in front, with Elijah and the Thompsons behind her, her smug look froze and her heart dropped.

The first time she'd used Paisley's name, and she'd been caught red-handed.

Then again, she wasn't wrong. Paisley really was her sister.

So she straightened up, lifted her chin, and put on a calm front.

Chase noticed the group coming out of the cave too. When he saw Elijah, his face changed slightly.

Falcon Squad's reputation was known to everyone at the base. He didn't want to take them on head-on, but he hated to give up what was almost in his hands.

Ashley frowned and stepped forward, her voice clear and cold.

"In broad daylight, you're openly robbing your own teammates' supplies outside the city? That's way out of line."

Orion's Metal Affinity stirred, a metallic glint flashing at his fingertip. He spoke evenly.

"The base's mission rules are clear. Supplies found in the field belong to whoever finds them.

Taking by force gets reported to the base, and the penalty isn't light."

Elijah took a step forward, his Lightning Affinity held back, his steady gaze on Chase.

"Captain Sullivan, what's your answer?"

Chase's face went from blue to white. The metal shards in his palm slowly drifted down.

He knew perfectly well that a clash with Falcon Squad would get him nothing.

A flash of resentment crossed Rachel's face as she glared at Paisley.

Her again. She always showed up at the worst moment and ruined everything.

But facing Falcon Squad at full strength, she didn't dare speak. She buried her hatred.

The ordinary team, seeing Paisley's group step in, felt like they'd grabbed a lifeline. Hope sparked in their eyes.

But they'd also heard Rachel announce loud and clear that she was Paisley's sister.

Would Falcon Squad's top team still stand up for them?

A heavy stone hung over them. They exchanged nervous looks.

If the two were really family, would Paisley take her side?

Would they be able to keep the little food they'd risked their lives for?

They unconsciously shifted the basket of potatoes and fruit farther behind them, bracing for a fight, and watched the group from the cave with tension.
