Chapter 250

"Don't be scared! He's just one guy! Affinity runs out eventually!"

"Nobody backs down! All together, wear him down!"

To push them further, he dangled something filthy in front of them, eyes gleaming with greed.

"That woman's prime goods! Once the boss gets tired of her, she's all of yours, taking turns! Everyone gets a piece tonight!"

The words were filthy, shameless.

The ones who'd been ready to back off lit up with greed instead, their fear burned away by lust and want.

With that prize in front of them, they surged forward again, howling, low-tier Affinities flying every which way, fireballs and stone and wind blades raining down on Elijah.

To them, Elijah was the real threat, something to gang up on. Paisley, pretty and slight, was nothing but a pretty prize for the taking.

Nobody paid her any real attention, too drowned in their own want, leering at her without a shred of shame.

The filth of it, said out loud like that, pushed Elijah past his limit.

Ice settled into his eyes. Lightning surged violently around him, crackling up both arms, his presence turning suffocating.

"You just signed your own death warrants."

His voice came out low, cold, and furious.

They'd looked at the woman he loved with eyes like that. That crossed a line.

Their eyes don't need to exist anymore.

He unleashed his Affinity again.

Countless threads of lightning shot out, each one finding the eyes of every man who'd been staring at her.

Zzzt—!

Faster than any of them could dodge, the sparks struck home.

Screams rose from one side of the courtyard to the other.

Their eyes burst, scorched, blood pouring hot between their fingers as they clutched at their faces.

They staggered and fell, convulsing, sobbing in agony.

"My eyes... it hurts..."

******

The leader, whose stare had been the filthiest of all, took the worst of it. Both eyes burned through, pain wracking his body until his mind nearly broke.

The ones still standing froze mid-charge, every scrap of greed burned out of them, replaced by pure terror.

This man was too strong, too protective of her. They stood no chance.

"Blind! I'm blind! My eyes!"

"Help! We can't fight him!"

"What are we waiting for... run..."

The handful still standing turned to flee, shaking with fear.

Right then, Paisley spoke up, her face utterly calm.

"Let me handle it."

She never cared much what people thought of her. But what this gang had done made her sick.

Grow.

The ground shook violently as thick, jade-green vines burst from the earth, cold and laced with paralytic venom, flooding the entire courtyard in seconds.

The vines wove together into a dense green cage, trapping every man who'd tried to run or lay wailing on the ground.

Thorns bit deep into flesh, the venom spreading through their veins instantly, leaving every one of them rigid, their Affinities gone, too weak even to scream.

In seconds, more than twenty men hung suspended in the vines, helpless, reduced to muffled groans, every scrap of their earlier swagger gone.

Clean. Quick. Done.

Elijah stood beside her, lightning fading, staring at her calm profile with something close to awe.

She's always like this. Calm. Devastating. One move, and it's over.

The others, finally hearing the commotion, burst out of the house.

Orion, Thomas, and Spencer came out ready to fight, Affinities already flaring. Then they saw the courtyard and just stopped.
