Chapter 21

Before Paisley could answer, Orion cut in with a grin. "Don't worry about it. Pais's space is already up to 500 square feet.

"We cleaned out the whole supermarket and the storeroom. Food, clothes, everyday stuff, we've got it all, so the whole team is set for the trip home."

Paisley met Elijah's surprised look and nodded sweetly. "Yeah. Orion and I just took down a Level Two mutant together.

"The Level Two core leveled up my ability and my space. It's about 500 square feet now."

She'd purposely hidden how big it really was. She planned to keep the other half for herself, to stash gold, luxury goods, and stuff like that.

Elijah looked surprised. "Good thing we ran into you," he said. "The trip back just got a lot easier."

Orion laughed and clapped him on the shoulder. "Wow, look at you, actually complimenting someone. That's a first."

Then, he puffed out his chest, unable to hide how proud he was. "But seriously, Pais's amazing.

"You should've seen that Level Two upstairs. It was insanely fast. I didn't even have time to react, and she took it out with one strike.

"I had no idea Level Two zombies and plants had crystal cores in them. If Paisley hadn't told me, I never would've known you could use them to level up.

"So on the way back, let's not skip any Level Two mutants we run into. If we grab as many cores as we can, we'll all get stronger, and the trip home will be safer."

Paisley followed the two of them quietly and didn't say a word, keeping up her sweet, obedient act. It was like they weren't even talking about her.

Before long, they were back on the first floor with the rest of the group. Everyone had a stuffed backpack on their shoulders and bags of food and supplies in their arms. They all looked pretty happy with their haul.

Ashley was carrying two heavy bags. As soon as she spotted Paisley, her face lit up, and she rushed over and shoved one of the bags into Paisley's hands.

"Paisley, I figured you didn't have time to pack anything, so I put this together just for you. Here, take it!"

Paisley held the bag, and a lump rose in her throat.

In her past life, she'd always been the one who got used and bossed around.

The four Lennoxes had only ever looked out for themselves. Not one of them had ever thought about her, and to them, she'd always been the least important person in the room.

But now, in this second life, the people around her thought of her first and looked out for her in everything.

That kind of real, hard-won care was slowly thawing a heart that had been frozen for years.

Besides, she could use a bag of supplies as cover, so nobody would get suspicious.

Paisley looked up at Ashley's earnest face and gave her a real smile. "Thanks, Ashley. You're a sweet little sister."

Ashley threw her arms around her, beaming. "You finally think of me as your sister! I'm so happy!

"From now on, you're my real sister, Paisley, and I'll protect you with my life!"

She'd wanted this for so long, and Paisley had finally accepted her. Ashley's eyes welled up as she hugged her even tighter.

Wrapped tight in Ashley's arms, Paisley froze awkwardly, unsure what to do with herself.

In three years of the apocalypse, she'd seen more betrayal, scheming, and killing than she could count. She'd almost forgotten what it felt like to be cared for, to be hugged by someone who meant it.

It had been way too long since she'd felt warmth this pure, with no strings attached.

That warmth seeped into her chest and slowly melted the ice that had been wrapped around her heart for years.

Thomas and Spencer watched for a second, then couldn't resist stepping in and wrapping their arms around both girls.

Orion watched his family hug, his eyes soft. Then, he walked over with a smile and leaned down to wrap them all in his arms. He'd gotten back something he'd thought was lost for good, and he was overjoyed.

This is perfect!

After all these years, they'd finally found Paisley. In that moment, their family was whole again.

Not far off, Elijah stood quietly. His fingers twitched, and the hint of a smile softened his eyes.

He was genuinely happy for Orion.

He'd come to Southspire on a secret mission. He'd never imagined he'd end up helping his best friend find his long-lost sister, something Orion had been dreaming of for years.

But what surprised him most was Paisley herself, the Thompsons' long-lost daughter.

She seemed sweet and obedient, but she was an incredible fighter, and she was sharp. She had more secrets than he could count.

He had to admit, though, that with her on the team, the dangerous road ahead would be a lot easier.

But right in the middle of that warm family moment, a deafening crash rang out!
