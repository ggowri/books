Chapter 114

Paisley was dangerous, and it didn't stand a chance against her.

If it didn't swear loyalty, it would die.

It didn't hesitate.

The next second, its trunk swayed gently, and a wisp of pure plant energy from its core drifted out. It wrapped obediently around Paisley's wrist and sealed a life-bond pact all on its own.

The pact was made, and Paisley and the tree were bound together.

Paisley's smile grew. She carefully poured spirit water over its roots and trunk as a reward.

The mutant willow soaked it up, and its green glow flared brighter. It grew even stronger, and its countless branches stretched and swayed happily.

"In you go," Paisley said softly.

With a single thought, the gentle power of her Storage Dimension wrapped around the whole tree. She transplanted it, roots, dirt, and all, right next to the spirit spring inside her dimension.

The second the mutant willow took root, energy surged through the whole dimension, and it got visibly richer right before her eyes.

Trovie cheered happily.

[It worked! Congratulations, Master! You got yourself a mutant plant!

[What a steal! You're so lucky!]

Paisley looked at the mutant willow in her dimension. She really was pretty lucky.

This mutant willow was only going to get stronger.

Just by swearing loyalty to her, it had already climbed to mid Level Four.

It really was something special.

As for her own Plant Affinity, she could feel it starting to loosen up. One more lucky break, and she should be able to level up.

She glanced at the darkening sky and gently ruffled the little white cat's head.

"Okay, let's go meet back up with everyone."

At the same time, on the other side of the district, Elijah had just reached Kelly. She, Rachel, Jasper, and the three Lennox brothers were all trapped inside an old, run-down house, surrounded by a massive swarm of mutant rats.

In the dim night, countless dark shapes scurried across the ground, along the walls, and through the cracks in the stone steps. There were so many that they stretched as far as the eye could see.

They were all low-level mutants, about the size of a house cat, and they couldn't do much damage. One on its own wasn't scary at all.

But there were insanely many of them. They kept pouring out of the sewers, the storm drains, and every dark corner of the street in a thick, black wave that cut off every way out.

Their shrill, piercing squeaks never stopped, and the noise was enough to drive anyone crazy.

The rats' red eyes glowed eerily in the dark. They bared their tiny, sharp fangs and lunged at the group in a frenzy, caked in mud and filth and reeking of rot.

The three Lennox brothers, Kelly, and the others were already panicking. They were pale and flailing, fighting with everything they had just to keep the rats off them.

But the rats kept coming, wave after wave, with no fear at all. Every time one group went down, another swarmed in right behind it. There was no end to them.

These low-level mutant rats had no minds of their own. All they knew was bloodlust. The swarm around them kept growing, and the circle kept closing in.

Everyone was out of stamina. Their arms ached, their energy was drained, and more and more holes opened up in their defense. The rats' teeth had ripped through their clothes in several places and left thin, bloody scratches on their skin.

The cuts oozed blood, itching and stinging at the same time.

By now, the Lennox brothers, Rachel, Kelly, and Jasper were completely falling apart. Their faces were white as paper, and all they could do was keep fighting on autopilot.

Trapped by the endless flood of mutant rats, they had nowhere to go and nowhere to hide.

Elijah darted forward and charged straight into the swarm to help.

Purple-blue lightning crackled in his palm, and bolts swept out in every direction. Whole clusters of rats were fried on the spot, and the smell of burnt fur filled the air.

But even with him in the fight, it still wasn't enough to save them.

His Lightning Affinity was powerful, but it couldn't handle a swarm this huge. New rats kept pouring out of the sewers, and he couldn't kill them as fast as they charged in.

Before long, Elijah's energy was running low.

He could wipe out whole swarms of rats at once, but he couldn't break through the circle. There was no way he could lead everyone out.

The others had already pushed themselves to their absolute limit. They were too weak to keep fighting.

Elijah stood firm in front of them, swinging his blade again and again.

Even with all his combat experience, he couldn't avoid getting bitten.

Just then, Paisley showed up in a hurry with the little white cat, along with Orion and the others, who'd just come back from scouting.

The second they spotted the dark swarm covering the open ground, they were on high alert.

Orion's face turned grim.

"It's a swarm of low-level mutant rats, and there are way too many of them. If we fight them head-on, we don't stand a chance. We could end up trapped too."
