Chapter 259

He sensed her presence and reached for his weapon, but she struck first with her Psychic Affinity.

An invisible blade of will tore through his mind. He dropped without a sound.

She walked straight to the storeroom and shoved the heavy wooden door open.

Inside, the storeroom was packed floor to ceiling with sacks of rice and flour. He'd probably stripped every village nearby of its grain.

She left a tenth of it behind, enough for the girls upstairs to survive on.

The rest went into her dimension.

Trovie's voice turned giddy as it rattled off the numbers.

[Host! Successfully stockpiled a huge amount of survival food, bonus points: 1,000!] [Previously collected gold and jewelry auto-converted: 50,000 points!] [Current total: 374,060!]

Paisley sighed.

[After all that, I'm still not even a third of the way there.] [Trovie, isn't there anything else? I'm cleaning out whole nests of killers and hauling supplies, and the progress is crawling. When does the Storage Dimension actually level up?]

Trovie panicked, right on cue.

[Host, you've still got an unused premium lucky draw! Want to use it now?]

A faint smile crossed Paisley's eyes, though her face stayed composed.

[Do it. Draw now.]

Trovie:

[Got it! Premium draw panel opening! Spinning now—]

A ribbon of gold light swirled in front of her, spinning in brilliant color, the premium draw's signature flash of light.

The wheel spun fast, prize after prize flickering past, until it stopped on a clear, ice-blue badge.

Trovie:

[Draw successful! Congratulations, Host, you've unlocked a brand-new mutant Affinity: Ice-Water Affinity!] [Affinity overview: a top-tier Water Affinity variant. Can conjure ice blades, freeze whole areas, and bind enemies in place, deadly in both offense and defense. Can also control water, nourish and grow living things.]

Power, warm and vast, surged through her, cold clean water and bone-deep frost weaving together perfectly in her veins.

She lifted a hand, and clear water flowed from her fingertips, then froze instantly into sharp slivers of ice, shifting between forms as easily as thought.

Layered on top of her already solid Level Five peak Water Affinity, this new power pushed her overall strength up another full tier.

Trovie cheered, thrilled.

[Host, you're so lucky! Ice-Water Affinity is insanely rare, barely anyone in the whole apocalypse has it! With this, Host is basically unstoppable!]

A mutant Water Affinity, pure and devastating.

This trip to Wexford had turned out well. Killers dealt with, supplies hauled in.

She'd taken out a warlord, freed a village's worth of captives, and come out with a top-tier new Affinity to boot.

A small smile touched her eyes.

Time to meet back up with Elijah and Orion.

She let the ice-blue glow fade and headed back toward the basement.

By the time she got there, the basement looked nothing like the slaughterhouse it had been.

Elijah and Orion had cleared out every last zombie.

Every caged villager and survivor had been freed.

They helped each other out of the dark, faces stunned with relief, barely believing they'd made it.

Orion spotted Paisley approaching and hurried over, checking her over before relaxing enough to report.

"Everything down here's handled. Zombies, the men, all of it. We got everyone out."

Elijah stood a step behind, his eyes gentle on her.

"Let's head back and rest for the night. We'll move again at dawn."

Paisley nodded.

The long night faded slowly, the sky turning pale at the edges, dawn light cutting through the gloom over the broken village of Wexford.
