Chapter 291

A flame sprang to life at her fingertip, clear and golden-red, warm yet carrying an undeniable weight of destruction, the air around it rising in temperature instantly, a few fallen twigs on the ground silently charring to ash.

Everyone in the room froze, their expressions shifting into open shock.

Ashley's eyes went round, her arms stiffening slightly around the fox, her face full of disbelief.

"Pais! Is that even possible?

Bonding with a mutant beast can awaken a whole new Affinity?

So how many do you have now?

That's incredible!"

Elijah's gaze stayed locked on the flame, something in his understanding of the world quietly rearranging itself.

He'd never once heard of anyone gaining a new Affinity through bonding with a beast.

Watching her break through her limits again and again, growing more dazzling with every step, he felt a flicker of urgency alongside a deep swell of happiness.

The person he loved kept growing stronger, more remarkable. He couldn't afford to fall behind.

He needed to push himself harder, grow faster, so he could stand beside her — someone who deserved to be there.

Orion, Thomas, and Spencer exchanged a glance, genuinely thrilled for their sister.

Watching her strength surge like this filled them with nothing but pride.

With power like this behind her, she'd have that much more protection on the dangerous road ahead.

Only Kelly, standing off to the side, felt her fingers curl tight at her sides, envy churning in her chest, tangled with jealousy she couldn't quite hide.

She stared fixedly at the golden-red flame dancing at Paisley's fingertip, bitterness twisting in her gut.

Why.

Why did everything come so easily to Paisley?

One trip outside, and she'd stumbled onto a rare Level Seven mutant fox, had it bond with her on the spot, and walked away with a brand-new Fire Affinity, stronger than ever.

Paisley had already been absurdly strong. Now, with Fire Affinity on top of everything else, taking her down had just gotten that much harder.

Resentment clogged Kelly's throat. She lowered her eyes, hiding the dark thoughts churning underneath, keeping her expression carefully arranged into something that looked like genuine joy, giving nothing away.

Night settled in slowly, the evening breeze turning cool as everyone gathered around the table for dinner.

The mood was quiet and easy. Elijah set down his fork and laid out the plan ahead, calm and steady.

"Tomorrow we head for Stillwater Base.

Their channels are solid, we should be able to get in touch with Aetheris without much trouble.

If things go smoothly, we might be able to fly back directly."

His gaze swept the group.

"Everyone's picked up a good number of crystal cores clearing zombies these past few days. Tonight's a good chance to rest and push for a breakthrough. Let's all try to level up at least once."

Everyone nodded, eyes bright with anticipation.

In this world, strength was everything.

Dinner wrapped up quickly, and everyone went their separate ways.

Paisley carried the little red fox up to a private room on the villa's second floor.

She pushed the door open, stepped inside, and locked it firmly behind her, sealing out every sound and prying eye.

With a thought, she slipped into her storage space.

She'd upgraded to Level Four trader so quickly today, she hadn't had a moment to properly take in the changes to her space.

Now, settling into the quiet, she felt the full scale of the transformation sweep over her.

The entire space had expanded to a full ten million square feet, vast and open, stretching as far as she could see.

The old cottage had transformed into an elegant, five-story mansion, sitting at the heart of the space, grand and airy, drenched in light.

The once-modest spring that had trickled gently now stretched into a fifty-thousand-square-foot lake, the water crystal clear, sunlight dancing across its surface, dense spirit energy curling over it — ten times its original size.

******

Meanwhile, the farmland available for planting had grown considerably too, the soil rich and fertile, thick with spirit energy, more than enough to grow massive quantities of grain, vegetables, and fruit.

A flicker of delight crossed Paisley's eyes, and she gave the order at once.

Trovie, put every bit of the open farmland to use. Plant it all — grain, vegetables, every kind of fruit.

Trovie responded.

Order received, Host! Starting right away.

Trovie worked fast, the mechanical display flickering rapidly as the entire open stretch of farmland was automatically leveled, tilled, and sown, seeds settling into neat rows, ready to take root.
