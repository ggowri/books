Chapter 112

Paisley smirked and kept digging. There was another layer underneath, and most of the rest of the safe was packed with military-grade weapons.

Sharp combat knives, brand-new pistols, and compact submachine guns were lined up in neat rows. Next to them were sorted boxes of ammo, packed full of bullets, along with several powerful grenades.

[Jackpot! Master, this is all top-tier stuff. We really hit the mother lode this time!]

Trovie's little voice was bursting with glee.

Paisley smiled faintly.

In the apocalypse, weapons were priceless, especially full sets of military guns with plenty of ammo. They were even rarer and more valuable than gold, and they could save the whole team when it counted.

Without another glance, she waved her hand. The whole heavy metal safe, along with every gold bar, stack of cash, weapon, and bullet inside, vanished into her Storage Dimension.

When she was done, the little white cat, which had been standing guard by the door the whole time, rubbed against her leg. It lifted its snowy head and waited patiently for praise.

Paisley bent down, ruffled its fur, and popped a chocolate lollipop from her Storage Dimension into its mouth. "Good job, kitty.

"Okay, we've got everything. Let's go."

Just as she turned to leave, a buried memory from her past life suddenly came back to her.

In her past life, she'd been forced to come to Rivergate with Rachel once, but that had been two years further down the road.

Back then, Rachel had been desperate to get stronger fast, so she'd been hunting for high-level mutant creatures everywhere. She'd heard about a terrifying Level Seven mutant willow tree in Rivergate, and top-tier ability users had come in droves to hunt it.

In the end, every single one of them had been killed by the willow's creepy, deadly power. Countless people died, and it became the nightmare of the whole region.

When she remembered that, Paisley's eyes narrowed.

She'd been reborn. If she found it two years early, maybe she could stop that tragedy before it happened.

If she could track down the mutant willow before it grew strong, she could kill a huge future threat before it started and save countless ability users who'd otherwise die. Maybe she could even tame it and make it hers.

Paisley didn't waste another second. She scooped up the little white cat, held it close, and hurried back to the elevator. She pressed the down button and followed her memories toward where the mutant willow had been hiding.

Then, she crossed the empty shopping street and circled behind the building to a quiet riverbank.

It was the quietest spot by the water on the whole outskirts. The river wound through a soft haze of mist, and rows of willow trees lined both banks.

A spring breeze drifted by, and the willow branches swayed gently. All that green stood out vividly against the bleak, ruined streets.

There were willow trees all along the riverbank, some tall, some short, with their branches tangled together. There were over 20 of them.

The normal willows had dry, yellow leaves, tainted by the deadness of the apocalypse. They were scattered along both banks, and they all looked the same.

Paisley slowed down, frowning.

With so many trees that all looked alike, how was she supposed to tell which one would become that infamous Level Seven mutant willow?

She slowly walked forward, studying each tree one by one. At the same time, she quietly sent out her psychic senses to check every tree's life force and energy.

The normal willows were lifeless, with withered roots and no energy at all.

Only the one closest to the middle of the river was different.

Its trunk was taller and thicker than the others, and its bark glowed with a faint, glossy green light. Its countless branches swayed gently even though there wasn't any wind, wrapped in a thin, misty layer of water energy.

Even though it was hiding its presence on purpose, it couldn't hide the spark of life and awareness inside it, or the faint hint of hostility underneath.

That was the one.

All the other willows were just dead wood. Only this one had already become a Level Three mutant willow.

It was only Level Three, but it had already awakened a mind of its own.

Trovie chimed in eagerly.

[Master, this tree is packed with pure energy! If you plant it in your dimension, the whole place will fill up with energy. It's a total steal, so grab it now!]

Paisley nodded. [Now that's a plan!]

She circled the tree, rubbing her chin. How was she supposed to tame this thing?

The mutant willow seemed to sense that someone had zeroed in on it, and it went tense.

Its soft, swaying branches went stiff and gleamed with cold light. The mist around it churned and gathered, giving off a faint pressure as it watched Paisley warily, ready to drive her off at any second.
