Chapter 486

Nobody knew where her shooting had come from.

Paisley remembered some unpleasant memories.

In her last life, she had never awakened a single Affinity. She was just the smallest, most helpless person in the apocalypse.

With no talent to lean on, no power to protect her, she had trained her body and her aim with everything she had, to live, to protect her family, to gather supplies, and to survive.

While others rested, she held a rifle steady in the snow and trained all night.

While others fought with their powers, she forced her body through the hardest paths, steeled her nerve, and endured what others wouldn't touch.

But in the end, even after she'd given everything and protected everyone, what she got back was betrayal and abandonment from every person she cared about, and a bitter death full of regret.

Luckily, heaven took pity and gave her a second life.

This time, she would never let anyone walk over her. She would also take revenge for her past self.

Boom!

A huge crash from the wilderness yanked her back.

The black helicopter slammed into the snowy field. It broke apart on impact, burning fuel shot up in a column of fire, black smoke covered half the sky, and metal fragments flew. The shockwave rattled the whole snowfield.

"Go! Let's check it out!"

Elijah snapped back, pushed down every feeling, and sprinted toward the crash site.

Downstairs, Orion, Thomas, and the others had finished clearing the low-level horde. They saw the fire and smoke in the distance, ended the fight, and followed fast.

Snow kept falling. Fine flakes landed on the hot wreckage and melted to water, then froze to ice again. Hot and cold mist drifted across the field, strange and heavy.

The group reached the crash site quickly.

All that was left was the charred, broken fuselage, snapped wings, twisted metal, and ash scattered around. Open flames still licked the cracks, and heat rolled out.

They spread out and searched every inch, making sure the masked man wasn't hiding or using the confusion to slip away.

"No footprints leading away."

"No signs of life in the wreckage!"

"No body, no mask fragments, nothing at all!"

........

A helicopter that fell from that height and blew apart should have left no survivors. Even a lucky survivor would have been badly hurt and stuck at the scene.

But after searching the whole field, they found no body, no blood, no scraps of clothing, not even a trace of Affinity power.

That cold, mysterious, scheming masked man had vanished into thin air!

Paisley stood at the edge of the snow, her eyes dark, her gaze sweeping over the burning wreckage. Alarm bells rang in her mind.

This man is not simple.

He may even be someone I know. Why else would he wear a mask?

He could control high-tier zombies, set up an ambush to kill Elijah, owned a private armed helicopter, and escaped from the crash without a sound or a trace.

His strength, his cards, and his identity were all far more terrifying and hidden than anyone thought.

Elijah looked at the empty crash site, his brow knotted, deep wariness in his eyes.

"He got away."

His voice was low and heavy, with a trace of caution. "He's tricky and careful and he's targeting me specifically. He keeps coming at me but stays in the shadows and never shows his face."

"He's either from the Quinn family or the Lennox family."

"He's definitely an enemy of the Blackwoods."

"Too bad he got away."

If Paisley hadn't kept pulling things back, he'd probably be dead already.

Ashley stepped closer, frowning at the wreckage. "No body. Who is this guy? That's so creepy."

"Whoever he is."

Paisley slowly pulled her gaze back. Her voice was cold and certain, a hidden edge in her eyes.

"He keeps coming at us. Sooner or later he'll slip."

In this life, she had the power and the strength to protect the people near her. She would settle every old score.
