Chapter 178

But they all knew that Paisley was the reason they'd finished such a high-level mission and come back alive and in one piece.

If it weren't for her, they'd all be dead by now, and they'd never have had the chance to sit here splitting rewards.

Madison was the first to speak, and she was dead serious as she objected.

"I don't agree with splitting it evenly. Paisley did the most. Without her, we all would've died in that flower field today, so an even split isn't fair to her."

"Exactly!" Mason agreed right away, his voice earnest.

"Paisley's the one who insisted on taking the hardest Level Six mission in the first place. She cleared the way and protected us the whole time, and she even handed out storage tokens for our supplies. From start to finish, she did the most work and took the biggest risks.

"Paisley should get a bigger share."

Marcus nodded firmly. "I agree. An even split ignores what our strongest fighter put in, and it's not fair to Paisley."

Ashley blinked her big, clear eyes and looked at Paisley earnestly. "Paisley, you did more than anyone, so you should get the biggest share."

Thomas gave a small nod. "We already got plenty of crystal cores from clearing out zombies along the way. Paisley did the real work on this mission, so she should definitely get more."

Without exception, all of them agreed Paisley should get a bigger share.

Thomas turned to her. "So, Paisley, what do you say?"

Faced with their sincere insistence, Paisley felt warm inside. Her face softened, and a faint smile tugged at her lips as she answered in a calm but firm voice.

"No need. Let's just split it evenly.

"We're all teammates who've been through life and death together, and we'll be working as a team for a long time. I can't get special treatment on every mission."

She looked around at all of them, her gaze clear and honest, a little exasperated.

"Just split it evenly. If you treat me differently every time, I'll feel too awkward to go on missions and fight alongside you guys again.

"Besides, we watch each other's backs, so there's no need to keep score."

She stood firm, so they let it go and agreed to the even split.

These guys really are the best.

Without meaning to, Paisley thought back to her past life, and her chest ached a little.

In her past life, no matter how hard she worked, how many people she protected, or how much deadly danger she shouldered, everyone had taken it for granted.

She was always the one charging into danger first. But when it came time to divide up supplies and rewards, she always got the least and was the one everyone forgot.

Looking back, none of it had been worth it.

Luckily, in this life, she had teammates who genuinely cared about her, who were grateful, and who always had each other's backs.

As her thoughts drifted, she gave a faint smile, a cold glint in her eyes.

Today, she'd used her psychic control to ambush Rachel's group twice, and that was enough to teach them a lesson.

This had only been a warning. If there was a next time, she'd hit them even harder.

Soon, Thomas split all the rewards evenly seven ways and sent everyone's share to their wristband accounts.

The moment the rewards landed, the other six exchanged a quick, knowing glance.

Each of them took 200 Level Two crystal cores and 100 Level Three crystal cores out of their own share and transferred them to Paisley.

They still owed Paisley another 100 Level Three crystal cores.

Paisley's account jumped by a full 1,200 Level Two crystal cores and 600 Level Three crystal cores.

With that done, everyone headed off to rest.

Soon, Paisley was the only one left in the room.

She looked down at the massive pile of crystal cores, a faint smile curving her lips.

What a great haul today.

With a thought, she slipped into her Storage Dimension and called out. [Trovie, add up everything we got today and give me the full count.]

A second later, Trovie's thrilled, high-pitched squeal exploded in her head. Its little voice trembled with excitement as it rattled off every item at top speed.

[Master, Master! I've checked every last thing!

[Kill rewards: 180 Level Two crystal cores, 50 Level Three, 15 Level Four, five Level Five, and one Level Six Plant crystal core! That Level Six Plant core alone is a priceless rarity. We made out like bandits!]
