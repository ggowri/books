Chapter 261

Elijah and Paisley walked side by side toward the supermarket's front doors.

The closer they got, the heavier the rot in the air, something thicker and wetter than ordinary zombie stench, clinging and uneasy.

Elijah caught the wrongness in the air and stopped, leaning in close to warn her.

"Careful. Stay behind me. There's probably something high-tier in there."

Lightning hummed faintly around him, every muscle ready.

Paisley's eyes narrowed as she sent out her Psychic Affinity, an invisible web settling over the whole building, mapping everything inside.

She laid it out clearly.

"There's a Level Six zombie on the third floor. That's the real threat."

"Three Level Fives on the second. Worth watching too."

"The rest are low-tier. Easy enough, but stay sharp anyway."

As she spoke, a Level Three zombie by the door turned and lunged at the sound of her voice.

Paisley flicked her wrist, drawing the Frostbloom Sword, her new Ice-Water Affinity spreading frost down the blade.

One flash, one clean cut.

The zombie never got close before its head split open and it dropped.

Elijah watched, genuinely impressed.

"I can barely sense danger in general. You're pulling out exact zombie levels. That's incredible."

His eyes drifted to the sword, curious.

"And this sword. Something's different about it."

"I don't remember it being this cold before. Is that new?"

"This thing's not ordinary. Feels like there's nothing it can't cut through."

Paisley rubbed her nose, a little self-conscious.

Truth was, the frost was just her fresh Ice-Water Affinity, tacked onto the blade on a whim. Mostly for the look. Mostly to seem cool.

And the precise readings were just her Psychic Affinity doing its job.

Naturally stronger than his.

Elijah, one of the strongest in this world, had only one Affinity. If he knew she had Plant, Ice-Water, and a rare Psychic Affinity all at once, he might start looking at her like some kind of anomaly.

People's hearts were hard to read in this world. Best not to reveal her Psychic Affinity carelessly.

Keep the blade sheathed. That was how you lasted.

She let it go without elaborating.

By then they'd reached the entrance, and together they pushed open the heavy, battered doors.

Creeeak—

******

The sound echoed through the empty store.

The long-dormant building stirred, low-tier zombies surging toward the door from every direction, a dark, screeching wave.

Elijah's face hardened, and he stepped forward to shield her, voice certain.

"These are all low-tier. I've got it."

"Go look around. See what you can find."

Paisley nodded.

The next moment, Kitty, who'd been curled on her shoulder, surged in size, white fur bristling, growing several times over.

It leapt forward and swiped, sending several zombies flying, heads cracking open on impact.

At the same time, Paisley's vines tore up through the floor, catching every zombie in their path.

Thorns punched through skulls, extracting cores in one smooth motion, clearing the area in seconds.

A flicker of disappointment crossed her eyes.

The shelves were nearly bare, whatever scraps remained picked clean by survivors long before them. Just an empty shell.

Resources were running thinner every day. Places like this would only get emptier.
