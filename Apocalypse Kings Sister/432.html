Chapter 432

She knew that without quick treatment, Elijah could die.

And now that the serpent was wary, destroying its core fast would be even harder.

Save him first. Elijah can't die.

In that split second she pulled her strike. The blade swerved and chopped down on the spot it had already wounded, cutting through flesh to the bone. Dark green blood sprayed out.

Using the recoil, she flipped backward off the vines, gave up her best chance to finish the mutant, and landed beside Elijah.

"Elijah!"

She dropped to her knees and caught him as he sagged. His skin was ice cold under her fingers.

His face was paper white, with blood foam at the corners of his mouth, and his combat suit was soaked in red and black. The poison was crushing his Affinity, and his presence had grown so faint she could barely feel it.

And this was more than poison. Paisley could sense a cold, twisted mental contaminant seeping through his blood into his mind, hammering at his defenses.

It was a toxin unique to lab-made mutants. It didn't just wreck the body, it ate away at the mind. Left untreated, he'd turn into a mindless zombie.

His pupils began to drift. The steady sharpness in his eyes went dull and gray, and his thoughts blurred and fell apart.

He tried to lift his eyes and say something, but only blood bubbled from his lips.

Then his head lolled to one side. He passed out in her arms, his breath so faint it seemed about to stop.

Across the room, the serpent mutant glared at them with cold, vicious eyes. Its wound still wouldn't close. It was hurt, but still terrifying.

Yet it sensed that Paisley was dangerous. The sword in her hand had scared it. It didn't dare stay and fight.

It let out a hoarse, hateful hiss, its eyes full of murder, and swept one long look over Elijah and Paisley as if memorizing their faces.

Then its thirty-three-foot tail whipped around, and it vanished like a black shadow down the broken alley behind the warehouse and into the dark, dead city.

The killing intent faded. The warehouse was left with poison fog, shattered concrete, blood, and the man in her arms, barely alive.

Paisley knelt there, holding him steady, two fingers on his wrist. His veins were wrecked, his life nearly drained, and the mental poison was still raging in his mind.

Her eyes turned cold.

She hadn't expected a Level Seven mutant to be this strong. Its healing was absurd, and it carried a mental toxin on top of that.

If it kept growing stronger, did humanity have any hope? Sooner or later it would hang over everyone's heads.

But first, Elijah.

She laid him flat on clean ground. His eyes were shut and his face had gone a sickly blue-white. Blood still stained his lips, and dark stains covered his chest, his skin tinged with the eerie color of venom.

His breath was shallow and faint, his pulse thin and erratic. In his mind, that icy contamination kept churning, eating away at his thoughts and his life.

Paisley steadied her breathing and focused completely.

She laid her slender palm over his worst wound.

A warm, vivid green glow flowed from her hand. Pure, high-level Plant Affinity seeped through his skin, gentle but firm, and poured into his bloodstream.
