Chapter 277

Thomas's Plant Affinity surged, vines tearing up from the earth in a dense tangle.

Thick green light burst up from below, roots and vines exploding out of the ground, weaving into a massive defensive net in seconds, holding the line and pinning down the lead wave of wolves.

Right behind him, Spencer's Fire Affinity detonated, fireballs bursting one after another into the sky.

Each blast punched into the diving crows, flame and heat rolling through the air, cutting off their attack lines entirely, scorched feathers scattering everywhere.

Ashley and Marcus threw up Earth Affinity shields together.

The combined force sent heavy slabs of earth and rock rising layer upon layer, forming a thick wall that sealed off the rear and blind spots, cutting off any chance of the wolves circling around.

Mason's Wind Affinity swept the battlefield.

Fierce gales howled, kicking up debris and dead branches, turning them into a storm of high-speed blades that tore through the wolves pressing in from every side.

Madison released her Water Affinity.

A fine curtain of water spread out, dousing the stray flames still smoldering on the earth walls and vines.

Beside her, Orion unleashed his Metal Affinity.

A burst of golden light cracked around him, sharp metal fragments tearing themselves loose from scrap iron and stone across the ground, forming a storm of high-speed blades that swept the entire field.

Metal Affinity cut through almost anything. Any black wolf caught in its path was torn open instantly, bones shattering, and the diving crows had their wings skewered clean through, their assault gutted by more than half in seconds.

Elijah's Lightning Affinity surged skyward.

Silver-blue lightning cracked and roared, a vast net of electricity blanketing the airspace, targeting the nimble, dangerous crows precisely. Each bolt struck true, electrocuting and paralyzing one crow after another.

Paisley stood tall and sharp, the Frostbloom Sword in her grip, Ice-Water Affinity flowing around her.

Frost coated the blade's edge. With every swing, countless ice shards tore through the air, cutting cold and clean. Any wolf struck froze solid instantly, motion stopped dead, all resistance gone.

The little white cat perched on her shoulder suddenly swelled in size, white fur bristling, unleashing its Ice Affinity at full force.

******

A wave of brutal cold swept across half the battlefield. Several of the closest wolves froze solid where they stood, limbs locked up entirely. The cat leapt free, one swipe of its claw tearing a wolf's head clean off.

The tide of battle had turned completely.

What had started as a brutal double-pincer trap became a one-sided slaughter under the group's seamless teamwork.

On the ground, the wolves pinned, frozen, and shredded by wind blades dropped one after another, their howls fading to nothing.

In the air, crows struck by lightning, sliced by metal, blasted by fireballs fell from the sky in a steady stream, scorched bodies piling up below.

Only the last high-tier crow remained, and seeing its entire flock wiped out, it panicked, no longer willing to fight, beating its wings in a desperate bid to flee the mountains.

Paisley's eyes went cold. She pushed off the ground and shot into the air.

The Frostbloom Sword swung down, and a single strike sealed the fleeing bird in ice.

A massive blue frost-blade of energy tore through the sky, catching up to the fleeing crow in an instant and piercing straight through its body.

A sharp crack rang out as ice spread across its entire form. The crow, frozen mid-flight, dropped out of the sky and shattered on the ground.

In just a few minutes, the crows and wolves were all wiped out, the valley falling back into silence, nothing left but the scattered remains of mutant beasts.

The group pulled back their powers, dug out the crystal cores, and burned the bodies.

Just then, Trovie's soft chime rang in Paisley's mind.

[Host, a temporary mission has been triggered!]

[Mission: Collect all supplies from the armory. Kill the Level Seven mutant giant serpent inside.]

[Base reward: 5,000 points!]

[Additional note: the armory is hiding a large stash of gold and jewels. Good luck, Host!]

Paisley couldn't help but grumble after hearing it out.

As much as I'd love that stash, isn't your system being a little cheap? Only 5,000 points for killing a Level Seven mutant beast?

That's a Level Seven mutant giant serpent we're talking about.

Trovie scrambled to reassure her.

Host, every little bit adds up! Even a mosquito's still meat — the points pile up fast!
