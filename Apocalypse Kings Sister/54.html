Chapter 54

"We can also pick out some high-performance vehicles. We're going to need all of this down the road."

Mason's eyes lit up. "There's a big car dealership just up ahead. Let's hit that first and grab some cars and supplies!"

Everyone perked up. Who didn't have a thing for cars?

But once the apocalypse hit, survival came first. Every hobby had to take a back seat, and just staying alive was hard enough.

Now, they had plenty of room and the chance to grab whatever they wanted. None of them could help feeling a little excited.

The car swung around and sped toward the dealership.

Within minutes, a big, flashy showroom came into view.

The dealership took up a huge lot. Its showroom had floor-to-ceiling glass walls, and rows of brand-new cars sat parked outside.

The apocalypse had hit so fast that nearly all of them were still in perfect shape. Only a few had a thin layer of dust on them.

The front doors stood wide open. Inside, it was dead quiet, except for the occasional shuffling footstep.

They got out and looked around. The lot had everything, from sedans and SUVs to off-road performance trucks, all brand new and in mint condition. In the apocalypse, that was a seriously rare haul.

"There are zombies inside. Not many." Elijah's eyes narrowed as he spotted a few dark shapes moving around the showroom, and he kept his voice low.

With a single thought, Paisley sent several dark green vines bursting out of the ground. They moved so fast they were just a blur, silently wrapping around the Level One zombies wandering inside.

The venomous vines tightened, and the paralyzing toxin spread through them fast. The zombies didn't even have time to snarl or struggle. They went stiff, then collapsed limp on the floor, all in a matter of seconds.

Orion and Mason had seen Paisley's ability before, but it still amazed them.

Even Elijah couldn't help giving her a thumbs-up.

Mason got excited and headed straight for the door.

Just as he reached for the glass door, Paisley yelled, "Mason, get back! It's dangerous!"

In a flash, several thick, dark green venom vines shot out of the ground. They wrapped around Mason's waist and yanked him back hard!

A blast of icy air roared past the tip of Mason's nose. The temperature plunged by more than twenty degrees in a heartbeat, frost filled the air, and a thin sheet of ice spread across the ground.

If he'd been one second slower, he'd have been frozen solid.

Mason stumbled but caught his balance. His face went white, and his back was drenched in cold sweat. He stared at the frozen ground ahead, his heart pounding like crazy.

Holy shit, that was close!

A second later, a silver-white mutant cat stepped into view.

Its whole body was armored in thick, pale blue ice, and it stood a full 16 feet tall, like a giant carved out of frost. Its legs were thick and powerful, and waves of freezing air rolled off it.

Everywhere it stepped, the ground, the walls, and the metal doors froze over. The frost kept spreading, and the pressure in the air was suffocating.

It didn't have the rotting stench of a regular zombie. Instead, it gave off a dead, freezing aura, and its slitted ice-blue eyes were cold and vicious.

It was a rare Level Four Ice Affinity mutant cat! It was only day four of the apocalypse, and something this powerful was already out there.

"Shit! That's a high-tier Level Four mutant cat! It's got Ice Affinity!"

Elijah gripped the long blade at his hip. Every muscle in his body tensed, ready to fight to the death.

Orion activated his Metal Affinity in a flash, and a sharp metal blade formed in his hand. He stepped in front of Paisley to shield her, his face deadly serious.

Nobody had expected a Level Four mutant cat to be hiding deep inside some random dealership in the city. It was strong enough to crush every single one of them.

The air hummed as the ice cat struck without hesitation. Its ice-blue eyes flashed, and the moment it raised a paw, countless razor-sharp icicles formed overhead!

Dozens of clear, palm-sized icicles shot toward them in a dense, vicious barrage, each one packed with brutal cold. They shrieked as they tore through the air.

One hit would freeze flesh solid on contact and turn a person into an ice statue on the spot.

"Don't take it head-on! Look for a chance to fall back!"

Elijah barked the order and charged forward. His blade flashed as he slashed through several incoming icicles.

Orion pushed his Metal Affinity to the max and formed a thick metal wall in front of him. He held his ground and took most of the barrage head-on.

The icicles shattered against the wall, spraying ice shards and freezing mist, and frost crept over the barrier within seconds.
