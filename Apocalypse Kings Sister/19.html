Chapter 19

Right behind the mall employee, a mass of thick green branches came crawling after her. They twisted and slithered like snakes, their leaves razor-sharp, and they were hunting her down.

Paisley's eyes narrowed. She recognized the thing at once, pulled the dagger from her belt, and stepped forward with a clean slash.

With a series of sharp snaps, the branches closing in on them were cut clean through. Pale-green sap oozed from the stumps, and it smelled like death.

Her face went cold. "It's a mutant money tree," she said quietly.

She sounded genuinely surprised. "I can't believe a regular potted money tree from the mall mutated into something this vicious overnight.

"It's a Level Two mutant plant, at least."

The employee they'd saved slumped to the floor, shaking and gasping for air. She looked like someone who'd just cheated death.

But before anyone could catch their breath, more branches came pouring down from above. They covered the hallway and surged toward the group again, even more vicious than before.

Just then, they heard quick, heavy footsteps coming their way.

It was Elijah. When the two of them hadn't come back, he'd gotten worried and come upstairs to look for them. He'd just reached the second floor when he walked right into this.

His face hardened, and he jumped into the fight without a second's hesitation.

Blinding blue lightning burst to life with a sharp crackle and coiled around him. He threw out his hand, and bolt after bolt slammed into the branches, scorching the leaves black.

At the same time, Orion pushed his Metal Affinity to full strength. Dozens of thin, razor-sharp metal blades formed at his fingertips and shot out in every direction, slicing through branch after branch.

With Elijah's lightning and Orion's metal, the two powerhouses made one terrifying team.

But the mutant money tree was freakishly hard to kill. Wherever a branch got cut or burned, new shoots sprang up right away. No matter how many they took out, more kept coming.

In fact, the harder they fought, the faster it spread, and soon, green branches had taken over almost the entire hallway. After a few rounds, it was clear they couldn't kill it this way.

Paisley had been watching closely the whole time. Thanks to her past life, she spotted its weakness right away. "Mutant plants hate fire!" she called out.

As she spoke, she pulled some old clothes and a lighter out of her dimension and set the clothes on fire. Then, she hurled the burning bundle right into the thick of the branches.

The fire caught fast, racing across the leaves, and for the moment, it held back the branches' advance.

But within moments, the wet sap started smothering the flames, and the fire died down. It could slow the tree down, but it was only a Band-Aid.

To kill the money tree for good, they had to go after its roots.

Paisley turned to the trembling employee. "You work here. You must know where this tree's roots are.

"Show us exactly where."

The employee's lips had gone white, and she was shaking all over. Under Paisley's calm, sharp gaze, she nodded quickly. "It's... it's in the middle of the third-floor atrium!

"It's the biggest money tree in the mall. All its roots are in the big planter underneath!"

Paisley didn't hesitate. "Take us there."

Elijah nodded. "Be careful," he told her, stepping up beside her to cover her.

Orion called back his metal blades and took her other side, watching for danger. "Don't be scared. I'm right here."

The branches in the hallway were still writhing like crazy, but they didn't dare come any closer. The fire and the two abilities had obviously spooked them.

The employee wasn't about to waste any time. Still shaking, she got to her feet and hurried ahead with her head down, leading the group up the stairs to the third floor.

The closer they got, the thicker and more humid the air became. The smell of plants mixed with the stench of rot, and it made it hard to breathe.

Thick green vines covered every inch of the walls and ceiling, crisscrossing like a giant spiderweb. Everywhere they looked, it was creepy.
