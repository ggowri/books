Chapter 162

Paisley couldn't take her eyes off the jewelry.

These are so pretty.

Back with the Lennox family, ever since Rachel came home, they'd felt they owed her and wanted to make it up to her. So anything of Paisley's that Rachel took a liking to, Rachel got to take.

Later, whenever they shopped for clothes or jewelry, Paisley only got whatever Rachel didn't want.

She couldn't even remember the last time she'd bought something she actually loved.

"Take all of it," Paisley said softly, sounding unusually cheerful. With a thought, she opened her Storage Dimension all the way.

Every stack of solid gold bars vanished, filing neatly into the precious metals section of her Storage Dimension in heavy, packed rows.

Every piece of gold jewelry, handcrafted goldwork, and bridal jewelry set on the counters went in too, without a single one left behind.

Then, she swept up the diamonds of every size, the diamond rings and necklaces, and the gemstone earrings, and all that sparkle disappeared from the counters in seconds.

Finally, she took the emeralds, pearls, rubies, sapphires, and rare collector's pieces, along with their gift boxes and dust covers, leaving nothing behind.

She worked fast, and the fourth floor, which had been dazzling with gold and luxury, was empty and bare in no time.

Trovie buzzed around the emptying counters, cheering nonstop.

[Take it all, every last bit! It's all ours! Master, you're amazing, and I love it!]

The little white cat sat obediently at Paisley's feet, its head tilted up as it watched the gold vanish. It blinked its big, curious eyes and let out a soft meow now and then, sweet as could be.

In a matter of minutes, every bit of gold, diamonds, jewelry, and gemstones on the fourth floor was gone. The counters were so empty it was hard to believe they'd ever been full of treasure.

Even after she finished, Paisley was still smiling, feeling more secure than ever.

This floor's treasure was worth way more than the 550 pounds of gold in the basement, and it would bring in a ton of points.

Paisley was about to head to the fifth floor when she glimpsed the counters off to the side. She'd been so busy grabbing the gold and jewels that she'd overlooked this whole section.

The whole section was luxurious and elegant, and its spotless glass cases held nothing but top designer brands.

Fine mechanical watches were lined up in neat rows, their leather straps beautifully textured and expertly made.

All kinds of limited-edition handbags, backpacks, and clutches were neatly arranged, with stiff, high-quality leather and classic designs. Every one was a rare, discontinued model.

On top of that, there were designer belts, scarves, sunglasses, and accessories, plus every kind of perfume. Each piece was exquisite and expensive, the kind of luxury ordinary people could only dream about even before the apocalypse.

Before the apocalypse, any single item here would've cost tens of thousands of dollars, but now it all belonged to no one.

And whatever she found was hers.

Paisley's eyes lit up at the cases full of designer goods. "Keep going," she said softly, and with a thought, she opened her Storage Dimension again.

Every high-end mechanical watch, automatic waterproof watch, and limited-edition timepiece went in first, along with their original gift boxes, warranty cards, and cases.

Next, every designer bag, leather handbag, rugged backpack, and discontinued clutch was sorted and stored in her Storage Dimension. The leather belts, custom scarves, designer sunglasses, fancy perfumes, and other high-end accessories went in last, every single one.

Within seconds, the whole designer section was empty, every precious item tucked away in her Storage Dimension.

Trovie got excited all over again, its little wings flapping fast as it cheered.

[Score! Master, these are so pretty!]

The little white cat joined in, bouncing on its tiny paws and letting out a soft meow, as if it agreed with Trovie.

Paisley looked over the fourth floor, now picked clean. Every bit of gold, jewelry, diamonds, designer watches, bags, and accessories was in her Storage Dimension now.

She was in a great mood.

She looked up toward the last floor and said cheerfully, "Fifth floor, the movie theater and food court. Let's keep going."

Just then, quick, light footsteps sounded behind her.

Ashley, Spencer, Thomas, and the others hurried up to the fourth floor. They looked around the empty floor, stunned.
