Chapter 162

These are beautiful.

Back at the Lennox house, ever since Rachel came home, everything Paisley had ever wanted got taken from her, out of some twisted sense of debt owed.

Clothes, jewelry — whatever was left after Rachel picked through it first, that's what came to her.

She couldn't even remember the last time she'd bought something she actually loved.

"Take all of it."

Paisley said it quietly, a rare lightness in her voice, and opened her storage fully.

Take it!

Every stacked gold bar, gone, sorted into her dedicated metals storage, layer after layer, packed full.

Take it!

Every gold ornament, antique piece, wedding set on the counter — not one left behind.

Take it!

Diamonds of every size, rings, necklaces, gemstone earrings — gone, the sparkle vanishing from the counters in an instant.

Take it!

Jade, pearls, rubies and sapphires, display pieces — along with the storage boxes and dust covers they came with — all of it, gone.

She moved fast. Everywhere she looked, the glittering treasure disappeared. What had been a blaze of gold and light turned hollow and quiet.

Trovie buzzed around the emptying counters, cheering nonstop.

[All gone! Every bit of it! It's ours! Host, you're amazing! I love this!]

Kitty sat obediently at Paisley's feet, watching the gold vanish, blinking, meowing softly now and then, adorable as ever.

In minutes, the entire fourth floor — gold, diamonds, jade, jewelry — was stripped bare, the counters spotless, like they'd never held treasure at all.

Done, Paisley's smile lingered, something settled and satisfied underneath.

This floor alone had been worth more than the five hundred fifty pounds of gold downstairs. Good for a solid haul of points.

She turned, ready for the fifth floor, when something at the edge of her vision caught her attention — a side section she'd missed while focused on the gold.

The section was done up elegantly, glass cases spotless, holding nothing but top luxury brands.

Precision watches lined up in neat rows, leather straps finely stitched, quality unmistakable.

Limited-edition bags — totes, backpacks, clutches — arranged by style, leather structured and classic, every piece a discontinued, hard-to-find model.

Beyond that, matching belts, scarves, sunglasses, accessories, perfume — every piece a luxury item that once cost a fortune.

Before the apocalypse, any single item here would've run tens of thousands. Now it all belonged to no one.

And whatever she came across belonged to her.

Paisley's eyes went bright at the sight of it.

"Keep going."

She said it softly, and her storage opened again.

Take it!

Every high-end mechanical watch, every waterproof automatic, every limited edition — along with the original boxes, warranty cards, cases — all preserved, all gone.

Take it!

Every luxury bag, leather tote, durable backpack, discontinued clutch — sorted and stored.

Take it!

Leather belts, custom scarves, designer sunglasses, perfume, every accessory — swept clean, nothing left behind.

In moments, the whole designer section sat empty, every fine object folded into her storage.

Trovie buzzed with fresh excitement, cheering nonstop.

[Score! Host, these are so pretty.]

Kitty tapped her paws together, meowing along like she agreed.

Paisley looked over the stripped fourth floor — gold, jewelry, diamonds, watches, bags, all of it hers now.

She felt good. Really good.

She looked toward the last floor, voice light.

"Fifth floor. Movie theater and the food court. Let's keep going."

Just then, quick, light footsteps approached from behind.

Ashley, Spencer, and Thomas arrived, scanning the empty floor, faces full of confusion.
