Chapter 361

She stared at her husband in disbelief, his color completely renewed, no longer curled up in pain, and nearly broke down crying right there.

Twenty-some days of anguish, countless sleepless nights, the blade that had hung over all their heads — it was finally gone.

The tight line of Elijah's jaw finally relaxed, the heavy shadow in his eyes clearing away, replaced by real, honest relief.

He'd always known Paisley was extraordinary, but seeing it unfold with his own eyes still left him stunned.

He knew full well — she'd just saved the Blackwoods again, saved his own father.

She was the family's savior, plain and simple.

Her mother watched the whole unbelievable scene, her heart full.

Her daughter was kind-hearted and extraordinarily capable both.

Her daughter had just saved a man's life, and she was so young, her abilities enough to upend everything the whole base thought it knew.

Her daughter was an absolute treasure.

Only Ashley looked entirely unsurprised, smiling easily, pride shining in her eyes.

She'd always known her sister would never let anyone down.

Early the next morning, dawn light filtering softly through the windows,

Paisley had just finished washing up when an official notice popped up on her system panel — a base-wide announcement.

All registered Affinity users were to report to the training and mission hall immediately.

Base rules required every registered user to train regularly and complete assigned missions on schedule.

It was a duty every Affinity user owed, for the base's survival and their own.

At almost the same moment, Ashley, Orion, and the others in the house all received the exact same notice.

The family finished breakfast quickly, and the moment they stepped out the front door, a military vehicle was waiting.

The door opened, and Elijah stepped out first.

He wore a crisp uniform, every line sharp, the exhaustion of the day before gone after a night's rest, looking steadier and more composed than ever, carrying the weight of base leadership.

"Get in."

His voice was cool but gentle as he pulled the passenger door open for Paisley himself.

Everyone climbed in, and once the car pulled out onto the base's main road, Elijah turned to Paisley, his tone open and sincere.

"After we got registered yesterday, the group of us got assigned as a single squad.

You should pick the squad name.

If you'd rather not be on my squad, or you've got other ideas, that's fine — I can dissolve it whenever you want."

A step back to gain ground.

Ashley bit back a laugh. She hadn't realized Elijah had a manipulative streak like this in him.

What was that little speech, if not textbook fishing for sympathy?

Paisley tilted her head slightly, catching Orion and Ashley's eyes in the rearview mirror, asking quietly.

"What do you two think?"

Orion answered easily, without a trace of hesitation.

"We've been through everything together this whole way. We're already one unit. Of course we're one squad."

He thought for a moment, then offered a suggestion.

"What about Falcon? Fast, sharp, fearless in a crisis — fits us well enough."

Paisley nodded, no objection at all.

"Fine. Squad Falcon it is."

She had a vague sense that after dinner last night, Nathaniel and Gerald had already mapped out most of what came next, back in the study.

The Blackwoods had deep roots here, years of connections, and Elijah was already one of the base's top fighters.

She didn't ask for much — in a world this chaotic, all she really wanted was to keep her family and the people she cared about safe, year after year.

Staying close to Elijah, the safest bet around, was how she'd keep them that way.

A faint smile crossed Elijah's face, his gaze warming. "Good. I'll file the registration right away."

Thomas and Spencer had no objections either, of course.

The car sped on, unhindered, straight into the heart of the base.

The training hall was massive, built entirely from solid alloy, spacious and already packed with people.

Every squad's Affinity users had turned out, standing in formation, the noise loud but orderly, discipline evident everywhere.

The car parked in the designated lot, and they stepped out together, walking into the hall side by side.
