Chapter 449

"Why can't my Water Affinity awaken healing?"

"We're both support types. Why's the gap this big? I wish I could help everyone more!"

She sounded quietly dejected.

Mason was completely stunned, mouth open, his rough face full of disbelief. His voice was thick with awe.

"Ms. Lennox is something else!"

"That's a Level Seven beast's venom! The kind that kills on contact or turns you into one of them! Cleared out clean in ten minutes, not a trace left!"

He swallowed hard, looking at Paisley with total respect.

"Ms. Lennox's strength is far beyond anything I understand. She can fight, kill, control the field, and save lives too... Being on the same team as her is the luckiest thing that ever happened to me."

Everyone's eyes were on Paisley, full of awe, respect, delight, and deep relief.

Before they could recover, Paisley didn't say a word. She glanced over everyone's wounds. None were as bad as Elijah's, but they still needed care, or the consequences could be serious.

Her gaze was soft and light. Wisps of green life force spread again from her fingertips.

This power was gentler and finer than what she'd used on Elijah. It covered every person, healing and purifying without a sound.

Threads of green seeped into their skin and into their bodies, stripping out the traces of serpent venom and mental contamination still in them. They calmed the lingering rage of battle in their minds. At the same time they quickly mended the wounds all over their bodies, the cuts, burns, tears, poison, and contamination.

Within moments the whole team was healed.

Everyone felt light all over.

They stared at the scene, awe building on awe.

It was healing for a whole group at once!

Paisley's power was this absurd?!

How many more surprises was she hiding?!

Surprise, shock, and relief churned inside everyone, so strong they nearly spilled over.

Elijah, mostly recovered, stood still and watched the cool, upright figure in front of him. His feelings were tangled, filled with endless guilt and a love that burned hotter.

He was captain of the Falcon Squad. He was supposed to be the team's sturdiest shield, the one standing in front of everyone, shielding them from the storm and taking the worst of every danger.

But this fight, he had lost again and again, nearly died, and not only failed to protect his team and hold the line, but become a burden. In the end it was Paisley who risked everything to save him and keep the whole squad alive.

As captain, he'd let her face danger again and again, and carry every deadly moment for them.

From breaking the first deadlock, to the life-or-death kill on the serpent, to the ugly ambush afterward, to healing his wounds, he'd lost count of how many times she'd saved him, and the whole squad.

Guilt swept over him like a tide. But with it came a love that went deeper and burned hotter.

A girl this clear-headed, strong, gentle, and unstoppable was worth everything he had to protect and love.

Every time she saved him without asking for anything, she filled his whole heart a little more, until he couldn't look away.

Elijah's eyes were warm and tender, locked on Paisley. In his heart he made a quiet vow. From now on he'd give everything to keep her safe.

Her safety came before everything else!

******

Just then, two small figures padded over.

The little white cat trotted up with its fluffy tail held high and its head raised proudly, and dropped a pile of stuffed storage pouches at Paisley's feet.

Beside it, the golden fox swayed its tails and set the confiscated rifles and crystal cores neatly in front of her.

When they were done, the cat and the fox looked up together with round, shining eyes and nuzzled her hand, cuddling and begging, plainly waiting to be praised.

Paisley's eyes softened at the sight of their good behavior. She ruffled the cat's head and stroked the fox's ears.

"Good job," she said softly.

With a thought, she took two lollipops from her space and put one in each of their mouths.

"Go play."

The two companions, lollipops in their mouths, were delighted. They rubbed against her even harder, tails wagging nonstop.

Then Paisley spread out all the spoils in front of everyone.
