Chapter 123

A huge wave of gentle energy spread through her whole body. It kept washing against the limits of her ability, repairing the wear and tear from the day's fighting and strengthening her body and mind.

The Level Four Psychic core packed the biggest punch. Its power was gentle but overwhelming and completely pure. It boosted her mental strength and made the foundation of her ability rock-solid.

As she absorbed the last bit of energy from the cores, the ability inside her suddenly surged. The barrier holding it back loosened, then shattered.

Her Psychic Affinity broke through, straight to mid Level Four!

Energy filled her to the brim. Her strength, recovery speed, and mental senses had all improved across the board.

Perfect. That's exactly what I wanted.

Her Plant Affinity sat at peak Level Five, one lucky break away from leveling up. On the Psychic side, she was now mid Level Four.

The Trading System should be ready to level up too.

With a thought, Paisley turned all the gold bars and jewelry from the office building's vault into Trading System points in one go.

Gold light swirled, and the points poured in.

When it was all tallied up, she'd broken 100,000 points. She now had 101,060, more than enough to level up.

The moment her points updated, Trovie's bright, excited voice went off in her head again and again.

[Master's points have shot up! Resource reserves are plentiful, and the dimension meets all requirements!

[Storage Dimension upgrade and expansion successful!

[Dimension size expanded from 100,000 square feet to a full million square feet! Mountains, open land, and planting zones have all grown!

[Spirit spring upgrade complete! The spring has grown into a private 1,000-square-foot pond of spirit water. The water is twice as potent, perfect for raising high-level plants and beasts!

[Upgrade bonus: one premium lucky draw! You can open it anytime!

[Master's overall strength, dimension level, and point reserves all meet requirements. Trader access upgraded!

[Congratulations, Master! Trading System upgrade successful. You're now a Level Three cross-dimensional trader!

[New high-level realm unlocked: the Galactic Realm! You can now access the Galactic Trading Plaza, Galactic supplies, Galactic technology, and high-level training resources!]

As the stream of announcements ended, Paisley smiled to herself.

Then, Trovie's soft, lively voice piped up again, full of excitement.

[Master, do you want to connect with a Galactic Realm trader right now and start trading?]

Paisley's eyes lit up, and she nodded right away. [Connect me now!]

[Connecting to the Galactic Realm channel... Connection successful! Matched with a verified Galactic Realm trader!]

The System's cool chime faded, and a glowing blue virtual trading panel popped up in front of Paisley. It looked seriously high-tech, with streams of data scrolling past at lightning speed. This was the brand-new Level Three Galactic trading panel.

This time, the trader she'd been matched with was a rare S-tier interstellar female.

The galaxy had suffered from radiation and resource shortages for years. Natural, unspoiled goods were incredibly scarce, and lab-made food couldn't meet the needs of its high-level races anymore.

Paisley was still in the early days of the apocalypse, and she happened to have a huge stockpile of clean, natural food and seeds. It was exactly what the other side needed.

The trader didn't waste any time. She refreshed her trading shelf, and a whole row of high-level Galactic goods appeared, way beyond anything Paisley had ever imagined.

On it was a high-precision portable energy gun, a full set of high-level radiation-proof combat gear, storage rings, and a portable smart computer. There was also a fully automatic hovercar, plus all kinds of standard Galactic healing potions and high-level protective gear.

None of this top-of-the-line gear could ever be made in the apocalypse. Every piece was worth a fortune, and together, they covered every weakness the team had in scouting, travel, combat, and storage.

Then, she posted what she wanted in return.

Her list included tons of natural fruit seeds and all kinds of high-yield vegetable seeds. She also wanted natural compressed rations and clean ingredients that would stay fresh for a long time.

For the Galactic races, natural goods like these, untouched by factories or radiation, were a once-in-a-lifetime luxury. They were worth far more than any nutrient formula in the galaxy.

Paisley perked up. Everything on the trader's list was something she had.

Without thinking twice, she listed a big bag of natural fruit and vegetable seeds, plus a large batch of fresh ingredients and additive-free compressed rations.
