Chapter 520

"My daughter is heaven's favorite."

The gloom lifted from Vivian's eyes, replaced by confident light.

She straightened her back and nodded, a perfect curve on her lips, and spoke with assurance.

"Yes, Dad. I won't let you down."

"Don't worry. Paisley isn't worthy of being my rival."

"I'm leaving right now."

Vivian changed into a clean, elegant, pale-white protective suit and made sure she looked composed and graceful before heading for the base's medical wing.

With the Crimson Rain raging, any team returning from outside picked up traces of contamination. Many of the wounded had festering wounds and spreading toxin. The medical wing was packed day and night, and a faint smell of rot hung in the air.

This was the place where ordinary people and Affinity users alike gathered, and all eyes turned.

She walked into the medical hall, two hand-picked Lennox aides behind her.

Plenty of the wounded lay in the wards. Some burned with fever, toxin eating at their meridians, moaning in pain.

The medical staff ran themselves ragged. With purifiers so scarce, a long line of wounded waited for treatment.

Vivian stepped forward, her voice gentle and soft.

"Please don't panic. I'm Vivian Lennox of the main Lennox family, a Level Six purifier.

If anyone still has Crimson Rain toxin in their body, I can purify it for free."

The medical hall went quiet for a moment before a small stir broke out.

"It's Miss Vivian of the Lennox family! She's a Level Six purifier!"

"We're saved!"

"I heard purifying costs a lot. Is she really doing it for free?"

Hope sparked in the wounded, and they thanked her one after another.

Vivian wore just the right, light smile. She walked to the patient with the heaviest toxin and raised her hand.

A soft, golden purifying force flowed out and seeped into him, slowly dissolving the Crimson Rain toxin that had settled in his meridians.

The patient's bluish-purple face regained its color. The burning pain faded fast, and he thanked her over and over.

"I feel so much better! The toxin's gone! Thank you, Miss Lennox!"

"Miss Vivian, you're a living saint. Thank you… thank you so much!"

She purified several patients in a row, patiently, every move calm and elegant, her face gentle and kind. Everyone watching warmed to her.

******

Her two aides mingled in the crowd, speaking in low voices, their idle chatter pitched so people nearby could hear.

"Worthy of the main family's young lady. Her purifying is so gentle and steady. She saves ordinary people's lives."

"Come to think of it, Paisley Lennox is incredible, but she's all about combat. She fights, she collects supplies, but that's for the base rewards. I hardly ever hear of her saving anyone."

"Supplies don't matter if people die of poison. Purifiers are what we really need."

"That's right. One goes out and fights for supplies, the other stays at the base and heals the wounded.

When things get dangerous, it's the purifier who steadily saves our lives that we can rely on."

"Miss Vivian, with her purifying power, is heaven's favorite!"

…

The rumor spread quietly.

Many of the wounded and their families heard it and kept it in mind. Without realizing it, they began to put the two women side by side.

Someone nodded along.

"True, supplies matter, but treating poison and saving lives matters more."

People were selfish, after all.

They needed something right now, so naturally that thing mattered most to them.

Vivian took in every whisper, a hint of satisfaction flickering in her eyes. Her face stayed tender and compassionate as she moved on to purify the next patient.

That was exactly the image she wanted.

Paisley was only charging around outside, grabbing glory. She did it all to get base rewards for herself, which was selfish at heart.

And Vivian, the guardian who stayed at the base and quietly saved everyone.

She meant to use the crowds in the medical wing to chip away at the reputation Paisley had built up.

Once the seed of comparison was planted, Paisley's reputation would slide sooner or later, and Vivian would pull ahead.

News from the medical wing spread quickly. Plenty of people came just to watch a Level Six purifier at work with their own eyes.
