Chapter 417

Behind him came the top Healing Affinity users and a team of researchers in white coats. Marcus was among them, his face cool and serious, studying the broken man in the bed with a clinical eye.

They stood by the bed, and their presence filled the room.

Damon looked down at Grant, pale, bloodshot, and sunk in despair. His voice was flat and carried absolute authority.

"You're awake."

"Then pull yourself together. I didn't spend all those resources to wake you up so you could mope and waste yourself."

"Listen carefully. From the moment you opened your eyes, your life belongs to me. I'm the one who pulled you back from the edge."

The words fell cold, with no warmth, just a bare deal and a demand for control that made it hard to breathe.

Grant lay stiff in bed, his whole body limp, his eyes dead.

He never expected the first face he'd see would belong to Damon Quinn, the Quinns' heir, with his vicious, calculating reputation.

His pale lips twitched in a rough, bitter laugh. "I'm already useless. I've lost an arm. I can't even live a normal life. What could I possibly do for you?"

The softest part of him churned.

Chloe had always aimed high. She was proud and bright, and she'd always adored strong men and glory.

Stripped of his rank, crippled, looking like this, she would never like him. She might even be disgusted.

The thought deepened his despair.

******

Damon understood every weak point and every struggle in him. His voice was flat as he pressed down on the one place that would hurt.

"Don't bother feeling sorry for yourself."

"Your beloved Chloe and her whole family are still locked up in the Security Office. They've probably suffered a lot."

He bent down slightly, his eyes dark and cold, completely certain of his hold.

"If you agree to my terms and work for me, I'll use the Quinns' authority to release them immediately, no strings attached, and restore their rights and freedom."

He paused and added, with a thin, mocking edge, "Or maybe you don't care whether she lives or dies, and you'd rather sulk until the end and let her suffer in there?"

Grant jolted. A flash of fury cracked through his eyes, and he looked up at Damon.

He knew exactly what kind of man Damon was. Vicious and cold, quick as a viper, never doing anything without a reason and never helping anyone for free.

All this effort to save him, and now he was using Chloe's family as leverage. There had to be a deeper scheme.

He suppressed his wariness and rage and forced himself to stay calm, his voice low and rough.

"State your terms first. You went to a lot of trouble to save me. What do you want from me?"

Seeing him give in, a faint cold curve touched Damon's lips. He stepped aside and motioned behind him.

Two researchers in white coats came forward and carefully carried in a custom metal prosthetic that had been resting nearby.

It was forged from meteoric iron alloy, rare in the apocalypse. Intricate lines ran along its surface, with a cold matte metal shine, and tiny Affinity storage chips glittered at the joints. It was heavy, cold, and deadly, a blend of technology and killing power.

"This is the base lab's newest high-grade combat prosthetic," Damon said evenly, utterly confident.

"Ordinary prosthetics only let you get around. They're basically decoration. This one is different."

"It matches a Level Six Affinity user's body perfectly and connects directly to the meridians that carry your power. Once it's installed, it replaces your arm completely. Its flexibility and reaction speed are as good as a real arm, and it amplifies your output and your close-combat power."

"In short, with this on, you don't just get your peak strength back. You get stronger and fiercer than before. Your weakness will become your weapon."

Marcus stepped up and added coolly, "This is part of the Quinns' classified armor program. It combines meteoric iron energy storage with Affinity amplification technology. It resists impact, explosions, and mutant beast bites. Once you put it on, you'll serve Damon for life."
