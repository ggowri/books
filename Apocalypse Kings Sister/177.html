Chapter 177

Paisley looked at everyone and said calmly, "Come on, let's head back to base."

Everyone nodded and set off with the rescued hostages, heading back to the safety of the main base.

******

Half an hour later, they made it safely back to Rivergate Base and went straight to the mission hall to turn in their missions.

The spacious, bright mission hall was crowded with Affinity users coming and going, most of them small teams taking low-level missions to gain experience. The place was busy but orderly.

A giant screen in the middle of the hall scrolled through the base's bounty missions and completion announcements in real time, and everyone's eyes naturally followed the teams coming in to turn in missions.

When Paisley's group walked in with the five or six hostages, who still looked haggard but were safe now, they grabbed the whole room's attention.

As it happened, one of the hostages was the wife of a senior Rivergate Base official.

When he saw his wife back safe and sound, he hurried over and pulled her into a tight hug. Once he was sure she was okay, he turned and shook hands with Paisley and the others again and again, thanking them with heartfelt gratitude.

"Thank you all for risking your lives to save her. Without you, my wife might never have come home.

"I can't thank you enough. Let me treat you all to dinner tonight. Please say you'll come!"

It was hard to turn down such a warm invitation, so they agreed.

Afterward, the group went to the registration counter to officially turn in the mission.

The staff verified the hostages' identities, confirmed everyone's safety, checked the proof of kill and the crystal core and petal samples from the Level Six flower, and pulled up the reward details, announcing them to the room.

"This team completed the Level Five hunting mission. Reward: 2,000 base contribution points and 500 Level Two crystal cores."

Before the room could react, the staffer read out a second, even bigger reward.

"Level Six high-risk bounty mission completed above target: the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower eliminated and all trapped hostages rescued. Reward: 5,000 base contribution points and 2,200 Level Two crystal cores!"

The whole hall erupted in shock. Every Affinity user there turned to stare in disbelief.

After all, the Level Six carnivorous flower mission at the amusement park had been on the board for days and was rated extremely difficult. Countless high-level teams had tried it, and none of them had pulled it off.

Everyone had assumed this high-risk mission was impossible, and nobody believed anyone could pull it off.

But now, this team from out of town had finished the mission without a single injury and rescued every trapped hostage, too.

"Someone actually finished the Level Six high-risk mission! That's unbelievable!"

"So many strong teams got wrecked out there, and these guys actually killed that Level Six mutant carnivorous flower!"

"This team is crazy strong! They look so young, but they fight like monsters. They just set a new mission record for the base!"

Gasps and chatter filled the whole mission hall, and everyone now looked at Paisley's group with admiration and envy.

Right after that, a mechanical alert chimed on everyone's wristbands at the same time, and the rewards went up yet again.

[Congratulations to the team on killing the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower. Mission rated SS-tier difficulty!

[Bonus reward: 5,000 base contribution points and 200 Level Three crystal cores!]

All the rewards went to Thomas, the team's acting captain, and the flashing gold pop-up was impossible to miss.

The mission hall was still buzzing, and the sight of such a huge haul left everyone jealous.

After a quick handoff, they all went back to Paisley's private room.

Thomas handed everyone a newly developed base wristband.

Then, he tallied up all the rewards, looked up at everyone, and spoke seriously.

"Everyone, link your wristband to your ID and add each other as contacts. I'll send everyone their share of the contribution points in a bit.

"Here's our total haul for this mission. We got 12,000 base contribution points, 2,700 Level Two crystal cores, and 200 Level Three crystal cores.

"There were seven of us, so split evenly, that's 1,714 contribution points, 385 Level Two cores, and 28 Level Three cores each. Any objections?"

As soon as he finished, the room went silent. Nobody answered.
