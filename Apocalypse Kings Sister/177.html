Chapter 177

Paisley looked over the group, voice even.

"Let's head back to base."

Everyone nodded and set off with the rescued hostages toward the main base.

******

Half an hour later, the group reached Rivergate base and headed straight for the mission hall.

The hall was bright and bustling, affinity users moving in and out, most fresh off low-tier missions, the atmosphere busy but orderly.

A massive screen in the center scrolled through active bounties and completed missions, pulling every eye in the room toward each returning team.

Paisley's group walked in with the five or six hostages, still a little shaken but visibly safe, and the whole room's attention snapped to them instantly.

Among the hostages was the wife of a senior base official.

He spotted her immediately and rushed over, pulling her into a tight embrace, checking she was unharmed before turning to bow deeply to Paisley's group, voice thick with gratitude.

"Thank you, all of you, for risking your lives to save her. If not for you, I might never have gotten her back.

Words aren't enough — let me host a dinner tonight, please, as a proper thank-you."

They couldn't refuse something like that and agreed.

The group headed to the registration counter to officially close out the mission.

The staff verified the hostages' identities, confirmed everyone's safety, checked the proof of kill and the crystal core and petal samples from the Level Six flower, and pulled up the reward details, announcing them to the room.

"This team completed the preliminary Level Five hunt. Reward: two thousand base contribution points, five hundred Level Two crystal cores."

Before the words had even settled, the second announcement hit, and the whole hall went quiet.

"Exceeding expectations on the Level Six high-risk bounty — successful elimination of the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower, full hostage rescue. Reward: five thousand base contribution points, two thousand two hundred Level Two crystal cores!"

The hall erupted. Every affinity user in the room turned, shock and disbelief plain on every face.

That mission had been posted for days, flagged as near-impossible. Countless elite teams had tried and failed, some losing members, some retreating in full defeat.

Everyone had assumed it simply couldn't be done. No one believed a team could pull it off.

And yet — this out-of-town team had done it without a single injury, and rescued every hostage besides.

"Someone actually cleared the Level Six mission? Unbelievable!"

"So many strong teams failed at that spot, and these guys actually killed it!"

"They look so young too — this just reset the base's whole mission record."

Gasps and murmurs filled the hall, every eye on Paisley's group shifting from disbelief to open admiration.

Then the system chime rang out across everyone's wristbands at once, stacking the reward even higher.

[Congratulations to the team — kill confirmed on Level Six mutant carnivorous flower. Mission re-rated: SS-tier!]

[Bonus reward: 5,000 base contribution points, 200 Level Three crystal cores!]

The full reward routed straight to Thomas, the team's temporary lead, a gold notification flashing prominently.

The hall buzzed louder than ever, envy thick in the air at the sheer size of the haul.

After wrapping up the handoff, the group headed back to Paisley's private room.

Thomas handed everyone a newly developed base wristband.

Then he tallied up the full mission rewards and looked around at the group, serious.

"Everyone bind your wristband to your ID and add each other. I'll transfer everyone's share shortly."

"Total haul this mission: twelve thousand base contribution points, two thousand seven hundred Level Two crystal cores, two hundred Level Three crystal cores.

Seven of us total. Split evenly, that's one thousand seven hundred fourteen contribution points, three hundred eighty-five Level Two crystal cores, and twenty-eight Level Three crystal cores each.

Any objections?"

The room went quiet. No one spoke.
