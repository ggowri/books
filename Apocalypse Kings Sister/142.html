Chapter 142

"Pais, meeting you is the best thing that's happened to me since the apocalypse started. Thank you!"

Last of all, Marcus lifted his can with a small smile, his voice sincere.

"I'm lucky to know all of you, but Paisley, I owe you the most. Thank you for saving me. Meeting you was the best luck I've ever had!"

Paisley laughed and raised her can too. "We're all in this together, so there's no need to be so formal. Let's all drink to that!"

The rich, energy-packed smell of the stew was incredibly strong. It slipped through the cracks around the door and windows and drifted into the room next door.

They were only one wall apart, but it was like two different worlds.

The four Lennoxes were sitting around a cold table, chewing on dry hardtack. It was rough and so dry they could barely swallow it, and it had zero flavor.

The rich smell of the stew and the sweet scent of Coke drifted through the wall and right up their noses. Compared to the dry stuff in their mouths, it made all of them crazy hungry.

The contrast hit them hard, and resentment, envy, and bitterness ate at them.

Rachel clenched the cold hardtack in her hand. The amazing smell hung in the air, and jealousy burned inside her. She turned to Owen and begged.

"Owen, do you have any extra crystal cores? Could you go buy us a few meals from the base cafeteria? I seriously can't eat any more of this hardtack."

Reid licked his dry lips, looking desperate, and his voice came out hoarse. "Owen, I haven't had a good stew in forever... I'd kill for something hot and spicy right now."

Ryan's stomach growled loudly, and he looked miserable.

"Owen, I can't eat this hardtack anymore. It doesn't taste like anything. I want a hot stew too!"

Owen looked at their hopeful, miserable faces and let out a deep sigh. He dug through every pocket, but they were all empty.

His face fell, and he spoke quietly.

"I don't have a single crystal core left. I spent them all on leveling up, hardtack, and medicine. Where am I supposed to get extra cores for food?"

Rachel didn't say anything out loud, but inside, she'd already called the three of them useless more times than she could count.

The three brothers were full of regret again. If they hadn't kicked Paisley out or treated her so badly, they'd be the ones sitting next door eating that stew right now.

Too bad there was no undo button in life.

All they could do now was grab Paisley's luck as fast as they could. Then, they'd get to eat hot, delicious stew too.

Next door, it was warm and lively, with a table full of food and laughter. Over here, it was cold and gloomy, and all they had was stale hardtack. The huge gap stabbed them right in the heart.

Meanwhile, Paisley and the others ate until they were stuffed. The pure energy from the warm chicken broth, the sacred fungus, and the high-level mutant chicken flowed down their throats and spread through their whole bodies. It was gentle but powerful, and it strengthened everyone's bodies and abilities.

Everyone's abilities stirred at once. Warm energy kept surging higher, and the walls holding them back quietly loosened and cracked.

Paisley broke through first.

A soft green glow bloomed around her, and rich, warm Plant energy wrapped around her in layers. The barrier that had kept her stuck at peak Level Five for so long shattered, and her Plant Affinity hit Level Six!

Right after that, her mental power shot up. Her Psychic Affinity climbed from Level Four to early Level Five, her senses got sharper and reached farther, and her control over her abilities doubled.

At the same time, her Water Affinity broke through too and settled firmly at early Level Three. All three affinities leveled up together, and she got a whole lot stronger.

Next, gold light flashed around Orion as sharp, heavy Metal energy surged through him. He went from early Level Five to mid Level Five, and his attack and defense both got a big boost.

Electricity crackled around Elijah as his fierce Lightning Affinity surged. He also went from early Level Five to mid Level Five, and he hit harder and looked scarier than ever.

Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, Madison, and Marcus all broke through their bottlenecks too. Every one of them reached peak late Level Four, just one step away from Level Five.

Light shimmered through the room. Everyone's energy felt deeper and stronger, and the whole team had leveled up across the board.
