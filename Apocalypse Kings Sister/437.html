Chapter 437

They stayed alert the whole way back to where they'd parked, then drove another twenty minutes before stopping in front of an abandoned shop on a street corner.

Its walls were solid, its windows intact, and the view was wide open. It was easy to defend, and far from the steel plant and the depots, so it was probably outside the serpent's territory. A good place to rest.

They quickly cleared the few low-level zombies wandering nearby and went up to the second floor.

Compared to the dust and rubble downstairs, the empty rooms up here were clean. The walls were whole, the air dry, and there was hardly a sign of zombies.

They brushed the dust off the floor, cleared a patch of space, and sat down. After days of travel and fighting, they were exhausted, and their tight nerves finally eased.

Paisley waved a hand.

A clean table appeared out of nowhere, loaded with food. A big communal stewpot simmered on it, a spicy red broth bubbling away, steam curling up in white clouds. The rich, peppery smell filled the whole room.

Thin-sliced beef and lamb, fresh vegetables, and meatballs were piled high, plump and fresh.

Thomas sniffed and lit up. "No way! It's still steaming! Straight out of the space and onto the table? That's insane!"

Ashley couldn't wait. She grabbed an ice-cold cola from the table, popped the tab, and swallowed the fizz. Her eyes curved with delight.

"The cola's still cold! Paisley, your space is unreal. Boiling stew and iced drinks side by side, and both stay perfect? I'm learning so much today!"

Spencer was already rubbing his hands, grinning as he sat down. "When's the last time we had a hot meal in the apocalypse? Who cares how it works. Food's food. Dig in! With Pais around, we're living like royalty!"

He grabbed a cold cola, chugged a long swallow, and sighed. "So good. I've been dying for an ice-cold soda."

Orion and Elijah traded a smile and pulled up chairs. A rare bit of warmth, in the middle of a deadly dead zone.

Everyone dug in. The spicy, savory broth warmed them all the way down and melted the tiredness out of them. The mood loosened, laughter came easily, and for a while they almost forgot the dead wasteland outside.

******

Right when everyone had relaxed, a faint sound drifted up from the street below. Soft footsteps, low and hurried voices, and a wary, careful presence.

Laughter stopped. Everyone put down their forks, and their faces went serious. Guns were quietly loaded, Affinities primed. They looked down at the street corner.

A few figures in combat gear and ordinary equipment came out from behind a broken wall. They looked like another field team out scavenging. Five people, tired, their strength somewhere between Level Four and Level Five.

They weren't from Aetheris Base. Probably survivors from some small base nearby.

They'd noticed the movement too and stopped short, on guard. Then they saw the Falcon Squad's top-tier gear and cold presence, and their hostility drained away. They stepped forward carefully.

"Easy, guys. We're a field team from the small base in Southspire. Just out scavenging. No bad intentions."

The man in front spoke first, sounding sincere and a little nervous.

Seeing no threat, Orion signaled the others to stand down. "No problem. Then you can go rest somewhere else."

The tension eased. Out here, field teams ran into each other all the time, and mostly they kept to themselves.

The Southspire team took in the Falcon Squad's gear and good condition. What they envied most was that these people were eating stew.
