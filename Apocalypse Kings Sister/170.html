Just then, the giant carnivorous flower suddenly moved. With a loud whoosh, its huge petals shuddered and snapped open, and countless pale purple poison seeds wrapped in corrosive gas shot toward them like a storm of arrows.

Every seed was deadly and sharp enough to pierce through anything. When they hit the ground, they'd sprout poison vines or burst into toxic clouds, and they filled the air so densely that there was nowhere left to dodge.

"Get your defenses up!" Spencer roared.

In that life-or-death moment, nobody dared hold back. They all unleashed their affinities at full power, throwing up their strongest defenses and fighting back hard.

Spencer's Fire Affinity exploded into a sea of flames, trying to burn the poison seeds out of the air. Thomas's vines wove into a thick net to block the sky, while Ashley raised heavy stone walls to hold the front line.

Mason's wind blades whipped through the air, trying to scatter the poison dust and slice the seeds apart. Marcus fired volleys of stone needles from a distance, and Madison formed a thick water barrier to shield them from the poison.

Fire, Plant, Earth, Wind, and Water powers poured out of all six of them at once in a dazzling, thundering display.

But then, something eerie happened.

Every attack that hit the poison seeds, petals, and roots only kicked up a faint wisp of smoke. It didn't leave a single mark, let alone do any damage.

Fire couldn't burn through the poison coating, and vines couldn't hold the thick roots. Stone couldn't stop the venom, wind couldn't cut the petals, and water couldn't purify any of it!

They'd thrown every affinity they had at it, and they actually hadn't done a single bit of damage to the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower.

The storm of poison seeds tore through every defense and closed in fast, carrying deadly poison. Their faces went white, despair hit them all at once, and fear turned their hands and feet ice-cold.

Spencer clenched his fists, his palms slick with cold sweat. His blazing fire dimmed without his meaning to, and all the strength drained out of his body.

He was always steady and good under pressure, but he'd never felt this helpless before. His throat tight, he gave a bitter laugh and muttered to himself.

"What do we do? Nothing's working... none of it. We threw every affinity we had at it and couldn't even crack its first layer of defense.

"I never thought a Level Six beast would be this strong. We're probably going to die here today."

Thomas stared at his shattered vine barrier and the poison seeds raining down with nowhere to dodge, and his confidence fell apart.

He'd always been careful and great at planning ahead, but right now every tactic and trump card he had was a joke. His voice was flat and hopeless.

"It's got no weakness, and it's immune to physical attacks and affinities. The poison gets in everywhere, and we can't even get close, so how are we supposed to fight? We don't stand a chance."

Ashley huddled in the middle of the group, her face drained of color and her eyes red and wet. She clutched the sleeve of the person in front of her, shaking hard.

The poison mist had just eaten right through her stone wall, crushing what little confidence she had left, and she started to sob.

"It's too strong... it's way too strong... We can't block it. We can't block it at all..."

Mason was always on the front line taking hits, tough as nails and afraid of nothing. But staring at the poison seeds blotting out the sky, even he felt helpless and scared, and his voice came out rough and shaky.

"We can't hurt it, we can't block it, and we can't dodge it. I've never run into a monster this strong. It's not even a fight, it's a slaughter, and we can't do a thing about it."

The stone needles forming at Marcus's fingertips crumbled apart, and his face darkened.

He was great at precise attacks and finding gaps, but now he couldn't even find a spot to aim at, and nothing he tried worked. His voice was low and hopeless.

"None of our attacks work, and there's no opening anywhere. Are we really going to die here today?"

Madison watched the water barrier she'd poured all her power into get eaten away by the poison mist, and her heart sank.

Water was supposed to defend and purify, but now it couldn't even block the poison. She'd lost all her confidence, and she sighed softly, her voice trembling.

"The poison's too corrosive, and my purification can't keep up. We... we can't hold out much longer."

All six of them broke down at once. Despair washed over them like a tidal wave, and the cold shadow of death hung over every one of them.

In their panic, they all turned to Paisley, who had stayed calm and unshaken the whole time.
