Chapter 83

They've always been selfish. I'm just learning from the best.

Reid and Ryan took the serum from her. Since she'd apologized and seemed sorry, they figured she really had just been terrified and hadn't meant to do it.

She was still their sister, after all. She'd never hurt them on purpose, would she?

After they injected the serum, they felt a lot better.

But they were still covered in wounds and needed someone to take care of them and treat their injuries.

The first person who came to mind was Paisley.

So they gritted their teeth through the pain and looked around without thinking, anxiously searching for her.

In that moment, memories came flooding back.

Every time they'd gotten hurt, landed in danger, or been poisoned before, Paisley had always been the one who worried the most. She'd rushed around cleaning their wounds, bandaging them, and treating the poison, patient and careful down to the last detail. Even when they treated her like dirt, she'd never once left them on their own.

Now, all three of them were badly hurt, poisoned, and in agony, and nobody was there to take care of them.

And the person they still thought of was Paisley.

The same Paisley they'd driven away and hurt so badly.

Trovie had already quietly swept the entire mutant grapevine and every last grape into Paisley's Storage Dimension.

Now, she was lounging against the hood of the SUV with a plump red apple in her right hand, taking slow bites, and the little white cat tucked in her left arm. She watched the whole scene calmly, like an outsider enjoying a show.

She'd only been watching for fun because she had nothing better to do. She'd never expected the whole fight to end up being about her.

The second Reid and Ryan spotted Paisley by the car, their faces lit up like they'd found a lifeline. They quickly put on warm smiles and called out to her.

"Pais! Come here, quick! We need you for something!"

They still sounded as condescending and superior as ever, like the second they called her, Paisley should be grateful and come running right back to them.

But Paisley didn't budge. She stayed where she was, relaxed, and didn't even look up. She just kept slowly eating her apple and ignored them completely.

Instead of getting the hint, the two of them got even more shameless. They talked down to her like they were doing her some huge favor.

"Pais, we're giving you a chance to come back to us.

"You saw it yourself. All three of us are badly hurt and poisoned, and we need someone to take good care of us.

"If you do your best to nurse us back to health, we'll act like all that drama never happened.

"You'll still be the Lennoxes' daughter, and our favorite little sister."

That finally made Paisley, who'd been watching with total indifference, look up.

So now, all of a sudden, they realized how good she was?

When they'd shut her out again and again, stomped all over her, believed every word Rachel said, and hurt her whenever they felt like it, why hadn't they thought about this day?

She wasn't some cheap toy they could pick up and toss aside whenever they wanted.

Why should they get to hurt her whenever they felt like it and call her back whenever it suited them?

Was she really that cheap and worthless to them?

They wanted her to come crawling back and wait on them hand and foot?

Yeah, fucking right. Keep dreaming.

Paisley slowly swallowed her bite of apple and casually pinched the core between her fingers. She looked straight at the two pathetic brothers and gave a faint, mocking smile.

"Your favorite?" she repeated softly, her voice bone-chillingly cold.

"I don't need that kind of love from the Lennoxes, and I don't want it anymore."

Reid and Ryan's smiles froze on their faces. They'd swallowed their pride and given her a way back, and they'd never expected Paisley, who'd always done what she was told, to turn them down flat.

Paisley leaned forward slightly, cold and distant, and every word cut straight to the bone.

"When you believed Rachel's lies and let her turn you against me, you shut me out, ignored me, and stomped all over my feelings. When you pushed me away, why didn't you remember I was the sister you grew up with?

"Only after she shoved you in front of that serpent did you suddenly wake up and remember how good I was to you.

"And now, since you're badly hurt and poisoned with nobody to take care of you, you're generously giving me a chance to come back and wait on you?

"You've really got it all figured out, don't you?"

She scoffed, and the last trace of old affection vanished from her eyes. All that was left was indifference.

"I'm not your maid, and you can't just call me over and dismiss me whenever you want. I'm definitely not some tool you can grab whenever you get hurt and need a backup plan."
