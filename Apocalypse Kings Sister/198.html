Chapter 198

Beside him, Orion felt the same wave rolling through him, moved more than he could say.

His sister had stood up for Elijah, and Elijah was practically his own brother. Close enough, then, that she'd stood up for him too.

Warmth spread through his usually cool eyes, his gaze on her growing gentler, more cherishing by the moment.

His sister really was something else.

In the middle of the room, Chase knelt rigid, pain still tearing through his mind in waves, humiliation and venom coiling deep in his chest.

His fists clenched white-knuckled, his face burning with a humiliation he'd never known in his life.

Ugly thoughts bred and multiplied in him, hatred taking root.

Revenge was a dish he was willing to wait on.

He wouldn't forget today. He'd pay it back a thousand times over.

Just let him find his opening.

But right now, pinned by her psychic hold, powerless to resist, he had no choice but to swallow it.

Under everyone's eyes, he gave a low, strangled nod, his voice dry and cracked.

“I'm sorry. I was wrong.”

“I agree. Elijah leads this mission.”

Satisfied, Paisley let her psychic hold release completely.

The invisible weight on Chase lifted all at once. His whole body sagged, and he hauled himself up off the floor in a graceless heap, crooked and bone-white, every trace of his old arrogance gone.

Just then, the office door eased open from outside.

The Director walked in, a seemingly meek figure trailing close behind him—Rachel.

Unaware of the standoff that had just ended, the Director smiled and made the introduction formally.

“I'd like to introduce a new addition to the mission team: Rachel Lennox.”

“She's a Level Four Water Affinity user. Her combat power's modest, but she has a rare gift—the ability to glimpse the future.”

“She's already foreseen exactly where the mission's data and equipment are hidden. Bringing her along should make this go twice as smoothly and cut out a lot of unknown risk.”

Rachel smiled, soft and gentle, and introduced herself in a quiet voice.

“Hello, everyone, I'm Rachel Lennox. I'm honored to work alongside all of you on this mission. I hope you'll look out for me.”

Her voice was soft, her manner humble, every bit the picture of someone easy to get along with.

But those gentle, innocent-looking eyes kept drifting, just barely, toward Chase, calculations clicking away underneath.

She'd fought to get onto this mission the moment she'd learned he'd be here.

She had no one backing her right now, not even enough to keep herself fed properly.

Everything she needed—food, cultivation resources—had to be bought with points, and a high-risk mission like this paid well.

With Elijah and Orion on the team, she was confident the mission would succeed.

But the real point was getting close to Chase.

In her last life, Chase had been one of her many devoted admirers, indulging her every whim.

She'd gone out of her way to learn that before he transferred back to Aetheris, Chase had held real power here at Rivergate, living like a king.

Even this early in the apocalypse, he was already a Level Five psychic—top-tier strength by any measure.

Latch onto him, and with him watching her back, she wouldn't just survive this mission. She could ride his influence all the way back to Southspire safely, and from there, on to Aetheris together.

At minimum, she'd never have to worry about food, resources, or her own safety again.

As for her so-called foresight—it was nothing but a cover story, dressed up around the fact that she'd come back from a past life.

She was working off scattered memories, pieces of news coverage she recalled from this exact mission the first time around.

Roughly which monsters they'd run into at this point, which zones carried the real danger, and about where the equipment had been found.

The Director, playing it safe as always, saw only upside in having one more person to flag risks and point the way—nothing to lose, everything to gain—so he'd believed her easily.

Most of the team held their doubts, but one more Affinity user meant one less unknown, and that was worth having.

Especially a treasured young lady who seemed this gentle, this well-mannered.

Paisley alone saw straight through it, and found the whole thing almost funny.

She couldn't help rolling her eyes, just slightly, utterly done with the whole act.

There was nowhere Rachel Lennox wouldn't show up.

Like some indestructible cockroach—no mission, no occasion, was off-limits to her shameless little schemes, latching onto whoever was useful and stealing whatever shine she could.
