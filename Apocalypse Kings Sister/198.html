Chapter 198

Beside him, Orion was just as stirred up, deeply touched.

Paisley had stood up for Elijah, and Elijah was like a brother to him, so basically, she'd stood up for him too.

Warmth spread through his usually cool eyes, and he looked at Paisley more gently and tenderly than ever. My sister's the best!

In the middle of the room, Chase knelt rigid, pain still tearing through his mind in waves, humiliation and venom coiling deep in his chest.

He clenched his fists so hard his knuckles turned white. His face burned with shame, a humiliation he'd never suffered in his entire life.

Vicious thoughts sprouted and spread wild inside him, and hatred quietly took root.

He could wait for his revenge, however long it took. I'll never forget this, and I'll pay it back a thousand times over! Just wait till I get my chance!

But right now, with her psychic hold pinning him down, he couldn't fight back, so he had no choice but to swallow his pride.

With everyone watching, he gave a stiff nod, his voice dry and hoarse, choking on the humiliation.

"I'm sorry. I was wrong!

"I agree. Elijah will be the captain for this mission."

Now that she had her answer, Paisley's expression relaxed, and she released her psychic hold.

The invisible pressure on Chase vanished all at once. He went limp and clumsily pushed himself up off the floor, swaying, his face white as paper, without a trace of his old arrogance.

Just then, the office door eased open from outside.

The head of the base walked in, followed closely by someone who looked sweet and meek. It was Rachel.

The head of the base didn't realize a tense standoff had just ended. He smiled and formally introduced her to everyone.

"I'd like to introduce a new member of this mission, Rachel Lennox.

"She's a Level Four Water Affinity user. She's not much of a fighter, but she has a special gift. She can see the future.

"She's already foreseen exactly where the classified data and precision equipment are hidden. If we bring her to the aquarium, it'll make the mission a lot easier and help us avoid a lot of unknown risks."

As soon as he finished, Rachel smiled softly and introduced herself in a gentle voice.

"Hi, everyone. I'm Rachel Lennox. I'm so happy to be working with all of you on this mission, and I hope you'll look out for me."

Her voice was soft and her manner humble, and she seemed sensible, sweet, and easy to get along with.

But out of the corner of her eye, she kept sneaking glances at Chase, her mind busy scheming.

She'd only pushed to join this mission because she'd found out Chase would be here.

Right now, she had nobody to rely on, and she was even struggling to get enough to eat.

Everything she needed to live and level up had to be bought with contribution points, and this high-risk mission paid well.

With heavy hitters like Elijah and Orion on the team, she was sure the mission would succeed.

But mostly, she wanted to get close to Chase.

In her past life, Chase had been one of her many lovesick doormats, giving in to her every whim.

Today, she'd made a point of asking around and found out that before Chase transferred back to Aetheris, he'd held a high position at Rivergate Base, with special privileges, living the good life.

Chase was already a Level Five psychic now, one of the strongest fighters in the early days of the apocalypse.

As long as she latched onto him and had him protecting her the whole way, she wouldn't just complete the mission. She could use his influence to get back to Southspire safely, and then go on to Aetheris with him.

At the very least, she'd have plenty of food and a steady supply of resources, and she wouldn't have to live in constant fear for her safety.

As for her so-called gift of foresight, it was just an excuse she'd made up, since she'd been reborn.

She pieced together scattered memories from her past life and recalled the news reports about this mission. She knew roughly which waves of monsters they'd face at the aquarium around this time, where the danger zones were, and where the equipment was hidden.

The head of the base always played it safe. Having one more person to predict danger and point the way had nothing but upside, so naturally, he was willing to believe her.

Most of the team only half believed it, but adding another Affinity user meant a little less danger, so they were willing to go along with it.

Especially since she was a rich girl from a good family who seemed so gentle and polite.

Only Paisley saw right through her, and she found it all ridiculous.

She couldn't help sneaking an eye roll, beyond fed up.

Rachel just shows up everywhere, doesn't she?

She was like a cockroach that wouldn't die. No matter the place or the mission, she'd shamelessly squeeze her way in to grab opportunities, cling to whoever could help her, and steal the spotlight.
