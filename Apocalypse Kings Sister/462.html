Chapter 462

The Lizard King lowered its body, ready to finish them.

Then a vast, invisible wave of psychic pressure slammed down from above.

Boom.

The pressure rolled in like a tidal wave and crushed down over the whole fourth floor. Every black-scale lizard on the level began to thrash in agony.

Shrieks tore through the air.

The Lizard King, invincible a moment ago, convulsed and writhed. It slammed its huge head into the floor again and again, as if ten thousand steel needles were driving into its mind. The pain stripped away its strength and dropped it into a frenzy.

Elijah and Cole felt the weight lift. They stared toward the source in a daze.

Paisley stood at the corridor entrance, sword in hand, backlit and calm.

She came.

She crossed to them fast and fed them spirit water to restore their power and chase off the exhaustion.

Five seconds later, the Lizard King tore itself free of the pressure. Its huge red slit pupils locked onto Paisley, burning with hatred. It remembered who had ruined its kill.

"ROAR!"

The bellow shook the whole mall. Black-scale lizards poured out of every floor and crack and surged toward her.

The Lizard King's armored tail came whistling at her waist, heavy with force and poison. One hit would tear a body apart.

Paisley's eyes stayed cold. If this thing doesn't die, the horde never breaks. None of us leaves here.

She summoned her Plant Affinity.

Thick green vines burst through the walls and wrapped the giant body in layer after layer, pinning it in place.

Then the impossible happened. The thorns on her vines, strong enough to hold a Level Six beast, couldn't even dent its scales. They left no mark at all.

The Lizard King's body was invulnerable.

Paisley's gaze sharpened. She raised the Frostbloom Sword, flashed forward, and put everything she had into one strike at the weak point at the base of its skull.

Clang!

Sparks flew. The blade that cut through anything left only a faint white scratch. It didn't draw a drop of blood. It didn't even move the beast.

It was the first time her sword had ever failed.

I'm just not strong enough yet.

She didn't dwell on it. Violet Oblivion Flame roared up in her palm and washed over the beast's whole body.

It only scorched the surface. To the Lizard King, it was a scratch on the skin.

It doesn't even fear fire.

Vines couldn't hold it. The sword couldn't cut it. Fire couldn't burn it.

More lizards crowded in, filling the corridor, and the stench of rot pressed closer. Death hung over the floor.

Elijah leaned against the wall to stay upright. For the first time, helplessness showed in his eyes.

If we can't kill it, we can't leave. We die here.

He had never seen a beast with a defense this absurd.

Cole's chest heaved, his body covered in blood. Watching a monster that steel, fire, and water couldn't touch, he felt only despair.

Our powers are a joke to this thing.
