Chapter 194

Orion's softened expression turned cool all of a sudden. He looked down to hide the frustration and annoyance churning inside him.

His fingers clenched slightly, and his cool presence turned even colder.

He didn't like strangers eyeing the sister he'd only just found. Nobody was good enough for Paisley.

He hadn't even added her as a contact himself yet. And the longer he looked at this pretty-boy doctor, the more he couldn't stand him.

Spencer frowned hard and grumbled to himself. Paisley's way too nice and innocent. She has no idea how nasty people can be.

Thomas, on the other hand, decided he'd find time later to teach Paisley how to turn down strangers, men and women alike.

In the apocalypse, being too nice and naive could get a person killed.

Ashley frowned too, clearly not happy with how Paisley had handled it.

Marcus was even less pleased.

His jaw tightened faintly, his cool eyes sweeping over the doctor, calm on the surface but carrying a sliver of real chill underneath.

A quiet, cold pressure spread off him, and he went even further into himself, saying nothing at all.

Inside, he was seething. He couldn't stand the doctor getting close to her.

I haven't even gotten my turn yet, and some doctor beats me to it?

He'd known all along that someone as amazing as Paisley would only attract more and more admirers.

But it still bothered him.

It was just a simple contact add, but it had thrown everyone into quiet turmoil.

Paisley had noticed every little change in their expressions, and she found it both exasperating and funny.

Do my family and friends really think I'm that weak? I'm not some naive little flower.

She didn't bother calling out their sneaky, childish little feelings. She just looked up and spoke softly, breaking the awkward silence.

"Come on, let's go back and have dinner."

With that, she led the way out.

A second later, everyone dropped their bad moods and hurried after her without a word.

The other doctors and nurses in the hallway watched them go and murmured enviously.

"Who is that girl, anyway?"

"Everyone around her is a top-tier Affinity user."

"Look at how protective they are of her, how tense they get. She obviously means a lot to them."

The attending doctor stayed where he was and opened his contact list. He stared at Paisley's simple, clean profile picture and couldn't help smiling a little.

Meanwhile, the group made their way to the base cafeteria.

The base cafeteria was flexible about payment and took both contribution points and crystal cores.

Paisley had just gotten another 10,000 contribution points, so she had plenty to spare. She booked a private room and said generously, "Tonight, the stewpot's on me."

In the apocalypse, supplies were scarce, fresh produce was rare, and meat was precious. Just getting a bite of hot food was a luxury, let alone a steaming stewpot packed with ingredients, a treat almost nobody dared to dream of.

Everyone's eyes lit up in delight.

Ashley was thrilled. "A stewpot?! Paisley, that's way too much!"

"No, no, we can't let you treat us, Paisley. We'll pay!"

But deep down, everyone was already overflowing with happiness, looking excited and eager.

Only Elijah stayed relaxed, smiling at her fondly as he nodded, his voice soft and warm. "All right. Thanks, Pais."

He looked up and smiled, so devastatingly handsome that no one could compare.

The sheer brilliance of that smile caught Paisley off guard, and for a second, she just stared at him, dazed.

Her heart skipped, heat crept up the back of her neck, and her cheeks turned a little pink.

She quickly pushed down the flutter in her chest, turned her flushed face away, and went back to ordering, pretending to be calm.

When they saw that, the others stopped protesting and called out the dishes they liked one by one. They ordered more than 20 plates, with a good mix of meat and veggies.

When they paid, the price was surprisingly cheap.

More than 20 generous plates only cost 24 contribution points in total, which came out to about one point a plate.

Paisley checked her balance, pleased.

She now had 61,714 contribution points. After paying 24 for the meal, she had 61,690 left, which made her pretty well-off these days.
