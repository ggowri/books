Chapter 241

With a loud crackle, Elijah's silver-blue lightning burst across the sky, and dense arcs of electricity slammed down on the giant bear's body with brutal force.

No one dared to hesitate. Everyone attacked at once, unleashing every kind of power at the giant mutant bear.

Orion went all out with his Metal Affinity, sending sharp golden metal blades flying in wave after wave. Thomas used his Plant Affinity, and countless sharp wooden spikes burst out of the ground and stabbed at the bear.

Spencer's Fire Affinity surged, and clusters of scorching flames rose into the air in a blaze of heat. Ashley used her Earth Affinity to slam a thick wall of earth and rocks into the giant bear.

Mason sent crisscrossing blades of wind to try to cut through the bear's hide, and Marcus formed heavy boulders out of rock and soil and hurled them at it with all his strength. Madison shaped freezing water into sharp blades, aiming them at the bear to break through its defenses.

Lightning, Metal, Plant, Fire, Earth, Wind, and Water, all seven kinds of powers, exploded together. Light and shadow crossed in a massive, stunning display.

But a second later, everyone's face went white.

The Level Six mutant bear had thick skin and tough flesh, and the apocalypse's dark energy had forged its body into something nearly unbreakable. Its defense was terrifying.

Everyone's full-power attacks hit its thick fur and only stirred up faint ripples of black mist. They couldn't even leave a scratch.

The attack had looked so huge, but to the giant bear, it was like being tickled. It didn't hurt at all.

The bear didn't even pause. The savagery in its blood-red eyes only grew stronger, and its deep roar shook the whole forest as it charged at them again.

Its huge black shadow blotted out the sky, and their chests went tight.

In the chaos of the fight, the shockwaves knocked Ashley, the youngest, off balance. She stumbled a few steps, and the giant bear zeroed in on the opening right away.

Her little face went white with fear, and she couldn't help crying out. "Paisley! Help... help me..."

In all the chaos, everyone had been focused on fighting the giant bear head-on, and nobody had paid any attention to Paisley.

While everyone was keeping the giant bear busy, she'd been secretly harvesting the whole strawberry patch.

These were mutant energy strawberries, a rare find in the apocalypse, packed with pure energy. They were incredibly valuable.

If this fight dragged on, the Level Six bear's savage destructive power would wreck and trample the whole strawberry patch. Forget picking and eating them. There wouldn't even be a single leaf left.

With an opportunity like this right in front of her, there was no way she'd let it go to waste.

"Take it... Take all of it!" Paisley's eyes were calm, and she swept her hand out in one clean motion.

The whole lush strawberry patch vanished into her Storage Dimension, roots, thriving plants, dark soil, and all.

At the same moment, Trovie's bright alert chimed in her head.

[Master, a huge amount of rare mutant resources has been stored! Each mutant energy strawberry is worth 30 points, and each mutant strawberry plant is worth 500! This harvest includes 200 strawberry plants and 5,000 ripe strawberries, so do you want to trade them all for points?]

Paisley answered silently. [Hold on, don't trade them yet. Even if I did, it still wouldn't be enough points to level up.]

Trovie couldn't help gasping.

[Master! The 200 strawberry plants alone are worth 100,000 points! That's not even counting the 5,000 strawberries, so are you really not trading them in?]

Paisley smiled a little.

A hundred thousand points really was a lot.

[If they're worth that much, I'd rather grow them in the Storage Dimension, so we can all eat them and trade for even more points later. Anyway, we got lucky today. Like I said, let's hold on to them for now.]

These mutant strawberry plants could keep flowering and bearing fruit in her Storage Dimension, a top-tier renewable resource that was worth way more than a one-time trade.

Trovie was convinced and gushed with admiration.

[You're right, Master. You're so smart.]

Just then, Spencer's panicked shout exploded beside her. "Paisley, why are you spacing out right now? Look out!"

The Level Six bear that everyone's powers had been holding back had gone into a total frenzy and thrown off all the powers restraining it. When it saw the strawberries suddenly vanish, it swung its huge body around and lunged straight at Paisley, who wasn't ready for it, with world-shattering force!
