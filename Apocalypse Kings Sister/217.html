Chapter 217

The scattered bits of tentacle on the floor twitched slightly, and the dark blue-green poisonous blood quickly gathered back together. In just a few seconds, the tentacle that had been blown to pieces grew back, as good as new, with no sign it had ever been badly hurt.

The old man watched his creations regenerate, looking proud and sick with satisfaction, and he tried to tempt them.

"I'll admit it, your weapons really are powerful. But do you see this? They can't die, and nothing can hurt them.

"Don't you want power like that, the kind that lives forever? Stop fighting it, be good test subjects, and evolve into what they are. That's the best place you could ever end up."

Everyone's heart sank. They watched that unstoppable regeneration and went pale, helpless and terrified.

They didn't want to become half-human freaks like that.

If the mutants could regenerate from any physical attack, what were they supposed to do? Wasn't there a more direct way to kill these monsters?

Paisley stayed calm. Suddenly, she remembered a news report she'd read in her past life about the underground research lab.

These man-made mutants could regrow their limbs and tentacles forever. The only exception was the brain, which was their lifeline and their one fatal weakness. The brain's nerves were bound up with the crystal core, and once they were destroyed, there was no regenerating or recovering.

Once she'd figured it out, Paisley's eyes turned cold and sharp, and she stopped hesitating.

She raised the gun, locked the scope steadily on the old man's laughing head, and gently squeezed the trigger.

A thin, perfectly focused beam of blue light shot through the air faster than the eye could follow, heading straight for the spot between the old man's eyebrows.

A second later, his arrogant laughter stopped dead.

The old man froze in place. His murky eyes went blank and empty, and a small, scorched hole had been blown in the middle of his head. He was dead.

His body stayed frozen in that arrogant pose, without a single twitch.

For a moment, the whole place went dead silent.

Every team member there, Elijah and Orion included, was stunned. They stared in shock and disbelief, frozen in place, unable to snap out of it for a long time.

The dark tunnel was so quiet they could've heard a pin drop, with only a cold draft still blowing through. The old man's crazy, arrogant words still seemed to echo in their ears, but the mad scientist who'd claimed to control eternal life and evolution was dead now.

A few seconds later, a team member swallowed hard and broke the dead silence with a shaky voice. "He... he's dead, just like that?"

One by one, everyone came to their senses, and the shock kept rolling through them like a tide. Their eyes stayed glued to Paisley's cool profile, filled with doubt and awe.

No one could figure it out. Even the energy guns couldn't kill those undying mutants by blasting their bodies, yet Paisley had taken out the man controlling them with one clean shot.

Their minds were reeling, full of questions and shock.

How had she found the weak spot so precisely and killed him with one shot? That kind of foresight, sharp eye, and ruthless skill was unbelievable. Could her real fighting power already be so insanely strong that it was far beyond what anyone here understood?

Before anyone could calm down, something happened to the six half-human, half-tentacle mutants that were left. Whether they lived or died had depended entirely on the old man.

Now that their master was suddenly dead, the mental chains that had locked down their minds and controlled their every move snapped.

A high-level beast's instinctive fear swallowed whatever reason they had left.

The mutants that had been savage and fearless a moment ago fell into total panic and terror.

Countless dark blue-green tentacles slapped and flailed in every direction, and their bodies ran wild through the tunnel, out of control. Some slammed into the walls, others crashed into each other, and they scrambled away in a total mess.

Without someone controlling them, they were just a bunch of mindless monsters that happened to regenerate.

Paisley saw it all, and her eyes stayed cold, without a shred of pity.

She raised the energy gun and pulled the trigger again and again.

One sharp, focused blue beam after another shot through the air, moving so fast they left only afterimages, each one locked precisely on a mutant's head.

Sharp cracks rang out one after another, and every single shot was a headshot!

Every mutant's head burst open, and its crystal core was destroyed, so its regeneration was gone for good.

One after another, all six high-level mutants dropped dead.

The dark blue-green tentacles went limp. All their life was gone, and their terrifying savagery and aura faded to nothing.

It had taken only a few seconds to wipe out all six.
