Chapter 217

The scattered chunks of tendril on the ground twitched, dark-teal blood pulling itself back together. Within seconds, the shredded tendril had reformed completely, no trace of damage left behind.

The old man watched his creation regenerate, pride and sick satisfaction written across his face as he spoke, voice coaxing:

"I'll admit, your weapons do pack a punch.

But do you see this?

These bodies can't die. They don't fear harm anymore."

"Wouldn't you want power like that? Power that never dies?

Stop fighting. Become my subjects. Evolve into what they are. It's the best future any of you could hope for."

Hope sank in every chest. Faces went pale as they stared at regeneration they had no answer for, fear and helplessness closing in.

None of them wanted to become something like that, not quite human, not quite anything.

If the body just healed itself no matter what, what could they even do?

Wasn't there a more direct way to kill these things?

Paisley stayed calm, her mind flashing back to a report she'd read in her last life about an underground research facility.

These engineered mutants could regrow any limb, any tendril, without limit. Except for one thing. The brain. The neural tissue fused with the crystal core was the one true weak point, and if it was destroyed, there was no regenerating from that.

Understanding the key, something cold and sharp flickered in her eyes. She didn't hesitate.

She raised her gun, scope locked on the old man's laughing, triumphant head, and squeezed the trigger.

A thin blue beam, condensed to near-perfect precision, shot out faster than the eye could follow, straight for the point between his brows.

The next second.

His arrogant laughter cut off mid-breath.

He froze in place, his clouded eyes going blank and empty, a small scorched hole blown through the center of his skull. Whatever life had been left in him was gone.

His body stayed locked in that boastful pose, utterly still.

The tunnel fell dead silent.

Every teammate there, Elijah and Orion included, stood frozen, pupils shrunk with disbelief, unable to move for a long moment.

The whole dark tunnel went quiet enough to hear a pin drop, only a faint draft stirring through it. The old man's mad, boastful words still seemed to echo, even as the man who'd claimed mastery over immortality and evolution lay dead.

A few seconds passed before someone's throat worked and they broke the silence, voice shaking:

"He... he's just dead? Like that?"

The shock kept rolling through the group in waves, every eye locked onto Paisley's cool profile, thick with awe and something close to fear.

No one could make sense of it. An undying mutant that their energy guns couldn't put down for good, and its creator had just been dropped in one clean shot.

Their minds reeled, a flood of questions and disbelief crashing through them.

How had she found the one weak point and hit it dead-on?

That level of judgment, of precision, of raw killing efficiency, it didn't make sense.

Was her real strength so far beyond anything they'd assumed, beyond anyone else here entirely?

Before they could gather themselves—

The six half-human creatures left standing had their whole existence tied to the old man's will.

Now that their controller was dead, the mental chains binding their minds, dictating their every move, snapped in an instant.

Pure animal fear swallowed whatever reason they had left.

What had been savage, fearless monsters a moment ago spiraled into pure panic.

Tendrils thrashed wildly in every direction, bodies slamming into walls, crashing into each other, scrambling in blind, desperate chaos.

Without their controller, they were nothing but mindless things with a gift for healing.

Paisley's eyes stayed cold, with no trace of mercy in them.

She raised the gun and fired again and again.

One blue beam after another tore through the air, faster than the eye could track, each one finding its mark dead center in a creature's skull.

Crack, crack, crack—

The sounds rang out in quick succession.

Headshot after headshot.

Every creature's skull burst open, its crystal core destroyed, its regeneration gone for good.

All six high-tier mutants dropped where they stood, dead within seconds.

The dark-teal tendrils went slack, every trace of life and menace draining out of them at once.

In a matter of seconds, all six mutants were gone.
