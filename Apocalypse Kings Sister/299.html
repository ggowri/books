Chapter 299

The words landed, and Rachel's face went bone white, her whole body frozen in place, disbelief written across every feature.

She panicked. The one weakness she'd always used to control Paisley had stopped working.

The Paisley standing in front of her no longer cared about any of it.

She felt like a stranger.

Like a completely different person.

"How... how could you be this heartless!"

Rachel's voice shook, but she pushed on, refusing to let it go.

"If your real parents knew what kind of person you've become, wouldn't they be disappointed too? Don't you care at all what they think?"

The words had barely left her mouth when a sharp, furious shout rang out.

Ashley had reached her limit. She stepped forward and swung her hand in one clean, decisive slap.

"You never shut up, do you!"

The girl's eyes went razor-sharp, her whole presence flaring, fiercely protective.

"However my sister turns out, we'll love her, respect her, and back whatever she decides, no questions asked!"

"Who do you think you are, you selfish little snake, judging my sister and lecturing her like you have any right to?"

"Get lost, you toxic little fake!"

The slap landed with a sharp crack, snapping Rachel's head to the side, her cheek flushing red and swollen in an instant, a burning sting spreading across her face.

She clutched her cheek, stunned, tears welling up fast, shock and fury warring across her face.

"You... you actually hit me?"

"Yeah, I hit you. You earned it."

Ashley's eyes stayed cold and unyielding, not backing down an inch.

Rachel, reeling from the hit, immediately turned to Chase beside her, eyes brimming with tears, playing up her helplessness.

"Chase, you saw that! She hit me in front of everyone! Make her pay for it!"

Chase's expression darkened at once, fists clenching as he moved to step forward.

But before he could take a single step, a tall figure shot out of nowhere.

Orion moved fast, his presence sharp and commanding, and without a word, drove his foot straight into Chase.

The force of the blow, backed by raw Affinity power, sent Chase flying clean off his feet.

He crashed to the ground hard, pain tearing through his whole body, a sharp coppery taste rising in his throat, unable to get back up for a long moment.

"You're nothing. And you thought you could touch my family?"

Orion's voice came out cold and merciless, pressure radiating off him, silencing the whole scene.

The Thompson siblings had all reached early Level Six strength now, worlds apart from where they'd started, while Chase remained stuck at early Level Five. The gap between them wasn't even close.

The scene went dead silent.

Rachel's group stared at Chase lying crumpled on the ground, faces draining white, too terrified to say another word.

If even their strongest member had just been crushed with a single kick, pushing their luck any further would be suicide.

Whatever arrogance, self-pity, or scheming had been left in them dissolved instantly into pure fear.

Paisley's gaze swept coolly over the pathetic group sprawled on the ground, utterly unmoved, and she didn't linger a second longer.

"Let's go."

Everyone piled into the vehicles without delay.

The SUVs roared to life and sped off toward Stillwater Base.

******

Only Rachel's group remained behind, frozen in place, utterly wrecked.

Chase, still reeling from Orion's kick, felt a dull ache radiating through his ribs, his whole body feeling like it had been taken apart. The exhaustion of walking all night combined with the humiliation of being crushed in front of everyone curdled into pure, simmering rage, his expression twisting dark and dangerous.

Gritting through the pain, he forced himself up off the ground, sweeping a cold glance over his cowering companions, his voice turning icy and commanding.

"You two, stay here and make breakfast."

He pointed to two others.

"You two, head into the nearby town, see if there's any usable vehicle left."

Finally, his voice went flat.

"We rest here for now. Figure out the rest after."

The words landed, but instead of complying, the group — having stewed in resentment all night — finally snapped, faces twisting in open defiance, pushing back on the spot.

"What gives you the right to order us around? Who do you think you are?"

"We're all Affinity users here on the same mission. We're supposed to be equals. You really think you're better than us?"

"Exactly! Don't you have hands and feet of your own? Why should I break my back making breakfast for you? Who even are you?"
