Chapter 106

They were already starving, and that slurping sound was crystal clear. It kept drilling into their ears inside the car, torturing their empty stomachs.

The rich smell of meat and sweet desserts was already enough to make their stomachs growl. Hearing someone eat on top of that was pure torture.

Kelly and the three Lennox brothers clenched their fists and gritted their teeth. They were bitter and miserable, but all they could do was take it.

Meanwhile, Paisley found a seat, and only then did everyone else start eating.

Thomas went first. He picked up a piece of the delicate butter-poached shrimp and popped it in his mouth, and after a few bites, his eyes lit up.

"Paisley, this is amazing! It's ridiculously fresh, and the texture's perfect."

"Seriously!" Spencer said, right behind him.

He bit into a tender, savory rib, and the meat slid right off the bone. With his mouth full, he couldn't stop raving about it.

"I've never had anything this good in my whole life. The meat's so tender and flavorful, it's way better than any fancy dinner we had before the apocalypse!"

Elijah calmly sipped the warm chicken soup. The rich, savory broth melted away all his exhaustion, and he looked up at Paisley with warm eyes.

"It's comforting, easy on the stomach, and the flavor's incredible. It could give a Michelin-starred chef a run for his money."

Everyone else chimed in too, and the compliments kept coming.

They ate until they were stuffed, warm and happy.

In the apocalypse, supplies were scarce and dead bodies were everywhere. Even one hot meal or a bowl of clean, warm water was a luxury.

Most survivors spent every day gnawing on cold, hard ration bars and expired instant meals just to stay alive.

In a world this ruined, who wouldn't dream of eating a feast this beautiful, this varied, and this delicious?

If even Elijah was praising it, it had to be seriously good.

Not far away, Jasper's mouth had been watering for ages.

He couldn't take it anymore. He crept toward the group with a piece of stale bread and a bottle of water in his hands. His eyes were glued to the steaming food, and he kept swallowing hard.

He swallowed his pride and asked in a quiet, sucking-up voice.

"Got room for one more? I'll trade whatever I have for just a bite."

Rachel stuck right by Jasper's side. She stared at the bright, glistening food just as hungrily and begged in a soft voice.

"Paisley, I'm so hungry. Could you give me a plate? I'll trade something for it too."

Paisley calmly put down her fork and looked up at the two of them. Her face was blank, and her voice gave nothing away as she named her price.

"Sure. One hundred Level Two crystal cores for a plate of food with one main dish."

Rachel froze, and her face went white.

She'd barely scraped together 60 Level Two crystal cores after risking her life, and she didn't even want to spend those.

She needed them to level up and get stronger.

When she heard the price, her face fell. It killed her to think about paying that much, and she couldn't stop herself from complaining loudly.

"That's way too expensive! What, is your food made of gold?

"This is highway robbery!"

Ashley shot up from her seat right away, fuming, and fired right back.

"My sister's cooking is worth way more than gold!

"It's the apocalypse. What good is gold anyway? Where else are you going to find food this good?"

She put her hands on her hips, not backing down an inch.

"We barely have enough for ourselves. Take it or leave it. Nobody's forcing you!"

Jasper quickly checked what he had on him. He only had 50 Level Two crystal cores.

Food mattered more than anything right now, and it looked like Paisley was about to take back her offer.

Without a second thought, he reached over and snatched the 60 crystal cores right out of Rachel's pocket.

He held out all 110 Level Two crystal cores to Paisley, practically begging.

"I'll take it! There are 110 Level Two crystal cores here. Please, pile the plate high, and give me lots of everything!"

Paisley glanced down at the crystal cores and dropped them right into her bag without batting an eye.

Then, she grabbed a clean plate and fork and loaded Jasper's plate with a huge scoop of buttery mashed potatoes. She added big braised meatballs, savory ribs, and a pile of tender shrimp until it was overflowing.
