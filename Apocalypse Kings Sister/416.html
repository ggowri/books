Chapter 416

With that problem settled, Russell continued, calculating.

"There's one more key piece. Go see him yourself."

"Grant."

"News came from the hospital last night. He was badly hurt and unconscious for days, but he woke briefly around midnight. He passed out again, but he's showing signs of coming back."

"He's a Level Six Affinity user, strong in a fight. If he fully recovers and joins the ambush, our chances go up by thirty percent."

"Take the best Healing Affinity users on the base and the research team straight to the ICU. Spare nothing. Clear the poison, heal him, get him back on the field."

Damon nodded, his cold face blank, killing intent hidden in his eyes.

"Understood, Father. I'll go now."

The Quinn leadership's meeting, full of scheming and killing, finally ended.

Damon didn't waste a second. He called together all of his people.

Within minutes, several of the Quinns' top Healing Affinity users had assembled. The base's core lab sent every researcher on duty. Even Marcus Whitfield, who had been deep in a sealed lab working on an experiment to break down Affinity poison, was dragged out and sent along.

The convoy raced to the hospital.

A few hours later, the ICU was silent. Monitors beeped, and cold white light flooded the room.

On the bed, Grant's lashes shook. Then he slowly opened his heavy eyes.

A cold, deep exhaustion rolled through him.

His bones felt crushed and reassembled. Every inch of him hurt, as if torn apart. The leftover poison still crept along his meridians, and even breathing was a struggle.

His head was foggy. Flashes of his last memory pushed in, the mutant trumpet flower biting his arm off, bloody and fragmented but clear.

He was alive!

He tried to prop himself up. By habit he reached with his left hand for leverage, and there was only emptiness and pain.

His heart sank. He looked down, stiff.

An empty sleeve lay flat and light.

His left hand, his whole left arm, was gone.

Despair gripped his heart. It was hard to breathe.

He had been a Level Six Affinity user who ruled the base, his squad's sharpest blade, an unstoppable ace who had survived countless deadly fights alone.

Now he'd lost an arm, and he was a cripple.

In the apocalypse, strength was everything.

Losing an arm meant poor balance and weaker fighting. It meant he could never return to his peak, never charge into battle again, and all his past glory was worthless.

Reality hit like ice water, soaking him to the bone.

Before he could recover from that, a flood of memories crashed in, one blow after another.

He had been out for so long that everything had changed by the time he opened his eyes.

His squad, the one that had fought beside him through everything, had lost the bet. The Scarlet Squad was disbanded and never regained its glory.

His friends were either bedridden and unconscious, or injured so badly their powers were ruined. His old elite squad was a wreck.

And the girl he'd tried so hard to protect, Chloe, wasn't at his side.

The last person he'd thought of before passing out wasn't here when he woke up.

His career, his glory, the woman he loved, his comrades. Everything he valued most in life had been destroyed at once.

The drop was crushing. This hardened soldier, who had never backed down once, felt his eyes go red and his chest ache.

A heavy helplessness wrapped around him until he couldn't breathe.

Then the heavy door swung open.

Steady footsteps broke the silence.

Damon, in black, tall and cold, walked in first, his presence harsh and commanding.
