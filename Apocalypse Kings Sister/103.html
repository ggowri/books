Chapter 103

They'd come this close to being completely taken over.

At the very last second, they'd felt another, gentler psychic power step in to protect them.

If it hadn't been for that, they might've lost their minds by now.

The two of them sat on the ground, gasping for air.

Being forced under someone else's control was a horrible feeling.

Their minds were still throbbing with pain.

They looked over at Paisley with complicated expressions.

They both knew it. Without her, they'd be dead right now.

Paisley had saved them again.

Only the two of them knew how close a call that had really been.

Luckily, it was all over now!

They were still too weak.

They had to get stronger, fast.

That way, the next time she was in danger, they'd actually be able to help her!

They'd never expected her Psychic Affinity to be this strong.

How many other surprises was she hiding from them?

Rachel stood off in the distance, watching the whole thing and grinding her teeth in fury.

Paisley is so damn lucky.

Rachel had smashed Paisley's Storage Dimension pendant, but somehow, Paisley had ended up with a crazy-strong pet that was practically human, plus a treasure of a sword she'd found who knew where.

And once again, she'd stolen the spotlight.

Sooner or later, she'd get rid of Paisley for good and take everything she had.

There's plenty of time. Once I'm strong enough, I won't have to be afraid of anything.

Then, she shot a disgusted look toward the three Lennox brothers.

All three of them were sprawled in the car, more dead than alive.

If she weren't worried about her reputation, she would've loved to just ditch them.

Paisley took a sip of spirit water. The cool, sweet water slid down her throat, and warm energy spread through her whole body. Her exhaustion melted away, and all the energy she'd used came flooding back.

Then, she pulled out the spirit water she'd already prepared and handed a bottle each to Elijah, Orion, Spencer, and the other main fighters.

"Everyone, drink up. We're not done yet.

"The horde lost its leader, and now they're all over the place. This is our best chance to hit back and wipe them out for good!"

Everyone grabbed a bottle and chugged it. Pure, powerful energy rushed through their bodies, and their drained stamina and abilities came flooding back.

Their exhaustion vanished completely, and they felt strong and sharp again.

Before anyone could even answer, Paisley was already charging into the horde with the Frostbloom Sword in her hand.

The giant 16-foot white cat was right behind her. Its snow-white fur rippled in the wind, and its Level Five Ice Affinity gave off terrifying pressure.

One lazy swipe of its huge claws sent a whole group of zombies flying, their bodies smashed to pieces.

The two of them worked together perfectly and cleared the field insanely fast.

Paisley's blade flashed, and every strike was deadly. Zombies dropped everywhere she went.

The cat's claws swept through everything in their way. Nothing could stop it.

The scattered low-level zombies had no leader and panicked, running every which way. They didn't stand a chance against the two of them. In just a few minutes, the rest of the horde was wiped out, leaving a thick layer of corpses and countless crystal cores all over the ground.

The spirit water hit the rest of the team like a shot of pure adrenaline. Their abilities were fully restored, and their whole bodies were buzzing with energy.

One after another, they shot Paisley grateful looks, then jumped back into the fight.

Everyone knew that in the apocalypse, crystal cores were the key to getting stronger. Fighting like this also gave them real combat experience.

A once-in-a-lifetime chance like this was way too good to pass up.

But Rachel, Kelly, Jasper, and the three Lennox brothers hadn't gotten any of Paisley's spirit water.

They were still sore and exhausted, their abilities completely drained. Fighting zombies in that condition would be suicide.

All they could do was hide out in the cars like cowards.

They watched the brutal fight through the windows with contempt. What a bunch of dumbasses, risking their lives just to get stronger.

Half an hour later, every last zombie was gone, and the fight was over.

Everyone who'd fought had a nice haul of crystal cores.

They were all a mess, but every one of them was grinning in victory.

Elijah looked around.

"Hang in there a little longer, everyone. We'll drive up ahead and rest."

Paisley was all for it.

This place really wasn't a great spot to stop.

In the car, she held the little white cat, which had shrunk back down, and gripped the Level Four Psychic crystal core. With this, her Psychic Affinity should hit late Level Three.

Add in the 300-something crystal cores of other affinities, and her Psychic Affinity should jump straight to Level Four.
