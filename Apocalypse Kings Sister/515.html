Chapter 515

They walked into the hall, and the registrar in charge of the mission hurried over with a happy face.

"Mr. Blackwood, you're back!

Did the water search go well?

I heard contamination outside is bad, and a lot of teams came back empty-handed."

Elijah nodded.

"We're here to turn in the mission."

The registrar's eyes lit up and he pulled out his logbook.

"Great! Bring out the water you collected so we can log and inspect it."

Everyone assumed the water they'd found had been loaded into containers and stored in someone's storage dimension.

No one imagined that Paisley had brought back an entire river.

The registrar noticed that Paisley hadn't moved and was about to ask.

Elijah explained.

"The water source we found is huge. Normal containers can't hold it. We'll need to move somewhere else, a large reservoir."

The registrar was startled and quickly said.

"I know a place that should work perfectly! Follow me."

The news spread, and many Affinity users waiting to register or hand over supplies followed out of curiosity.

They whispered to each other and speculated.

One person was half convinced and muttered to a friend.

"Huge? How big can it be? A few dozen barrels at most. Don't tell me they brought back a whole pond."

The person next to him shook his head, watching with interest.

"Hard to say. Paisley's team took a high-difficulty water mission. Maybe they found a decent-sized spring. But how big? I think it's mostly exaggeration."

Another person was watching for fun, a hint of disbelief at his mouth.

"After the Crimson Rain, even a little clean water is next to impossible to find. If they brought back a few tons, that's already great. They probably mistook slightly contaminated water for clean water."

"I've got nothing better to do, so let's go see. If they really can fix our water shortage, that's good for everyone."

The group followed the registrar down the base corridors to the huge open-air reservoir at the back.

It had been built years ago and held a huge volume. In normal times it stored the base's water. Now the level had fallen almost to the bottom, most of the floor bare, only a thin layer of water left.

The registrar stood at the edge and turned to Paisley's group.

"This is it, the base's biggest reservoir. It's mostly empty, so it should be big enough for the water you brought back!"

Elijah turned to Paisley.

"Can you let out half first?"

"I guess there really isn't a better place."

Paisley walked to the edge of the reservoir. "I'll try."

Trovie, it's all up to you.

Trovie nodded eagerly.

Don't worry, Master. Everything in your dimension is yours to command. Whatever you've put inside, you can do whatever you want with it.

Paisley thought a command.

In the next moment, a rushing river appeared out of nowhere!

The clear water poured down like a silver dragon that had broken its chains, roaring into the reservoir without stopping. Water thundered and sprayed, clean, cold river water pouring in, and the level rose visibly fast.

The crowd that had followed went dead silent. Every eye widened as they stared at the scene.

The team members who'd doubted it a moment ago gaped, unable to believe it.

"This… this isn't a few dozen barrels!"

"My God, it's so powerful. Paisley brought back a whole river?!"

The man who'd doubted her went pale, speechless.

No one had imagined Paisley would bring back an entire living river.

They all wondered how she'd done it.

Was that even something a person could do?

Paisley was like a goddess to them.

Orion, Thomas, and Ashley stood quietly to one side, calm. They'd already seen it once.

The registrar gripped his tester and hurried to the edge to take a sample.

Numbers flashed on the screen, and soon a steady green light came on.

"No toxins! No contamination from the Crimson Rain at all!"

The registrar's voice shook with excitement.

"This much clean water can keep tens of thousands of ordinary people going for a good while!"
