Chapter 262

Right then, Trovie's voice chimed in, bright with excitement.

[Host, there's a hidden storeroom in the back of the supermarket, loaded with treasure and supplies!]

That got her attention.

[Trovie, lead the way.]

she murmured.

Kitty fought on one side, vines cleared the other, and Paisley headed toward the back of the store.

Following Trovie's directions, she found a hidden door, well concealed.

It blended almost perfectly with the wall, no handle, no marking, easy to walk right past without ever knowing it was there.

No wonder every previous scavenger had missed it.

A flick of her fingers undid the hidden lock.

Click.

The door swung slowly inward.

The instant it opened, a Level Four zombie lunged from the shadows, claws out.

But nothing was faster than her vines.

As if alive, they punched straight through its skull.

A wet burst, black blood spraying.

The zombie went rigid and dropped.

A vine curled back, pulling the intact core free and dropping it into her palm, smooth as practice.

She pocketed the core and walked in.

The room was far bigger than it looked from outside, nearly five thousand square feet.

Unopened crates were stacked floor to ceiling, dense and orderly.

Every box was clearly labeled.

Her eyes swept the shelves, the variety almost overwhelming.

Everyday goods, toiletries, laundry supplies.

Rice, oil, salt, seasonings, boxed meals, vacuum-sealed rations.

Even brand-new generators, backup batteries, wiring, and stacks of untouched computers, phones, and chargers.

Everything scarce in this world: survival, power, communication, daily life.

"Take it. Take all of it."

A single thought was all it took.

In a flash, every crate, every machine, every device vanished.

In seconds, the warehouse stood stripped bare.

Trovie's voice chimed in again.

[Host! The real treasure's over there! All the gold and jewels are locked in a safe in the corner!]

She looked over to find three heavy steel safes in the corner, reinforced, complex locks, clearly built for valuables alone.

She wove a thread of Psychic energy together with her Plant Affinity, slipping it quietly into the locks.

Click. Click.

One by one, the locks gave way, the heavy doors swinging open.

Gold filled her vision.

Rows of top-grade gold bars, stacked neat and gleaming.

Beside them, jade pendants, bracelets, diamond necklaces, gemstone rings, antique jewelry, all pristine.

At the bottom, uncirculated gold and silver coins, valuable decorative pieces, every one of them worth a fortune before the world ended.

Trovie practically spun in circles with excitement.

[Jackpot! Host's the best! This gold alone has to be 5,500 pounds, more points coming!]

A small smile crossed Paisley's eyes, and with a wave, the safes and everything in them vanished into her dimension.

But the instant the last safe disappeared—

BOOM!!

The whole building shook, dust raining from the ceiling, a savage roar erupting from the third floor, pressure flooding through every level.

The dormant high-tier zombie had finally been roused by all the disturbance.

ROAR—!!

The sound tore through every floor, nothing like an ordinary zombie's growl, something deeper, more brutal, carrying the full weight of a Level Six.
