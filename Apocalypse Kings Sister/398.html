Chapter 398

But the duty captain only shook his head again, his resolve unwavering.

"Ms. Thompson, and you as well, it's not that I won't give either of you face, and it's not that I'm deliberately making this difficult."

"This case is under the direct, personal oversight of Mr. Quinn himself. He's made it explicitly clear, no leniency, no favoritism, under any circumstances. Unless he personally gives the order to release her, no one's intervention will matter here. Not even your squad's own leadership."

"And the evidence is airtight. The surveillance footage clearly documents Brenda Thompson repeatedly using her Affinity to bully ordinary residents, assault them, misappropriate base-allocated supplies, and disrupt public order. This is not some minor neighborhood squabble, whatever you'd like to call it."

"If either of you knows Mr. Quinn personally, I'd suggest you go plead your case with him directly. Otherwise, she stays exactly where she is."

His words slammed every remaining door shut.

Her teammate's expression shifted abruptly.

As strong as he was, he was still just a core squad member, when it came to actual influence and connections at the base, he was nowhere close to Jasper Quinn's level.

Jasper was the base's untouchable troublemaker, someone who could speak directly with the base's upper leadership, simply not in the same league as him.

Some people were born with a head start.

His own standing, next to Jasper's, counted for absolutely nothing.

Chloe froze completely, the color draining from her perfectly made-up face, her haughty composure collapsing in an instant, pure disbelief flooding her eyes.

How was this possible?!

She was a rare Healing Affinity user, the team's adored darling, and a mere security captain had just flatly refused her personal request?

Jasper Quinn?

She knew he was arrogant, reckless, full of himself, but she'd never actually crossed paths with him, never done anything to offend him. Why would he, of all people, be the one personally pursuing this case against her mother?

Unwilling to accept it, Chloe pressed through gritted teeth.

"Why would Mr. Quinn care about our family's business? This has nothing to do with him! Why is he targeting my mother specifically?!"

The duty captain glanced at her, his tone flat but cutting straight to the point, shattering whatever false hope she had left.

"It's not that Mr. Quinn is targeting you two. It's that you've offended the wrong person."

He lowered his voice slightly, just enough for her to hear.

"Mr. Quinn is doing this specifically on Ms. Lennox's behalf."

The name landed like a lightning strike. Chloe's whole body jolted, and she froze completely on the spot.

Paisley Lennox.

That name had swept through the entire base in a single day, eclipsing every powerhouse of the younger generation, even putting Elijah Blackwood a step behind, an unbelievable, overwhelming force.

Only now, belatedly, did Chloe realize, the person her mother had bullied and humiliated today was Paisley's own mother!

Someone so precious to a top-tier powerhouse that even the base's upper leadership didn't dare offend her, that everyone scrambled to please and stay on her good side!

Of course.

Beside her, her teammate's eyes had gone completely flat, drained of all hope.

He'd planned to leverage his status as a core flagship squad member to apply pressure, to plead for leniency, now, it all felt absurd, even laughable.

He understood now, fully, there was no room to negotiate here at all.

Crossing Paisley meant that detention and formal investigation was the least of what could happen, even outright expulsion from the base wouldn't be out of the question, and no one, no faction, would dare speak a word on Brenda's behalf.

After the interrogation room door closed, Brenda had heard every word of the exchange outside, clear as day.

Her legs gave out completely, her face turning ashen, her whole body going rigid and cold.

Every trace of her old arrogance, every bit of her bullying confidence, dissolved completely in that moment, replaced by pure despair, seeping into every inch of her.

She'd thrown her weight around for half her life, relying on her daughter and a scrap of Affinity power, bullying countless people, taking advantage wherever she could.

She never imagined she'd end up brought down by the very person she'd looked down on most and bullied without mercy, her own sister-in-law.

Despair and panic crushed whatever composure she had left. Brenda clung to the window frame through the glass, tears streaming down her face, sobbing hysterically.

"Chloe! Chloe! Mom really doesn't want to stay in this awful place! It's cold and dark in here!"

"Go beg Mr. Quinn! Go find Jasper! With your looks, he'll probably cave fast, and Mom can get out!"

Chloe stood frozen, her once-perfect makeup now ruined and pale, her fingers ice-cold, consumed by a humiliation she could barely stand.

She stared at her mother, hysterical and completely unraveled, her voice trembling.

"Mom, what are you even saying?"

"You know exactly what kind of person Jasper Quinn is! He's a reckless playboy, completely out of control, you want me to go beg him?"
