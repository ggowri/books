Chapter 397

"Doesn't matter what you say, you broke the rules here, and this case is under direct oversight. Nothing outweighs that."

"Mr. Quinn is personally overseeing this case. Until he says otherwise, no one's going to speak up for you."

With that, they hauled the still-struggling Brenda away without further delay.

The moment the gate locked behind her, Brenda collapsed entirely, overcome with fear and regret.

Mr. Quinn?

It was him who'd ordered this.

That playboy might not have much going for him personally, but he was the one person the head of the Quinn family doted on most.

Someone no one dared casually provoke.

She couldn't remember ever offending him.

Why was something this small landing her in a cell?

What she'd thought was a minor incident had turned into an unrecoverable disaster.

Her years of arrogance and bullying were finally catching up with her, the price steep.

Outside the villa, the scene lay in chaos, the crowd slowly dispersing, leaving Grandpa Thompson standing frozen, body cold, regret with nowhere to go.

In just half a day, the senior branch had fallen from its high perch, bullying everyone in sight, to public disgrace and arrest, the whiplash leaving him unable to breathe.

Had he really been wrong this whole time?

He was the one who'd ruined the Thompson family.

Word of Brenda's arrest spread fast.

Chloe, who was at the hospital at that moment, putting on a show of devoted waiting by Grant's bedside, got the news of her mother's public arrest almost immediately.

The second the call came through, Chloe's face went white, shock and fury flooding through her.

As far as she knew, her mother, though arrogant and fond of taking advantage, always knew how to stay within limits, only ever picking on powerless, ordinary people, never anything serious enough to cause real trouble.

And besides, she was the base's rare Healing Affinity user, the team's cherished darling, and her boyfriend Grant was a core powerhouse of the flagship squad, a formidable figure at the base in his own right.

Not to mention, she was a valued relation to the Quinn family.

With all that backing her, her mother's behavior was, at worst, minor mischief, the enforcement officers would look the other way, never take it seriously.

How could a simple supply dispute, a bit of arguing, end with her mother arrested and formally investigated by the Security Patrol?

Furious and panicked, Chloe didn't waste a second thinking it through. She dropped everything and rushed out of the hospital, heading straight for the Security Office.

Her face, perfectly made up, carried its usual haughty, entitled edge, certain that once she showed up, this whole mess would be resolved immediately, and her mother released without a scratch.

Her teammate, who'd already regained consciousness and was worried about her, followed close behind. His once cool, composed expression now carried a protective heaviness, intent on helping Chloe settle this.

By the time the two of them reached the Security Office, Brenda was being held temporarily in an interrogation room, disheveled and wretched, eyes bloodshot, pounding on the door and shouting without pause, nowhere near her usual high-and-mighty self.

The moment she spotted her daughter arriving, Brenda completely broke down, screaming through the glass.

"Chloe! Get Mom out of here! They're arresting people for no reason! They're deliberately targeting me! Do something, quick!"

Seeing this, Chloe's anger flared hotter. She turned to face the Security Office's duty captain directly, her posture haughty, her tone dripping with entitled pressure.

"I'm Chloe Thompson, Healing Affinity user of the flagship squad. That's my mother in there. This is just an ordinary neighborhood dispute, there's no need to blow it this far out of proportion."

"Please release her immediately. I'll personally vouch for this, and I'll make sure to discipline my mother properly going forward."

Her tone was dismissive, as if her mother's repeated bullying, assault, and theft of other people's property were nothing but a trivial matter not even worth mentioning.

In the past, the moment she'd announced her identity, every department at the base would have shown her deference, bending over backward to accommodate her.

But today, the duty captain only glanced at her briefly, his expression completely unmoved, his tone flat and procedural.

"Ms. Thompson, I'm sorry, but this detainee is not eligible for release."

Chloe's face froze instantly, disbelief flooding her eyes.

"Not eligible for release? Do you know who I am? I'm the squad's Healing Affinity user, Grant Quinn's girlfriend!"

The teammate beside her stepped in at the right moment, his presence steady, carrying the natural pressure of a high-tier Affinity user, his voice low and serious.

"Captain, this is a small matter. There's no need to make it this ugly. Please, grant us this one courtesy, I'll remember the favor."

In his view, he and Chloe showing up in person had already given the Security Office more than enough face; there was no reasonable way the officer could refuse.
