Chapter 414

He wanted to stick close to her, even if he only trailed along quietly. It would be enough.

Before Paisley could answer, Elijah spoke first.

His voice stayed low and smooth, with no edge in it, but there was no room to argue in it, and he drew a quiet line between them.

"Mr. Quinn, isn't it? Aren't you here with your girlfriend?"

"I won't keep you from your date. No need to trouble yourself. I can walk Pais around myself."

It was polite and firm. He turned the offer down without a fuss and reminded Jasper he had a girlfriend, drawing a boundary.

Jasper froze. It slowly sank in that Elijah's tone held a faint chill, and he caught the look in Elijah's eyes as they rested on Paisley, soft in a way that was meant for her alone.

Then he glanced back at his date.

It dawned on him that he had done something that looked bad.

His date had finally gotten his attention. She was already unhappy, and now she couldn't hold back the jealousy and muttered something under her breath, rude and insulting. She clearly didn't think much of Paisley.

Jasper's brows snapped together.

Who did she think she was, talking about Paisley like that?

Paisley was the big thigh he intended to cling to.

He whipped around and glared at her, his voice cold. "You're nobody. Get out of here."

It wasn't loud, but it carried a Quinn heir's innate authority. The woman went white, and the dress dropped from her hands.

She'd never seen him this vicious. She didn't dare say another word. She bit her lip and ran off in tears.

Jasper turned back to Paisley, back to his fawning look.

"Sorry about that. She doesn't know better. Please don't be mad at me."

"Okay, I'll leave you two alone."

He backed away reluctantly, repeating, "Paisley, if you ever need anything, call me on the communicator. I'm online twenty-four seven. I'll come right away!"

"Mm."

Paisley nodded lightly, still distant.

Having gotten a reply, Jasper finally left, satisfied.

The two of them were alone again.

They kept walking side by side.

Paisley was sharp, and she'd noticed Elijah's mood had shifted.

She glanced at the calm man beside her, who showed nothing, and a playful smile touched her eyes.

She slowed on purpose and fished for it. "You don't like Jasper, do you?"

Elijah paused and looked down into her bright, smiling eyes. He didn't pretend and he didn't deny it.

He answered honestly, his voice low and soft, with a trace of stubborn possessiveness.

"No."

"I just want to be alone with you."

It was the first time he'd said anything this direct, showing her what was really in his heart.

The words landed softly and left her heart burning.

Paisley's ears went warm. She looked away from his intense gaze and pretended to study the window display. She couldn't stop the corner of her mouth from lifting.

Elijah saw her red ears, and the warmth in his eyes grew.

He leaned in half a step and lowered his voice, tentative and soft.

"You promised to help him once. What about me?"

"If I ever ran into trouble, Pais, would you stand with me, no questions asked?"

He was a Blackwood heir whom nobody dared cross. Yet now he had dropped all his edges and pride, and he sounded like a kid asking for special treatment, hoping for an answer.

Paisley's heart softened. She turned to him, eyes bright and warm, her voice certain.

"You're different."

Just a few words, worth more than a thousand.

Elijah's eyes lit up, and every trace of jealousy vanished. Only warmth and joy filled him.

His heart skipped out of rhythm, fast and burning.

He turned those words over and over in his mind, his throat tight, a hundred quiet hopes rising.

So I've always been one of a kind to her.

Does that mean she likes me a little too?

He stared at her, intense and hot, his gaze fixed on her face. It was so focused, so open, that it nearly wrapped around her.
