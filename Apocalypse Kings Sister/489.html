Chapter 489

"Impossible..."

A Quinn core member mumbled, lost. "After a trap like that, plus the Great Freeze, how could they come back whole?"

Because only they knew how many pits they'd dug for them, and how many traps the team must have crossed to get back.

To stop them from coming home alive, they'd used almost every resource they had.

They'd even sent out every mutant creature and mutant beast they had spent so long raising.

And yet they all came back safe.

Is that even something people can do?

Nobody answered. Only humiliation and silence remained.

The senior officials swallowed their shock and stood. They organized everyone to head to the rear supply depots to inspect the mission results.

Everyone had the same question.

The Freeze had struck without warning, the road was deadly, and they'd been ambushed at every turn. Yet Falcon Squad had not only returned in full but claimed they'd brought back huge supplies of winter grain.

How had they managed to bring out enough to carry the base through winter?

The senior officials and staff hurried to the base's biggest supply depot area. The depots were still empty. The stockpile was nearly gone, and it was the base's biggest problem for winter.

Everyone stopped and watched Falcon with curiosity and hope.

With everyone watching, Paisley walked calmly to the center of the depot and made a thought.

Hum!

A soft white glimmer of space light lit up, and gentle, vast spatial power spread out.

Next moment, a mountain of supplies poured out like a flood!

Sacks of oats and flour, neat piles of ration bars, dehydrated vegetables, canned meat, all flowed out of her Storage Dimension and quickly filled the empty depot.

In just one minute!

The five empty depots were packed full, stacked tight and neat. Every depot held enough stock to carry the whole base through the winter.

A Storage Dimension that could hold this much was astonishing, a top-tier, enormous Affinity that had never existed at Aetheris Base.

Shock, joy, awe, and excitement flooded everyone.

Everyone looked at Paisley differently. It was no longer simple admiration for a gifted Affinity user. It was real awe.

Combat Affinities, plus a top-tier, enormous Storage Dimension!

How many cards was Paisley hiding?!

What kind of terrifying genius was she?!

Beside them, the Quinn faction's faces were white as paper and their bodies stiff. They stood out in the crowd, utterly humiliated.

Their earlier mockery, certainty, trouble-making, and pressure looked absurd now.

Falcon Squad had really done it. They'd brought back all the supplies.

They'd turned a dead end into a win, come back whole, and loaded with supplies!

Falcon Squad had used the best possible results to slap every doubter and schemer in the face!

The base commander stepped forward at once, solemn and formal, and in front of the senior officials and squad members, he read out the commendation order, his voice carrying across the whole depot.

"Falcon Squad's mission this time was a desperate one. They broke through repeated ambushes, withstood the Freeze, completed the reconnaissance mission in full, and brought back enough winter supplies. Their service is outstanding!"

"The base now issues its highest commendation.

Every Falcon Squad member who took part receives fifty thousand contribution points!

Falcon Squad is awarded the First-Class Merit as a unit, and will permanently hold elite squad status and priority on resources!"

When the order finished, the whole room erupted!

Paisley's eyes flickered. She looked down at her personal contribution points panel, and the corner of her mouth lifted.

Fifty thousand contribution points hit her account, the numbers on the panel flying up.

Total contribution points, 261,390.

A fortune in contribution points, enough to trade for most of the base's rare resources.

The Falcon members' faces lit up with bright, real smiles, joy and excitement in their eyes.

More than fifty thousand points, the collective First-Class Merit and permanent elite squad status made everyone's blood run hot and filled them with pride.

Looking at the five full depots and the grain that would see the whole city through the Freeze, every base worker and every onlooker cheered out loud.
