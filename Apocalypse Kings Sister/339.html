Chapter 339

[What nerve! Saying that to his face — she must really be banking on young Blackwood backing her up.]

[Seriously, who IS she?]

Whispers kept rippling through the crowd, everyone certain Paisley was pushing her luck way too far.

Damon's face went livid, a vein throbbing at his temple, fury roiling in his chest.

Five thousand points was nothing to him financially. This was never about the number.

This was about face!

Here he was, second-in-command of the Quinn family, a man of real power, being squeezed for cash by some no-name upstart in front of everyone.

If he actually paid it, he'd become a laughingstock across all of Aetheris and the base both.

He glared at Paisley, ice in his eyes, the threat behind it unmistakable.

But Paisley's expression stayed open and unbothered — he was eating this loss today whether he liked it or not.

******

Elijah sat quietly beside her, his gaze soft, not a single word of caution, his presence alone a silent show of support.

Whatever Paisley wanted, five thousand points or ten times that, he'd see she got it.

His Pais really is a handful.

"Surely you've got at least that much sincerity in you, Mr. Quinn." "Or should I tell everyone what you actually came here planning to do today?"

After a few tense seconds, Damon realized there was no way around it. Refusing outright would only burn his bridge with Elijah for nothing.

He forced the rage down, knuckles white where his fingers clenched, his voice ice. "Fine."

"I'll pay."

He pulled up his terminal and, swallowing the humiliation, transferred five thousand points straight to Paisley's account.

[Notice: 5,000 contribution points received!]

The system's chime rang out, crisp and clear.

Damon held down the fury seething inside him, his smile not reaching his eyes.

"Satisfied now, miss?"

He wished, in that moment, that she'd just drop dead.

Paisley nodded, unbothered.

"Thank you for such a sincere apology, Mr. Quinn."

She said it plainly, without a hint of guilt, as if the money had simply been owed to her all along.

Damon said nothing more. He gave her one last look, dark and cold, full of buried resentment and calculation, then turned and strode back to his seat up front.

The moment he sat down, the air around him turned so heavy that none of the officials nearby dared come close.

I'm keeping a tally. This one's going on the list.

So what if Elijah's protecting her?

I have patience, and I have methods. There's plenty of time. One day I'll wash every bit of this humiliation away myself.

Not far away, Ashley clenched her fists under the table, eyes shining with admiration, whispering to herself.

"Paisley's so cool! That's exactly how you deal with bullies like him."

Orion let out a breath of his own, a proud smile tugging at his mouth. His little sister had never been the type to get pushed around.

Elijah turned his head slightly, gaze settling on the girl beside him, so composed through all of it. A faint, doting smile tugged at his mouth as he leaned in close to her ear, his voice low and warm.

"Feel better?"

Paisley's eyes curved into a smile, and she nodded, a small brightness flickering in her gaze.

Having someone in her corner — not a bad feeling at all.

Still, five thousand points was only the interest.

She'd settle the full account with Damon eventually, for every bit of the harassment and the provocation. One day he'd learn exactly why some flowers bloom so red.

The brief storm passed, and the auction everyone had been waiting for officially began.

The lights on stage snapped into focus, and the auctioneer, sharp and composed, raised a hand to present the first item.

A temperature-controlled glass case rose slowly, a brilliant red crystal core resting at its center, flame-like patterns swirling inside it, its energy drifting faintly through the protective shield.

"Our first item — a Level Six pure Fire Affinity crystal core!"

"You'll notice something different about this one — it's a mutant Fire Affinity core."

"If you're not confident you can absorb it, don't waste your points."

"Starting bid: one thousand points. Minimum increment: fifty points!"

The moment the core appeared, the dormant Fire Affinity inside Paisley stirred violently, her meridians burning, the energy surging on its own, as if it had found a perfect match it was desperate to merge with.
