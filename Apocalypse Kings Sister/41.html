Chapter 41

Orion's and Mason's machetes flashed again and again, chopping into zombie heads with quick, practiced strikes.

The ordinary zombies didn't stand a chance, and they dropped one after another.

That sent the second Level Two mutant into a rage. It hunched over, then shot forward in a burst of speed. It forgot all about Jasper and Rachel on the ground and charged straight at Paisley instead.

In one glance, it had picked her out. She looked like the weakest, least threatening one in the group, and it wanted to tear her apart.

"Watch out!" Orion yelled, his heart in his throat. Metal blades formed in his palm as he raised his hand, ready to send them flying in to protect her!

But they were all a beat too slow.

Paisley was already moving, her dagger gripped tight. She stepped lightly aside as the zombie charged, so fast she was just a blur.

Then, she snapped her wrist, and the blade swept up in one clean arc!

With a wet slice, her dagger cut straight through the mutant's neck with brutal force.

Its huge head flew right off its body and hit the ground, rolling a few times before it stopped. Black blood sprayed everywhere.

The headless body went stiff and twitched twice, then crashed to the ground. The snarling had stopped for good.

Before anyone could get over the shock, Paisley bent down in one smooth motion. She reached into the zombie's skull, pulled out a clear, glossy Level Two crystal core, and tucked it into her pocket.

She did it like killing a high-level zombie and digging out its core was just part of her daily routine.

The whole thing took barely two seconds. She struck, killed it, and took the core, all in one go.

Outside the west building, everything went dead silent. Even the wind seemed to stop, and it was so quiet they could've heard a pin drop.

Everyone froze in place, staring in total shock.

She didn't even have an ability, and she'd taken out a Level Two mutant like it was nothing. This was insane!

Still on the ground, Jasper had watched the whole thing, and now he couldn't move.

He stared at Paisley, who stood there perfectly calm, and his heart skipped a beat. His mind went blank.

That was a Level Two speed mutant!

Those things were fast, vicious, and tough. He'd just fought one with everything he had, burned through most of his ability and stamina, and almost died.

And this fragile-looking girl had killed it with one strike?

She'd done it with no ability and no help, using nothing but a little dagger and her own strength, in two seconds flat!

Since when was she some helpless little orphan who needed protecting?

She was scarier in a fight than almost anyone here!

The way he'd looked down on her and lusted after her seemed like a joke now.

Thank God he hadn't made a move on her yet. Otherwise, he'd be dead before he knew what hit him.

After seeing how fast and deadly she was, how could he even think about her that way now? He had zero interest in ending up like that Level Two zombie.

Still slumped on the ground, Rachel was eaten up by shock and jealousy. Her blood ran cold, and her whole body felt frozen stiff.

The tears on her face had dried, and under all the dirt, she was white as a ghost. The fear in her eyes had turned into pure venom.

She glared at Paisley's back. Paisley stood tall, tucking the core away like it was nothing, and Rachel's heart squeezed so tight it hurt. Bitterness and hate swept through her until she could hardly breathe.

Why her? Why does it always have to be her?

Paisley was nothing but a stand-in, a throwaway Rachel had walked all over for years. Rachel was the real Lennox daughter, the one everyone adored.

Paisley was supposed to kiss up to her.

But now, everything had been turned upside down!
