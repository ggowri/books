Chapter 245

Ashley's eyes sparkled as she ran over to touch the car, delighted.

"Paisley, your Affinity is unreal. No more walking everywhere! You're like a magic treasure chest!"

Elijah stepped forward, steady and composed, his eyes settling on Paisley with a trace of something he didn't quite let show.

"Orion and I will each drive. We'll split up, move faster."

He paused, then added, quieter.

"Pais, ride with me. There's something I want to ask you."

"Sure."

Paisley nodded, agreeing without a second thought.

Orion shot Elijah a look. He knew exactly what that old fox was up to.

Trying to make a move on my sister, right out in the open. We'll settle this.

He stepped in at once to block it.

"Paisley, ride with me. I've got something to ask you too."

Paisley, Kitty curled in her arms, frowned a little, caught in the middle.

"Orion, I already told Captain Blackwood I'd ride with him. How about tomorrow?"

Ashley darted over and latched onto Paisley's arm, clinging tight.

"I don't care, wherever Paisley sits, I'm sitting! I want to be with her every second!"

Orion finally gave in, grudgingly.

At least with Ashley there, someone can keep an eye on them.

With that settled, everyone climbed in without further delay.

Elijah and Orion took the driver's seats, Affinity sparking the engines to life. The low rumble cut through the forest quiet.

Windows rolled down, both cars turned together and sped out of the forest, heading for the armory.

Tires rolled over the broken dirt road, wind rushing past, the open road ahead empty and bleak.

Just past the tree line, a thin, battered figure stumbled out of nowhere and planted itself directly in their path.

Everyone's eyes locked on, and for a moment, they all froze.

Kelly Walker.

Since parting ways at the Rivergate base, she'd been traveling with Jasper Quinn, and had managed to get herself a seat on a military transport heading back to Aetheris.

But the plane had gone down mid-flight, and she'd barely survived the crash.

She was covered in scrapes and bruises, her clothes torn and caked in mud, hair matted to a pale, bloodied face. Every inch of her looked wrecked.

The crash had taken everything, her supplies, her food, her clothes. She had nothing left, stranded alone in this empty wasteland.

She'd wandered alone for hours, finding no one, cold and starving and in pain, right on the edge of giving up.

******

Then the sound of engines reached her, and hope flared up fast enough that she threw herself into the road without a second thought.

But the moment she saw who was in the car, humiliation, envy, and resentment hit her all at once.

Her eyes landed on Paisley, and it was like salt in the wound.

Paisley sat there in clean white, not a speck of dirt on her, composed as ever, Kitty curled in her lap, taking slow bites of an apple-sized strawberry.

Same apocalypse, same crash, and Paisley looked like she was on vacation.

And here she was, covered in wounds and mud, with nothing, barely more than dirt herself.

The contrast was so stark it cut through her pride like a blade. She wanted to crawl into the ground.

She'd never been this humiliated in her life, and never in front of Paisley, the one person she least wanted to lose to.

But under all that shame and envy was something more basic: the need to survive.

Pride wasn't worth anything if she was dead.

She swallowed every scrap of resentment and looked up at Elijah in the driver's seat, her voice cracked and pleading.
