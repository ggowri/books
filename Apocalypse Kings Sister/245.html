Chapter 245

Ashley's eyes sparkled as she ran over to the car and touched it, delighted.

"Paisley, I'm so jealous of your space power! Now we've got cars, so we don't have to walk everywhere. You're like a magic treasure chest!"

Elijah stepped forward, tall and steady, and took charge. His eyes landed on Paisley, though he kept his real motive to himself. "Orion and I will each drive one car and split everyone up so we can go faster."

He paused and asked softly, "Pais, ride with me. There's something I want to ask you."

"Sure." Paisley nodded lightly, agreeing like it was nothing.

Orion immediately glared at Elijah. He knew right away what that sly old fox was up to.

He's actually trying to make a move on my sister right in front of me? This isn't over.

He stepped in right away to stop it. "Paisley, ride with me. I've got something to ask you too."

Paisley held the little white cat curled up in her arms and frowned slightly, caught in the middle. "Orion, I already told Captain Blackwood I'd ride with him. How about I ride in your car tomorrow?"

Ashley hurried over and hooked her arm through Paisley's, clinging to her and whining. "I don't care, wherever you sit, Paisley, I'm sitting there too! I want to be with you every second!"

Only then did Orion grudgingly agree. With Ashley around, at least someone would keep an eye on them.

With that settled, everyone got into the cars without any more hesitation.

Elijah and Orion each took the driver's seat of an SUV and used their powers to start the engines. The engines rumbled to life, and the sound of the two cars broke the silence of the woods.

The windows slowly rolled down, and the two cars turned around together and sped out of the deep, wild woods toward the munitions depot.

The tires bumped over the rough dirt road, the wind whistled past, and the cars sped along. The road ahead was wide open and desolate.

As soon as they drove out of the narrow forest path, someone thin and battered suddenly stumbled out from the side of the road and planted herself right in front of the cars, blocking their way.

Everyone focused on her, and when they saw who it was, they were all stunned for a moment.

It was Kelly Walker.

After they'd parted ways at Rivergate Base, she'd been traveling with Jasper. This time, she'd managed to sneak onto the military plane heading home, desperate to get back to Aetheris with everyone.

But the plane had suddenly crashed halfway, losing control and falling from the sky. She'd used every ounce of strength she had and barely survived.

By now, she was covered in scrapes and bruises of all kinds, and her clothes were torn and caked with mud and dirt. Her messy hair stuck to her pale cheeks, and blood was still oozing from her forehead. She looked like a total wreck, weak and exhausted.

The plane had burned up in the crash, and all her supplies, food, and clothes were gone. She was left with nothing, stuck all alone in this empty wilderness.

After landing, she'd wandered around alone for a long time, but the whole place was dead silent, and she couldn't find a single soul. She was hurting, cold, and starving, and she was on the edge of giving up.

Just then, she heard a car horn in the distance, and suddenly there was hope. That was why she'd charged out to stop the car without thinking twice.

But when she saw who was in the car, a wave of mixed feelings hit her: humiliation, jealousy, and bitterness all rushing up at once.

Seeing Paisley stung her most of all.

Paisley wore a clean, simple white casual outfit without a speck of dirt on it, looking as cool and graceful as ever. She was holding the sweet little white cat in her arms and slowly nibbling on a bright red mutant strawberry the size of an apple.

They were in the same apocalypse and had survived the same plane crash, yet Paisley was calm and relaxed, like she was on vacation in the wild.

Meanwhile, Kelly was covered in wounds and mud and had nothing left. She'd hit rock bottom.

The huge contrast hit Kelly's pride hard, and she wished she could crawl into a hole and disappear.

Kelly had never been this humiliated in her life, and it had to happen in front of Paisley, the one person she least wanted to lose to.

But beneath all the humiliation and jealousy, her instinct to survive won out.

Pride and dignity were worth nothing when survival was on the line.

She forced down all her bitterness and awkwardness and looked up with tired, pleading eyes. She stared right at Elijah in the driver's seat and spoke in a hoarse, weak voice, almost begging.
