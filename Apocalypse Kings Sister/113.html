Chapter 113

Trovie sounded confused. [Master, what are you doing?]

Paisley smiled a little. [Trovie, how do you tame a tree that can think for itself?]

How was she supposed to win over a mutant willow this smart and this self-aware?

As Paisley got closer, the mutant willow sensed danger and attacked first.

Its branches whipped out like steel cables, carrying cold river mist and tiny droplets of water. They lashed at Paisley with brutal force and blinding speed.

Paisley was ready for it. With the little white cat in her arms, she slipped lightly to the side and dodged the storm of whipping branches. Cold light flashed from the Frostbloom Sword in her hand.

Freezing energy sliced through the air. Every swing cut through the most dangerous branches, but she held back on purpose so she wouldn't hurt the trunk or roots.

The little white cat sat comfortably in her arms, watching the fight with big, bright eyes. Every so often, it swatted out a blast of frost and froze a few branches flying at them.

Paisley was practically playing with it. In just a few rounds, she'd cut off every one of its thin, whipping branches.

The cut branches scattered across the riverbank and withered, and the churning mist cleared away.

The mutant willow had no fight left. Its trunk tilted a little, and its leaves went limp all at once.

The willow was smart and sneaky. It knew it couldn't win, so it just played possum.

The whole tree drooped and didn't move, pretending it was out for the count. It was hoping to trick Paisley into leaving it alone.

Paisley saw right through its little act and couldn't help laughing.

Smart mutant plants really were way more trouble than regular zombies. When they lost, they played dead, and they were full of tricks.

Good thing it's only Level Three right now.

If it reached Level Seven like it had in her past life, it would be a nightmare to deal with.

Looked like she'd need to get creative to win it over.

With a single thought, Paisley pulled out a whole pile of spicy jerky, chips, and top-quality treats she'd bought from the Trading Plaza. She laid them out in front of the willow's trunk to tempt it.

Spicy jerky, chips, and good food were all hard to come by in the apocalypse. Any normal mutant creature would've gone crazy fighting over them.

But the mutant willow didn't even twitch. Its leaves stayed droopy, and it kept playing dead.

Apparently, regular food wasn't good enough for this magic tree.

Trovie made a suggestion.

[Master, try spirit water. There isn't a mutant beast or mutant plant out there that doesn't love spirit water.]

Paisley's eyes lit up. She took out a bottle of rich, powerful spirit water she'd already filled and gently twisted off the cap.

A clean, sweet scent spread out, and soft wisps of pure water energy drifted through the air.

She let one drop fall onto the mutant willow.

That one drop of spirit water landed on the rough bark and soaked right in.

The willow, which had been perfectly still, suddenly started shaking like crazy!

The whole tree glowed with a rich, bright green light. Its branches stretched out and lifted, and the withered, droopy ones sprang back to life.

It couldn't keep up the act anymore.

It had never once felt that kind of deep, soothing nourishment since it had first taken root on this riverbank.

The spirit water was the best plant food imaginable. It flooded through every inch of its roots, branches, and leaves.

When Paisley saw that, she smiled knowingly.

It really did love spirit water.

Now, she knew exactly how to win it over.

Paisley tipped the bottle cap toward the little white cat and let it lap up some spirit water.

The little white cat squinted happily.

When the mutant willow saw that, it copied the cat. It carefully, gently nudged the back of Paisley's hand. All its hostility was gone, and it was nothing but sweet and eager to please.

It was like a puppy saying sorry.

It swayed its branches gently, humble and well-behaved, like it was saying, "Please, give me some spirit water too."

Trovie cheered.

[Master! It's giving in! It wants spirit water!]

Paisley spoke calmly, dangling the offer.

"You want some? Fine.

"Swear yourself to me and come live in my Storage Dimension. You can have all the spirit water you want.

"If you won't, I'll dig out your crystal core right now."

The mutant willow's branches shook hard, and its green glow flickered faster and faster as it nodded frantically.
