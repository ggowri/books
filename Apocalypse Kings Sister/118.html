Chapter 118

But ever since she'd been reborn, she'd lost the Storage Dimension pendant, her Plant Affinity, and every lucky break she was supposed to get. She'd lost everything.

And Paisley, the useless nobody who'd never even awakened an ability in her past life, could now take down a Level Four mutant Rat King with a wave of her hand.

Why? Why her?

Everyone's eyes were on Paisley now.

Even Rachel's own three brothers only had eyes for her.

And when Rachel was in danger, the only way she could survive was hiding behind Paisley.

This isn't how it's supposed to go. Paisley's supposed to be under my heel forever.

Everyone's praise for Paisley and the awe in their eyes felt like needles stabbing into Rachel's heart.

All the glory and attention that should've been hers belonged to Paisley now.

It wasn't supposed to be like this.

Her jealousy over the brutal gap between them grew out of control and ate away at whatever sanity she had left. Shame, resentment, and envy wrapped so tight around her chest that she could barely breathe.

She glared at Paisley, who stood there so calmly. Deep in her eyes was a dark, hidden hatred that no one else could see, and she dug her nails into her palms so hard that they bled, but she didn't even notice.

She wasn't going to let this go.

The fight was over!

The giant white cat slowly shrank back down into its tiny, adorable form.

It padded over to the Rat King's shredded body and neatly pulled out a glowing Level Four Psychic crystal core. Then, it trotted back with little steps, set the core at Paisley's feet, and rubbed against her leg, waiting to be praised.

Paisley bent down and picked up the core. She ran her fingers over its clear surface, smiled faintly, and closed her hand around it.

The ground was covered in countless low-level crystal cores. They were scattered across the scorched dirt in every size, glittering faintly. There was so much loot that it was almost blinding.

Once everyone caught their breath and saw all those crystal cores, their eyes lit up, and they started picking them up.

The fight against the swarm had paid off big. They'd gotten 1,600 Level One crystal cores in total.

By now, night had completely fallen, and pitch-black darkness covered the outskirts of the water city.

After everyone packed up, they found a nicely furnished villa deeper in the district.

The yard was neat, the walls were solid, and the doors and windows were all intact. It was clean and safe, the perfect place to stay for the night.

On top of that, the house was fully furnished, with working water and power and plenty of rooms.

They quickly cleaned the dust out of the yard and the house, locked up the doors and windows, and set up a lookout. Finally, they could settle in, and the tension from the long, brutal day started to fade.

Once they'd rested up a bit, it was time to split up the loot.

Everyone just assumed Elijah, their captain, would handle dividing up the crystal cores.

Elijah sorted all the cores and figured out how much each person had fought, how much energy they'd used, and how much they'd helped. He quickly came up with a plan and started handing them out.

First, he put 600 Level One crystal cores in front of Paisley. "This is your share."

That was way more than a fifth of the whole haul, more than anyone else got by far.

Paisley calmly took the cores without a second thought. She'd earned them.

Rachel had been watching the whole thing closely, and she turned green with envy as she glared at the cores in Paisley's hands. As far as she was concerned, it was totally unfair.

She couldn't hold it in anymore. She stepped forward and called it out in front of everyone, her voice dripping with resentment.

"Why does she get so many crystal cores? We all fought together, so why does she get way more than everyone else?

"And why isn't anybody splitting up that Level Four Psychic crystal core? Nobody's going to say anything about that?"

Her chest heaved, and she got more worked up with every word.

"We all fought those low-level mutant rats together with everything we had. It wasn't just her!

"Paisley showed up last, and she did the least work in the least time. So why does she get way more than the rest of us? This isn't fair!

"Not unless she hands over that Level Four Psychic crystal core."

The second she finished, a sharp, cool voice cut in.

Ashley stepped forward with a cold expression and stared right at Rachel, who was all worked up.

"Unfair? Rachel, go ahead and tell me. What exactly is unfair about it?"
