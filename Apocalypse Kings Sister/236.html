Chapter 236

All those years they'd indulged and favored Rachel, had treated Paisley, the one who'd actually meant them well, with cruelty instead, and in the end, when it came down to life and death, this was how the sister they'd protected treated them.

It was almost too ironic to bear.

Ending up like this now, it was exactly what their blindness had earned them.

Regret surged up again, crashing over them as they watched Paisley patiently calming a terrified Ashley.

They really had been wrong, and now it was too late for any of it.

Some damage, once done, couldn't be undone with a few words of apology.

Ending up abandoned by everyone, friend and family alike, was entirely their own doing.

The three of them instinctively looked over toward the other side of the cabin.

Paisley stood there, head bent, gently soothing a frightened Ashley, infinitely patient.

That was exactly how Paisley used to treat them too.

The contrast hit like a tidal wave of regret, crashing through them without mercy.

They really had been wrong. Wrong in every way.

But now, it was too late for any of it.

All that coldness, all that prejudice, all that harm done over the years had already left its mark. It couldn't be erased by a careless "I was wrong," couldn't be forgiven that easily.

Owen's eyes stung, rimmed red as he quietly made himself a promise. If he survived today, he'd do everything in his power to make things right with Paisley, to earn her back somehow.

Just then, the plane pitched into a violent dive, thick smoke flooding the cabin as it went completely dead in the air.

Elijah's voice boomed through the chaos.

"We've lost all control of the aircraft! Everyone jump now! Go!"

******

Wind tore through the cabin, weightlessness hitting them all at once. No one hesitated any longer, throwing themselves out of the plane one after another, diving down toward the sea of clouds far below.

The dense swarm of mutant crows followed close behind, eyes burning red with bloodlust, diving and pecking at the falling bodies without mercy, their sharp beaks tearing at parachute fabric. A few of them even spat small bursts of black flame, scorching the canopies mid-fall.

The high-altitude winds were chaotic, fire and bird strikes tearing through the group. Several parachutes got pecked through or burned, sending their owners into sudden, uncontrolled freefall.

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Elijah, drawing on their considerable strength, managed to fend off the attacking crows while steadying their own descents, barely holding their balance.

But Ashley, still young, with the combined force of weightlessness and the crows' assault, lost control fast, her parachute twisting and tearing in the turbulent air, sending her plummeting out of control.

"Paisley! Help me..."

Ashley's scream cut through the chaos, panic flooding her voice.

In that split second, Paisley's gaze sharpened, power surging from her fingertips.

From the ground far below, countless green shoots burst upward, growing and thickening and stretching at impossible speed, shooting up into towering trees within seconds, vines spreading and weaving overhead into a massive, resilient green net.

SHWA—!

Layer after layer of vine wrapped precisely around the falling Ashley, the Thompson brothers, and Elijah's group, cushioning their fall gently, absorbing every ounce of impact, catching everyone safely in place.

They settled into the branches, breath still ragged from the shock of survival, every face stunned, disbelief rolling through them in waves.

A crisis like this, chaotic winds, flames, a swarm of attacking crows, should have killed nearly everyone. And Paisley had single-handedly grown a forest out of thin air to save them all.

That kind of effortless, absolute control over plant life was beyond anything any of them had ever seen or even heard of.

Ashley pushed herself upright against the branches, watching the trees slowly shrink back down toward the earth, awe and worship shining in her eyes as she cried out, voice soft and amazed.

"I'm not dead! Paisley is unbelievable!"

Thomas let out a long breath, relief and lingering fear thick in his voice.

"We're all okay. We all made it.

Paisley, thank you. You saved every one of us."

Spencer's eyes shone, admiration and shock plain on his face.

"Paisley, your Affinity is on a whole different level. Pulling that off at the last second, saving all of us, that was incredible."

Madison, Mason, and Marcus Whitfield climbed up through the branches as well, looking at Paisley with nothing but reverence and gratitude, voicing their thanks one after another.

The pilot, who'd barely survived the fall, bowed his head toward her, voice heavy with sincerity.

"Thank you, Ms. Lennox. If not for you today, I would be dead."
