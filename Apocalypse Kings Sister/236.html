Chapter 236

The three Lennox brothers had always taken Rachel's side and spoiled her, even treating Paisley cruelly when she was the one who truly cared about them. And in the end, when it came down to life or death, this was how the sister they'd fought so hard to protect treated them?

Talk about irony.

They'd ended up like this because they'd been blind.

Endless regret welled up inside them again.

They really had been wrong, but now it was too late.

Some damage had already been done, and saying sorry a few times wasn't going to fix it.

Their family and friends had all turned their backs on them, and they'd brought it all on themselves.

The three of them looked up without thinking and glanced over.

Paisley was looking down, gently and patiently comforting a frightened Ashley.

That was exactly how Paisley used to treat them.

When they compared it to how they'd treated her, regret swallowed them like a tide.

They'd been so incredibly wrong.

And now, there was no going back.

All the coldness, bias, and hurt they'd caused had already happened, and nothing could undo it.

Owen's eyes stung and went red, and he silently made a wish. If he was lucky enough to survive today, he'd do everything he could to make up for his mistakes and win Paisley back.

Just then, the plane suddenly dropped hard, thick smoke poured into the cabin, and the controls died.

Elijah roared, "We've lost all control of the plane! Everyone, jump now! Hurry!"

Fierce winds swept through, and a wave of weightlessness hit them. No one dared to waste any time, and one after another, they jumped out of the cabin and dove toward the sea of clouds below.

The dense swarm of mutant crows followed right behind them, their blood-red eyes blazing. They dove and pecked like crazy at the falling people, their sharp beaks tearing at the parachute fabric, and a few of the crows even spat little bursts of black fire that scorched the parachutes in midair.

The high-altitude winds were chaotic, and between the flames and the attacking birds, plenty of team members had their parachutes pecked through or burned. They lost control and plummeted fast.

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Elijah used their strong powers to fend off the attacking crows while steadying themselves, barely keeping their balance.

But Ashley was still young. Between the weightlessness and the attacking birds, she couldn't stay steady. The wind twisted her parachute out of shape, and she plunged downward, out of control.

"Paisley! Help me..." Ashley cried out, panicking even more.

At the last possible moment, Paisley's eyes turned sharp, and power burst from her fingertips.

Countless tender green shoots burst out of the ground below and grew wildly, sprouting branches, shooting up, and spreading out fast enough to see. In seconds, they turned into towering trees, and countless thick, flexible vines grew up into the sky, crisscrossing into a huge, tough green net.

With a sudden whoosh, layer after layer of vines wrapped precisely around Ashley, the Thompson brothers, Elijah, and the others as they fell. The flexible branches and leaves cushioned them layer by layer, gently absorbing the force of their fall and catching everyone safely.

Everyone relaxed and lay still among the branches and leaves, still dazed from surviving. Every one of them was stunned, and they could hardly believe it.

Trapped high in the sky with chaotic winds, flames, and a swarm of crows trying to kill them, they should've had almost no chance of surviving. But Paisley had made towering trees grow out of thin air all on her own and saved everyone.

Plant control this effortless and precise was way too insane. No one had ever seen or even heard of anything like it.

Ashley sat up on the branches and watched the trees slowly shrink closer to the ground, and once the shock wore off, her eyes sparkled with adoration. "I didn't die! Paisley, you're unbelievable!"

Thomas let out a long breath, shaken and moved. "We're all okay. Every one of us made it.

"Paisley, thank you. You saved all of us."

Spencer's eyes lit up, and he couldn't hide how amazed he was. "Paisley, your power is insane! You pulled that off right when it counted, and it was so cool!"

Madison, Mason, and Marcus got up from among the branches too. They looked at Paisley with awe and gratitude, and one by one, they thanked her.

The pilot, who'd barely survived, looked stunned and shook her hand gratefully. "Thank you for saving me, Ms. Lennox. If it weren't for you, I'd be dead for sure."
