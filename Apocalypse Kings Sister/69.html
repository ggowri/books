Chapter 69

[Master, it's Orion. He's looking for you.]

With a single thought, Paisley left her Storage Dimension, returned to her room, and opened the door.

"Hey, Orion. What's up?"

Orion handed her a bowl of fresh fruit.

"Paisley, you're not upset, are you? Don't let what Kelly said today get to you.

"She wasn't always like this. I don't know what's gotten into her.

"But as long as I'm around, you don't have to worry about a thing, and you don't have to care what anyone says.

"I grabbed this fruit for you this morning. Try some. Is it sweet?"

Paisley took the fruit, a little stunned.

Paisley couldn't even remember the last time anyone had looked out for her like this. It hadn't happened once since Rachel came back to the Lennoxes.

Orion really seemed to care about her.

From the moment they'd first met, he'd always had her back, no questions asked.

It felt pretty nice.

She popped a grape into her mouth and smiled.

"It's really sweet, Orion. It's delicious! Thank you!

"And I'm not upset, because I honestly don't care what she thinks.

"Don't worry about me, okay? You should focus on leveling up."

Orion could tell she really wasn't upset, and he finally relaxed.

"Glad you like it. I'll find you some more when I get the chance.

"Get some rest, then. I'm going to my room to level up.

"If you need anything, go to Thomas. He's right next door."

Paisley nodded. "Got it. Go level up, and don't worry about me."

Back in her room, Paisley pulled four cups of spirit water out of her Storage Dimension.

She knocked on Thomas's door first.

The second Thomas saw it was Paisley, his face lit up with a big smile. "Hey, Pais, what's up? Is something wrong?"

Paisley handed him a cup. "I brought this for you. Drink up."

Thomas didn't ask a single question. He just took the cup and drank it down.

When he finished, he grinned at her. "Water always tastes sweeter when you bring it."

His whole body felt way more relaxed, and a fresh surge of strength was flowing through him.

"Pais, what was that? I feel like I could run a marathon."

Paisley didn't answer. She just told him to go get some rest.

Then, she went to knock on Spencer's door.

Spencer had already been listening hard for any sound in the hallway.

He'd decided that if Pais didn't bring him any water, he'd throw open his door and start bawling right in front of her.

Luckily, Pais hadn't forgotten him.

If Thomas got one, he'd better get one too.

He took the cup from her happily and gulped it down in one go.

"It really is sweet! Thanks, Paisley. Thomas was right, I feel totally pumped now too.

"What is this stuff, some kind of magic potion? Thanks, Pais!"

Paisley cleared her throat and went to knock on Ashley's door next.

Ashley shared a room with Madison.

Paisley handed each of them a cup of spirit water and said only two words. "Stay hydrated."

She didn't explain anything else, and she didn't bother saying where the spirit water came from or what it did.

If they trusted her, they'd drink it. If they didn't, that was their loss.

She'd always done things her own way.

But Ashley and Madison trusted Paisley completely. Without a second's hesitation, they lifted their cups and drank slowly.

The clear, sweet spring water slid down their throats, and a gentle warmth spread through their whole bodies.

The second they tasted it, days of built-up exhaustion melted away, and the dry ache in their throats disappeared completely.

Right after that, a soft, pure energy quietly soaked into every inch of them.

Their heavy, worn-out bodies suddenly felt light and refreshed, like a huge weight had been lifted off their shoulders.

"That feels so good..." Ashley murmured, her whole face relaxing.

"I feel all warm, and I'm not tired at all anymore! My head feels so much clearer too!"

The second she finished speaking, warmth flared deep in her belly. A heavy, steady force surged up and rushed through her whole body!

The ground trembled slightly, and a few small pebbles by her feet floated up into the air.

Ashley froze. Then, her face exploded with wild, disbelieving joy.

"I... I just awakened an Earth Affinity!"

Thick, steady Earth Affinity energy swirled around her, wrapping her whole body in its power.
