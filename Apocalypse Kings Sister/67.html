Chapter 67

Paisley still didn't say a word. She went straight back to her room.

Ashley could tell Kelly was about to start something again, so she quickly cut her off, not bothering to be polite.

"Ms. Walker, just leave the dishes. I'll clean up.

"And please stop picking on my sister.

"Paisley's too nice to hold it against you, but the rest of us aren't.

"We finally got her back after all this time. Why should she have to put up with your crap?

"If you don't want to cook anymore, then don't. I'll cook for Paisley myself."

Kelly's face got even darker.

Ashley used to call her Kelly all the time, sweet as could be.

Now, all of a sudden, it was "Ms. Walker." On top of that, Ashley was saying all this nasty stuff to her face.

It made Kelly furious.

But she couldn't exactly blow up at a kid.

Madison sighed and stepped in right on cue.

"Kelly, why don't you go get some rest? Ashley and I can handle the cleanup.

"We're all on the same team, so just let it go. We've still got a long way to go, and it's not worth getting worked up over something this small."

Why is Madison saying this too? Am I the only one who cares?

All the hurt and jealousy came flooding back.

Kelly stared down at the table, hiding the venom and hostility churning inside her.

Was this really such a small thing?

Why did everyone just assume Paisley deserved special treatment? Why did she get to sit back and reap the rewards while everyone defended her no matter what?

A thousand protests were stuck in her throat. But everyone had rallied around Paisley, so she swallowed every last one of them.

Fine. I'll deal with her some other time.

Kelly got up and walked out of the dining room without a word, heading straight to her room.

The second she shut the door, her whole face twisted with rage.

She'd already awakened her ability.

In this dog-eat-dog apocalypse, getting stronger was the only thing that mattered right now.

Once I'm strong enough, how hard could it be to take down a useless nobody like Paisley?

Plus, it looked like the Lennox siblings didn't get along with Paisley either.

Maybe she could get them to do her dirty work for her.

Outside, the wind kept howling, and the rain was coming down harder and harder.

Back in her room, Paisley found the little white ice cat curled up on her pillow, its eyes closed as it dozed lazily, sweet and calm.

She walked over and sat on the edge of the bed. Then, she opened her hand to reveal a clear Level Four Plant Affinity core, swirling with pure, powerful energy.

Her Plant Affinity was at mid Level Four now.

With this core, she should be able to push all the way to peak Level Four.

Once the storm passed, there was no telling what kind of danger was waiting out there. She needed to get stronger, fast.

She went straight into her Storage Dimension.

Paisley sat down on the couch and closed her eyes, clearing her mind. Plant energy flowed slowly through her body and wrapped gently around the core in her palm.

The pure, powerful energy flowed through her smoothly, soaking into every inch of her. Since it was the same kind of energy as hers, it blended right in, strengthening her body and building up her power.

Rich plant energy laced with poison swirled slowly around her, and the room filled with a warm, endless flow of power.

Time ticked by as she kept absorbing and merging the energy from the core.

Half an hour later, she absorbed the very last bit of energy. The Level Four Plant Affinity core in her palm broke apart into tiny specks of light and vanished.

With a deep boom, Paisley's power surged all at once. It shot up fast and broke through to peak Level Four!

She could feel it. Her Plant Affinity was denser and more refined than ever.

She slowly opened her eyes, and they flashed bright green before fading back to normal.

She was so close to Level Five now.

The kitten opened its eyes, glanced at her once, then went right back to lying still like a good little cat.

This girl is not someone to mess with. Better keep my head down.

Besides, it was way more comfortable inside the dimension, where the energy was so rich.

Trovie's sweet, excited voice rang out with a cheerful chime.

[Congratulations, Master! You broke through to peak Level Four! You're amazing!]

Its round little body spun around and around inside the dimension.

[Master, the Ancient Realm trader keeps trying to reach you. Want me to connect?]

Paisley thought of the gorgeous Ancient Realm princess. That girl was a walking cash cow.

It's only been half a day, and she's already made ice?
