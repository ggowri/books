Chapter 221

Night settled in, a cool breeze clearing away the last of the blood-tinged air.

Paisley sat sprawled in the back seat of the car, a lollipop between her teeth, absently petting the little white cat curled in her lap, eyes fixed on the dark, winding road outside.

The 2S hidden quest kept replaying in her mind, her expression growing heavier by the mile.

Human experimentation, secret bases scattered across the country, someone pulling the strings from above. This apocalypse might not have been a natural disaster at all. It might have been a carefully staged grab for power.

The scale of it, the web of interests tangled through it, was far more complicated and far more dangerous than she'd first imagined.

Meanwhile, back at the safety of the main base, everything looked different, comfortable and indulgent, a world away from the bloodshed underground.

Rachel Lennox and Chase Sullivan, who'd fled the squad early and made it back by sheer luck, had already put the danger behind them and were curled up together, wrapped in each other.

The two of them were tucked away in Chase's private quarters, a clean, plush couch beneath them, warm light filling the room, supplies in abundance, everything comfortable, proof enough of the kind of treatment the base gave him.

Rachel leaned into Chase, all sweetness and softness, pinching a plump grape between her fingers and feeding it to him, her whole body language intimate and close.

"Ten more minutes. If they're not back by then, they're definitely dead."

She looked up at him, something calculating hidden in her eyes.

"When they ask, make sure you look wrecked. Tell them you fought your way out with your mental Affinity and my precognition, the two of us barely scraping back here alive together."

Chase took the grape between his lips lazily, tongue flicking against her fingers, leaning in now and then to press a lazy, presumptuous kiss to her cheek, his voice smug.

"Relax, sweetheart. I've got this handled."

Something dark and pleased flickered across his face.

"They're dead today. No question about it.

Whether they found the classified files or not, whether they finished the mission or not, none of them are walking back into this base."

Rachel's eyes lit up instantly. She sat up straighter, practically buzzing.

"You're that sure?"

She wanted Paisley dead more than anything.

Nothing had gone right since Paisley came back into her life.

It felt like Paisley had become a completely different person this time around.

So much of what should have gone her way kept slipping out of her grasp instead.

She'd lost too much ground to Paisley already.

All she wanted now was for Paisley to die, the sooner the better.

Chase ran his fingers through her hair, lips curling into a smug, mysterious smile.

"Want to guess how someone with my level of power ends up with treatment this good here, with everything going my way the whole time?"

Before Rachel could ask, he lowered his voice, a note of pride slipping in.

"Truth is, I've got someone backing me. Someone at the very top, someone who actually runs things here."

Rachel's eyes went wide with delight, her heart racing.

She'd finally landed herself a real patron.

She leaned in immediately, playful and coy, pressing a kiss to his cheek, voice sweet and wheedling.

"That's amazing! Chase, come on, tell me who's backing you?"

Chase pulled her in close, something sly and shadowed flickering behind his eyes, and gave a faint smile.

"You don't need to ask. Just stick with me and you'll have everything you want."

His tone shifted, going cold.

"But I'll tell you this much. Every single one of them dies today. Once they're wiped out, our story about fighting our way back becomes the official version. No question asked."

"No one will ever know we bailed early."

The malice in Rachel's eyes was nearly impossible to hide now, even more delighted than Chase at the thought.

******

Just picturing Paisley, the one person who always managed to outshine her, dying today and never coming back, sent a rush of satisfaction through her.

She wrapped her arms around Chase's neck, voice turning sweet and demanding.

"Don't forget what you promised me. I want that pendant off Paisley's neck."

Chase squeezed her waist, something smug and shameless in his gaze, and agreed without hesitation.

"That's barely even a favor."

He leaned in close, voice dropping, thick with suggestion.

"So you'd better treat me right. Make me happy, and whatever you want, it's yours."
