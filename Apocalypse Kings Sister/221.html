Chapter 221

Night had fallen, and the cool evening breeze blew away the stench of blood.

Paisley lounged in the back seat with a sweet lollipop in her mouth, absently petting the little white cat curled up in her arms as she gazed out at the dark, winding road.

The 2S hidden main quest she'd just triggered kept replaying in her mind, and her expression grew more and more serious.

Human experiments, secret bases all over the country, people in power pulling the strings from behind the scenes... This apocalypse might not be a natural disaster at all. It might be a carefully planned power grab.

It involved so much, and the forces behind it were so tangled together. It was way more complicated and dangerous than she'd imagined.

******

Meanwhile, back inside Rivergate Base, everything was cozy and indulgent, a world away from the bloody danger underground.

Rachel and Chase, who'd ditched the team early and gotten lucky enough to escape, were long out of danger and cuddled up together.

They were hiding out in Chase's private quarters. The soft couch was spotless, the room was bright and warm, and there were plenty of supplies, all proof of the special treatment the base gave him.

Rachel snuggled seductively into Chase's arms, holding a plump grape between her fingers and sweetly feeding it to him. "Ten more minutes. If they're not back by then, they're definitely all dead down there."

She looked up at Chase, hiding a secret scheme in her eyes. "When that happens, you have to act like you went through hell. Tell everyone that your strong psychic power and my foresight got us out, that we fought for our lives and barely made it back to base alive."

Chase lazily took the grape she offered into his mouth and flicked his tongue against her fingers. Every now and then, he leaned in and gave her cheek a sleazy kiss, looking smug and full of himself.

"Relax, you little minx. I've got this handled."

A cold, vicious smirk crossed his face, and he spoke with total certainty. "They're dead today, no question. Truth is, whether or not they found the classified files or finished the mission, there's no way they're making it back to base alive."

Rachel's eyes lit up, and she sat up a little straighter, looking at him excitedly. "You're that sure?"

She wanted Paisley dead so badly. As long as Paisley was alive, Rachel would never have any peace.

For some reason, it felt like Paisley had become a whole different person in this second life. So many things weren't going the way Rachel wanted at all.

She'd been burned by Paisley way too many times, and now all she wanted was for Paisley to die, the sooner the better.

Chase toyed with her hair, a smug, mysterious smile on his face. "Guess why someone like me, who isn't even top-tier, gets such great treatment at the base and has everything go my way?"

Before Rachel could ask, he lowered his voice and bragged a little. "To tell you the truth, I've got someone backing me. He's a big shot at the top of the base, someone with real power."

When Rachel heard that, she was over the moon, her heart pounding with excitement.

I've finally got a real big shot in my corner!

She immediately leaned in coyly and kissed his cheek, her voice soft and sweet. "That's amazing! Chase, can you tell me who it is? Just between us?"

Chase pulled her tight into his arms, a sleazy, shady look in his eyes, and smiled faintly. "You don't need to know that. Just be a good girl and stick with me, and I promise you'll live like a queen."

Then, his voice turned cold. "But I'll tell you this much: every one of them is dying this time. Once they're wiped out, we're officially the heroes who fought our way out.

"No one will ever know we ran."

Rachel could barely hide the malice in her eyes, and she was even more excited than Chase. Just thinking about Paisley, who'd always outshone her, dying today and never coming back filled her with a wild, vicious thrill.

She wrapped her arms around Chase's neck, her voice sugary and whiny. "Don't forget what you promised me. I have to get that pendant on Paisley's chest."

Chase squeezed her waist with a flirty, cocky look and agreed. "Come on, that just takes one word from me."

He leaned in close, his voice dripping with suggestion. "Then you'd better take real good care of me in bed. Make me feel good, and I'll give you anything you want..."
