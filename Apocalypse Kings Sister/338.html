Chapter 338

Damon Quinn — the Quinn family's second-in-command, ruthless and powerful enough that even the base's top leadership gave him deference. No one crossed him lightly.

And yet here he was, extending an olive branch only to be coldly brushed off by some junior, his face lost in front of the entire hall.

He cut a glance at Paisley, calm and unbothered beside Elijah, something darker and more calculating flickering behind his eyes.

Shock, disbelief, and speculation swept through the hall all at once.

The tension stretched unbearably, awkward and taut, the air thick enough that no one dared make a sound.

Guests bent their heads together, whispering frantically, astonishment plain on every face.

[Oh my god, Mr. Quinn reached out and got iced out in front of everyone? That's never happened at this base before!]

[Who even is this guy? I've never seen him around here — that presence is insane.]

[Looks like they already know each other. Is he some other top-tier figure from Aetheris? Maybe even outranks Mr. Quinn?]

[Maybe he's some crown prince type, slumming it incognito. How else do you explain him being here?]

...

Fragments of speculation kept bubbling up, every eye fixed on Elijah, equal parts curiosity and awe.

Every trace of warmth drained from Damon's face, something dark and venomous churning in his eyes, fury burning in his chest.

When had a man with his power ever been humiliated like this in public?

But he knew exactly what he was dealing with — the Blackwood heir, with resources far beyond his own family's. Breaking things off here would gain him nothing.

So he forced the anger down, clinging to what dignity he could, though his voice had already hardened with open displeasure.

"What, young Blackwood? Not willing to spare me even a little face?"

Elijah's gaze dropped slowly, his dark eyes cold as ice, his voice low and cutting, every word final.

"Is your face the kind you use to bully people younger than you and throw your weight around?"

He looked up, radiating raw possessiveness.

"She's under my protection. I'd suggest you don't go near her again."

That single sentence, so blatantly protective, sent a visible jolt through the whole hall.

Damon's expression darkened completely, fury roiling beneath the surface, a vein standing out at his temple, but he had no choice but to swallow it.

A flash of mockery and sudden understanding crossed his eyes as the pieces clicked into place.

So that's it.

Everyone said even heroes stumbled over a pretty face. So the famously cold, untouchable Blackwood heir hadn't escaped that trap after all.

He'd always been wary of Elijah precisely because the man had no soft spot, no crack in his armor, no weakness at all.

Apparently that wasn't quite true anymore.

The mysterious, formidable Blackwood heir — and this striking girl was his one weak point.

All that caution about Elijah before now seemed overblown.

Damon swallowed every trace of contempt and forced another gracious smile, his posture softening deliberately.

"You're joking, young Blackwood. There's no bad blood here — my men just overstepped. That's on me."

He turned to Paisley, his tone polished but hollow.

"I didn't keep my people in line. I apologize on their behalf, miss, and I'll see they're properly punished."

A single flimsy apology, meant to wave away soldiers showing up with guns, trying to drag her off by force.

Paisley's mouth curved, cool and faintly teasing, as she looked straight at him, neither deferential nor hostile.

"An apology's fine. Where's the gesture to back it up? Surely you're not planning to get away with just words, Mr. Quinn?"

"That seems a little lacking in sincerity."

That set the whole room buzzing again, everyone holding their breath.

Who in their right mind would make the Quinn family's second-in-command pay up? This girl had nerve.

The air around Damon dropped a few more degrees, storm clouds gathering in his eyes. This girl really knew how to push her luck.

He practically ground the words out between his teeth.

"And what exactly would count as a sincere apology, in your eyes?"

Paisley answered easily, perfectly unbothered.

"Emotional damages. Five thousand contribution points."

"Anything less wouldn't really match an apology from someone of your standing. Otherwise people might start thinking you're cheap — how would you ever find a wife at that rate?"

The hall erupted. Shock rippled through the crowd at the sheer audacity of the number.

[Five thousand points?! That's insane! Most people would never save that much in their life!]

[That's a king's ransom! This isn't about sincerity, she's just fleecing him!]
