Chapter 426

"Everyone's been issued standard ammo, and we only have 120 rounds each.

Besides unknown enemies and ambushes, we'll clear mutant zombie packs along the way. That ammo is tight. If we get stuck in a long fight, we'll run out of bullets and be cornered."

The mood in the vehicle sank at once. A heavy worry settled in everyone's chest.

Just as the group sat there grim and worried, Paisley, who had been resting on Ashley's shoulder, suddenly opened her eyes.

Her gaze was calm and cool. She sat up slowly, looked up, and focused her mind.

A second later, the empty space in the vehicle flickered.

A pile of gleaming, brand-new weapons appeared out of nowhere and crashed onto the floor, covering the whole open space.

Matte black, lightweight armor-piercing pistols and special high-frequency close-combat guns were lined up neatly. The bodies were finely marked and inset with Affinity amplification chips. They were top-tier weapons, rarely issued, and far better than the squad's standard gear.

Stacks of alloy ammunition sat in neat piles, the bullet tips gleaming cold, so plentiful the pile seemed endless.

The sudden sight made every pupil shrink. Everyone froze.

Paisley said calmly, "I stocked this up when I cleaned out the munitions depot last time. I never touched it."

"There's plenty of weapons and ammo. Take what you want. If it runs out, come to me for more."

She said it so casually, but it landed like thunder in the vehicle.

After a moment of dead silence, everyone burst into excitement. The worry vanished, replaced by shock and delight.

Elijah recovered first. Always calm and unreadable, even he showed a flash of surprise and lit-up eyes.

He leaned over and picked up a new rifle, ran his fingers over its precise, smooth body, and laughed softly, impressed and relieved.

"Pais, this is a lifesaver. With this batch of top-tier weapons backing us up, we could take one ambush, or even two rounds of encirclement, and still hold.

Thank goodness you're here. This mission just got a lot easier."

Orion was always cool and rational, but even he couldn't hide his shock. He picked up a heavy magazine and tested it, awed.

"Wow, these are the base's newest Affinity-linked rifles! The military only issues them in tiny batches. We never see them on field missions!

And five hundred rounds each, that's over four times what we had!

Now we don't have to hold back. If we get ambushed, we can fight head-on. We don't have to worry about running out."

Thomas couldn't wait. He grabbed a new gun, grinning, his voice light.

"We were worried about ammo in a long fight, and now we're fully loaded! Pais coming through is like water in a desert. This is so freaking awesome!"

Spencer, outgoing as ever, let out an excited whisper, his eyes shining.

"This... this is insane!

Every other squad has to count their bullets on missions!

We've got unlimited ammo, and it's top-tier! Pais is our lucky star, Falcon Squad's best trump card, our walking arsenal! With you here, we've got nothing to worry about!"

Ashley sat straight up, looked at the mountain of weapons with stars in her eyes, and her soft voice rang with joy and pride.

"My sister is amazing!

She can pull this much top-tier gear out anytime!

Now we never have to worry about ambushes. We can hit back at anyone!

So cool!"

Mason couldn't stop glancing back as he drove steadily, shocked.

"Following Paisley is how you eat well! I've never seen anyone carry a whole armory around!

Her Storage Dimension is the best in the apocalypse!

With this hardware behind us, this mission is as good as done!"

Madison's eyes were bright, full of real surprise and gratitude. She said softly, "I was worried about the road ahead and whether we'd have enough bullets. Now we're set. Paisley always gives us hope at the right moment. Thank goodness she's here."
