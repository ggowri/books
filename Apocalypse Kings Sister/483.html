Chapter 483

Spencer, Mason, and Madison split roles and worked together, clearing the zombies swarming around them.

Everyone moved clean and crisp, at full strength.

Elijah's eyes never left the mysterious masked man on the roof.

He could feel it clearly. That man was dangerous. He could control all the zombies in the area, and his methods were strange and unpredictable. He was the one who had brought the zombies out.

An enemy, not a friend.

"Hold steady and stay safe," Elijah said, low. His dark eyes sharpened on the figure on the roof.

"I'm going to have a word with him."

He leapt, used the broken walls and stairs as a springboard, and raced up toward the roof of the gas station.

Beside the SUV, Paisley's brow creased. A strong, unexplained unease rose in her.

She scanned the whole field fast and swept her gaze over every zombie rushing in.

They were all common low-level zombies, weak and clumsy. With Falcon's strength, they could clear them without effort. They weren't a threat.

But the more she thought so, the louder the alarm in her mind rang.

This ambush is too deliberate. A whistle that calls zombies, layers of siege. This is no simple zombie attack.

Especially that masked man. I bet he's here for Elijah.

This is wrong. Very wrong.

She didn't have time to think. Elijah was strong, but he could easily walk into a trap built specifically for him.

She flashed away from the low-level zombies around her and raced after Elijah toward the roof.

When she reached the rooftop, the air around it suddenly dropped.

Boom!

Several fierce, violent presences erupted from the ruins around the building!

High-tier zombies hidden deep in the horde burst into view!

Two power-type zombies stood over ten feet tall, muscles bulging, skin tough, fists loaded with enormous force, blocking the way up the roof.

Two speed-type mutant zombies blurred into afterimages and scrambled up the walls on all fours, claws like knives, going for Elijah's back.

One defense-type zombie, huge and solid as a mountain, plugged the roof's only exit.

And one poison-type zombie hung to one side, its body wreathed in dark green poison mist that ate at the snow and the concrete, closing off every dodge.

Six Level Seven zombies in all, built to cut off every way out for Elijah!

Every one of them was aimed at him alone, forming a kill box.

They were focused and moved together, under the whistle's control. They sealed the front and cut off the rear.

It was clear. This ambush had always been a deathtrap for Elijah.

The low-level horde was just a decoy. The real blow was this squad of high-tier zombies made to kill him.

Level Six to Level Seven isn't a step. It's a cliff.

Peak Level Six was already the best any human Affinity user had reached, but Level Seven was a different world. Body, speed, recovery, and resistance to Affinities all crushed anything below it. Fighting one was like an ant trying to shake a tree.

The six Level Seven zombies each did their jobs perfectly. They were clearly controlled, and clearly aimed.

The two power-type zombies had bodies hard as iron. Every punch carried enough force to break stone and bend steel, and they blocked every dodge from the front.

The two speed-type zombies circled in afterimages, black claws trailing a rotten wind, going after weak points and the rear.

The defense-type zombie stood like a wall at the only exit.

The poison-type kept spitting dark green corrosive mist. The mist ate through concrete and snow, tightening the circle and turning the whole roof into a death zone.

On the high ground, the masked man stood cold and alone, not moving.

The whistle between his fingers never stopped. Its sharp, strange notes rolled on, each one steering the six Level Seven zombies' attacks.

The attacks came in waves, each fiercer than the last, with no end.

Elijah's body crackled with silver lightning. His peak Level Six Lightning Affinity was fully unleashed, silver arcs wrapped around him, the thunder deafening. His storm shredded the layers of the horde.

His Lightning Affinity was an aggressive power, with huge burst and top-tier armor breaking. Ordinary Level Six zombies would be charred to ash at one touch.
