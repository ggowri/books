Chapter 431

It was nearly unkillable. Its one weakness was the crystal core inside its skull.

Only by smashing that core could she kill it. Anything else just wore down whoever was attacking.

But with its explosive power and the hardness of its body, getting close enough to destroy the core would be brutally hard.

Hard, not impossible.

Paisley had noticed something. Each time it took a hit, it healed a little slower, and its body locked up for a split second.

That tiny gap was their only chance.

"Elijah, keep it busy!" she said urgently. "I'll go for the core!"

Elijah nodded. No questions.

He tightened his grip on the affinity rifle, pulled up a dense surge of Level Six power through his fingertips, and raked the mutant's joints with fire.

Boom, boom, boom!

Blasts lit up the warehouse. The shockwave rolled outward, tearing black scales off the mutant's body and peeling its flesh open into ugly wounds.

Then the wounds closed. Torn flesh squirmed back together, fresh scales grew over the gaps, and in under a second the damage was gone without a scar.

But Paisley had been right. The constant hits worked. The huge body stiffened, its assault stalled for a few seconds, and its movements slowed.

That was the opening!

******

Paisley's power exploded outward, the shockwave blowing the poison mist away. Frostbloom Sword in hand, she shot forward like a ghost while calling on her Plant Affinity.

Vines tore up from the floor, thick and supple, coiling higher and lifting her level with the serpent mutant's head.

The sword flared with cold light. She held her breath and brought it down hard.

But in the instant before the blade landed, the Level Seven mutant ignored it completely.

Elijah's nonstop attacks had enraged it. All it wanted now was to tear apart the little human who kept pestering it.

It surged faster!

Elijah's stomach dropped. Alarms rang in his head, and with no time to think he threw up a thin blue shield.

Crack!

The Level Six barrier shattered like paper under the Level Seven's brute strength and dissolved into drifting sparks.

There was nowhere to dodge.

Thud!

The thick tail slammed into his chest and gut. The force punched straight through him, and Elijah flew like a snapped kite, hit the concrete wall, and slid to the floor, limp.

He coughed up a mouthful of blood that splattered across the gray ground.

His organs felt shifted out of place. His chest burned as if a boulder were crushing it, and half his bones seemed broken. His breathing turned ragged and weak.

Worse was the venom on that tail. Black poison poured through his torn skin into his blood, and an ice-cold numbness swept through him. His skin darkened to a bruised blue-black as the toxin raced toward his limbs and organs.

In seconds he was badly hurt, poisoned, and too weak to fight or protect himself.

Midair, Paisley's sword was an inch from the mutant's scalp. When it twisted away, her cut landed off target and left only a deep gash across its body.

Strangely, the wound from the Frostbloom Sword didn't heal.

The serpent mutant stopped, halfway to devouring Elijah.

Paisley saw him bloodied and poisoned, and her heart clenched.
