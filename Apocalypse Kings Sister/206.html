Chapter 206

She drew the Frostbloom Sword and took them down one strike at a time.

In no time at all, most of the swarm was gone.

Kitty, woken by all the noise, appeared from the Storage Dimension to start digging out crystal cores.

The team let out a collective breath, relief plain on every face.

“Guess we get to keep living after all.”

There was no denying it—these three were just operating on another level.

Chase let the barrier drop, gasping for breath, face bloodless, watching the three of them fight. His expression was a mess—shame, embarrassment, and underneath it all, a wariness he refused to admit to.

The three of them had turned out to be far beyond anything he'd expected—sharp, coordinated, lethal. Not people he could afford to provoke.

But the resentment in his chest wouldn't go away either.

Especially those two blue-lit guns, powerful enough to make a mockery of every weapon this world had, far beyond anything that should exist.

Why did they get to have weapons like that?

The Director must be playing favorites, opening up some private channel just for them.

Something ugly took root in him, and he decided, once this mission was over, he'd demand answers from the Director himself.

Of course they'd saved him. That was just how it should be.

A Level Five psychic deserved protection, always.

Chase couldn't help snapping at them.

“Why didn't you get here sooner? I almost died in those things' jaws.”

Before he could say more, Kitty suddenly grew into a sixteen-foot white cat and bared its teeth at him, and that shut him right up.

She really would set this thing on him.

His knees were still aching.

Even her cat wasn't someone to mess with.

One more word out of him and that paw would be coming for his face.

Beside him, Rachel lifted her tear-streaked face, watching the three of them clear out dozens of Level Five mutants without even breaking a sweat, envy and fear tangling together inside her.

She didn't dare try anything else, shrinking into herself, trying to disappear, terrified they'd come after her for it later.

Fear or no fear, her eyes kept dragging back to the guns in Elijah's and Orion's hands, hungry with envy.

Nothing like the standard-issue weapons back at base—terrifying power, blinding speed, able to one-shot high-tier mutants without any effort at all.

If she had something like that, every mission would go her way, and she'd never have to depend on anyone else again.

She bit down hard on her lip, drowning in envy.

They must have stumbled onto something huge.

Why them?

Paisley Lennox, you miserable—

Why do you get every scrap of luck, every opportunity, every bit of favor, in this second life too?

Her eyes landed again on the pendant at Paisley's chest.

It had to be the pendant. It had to be.

She'd find a way to get it today, no matter what.

That pendant belonged to her.

Orion caught the strange look in her eyes, stepped between her and Paisley, and gave her a warning glance.

“Look at Pais like that one more time and I won't mind taking your eyes out.”

Rachel flinched and dropped her head.

The last mutant still fighting went down to a single stroke of Paisley's sword, straight through the throat.

The fight was over.

She stood in the wreckage of the flooded corridor, upright and composed, and began wiping blood from the Frostbloom Sword, her face blank, her movements unhurried.

Kitty padded over, nuzzling her furry head against Paisley's hand, and dropped twenty gleaming Level Five Water Affinity cores into her palm, then tipped its head back with a soft meow, like it was angling for praise.

She pocketed all of them.

That still wasn't the end of it.

Elijah and Orion both came forward, holding out more than a dozen Level Five Water Affinity cores of their own.

“Pais, these are yours.”

“Mine too, all of them.”

But she shook her head, turning down the offer.

“No need. These are Level Five cores—keep them, use them to level up your own Affinity.”

She never took what belonged to someone else's growth. She had plenty of her own, no need to lean on anyone's gifts.

Watching it, Rachel felt the jealousy burning her up from the inside.

Level Five Water Affinity cores.

Her own Water Affinity was stuck at Level Four, just from lack of cores.
