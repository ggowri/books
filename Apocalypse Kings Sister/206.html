Chapter 206

Paisley gripped the Frostbloom Sword and took out the trapped beasts one slash at a time, and in no time, she'd cleared out most of them.

Trovie's chatter woke up the little white cat, and it popped out of the Storage Dimension to dig out crystal cores.

The team members all let out a sigh of relief, grateful to be alive. "Looks like we get to live after all."

They had to admit it. These three were way stronger than they were.

Chase dropped the psychic barrier, gasping for air, his face drained of color. He watched the three of them fight, and his feelings were all over the place. Along with the shame and embarrassment, there was a fear he didn't want to admit.

Elijah, Orion, and Paisley were far more powerful than he'd expected. They worked together perfectly and fought with brutal force, and they definitely weren't people he could mess with.

But he still couldn't swallow his resentment.

What bugged him most were the two blue-glowing energy guns in their hands, which blew every normal weapon in the apocalypse out of the water. Why the hell do they get weapons like that?

The head of the base had to be playing favorites, secretly opening up a special supply line just for those three.

Anger festered in Chase, and he made up his mind. Once the mission was over and he was back at base, he'd go straight to the head of the base and demand an explanation.

As far as he was concerned, it was only natural for them to save him. He was a Level Five psychic, after all, and he deserved protection at all times.

Chase couldn't help blaming them. "Why didn't you come save us sooner? Those disgusting things almost ate me alive."

Before he could say anything else, the little white cat suddenly grew into a giant, 16 feet tall, and bared its teeth at him. Chase got so scared he didn't dare say another word.

Paisley wouldn't hesitate to turn on him. His knees still hurt from earlier. Even that cat of hers was dangerous, and he had no doubt that one more word out of him would bring its claws straight at his face.

Next to him, Rachel lifted her tear-streaked face, a total mess. She watched the three of them, so fierce and powerful, wipe out all those Level Five mutant sea beasts with ease, and she felt jealous and scared at the same time.

Now, she didn't dare try anything else. All she could do was curl up and try to make herself invisible, terrified they'd settle the score with her later.

But even through her fear, her greedy eyes stayed glued to the energy guns in Elijah's and Orion's hands, and she was green with envy.

These weapons were nothing like the regular guns the base handed out. They were terrifyingly powerful and insanely fast, and one shot could take out a high-level beast.

If she had a weapon like that, every mission would go her way, and she'd never have to rely on anyone again.

Rachel bit down hard on her lip, insanely jealous. Paisley and her crew must have stumbled onto some kind of huge lucky break.

Why them? Paisley, you fucking bitch!

She'd been given a second life too, so why did Paisley get to steal all the luck, all the breaks, and all the love?

Her eyes landed on the pendant on Paisley's chest again.

It had to have something to do with that pendant, and she had to find a way to get it today. That pendant is supposed to be mine!

Orion noticed the strange look in Rachel's eyes. He pulled out the cores from the beasts he'd just killed, stepped in front of Paisley, and shot Rachel a warning look. "Look at Pais like that one more time, and I'll gouge your eyes out."

Rachel shrank back and hung her head.

With one stroke of her sword, Paisley slit the throat of the last Level Five mutant sea beast still putting up a fight.

The fight was over.

She stood in the flooded wreckage, cool and composed. With a blank face, she carefully wiped the bits of blood off the Frostbloom Sword, calm and unhurried.

The little white cat trotted over and rubbed its fluffy head against the back of her hand. It set 20 glossy Level Five Water crystal cores in her palm, then looked up at her and meowed softly twice, like it was begging for praise.

Paisley put away all the Water cores.

And that wasn't all.

Elijah and Orion both stepped forward and held out the dozen or so Level Five Water cores they'd gotten from the beasts they'd killed.

"Pais, these are for you," Elijah said.

"Mine too, Paisley," Orion added. "Take all of them."

But Paisley just shook her head and turned them both down. "No need. These are Level Five cores, so keep them and use them to level up."

She never took other people's chances to grow stronger. She had plenty of resources of her own and didn't need anyone's handouts.

Rachel watched all this, so jealous she was about to lose her mind.

Those were Level Five Water cores. Her own Water Affinity was stuck at Level Four, and it was all because she didn't have enough cores.
