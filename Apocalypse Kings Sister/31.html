Chapter 31

In that moment, the Matriarch's eyes lit up with pure ambition and joy.

She was truly grateful to the mysterious trader on the other side of the screen.

Thank you, beautiful stranger, for giving me something this sacred.

With these goods, her tribe would never be the same, and her own status would skyrocket.

She turned to the handsome mates behind her and barked an order. "All of you, go get more high-level crystal gems, mutant herbs, and animals we can eat! As much as you can find!

"I want to trade for more!"

Her mates bowed their heads and left to do as she said.

Back in her dimension, Paisley looked over the healthy cow, the lively hens, and the piles of shimmering crystal gems. A faint smile spread across her face, because this trade had paid off big time.

Trovie suddenly squealed.

[Master! These crystal gems are perfect for leveling up! The pure energy inside can power up your ability, or we can trade them in for points to boost your trader level!

[We hit the jackpot!]

Paisley's eyes lit up. Getting stronger was obviously her top priority, since there was danger around every corner in the apocalypse.

Without wasting any time, she sat down cross-legged, cleared her mind, and started absorbing the stones' energy.

All the colorful crystal gems lit up at once, glowing softly. A steady stream of pure energy flowed out of them and surged through her body.

She shot from early Level Two to mid Level Two, then to the very peak of Level Two!

The green energy around her grew thicker and more concentrated by the second.

With all that energy pouring in, her Plant Affinity kept climbing.

With a deep hum, it was done. In one go, her Plant Affinity had jumped all the way from early Level Two to mid Level Three!

Now, with a wave of her hand, she could grow vines hundreds of feet long and control whole fields of plants to attack or defend. Her healing was a lot stronger now too.

Paisley slowly opened her eyes. A deep green glow flashed through them, then disappeared.

Power surged through her whole body. Every muscle felt alive, and her senses were sharper than ever.

She could pick up every faint movement within 100 yards. She could sense zombies hiding in the shadows and even feel life stirring in the roots underground.

Is this what being strong feels like? Because it's freaking amazing!

Trovie was even more excited than she was, doing loops in midair and squealing.

[You leveled up again, Master! Congratulations!

[Keep stocking up and trading as much as you can! You'll get stronger fast and rack up points in no time, enough to become a Level Two trader!

[Current total: 6,730 points! You earn points with every trade!]

The trading panel was empty now, ready for new listings.

Paisley gave Trovie an order right away. [Trovie, keep listing things we can trade. Go through everything in the dimension we don't need and put it all up for sale.]

Trovie got right to work. [On it, Master!]

Then, it added, [Keep leveling up, Master! Once you're a Level Five trader, I'll be able to take on a physical form and stay by your side all the time!

[I can't wait!]

Paisley had no idea Trovie could take physical form. [I'll do my best. I can't wait either!]

Just then, an annoyed voice drifted in from outside the car, breaking the silence.

The voice was low and full of spite, like the speaker thought Paisley couldn't hear.

"Does she really think she's some kind of princess? She's just an orphan who got lost and raised by strangers.

"Who does she think she is?

"She hasn't even awakened, and now she won't lift a finger. She just wants a free ride. Why does everybody keep babying her?"

It was Kelly.

With her Plant Affinity at mid Level Three, Paisley's senses were way sharper than a normal person's. Kelly was keeping her voice down on purpose, but Paisley heard every snide word loud and clear.
