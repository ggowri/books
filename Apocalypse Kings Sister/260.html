Chapter 260

The next morning,

a cool wind swept through the village, scattering the rot and blood that had hung over it for days. Something like peace had finally settled over this battered ground.

Paisley's group ate an early breakfast, packed up, and got ready to move.

******

Every freed villager, every survivor, and the girls who'd found their fight gathered at the village entrance to see them off.

They knew better than anyone that these were the people who'd torn down the devil ruling this place and given them a second chance at life.

Several of the older villagers wept openly, bowing again and again, their thanks choked but sincere.

"Thank you... thank you for saving all of us."

"If it weren't for you, we'd have been trapped in this hell forever, with nothing left of us at the end."

"Good people get good things back. Safe travels, all of you."

The girls from the night before stood at the front, watching Paisley steadily, awe and gratitude in their eyes.

No trace of that old fear left. A night's rest had sharpened them.

Everyone at the gate held something in their hands.

They'd lost nearly everything, but each of them had dug up whatever was left, their best, to give away.

Saved-up grain, clean clothes, sturdy shoes, makeshift medicine, clean water, the most precious things left in this world, offered without hesitation.

They pressed the gifts into the group's hands, insisting, repaying a debt they could never fully settle.

Paisley stood back and let the others handle it.

Madison turned them down firmly.

"Keep it. You'll need it more than we do."

They waved goodbye and moved on.

Heading southwest, toward their target: the armory at Stillwater.

Once Wexford was behind them, the landscape grew even more bleak.

Cracked roads, collapsed houses, streets choked with wild grass. Not a single trace of the life that had once filled this place.

A cold wind stirred up dead leaves and branches, the desolation absolute.

Stray zombies wandered the empty road, slow and stiff, their hoarse growls adding to the overall unease.

The road ahead was long, and dangerous.

Half an hour later, they reached a large supermarket on the edge of Stillwater's territory.

It was the biggest supply point before Stillwater proper, its outer walls peeling but the display windows intact, dust and rubble piled at the entrance, not a soul in sight, just the wind moaning through broken glass.

Everyone got out.

Elijah looked them over, calm and efficient.

"Pair up, split into groups. Go in, see what's left. Try to restock what you can."

His tone turned serious.

"Stay careful, no one goes in alone. If anything happens, use your pager and call for backup."

Everyone agreed and started sorting into pairs.

Marcus, quiet at the back of the group, stepped forward and tugged gently at Paisley's sleeve, his voice a little shy.

"Ms. Lennox..."

Before he could finish, a cool gaze landed on them.

Elijah turned toward her, his presence easing back, his voice calm and certain.

"You're with me."

Paisley nodded without hesitation.

He probably needs something specific.

Marcus's fingers stilled, and he let go of her sleeve quietly, a trace of disappointment crossing his eyes before he stepped back without a word.

Orion wasted no time sorting the rest.

"I'll go with Madison. Spencer and Ashley together. Mason and Kelly. Thomas and Marcus."

"Everyone good with that?"

"Back here in an hour!"

With the groups set, everyone checked their weapons and split off in different directions.
