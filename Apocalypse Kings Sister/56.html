Chapter 56

A few punches from Paisley's bare fists had turned that terrifying, world-ending monster into a soft, clingy house cat the size of her forearm. It had begged for mercy, chosen her as its master, and even handed over its precious Level Four crystal core!

The contrast was so insane that it flipped everything they thought they knew about mutant beasts on its head.

All three guys could only stare. They were too shocked for words, and their heads were spinning with questions.

What kind of insane move was that? What was Paisley, some kind of monster? Was she even human?

Just how ridiculously strong was she?

She hadn't used her ability or any outside help. With nothing but her bare hands, she'd easily taken down a high-tier Level Four mutant beast at full strength.

They could hardly believe their eyes.

Orion stood there stunned for a long moment. Finally, he swallowed hard, and his voice came out dry and shaky.

"Damn, my sister is unreal... She beat a Level Four mutant into submission with her bare hands and turned it into a pet?"

Mason sucked in a sharp breath, too. The cold sweat on his back hadn't even dried yet, and his heart was still racing.

"That thing almost froze us solid, and I'll never forget that feeling as long as I live. And now it's acting like a sweet little kitty? This is unbelievable.

"Pais saved my life again!"

Elijah watched Paisley closely as she petted the cat, looking soft and relaxed. He was amazed and curious, and he couldn't shake how stunned he was.

She kept surprising him, over and over. She was calm, sharp, mysterious, and powerful, always hiding another secret. The more he saw, the more he wanted to know everything about her!

"The danger's gone. Go ahead and pick out whatever cars you like."

Paisley never stopped petting the cat, and the kitten snuggled happily in her arms.

Mason didn't hold back either.

He'd been driving for years, so he went straight for a few top-of-the-line four-wheel-drive off-roaders. They were heavy, sturdy, and powerful, and they could handle any rough road. They'd be perfect for long trips through ruins and wilderness in the apocalypse.

Elijah made the most practical pick, a high-end luxury sedan with a low-key look. It was smooth, roomy, and good for long distances, and it wouldn't draw attention. That made it perfect for road trips and quick getaways.

Orion loved speed and power, so he zeroed in on two supercars right away. They were sleek, crazy fast, and gorgeous, and his eyes lit up like a kid's on Christmas morning.

Paisley looked over the whole lot and didn't bother choosing. She took all of it.

Her dimension could easily hold dozens of cars. She could have swallowed the entire dealership and still had room to spare.

With a single thought, every brand-new car in the showroom and the lot vanished, one after another. They reappeared in neat rows on the open ground inside her dimension.

Rugged off-roaders, luxury sedans, supercars, family cars, and cargo trucks: 37 brand-new vehicles in all, stored away in a matter of moments.

Orion's jaw dropped, and he swallowed hard without realizing it. "Paisley... that was freaking badass!"

When she'd said 10,000 square feet earlier, he'd figured she was exaggerating. But watching dozens of cars vanish into thin air with his own eyes, he finally got it. Paisley was the real deal!

Elijah studied Paisley for a long moment. She was so calm and collected, and he liked her more by the minute!

With the cars taken care of, the four of them kept raiding everything else.

The dealership's service warehouse was fully stocked, packed with all kinds of supplies.

There were crates of premium gasoline, diesel, motor oil, and antifreeze, plus spare tires, tools, batteries, chargers, and car parts. They had everything.

There were also brand-new car covers, mini fridges, emergency kits, tow ropes, snow chains, and tire repair kits. All of it was must-have gear for traveling in the apocalypse.

Paisley took every last bit of it!

Crates of fuel stacked up neatly in her dimension, and the parts were sorted and put away. The warehouse was empty in no time.

On top of that, she grabbed the store's brand-new couches, cots, folding tables and chairs, pop-up canopies, water coolers, emergency lights, and a full set of repair equipment. She even took the unopened work safety gear, insulated workwear, and rain gear from the stockroom.

After the hell she'd lived through in her past life, she knew one thing for sure. When the storm cut the city off and supplies ran out, even the smallest, most random thing could end up saving her life.

Paisley never left a place without picking it clean.

As they walked, Elijah pulled a creased but intact map of Southspire out of his pocket. He quickly traced a finger across it to find their location, then spoke in a low voice.
