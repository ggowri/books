Chapter 13

Whenever danger came calling, the four Lennoxes had always shoved Paisley out in front. Not one of them had ever cared whether she was scared, or whether she'd make it out alive.

To them, she was nothing but a tool to keep them safe.

This was the first time in two lifetimes that someone had actually stood between her and danger.

So this is what it feels like to be someone's favorite. To be protected.

Just then, the noise of the fight drew more and more zombies from all around, and they kept closing in on the car.

Paisley pulled a sharp machete out of her dimension and handed it to Spencer. "Spencer, we don't need a guard," she said calmly. "Go help them out there.

"The more zombies show up, the more danger they're in.

"And remember, the only way to finish a zombie off is to cut off its head. You can hack off anything else, and it'll still keep coming at you."

Spencer looked at how calm she was, then at the zombies piling up outside. Things were getting bad, and he knew it.

He gave a firm nod. "Okay. Paisley, Madison, be careful, both of you."

He gripped the machete, shoved the door open, and charged straight into the horde.

Several zombies quickly surrounded the car, their rotting bodies slamming against the doors again and again.

Beside Paisley, Madison's face had gone tense. She was gripping her knife so hard her knuckles had turned white, and her whole body was rigid with fear.

Paisley knew exactly how she felt. It reminded her of the first time she'd ever had to kill a zombie.

It had been right after the apocalypse broke out. She'd been terrified too, but Rachel had grabbed her and held her out in front like a shield.

Rachel had hidden behind her, acting all scared and helpless. "Paisley, protect me! I'm so scared," she'd whimpered.

And Paisley had been stupid enough to fall for it. Soft and naive, she'd stood in front of Rachel like an idiot, killing zombies with everything she had to keep her safe.

Now that she thought about it, the signs had been there all along.

From day one, Rachel had used her as a human shield on purpose, hoping a zombie would rip her apart. Every single thing Rachel did had been calculated.

Paisley snapped back to the present as several rotting zombies slammed into the window, rattling the glass. Madison held her breath, shaking all over.

She gave Madison's hand a gentle pat. "Don't be scared," she said softly. "They can't get in."

As she spoke, she reached into her dimension and pulled out a sleek little dagger. It was the diamond-studded one Orion had given her for self-defense, and the blade was wickedly sharp and fit her hand perfectly.

Paisley pushed the door open and swung herself out of the car in one smooth motion, calm and steady, without a trace of panic.

The three zombies circling the car turned at the sound and lurched toward her on stiff limbs. Their rotten stench hit her hard, thick enough to make anyone gag.

Inside the car, Madison's eyes went wide with worry. She was about to shout a warning when Paisley slipped lightly to the side, dodging the zombie's grab with pinpoint precision.

She moved fast and clean, nothing like a first-timer. Her dagger flashed, slicing across the zombie's throat before driving straight into its skull.

With a wet squelch, the first zombie went rigid and hit the ground. It didn't move again.

Before the other two could react, Paisley was on them. She sidestepped, struck, and drove her blade into each skull in one smooth, flowing move.

In a matter of seconds, all three zombies were dead on the ground, and the car was safe.

Not far away, Orion, Spencer, and the others were still cutting through the horde. They caught the whole thing out of the corner of their eyes and froze, stunned.

To them, Paisley was a sweet, gentle girl who needed protecting.

Nobody had expected her to be so calm, so fierce, and so ruthless the very first time she faced a zombie.

Even Elijah, who'd stayed cool through the whole fight, glanced over. He kept his eyes on Paisley, deep in thought.

I knew it. There's way more to this girl than meets the eye.

Paisley ignored the stares completely. She calmly wiped the filthy blood off her dagger.
