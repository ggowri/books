Chapter 173

The moment the spicy jerky went down, the carnivorous flower chomped down on it over and over. Then, its huge body, which had been gearing up for a vicious attack, suddenly went rigid and stopped dead.

A second later, a burst of searing agony exploded through its softest, most vital core.

It fed on rot and poison, so its body was immune to every kind of toxin, and even strong acid couldn't hurt it. But insanely spicy food just happened to be its natural weakness.

The fierce, brutal heat raced through its whole body and burned it from the inside. Its core, which had been soft and nourished by poison, quickly shriveled and rotted, as if wildfire were raging through its veins.

With a deafening rumble, the towering flower shook violently and went into wild spasms.

The huge, sky-blotting petals burst apart out of control. Thick, dark green venom and shredded petals rained down, burning countless black craters into the ground as white smoke hissed up and a sharp, poisonous stench filled the whole field.

Driven mad with pain, it thrashed its massive roots underground. Its thick, tangled roots ripped through the earth, and the whole field cracked and caved in, crushing countless poisonous flowers under its out-of-control strength.

It couldn't gather even a speck of poison pollen anymore. Its once-endless poison energy fell into chaos and broke apart, and its deadly, sinister presence faded fast.

High above, Paisley stood steady on her platform of willow branches, eyes cold and clear, watching the Level Six monster thrash below.

Down below, the mutant willow pressed its advantage. Thousands of green willow branches shot out like drawn blades, stabbing through everything in their path.

The branches that had just cleared the perimeter surged forward, locking around the flower's thick, twisted roots, tightening layer after layer, cracking the hardened plant flesh, dark green sap oozing out.

Its lush, living plant energy devoured and suppressed the twisted poison, breaking down the flower's core energy bit by bit and cutting off any chance for it to fight back or escape.

The mutant willow visibly grew bigger and stronger by the second.

Inside the ward, everyone watched in open-mouthed shock, too stunned to snap out of it for a long time. Who knew that would even work?

"Paisley actually took down the flower with a bag of spicy jerky? That's unbelievable!" Ashley's eyes went wide.

"I guess it's true that everything has a natural enemy. Paisley's amazing for even thinking of that!" Spencer murmured, shaking his head in awe, won over for good.

The others all looked at Paisley with pure admiration.

Her power had reached a truly terrifying level.

The flower's convulsions grew worse, its massive body withering faster now, the shattered petals hanging limp, the hardened roots turning soft and rotten, its once-overwhelming Level Six presence crashing fast.

It twisted wildly, trying to break free of the willow branches and escape, but the willow's grip only grew tighter, layer upon layer. Its strong life force kept flooding the flower's poison core, eating away at its very life.

As the last of the heat burned through its core, the massive body locked up, every struggle stopping at once.

The huge flower withered, collapsed, and turned gray right before their eyes. Its giant roots across the field quickly dried up and crumbled, losing every bit of life and poison.

Even with most of its body shattered and shriveled, the Level Six flower's main stem was still rooted deep underground. The tough trunk that remained clung to a faint spark of life, lurking beneath the soil with a lingering trace of poison.

Mutant beasts in the apocalypse were incredibly hard to kill. As long as the stem survived and the crystal core was still inside, it could eventually soak up nutrients from the poisoned soil and come back.

She had to wipe it out, root and all, and leave nothing behind that could come back to bite them.

Paisley's eyes were sharp. The willow branches beneath her slowly lowered, setting her down safely on the ground.

She gripped the Frostbloom Sword, frost coiling along the blade, pale light cutting through the last traces of thin poison mist.

She stepped forward to face the thick, tough stem that was left. The flower's poison defenses and affinity barriers had shattered when its body collapsed, and only the main stem was barely holding on.
