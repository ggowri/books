Chapter 476

Two Affinities and new cards every time. This woman's far stronger than the intel said, a hundred times stronger.

The other Quinn Affinity users all took a step back.

She's even stronger than the rumors. We shouldn't have come.

Even if we all went at her together, we'd lose.

If she uses that on us, we're finished.

Time to run. Save yourself.

Elijah stood still, his eyes fixed on that slim, straight figure. His chest tightened, and a storm tore through it.

He was calm by nature, restrained, steady. He had led Falcon Squad for years and had a mind that stayed still in any crisis. He'd seen countless high-level fights and impossible battles. Never before had he lost his composure like this.

He knew Paisley was gifted and strong. He knew she had many Affinities, that her Storage Dimension and Plant Affinity were stunning, that she grew fast. He'd gotten used to watching her quietly, used to the surprises she brought everyone. He thought he understood her well.

Only now did he see how bright the edge she'd hidden really was.

An instant, absolute freeze. A whole river locked in a heartbeat. A high-tier beast sealed in a single thought.

A power this terrifying, a mind this steady in a crisis, a knack for turning losing fights around, all of it was beyond any Affinity user.

How many more surprises does she have that I don't know about?

She's become a star that can carry the whole team on her back.

The wind brushed his face and blew his calm away. In his eyes was awe, shock, and the burning feeling he kept buried deep in his heart.

He watched her stand alone on the frozen river. His gaze was fixed and tender. Everything else seemed to lose its color. In all the world, she was the only thing shining.

So this isn't a fleeting crush. Every time I see her shine, I fall deeper.

While everyone was still reeling, Paisley raised vines and lifted Mason, Madison, Ashley, and Spencer to safe ground.

When they were safe, her eyes turned cold again, and she charged at the mutant shark sealed in the ice.

She gripped the Frostbloom Sword at her waist. A cold flash leapt from it, the blade clear and sharp under the sky.

She flashed onto the frozen shark's body, her stance clean.

With a flick of her wrist, the point found the exact spot on its head where the core sat, drove in, cut free, and pulled out. Every move was smooth and exact, done in one breath.

Thirty seconds, and a huge, dark, full-bodied high-tier core, heavy with energy, was out in one piece.

With the core gone, the frozen shark went dead.

Another wave of shock hit the whole field!

Orion's pupils shrank, awe growing.

Pais is just too good. Thirty seconds to kill a high-tier shark.

She's so strong I feel useless.

But as her big brother, I'm so proud.

Thomas drew a slow breath, filled with awe.

A high-tier mutant shark has hide so hard that a whole squad can't kill it. Pais did it alone in thirty seconds.

That's my sister. She's amazing. I'm so proud.

Spencer was just in awe.

Even if I train for decades, I'll never reach her level.

Ashley watched Paisley's cool, straight back, full of pride and worship, and despised Chloe on the far bank even more.

Mason and Madison traded a look. Both saw the same deep shock in the other's eyes. Paisley was the one who could protect the whole squad.

On the far bank, Chloe dug her nails into her palms, envy and fear twisting together.

Grant's face was black as water, killing intent boiling in his eyes, but he never dared look down on her again.

The rest of them felt a chill run down their necks.

That shark was the Quinn family's high-tier weapon. They paid a fortune to raise it, and she cut it down in a single stroke?

Her strength is insane.

We're no match for her.

Is there any point in finishing this mission?

It'd be a suicide run!!!
