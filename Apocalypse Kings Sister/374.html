Chapter 374

Elijah turned to Paisley, who was sheathing her sword, and gave the instruction.

Paisley nodded, her cool gaze sweeping once across the reservoir and the silo grounds. "Okay."

With the division of labor settled, Elijah led the remaining seven members of Falcon Squad into the pitch-black interior of the silo, moving with caution to search for the trapped hostages.

Outside, only Paisley remained.

She sheathed the Frostbloom Sword and let her gaze drift calmly over the battlefield, beginning a methodical sweep for anything worth salvaging.

First came the remains of the seven Level Seven mutant trumpet flowers.

High-tier mutant plants were valuable from root to petal.

Paisley crouched down, slender vines of Plant Affinity condensing at her fingertips, precisely stripping each flower of its thick petals, sturdy stems, and plump seeds.

The deep purple petals, saturated with high-tier toxin, could be refined into powerful paralytic agents and hallucinogens once properly processed, exceptionally rare poison-crafting materials in the apocalypse.

The tough, fibrous stems could be worked into high-tier protective gear and weapon fittings.

And the small, plump seeds were just as precious, usable both for cultivating new mutant plants and for alchemy, their value extraordinary.

Paisley sorted everything carefully, storing all the material away in her Storage Dimension.

Once she'd cleared the trumpet flower material, her attention turned to the abandoned grain silo.

This silo had been a national grain reserve before the apocalypse hit, its stockpile was massive.

Paisley made her way inside.

Rows of sealed packages held aging wheat, corn, and ground grain in bulk, along with a huge stock of unopened ration bars, canned goods, and dehydrated vegetables, more than enough to keep Falcon Squad stocked for a long time.

A metal cabinet in the corner held an unexpected surprise.

A cabinet packed full of unexpired medical supplies: sterile gauze, hemostatic cotton, suture thread, anti-inflammatory medication, antibiotics, and several boxes of intact emergency injections, far more refined and rare than the standard-issue medicine distributed by the base.

In the storage closet nearby sat a large stash of intact rope, reinforced steel cable, anti-slip buckles, explosion-proof flashlights, backup batteries, repair tools, and even two complete, unopened sets of field protective combat gear.

Paisley stored it all away.

With that done, she extended her psychic senses again and turned toward the reservoir.

The whole lake looked calm on the surface, but the water below held secrets of its own.

Paisley sent her psychic power down into the depths, sweeping carefully over every inch of silt and water. A light twitch of her fingers, and a warm, pale jade stone, wrapped in dense, pure energy, settled quietly into her palm.

She asked her system.

[Trovie, can this jade be traded for system points?]

Trovie's voice rang out in her mind at once, bright and bouncing with excitement.

[Host, you finally remembered I exist.]

[You can! This kind of energy jade is extremely valuable!]

Paisley looked up at the vast stretch of water before her, a faint light sparking in her eyes.

One idle scoop, and she'd pulled up a piece of energy jade. How much more was hidden under this entire reservoir?

She'd already noticed, during her earlier probe, that every fish and shrimp in these waters had doubled in size compared to pre-apocalypse standards, their flesh dense with rich Affinity energy, extraordinarily nourishing.

Clearly, everything in this reservoir, every creature, every resource, was top-tier treasure, hard to come by anywhere in the apocalypse.

Paisley made up her mind on the spot, instructing silently.

[Trovie, help me pull this whole lake into the Storage Dimension.]

Trovie grew even more excited.

[Host, you really are something! Finding a hidden treasure like this in the apocalypse!]

[I did a rough scan, there's at least a hundred and ten thousand pounds of energy jade at the bottom of this lake!]

[And every fish, shrimp, and water-dwelling thing in here is top-tier nourishment for Affinity users. Eating it regularly strengthens the meridians and builds the body!]

Paisley's eyes brightened, her tone crisp.

[Pull it in, quick.]

Trovie, brimming with fighting spirit.

[Storing a whole lake like this is no small task, but I love a challenge! I'll get it done for you!]

The next instant, Paisley's focus sharpened.

The massive, still reservoir, water, mud, every bit of jade, every fish and shrimp, every piece of aquatic plant life at the bottom, vanished in an instant, swallowed whole into the system's Storage Dimension, leaving nothing behind but bare, empty ground.

Trovie's notification chimed immediately.

[Host, the entire body of water has been stored! Would you like to exchange all the energy jade for system points right now?]

Paisley didn't hesitate.

[Do it now.]

Trovie: [Exchange successful! A hundred and ten thousand pounds of energy jade converted into one million points!]

[Hidden bonus triggered: for fully storing a complete natural body of water for the first time, an extra ten thousand points awarded!]
