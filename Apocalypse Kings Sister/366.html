Chapter 366

Night before, before turning in, she'd received her father's secret orders: Elijah Blackwood was officially back, reclaiming power, and the long-standing balance in Aetheris Base was about to shatter completely.

Her father had told her to stir up trouble from the shadows, to find any way to inflame the conflict between the Blackwood faction and the Quinn faction and let them wear each other down.

She'd been racking her brain for a way to start something. Then, out of nowhere, the two sides collided on their own. A mission wager with an entire squad's survival on the line had handed her the spark she needed.

Vivian Lennox's gaze finally settled, with pinpoint precision, on Paisley in the crowd.

She studied this branch-family foster daughter the main Lennox family had never once taken seriously. Paisley stood there, cool and composed, utterly unbothered. There wasn't a trace of respect in Vivian's eyes, only the faintest, most contemptuous curl of a smile.

Pretty enough, lucky enough, and she'd managed to latch onto Elijah Blackwood. That was all there was to it.

A pretty face and nothing underneath. Even with a lucky Affinity upgrade and a moment in the spotlight, she'd never amount to anything. She'd never make real waves.

As far as Vivian was concerned, win or lose, Paisley was nothing but a hanger-on, an insignificant piece on someone else's board.

Per base mission protocol, once a wager match was confirmed, the two captains had to step forward in turn to claim this round's Level Eight high-risk field mission.

Chloe Thompson and Grant Quinn's team, the Scarlet Squad, the veteran outfit.

This squad had topped the base's mission leaderboard for years running without a single loss. Covered in commendations, they were the acknowledged top squad at the base, their depth and combat experience far outstripping any ordinary team.

The Scarlet Squad stepped up first to register and claim their mission. The staff, already familiar with this flagship team, moved briskly to bind them to the Level Eight field exploration mission.

Grant took the mission token, fingers brushing the cold metal. His profile was hard and arrogant. He glanced back at Falcon Squad once, not bothering to hide the challenge, or the certainty of victory, in his eyes.

Chloe straightened up, chin lifted, face full of pride, certain they couldn't lose.

Right behind them, Elijah Blackwood led Falcon Squad forward.

His bearing was tall and solemn. He completed the registration process without a hitch; all eight members' information checked out, and they claimed an identical Level Eight mission token.

Two identical tokens, meaning the exact same mission zone, the exact same objective. Only skill would decide the outcome.

Every onlooking Affinity-user in the hall held their breath, watching the two teams, tense.

On one side, the seasoned, battle-hardened veterans of the Scarlet Squad, undefeated for years.

On the other, the newly minted, top-tier Falcon Squad, every member Level Six, talent beyond belief.

A high-stakes mission showdown that would decide a squad's survival, and shake the base's entire power structure, was about to begin.

A staff member spoke up, solemn.

"Both missions are now active. You may depart immediately for the wasteland ruins zone. The time limit is three days. Failure to complete the mission or eliminate the target mutant plant within that window will be recorded as a mission failure."

The moment the words fell, both teams turned at once and walked toward the hall's exit, heading in opposite directions.

The air crackled with tension thick enough to ignite.

Standing in the crowd, Vivian watched the two teams disappear, one after the other. Her smile deepened.

The chaos was set in motion. Now all that was left was to watch the tigers fight and let them tear each other apart.

She'd reap the rewards while sitting back and doing nothing.

Whether the Blackwood faction took a hit, or the Quinn faction's flagship squad was disbanded, either outcome was pure profit for the Lennox family.

All she had to do was stand quietly in the background, let the two sides exhaust each other, and the base's current balance of power would crumble on its own, clearing the way for the Lennox family to seize the top.

Meanwhile, Falcon Squad and the Scarlet Squad walked out of the mission hall one after another, heading straight for the base's supply depot.

A Level Eight high-risk field mission was treacherous and unpredictable. The base had strict rules for provisioning. Every deploying squad received a standard field survival kit, and the higher the difficulty, the more supplies they were issued.

The depot was spacious, its shelves arranged in neat rows, stocked with every kind of field gear and combat equipment, all specially reinforced to withstand the apocalypse's harshest conditions.

Following the base's top-tier protocol, each team member received a black, wear-resistant, waterproof tactical backpack.

Each pack came fully equipped.

Compressed rations, temperature-stable drinking water, and high-energy nutrition bars, enough to last the full three days in the field.

Fast-acting wound treatment, hemostatic spray, and antibacterial ointment, ready for any sudden injury out in the wild.

And beyond that, reinforced tactical cord, a sharp, sturdy combat knife, a standard-issue sidearm, and a full complement of ammunition, every piece top-grade base equipment.
