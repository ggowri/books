Chapter 344

All the humiliation he'd endured, every wall he'd hit, every beating and every sneer — it all came down to having no strength, no Affinity!

If he could just awaken one, gain the power to protect himself, he could finally turn things around, never be pushed around again!

He'd pay her back somehow, eventually.

He wasn't wrong to do this.

The moment the broth hit his stomach, warm, pure power surged through his entire body.

Within moments, Chase stirred first, his lashes fluttering as his eyes slowly opened.

He still looked pale and weak, his breath unsteady, his Affinity signature faint — his once-high level had collapsed entirely back down to Level One.

Right behind him, Rachel, who'd had only half a bowl, blinked her eyes open too.

But her eyes were hollow, utterly empty. Whatever Affinity she'd once had was simply gone, vanished without a trace.

She was back to being nothing at all.

Rachel didn't dare say a word about it.

As for Reid, a faint gust of wind suddenly swirled around him, Wind Affinity energy stirring restlessly through his body!

He'd actually done it — awakened a Wind Affinity!

Feeling that new power move through him for real, Reid's eyes blazed with triumph, his whole body shaking, barely able to keep the grin off his face.

He wasn't just an ordinary person anymore!

He finally had an Affinity of his own!

But now wasn't the time to flaunt it.

He couldn't let anyone catch on to what he'd done.

Damon looked at his nephew Chase, now awake, his expression still cold, no warmth in it at all, his tone flat.

"Your parents already know you've been hurt and unconscious. They've asked me to bring you back to Aetheris tomorrow."

"Rest tonight. I'll send someone for you in the morning."

Chase, pale and weak against the headboard, nodded.

"Whatever you say, cousin."

Then he glanced at Rachel, still weak, and asked:

"Can I bring Rachel with me?"

Damon nodded, unbothered.

"Suit yourself."

Hearing that, Reid saw his chance to finally get out of this place and grabbed it like a lifeline, pleading carefully.

"Mr. Quinn, could you bring my siblings along too? We'll be loyal, we'll do whatever you ask!"

He pointed at himself, eyes urgent.

"I've already awakened a Wind Affinity. I'm one of you now — I'll get strong. I'll be useful."

After everything he'd been through, Reid had learned the lesson well.

Only usefulness ever won anyone over.

Rachel, newly awake and realizing her Affinity was gone for good, was already drowning in panic and despair.

In this world, someone without an Affinity was worthless, discarded by everyone sooner or later.

She didn't dare show her panic, forcing it down and pretending nothing but weakness.

At least with her brother's new Affinity, the Lennoxes still had something to offer — and she still had a reason to be worth keeping around.

She grabbed Chase's arm at once, voice soft and pleading.

"Chase, they're my family. Please let them come to Aetheris too. My parents are there as well — we could all be together, look out for each other."

Chase rather liked being on the receiving end of her pleading and softened enough to ask Damon on her behalf.

In the end, Damon agreed to Chase's request.

The night passed in a blink.

The next morning, under a clear bright sky, Paisley, Elijah, Orion, Rachel, Chase, and the others boarded a private flight back to Aetheris.

At three in the afternoon, the plane broke through the clouds and touched down smoothly at the base's private airfield.

The airfield was tightly secured, its walls imposing, armed patrols everywhere — a different tier entirely from any regional base.

At the arrivals gate, Desmond Vance was already waiting with a small detail, standing ramrod straight.

A line of black luxury cars sat behind him, understated but unmistakably expensive.

The moment he spotted Elijah stepping off the plane, Vance bowed deeply, his tone the height of respect.

"Young master, welcome home. The Chief sent me personally to meet you."

Elijah nodded slightly, unbothered.

"Thank you, Vance. Arrange the cars so everyone gets home."

Vance acknowledged the order at once, then bowed in turn to Orion, Mason, Madison, and the Walkers, every gesture correct.
