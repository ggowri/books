Chapter 408

"If we bring back all of the grain, every member gets 50,000 contribution points, Falcon Squad gets First-Class Merit, and we keep elite squad status permanently."

Damon thought it over.

To him, no matter how strong Falcon Squad was, the Exclusion Zone meant near-certain death. They'd most likely lose too many people to bring the grain back. The promises were empty.

"Fine," he said. "If you complete it, those rewards are fair."

Then his voice went cold. "But as captain, you sign a mission pledge guaranteeing success."

"If you fail and don't bring back enough grain, everyone loses half their contribution points, Elijah steps down as captain, and he goes under investigation."

Without its core strength and its leader, the Blackwood family would lose its voice. It could never hold the base's top authority again.

It was a bet on everything, his future, his power, and the Blackwood family's foundation.

Every official in the room held their breath and watched the young man in the center, waiting for his answer.

Under all those watching, calculating, tense eyes, Elijah sat with his spine straight and his shoulders hard as blades. His dark eyes held no hesitation, and he looked nothing like a teenager.

His lips parted, and his voice was steady and clear.

"Fine. I'll sign."

Two words, and the room exploded.

Whispers rose in waves. The Blackwood faction paled, and their hearts sank. They couldn't sit still.

Nathaniel leaned forward, face grim, voice low and urgent. "Elijah! Think twice!"

"This is a death trap Damon set for you. The Exclusion Zone is far deadlier than anyone predicted. No one can guarantee success."

"If you sign this pledge, you hand your lifeline and the Blackwoods' initiative to someone else."

His eyes were full of pain and worry. He wanted to take the bet himself.

The Blackwoods stood at the top of the base because of Elijah's strength and Falcon Squad's deterrence. If Elijah lost his authority and went under investigation, decades of foundation could collapse overnight.

But Elijah didn't move. His eyes were clear, his mind made up.

The Quinns, the Lennoxes, and the neutral factions leaning on them were a sharp contrast.

Every one of them was hiding a smirk, thrilled inside.

They watched the Blackwood side panic and try to talk him out of it, and it felt great.

For a long time the Blackwoods had pressed down on every family. Elijah had stolen the spotlight on the base while still a teenager, and everyone resented it. No one had been able to push back.

Now fate had handed them an opening. Elijah was walking into a death trap and signing a pledge. He was cutting off his own arm and ruining his own future.

Damon sat to one side, his eyes dark, a thin, cold smile on his lips. He looked calm, but in his heart the outcome was already decided.

In his mind, Elijah would lose.

Once the mission failed, Elijah would fall, lose his position, and be investigated, and Falcon Squad's name would be wrecked. The Blackwoods would plunge.

Then the Quinns could step in and rewrite the base's power structure.

******

The Lennox officials across the table exchanged glances, each knowing what the others were thinking, their eyes full of hope.

They had waited years for the Blackwoods to fall and for power to be reshuffled.

The fence-sitters and neutral factions whispered to each other, enjoying the show.

"Too impulsive. He's just too young to keep his cool."

"Signing that pledge is basically ruining his own future. One wrong step and he's finished."

"Now we get to watch. The high and mighty young Blackwood is about to take a big fall."

"Where does he get the nerve to accept the bet?"

"If Falcon Squad loses, the sky over this base changes."

Mockery, curiosity, hope, pity, anxiety, all mixed together in the solemn room.

Everyone thought Elijah had been baited into acting rashly. Only he knew better.
