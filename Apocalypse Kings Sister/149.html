Chapter 149

The whole process went smoothly, without any effort at all.

In less than ten minutes, Paisley had completely purified all five huge crates of Galactic energy crystals.

Every crystal had lost its murky haze. They were all crystal clear and shimmering with energy, 100 percent pure.

Whether she used them to strengthen her abilities, level up, make high-level gear, or power things, they were top-quality material for all of it.

Paisley slowly opened her clear, cool eyes, and a faint smile crossed her face. She could feel more clearly than ever how powerful her Level Six Plant Affinity was.

At Level Six, her Plant Affinity came with three support abilities: purifying, healing, and speeding up growth. All three were incredibly rare in the apocalypse.

She knew she'd be welcome anywhere she went from now on.

In her past life, Rachel had only been so popular because she'd stolen the luck that should have been Paisley's.

After all that, Paisley cleared her head, closed her eyes again, and steadied her breathing. She laid her hands lightly on the crystals and quietly absorbed both streams of pure energy.

A huge, warm flood of energy poured in through her palms and spread through her whole body. It gently strengthened every muscle and the core of her abilities, and the bottlenecks she'd built up quietly loosened and broke apart.

Her Psychic Affinity leveled up first.

Her mental power had been stuck at early Level Five, but with all that high-level energy, it shot up fast. In no time, it broke through and hit mid Level Five.

The moment she broke through, all five of her senses sharpened dramatically. Her vision was crystal clear, and she could pick up even the slightest movement in her dimension.

Her mental power grew deeper and stronger, and her control got way better. With a single thought, she could move the energy around her however she wanted.

Right after that, her Water Affinity leveled up too.

The barrier at early Level Three shattered, and her energy kept building and climbing. She shot through mid Level Three, late Level Three, early Level Four, and mid Level Four before finally settling at peak Level Four, just a hair away from Level Five.

Her Water energy was clearer and fuller, and her control over water was both precise and powerful. She could form droplets, waves, or shields at will. Her attack, defense, and support skills had all improved, and she was much better at healing and purifying too.

As for her Level Six Plant Affinity, it barely changed at all.

When it was over, Paisley felt light and clear all over. Energy flowed through her freely, and her new levels felt rock-solid.

She slowly opened her eyes, bright and clear. She seemed even cooler and more untouchable now, and a steady, powerful aura surrounded her. It was like she'd been completely transformed, and she was stronger than ever.

Then, she poured herself a cup of clear, sweet spirit water from her dimension and slowly drank it.

The spirit water was warm and soothing going down. It washed away the last bit of tiredness from training, drew the leftover energy into her body, and locked in her new levels.

Once she was in peak condition, Paisley stood up and left her Storage Dimension with a thought.

Her room was quiet, warm, and cozy, shutting out the chill of the base at night.

She lay down in her soft, comfy bed and closed her eyes to rest.

As the night went on, the whole dorm building went still. Every light went out, everyone fell asleep, and the hallway was dead silent.

But in a dark, shadowy corner of the base, a cold wind blew, and the air felt heavy and gloomy.

Rachel was still hurting from her wounds, and she hadn't closed her eyes once.

She wanted to be 100 percent sure she could steal Paisley's pendant without a hitch. So, she waited patiently until she was sure the whole building was quiet and everyone was fast asleep. Then, she snuck off to meet Kelly.

She knew Kelly hated and envied Paisley just as much as she did, which made her the perfect partner on the inside.

Under the cover of night, Kelly showed up fast.

The two of them started plotting in whispers.

Kelly had been holding a grudge against Paisley for a long time.

Ever since Paisley had shown up, she'd stolen all the attention, affection, and admiration.

Kelly had loved Elijah and followed him around since they were kids. But now, he only had eyes for Paisley and didn't notice anything Kelly did for him.

Tonight's stew dinner was the worst. Everyone on the team had been invited except her. She'd been left out on purpose, and nobody had even asked about her or seemed to care.

What hurt her the most was that nobody on the whole team had objected. They'd all just gone along with leaving her out and pushing her aside.

In that moment, all her years of loyal devotion, all her careful efforts, and all the love she'd poured into him felt like a total joke. It was ridiculous and pathetic.

Bitterness, hurt, jealousy, and spite boiled inside her and swallowed whatever common sense she had left.
