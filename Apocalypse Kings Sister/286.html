Chapter 286

No wonder the whole trip here had gone so smoothly. No high-tier zombies, no mutant beasts, nothing.

Someone had clearly beaten them to it and taken everything.

They'd wasted the whole trip. Rotten luck.

"Whoever did this," Chase said, "when I find out who, they won't get off easy."

Just then, one of the Affinity users came jogging in from outside, face pale, voice urgent.

"Chase! Bad news! All three jeeps parked at the mountain entrance have their tires slashed. Completely wrecked. They won't drive!"

The words landed like a thunderclap.

Chase's pupils contracted, his face going dark and ugly.

Supplies gone, vehicles destroyed. That wasn't a coincidence.

Someone had been watching them from the shadows, playing them.

The thought that someone had dared set him up sent rage boiling through his chest, barely contained.

"Great. Just great."

He let out a low, cold laugh, his voice dripping with venom, murder flickering in his eyes.

"Someone has the nerve to steal my supplies and make a fool of me. I want to know exactly who."

"Don't let me find out it was anyone I know."

For now, all he had left was his own useless fury.

Half an hour later, the vehicles finally cleared the mountain forest behind them, leaving the armory's deep mountain region far behind.

The road led them into an abandoned little town — Garnet Hollow.

Not a single soul remained there.

The group found a reasonably clean villa to spend the night in.

The outer walls stood intact, the yard tidy, the doors and windows unbroken, no bloodstains or signs of struggle anywhere — the best-looking shelter in the whole deserted town.

Just then, Trovie's whiny voice started up in Paisley's mind.

Host, I'm so hungry! So hungry!

Paisley rubbed her temple and answered patiently in her mind.

Trovie, that's not a real feeling. You're a system. You don't have a body. How can you be hungry? Settle down.

Trovie whined back.

Host, I'm hungry! Hungry! Really, really hungry! I need food, now!

Worn down by the pestering, Paisley finally gave in with a sigh.

Fine, fine, you win. What do you want? I'll find it for you.

Trovie perked right up, practically vibrating with excitement, and spelled out exactly what it wanted.

Host! I want lots of energy stones! The purer, the better!

Energy stones were just the jade and gem ore scattered across this world, faintly laced with spirit energy.

Paisley glanced around outside. She'd noticed on the way in — this was Garnet Hollow.

She agreed.

Fine, lead the way.

She got up and told the rest of the group, who were settling in for the night.

"Orion, Captain, I'm going to step out for a bit."

Orion stood up at once, concerned.

"Pais, I'll come with you."

"No need."

Paisley shook her head, a small reassuring smile on her face.

"The town's safe. No high-tier zombies around. I'll just take a quick walk around the area. I'll be back soon."

Orion still gave her a warning.

"Be careful out there. Don't wander too far. In and out quick."

"Got it."

Paisley slipped out the door.

Trovie began scanning.

Host! Three hundred yards ahead! There's an abandoned jade processing plant. A ton of discarded jade and high-tier energy ore buried underground!

Paisley followed the system's directions and quickly arrived at the edge of town, where the abandoned jade processing plant stood.

Before the apocalypse, this had been a famous jade-processing hub, once bustling with merchants and traders. Now it was nothing but ruins.

The factory walls had collapsed in large sections, the steel frame rusted and twisted, the ground littered with raw stone, scrap, and cut-off edges from processing, piles of rock scattered everywhere, forgotten and untouched.

Nobody cared about any of this anymore.

Paisley looked down at the scattered stones, clueless about which ones held any value. She picked one up at random and shrugged.

I have no idea how to judge jade quality. Trovie, scan and sort it yourself. Eat whatever's useful.

Trovie buzzed with excitement.

Got it! Activating scan mode now!

The moment the words landed, an invisible blue scanning wave rippled outward from Paisley, sweeping across the entire ruined factory grounds.
