Chapter 404

She bent slightly and let her voice go soft and shaky, slipping easily into her act.

"Uncle Gerald, please, have mercy on my mom. She's been in that cold Security Office all this time. It's easy to get into a place like that, and so hard to get out. She's just a woman. I'm going out of my mind."

Most people would have felt sorry for her.

But the family sat at the table and watched the two of them play off each other, one hard and one soft, and all they felt was disgust.

They remembered how the senior branch had treated them, and how Brenda had bullied Diane. They knew exactly what had happened today.

Brenda had thrown her weight around, picked fights, seized their supplies, and bullied Diane. She had it coming. She deserved no pity.

Gerald's brows locked. His warm eyes went cold, leaving only distance and impatience.

Diane's gaze turned icy. The anger from earlier hadn't faded, and now they showed up at her door, and her chest burned.

Orion didn't move. His fingers curled slightly, his eyes cold, his presence heavy. He was already ready to protect his family.

Thomas scoffed outright, not bothering to hide his contempt.

Spencer's face was tight with anger.

Ashley frowned and edged closer to Paisley, silently picking her side.

Paisley had not said a word since they walked in. She sat in her chair, calm and cold, her lashes hiding everything.

She was so quiet it was almost eerie, and no one dared test her. She waited for the two of them to finish their show.

Then Dennis's eyes landed on the dishes, and greed and envy flashed across his face.

A full table of chicken, duck, fish, and meat. The colors were bright and the smell was rich. Even by pre-apocalypse standards, this was a banquet.

Most of those supplies had been taken from his own house that day.

Why? Why did the junior branch get to eat like kings, all together and happy, while his family became a joke?

The envy swelled until it swallowed his judgment.

He forgot why he had come. He ignored the cold faces around him. He pulled out an empty chair and dropped into it as if he owned the place, grabbed a bowl and fork, and started wolfing down food without a word.

Chloe's throat tightened as she watched. She had been humiliated all night, begging on an empty stomach, and the liquor had burned her insides raw. She was starving.

She hadn't seen a meal like this in a long time, with meat and vegetables and real smells.

Her greed showed. She dropped her act of the pampered, aloof princess, slid in beside her father, and shoveled food into her mouth.

The two of them ate as if no one else was there. The only sound was bowls clinking, and the silence was ugly.

The family's faces turned colder.

Thomas muttered, "No manners. Come to beg and act like bandits. Thick skin."

Ashley nodded, disgusted. "Seriously. Brenda started this. How can they even sit at our table? Do they act like they haven't eaten in a week?"

The family held back and watched them humiliate themselves.

After a while, the two were half full, and their courage came back with it.

Dennis put down his bowl and wiped his mouth. His eyes landed on Paisley, the quietest person in the room, and his voice was full of command.

"You're Gerald's new daughter, Paisley?"

He didn't wait for an answer.
