Chapter 285

In the passage, Chase and his group wandered deeper into the armory at an unhurried pace, completely unaware anyone was hiding nearby.

Only once their footsteps had faded completely into silence did Elijah slowly release his hand from Paisley's mouth, his arm sliding from her waist, movements gentle and controlled.

In the cramped, shadowed corner, the two of them stood close, eyes meeting in silence. Neither needed to say what they wanted to do next. They understood each other perfectly.

Moving quietly, breath held, they slipped out of the underground armory without a sound.

Once outside, they rejoined the group and urged everyone to get moving right away.

Paisley's gaze landed precisely on three military jeeps parked along the road — the same vehicles Chase's group had arrived in.

A mischievous glint crossed her eyes. She raised a hand, letting a cold surge of Ice Affinity ripple out, forming countless paper-thin, razor-sharp ice blades.

A series of sharp, quick whistles cut through the air, blades flying with precision straight into every tire on all three jeeps.

Thin cracks split open instantly, air hissing out fast, the tires sagging flat and useless, every wheel completely ruined.

Once all three vehicles were thoroughly disabled, Paisley pulled back her power, a satisfied little smirk tugging at her lips.

Just a small lesson for them.

Not the time to confront them directly yet.

"Get in the car. Let's move."

Elijah watched the mischief dancing in her eyes and gave in with an indulgent tilt of his mouth, signaling the group to head out.

Engines rumbled to life, and the convoy turned sharply, pulling away from the mountains.

The forest flew past the windows, and the thought of Chase and Rachel's group having to hike all the way back to base on foot was enough to make Paisley laugh out loud.

******

Meanwhile, deep in the underground armory, Chase strode through with Rachel on his arm and a crowd of hangers-on in tow, searching every corner of the space meticulously.

But there was nothing. Just empty rooms, dust, and the lingering traces of the earlier fight. Not a shred of supplies anywhere.

No weapons, no equipment, no fuel. Not even a scrap of food.

This was supposed to be Stillwater's largest armory, famous for its massive reserves. And it was completely empty.

Chase's smug confidence shattered on the spot, his smile cracking into disbelief and a rising, furious anger.

He came to a dead stop, scanning the empty warehouse, his voice tight with disbelief.

"Where are the weapons? The guns? The supplies?"

"Command said this place had years of reserves stockpiled, the region's prime weapons depot. How is it empty?"

He scanned the room top to bottom, his earlier triumph collapsing into nothing, the twenty thousand contribution points and easy glory he'd counted on vanishing into thin air.

And he'd gone and bragged to his superiors, promising he'd pull this mission off.

Now there was nothing here. What was he supposed to do?

The Affinity users with him looked just as lost, exchanging blank, bewildered glances.

They'd traveled all this way, carefully dodging zombies and mutant beasts, full of hope, only to end up with nothing.

Though, on second thought, it was almost a relief. Better this than watching Chase and Rachel claim it all for themselves.

Rachel's ingratiating smile had frozen on her face too, her heart sinking.

She'd been counting on those contribution points, imagining new clothes and jewelry, and now all of that had crumbled to nothing.

She tugged nervously at Chase's sleeve, her voice edged with panic and frustration.

"Chase, how could this be? Did we get the wrong location? Is there another hidden storeroom somewhere?"

Chase's fists clenched hard enough to whiten his knuckles, his presence turning oppressive, the weight of his Level Five psychic power leaking out in waves.

"We didn't get it wrong."

He jabbed a finger at the map, his voice tight with fury.

"The terrain, the layout, the coordinates. All of it matches. This is the place."

"There's only one explanation. Someone got here before us and cleaned the place out completely!"

The words landed, and realization swept through the group, bitterness and anger rising fast.
