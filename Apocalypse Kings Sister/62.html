Chapter 62

She hated Paisley for being so cold and cruel, for mocking her and leaving her to die.

She hated Orion for kicking her when she was down, Elijah for watching like he didn't care, and Mason for humiliating her in front of everyone.

I hope every last one of them dies screaming.

Why?! Why did a useless loser like Paisley get so lucky and get to live safe and sound in the apocalypse?

And Rachel? She'd been humiliated, nearly killed again and again, and dumped in the wilderness like trash for anyone to mock. How was that fair?

She'd been reborn. So why was everything different this time?

In her past life, she'd been the center of attention, the Plant Affinity healer everyone fawned over. She'd even had her own Storage Dimension. Paisley had just been the adopted daughter nobody noticed, living under someone else's roof.

After the apocalypse hit, Paisley had been a useless nobody who never even awakened an ability.

But now, everything had changed.

Where did it all go wrong? It wasn't supposed to be like this!

She refused to accept it! The bitterness gnawed at her soul!

The wind grew wilder. It whipped her mud-caked hair, sent gravel skittering across the ground, and snuffed out the last bit of kindness left in her.

Rachel slowly lifted her head. Her face, smeared with mud and tears, was twisted and ugly. Her once-clear eyes had gone dark and wild with hatred.

"Paisley, Orion, Elijah, Mason! I swear it right here and now!

"Every bit of humiliation and pain you put me through today, I'll pay you back a hundred times over, a thousand times over!

"Even if I have to go to hell and come back as a demon, I'll claw my way out and rip you all apart with my bare hands. One day, I'll make you pay in blood, and you'll burn for it!"

The hoarse, broken, half-crazed vow echoed through the air, as cold as the storm rolling in. In the dead silence of the apocalypse, a seed of revenge had been planted.

The pendant! That's it.

She had to get the pendant back first. Then, everything would go back to the way it was in her past life.

Far off on the horizon, the first light drops of rain drifted down on the wind.

The wild wind churned the mud, and heavy clouds swallowed the whole sky. The dim air was damp, cold, and heavy, like the storm could break any second.

Then, the rain came pouring down, pounding the ground. It washed away the dust and zombie remains in the yard and drenched Rachel where she knelt.

The icy rain ran down her mud-caked hair and torn clothes and soaked her to the bone. The cold, the humiliation, and the despair almost crushed her mind completely.

She stayed hunched over on her knees with her fingers dug into the mud. Her fingertips were a bloody mess, smeared with rain, mud, and blood.

The despair of being abandoned, the humiliation of being played, and the burning unfairness of it all fused into pure, poisonous resentment that tore through her soul.

Hatred swallowed the last of her sanity!

With a low hum, a surge of clear, violent Water Affinity energy exploded out of her and raced through her whole body!

The rain froze in midair all around her and started swirling around her body. The cold, harmless rainwater now pulsed with aggressive, overwhelming power!

On the brink of death, Rachel had awakened a Water Affinity!

The rain washed the mud off her body, but it couldn't wash away the hatred burned into her bones.

After a moment of silence, Rachel burst into wild, raspy laughter. It tore through the dead quiet of the rainy night, shrill and creepy, and it only got louder.

She slowly lifted her head. The rain had washed her face clean, but it was twisted with sick, crazed madness. Her bloodshot eyes locked on the direction the car had gone as she laughed harder and harder.

"I really am the chosen one! God hasn't given up on me, and neither has the apocalypse!

"Paisley! You really thought dumping me and humiliating me would be enough to break me?!"

She pushed herself up on shaky legs and rose unsteadily out of the mud. Water swirled and surged around her, and razor-sharp blades of water spun at her sides, gleaming with deadly light.

The rain soaked her face, but it only made her look more unhinged.

Twenty minutes later, Paisley and the others made it back to the villa.

The rain was coming down harder now, drumming against the villa's floor-to-ceiling windows.

Inside, every light in the villa was on.

They'd just come in from the car, cleaned up, and changed clothes.

Paisley hadn't even sat down yet when someone started pounding on the door, loud and aggressive, shattering the quiet. Whoever it was sounded furious.
