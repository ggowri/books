Chapter 141

In the dim room, Rachel kept her eyes down to hide the scheming behind them and laughed coldly to herself.

Paisley, everything you have is going to be mine soon enough.

As night fell, the lights of the Rivergate base came on one by one. The daytime noise faded, and a rare calm settled over the place.

Paisley was alone in her spacious, clean single room. It was warm and cozy inside, shut off from all the danger and filth of the apocalypse.

Ever since she'd been reborn, she'd been fighting and running nonstop, always on edge. She hadn't felt this relaxed in a long time.

There was no endless scheming, no nasty provocations, and no need to keep her guard up every second. It was a rare break.

She'd planned to have dinner alone and treat herself to something special.

When the water in the pot came to a boil, she added the age-old sacred fungus she'd picked deep in the forest and the high-level mutant chicken she'd hunted that day. A rich, savory smell rose with the steam and filled the whole room.

The clean, fresh flavor of the fungus mixed with the rich taste of the chicken. It was nothing like the dry, tasteless rations of the apocalypse. It smelled like a real home-cooked meal, the kind she hadn't had in forever.

Right when the stew smelled its best, there was a soft knock at the door.

It was Ashley. She'd stood up for Paisley plenty of times and truly thought of her as a big sister.

Paisley told her to come in and invited her to stay for dinner.

Once one person showed up, more followed. Soon, Spencer and Thomas were knocking on the door too.

Paisley sent them to invite Orion, Elijah, Madison, and Marcus too.

They all hurried over. The second they got near the door, the rich smell hit them, and their faces lit up with excitement.

They knew this energy-packed smell all too well.

The last time they'd eaten the soup Paisley made with the sacred fungus and an energy-packed chicken, every one of them had leveled up, and their abilities had gotten stronger and purer.

Now that they smelled it again, they perked up right away, and all the exhaustion from days of traveling and getting ready for battle disappeared.

The steaming stewpot bubbled away, and pure energy from the fungus drifted up with the steam. The room was warm and full of laughter, and the heavy weight of the apocalypse melted away.

The table was loaded with more food than anyone could have imagined, all of it fresh ingredients that were nearly impossible to find in the apocalypse.

There was juicy high-level mutant chicken, thin-sliced mutant beef, fresh-cut lamb, plump meatballs bursting with juice, shrimp dumplings, blood sausage, and potato noodles.

There was crisp lettuce, kale, Brussels sprouts, button mushrooms, portobellos, sliced potatoes, parsnip slices, green beans, and celery. Every bit of it looked fresh and bright, without a single wilted leaf.

On top of that, there were hard-boiled eggs, sweet corn on the cob, crisp asparagus tips, handmade ravioli, baby potatoes, and egg noodles. With meat and veggies piled high, it was a true feast.

Paisley even pulled a few cases of ice-cold Coke out of her Storage Dimension. The cans were covered in tiny cold drops, and in the apocalypse, they were the ultimate luxury.

"Dig in, everyone," Paisley said, her face softening.

Everyone sat down and cracked open their cans. The tabs popped one after another, and the soda fizzed.

"Cheers!" They clinked their cans together with a bright, happy sound. Laughter filled the room, and for once, life in the apocalypse felt normal.

Ashley smiled sweetly, her eyes crinkling, and raised her Coke. "Thanks for making us dinner, Paisley. This is heaven!"

Thomas let out a soft sigh, clearly moved, and raised his can too. "I never thought we'd get to eat a hot, steaming stew and drink ice-cold Coke in the middle of this brutal apocalypse. It's all thanks to our Pais."

Spencer grinned and lifted his Coke. "This is amazing, Pais, thanks! Any chance you'd make it again tomorrow if I asked?"

Madison smiled warmly and raised her can, thanking her softly. "Thanks to you, Pais, we get to eat this amazing food. I feel great, and I'm not tired at all anymore."

Orion held up his can and gave Paisley a warm, protective look. "Thanks for all your hard work, Pais. We're this happy because you're here!"

Elijah looked calm, but his eyes were warm as he raised his can.
