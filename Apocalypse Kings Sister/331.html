Chapter 331

The afternoon lull, and a perfectly good nap, got shattered by a fist hammering on the door.

Whoever was out there hit hard. The banging echoed through the whole villa, backed by a furious, snarling voice.

"Paisley Lennox! Get out here!"

******

"Rachel's poisoned, in a coma, and you did it, didn't you?!"

"I know you can cure her. You're refusing on purpose!"

"You're going to make it right. Come to the base hospital with me right now and wake her up!"

Inside, the racket brought Ashley Thompson storming over, brows knitting as she yanked the door open.

Reid Lennox stood there, his face a mess of bruises, eyes burning with fury, glaring past her into the house.

Seeing her hadn't cooled him off in the slightest. He barreled past any pretense of manners straight into guilt-tripping.

"Tell Paisley to get out here! I want answers today! Don't think you can just hide in there and play dead — not a chance."

Ashley worshipped her sister and didn't tolerate anyone messing with her. Watching him show up like this, all threats and no sense, her pretty features went ice-cold, every trace of softness gone.

She planted herself in the doorway, blocking him cold. Small as she was, there was nothing soft about the look she gave him.

"Reid, are you done? This is our house, not your personal stage. You don't just show up at someone else's place screaming. Don't you have any manners?"

"Honestly, today's been eye-opening. What does your sister have to do with mine?"

"If you know what's good for you, get out. Don't make me hit you."

Reid's face twisted with rage, his voice dropping to a growl.

"Manners? Paisley did this to Rachel on purpose, and she's hiding in there like a coward! I have every right to demand justice!"

"Paisley, you spineless coward, get out here!"

"Justice?"

Ashley let out a short, sharp laugh, her eyes like knives, every word landing like a hammer.

"The mission was brutal — the whole team fought for their lives. Your sister and Chase were poisoned from the start and rode it out unconscious while everyone else bled. How is that my sister's fault?"

"If she won't wake up, blame her bad luck. Don't you dare dump this on my sister."

Reid's face flushed red with humiliated fury.

"Everyone else made it out fine. Rachel's the only one who hasn't woken up. I found out the truth — Paisley refuses to save her. If it's not her fault, whose is it? She has the power to help and she's letting her die. What kind of monster does that?"

Ashley's gaze turned sharp as glass.

"You keep demanding compensation, demanding she save your sister — who do you think you are? What gives you the right to order my sister around?"

That left him speechless, but he refused to budge.

"I'm not wasting my breath on some shrew. Get Paisley out here. I want to face her myself!"

He stuck his chin out and reached for the old guilt trip, turning it into a threat.

"The Lennoxes raised her. How can she be this cold, letting someone die right in front of her?!"

"If she won't help, I'll make sure the whole base hears about it! Everyone's going to know exactly how selfish and ungrateful she really is!"

That twisted, upside-down threat was the last straw for Ashley.

She was sweet and soft most days, but nothing lit her temper like someone going after her sister. Without a flicker of hesitation, she drove her foot into him.

A dull thud rang out.

Reid flew backward several yards and slammed onto the stone path outside, his back screaming in pain, the bruises on his face looking even uglier now.

Ashley stepped out after him, looking down with nothing but cold contempt in her eyes.

"You think I was being polite?"

"You know exactly how many rotten things your family did to my sister behind closed doors."

"If your family hadn't dragged her off as a child, she'd have grown up pampered and carefree with us. Instead she spent years enduring your cold shoulders and your abuse!"

"And you still have the nerve to bring up some joke of a debt for raising her? Shameless."

She leaned down slightly, her eyes sharp as blades, every word a warning.

"If you know what's good for you, get out of here now, and don't ever come bother my sister again.
