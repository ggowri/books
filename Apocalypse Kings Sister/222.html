By then, it was already 7 p.m.

The sky over the apocalypse was dark and heavy. It wasn't fully black yet, but it was already gloomy.

The wild road was empty and run-down, with no streetlights. The only light came from the faint yellow headlights of the team's two SUVs.

The car that had been cruising along suddenly braked hard, its wheels locking, and the whole car lurched.

Several huge rocks sat right in the middle of the road, blocking the way forward.

Everyone's heart sank. Before anyone could figure out what was going on, a dense, harsh chorus of dragging feet and low, raspy growls suddenly rose from the weeds and crumbling ruins all around them.

A second later, a huge black mass of mutant zombies came pouring out from every direction. There were so many they made everyone's skin crawl, and they had the SUVs surrounded.

"It's a zombie horde! There are so many! I can't even see the end of it!"

The team members in the car went white, and a wave of terror swept over them.

Nobody hesitated. They threw the doors open and jumped out, got ready to fight, and formed a defensive line right away.

Ice, fire, lightning, and speed powers burst out all at once, and flashes of every color exploded across the dark wasteland as they fought with everything they had to cut down the incoming zombies.

But there were way too many zombies. They kept coming like a tide, one wave after another, and as soon as one batch fell, another surged in. There was no way to kill them all.

They were all low-level ordinary zombies, so one alone wasn't much of a threat, but there were just too many of them.

Worst of all, after a whole day of fighting for their lives underground against high-level mutants, everyone was worn out to the bone. They barely had any power left.

There was no winning a never-ending fight like this. Even if they killed until their arms gave out, they couldn't outlast the endless horde, and the moment their powers ran dry, they'd all die.

A sense of despair quietly settled over the whole team.

In the middle of the chaos and danger, only Paisley stayed calm and cool.

She gripped the icy, gleaming Frostbloom Sword and moved through the horde with ease.

Every flash of her blade took one out, and every strike pierced a zombie's crystal core, quick and clean.

The sweet little white cat suddenly turned into a big white cat and followed at her side, working perfectly with her.

Every now and then, it leaped into the air and tore apart charging zombies with its razor-sharp claws, clearing threats out of Paisley's way. Paisley and the cat moved in sync, and nothing could stop them.

Wherever they went, zombies dropped in heaps.

Paisley watched the endless zombie horde keep coming and her teammates grow weaker, their powers drained and their movements obviously slowing. Her expression grew darker and more serious.

Orion stood firmly at her side, on edge the whole time. Even though it was draining him fast, he never stopped watching out for her.

He cut down the zombies closing in while comforting her quietly. "Paisley, don't be scared. I'm here, and I won't let anything hurt you."

Not far away, Elijah looked extremely grim. He cut down the zombies pressing in as he hurried over to the two of them, his voice heavy.

"This is worse than we thought. If we keep burning power like this, we'll all run dry sooner or later, and we'll probably die here.

"This isn't a normal zombie horde, either. It's like someone's controlling them..."

Paisley looked out at the dark, empty wilderness in the distance, her eyes cutting through wave after wave of zombies, and spoke in a cool, certain voice. "I think something's off too. Someone's definitely controlling these zombies, and if we find whoever's behind this and kill them, we'll be out of this mess."

After seeing the old man underground control the mutants with his twisted tricks, she understood right away.

Elijah and Orion didn't hesitate at all. They trusted her judgment without question.

Orion kept fighting hard as he spoke up urgently. "But it's way too dark, and the area's huge. We can't sense where whoever's behind this is, so we have no way to find them."

Paisley looked around and made a quick decision. "You two lead the team and protect the others. Keep things under control, and I'll go find whoever's behind this."

Orion's heart clenched, worried about her heading into danger alone, and he spoke up right away. "I'll come with you. I can protect you!"

Paisley reached out and gently stroked the little white cat's soft fur. "Orion, you don't have to. Don't worry, with this little guy watching my back, nothing's going to happen to me."

With that, she suddenly unleashed her massive, pure psychic power, trying to take control of the zombies herself. But a second later, she sensed something was wrong.
