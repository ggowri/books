Chapter 222

With that, the two of them pulled close again, sinking back into their indulgence.

The clock had already crept past seven in the evening.

The sky hung low and dark over the ruined world, not fully black yet, but heavy with shadow.

The road stretched out abandoned and overgrown, no streetlights anywhere, just two weak headlight beams cutting through the gloom from the squad's vehicles.

The steady drive came to a sudden stop, tires skidding, the whole frame of the car shuddering.

A pile of massive boulders sat dead center in the road, blocking the way forward.

Before anyone could get out to check the scene, a chorus of dragging footsteps and ragged snarls erupted from the overgrown brush and crumbling ruins around them.

The next second, a wall of mutated undead came pouring out from every direction, thick and endless, enough to turn stomachs, swarming the SUV completely.

"It's a horde! There's so many! I can't even see where it ends!"

Everyone inside went pale, dread flooding through them fast.

No one hesitated. They shoved the doors open and leapt out, snapping into formation on instinct.

Ice, fire, lightning, speed, every Affinity burst to life, bright flashes cutting through the dim wasteland as they fought to clear a path through the horde.

But the numbers were staggering, wave after wave rolling in without end, each fallen body replaced by another, no end in sight.

These were low-tier undead, barely a threat one at a time, but sheer volume made up for it.

Worse, after a full day of brutal fighting underground, battling high-tier mutants and burning through every reserve of strength, everyone was running close to empty.

A war of attrition like this had no clean answer. Even killing without mercy couldn't outpace an endless tide. Once their Affinities ran dry, that would be the end of all of them.

A quiet, creeping despair settled over the group.

In the chaos, only Paisley stayed composed and clear-headed.

She moved through the horde with the Frostbloom Sword in hand, cutting through without a single wasted step.

Each swing dropped one, every strike finding the crystal core dead-on, clean and precise.

The little white cat shifted into its larger form beside her, matching her movements perfectly.

It leapt in and out of the fight, claws tearing through anything that got close, clearing a path, the two of them working together like they'd done it a hundred times.

Everywhere they moved, bodies dropped in heaps.

Watching the endless tide roll on, watching her teammates slow and flag as their reserves ran dry, Paisley's expression turned grave.

Orion stood solid at her right, every nerve on edge, holding her left side without fail, never once letting his guard down despite the toll it was taking on him.

He cut down anything that got close, murmuring low as he fought.

"Paisley, don't be scared. Orion's here. Nothing's touching you."

Not far off, Elijah fought his way through the crowd with grim focus, closing the distance to the two of them, voice low and heavy.

"This is worse than we thought. If this drags on, everyone's Affinity is going to run dry, and we'll probably die right here."

"This isn't a normal horde either. It's like something's controlling them."

Paisley looked out across the dark, empty wasteland, her gaze cutting straight through the swarm, voice steady and cold.

"I think so too. Someone's pulling the strings on these zombies. Find whoever's behind it, kill them, and this ends."

Having seen the old man control the lab's mutants firsthand, she understood immediately.

Neither Elijah nor Orion hesitated. They trusted her judgment completely.

Orion kept fighting, voice urgent as he spoke.

"It's pitch dark and this stretches for miles. We can't sense whoever's behind this. We have no way to track them."

Paisley scanned the field and made her decision fast.

"You two hold the line, protect the others, keep the formation steady. I'll find whoever's controlling this."

Orion's chest tightened at the thought of her going alone.

"I'll come with you. I can protect you!"

Paisley reached out and stroked the little white cat's fur.

"You don't need to. I've got backup. I won't get hurt."

The moment she said it, she unleashed the full weight of her psychic power, trying to seize control of the horde. That was when she felt something off.
