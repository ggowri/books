Chapter 422

"Mom, you don't need to be afraid."

Orion's eyes were icy, his voice steady. "I'm much stronger now. I'll protect this family. Anyone who dares hurt them, I won't let go."

Ashley's eyes were sharp. "Mom, I'm really strong now! If they're stupid enough to come to our door and bully us, I'll break their legs.

I'm not weak anymore."

"Right! We can protect this family too! Nobody gets to hurt it."

Thomas and Spencer agreed, their eyes wary.

It was the apocalypse, but they loved this home.

It was warm.

Paisley said nothing the whole time.

She looked at the food in her bowl, calm and cold, her eyes heavy.

Brenda out, Grant awake, the Quinns plotting, the Lennoxes watching, undercurrents everywhere.

In the apocalypse, people and power struggles were scarier than zombies.

Tomorrow's mission would be dangerous. They had to be even more careful.

Just then, the doorbell rang.

It cut right through the quiet of the villa.

Ashley frowned and groaned. "They ruined dinner last night, and the one night we finally get to eat in peace, someone shows up again? Who is it this time?"

Everyone looked toward the gate, faces darkening.

Gerald stood and opened the door. A middle-aged couple stood outside.

They were well dressed and looked mild-mannered. They were Paisley's adoptive parents, Gordon Lennox and Helen Lennox.

They stood outside with awkward smiles, but calculation and self-interest sat in their eyes. They had obviously come with a plan.

Gerald saw them, and a flash of dislike and ice crossed his face.

He had looked into how these two had treated Paisley all these years. He knew every detail.

Thinking of what his daughter had suffered, he wanted to tear them apart.

What right did they have to show up at this door? It was disgusting.

Before he could say anything, Helen pushed past him into the yard as if it were her own house, with no manners and no humility.

Her eyes went straight to Paisley at the table. A thick, fake tenderness spread across her face, and she hurried over, her voice syrupy and every word weighted with guilt.

"Paisley, honey, I finally found you!"

"We're so sorry for all these years. We owe you so much, and we made you suffer. Please don't be angry with us. Family doesn't hold grudges. Let's sit down and talk it all out, okay?"

"You don't know how worried we were. Your brothers and Rachel all came back, and you were the only one missing. Thank goodness you're safe."

******

The sweet words made her skin crawl. She wanted to cancel out all those years of coldness and cruelty with a few light apologies, and use a hollow debt of upbringing to tie down the powerful Paisley.

Gordon followed her in, wearing a kindly father's face.

"Paisley, your mother and I have thought about you all along.

Even if you've found your birth parents, in our hearts you'll always be a Lennox, the daughter we raised."

"We came tonight to see how you're doing.

If you're willing, we'd like to take you back home and make up for what we owed you. We want to finally be real parents."

Every word was soft, and every word was a chain.

They didn't mention the years of cold shoulders, neglect, and cruelty. They didn't mention her loneliness or the countless nights nobody stood up for her. They only talked about "upbringing" and "family," hoping she'd remember what they had done for her.

They were sure Paisley was soft-hearted and desperate for their care and love. If they just lowered themselves and said a few kind words, she would take them back.

At the table, Paisley slowly looked up.

Her clear, calm eyes rested on their fake faces. There was no ripple in them, only a cold, dead indifference.

She was silent for a moment, then her lips parted, and her cool voice came out flat and sharp, laying out years of buried hurt.
