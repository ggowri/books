Chapter 49

But Paisley wasn't in a hurry. Cross-dimensional trading was a slow and steady game. She had two traders to work with now, so the points would keep trickling in.

Just then, Trovie spoke up again. [Master, the Beast Realm trader keeps asking to connect! She's been waiting for you for ages!]

Paisley perked up. [Connect me now.]

A second later, a different screen popped up, showing that wild, rugged world.

On the other side was a cave again. The Matriarch stood there, fit and fierce-looking, wrapped in thick animal hides. She was still shivering in the bitter cold, her face pale from the chill.

The second she saw Paisley, her face lit up with excitement.

She pointed at the lighters, the padded winter clothes, and the fine salt on the listing. [These! I need tons of these, as many as you can give me!]

From the way she was dressed, the Beast Realm was clearly stuck in a brutal winter. Fire, warm clothes, and salt were exactly what they needed to survive.

Then, the Matriarch stepped aside to show off a huge pile of trade goods behind her.

There was a whole crate of brightly colored gemstones, glittering like crazy. On top of that, she had five each of cows, sheep, pigs, and chickens, all healthy and penned up.

The Matriarch's voice was firm. [All of this, in exchange for those!]

Paisley took one look at the gemstones and the livestock and grinned. [Deal! Let's do it right now!]

Her dimension had grown a lot since the upgrade. The empty land outside the villa would be perfect for a little farm, and she could butcher the animals whenever she needed meat.

Paisley gave the order. [Trovie, pack one crate each of lighters, thick winter clothes, and fine salt, and put them in the trading slot.]

Trovie got right on it, and the crates popped into the trading slot one after another.

Paisley made a mental note to stock up on more everyday stuff.

A chime rang out. [Trade complete!]

On the other side, the Matriarch hugged the winter clothes, salt, and lighters, beaming with joy. She kept thanking Paisley over and over.

With these supplies, a lot fewer of her people would die this winter.

Paisley penned her new livestock on the open ground outside the villa.

Since she'd made Level Two as a trader, her spirit water had grown from a single bowl to a whole bucket.

She filled a big bowl to the brim and gulped it down. All her exhaustion melted away in an instant.

This time, though, nothing new awakened. Apparently, spirit water only triggered an awakening the first time someone drank it.

But does it work the same way for everyone?

If she got the chance, she'd give some to Thomas and Spencer and see if it helped them awaken abilities too.

Her Plant Affinity had been stuck at mid Level Three. Luckily, she had the crystal core from the Level Three Venom-Devouring Pothos she'd killed today, plus a crate of energy-packed gemstones. That was exactly what she needed to level up.

Paisley sat cross-legged on the couch in her dimension's villa and gripped the dark green Level Three crystal core.

Pure, powerful life force poured in through her palm in an endless stream. The plant energy was gentle but relentless, flooding every inch of her body and strengthening her from the inside out.

The gemstones beside her gave off their own energy, and it drifted into her body in thin, steady wisps.

Soon, a thick swirl of power had gathered around her.

Late Level Three! She smashed through the wall and kept on climbing!

A moment later, something inside her gave a faint crack as the last barrier shattered. Paisley's Plant Affinity rose steadily and landed right at early Level Four!

The plant energy around her grew deeper and stronger, and her command over every green thing felt heavier and more absolute. She'd leveled up again.

Paisley slowly opened her eyes. They flashed green, then faded back to normal.

The higher an ability's level, the harder it was to climb, and the difficulty multiplied every time.

She'd used a Level Three Venom-Devouring Pothos core and a whole crate of rare gemstones, two top-tier resources. Even so, they'd barely pushed her from mid Level Three to early Level Four.

Still, on only the fourth day of the apocalypse, that kind of power was insane.

Right now, the strongest mutant zombies and creatures maxed out at Level Three. With a Level Four ability, Paisley was probably the strongest thing out there.
