Chapter 477

God, why is the world so unfair? Why is she this ridiculously strong?

Only Elijah kept his eyes fixed on her, awe and love deepening with every second.

How lucky I am to watch her break through every wall and shine like this.

She's the girl I've held in my heart, and loved in silence, all this time.

A hot wave of feeling swelled in his chest, but his head stayed cool.

This is no time to stand around daydreaming. We're stuck, enemies are watching, danger is everywhere. The most important thing is to get across the river and back to the base.

He put away every cold edge, looked across at the blockers, and spoke in a clear, sure voice.

"Everyone, follow me across."

When Paisley had cast Deep Freeze, she had frozen the river and also joined the broken ends of the bridge with a layer of ice, welding the collapsed pieces back together into a road.

The thousand-yard bridge that had been deadly broken now stretched across the river as a flat, wide, clear ice bridge, thick and sturdy. It carried real weight and joined both banks, enough for everyone to cross.

Paisley made a thought, and eight pairs of brand-new ice skates appeared in a neat row in front of everyone.

"Put these on."

A playful little smile crossed her eyes. "We'll cross faster this way."

Everyone blinked, then lit up.

Ashley grabbed a pair first, her eyes bright, a quick smile on her lips. "Paisley thinks of everything! Since the apocalypse started, it's all been fighting and running. I haven't skated in forever, or relaxed like this."

She dropped to one knee and swapped her boots like she'd done it a thousand times.

The tension that had lasted so long eased a little. Orion, Thomas, Spencer, Mason, and Madison came forward and changed too.

Elijah picked up a pair, his fingers running over the cool, clean skate. A faint curve tugged at the corner of his usually cool mouth.

Everyone knew her as decisive, deadly, and calm. Only he kept seeing the playful, mischievous girl behind that edge.

While everyone else was tense, she stayed calm, broke the siege, and came up with a clever, fast way to cross.

Warmth rose in his chest. He murmured softly, with a tenderness all his own.

"Only she would come up with something like this."

He bent to lace up, eyes still locked on her slim, straight figure, love and admiration clear in his gaze.

On the far side of the broken bridge,

Chloe watched Falcon Squad calmly change skates and line up, looking at the wide ice bridge joining both banks. Her face went white with panic, and a terrible feeling swelled inside her. She yanked at Grant's sleeve.

"What do we do? They're about to skate over! If we don't stop them they'll be here!"

"Paisley's so sneaky. She froze the river and built a road too! Think of something. Break the bridge! If they get here, we're not a match for them!"

A moment ago she'd been drunk on revenge, sure Paisley and her group would be stuck over there until the mission failed. All her plans had fallen apart in a flash.

Grant's face was dark and furious, murder in his eyes, the earlier amusement gone.

He stared at the thousand-yard ice bridge and at Paisley lacing up her skates, wariness growing.

But he'd set this ambush up carefully. He had more than twenty strong core members with him and the high ground on this bank. He wasn't about to give up and let them cross safe.

He stepped forward and barked, harsh and merciless.

"All Fire users, step out!"

A few Fire users, flames curling around them, restless, came forward fast.

"All of you, burn the whole ice bridge. Full power! Melt the ice, fast!"

Grant's eyes were vicious, his tone certain.

"Ice fears fire!

I don't believe this ice can survive several of you burning it nonstop!
