Chapter 378

"Mom, now that Uncle's family has their biological daughter back, shouldn't we go over and congratulate them?"

"Whatever's happened between us, we're still family."

Brenda froze on the spot at that, staring at her daughter in open shock, as if she'd never met her before.

"Chloe, honey, are you alright?"

She frowned, her voice full of disbelief.

"Are you seriously telling me to go suck up to your uncle's family? Absolutely not! Never!"

The two families had been on bad terms for years, ever since an old falling-out over inheritance and family assets. They hadn't spoken in ages.

And Chloe, who'd always carried herself with pride as a flagship squad member and the base's golden girl, had always looked down on her uncle's ordinary, unremarkable family, barely sparing them a glance, repeatedly declaring she'd never associate with them.

"That's not what you used to say."

Brenda murmured, utterly baffled.

Beside her, Dennis's own worried expression sharpened as he studied his daughter closely.

He knew her too well, proud, vain, fiercely ambitious. She'd never casually say something so humble, so deferential. This sudden change in attitude was no impulse. She had to have thought it through completely.

Facing her parents' stunned looks, Chloe showed no sign of panic at all. She laid out her reasoning calmly, every word grounded and clear-eyed.

"That was then. This is now."

"Before, we had the Quinn family behind us, the Scarlet Squad backing us up, we never had to watch our step, never had to suck up to anyone."

"But now the Scarlet Squad's completely gone. My backing is gone."

She glanced up, a calculating glint in her eyes.

"I've already thought this through. We can't put all our eggs in the Quinn family's basket. What if they fail?"

Brenda stared at her, dazed.

"So what's your plan? The two families are already on bad terms. I really don't want to go crawling to them."

"Uncle's family just found their biological daughter, and she's very useful."

"Uncle's biological daughter is Paisley Lennox."

"And now that Orion's back too, he's closest with Elijah Blackwood."

Chloe's tone stayed flat, though ambition burned underneath every word.

"Getting on good terms with Uncle's family is pure upside, no downside."

Her words sent a jolt through both her parents, and realization dawned on them at once.

A satisfied, confident smile curled at the corner of Chloe's mouth as she kept breaking down the logic, her mind already clicking through the calculations.

"What's Paisley's standing right now? Number one of the new generation at the base, the backbone of Falcon Squad, the base's brightest rising star, every faction wants to court her."

"And I've found a new target: Falcon Squad's captain, Elijah Blackwood."

A confident gleam flashed through her eyes, full of self-assurance.

"With my looks, my figure, and my rare Level Five Healing Affinity, if I'm willing to lower myself a little, play the long game patiently, given the chance, I can land him."

As far as she was concerned, Grant, crippled, a minor branch of the Quinn family, had already lost all his value.

Brenda asked, worried.

"What about Grant? You're just going to leave him?"

Chloe's eyes went cold, her voice merciless.

"Elijah Blackwood is a height Grant could never have reached in his life. And now he's completely done for anyway."

"How could he ever compare to Elijah? Elijah Blackwood is the legitimate heir to the Blackwood family, young, accomplished, powerful beyond measure. Marrying into that family means real access to the top, that's our actual way out."

The words were chillingly rational, laying bare her ruthless pragmatism in full.

Brenda still couldn't quite bring herself to do it, hesitating.

"But we... we already burned that bridge with your uncle's family. Going crawling back now, after everything, it's just so embarrassing."

"Does pride pay the bills?"

Chloe looked up, her gaze sharp and unyielding as she locked eyes with her mother, driving home the harshest truth.

"Mom, do you still want to keep living in this standalone villa?"

"If we keep clinging to our so-called dignity and refuse to bend, there's a good chance that by next month, we'll lose this house and get moved into some five-hundred-square-foot unit at the base."

"Cramped, poorly secured, worlds away from the comfortable life we have now."

She spelled it out clearly, piling on the pressure.

"And when that happens, you won't get to stay home resting all day with food and clothes handed to you. You'll have to go work at the base, scraping together meager survival supplies, grinding through every single day."
