Chapter 87

An ability that could crush every powerhouse in the apocalypse, and she made it sound like something she could do with her eyes closed.

Both of them quietly took a deep breath, trying to calm the storm raging inside them. They didn't feel like talking anymore. They just needed a minute.

Before either of them could recover, Paisley kept going.

"If we fight, I can take out that Level Four leader by myself, no problem. He's the toughest one.

"The little white cat can keep the four Level Three ability users busy and take them down.

"You guys can clean up the rest, the Level Two and Level One ability users. Sound good?"

Elijah and Orion had only just pulled themselves together, but her words hit them with a whole new wave of shock.

A Level Four ability user was one of the strongest fighters in the whole area. An ordinary team could throw everything they had at one and still lose, and one tiny mistake could get them all killed.

But Paisley made taking down a Level Four powerhouse sound like the easiest thing in the world.

On top of that, her cat could handle four Level Three ability users at once.

She'd taken the toughest opponents for herself and her cat.

The Level Two and Level One ability users she'd left for them really would be easy to handle.

They stared at the calm young woman in front of them, dazed all over again.

Only then did they truly get it. Paisley was way stronger than they'd ever imagined, a hundred, even a thousand times stronger.

What kind of freak of nature is my sister?

Orion had wanted to protect her, but she was the one protecting him.

He swallowed hard, his eyes locked on her calm profile. A tangle of complicated feelings churned inside him.

As the eldest son of the Thompson family, he'd been raised with strict discipline. He'd carried the whole family's future and honor on his shoulders, and he was used to being strong, standing on his own, and pushing through no matter how heavy the load got.

After the apocalypse hit, he'd stayed wound tight. He'd pushed himself to get stronger fast and sworn to find his sister and protect his whole family.

He'd wanted to shield them from every storm, so they'd never have to face how brutal and dangerous the apocalypse really was.

But every single time they'd landed in danger along the way, he hadn't been the one protecting her.

Instead, it had been Paisley, who looked so cool and delicate, quietly protecting him and everyone behind him, over and over again.

The strength he'd been so proud of was nothing compared to hers. Now, the idea that he'd wanted to keep her safe seemed almost childish.

He felt a bittersweet rush of emotion, mixed with something helpless and awkward.

Being protected by the very person he'd wanted to protect was something he couldn't get used to yet, but it still touched him deeply.

What on earth was this long-lost sister of his?

Next to him, Elijah was even more shaken. He was usually so calm and steady, but right now, he was completely thrown.

As the only heir of the Blackwood family, he'd been at the very top of the food chain since he was a kid. He was decisive, ruthless, and always the first to act, and he was used to standing in front of everyone alone and taking on every danger himself.

No matter how dangerous things got, Elijah had always been the first to charge in. He'd never been weak, and he'd never needed anyone to protect him.

But ever since he'd met Paisley, all of that had changed.

She kept protecting him, whether she meant to or not.

He had to admit it. She was stronger than him.

Shock, admiration, and a feeling he couldn't put into words twisted together inside him.

For the first time, he truly understood that even he could have weak moments, and that being protected and having someone to fall back on felt amazing!

I'm going to get stronger, and I'm going to protect her!

The two of them traded a look and smiled, both of them already making plans.

Elijah's eyes softened as he looked at Paisley. "Okay, we'll do it your way. Thanks for this."

Then, he turned to Orion. "Go tell the rest of the team the plan, and make sure everyone's ready to fight at any second.

"Pais and I will go have a little chat with those guys first."

Orion nodded.

But something felt off.

When had Elijah and Paisley gotten so close?

Pais? Since when does he get to call her that?

He was probably overthinking it.

They'd grown up together, so nobody knew better than Orion that Elijah had never shown any interest in women. Not once, his whole life.

Still, Orion wasn't entirely at ease. "Take good care of my sister."

With that, he turned and walked away.
