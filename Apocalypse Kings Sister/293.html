Chapter 293

Paisley waved a hand, and a wave of cash lifted into the air. She precisely pulled out a billion in currency and sent it through the trade channel directly.

This is a one-billion deposit.

The rest depends on how much you manage to gather. The more supplies, the better.

On the other side of the screen, the woman stared at the mountain of cash that had materialized on her desk out of thin air, completely frozen, pupils blown wide. She'd never seen a trader this generous before.

A full billion in cash. No small number.

Her hands trembled slightly, and a wide, genuine, eager smile spread across her face.

No problem! It's an honor to work with you! I'll use every connection and resource I have over the next three days to get you the fullest, most complete haul of supplies possible! I won't let you down!

Settling her excitement, the woman couldn't resist a question, her voice careful and curious.

Can I ask you something? Is the apocalypse really full of zombies out there? Is it as dangerous as it sounds?

Paisley gave a small nod, a faint, cool shadow crossing her eyes, her voice calm and even.

Yes. Zombies everywhere, mutant beasts roaming freely, no order left at all. Danger around every corner.

She didn't embellish it. A few plain words were enough to make the woman on the other end shiver, truly grasping the brutal cold of that world for the first time.

She felt a deep rush of gratitude for the peaceful era she lived in, and an even stronger appreciation for this rare trading opportunity, quickly reassuring Paisley.

I understand! I'll do everything I can to prepare the supplies! Fresh produce, frozen meat, grain and oil, everything for daily life, I'll stock up in bulk on all of it, ready in three days!

******

Paisley answered simply.

Good.

With the deal settled, she skipped the small talk and cut the connection.

A moment later, Trovie's chime rang again.

Host! An interstellar trader is requesting a connection, hoping to work with you again!

A flicker of interest crossed Paisley's eyes, and she gave the order briskly.

Connect.

The light screen unfolded again, images shifting across time and space, and on the other end appeared that same stunning interstellar female trader, poised and elegant.

Dressed in luxurious interstellar silks, her features striking and radiant, carrying the refined authority of a high-tier civilization, her eyes were full of barely contained excitement and delight.

It's been a while!

Paisley's expression stayed composed and warm.

It has.

The trader's tone turned eager and sincere as she laid out her request.

The produce, cooked goods, and crop seeds you sold me before have been a massive hit across our interstellar territory! Completely pure, completely natural, and it turns out eating them helps suppress psychic volatility too. Even the Federation's nobility are fighting over it! Extremely popular right now!

Trading with you helped me secure a position as the Imperial Merchant. My standing's risen enormously. Now I need even more supplies. Produce, fruit, grain, seeds, I'll take everything you've got.

Paisley nodded.

We can keep working together. I need large quantities of gold and energy crystals. Whatever you bring, I'll match it. I hope you can gather as much as possible.

No problem at all!

The trader turned and waved a hand, and her personal storage space opened behind her.

Brilliant golden light poured out, carrying pure, concentrated energy, and two hundred twenty thousand pounds of solid gold rose into a towering mountain, beside an equally massive pile of high-tier energy crystals.

Here's two hundred twenty thousand pounds of pure gold and two hundred twenty thousand pounds of high-tier energy crystals, this round's trade offer.

Paisley looked over the generous offer, well pleased.

If this had happened before, she wouldn't have dared take on a deal this large.

But things were different now. She'd activated the modern-world trade channel, and with her expanded storage, she had farmland to spare, growing produce and grain on her own without any risk of running out.

Deal.

The words had barely left her when, with a thought, five crates of fresh, vibrant seasonal vegetables, five crates of plump, bursting-sweet fruit, and five crates of top-quality grain appeared neatly stacked before her.

To secure the long-term partnership, she generously threw in a large bag of premium seeds, covering grain, vegetables, and fruit alike.

All the supplies transferred instantly through the trade channel to the interstellar trader.
