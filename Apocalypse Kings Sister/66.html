Chapter 66

It was bad enough that Paisley never lifted a finger and just ate whatever was put in front of her.

Now, she'd gone and picked up a cat, too.

When Kelly thought about all the time she'd spent washing vegetables and cooking, her resentment finally boiled over.

She tightened her grip on her fork and deliberately slowed down her eating. She looked openly mocking and hostile, and when she finally spoke, her voice was sour and snide.

"Some people have zero shame. They don't contribute a damn thing, they eat whatever's handed to them without feeling guilty at all, and now they've even brought home a cat to freeload off the rest of us."

The jab was obvious. The whole table went quiet, and the air turned tense.

Mason honestly wanted to give Kelly a thumbs-up for her guts.

Does she seriously think that's a normal cat? It's a Level Four Ice Affinity mutant!

If that thing got cranky, it could send her six feet under. And she actually had the nerve to mess with it?

Kelly didn't seem to notice the weird looks on everyone's faces. She kept going after Paisley.

"There are four girls on this team. Ashley, Madison, and I take turns cooking, and you just sit there and do nothing.

"Cooking's pretty basic. You can cook, can't you?

"Everybody else pulls their weight, so why do you get to sit back and reap the rewards? You can't keep freeloading forever."

Then, she turned to Elijah, who sat calmly beside her. Hope and a hidden crush flickered across her face as she demanded what she called fairness.

"Elijah, say something. Everyone on this team is supposed to be equal, and everyone should pitch in. Why does she get special treatment?"

She was sure that Elijah, who was always fair and never played favorites, would back her up.

But a second later, his answer crushed that hope completely.

Elijah looked up, calm and cold, and sided with Paisley without a second thought.

"That's my call. Paisley has her own jobs, and she's worth a lot to this team. From now on, the three of you will keep taking turns with small stuff like cooking."

His tone turned hard. "If you don't like how I run things, you're free to pack your stuff and leave."

His blunt, icy words shut Kelly up on the spot, and the color drained from her face.

Orion's face darkened too. He was seriously pissed off.

This wasn't the first time Kelly had picked on Paisley. Up until now, he'd let it slide because they'd been friends for so long.

But she just wouldn't stop nitpicking and going after Paisley. This time, she'd crossed the line.

His eyes went cold as he warned her.

"Kelly, every other time you gave Paisley a hard time, I let it go because we go way back.

"But this is the last time. If it happens again, don't expect me to go easy on you. I'm not always going to be this nice.

"Pais is off-limits. I won't let anyone bully her!"

Kelly opened her mouth, then froze in her seat. Her fingers trembled, and her lips had gone white. She felt stunned, hurt, and cold inside.

All she'd wanted was a little fairness, and instead, every single one of them had taken Paisley's side.

The captain and his second-in-command were both playing favorites. Was there even any point in staying?

Isn't Elijah supposed to be fair to everyone? Or is Paisley his one exception?

The feelings she'd hidden for years turned bitter and resentful.

How could Elijah not know how she felt about him?

And yet he'd defended Paisley without a second's hesitation, without sparing her feelings at all. He kept hurting her, over and over.

Hurt, jealousy, and resentment churned inside her until she was almost drowning in them.

Ever since Paisley had shown up, everything had changed. Everyone's attention and affection had landed on her and her alone.

Why? What made Paisley so special that she got everything without lifting a finger, and everyone fell for her so easily?

Kelly lowered her eyes, clenched her fists, and silently gritted her teeth.

Fine. I can wait.

Sooner or later, she'd find the right moment to get rid of Paisley and win Elijah back.

Paisley didn't say a single word the whole time.

She fed the kitten first, then started eating herself. She didn't even bother defending herself.

Dinner ended soon after.
