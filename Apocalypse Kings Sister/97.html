Chapter 97

Elijah and Orion said it at the exact same time. "No."

Who the hell did this guy think he was, latching onto Pais like that?

Paisley gave them both a confused look.

That's weird.

As far as she remembered, later in the apocalypse, the three of them had been friends.

Part of the reason Marcus had eventually created the cure was that Elijah and Orion had backed him all the way.

But right now, it looked like they had some kind of beef with him.

I need to explain this to them soon. This guy can't die.

Paisley pushed those thoughts aside and told him the truth. "We're heading to Aetheris. Our next stop is Rivergate."

Marcus's tired eyes lit up, and he broke into a huge smile.

"What a coincidence! I was heading to Aetheris too, to find my family after we got separated. Would it be okay if I traveled with you?"

Paisley turned to Elijah, whose face was still ice-cold, and Orion, who clearly hated the idea.

"Let him come with us. It's way too dangerous out there, and if he's on his own, he's dead for sure. He'll never make it to Aetheris."

Elijah's voice was hard and cold. "This team doesn't carry dead weight."

"Exactly," Orion jumped in right away.

"We're not running a charity here. We can't just take in every stray we find.

"Paisley, you already saved him. Do we really have to take him in too? Don't let that sweet, pretty face fool you."

Paisley sighed, not sure whether to laugh or cry.

What is even happening right now?

All she wanted was to protect the future savior of the world, humanity's only hope of ending the apocalypse. How had that turned into her falling for a pretty face?

She really needed to find a chance to explain everything to them.

Paisley tried again. "Can't you make an exception, just this once?"

Marcus quickly jumped in.

"Actually, there's a good reason to keep me around."

He forced his weak body to sit up straight and released a burst of Earth Affinity energy.

"I'm an ability user, a Level Three Earth Affinity. I can hold my own in a fight, and I won't drag the team down.

"I promise, from now on, I'll follow the team's orders, stick to the rules, pull my weight, and never cause trouble."

Elijah and Orion both paused at that. Their hostility faded a little, and they started to actually think it over.

At this point in the apocalypse, a Level Three ability user was pretty solid.

But more importantly, Paisley was dead set on keeping him.

If they said no, they were honestly afraid their little princess would throw a tantrum.

Orion nodded.

"Fine, you can stay. But don't even think about pulling anything. If we find out you've hurt anyone here, you'll die a very painful death."

Marcus promised over and over. "I'd never do anything to hurt any of you."

The whole time he talked, his eyes stayed on Paisley.

Ashley planted herself in front of him, fierce as an angry kitten.

"What are you staring at? Never seen a pretty girl before?"

Paisley couldn't help laughing. She gently patted Ashley's head and stopped her in a soft voice. "Hey, don't be rude."

Then, she smoothly changed the subject. The faint smell of cooking was drifting from the kitchen, and her voice turned gentle.

"Did you make breakfast? It smells amazing, and it looks delicious. Come on, let's go eat."

Just like that, Ashley's fierce act was gone, and she happily followed Paisley to the table.

After a simple, cozy breakfast, everyone got ready and packed up their stuff. Then, the group set off again.

Elijah called Paisley over and pointed at the abandoned gas station nearby, his voice serious.

"How much room do you have left in your Storage Dimension? Can you fit all the diesel and gasoline here?

"We've still got a long way to go, and the cars can't run without fuel. We have to have it."

Paisley looked where he was pointing and saw a bunch of fuel barrels. "No problem. I've got plenty of space left, so it'll all fit.

"Step back."

Elijah took a few steps back.

Paisley walked over and gently laid her hand on the neat rows of fuel barrels. Then, with a single thought, she made her move.

With a series of soft whooshes, dozens of sealed, full barrels of gasoline and diesel vanished into her Storage Dimension.
