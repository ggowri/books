Chapter 95

Elijah stared at Paisley, so curious and surprised that he couldn't hold it in.

"What did you put in this soup? Why did all of our abilities go up a level? And how did it wipe out all the exhaustion from days of traveling and fighting?"

As soon as he finished, the rest of the team looked up too. Every one of them stared at Paisley, dying to know the answer.

With everyone staring at her, Paisley felt a little awkward. She cleared her throat, then quickly went back to her usual calm, composed self.

"When I was clearing out those grapevines this afternoon, I happened to find a sacred fungus.

"I put it in the soup when I made it. Sacred fungus strengthens the body and boosts abilities, so of course drinking the soup made everyone stronger."

Paisley didn't mention the spirit water or the Beast Realm spirit hen. She just gave them the most believable explanation.

Her own ability had gone from early Level Five to mid Level Five too.

In the apocalypse, the higher the level, the thicker the wall to the next one, and every breakthrough was harder than the last. A regular person could spend months without making any progress, so leveling up from one meal was a straight-up miracle.

Next to her, Orion looked at Paisley in shock and delight. He couldn't believe it.

"An age-old sacred fungus? That's a legendary treasure! Paisley, your luck is insane.

"You found something that rare, and you just cooked it into soup for all of us?"

Thomas looked warm and grateful.

"I've heard about it before. An age-old sacred fungus is a priceless treasure. Even a regular person who eats it by accident has a shot at awakening an ability, that's how rare it is.

"We all leveled up today because of Pais. We got seriously lucky."

Spencer was overflowing with gratitude too.

"Thanks, Paisley! My ability actually went up a whole level, and it feels way stronger now. I never knew leveling up could be this easy."

Then, Elijah made a suggestion.

"We all owe Paisley a real thank-you. Starting tomorrow, I think everyone should give her a third of the crystal cores they get as payment."

Paisley was about to say no, but to her surprise, everyone agreed right away.

"I'm in."

"Me too. If it weren't for Pais, I'd probably still be a regular person."

"Same here. I only awakened an ability because of Pais."

"I'm in too. Without Pais, my ability would be stuck at Level Three, tops."

Paisley shook her head. "Honestly, you really don't have to."

Elijah's voice turned serious.

"Don't say no. Compared to everything you've done for us, this is nothing.

"It can't always be you doing everything for us, right?"

In the end, Paisley came up with a number everyone could live with.

"Fine. Then just give me 50 Level Two crystal cores each. Call it payment for tonight's soup."

Of course, nobody argued.

Fifty Level Two crystal cores wouldn't even be enough to go from Level Three to Level Four.

They were still getting the better end of the deal.

They knew Paisley was just going easy on them.

Everyone on the team had leveled up and gotten way stronger, except Kelly. She'd claimed she was worn out from the trip and hidden in her room early to "rest," skipping dinner on purpose.

She thought she was being clever and saving herself some work. She had no idea she'd just thrown away a once-in-a-lifetime chance to change her fate.

Late that night, after everyone else had gone to bed, she finally wandered downstairs to make herself some instant food. But the pots and bowls in the kitchen were already washed. Everyone had eaten every last bit of dinner, soup and all.

The whole team had leveled up and gotten much stronger, but her Water Affinity was still stuck at Level Two.

She used to be somewhere in the middle, but now, she was dead last. She was the only Level Two ability user left on the team, the lowest level and the weakest of them all.

Kelly had no idea how huge that gap would get down the road. All she felt was bitterness and resentment, and she quietly blamed Paisley for everything.

As the night went on, the building went completely quiet, and everyone rested in their own rooms.

Paisley was alone in a clean guest room. She put up a simple soundproof barrier, then sent her mind straight into her Storage Dimension with a single thought.

Inside, the air was thick with clear, warm energy, several times richer than outside.
