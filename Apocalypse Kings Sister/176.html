Chapter 176

The hostages kept choking out their thanks, their voices shaking, overflowing with relief and gratitude at having survived.

Paisley and the others patiently calmed them down and made sure everyone only had minor injuries. But they'd gone days without water.

Thomas immediately gave each of them a bottle of water. It just so happened they'd grabbed bottled water from the mall today.

Once they'd mostly recovered, the group led them out to head back to the base.

Just as they were about to leave, Paisley suddenly stopped. The softness in her eyes vanished, replaced by a cold edge.

Her psychic senses were razor-sharp, and she clearly picked up several people hiding at a street corner in the distance. They were keeping their presence low on purpose, but they'd been tailing her like ghosts the whole time.

It was Rachel's group again. They'd gotten lucky, surviving the Level Five mutant zombie and even managing to track her here.

Paisley didn't feel a thing for them anymore, just indifference.

Without bothering to look back, she quietly spread out her psychic power and locked onto a massive mutant ancient tree near the corner.

With pinpoint control, Paisley gave it an order. With a loud rumble, the mutant ancient tree by the road shook violently. Its thick branches thrashed, and countless hard, dead limbs lashed toward the spot where Rachel's group was hiding with vicious force.

Hidden behind a broken wall, Rachel's group had no time to react.

They'd been holding their breath, focused entirely on Paisley, and never expected the trees around them to suddenly attack.

"Look out!" Reid shouted, frantically throwing up a fire barrier. But the ancient tree's thick, hard branches slammed into the wall of flames with raw Plant Affinity power behind them.

The fire wall shattered, sparks flying, and a snapped branch caught Reid's arm, tearing open a bloody gash. He grunted and stumbled back.

Rachel's face went white with fear, her carefully done makeup smeared with dust, hair whipped loose by the force of the wind.

She frantically summoned her Water Affinity to block it, but her wall of water was as flimsy as paper against the mutant tree's brute strength, and the branches ripped it apart in seconds.

A thumb-thick branch whipped across her forearm. Burning pain spread through her skin, and her arm quickly swelled up red.

She stumbled and fell hard onto the rubble, her expensive jacket torn wide open, and there was nothing left of her usual high-and-mighty rich-girl image.

Kelly screamed and curled up, covering her head, but a branch still caught her across the back, and a bruise quickly appeared.

Owen and Ryan scattered in a panic, but the tree's branches seemed to track them, lashing out from every direction, debris flying everywhere, every escape route sealed off.

Ryan couldn't dodge in time and tripped over a root. He went down hard, covered in dirt, and his palms scraped across jagged rocks, leaving them full of tiny cuts.

Screams and cries of pain rang out as the whole group scrambled to dodge, looking pathetic.

Furious and scared, Rachel clutched her stinging, swollen arm, her face twisted with hatred.

"Damn it! This has to be Paisley's doing. She can actually control mutant trees from a distance!"

She cursed under her breath through gritted teeth. Cold sweat had broken out across her back, and she was covered in dust, a total mess.

Now, she was even more convinced that Paisley had awakened the pendant's Storage Dimension, and that it had given her a Plant Affinity too.

Kelly was shaking all over. "Rachel, this is way too dangerous," she whimpered. "Let's stop messing with her."

Rachel clenched her fists and stared toward the park, refusing to accept it. The space pendant was right there within reach, and she hated the thought of giving up now, but the ancient tree kept attacking them.

Another round of attacks was about to hit.

At the park entrance, Spencer and the others looked toward the commotion in the distance and caught sight of several figures scrambling around.

"Is that Rachel's group? They actually followed us here," Thomas said, frowning.

Paisley glanced into the distance. With a slight flick of her fingers, she bore down with her psychic power again.

The ancient tree swung its trunk hard, slamming into the wall Rachel was hiding behind, cracks spiderwebbing across the surface.

Rachel's heart pounded with fear. She didn't dare linger any longer, dragging her battered group into a stumbling retreat down a nearby alley.

For Paisley, this was just a warning. Next time, she wouldn't let them off so easy.

Paisley gave a faint smile and pulled back her psychic power.

The little willow on her shoulder lazily swayed its leaves, and the little white cat gave a slow yawn.
