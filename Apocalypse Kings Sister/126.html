Chapter 126

"Dr. Whitfield, you've only just recovered. Go have a seat over there and rest. You don't need to keep standing."

Thomas came right after him, holding up a breakfast plate like a shield between them.

"No need to keep following my sister around. Come sit with me."

Spencer was even more blunt. He quietly moved to Paisley's other side, blocking Marcus from getting any closer and making it very clear he wasn't welcome.

They took turns, every word polite on the surface and every one meant to shove him out. Quiet, gentle Marcus was stuck, and he didn't know whether to stand or sit.

Marcus had no idea how he'd offended them. All he could do was stand there awkwardly and give Paisley a hurt look.

Paisley saw it all and couldn't help laughing. She pointed to a seat nearby.

"You're on the team now, so you're free to do your own thing. Sit down and eat. You don't have to follow me around."

After a quick breakfast, everyone packed up their supplies and gear and headed out again. Their destination was Rivergate.

The only way into the city was by water.

When they reached the dock on the river, they immediately spotted several big cruise ships moored in the harbor.

The hulls looked clean and undamaged. The ships must have docked here in a hurry when the apocalypse hit.

A few stray low-level zombies were still wandering around the shore and the ships. Everyone worked together smoothly and cleared the whole dock in no time, so nothing stood between them and boarding.

"I'll take the helm."

Elijah was heir to the Blackwood family. He'd learned all kinds of skills before the apocalypse, and piloting a cruise ship just happened to be one of them.

Everyone was thrilled.

But when Elijah went up to the bridge and tried to start it, over and over, nothing happened. The dashboard stayed dark, and the engine stayed silent.

"This ship's busted. It won't start," Elijah said, frowning. "Everyone split up and figure out what's wrong."

They went looking for the problem, and they found it fast.

The wiring inside the ship had worn out and snapped, and the core engine parts had rusted through. The whole power system was dead.

Madison sounded worried. "So what do we do now?"

After all, who would bother learning how to fix a cruise ship for fun?

Even Elijah had probably never imagined he'd need to.

"I think I can fix it."

Orion stepped forward. As a Metal Affinity user, he could control metal with precision, reshaping parts and reconnecting wires, so fixing machines had always been his thing.

Gold light swirled at his fingertips, and tiny metal particles floated into the air. They traveled along the broken wires, joining and reshaping them.

Rusted parts were pulled out, recast, and replaced, and every broken circuit snapped back into place one by one.

In just over ten minutes, the wiring was working again and every part was back where it belonged. The power system was fully restored.

"There. It should start now."

Everyone stared at him in shock. He could do that?

Orion rubbed the back of his neck, a little embarrassed. "Honestly, I didn't think it would actually work either."

Elijah started it up again right away. The dashboard lit up, and the engine let out a low rumble. The huge ship slowly pulled away from the dock and glided out onto the wide river.

The river was wide and hazy, with mist drifting over the rippling water. It was eerily quiet, and even the wind was dying down.

The ship moved forward at a steady pace.

Everyone stood in the breeze off the water. There were no zombies shrieking and no mutant beasts attacking, just gentle water and open sky. For once, they let their guard down and enjoyed a rare moment of peace.

But just as the ship reached the deepest part of the river, the calm water suddenly started churning like crazy!

Huge sprays of water exploded upward. Black currents swirled beneath the surface, and murky waves piled up one after another.

Then, a cold, savage pressure surged up from the dark depths and swallowed the whole ship. Everyone could barely breathe!

With a deafening boom, the whole river shook violently. Deep under the dark water, a gigantic shadow shot toward them and slammed into the ship's hull!

The huge ship lurched and tipped hard. Its solid alloy hull caved in on impact, and the whole frame let out an awful shriek of twisting, cracking metal.

The deck heaved, and everyone lost their balance and went down hard. Tables, supplies, and backpacks went sliding and tumbling everywhere, and in seconds, the whole place was chaos.
