Chapter 163

None of them could believe it. How was this place so clean? Was there really nothing left?

They all turned to Paisley, suspicious, feeling like something was off.

"Paisley, we've been looking all over for you!" Ashley jogged up and clung to her side, looking around curiously.

"The third and fourth floors are so empty. What did they even sell up here?"

Thomas glanced around at the empty, fancy counters and raised an eyebrow. "The whole floor's spotless, without a single scrap of stock left. Did the owner clear this place out ahead of time?"

None of them had a clue. They never would've guessed that Paisley had just emptied this whole treasure trove all by herself.

Paisley felt a little sheepish and didn't explain.

Spencer didn't linger on the oddity. He stepped forward, looking at her with open admiration.

"Paisley, are you okay?

"We cleared out the stray zombies around the mall and swung by the basement. Our mission target, the Level Five werewolf, was already dead. That was you, wasn't it?"

Paisley nodded calmly. "Yeah, I killed it."

"Paisley, you're amazing!

"That werewolf was vicious, and it'd been holed up here forever. You actually took it down that fast and finished our first mission, just like that!"

Spencer meant every word. Then, he added, "I already put the werewolf's body in my storage token, so we can turn it in at the base for the reward."

Paisley blinked. She'd almost forgotten they still had to turn in the mission and collect the reward, so she nodded. "Good thing you thought of that, Spencer."

Thomas's eyes lit up at that, and he looked up toward the fifth floor. "I remember the directory. The fifth floor has a food court and a movie theater, so let's check it out and see if there's anything good to eat.

"It's almost noon anyway, and I'm starving."

Everyone loved the idea. Their tiredness from the mission vanished, and they couldn't wait.

"Come on, let's go up!"

The group headed up the stairs to the fifth floor, the little white cat trotting along at their feet on its short little legs.

The moment they reached the fifth floor, it felt warm and lively, nothing like the cold, deadly floors below.

The whole floor still looked just like it had before the apocalypse. On one side was a food court packed with stands, including a milkshake bar, a coffee shop, a kettle corn stand, a hot dog roller, and a dessert counter, and all the equipment was clean and rust-free.

Behind the counters, the prepped ingredients, toppings, syrups, powdered milk, coffee beans, and meat were all sealed tight and still fresh.

The other side was a huge arcade, with rows of claw machines, dance games, basketball hoops, and racing games, all in working order.

A movie theater and some cozy booths took up the corner, the perfect spot to relax.

Everyone's eyes lit up at the sight of the food and games.

"No way, there's even a milkshake machine!" Ashley rushed over to the milkshake bar, thrilled, and looked it over.

"All the ingredients are still here too! We can make our own milkshakes!"

That got everyone excited, and they all got to work.

Spencer expertly cleaned off the hot dog roller and loaded it with fresh sealed hot dogs. As the machine warmed up, they soon started to sizzle, and the rich smell of grilled meat filled the air.

Thomas fiddled with the kettle corn machine, pouring in kernels and buttery syrup. Moments later, the kernels started popping, and golden, crunchy kettle corn spilled out, smelling amazing.

Ashley dragged Paisley over to help mix milkshakes and brew coffee.

Cold milk, scoops of ice cream, and a splash of syrup went into the blender, topped with sprinkles and cookie crumbs, and one smooth milkshake after another came out. Meanwhile, freshly ground coffee beans brewed into rich, fragrant coffee.

They gathered around the stands, splitting up the work, chatting and laughing as they enjoyed the food they'd made themselves. The crunchy kettle corn, juicy hot dogs, sweet milkshakes, and rich coffee felt like a warm hug, and for a moment, the terror of the apocalypse melted away.

After eating, the group ran off to the arcade, hitting the dance machines and claw games, laughing like they hadn't laughed in ages, their voices filling the whole floor.
