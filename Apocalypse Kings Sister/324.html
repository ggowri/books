Chapter 324

Chase's team, meanwhile, skirted around the main forest path, picking their way along a rough, overgrown trail toward the sunken tomb site.

Everyone moved with rifles raised, nerves strung tight.

The closer they got to the tomb, the colder it got, a bone-deep chill creeping up from the ground, the air itself feeling thick with the damp decay of something buried too long.

The soil underfoot had gone soft and wet, crisscrossed with deep, winding drag marks, flecks of dark, metallic-looking scales embedded in the mud, crunching faintly underfoot as the team passed.

The forest stayed eerily silent — no wind, no insects, just the uneven rhythm of footsteps, loud in all that quiet.

The sickly-sweet smell in the air grew stronger, mixed with the chemical tang of lab residue, thick enough to tighten chests.

"Careful."

Chase raised a hand, signaling a halt, his voice dropping to a warning.

"These tracks are fresh. The mutants are probably nesting right near the entrance."

The words had barely left his mouth when the fighter at the front of the group suddenly dropped, his face going white.

"Captain! The ground's hollow here!"

Before he could finish, the soft earth beneath him caved in, a two-yard crater blasting open, dirt flying everywhere.

The pit bottom wasn't soil — it was a dense bed of jagged bone spikes, bleached white and razor-sharp, each one caked in dried, blackened blood. A kill trap, unmistakably.

The fighter reacted fast, firing a shot to launch himself clear while channeling his Affinity to steady his landing, narrowly avoiding the spikes.

But the moment he hit the ground, the earth shifted again, and two dark shapes exploded out from the underbrush on either side, moving too fast to track.

Serpent mutants.

They kept the rough shape of a human body, but their limbs were long and warped, covered in dense black scales that caught the dim light with a cold sheen.

Their heads weren't human at all — flat, serpentine, blood-red slit pupils, forked tongues flicking constantly, venom dripping from their jaws and burning small black holes into the dirt where it landed.

The two creatures struck from opposite sides, claws slicing through the air with a sharp whistle, zeroing in on the isolated fighter with brutal, precise speed.

"Fire!"

Chase barked the order, rifle already up, firing clean and fast.

Gunfire tore through the silence, bullets finding their mark.

But the shots sparked uselessly off the dense scales, deflecting without penetrating at all.

"These rifles can't touch them!"

The Level Five Plant Affinity user reacted instantly, green light surging from his fingers as thick branches tore up from the ground, lashing out at both serpent mutants, trying to drive them back.

The branches struck hard, but only knocked the creatures back half a step — no real damage done.

If anything, it enraged them further. Low, guttural hisses rose from their throats, muscles bulging under their scales, a thin black haze of venom beginning to coil around their bodies.

Then came the real threat.

From deep inside the tomb's dark entrance, the scrape of scales against stone swelled into a deafening chorus, drawing closer. In the darkness, pair after pair of blood-red slit pupils flickered open, dense and endless, like something monstrous slowly waking.

Not just two.

An entire nest of serpent mutants lived beneath that tomb.

Chase's expression went grim, his stomach dropping.

He'd actually felt lucky, a minute ago, getting the supposedly easier underground route. Now it was clear — this wasn't a shortcut. It was a death trap.

The surface facility only held restless humanoid subjects. What he'd led his team into, an entire nest of venomous serpent mutants, was far worse.

"Fall back! Form up!"

Chase barked the order fast, his expression tight.

"Plant Affinity, hold the front! Everyone else, keep firing, move now!"

But it was already too late.

The two lead serpent mutants surged forward again, claws tearing the air, thick with venomous stench, and the shapes from the tomb's mouth poured out behind them, closing off every escape route, trapping the entire squad between the forest and the tomb.
