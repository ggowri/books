Chapter 399

"That's not pleading for mercy, that's walking straight into the wolf's den! That's asking for humiliation!"

Brenda sobbed, heartbroken, her eyes full of nothing but selfish desperation, completely ignoring her daughter's position, focused only on saving herself.

"So you're just going to stand there and watch your mother die in here?!"

"Grant doesn't have the power or the pull! The only one who can save me now is Mr. Quinn!"

"All you have to do is get close to him, beg him properly, and he'll surely give you this one favor!

Chloe, you can't just abandon me! This is my only chance!"

Each pleading word struck Chloe's heart like a needle.

Chloe felt an icy chill wash through her, the understanding she'd built up over years shattering in an instant.

In this moment, she finally saw her mother clearly.

Selfish. Narrow. Domineering. Always, only, thinking of herself.

All those years riding on Chloe's status to throw her weight around, and the instant trouble struck, she'd simply dumped every bit of pressure, every bit of humiliation, onto her daughter without a second thought.

Never once considering her situation, never once feeling for her, only ever taking, only ever demanding.

In the past, blinded by her mother's constant praise, by everyone's flattery, by the glow of being the team's darling, she'd believed their family was happy, believed her mother loved her most, believed she had the most solid support behind her.

But now, in this moment of crisis, she finally woke up completely. That so-called support had been nothing but an illusion.

Her mother only ever caused trouble, only ever dragged her down, and in a real crisis, wouldn't spare a single thought for her.

Disappointment, exhaustion, humiliation, and resentment churned together inside her, countless emotions tangled together, nearly crushing what little reason she had left.

She didn't want to go. She refused, every instinct screaming against it.

She knew exactly what kind of person Jasper Quinn was, going to beg him would only earn her endless humiliation and torment.

Possibly worse than that.

But looking at her mother through the glass, sobbing hysterically, on the verge of collapse, she still couldn't bring herself to simply walk away.

That was still the woman who'd given birth to her, who'd raised her.

After an agonizing internal struggle, every bit of Chloe's pride, dignity, and composure crumbled away, piece by piece.

She closed her eyes, a flash of bitter, wretched resignation crossing her face, her voice rough and dry.

"...Fine. I'll go."

The moment the words left her mouth, her fate for the night was sealed.

Jasper Quinn's residence was one of the most elite standalone villas at the base, sprawling in scale, lavishly furnished, the kind of place most people could only dream of.

Night had fallen, the villa blazing with light inside and out, loud music spilling from every corner, the atmosphere a stark, jarring contrast to the cold, oppressive Security Office.

Chloe straightened her rumpled clothes, forced down the humiliation churning inside her, and drove over with her teammate.

Standing outside the villa gate, she could already hear the raucous laughter and noise from within, extravagance and excess radiating from the place.

She knocked. The housekeeper only let her in alone.

The moment she pushed through the door, she froze completely.

The courtyard pool shimmered clean and bright, ripples catching the light, a lively pool party in full swing.

A crowd of scantily dressed, immaculately styled men and women lounged around the water's edge, drinking, laughing, splashing around, the whole scene dripping with excess and indulgence.

And at the very center, on the most prominent lounge chair, Jasper Quinn sprawled lazily, his posture loose and showy.

He wore black patterned swim trunks, shirt hanging open, his frame lean and sharp, his expression arrogant and careless, every inch of him radiating spoiled, playboy privilege.

Two attractive women flanked him on either side, murmuring sweet nothings, fawning over him attentively, while he idly swirled a tall glass in hand, only half-listening to the chatter around him, his gaze carrying its usual careless, aloof detachment.

Chloe's sudden entrance shattered the mood of the party instantly.

The noisy laughter cut off at once, every eye in the place snapping toward her, curious, assessing, mocking gazes tangling together, leaving her rigid and completely at a loss.

Someone let out a flirtatious whistle, the teasing unmistakable.

"Isn't that the flagship squad's darling, Chloe Thompson?"

"Isn't she Grant Quinn's girlfriend?"

"Didn't she always act so high and mighty, looking down on the rest of us?"

"Don't tell me, Grant's unconscious, so she's here trying to land his cousin instead?"

Each cruel comment hit her like a blow.

Chloe's face went paler by the second. Humiliation washed over her like a tide, but she couldn't leave.

Facing her uninvited guest, Jasper didn't even bother lifting his eyelids, let alone moving. He stayed sprawled on his lounge chair, basking in everyone's attention, treating her as though she were nothing, invisible, irrelevant.
