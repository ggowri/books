Chapter 267

Paisley climbed in beside him and shut the door, the dim interior lights the only glow.

She pulled fever medicine and a bottle of water from the storage box, shaking two white pills into her palm.

"Take these. You'll feel better after some rest."

He leaned back against the seat, a faint flush still on his face, his body fully healed yet somehow weaker, heat spreading through him.

He watched her hold out the pills, his throat working, but didn't reach for them.

It reminded her of how good it had felt to be cared for when she was sick.

She leaned in closer, pills in one hand, water in the other, her voice soft.

"Open up."

The distance between them closed fast.

His pupils shrank, his breath catching.

Warm breath mingled between them, her eyes close enough to study, her lashes impossibly soft in the low light.

Heat climbed back up his neck and face, and he stayed frozen in place, opening his mouth without a word.

She slipped the pills in gently, then handed him the water.

"Slow. Don't choke."

The cool water carried the pills down easily, faintly sweet.

He swallowed, still barely breathing, his heartbeat loud enough to drown out everything else.

In that tiny, sealed space, he could see every detail of her skin, every flicker of her brow, could still feel the warmth her fingers had left behind.

As she pulled back, a warm, trembling hand caught her wrist.

Barely any pressure, careful and uncertain, like he was afraid to startle her, and still unwilling to let go.

He kept his eyes down, lashes hiding what churned beneath them, his voice rough and warm.

"Pais... thank you. You saved me again, right when it mattered."

How am I supposed to not fall for someone like you?

Paisley kept it light.

"That's what teammates do."

"If you're still feeling off, just close your eyes and rest. I'll keep watch until the others get back."

No reason to go back upstairs anyway, not with the cores and supplies already secured.

Something bittersweet tightened in his chest, the words I feel more for you than that sitting right at the edge of his tongue.

He swallowed them back down.

He didn't dare risk it.

All he saw in her eyes was the care of a comrade, nothing more.

If he said it and it broke whatever this was, he might lose even this, the fighting side by side, the trust between them.

He didn't dare ask for more. Just held onto this moment, careful not to lose it.

In the faint glow of the car's light, he finally looked up at her, his eyes dark with something burning, something he'd kept hidden too long.

The cabin stayed quiet, wind brushing past the car outside, the small space thick with something unspoken.

"Then I'll leave myself in your hands, I suppose. Poor, sickly me."

Savoring the quiet, he closed his eyes and settled back against the seat.

Paisley sat beside him quietly and used the lull to call on Trovie.

[Trovie, convert all the gold and jewelry from this haul into points.]

Trovie's voice chimed back right away.

[On it, Host! Converting the gold and jewelry in bulk now...]

A few seconds later, the system chimed again.

[Host, conversion complete! Total earned this round: 80,000 points!] [Bulk conversion bonus triggered: 5,000 points!] [Current total: 459,060!]

Paisley had nothing to say to that. Just exasperation.
