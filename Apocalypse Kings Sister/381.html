Chapter 381

Sitting beside her, Ashley set down her fork, her eyes taking on a serious note of concern as she said quietly.

"Pais, someone's definitely talking about you behind your back. You've been getting a lot of attention lately, that draws jealousy. Be careful from now on."

Paisley's lips curved faintly at that. She figured it was just a young girl worrying too much, and with a faint trace of amusement, she shook her head lightly, unbothered, and went back to quietly eating, her expression calm and easy.

Across the table, Diane, who knew everything that had happened today, looked at her capable, composed daughter with undisguised pride and affection. She picked up a piece of glossy braised pork and set it in Paisley's bowl, her voice warm and doting.

"Only my daughter could pull off something like that, effortlessly dominating the whole field. So impressive."

Beside her, Gerald nodded along eagerly, pride written all over his face, his voice firm.

"That's right. Our Pais was born capable, steady beyond her years, way ahead of anyone else."

"And then look at you three boys. Every one of you is more of a handful than the last. I used to hope you'd grow up fast enough to protect her, shield her from harm."

"Turns out it's the other way around. If real danger ever came knocking, it'd probably be Pais protecting the three of you."

Called out in front of everyone, Orion, steady, reserved by nature, long used to his father's teasing favoritism, kept a completely straight face, quietly eating his rice, playing the invisible man, offering no defense, no comeback.

Thomas, more easily riled, immediately pouted and looked up, protesting with a teenager's playful indignation.

"Dad! I'm plenty impressive too, you know! This favoritism is way too obvious!"

Spencer, the youngest, set down his fork, his eyes deadly serious and resolute, declaring with conviction.

"Dad, don't underestimate us."

"If real danger ever comes, I'll be the first one standing in front of Pais. I'll do everything I can to protect her, I won't let her get hurt, not even a little."

Warm yellow light spilled across the table, the family laughing and teasing each other, full of homely warmth.

No one knew that, far away in the Lennox family villa, a scheme targeting Paisley had already begun.

After dinner, as night deepened, a gentle breeze drifted in through the window.

Paisley returned to her room, closing the door behind her. Her gaze dimmed slightly, and with a single thought, she slipped into her Storage Dimension.

The vast space inside was packed with neatly arranged supplies, dazzling in variety, with Trovie floating amid it all, perfectly organized.

At the center, on the jade platform, sat a dense cluster of crystal cores, radiating pure, multicolored Affinity light, spirit energy swirling thick in the air.

Today's haul had been substantial.

Seven pristine, dense Level Seven Plant Affinity crystal cores, spoils from killing the mutant trumpet flowers.

On top of that, a reward of a hundred clear, bright Level Four crystal cores, plus two hundred assorted crystal cores collected from the battlefield zombies, more than enough, altogether, to push her Plant Affinity up a level this round.

Paisley settled into a cross-legged seat, posture straight, eyes closed, focusing her mind.

She drew on the Level Seven Plant Affinity cores first.

Warm, dense green Plant Affinity energy burst free of the cores' confines at once, turning into gentle streams of light that seeped slowly into her meridians through every pore.

The mild Plant energy traveled through her meridians, refining every part of her body.

One after another.

The ceiling of her Level Six peak shattered under the relentless flood of pure, concentrated energy pouring in.

Crack!

The invisible barrier of her realm shattered with an audible snap.

A dense wash of green spirit light enveloped Paisley's whole body, her Affinity surging, climbing layer by layer, her Plant Affinity breaking through, steadily settling into early Level Seven.

The breakthrough didn't stop there.

Within moments, her Affinity climbed further still, advancing steadily from early Level Seven straight through to mid Level Seven, where it finally settled.

Paisley slowly opened her eyes, a flash of pure, vivid green light flickering in her gaze, clear and deep, as though an entire forest's worth of life thrived behind her eyes, before it faded, and her usual cool composure returned.

By now, her meridians ran completely clear, every inch of her refined and nourished by rich, warm Plant Affinity.

The Affinity coursing through her body ran hot and full, flowing without the slightest obstruction, her whole body light and strong, completely transformed.

With her Plant Affinity now at Level Seven, a whole new set of abilities unlocked, bringing endless surprises.
