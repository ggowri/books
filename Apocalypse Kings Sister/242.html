Chapter 242

A rank gust of wind hit her first, fangs glinting cold and sharp, inches from tearing her apart.

In that split second, Paisley stepped back, her eyes cold and clear, without a trace of fear.

She raised her hand, and the pale, frost-white Frostbloom Sword appeared in her grip, its blade shimmering with cold light.

The next instant, she unleashed her Plant Affinity, a green glow blazing beneath her feet.

Thick dark-green vines tore up from the ground, crisscrossing with overwhelming force, lashing around the bear's enormous body.

Coil after coil wrapped tighter, binding its limbs, torso, and neck until the ferocious Level Six beast was locked in place, unable to move.

It roared in fury, black mist surging off its body as it thrashed, trying to break free.

But the vines, grown from her own pure spirit energy, were nearly unbreakable, and tiny hidden thorns laced with paralytic venom dug deep into its hide.

The harder it struggled, the tighter the vines drew, driving the thorns deeper and spreading the paralysis faster.

Within moments, the bear's movements slowed. Its limbs stiffened, its massive frame swayed, and its crimson eyes clouded over. Its roar dropped to a weak, muffled growl.

The opening wouldn't last. Paisley's gaze sharpened.

"Now! Everyone, together!"

She leveled her sword at its head, calling out the fatal weak point.

"The base of its ear, that's the weakest point on its whole body. That's where its Affinity core is. Everyone, hit it there, full force!"

Everyone had been reeling under the bear's sheer presence, but hearing the weak point called out, and seeing the creature finally weakened, their fear vanished and their fighting spirit roared back.

They redirected everything at once, Affinities converging on the base of its ear.

Elijah's eyes flashed cold, silver-blue lightning gathering into a single massive pillar that crashed down behind the bear's ear with world-ending force.

Orion's Metal Affinity burst out, countless blades fusing into a single golden spear that struck the weak point with pinpoint precision.

Spencer's flames surged, a scorching fireball crashing down in a wave of heat.

******

Thomas's wooden spikes rained down in volleys, sealing off every path the beast might use to dodge.

Ashley's boulders slammed into the side of its head.

Mason's wind blades cut and tore, shredding away its outer defenses.

Marcus and Madison's water and earth Affinities intertwined, forming spiraling blades that joined the barrage.

The once-invincible bear, paralyzed and barely able to move, had no way to withstand the combined assault.

Hit after hit landed on its weak point. It let out an agonized howl, its body convulsing as the black mist around it dissolved and the fury drained from its eyes.

Paisley leapt, light as air, flipping through the sky in a single fluid arc.

Her blade, charged with pure spirit energy, came down through the opening the others had carved, a single clean strike landing square on the Affinity core.

Shhhk—!

The blade punched straight through, tearing the crystal core free.

The bear's massive body went rigid. Its head dropped, its crimson eyes going dark for good.

With a thunderous crash, the twenty-six-foot creature hit the ground, shaking the earth and sending up a wall of dust.

The Level Six mutant bear was dead.

Silence fell over the clearing.

Everyone let out a long breath, staring at the massive corpse. Then, as one, their eyes turned to Paisley, landing lightly on the ground, and to the sword that seemed capable of cutting down anything. Awe, respect, and something close to reverence filled their faces.

From spotting the orchard, to binding the Level Six beast with vines, to calling the kill shot, she'd stayed calm through every second of it.
