Chapter 242

A foul wind hit Paisley in the face. The bear's sharp fangs gleamed coldly, just inches from ripping her apart.

At the last second, Paisley took a few steps back, her eyes cool and sharp, without a trace of fear.

She raised her hand, and the pure white, gleaming Frostbloom Sword appeared in her grip. Its blade shimmered with a clear, cold light, giving off a chilling sword aura.

A second later, she unleashed her Plant Affinity at full power, and green light blazed under her feet.

Countless thick, tough, dark-green vines burst out of the ground, crisscrossing and wrapping around each other. With enough force to trap anything, they quickly coiled around the giant bear's huge body.

Layer by layer, the vines bound it and squeezed tighter and tighter, locking down the bear's limbs, body, and neck. The fierce Level Six beast was trapped in place, unable to move.

The giant bear roared in fury, so loud their ears rang. Black mist surged around it as it struggled desperately, its massive body shaking as it tried to snap the vines.

But the vines Paisley had grown were full of pure, original power, incredibly tough and unbreakable. Their surface was also covered in tiny hidden paralyzing thorns that dug deep into the bear's thick fur.

The harder the beast struggled, the tighter the vines got, the deeper the thorns stabbed into its flesh, and the faster the paralyzing poison spread through its body.

Within a few breaths, the raging bear slowed down noticeably, and most of its overwhelming strength drained away. Its limbs went stiff and numb, its heavy body swayed slightly, and its blood-red eyes clouded over. Its roars turned dull and weak, nothing like the savage beast from before.

Paisley knew a perfect opening like this would vanish in a heartbeat, and her eyes turned sharp. "Now! Everyone, attack together!"

She pointed her sword straight at the bear's head and named its fatal weakness. "The base of its ears is the weakest spot on its whole body, and that's where its power core is! Everyone, focus your fire there and hit it with everything you've got!"

Everyone had still been crushed by the Level Six bear's pressure. But now that Paisley had pinpointed its weak spot, and they could see the poison had weakened it, their spirits soared. Their panic faded, and their will to fight came roaring back.

Everyone adjusted their attacks right away, aiming their powers at the weak spot at the base of the bear's ears.

Elijah's eyes went cold, and silver-blue lightning gathered across the sky into a thick, fierce pillar. It came crashing down on the spot behind the bear's ears.

Orion's Metal Affinity exploded at full power. Countless sharp golden metal blades stacked on top of each other and turned into a streak of golden light that pierced the weak spot precisely.

Spencer's flames surged in his palm, forming a white-hot fireball that came crashing down with rolling waves of heat. Thomas shot sharp wooden spikes in volley after volley, blocking every direction the beast could dodge.

Ashley formed thick boulders of rock and earth and slammed them into the side of the bear's head. Mason's sharp wind blades kept slicing and tearing at its hide, ripping through its outer defenses.

Marcus and Madison followed close behind, weaving their Earth and Water powers together into binding blades that joined everyone's barrage.

The Level Six bear's thick hide had made it nearly invincible, but now, paralyzed by poison and barely able to move, it couldn't hold up against everyone's combined attack.

Its weak spot by the ears took hit after hit, and it let out a painful, piercing howl. Its huge body convulsed violently, the black mist around it fell apart inch by inch, and the savage light in its eyes faded fast.

Paisley gripped the Frostbloom Sword and leaped lightly into the air, flipping as she cut across the sky.

Her blade was full of pure power, glowing with a fierce white light and a bone-chilling aura. Through the opening everyone had blasted open, she brought it down from above in one clean, deadly strike, landing precisely on the bear's power core.

With a ripping sound, the sword aura pierced the weak spot and dug out the beast's crystal core.

The bear's huge body went stiff, and all its struggling stopped. Its massive head dropped heavily, and its blood-red eyes went dark as the last of its life left it.

With a thunderous crash, the 26-foot beast hit the ground, shaking the earth and sending dust flying.

The Level Six mutant bear was dead.

The whole place went silent.

Everyone looked at the giant beast's body on the ground and let out a long sigh of relief. Without meaning to, they all turned to look at Paisley as she landed, and at that incredible sword that seemed like it could destroy anything. They were stunned and impressed, and every one of them was won over.

From finding the strawberry patch to trapping the Level Six beast with vines, and then spotting its weakness and leading the deadly finish, she'd stayed calm and in control the whole time.
