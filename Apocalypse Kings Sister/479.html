Chapter 479

In the air, countless ice fragments formed and gathered into dense, razor-sharp icicles, hanging in the sky and ready to fall.

"Icicle Rain, fall!"

At her order, thousands of icicles screamed through the air, pierced the steam, and plunged down on the Fire users across the river!

Each icicle carried freezing power and flew at incredible speed, thick and wide, impossible to dodge!

The enemy line broke into chaos. The Fire users who had been pouring out flame had to stop, shield themselves, and scramble out of the way. Their formation collapsed.

The fire attack broke off, and the sea of flame fell apart.

While their line was in chaos, Paisley pushed again, speed climbing to its limit. She shot toward the end of the bridge like a streak of light and landed on the far bank!

The moment her feet touched down, she shed momentum and stood, her skates retracting automatically. She drew the Frostbloom Sword, its blade cold and sharp, its edge cutting.

Elijah, Orion, Thomas, Ashley, Spencer, Mason, and Madison landed in turn and formed up fast, spirits high, ready.

In a few dozen seconds, the whole team had crossed the thousand-yard river through the fire and ice, and broken the trap!

Chloe's face went white. She stepped back, and her arrogance shattered into fear and panic.

How did they cross so easily?

This isn't something a normal person can do.

Paisley's way stronger than I knew!

Grant's face was dark as stormwater, his power surging wildly. He glared at the assembled Falcon Squad, killing intent flooding his eyes.

"If water and fire can't stop you."

He raised a hand slowly. His voice was ice cold and full of reckless fury.

"Then we fight!"

Boom!

At his order, the twenty-plus elite Affinity users behind him erupted with everything they had!

Red fire, heavy earth shields, sharp metal blades, howling wind. All kinds of Affinity light flared up, dazzling and dangerous, covering the whole riverbank. The air shook, wind tore dirt up, fire burned, metal rang, and a layer of killing intent pressed down on Falcon.

The long-planned ambush battle had begun.

On the eve of the fight, Elijah moved first. He stepped up and stood half a pace to Paisley's side, in front of her.

He stood like a mountain, shoulders wide and steady, the pressure of someone born to lead rolling off him. His dark eyes swept over the enemy. His voice was low and sure, protective and gentle.

"Pais, take a break. Leave the rest to us."

He knew she'd frozen the river, killed the high-tier shark, and held up the ice bridge, and that her power was drained. He didn't want her hurt.

His last look went to Grant.

"If you want a fight, let's fight!"

Paisley shook her head lightly. Sharpness bloomed in her dark eyes, with no fear, only fighting spirit.

Her voice was cold and firm. "I don't need a break. It's not right for the whole team to fight while I hide behind."

She stepped up beside Elijah, sword in hand.

The Falcon members saw this and moved half a step forward together, power ready, spirits high.

Orion's metal blades gleamed. Thomas's vines gathered life and killing intent. Ashley's earth power hardened into a heavy shield. Spencer, Mason, and Madison took their posts. The formation was tight, ready to fight to the death.

Across from them, the twenty-plus Affinity users saw Falcon's aura, and their rush faltered. A growing dread crept into their hearts.

Especially facing Elijah in front. Their fear swelled.

He was the Blackwood family's rightful heir and captain of Falcon Squad, famous across Aetheris Base, wielding peak Level Six Lightning Affinity, strong and ruthless, someone every Affinity user knew better than to cross.

Standing there, he gave off lightning pressure that crept through the field and weighed on every heart. Their breath caught, their limbs stiffened, and the urge to flee grew in them.

These people had been sent by Grant for one job. Hold Falcon Squad back until the Great Freeze hit, make them miss the deadline, and if needed, kill them.
