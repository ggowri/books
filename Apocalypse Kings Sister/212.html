Chapter 212

"Paisley, you had this thing the whole time? Why didn't you use it before?" Orion asked.

Paisley gripped the cold energy gun and aimed it at the restless mutant ahead. "The things we ran into before weren't worth wasting the gun's energy on," she said casually.

Orion and Elijah just stared at her, speechless.

Everyone else was fighting for their lives in this hopeless spot, but Paisley had everything under control and looked as relaxed as ever. She was freakishly strong.

After a brief silence, Elijah quickly pulled himself together and pushed down his shock. "Pais, go for it. Our poisoned teammate can't hold on much longer."

The humanoid mutant was still shrieking, and the shrill sound waves kept crushing everyone. The long, dark blue-green tentacles squeezed tighter and tighter, and the trapped team member's face turned an even darker bluish black. His twitching got worse, and he could barely breathe.

Paisley's eyes turned sharp, and all her casual ease disappeared.

She raised her arm steadily, aimed, and gently squeezed the trigger.

A dazzling, pure blue beam shot through the air with a roar, tearing through layer after layer of chaotic sound waves and flying straight at the core of the humanoid mutant's head.

At the last second, the humanoid mutant sensed the deadly threat. Its empty eyes suddenly blazed with savage fury, and its shriek cut off.

Its mutated body reacted incredibly fast. It jerked up one long, poisonous, mutated hand and actually tried to catch the energy beam barehanded, even though that beam could kill a high-level beast.

A split second later, the blue light exploded.

There was no cushion at all. Its hard, mutated flesh, bones, and poisoned sinew were no match for the sheer destructive power of top-tier energy.

With a dull, ear-splitting blast, the beam wiped out the mutant's whole arm. Bits of flesh and blood burst into the air and turned into nothing.

The defense and reflexes it was so proud of were useless against the energy gun.

Searing pain shot through its whole body. The humanoid mutant shook violently, and its floating body staggered back several feet. Its sound-wave attack broke off, and the pressure pinning everyone down was suddenly gone.

The splitting pain in their minds disappeared, and the weakness in their limbs slowly faded. Everyone snapped back to their senses, gasping for air, still shaken as they stared at what had just happened in total shock.

After a moment of stunned silence, someone came to and shouted through gritted teeth, "Keep going! Kill it, and don't give it a chance to recover!"

Every eye was glued to the cold, blue-glowing energy gun in Paisley's hand, burning with envy, jealousy, and longing.

Nobody had expected that small, sleek gun to be so terrifyingly powerful. With one shot, it had wrecked a high-level mutant's entire arm.

A gun like that... If they'd known, they never would've passed on it just because the price was too high when Elijah asked in the team chat.

They didn't even know if they could still buy one. If they made it out of here alive, they'd definitely ask about it again.

Just then, something so freaky it made their skin crawl happened right in front of them.

The dark blue-green flesh at the mutant's ruined shoulder squirmed and grew like crazy. Its broken bones, torn skin, and poisoned sinew put themselves back together fast enough to see.

In just a few seconds, a brand-new mutated arm had formed. Its skin was still a sickly bluish black, and its fingertips were sharp and poisonous, as good as new.

The whole place went dead silent. Everyone went ice cold, and pure terror swallowed them.

That kind of regeneration was terrifying! The arm they'd blown off had grown right back in no time!

The team members went white as paper, panic took over, and they couldn't stop shaking.

The mutant already had a brutal sound-wave attack, and now it had this insane regeneration too. Could they really kill a monster like this?

Despair washed over them all over again.

Only Paisley's expression didn't change. She was scary calm.

She raised the gun and pulled the trigger again and again. Dazzling blue beams flew through the air one after another, piercing the mutant's torso, chest, stomach, shoulders, back, and tentacles. She hit every vital spot on its body.

Every wound rotted away and vanished in seconds, but each time, the mutant's flesh grew back and healed.

But the healing was getting slower and slower.

Paisley had found the pattern. It couldn't regenerate forever after all. As long as she kept...
