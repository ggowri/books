Chapter 249

This gang had taken over Wexford a long time ago and ruled it like their own turf. They'd made a habit of robbing and killing, and their hands were covered in blood.

Under the cover of the pitch-black night, they kept low and moved fast along the shadows of the village roads, careful not to make a sound. They closed in on the standalone mansion, planning to launch a surprise attack in the dark and grab Paisley and the others all at once.

Back at the mansion, the night wind blew cold and bleak through the yard, and the darkness was thick as ink. All of Wexford was eerily quiet.

Paisley clearly sensed a large group of people coming. There were more than 20 of them, and every one of them had powers.

She looked toward the dark, dead-silent road deep in the village, and her clear voice turned cold as she warned Elijah. "Captain Blackwood, someone's coming. There are more than 20 of them, all with powers, and they're coming for us."

Elijah's face turned grim, and he went on full alert. He turned to head inside. "I'll get everyone down here to fight!"

"There's no time," Paisley said coldly.

The words had barely left her mouth when a huge crash shook the night. Someone had smashed through the iron fence around the mansion! Rocks flew everywhere, the metal bars twisted and snapped, and a sharp screech tore through the darkness.

More than 20 thugs burst in through the gap, every one of them oozing menace.

Each of them gave off a faint, uneven buzz of power, and they all held weapons, their eyes vicious.

They were obviously no ordinary street punks.

Pale blades gleamed cold and deadly in the dark. The man in the lead had a pointy, ratlike face, and his eyes were glued to Paisley. He looked her up and down with open, sleazy greed and licked his lips, practically drooling.

He swaggered forward, cocky and vicious. "If you want to live, come with us nice and quiet. Otherwise, I'll fucking kill every last one of you."

Elijah's face went hard. He took one quick step to the side and planted himself in front of Paisley, keeping her safely behind him.

Fine, sharp arcs of silver-blue lightning crackled to life at his fingertips and snapped through the air. A wave of savage pressure rolled out from him, and his eyes turned ice cold. "Just try it."

Paisley slowly stepped out from behind Elijah, her white clothes fluttering in the night wind. She wasn't big, but her presence crushed all 20-plus of those Affinity users.

Her cold gaze swept over them and stopped on the leader with the sleazy eyes, and the last bit of warmth left her face.

"Surviving the apocalypse is hard enough. Instead of just living your lives, you camp out here to rob people, and you burn and kill anyone who passes through.

"You think having more people and having powers means you can push around any survivor who comes along."

She raised her eyes a little, and her voice turned as cold as winter ice. "Who the hell gave you the nerve?"

Her presence shook the leader for a second. Then, his embarrassment turned to anger, and he let out a sneering laugh.

"Hey, sweetheart, you look all sweet and innocent, but you've sure got a mouth on you!

"Don't be stupid and throw away a good thing. Our boss is about to break through to Level Five and take over all of Wexford!

"Everything for miles around is his turf. Be a good girl and spread your legs for him, and you'll have it easy. Otherwise, you're all dying here tonight!"

The other Affinity users around them jeered and hollered, their eyes savage. They sealed off the whole yard, clearly planning to take the group by force.

Someone barked out, cruel and greedy. "Enough talk, just grab them! Throw the men to the zombies, and take the women back for the boss to fuck!"

Elijah watched the dense crowd of vicious-eyed thugs close in, and his expression turned deadly. Lightning surged around his body, silver-blue streaks crawled up his forearms, and massive power exploded out of him as he struck first!

With a crackle and a deafening boom, a thick, vicious bolt of lightning split the air and slammed straight into the fastest attackers in the front row.

They never saw it coming and had no time to dodge. The savage lightning ripped through their bodies, sparks burst all over them, and black smoke poured off them. Their bodies went stiff and twitched, their screams caught in their throats, and they crashed to the ground half dead, out of the fight for good.

The others who'd been closing in saw it with their own eyes and stopped dead. The menace drained from their faces, and deep fear filled their eyes as they took half a step back without thinking.

Someone's throat went tight, and he muttered in a shaky voice. "A Lightning Affinity user... He's got a mutated Lightning Affinity, and he's so strong!"

Most of these rogue Affinity users were only Level Two or Three at best. They'd never seen such a fierce, fast, high-level Lightning attack before, and they were starting to lose their nerve.

When the rat-faced leader saw that, his face darkened, and he snapped at them, trying to keep his men in line.
