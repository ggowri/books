Chapter 509

The base leadership met through the night. By the next morning, the giant screen at the mission hall had loaded a batch of emergency missions, red warning marks blazing.

Mission after mission scrolled up.

[Mission One: Search outside the city for uncontaminated groundwater. Danger rating: Level Seven. Mutant creatures in the area have been strengthened by the Crimson Rain. Rewards: 30,000 contribution points, 800 Level Four crystal cores.]

[Mission Two: Search sealed warehouses for sealed grain, canned goods, and dehydrated vegetables. Danger rating: Level Six. Rewards: 15,000 contribution points, 500 Level Three crystal cores.]

There was also a separate recruitment notice in large type.

[URGENT: HIGH-PAY RECRUITMENT FOR PURIFIERS!]

The base had a huge backlog of supplies to purify and far too few purifiers.

Any Affinity user with purifying ability could register at the mission hall.

Registered purifiers would be paid a high number of contribution points for each batch of supplies purified. Purifiers accompanying outside missions would get extra protective gear, emergency rations, and high-tier crystal cores, and priority for housing and heating supplies.

The moment it went up, the mission hall flooded with people.

Many Affinity users gathered around the screen, murmuring, uneasy.

"No wonder that team got sick earlier. The crystal cores were contaminated."

"This is a mess. From now on, every core we get from zombies and beasts can't be absorbed directly. We have to find a purifier."

"Purifiers are going to be in huge demand. The whole base needs them."

"Every fruit and every drop of water out there is off-limits now. Finding food is going to be even harder."

"At least our base is somewhat humane. The people at other bases are going to have a harder and harder time staying alive."

"Water Affinity users are in demand now too."

Many people asked around, looking for anyone with purifying ability, hoping to team up early and go out to collect supplies.

Everyone knew that after the Crimson Rain, the bar for survival had gone up another notch.

Paisley stood at the back of the crowd and watched the recruitment notice, her face blank.

Nobody could tell that she was the high-tier purifier everyone was looking for.

Elijah stepped up beside her and bent down.

"Pais, what about this Level Seven water-search mission? Let's take it."

The hall was loud. The red water-source mission stood out on the screen.

Trovie's voice rang in her head.

[Main quest issued: Help the base find a new water source and resolve the current crisis.]

[Quest reward: 30,000 points and one Affinity Awakening Pill.]

Paisley had planned to take this mission anyway. A bonus like this was a nice surprise.

An Awakening Pill was a real treasure. Priceless.

Trovie, I accept. Get the reward ready!

She nodded.

"Yeah, let's take this one."

Elijah went to the counter to claim the mission.

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley stayed close at her side.

The clerk saw Elijah and Paisley's names, perked up, and reminded them again and again to pick up gas masks, hazmat suits, water test kits, emergency antidote shots, and flares from the warehouse.

"After the Crimson Rain, the beasts and mutant plants outside have all been strengthened. There are a lot of caves around the groundwater areas, and you could easily be ambushed.

Falcon Squad is the strongest team we've got. If you go, you'll get this done."

The clerk spoke seriously.

"If you find water, test it right away. Don't touch it or drink it directly."

They picked up supplies from the warehouse and drove out through the heavy alloy gates.

Outside, the world was gray and murky, the ground a muddy swamp of dark red washed out by the rain. A faint stench of rot hung in the air.

Rocks were stained with dark red that wouldn't wash off. Withered grass lay twisted and black. A few mutant plants swayed, their branches grotesque, and the contamination energy coming off them was obvious even from far away.

The SUV crept along, dodging big mud pits.

Ashley sat in the passenger seat, scanning every direction.

"Everything out here feels stronger after the Crimson Rain."
