Chapter 371

On the other side of the battlefield below, Elijah looked up and saw that single, slight but resolute figure in the sky. His breath caught, and raw panic surged through him.

A Level Seven mutant flower was unimaginably dangerous, its toxic mist alone could kill. He would not allow Paisley to take so much as a scratch.

"I'm going to help!"

Elijah barked out, his Lightning Affinity flaring up around him as he shot off toward her position.

Orion, right beside him, had the same thought. Golden alloy blades condensed at his fingertips in an instant, and he fell into step right behind Elijah, ready to shield Paisley and lend a hand.

But at that exact moment, high above, a flash of cold light split the sky.

Paisley's wrist flicked. One clean strike.

Nothing fancy, just one simple, decisive cut.

A blade of frigid cold swept clean through the massive flower's thick, monstrous disk. Ice spread through the entire plant in an instant, and the raging purple mist froze solid, then crumbled away to nothing.

A thunderous crash rang out.

The enormous Level Seven mutant trumpet flower snapped off at the root and shattered, petals of eerie purple-red scattering across the sky as the massive flower disk plummeted from the top of the silo, slamming into the ground, every trace of life gone from it.

The entire battlefield went dead silent.

Even the wind seemed to stop. The sound died. The zombies' groans cut off all at once.

Elijah and Orion froze mid-stride, their rescue run stopped cold, eyes wide with pure shock, neither had expected this.

Paisley's strength had grown again.

That had been a Level Seven mutant plant. Killed in one strike.

The entire Scarlet Squad stood frozen, eyes wide, bodies rigid, every trace of mockery and disdain locked on their faces, minds blank, barely remembering to breathe.

One strike.

Just one.

A Level Seven mutant trumpet flower, something that had left countless high-level Affinity users helpless, something that had claimed death after death, something that supposedly required three Level Seven fighters working together just to stand a chance, had been cut down, clean and simple, by Paisley, a mere Level Six.

High above, she stood atop the silo, Frostbloom Sword in hand, her clothes stirring faintly in the breeze, her expression still cool and composed, as though the godlike feat she'd just pulled off was nothing at all, barely worth a second thought.

A thunderclap of disbelief went off in every mind on that battlefield at once.

How was this possible?!

That was the Level Seven mutant trumpet flower that had terrorized the wilderness, crushed countless elite squads, and claimed the lives of so many Affinity users!

A monster that supposedly needed three Level Seven fighters locked in a desperate, coordinated struggle just for a sliver of a chance, killed clean, with zero suspense, by a Level Six user.

Everyone's mind buzzed, their entire understanding of Affinity ranks and high-tier mutant beasts turned on its head.

"Is she even human?"

"We're both Level Six, how can the gap between people be this absurd?!"

"I used to think she was just some pretty face riding on someone else's coattails. Turns out she's the real powerhouse hiding in plain sight!"

Stunned murmurs rippled through the group, broken and disjointed. Every trace of mockery and contempt on the Scarlet Squad's faces shattered completely, replaced by bone-deep shock and panic.

High above, Paisley's toes touched down lightly, her form drifting weightless atop the silo.

The thorned vines that had carried her up slowly retracted, sinking back into the earth without a trace, vanishing completely.

Holding the still-glinting Frostbloom Sword in one hand, she walked over to the flower's remains and bent down, pulling out a clear, warm purple crystal core with precise ease.

The moment it touched her palm, a pure, gentle surge of energy spread from her hand through her entire body, warm and soothing, washing away the heat and violence of the battlefield, leaving her feeling thoroughly refreshed.

A flicker of understanding passed through Paisley's eyes.

This trumpet flower's crystal core was remarkably pure.

If she killed every one of them hidden here and absorbed all their cores, her Plant Affinity would break through to Level Seven without any trouble.

No time to waste. She lifted her gaze, scanning the hidden pockets of toxic mist around the area, eyes sharpening as she prepared for the next round of extermination.

Meanwhile, the Scarlet Squad had clearly drawn the wrong conclusion from what they'd just seen.

They mistakenly believed that the Level Seven mutant trumpet flower, which had plagued the base for so long, was nothing but an exaggerated threat, that people had simply overstated how dangerous it was.
