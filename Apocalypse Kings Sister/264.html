Chapter 264

"You're all dead. Let. Him. GO."

Her Ice-Water Affinity exploded outward.

Ice swept through the room, blades forming out of thin air, launching forward in a glittering wave of cold.

Shhk-shhk-shhk—!!

The blades sliced through the rotten vines binding Elijah, shattering them on contact, severing every strand in seconds.

The instant he was free and starting to fall, she was already there, catching him, pulling him into her arms.

Warmth met the cold of her clothes, her voice tight with worry.

"Are you okay? How did they do this to you? Hold on, I'm getting you out of here..."

Still numb, still struggling to breathe, Elijah was consumed by fear, not for himself.

He pushed her back despite his injuries, voice shaking.

"Run! Get away from here! There's a Level Six, you can't handle it!"

The one person I'd die for. She can't die here.

Nearby, the Level Six straightened up, fixing them both with its gaze, a mocking grin splitting its rotted face.

"Tsk, tsk. A little pair of lovebirds?"

"If you love each other that much, let me do you a favor. You can rot together in hell."

Its vines surged, the stench of rot flooding the room, the three Level Fives readying another assault. A new kill-box, forming fast.

The killing intent pressed down hard, but there wasn't a trace of fear in Paisley's eyes. Just fury, and something redder.

He'd been one second from blowing himself up.

The sight of him, pale, weak, poisoned, cut into her. Something close to rage filled her, something she'd never felt before.

Whoever did this is already dead.

"You brought this on yourselves."

Her voice came out without a trace of warmth, her whole presence shifting as her Plant Affinity flared fully.

For the first time since the apocalypse began, she called on her deadliest move. Parasitize.

The ground heaved as vines thicker than anything she'd summoned before tore up through it, bristling with thorns, pulsing with something hungry and vicious.

Before the three Level Fives could close in, the vines shot forward and bored straight into their skulls, through ears, through eye sockets, as if guided by will.

ROAR—!!

All three screamed in agony, thrashing, unable to break free.

Paisley's eyes stayed cold, her power still flowing.

The vines burst inward, wrapping around each core, and ripped it free in one motion.

Squelch! Squelch! Squelch!

Three wet bursts, dark fluid mixed with bright crystal fragments spraying out.

In seconds, three ravenous Level Fives had their cores torn out clean.

Their massive bodies seized, then dropped, lifeless.

All of it, fast.

Elijah stared, stunned, unable to believe what he'd just seen.

******

He'd known she was strong. He'd never guessed her Plant Affinity could be this terrifying.

Taking root in a skull, killing a Level Five in an instant, an ability he hadn't even known existed.

The Level Six's mocking grin froze, its eyes contracting, every trace of arrogance replaced with dread.

As a fellow Plant-type, it knew exactly how terrifying that move was. Nothing ordinary could match it. This was a complete mismatch.

This slip of a girl was this strong.
