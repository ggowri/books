Chapter 377

She didn't offer a single word of explanation. She buried her face in Brenda's arms and sobbed.

Her crying was heavy, muffled, full of resentment, fear, and the panicked dread of losing everything.

Brenda, flustered by her daughter's tears, did everything she could to soothe her, asking question after question. But Chloe said nothing, only kept crying, burying every grievance and every calculation deep inside.

Night fell, darkness settling thick over the base.

By the time the sky went fully dark, Chloe's father, Dennis Thompson, finally dragged himself home, utterly exhausted.

Normally composed, confident, even arrogant, the man looked completely different tonight, his face lined with worry, a dark cloud hanging over him. He sighed the moment he walked through the door, an air of heavy gloom following him in.

Brenda had just set a hot dinner on the table. Seeing her husband like this, and thinking of her daughter crying silently all day, her stomach twisted with worry. She hurried over, anxious.

"What on earth happened? Why are you both like this?"

"Our daughter's been crying nonstop since she got home, won't say a word about why, and now you walk in sighing like the world's ending."

Dennis tugged wearily at his collar and sank heavily into a chair at the table, looking at Chloe, still red-eyed, silent, staring blankly under the lamplight, his voice low and heavy.

"Our daughter's squad, Scarlet, it's been disbanded."

The single sentence landed on the table like a dropped boulder, crushing the room into dead silence.

Brenda's face went white in an instant, nearly dropping her bowl and chopsticks, her eyes wide with disbelief.

"Disbanded? How could they just disband out of nowhere? That was the number one flagship squad at the base!"

Dennis let out a bitter laugh, his eyes full of helpless anxiety as he looked up at his wife and daughter, his tone grave.

"Do you understand what this means?"

Before either could answer, he explained, his words striking home one by one.

"It means our family just lost its biggest backer."

"We've been living in a standalone villa, walking around this base like we owned the place, getting priority on supplies and allocations, with no one daring to cross us or slight us, all because Chloe was on the Scarlet Squad, all because the Quinn family had our backs."

"Now the Scarlet Squad's gone. Grant's lost an arm, still hasn't woken up, and the Quinn family's influence at the base just took a massive hit. From here on, things are only going to get harder for us."

He let out a long sigh, his eyes full of dread and worry.

"The news has already spread through the upper levels of the base today. Falcon Squad made their name in one battle, and Paisley, the biological daughter my younger brother just got back, shot straight to the top of the new generation."

"From now on, every resource, every connection, every bit of momentum at this base is going to flow toward Falcon Squad."

"My brother's family is going to be looking down on us from here on out."

Brenda froze, panic flooding her face as she turned to her daughter.

"So what do we do? Chloe! What are you going to do now?"

"Your Healing Affinity is so rare, can't you find an even stronger team to join?"

"Our Chloe is amazing, there's no way she can't compete with some girl who just got found and brought back."

"The second branch will never beat us."

Hearing this, Chloe, who'd been silently crying this whole time, finally raised her head.

The tears in her eyes had long since dried up, leaving nothing but cold jealousy and resentment, her clenched fingers bone-white.

Beat Paisley? How?

She'd seen it with her own eyes, Paisley killing a Level Seven mutant trumpet flower in one strike, without even using her Affinity.

That kind of absurd combat strength wasn't something an ordinary person could match.

The thought made Chloe's chest tighten, bitterness and envy tangling together until they nearly swallowed her whole.

But the panic in her eyes lasted only a few seconds before being replaced by pure ambition and cold calculation.

No point panicking.

She still had a shot.

She was a rare Plant Healing Affinity user, the whole base couldn't boast more than a handful of them.

Even having lost the Scarlet Squad, even having lost Grant, her own value remained intact.

Elijah Blackwood. The Blackwood family's finest heir. Falcon Squad's captain.

If she could become his girlfriend, latch onto him, every problem would solve itself.

She pressed her lips together, calculating fast.

She'd set aside every bit of pride, find whatever way she could to get close to Falcon Squad, to Paisley.

And through that, to Elijah.

The warm yellow light of the villa fell across Chloe's face, revealing not the vulnerability of someone who'd just survived a disaster, but the calculated restraint of someone plotting her next move.

The last traces of confusion faded from her eyes. Her tone turned cold, almost detached, as she broke the silence at the table.
