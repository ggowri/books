Chapter 184

"The next three hours are the critical window for treatment," the doctor said. "If we miss it, even the best high-level healer won't be able to save them!

"Right now, the best way to save them is to get a high-level healer with advanced purification and healing abilities. They could strip away the toxin layer by layer and cleanse the contamination!"

High-level healers were already the rarest Affinity users in the apocalypse, and high-level purification healers were rarer still. Right now, they were exactly what everyone needed.

The head of the base looked even worse after hearing that, shaken to the core. His voice shot up, frantic and full of panic.

"Nothing can happen to them! Two of the injured are heirs of the Blackwood and Thompson families from Aetheris, and they're extremely important!

"If anything happens to them here at Rivergate Base, our whole base won't survive the fallout! Nobody can take the blame for that!"

The situation was urgent, and there was no time to waste, so he barked out an order.

"Send out the word now! Gather every registered healer in Rivergate Base, no matter their level or whether they're on a mission, and get them here immediately. Do whatever it takes to keep these men alive!"

In the crowd, the senior officer who'd invited them to dinner, the one whose wife Paisley's team had saved, immediately turned serious. He gave a firm nod, then turned and hurried off without wasting a second to round up all the healers.

The whole place was gripped by panic, and the air was so heavy it was suffocating.

Everyone there was either a doctor or a soldier. Nobody said a word, and they were all on edge.

Paisley stood at the edge of the crowd, her face dark, torn by a fierce inner struggle.

She had high-level purification and healing powers, and she was a hundred percent sure she could wipe out this strange, corrosive toxin.

But if she stepped up now in front of everyone and showed off her healing and purification abilities, the whole base, maybe even every major power out there, would have their eyes on her. Keeping a low profile would be impossible after that, and endless trouble, prying, and scheming would follow.

But when she looked at Orion on the stretcher, his breathing faint and his face a bluish purple, and at Elijah, who was also unconscious and badly hurt, she couldn't stop worrying about them.

Orion was the brother who'd quietly protected her and taken her side ever since she was reborn.

Elijah had always been gentle and thoughtful too. He looked out for her in every way, treated her with sincerity, and always believed in her without question. In her heart, he was just as good to her as Orion.

Since she'd been reborn, they were among the few people who'd cared about her and protected her with no strings attached.

Besides, the two of them were humanity's best hope in this apocalypse.

They couldn't die here.

Why had they been hurt this badly? Had her rebirth set off a butterfly effect and changed things? If so, then she was even more responsible.

They can't die!

Paisley clenched her fists tight, torn back and forth.

There were still three hours left. Maybe... maybe the base's healers could pull off a miracle and save them.

She'd wait a little longer. She didn't want to reveal herself unless she absolutely had to.

Time ticked by, and every second was torture.

Before she knew it, half an hour had passed.

The senior officer who'd left earlier hurried back with a whole crowd of healers behind him. Every registered Affinity user in Rivergate Base with healing or purification abilities was there, from low level to mid level.

No one wasted time. They moved in at once, taking turns working on the five wounded soldiers.

Faint white, pale green, and warm yellow healing lights flickered on one after another as streams of healing energy flowed into the wounded. They used every antidote and purifying solution they had, all trying their hardest to hold back the toxin, clear the contamination, and repair the damage.

But the same strange thing kept happening, over and over. The instant any healing glow touched a wound, some eerie black mist swallowed it whole, leaving no effect at all.

Worse, after every treatment, the dark, poisonous tinge on the soldiers' faces grew even deeper, and their breathing got weaker and weaker.

One healer after another stopped, faces pale, foreheads beaded with sweat, shaking their heads as they stepped back.

"It's no use... This toxin is too weird. It eats up our affinities, so healing doesn't work at all."

"I can't purify the contamination inside them at all. I'm just making the poison spread faster."

"This is way beyond what I can do. I can't heal them, and I've never seen such a vicious, corrosive toxin!"

Finally, the highest-level healer of them all ran out of power. He pulled his hands back, his face pale, and shook his head painfully as he stared at the readings on the scanner, which kept getting worse.
