Chapter 493

The interrogator didn't pause. He moved on to the others.

"The remaining ambush team members acted as accomplices and are sentenced alike. All are assigned to high-risk frontline squads for one year of unpaid hazard duty."

Frontline hazard duty meant working year-round in contaminated zones, thick with zombies and radiation. A high-tier beast could appear at any moment. Injury, mutation, even death were routine.

Chloe's whole body went cold. She refused to accept it and cried out to protest, but a guard clamped a hand on her shoulder.

She turned to Grant. He only looked away, calmly, and never glanced at her again.

Her clever scheme to shift the blame had still ended in punishment.

And it had frozen Grant's heart for good.

******

The white lights of the Security Office hall glared off the floor. Dennis and Brenda had finally gotten a message through, and they arrived just in time to hear the ruling.

When the officer read out that Chloe would serve a year of unpaid hazard duty on the contaminated frontline, Brenda looked like she'd been struck by lightning. Her legs buckled and she nearly collapsed.

"A year? The frontline? No, absolutely not!"

Her voice rose, her face drained of color, her lips shaking. She lunged for the officer's sleeve and a guard stopped her. Her eyes were red and wild.

"That place is swarming with high-tier zombies and mutant beasts! The radiation is deadly. One slip and she mutates or dies! How can Chloe go there?"

Dennis was shaken too, his brows locked together, his face dark enough to drip.

He'd clung to a hope that she'd just be held for a while. With his old connections and some money, he could get her out. He never imagined a sentence that was nearly a death sentence.

"Officer, please reconsider! My daughter made a mistake. She isn't a bad person."

"And she went on missions all the time. She did a lot for this base. You can't be so heartless."

He forced down his panic and tried to keep his composure.

"We know people in the leadership. Could the file be reviewed? Could the sentence be reduced?"

The officer gave him a cool glance. His tone didn't budge.

"The evidence is solid. The ruling was decided jointly by several neutral-faction leaders and won't be changed easily."

"If you object, file an appeal through the proper channels. But don't think you can pull strings behind the scenes."

Outside the Security Office, a bitter wind drove sleet into their faces. Brenda gripped Dennis's arm, her voice thick with tears, still refusing to give up.

"Dennis, we can't let this stand! Chloe can't go to the front to die! Let's go to our old friends, to what's left of the Quinn family. We have to save her, whatever it takes!"

Dennis's face was grim. He sighed heavily.

"I know. I'll visit them one by one. I'll find someone to pull strings, even if it costs us."

But he knew the Quinn family was badly hurt and barely protecting itself. Few would lend a hand. The road was close to impossible.

By the base's orders, all the sentenced were gathered into one high-risk field squad, ready to head out to the contaminated zone.

Chloe stood in the line with a bare-bones kit on her back. She spotted Grant at once, standing silent a short distance away, one arm gone.

Her heart stirred. She slipped back into her old act, weak and fragile, and hurried to his side. Her voice went soft, her eyes damp, and she reached for his sleeve with a pleading, coaxing air.

"Grant, I'm so glad I get to see you again! I'm sorry about the interrogation. I was just so scared when I said that… Please forgive me. We're going on missions together, and I need your help."

Before, whenever she'd acted helpless like this, Grant had softened and protected her.

This time, he sensed her coming and sidestepped her hand without a word. He stared straight ahead, never even glancing at her. Cold radiated off him.

Chloe's hand hung in the air. She panicked, and her voice dropped lower.

"Grant, I know I was wrong. I shouldn't have put everything on you."

"Out in the field it'll be deadly. We can only survive if we look out for each other. Please don't ignore me."
