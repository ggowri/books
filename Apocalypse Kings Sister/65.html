Chapter 65

Reid tried to butter her up.

"Rachel, we really didn't mean to leave you out there.

"You know how sneaky Paisley is. I was just worried she was pulling another one of her tricks.

"I was planning to come find you the second the rain stopped. Right, Ryan?"

Ryan nodded fast. "That's right. That was the plan all along.

"We had no idea you'd make it back on your own, Rachel. We're so relieved.

"Are you hungry, Rachel? I'll go make you something to eat right now."

Rachel felt nothing but numb as they sucked up to her.

She'd already seen right through them. They were selfish hypocrites who only ever looked out for themselves.

But she didn't let a trace of her hatred or disappointment show. She kept up her fragile, hurt, slightly dazed act and patiently listened to their excuses, without calling them out or asking a single question.

Her head was perfectly clear.

Right now, she didn't have a solid footing or any real power. She needed their help.

In this world, it was every woman for herself.

From now on, she was done craving fake family love. She'd stop hoping anyone would protect her or put her first, and she'd rely on nobody but herself.

Her two-faced brothers were the perfect pawns for now. She'd milk their cheap affection for all it was worth and use it to get stronger fast.

Jasper was sitting on the couch. When he saw Rachel walk in safe and sound, he couldn't believe his eyes.

He sneered at her.

"I can't believe how lucky you are. That mutant carried you off, you got stuck out in the storm in the middle of nowhere, and you still made it back alive?

"It's a damn miracle. You really are hard to kill."

Then, he looked her up and down, his voice snide and mocking. "Or was that mutant just a total wimp?"

Rachel looked up and stared straight at him with ice-cold eyes.

That piece of shit almost got me killed three times. Lucky for me, I don't die easy.

Sooner or later, she'd kill that scumbag with her own two hands. But not yet.

After everything she'd just survived, she wasn't the sweet, naive pushover she used to be.

A cold, creepy smile spread across her face. She didn't bother arguing. Instead, she called on her power with a single thought!

With a rushing splash, the air in the room started to churn. The dry, still air suddenly condensed into tiny droplets, and mist appeared out of nowhere. It spun around her body faster and faster, forming razor-sharp blades of water that hung in midair, gleaming coldly.

A wave of pure, powerful Water Affinity energy swept across the room. The raw force of her newly awakened power stunned everyone into silence.

Rachel stood tall. The pathetic, groveling girl from earlier was gone. Her face had turned cold and hostile, and her bloodshot eyes were icy as she spoke, every word sharp and clear.

"It couldn't touch me. I awakened a Water Affinity! That's how I made it back alive."

Owen stared at Rachel in shock, then broke into a delighted grin. "Rachel, I can't believe you awakened a Water Affinity! I'm so happy for you."

Reid and Ryan sucked up to her too. "Rachel, we're really happy for you."

Jasper changed his tune just like that.

"Wow, Rachel, I can't believe you actually awakened an ability.

"You should be thanking me, then. If I hadn't taken you out today, you never would've gotten this lucky break.

"As of now, you're officially on my team!"

He turned to the three Lennox brothers and barked at them impatiently.

"What the hell is wrong with you three? Why aren't you making dinner yet? Are you waiting for Rachel to do it?

"Right now, you three are the only dead weight around here."

Rachel ignored all of them. She tossed out one line and headed upstairs.

"I'm going to my room to rest. Call me when dinner's ready."

In the apocalypse, strength was everything.

As long as she was strong enough, she'd never have to suck up to anyone.

Right now, she was more useful than her three brothers, which made them the weak ones.

Meanwhile, over in the other villa, everyone was sitting down to dinner in the dining room.

Once again, Ashley, Madison, and Kelly had made dinner.

Paisley was casually feeding her cat.

Kelly saw it and got seriously annoyed.

What a waste of food! It's the freaking apocalypse, and she's feeding a cat?

Kelly had had a problem with Paisley for a long time. Jealousy and resentment had been building up inside her for ages.
