On one side were people who'd go to bat for Paisley without a second thought. On the other were the Lennoxes, selfish and cold to the core. Next to each other, the difference couldn't have been clearer.

Rachel's face darkened as they tore into her. She'd never expected Paisley to find people willing to stand up for her after just one night.

Things were spiraling out of control.

No. This might be my last shot at that pendant.

She had to find the right moment, and she couldn't afford to fail.

Jasper was out of patience. He'd been humiliated in front of everyone and cut across the face, and now these people kept mouthing off at him. His rage finally boiled over.

"Shut the fuck up! If you won't apologize, then you can all die!"

He roared and pulled the trigger!

A sharp gunshot cracked through the air, and the bullet flew straight at Orion!

In that split second, Elijah's face went cold, and the lightning he'd been holding back exploded!

Blue electricity burst out everywhere and formed a massive web of lightning that came crashing down on everyone across from them!

With a vicious crackle, wild currents tore through the room and hit Jasper and every one of his men.

They didn't even have time to scream before the current fried their hair black and frizzy. The stench of burnt hair filled the lobby.

The whole group froze in place, numb and unable to move, and every trace of their swagger was gone.

Their guns slipped from their hands, and the bullet dropped out of midair and clattered to the floor.

But the gunshot and the blast of lightning had echoed far beyond the mall.

Within seconds, they heard the growls of countless zombies from the streets outside. The heavy, shuffling footsteps got closer and closer until the floor started to shake.

All that noise had drawn a massive wave of zombies, and they were surging toward the mall!

Elijah barked out an order. "The horde's coming! Everyone, get to the cars now!"

Nobody wasted a second. They sprinted out of the mall toward the cars parked by the road, piled in, slammed the doors, and peeled out of there.

In the back seat, Paisley watched Jasper's sorry-looking crew shrink in the distance. For the first time, a real, wide smile broke across her usually cool face.

When she saw Rachel and the rest of them like that, she felt two lifetimes' worth of anger finally lift off her chest. It was the most satisfying thing she'd ever felt.

In her past life, she'd been all alone. She'd been betrayed and pushed around, with no one to protect her or care about her, and she'd had to scrape by however she could.

But this time, she had a family who'd fight tooth and nail for her and love her no matter what.

So this is what it's like to have people who've got my back.

More zombies were swarming the mall by the second, and staying meant certain death.

Jasper was the first to bolt for the cars, and everyone else scrambled after him.

Rachel sat next to Jasper and kept her eyes glued to Paisley in the car up ahead.

She still hadn't gotten her hands on that pendant, and she wasn't about to give up.

The hard part would be talking that reckless idiot Jasper into it.

If he hadn't lost his temper back there, she'd have the pendant by now.

If she didn't need him to get to his brother, she wouldn't waste a second on a loser like him.

Then again, a guy who thought with his dick was easy to control.

Rachel melted against Jasper, tracing little circles on his chest with her finger, and put on her sweetest, whiniest voice.

"Mr. Quinn, Paisley acts so sweet and innocent. Don't you want a hot little thing like that all to yourself?

"She stole my pendant. Won't you help me get it back? Please?"

Jasper ate it up. Just picturing Paisley's face made him hard, and his hands started to wander.
