Chapter 20

The moment they reached the third-floor atrium, they finally saw what they were dealing with.

A giant money tree towered in the middle of the atrium, several yards tall. Its trunk was so thick it would take two people to wrap their arms around it, and its bark had an oily, sickly green sheen. Gnarled roots stuck out everywhere, buried deep in the marble planter.

Every branch and vine in the building had come from this thing.

The huge tree sensed intruders and went wild. Its trunk shook hard, and countless branches whistled through the air, lashing at the group. This attack was way more brutal than anything downstairs.

"Everyone, watch out!" Elijah barked. Lightning surged around him again, weaving into a huge net of electricity that tore into the vines.

Orion was right behind him, sending out a nonstop stream of metal blades that sliced through branch after branch and held the front line.

Paisley's eyes were glued to the center of the trunk. Thanks to her past life, she knew exactly where the core was.

"There's a plant crystal core deep in the roots! That's what's powering it. Destroy the core, and the whole tree dies for good!"

Before she'd even finished, she pulled a pile of dry rags, rubbing alcohol, and wood shavings out of her dimension. She heaped it all around the roots, and one flick of her lighter sent a roaring fire shooting up.

The flames ate into the roots. The green bark curled up and oozed thick sap, and the trunk shook violently, letting out a weird droning hum. Countless vines lashed out in a frenzy, like they were fighting to the death to protect it.

As the fire grew hotter, the roots weakened fast. All the branches and vines across the mall suddenly went limp.

Paisley seized the moment. She darted forward, landed right in the middle of the planter, and drove her dagger into the base of the trunk, straight into the core.

With a loud crack, the trunk split open, and she dug out a high-level plant crystal core, bright green and clear as glass.

It felt warm in her hand and was packed with pure, natural energy, much gentler than a zombie's core. She could use it to level up her Plant Affinity.

Without its core, the giant money tree went completely still. Its leaves withered and shriveled right before their eyes. Every vine in the mall wilted and dropped to the floor in piles of dead branches and rotting leaves.

Just like that, the atrium went quiet. The danger was over.

Orion let out a breath, staring at the glowing green core in Paisley's hand. "Plants have crystal cores too? I thought only zombies had those."

"Since the red rain, anything can evolve," Paisley explained. "Plant cores are rarer than zombie cores, and they're worth more for leveling up a Plant Affinity.

"Orion, Elijah, can I keep this one?"

Orion didn't mind one bit. Paisley deserved to be spoiled, and whatever she wanted, she got.

Besides, she was the one who'd figured out how to kill the thing. Without her, they wouldn't even have known mutant plants had cores.

Elijah stepped closer and looked at the core, surprised. Then, he gave Paisley a long, searching look.

Somehow, she always knew exactly where a monster's core was. She knew how the mutations worked inside and out, and she fought like someone with way more experience than anyone else. The more he saw, the harder she was to figure out.

Still, the way she'd dug that core out so matter-of-factly had been kind of cute.

"You found it," he said. "It's yours."

Paisley tucked the core into her dimension right away.

In her head, Trovie went nuts.

[You're amazing, Master! A Level Two plant crystal core! It'll level up your Plant Affinity!

[What a jackpot! Master, your luck is seriously unreal!]

Off to the side, the mall employee had collapsed on the floor, still shaking after her close call.

With the money tree dead, Elijah stopped staring at Paisley. "We shouldn't stick around," he said. "Let's meet up with the others and get out of here."

Paisley didn't argue. She followed the two of them, quiet and sweet, with no trace of the ruthless fighter she'd been a minute ago.

Elijah glanced over at her. "How'd the supply run go?" he asked. "Did you get everything you needed?"
