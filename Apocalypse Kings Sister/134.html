Chapter 134

Paisley shot the Lennoxes a cold, sharp look that left no room for argument.

"This is your last warning. If any of you come near me again, I won't go easy on you."

After that final warning, Paisley turned and left with the Thompsons.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan stood there fuming, but none of them dared to stop her.

Rachel stayed where she was, already cooking up something nasty. She swore to herself that this wasn't over.

The group went back to a lounge in the base's rest area. Before long, Elijah came back from his meeting with Rivergate's leaders.

He put away the papers in his hand and turned to the whole team.

"Change of plans. We're staying in Rivergate for a few days to rest up, so we won't be heading out anytime soon.

"You've all got free time for the next few days.

"The mission hall is open to everyone. Team up and take missions, like hunting mutant beasts, scavenging for supplies, or patrolling the defense line. Rack up crystal cores and contribution points, and use the downtime to level up."

Everyone relaxed and started talking about who to team up with. Before long, they all went their separate ways.

The rest area emptied out fast. Elijah looked around to make sure everyone else was gone, then asked Paisley and Orion to stay behind. His face suddenly turned serious.

He walked over to them and lowered his voice. "You're probably wondering why we're sticking around."

The doors and windows of the lounge were shut tight, and the heavy blackout curtains were drawn, blocking out every sound from outside.

Elijah tapped his fingers on the table. His eyes were heavy with worry, and he spoke slowly.

"The aquarium has four underground levels. The lowest one, Basement Level Four, is a virus research lab.

"Right before the apocalypse broke out, the researchers locked away all the original virus data and samples and sealed off the entire lab.

"Now the entrance is blocked by mutants, and the place is crawling with high-level mutant zombies, mutant beasts, and freaky test subjects from experiments gone wrong. Any regular team that goes in there is as good as dead."

Orion frowned. "They've already tried?"

"Three times." Elijah gave a bitter laugh and tapped the rough hand-drawn map spread out on the table.

"Every single time, they lost more than half the team. Some of the ability users still haven't recovered. They were hit with some nasty poison, and nobody can find a cure.

"There's weird radiation down there too, plus a lot of high-level mutant beasts and mutant test subjects.

"Plant Affinity is the only ability the radiation and the virus can't mess with. It can even use the roots underground to scout the whole layout. This is an S-tier mission."

When he finished, both of them turned to look at Paisley.

Paisley gently stroked the cat's soft back. She already had a good idea where this was going. "So what do you need me to do?"

"Paisley, I know what you can do.

"You've got two affinities, Plant and Space. You can purify and block the radiation and the virus, and you can bring back all the research data and equipment without a scratch.

"Our country needs your help, and so do I..."

Elijah looked serious, and his voice turned more earnest.

"I know this mission is extremely dangerous. But this virus data could be the breakthrough humanity needs to fight the zombies. If we can crack it, we might be able to make a broad-spectrum antiviral suppressant, or even an antidote."

He looked deep into Paisley's eyes, his voice firm and steady.

"Right now, you're the only one who's awakened both of these rare affinities. You're the only person who can pull this off.

"When the mission's over, I'll personally request 10,000 contribution points for you.

"And don't worry. I'll stay by your side the whole time, and I'll do whatever it takes to keep you safe. I won't let anything happen to you!"

Orion had been standing quietly off to the side. At that, he looked up, and his usual easygoing look was gone. All that was left was dead seriousness.

He was worried about Paisley's safety, but he knew this was something only she could do.

He couldn't stop her from helping the country just because he was scared for her. But he could stay by her side and protect her.

Orion took half a step forward and looked at Paisley, his face serious.

"Paisley, I'll be there to protect you too. As long as I'm alive, I'll take every hit meant for you."
