Chapter 86

The cars sped smoothly down the road.

Paisley slept for three straight hours.

Along the way, they ran into a few scattered groups of low-level zombies, and everyone on the team was getting stronger fast.

Paisley didn't need to lift a finger against those stray, low-level zombies. The others took them out easily on their own.

It was great practice for sharpening their real combat skills.

She had no idea how long she'd been out when Ashley gently shook her awake.

Ashley kept her voice low, sounding a little nervous.

"Paisley, wake up. Something's going on."

Paisley slowly opened her eyes. The sleepiness cleared right away, and she was sharp and alert.

"What's wrong?"

"The gas station where we're staying tonight is about 500 yards ahead," Ashley said, pointing up the road. Her voice was grim. "But the road's blocked."

Paisley followed her gaze. A bright red warning sign stood in the middle of the road, and barbed wire was strung across the pavement. There was only a tiny gap left, barely big enough for one person to squeeze through.

Four huge, mean-looking men stood next to the sign, each gripping a rifle. They looked savage and violent, the kind of raiders who robbed anyone who came their way.

Rules were spray-painted across the sign in big, blood-red letters.

[Toll: 100 Level Two crystal cores per person.]

Ashley was outraged. "Isn't that just straight-up robbery?"

"Stay in the car for now," Paisley said. "I'll go check it out."

Paisley pushed the door open, got out, and walked over to Orion and Elijah.

They were standing side by side in front of the car. One was looking through a pair of high-powered binoculars, and the other was staring hard at the men blocking the road. They both looked grim.

Elijah lowered the binoculars and spoke in a low voice.

"I can't tell exactly how many there are. Every one of them has a rifle, and they're well armed. Looks like a gang of raiders that's set up camp here."

Orion frowned hard, his voice cautious.

"We don't know how many they've got or how strong they are. Charging in blind and starting a fight is way too risky. We'd be at a huge disadvantage."

He glanced back at the others. "Our team's still too weak."

As soon as he said it, Paisley closed her eyes. Energy rippled faintly around her as she sent out an invisible wave of perception that covered the whole 500 yards ahead.

A second later, she opened her eyes and laid out everything about them, clear as day.

"There are 35 of them. Their leader is a Level Four ability user, the bald guy with the beer belly lying down at the very back. He's the strongest and the hardest to deal with.

"On top of him, there are four Level Three ability users, eight Level Two, and twelve Level One.

"The other ten are regular people with no abilities. They're weak, and all of them are hurt, so they're probably prisoners.

"The only ones we actually need to take out are the 25 ability users."

Orion and Elijah froze at her detailed breakdown. They turned their heads at the same time and stared at Paisley in shock.

She was just standing there quietly, perfectly calm. Without moving or using any equipment, she'd somehow learned everything within 500 yards in a matter of seconds.

Orion's mind was racing.

Just how strong is Paisley?

Everything within 500 yards was inside her range.

She could even pin down every single person's ability level.

A sense that terrifying probably reached way farther than that, too.

Pais really was his pride and joy.

Elijah was just as stunned.

He'd always been proud of his own top-tier strength and sharp mind.

But as he looked at Paisley now, for the first time, he felt a deep sense of caution.

Her power was just insane. There was no telling how deep it went.

Thank God we're not enemies.

If she'd been against them and turned on them anywhere along this road, none of them would've stood a chance of surviving.

The two of them stared at her, still reeling.

Paisley tilted her head, so smug it was almost annoying.

"Why are you guys so shocked? Is it really that hard? Can't you just sense it and know everything?"

Those two breezy little questions left Orion and Elijah speechless. They looked like they were having a mini existential crisis.

They just stood there frozen, with no idea how to react. That one casual question had turned everything they thought they knew upside down.
