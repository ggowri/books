Chapter 71

Elijah hadn't been sitting around these past two days either. He'd snuck out to hunt zombies, and with the Level Two Lightning Affinity core Paisley had given him, his Lightning Affinity had already hit Level Three.

As team captain, he was the first to notice the change in Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, and Madison.

"Now that you've all awakened abilities, our team's a lot stronger. There's no time to waste. We're heading back to Aetheris now."

He glanced at the sky outside and gave the order. "We leave in five minutes."

Paisley didn't have anything to pack. She leaned against the car, relaxed, and slowly petted the cat in her arms.

For the past two days, Rachel had been looking for a chance to steal Paisley's pendant.

Too bad it had poured nonstop, and Paisley had stayed holed up in the villa the whole time.

Now, she finally had her chance.

Rachel walked up to Paisley with a smile, holding out an olive branch.

"Paisley, I came to make peace.

"We're still sisters, after all. There's nothing between us we can't get past.

"I was selfish before, and I did a lot of things that hurt you. Can you forgive me?"

She sounded like she really meant it.

If Rachel's greedy eyes hadn't kept darting to the pendant on Paisley's chest, Paisley might have thought she'd been possessed.

So she's still after the pendant.

The little white cat resting in Paisley's arms picked up on the stranger's prying eyes and hostility right away. It didn't like being bothered for no reason, and its mood soured fast.

It lifted its little head, glared at Rachel with its big round eyes, and let out two grumpy meows. They were soft little cries, but they were clearly a warning to back off.

Paisley gently stroked the kitten's fluffy fur and spoke to it in a soft, soothing voice. "Shh, it's okay. Settle down."

Its master's touch calmed it right down. The little white cat let go of its hostility, curled back up in her arms, and went back to dozing with its eyes half-closed.

Once the kitten was settled, Paisley finally looked up at Rachel. She didn't bother hiding her disgust, and she didn't mince words.

"We don't need to make peace, and you don't need to apologize. Just stay the hell away from me from now on, and we're good!"

Any other day, a brush-off like that would've sent Rachel into a rage.

But today, she didn't get angry at all. The faint smile never left her face, and it was even a little smug.

Rachel couldn't resist showing off.

"Don't be so sure, Paisley. I bet you haven't heard yet.

"That same evening you left me out in the storm, I awakened a Water Affinity. I'm already Level Two.

"And it's not just me. Owen awakened a Fire Affinity these past two days too.

"Paisley, why don't you come back to us? Owen and I are both strong now. We can protect you."

Then, her tone changed, and she sounded a little contemptuous as she tried to put Paisley in her place.

"You're just a regular person with no ability. You're no use to these people, and sooner or later, they're going to dump you.

"At the end of the day, we're your real family. We're the only ones who'll never leave you behind."

It sounded warm and caring on the surface. But every word was really just Rachel bragging about how superior she was and tearing Paisley down.

Paisley just raised an eyebrow. She stayed perfectly calm and took her time answering with a question of her own.

"So by your logic, I'm just a useless nobody who couldn't help at all if things got dangerous.

"Since when are you this nice? What do you want? Just spit it out."

That was the exact question Rachel had been waiting for. Her fake sweetness couldn't hide the greed underneath anymore.

Her eyes lit up as she came right out with it.

"It's simple. Just give me that pendant around your neck, and I'll wipe the slate clean.

"From now on, Owen and I will both look out for you."

Paisley smirked at the ridiculous offer. "You want my pendant?"

She lifted the pendant and studied it closely.

"Rachel, what's so special about this thing, anyway? Why do you want it so bad?

"Either way, this pendant is how my real family found me. Sorry, but I can't give it to you."
