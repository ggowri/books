Chapter 406

"Gerald, tell them to let us go. This is nothing. How could you call the patrol?"

Gerald opened his mouth. He looked at the daughter he had just found, then at his wife, who had endured so much without a word of complaint. He said nothing.

His silence was an answer. It was what the senior branch had always been, selfish and cold, and that had never changed.

The officers didn't waste words. They stepped in, clamped down on Dennis and Chloe's arms, and held them firm.

A moment earlier, the two of them had been sitting at the table, eating their fill, threatening, playing the elder. Now their hands were pinned and they couldn't move.

Their dignity collapsed. Hair a mess, clothes reeking of food, faces gray, they didn't have the nerve to struggle or explain. They looked pathetic and almost funny.

"Take them away!"

They were dragged out the door, stumbling, with no pride left.

The senior branch had fallen apart.

Brenda was in custody for bullying, taking supplies, and disturbing order on the base. Dennis and Chloe had been arrested for harassment, threats, and trying to take private supplies.

The family that had swaggered around the base for years became the biggest joke in the place overnight. Everyone talked about them over dinner, mockingly. They had no face left.

The noise inside the villa died away. The night wind was shut out, the house was calm again, and the smell of dinner lingered.

Diane took Paisley's cool hand, her eyes full of relief. "Thank goodness you were here, Pais. If we'd let them walk all over us tonight, who knows how much more we'd have had to take."

Gerald nodded slowly. The old softness was gone from his face, replaced by clarity.

"My brother changed a long time ago. He's greedy, he bullies the weak, and he's forgotten what family means."

"I held on to our brotherhood for too long and kept giving in. I let you all down. From now on, I won't go easy."

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley exchanged a look. They felt vindicated. The senior branch had earned this. No one pitied them.

The corner of Paisley's mouth curved faintly, cold. A thin chill passed through her dark eyes.

As long as she was a Thompson, nobody was going to bully her family.

******

By eleven that night, the Intensive Care Unit at the base's central hospital was dead quiet.

Grant had lost an arm, still carried leftover poison, and had a fever that kept coming back. Held up by IV nutrients and emergency drugs, he finally forced his heavy eyelids open.

The room was cold and empty. The monitors beeped, and all he could see was a white ceiling and cold equipment.

Chloe wasn't there.

His heart dropped. He fought to move, grabbed the communicator from his bedside, and started calling squad members and friends, desperate to find her.

Call after call, the news hit him like thunder.

Brenda had been arrested. Chloe had gone to beg Jasper Quinn in the middle of the night and been humiliated. Then she and her father had been taken in at Gerald's door. The whole senior branch had fallen and become a joke.

Grant's eyes nearly burst. Fury exploded in his chest, and his face turned red.

His girlfriend, the girl he had shielded, had been publicly humiliated and thrown in a cell while he lay unconscious!

It was Paisley's family. It had to be.

Fine. They had come after his girl while he lay sick.

Rage and heartache crashed together and wiped out the last of his strength.

He gritted his teeth against the pain, ignored the nurses, and pushed himself up like a man possessed, desperate to get out of bed and use every connection he had to get her out.

But he'd lost an arm, the poison hadn't cleared, and his fever kept coming back. His body had nothing left.
