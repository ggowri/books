Chapter 16

The Level Two mutant was tall and wiry, with weirdly stretched-out limbs and pitch-black claws over an inch long. Its skin was hard and tight, and it was more savage than any low-level zombie. It was terrifyingly fast and deadly.

Orion's stomach dropped. Without wasting a second, he pushed his Metal Affinity to full power and swung his machete in a wide slash!

But the Level Two was just too fast. It twisted at an impossible angle and dodged his full-strength blow with ease. Then, its claws whipped through the air toward his shoulder, ready to rip through flesh and bone!

In that split second, Paisley darted out from behind Orion. She sidestepped the zombie's deadly lunge and spun with the momentum. Then, she drove her dagger straight into the crystal core at the back of its skull!

It was one smooth, clean move, without a single wasted motion.

With a heavy thud, the Level Two mutant that had been so vicious a moment ago went stiff. Its limbs jerked once, and it dropped to the ground, dead.

From the moment it attacked to the moment it died, less than two seconds had passed.

Orion stood frozen with his machete still raised, staring in disbelief.

He had an awakened ability, and even if he'd given it everything, he might not have been able to hold that thing off. Paisley had killed it with one strike.

She didn't even use her ability. Just how strong is she?

In that moment, Orion stopped seeing Paisley as someone who needed protecting.

"Thanks for saving me, Pais. If it weren't for you, I'd probably have gotten bitten..." Just thinking about it still made his heart pound.

Paisley just nodded, totally calm, like she'd swatted a fly instead of killing a rare Level Two mutant.

She crouched down and, with practiced ease, pried at the crack in the back of the zombie's skull.

With a soft click, she pulled out a clear, diamond-shaped crystal core with a faint green glow.

Crystal cores were the energy source inside mutant zombies, and the most valuable resource in the apocalypse. They could power up an ability and speed up leveling, and later on, they'd become hard currency.

She tucked the glittering Level Two core into her dimension and wiped the gunk off her dagger, cool as ever.

Only then did Orion snap out of it. He watched Paisley calmly dig around in a zombie's head and couldn't believe his eyes.

"Pais... what are you doing..."

He could be pretty slow sometimes, but even he could tell something was off.

She'd one-shot a Level Two mutant, dug out its core like a pro, and killed with deadly precision. No ordinary girl in the apocalypse should be able to do any of that.

Paisley had her answer ready. "That was just instinct," she said evenly. "When I awakened my Storage Dimension, it boosted my speed and my body too.

"I'm stronger now, and my senses are sharper. That's why fighting zombies feels so easy."

"Besides, zombies are weakest at the neck and the back of the head," she added after a pause, trying to sound casual. "Hit the right spot, and you only need one strike. It's honestly not that hard."

She'd learned every one of those tricks the hard way, in three bloody years of fighting to survive in her past life.

Orion bought it. He figured Paisley was just ridiculously talented and her ability had given her a huge boost. He felt proud of her and sorry for her all at once.

How many hard days had she quietly survived while he wasn't around, to end up this steady and this tough?

Just then, Trovie let out an excited squeal in Paisley's head.

[Master, there's gold up ahead! Real gold! It's top-tier currency, and if you grab it all, we'll rake in a ton of points!]

Paisley perked up and looked where Trovie was pointing.

There was a high-end gold jewelry store at the end of the hallway.

Its glass cases were spotless and full of gold jewelry, gold bars, and gold figurines. Even in the dim, silent mall, they still gleamed.

Once the apocalypse hit, cash became worthless and supplies were everything. But gold was always good. Early on, people traded it for supplies, and later, every major base wanted it.

She just hadn't expected it to be a hot item in cross-dimensional trading too.

"Orion, give me a sec. I want to check that place out." Paisley headed straight for the jewelry store.
