Chapter 124

Then, she picked exactly what she wanted: the energy guns, the full set of radiation-proof combat gear, the storage rings, the smart computer, and the hovercar. With one tap, she confirmed the trade.

[Supply and demand are a close match, and the goods are equal in value. Trade approved!

[Trade complete! Galactic goods and natural goods are now transferring both ways!]

A flash of pale blue light passed between the realms, and just like that, the exchange was done.

Paisley's storage area looked completely different now. A row of high-tech Galactic gear stood neatly lined up.

First, there was a crate of high-precision energy guns with barrels that glowed a cold blue. They ran on crystal cores, and their firepower blew away any gun in the apocalypse.

Next to it hung a form-fitting, high-level radiation-proof combat suit. It was light and breathable, but it could stand up to heavy radiation and powerful affinity attacks.

Five sleek silver storage rings sat beside it. Each one held its own storage space, only 50 square feet, but they were perfect for carrying supplies around.

There was also the latest smart computer, which could connect across realms, scan terrain, and measure affinity and radiation levels.

Last of all was a sleek hovercar that could travel on land, on water, or in the air. Busted roads and rough terrain wouldn't slow it down at all.

Paisley ran her fingertips over the hovercar's control panel, then rubbed one of the cool storage rings, and her smile grew.

She'd made out like a bandit with this trade.

With this high-level Galactic gear, scouting dangerous radiation zones, traveling long distances, fighting, and hauling supplies wouldn't be a problem anymore. The whole team's odds of survival and fighting power had just gone up several levels.

Trovie saw all the new Galactic gear and zipped around the dimension in excitement, cheering at the top of its sweet little voice.

[Congratulations, Master, what a haul! We totally cleaned up on this trade. All this gear is going to come in so handy in the apocalypse!]

Paisley smiled a little. She looked relaxed and at ease.

With a thought, she took a cold, sweet popsicle out of her summer stockpile and held it out to Trovie. It was crystal clear, with a faint chill drifting off it.

[Wow, a popsicle! Thank you, Master!]

Trovie happily took it and nibbled away in tiny bites.

Paisley's voice was soft and warm. [You've worked hard too.]

Trovie kept munching on its popsicle and quickly chimed in.

[Master, you still have one lucky draw left! You could win great items, rare talents, or high-level supplies. Try it now!]

That caught Paisley's attention, and she wasted no time.

[Trovie, start the lucky draw for me.]

[Lucky draw started! The prize wheel is spinning... Congratulations, Master! You won a rare treasure, an Affinity Awakening Pill!]

A pill appeared in her palm out of nowhere. It was crystal clear and wrapped in a faint, pale glow. Its fresh, rich scent drifted up to her nose, gentle and completely pure.

[Affinity Awakening Pill: Guaranteed to awaken a brand-new affinity safely. No side effects and no conflicts with existing affinities. Works for every ability user!]

Paisley was thrilled. Without a second's hesitation, she popped the pill into her mouth.

The pill melted the moment it touched her tongue. A warm, powerful wave of Water energy rushed through her whole body, and a faint, watery glow surrounded her.

Deep inside her, a brand-new ability awakened and took shape. It was clear and gentle, but it packed a serious punch.

It was a Water Affinity! From that moment on, she had three affinities.

Plant Affinity kept life growing and fed everything around it. Water Affinity was steady and lasting, good for both attack and defense, and it could heal and control water too. The two worked together perfectly.

And her Psychic Affinity could control things without anyone noticing.

The instant her Water Affinity awakened, all the moisture in the air around her answered her call. With a flick of her finger, she could gather tiny droplets, and she controlled them with total ease.

With the new power settling inside her, Paisley felt more grounded than ever.

She turned her attention to her Storage Dimension, which had grown to a full million square feet, and a thoughtful look came over her face.

Since the apocalypse began, the land had been poisoned, crops had died, and supplies were running low everywhere. Food and fresh produce would always be in short supply.

But her dimension was huge, and its soil was rich and clean. The 1,000-square-foot spirit pond kept the land well watered and full of energy, and nobody else had anything like it.

All the fruit and vegetable seeds she'd collected would finally come in handy.
