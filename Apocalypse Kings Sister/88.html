Chapter 88

Elijah and Paisley walked side by side down the empty, desolate road. Neither of them said a word, and after five minutes, they stopped in front of the roadblock.

Paisley held the sweet little white cat in her arms and spoke politely, testing the waters.

"Hey, guys! Could you cut us a break and let us through?"

Behind the barricade, the four big men swung their rifles toward the two of them the second they heard her. The tension was thick enough to cut.

The one in charge narrowed his eyes and shamelessly looked Paisley up and down, from her gorgeous face to her curvy body. He didn't hide his lust and greed at all, and his face twisted into a sleazy smirk.

In the apocalypse, they'd gotten used to doing whatever the hell they wanted.

A girl this clean, this beautiful, and this classy was a seriously rare find.

He spoke up, cocky as hell.

"Who are you two? Where are you coming from, and where are you going?"

He pointed at the blood-red sign next to him, throwing his weight around.

"You want to get through here? Read the rules. It's 100 Level Two crystal cores per person, and not one less."

Then, a filthy grin spread across his face, and he licked his cracked lips like a total creep.

"Of course, if you don't have the cores, that's fine too. I don't mind taking payment some other way.

"No cores? Then you can stay here and let us have some fun with you. You can pay with that sweet little body of yours."

The other three men burst into dirty laughter.

Their disgusting eyes stayed glued to Paisley, roaming all over her body.

They were lawless, vicious, and downright revolting.

Next to her, the air around Elijah turned ice-cold.

His usually calm face hardened, and a cold fury came over him. He looked like he was a second away from snapping.

Nothing ever rattled him. But he couldn't stand these filthy thugs looking at Paisley like that, not for one second.

How dare they?

He wanted to rip them apart right then and there.

But just as Elijah was about to make his move, Paisley stopped him.

Instead, she gave the men a sweet smile.

Anyone who knew her would know that the sweeter her smile got, the more trouble someone was in.

Without another word, Paisley gave the little white cat in her arms a light pat on the back. "Your turn," she said coldly.

The cat knew exactly what she meant, and every strand of its snow-white fur stood on end.

The next second, it sprang into the air, so fast it was just a white blur, and pounced on all four men.

Its claws flashed in the light and raked brutally across each of their faces, and blood welled up right away.

The men never saw it coming. Before they could even make a sound, their faces were shredded into a bloody mess.

In midair, the little white cat summoned a swarm of tiny, razor-sharp ice blades. They glittered with freezing cold, aimed perfectly at all four men's throats.

The ice blades whistled through the air in a flash of cold light, and four throats were slit almost at the same instant.

Four heads hit the dusty road and rolled. Their faces were still frozen in terror, and their eyes were wide open, staring at nothing.

Warm blood gushed out, soaking the road red.

In just a few seconds, the four cocky Level Two ability users were dead. They hadn't even gotten a chance to fight back!

The second all four of them were dead, the ice blades disappeared. The little white cat dropped its deadly aura, turned back into a soft, fluffy ball of white fur, and leaped back into Paisley's arms.

It nuzzled her neck with bright, shining eyes and swished its tail. It kept bumping its little head into her palm, looking soft and clingy and begging to be praised. There wasn't a trace left of the vicious killer it had been a second ago.

Paisley looked down at the sweet, clingy little thing in her arms, and her eyes softened. She gently ruffled its fluffy fur, then unwrapped a piece of candy and popped it into its mouth. "Good job!"

The little white cat was even happier after the praise. It curled up in her arms, closed its eyes, and happily ate its candy.
