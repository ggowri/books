Chapter 487

Every plan the hidden enemy had made, every trap he'd set, she would take them all on and settle each one.

The wind turned fiercer. The cold of the Great Freeze covered the whole sky.

The group didn't stay long. They set out again.

The snow kept falling harder.

........

In this life, she had two unmatched Affinities and the combat skills she had forged in blood in her last life. She was no longer the weak ordinary person anyone could push around and betray. She had every ability to protect the people near her. All the old grudges and unsettled debts, she would clear one by one, never missing a single one.

Whatever tricks and ambushes hid in the dark, she would take them all and pay them back with interest.

Wind howled and bit like knives. The thick cold of the Great Freeze covered the world. The light snow had turned into thick, drifting flakes that froze where they landed. The Great Freeze had officially begun.

The temperature crashed. The land quickly turned white, and everything froze and went still.

The team didn't waste a second. They regrouped, secured the prisoners, and set out again. The SUV tore through the snow at full speed toward Aetheris Base.

Snow blotted out the sky. The road ahead was long and dangerous.

******

At the same moment, on the top floor of the central office tower at Aetheris Base, in the highest-security conference room.

The sealed, solemn room was brightly lit, but the air was stifling.

Every senior official at the base was present. The heads of each faction sat in their seats with grim faces. Nobody chatted.

The whole meeting was tense and ran on hidden currents. An attack on the Blackwood family and General Nathaniel Blackwood was ready to begin.

The Quinn faction officials, led by the Quinn family, had sat together early. Dark calculation lay behind their eyes, and confident cold smiles hung on their lips.

The three-day mission window ended today. They couldn't wait to start assigning blame.

Falcon Squad had gone out on the supply run and was overdue and silent. They'd sent no progress and brought back no data. To everyone there, the mission was a clear failure.

They were certain of it. Damon Quinn had personally laid this trap. High-tier beasts to intercept them, human ambushes, broken bridges and roads, trap after trap, and now the Great Freeze had come early.

Falcon Squad had no chance of coming back in one piece. They were probably wiped out, dead from fighting or frozen in the wilds. There was no way they'd make it back alive.

After a silence, an official from the Quinn faction spoke first, in a tone that leaned on the room.

"The Freeze is here. It's the dead of winter. The whole outside world is frozen solid and supplies are gone.

The base stockpile was already thin. Now the supply run has failed, and the food and warmth for countless people and Affinity users this winter has nowhere to come from!"

When he finished, a deputy official picked it up at once, every word aimed at the man in the top seat, General Blackwood.

"The Freeze has come. Everything's frozen. No supplies, no food. What happens to the whole base?"

"No telling how many people will freeze or starve in this."

Then a Quinn family core official raised his eyes slowly, his sarcasm plain, his tone barbed.

"At the end of the day, it's about picking the wrong people and making the wrong call.

If our Quinn squads had been the ones sent out, with our experience and planning, nothing would have gone wrong. We'd have finished the job, and we wouldn't be in this mess."

Question after question, accusation after accusation, the blame was piled onto the Blackwoods.

The faction people chimed in, a wave of pressure that amounted to a coup attempt.

"Time's up. The mission's a total failure. Falcon Squad's been silent this whole time. They're probably dead out there!"

"General Blackwood sits at the top and runs the base. This failure of judgment and oversight has thrown the base into a winter crisis. He has to take the main responsibility!"

"Everyone in this room, and everyone at the base, wants an explanation today! General Blackwood, what do you say to everyone?"
