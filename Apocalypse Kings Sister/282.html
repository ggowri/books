Chapter 282

With that done, she turned and headed back toward the dark entrance of the underground armory.

Elijah followed quietly behind her.

He unscrewed the cap of his bottle and tipped his head back, drinking down half of it.

The cool, sweet water slid down his throat and turned instantly into pure, warm energy, spreading fast through his limbs.

His drained reserves refilled visibly, exhaustion lifting from his body entirely, his overworked channels mending in moments, bringing him back to full strength almost instantly.

This wasn't the first time he'd had it, and he still hadn't asked a single question about it.

Everyone had their secrets, especially now.

The two of them walked into the underground passage, one after the other.

Signs of the earlier battle were still everywhere — cracked alloy shelves, scattered gun parts, dried black serpent blood staining the floor, the air still carrying the faint smell of venom and spent gunpowder.

But compared to the chaos before, the armory now sat dead quiet, only the cold white lighting falling softly across the empty space, making it feel even more hollow.

"You really could've stayed outside and rested," Paisley said evenly as she walked.

"There might not even be anything else in here."

******

Elijah kept pace with her easily, his gaze settling on her slender back, his voice gentle.

"I wouldn't feel right letting you go alone."

No matter how strong she'd become, in his mind she was still the girl who needed protecting.

He never wanted her facing an unknown danger by herself again.

As they walked, Trovie's voice came back online, practically buzzing with excitement.

Host! There's another large storage room up ahead! There's a hidden vault inside, and that's where all the real treasure is!

Paisley's eyes lit up with anticipation, and she headed straight for the storage room.

A few low-level zombies wandered along the way, drawn in by the noise of the earlier fight, their movements stiff and slow. They never got close before Elijah cut them down instantly.

A flash of silver-blue light, and the zombies dropped to the ground, turned to ash.

They crossed through the alloy corridor without resistance, and a massive five-thousand-square-foot grain storehouse appeared before them.

The whole warehouse was packed floor to ceiling with vacuum-sealed oats, flour, grains, instant noodles, and ration bars, stacked in neat, towering rows, bone-dry and untouched by decay, enough to feed an entire country for a year.

"Take all of it."

Thank goodness her storage had expanded, or there'd be no way to haul it all.

Paisley gave the thought a push, and in an instant the entire storehouse emptied into her storage, leaving the massive room bare.

Elijah watched it happen and said nothing.

After clearing out the grain store, the two of them kept moving deeper underground.

At the end of the corridor, a smooth, seamless alloy wall blocked the way, heavy and dense, clearly a disguised vault door.

Paisley raised a hand, letting a thread of energy sweep across the surface. The lock clicked open, and the heavy alloy vault door slowly swung inward.

Inside the vault, five identical, expertly made leather suitcases sat in a neat row, thick and sturdy, sealed tight — clearly built to keep valuables safe.

Paisley stepped forward and flipped one open, and a blinding flash of gold light filled the entire chamber.

The case was packed solid with pure gold bars, flawless and dense, each one heavy and gleaming, dazzling in the light.

All five cases held the same thing, stuffed full of gold.

For once, Paisley's usually cool, composed eyes lit up with genuine delight, sparkling at the sight.

Elijah realized then that she really did love gold.

He so rarely got to see her this happy.

He made a note to find her more of it in the future.

In Paisley's mind, Trovie was practically drooling with excitement, bouncing around.

Host! We're actually rich now! Ten thousand gold bars!

Without hesitation, Paisley pulled the five heavy cases into her storage, clearing the vault completely.

She gave the order.

Convert all of it to points, right now!

Trovie reported back, buzzing with excitement.

Successfully exchanged eleven thousand pounds of gold! Points earned: 100,000!

Large stockpile of survival supplies detected! Bonus 5,000 points!

Current total points: 626,060!
