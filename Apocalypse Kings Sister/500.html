Chapter 500

"My sister is incredible. She awakened Storage Affinity."

"All those supplies just went into her storage dimension. Impressive, right?"

While they were marveling, Paisley had already cleared the entire third floor.

"The fourth floor is electronics. Let's go up."

They climbed to the fourth floor.

Only two or three low-tier zombies were wandering the floor. The group dispatched them easily.

The whole fourth floor was home appliances. Heaters and wall-mounted air conditioners stood in neat rows on the shelves, just as they'd been left before the mall closed.

Paisley didn't hold back. With a flick of her hand, row after row of appliances disappeared into her storage dimension.

In a corner pile of junk she found five intact large diesel generators.

In the apocalypse, those were gold.

A flash of delight crossed her face, and she took those too.

With every last supply collected, they stopped lingering. They turned and headed back down the stairs toward the SUV.

As they reached the second-floor corridor, faint snarls drifted in from far off. Not good. Rachel's group had made it out and was running this way.

The five of them sprinted for the first-floor exit across the icy floor.

Ashley glanced back as she ran and spotted the Lennox group fleeing in a mess. "Hurry, they're coming this way!" she hissed.

Jasper and his two teammates sped up, kicking through snow and ice shards.

Only Paisley was calm. She strolled at the back, steps unhurried, without a trace of fear.

Rachel's group ran for their lives, still bleeding, breath ragged, heads down.

When they passed Paisley, none of them could spare a glance. They brushed her shoulder and kept going.

Owen, Ryan, and the others did the same, head down, running for their lives.

The swarm of Level Five snow rats behind them sensed the overwhelming aura rolling off Paisley and treated her as a no-go zone.

They swung around her, red-eyed and shrieking, and chased the Lennox group, ignoring the girl strolling along.

Paisley stood still and watched the rats pour past her feet. Their white fur carried a cold, coppery wind, yet not one dared come near her.

******

A moment later, Paisley walked out the mall's front doors.

The storm still howled. The world was a vast white blur.

Rachel's group had barely escaped the mall and hadn't had time to relax when the dark mass of Level Five snow rats burst out behind them and pinned them in the open lot outside.

Two groups, one swarm of beasts. A dead end all over again.

Rachel shook all over. Her frozen purple hands gripped the vines she'd formed with her Plant Affinity, her face bloodless.

She'd thought she was safe after sprinting out of the corridor. The swarm had never let up and trapped them again outside.

Chase pushed his Level Five mental blasts harder. His head throbbed and his power was draining fast. Sweat beaded on his forehead and froze into thin ice.

Reid and Owen hacked at the rats pouncing on them, their movements already slow.

Ryan, the most badly hurt, stumbled and fell in the snow. He wheezed weakly and couldn't fight back.

Rachel spotted Paisley standing on the steps above, and a flicker of hope lit her eyes. She ignored the rats tearing at her and cried out.

"Paisley! Help us! You're so strong, you can't just let us die!"

Owen roared through the pain.

"Paisley! You can't just stand there!"

"They're your teammates. You're really going to watch them die?"

Jasper and Ashley had reached the SUV. They heard the shouting and turned, scowling.

Ashley was fighting with her Earth Affinity. She shouted back to Paisley. "Paisley, you wait in the car, I'm just warming up."

Jasper nodded. "They tried to set you up before. They don't deserve your help."

"Don't worry about us. It's good training."

"It's not that easy to kill me."

Paisley stood on the steps. The wind lifted the hem of her down jacket as she looked coolly down at the group in the swarm. Her eyes settled on Ashley last, and her voice cut clearly through the wind.
