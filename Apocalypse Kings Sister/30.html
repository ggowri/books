Chapter 30

The Matriarch reined in her excitement and brushed back the little bone charms by her ear, pulling herself together. But she still couldn't hide how eager she was.

[The Beast Realm is all mountains and forests, and we've got resources for days. We have more animals than we can count, pretty, shiny crystal gems everywhere, and all kinds of rare plants and herbs.

[Tamed beasts, fighting beasts, high-level crystal gems, special herbs, mutant plants... Whatever you want, I'll lead a team into the mountains myself and get it for you!]

She was being humble and totally sincere. All she wanted was to close this deal.

Several tall, handsome beast-men stood behind her, and none of them said a word. Clearly, she was the one in charge of all her mates.

Paisley quickly ran the numbers in her head.

The Beast Realm was full of raw resources, the kind of stuff that was super scarce in the apocalypse.

All those animals she'd mentioned would make great food for the group.

And those pretty, colorful stones? For all she knew, some of them could be gold, high-level beast cores, or rare minerals packed with energy. She might even be able to use them to level up.

Paisley stayed cool. [Bring me some birds we can eat and a batch of your prettiest, clearest crystal gems. I'll take a look and decide if we've got a deal.]

The Matriarch didn't waste a second. [You got it! Hang on!]

She turned and barked orders at her mates, who jumped into action.

Before long, she was back with her people, and they laid out everything they'd gathered in front of the screen.

There was a tied-up cow that looked solid and meaty, plus five lively hens hopping and clucking beside it.

They'd also brought a heap of raw crystal gems in fire red, ocean blue, and sunset purple. The stones sparkled like top-quality diamonds, with faint traces of pure natural energy swirling inside.

Paisley took one look and was quietly pleased. This trade was totally worth it.

The stones especially were way better than she'd expected. They were gorgeous, and a few of the deep purple ones held pure, concentrated energy. Even if they couldn't help her level up, they were still nice to look at.

Paisley didn't hesitate. With one thought, she laid out a huge spread of modern goods from her dimension on the trading panel.

She put up a few of each kind of thing. There were pretty dresses, easy everyday outfits, and soft underwear, plus a set of new lighters, pocket multi-tools, toiletries, mirrors, hair accessories, fine white salt, sauces, vacuum-sealed meals, snacks, and sweets.

It was all so new, practical, and pretty that the people on the Beast Realm side couldn't take it all in.

Paisley's voice was cool and clear. [All of this for the animals and the crystal gems.]

The Matriarch was over the moon. [That's more than enough! Thank you!

[If you ever want to trade again, whether it's livestock, birds, mutant beasts, high-level crystal gems, or rare herbs, I'll get you as much as you need!

[Come find me anytime!]

Then, a bright chime went off. [Cross-dimensional goods verified! Trade complete!]

In an instant, the goods on both sides swapped places.

Suddenly, Paisley's dimension had a healthy cow, a bunch of lively hens, and a big pile of shimmering crystal gems. Their rich energy filled the whole space. Just like that, her supplies had doubled again.

Over in the Beast Realm, piles of brand-new clothes, handy tools, cute little accessories, salt, snacks, and ready-made food appeared on the ground, covering a big patch of it.

The tribespeople crowded around, wide-eyed and amazed, whispering to each other nonstop. The whole tribe was buzzing with excitement.

The Matriarch crouched down and ran her fingers over the soft, smooth fabric of a dress, already in love with it. Then, she picked up a lighter and pressed down on it, just like she'd seen Paisley do.

With a click, a bright little flame leaped up and danced at her fingertips.

Her eyes went wide. She was stunned.

Life can really be this easy?

In the Beast Realm, fire was sacred. Her people called it the holy flame.

The tribe could only get fire from lightning strikes, from fire-breathing mutant beasts, or by rubbing sticks together for ages. It was hard to come by and incredibly precious.

Anyone who could make fire at will was seen as a holy woman blessed by the gods. She'd instantly rank above everyone in the tribe, even the chiefs of other tribes, and her word would be law.

And now, with this lighter, she could summon her own holy flame anytime, anywhere!
