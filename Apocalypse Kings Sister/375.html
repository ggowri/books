Millions of points landed in her account all at once, and a faint smile crossed Paisley's eyes.

That million points was basically found money. Today had been a serious haul.

Meanwhile, deep inside the dark, hushed grain silo, faint, fragmented human voices finally broke through the silence.

Falcon Squad's hostage rescue was drawing to a close, the mission wrapping up perfectly.

In the end, they found the hostages in a hidden basement room at the very bottom of the silo. Ten people in total, including two low-tier Affinity users.

Those two had barely any power to speak of, Level Three at most. Normally they'd be the ones sent to fetch supplies from the warehouse, but they'd had no way to stand against the trumpet flower's lethal toxin.

With no other option, they'd been forced to hide in the silo's sealed basement, waiting for rescue.

Moments later, light spilled out from the silo's dark entrance. Elijah and the others emerged, escorting ten surviving hostages out of the darkness.

The rescued group looked pale and weak, their clothes ragged and caked in dust, eyes full of the fear and relief of narrowly surviving disaster. The looks they gave Falcon Squad were full of genuine gratitude and awe.

The moment they stepped outside, the first thing they noticed was that the massive lake that had covered the area was simply gone, nothing left but an empty crater.

Elijah.

"..."

That was definitely her doing. What in the world did she need an entire lake for?

Orion.

"..."

My sister really is something, pulling an entire lake into her storage space like that.

Ashley.

"..."

Pais is unbelievable. That huge lake just disappears, and apparently she can move mountains and reroute rivers now, too.

Spencer, Thomas, Mason, and Madison were all having similarly stunned reactions internally.

But by now, everyone was used to her endless string of jaw-dropping feats. After a brief moment of shock, they snapped back to themselves, left with nothing but deep, quiet admiration.

With the hostages secured, it was time to head back.

While no one was looking, Paisley conjured a jeep out of her Storage Dimension.

Falcon Squad, well used to this by now, loaded the hostages in with practiced efficiency and set off for home.

Not far away, inside the battered Scarlet Squad vehicle, the mood was miserable.

Nearly the entire team was wounded, at best, poisoned and aching all over, at worst, bleeding from open wounds, every bit of combat strength gone.

Grant, missing an arm and having lost a dangerous amount of blood, had gone pale as paper and passed out completely. The rest of the team was wracked by the trumpet flower's lingering toxin, groaning weakly, struggling just to breathe.

Only Chloe remained unhurt, though her heart was full of bitter resentment, her plans having completely fallen apart.

This wager, the Scarlet Squad had lost in absolute, humiliating fashion. Their team would have to disband.

They'd gone in hoping to force Falcon Squad's disbandment and secure their own place as the base's top team.

Reality had turned out to be the exact opposite.

Falcon Squad had come through without a single injury, completing the mission flawlessly, wiping out every high-tier mutant flower and zombie, rescuing every single hostage.

And the Scarlet Squad, in trying to cheat their way to victory, had lost everything instead: the entire team poisoned, their star captain down an arm, their combat strength gutted, turned into a complete laughingstock.

Per the public terms of the wager, the Scarlet Squad had no choice but to disband, never again to hold the title of the base's flagship team.

Watching Falcon Squad head home in calm, orderly fashion, then looking at her own team's wretched, battered state, Chloe clenched her fists so hard her knuckles went white, her eyes a mix of resentment, regret, and cold calculation.

She'd made up her mind, the second they got back to the city, she'd cut ties with this ruined team and chart a new path for herself.

A crippled Grant, a pack of dead-weight teammates, they'd only drag down her future from here on out.

The two vehicles drove on, one after the other, heading home.

At three in the afternoon, the group made it safely back to base.

The moment they arrived, Falcon Squad headed straight to the mission hall to turn in the mission and collect their reward.

When the mission officer finished reviewing the report and learned that Falcon Squad had wiped out all seven Level Seven mutant trumpet flowers, cleared the entire zombie presence, and successfully rescued all ten hostages, with zero casualties, he was floored, double- and triple-checking the records, visibly unable to believe it.

This trumpet-flower dead zone had plagued the base for ages. Multiple high-tier squads, dozens of Level Seven Affinity users working together, had all tried and failed, suffering heavy losses, no one had ever managed to wipe them out completely.
