Paisley's gaze stayed calm as she laid out her request, unhurried.

[Stock me a warehouse of all kinds of fruit and vegetable seeds, old varieties and new, every season covered. Then another warehouse of fresh fruit and vegetables, at least ten different kinds. I'll give you ten bottles of spirit water for this.]

The modern trader agreed instantly, thrilled, locking in the deal without a second's hesitation.

[Deal! No problem at all!]

This time, the request was simpler, one fewer warehouse, for the same ten bottles.

She'd come out way ahead on this one.

Spirit water was worth far more than seeds, it could strengthen the body, bring health to her family, and whatever was left over could go to a high-end auction, the proceeds more than enough to keep buying premium seeds and maintain a long-term partnership with this trader.

[I'll get everything organized as fast as I can. The trade will be done within a week!]

With everything settled, Paisley tapped the interface and cleanly disconnected, ending this round of trade with the modern timeline.

She turned her attention to the system space, where the traded goods had already been fully logged in.

Three massive warehouses sat neatly in place, stacked floor to ceiling with daily necessities and seeds for every season of fruit and vegetable, rich in variety and in perfect condition, sitting quietly in the spirit-infused space, silent proof of just how lucrative this trade had been.

A faint smile crossed Paisley's eyes.

Spirit water, to her, was nothing more than a byproduct of her system space, cost her no effort at all, an endless, inexhaustible supply.

But to ordinary modern humans, it was a life-saving treasure beyond price, something capable of cleansing the body to its core, curing stubborn illness, and extending lifespan.

Trading her space's spirit water for the most scarce, most essential planting resources and daily supplies of the apocalypse, this was, without question, a guaranteed-profit deal, extremely worthwhile.

Just then, Trovie's crisp, excited voice rang out again.

[Congratulations, Host, on completing a major cross-dimensional trade! System reward: 5,000 points!]

The stark white points panel refreshed in real time, the numbers ticking upward.

[Current total points: 2,430,360!]

Paisley's gaze dimmed slightly as she prepared to exit the system space, when the interface suddenly flashed with a gilded connection notice from the Galactic Realm, carrying that distinct, cold, high-tech sheen unique to that dimension.

[High-tier Galactic trader requesting cross-dimensional connection. Accept?]

A Galactic trader?

Paisley's brow lifted slightly. She tapped the interface and accepted the cross-dimensional connection at once.

The next moment, a cool, clear voice, carrying that same cold, futuristic edge unique to the Galactic Realm, came through the dimensional barrier.

Beside the gold mountain, dense rows of high-tier energy crystal gems, uniform in size, sat stacked neatly.

The crystals were fully transparent, streaks of dazzling light swirling within, holding wild but pure, concentrated energy.
