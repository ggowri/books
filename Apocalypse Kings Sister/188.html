Chapter 188

Paisley had actually pulled off a miracle.

A doctor nearby immediately ran full scans on all of them, and every result actually came back normal.

His hands shook as he stared at the fresh results.

"All their vitals are back to normal. As soon as they wake up, they can be discharged."

He turned to Paisley, overjoyed. "How on earth did you do it? This is a miracle, a real miracle."

The head of the base hurried over, extremely respectful and unable to hide his excitement.

"Ms. Lennox, you're truly amazing! Thank you, and please forgive me for doubting you earlier. You've saved all of Rivergate Base!"

Without wasting a second, he kept his promise and pulled up the base system right then and there. "I'm sending you the 50,000 nationwide contribution points right now!"

As soon as he said it, Paisley's ID terminal chimed, and the 50,000 contribution points landed in her account in a heartbeat.

Paisley was exhausted by now, but when she saw the 50,000 contribution points she'd just received, she finally smiled. "I've kept my end of the deal. Please order everyone here to keep what I did strictly confidential, like we agreed."

"Of course! Absolutely!" the head of the base answered right away.

"Nobody breathes a word about anything that happened today! Anyone who does gets kicked out of Rivergate Base to fend for themselves!"

Everyone present swore to it.

But their eyes lit up brighter and brighter every time they looked at Paisley.

No one wanted to cross someone with both that kind of combat strength and that kind of healing power.

"Then I'll head back and get some rest," Paisley said.

Without another word, she gave everyone a quick nod and left, dragging her exhausted body back to her room alone.

Her whole body ached, and the moment she hit the bed, she collapsed. She took a cup of sweet spirit water from her Storage Dimension and slowly drank it, and the warm energy soothed her drained body a little. Then, her eyelids grew heavy, and she fell into a deep sleep.

She slept hard, straight through from evening to the next afternoon.

When Paisley finally opened her eyes, she felt clearheaded and refreshed, and yesterday's exhaustion was gone.

She got up, washed quickly, and pulled some fresh fruit and snacks from her Storage Dimension, eating a little to refuel.

Just as she bit into a sweet, juicy apple, Trovie's voice rang out in her head, giddy with excitement.

[Master, urgent call! The beautiful interstellar female trader is requesting a remote connection!]

Paisley kept chewing, relaxed and unbothered. [Put her through.]

A moment later, a shimmering holographic screen unfurled in front of her, light rippling across it as the connection went through.

On the other side of the screen was the stunning interstellar female trader. She had delicate features and an elegant air, but right now she couldn't hide how excited she was, and she spoke eagerly.

[Hi! The fresh supplies, clean fruit, seeds, and food I got from you last time are a huge hit in the Galactic market, and we can't keep them in stock! Could we trade again, in bulk this time?]

Paisley ran a finger over the apple's skin, her tone calm.

[I've got fresh fruit, seasonal vegetables, all kinds of seeds, cooked food, and whole-grain staples, all contamination-free and high quality. But what do you have that's worth trading for them?]

At that, the trader's eyes lit up. She shifted, and the view pulled back.

Behind her sat a huge, dazzling mountain of gold. But a faint black haze hung over the surface of the shiny ore, a sign of radiation contamination.

The trader pointed at the gold mountain behind her and explained sincerely.

[After our last trade, I had people search the whole Galactic Realm, and we finally found this gold vein! The only problem is it's all tainted with deep-space contamination.

[In our realm, this refined gold is the core material for high-tier mechs, starships, and primary weapons. It's worth a fortune.

[But purifying it is the hard part. Regular methods only treat the surface, and only high-level purifiers can get rid of the contamination for good. Real top-tier purifiers all have 3S-level psychic power, and hiring them costs a fortune, so we can only purify tiny bits at a time and don't dare mine it in bulk.]

She looked at Paisley curiously.

[I'm curious, though. What do you need so much contaminated ore for?]
