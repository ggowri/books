Chapter 188

A miracle. One young woman had actually pulled it off.

A doctor ran a full scan on each of them at once, and every reading came back normal.

His hands shook as he stared at the fresh results.

“Every vital sign is back to normal. The moment they wake up, they can be discharged.”

He turned to Paisley, his face alight.

“How did you even do that? This is— it's a miracle, plain and simple.”

The Director hurried forward, his manner deeply respectful, his voice unable to hide his excitement.

“Ms. Lennox! You're extraordinary. Thank you—and I apologize for any disrespect earlier. You just saved this entire base.”

He didn't waste a moment making good on his word, pulling up the base system right there.

“I'm transferring the fifty thousand national contribution points to you right now.”

The moment he finished speaking, her terminal chimed, and fifty thousand points landed in her account instantly.

Tired as she was, Paisley looked at the number and finally let a small smile show.

“I've held up my end. For the rest, I'll need you to make sure everyone here keeps this quiet, as agreed.”

“Understood! Completely understood!”

The Director answered at once.

“Everything that happened here today stays here. Not a word to anyone, outside this room or in it. Anyone who breaks that gets thrown out of Rivergate to fend for themselves.”

Everyone present swore to it.

But their eyes kept drifting back to her, brighter every time.

No one wanted to cross someone with both that kind of combat strength and that kind of healing power.

“I'll head back and rest, then.”

Paisley said nothing more and left, her tired body moving on its own momentum.

******

With a quick nod to the room, she made her own way back to her quarters.

Every muscle ached. She collapsed onto the bed, pulled a cup of sweet spirit water from her Storage Dimension and drank it down slowly, the warm energy easing her depleted meridians a little. Then her eyes fell shut, and she dropped into sleep without any fight left in her.

She slept hard, straight through from evening to the next afternoon.

When she finally opened her eyes, she felt clear-headed and refreshed, yesterday's exhaustion gone completely.

She got up, washed quickly, and pulled some fresh fruit and snacks from her Storage Dimension, eating a little to refuel.

Just as she bit into a sweet, juicy apple, Trovie's voice rang out in her head, giddy with excitement.

[Urgent call, Host! An interstellar female trader is requesting a remote connection!]

Paisley kept chewing, unbothered, and gave the order lazily.

[Patch her through.]

A moment later, a shimmering holographic screen unfurled in front of her, light rippling across it as the connection went through.

On the other side stood the same strikingly beautiful interstellar trader, elegant, fine-featured, though right now she couldn't hide her excitement, her voice eager.

[Hello! The fresh, uncontaminated produce, seeds, and prepared food I bought from you last time are selling like crazy on the Galactic market, we can't keep them in stock! Any chance we could open another trade? I'd like to buy in bulk!]

Paisley ran a finger over the apple's skin, her tone calm.

[Fresh fruit, seasonal vegetables, all kinds of planting seeds, cooked food, grain staples—I have all of it, clean and high quality. What have you got to trade that's worth the same?]

At that, the trader's eyes lit up. She shifted, and the view pulled back.

Behind her sat a small mountain of gleaming gold, massive and dazzling, except for a faint dark haze clinging to the surface of the ore—traces of radiation contamination.

The trader gestured at it, her explanation earnest.

[After our last trade, I had people search the whole Galactic Realm, and we finally found this gold vein! The only problem is it's all tainted with deep-space contamination.]

[In our realm, this refined gold is the core material for high-tier mechs, starships, and primary weapons. It's worth a fortune.]

[But purification's the real problem. Standard methods don't get to the root of it—only a true purification master can clear it out completely, and genuine top-tier purifiers are all 3S-tier psychics. Hiring one costs a fortune, so we only ever purify a tiny amount at a time. We don't dare mine it in bulk.]

She looked at Paisley, pure curiosity in her eyes.

[I'm curious—what do you even want with this much contaminated ore?]
