Chapter 144

Inside her dimension, the air was full of energy, and the temperature stayed perfect all year round. Plants grew everywhere, and it was peaceful and beautiful, nothing like the wasteland outside.

She took a long, relaxing rose-and-milk bath and let all her exhaustion melt away, savoring this rare chance to unwind.

Afterward, she changed into soft, clean clothes and stretched out lazily on the sofa. She was just about to close her eyes and rest.

Suddenly, Trovie's sweet little voice went off in her head.

[Master! Three realm traders are trying to reach you at the same time!]

Paisley didn't even open her eyes. "Put them through," she said lazily.

A glowing video screen popped up in front of her right away. The first face on it was Princess Aurelia from the Ancient Realm, dressed in an elaborate royal gown and as poised as ever.

For the past few days, Aurelia had been worn out from personally handling the plague tearing through her kingdom, working day and night. With the remedies and prevention methods Paisley had given her, she'd wiped it out completely, and only now did she have time to call.

The princess looked relaxed now, all that exhaustion gone, and she looked genuinely grateful as she spoke.

"I came to thank you in person. Without your help, countless people in my kingdom would have suffered terribly."

Paisley stayed calm. "No need to thank me. It was a fair trade, and we both got what we needed."

The princess gently shook her head. Her eyes were shining with joy and pride, and she sounded excited.

"Because I stopped the plague, my father, the king, broke with tradition and gave me a seat at court. I'm now the first woman in our kingdom's history to serve as Chief Minister!

"Everyone at court is in awe. Because of you, people finally see what I'm worth."

Paisley smiled, and she meant it. "Congratulations. You've earned every bit of it, and that's amazing news."

After the excitement faded, a troubled look crossed the princess's face. She frowned a little and admitted she was stuck.

"But now there's a new problem. My older brothers don't like how much credit I've gotten or how fast my reputation's growing. So, they've set me up to fail by putting me in charge of the kingdom's food shortage.

"It's a problem that's haunted our kingdom for generations. The soil is poor, disasters hit constantly, and the harvests are terrible, so there's a famine every year and people starve to death in the streets.

"I don't know the first thing about farming, and I have no idea where to start. Could you help me one more time?"

Paisley's interest was piqued, but she stayed cool and collected.

"I can help. But for a problem this big, what can you offer me in return?"

The princess excitedly turned and pointed at five big crates lined up neatly behind her. The lids were open, and they were packed with shining gold bars and gorgeous gemstone jewelry that sparkled like crazy.

She sounded desperate. "If you help me solve this food crisis, it's all yours. This is every reward I got for stopping the plague!"

Paisley looked at the crates full of gold and jewels, and her face brightened.

Next to her, Trovie got so excited it was practically fizzing. Its little voice went up several octaves as it urged her on.

[Master, we're rich! That's at least 5,500 pounds of pure gold, plus tons of rare jewelry and gems!

[This is a total steal, so say yes! A deal like this only comes once in a lifetime!]

Paisley watched the little thing bounce around and couldn't help smiling. "Deal," she said without hesitation.

"Since we've got a deal, I'll go ahead and fix the root of the problem for you."

Paisley tapped the air and sent over a stream of detailed files and seed charts, sounding completely relaxed.

"I've got three crops for you. There's an improved high-yield wheat, a sweet potato that can handle drought and floods, and a super high-yield corn.

"None of them are picky about soil, and they can handle terrible weather. They yield over 130,000 pounds per acre, and they almost never fail.

"I'll send you the whole growing guide, including when to plant, how to care for the soil and fields, and how to deal with pests. I'll also send you every way to cook them, from boiling to roasting, plus how to store them.

"With these crops, your kingdom won't have to worry about famine within half a year. Everyone will have enough to eat, and your food shortage will be solved for good."

The moment she finished, the princess's face lit up with pure joy on the other side of the screen. All the stress and fear that had been weighing on her for days vanished.

She knew better than anyone how bad the food shortage was. The soil was poor, droughts and floods came out of nowhere, and generations of brilliant advisors had never found a fix.

If this is real, my people will never have to worry about starving to death again.

"Thank you! Thank you so much!"
