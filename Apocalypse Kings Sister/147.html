Chapter 147

Paisley flicked a finger, and the energy crystals and shiny gold bars she'd just gotten floated in front of the screen so the trader could see them clearly.

[If you can, I'd like you to find me a lot of these two things. No limit. I'll take as many as you can get.]

The trader took a close look and laughed softly. Without hesitating, she waved her hand, and a stream of colorful Galactic energy crystals poured out of her storage ring and piled up into a small mountain.

She explained patiently.

[These energy crystals are the main power source across the galaxy. Hovercars, starships, and high-level weapons all run on them, and I've got plenty. But they're contaminated, so you'll have to purify them before you can use them.]

Then, she looked at the pile of gold bars, puzzled.

[But these yellow rocks are useless in the galaxy. They don't have any energy, and they're everywhere. Why would you want so many of them?]

Paisley was thrilled inside, but she kept a straight face and stayed calm.

[That's okay, I have other uses for them. Junk or treasure, it doesn't matter to me. I'll take as many of those yellow rocks as you can find, if you don't mind gathering them.]

[No problem,] the trader agreed right away. [I'll send all my people out to gather energy crystals and these yellow rocks in bulk. I'll come back in a few days to finish the trade.]

With everything settled, they quickly swapped goods.

Besides tons of seeds, Paisley also traded a big batch of fresh fruits, vegetables, and natural ingredients from her dimension. There was a huge variety, and all of it was top quality.

The interstellar female got rare natural seeds and clean food, which solved the galaxy's food shortage. Paisley got a huge stockpile of Galactic energy crystals, plus several hovercars, storage tokens, energy guns, and energy swords.

Both sides got exactly what they needed, and they were both thrilled with the trade.

Paisley was in a great mood, and she couldn't stop smiling.

Trovie let out a sweet, over-the-top squeal and bounced around happily. [Ahhh, Master, we're rich! Look at all these treasures!]

[Galactic Realm trade complete! Bonus: 5,000 points!

[Current total: 160,060 points!]

At her feet, the snow-white cat trotted around in little circles, meowing softly over and over like it was begging for attention.

It had also had a big bowl of the rich sacred fungus chicken stew tonight. All that pure energy had leveled it up to mid Level Five, and its energy was purer and stronger than ever. It moved quicker and lighter too.

Paisley looked down at the clingy little thing and tapped the ground. A basket of clear, pure Beast Realm energy crystals appeared, and she spoke casually.

"These are for you. Go somewhere quiet and train, and don't bug me."

The little white cat rubbed against her fingertip, then obediently picked up the whole basket of crystals in its mouth. It curled up on a quiet patch of grass in the dimension and closed its eyes to train.

Paisley looked up at the dimension Trovie had kept so neat and organized.

Her dimension now covered a full million square feet, and everything was laid out neatly and in order.

Half of it was used to store every kind of supply that was hard to find in the apocalypse. Food, clothes, everyday items, weapons, ammo, medicine, and tools were all sorted and stacked in rows, and there was everything she could imagine.

The other half had been turned into a huge farm. The soil was rich, soft, and full of energy, and Trovie kept it neat and beautifully organized.

The farm had an amazing variety of crops, and the orchard was full of all kinds of fruit getting ready to ripen.

There were juicy peaches, crisp, sweet pears, honey-sweet grapes, soft bananas, plump mangoes, refreshing watermelons, tiny cherries, tangy strawberries, fragrant pineapples, and juicy blueberries. More than a dozen kinds of fruit were growing in the rich soil, with thick, lush leaves.

The vegetable patch was even greener and full of life.

There were fresh greens, glossy spinach, crisp lettuce, firm cabbage, tender Brussels sprouts, long green beans, plump tomatoes, bright red peppers, sweet, crunchy cucumbers, fluffy potatoes, sweet parsnips, button mushrooms, and meaty portobellos. Every everyday vegetable she could think of was there.

On top of that, a big stretch of flat, fertile farmland had been neatly divided up, and rows of wheat stood tall and healthy.
