Chapter 175

The little willow on Paisley's shoulder swayed its leaves, scattering tiny flecks of green light, as if answering her. It clearly adored her and depended on her.

It was a sweet, heartwarming sight, but it left everyone else staring in disbelief.

They all froze where they stood, so shocked and jealous they couldn't look away.

Was that really the same vicious mutant willow from a minute ago? How could it get so small?

They'd just seen with their own eyes how powerful this mutant willow was. On the battlefield, its branches had locked down the Level Six boss, devoured all its poison energy, and sucked the whole field of mutant carnivorous flowers dry. It had used their energy to shoot all the way up to mid Level Six, fierce and terrifyingly strong.

But who could've guessed that such a fierce, overpowered tree would turn so sweet and cuddly after leveling up? The contrast was so extreme it left everyone stunned.

They were jealous, too. Who wouldn't envy Paisley?

Her own strength was off the charts, and even her bonded companion was this powerful, grew this fast, and was ridiculously cute and sweet on top of it.

Who wouldn't want a companion like that?

Just as everyone was green with envy, a streak of white darted over.

The little white cat trotted lightly back to Paisley. Its round, sapphire-blue eyes narrowed as it glared at the mini willow on her shoulder, clearly annoyed and jealous.

It was supposed to be Paisley's favorite, most loyal companion. Now, out of nowhere, a tree was trying to steal her attention, and the cat bristled with hostility.

The little white cat arched its back, its fur puffing up, and swatted at the dangling willow branches with a tiny paw, haughty and challenging.

The willow wasn't backing down either. Its slender branches curled out and brushed teasingly across the cat's head, refusing to give an inch.

In seconds, the cat and the tree were locked in a tiny turf war. They swatted and swished back and forth, never attacking for real, just squabbling like spoiled kids fighting for attention, playful and hilarious.

As the two little troublemakers got more and more worked up, Paisley pressed a hand to her forehead and pulled two fruit lollipops out of her Storage Dimension.

She held one up to the little white cat's mouth and gently tucked the other between the willow's branches.

"That's enough, you two. There's one for each of you."

The sweet scent spread, and the squabbling cat and tree settled down right away.

The little white cat happily licked its lollipop, eyes half-closed in contentment, and forgot all about the fight.

The willow swayed gently, candy tucked in its branches, leaning docile against Paisley's shoulder, perfectly behaved.

The others watched the sweet, heartwarming scene and grew even more envious.

The cat and the tree were both so smart and adorable, and so easy to win over. One lollipop was all it took to make them happy and well-behaved.

Companions this smart and this in tune with people were incredibly rare.

Ashley's eyes lit up with curiosity, and she leaned in closer. "Paisley, can I pet them?" she asked softly.

Paisley nodded and gently set the soft little white cat in Ashley's arms. "Don't worry, it doesn't bite."

Given the go-ahead, Ashley beamed. She carefully cradled the fluffy little white cat and ran her fingers through its soft, snowy fur, which was so warm and silky that she couldn't stop petting it.

The others crowded around too, curious, taking turns gently petting the cat.

Far from minding all the attention, the little white cat meowed softly over and over, rubbing against everyone's palms and turning on the charm. It was so cute it melted their hearts.

On Paisley's shoulder, the little willow swayed its tender green leaves too, almost like it wanted in on the affection.

Thomas quickly gathered up a few intact petals from the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower, still humming with a little leftover energy, and spoke up in a serious voice. "We should go rescue the hostages."

Everyone snapped back to reality and headed to where the hostages were trapped.

The five or six hostages were scared and haggard. After being trapped for days, they were worn out and had nearly given up hope.

When they saw Paisley's group walking toward them, their eyes lit up. Life came back into their dull, hopeless faces, and tears poured down their cheeks.

"S-save us! Please save us!"

"We thought we were done for. Thank you, thank you so much!"
