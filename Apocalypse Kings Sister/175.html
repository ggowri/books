Chapter 175

The little willow on her shoulder swayed its leaves, scattering faint flecks of plant-light, responding like it understood, radiating affection and attachment.

The scene was oddly heartwarming — and it left everyone else completely stunned.

They all froze where they stood, torn between shock and open envy, unable to look away.

Wasn't that the same vicious mutant willow from a minute ago?

It can get that small?

They'd just watched this tree's full power on display — vines locking down a Level Six boss, devouring an entire field's worth of toxic energy, surging to mid Level Six off the back of that kill. Ruthless. Terrifying.

And now, after leveling up, it had turned into this soft, adorable little thing.

The contrast hit everyone hard.

And it left them more than a little jealous.

Who wouldn't envy Paisley?

Not only was her own strength absurd — her bonded companion was just as powerful, grew just as fast, and was unbearably cute on top of it.

Who wouldn't want a companion like that?

Just as the envy peaked, a streak of white shot past.

Kitty came bounding back to Paisley's side, sapphire eyes narrowed, locked on the little willow perched on her shoulder, visibly annoyed and jealous.

She was supposed to be the favorite, the most devoted companion — and now there was a tree competing for the spot. Hostility flared instantly.

Kitty arched her back, fur bristling, and took a lazy swipe at the dangling willow branches, pure attitude.

The willow wasn't backing down either, its slender branches flicking out, brushing teasingly across Kitty's head, refusing to yield an inch.

A tiny turf war broke out on the spot — paw swipe, branch flick, back and forth, nothing dangerous about it, just two spoiled companions squabbling for attention, equal parts ridiculous and adorable.

As the two of them escalated, Paisley rubbed her temple and pulled two fruit lollipops from her storage.

She passed one to Kitty's mouth and tucked the other between the willow's branches.

"Enough. You both get one."

The sweetness hit instantly, and both troublemakers went quiet at once.

Kitty licked happily at her lollipop, eyes narrowed in satisfaction, dispute forgotten.

The willow swayed gently, candy tucked in its branches, leaning docile against Paisley's shoulder, perfectly behaved.

Watching the soft, heartwarming scene, the envy in the group only deepened.

A cat and a tree, both this intelligent, both this easy to win over — one lollipop and they were both perfectly content.

Companions this smart, this attuned to people, were almost unheard of.

Ashley's eyes lit up with curiosity, and she leaned in, asking shyly.

"Paisley, can I pet them?"

Paisley nodded easily, lifting the soft, content cat from her arms and handing her over.

"Go ahead. She won't bite."

Given the go-ahead, Ashley's whole face brightened, and she cradled the fluffy cat carefully, running her fingers through the soft white fur — warm, smooth, impossible not to love.

The others crowded in too, each taking turns, gently petting Kitty's fur, fascinated.

Far from minding the attention, Kitty leaned into it, meowing softly, rubbing against every hand, showing off shamelessly, melting hearts left and right.

On Paisley's shoulder, the little willow swayed its tender green leaves too, almost like it wanted in on the affection.

Thomas reached down and gathered a few intact flower petals from the Level Six flower, still humming faintly with residual energy, and spoke up.

"We should go get the hostages."

Everyone snapped back to focus, heading toward where the hostages were held.

Five or six people sat there, exhausted and visibly shaken, days of captivity wearing them down to nothing.

The moment they saw Paisley's group approaching, their eyes lit up, the dead look in them replaced instantly with something alive again, tears streaming freely.

"Please — help us!"

"We thought we were done for. Thank you, thank you so much!"
