Chapter 181

She could easily take them to the base's supply exchange and swap them for crystal cores.

Paisley glanced out the window. The sun was sinking, warm orange light spilling over the base's high walls and rooftops. Dusk had settled in.

Are Orion and Elijah back from their mission yet?

Conveniently, there was a supply exchange right next to the mission hall, so she could stop by and trade for a batch of crystal cores on the way.

There was no time to waste.

Paisley reined in her sharp aura and stepped out of her Storage Dimension. Her eyes were clear and calm, and she looked cool and composed.

She smoothed her clothes with one hand, then turned, pulled open the door, and headed for the base's supply exchange at an easy pace.

The mission hall was still packed, no quieter than it had been during the day.

News of her group clearing the SS-tier Level Six carnivorous flower mission had already spread across the whole base. To everyone there, Paisley was now a genius, an idol, practically a legend.

The moment Paisley appeared in the exchange area's hallway, the noise around her died down almost by reflex.

Affinity users passing by turned to look, and even people busy with their own things glanced her way, openly admiring, envious, and a little nervous.

Several who were close enough worked up the nerve to smile and step forward, their greetings warm and genuinely respectful.

"Hi, Paisley! You're a legend!"

"Congrats on finishing that Level Six high-risk mission! That was amazing!"

"You were incredible today. You didn't just kill the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower, you saved the hostages too. That's awesome!"

Everyone looked excited, and they meant every word.

Paisley gave a small nod at their warm greetings, a faint smile on her lips. "Thank you," she said gently.

Her short, soft reply was cool yet gentle, and it left the people who'd come up to her thrilled and flattered. They admired her even more.

Further back, a crowd of younger Affinity users and base newcomers kept their distance, too intimidated to approach, only daring to steal glances from afar.

When anyone accidentally met her eyes, they quickly looked down, flustered, the backs of their necks turning red. They looked shy and stiff, like they'd just seen an idol far out of reach, their hearts racing and their breathing quiet.

Men and women alike clearly saw Paisley as their idol.

"Oh my God, I can't believe I'm this close to Paisley..."

"She's even prettier in person than people say. Her vibe is unreal, so cool but so gentle."

"She's the badass who took down a Level Six mutant carnivorous flower, and she's this sweet and this pretty too? That should be illegal..."

Hushed gasps and whispers rose all around, and everyone's eyes stayed glued to her in awe and admiration.

By the time she'd walked the length of the corridor, everyone in it had turned to watch her pass, awestruck.

Paisley kept her expression easy, her stride unhurried, and walked straight to the exchange window.

The staffer at the window looked up. The moment he saw it was her, his eyes lit up, and he immediately sat up straight, extremely respectful.

"Ms. Lennox, are you here to trade? What would you like to exchange?"

Paisley nodded, thought for a second, and then pulled a bunch of plump, glossy, purple-black grapes out of her Storage Dimension, setting them on the counter.

Every grape was round and full, with shiny, translucent skin and a faint, fresh energy around it. In this ruined, barren apocalypse, they were incredibly precious.

Paisley's voice was calm.

"How many crystal cores can I get for these grapes?"

The staffer didn't dare take anything she handed over lightly. He pulled out the base's precision scanner at once and leaned in carefully to run a full check on the grapes, his expression wary.

A few seconds later, the scanner screen lit up with clear green data, and a mechanical voice rang out.

[Item scanned: fresh energy grapes

[Contamination: 0 (fully purified, no apocalypse toxins left)

[Energy content: 35%

[Effects: quickly restores stamina, nourishes the body, and eases Affinity fatigue. Eating them regularly gently strengthens Affinities.]

The staffer read all the data and gasped, unable to hold back a low cry.
