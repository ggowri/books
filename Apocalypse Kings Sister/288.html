Chapter 288

Without wasting more time, Paisley gripped the Frostbloom Sword, her eyes sharpening, and swung a single clean strike straight into the heart of the mountain's rock face.

A booming crack tore through the mountains, the force of her strike sweeping across the whole slope.

The solid, heavy stone shuddered and split along its natural vein lines, shattering into countless chunks of rock and raw ore, scattering across the ground.

She gave a quiet mental order.

Trovie, this is all yours to sort and convert. One whole vein should be plenty to hit the next level.

Trovie answered eagerly.

Got it! I'll make sure we get every last bit!

Yes, Host! I promise I won't let you down.

Starting the scan! Bulk-collecting all vein resources!

Trovie kicked into high gear, countless stones and buried ore deposits rising up continuously, pulled straight into storage, exchange chimes ringing nonstop.

[Ruby exchanged for 6,000 points]

[Topaz exchanged for 8,000 points]

[Emerald exchanged for 30,000 points]

[Amethyst exchanged for 50,000 points]

[Sapphire exchanged for 80,000 points]

[Total points from this exchange: 174,000]

[Current total points: 1,007,060]

Trovie's voice rang out, bright and triumphant.

Congratulations, Host! Points threshold reached! You've successfully upgraded to Level Four trader, unlocking the fourth trader channel!

Matched trading partner found: a merchant from the 21st century, pre-apocalypse era.

Paisley's eyes brightened, and she was just about to accept the connection when, deep in the quiet mountain forest, the swaying grass stirred, and a faint, soft whimper drifted through the air.

It was a stunning mutant fox, small and delicate, its build slight and graceful.

Its fur was a pure, vivid red from head to tail, smooth and glossy without a speck of dust, except for a patch of snow-white fur across its chest and belly, the red and white contrast striking and beautiful.

Its eyes were the most remarkable part, clear and bright, a brilliant translucent red like polished rubies catching sunlight, carrying none of the savage, mindless hostility typical of other mutant beasts in this world.

Its red eyes watched Paisley nervously, the intruder in its territory, wary but not hostile — gentle, timid.

Paisley met its clear, pure gaze, and her chest softened instantly. She'd fallen for this intelligent, graceful little creature at first sight.

Trovie's scan chimed in her mind.

Host, this is — a Level Seven mutant red fox!

It's been absorbing energy from this jade vein for years, which explains the high level. Fire-type.

Paisley's heart gave a small jolt.

She hadn't expected it to be a Level Seven mutant fox.

******

Paisley didn't disturb it. She stopped collecting ore and turned to leave.

But she hadn't gone more than a few steps before light, careful footsteps followed behind her.

The little red fox crept cautiously out of the grass, its red coat catching flickers of light through the trees, keeping a careful distance several yards back — not too close, not too far.

Paisley paused and turned to look back.

The fox froze immediately, standing perfectly still, blinking its red eyes nervously, still wearing that same shy, timid expression.

Something in Paisley's chest softened further. She crouched down slowly, pulled out a piece of jerky, and held it out, her voice gentle.

"Come here."

The rich scent of jerky drifted toward its nose, and sensing no danger, the fox's wariness melted away. It trotted forward in quick, light little steps, pitter-pattering toward her.

Still timid, it hesitated right before reaching her, glancing up warily, then carefully stretched its neck, its small pink tongue darting out to lick the jerky before taking small, careful bites.

Watching it eat was unbearably cute, its tiny mouth working fast, red fur fluffing with each movement, soft and full. Paisley's heart melted completely.

It seemed like it had never tasted anything this good before, its eyes glossing over with something like contentment.

It finished the whole piece in a few bites, then nuzzled against her fingertip, its warm, soft touch gentle and affectionate against her skin.
