Chapter 165

The threat of death bore down on her. Rachel shuddered, panic flooding her face.

She didn't have time to think. Her survival instinct kicked in, and she dodged to the side, then grabbed Owen, who was closest to her, and yanked him forward without a second's hesitation.

Owen stumbled, caught off guard, and found himself suddenly square in the zombie's line of attack.

He stared in disbelief at Rachel, who'd just thrown him to the wolves, and a chill ran through him.

This is the sister I protected and spoiled my whole life? How dare she?

In that life-or-death moment, he had no time to argue. He unleashed his Level Three Lightning Affinity with everything he had.

Purple lightning crackled around his body. He rode the force of the blast, rolled sideways, and barely dodged the zombie's deadly lunge, escaping death by a hair.

Reid and Ryan had been burned by Rachel's habit of dumping the blame and using people as human shields more than once, so they'd been on guard. The moment danger struck, they'd stepped back without thinking and kept their distance from her, so neither of them ended up as her shield.

Owen had barely escaped death. His face turned dark, lightning still swirling around him, and he was seething.

Rachel panicked, terrified he'd blame her. She quickly hid her cold, selfish streak behind a frightened, guilty look and rushed over to apologize in a soft voice.

"Owen, I'm so sorry! I panicked and didn't even see it was you, so I swear I didn't mean to pull you. I'm just so glad you're okay!"

She played it as humble and guilty as she could, acting shaken and full of regret to cover up how she'd just deliberately sold out her own brother.

Kelly, meanwhile, wasn't about to risk her own neck for anyone. Once she saw how outmatched they were against a Level Five zombie, she bolted for the parked SUV.

She yanked the door open, jumped in, and leaned out to yell at the other four. "Hurry, get in! We have to go!"

That snapped the other four out of their panic. With death breathing down their necks, nobody dared to stick around, and they scrambled toward the SUV, tripping over themselves, and piled in.

But the Level Five zombie wasn't letting its targets go. It chased right behind them, its heavy footsteps kicking up dust, its vicious roars exploding right in their ears.

The moment they slammed the doors shut, the zombie leaped up and smashed its huge, clawed paw down on the roof. With a deafening bang, the solid roof caved in and crumpled flat. The whole SUV shook violently, and the windows burst, showering glass all over the inside.

Kelly didn't hesitate. She floored it, tires screeching and smoking, and the SUV shot forward, desperately trying to outrun the thing chasing them.

The Level Five mutant zombie stayed right on their tail. It lunged and clawed at the SUV again and again, leaving deep gouges along the body and ripping off the tailgate and mirrors.

Inside the SUV, it was total chaos. Flying glass had cut their arms and faces, and every violent bump threw them against each other. They were all bleeding and in terrible shape.

They drove like maniacs for more than a dozen blocks before they finally shook off the Level Five mutant zombie.

The SUV slowed and pulled over in a quiet corner of the ruins. The danger had finally passed, and all the tension in the car exploded.

Owen's face was dark, and he was the first to blow up.

There were still bloody cuts from the glass on his arm, and lightning flickered restlessly around him. He glared at Rachel, his voice cold with disappointment.

"Rachel! You yanked me in front of that zombie on purpose. What the hell is wrong with you?!"

"I always thought Paisley was the cruel one, always picking on you. She told us over and over it wasn't her, and I still took your side, no matter what.

"I thought that even though you grew up in the country, you were sweet and innocent. But now I can see that's not true.

"Maybe Paisley was right all along, and we wronged her. You're the one who's really cruel."
