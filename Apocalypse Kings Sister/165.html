Chapter 165

The pressure of imminent death crashed down, and Rachel's whole body shook, eyes flooding with panic.

She didn't have time to think. Pure instinct took over. She twisted sideways and shoved the person closest to her — Owen — straight into the thing's path.

Owen stumbled, caught off guard, and found himself suddenly square in the zombie's line of attack.

His eyes went wide with disbelief, staring at the sister who'd just sold him out without a flicker of hesitation. Cold settled deep in his chest.

This is the sister I spent years protecting. Spoiling. Loving.

How could she.

No time to argue, no time to process it. Survival instinct took over, and he unleashed his Level Three Lightning Affinity.

Crackling violet light wrapped around him, and the force of the burst threw him clear of the attack at the last possible second. A narrow escape.

Reid and Ryan, who'd both been burned before by Rachel's habit of throwing people in harm's way to save herself, had already stepped back the moment danger appeared, putting distance between themselves and her.

With the immediate threat survived, Owen's face went hard, lightning still crackling around him, fury simmering beneath the surface.

Rachel's stomach dropped, afraid he'd turn that anger on her. She dropped the cold calculation from her eyes instantly, replacing it with panic and guilt, rushing to apologize.

"Owen, I'm so sorry! I panicked — I wasn't thinking straight, I didn't even realize it was you, I swear it wasn't on purpose! I'm just so relieved you're okay!"

She kept her posture small, playing the part of someone wracked with regret, trying to bury what she'd actually done.

Kelly, meanwhile, wasn't about to risk her own neck for anyone. Seeing how outmatched they were against a Level Five zombie, she bolted for the parked SUV.

She yanked the door open, jumped in, and leaned out to yell at the other four.

"Get in! Now! Drive!"

That snapped the rest of them out of their panic. Death closing in, no one wasted another second — they scrambled, stumbling, toward the car and piled in.

The zombie, locked on and relentless, kept coming, footsteps shaking the ground, its roar right on their heels.

The second the doors shut, it leapt, slamming a claw-studded paw down on the roof.

Bang —

The impact caved the roof in, metal crumpling, the whole car shuddering, windows shattering, glass raining down inside.

Kelly didn't hesitate. She floored it, tires screeching, and the SUV shot forward, trying to outrun the thing chasing them.

The zombie stayed locked on, clawing at the car again and again, tearing deep gouges into the body, shredding the rear bumper and mirrors.

Inside, chaos. Glass shards cut arms and faces, bodies thrown around with every violent bump, everyone bleeding and bruised.

They drove flat out for a dozen blocks before finally losing it.

The car slowed and pulled over in a quiet ruin, the tension in the cabin finally breaking — straight into an explosion of anger.

Owen's face went hard, the fury he'd been holding back finally surfacing.

Glass cuts still bled on his arm, lightning sparking faintly around him, eyes locked on Rachel, voice cold and disappointed.

"Rachel. You shoved me in front of that thing on purpose. What's wrong with you?"

"I used to think Paisley was the cruel one, always picking on you. I never believed her, no matter how many times she said it wasn't her fault."

"Because I thought you were simple, good-hearted, even if you grew up somewhere rough."

"But now I'm not so sure."

"Maybe Paisley was telling the truth the whole time. Maybe we wronged her."

"Maybe you're the cruel one."
