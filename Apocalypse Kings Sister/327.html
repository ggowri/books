Chapter 327

The vines tightened, locking down every subject's movement completely. No matter how hard they thrashed or tore, there was no breaking free.

Right after, she unleashed a burst of ice, frost sweeping across the room, racing along the vines and locking down flesh and joints, sealing their bodies in solid ice.

Gabriel's eyes sharpened, and he seized the opening instantly. His Level Six Metal Affinity condensed into a long, razor-thin blade of gold, and he flashed forward to the nearest frozen subject.

The blade struck true, driving straight into the forehead, clean and efficient, slicing through flesh in one motion. He hooked his fingers in and pulled out a small, dark-red crystal core, pulsing faintly with energy.

The instant the core left its body, the subject's thrashing stopped dead. Whatever strange energy had been sustaining it collapsed completely, and the flesh shriveled fast, the whole body crashing to the floor, lifeless.

"Finally, something that actually kills them!"

The team leader shouted, spirits lifting instantly.

Elijah moved through the chaos, every strike landing precisely on a frozen subject's forehead, pulling cores with brutal efficiency. One by one, the unkillable subjects dropped and stayed down.

The tide of the battle turned completely.

With a method that actually worked, the remaining dozen-plus mutants were cleared out in short order.

The group caught their breath for a second before remembering the team still trapped below.

They didn't waste time, moving fast through the wreckage of rusted equipment and shattered glass, heading toward a sealed inner chamber.

A thin stream of gray-black mist seeped from the gaps around the alloy door, colder and heavier than the poison from the battle outside. The door hung slightly open, the room beyond dead silent, a stillness that set everyone's nerves on edge.

The moment they pushed through, the blood in their veins seemed to freeze.

A single figure stood at the center of the room.

It wore a lab coat, its face eerily human, refined features, skin pale as paper, except for its eyes — pure black, no whites at all, radiating the kind of cold contempt someone might feel looking down at insects.

If not for the serpent tail trailing behind it, it would have passed for human at a glance.

This thing was far beyond any mutant they'd encountered so far — the true overseer of this entire facility.

It didn't radiate violence the way the others had. Instead it carried something worse — the calm, absolute authority of something that controlled life and death, able to command every mutant in the building.

Chase, Rachel, and the rest of their team lay collapsed on the floor, eyes shut, faces a sickly blue-gray, already unconscious, breath barely there.

Faint black mist clung to their bodies, their channels locked down by poison, Affinities completely sealed off. They had no way to fight back.

Worse, the medical cots and restraint equipment lining the room's walls were all active, cold metal clasps gleaming — this thing clearly intended to use the unconscious group as fresh material for genetic experimentation.

The mutant in the lab coat lifted its gaze slowly, black eyes sweeping over Paisley's group, the corner of its mouth curling into something cold and unsettling. Its voice came low and lazy, dripping with cruelty.

"You really do have a death wish, coming all the way in here."

It stepped forward, fingers trailing idly along a cold operating table, its tone turning sharp and merciless.

"Since you're here, I suppose you'll all be staying."

The moment it finished speaking, it hit a switch.

The entire sealed chamber flooded instantly with thick black gas, pouring in through the vents and the cracks in the floor, rolling in dense waves, carrying a brutal corrosive and paralytic effect that filled the room in seconds.

The gas targeted an Affinity user's channels and mind specifically — even a high-tier Affinity user, hit with even a trace of it, would lose their strength instantly, their mind going numb, leaving them helpless, ready to be harvested.

Within two breaths, Gabriel, Elijah, and Orion all went rigid at once, their limbs giving out, their whole bodies turning weak and useless, not even enough strength left to lift a hand.

Orion's legs gave out and he crashed to the floor, his breathing shallow and ragged, his eyes locked on Paisley nearby, voice trembling with raw regret.

"I'm so sorry… I shouldn't have brought you somewhere this dangerous. I failed. I couldn't protect you."
