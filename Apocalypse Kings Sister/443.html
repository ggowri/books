Chapter 443

One cat, one fox, ice and fire together, drove back the mutants and the zombie horde.

At the edge of life and death, the wounded gritted their teeth, stood up, and fought on.

Paisley called on her Plant Affinity.

Thousands of knotted vines tore up from beneath the ground, branches crossing, thorns everywhere. Every barb on the vines was soaked in strong paralyzing toxin.

The endless vines coiled, stacked, and closed in like ten thousand snakes, layer on layer, packed tight. In midair they wove into a huge vine cage and locked the peak Level Seven serpent mutant inside.

The serpent roared with rage. Its tail lashed at the cage, and its poison claws tore at the branches. The wall of wood shook and cracked, but it grew back just as fast and squeezed tighter, holding it in place.

Paisley's eyes flashed with cold light. She spoke one word, her voice as frosty as ice.

"Go."

Mutant flame rose from her palm. It wasn't ordinary Affinity fire but a fire that burned everything. Its core was a dead, pale white, not blazing and bright like normal flame, but with an eerie, annihilating power that devoured poison and dissolved a beast's very source.

Violet flames poured through the gaps in the cage, filling the whole sealed prison, wrapping the raging peak Level Seven serpent in fire. The extreme, scorching heat burned its flesh layer by layer and boiled off the black venom mist flowing from its skin.

The exhausted Falcon Squad watched the scene that overturned everything they knew. Every one of them froze, shocked.

Ashley was the first to speak. Her eyes were wide on the strange violet fire in the air, her voice full of disbelief.

"Fire... Fire Affinity?! Wait, Paisley only had Storage and Plant, didn't she? When did she awaken Fire?!"

"She awakened three Affinities? That's so amazing!"

Thomas stood rooted to the spot, the blood at the corner of his mouth stark against his skin. His voice was full of shock.

"That's not ordinary Fire Affinity. I've seen the flames of Fire users. None look like that cold violet fire. It's so strange, it doesn't fit any normal Affinity."

Orion gripped his hot rifle hard, his eyes full of awe and unease.

"Pais has Fire Affinity too!"

"She's a rare triple-Affinity user! Incredible!"

Even a double-Affinity user was one in a million. A triple was unheard of.

Spencer's shoulder throbbed, but he ignored the pain, his heart pounding with awe.

"Pais is just that good!"

Madison looked at Paisley with deep envy. Her own Water Affinity had almost no attack power in an emergency. If only she had two Affinities!

Mason lay on the ground clutching his stomach. Watching, he felt nothing but admiration for Paisley.

Elijah knelt on the ground, his chest wound torn open, and his eyes were locked on the slender figure fighting alone in midair. His heart squeezed tight.

He didn't say a word, but his eyes never left her.

A storm rose in everyone. None of them knew when Paisley had awakened Fire Affinity.

She was like a favorite of heaven. Surprises just kept coming for them to discover.

They hoped, desperately, that her mutant flame could really kill the serpent mutant.

But the real counterattack was only beginning. The cage was full of killing intent, with fire burning and thorns binding it. Fire and wood pinned it together.

The scene they'd pictured, the serpent burned to ash in an instant, didn't happen.

This peak Level Seven serpent mutant had evolved a terrifyingly tough body. Add its absurd regeneration and the strange constitution of an artificial mutant, and its resistance was extreme.

A poisonous body that put out ordinary flames on contact had survived most of the mutant flame's burn.

Its body twisted and convulsed. The burned flesh squirmed and closed again, and even as its outer scales charred black and the flesh beneath tore open, everything inside stayed intact.

Paisley's mutant flame could only scorch its surface. It couldn't reach the source within.

"A feeble human Affinity... who are you to destroy me?"

The serpent's hoarse, savage roar cut through the flames, full of venom and arrogance.

It was truly enraged.

It stopped smashing blindly against the cage. Instead it sucked all the fire into its mouth, then spat it out at the weakest spot it had been battering.
