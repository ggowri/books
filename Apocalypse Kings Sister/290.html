Chapter 290

A faint cracking sound rang out as the little fox's soft ear tore, a thin trickle of blood seeping through its red fur, stark and jarring.

The fox flinched in pain, its body trembling slightly, ears drooping in hurt silence, eyes welling with thin tears as it looked up at Paisley, timid and pleading.

Paisley's chest tightened. All the warmth drained from her eyes at once. She reached up and firmly held the cat still, her voice sharp with reprimand.

"Stop this."

"How could you hurt her?"

Her voice wasn't loud, but it carried unmistakable disapproval.

The little white cat had never been scolded like this before. It froze on her shoulder, its sapphire eyes dimming instantly, hurt lodging deep in its chest, head drooping, silent.

******

Paisley didn't say anything more, her attention entirely on the injured fox in her arms.

Watching its torn ear bleed, its quiet, suffering expression, she felt a sharp pang of sympathy.

She carefully cradled the fox's soft body, her movements gentle, and pulled a soothing healing balm from her storage, dabbing it carefully onto the wound, bit by bit.

The whole time, her touch stayed gentle, her voice low and soothing.

The little fox stayed still in her arms, letting her tend to it, whimpering softly, obedient and pitiful.

Once the wound was treated, Paisley carried the fox the rest of the way, never once glancing back at the cat on her shoulder.

The little white cat sat alone on her shoulder, watching its master's full attention go to the red fox, feeling the loss of its special place keenly, hurt and disappointment welling up inside.

Its tail drooped, its small body sagging, amber eyes dull and clouded, overwhelmed by sadness and confusion.

Eventually it slipped quietly back into the storage space, curling up alone in a corner, hiding.

Paisley decided not to coddle it and left it be.

The moment she stepped through the villa door, Ashley spotted the little fox in her arms and lit up instantly, hurrying over.

The girl took one look at the fox's sleek fur and bright, lively eyes and fell completely in love, tilting her face up hopefully.

"Pais, it's so pretty! I love it so much, can I hold it, please?"

She reached out carefully, trying to touch the little fox.

The fox, which had been relaxed and content in Paisley's arms, tensed up instantly, its red eyes flashing with obvious wariness and resistance, its small head pulling back, fur bristling, clearly unwilling to let a stranger touch it.

Paisley gently smoothed its fur, her voice soft and reassuring.

"Don't be scared. This is my sister. She likes you too. Let her hold you for a minute, okay?"

The fox understood her words and, out of deference to Paisley, swallowed its resistance and stopped squirming.

Still, its body stayed stiff the entire time, red eyes drooping miserably, obedient but distant, radiating the unmistakable energy of someone enduring something against its will.

Orion, watching nearby, frowned, his expression turning serious.

"Pais, where did this fox come from?

I can't even tell what level it is.

Unidentified mutant beasts are dangerous. We don't know where it's really from. Might be better to put it back wherever you found it."

Elijah walked over too, glancing at the fox, backing Orion up.

"Your brother's right. We've got a lot of traveling ahead, through dangerous territory. It's not really the time to be keeping a pet."

He caught the fox's displeased glare and added another thought.

"Foxes are naturally cunning too, hard to tame. Even if it seems gentle now, there's no guarantee it won't turn on you later. The risk's too high."

Listening to their objections, Paisley sighed softly and explained, calm and unhurried.

"It's not some random wild mutant. It's a Level Seven mutant fox."

"It bonded with me willingly. It chose me as its master. It belongs to me completely now and would never hurt me. You don't need to worry."

She paused, a faint, content smile touching her eyes, and continued.

"And that's not all. Bonding with it is actually what triggered me to awaken Fire Affinity."

With that, Paisley lifted a hand, focused her will.
