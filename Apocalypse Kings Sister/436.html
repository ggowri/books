Chapter 436

Paisley's eyes lit up, and interest flared in her chest.

She kept her expression calm and told the others, "Spread out and sweep the whole area. See if we missed anything."

Before they could answer, she was already following Trovie's directions to a hidden door deep in the depot.

A mechanism was tucked into the innermost wall, so subtle you'd never spot it by looking. Her fingertips brushed the surface and found tiny grooves. She worked them, and a heavy stone door slid open. A dark stairway led down.

At the bottom lay the secret vault, and the sight stopped her cold.

The huge chamber was square and spacious, its walls built entirely of solid gold bricks. Gold light filled the room, dazzling.

Walls of gold ran on and on. Conservatively, there were over 110,000 pounds of pure gold, worth beyond measure.

Along both sides, bulletproof glass cases held rows of dust-covered national treasures. Ancient gemstone carvings, heirloom ceramics, rare antiques, one-of-a-kind artifacts, historical manuscripts. Each was priceless, all perfectly preserved.

A smile tugged at her lips, and her heart raced.

She was about to get rich!

Trovie spun in circles in her mind, delighted. Master, you're amazing! This haul is huge! A few more like this and you'll be a Level Five trader!

Take everything, Paisley ordered. Store it all. Convert the gold into points at the exchange. We'll keep the national treasures safe and turn them over to the base at the right time, so our history is preserved.

Her space spread out and wrapped the whole vault. The glittering gold, the rare relics, every piece in every case lifted into the air and flowed into her space in orderly lines.

In seconds the vault was bare.

Then the system chimed.

[Congratulations, Master! You have stored 110,000 pounds of pure gold!]

[The gold has been converted automatically. You received 1,000,000 points!]

[Congratulations, Master! You've stockpiled massive strategic supplies and triggered a bulk-hoarding bonus of 10,000 points!]

[Current total: 3,440,360 points!]

A million points in one go. A few more hauls like this and she'd reach Level Five.

Paisley rarely let anything show on her face, but now a real, bright smile crossed it. Her lips curved, and her eyes shone.

She reined it in quickly and climbed the stairs back out.

The others had finished searching the rest of the area. They'd gathered the leftover equipment and fuel and were waiting for her.

When she appeared, they crowded around happily.

Ashley noticed her mood right away. "Paisley, did you find something good? You look so happy!"

"Some national treasures," Paisley said lightly. "What about you?"

Elijah, fully recovered, answered for the group. "A few cases of gasoline and some equipment. All stored in the storage tokens."

He glanced at the sky. "It's five p.m., and it'll be dark soon. The mission's basically done, and this place is dangerous. Let's find somewhere safe to rest and recover, then figure out how to deal with the serpent."

Everyone agreed. After the long drive and the fights against zombies and a high-level mutant, they were worn out and needed the rest.

The group left the depot area, avoiding dark corners, and followed the open main road in search of a safe spot.

The ruined city at dusk was dead silent. Wind rattled through, weeds swayed, and a few distant zombies groaned now and then, making the wreckage feel even bleaker.
