Chapter 289

Seeing that Paisley meant it no harm, the little red fox lost the last of its shyness and hopped up into her palm on its own.

It lowered its head and rubbed its smooth forehead against her wrist, over and over, its red eyes soft and docile, obedient.

A faint, pure strand of its own life energy drifted from between its brows, wrapping gently around Paisley's limbs, binding gently, blood to blood.

It was offering itself to her as its master.

The bonding was complete.

Warmth spread through Paisley's chest. She stroked its sleek red fur, her eyes soft and tender, her voice low.

"All right then. Stay with me from now on. I'll keep you safe."

Now that the bond was complete, the fox could understand her words.

It let out a soft whimper, curling up contentedly in her palm, completely relaxed, leaning into her without reservation.

Right at that moment, a surge of warmth rolled through her, spreading fast from her palm through her whole body.

Her channels ran hot, and a wild, pure fire energy surged through her chest, scorching yet harmless, settling quietly into her core.

Paisley's body went briefly rigid, surprise flickering through her eyes as she realized clearly — she'd just awakened Fire Affinity.

She called out to Trovie at once.

Trovie, what's going on? Why do I suddenly have Fire Affinity?

Trovie checked quickly, delighted.

Host! This is the Level Seven mutant fox's gift, from binding to you willingly!

When a beast offers its life energy to bond with its master, it can trigger shared affinity inheritance!

You've successfully inherited the little fox's power — mutant flame!

This isn't ordinary fire. It's a high-tier mutated flame, inherited at full strength!

Congratulations, Host, on gaining Level Seven mutant Fire Affinity! Your strength just jumped another level!

Paisley's chest tightened with shock, delighted and completely caught off guard. She hadn't expected taking in this little fox to come with such a gift.

Pushing down the surprise, she lifted a hand, focused her mind, and tried releasing the fire.

The moment her fingers moved, a flame sprang up, clear and bright, tinged with a faint golden-red glow.

It carried none of a normal flame's harsh heat, the light clean and warm, yet wrapped in an overwhelming destructive pressure.

This was the Level Seven mutant flame.

Paisley raised her fingers, focused, and the golden-red flame leapt forward, landing on a discarded boulder nearby.

No explosion, no roar. The solid rock simply charred, cracked, and crumbled to fine ash in the span of a breath, not a trace left behind.

The destructive power was staggering.

Paisley's eyes went wide with astonishment, her chest full of disbelief.

Ordinary fire could burn flesh and scorch skin at best, but this mutant flame could dissolve solid stone entirely. This fire was something else.

Like it could burn away anything in the world.

Sensing the kindred fire energy, the little fox nuzzled happily against her palm, letting out a soft whimper, its red eyes full of affection and something like pride, as if celebrating its master's new power.

Paisley's wristband buzzed.

Orion's voice came through.

"Pais, where are you? Dinner's ready, come back. Want me to come get you?"

Paisley glanced at the sky, surprised to see evening had already fallen, the sunset beautiful over the land.

"Orion, I'm heading back now."

She pulled out a piece of jerky and carried the fox in her arms, feeding it little bites as she walked.

The little white cat appeared from her storage, settling on her shoulder, only to find the spot that used to be its own now occupied by a red fox.

The little fox curled contentedly in her arms, receiving gentle strokes and careful attention.

That used to be the cat's privilege alone.

This sneaky fox. How dare it.

The little white cat's round face scrunched up, its amber eyes narrowing, overflowing with hurt and indignation.

It pressed its small mouth into a thin line and let out a soft, grumpy sound.

"Meow..."

Trying to get Paisley's attention.

But her gaze stayed fixed on that silly fox.

An enemy who stole its master's affection was unforgivable.

The little white cat raised a soft pink paw, and without hesitation, swiped it down hard at the little fox's head.

Given the cat's speed and strength, that strike came fast and sharp. Most mutant beasts wouldn't have had time to react.

But the Level Seven fox had the reflexes of a top-tier beast and could've easily dodged it. Instead it just froze, taking the hit square on.
