Chapter 365

"Do you have any idea who I am?!

My word is my bond. Every single thing I say, I follow through on, no exceptions! How dare you insult me, question me, in front of everyone like this?!"

"Fine, you want it in writing? Done."

Cold fury in his eyes, Grant agreed to the written contract without hesitation.

Staff didn't waste a moment, bringing out a standard wager contract template, entering both squads' terms and the stakes, printing out two official copies.

Black and white, every clause clear, the consequences spelled out in full, officially binding through the base.

Every member of both squads stepped up in turn, signing their names, pressing their thumbprints in red ink.

Squad Falcon signed with easy confidence, no hesitation at all, trust and certainty plain in every face.

Chloe's squad, by contrast, carried visible tension, every member a little uneasy — except Chloe and Grant themselves, still utterly certain of victory.

Once every print was set, the base kept one copy and Squad Falcon the other.

The tension in the hall broke wide open, the other users unable to hold back any longer, murmurs rippling through the crowd.

"They actually signed it?! That's a squad on the line — lose, and you're disbanded outright. Brutal."

"Full Level Six rookies against a battle-hardened elite squad — this is going to be a show."

"That mutant morning glory's no joke. Plenty of Level Five and Six names have gone down to its toxin. This isn't the kind of mission you win on level alone."

"Hard to call. Squad Falcon's got the raw numbers, but no field experience. The other side's got experience to spare but can't match the levels. Could really go either way."

"My money's on Squad Falcon. Hitting Level Six across the board in twenty days, with Elijah leading? These people are no joke."

"I'll wait and see... that mental fog from a Level Seven mutant's basically unbeatable. If Squad Falcon's rookies can't hold up against it, they're going down hard.

The old squad's got the base's best field record. I think they take it."

...

The chatter kept filling the hall, every eye fixed on the two squads, waiting eagerly for what came next.

Listening to the murmurs around her only fed Chloe's confidence further. She shot a cold glance at Ashley's group, her tone dripping scorn.

"Just wait. You'll learn fast enough that fancy numbers don't mean a thing out on an actual battlefield."

******

Grant let out a scornful laugh, contempt plain in his eyes.

"We'll see. Just hope you don't embarrass yourselves too badly when it's over."

Squad Falcon didn't react at all to the repeated provocation.

Paisley's gaze stayed light and cool, settling on the pair as she said simply:

"We'll see, then."

Four plain words, nothing sharp about them, yet they carried enough weight to flatten every bit of the other side's bravado.

The hall seemed to quiet for a second, Grant and Chloe's next jab dying in their throats against that unbothered calm, their faces souring further.

At the back of the crowd, a quiet, striking figure had been watching the whole standoff unfold.

Vivian Lennox stood elegantly in the corner, her clean base uniform softening her gentle air, the picture of someone above the fray — though a calculating calm lurked behind her eyes.

She was the eldest daughter of the main Lennox family, a Level Six Plant Affinity user, and one of the base's exceedingly rare Healing Affinity users.

A rare healing gift, top-tier level, the weight of the Lennox main family behind her — her looks were the least remarkable thing about her. All of it combined made her someone with real influence in the base, someone even squad captains treated with deference.

Watching the two squads lock themselves into this life-or-death wager, her mouth curved faintly, something amused flickering behind her eyes.

This should be entertaining.

She hadn't even had the chance to start stirring things up yet, and Elijah's new Squad Falcon was already locked in open conflict with Grant and Chloe's elite squad.

This was getting more interesting by the minute.
