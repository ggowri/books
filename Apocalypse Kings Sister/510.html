Chapter 510

Thomas focused on his surroundings. His Plant Affinity spread out, vines hidden under the soil as an early warning.

"The target area's just ahead. They say there's an old civil defense bunker with an underground river below. But the caves crisscross, so we could easily get ambushed."

Elijah gripped the steering wheel, Lightning Affinity crackling faintly at his fingertips.

"Everyone stay sharp. High-tier mutant beasts love to ambush in caves. I hope the mutants after the Crimson Rain aren't too tough."

Paisley sat in the back seat, fingertips rolling lightly. Her Psychic power spread out silently, a wave of invisible force sweeping the area.

At peak Level Seven, her perception reached farther than ever.

"Five hundred yards ahead is the cave entrance. There are a lot of low-tier mutant crawlers, and something much stronger is hidden deep inside. Everyone be careful."

They parked about a hundred yards from the cave and put on full protective suits and gas masks before getting out.

The ground was slick and muddy. Every step splashed dark red mud.

They walked slowly toward the cave entrance.

The mouth was pitch black. Cold, damp air rolled out, carrying the thick smell of earth and polluted filth.

They'd barely stepped inside when palm-sized mutant pill bugs with gray shells swarmed the walls and surged toward them.

Thomas called up thick vines from the ground and wrapped a huge clump of the bugs. Ashley raised a heavy earth wall to block their charge, spikes bursting up to stab through them.

Spencer unleashed his mutant Fire Affinity. Pale-gold flames rolled out and burned the surging mass.

Elijah poured out lightning, crackling arcs tearing through the dark and cleaning up the stragglers.

In moments the swarm was gone.

They pushed deeper. The air got more humid as they went.

They could faintly hear flowing water.

"That's water!"

The walls of the deep passage kept seeping, drops dripping onto the muddy floor, loud in the silent cave.

The air was cold and damp, mixed with earth and a faint plant stench. The deeper they went, the heavier and eerier the feeling got, as if countless eyes were silently watching the intruders from the dark.

They advanced cautiously. The soles of their suits crunched over slick gravel, and their lights swung across the rough walls.

They rounded a narrow stone choke point, and the scene changed in an instant.

The cramped passage opened up. A hidden underground paradise lay before them.

Fruit trees grew in clusters, apple trees, pear trees, berry vines, all heavy with fruit. The skins were bright and plump.

Red apples weighed down the branches, and a sweet fruity smell drifted through the air. In an apocalypse choked with contamination, it looked like a dream.

A small river flowed beside them, the water clear and bright, with none of the grayish-red the rain had left elsewhere. It was transparent to the bottom.

Ashley's pupils lit up and she blurted out in delight.

"No way, an actual paradise in the apocalypse?"

She stared at the plump apples on the branches, barely hiding her excitement.

"The fruit here must taste amazing!"

Thrilled, she couldn't wait. She stepped forward to pick an apple and try it.

But she was still several yards from the trees when the ordinary-looking weeping willow beside her suddenly came alive!

The soft, drooping branches snapped taut and became countless thick, tough, dark brown vines tipped with sharp thorns. They whistled through the air and whipped toward Ashley in a dense storm.

Too fast to react to.

Ashley's heart jumped. She scrambled to call up Earth Affinity, and a thin wall rose in front of her, but the vines hit with enormous force. With a "boom," the wall cracked into a spiderweb of lines.

Dense vines were already on her. She jumped back, slipped on wet ground, nearly fell, and yelled.

"Paisley, help!"

"Look out!"

Orion shouted, Metal Affinity surging. Several sharp metal blades formed in his palms and he slashed at the incoming vines.

The blades only cut through a few. The severed ends oozed dark green sap and regrew almost at once.

Thomas called up his Plant Affinity too. Green vines burst from the ground and met the willow's branches in a tangle.

Both were Plant Affinity, but the enemy's power carried thick, contaminated rage. Where the two forces collided, the ground churned and heaved.
