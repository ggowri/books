Chapter 420

Paisley was powerful and unfathomable, an undisputed top fighter on the base. She couldn't be shaken easily.

Grant had lost an arm. Even with an amplifying prosthetic, fighting her would still be deadly and almost hopeless.

No matter which of them died, or if both were ruined, it was a win for Chloe either way.

If Paisley lost, she'd have her revenge and no more worries.

If Grant fell, she could walk away from this crippled burden and find a new backer.

A blood feud on one side, a devoted lover on the other. In the end it was just her game, played carefully, and she would collect the winnings.

On the surface she was still the same weak, innocent girl, tears in her eyes, leaning obediently against him, acting deeply moved.

******

At the same time, in the Lennox family's most secret headquarters, the mood was grim and full of scheming.

The place was far from the crowds, shielded by layers of barriers against spying, with only core members of the main family coming and going. The building was dark and severe, every corner carrying the quiet menace of an old, powerful family.

In the top-floor conference room, the lights were cold and low, the atmosphere still and suffocating.

Harold Lennox, head of the family, sat in the high seat in a dark tunic, steady and commanding, his eyes holding decades of scheming.

He tapped the cold wood tabletop and swept his gaze over the family's senior leaders and direct heirs, his face grim.

Moments ago, he had received a secret message from Russell Quinn. It proposed an alliance. The Quinns wanted to join forces on the Exclusion Zone mission, ambush Elijah on the way, wipe out Falcon Squad, and tear the Blackwood family's top force out of the base for good.

After a pause, Harold spoke, his voice low and heavy, full of careful weighing.

"I just received a secret letter from Russell Quinn. The Quinns want an alliance with us. They want to use this grain depot mission to set a trap, kill Elijah, and wipe out Falcon Squad."

A soft rustle of breathing ran through the room. Everyone looked shocked, hesitant, and calculating.

The Blackwoods held the base's highest authority. Elijah, as Falcon Squad's captain, was formidable. Even before the apocalypse, the Blackwoods had been a mountain over every family.

But the Quinns were going all in, a fight to the death. Teaming up carried huge risk.

Harold's gaze fell on the young woman at the head of the line, cool and sharp, and asked.

"Vivian, you're careful and good at planning. What do you think? Should the Lennoxes take this deal?"

Vivian stood. She was slender and upright, her face cool and clear. There was no panic in her eyes, only calm certainty.

She lowered her head in a bow, then looked up and laid out her plan in clean, ordered words.

"Father, in my view, we shouldn't rush to accept, and we shouldn't rush to refuse."

She paused, her gaze sharp as she cut to the heart of it.

"The Quinns are deeply rooted and ruthless. They've waited and schemed for years, and their ambitions are huge. They are not as harmless as they look.

They're offering this alliance because they want us to share the risk. Once they succeed, they won't let us have any of the spoils. They'll suppress us. The aftermath would be dangerous.

The Quinns are not easy to handle."

"Damon Quinn most of all. He's a black-hearted fox."

Her words cut through the pros and cons, and the people below nodded.

******

Vivian went on calmly, and she delivered the core of her plan.

"But we don't need to refuse outright. I have a plan that lets us stay safe and still come out ahead."

"I'll send someone right away. Paisley's adoptive mother can go to her, using old kindness and family ties, to claim her and win her over."

"Paisley is now incredibly gifted, with the strongest fighting power on the base. If we can use that old tie to bring her back to the Lennoxes, she becomes our greatest asset. With her, whoever wins between the Quinns and the Blackwoods, we'll rise above every family."

Then she turned.
