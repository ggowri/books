Chapter 225

Hot food was soon on the table, and everyone could finally eat in peace.

Half an hour later, everyone was done eating, and with full stomachs, they felt their strength coming back.

As soon as Paisley reached the cafeteria entrance, she saw Ashley, Spencer, and Thomas waiting quietly not far away. They'd obviously come just to wait for the team.

When they spotted Paisley, their eyes lit up.

Ashley, the youngest, rushed over first. With red eyes, she threw her arms around Paisley, still choked up. "Paisley, you scared me to death!

"Everyone at the base was saying you'd all been wiped out and were dead, that you were never coming back."

She buried her face in Paisley's shoulder and sobbed. "I didn't believe it. I've been waiting here the whole time, and I knew you were the strongest, so nothing could happen to you that easily.

"I'm so glad you're back!"

Spencer hurried over and gently ruffled Paisley's hair, shaken but relieved. "You're back, and that's all that matters. Thank God you're okay.

"If something had really happened to you, we wouldn't even know how to explain it to Mom and Dad back in Aetheris."

Then, Thomas stepped forward, gently pulled Ashley off her, and hugged Paisley himself, his voice low and shaken. "As long as you're okay, Paisley, that's all that matters... You really scared us this time."

Madison, standing behind them, already had red eyes. She hurried forward, unable to hide how worried she was. "Pais, where's Orion?

"Why... why isn't he with you? Did something happen to him? Why didn't he come out with you?"

Mason looked around too and asked anxiously, "Yeah, Ms. Lennox, where are Captain Blackwood and Orion? Why don't I see them?"

Paisley could see how genuinely worried they all were, and it warmed her heart. "Don't worry, they're fine. The leaders just asked them to stay behind."

She looked around at all of them. "We're all safe, and nothing happened to us. You don't need to worry."

Since the apocalypse began, this was the first time Paisley had felt such pure, genuine kindness and care from other people. It had nothing to do with self-interest or scheming. These teammates who fought beside her and the family who lived with her every day truly cared about her, and their care was warm and pure.

She was deeply touched and patiently answered their questions.

Ashley tilted her little face up and asked, worried, "Paisley, was the place you went today really dangerous?

"What scary things did you run into this time? Did the mission go okay? Does this mean you won't have to go somewhere that dangerous again?"

Paisley gently ruffled her hair and comforted her with a few simple words. She didn't go into detail about the bloody, twisted experiments and the conspiracy underground.

Everyone chatted with her a little longer, and once they finally stopped worrying, they went off to rest.

Paisley went back to her room, let go of the day's exhaustion, and quietly leaned against the headboard.

Scenes from the lab under the aquarium kept replaying in her mind: the brutal human mutation experiments, the secret bases all over the country, and the conspiracy of people in power pulling all the strings.

This world-shattering apocalypse wasn't a natural disaster. It was a man-made catastrophe.

The human heart was the most terrifying, chilling thing of all.

Countless heavy thoughts weighed on her. She gently closed her eyes, and a deep gloom settled over her.

She slept soundly, and the night flew by.

******

Early the next morning, the group went to the base cafeteria and found a quiet private room to eat in.

During the meal, Elijah put down his fork and spoke up, looking very serious. "The mission here is over, so pack your things. We'll head back to Aetheris right after."

Everyone had been worn out ever since the apocalypse began, so nobody objected, and they all nodded.

Thomas couldn't hide how excited he was. "We're finally going home! I've been dying to get back to Aetheris."

Spencer chimed in. "Captain Blackwood, are we driving back? With the roads this bad and zombies and mutant beasts everywhere, it'll take us a week or two to reach Aetheris, and it'll be dangerous the whole way."

Elijah shook his head. "I already got flight clearance and a private plane from the base. In half an hour, we'll fly straight back."
