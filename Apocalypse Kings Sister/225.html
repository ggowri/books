Chapter 225

Hot food hit the table fast, and everyone settled in to eat without worry.

Half an hour later, the meal wound down.

A full stomach brought a little strength back into their limbs.

Paisley had barely reached the mess hall entrance when she spotted Ashley Thompson, Spencer Thompson, and Thomas Thompson waiting a short distance off, clearly there just for them.

******

The moment they caught sight of that familiar, composed figure, their eyes lit up.

Ashley, the youngest, got there first, throwing her arms around Paisley, eyes already red, voice thick with leftover tears.

"Paisley, you scared me so bad!

Everyone at base was saying you'd all died, that none of you were coming back."

She pressed her face into Paisley's shoulder, voice breaking.

"I didn't believe it for a second. I knew you were too strong for something like that to happen.

I'm so glad you're back."

Spencer stepped up beside her, ruffling Paisley's hair gently, relief plain on his face.

"Good thing you're okay.

If something had actually happened to you, I don't know how we'd ever explain it to Mom and Dad back home."

Thomas moved in next, gently pulling Ashley off Paisley so he could pull her into a hug of his own, his voice low, carrying real fear he couldn't quite hide.

"As long as you're okay. Paisley, as long as you're okay. This one really scared us."

Madison Hughes, standing just behind them, had eyes already red. She stepped forward fast, worry spilling into her voice.

"Pais, where's Orion?

Why isn't he with you?

Did something happen to him?

Why didn't he come out with you?"

Mason Coleman was scanning the crowd too, concern in his voice.

"Yeah, Ms. Lennox, where are Captain Blackwood and Orion? I don't see them anywhere."

Seeing the sincere worry on every face, warmth spread through Paisley's chest. She spoke to reassure them.

"Don't worry, they're fine. The leadership just held them back for a bit."

She looked around at everyone.

"We're all safe. Nothing happened to any of us. You don't need to worry."

This was the first time since the world had fallen that Paisley had felt something this genuine and this clean, people caring about her with nothing attached to it, no agenda, no calculation.

These were the people fighting beside her day after day, living beside her, and their worry for her was real, warm all the way through.

Something in her softened. She answered their questions patiently.

Ashley tilted her small face up, looking at Paisley with worry.

"Paisley, was the place you went today really dangerous?

What did you run into out there?

Did you finish the mission?

Does that mean you won't have to go somewhere that dangerous again?"

Paisley reached out and ruffled her hair gently, giving her a soft, simple answer, keeping the horror of the underground lab and the conspiracy behind it to herself.

They stood around chatting a while longer before finally settling down and heading off to rest.

Back in her own room, Paisley let the exhaustion of the day settle over her, leaning back against her bed.

Every scene from the lab beneath the aquarium kept replaying in her mind.

The brutal human experiments, the secret bases scattered across the country, the hidden hand pulling every string from above.

This apocalypse, this entire world turned upside down, hadn't been nature's doing. It had been engineered by people.

Human nature really was the most terrifying thing of all.

A heavy weight settled over her thoughts. She closed her eyes, a quiet heaviness settling behind them.

A night of sleep passed in what felt like an instant.

The next morning, early,

the group gathered in a quiet private room at the base mess hall to eat.

Partway through the meal, Elijah set down his fork, his expression turning serious as he laid out the plan.

"This base's mission is done. Pack up, and we leave for Aetheris shortly after."

Since the apocalypse began, exhaustion had worn on everyone enough that no one objected. They all nodded in agreement.

Thomas's eyes lit up with barely-contained excitement.

"Finally heading home. I've been dying to get back to Aetheris."

Spencer added,

"Captain, are we driving back? With roads this bad, zombies and mutants everywhere, at this pace it'll take us two weeks just to reach Aetheris, and every mile of it dangerous."

Elijah shook his head.

"I've already secured flight clearance and a private plane through the base. We leave by air in half an hour."
