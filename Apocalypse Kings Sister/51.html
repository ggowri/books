Chapter 51

Things weren't looking good for Orion and the others. Their abilities were still stuck at Level One.

They were completely outmatched and couldn't hold off a Level Three mutant. All they could do was dodge and stall as they kept losing ground.

But the creature was actually after Rachel, who was hiding behind them. Every attack it made was just to get closer to her so it could grab her and carry her off.

"Help! Somebody help me!"

Rachel's face went white as a sheet. Her legs gave out as she stumbled backward, and her voice shook so hard she could barely get the words out.

Paisley stood a short distance away. Orion, Elijah, and Mason looked rough, but none of them were hurt, so she let out a breath and relaxed.

She hopped lightly onto a nearby railing and made herself comfortable. Then, she pulled a bag of popcorn out of her Storage Dimension and started munching like she was at the movies.

Danger was everywhere, yet Paisley sat off to the side snacking on popcorn, relaxed and completely out of place.

Rachel was shaking all over. In her panic, she spotted Paisley lounging on the railing and cried out to her through her tears, like Paisley was her last lifeline.

"Paisley! Help me! Please, help me!"

Everyone in the fight whipped around to look at Paisley.

The brutal battle had burned through most of their energy. They were all wiped out and still shaken.

Against a smart, vicious Level Three mutant humanoid, they didn't stand a chance.

And in the middle of a life-or-death fight, Paisley was actually sitting there munching popcorn like it was movie night.

When Orion saw Paisley come back safe and sound, a huge weight lifted off his chest.

He didn't give a damn whether Rachel lived or died. All he cared about was Paisley, and as long as she was safe, nothing else mattered.

Elijah watched Paisley lazily munching her popcorn. His usually cool face softened into a faint smile, half exasperated and half fond.

She really does whatever she wants. I like it.

Jasper, on the other hand, was scared out of his mind. His legs had turned to jelly, and he'd pissed himself right there on the spot. He could barely stay on his feet.

Paisley kept munching as she watched Rachel flail around. Then, she casually twisted the knife.

"Rachel, it's pretty obvious that thing has the hots for you. Why are you screaming for me?

"That's a Level Three mutant humanoid. It could wipe the floor with all of us. You don't seriously think the loser you always looked down on can beat it, do you?

"Go get 'em, Rachel! I'm rooting for you! And if it doesn't work out, hey, just go home with it..."

Orion's mouth twitched as he listened to Paisley's sly, cutting teasing.

He'd never known that calm, steady Paisley had such a petty, grudge-holding side.

Rachel must have treated her terribly in the past. Paisley hated drama, and she'd never kick someone when they were down without a good reason.

Trovie bounced around, giggling nonstop. [Master, you're so evil! But I love it!]

Elijah's eyes softened. He smiled up at Paisley on the railing and murmured, "Little brat."

The Level Three mutant humanoid had noticed Paisley's sudden arrival, too.

She'd hidden every trace of her energy, but the creature's instincts still screamed danger.

It got jumpy at once, a low, vicious growl rumbling in its throat. It didn't dare stick around, so it scooped Rachel off the ground by her waist and turned to bust its way out.

The moment her feet left the ground, Rachel completely lost it. She was terrified out of her mind.

She dropped every last scrap of pride and begged Paisley for her life.

"Paisley, I'm sorry! I swear I know I was wrong! Please, save me!

"Come on, the Lennoxes raised you for 20 years! How can you just stand there and watch me die? Are you really that heartless?

"Aren't you scared of how heartbroken Mom and Dad will be when they find out?"

Hearing the same tired lines over and over was enough to drive anyone crazy.

Paisley rolled her eyes and cut Rachel off, her voice ice-cold.

"Aren't you sick of this yet? Every time you open your mouth, it's all about how the Lennoxes raised me. It's the same old crap over and over, and you use it to guilt-trip me every single time.

"Whatever I owed the Lennoxes is long gone. Your dirty schemes and all the awful stuff you did to me wiped it out ages ago."
