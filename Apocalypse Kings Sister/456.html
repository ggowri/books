Chapter 456

"Since we're in the same spot, there's no reason to fight each other."

He looked straight at the man and cut to the point.

"There are over a hundred pack-hunting Level Six lizards downstairs. Alone, neither of our teams would last. If we eye each other and fight each other, we'll both lose and end up as beast food."

"Better to team up for now."

A short silence. The man's eyes flickered as he weighed it quickly.

He knew Elijah was right.

One team alone couldn't break out of a beast tide this big. Staying locked in a standoff would only burn strength and Affinity, and they'd die waiting.

Teaming up was the only way out.

A few seconds later he slowly lowered his rifle. The Affinity in his palm faded, his shoulders loosened a little, and he spoke in a low voice.

"I agree to cooperate for now."

He stepped forward and held out his hand to seal it.

"Cole Mercer, captain of the Lone Wolf Squad, out of Highcrest Base."

Elijah gripped his hand firmly. "Elijah Blackwood, captain of the Falcon Squad. Aetheris Base."

That short introduction made Cole's pupils tighten. The calm on his face broke, and shock and respect surged in his eyes.

He let go and looked at the whole group from head to toe, his voice full of disbelief.

"You're the Falcon Squad from Aetheris Base?"

Among all the bases around, the Falcon Squad's name was legend.

They'd been red-hot lately, turning impossible fights around, racking up big wins, top-tier in strength. Every small and mid-sized base held them in awe as a legendary squad.

The coldness left Cole's face, replaced by plain respect and delight. His whole attitude flipped, and he spoke sincerely.

"I've heard a lot about you! I've been hearing the stories for a while. Meeting you today is our luck."

Seeing the mood ease, a trace of weariness and anxiety crossed Cole's eyes. He stopped circling the point, and spoke with bare helplessness.

"To be honest, we've been trapped here for two full days."

"My people keep getting hurt, and we're out of antibiotics and antidote. All gone."

He looked at Elijah, with a hint of an earnest plea.

"If you have any spare medicine, could you spare us some? And if it's not too much, some food as well. My people haven't eaten in a day."

As he spoke, Cole stepped aside to show the rest of the Lone Wolf Squad.

A dozen members, half of them pale and weak, covered in scratches and poison wounds, their clothes soaked with blood.

A young man lay on a makeshift stretcher of stacked crates, eyes shut and face flushed with fever, brows clenched in pain. He was deeply unconscious, his breath faint and quick, in terrible shape.

The rest were holding on, but all were hurt and drained.

Elijah stepped forward and bent to check everyone's wounds, especially the unconscious man. He saw the blackened wounds and ragged breathing, and didn't hesitate. He pressed his storage token.

Light flashed, and a dozen packs of vacuum-sealed bread, clean water, and a fully stocked medical kit appeared in his hands.

He handed it all to Cole. "Take these first."

Cole took the heavy supplies and kit in both hands, his fingers shaking slightly, moved and grateful.

"Thank you! Thank you so much. We won't forget this."

Elijah gave a small smile. "Don't mention it. Just remember you owe us."

Then his eyes went to the unconscious man, and his face turned serious.

"Get your people treated and fed fast. We'll work out a breakout plan later. Your man there can't wait."

"Right!"

Cole turned and quickly directed his team to treat wounds and eat.

The sudden shift tightened the mood again!

A Lone Wolf member who'd been wiping the wounds of the badly sick man scrambled back, her legs gave out, and she fell hard to the cold floor. Her face went white as paper, her eyes full of despair.

"No! He's... he's turning!"

"The lizard poison's all through him! Most of his mind is contaminated. He can't hold on!"
