Chapter 229

Up on the high wall, the cannons kept roaring, and the blazing flashes lit up the whole mudflat again and again, keeping every mutant sea beast focused on the front line.

Countless sea beasts charged mindlessly and kept slamming into the wall.

Paisley lay low on the cat's back, keeping her breathing very light. She slowly spread out her massive psychic power again and zeroed in on the psychic signal hiding behind the reefs.

She could clearly sense someone sending out a constant stream of powerful psychic threads. Like a dense, invisible spiderweb, they controlled the thousands of sea beasts along the whole coast from far away.

The closer they got, the heavier the sinister menace in the air became. The seawater was murky and dark, bits of beast bodies floated on the surface, and everything had an eerie, dead stillness.

The little white cat moved lightly and nimbly across the slick reefs, perfectly avoiding every loose stone and puddle that might make noise as it carried Paisley forward.

Deep in the heart of the reefs, they came to a raised platform surrounded by giant rocks.

That was where the sinister psychic control was coming from.

Paisley gently patted the cat's back. It understood right away and quietly stopped, pulling in all its fierce aura and hiding in the shadows of the dark reefs.

She looked up slightly and peered through a gap in the rocks, and she spotted the figure sitting in the middle of the platform at a glance.

It was a man in a black hooded robe. His whole face was hidden in the shadow of his hood, with only a pale, cold jawline showing.

He sat cross-legged on the rock, holding a bell in his right hand too, and he rang it in a steady rhythm. A faint black mist swirled around him, and countless gray psychic threads as thin as hair spread out from his body. They sank into the seawater and drifted into the distance, controlling the whole beast horde nonstop.

His eyes stayed closed the whole time, and nothing in front of him seemed to move him at all. All those dead and wounded creatures were just chess pieces he moved around without a thought.

A deep chill settled in Paisley's eyes.

She'd been right. Someone was controlling it all. From last night's zombie horde in the wilderness to today's coastal beast horde, both deadly ambushes were man-made.

Her expression hardened. Her fingers slowly tightened around the Frostbloom Sword, its cold spreading through her palm, and she crouched slightly, ready to fight.

Suddenly, the hooded man's eyes snapped open. His cold gaze cut through the heavy darkness and locked right onto Paisley and the cat hiding in the shadows of the reefs.

"I never thought anyone could break through my psychic barrier and find me here." His voice was raspy and cold, carrying the bite of the sea wind. There was a hint of surprise in it, but mostly it was ice-cold indifference and cruelty.

"Looks like you know quite a bit. You've got some skills."

He slowly looked up, dark hostility churning in his eyes beneath the hood, and stared Paisley down, his voice suddenly freezing. "But this is as far as you go.

"A little girl like you couldn't just stay put. You had to come looking for death, so fine, I'll grant your wish."

Before he even finished, the black mist around him exploded wildly. The gray psychic threads that had been controlling the beast horde from afar suddenly trembled hard and swept in from every direction, turning into countless razor-sharp black lines that lashed toward Paisley to slice her apart.

He was a high-level psychic, and his tricks were sinister and ruthless. He was best at killing without a trace and controlling minds, and ordinary Affinity users only had to touch a wisp of his black mist for their minds to shatter, killing them on the spot.

The little white cat was hiding in the dark, and it bristled, every hair of its snow-white fur standing on end. A low, vicious growl rose from its throat, and an icy white aura burst out around it as it stepped in front of Paisley to protect her.

"Don't be scared," Paisley said softly. There wasn't a trace of fear in her eyes, only calm and cold.

She frowned slightly, and her massive, pure psychic power exploded out as she fought back.

Other people were terrified of his sinister black mist, but her psychic power was far beyond his in grade, level, and strength. It was naturally built to crush this kind of evil, twisted mind control.

The black and white psychic forces slammed into each other with a sharp, ear-splitting crack.

The razor-sharp black threads the hooded man had sent shattered inch by inch the moment they touched Paisley's white psychic barrier. They melted away to nothing without causing even a ripple.

The hooded man's whole aura sank. For the first time, his calm indifference vanished, replaced by stunned disbelief.

"Psychic power this strong? You're a psychic too?

"That's impossible! How could there be a psychic in this apocalypse who's so much stronger than me?"
