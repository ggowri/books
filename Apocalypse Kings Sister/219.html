Chapter 219

All these mutants who'd been forced into these changes and tortured, these mutated bodies that could heal, regenerate, and fight with insane strength, were nothing but test sacrifices in someone's hunt for eternal life and power.

In that moment, it all became clear to Elijah.

He couldn't blindly follow orders from the top anymore.

This went too far and too deep. It affected the whole state of the apocalypse, involved secrets at the highest levels, and put humanity's hope on the line!

He had to tell his family right away.

His grandfather was one of the country's elder statesmen, with a high position and real power.

Maybe his grandfather knew something about what was really going on.

He felt deeply uneasy. He didn't know if his grandfather already knew about this conspiracy, or even if he was safe right now.

Elijah pushed down the storm raging inside him and looked at Paisley, his voice serious and full of trust. "Pais, please put all the medical equipment, precision instruments, and experiment files in here into your Storage Dimension.

"Remember, everything that happened here and the whole truth have to stay top secret. Right now, you're the only one I can trust."

Paisley realized how serious this was too. She nodded slightly without any hesitation.

In her past life, she'd only seen bits and pieces of the news. She'd known there were terrifying mutant monsters hidden under this aquarium, but she'd never heard about the shocking conspiracy behind them. Ordinary people had been kept in the dark the whole time, with no idea this disaster was man-made.

With a thought, she made her storage ring glow faintly. In a heartbeat, every paper file, digital archive, precision instrument, and chemical in the room disappeared into her Storage Dimension without a trace.

A cold, hard look crossed Elijah's face, and he set the place on fire to destroy all the evidence.

Raging flames swept through the whole lab, devouring everything around them and burning every trace of those evil experiments to ash.

By the time the other team members rushed over at the noise, all they could see was a huge fire and rolling waves of heat.

Everyone looked stunned. They hurried forward and asked one after another, "Captain Blackwood, did you find the mission files and the target equipment?"

"What happened here? Why is there such a huge fire all of a sudden?"

Elijah's face stayed calm and unreadable, and he looked perfectly at ease. "We didn't find anything when we came in," he said casually.

"The room was full of contaminants and leftover toxins that spread easily, so I started a fire to burn it all out and clean it up.

"Spread out and search the whole area carefully. See if we missed any supplies or leads."

No one suspected a thing. They answered right away, spread out, and started searching.

Paisley watched from the side and laughed to herself. She'd never expected that Elijah, who was always so upright and steady, could lie like a pro, airtight and without batting an eye.

Just then, Trovie's sweet voice suddenly piped up in Paisley's head, thrilled and bouncy.

[Master! I'm picking up a high-level treasure signature! It's close by, about 110 yards straight to your right!]

Trovie's sensors were never wrong. Anything it called a treasure was always worth a fortune.

Paisley didn't hesitate. She quietly slipped out of everyone's sight and sped off in the direction Trovie pointed.

After two dark corners, a sealed, separate storeroom came into view.

The storeroom door was thick and solid, and its layers of locks were covered in rust. It was extremely well hidden, and if Trovie hadn't pinpointed it, no one would ever have found this secret room.

Paisley's eyes lit up. She broke through the old locks with ease and pushed the heavy door open.

The second it opened, a dazzling golden light flooded out, so bright she could barely keep her eyes open.

The huge storeroom was piled with gold bars, top-quality gems, and all kinds of rare jewelry, antiques, and paintings.

Deep inside this dark, crumbling underground lab full of blood and evil, there was a secret vault packed with worldly treasures. That was a real surprise.

In a world this lawless, treasure had no owner. Finders keepers.

[Congratulations, Master! You're about to get rich! These solid gold bars weigh at least 550 pounds!

[These antiques and gemstones can be traded in for points too.]

Paisley's mind was clear. With a single thought, all the gold, jewels, antiques, and paintings in the storeroom floated into the air and flew neatly into her Storage Dimension.

Trovie started the exchange right away, and its announcement rang out.

[All treasures exchanged successfully, for a total of 70,000 points!

[Current total: 368,060 points.]

Before it had even finished, Trovie's alert refreshed again, and another reward came right after.
