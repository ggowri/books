Chapter 219

Every mutant down here, forced into this, tortured through it, every regenerating, superpowered freak of a thing, had been nothing but a sacrifice in someone's hunt for immortality and absolute power.

In that moment, Elijah's view of everything shifted completely.

He could no longer blindly follow orders from above.

This went too deep, touched too much, threatened the entire structure of this new world, humanity's last hope itself.

He had to warn his family immediately.

His grandfather was one of the senior statesmen, a man who held real power.

His grandfather might already know something about this.

Unease churned in his chest. He had no idea whether his grandfather already knew about this conspiracy, or whether he was even safe right now.

Pushing down the storm in his mind, Elijah looked at Paisley, his voice turning grave, carrying total trust.

"Pais, I need you to take every piece of medical equipment, every precision device, every file in this lab into your Storage Dimension."

"Remember, everything we've seen here stays absolutely secret.

Right now, you're the only person I can trust."

Paisley understood how serious this was. She didn't hesitate. She gave a small nod.

In her last life, she'd only seen scattered reports about mutant horrors buried beneath this aquarium, nothing about the conspiracy behind it. Ordinary people had been kept in the dark the whole time, completely unaware of what had really happened.

A thought stirred, and her storage ring flickered faintly. In an instant, every file, every digital archive, every piece of equipment and every chemical vial vanished into her Storage Dimension, leaving no trace behind.

Something cold flickered through Elijah's eyes. He set the lab on fire without a second thought.

Flames roared through the room, devouring everything in their path, burning away every shred of evidence of what had happened here.

When the rest of the squad came running at the sound, all they found was a wall of fire and heat rolling toward them.

Shock washed over their faces as they rushed forward, asking all at once:

"Captain! Did you find the mission files and the equipment?"

"What happened in here? Why's there a fire this big?"

Elijah's expression stayed completely level, calm and natural, not a flicker of anything off about him.

"We didn't find anything when we got in here.

The whole place was full of contaminants and leftover toxins, the kind that spread fast. Burning it out was the only way to keep it contained."

"Spread out and search the area. Check for anything we might've missed, supplies, leads, anything."

No one doubted him. They scattered immediately, splitting up to search.

Watching all this, Paisley almost laughed to herself. She hadn't expected the steady, upright Elijah Blackwood to lie this smoothly, without so much as a flicker on his face.

That was when Trovie's voice chimed in her head, bright and eager:

[Host! High-tier treasure signature detected! Close by, a hundred yards to your right!]

Trovie's readings never missed. Whatever it flagged as valuable always turned out to be worth something.

Paisley didn't hesitate. She slipped away from the group unnoticed and moved fast toward the direction Trovie had marked.

Past two dim corners, a sealed, standalone storeroom came into view.

The door was heavy and reinforced, locks rusted shut, well hidden. Without Trovie's precise reading, no one would've ever found this room.

Paisley's eyes lit up. She made quick work of the old locks and pushed the heavy door open.

The moment it swung wide, golden light flooded out, bright enough to make her squint.

The storeroom was packed with gold bars, flawless gems, rare jewelry, antiques, and paintings.

Finding a treasure vault like this, buried deep in a lab soaked in blood and horror, was almost funny.

No owner left to claim it. Finders keepers.

[Congratulations, Host! Easy money! These solid gold bars weigh at least five hundred fifty pounds!]

[The antiques and gemstones can also be converted into points.]

Paisley's mind went clear and steady. With a thought, the gold, jewels, antiques, and paintings all floated up and sorted themselves neatly into her Storage Dimension.

Trovie immediately started the exchange, voice chiming in:

[Treasure successfully converted! Total gain: 70,000 points!]

[Current total points: 358,060!]

The announcement had barely finished when Trovie chimed in again, another reward following close behind.
