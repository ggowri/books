Chapter 337

That they'd landed fifth-row seats at all was entirely thanks to the 3S-tier lab mission they'd completed.

The hall buzzed with low conversation, but undercurrents ran deep — every voice belonged to someone who controlled real power in this new world.

Just as the auction was about to begin, a measured, heavy tread sounded at the entrance. The murmur of the crowd faded at once, every eye drawn that way.

A cluster of base officials swept in, flanking a man at the center of the group.

He walked tall, sharp-eyed and brooding, radiating the pressure of someone long used to sitting at the top, every motion dripping with cold, practiced arrogance.

Surrounded by officials, he walked straight to the center seat of the front row and sat.

Elijah's expression darkened slightly, fingers curling. He kept his voice low but serious.

"That's the Quinn family's second-in-command."

Orion's face went grim at once, following his gaze. A wary edge crept into his eyes as he turned to murmur to Paisley.

"Be careful with that one, Pais."

"I didn't expect him to show up here."

Those few words settled over the group like a silent weight.

Paisley followed his gaze, her eyes settling calmly on the man in the front row without a flicker of unease.

She studied him quietly, her gaze deepening.

So he's the one who wants me dealt with.

As the Quinn family's real second-in-command, he was ruthless, calculating, and famous for holding grudges and crushing anyone beneath him with raw power.

Given what his men had just suffered back at the villa district, a man like him wasn't going to let that go easily.

As if sensing something, Damon glanced back, his eyes landing directly on Paisley in the fifth row.

The instant their eyes met, his mouth curved into something thin and cold, a flicker of calculation sliding through his gaze, unmistakably predatory.

It was the look of a predator sizing up prey, cold enough to cut.

******

Something in Paisley's chest sank — an inexplicable chill, like being eyed by a snake, a faint cold crawling over her skin as if she'd wandered into his hunting ground.

Before she could react, Elijah had already noticed. He turned his gaze that way, his dark eyes cold as a frozen lake, staring right back.

The two gazes collided, sharp as crossed blades, a silent tension crackling through the air.

Damon's eyes widened slightly.

He recognized Elijah, of course.

The heir the Blackwood family had poured everything into — young, already powerful, his record impressive enough that not even the top families dared cross him.

He hadn't expected to see him here at all.

And he certainly hadn't expected him to be guarding some unknown girl this fiercely.

In the Quinn family's long game, Elijah was a heavyweight they could never afford to overlook — an opponent who could shift the entire balance of power.

In an instant, Damon swallowed the hostility, stripping every trace of malice toward Paisley from his expression and replacing it with a convincingly warm smile — the mask of a smiling tiger, flawless and fast.

He rose unhurriedly, ignoring every eye on him, and walked straight to the fifth row with an easy, affable air.

"It's been a while, young Blackwood."

His tone was warm and familiar, the practiced smile of a kindly elder trying to close the distance between them.

Elijah didn't budge an inch at the overture.

His expression stayed frost-cold, not a hint of warmth in his eyes, and he didn't even bother with a perfunctory smile — just silence, and not an ounce of face given.

That blunt a cold shoulder froze the smile on Damon's face.

The hall went dead silent.

Every guest's eyes locked onto the two of them, disbelief written across every face.

They'd just watched one of the base's most formidable figures openly snub Damon Quinn.
