Chapter 482

Chloe's sore spot had been hit. Cold and furious, her face flipping between blue and white, she ground out a jab.

"It's just a jacket!

Just a jacket, and you, a Thompson adopted daughter, are so easy to impress. A little kindness and you swell up like this!"

She forced herself to sound tough and threatened.

"Don't get too smug!

You and your 'real daughter and fake daughter' act. It looks sweet now, but you'll be at each other's throats soon enough.

And let me down right now!

If I freeze to death out here, my parents and my grandpa won't let Falcon Squad off. They won't let Paisley off!"

Ashley and Chloe went back and forth the whole way, trading barbs nonstop.

They'd been like this before the apocalypse too. They had never once gotten along.

The SUV raced on. The snow thickened, and the road ahead stretched out.

Half an hour passed.

A building came into view beside the road, a crooked, abandoned sign swinging from it. The faded letters read: Suburban Gas Station.

It was the planned rest stop.

The group in the car got ready to get out.

Paisley had been resting with her eyes closed, calming her breathing. Suddenly her eyes snapped open.

A cold light flashed through them. She sent her mind out in a sweep and sensed danger hiding ahead. Her voice dropped as she warned everyone.

"Careful."

"There are a lot of people at the gas station."

Her cold warning shattered the loosened mood in the car.

Her psychic sweep passed through the falling snow and the broken buildings and caught the tightly packed, deliberately hidden signs of living people around the station, plus plenty of lurking zombie aggression.

Her voice stayed icy and tight as she warned everyone.

"Be careful. There are a lot of people at the station ahead."

Mason hit the brakes. The SUV stopped steady a hundred yards from the station, the body shuddering, and everyone dropped straight into alert.

They pushed the doors open and stepped out. Their feet had barely touched the snowy road when a sharp, familiar whistle cut through the wind and rang across the wilderness!

Whee, whee!

The strange whistle had a particular frequency. It cut through the wind and the silence like a silent summons.

The next second, from the abandoned streets, broken walls, grass, and under the snow, countless zombies slowly raised their heads. Their hollow eyes locked onto the group, and low, hoarse snarls rose from every direction.

Thud, thump, rustle.

Messy, heavy footsteps closed in from all sides, and swarms of zombies burst out of cover and rushed at them.

Gray skin, rotting flesh, and a thick stench of decay rolled over them.

On the flat roof of the abandoned station, a tall, cold figure in black stood still.

The man was dressed in black from head to toe, a black mask covering his whole face, only a hard line of jaw showing. A cold, dark aura came off him, warning everyone off.

He held a custom whistle between his fingers and kept blowing the command notes. Each blast made the zombie crowd multiply several times over.

The zombie horde filled the world, sealing every escape route front and back, a black wave that made scalps crawl.

The captives tied to the SUV roof, already frozen stiff and close to breaking, saw the endless tide of zombies and lost their nerve completely.

Terror swept through them. They couldn't keep any dignity. They thrashed and twisted, screaming and sobbing.

"Let me down! Let me down now!"

"The zombies are coming! I don't want to die!!"

"Help! Somebody save me! Please...!"

Their shrieks and begging mixed with the zombies' hoarse snarls and the howl of the wind. The scene was chaos and horror.

The Falcon members had been through countless fights and didn't panic at the horde. They went straight into battle.

Orion's Metal Affinity flared, blades forming around him, and he cut down the low-level zombies closing in.

Thomas's vines erupted, wrapping whole groups of zombies and pinning them down.

Ashley stood in front and raised an earth shield, a thick wall to take the horde's charge.
