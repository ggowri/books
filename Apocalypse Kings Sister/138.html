Chapter 138

Without warning, the branches suddenly tightened and yanked the three unsuspecting brothers up into the air!

They never saw it coming. They dangled in midair with no balance at all, stunned and panicked, and they didn't even have time to use their abilities to fight back.

Paisley's eyes turned cold, and she flicked her fingers hard!

With three heavy thuds, the Lennox brothers were slammed down from midair onto the hard concrete floor.

Their bones hit the floor with a sickening crack, and all three of them groaned at the same time. Their backs, stomachs, and elbows were bruised black and blue, and every one of them was hurt.

The brothers lay sprawled on the floor, aching and weak, too drained to even push themselves up. They felt wronged and confused, with no way to let it out.

In shock, they stared at Paisley. All they'd done was show up, without doing or saying a thing, and she'd hurt them for no reason at all.

Owen sounded hurt. "Why?"

It wasn't supposed to be like this.

Reid and Ryan sounded just as hurt. "We didn't do anything. Why would you hurt us?"

Paisley looked down at them coldly. "Because I don't like your faces. Blame your precious Rachel for being such a bitch."

Kelly was frozen in place, scared stiff, her eyes wide with terror.

As she watched Paisley strike so ruthlessly, only one thought ran through her head.

Paisley's lost her mind!

When did she get this brutal? She doesn't hold back at all, and the second something ticks her off, she goes for the kill!

In that moment, fear swallowed all the jealousy Kelly had been building up. She barely dared to breathe, terrified she'd be next.

The big hallway went deathly quiet except for the moaning and muffled groans. Bodies lay sprawled across the floor, and the scene was both ridiculous and brutal.

Paisley gave the groaning bunch on the floor a cold look, and her face didn't change at all.

She hadn't said a word the whole time. There was no point in warning them anymore.

With selfish, greedy people like the Lennoxes who never learned their lesson, force was the only thing that worked.

She turned, pushed her door open, and walked into her room. Then, with a click, she locked the door behind her and shut out everything outside.

In the room next door, Ashley heard the commotion, the heavy thuds, and the shouting. Her heart lurched, and she rushed out to check, terrified that someone was picking on Paisley.

But when she saw what was going on in the hallway, she froze for a second. Then, she broke into a huge grin.

Rachel was coughing up blood and twitching on the floor, and the three Lennox brothers were sprawled out, too hurt to move. Kelly stood frozen nearby, white as a ghost. The whole bunch looked pathetic, and all their usual swagger was gone.

Ashley lifted her chin and marched over, more than happy to kick them while they were down. "Serves you right!

"You bullied my sister and schemed against her over and over, and you kept pushing your luck. Now look at you. You totally brought this on yourselves!"

She raised her small fist high, her eyes fierce, ready to fight.

"Let me make one thing clear. If you go running to the base administration to snitch, spread lies about Paisley, or ever bother her again, I won't go easy on you. I'll beat you so hard you won't know which way is up!"

The Rivergate base had strict rules against fighting inside the city. Anyone who broke them lost contribution points and had their crystal cores taken away, and serious offenders got kicked out for good.

Rachel had started it by trying to grab the pendant on purpose, so the fault was hers from the start.

But Paisley had gone way past self-defense. If this got reported, it could come back on her.

And these people were fake, selfish, and shameless enough to try.

If they dared to twist the truth and snitch, Ashley would secretly beat them up so badly they couldn't get out of bed.

The injured Lennoxes on the floor were furious and frustrated when they heard that. But they knew they'd still be traveling with the Thompsons, and making enemies of them wouldn't do them any good.

If things turned ugly, the rest of the trip would be a nightmare. They had no choice but to swallow their anger.

Kelly bit her lower lip so hard she nearly broke the skin. Fear had pushed her jealousy aside for now, and she didn't dare try anything else.

They leaned on each other, fought through the pain, and struggled to their feet. Then, they limped back to their room in a sorry state and quietly patched up their wounds.
