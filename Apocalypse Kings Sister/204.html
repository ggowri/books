Chapter 204

Paisley's psychic sense was still pinned down by the invisible field, and she had nothing to go on.

She silently called for Trovie.

[Trovie, come on out. Time to get to work. Where exactly are the files and the equipment?

[What's going on with this place? Why can't I sense a thing?]

A sweet, childlike voice answered in her head right away.

[Scanning...]

The cold, dim left corridor was covered in thick dust. Big chunks of paint had peeled off the walls, the once-bright ocean murals had faded and cracked, and everything looked abandoned and dead.

Sea wind blew in through the cracks in the broken windows, kicking up dust from the floor and carrying a bone-deep chill. The whole building felt like a sealed grave, so heavy it was hard to breathe.

The scan took only a few seconds, but they seemed to drag on forever.

Then, Trovie's sweet voice spoke up in Paisley's head again.

[Master, scan complete! I've detected a special high-level interference field covering the entire aquarium. It's blocking all psychic scans at Level Seven and below, and it's forcing your psychic power down.

[The field comes from mutant nuclear radiation sealed under the aquarium. That's also the main reason this area's danger level is off the charts and why so many mutants are gathered here.

[The classified research files and the precision equipment are down there too.]

Paisley's eyes darkened slightly. She'd already figured out most of it.

Trovie kept reporting.

[Warning! A huge number of high-level mutant sea creatures are waking up in the main passage, and Chase's team is about to get surrounded. Danger level: high!]

Paisley slowed her steps, her face cold and indifferent.

Figures. They're digging their own graves, and they've got it coming.

Elijah noticed her stop and asked quietly, "What's wrong? Did you find something?"

Orion turned to look at her too, on high alert.

Paisley looked up and told them straight out, her voice calm and clear. "There's a field blocking this whole aquarium, so psychic senses and equipment scans are both useless in here.

"Rachel lied about the location. The files and the equipment are actually underground, and that's the most dangerous part of this place.

"Tons of high-level mutants are gathered down there."

Elijah and Orion realized right away how serious this was, and they trusted Paisley without question.

"This mission's too important to fail, so we have to do whatever it takes," Elijah said. "Pais, I trust you. Lead the way."

He'd barely finished speaking when the team channel blew up.

Savage beast howls, the blasts of affinity attacks, and the panicked screams of the team all mixed together with the roar of rushing seawater. It all came blasting through the comms in a chaotic mess.

"Oh no! A whole swarm of mutant fish-beasts is coming at us! They're Level Five mutants!"

"There are too many! We can't kill them fast enough, and we're out of stamina and power! Chase, get a psychic shield up and cover us!"

"We can't hold them off! We need backup! Left and right teams, get to the middle now!"

Panicked, desperate screams rose one after another, coming through loud and clear for everyone to hear.

In just moments, the middle team had charged blindly into a dead end, and now they were trapped, surrounded by a swarm of Level Five mutant beasts.

Every one of them deeply regretted it. Why had they ever trusted Chase and joined his team?

Chase had been so cocky and so dismissive of this mission, but now he was so scared his whole body was shaking.

Swarms of mutant fish-beasts surged out of dark channels, broken tanks, and shadowy corners. Foul seawater flooded the floor, and their sharp teeth and fins glinted coldly in the dim light as they closed in from every side, leaving no way out.

Chase scrambled to put up a Level Five psychic barrier, and the invisible wall barely held back the first wave.

But the mutant beasts kept slamming into it, wave after wave, with no fear of death. The barrier shook violently, and cracks kept spreading across it.

The nonstop drain on his psychic power made his temples throb and his head spin, and his face went a sickly gray.

Next to him, Rachel had gone white with fear. She was shaking all over and hiding behind Chase, and nothing was left in her but pure terror.

I never should've come. Death is right here this time.

All she could do was cling to Chase's arm and cry in a panic. "Chase, what do we do?! There are so many of them, and we can't hold on much longer!

"When's help getting here? They're coming for us, right?!"

Chase gritted his teeth and held off the horde, furious and scared at the same time, with no time to deal with her. All he could do was snarl into the comm for help again, and his arrogance was gone.
