Chapter 204

Her psychic senses stayed locked down, nothing to work with.

She called on Trovie, silently.

[Trovie. Time to go to work.]

[Where are the equipment and the data, exactly?]

[What's wrong with this place? Why can't I sense anything at all?]

The soft, childish voice answered at once in her head.

[Scanning…]

The left corridor sat thick with dust, cold and dim, chunks of wall peeling away, the once-bright ocean murals faded to patchwork. Every corner of it looked abandoned and dead.

Sea wind slipped in through broken windows, stirring dust off the floor, carrying a bone-deep chill. The whole building felt like a sealed tomb, heavy enough to choke on.

The scan took only seconds, but it felt endless.

Then Trovie's voice came through again.

[Host, scan complete! I've detected a special high-tier interference field covering the whole aquarium, blocking all psychic detection at Level Seven and below. Host's psychic power is being suppressed.]

[The field is coming from mutant radiation sealed underground. That's also the main reason this mission's danger rating is so high, and why mutants have gathered here in such numbers.]

[The classified research data and equipment are down there.]

Paisley's eyes darkened slightly. She'd already half-guessed as much.

Trovie went on.

[Warning! A large number of high-tier mutant aquatic creatures are waking up in the central main passage. Chase's team is about to face a mass attack. Danger level: high!]

Paisley's steps slowed, something flat and cold crossing her eyes.

Of course.

This was them getting exactly what they'd asked for.

Beside her, Elijah noticed her pause and asked, gentle, low.

“What is it? Something wrong?”

******

Orion glanced over too, alert.

Paisley looked up, her voice calm and clear, and said it plainly.

“The whole building's covered by an interference field. Psychic detection and equipment scans are both useless here. Rachel lied about the location—the real data and equipment are underground. That's where it's worst.”

“That's where all the high-tier mutants are gathered.”

Elijah and Orion both registered how serious that was, and neither of them doubted her for a second.

“This mission's too important to fail. We'll do whatever it takes to get it done. I trust you, Pais—lead the way.”

The words had barely left Elijah's mouth when the team channel erupted.

Beast shrieks, the crash of Affinity attacks, screams of raw terror, all tangled with the roar of churning water, blasting through the comm in total chaos.

“It's bad! A swarm of mutant fish-beasts, incoming! Level Five mutants!”

“There's too many! We can't kill them fast enough! Stamina and Affinity are both running out! Chase, put up a psychic shield, now!”

“We can't hold! Requesting backup! Left and right teams, get to the center, now!”

Panic and desperation rang through the comm, loud and clear for everyone to hear.

In minutes, the central team, marching blind into a trap, found themselves surrounded by a swarm of Level Five mutants.

Every one of them was already regretting trusting Chase enough to be on his team at all.

Chase, who'd walked in here arrogant and dismissive, was shaking now, top to bottom.

Mutant fish-beasts poured out of dark channels, broken tanks, every shadowed corner, foul water flooding the floor, teeth and fins catching the dim light as they closed in, no gap anywhere to run.

He threw up a Level Five psychic barrier in a rush, just barely holding against the first wave.

But wave after wave of mutants kept slamming into it with no regard for their own lives, and cracks spread across the barrier under the strain.

The constant drain pounded through his skull, his mind spinning, his face cycling through white and green.

Beside him, Rachel had gone white, shaking, hiding behind him with nothing left in her but terror.

If she'd known it would be this dangerous, she'd never have come.

Death was close enough to touch, this time.

She clung to Chase's arm, sobbing, her voice frantic.

“What do we do?! Chase! There's so many of them, it's so dangerous, we can't hold out much longer! When's help coming? They're coming for us, right?!”

Chase gritted his teeth against the surge, fury and fear tangled together, no room left to comfort her. He snarled into the comm again, his old arrogance nowhere to be found.
