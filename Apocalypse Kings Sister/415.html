Chapter 415

A look this blunt and hot made even calm, composed Paisley feel her ears heat up, and a small flutter rose in her chest.

She couldn't hold his gaze any longer. She turned away and pointed down the hall, changing the subject on purpose.

"There's a weapons exchange up ahead. Let's go take a look."

Elijah watched her dodge him, and he smiled, soft and fond.

She's shy.

He didn't call her on it. He nodded and followed her inside.

******

Meanwhile, at the Quinn family's luxury villa, the mood was heavy.

The villa sat in the base's most exclusive neighborhood, with a vast yard and grim, expensive decor. In the sealed meeting hall, the doors and windows were shut, the lights were cold and white, and every breath carried scheming and killing intent.

A secret meeting of senior leaders had been called to deal with Elijah and the mission to the Exclusion Zone.

Damon ran it.

He sat at the head, upright and cold, his eyes deep and sharp. His polished mask was gone, and only calculation and cruelty remained.

After the morning's clash, he had made up his mind. This time he would use the mission to get rid of Elijah.

He couldn't fail. So Elijah had to die.

The room was filled with the Quinns' core leadership, all of them grim and heavy-faced.

After a long moment, Damon spoke, his voice hard and low.

"After this morning's bet, we have no way back."

"We can't let Elijah win this grain depot mission."

"He's arrogant. He signed a pledge that could cost him his life. This time I'll make sure he's buried in the Exclusion Zone and never comes back."

The few words were full of murder.

The Quinn elders and senior leaders nodded, all of them ruthless.

Ever since the apocalypse, they'd been unwilling to stay under the Blackwoods. The grudge had built for years, and they couldn't wait to take their place.

"The Exclusion Zone is crawling with zombies and high-level mutant beasts. It's already close to a death sentence."

Damon tapped the table with one finger, his eyes dark, laying out his plan step by step.

"We don't need to fight Falcon Squad head on. We just set a trap and intercept them partway. We let the apocalypse do the killing, and no one will know."

"I'll lead the team myself."

One of the men objected. "Sir, you're too important to take that risk. Send an elite squad instead."

"The Lennoxes fear the Blackwoods' power too. They want what we want. They'll team up, and we all profit. With both families, we can't fail."

Damon replied, "I'll contact the Lennoxes personally. I'll promise them a share of the gains. They'll back us at the critical moment and hit Falcon Squad from the other side."

Russell's expression turned grave, and he warned him solemnly.

"But Damon, don't get careless. Besides Elijah, there's one more person you have to watch."

"Paisley."

His voice was heavy. "The rumor on the base is that the Level Eight mission, the one that should've wiped out everyone, ended with everyone coming back alive. The key to it was Paisley."

"She's not simple. Her power is strong and she hides a lot. She's the biggest variable. Don't underestimate her."

Damon's eyes narrowed. "Father, I understand."

"One more thing we need to deal with."

He suddenly remembered, his brows drawing together.

"I've heard that Jasper has been close with Paisley these past couple of days. He even offended the Thompson senior branch for her and had all three of them locked up."

"He's always been reckless and childish. But he's a Level Five Affinity user now, and he can't be ignored."

"If he lets his feelings get in the way, he could secretly help her and interfere with our plan. He'd ruin everything."

A cold look crossed Russell's face, and he decided at once.

"That's easy. Send people now to lock Jasper in the annex and watch him closely. He's going nowhere for the next few days, and no visitors."
