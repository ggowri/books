Chapter 202

Chase and Rachel each had their own angle, but they hit it off right away. Together, they settled on a vicious plan against Paisley's group, and now they were just waiting to make their move once they arrived.

The cars raced on, bouncing through the bleak ruins on the edge of the city.

Half an hour later, three military SUVs pulled up at the gates of the abandoned aquarium, which sat as silent as a grave.

The once-busy aquarium had crumbled into ruins. Its walls were cracked and peeling, broken glass covered the ground, and dark, contaminated moss grew over everything. The sea wind blew a thick, rotting stench into their faces, and the whole place felt heavy and eerie.

The moment the convoy stopped, it stirred up the monsters lurking nearby.

Swarms of low-level zombies poured out of the ruins, the overgrown medians, and every alley around them. They staggered toward the vehicles, shrieking, a dark mass closing in from every side.

Everyone pushed the doors open, jumped out, and got ready to fight.

Paisley's eyes narrowed. On instinct, she opened her mind all the way and sent out her high-level psychic power to scan the area and sense any hidden danger.

But a second later, she frowned in surprise. Her psychic sense should've covered the whole aquarium, but something was blocking it, and she couldn't sense a thing.

That's weird. At my level, nothing should be able to block me like this.

There was only one explanation. Something inside the aquarium was blocking every psychic probe, whether it was some kind of seal, a strange magnetic field, or a psychic mutant even stronger than her.

A blind spot like this, where no one knew what was waiting inside, usually hid the worst danger. Paisley's guard went up at once.

By then, Elijah had gotten replies in the team chat. Two teammates wanted to buy energy guns, but each of them only had 5,000 contribution points right now and would have to pay off the other 5,000 over time.

Once both of them had sent her 5,000 points, Paisley's balance came to 71,690 contribution points.

With a thought, she made two more energy guns appear in her hands and passed them to the two teammates. "You can owe me the rest. Pay it back whenever you've got it."

The two of them took the sleek, high-tech guns, stunned and thrilled. These things really were the real deal. No wonder they cost 10,000 points.

They couldn't wait to see what the guns could do.

Blue light streaked through the air again and again, far more powerful than any ordinary gun. The swarm of low-level zombies didn't stand a chance and went down in droves.

In just a few minutes, every zombie that had swarmed them was dead. Bodies covered the ground, along with a scattering of low-level cores.

They scooped up the cores, practically buzzing. This gun is insane! Worth every single point.

The others hadn't wanted to pay for energy guns, and they were worried about burning through their affinities. Now, all they had were regular pistols and blades, and they were hacking away at the zombies with everything they had.

The contrast was huge. One look was enough to see just how good the energy guns were.

Now, the rest of them were itching to get one too.

Soon, the entrance was clear.

Only then did Chase get out of the car, taking his sweet time, one arm slung lazily around Rachel's waist.

The two of them were all over each other and acting like it was a joke, with none of the tension or focus a high-risk mission called for. They looked like they'd come out for a picnic.

Everyone watched them goof around like that and felt nothing but irritation and contempt.

Elijah stood at the head of the team, tall and stern. His usually gentle face had gone ice cold.

He watched the two of them stroll over and spoke in a hard, flat voice. "Chase, use your psychic power to scan the area right now. I need to know if anything dangerous is hiding around here."

At the same time, the team's scanning gear powered up. Blue light swept across the ruined aquarium as it scanned the building floor by floor for its layout and any movement inside.

Then, Elijah turned to Rachel, who was still playing the fragile damsel, his voice carrying a captain's absolute authority. "Ms. Lennox, you foresaw where the mission targets are. Now show us exactly which room has the classified files and the precision equipment."

The moment he said it, every eye turned to Rachel.

Some were hopeful, some were waiting to see, and plenty were skeptical. After all, she'd gotten onto this high-risk mission on nothing but one vague prediction, and everyone wanted to see if she really had that kind of power.

Chase might be a little full of himself, but at least his Level Five psychic power was real.

Rachel's heart clenched. Her fingers curled into fists, and panic started rising inside her.
