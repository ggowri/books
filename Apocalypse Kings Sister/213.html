Chapter 213

After a few more rounds, the mutant finally figured it out. The energy gun in Paisley's hand could actually kill it, and it didn't dare keep fighting.

Its broken and regrown fingers slammed a button on its right side, and black mist suddenly swirled up around it.

A second later, its body melted into the darkness of the tunnel. With the chaotic underground magnetic field as cover, it vanished in a flash, leaving nothing behind but puddles of sticky poison and a lingering sense of menace.

Everyone rushed over to their poisoned teammate and propped him up.

His face had turned ink-black, and his lips were bluish purple. He kept twitching all over and could barely breathe. The poison had already gotten into his organs, and he was in critical condition.

Someone looked at him dying and said in a shaky, panicked voice, "He's way too poisoned, and he can't hold on much longer! What do we do?"

Another team member quickly dug the last of the emergency antibiotics out of his pocket and fed them to him, trying to keep him calm. "Hang in there, man! You're gonna make it!

"As soon as we get back, we'll get the best healer at the base to fix you up! They saved the captain and the others when they were dying, didn't they?"

But the medicine couldn't stop the raging poison at all. It did absolutely nothing.

Despair swallowed the dying man. He gasped for breath, his eyelids so heavy he could barely keep them open, and with the last of his strength, he weakly said his final words.

"I... I'm not gonna make it... Please, when you get back... tell my wife and kids... to keep living... I'm useless... I can't be there for them anymore..."

As soon as he finished, he tasted blood and coughed up a mouthful of reeking black, poisoned blood. His body went limp, and his breathing grew even weaker, like he could stop breathing any second.

Elijah couldn't bear to watch, and he turned to the other two with a heavy voice. "We can't save him here. Ask him if there's anything else he wants, so he can go without regrets...

"Yesterday I went to the head of the base, but he didn't give us a high-level healer. All he sent were Chase and Rachel, and they only came to cause trouble. I've never seen a leader this unreliable."

When everyone heard that, they felt even more hopeless. All they could do was watch their teammate die.

Just then, Paisley finished scanning the dark tunnel and made sure the mutant was really gone. Then, she walked over slowly and crouched down to check the team member's wounds.

When she was done, she spoke calmly. "Carry him over to that clear spot. I'll treat him."

The moment she said it, the whole place went silent. Everyone stared at her in shock, frozen where they stood.

Even Elijah and Orion stared at her in disbelief.

After everything they'd been through together, they knew what Paisley was like.

She was always calm and in control, and she handled things carefully. She never talked big, and she never did anything she wasn't sure about.

If she said she could treat him, then she really had the skills to heal.

Still, the news hit them hard.

Paisley could even heal, a rare power on top of everything else. How many more surprises was she hiding?

They both respected her from the bottom of their hearts.

One of his teammates was still frozen in place, staring at Paisley in doubt and disbelief, forgetting to move.

"You can heal people? You can really save him? How sure are you?

"He's got a wife and kids waiting for him to come home.

"I'm not asking you to fully cure him. Just keep him alive until we get back to base, and I'll get that mysterious healer to treat him, even if I have to beg."

Paisley had figured they might not believe her. Her face stayed calm, but there was a serious edge to it as she warned them.

"Go ahead and keep doubting me, but right now, you've got no other options. Every minute you waste, he gets closer to missing his best shot at being saved, and he doesn't have much time left.

"Whether you trust me and let me treat him is up to you."

The poisoned team member had collapsed in the others' arms, and he was already on the edge of passing out. The voices around him sounded faint and blurry, but his will to survive still held on to the last bit of his awareness.

He struggled to lift his heavy eyelids. His bluish lips kept trembling, and his twitching grew weaker. His chest rose and fell faintly, and every breath came with stabbing pain and the taste of blood.

The poison had already eaten into his organs. If this dragged on any longer, he was going to die for sure.
