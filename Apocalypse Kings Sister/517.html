Chapter 517

Ashley glanced at the new crystal cores and contribution points on her panel. Her eyes lit up and a bright smile spread across her face.

Another day of big gains.

"Thanks, Paisley!"

Thomas smiled softly and looked at Paisley.

"We got this much because of you, Pais!"

Elijah looked at Paisley and lowered his voice, thick with emotion.

"Thank you."

He knew that without Paisley, the team could never have brought back this much clean water in one trip.

Once the base ran out of water, chaos would break out fast. The Quinn family and other rivals would seize the chance to attack, and the one who'd clean up the mess and carry the pressure would be him, or his father.

Paisley had stabilized things ahead of time and avoided a huge disaster.

Deep down he marveled that the girl he loved was amazing.

Paisley nodded.

"No problem. We're a team."

She was already thinking that the mission was done and Trovie's promised reward should be in.

In the next instant, Trovie's chime rang in her head.

[Master has completed the main quest and found a new water source for the base. Crisis resolved.]

[Reward: 30,000 points and one Awakening Pill. Already issued, Master. Please check.]

[Current total: 3,600,360 points! Affinity Awakening Pill x1]

The onlookers watched the squad split the rewards, full of emotion and envy.

To outsiders it looked like they earned contribution points effortlessly, but only those who'd lived at the base for a long time knew how hard points were to earn.

Supplies were running thin and conditions kept getting worse. Missions would only get more dangerous, and earning points would only get harder.

Many sighed quietly, their eyes full of longing. If only they had Paisley's strength.

People really were different. Fate was never fair!

The registrar put away his logbook, said a quick goodbye, and left.

Elijah turned to Paisley.

"What about the public notice about cutting ties? How about we put it out now?"

Paisley looked at him with big, pitiful eyes.

"Okay. Why don't you help me?"

"I'm not good with statements, and I'm afraid it won't be airtight and someone will use it against me."

"Sure!"

Elijah couldn't resist her when she asked like that.

He melted.

He turned to the others.

"If you're not busy, come to my office. I'll have someone handle this."

As Paisley's big brother, Orion wanted to help his sister finally put this headache to rest.

Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley had no objections.

They left the mission hall together by car and went to the office building, then walked into Elijah's private office.

Elijah called in his aide and gave the order in a low voice to draft and issue the statement.

The notice stated in black and white that Paisley had cut all family ties with the Lennox family long ago, with a scanned copy of the severance agreement she'd signed uploaded to the base's public information board.

It stated clearly:

From now on, nothing the Lennox family did had anything to do with Paisley.

If anyone in the Lennox family went on using Paisley's name to commit crimes outside, it was entirely their own doing and had nothing to do with Paisley herself.

Paisley herself would pursue full accountability under base law.

Once it was out, it spread through every residential block and the mission hall in no time.

Countless base residents and Affinity team members saw the notice and the talk began.

Some suddenly understood. They remembered hearing that Rachel had been using Paisley's name to take supplies outside. She'd been faking an identity all along.

Others admired how openly Paisley had drawn the line early, so she wouldn't be dragged down again.

Of course, some people quietly disliked what she'd done and called her an ungrateful wretch behind her back.

At the Lennox villa, Gordon and Helen saw the notice on their terminal, and their faces went dark.

Helen slammed the table and shrieked.

"Ungrateful wretch! A real ungrateful wretch we never could win over!"

Gordon's face was iron, his chest heaving, furious.

"Why would she go out of her way to post this? It makes it look like the Lennox family was begging to claim her as family!"

"She's gotten somewhere, and this is how she treats us. We've lost all face. I should've strangled her long ago."
