Chapter 128

If it weren't for her, they might all be lying at the bottom of the river right now.

It was also the first time any of them had seen Paisley use her ability.

So she'd awakened a Plant Affinity, and a high-level one at that.

With someone that strong on their side, they'd all be a lot safer.

The way Elijah looked at Paisley changed. There was something deeper in it now.

He and Orion already knew about her Storage Dimension.

She'd asked for Plant cores before, and now it turned out she'd been hiding this all along.

She'd even kept him and Orion in the dark.

Paisley actually had two affinities.

How many other secrets is she keeping from me?

Elijah was more curious about her than ever, and he admired her like crazy!

He'd work hard to get stronger, strong enough to deserve her.

When Rachel saw this, she finally realized something was off about Paisley.

Paisley had actually awakened a Plant Affinity, which meant she must have discovered the pendant's secret.

When did she figure it out? Was everything she did before just an act to fool me?

So Paisley had been playing her this whole time. That explained why she'd changed so much.

Not so fast. I need to find a way to test her.

Now that they were safe, everyone finally relaxed. They all looked relieved and happy to have made it out alive.

But before their smiles had even faded, disaster struck again!

All the noise from the fight had stirred up the whole middle of the river!

The quiet river surged with huge waves again, and countless massive black shadows shot up from the depths toward them!

One, two, three... More mutant sea beasts than they could count came at them in wave after wave. They blocked every escape route and trapped the ship in the middle of a ring of beasts.

A rotten, fishy stench swept over the deck. Low- and mid-level sea beasts roared and thrashed nonstop, and water exploded everywhere. A whole new giant horde of sea beasts was closing in!

Everyone's faces turned grim, and their blood ran cold.

Just yesterday, they'd barely survived a deadly rat swarm, and they'd only just killed a Level Five sea beast. Now, they had to face a terrifying army of sea beasts!

What were they supposed to do now?

Everyone felt helpless and panicked.

There was nowhere to hide on the open water. Unlike fighting on land, the terrain out here worked completely against them.

All they had under their feet was one lone ship. With no way to retreat and no room to move, all they could do was take the hits. It was a death trap.

The horde launched a fierce attack, and countless tentacles and gaping mouths took turns slamming into the hull and chewing at it!

The pounding went on and on, deafeningly loud. The old ship had been built long before the apocalypse and had seen better days. It couldn't stand up to this many mutant beasts hammering at it.

Within moments, the sides of the ship were covered in dents and cracks. The frame twisted out of shape, and the damage kept getting worse.

Cold river water gushed in through the cracks and flooded the cabins faster and faster. They could actually see the water rising.

The ship rocked violently and kept tilting. It was on the verge of falling apart and couldn't hold out much longer.

They all knew it wouldn't be long before the ship broke apart and sank.

When that happened, the sea beasts wouldn't even have to lift a tentacle. They'd all drown in the river, and nobody would ever find their bodies.

Death hung over all of them, and despair spread across the deck.

Paisley's face grew serious.

The hovercar she'd traded for was sitting in her dimension. If things got desperate, it was her only way out.

But it came with a risk.

Not everyone on the team was someone she could trust. The three Lennox brothers were here too, and so was Rachel, who'd been secretly jealous of her and out to get her all along.

A hovercar was high-tech beyond anything anyone in the apocalypse could imagine. It was way too unbelievable.

The second she took it out in front of everyone, people would notice.

Rachel was vicious enough to tip someone off behind Paisley's back. She'd paint Paisley as a freak or a test subject and get the higher-ups at the base interested. In the end, Paisley could get captured and locked up in some lab, and the trouble would never end.

Unless she absolutely had to, she wasn't going to show her hand.

Orion came over to comfort her.

"Paisley, don't be scared. I'm right here, and I'll protect you. Even if it kills me, I'll fight our way out of this for you."

Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley gathered around her too.

"I'll protect you too," Thomas said.

"Same here, Paisley. I've got your back," Spencer added.

"Me too, Paisley! I'll protect you!" Ashley chimed in.
