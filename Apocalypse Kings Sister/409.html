Chapter 409

The base was running out of food and everyone's survival was on the line. That was a dead end no one could avoid.

If he backed down today, Falcon Squad's elite name would become a joke, and the Blackwoods' standing would drop anyway.

Rather than be forced back step by step, he would take the death trap himself and turn it around.

Elijah took the printed pledge, picked up a pen, and signed in sharp, decisive strokes in front of everyone. He stamped his seal.

With that single sheet of paper, he had put his future, his squad's honor, and the Blackwoods' foundation on the line.

Still, if he succeeded, Falcon Squad's reputation would only grow.

The tense meeting finally ended. The powerful stood and drifted out, murmuring, each carrying their own thoughts, mockery, curiosity, hope, pity.

The room that had been so charged went quiet and empty.

Everyone left except Damon.

He leaned lazily against a pillar by the door, one hand in his pocket, his eyes fixed on Elijah as he walked out.

When Elijah got close, Damon smirked, his voice full of mockery and challenge.

"All these years, and you're still good at pretending. Always calm, always in control."

"But I have to admit, you're lucky. Every time you're trapped, someone shows up to help."

He leaned closer, studying Elijah's eyes. "So what is it? Is that new Thompson girl, Paisley, why you're brave enough to take a suicide mission?"

Before Elijah could answer, Damon pushed on, every word a deliberate prod at the one spot he knew was sore.

"What if I decided to go after her for real? Win her over, get her to stand with me and join the Quinns. Wouldn't that be fun?"

It was flippant and reckless, a naked provocation.

Elijah had stayed expressionless all day. The scheming, the mockery, the pressure had never rattled him once.

He had been trained since childhood to restrain himself, to keep his face blank.

But the moment someone talked about Paisley that way, the wall inside him collapsed.

Rage tore through him.

His eyes iced over, the air around him dropped to freezing, and before Damon could say another word, Elijah drove his fist into Damon's cheek.

A heavy thud echoed.

"I'm warning you. Stay away from her."

His voice was low and rough, cold and possessive, a line that could not be crossed.

Damon took the full blow. His cheek went red and numb, and he tasted blood.

But he wasn't angry. He laughed out loud, wild and delighted, as if he had figured everything out.

"Elijah, I never thought I'd see this."

"The untouchable, unfeeling, always-calm Blackwood heir, losing it over one person."

He tilted his head and spat a mouthful of blood onto the floor, his eyes sharpening. "This is getting more interesting by the minute."

"Before, you had nothing to lose. No weak spot. I was always a step behind. But now, huh. You've got a weakness."

"We have plenty of time. Let's see how it goes."

Elijah stared down at his trembling fist. His dark eyes held a panic and heat he hadn't recognized in himself.

He froze.

He'd spent more than twenty years in life-and-death battles and power games, and his mind was solid as stone. He had never lost control for even a second.

But just hearing someone toy with Paisley's name had broken him.

He was done for.

It wasn't simple attraction. It was something that had sunk into his bones and run through his blood.

Paisley was his only weakness, and his only obsession.

The feeling surged up and longing flooded him, drowning everything else. Right now he didn't care about anything.

He just wanted to see her. Immediately.
