Chapter 309

"These are gifts from me. I'm paying for them myself."

She ruffled Ashley's hair as she said it.

"Help me pick something out. What would Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Elijah actually like?"

Ashley blinked, scanning the shop, and her eyes landed on a short blade tucked in a corner case, the blade catching the light, sharp and cold. Her face lit up.

She grabbed Paisley's arm.

"Paisley! Honestly, with everything the way it is now, fancy accessories don't really matter much. What if you got them each a knife instead? Something sharp, easy to carry, actually useful if things go bad?"

The shopkeeper behind the counter chuckled at that, stepping in with a patient smile.

"That's not quite the whole story, young lady."

He picked up his bestselling watch, a sleek, minimalist piece, and held it out.

"This runs three thousand points, and it's not just for show."

"Inside the casing are ten ultra-fine alloy needles. Completely hidden — you'd never spot them with the naked eye. In an emergency, they fire out fast enough to disable an attacker, or hit a precise point to save your life."

"The needles can also be coated with poison or sedative, whatever suits the wearer's fighting style. Extremely practical."

Ashley's interest spiked instantly.

"Can you show us how it works? We'd love to see it."

Paisley's brow lifted slightly, genuinely curious. A watch this small, this unassuming, hiding ten needles seemed almost unbelievable.

The shopkeeper slid the watch on, aimed at a spare wooden board, and pressed a hidden button on the side of the case.

There was a faint hiss, and needles thinner than hair shot out, too fast to track, burying themselves in the wood with a force that left only the tips trembling.

He tapped the button again, and the needles retracted silently, vanishing back into the casing like they'd never been there.

"Silent, unexpected, works in close range or at a distance. Great for taking out the dead or holding your ground against people."

The shopkeeper kept going.

"Waterproof, shock-resistant, built to take a hit. You could wear it into a fight and never think twice."

Ashley's eyes went wide.

"That's incredible! It looks so plain, but it's that good?"

Paisley had already made up her mind.

Understated, functional, nearly impossible to detect — perfect for Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Elijah.

"I'll take four, one of each pattern."

She pointed to the four watches in the case, her tone decisive.

"And wrap up that knife too."

The shopkeeper blinked. This was easily the biggest sale he'd made in ages, the total coming to roughly twenty thousand points. Half-expecting her to reconsider, he quickly started boxing everything up.

"Right away! Let me wrap these up for you."

He carefully packaged the four watches, wiped down the blade, and slid it into a custom sheath.

"Good eye, miss. This blade's forged steel, cuts through almost anything. It'll go through the dead without any trouble, and it's light enough for a smaller frame to handle easily."

Ashley's eyes went round, and she waved her hands.

"Paisley, you don't have to get me one! It's way too expensive, I already have something for protection!"

"Take it."

Paisley's tone was gentle but left no room for argument.

"Out here, one more weapon means one more layer of safety."

"Consider it a welcome gift. If you really think of me as your sister, you'll take it."

The four watches came to three thousand points each, twelve thousand total. The blade added another eight thousand on top of that. Twenty thousand points, all told.

Enough to carry an average survivor through several comfortable years at the base. Not a small sum by any measure.

The shopkeeper watched her pay without so much as a blink and felt a quiet jolt of disbelief. This mild, elegant girl had more money to burn than he'd seen from anyone else all season.
