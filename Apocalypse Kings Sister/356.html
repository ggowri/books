Chapter 356

Mrs. Blackwood took the wariness in stride, not a flicker of annoyance, her smile gentle as she explained.

"Don't worry, Mrs. Thompson. I'm not trying to push anything on her."

"My Elijah's always been cold, hard to read, never once cared about anyone that way his whole life.

But after this trip, every other word out of his mouth was praise for Pais. She saved my son's life more than once — doesn't that mean more than any bracelet?"

There was real sincerity in her eyes.

"As his parents, we've never once seen him this genuine about anyone.

This pendant's a family heirloom, sure, but to me it doesn't come close to what my son feels for her.

I just genuinely like Pais, that's all. This is just a welcome gift, nothing to do with any engagement."

******

Nathaniel chimed in right on cue, his tone earnest.

"That's right. We don't mean anything by it.

That old betrothal was just something we joked about as kids ourselves. Times have changed — it's entirely up to the two of them now. We won't force anything."

Everyone's eyes turned to Elijah, who'd stayed quiet this whole time.

He stood tall under the lamplight, glancing from her mother, still guarding Paisley fiercely, to Paisley herself, calm and composed. Something gentle settled in his eyes, his tone measured and careful.

"Mrs. Thompson, my mother genuinely just likes Pais and wants to give her a gift. Nothing more."

"Liking her is my own business. I'd never use some old contract or family obligation to push her into anything."

"I just want to earn her affection on my own terms. If she doesn't want it, I'll let it go, for good, and never bother her again."

Every word was open, sincere, with no trace of calculation left in it, and the whole room's tension eased at once.

Paisley hadn't expected to end up as the subject of her own gossip.

Elijah likes me?

Of all people, him, confessing right in front of both families.

This was... beyond awkward.

She looked at him, their eyes met, and she quickly looked away.

Her heart seemed to skip a few beats.

Do I like him?

Honestly, she didn't know.

Romance wasn't something she'd ever let herself think about, not since coming back.

Back before the apocalypse, at the Lennoxes', she'd had a childhood friend too, a betrothal arranged before she turned ten.

But everything changed the moment Rachel came back to the Lennox family.

He'd started taking everything she did for granted, pouring all his effort into winning Rachel over instead.

Since coming back, love had simply never been on her mind at all.

Hearing Elijah's confession this bluntly left her completely at a loss.

Watching the pure, restrained affection in Elijah's eyes, and the open, warm expressions on his parents' faces, her mother's tension finally began to ease.

She could tell plainly — every word from this family was genuine, no scheming behind it, and Elijah's feelings for her daughter ran deep and clean.

After a long moment, she let out a slow breath, her expression softening, though her voice still carried a protective edge.

"I understand how you feel, and I'm grateful for how much Elijah cares about Pais."

"But she's been through so much, drifting for years, and she's only just come home. All I want is for her to be safe and happy, to live however she wants, free of anyone's expectations."

"Who she likes is entirely up to her."

Mrs. Blackwood nodded gently, letting the matter rest, setting the rosewood box down on the side table, her smile warm.

"I understand completely. I'll just leave this here. Whether she takes it is entirely up to Pais. We won't push."

Her mother changed the subject smoothly, gesturing everyone toward their seats with a smile.

"All right, enough of that. Tonight's about welcoming the kids home. Let's not talk about anything else — shall we eat?"

Her father jumped in to smooth things over, waving everyone to their seats, and warm laughter filled the hall at once.

Dishes circled the table, laughter rose easily, and the whole dinner settled into something warm and lively.

Glasses clinked and old stories were traded back and forth, and an hour slipped by almost without notice.

Her father and Nathaniel excused themselves to the study, to work through family affairs, alliances, and what came next — a quiet, serious conversation unfolding behind closed doors.

That left just Diane, Mrs. Blackwood, Paisley, and Ashley in the hall.
