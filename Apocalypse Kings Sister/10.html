Chapter 10

Elijah moved in a blur. In the blink of an eye, he closed the distance, his hand shooting out to clamp around the zombie's neck. A sharp, brutal twist followed, and with a sickening crack, the creature went limp.

"Thud."

It hit the floor, motionless.

The whole thing took less than a second. Clean, efficient, deadly.

Paisley watched, her gaze thoughtful. Even weakened by fever, Elijah moved like someone who'd seen combat long before the world fell apart. No wonder he'd become one of the legends later on. That uniform—probably the special forces.

Once the mutated zombie was down, Elijah's expression didn't soften. His voice was low, edged with warning.

"Check yourselves. If you feel disoriented, nauseous, anything off—speak up immediately. Tonight, anything can go wrong at any moment."

Then his strength gave out, and he passed out cold.

So he'd been pushing through it. With their de facto leader down, the tension in the room thickened.

At first it was just the fever making everyone miserable. But after seeing someone turn right in front of them, fear hung heavy in the air.

Another ten minutes ticked by.

Suddenly, Mason's body jerked. The heat radiating off him vanished, and his eyes flew open. He stared at his own palm, disbelief all over his face.

"I-I think I can... I can feel the air. I can move it."

He flexed his hand slightly, and a small gust swept across the table, rustling loose papers.

Wind Affinity.

A murmur of excitement went through the group.

Not long after, Orion and Kelly came to as well, their fevers breaking one after the other. Orion could shape metal with his mind. Kelly summoned water from the air.

But Elijah still hadn't stirred, forehead damp with sweat, breathing labored.

Paisley watched him, noting the tight clench of his jaw, the pain he held back even unconscious. She knew what was coming. Lightning Affinity—a mutated high-tier ability. The awakening was brutal, and it took time.

Just as the worry in the room was peaking, the system pinged softly in Paisley's mind.

"Ding! One of your listed items has been sold!

"Twenty points have been added. Your current total stands at forty."

The crimson rain kept falling through the night.

They took shifts keeping watch. By the time dawn washed the sky pale, the downpour outside had finally ceased.

As light crept into the room, Elijah's eyes opened.

The moment he focused, an arc of sharp, crackling electricity flashed over his palm. A soft snap echoed, blue-white current flickering once before vanishing.

Lightning Affinity—mutated variant.

A collective sigh of relief filled the space. After what happened last night, everyone had been on edge, especially about Elijah.

He was their anchor. Seeing him wake up with a power like that... it let the tension drain right out of them.

They ate a quick breakfast, and Elijah called everyone together.

His tone was all business, calm and focused.

"The red rain's stopped, but after a night like that, the outside's more dangerous than ever. Zombie numbers are up. Mutated plants, animals—everything's more active.

"Our goal hasn't changed. We get back to Aetheris, find our families. But before we hit the road, we're making a supply run. We head to the Southspire Mall—biggest one in the area.

"Grab your personal gear now. We move in five."

He scanned the group, mentally tallying.

"Nine of us. Five men, four women. Four have awakened abilities so far. From here on, those with powers look out for those without.

"In times like these, we stick together. That's the only way we make it to Aetheris in one piece."

Orion spoke up first, "I'll cover Paisley and Madison."

Thomas and Spencer hadn't awakened yet. They knew better than to argue when they couldn't protect their little sister themselves, so they just nodded.

Ashley let out a quiet sigh, disappointment shadowing her eyes. She wanted to stay close to her sister, but without an ability, she felt useless in a fight. She stayed silent, looking down.

The others quickly paired off, making sure everyone had someone watching their back.

Elijah's voice cut through one last time.

"Gear up. We roll out on schedule."

Everyone scattered to their rooms—everyone except Paisley. She stayed on the couch, turning Orion's diamond-edged blade over in her hands, calm amid the hurry.

All her things were already tucked away in her pendant's Storage Dimension. She had nothing to pack.
