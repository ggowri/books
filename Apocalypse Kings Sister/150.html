Chapter 150

She hated how Paisley got to shine, she hated everyone who'd just stood by and watched, and most of all, she hated Elijah for being so cold and playing favorites.

She wanted revenge for every injustice, and she'd make every last person who'd broken her heart pay!

In the pitch-black night, Kelly lowered her voice, cold and determined.

"I'm in. So, what do you need me to do?"

At that, a vicious, satisfied smile slowly spread across Rachel's face. She was clearly calculating every move.

"You get to spend more time with Paisley than anyone else on the team. When base missions open up, if she takes a mission outside the walls, volunteer to go with her.

"While you're out there, use a pager to secretly keep me posted.

"We'll work it from both sides and make our move when she's alone outside and her guard is down."

She was afraid something might go wrong, so she quickly made Kelly an offer too good to refuse.

"All I want is that broken pendant Paisley wears. Everything else, all her crystal cores and supplies, is yours."

With so much to gain, the two of them, each with a hidden agenda of her own, struck a deal on the spot.

Kelly's face hardened with hatred. "Deal."

Once they'd worked out every detail, the two of them snuck back to their crowded room like nothing had happened.

The night slowly faded, and dawn broke. Pale morning light came through the base's windows and chased away the darkness, and a brand-new day began.

As soon as the sun came up, the base was bustling again. Teams hurried back and forth, busy taking missions and earning crystal cores.

Inside Paisley's single room, everything was calm and quiet.

Breaking through and locking in her new levels last night had worn her out a little, so she was sleeping deeply. Her breathing was slow and even, and she looked completely relaxed.

Suddenly, there was a soft but urgent knock at the door, followed by Ashley's bright, sweet voice.

"Paisley! Paisley, wake up! We need to go pick up missions at the mission hall!"

Ashley stood outside the door, looking eager.

Spencer and Thomas stood tall behind her, waiting patiently for the door to open. The three of them had gotten ready first thing that morning to come get Paisley.

In bed, Paisley slowly woke up, still a little drowsy.

She gently rubbed the fluffy belly of the little white cat in her arms. Her voice was soft and a little hoarse from sleep.

"Go open the door."

"Meow!" The little white cat answered sweetly, its big, clear eyes shiny and adorable.

It rolled over, hopped off the bed, and pattered across the floor on its little legs. Then, it stood up on its hind legs, pulled down the door handle like a pro, and pushed the door wide open.

The three of them walked right in.

Paisley slowly got up and changed into a simple tracksuit. Then, she went into her private bathroom and quickly brushed her teeth and washed her face.

When she was done, she put on her skincare and asked, "Is it just you three? Where are Elijah and Orion? Why aren't they with you?"

Spencer answered right away. "Captain Blackwood and Orion had something else to take care of."

He paused, then added, "Before he left, Captain Blackwood told us to go take some missions at the mission hall today."

Paisley nodded and pulled her long hair up into a high ponytail. The clean, sporty look made her seem sharp and full of energy, ready to head out on a mission.

She looked at the three of them and asked gently, "Have you guys eaten breakfast?"

Ashley's face fell, and she looked pitiful. "Paisley, I was in such a rush this morning that I only had a piece of dry bread. I'm still starving, and it tasted like nothing."

Spencer laughed helplessly. "I'm not much better. I just choked down a cold piece of hardtack with some water, and it didn't taste like anything either."

Right after he finished, Thomas's stomach let out a loud, perfectly timed growl. It was super obvious in the quiet room, and things got a little awkward in a cute way.

Clearly, they'd run out of food.

That was why they were heading out to take missions and earn crystal cores today.

Paisley couldn't help laughing, and she gave them a soft smile.
