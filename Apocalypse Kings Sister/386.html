Chapter 386

Flowing water could be soft or sharp, able to form into gentle streams for everyday use, or condense into blades and shields, endlessly adaptable, under her complete command.

With her Water Affinity upgrade complete, the remaining vast store of pure energy surged entirely into her consciousness, refining her mind and soul relentlessly.

Fed by the massive influx of energy, her psychic power underwent another leap forward.

Her vast mental space was further widened, thickened, and solidified, the once-fine threads of her mind stretched, refined, and condensed, growing tough and razor-sharp.

Her psychic perception range expanded again, many times over, its precision down to the smallest detail; her psychic assault, mental restraint, and mind-piercing lethality surged layer by layer, overwhelming and unstoppable.

Every form of psychic illusion, mental suppression, and emotional interference now failed completely against her utterly unshakable soul.

The barrier shattered. Spirit light bloomed.

Her Psychic Affinity broke through in one continuous surge, climbing straight from mid Level Six through late Level Six, finally settling at the very peak of Level Six, just one step from Level Seven.

Time slipped by as crystal after crystal of high-tier Galactic energy shattered and dissolved.

The pure energy kept flowing in without end, nourishing both Affinities, tempering her body, and reinforcing her foundation the whole time.

Until, finally, the last crystal was spent, and all five hundred precious high-tier Galactic energy crystals turned to fine gray-white dust, scattering across the floor of the system space, completely consumed.

The upgrade was complete.

Paisley let out a long, slow breath and opened her eyes.

In that moment, she felt, clearly and completely, the sweeping transformation within her.

Her body had been thoroughly refined, muscle dense and tough, blood and energy running hot and full, her self-healing resistance far beyond what it had been.

Her meridians ran wide and clear, her Affinity flowing smooth and unimpeded, all three Affinities working in harmony, each amplifying the others.

Mid-to-peak Level Seven Plant Affinity, with vast life force capable of regrowing limbs, purifying anything, and the lethal Parasitize kill move.

The Level Seven mutant Fire Affinity gifted by the little red fox, able to burn anything to ash.

Peak Level Six mutant Water Affinity, offense and healing both covered, water control fully at her command, soft yet sharp, flexible in every direction.

Peak late-Level Six Psychic Affinity, with vast perception reaching miles out, an unbreakable soul, and the power to kill with a single thought.

Three high-tier Affinities stacked together, her foundation was terrifyingly deep.

Only now did Paisley truly let out a breath of relief.

Growing stronger meant she wouldn't be easily discarded. It meant she wouldn't be so easily killed.

In the apocalypse, the most dangerous thing was always people's hearts, anyone could betray you. Only your own strength was something you could truly rely on.

Her power had grown strong enough now that she no longer needed to hide it.

If her enemies still wanted to hurt her, they'd better think carefully about whether they actually had what it took.

After a long, relaxing bath, Paisley settled in for a peaceful night's sleep.

The little white cat and the little fox each curled up nearby, quietly nestled at the foot of her bed, their established routine these past few days.

The next morning.

After breakfast, Paisley sprawled lazily across the wide lounge chair in the courtyard, basking in the sun.

The courtyard was open and spacious, sunlight falling warm across her, a soft blanket draped over her, gentle music playing nearby. The cat and the fox curled up at her feet, dozing in the warmth along with her.

This was the most relaxed stretch of time she'd had since her rebirth.

The mission was wrapped up, flawlessly. She could afford a few days of rest.

Diane walked over carrying a plate of sliced grapes and set it gently on the wooden table beside her.

"Pais, these are grapes from the base's latest supply drop. Try some."

Paisley caught a faint catch in her mother's voice and slowly opened her eyes, immediately noticing Diane's reddened eyes.

"Mom, what happened? Did someone bully you?"

Since the apocalypse, every household had to collect their rations from a designated supply depot.

Her mother had left that morning looking perfectly fine. One trip to collect supplies later, and something was clearly wrong.

Diane shook her head gently, forcing out a weak smile.

"Nothing. No one could ever wrong me."

Paisley's gaze sharpened. She caught sight of fresh scrapes along the backs of Diane's hands and ankles, the skin raw and reddened, still dusted with dirt.

She stood up at once, helping Diane sit down, carefully examining the injuries.

"Besides your arms and legs, is there anywhere else that's hurt?"

A soft, green glow of Plant Affinity life force flowed from her palm, settling gently over the scraped skin.

The wounds scabbed over and healed at a visible pace, the shallow scrapes fading fast.

The external injuries healed in moments.
