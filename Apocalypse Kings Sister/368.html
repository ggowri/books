Chapter 368

The other vehicle, carrying the Scarlet Squad, had the opposite mood entirely, tense and stifling.

The cabin sat in dead silence, every face grim.

Grant kept his expression dark, discussing strategy with the others in a low voice.

"That Level Seven mutant trumpet flower's toxic mist is nearly impossible to guard against. Even with our combat experience, we're not guaranteed a clean win."

"Falcon Squad's full of Level Six members. Going head-to-head, we might not come out on top."

The words only made the mood in the car heavier.

One teammate's eyes flashed with something vicious. He lowered his voice, making a suggestion.

"Captain, it's a field mission, remote, no witnesses, danger everywhere. Why don't we take the opportunity to make a move."

"Once we reach the trumpet flower's territory, we find our moment and quietly take out every member of Falcon Squad."

"If they die out in the mission zone, no one will ever know it was us. With Falcon Squad gone, we won't have to worry about losing the bet, and we definitely won't have to disband."

It was a vicious, underhanded plan, turning a public duel into a secret massacre. Nothing could be lower.

But in that moment, blinded by the drive to win, terrified of losing the wager and seeing their flagship squad dissolved, no one objected. The proposal was met with unanimous agreement.

A cold, cruel light flickered through Chloe's eyes as she nodded firmly.

"That's the plan. Cut the root, leave no trouble behind."

"Besides, they were always our biggest rivals anyway."

Both vehicles sped along, driving deep into the outskirts' ruined sprawl.

Along the road, scattered zombies and low-tier mutant beasts wandered among crumbling walls, death hanging thick in the air.

But both vehicles were heavily armored, and the fighters inside more than capable. Every stray zombie they encountered was dispatched in seconds, and the road stayed clear the whole way.

A full hour of driving later, they left the city ruins behind completely and arrived at the mission's core zone.

The view opened up all at once. A massive man-made reservoir stretched before them, its surface dark and still, surrounded by overgrown wild grass.

On the reservoir's bank stood a massive, abandoned grain silo, thirty-three feet tall.

Its walls were cracked and peeling in huge patches, exposing rusted, twisted rebar beneath. The broken window frames gaped like black hollows, a scene of pure desolation, the bleakness of the apocalypse laid bare.

And at the very top of the silo, a shade of violent, eerily beautiful purple-red was anchored in place, impossible to look away from.

Massive flower disks layered and unfurled, petals thick and strange, the whole plant monstrously, unbelievably large. This was the mission's target, the Level Seven mutant trumpet flower.

Its blossoms drooped slightly, trumpet-shaped petals swaying faintly. A pale purple mist, almost invisible to the naked eye, drifted silently around it, a mind-altering toxin, lethal and undetectable, extraordinarily dangerous.

Before anyone could move to investigate, Paisley's gaze went still and calm. Her fingers twitched, and she drew eight thin anti-toxin masks from her Storage Dimension, along with a Universal Antidote Pill, faintly fragrant with medicine, for each of them.

"Put the masks on. Take the pills."

Her voice was cool and even.

"Just in case."

Falcon Squad didn't hesitate for a second. Their trust in Paisley was absolute, unspoken.

Everyone masked up and swallowed the pills at once.

"Get out here."

Elijah said.

The sound of the engine broke the stillness.

Around the silo, deep in the grass, and in the ruined crevices along the reservoir's bank, countless dormant zombies jolted awake, their groaning rising in overlapping waves.

A dense horde of zombies, limbs stiff but moving with terrifying speed, surged toward the vehicles, a black, churning mass, the numbers staggering.

"Get ready to fight."

At Elijah's command, all eight of them shoved the doors open at once and leapt out, falling into a tight formation in an instant, every one of them braced and ready.

Standing at the edge of the formation, Ashley tilted her head back, staring up at the massive mutant flower atop the silo, eyes wide with shock. She couldn't help a startled exclamation.

"That flower is way too big!"

"It looks even taller than the silo itself, easily over thirty-five feet. If I hadn't seen it with my own eyes, I'd never believe a mutant plant this extreme could exist in the apocalypse."

The monstrous bloom before them went far beyond anything described in the mission briefing. Its sheer, eerie scale radiated an oppressive menace that made people want to look away.

Thomas stared hard at the pale purple mist drifting through the air, brow furrowed, a note of caution in his voice.
