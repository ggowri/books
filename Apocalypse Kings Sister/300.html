Chapter 300

The group's pent-up resentment finally poured out, every word dripping with mockery and bitterness, some of them even shoving all the blame squarely onto Rachel and Chase.

"Honestly, you're just bad luck! That group back there only had a grudge against you! This was all about you!"

"If you two hadn't stirred up trouble, they'd have helped us. Why do we have to end up like this because of you?"

One young man, especially emboldened, straightened up and shot back without an ounce of restraint.

"My brother's Captain Winslow at Stillwater Base. Who are you to order me around? You just got here yourself."

The chorus of insubordination and accusations finally ignited the fury Chase had been holding back.

Already seething from back-to-back humiliation and defeat, he wasn't about to let this pack of nobodies mock him to his face too.

Thinking back to how everyone at Rivergate Base used to grovel and defer to him, never once daring to talk back, the contrast with his current wretched state only fed the murderous intent building behind his eyes.

Chase's expression went pitch black. The psychic power coiled inside him burst free.

An invisible, formless storm of psychic force tore across the group, slamming with brutal precision into the young man who'd just talked back.

The man, who'd just claimed his brother was Captain Winslow, didn't even manage a full scream. His pupils blew wide and unfocused, his head seizing with sudden, splitting pain, his body locking rigid before he crashed to the ground, lifeless.

Dead silence fell over the scene.

The remaining few froze in horror, faces drained of all color, shrieking in terrified disbelief.

"He's dead! You actually killed him!"

"He was Captain Winslow's own brother! Are you insane? You're not even worried about the base coming after you for this?"

The panicked shouting hadn't even faded before Chase's eyes turned colder, murder still simmering, no sign of restraint.

After today's humiliation, he'd dropped the act entirely. Might as well kill to establish dominance, put the fear of god into this pack of fickle, opportunistic cowards.

His psychic attack struck again, invisible blades cutting precisely through the remaining few who'd dared mock and defy him moments ago.

Three heavy thuds rang out in quick succession as the four who'd led the defiance and mockery dropped dead on the spot.

Blood soaked slowly into the dirt beneath them, the metallic smell spreading fast, grim and unsettling.

Chase stood over the bodies, his presence radiating menace, his eyes cold and merciless, as he swept a slow glance over the handful of survivors left standing.

"Now. Anyone else have an opinion?"

The remaining survivors had already been scared out of their minds by the bloodshed, legs trembling, bodies shaking uncontrollably. Not one of them dared so much as think about defiance.

Only raw, bone-deep terror remained.

One of them scrambled up, voice shaking apart, hurrying to grovel.

"No— no opinion! I won't do it again! I'll do whatever you say! I'm going to make breakfast right now!"

Another chimed in just as fast, babbling in panic.

"I— I'll go find a vehicle in town right away! I promise I'll find something usable!"

Everyone bowed their heads low, docile as lambs, terrified they'd be the next one to die.

Off to the side, Rachel had gone pale with fear, curling into herself in a corner, too scared to even breathe loudly.

Her eyes were full of horror, a deep chill settling into her chest.

This was the first time she'd ever witnessed anything like this. He'd actually killed people.

She'd always thought Chase was just proud and domineering. She'd never imagined he was this unstable, this much of a monster underneath it all.

A few words of pushback, a little defiance, and he'd killed four people without a flicker of hesitation, brutal and merciless, utterly without humanity.

In that moment, all Rachel felt was deep, overwhelming dread.

What if he got angry one day and decided to kill her too?

She gritted her teeth, making up her mind.

Chase was too dangerous, too unpredictable. Staying with him would only get her killed eventually.

She had to bide her time, wait for the right moment, and get away from this madman.

Owen, Reid, Ryan, wait for me. I'll find my way back to you soon, and then we'll go find Mom together.

Meanwhile, Paisley's group drove on without incident, the road clear the whole way.

By midday, the sun hung high overhead, blazing light spilling across the land, and a massive, imposing base finally came into view in the distance.

Stillwater Base turned out to be even more impressive than any of them had imagined.

Tall, thick stone walls rose up, a full thirty-three feet high, reinforced and fortified, scarred with marks from countless zombie assaults, yet still standing solid and unshaken.
