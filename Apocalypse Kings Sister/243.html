Chapter 243

This kind of strength, this kind of composure under pressure, put her far beyond anyone else here. No one could deny it.

Ashley was the first to snap out of it, eyes shining with relief and admiration, and let out a cheer.

"We won! We actually beat that unstoppable Level Six bear!"

Curious, she crouched down and brushed her fingers over the vines still lying on the ground. The instant she felt the tiny thorns bristling along the surface, her eyes went wide.

"Oh my god! Paisley, your vines have thorns on them!"

She suddenly remembered how the bear had grown weaker the more it struggled, and looked up, curious.

"Do they paralyze too? Come on, Paisley, tell me!"

At her bright voice, everyone's attention snapped toward Paisley, curiosity written all over their faces.

They'd all seen how the bear had lost its strength so fast, and they'd all been wondering the same thing.

Thomas, who shared her Plant Affinity, was the most curious of all. He picked up a length of vine to study it, then grew one of his own to compare.

Side by side, the difference was obvious. They weren't even in the same league.

His vine was thin and soft, utterly unremarkable. Hers was thick, tough, bristling with thorns, and faintly wreathed in spirit energy.

The others crowded in to look, and the more they compared, the more unsettled they looked.

Thomas, utterly baffled, asked her outright.

"Pais, we've got the same Affinity. Why is yours nothing like mine? The gap's insane."

It wasn't until then that Paisley actually noticed the gap herself.

She'd grown so used to her own Affinity that she'd never thought to compare it to anyone else's.

Looking closer now, she understood why her vines alone had been enough to trap a Level Six bear that the whole group together couldn't dent.

Her vines weren't just tough and thorned. They carried a potent natural paralytic that specifically targeted mutant beast physiology, weakening them fast. No ordinary Plant Affinity came with that.

The group's confusion only deepened. Mason finally asked, low and serious.

"Pais, what level is your Plant Affinity, exactly?"

They'd fought side by side, bled together. There was no point hiding it from them anymore.

Paisley answered plainly, without hesitation.

"Level Six, mid-stage."

Four words, and the clearing went dead silent. The air itself seemed to freeze.

Everyone shut down a little inside.

The rest of them were still stuck at Level Four or Five. The gap was staggering.

The higher the level, the more a single rank meant.

Worse still, Paisley didn't just have a top-tier Plant Affinity with a built-in toxin that crushed the competition. She also had a Storage Affinity, rare beyond measure.

Two rare Affinities, both far beyond anyone else in the group.

They all exchanged looks, their feelings tangled into something close to despair.

They were all just survivors scraping by in the apocalypse. How could the gap between people be this wide?

Even Elijah, normally so composed, lowered his eyes, his thoughts in turmoil.

A Storage Affinity. A high-tier Plant Affinity. Two broken abilities stacked on top of each other, and she was barely out of her teens.

What was even left for him to chase?

Something complicated churned in his chest, hard to name.

He'd always known he was gifted, always carried himself with quiet confidence. Young, already at the top, a cut above his peers. Everyone called him a prodigy.

But next to Paisley, every advantage he had felt small. Fragile.

How was he supposed to make someone this dazzling, this powerful, this unreadable, fall for him?

For the first time, he felt genuinely small, genuinely powerless. Was he even enough for her, as he was now?

Was he just not strong enough?

Elijah, who'd never doubted himself in his life, had started to.

He wasn't the only one. Everyone there felt some version of the same shock, the same churn of thoughts.

Paisley, standing at the center of all that attention, hadn't noticed any of it.
