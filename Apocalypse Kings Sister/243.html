Chapter 243

Paisley's strength, mindset, and quick decisions in the moment were way beyond everyone else's, and they all had to admire her.

Ashley was the first to snap out of it, thrilled to be alive and full of admiration, and she couldn't help cheering. "We won! We actually beat that unstoppable Level Six bear!"

Curious, she hurried over, crouched down, and gently touched the vines left on the ground. As her fingertips brushed the surface, she felt dense little thorns, and her eyes went wide in surprise.

"Oh my God! Paisley, the vines you grew even have thorns!"

She suddenly remembered how strangely the bear had gotten weaker the more it struggled, and she quickly looked up, curious. "Do these vines paralyze things too? Paisley, tell me, do they?"

At her clear, bright voice, everyone turned to look at Paisley, full of curiosity.

They'd all seen how the giant bear had strangely lost its strength and weakened so fast, and they'd all been wondering about it too.

Thomas, who also had a Plant Affinity, was the most curious of all. He bent down, picked up a piece of vine, and studied it closely. Then, he used his own Plant Affinity to grow an ordinary vine and put the two side by side.

With the two vines next to each other, the difference was obvious. They weren't even in the same league.

The ordinary vine was thin and soft, not dangerous at all, and nothing special. Paisley's vine was thick and tough, covered in sharp thorns, and surrounded by a faint glow of energy.

Everyone crowded around to take a closer look, and the more they compared, the more shocked they were.

Thomas was baffled and asked her straight out. "Pais, we both have a Plant Affinity, so why are your vines so different from ours? The gap is way too big."

Only then did Paisley really notice how huge the difference was.

Before, she'd gotten so used to her own power that she'd never compared it to anyone else's or noticed anything unusual.

Now that she looked closely, she understood why her vines alone had been able to quickly trap and pin down the Level Six mutant bear, when even the whole team together could barely break through its defense.

Her Plant vines weren't just unbreakable and covered in sharp thorns. Most importantly, they naturally carried a strong paralyzing poison that worked especially well against mutant beasts and could quickly weaken them. Ordinary Plant powers didn't have anything close to that.

Everyone looked at her, more and more puzzled, and Mason couldn't help asking in a serious voice. "Pais, what level is your Plant Affinity, anyway?"

They were teammates who'd fought side by side and faced death together. They trusted each other with their lives, so there was no reason to keep hiding it.

Paisley answered calmly and openly. "Level Six, mid-stage."

Those few words left the whole place dead silent, and the air seemed to freeze.

Every single one of them felt crushed.

The rest of them were still stuck at Level Five or Level Four, and the gap was huge.

The higher the level, the bigger the difference just one level made.

Hardest of all to swallow, Paisley didn't just have a top-level Plant Affinity with a special poison that crushed everyone else. She also had an incredibly rare space power.

She had two rare powers, and her level was way beyond everyone else on the team.

They all looked at each other, their feelings complicated, their confidence shattered.

They were all Affinity users fighting to survive the apocalypse, so how could the gap between people be this huge?

Even Elijah, who was always calm, steady, and strong-minded, looked down, lost in thought.

She had a space power and a high-level Plant Affinity, two insanely powerful abilities, and even though she was so young, her strength was already impossible to measure.

How am I ever supposed to catch up?

A complicated mix of feelings he couldn't put into words churned inside him.

He'd always been proud of his talent and his steady mind. He'd reached a high position at a young age, always a step ahead of people his age, and everyone saw him as a genius.

But next to Paisley, every advantage he had seemed tiny and weak.

She was so dazzling, so powerful, and so impossible to read, so how was he supposed to make her fall for him?

For the first time, he felt small and powerless, and he couldn't help asking himself whether he was really good enough for her as he was now. Am I really just too weak?

Elijah, who'd always been so confident, started to feel insecure.

It wasn't just Elijah. Everyone there felt the same kind of shock and amazement, their heads spinning.

But Paisley, at the center of everyone's attention, had no idea what any of them were thinking.
