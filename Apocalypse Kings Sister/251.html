Chapter 251

Grown men bound in vines, sprawled across the ground, completely helpless. It was a lot to take in.

"It's... already over?"

"Would someone please tell me what just happened?"

"Gotta be Paisley's work. Ruthless as always. I love it."

Thomas's eyes went wide, unable to believe it.

They'd come out only seconds late, and the whole fight was already over.

Kelly came out with the rest and, seeing the scene, felt her heart clench hard.

She stared at the figure standing calm in the night wind, envy and helplessness clawing through her, nearly swallowing her whole.

Same apocalypse. She was a wreck, begging for scraps, while Paisley stayed unbeatable, like nothing could ever touch her.

The gap between people could really be this absurd.

And Elijah's eyes never left her.

It's not fair.

"Help... I don't want to die..."

"Please, let me down. I swear I'll never do wrong again."

...

Only now did they understand just what kind of monster they'd picked a fight with.

They hadn't even seen her move before they were strung up like this.

The leader had it worst, the thickest vine coiled around his throat, his face purple, eyes full of terror and regret.

He never imagined that cold, delicate-looking girl could hold this kind of crushing, overwhelming power.

"Help..."

Paisley walked forward at an easy pace, the night wind stirring her white hem, her eyes without a trace of warmth.

"So you're afraid to die too."

She looked down at the barely-conscious leader, her voice cold enough to cut.

"Behave, and I'll make it quick. Otherwise..."

"Who sent you? What's this village hiding?"

"Where's your boss? How many of you are there?"

Bound in the vines, numb head to toe, the leader still clung to what defiance he had left.

"Y-you outsiders think you're tough! Our boss is about to hit Level Five! None of you are leaving Wexford!"

"Be smart, surrender nice and quiet, and maybe you live! Otherwise you're all zombie food!"

The threat, loud and empty, only sounded pathetic to everyone listening.

Paisley's eyebrow lifted slightly, the cold in her eyes deepening, every trace of patience gone from her face.

She didn't waste any more words, just a flick of her fingers.

The vines binding all twenty men tightened at once.

The thorns that had barely pricked their skin now drove deep, a far harsher paralysis tearing through every nerve.

Searing pain, numbness, and pressure all at once, as if every bone in their bodies was being ground apart.

"AAAH!"

The pain shattered what little resolve they had left. Every one of them broke into agonized screaming.

Whatever defiance remained crumbled completely.

"I'll talk! I'll tell you everything! Please, stop!"

"We'll confess, all of it, just please, loosen the vines!"

The blinded leader, strung up by the thickest vine, his face a mask of blood and bruising, had nothing left in his eyes but terror and regret.

He finally understood just how badly he'd misjudged everything—

This girl who looked so delicate was the real monster here. They'd been blind, and they'd walked right into their own deaths.

Why did they have to run their mouths about her? None of this would've happened otherwise.

He never should have underestimated anyone.

Paisley stepped closer again, wind moving her white hem, her eyes cold as ever.

She looked down at him, her voice ice.

"Talk fast. I don't have much patience."

Terrified she'd choke the life out of him on the spot, he started talking fast, holding nothing back.

"It's, it's our boss, Fat Wagner! He lives in the mansion at the far end of the village! He's the one who sent us to grab you!"
