Chapter 471

Her Water Affinity came next.

Cool, flowing power rinsed her whole body, soft but strong, and broke through its limit with ease.

The water power grew denser and heavier. Her control range, her speed at forming water, and its range of attack and defense all rose. It also advanced to mid Level Seven.

Last was the deadliest of them all, the Oblivion Flame.

Her mutant flame had been sitting at early Level Seven. As a high-end killing power, it demanded the most resources to advance.

A flood of energy from the cores poured into her fire core. Violet Oblivion Flame burned in her lower abdomen, refined and purified. Its heat, corrosion, armor-piercing power, and bloodline suppression all surged.

The barrier shattered. The fire core transformed. The flame turned denser, fiercer, and utterly merciless.

Fire flared for a moment, and then it settled. The Oblivion Flame had reached mid Level Seven.

And there was her newly awakened Lightning Affinity, only Level One until now.

Under this endless flood of resources, it was pulled up all the way. Its foundation hardened, the climb went smoothly with no bottlenecks, and it reached mid Level Seven too.

She lost track of time. The mountain of cores in the dimension faded from brilliant to dull and gray, then crumbled to powder and drifted away, completely absorbed.

Every last core was gone.

And Paisley's whole presence felt brand new.

Every Affinity had advanced together, all her cores settled at high levels. Her aura was calm and vast and held in deep reserve. She looked gentle and soft, but within her lay a top-tier power that could shake the world.

She let out a long, slow breath and opened her eyes.

Light flashed through them for a moment. Green life, violet oblivion, blue clarity, silver cold. The four-colored glow vanished into her eyes and left them clear and calm.

She was transformed in every way.

She didn't leave the dimension. She walked to the farmland she had set up in her space.

Trovie had picked all the vegetables and grain and stacked them to one side.

Paisley used her Plant Affinity again.

Her mid Level Seven life energy flowed out, gentle and mighty as spring wind, and settled over the empty soil.

A new round of vegetables and grain sprouted, climbed, leafed, budded, and fruited before her eyes. In a few breaths a whole growth cycle was done.

Green covered the fields again, a cycle with no end.

When she finished, she went back to the room and lay down on the bed to rest.

******

The next morning, early.

Dawn broke through the thick mist. Pale light fell on the ruined city and drove off the night's chill.

The two squads ate a quick breakfast, packed their gear, and lined up ready.

After a night of fighting side by side and breaking through a trap together, Falcon and Lone Wolf had dropped their early stiffness and gotten comfortable with each other.

At the parting, Cole led his whole squad forward to say a formal goodbye, looking at Paisley with awe and gratitude.

"Thank you, Falcon Squad. Thank you, Ms. Lennox, for saving our lives and getting us through. We won't forget it. When we meet again at the base, we owe you big."

Elijah nodded, calm and courteous. "We're all in this together. Look out for each other. Stay safe."

After a few more words, the two squads parted ways.

The Falcon members climbed into the SUV. The doors shut, and the engine rumbled low. Wheels rolled through the cool morning air, and the team set off for Aetheris Base at full speed.

The abandoned streets were dead quiet. Broken walls everywhere, dry yellow weeds, no sign of people, only ruin.

Now and then a lone low-level zombie wandered out, sensed the engine, and lunged at them, snarling.

But this SUV had been heavily modified and armored at the base, with an alloy ram blade at the front. The team didn't need to lift a finger.

The SUV never slowed. It plowed through.

A few dull thuds, and the zombies were crushed under the alloy blade and dropped, never to move again.

The road ahead was clear all the way.

The mood in the car wasn't light.

Orion watched the broken streets slide past outside. He frowned and spoke quietly, worry in his voice.

"Today's the last day to turn in the mission, and the Great Freeze is less than twenty-four hours away. I hope this last stretch goes without incident."
