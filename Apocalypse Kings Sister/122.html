Chapter 122

Orion stood up and raised his glass. "To Paisley!"

Thomas was on his feet right after him. "To Paisley!"

Ashley jumped up with her glass. "To Paisley!"

Madison raised hers. "To Pais! Thanks for saving our lives!"

Kelly stood up too and lifted her glass, clearly not happy about it. "To everyone!"

Paisley stood and raised her glass. "To all of us!"

They clinked glasses, and everyone downed their cola in one go.

Spencer poured himself another glass. "I haven't had a Coke in forever. This tastes amazing!"

Thomas sighed. "Yeah, who knows when this apocalypse is going to end? I really miss the way things were before.

"I kind of wish I'd appreciated that life more while I had it."

Ashley's voice turned sad. "I miss Mom and Dad, and Grandma and Grandpa. Are they even okay out there?"

Paisley had already died once, betrayed by the people closest to her, so she didn't let herself get sentimental.

The apocalypse would last three long years, and today was only day nine. Plenty of unknown dangers still lay ahead.

Suddenly, a peeled shrimp landed on her plate. "Eat up. You need to keep your strength up," Elijah said gently.

Paisley glanced up at him. "Thanks!"

How could Orion let another guy make a move? Especially Elijah, who'd never shown the slightest interest in women but had them chasing him ever since they were kids.

No way. We just got Paisley back, and I'm not letting anybody sweep her off her feet that easily.

So Orion hurried to peel some shrimp and piled them on her plate. "Eat up, Paisley. From now on, I've got your shrimp covered."

Thomas and Spencer felt threatened right away and started peeling shrimp too.

Before long, the food on Paisley's plate was piled up like a mountain.

"Thanks, guys, but that's plenty. There's no room left on my plate."

It was a small thing, but ever since Rachel had come back to the Lennox family, nobody had treated Paisley like this.

All of them, even her childhood sweetheart and fiancé, Julian Ashford, had given every bit of their love and attention to Rachel.

Now, she didn't have to envy anyone ever again.

Everyone had a great time at dinner.

Meanwhile, in the villa next door, Jasper and the others were finally getting a hot meal too. They didn't have to chew on hardtack anymore.

Ten of them sat around the table together.

Jasper took a big swig from a can of beer and let out a satisfied burp. "Now this is living!

"From now on, every meal better be this good. If you can't make that happen, get the fuck out."

Rachel and the others didn't dare say a word.

Jasper was a mean drunk, and he couldn't hold his liquor.

He'd only had one beer, and he was already pretty wasted.

"Leave Paisley alone, all of you. She's Elijah's precious little princess now. Piss her off, and we won't even know what hit us.

"Got it? Stay away from her."

Nobody said a word, so Jasper raised his voice. "Did you all hear me?

"I'm warning you. Anyone who breaks the rules is out on their ass.

"And if anyone screws things up for me, every last one of you is gone.

"I'm done putting up with your shit."

With that, he shot Rachel one last warning look.

Rachel nodded like she agreed, but inside, she'd already made up her mind. The first chance she got, she'd kill Paisley.

Once Paisley was dead, everything would go back to normal. Rachel would be the adored one again, the only one everyone ever looked at.

Just you wait, Paisley. Sooner or later, there won't be enough left of you to bury.

That night, Paisley went back to her room, locked the door, and got to work on leveling up like she did every night.

She went into her Storage Dimension, sat cross-legged on the sofa, and dumped every crystal core she'd collected that day onto the table.

The pile glittered in every color, and plenty of the cores were Level Three and Level Four.

The best of all was the Level Four Psychic core from the Rat King. It was crystal clear and gave off a faint glow, packed with rich, steady energy. It was the biggest prize of the day.

Paisley closed her eyes and focused. She called on her Psychic Affinity and began absorbing the pure energy from all the cores.
