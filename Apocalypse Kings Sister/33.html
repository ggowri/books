Chapter 33

Kelly felt humiliated and bitter. She turned to Elijah right away, hoping he'd step in.

Their families went way back. They'd grown up together and gone to school together for years, and they were close.

He was the team captain, the strongest fighter, and the one who called the shots. If he'd just say one word in her defense, this whole embarrassing mess would go away, and Paisley would be the one everyone laughed at.

But Elijah just looked up coolly. He didn't even glance her way, and his tone didn't change one bit.

"We're one team right now. We've got enemies all around us, and I won't let anyone stir up drama in this group. If we start turning on each other, none of us are making it back to Aetheris alive.

"I don't want to hear that kind of selfish talk ever again.

"My decision stands. After lunch, Orion, Mason, Paisley, and I head out for supplies, and everyone else stays at the villa."

He said it flatly, and there was no room for argument.

Kelly's face went white, then red. She'd never been so embarrassed in her life.

Elijah hadn't stuck up for her at all. If anything, he'd embarrassed her in front of everyone.

She snuck a glance at him and caught something soft in his eyes when he looked at Paisley. He was cold and strict with everyone else, but not with her.

A crazy, jealous thought took hold of her and wouldn't let go.

Was Elijah only taking Paisley's side because she was pretty? Was he really that shallow?

Then what had all those years of following him around been for? Had she come all this way and gone through hell for nothing?

Jealousy and resentment drowned out every last bit of common sense she had.

This is all Paisley's fault! That fake, scheming little bitch!

Kelly gritted her teeth and swore to herself she'd find a way to prove herself. She'd make everyone see that she was the most useful person on this team, and the one who deserved Elijah!

And Paisley was nothing but a useless airhead with a pretty face!

Paisley caught the poison in Kelly's eyes right away and shot her a cold, flat look.

Petty jealousy. It's honestly a joke.

She couldn't be bothered to think about it. But if Kelly ever came after her, Paisley wouldn't go easy on her.

Lunch was ready, and everyone sat down.

The table was loaded with food again. There were platters of meat, sides of veggies, and a big pot of soup, and it all smelled amazing.

The apocalypse had just started, and things hadn't gone completely to hell yet, so they could still have a hot meal like this.

But they all knew it wouldn't last. As the city fell apart, supplies would get scarcer every day, and full stomachs like this would be harder and harder to come by.

Paisley sat between Ashley and Orion, same as before.

She reached over and scooped some buttery green beans onto Ashley's plate.

Ashley's face lit up. She shot a smug look across the table at Thomas and Spencer, looking pleased with herself.

The message was crystal clear. Paisley had served her first, so Paisley liked her best!

Thomas and Spencer exchanged a look and pouted. They gave Paisley puppy-dog eyes, hoping she'd serve them something too.

On her other side, Orion was quietly piling food onto her plate, doting on her the whole time.

A little farther down the table, Kelly watched all of this, and her chest felt tight.

What was so special about her?

Paisley didn't have an ability, and she was totally useless. So why did the whole Thompson family baby her like this, like the world revolved around her?

It wasn't fair!

She gripped her fork so hard her knuckles turned white, her eyes burning with hate.

She had to find a way to get rid of Paisley.

And if she couldn't drive her away, she'd just make her disappear for good.

In the apocalypse, a life wasn't worth much. Quietly getting rid of a useless nobody would be way too easy.

Lunch was over soon, and Elijah, Paisley, Orion, and Mason got their gear together, ready to go.

Just then, the doorbell rang.

Orion frowned and went to get the door.
