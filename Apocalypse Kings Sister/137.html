Chapter 137

She propped up her battered body and forced her head up. She glared at Paisley, who was standing over her, and screamed curses in a hoarse, broken voice.

"Paisley! You ungrateful wretch! You backstabbing piece of shit!

"The Lennox family raised you! Now you've got a little power, and you've got the nerve to hurt me like this? I hope you die a horrible death!"

Every word sent a stab of pain through her chest. She kept coughing up blood that stained the floor red, and she looked pitiful and half-crazed.

Bitterness ate her alive. She hated how cold Paisley was and how ridiculously lucky she was, and most of all, she hated how pathetic she looked now.

The shrieking and fighting echoed down the hallway. Nearby dorm doors swung open one after another, and Owen, Reid, and Ryan came rushing over.

When they saw Rachel sprawled on the floor, coughing up blood, covered in cuts, and twitching, all three of them froze in shock.

This was the little sister they'd spoiled and doted on ever since she was a kid.

Before, seeing Rachel beaten this badly would've made them see red. They would've rushed over to protect her and stood up for her no matter what, and they would've made whoever did it pay without mercy.

But now, the three of them stood rooted to the spot with complicated looks on their faces. Not one of them dared take a single step forward.

They couldn't stop picturing the four Thompsons closing ranks and crushing everyone with their high-level abilities.

And they knew Paisley wasn't who she used to be. She was strong now, with the Thompsons behind her, and she wasn't the lowly adopted daughter they could push around anymore.

Anger, heartache, and frustration twisted together inside them, but what ran deepest was a bone-deep fear.

They knew perfectly well that they couldn't afford to cross Paisley now.

Jumping in wouldn't get them justice. It would only humiliate them, and they might end up just as hurt.

All their old favoritism and recklessness shattered in the face of a gap in power this huge.

Owen clenched his fists so hard his knuckles turned white, and his face went ashen. But all he could do was swallow his rage. He didn't even have the guts to ask what had happened.

Reid and Ryan's hands shook at their sides as they watched Rachel scream and bleed. They felt guilty and helpless, and all they could do was stand there in humiliated silence.

Through her blurry vision, Rachel saw her three brothers coming. A spark of hope flared in her chest, and she raised her hand with all her strength, crying for help.

"Owen... Reid... Ryan... Help me... It was Paisley... Make her pay..."

But when she saw the three of them hanging back like cowards, the light in her eyes slowly died.

The brothers who always spoiled me are too chicken to even say a damn word.

In that moment, all her arrogance and all the backing she'd counted on came crashing down. All that was left was bone-deep bitterness and a crazy hatred.

Rachel lay on the cold floor with blood soaking through her clothes. Her whole body was in agony, but her heart hurt even more than her wounds.

Only now did she finally see the Lennox brothers for who they really were.

All their so-called love and their close sibling bond had been nothing but a fake show.

They were selfish cowards with no backbone. They always talked about spoiling her and protecting her, but the moment their own safety or interests were on the line, they looked out for themselves and left her hanging.

She used to do whatever she wanted and bully Paisley because she had their favor. Only now did she realize that what she'd been leaning on was never real support. It was just a joke she'd been telling herself.

The last bit of trust she had in her brothers shattered, and all that was left was a raging hatred for Paisley.

This is all Paisley's fault! Why won't she just die already?

She doesn't even deserve to live.

Paisley felt Rachel's hateful glare, but she'd watched the whole circus coldly from start to finish without a flicker of emotion.

She'd been given a second life, and she wasn't going to let any of them push her around again.

Before, they'd taken Rachel's side and schemed against her over and over, pushing their luck every time. She'd decided to call that payback for the Lennoxes raising her.

From now on, if any of them tried to scheme against her again, she wouldn't hesitate to fight back.

No, scratch that. Just looking at them pisses me off, so if they show up in front of me, that's on them!

Paisley didn't waste any time. Energy stirred at her fingertips, and sharp green willow branches burst out of the ground. They turned into long whips and wrapped tightly around Owen, Reid, and Ryan's arms, legs, and waists.
