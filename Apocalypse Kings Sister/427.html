Chapter 427

Everyone came forward and picked the new guns that fit their hands best. Each took five hundred rounds of spare ammo, and their bags were stuffed full.

With a thought, Paisley summoned clear glass bottles that appeared in a stack in the middle of the vehicle.

"Two bottles of spirit water each. In a pinch, it'll restore your Affinity."

"Spirit water?!"

Ashley's eyes lit up and she leaned in, excited.

She knew this stuff well. She had awakened her Affinity thanks to Paisley's spirit water.

It could wipe out exhaustion in seconds and restore overdrawn Affinity in a short time. In the apocalypse it was priceless, impossible to buy.

She carefully took two bottles, looked up, and said happily, "Thank you, Paisley!"

The others went still when they saw the bottles.

They had all tried spirit water, and they knew how rare it was.

Nowhere else had it. Only Paisley.

Elijah took his bottles, feeling the cool glass, his eyes soft. "Thank you.

With this, if our Affinity gets drained in a fight, we have another way out."

Orion put his two bottles away carefully, moved. "This is so precious. Pais, you always give us the best of everything."

Thomas tucked his into an inner pocket of his bag. "Thank you, Paisley! With spirit water, we won't worry about running dry in a long fight."

Spencer grinned, barely able to hold back his excitement. "Wow. Top-tier weapons plus spirit water. You won't find a team like this anywhere on the base!"

Mason held the wheel with one hand, looked back at the bottles, and shook his head. "Today I learned something. Thanks, Paisley."

Madison tucked hers away, smiling. "This is such a precious gift. Whatever reward we get from this mission, I'll give half to Paisley."

Everyone else offered to give half of their reward too.

Paisley refused flatly. "We're comrades who've been through life and death. Let's not count it. I just want us all to make it back alive."

They knew it was her kindness, so they didn't argue. But each of them understood clearly that they would remember her goodness and find a way to repay her.

By unspoken agreement, no one mentioned where the spirit water came from. They all guessed it came from Paisley's mysterious, powerful Storage Dimension.

No one asked or pried. It was the squad's biggest secret.

******

The black armored SUV tore down the cracked, barren dirt road and raced across the empty wilderness.

Weeds overgrew both sides of the road, and broken walls stretched along it. The towns and villages of the past had been swallowed by sandstorms and decay, a bleak, desolate view.

Scattered low-level zombies along the road were startled by the engine's roar. Their stiff bodies jerked around, their rotting, hideous faces turned toward the vehicle, and raspy howls came from their throats as they lurched forward, stiff but fast.

The Falcon Squad members inside stayed calm.

After all these days in the apocalypse, everyone's Affinity had risen to mid Level Six. Their power was dense and refined, and they could use it freely. Their strength had been transformed.

Sitting in the vehicle, they could kill the charging zombies without effort.

In one corner, Paisley leaned against the seat, eyes closed, resting peacefully.

The others silently understood and lowered all their movements, speaking as quietly as they could.

The armored vehicle rolled smoothly on through the wilderness.

They ate a quick lunch in the vehicle.

After hours of driving, they finally reached the abandoned area of Ridgemont at four in the afternoon, right on time.

The sight was bleak and ruined. The huge city was a pile of rubble, with broken walls and overgrown weeds everywhere.

In the middle of the view stood a giant abandoned warehouse. Its walls were peeling and its iron doors were thick with rust, the only intact building in the area.

Beside the warehouse stood an abandoned steel mill, its steel frames tilted and collapsed, covered in rust. The crisscrossing steel structure blocked their view like a dense forest, perfect for an ambush and radiating a deadly silence.

The armored vehicle slowed and finally stopped in the open lot in front of the warehouse.

******

The moment the vehicle stopped, Paisley's eyes, closed in rest, snapped open.
