Chapter 17

The glass doors were locked, but Paisley could see that everything inside was untouched. Someone must have locked up right when the apocalypse hit, and the place had gotten lucky.

Orion followed her in without thinking twice. There wasn't a soul inside, just gold gleaming everywhere.

Paisley didn't hold back. With a thought, she opened her dimension and started cleaning the place out.

Row after row of gold necklaces, bracelets, rings, bars, collector's coins, and luxury watches vanished into her dimension and lined up neatly inside.

She took everything, from the pieces on display to the sealed new stock and spare gold bars in the back drawers.

In just over ten seconds, the whole store was empty, and its packed display cases were picked clean.

Trovie was losing its mind with joy, cheering nonstop in her head.

[We're rich, Master! Filthy rich! List all this gold in the Trading Plaza, and your points will go through the roof!

[Level Two trader, here we come!]

Paisley smiled to herself.

Free loot is the best feeling ever.

She had food, medicine, weapons, and gold now, everything she could need.

Orion stood off to the side, looking at the store she'd cleared out in the blink of an eye. He was getting used to how overpowered Paisley's Storage Dimension was, and he couldn't help smiling at her fondly.

Then, Trovie piped up in Paisley's head again, all excited.

[Master, there's a storeroom in the back! I bet there's tons of good stuff stashed in there! Don't miss it!]

Paisley couldn't help laughing a little to herself. Who knew the little thing came with its own treasure radar?

She looked up at Orion. "Orion, I want to go farther in," she said softly. "There's probably a lot more stuff back there."

Orion followed her gaze. Deeper inside the mall, the power was out and everything was pitch-black. It was eerily quiet back there, and it felt dangerous.

But one look at Paisley's bright, hopeful eyes, and he couldn't bring himself to say no.

He'd worked so hard to find her, so of course he was going to spoil her. Whatever she wanted to do, he'd be right there with her.

Orion nodded right away. "Sure, whatever you want," he said warmly.

But a question nagged at him.

Her space had only been about 100 square feet, and she'd already crammed a whole supermarket into it. Now, she'd cleared out a jewelry store too. Did she even have room left?

Paisley seemed to read his mind. She cleared her throat and explained, "When I killed that Level Two mutant and absorbed its core's energy, my space got a little bigger.

"It's a good 500 square feet now, so there's way more room. You don't have to worry about running out of space."

Orion's jaw dropped. "It just leveled up, like that?"

He stared at her for a second, then broke into a grin. "Five hundred square feet? That'll hold a ton!

"Let's not waste any time, then. When it comes to supplies, the more, the better."

Side by side, they hurried through the front of the store and reached the storeroom door in the back.

The heavy metal door was locked and sealed tight. There was no way to open it the normal way.

Paisley turned to Orion and suggested, "Orion, try using your Metal Affinity on the lock."

Orion blinked, caught off guard. Ever since he'd awakened his Metal Affinity, he'd only used it to defend himself up close and to reinforce weapons. It had never occurred to him to use it like this.

But he didn't hesitate. If Paisley said it would work, he believed her.

He called up his ability right away, forming fine threads of metal at his fingertips and slipping them into the lock. With a soft click, the lock popped open.

Paisley stepped up and kicked the heavy door open.

Orion followed her in and locked the door behind them. He stayed on high alert, scanning the pitch-black room for any sign of danger.

Paisley pulled out two flashlights, handed him one, and got to work.

The storeroom was a good 500 square feet and stocked with all kinds of supplies, each type neatly sorted.
