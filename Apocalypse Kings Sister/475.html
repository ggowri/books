Chapter 475

White frost raced over the whole river at terrifying speed, freezing miles of water and sealing the current.

The four falling bodies stopped in the air just above a flat, solid, glittering sheet of thick ice instead of deadly water.

The vicious mutant shark, its jaws still half open and its body in the air, froze solid in the ice, trapped, bloodlust sealed.

The wild wind died. The roaring waves went silent.

The dangerous, noisy river fell dead still. A vast, flat, crystal sheet of ice stretched across it.

The reversal stunned everyone into stillness.

Orion's eyes went wide and his spine locked.

He knew how dangerous a high-tier water beast was. Beasts like that were born for the water, crushing in strength, nearly unstoppable in a rushing river. And Paisley had frozen it in an instant and killed it!

He had always thought her best card was her Plant Affinity and its terrifying parasite kill. He had never imagined she'd hide a power like this.

Ice-Water Affinity?

A power this wide, instant, and absolute couldn't compare to normal Water Affinity. Even the base's best Water users couldn't flash-freeze a whole river and kill a high-tier shark.

She's famous across the base for her Plant Affinity. When did she quietly awaken a power this strong?

Thomas was completely stunned, disbelief in his eyes.

He was a Plant user himself, and he knew mastering one Affinity was hard enough. Paisley had pushed her Plant Affinity to the limit, had her mutant fire, and now her water was this powerful.

Is this something a human can do?

Talent like this is beyond anything the base has seen. It's a miracle of the apocalypse.

Spencer's jaw dropped. His fighting spirit stalled, and only awe was left.

He had always chased her pace with everything he had and thought he was working hard enough. Now he saw how wide the gap really was.

Freezing a river and crushing a high-tier beast. It's brutal. It's beautiful. It's terrifying.

Ashley, fresh from the fall, was frozen in shock.

Her Earth Affinity was good for filling ground and holding lines, and she'd thought it was useful. Next to Paisley's miracle, it felt small.

So many cards. She hid so much. She's so strong.

She's my pride.

Mason had barely found his footing and still couldn't come back to himself. His mind was in an uproar.

The four of them had been dead. Falling into the river, facing the shark, with no way out. Paisley had frozen the whole river and sealed the beast with a thought, and pulled them back from the edge of death.

Her reaction and the power were beyond belief.

Madison's eyes trembled with amazement and awe.

She was a Water user too, and the gap was staggering.

Nobody had ever pushed Water Affinity this far. Freezing everything, locking life and death in place. That wasn't water. That was a top-tier art of controlling the cold itself.

On the far bank, Chloe's smile was dead on her face. The blood drained from her cheeks, and she stood frozen, gone white.

A second ago she'd been mocking, sure Paisley was trapped with no way out, waiting to watch her die. Then Paisley had flipped it in an instant, saving them all.

The icy cold had frozen the river and also her arrogance.

She had always believed Paisley had only one card, her Plant Affinity. She'd lived in the fantasy that she could crush her given the chance. Only now did she see that she'd never even bothered to learn her enemy's real strength.

I underestimated her.

Still, no matter what, she has to die today.

Grant's face went black. The confidence in his eyes cracked, and wary dread took its place.

He'd been so sure the ambush was airtight, that his new strength would crush Paisley. But this Ice-Water Affinity wrecked every plan he had.
