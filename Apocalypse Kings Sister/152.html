Chapter 152

Kelly kept her face perfectly blank.

Just wait. Today, Paisley dies, and nobody will ever find the body.

Paisley sensed two hostile stares from the shadows and turned to give them a cool, brief look.

The second their eyes met, Rachel wiped the dark look off her face and glanced away as if nothing had happened.

Kelly dropped her eyes too, burying every trace of malice, suddenly very absorbed in the mission board.

Paisley gave a faint, mocking smile.

Interesting.

She scoffed to herself and looked away, her face settling back into easy calm. Then, she turned to the three beside her. "You three pick the missions."

"Okay!" Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer answered at once and hurried up to the crowded board to look for missions that suited them.

Not far off, Rachel was picking out a mission too. She'd seen the whole thing out of the corner of her eye, and she gave Kelly a subtle look.

Here's our chance.

As soon as Paisley locked in a field mission, they could find a way onto her team and spring the trap from the inside.

Just then, Elijah, who'd been about to leave, turned and pointed at a highlighted listing at the top of the board. "This one's good training for you guys. It'll sharpen your skills and level you up fast."

It was a Level Five hunt paying 2,000 contribution points and 500 Level Two crystal cores. The target was a Level Five mutant wolf holed up in an abandoned mall on the eastern outskirts.

Paisley skimmed the details. It looked like solid practice for the three of them, so she nodded.

Ashley spoke up right away. "Great, we'll take this one."

The staffer logging missions looked up, surprised, and couldn't help warning them.

"This one's been sitting on the board for days. It's tough and risky, so nobody's dared to take it. You sure you want a Level Five this hard?"

Before he'd even finished, Trovie's voice rang in Paisley's head, urgent and thrilled.

[Master, an emergency mission has been triggered! Take the Level Six mission! Eliminate the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower and rescue the hostages!

[Complete this Level Six high-risk mission for 10,000 points and a bonus lucky draw!]

Paisley's interest stirred. She scanned the rest of the high-risk board and zeroed in on a gray, high-difficulty listing pinned at the top.

The mission site was an abandoned amusement park near that same mall on the eastern outskirts.

The job was to rescue the trapped hostages and kill the guardian beast, a Level Six mutant carnivorous flower.

The two sites were practically next door, and the routes overlapped, so she could easily handle both.

Talk about a lucky break.

Her eyes lit up, and she answered firmly. [Deal!]

She pointed at the gray listing nobody wanted and added, "I'll take this Level Six mission too."

The whole mission hall went dead silent. Every head turned toward her, and everyone stared in stunned disbelief.

The strongest Affinity users in all of Rivergate Base had only reached peak Level Four, so a Level Six high-risk mission was out of the question for them.

This Level Six listing had been up for days, and nobody had dared to take it. The base had already sent several elite teams after it, and not one person came back alive, so everyone saw it as a suicide mission.

The staffer's face changed. He hurried to talk her out of it, clearly meaning well.

"Kid, this is the hardest mission on the board. Sure, it pays 5,000 contribution points and 2,000 Level Two cores, but it's way too risky. Betting your life for points just isn't worth it.

"You're young, and you've got real talent. Don't throw your life away trying to prove something."

The Affinity users crowding around chimed in too, each with a different look on their face.

Some felt genuinely sorry for her. Someone so young and talented was being way too reckless, and it would be a shame to see her throw her life away.

Others were just there for the show, waiting to watch her crash and burn.

And some were jealous enough that they couldn't help sneering at her.

"So these are the out-of-town Affinity users? Typical young hotshots. They get a little power and suddenly think they're invincible?"

"They'd actually take a Level Six mission? That place wiped out a whole team of peak Level Fours. They have no clue what they're getting into, and they won't even know what killed them."

Jeers, warnings, and doubts came at them from every side.
