Chapter 152

Just wait.

Today, Paisley dies, and there's nothing left to bury.

Paisley felt the two pairs of eyes watching from the shadows and turned, gaze cool, flicking over them once.

The instant their eyes met, Rachel smoothed the darkness out of her own, looking away fast, pretending nothing had happened.

Kelly dropped her eyes too, burying every trace of malice, suddenly very absorbed in the mission board.

A thin thread of mockery passed through Paisley's gaze.

Interesting.

She let it go without a word, her expression settling back into easy calm. She turned to the three beside her.

"Pick your missions."

"Okay."

Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer answered at once, stepping up to scan the crowded board for something that fit them.

Not far off, Rachel was pretending to browse missions too, watching the whole scene out of the corner of her eye. She gave Kelly a subtle look.

There's the opening.

Once Paisley locked in a field mission, they could angle their way into the same team — and the setup would fall into place.

Just then, Elijah, already set to leave, turned and pointed to a highlighted listing at the top of the board.

"This one's good for training. It'll sharpen your skills fast."

A Level Five hunt. Two thousand contribution points, five hundred Level Two crystal cores. Target: a Level Five mutant wolf holed up in an abandoned mall in the eastern district.

Paisley skimmed the details — solid training material — and nodded.

Ashley agreed at once.

"Good, we'll take it."

The staffer logging the mission looked up, surprised, and couldn't help a warning.

"This one's been up for days. High difficulty, high risk — no one's touched it. You sure about a Level Five mission?"

Before he'd even finished, Trovie's voice rang in Paisley's head, urgent and thrilled.

[Host! Emergency mission triggered! Accept the Level Six mission! Eliminate the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower, rescue the hostages!]

[Complete this S-tier mission for ten thousand points, plus one bonus lucky draw!]

Something sparked in Paisley's eyes. Her gaze cut across the rest of the high-risk board and landed on a gray listing, pinned at the top, no one's claimed it.

The mission site: an abandoned amusement park, just past the mall.

The objective: rescue the hostages, kill the guardian — a Level Six mutant carnivorous flower.

The two locations were practically next door. The routes overlapped. Easy enough to do both.

Didn't expect this kind of luck.

A flicker of brightness crossed her eyes, and her voice came out certain.

[Deal!]

She pointed to the gray listing no one wanted.

"We'll take this one too. The Level Six."

The entire hall went quiet.

Every head turned. Every face went slack with shock.

No one in Rivergate base had broken past Level Four peak. No one touched a Level Six mission — not willingly.

That listing had been up for days. Untouched. Rumor had it the base had already sent elite teams after it. None came back.

The mission officer's face went white. He hurried to talk her down, his tone turning gentle.

"Kid, this is the hardest thing on the board. Sure, the payout's five thousand contribution points and two thousand Level Two cores, but the risk isn't worth your life."

"You've got talent — don't waste it being reckless."

The crowd around them chimed in too, each with their own take.

Some genuinely pitied her — talented, and about to get herself killed.

Some just wanted a show, waiting to watch her fail.

And some let the envy slip through as mockery.

"These must be the out-of-towners. Barely any real skill and already acting like they own the place."

"A Level Six mission? That's the one that wiped out a whole squad of Level Four peak fighters. They won't even know how they died."

The jeers, the warnings, the doubt — it all piled up, thick and loud.
