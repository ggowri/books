Chapter 453

Stairwells, gaps between shelves, under counters, spaces in the ceiling. Blind spots hid everywhere.

Stranger still, all the surveillance cameras along their path were intact. Their black lenses stared down every aisle, silently watching the intruders.

Mason's neck hair stood up. He muttered, "This place gives me the creeps. It feels like someone set it up as a trap, just waiting for us to walk in."

Elijah signaled the team to crouch low and advance slowly, his eyes sharp as a hawk's, fully on guard.

"Pairs, nobody alone. Watch your spacing."

The squad quickly formed up and steadily swept the first-floor supermarket.

The vacuum-sealed food, canned goods, instant meals, bottled water, and daily goods were perfectly intact, piled high.

Paisley had no qualms. She stored it all.

They headed up toward the second floor, Paisley's senses fully open.

The little white cat's back tensed. It stepped lightly on the ground without a sound, its amber eyes fixed on the pitch-black top of the escalator.

The golden fox's tails trembled, its wind sense spreading to catch every tiny stir in the air.

"Scritch, scritch..."

A very faint, delicate scraping sound came from deep in the dark second-floor corridor.

It was short and hidden, gone in an instant. If the whole mall hadn't been so quiet, they'd never have noticed.

The squad stopped dead, breath held.

"A person? A beast? Or a zombie?"

Ashley's palms tightened, Earth Affinity quietly gathering.

"Not a person," Paisley said calmly. "It's a crawling beast. Small, but very fast. Be careful, everyone."

Before she finished speaking!

Whoosh!

A black shadow burst out of the dark at the top of the escalator.

It was a mutant black-scale lizard, jet black with a slick, oily hide, nearly seven feet long, with razor claws on all four legs dripping corrosive venom. Its eyes

It was a high-level mutant beast that lived in packs!

The clean floor, the dead quiet, the hidden metallic smell, all of it had been a hunting trap.

Their intelligence had clearly evolved. They were getting closer and closer to a human way of living.

They'd waited patiently, letting intruders relax and start looting. Once the squad spread out and lowered its guard, they'd strike from every side.

"Everyone on guard!"

Elijah's roar shook the empty mall. The black muzzle of his rifle swung up and locked on the diving beast, his finger pressed hard on the trigger, every muscle tense.

But these lizards were much faster than anyone had guessed!

The first one slammed to the ground. The heavy scales blasted dust into the air as the seven-foot black body slid across the floor, claws pale and cold, trailing a stinking, corrosive wind, and went straight for Ashley's face at a tricky angle.

It gave no time to react. It opened its jaws and spat a mouthful of dark green venom. Where it hit the floor, smoke hissed up, terrifyingly corrosive.

And it wasn't alone!

The ceiling panels above burst apart, scattering splinters and dust. Three black-scale lizards dropped through and landed in a triangle, sealing off the way forward.

On the right, two more sprang from the shelf tops, aiming for Spencer and Mason's flank. On the left, from the shadows beside the escalator, the last two slid low along the floor and circled behind, going for Madison and Thomas in the back row.

In an instant, eight high-level mutant black-scale lizards had completed an all-around kill zone.

Blocked front and back, striking from high and low, near and far, working together with no gaps. It was the tactics of a pack hunter, aimed precisely at the holes in the squad's formation, and the squad was caught flat-footed!

"They're too fast! They're pack-hunting Level Six beasts!"

Orion's gut dropped as he roared the warning.

Pack beasts were the most dangerous. Each one was strong on its own, and when they coordinated, their killing power doubled, far worse than a lone beast.

The mall was cramped and full of obstacles, which limited human movement, and made it the perfect hunting ground for the beasts.

Facing the venom and claws head-on, Ashley paled and forced out her Earth Affinity at the last second!

A thick earthen wall rose from the floor and slammed up in front of her.

Sizzle!

The caustic venom splashed across the wall. The hard earth bubbled and pitted at once, and the wall melted away, thinner by the second.
