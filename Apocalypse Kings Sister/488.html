Chapter 488

A mass of eyes turned toward the middle-aged man in the head seat, doubtful, mocking, gloating, and pushing. Every kind of look piled on him.

Under the pressure and the barrage, General Blackwood sat steady in the top seat, his posture straight and calm. He showed no panic and no anxiety.

His fingertips tapped the table. His face was still, and deep in his eyes lay absolute certainty. He let them question and blame him, and stayed silent, steady as a mountain.

To everyone else, it looked like a man with no defense, accepting all the blame.

The Quinn faction's smiles deepened. They were already calculating how to use this failure to crush the Blackwood family's power, strip the General of authority, and seize the base.

The accusations, mockery, and pressure in the room grew louder, almost raising the roof.

Right then, with the whole room pressing down and the balance tipping, the conference room door flew open from outside!

A bright, excited voice cut through the noise and rang clear across the room, silencing every other voice.

"Report!

Falcon Squad has returned safely!

No casualties!

They're back at the base!"

Boom!

One sentence hit like thunder.

The room fell dead silent. All the questions, mockery, and pressure stopped at once.

The Quinn faction people, who had been so smug and pushy a moment ago, froze with their smiles stuck on their faces. Their pupils shrank, and they stared in disbelief.

No casualties?

The whole team back safe?

Impossible!

After a deadly trap like that, plus the Great Freeze, they'd come back alive, every one of them, safe?!

Before anyone could recover, more excited reports came from outside, one after another, slapping every face in the room.

"Falcon Squad completed the grain mission!!"

"They brought back a huge amount of grain, enough to get us through winter. It's a godsend!"

"They also captured several Affinity users who ambushed them and sabotaged the mission, and they're people the base knows."

Each report rang out, every word hitting like a slap!

The senior officials were stunned. Everyone was dumbstruck. The bullying force from before was gone, their faces flushing blue and white, humiliated.

Only then did General Blackwood lift his eyes slowly. A faint, calm smile touched his lips, and he swept his gaze across the Quinn faction who had just been attacking him. His voice was steady and strong, with the weight of absolute authority.

"My people have never let this base down."

"The talk of failure and annihilation was only your guesswork, baseless."

"Anything more to say now?"

"Shouldn't you be the ones explaining yourselves to me?"

As his words ended, the wind and snow outside slowly eased, and the biting cold was shut out by the heavy base doors.

A tall figure came into view first.

Elijah led the way in a clean blue down jacket that made him look even taller and straighter. The battlefield chill was gone, replaced by a calm warmth, but the command in him was as strong as ever. His face was cool and composed, with no trace of the road's fatigue.

Orion followed close by, solid and steady, sharp and clean, with none of the weariness of a man who'd just been through danger.

Paisley stood quietly beside Elijah, cool and poised, her back straight.

Her eyes were clear and clean, but they held a sharpness tempered by fire. She swept them calmly over the senior officials with their mixed expressions, composed, neither humble nor proud.

Behind her, Ashley, Thomas, Spencer, Mason, and Madison stood in line, and the Falcon members stood tall and sharp, steady and full of energy, their clothes clean, with no wounds on any of them.

After the broken bridge, the ambush, the high-tier zombie trap, and the trek through the snow, the whole team was perfectly fine, with no injuries at all.

Silence spread through the conference room. A pin drop would have been loud.

The Quinn faction people, who had been pushing so hard a moment ago, had their smirks and certainty frozen and gone, their faces pale.

They gripped their palms, knuckles white, an enormous resentment and disbelief rolling through them. Their faces burned, as if they'd been slapped hard.

They had been so sure Falcon Squad was wiped out and the mission was a collapse. Now the whole team stood before them, victorious, in great shape. Every plan, every guess, every hope of forcing the General out was shattered.
