Chapter 159

[Master, this is it! I smell tons of precious metal, so much gold!]

Trovie sounded thrilled as it spun around and around in the air.

Paisley crossed to the safe and ran her fingers over the cold metal.

The safe was way tougher than it looked, with thick alloy steel plating and a digital lock set in the center. The screen was dark, and the keypad didn't respond at all. Its owner had locked it down for good, so there was no cracking or prying it open.

She couldn't be bothered to waste time on the code. Her eyes glinted, and she gripped the Frostbloom Sword, pouring energy into the blade. Cold light flared along its edge, piercing and razor-sharp.

Her blade whistled through the air in one sweeping slash. The thick alloy wall of the safe split apart, dead lock and all, in one smooth, clean cut, and the heavy door burst open.

It was brutal, but it worked like a charm.

Paisley couldn't help thinking the Frostbloom Sword had been worth every single point.

Alloy steel plates, locked precision hardware, and solid stone that ordinary blades couldn't touch all fell apart in front of it. Just like Trovie had said, this sword could cut through almost anything.

As the safe split open, dazzling golden light spilled out and lit up the whole dim office.

The safe was packed with neat stacks of gleaming gold bars. Each bar was solid, heavy, and pure, and they filled every inch of the inside.

Besides the bars, there were sealed rolls of gold coins, gold jewelry, platinum ornaments, and even a few polished nuggets of natural gold, some of them huge. It was worth a fortune.

In the dim, ruined world of the apocalypse, a sudden pile of golden treasure like this was a stunning sight.

Ever since the apocalypse hit, the money system had collapsed, and gold and jewels had become pretty much useless. She was probably the only person left who'd get this excited over a pile of gold.

Trovie chimed in. [Master, we struck it rich! There's 550 pounds of gold!]

Without a moment's hesitation, Paisley opened her Storage Dimension with a single thought.

First, she took every last gold bar. Then, she swept in the loose coins, the gold jewelry, and the raw gold nuggets without missing a single piece.

In just a few seconds, the huge safe was picked clean.

Trovie's little wings flapped like crazy, and it cheered in its sweet, excited voice.

[Congratulations, Master! We're rich!]

Paisley had figured out by now that collecting things always got Trovie extra excited.

She smiled faintly, as calm as ever. [It's still not much. Only 550 pounds.]

Then, she gave a quiet order. [Trovie, scan the mall. Where else is there gold?]

Trovie immediately scanned the entire mall at full power. Blue light swept across every floor, and it quickly came back with an answer.

[Master, the fourth floor! I'm picking up tons of precious metal and jewelry up there!]

Paisley pictured the mall directory she'd spotted on the way in.

The first floor was the supermarket, stocked with all kinds of food and household supplies. The second floor mostly sold clothing, bedding, and home goods. The third floor carried electronics and digital devices.

The entire fourth floor was a luxury section full of gold, jewelry, and accessories, the most valuable floor in the whole mall. The fifth floor had a movie theater and a bunch of specialty food stands.

She'd already cleaned out the basement's supplies, the crystal cores, and the gold in the safe.

There was no time to waste. Paisley turned and left the office, calmly heading up to the second floor to keep harvesting, and the little white cat hurried after her.

The whole second floor was dead silent. Unlike the first floor, which had been crawling with snarling zombies, only a few Level Three rotting zombies wandered around up here, probably too scared of the fighting downstairs to come near.

The handful that sensed her turned stiffly and lunged with a snarl. Before they could get close, Paisley cut them down with a few casual slashes, and they collapsed into heaps of rotten flesh. It wasn't even a warm-up.

With the floor cleared, she could finally take in the whole second floor.
