Chapter 61

The second Rachel turned away, the mocking smile vanished from Paisley's face. All that was left was cold, distant indifference.

She got back in the car. "Drive," she said coldly.

Mason didn't hesitate for a second. He started the engine and threw the car into gear in one smooth motion.

The next second, the engine roared, and the tires tore across the ground in a cloud of dust. The black SUV shot off like a bullet and sped away from the ruins.

The back window was halfway down. Paisley rested her elbow on the edge and glanced at the side mirror.

In the mirror, Rachel was kneeling in the mud. She got smaller and blurrier until she disappeared completely into the dust and the darkening sky.

The whole time, Paisley's face didn't change at all. Her eyes stayed ice-cold.

This isn't nearly enough, Rachel.

Rachel was humiliated and hopeless now, but that wasn't even a tenth of the agony Paisley had suffered before she died in her past life.

She wasn't sparing Rachel out of mercy, and she wasn't saving her either. She just wanted Rachel to have a chance to stay alive.

Let her suffer through this hell on earth and get a taste of everything Paisley had been through.

I want you alive, Rachel. That way, I can keep making you pay.

Next to her, Orion picked up on Paisley's sudden chill right away.

A minute ago, when she'd been teasing Rachel, she'd seemed playful and full of life. Now, she was giving off a cold, stay-away vibe, and the air around her felt heavy. It was nothing like her usual easygoing self.

He turned toward her, clearly worried.

"Paisley, what's wrong? Is something bothering you?

"If you really can't stand Rachel, I'll turn around right now and take care of her. I hate seeing her get to you like this."

Paisley forced a small smile. "No need. She's not worth getting your hands dirty.

"Don't worry about me, Orion. I'm fine."

I just can't let it go!

Meanwhile, Rachel was dragging herself toward the river, sure they'd take her once she cleaned up. Then, she heard an engine roar behind her and whipped around in terror.

All she saw was a trail of dust and a car disappearing into the distance. She was all alone at the empty reserve, abandoned in a wasteland crawling with danger.

The howling evening wind flung dust into her filthy face. A bone-deep chill crept through her torn clothes and froze every last shred of hope she had left.

Rachel froze. Then, her legs gave out, and she dropped hard to her knees on the muddy gravel.

The cold, jagged gravel scraped her knees raw, and blood mixed with the mud. But that pain was nothing compared to the agony ripping through her chest.

She'd swallowed her pride, groveled, and thrown away every last bit of dignity. Now, all of it cut into her like a knife, crushing what little hope and self-respect she had left.

Like an idiot, she'd done what she was told. She'd put up with the filth and the exhaustion, believing that if she just washed off the mud, she might get a chance to go with them.

But Paisley had just been toying with her the whole time, mocking her and stomping all over her dignity!

All that crap about washing up was bullshit. She never planned to take me at all!

She broke down completely. Despair clamped around her throat so tight that she couldn't even scream. All she could do was let out broken, choking sobs, like she was about to lose her mind.

Every close call she'd survived and every insult she'd swallowed had turned into one huge, cruel joke.

She'd only just escaped the mutant's torture. When she'd run into them, she'd thought there was finally hope, but all she got was more humiliation and a heartless brush-off.

God, why are you doing this to me?

The sky had gone completely dark. Thick storm clouds hung low over the horizon, and a wild wind howled, warning that the downpour was about to hit.

Across the empty, silent reserve, zombie snarls drifted in every now and then from the ruins all around.

She had nowhere to go and no way out.

Rachel lay sprawled in the mud, shaking violently. Her pale face twisted with a vicious, almost insane hatred.

She glared in the direction the car had gone and dug her fingers deep into the mud. Her nails cracked and her fingertips bled, but she was too far gone to feel any pain.

She hated them with everything she had! The hatred burned through her soul and seared every inch of her body!
