Chapter 400

After a long moment, he finally, lazily lifted his eyelids, his narrow eyes carrying thirty percent mockery and seventy percent indifference, raising an eyebrow idly, his tone light and cutting.

"Oh? If it isn't the flagship squad's healer. What brings you all the way to my humble little corner of the world?"

Every word dripped with backhanded flattery, every syllable soaked in mockery.

Chloe's face burned hot, countless eyes fixed on her, the pressure making her want nothing more than to turn and run.

She bit down on her lower lip, forcing down every trace of pride and humiliation, lowering herself in a way she never had before, and spoke quietly.

"Mr. Quinn, I need a favor."

"My mother's situation, please, show her mercy, just this once."

The moment she said it, scattered laughter rippled through the crowd.

Before she could say another word in her defense, Jasper casually held out a glass of extremely strong liquor, the glass catching the light with a cold shine.

"You want to beg? Sure, that can be arranged."

He arched an eyebrow, his tone thick with mockery.

"Drink this whole thing, down to the last drop, and I'll consider it."

The crowd around them erupted into excited jeers, every mocking gaze fixed on her.

Chloe stared at the liquor burning in the glass, a sharp ache rising in her nose.

She'd never been able to hold her liquor, had never once in her life lowered herself this far.

But thinking of her mother, sobbing and breaking down at the Security Office, as reckless as Jasper was, this might be her only shot. She had no other choice.

Hands trembling, she took the glass, tilted her head back, closed her eyes, and forced the entire thing down in one go, gritting her teeth through it.

The fiery liquor burned down her throat and into her stomach, a sharp, searing sting spreading through her whole body. Her eyes went red instantly, tears welling up involuntarily.

But no one felt sorry for her. No one sympathized with her desperation.

Jasper watched her force it down, coldly, not a trace of sympathy in his eyes, only growing more disdainful and mocking.

"This is what happens when you don't know better than to cross Pais."

"Making yourself this cheap for a selfish, ungrateful mother who doesn't know any better, was it worth it?"

"Your mother rode on your status to throw her weight around and bully people, and when she got caught, she sent you to come grovel for her. Brenda's useless, but you're certainly obedient, humble enough for both of you."

Every word landed like a blow, striking straight at Chloe's heart.

With the liquor hitting her system fast, dizziness swept over her, the figures around her beginning to blur and double.

She regretted this already, though it was too late now.

Whatever else was true, that woman was still her mother.

Today's humiliation, she'd pay it back someday.

Forcing herself to hold onto the last of her clarity, Chloe slumped forward against the table, her voice hoarse as she pressed.

"Mr. Quinn... I drank it. Will you... will you let my mother go?"

Jasper waved a lazy hand, his tone flat and final, without a shred of sympathy.

"No."

"Once you've crossed Paisley, it's not about one glass of liquor. You could drink every bottle I own tonight and it wouldn't change a thing."

"Giving you the chance to drink was just me doing you a favor, a little game, nothing more."

Hearing this, Chloe's whole body jolted, the last flicker of hope inside her extinguished completely.

The alcohol, mixed with overwhelming humiliation, resentment, exhaustion, and despair, shattered what little composure she had left.

She couldn't hold herself together any longer, swaying unsteadily, on the verge of collapse.

Amid the crowd's mocking stares and pointed whispers, she turned and stumbled away, not daring to look back, not daring to see the smug, amused faces behind her, fleeing in something close to a panicked run, out of the dazzling but ice-cold, merciless villa.

Most of the liquor she'd been forced to drink had spilled, soaking into her fine clothes, the cold liquid clinging to her skin. The night wind cut through it instantly, a bone-deep chill spreading through her whole body, leaving her shivering uncontrollably.

Her hair hung in disarray against her pale, haggard face, her makeup smeared and ruined, her eyes rimmed red, every trace of her once-radiant, universally adored image completely gone, replaced by nothing but wretched defeat.

Tonight, she'd set aside every bit of pride and dignity she'd guarded for years, groveled, swallowed her humiliation, begged for mercy, and in the end, all she'd gained was disgrace and ruin.

The night wind howled around her, leaving her head spinning, her heart sinking into a bleak, empty despair.

What was she supposed to do now?

Grant lay unconscious, her one reliable backer useless when it mattered most. Her humiliating plea for mercy had earned her nothing but ridicule, and Jasper's resolve hadn't budged an inch.

In this vast apocalypse base, she couldn't find a single way to save her mother.

A crushing sense of helplessness, wrapped in utter despair, swallowed her whole, leaving her nearly unable to breathe.
