Chapter 430

Since the apocalypse began, every mutated species anyone had met fell cleanly into two kinds, mindless zombies or beast-shaped mutant beasts. They had only seen mutant humanoids in two labs, and those were monsters made in experiments.

But this was a fusion with a human upper half and a beast lower half!

Its aura was vast and wild. Its strength had reached Level Seven high-tier. The pressure around it was biting cold, stronger and fiercer than any high-level mutant they had ever faced.

The invisible gaze with mental power matching Paisley's had come from this strange serpent-tailed mutant!

"Human. Intruders die."

A hoarse, odd voice suddenly spoke, mixed with serpent hisses, harsh and eerie, the words uneven.

The serpent-tailed mutant tilted its head, narrow slit eyes locked on the two of them, full of mockery and a hunger to kill.

"It's been a long time since a living human dared enter my territory."

The thirty-three-foot serpent tail swung and swept across the ground!

A thunderous crash. The hard concrete floor split into a dense web of cracks. Rubble flew and the ground shook with terrifying force.

Elijah's face was grim. He raised his gun and fired in one motion!

The Affinity rifle's high-power bullets whistled out, aimed at the mutant's head.

But when the bullets hit the scales, they only threw off blinding sparks. They didn't leave a single mark, and they bounced away, useless.

"Useless struggle."

The serpent-tailed mutant's lips curled into a cold, strange smile, and the slits of its eyes flared with cold light. Its huge body shot forward!

The thirty-three-foot tail swung through the air in a sweeping blow, carrying cutting wind and deadly venom, attack and defense in one, so fast the eye couldn't follow!

Paisley leaped back, her movement light and sharp, dodging the deadly strike in an instant, but the shock inside her had already turned into a storm.

An ordinary mutation could never produce a human-beast fusion like this, and certainly not one with such intelligence, such a powerful body, and such precise mental probing.

It was strong!

Man-made engineering really was terrifying.

She grew even more certain it had been created by a secret lab, through artificial gene modification, splicing, fusion, and breeding, a high-level mutant made by humans.

The mutants they'd met before weren't as smart and weren't as strong as this one.

Paisley's eyes darkened and a chill rose in her heart.

A man-made high-level mutant here was no accident!

It meant there had always been a secret laboratory near Aetheris, quietly carrying out forbidden gene-splicing and live-body experiments, trampling on life and rewriting what species were.

And that wasn't all.

Raising a Level Seven top-tier man-made mutant in the outskirts of the Exclusion Zone was beyond what any small underground lab could do.

Technology this mature, this advanced, this systematic required huge equipment, resources, technical teams, and years of experiments behind it.

From that, she could infer that Aetheris Base might be the earliest, biggest, most central secret experiment site in the whole apocalypse!

The base that everyone relied on, the one they saw as their only shelter, had the biggest root of the apocalypse's mutations and twisted lives buried beneath it!

The appearance of this strange mutant and the spread of disaster in the Exclusion Zone confirmed it once more. The apocalypse wasn't a natural disaster. It was a catastrophe made by people.

The realization was too shocking, too terrifying. Elijah had worked it out too, and even he, always so steady, was horrified.

Everyone called Aetheris Base the safest place there was, but no one knew that deep inside this peaceful land lay such a dark, terrifying secret.

"It looks like," Paisley said, her lips pressed tight, her voice cold to the bone, a sharp edge flashing in her eyes, "what we know has never been the whole apocalypse."

The words had barely left her mouth when the serpent-tailed mutant charged again!

The thirty-three-foot black serpent tail swept in with fierce wind and dense black mist. The poison fog hit the concrete and ate at it with a hiss. Killing intent locked down the space around them, and the sealed warehouse became a cage of death. A deadly fight was about to begin.

Boom!

The tail slammed down, the thick floor cracked layer by layer, and rubble flew mixed with poison mist.

Paisley moved like wind passing, tapped the floor lightly with one toe, and slid several yards sideways, dodging the blow completely.

She knew this Level Seven mutant's most terrifying card.

Ultra-fast self-healing.

Ordinary blades, guns, or Affinity blasts could tear its flesh or blow off its scales, but the wound would close in an instant and the flesh would regrow. It was almost impossible to deal a lethal injury.
