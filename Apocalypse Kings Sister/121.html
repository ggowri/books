Chapter 121

Those cores should've been mine! Paisley's a Lennox, so everything she has belongs to the Lennoxes!

We're the family who grew up with her! What right does a bunch of teammates she met along the way have to anything she gives?

Greed and jealousy had gone straight to Owen's head. He'd completely forgotten how badly he'd treated Paisley and how he'd driven her out. All he could see was the table full of crystal cores.

His wounds still hadn't healed, but he didn't care. He lunged forward and grabbed for the cores.

"Hands off!" several voices snapped at the same time.

Orion, Ashley, and the others moved fast. They stepped in front of the table and stopped Owen cold.

Owen's hand froze in midair. His fingers were just inches from the cores, but he couldn't get any closer.

He looked up at the people blocking him, his face twisted with rage and resentment, and growled through gritted teeth.

"Get out of my way! This is Lennox property, and it's none of your damn business!"

"Lennox property?"

Ashley was so mad she laughed. Her face went cold, and every word she said cut deep.

"Owen, do you have any shame at all? Paisley earned these. Since when do they belong to the Lennoxes?

"When it was life or death, you three hid in the back and let everyone else protect you. Now the danger's gone, and suddenly you've got the nerve to grab the rewards? Nice try, but that's not how this works!"

Orion's face was hard, and his voice was ice-cold. The pressure coming off him was suffocating.

"You want to talk about blood? You abandoned her, humiliated her, and threw her out. You cut those ties yourselves.

"You want to talk about who did the work? You did nothing but drag us down the whole time. You have no right to touch these cores, let alone take them."

Owen's face turned bright red. He was humiliated and furious, but he still wouldn't let it go, and his eyes stayed locked on the cores.

"She grew up under our roof, eating our food! She's my sister, and everything she has belongs to the Lennoxes!

"We're her real family, and you're just outsiders! What right do you have to take this from us?"

Paisley had been standing there quietly the whole time. Now, she finally looked up.

She looked cool and calm. There was no anger in her face, only an icy distance, like she was watching some ridiculous little show.

"Family?" she said softly. Her tone was flat, but the word cut deep and carried through the silent living room.

"You beat me, cursed me, and treated me like dirt. You threw me out to die. Where was all that family talk then?

"Where were you when I was nearly dying out there?"

She raised her eyes a little and glanced at Owen's pale face, then at Reid and Ryan behind him. They had their heads down and couldn't look her in the eye. Her voice stayed perfectly even.

"I cut ties with the Lennoxes a long time ago. Whatever we had, you tore it apart with your own hands. I have nothing to do with your family anymore."

She looked at the Thompsons beside her. They truly cared about her and had stood up for her, and she felt a little warmth spread through her chest.

"They're my real family.

"What's mine is mine, and I'll give it to whoever I want. You don't get to come sniffing around for it.

"Feeling sorry now? Where was all that regret back then?"

Paisley didn't give them an inch.

The moment she finished, she lifted a finger, and a wave of invisible, overwhelming energy burst out of her. It slammed straight into Owen.

Owen was still covered in wounds that hadn't healed. The blast knocked him off his feet, and he sprawled on the floor.

His half-healed wounds tore open all at once. Searing pain shot through his whole body, and he shook uncontrollably, his face as white as a sheet.

Reid and Ryan rushed over to help him up. "Owen, are you okay?"

But Owen only had eyes for Paisley. He hoped she'd feel sorry for him like she used to and worry about his injuries.

He was out of luck. Paisley didn't even look at him again.

When Madison called everyone to dinner, Paisley walked away, and he lost sight of her completely.

Dinner was simple, home-cooked food, but they'd brought back lots of supplies that day, so the spread was bigger than usual.

Elijah poured himself a glass of cola first and stood up. "Everyone, raise your glasses. To Paisley, for saving our lives today."
