Chapter 75

Die, Paisley! And that damn cat's going down with you!

When Rachel saw it, she couldn't stop a nasty, satisfied smile from creeping across her face.

Those two powerful fireballs carried the full force of Owen's Fire Affinity. They could burn anything to a crisp.

Paisley was just a regular person with no ability. There was no way she'd survive a hit like that!

Finally, payback for everything she did to us today!

Jasper stood off to the side, coolly watching like it was a show. He had zero intention of stepping in.

It had nothing to do with him, after all.

Elijah, meanwhile, looked perfectly calm. He didn't so much as blink.

He knew exactly how strong Paisley really was, and he knew that little cat was anything but ordinary. A couple of measly fireballs wouldn't even scratch her, so he wasn't worried at all.

As the roaring fireballs flew straight at her, Paisley didn't even bother looking at them.

Just before they hit, the little white cat leaped out of her arms. Its tiny white body twisted nimbly in midair, and it whipped its fluffy tail around hard!

Two powerful blasts of wind tore across the yard, packed with terrifying force. They sent both fireballs rocketing right back the way they'd come!

It happened so fast that nobody had time to react, and the flames were burning even hotter than before!

Owen was caught off guard with no time to dodge. The fire swept over him and swallowed him whole!

With a deafening roar, flames shot toward the sky, and the searing pain ripped a horrible scream out of Owen.

The fire tore through his clothes and his skin. His hair curled, blackened, and fell out in clumps. Blisters swelled across his exposed skin, and in seconds, the burns all over his body had split open, charred and bleeding.

Agony ripped through every inch of him, like someone was jabbing red-hot pokers straight into his flesh. He curled up on the ground, convulsing violently.

His shrill, desperate screams were hoarse and broken, and they echoed again and again across the open yard. Just hearing them was enough to make anyone's skin crawl.

He slapped frantically at the flames with both hands. But the energy packed into those fireballs was too strong, and slapping did nothing. The fire just kept burning hotter, raging all over his body.

The pain crushed every last bit of his pride and attitude. Owen couldn't keep up his tough-guy front anymore. He thrashed wildly on the ground and screamed for help.

"Help! Somebody help me! I'm burning to death!

"Rachel! Save me!"

Rachel had been frozen stiff with fear. Hearing Owen's desperate, broken screams finally snapped her out of it.

She stared at him, covered in flames, his skin charred and melting, and stumbled back several steps in terror.

In a panic, she gritted her teeth and called on her Level Two Water Affinity. Cold mist formed fast at her fingertips, and wave after wave of water poured out and crashed over the flames on Owen's body.

The water hit the fire with a loud sizzle, sending up a thick cloud of scalding white steam that rolled over everyone nearby.

A full minute later, after burning through most of her energy, Rachel finally put out the fire.

Owen collapsed on the ground, completely drained. His clothes had burned to black ash, and huge patches of his skin were blistered and charred. He was covered in blood, and every weak breath he took brought a fresh wave of searing pain.

He was burned almost beyond recognition, and every last bit of his rich-boy dignity was gone.

Rachel had pushed her ability way too hard. She stood there pale and swaying, barely able to stay on her feet, with fear, helplessness, and bitter resentment written all over her face.

She couldn't believe it. A cat that looked so soft and harmless could reflect an ability right back and seriously wound a Level Two ability user. It was terrifying enough to make her blood run cold.

Not far off, Jasper watched, shocked and darkly amused, and let out a low whistle.

He'd just wanted to watch a good show.

He'd never imagined that the cute, fluffy little white cat in Paisley's arms was hiding this kind of terrifying power. The way it threw that attack right back was fast, clean, and absolutely brutal.

A random little animal had crushed a Level Two Fire Affinity user and messed Owen up this badly without even trying.

Paisley's luck is unreal. Even some cat she picked up off the street is this strong?
