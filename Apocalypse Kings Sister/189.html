Chapter 189

Meanwhile, in the space in Paisley's mind, Trovie's eyes had gone wide as saucers. It stared at the gleaming mountain of ore, its eyes shining, and flapped its little wings like crazy as it squealed.

[Master, it's gold, so much refined gold, at least 11,000 pounds of it! We're going to be rich! Say yes, Master, it's a guaranteed win!]

Paisley stared at the dazzling gold mountain on the screen, and her own eyes lit up a little too.

Yesterday's intense round of healing five people had pushed her purification ability up a level. It was sharper and more precise now, and ordinary radiation and toxin contamination would be easy for her to clear.

She pulled herself together and stayed calm. [I can take care of it. Let's trade!

[I'll give you five big crates of fresh fruit, five of seasonal vegetables, five of premium oats and assorted whole grains, five of complete cooking spice sets, and five big sacks of high-yield seeds. All of it is contamination-free and high quality.]

The interstellar trader's eyes lit up. It was even more than she'd hoped for, and she agreed happily without a second's hesitation.

[Deal! That's more than worth it! Clean food is priceless in the Galactic Realm, and tons of rich and powerful people are fighting over it, so trading this contaminated refined gold for it is a steal!]

To her, nothing could compare to clean fruits and vegetables, especially the contamination-free seeds. They were seeds from an ancient planet, and last time, the research institute had found that they grew successfully at an incredibly high rate.

Her business now dealt directly with the Federation Research Institute.

Later, she'd learned that the suppressants made from these fruits and vegetables could hold back people's psychic frenzy better than anything else. They were a treasure for the whole planet, bringing people hope.

[I'll start the cross-dimensional transfer right now!]

She reached over and pressed the transfer button beside her. Brilliant blue starlight flooded the screen, and waves of spatial energy rippled through it.

The next moment, a heavy mountain of refined gold wrapped in a faint black haze of contamination appeared out of nowhere on the empty floor in front of Paisley. It weighed 11,000 pounds.

Almost at once, Paisley sent Trovie off to assemble everything she'd promised in return.

Crate after crate went through, packed with plump, juicy fruit, fresh green vegetables, premium oats and grains, and cooking spices. The five big sacks of round, high-yield seeds bursting with life were sent over too.

The trade was done in seconds.

The trader watched the steady stream of fresh, perfect goods pour in and beamed with joy.

[I really enjoy working with you. I'm looking forward to next time!]

With that, the screen flickered, and the connection cut out.

All that was left in the room was the glittering mountain of refined gold, the black haze of contamination drifting faintly over its surface.

Trovie spun circles around it, wings flapping nonstop, babbling with excitement.

[We're rich, we're rich! A whole 11,000 pounds of gold, worth 100,000 points! But these black spots are such an eyesore, so hurry up and purify it, Master...]

Paisley stood in front of it, a small smile in her eyes, still perfectly composed.

After yesterday's intense healing session, she could control her Plant Affinity's purifying power with ease, handling contamination and toxins with perfect precision. Deep-space contamination like this was no problem at all.

She raised a hand, and rich green light swept out, settling gently but firmly over the whole mountain of gold.

Pure, living purification seeped into the ore grain by grain, stripping the black contamination away inch by inch.

The stubborn deep-space contamination vanished fast under her pure Plant Affinity.

The dark haze thinned, and thinned further, until it was gone entirely.

The whole mountain shed its dullness, and the gold underneath shone through.

In a matter of minutes, every bit of it was clean.

She slowly lowered her hand and pulled back her Plant Affinity. For once, a bright spark of excitement showed in her usually cool, calm eyes, and her lips curved into a small smile.

This was 11,000 pounds of gold. Before the apocalypse, it would've made anyone rich, and anyone would be thrilled to see it.

Trovie, meanwhile, was beside itself, flapping circles around the gleaming pile without stopping.

It pounced on a small chunk of refined gold, hugged it up with both little paws, and eagerly took a big bite. A second later, it froze, and its little face scrunched up in pain.
