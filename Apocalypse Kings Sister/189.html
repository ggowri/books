Chapter 189

In her mind, Trovie's eyes had gone round as saucers, fixed on the gleaming mountain of ore, practically glowing, wings flapping in a frenzy.

[Host! That's gold! So much gold! I'd guess eleven thousand pounds, easy! We're going to be rich! Host, say yes! This is a guaranteed win!]

Even Paisley couldn't help a small gleam in her own eyes at the sight of it.

Yesterday's intense round of healing had pushed her purification ability up a level. It was sharper now, more precise. Ordinary radiation and toxin contamination would be nothing to clear.

She steadied herself, her tone staying even.

[I can handle it. Let's trade.]

[I'll give you five large crates of fresh fruit, five of seasonal vegetables, five of premium rice and assorted grains, five of a full cooking spice set, plus five sacks of high-yield seeds. All of it clean, all top grade.]

The trader's eyes went wide—more than she'd expected. She agreed without a moment's hesitation, delighted.

[Deal! More than worth it! Uncontaminated produce doesn't exist on our market at any price—half the elite out here would fight over it. Trading this tainted gold for it is an easy call.]

To her, nothing was worth more than clean produce, especially the seeds.

These were old-world seeds, the kind the research institute had specifically analyzed last time—the germination rate was remarkably high.

Her trade now ran straight through the Federation's research institute.

She'd since learned that the suppressants made from this produce could hold back psychic frenzy better than almost anything else available.

It was, in a real sense, a treasure for the whole planet.

It gave people hope.

[I'm starting the cross-dimensional transfer now!]

She reached over and pressed the transfer switch. Brilliant blue light swept across the screen, ripples of spatial distortion rolling through it.

The next instant, a heavy mountain of refined gold, wrapped in a faint dark haze of contamination, appeared out of nowhere on the open ground beside her—eleven thousand pounds of it.

Almost at once, Paisley sent Trovie off to assemble everything she'd promised in return.

Crate after crate went through—plump, juicy fruit, tender green vegetables, premium rice and grains, cooking spices, and five sacks of plump, vigorous high-yield seeds.

The trade was done in an instant.

The trader looked at the steady stream of fresh, flawless goods arriving and smiled, pure delight on her face.

[It's always a pleasure working with you. Looking forward to next time!]

The screen flickered, and the connection cut out.

In the room, all that remained was the gleaming mountain of gold, the dark haze on its surface drifting faintly.

Trovie spun circles around it, wings flapping nonstop, babbling with excitement.

[We're rich, we're rich! A full eleven thousand pounds of gold! That's a straight hundred thousand points, Host, we struck it big... except, ugh, these black spots are so ugly. Host, purify it, quick.]

Paisley stood in front of it, a small smile in her eyes, still perfectly composed.

Yesterday's intensive healing had sharpened her Plant Affinity to a fine edge. Contamination and toxins were hers to control now with precision. A deep-space taint like this was nothing to her.

She raised a hand, and rich green light swept out, settling gently but completely over the mountain of gold.

Pure, living purification seeped into the ore grain by grain, stripping the black contamination away inch by inch.

The stubborn taint that had resisted every other method vanished fast under her Affinity.

The dark haze thinned, and thinned further, until it was gone entirely.

The whole mountain shed its dullness, and the gold underneath shone through.

In a matter of minutes, every bit of it was clean.

She let the light fade. For once, a bright spark of excitement broke through her usual calm, and the corner of her mouth curved up.

Eleven thousand pounds of gold. Before the world ended, this alone would have made anyone rich. No wonder it made her smile.

******

Trovie, meanwhile, was beside itself, flapping circles around the gleaming pile without stopping.

It dove at a small chunk of gold, wrestled it up in both little paws, and bit down hard, delighted—then froze, its small face scrunching in distress.
