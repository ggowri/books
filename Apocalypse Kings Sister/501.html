Chapter 501

"Be careful."

"If it gets bad, get back to the car. Don't push your luck."

Her gaze moved to Rachel and the others, and it turned cold.

"In the apocalypse, everyone answers for their own choices."

"We cut ties long ago. We have nothing to do with each other."

"After what you did to me, be grateful I'm not kicking you while you're down!"

Rachel burned with fury and frustration. She stared at the idling SUV and tried another guilt trip.

But a huge lead rat, far bigger than the rest, leapt through the air. A stench of cold rot hit her, and its razor teeth flashed as it dove at Rachel.

She panicked and called up her last scraps of Plant Affinity. Dry, brittle vines stuttered up from the frozen ground and barely blocked it.

The force of the blow numbed her arms. She had no energy left to cry for help.

Paisley turned and walked to the SUV.

The two sides couldn't have been in more different shape.

Ashley and Jasper's group wore the thick winter gear they'd just picked out. They were full of energy and could handle the rats easily.

Rachel's team, by contrast, was long out of power and almost all of them were hurt.

They were running on pure survival instinct.

They knew nobody was coming. If they wanted to live, they'd have to save themselves.

In minus-forty cold, their wounds had gone stiff. Every use of their powers pulled at torn flesh, and sharp pain ripped through them.

Paisley took out a box of freshly washed strawberries from her storage dimension.

She popped one into her mouth, and sweet juice spread across her tongue.

She stood at the edge of the storm, eating strawberries and calmly watching the brutal fight, completely at ease.

That scene cut Rachel to the core.

She was throwing everything into blocking the lead rat when she caught sight of Paisley idly eating strawberries. Rage and frustration swelled in her chest, a metallic taste surged up, and she coughed a mouthful of blood onto the snow, staining it red.

Hatred was growing wild inside her, but she didn't dare lash out. If she lost focus for a second, the rats would tear her apart.

She could only choke down her fury and grit her teeth through wave after wave of attacks.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan fought off the rats with all the power they had left.

When they glimpsed Paisley on the steps, regret poured over them like ice water.

They began to replay everything. Back then, Rachel had whispered in their ears, invented lies and framed Paisley. They'd believed her without question, no hesitation.

Again and again they'd mistreated Paisley and blamed her unfairly.

Only now, trapped and dying, did the truth hit them.

Maybe Rachel had been deceiving them from the start, and Paisley was the one who'd been wronged.

That was why she was so determined to cut ties and never look back.

There was something fishy here. When they got back, they'd investigate it thoroughly.

Ryan, with the worst injuries, felt the wind sting his wounds. Moments ago Rachel had shoved him out as a human shield. He shook with pain.

His regret nearly drowned him.

Paisley would never have pushed family into a beast's jaws to save herself.

He regretted everything.

Rachel. The little sister he'd always doted on.

He couldn't even count how many times she'd shoved him out to take the hit.

He wished he hadn't blindly trusted Rachel's lies and hurt Paisley over and over, the girl who had given them everything she had.

But there was no medicine for regret. They were hanging by a thread. Whether they'd survive was anyone's guess.

He had no right to ask her to save him.

Jasper drove his dagger through the skull of the last rat that jumped him, flicked the blood off the blade, and glanced at Paisley, who was happily eating strawberries. Wind blew snow onto his shoulders and he shrank a little.

"Paisley, it's freezing. We've had enough practice. Let's head back."

Paisley swallowed her sweet strawberry, slipped the empty container into her storage dimension, and nodded.

"Okay. Everyone in the car. We're going back to base."

Ashley raised a hand and an earth spike broke through the ground and skewered a rat bearing down on her. With the threat beside her gone, she dashed to the SUV and slid in.
