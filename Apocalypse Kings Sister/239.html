Chapter 239

"You mean we just go over there and clean out the whole munitions depot? That doesn't sound like you at all," Orion said.

Elijah looked around at everyone, his eyes unusually firm. "In this apocalypse, it's survival of the fittest. Having weapons and strength in your own hands is the only way that really works."

He paused and spoke honestly. "There might be unknown dangers on the way. Anyone who doesn't want to take the risk can stay here, rest, and wait.

"You can also go your own way right now. I won't blame anyone."

Paisley's eyes were clear and bright, and she saw right through what he was thinking.

After everything they'd been through, they all knew how terrifying the people behind this were. More weapons meant more confidence to protect themselves and more strength to fight back against the darkness.

Besides, three years from now, Elijah would be the most powerful big shot of all. She was definitely hitching her wagon to him.

She didn't hesitate at all and spoke up. "I'm in."

With Paisley taking the lead, the Thompson brothers, Madison, Mason, and Marcus all nodded and agreed. Everyone was on board, and nobody backed out.

"Since everyone agrees, we're leaving right now," Elijah said.

As soon as he finished, Paisley gave a mental command. A second later, two jeeps appeared out of thin air on the clearing, right in front of everyone.

This was the second time Ashley had seen Paisley use her Storage Dimension with her own eyes. Her eyes sparkled with awe as she hurried over and looked up at Paisley in disbelief.

"Paisley! That's so cool! I was already shocked when you made the table and lunch appear earlier.

"How big is your Storage Dimension, anyway? You made two whole jeeps appear, just like that!"

She hugged Paisley's arm tight, smiling, full of warmth and trust. "It's so nice having you around! I can always count on you!"

Next to them, Thomas, Spencer, Madison, Mason, and Marcus had frozen where they stood. They were so stunned they couldn't snap out of it for a long time.

Until now, everyone had only known that Paisley had a powerful Plant Affinity. She could make giant trees grow out of thin air and save people from hopeless situations, a talent way beyond ordinary people. But they never imagined she'd also have an extremely rare space power!

Paisley actually had two powers.

Her Plant Affinity could heal, attack, and defend, and her space power could store supplies. Both were among the rarest, most precious abilities in the apocalypse.

Paisley was basically the universe's favorite, and it was enough to make anyone jealous.

Spencer sighed in genuine admiration, full of pride. "Paisley's seriously amazing, and she sure kept it well hidden.

"Pais, I'm proud of you. From now on, I'm hanging on to you for dear life."

Madison nodded over and over, relieved and in awe, and thanked her from the heart. "Pais is really strong. We hit the jackpot.

"Because she's here, we get hot meals in the apocalypse and make it out of hopeless situations alive!

"This time, if it weren't for her, I'd already be dead."

Next to them, Marcus kept his dark eyes glued to Paisley, and the feelings in them grew more and more obsessive and intense.

She'd saved him from hopeless situations again and again, and her strength had stunned him over and over. The crush in his heart had taken root long ago and grown wildly into an obsession.

He repeated it silently to himself, obsessive and devout. Everyone said a person whose life was saved should repay it with their whole heart. She'd already saved him twice, pulling him back from death both times, so for the rest of his life, he belonged to her.

Not just in this life, but in the next one and every life after that, he only wanted to belong to Paisley and do whatever she said!

All that burning obsession didn't escape Elijah, who was standing not far away. He sensed it, his eyes darkening, and looked straight at Marcus.

A second later, he calmly stepped forward and stood in front of Paisley, blocking Marcus's burning stare.

He raised a finger slightly, and tiny sparks of lightning crackled at its tip. With a low whoosh through the air, a sharp bolt of lightning shot out and hit the tree right next to Marcus.

With a deafening crash, the thick trunk snapped and fell, sending dust flying everywhere. The threat couldn't have been clearer.

It was a blatant warning.

Marcus looked up and stared straight at Elijah, and their eyes met.

An invisible clash of wills exploded between them. In a split second, sparks of open hostility flew, and tension churned beneath the surface.
