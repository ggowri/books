Chapter 239

"You're saying we go raid that munitions depot ourselves? That doesn't sound like you at all."

Elijah looked around at the group, his gaze unwavering.

"In a world this broken, the only law left is survival of the fittest. Weapons and strength, held in your own hands, that's the only real power there is."

He paused, then added honestly.

"There could be unknown danger involved. Anyone who'd rather not risk it is welcome to stay behind and rest here."

"You can part ways with me right now too, if that's what you want. I won't hold it against anyone."

Paisley's gaze stayed clear and sharp, seeing straight through to what he was really thinking.

After everything they'd been through, everyone understood just how dangerous the people behind this were. More weapons meant more ability to protect themselves, more leverage against the darkness coming for them.

Besides, three years from now, he'd be one of the most powerful men alive.

That was a connection worth holding onto, and she wasn't letting go.

She answered without a moment's hesitation.

"I'm in."

With Paisley leading the way, the Thompson brothers, Madison, Mason, and Marcus all nodded in agreement right after, every single one of them on board without exception.

"Since everyone's agreed, we move out now."

The moment the words left her, Paisley's mind stirred.

The next instant, two jeeps appeared out of nowhere on the open ground in front of them.

This was the second time Ashley had watched Paisley pull something straight out of her Storage Dimension. The girl's eyes went wide with wonder, and she hurried over, looking up at Paisley with pure disbelief.

"Paisley! You're unbelievable!

I was already shocked when you conjured a whole table and lunch earlier.

Just how big is your Storage Dimension?

You made two whole jeeps appear, just like that!"

She looped her arm tight through Paisley's, eyes crinkling with warmth and trust.

"Having you around really is the best. I can always count on you, no matter what!"

Beside them, Thomas, Spencer, Madison, Mason, and Marcus stood frozen, shock rolling through them in waves, unable to process it for a long moment.

******

Until now, they'd only known Paisley had an incredibly powerful Plant Affinity, capable of conjuring towering trees out of thin air to save them from disaster, already a gift far beyond anything ordinary.

But they'd never guessed she was also an exceptionally rare space-based Affinity user.

Paisley really was a dual-Affinity user.

Plant Affinity that could heal, attack, and defend, paired with a Storage Dimension that could carry entire stockpiles of supplies. Both were among the rarest, most coveted gifts in this broken world.

Paisley really was the universe's favorite. It was almost unfair how much she had going for her.

Spencer couldn't help but voice his admiration, pride thick in his tone.

"Paisley really is something else. She's been hiding way more than any of us realized."

"Pais, I'm proud of you. From now on, I'm sticking close and riding your coattails."

Madison nodded enthusiastically, relief and awe plain in her eyes, gratitude pouring out of her.

"Pais is incredibly strong. We really lucked out having her with us.

Because of her, we get hot meals even in this ruined world, and we keep surviving the impossible."

"If it weren't for her today, I'd already be dead."

Beside them, Marcus's gaze stayed locked on Paisley, something deep and obsessive growing behind his eyes.

Saved from the brink of death not once but twice, watching her overwhelming strength firsthand again and again, whatever feeling had taken root in his chest had grown into something close to devotion.

He repeated the thought to himself silently, reverent and unwavering.

They always said a life saved deserved a life given in return.

She'd already pulled him back from death's door twice. That meant his life belonged to her now, completely.

Not just this life. Every life after this one too. He wanted nothing more than to belong to Paisley Lennox, to follow her lead without question, forever.

That kind of raw, obsessive devotion hadn't gone unnoticed by the man standing nearby.

Elijah caught it immediately, his gaze darkening as he looked directly at where Marcus stood.

A second later, without a flicker of expression, he stepped forward, placing himself between Paisley and the heat of that stare, cutting it off completely.

He lifted a finger, a thread of lightning crackling faintly at the tip, and with a low crack of sound, a sharp bolt shot out, striking a tree right beside Marcus.

A deafening crack rang out as the thick trunk split and crashed to the ground, dust kicking up everywhere, the message unmistakable.

A warning, plain and simple.

Marcus looked up, meeting Elijah's eyes directly.

An invisible clash of will sparked between the two of them, electric and sharp, something volatile simmering just beneath the surface.
