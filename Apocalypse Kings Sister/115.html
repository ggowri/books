Chapter 115

Paisley stayed cool and steady.

Elijah's in there too. We can't just leave him.

She quickly scanned the scene, and a plan came to her right away.

"We use fire. Animals and plants are all scared of fire."

With that, she pulled two big drums of gasoline out of her Storage Dimension.

Orion got it right away. He grabbed the drums, ripped off the seals, and poured the gasoline evenly around the outside of the rat swarm.

The gasoline spread fast across the ground and covered the whole area where the rats had gathered, soaking into every crack and corner. The strong smell of gas quickly drowned out the stench of rot.

The huge swarm of rats had no idea what was coming. They just kept charging like crazy.

"Get back!" Paisley shouted.

Elijah let out a breath of relief when he saw them, and he got the message right away. He hurried the exhausted group back, out of range of the fire.

Once everyone was safe, Spencer's eyes flashed. A blazing flame burst to life in his palm, and he threw it right at the gas-soaked ground.

With a deep boom, the fire hit the gasoline and exploded. A column of flame shot into the sky, and the blaze swept across the whole area.

A wall of raging fire spread out and cut off every escape route the rats had.

Countless mutant rats shrieked and thrashed in the flames, but the walls of fire had them trapped. They had nowhere to run.

The fire blocked the sewer openings that had been spitting out rats nonstop too, and the heat cut off any more from coming.

In just moments, the raging swarm was swallowed by the roaring flames and burned to ash.

When they saw that, everyone finally relaxed, and a huge weight lifted off their shoulders. They all thought the nightmare swarm was finally over.

But just as everyone let their guard down, the ground started shaking violently again!

The dirt under their feet churned and split apart, sending rocks and dirt flying. A deep, heavy rumble came from underground, and a pressure way more savage and powerful than the rats burst up from below!

With a thunderous roar, a huge crater blew open in the ground, and a gigantic mutant rat came bursting up from deep underground!

It was a Level Four mutant Rat King!

Its whole body was covered in thick, hard, gray-black armor. It was monstrously huge, a full 16 feet from nose to tail and over six feet tall, like a giant beast that had been lurking underground.

Its slitted eyes were blood-red and bloodshot, and they locked coldly onto everyone there.

Its sharp fangs gleamed, and its thick claws were hard as iron. One tap could crack the ground. A thick cloud of rotting poison swirled around it, and the pressure it gave off was suffocating.

It was the real source of the whole swarm. It had been hiding deep underground the whole time, and burning its swarm alive had finally made it mad enough to come out.

The second it broke through the surface, the Rat King let out a deafening, shrill shriek and lunged forward. It was out for blood, and it launched a brutal psychic attack straight at the group!

An invisible Level Four psychic shockwave exploded out of its massive body like a violent tidal wave. It swept across the whole area and slammed into everyone there!

Nobody saw it coming, and the psychic storm ripped through all their minds at once.

Elijah, Orion, and the rest of the team got hit the hardest. Splitting pain exploded in their heads, their temples pounded, and their minds went blank and ringing.

The Level Four psychic pressure was way more than they could handle. Their vision went black, their ears rang, and their abilities locked up completely. They couldn't use them at all.

The lower-level fighters like Kelly and Rachel dropped to the ground clutching their heads. They were convulsing in pain, drenched in cold sweat, and white as sheets. They couldn't even open their eyes or stand up, let alone call for help.

The psychic attack had everyone pinned down, and they were in a hopeless situation again.

Only Paisley stood tall and steady as a rock. Her face didn't even flinch.

Her Plant Affinity was already at peak Level Five, and since she had two abilities, her mental strength was way deeper and tougher than anyone else's.

The Level Four Rat King's psychic attack hit her like a breeze against a boulder. It couldn't budge her at all.

Her expression turned deadly.
