Chapter 139

The mood in the room was heavy and tense.

All four Lennoxes were hurt, some worse than others, with bruises, scrapes, and internal injuries all over. Rachel was hurt the worst.

Her back was bruised, and the skin on her elbows and knees was torn open. She kept wiping the blood from the corner of her mouth, but it wouldn't stop, and her face was pale and weak. She looked pitiful.

The three brothers were used to putting Rachel first. Even though they were badly hurt themselves, they grabbed the first aid supplies right away and carefully cleaned and bandaged her wounds first.

Owen gently wiped the blood from the corner of her mouth. He looked worried and scared.

"Rachel, you need to stay away from Paisley from now on.

"She's not that weak pushover we used to boss around anymore. It's like she's a completely different person. She's cold and ruthless, and she doesn't hold back at all.

"She's strong now, and she's got all four Thompsons guarding her, plus that crazy-strong feral cat. We don't stand a chance against her.

"Listen to me. From now on, we lie low, stay out of her way, and stop messing with her. I'm seriously scared she'll kill us."

Next to him, Reid nodded hard as he cleaned the scrape on her arm. He sounded helpless and still shaken.

"Owen's right, Rachel. It's not like we didn't want to help you back there.

"Even if all of us teamed up, we wouldn't stand a chance against Paisley now. She's way out of our league.

"You saw it yourself. She doesn't care about the old days anymore, and she's not going to cut us any slack.

"At least this happened inside the Rivergate base, where there are rules to keep her in check. She was just teaching you a lesson, and she held back.

"But if you pull a stunt like this out in the wild with no rules, she might actually kill us next time. I don't want to die."

Ryan touched his cheek, which was swollen and burning where the willow branch had hit him, and winced in pain. All his pent-up frustration finally boiled over, and he couldn't help blaming her.

"Rachel, you were way too reckless this time.

"You know you're no match for Paisley, so why do you keep going after her? You're asking for it, and you drag us down with you every time!

"We're all badly hurt now, and we hadn't even healed from last time. Are you happy now?

"Can't you just behave for once? Wouldn't it be better if we all just made it home safe?"

As her three brothers complained and begged her to back off, Rachel slowly hid the bitter hatred in her eyes. Right away, she put on her usual hurt, pitiful, sweet face.

Underneath, her mind was perfectly clear. Crying, complaining, and holding grudges wouldn't do any good right now. The only card she had left to turn things around was the pendant on Paisley's chest.

These three act like they protect me, but they're just selfish, spineless cowards. They're idiots I can use however I want.

As it happened, she needed help and some pawns, and these three would make perfect stepping stones.

Rachel started crying softly. She sounded sincere and heartfelt, and every word hit their ambition and frustration right where it counted.

"Reid, Ryan, Owen, I never did any of this for myself. I did it all for you.

"Don't you want to awaken an affinity and become someone everyone respects and fears in the apocalypse?

"You know it deep down. In this brutal apocalypse, ordinary people with no affinity are just easy prey. They have to rely on others to survive, and they'll never get ahead."

She slowed down on purpose and coaxed them along, stirring up the frustration and longing buried deep inside them.

"Think about it. Everyone on the team has an affinity, from Paisley and the four Thompsons to Elijah and even the regular team members. Every one of them is stronger than us.

"We're the only ones stuck in place. We'll always be beneath everyone else, and they'll always look down on us.

"Paisley is the root of all of this. She's definitely hiding some crazy secret nobody knows about.

"And don't forget, who is Elijah, really? Why is he protecting Paisley too?"

Rachel looked sure of herself, and she dangled the bait.

"I promise you. If you help me get that gold-mended pendant off her, I'll find a way to help Reid and Ryan awaken their affinities. You'll never have to be ordinary again!"
