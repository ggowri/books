Chapter 275

Within seconds, the two of them landed on the hilltop.

On the open rooftop, the Level Five psychic zombie stood perfectly still, its posture almost human, except for those crimson eyes carrying something utterly inhuman.

It still showed no intention of fighting hand to hand. Instead it pushed its psychic power further, trying to force its way into their minds, twist their thoughts, conjure an illusion to turn them against each other.

But their willpower far outstripped any ordinary Affinity user's. The moment the illusion began to form, the two of them tore through it together.

The instant the illusion shattered, the backlash slammed into the creature's own mind. Its body jerked violently, pale skin twitching, and for the first time, something like fear crept into its eyes.

It had lain in wait a long time, sending hordes to kill, watching countless strong fighters crumble inside its illusions. It had never imagined mere human survivors could withstand its control, let alone tear its illusion apart with their bare hands.

Too late for regret now.

In the next instant, Elijah's eyes went cold, and his Lightning Affinity exploded outward.

Blinding blue-white lightning cracked in his palm, locking down the entire space in an instant, dense bolts of lightning binding the Level Five zombie's limbs and the psychic energy swirling around it, sealing off every chance of escape.

At almost the same moment, Paisley's voice rang out, clear and cold.

"Bind."

Thick green vines tore up through the ground beneath her feet, tough and razor-edged, lashing around the zombie's body in an instant, pinning it in place, unable to move an inch.

Paisley raised her hand again, and the Frostbloom Sword took shape in her grip, its blade coated in a thin layer of frost, cold enough to bite.

She spun, clean and sharp, and brought the blade down in one decisive strike.

Cold light flashed.

The Level Five psychic zombie didn't even manage a scream before its body split apart.

Its crimson eyes blew wide, locked open in disbelief and fury, staring straight ahead even as the life drained out of them.

It had never imagined its end would come this easily, this suddenly.

The massive body crashed to the ground, all signs of life gone for good.

Paisley crouched down, fingers slipping precisely into the zombie's skull, and pulled out the plump, gleaming Level Five psychic crystal core.

The core sat perfectly clear in her palm, wrapped in a faint black psychic glow, pulsing with dense, overwhelming psychic energy. Warmth spread from her fingertips, rich and commanding.

A flicker of brightness passed through Paisley's eyes as she tucked the core away into her storage.

With this, her Psychic Affinity could jump straight to Level Six.

Elijah hadn't expected it to fall so easily.

Paisley seemed completely unaffected by its psychic power.

Her mind was just that strong.

Once again, Elijah felt it, plain and undeniable. Paisley was powerful.

Far more powerful than he was.

He needed to get stronger, fast, if he wanted to be able to protect her.

He walked over to stand beside her.

"Let's head back."

Paisley nodded and fell into step behind him.

With the mastermind gone, the suffocating psychic pressure over the battlefield vanished instantly.

The once-coordinated, fearless horde lost every ounce of direction, their minds going blank, scattering into clumsy chaos, left with nothing but raw, mindless bloodlust, stumbling aimlessly.

The crushing weight on everyone's chest finally lifted. Minds cleared, bodies felt lighter, exhaustion faded, and fighting spirit flared back to life in every pair of eyes.

"The horde's lost its direction! Everyone, push forward!"

A soldier captain seized the moment, his voice rallying the whole line as he led the counterattack.

Without the high-tier zombie's control and pressure, what had looked like an unstoppable horde collapsed into disorganized chaos within moments.

The group spread out across the battlefield, working with the soldiers and survivors to mop up the stragglers. What had been a losing fight turned into a one-sided massacre.

******

An hour of clean-up later, every zombie was either dead or scattered and gone.

The group burned the bodies, and the battle finally came to an end.

The convoy's commander strode over to Elijah and Paisley's group, eyes full of respect and gratitude, his voice solemn as he thanked them.

"If you hadn't stepped in, we never would've held against that horde, let alone won this fight.
