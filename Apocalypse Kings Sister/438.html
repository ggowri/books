Chapter 438

Envy filled their eyes, and they lingered, striking up a conversation.

After a nerve-wracking trip, they were relieved to meet another team, and they couldn't help unloading about how dangerous this place had been.

"This area is creepy. We've scavenged around here a few times and never felt like this."

A young teammate wiped cold sweat from his forehead. "It's weirdly quiet today. Way fewer zombies. But it feels like something's watching us. Our skin's been crawling. We didn't dare go any deeper."

Another teammate cut in, his face pale. "It's worse than creepy! Yesterday we saw something weird and almost got killed!"

That got the Falcon Squad's attention. Every head turned toward him.

The man swallowed. His eyes were full of leftover terror, and he lowered his voice.

"Yesterday we scouted the edge of the steel plant. We caught a glimpse of a huge black shape, insanely fast, lurking deep in the ruins. And a sound that was half snake hiss, half human growl."

"Worst part is, a Level Five Affinity user from another team just got a little too close. A cold psychic pressure knocked him out cold. When he woke up, he was covered in black marks, full of poison, and his Affinity was gone. He's a vegetable now!"

******

The team leader sighed heavily, wary. "We think a high-level mutant beast is living in these ruins. Not an ordinary one. It seems to have a human's brain. Cunning. It lies low, patient, watching people from the dark."

"Everyone on our team has been saying there's a snake monster in Ridgemont that kills without a trace. Plenty of field teams that went deep never came back. Nobody ever found the bodies."

"Today we only dared pick through the edges for scraps. We came for those five grain depots, but there's no way we're going deeper now, not with that thing around."

A few words was all it took. The Falcon Squad traded glances and understood.

They had met the same serpent mutant.

So it had been lurking in the Ridgemont ruins for a long time, hunting survivors in secret. Its hands were covered in blood, and the danger ran far deeper than they'd thought.

Paisley's eyes darkened. "The shape you saw. Was it human on top, a snake's tail below, huge and very fast?"

The Southspire team froze, eyes wide with shock.

"You... you've seen it too?! So you know about that thing!"

With her confirmation, their last bit of hope was gone. Fear set them shaking.

This artificial Level Seven serpent mutant ruled the entire Ridgemont ruins. It was the true lord of the Exclusion Zone, hidden in the dark, killing without a sound. Nobody was a match for it.

Elijah's face was grim. "That's a Level Seven artificial mutant. Very smart, terrifyingly strong, with deadly venom and mental contamination. It heals fast, and you can't kill it without destroying its crystal core."

"Don't go near the depots or the steel plant's core area, and never fight it. If you do, you're dead."

The Southspire team went white and cold. They nodded again and again and thanked him, shaken to the bone.

Any idea of sneaking deeper was gone. They'd leave as soon as it was light.

As long as the serpent mutant lived, the Ridgemont Exclusion Zone was a death trap, and every survivor who passed through, scouted, or scavenged would end up its prey.

When it was time to leave, the five men's eyes drifted to the rising steam and the rich, tempting stew.

The spicy smell reached their noses, and they swallowed hard.

Their faces showed envy they couldn't hide, and their feelings were mixed.

In the apocalypse, everyone struggled to survive. Gnawing on dry hardtack and sipping cold water counted as a lucky day.

Yet this team sat in the middle of the deadly Ridgemont ruins eating steaming hot stew, with iced drinks to go with it, full of everyday comfort.

Some people get all the luck.

Their leader gritted his teeth, swallowed his pride, and spoke, awkward but earnest.
