Chapter 373

Resentment churned in her chest. She felt nothing but growing contempt for the pathetic wreck of a man in front of her, and even less willingness to burn her own Affinity healing him.

In the cabin, Grant clung to the last scrap of consciousness, turning his head with great effort to look at the silent Chloe beside him, his eyes full of need and desperate dependence.

"Chloe, what are you thinking? Hurry, use your Healing Affinity, save me. It hurts so much... I don't think I can hold on."

His breath was faint, his voice trembling as he pressed on, a last, desperate hope in his tone.

"Chloe, my arm, it'll grow back, right? It has to. Tell me it will."

Chloe snapped back to herself, wiping all the disgust and coldness from her eyes, forcing on an expression of reluctance and concern. Her voice came out soft and gentle, every word cold and merciless underneath.

"Grant, calm down."

She furrowed her brow lightly, feigning helplessness.

"Only a Level Seven Plant Affinity can regrow broken bones and regenerate flesh, only then could your arm be fixed. My Affinity's only Level Five. I can't do that."

She raised a hand, calling up a faint green glow of healing light, laying it gently over his gruesome wound, slowly stemming the bleeding and easing the pain.

"Right now, all I can do is stop the bleeding and stabilize you. Once I break through to Level Seven, I'll find a way to fix your arm."

Her words came out soft and tender, but underneath, she'd long since run out of patience, nothing left but cold indifference and calculation.

Level Seven was nearly impossible to reach. The road ahead was uncertain, no one knew when, or if, she'd ever get there. More likely than not, never.

Bottom line, his arm was done for.

A one-armed Level Six Affinity user was no longer any kind of prodigy. From here on, he'd be nothing but dead weight at the base, no use to anyone.

Hearing this, the last light in Grant's eyes went out completely. His whole body went rigid, and even the bone-deep pain of his wound seemed to fade into numbness, replaced by an icy chill that swept through him.

But it didn't take long before he forced down the despair and pulled himself back together.

He was a powerhouse of the apocalypse. His will ran far steadier than most people's.

So he'd lost his left arm. His dominant right hand was still whole, he could still channel his Affinity, still fight.

Grant looked up at Chloe, his voice firm.

"Chloe, don't worry. I'm not giving up."

"I'll work hard, gather as many crystal cores as I can, and help you break through to Level Seven as fast as possible. Then you can fix my arm."

Chloe, hearing this, managed only a perfunctory, half-hearted smile, her eyes flat and unmoved, tinged with faint mockery.

She'd already made up her mind to cut him loose.

The wager against Falcon Squad was a total loss. Per the terms, the Scarlet Squad had to disband.

Now that everyone was wounded, she couldn't stand being around this team of dead weight for one more second.

In the cabin, the rest of the Scarlet Squad had all been poisoned by the miasma, bodies weak and aching, heads spinning with nausea, sprawled across the seats, groaning in low, pained voices, a pathetic scene.

Someone, scraping together the last of their strength, called out weakly to Chloe for help.

"Chloe, help us... we're poisoned, we can't take much more."

Chloe's expression stayed calm, her heart already cold and resolved to stand by and do nothing.

She went through the motions, checking each of them in turn, feigning a careful inspection before shaking her head with a show of reluctance, her voice full of a false apology.

"I'm sorry. This trumpet flower's toxin is too stubborn, too aggressive. My healing ability has its limits. There's nothing I can do."

In truth, with her current Level Six Healing Affinity, if she burned through everything she had, she could fully cure one of them.

But these people were gravely wounded, poisoned, their combat strength gone, no use to her anymore, nothing but dead weight on her path forward. She had no intention of spending herself on a bunch of has-beens.

Meanwhile, outside, the battle had long since settled.

Paisley cut a cold, striking figure across the field, Frostbloom Sword in hand, moving from spot to spot with clean, efficient strikes, cutting down one Level Seven mutant trumpet flower after another, utterly dominant the whole way through.

In a matter of moments, she'd wiped out all seven hidden trumpet flowers in the area, and seven dense, energy-rich Plant Affinity crystal cores had fallen into her hands.

Elsewhere, Falcon Squad worked in perfect coordination, clearing out every last zombie.

"Let's go rescue the hostages."

Elijah said, his voice low, gaze shifting toward the silo ahead.

"Pais, we're heading in to pull out the hostages. Take a break for a bit."
