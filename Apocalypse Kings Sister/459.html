Chapter 459

He could see Cole liked Paisley. Whenever anyone had those feelings and tried to get close to her, something in him rejected it on instinct.

Elijah put the cup in Paisley's hand, his movements gentle, his favoritism in his eyes unhidden. But toward Cole he was cool and distant.

"Captain Mercer, did you need something? Pais just used up a lot of psychic force and Affinity saving your teammate. She's worn out and needs quiet rest. She can't deal with socializing right now."

One sentence of protection, gentle but firm, and it quietly put space between them, cutting off any chance of getting closer.

Before Cole could speak, Elijah shifted, his tone turning businesslike.

"Also, saving a life is no small thing. She saved your teammate. Five thousand contribution points is fair, isn't it?"

Cole snapped out of it and quickly pulled in all his thoughts, nodding, apologetic and honest.

"Fair, completely fair! We're the ones getting a bargain."

He didn't dare delay. He tapped his wristband and transferred five thousand contribution points into Paisley's account.

Once the transfer went through, he caught the hidden protectiveness and distance in Elijah's eyes and knew exactly what was going on. He quickly dropped all other ideas, smiled awkwardly, murmured "Thanks, Ms. Lennox, thanks, Captain Blackwood," and walked off briskly, not bothering them again.

The quiet corner settled again.

Not far away, Orion, Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer leaned against the crates. They'd watched the whole thing, and exchanged knowing, amused looks. They lowered their voices and gossiped.

Ashley covered her mouth and whispered to Thomas, giggling.

"Wow, Captain's possessiveness is so obvious! The second Captain Mercer tried to get close to Paisley, Captain shut him down. Not a single chance."

Thomas smiled and nodded, whispering back. "Captain Blackwood never goes near girls and stays calm all the time. He doesn't go out of his way to go after people. But with Pais, it's always a double standard. He guards her as tight as it gets."

******

Orion watched the figure tenderly looking after her in the corner, and summed it up quietly.

"Haven't you noticed? With everyone else he's all business and restraint. Only with Paisley, there's always a special kind of favoritism, exceptions, and protectiveness."

"Anyone gets close to her, he shuts them out without a word. When she's tired, he notices right away."

"He's not even trying to hide it."

"As Pais's big brother, I see all of it. As long as he doesn't go too far, I won't say anything. But if he does go too far, I won't agree to it."

Spencer said, "Pais is so good. She deserves the best. Anyone who tries to hurt her, I won't let it slide."

Ashley said, "Right. Paisley's the prettiest person I've ever seen. She's strong, and she awakened healing. To me, nobody's good enough for her."

"Guys, stay away from my sister. Honestly, Captain Blackwood's aura is just too scary. I'm afraid of him."

There weren't many people on the team who weren't afraid of Elijah.

They all nodded. They'd seen it clearly.

Elijah's selfish feelings, his possessiveness, his unconditional favoritism, all of it belonged to Paisley alone.

Elijah ignored the gossiping eyes behind him. He just looked down tenderly at the girl in front of him and said quietly, full of fondness.

"Drink some water and get some rest. I'll take care of the rest."

******

Night fell.

Gray night light came through the fifth-floor windows and lit the corridor, the crates, and the empty storage floor in an eerie haze.

During those hours of rest, the sound of crawling lizards below never stopped. But its rhythm was slowing and its aggression visibly weakening, just as they'd predicted. The black-scale tide was entering its sluggish stretch in the second half of the night.

Beasts that were cold-blooded and sleepy had slower Affinity flow at night, dulled senses, and far less burst power than by day. It was the weakest point in their siege.

Both teams were fully rested.

The Lone Wolf wounded were stable. Fevers were gone, mutation risk was cleared, and they'd mostly recovered.

The Falcon Squad were healed and in good spirits.

Everyone checked their rifles and gear and got ready to fight.

No wasted words. Just looks and understanding.

Elijah stood at the front and spoke in a very low voice.

"As agreed. Falcon Squad takes the front and opens the way. Lone Wolf covers the flanks and cleans up stragglers. The moment we're out of the stairwell, grab the exit on the first-floor lobby. Don't linger."
