Chapter 469

The footsteps faded down the hall. Once she was sure nobody was outside, a quick, bright flicker of excitement lit Paisley's eyes.

First, restore my power.

She slipped into her Storage Dimension, drank a full bowl of spirit water, and split a big watermelon with Kitty and the fox. Satisfied, she came back out to the room.

Time to go find treasure.

She stood at the door and listened. The hotel was silent, apart from a few low voices from the night watch on the first floor.

She changed into a light, fitted dark jacket and tied her hair back. Brisk and sharp, she gave her order in her head.

Trovie, lock onto the exact location.

[Command received! Locking on...]

Trovie's voice came back soft and bouncy, thrilled at the find.

[The energy is super strong! It's on the lowest level! There's a hidden basement! Master, hurry!]

A smile tugged at Paisley's lips and spread to her eyes.

Perfect. Free loot.

She eased the door open and slipped down the dim hallway like a shadow, hugging the walls. She avoided every room where someone was resting and made her way to the quiet stairwell at the back.

The upper floors of the abandoned hotel were empty, nothing but dusty tables and scattered junk. The lower she went, the creepier it got.

At the corner by the basement storage room, Trovie spoke up fast.

[Master! This is it! There's a hidden mechanism in the floor tile!]

Paisley crouched. One tile looked different from the rest. Its edges were clean and neat, clearly added later, part of a hidden mechanism.

She pushed a thread of Plant energy into the tile, pressed down, and twisted.

Click.

The tile sank slowly with a low mechanical groan. A dark stairway opened below, and cold air rolled up.

The passage was deep and the steps ran straight down.

She didn't hesitate. She stepped in and went down the stairs.

The tunnel was short. A few dozen seconds later, the space opened up.

When she saw the whole basement, her eyes lit up, and she grinned from ear to ear, flooded with joy.

A huge hidden room lay before her, lined with rows of solid wood shelves stacked full.

The top shelves held box after box of crystal cores.

Level Three and Level Four cores in white, pale blue, and light green sat sorted and gleaming, full of power, a mountain of them, too many to count.

The middle shelves held stacks of thick gold bars, heavy and glittering under the dim light. She estimated at least 5,500 pounds.

On the bottom shelves, bundles of pristine pre-apocalypse cash sat bound tight and piled high.

She guessed at least five hundred million dollars.

Money buys a lot of supplies.

In the corners were jewelry, antiques, and guns with ammunition.

A mountain of treasure dazzled her eyes.

"So many crystal cores... So much gold!"

Paisley couldn't help a soft cry. Her eyes crinkled, her voice bright with delight.

I'm rich.

[Hehe! Master's rich now!]

Trovie bounced around in her mind, soft and thrilled.

[I told you there was a big treasure! This must be their secret stash! Who knew it would all end up with Master!]

Paisley hurried forward and brushed her fingertips over the cool, clear cores. They felt smooth, and the energy inside was pure.

She couldn't hide her smile. "This is a huge win. Level Three, Level Four, and some high-grade cores too. They must have grabbed them all on the side."

"The low-grade ones will trade for plenty of supplies."

"The high-grade ones can power my upgrades."

[Yes yes! Master's luck is the best!]

Trovie cheered wildly.

[Master, take it all! There are more supplies in the next room too.]

Paisley looked over the room full of treasure and felt her exhaustion vanish. Pure joy filled her.

She didn't hesitate.

Take it... take it all...
