Chapter 392

"Family? If we're family, why does she keep bullying me, stealing my supplies, humiliating me in public, over and over?"

"Letting it slide once is generosity. Letting it slide every single time, do you really think I'm that easy to push around? Don't blame me for not showing you any courtesy anymore."

Seeing the golden Affinity light flickering at Diane's palm, both Brenda and Grandpa Thompson's faces changed in an instant, disbelief written all over them.

Brenda's pupils shrank, her arrogance freezing solid, replaced by pure, stunned shock.

She stared hard at the dense golden light condensing in Diane's palm, her throat going tight, a tidal wave of panic rising inside her.

How was this possible?! Diane was nothing but a pushover housewife, completely helpless! This long into the apocalypse with no sign of change, how had she suddenly awakened an Affinity?!

Standing beside her, Paisley watched with a faint layer of cold frost settling over her gaze, her tone light and offhand, though every word hit like a blow.

"Not just awakened. Peak Level Four Metal Affinity."

She went on, twisting the knife.

"Nowhere near your half-baked Level Three Water Affinity. Jealous? Envious? Too bad. That's a level of depth you'll never reach, not in this lifetime."

The words struck Brenda right where it hurt.

She'd worked so hard to awaken her Water Affinity, had been riding high on her Level Three status, lording it over ordinary residents, her superiority complex through the roof, fully convinced she was a cut above everyone else.

And now, the very pushover she'd been freely bullying had turned around and crushed her completely.

The sheer gap snapped something inside her. Her face flushed red and white with rage, and she snarled.

"Nonsense! You just awakened out of nowhere, and so what if you're Level Four? All level and no skill! If we actually fought, you might not even win!"

"And you, Paisley! This is Thompson family business, it's not your place to butt in as a junior! Don't come here stirring trouble!"

She put on a bold front despite her unease, raising her hand to gather her Water Affinity.

Water surged and churned at her palm, fine, sharp water-blades spinning with a bitter chill, her usual go-to move for hurting and intimidating people.

Grandpa Thompson immediately jumped in to back her up, slamming his cane against the ground, his face full of partisan fury.

"Second daughter-in-law! You just awakened your Affinity and you think you can act tough in this house? Stand down now, or don't blame me for being harsh!"

From start to finish, he hadn't asked a single question about what had actually happened, hadn't said one word about Brenda's repeated bullying and theft, only kept blaming the wronged party, Diane.

In the past, Diane would have panicked and backed down without a word.

But now, with peak Level Four Metal Affinity at her command, her daughter standing behind her, and her whole family's confidence backing her up, she had no intention of swallowing this one, too.

Diane looked up, every last trace of her old mildness gone, leaving only sharp, unflinching resolve.

"Can't win? Then go ahead and try."

The moment the words left her mouth, golden light surged violently from her palm.

Compared to the cold, scattered sharpness of Water Affinity, Metal Affinity's power was heavy, overwhelming, and unstoppable.

Brilliant golden energy burst outward, condensing into a thick, solid golden shield, standing firm in front of her.

A sharp, crackling sound rang out.

The water-blade Brenda had thrown slammed into the golden shield, and instead of doing any damage at all, it shattered instantly, scattering into a fine mist of droplets that splashed across the ground.

Before Brenda could even process what had happened, Diane's wrist twisted. The golden shield dissolved in an instant, reforming into several razor-sharp blades of golden light.

The speed was staggering, the force brutal and overwhelming.

The blades of light shot out, grazing precisely across Brenda's arms and wrists, not aiming to kill, but slicing clean through her sleeves, shredding apart the protective layer of water-affinity energy around her skin.

"Ah!"

Brenda cried out in pain, a burning sting shooting through her arm, her Affinity energy thrown into chaos and collapsing on the spot. The force of the blow sent her stumbling backward several steps, slamming into the iron gate.

The Water Affinity she'd worked so hard to muster proved utterly fragile against Level Four Metal Affinity.

Level suppression. Attribute suppression. Complete dominance in skill.

Victory and defeat, decided in a single second.

Diane stood tall, golden light wreathing her body, her presence commanding and powerful, her tone cold and absolute.

"I let you off before out of generosity, not because I was weak."

"Cutting in line to steal my supplies, pushing and humiliating me in public, adding up to at least five or six times now, each one worse than the last."

"Brenda, what makes you think I'd keep tolerating you?"

Brenda, caught between pain and fear, went pale with humiliation and fury, but didn't dare lift a hand again.
