Chapter 90

The women who'd been kidnapped and abused scrambled back in terror and huddled in the corners. They stared at the bloody scene, horrified and shocked, shaking uncontrollably.

Just then, the heavyset, bald Level Four leader, who'd been drinking and partying the whole time, was finally stone-cold sober.

The drunken haze vanished from his eyes, replaced by rage and fear. Red flames blazed up around his body as his Fire Affinity erupted.

He raised his hand, and a roaring fireball formed in his palm. Scorching heat and explosive force poured off it as it shot through the air straight at Paisley!

The air rippled from the heat as it flew.

But the little white cat just flicked its tail and sent it flying right back.

The bald leader dove out of the way in terror. There was no telling how strong these two were.

Shit. We picked a fight with the wrong people.

He glared at Paisley, Elijah, and the cat, furious and scared at the same time.

"We've never done anything to you! Why the hell are you attacking us?"

Then, he thought better of it and softened his tone a little.

"Let my guys go, and we can talk this out!

"Whatever supplies you want, however many crystal cores, if I've got it, it's yours! Just take what you want and go. Let us live."

Paisley's voice was firm. "There's nothing to talk about.

"You're scum. You hurt people, treat human lives like garbage, and prey on anyone weaker than you. You should've died a long time ago.

"Consider this justice."

The bald leader had been secretly gathering power the whole time. When he heard that, he figured there was nothing left to say.

He summoned his Fire Affinity again and hurled another fireball at her.

The scorching fireball was almost on her, and energy had just started to stir at Paisley's fingertips, when the little white cat suddenly leaped out of her arms.

In midair, its snow-white body grew into a 16-foot beast. Its fur was coated in a layer of biting frost, and its Ice Affinity pressure exploded outward. The extreme cold froze everything around it solid.

A frosty glint lit up its eyes, and it unleashed the full force of its freezing power.

Unbelievably, the blazing fireball, which had been throwing off waves of heat a moment before, froze solid in midair. The red flames hardened and went out, and it dropped to the floor as a cold lump of ice.

The chill spread through the whole building at once.

Every raider still writhing and twitching inside, including the shocked, horrified Level Four leader, was encased in ice.

In the blink of an eye, all thirty-some men had been frozen into ice statues.

Not one of them could make another sound.

The whole room went dead silent, and the cold cut to the bone.

The little white cat lifted a paw and gave a light swipe, and its Ice Affinity exploded a second time.

Sharp cracks rang out one after another as every ice statue in the room shattered. They broke apart into a fine spray of ice chips and frozen bits of flesh, and then they were gone.

All that was left on the floor were the crystal cores, every one of them intact and full of energy.

The little white cat darted around so fast it was just a blur, picking up every core in its mouth one by one. It carried them over to Paisley, set the whole pile down in front of her, and waited patiently to be praised.

In just a few seconds, what should've been a long, brutal fight was over, just like that.

Elijah had been standing off to the side, and the fight was over before he could even lift a finger. He was stunned.

He'd thought he already had a pretty good idea of how strong Paisley was.

But after seeing this, he realized he'd still seriously underestimated how terrifying Paisley and her cat really were.

They hadn't needed anyone's help at all.

Even what Paisley had said earlier had been way too modest.

This wasn't a fight. It was a total beatdown.

With a Level Four leader and a bunch of Level Two and Level Three ability users, this gang was a serious threat anywhere in the region. But against one girl and her cat, they hadn't stood a chance. They'd been wiped out in the blink of an eye.

Elijah stared at Paisley and the cat in disbelief. His head was spinning, and it took him a long time to pull himself together.

Just then, Trovie's voice rang out in Paisley's head, bursting with excitement.

[Treasure, Master! I can smell gold! Tons and tons of it!]

The little white cat had shrunk back down and curled up in her arms again. Paisley gently stroked it, then looked up at Elijah, who still hadn't recovered. She pointed at the group of terrified, shaking women.

"I need to go find something. I'll leave things here to you."

Before Elijah could even answer, she headed off toward the rooms on the second floor with the little white cat in her arms, following Trovie's directions.
