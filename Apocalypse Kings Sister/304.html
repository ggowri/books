Chapter 304

"Of course, you won't be going in alone."

"The base will send its full elite force alongside you — you'd just be lending support. And on top of that, I'll pay out ten thousand contribution points per person once the mission's done."

Elijah's gaze sharpened.

"And if we refuse?"

The room went tight for a second.

The director's smile didn't move, but his tone hardened underneath it, every word deliberate.

"This is the only condition under which I send you home."

The silence stretched a beat too long before a clear, even voice broke it.

"We accept."

Paisley's eyes were calm, her expression untroubled, as she took on the mission without a flicker of hesitation.

The director glanced at her, something dismissive flashing through his eyes before he could stop it.

The girl in front of him was striking — pale skin, delicate features, the kind of beauty that looked completely out of place in a ruined world. In this new order of things, that kind of face without the power to back it up was usually a liability, not an asset.

He hadn't given her a second thought. His attention the whole meeting had been on Elijah, the one who clearly held the reins.

He hadn't expected her to be the one who spoke first — not in a negotiation this important, with this much riding on it.

What surprised him more was what happened next. The instant Paisley finished speaking, Elijah nodded along without a trace of hesitation, backing her completely.

"We'll take the mission."

"Let us know your departure time and we'll be ready."

"Sooner is better. We'd like to leave as soon as possible."

The director studied the two of them, how naturally they moved in sync, and filed it away behind his smile.

"Very well."

From what he'd heard, the Blackwood heir wasn't the type to lose his head over a pretty face. He and his grandfather had both been untouchable names in the military before the world fell apart.

So who was this girl, really?

He needed to know. Anything out of place had to be accounted for.

He turned toward the door and gave the order.

"Get our guests settled in the villa district immediately."

"Full accommodations — top tier, nothing withheld. Spirit water, crystal cores, whatever they need, make sure it's there."

The aide waiting outside bowed his head.

"Understood, sir."

It looked like hospitality. It was anything but. The smiling fox's usual move — win people over with generosity, keep them comfortable, and at the same time keep them somewhere he could watch every move they made.

Elijah saw through it instantly, but said nothing, accepting the arrangement without complaint.

They all needed the rest anyway, and a private villa, well-secured and comfortable, saved them the trouble of finding somewhere safer themselves.

The director walked them to the door, his tone smooth as ever.

"You've all had a long road. Rest up."

"I'll have the lab intel pulled together and the strike team assembled. We'll have a departure date within three days."

"This mission's a dangerous one. Make sure you're all in good shape before you go."

Elijah gave a short nod.

Paisley said nothing more, the little red fox nuzzling her palm, content and unbothered.

Her expression gave nothing away. No one could have read what she was thinking.

Without another word, the group followed the aide out of the room.

The moment the door closed behind them, the warmth drained out of the director's face. His eyes turned cold and distant, every trace of the mild, approachable man gone.

He tapped a finger against the desk, voice low.

"Find out everything about that girl. What's her connection to Elijah Blackwood?"

"Who is she, really?"

"For him to defer to someone like that, she can't be ordinary."

If she hadn't spoken up, he'd never have even known she existed until now.

Someone who could sway Elijah Blackwood's decisions wasn't nobody.

The aide answered quietly.

"Understood. I'll pull the entry records and dig into their backgrounds."
