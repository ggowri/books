Chapter 145

The princess was trembling with excitement and gratitude. Without a moment's hesitation, she waved her guards forward, and they pushed all five heavy crates of gold and jewels up to the screen.

"Send them over right now."

The gold bars inside were stacked in neat rows and gleamed brightly, and the gemstone jewelry was rich and flawless. Five whole crates of treasure were almost too dazzling to look at.

A moment later, five crates came through the realm channel and landed solidly on the open ground in Paisley's dimension, glowing gold and full of energy.

Trovie happily zipped around the crates of gold, its little body shaking with excitement. Its sweet voice cheered nonstop.

[So much gold! Master's rich! This gold is super pure, and it's going to turn into a ton of points!]

Paisley looked at her big reward and smiled a little. With a thought, she immediately sent over ten big crates of corn, sweet potatoes, and wheat.

"Princess, just plant these the way I showed you. I promise it'll solve your food problem for good."

On the other side of the screen, the princess looked down at the heavy, plump seeds in her hands. Her fingers trembled, and she was beaming.

These seeds could save her whole kingdom. They were the last hope for countless people.

She sank into a deep, formal curtsy.

"Words can't thank you enough. I'll never forget this as long as I live. I'll start the planting right away, and someday, I'll repay you properly."

As soon as she finished, the princess ended the call, and the screen went dark.

A second later, Trovie's chime went off in Paisley's head right on cue.

[Trade with the ancient kingdom complete! Bonus: 1,000 points!

[Gold from this trade automatically converted. 50,000 points added!

[Current total: 153,060 points!]

After the announcement, Trovie started matching realms on its own and quickly locked onto the next regular trading partner.

[Connecting to the Matriarch of the Beast Realm... Connection successful!]

The screen lit up again, and the Matriarch appeared.

She was wearing the full set of winter gear she'd traded from Paisley before. She had on a thick down jacket, a windproof scarf and gloves, and sturdy, non-slip boots.

Even through the screen, Paisley could tell how bitterly cold it was there. The plants around the Matriarch were bare and withered, and the wind howled. It was the dead of winter, the coldest time of year in the Beast Realm.

The Matriarch's eyes lit up as she stared at the screen, and she sounded eager.

[Do you have any more of those winter clothes you gave me? I'll take as many as you've got! Your clothes are way warmer, and my tribe really needs them to get through the winter!]

Paisley's interest stirred, and she scanned her huge storage area with her mind.

Over the past few days, she'd stockpiled mountains of supplies. She had every kind of warm clothing and everyday item she could think of, more than enough to go around.

She sounded calm and sure. [We can trade. What are you offering?]

The Matriarch stepped aside right away, showing the huge piles of goods behind her.

Five huge wooden crates were packed with sparkling crystal gems in every color, red, green, blue, and purple. They shimmered with light, and every single one was full of pure, rich energy.

Next to the crates stood herds of sturdy mutant cattle, sheep, and chickens. They were plump and healthy, and each one glowed faintly with energy. These high-energy mutant animals could only be found in the Beast Realm.

[For days, I've had my whole tribe digging and gathering day and night just so we could trade with you.] The Matriarch sounded sincere and hopeful.

Paisley looked at the screen full of crystal gems and high-energy mutant animals, and her eyes sparkled. Excitement rushed through her.

The energy crystals could quickly strengthen abilities and purify energy, and the mutant livestock would make perfect power food for leveling up and building strength. They were worth a fortune.

She was going to make a killing on this trade.

With a flick of her finger, a solid, pure gold bar floated in front of the screen, shining in the light.

[If your tribe can find gold like this, I'll take all of it. There's no limit.]

The Beast Realm probably didn't use gold for trading yet, but that didn't mean they didn't have any.

Her total points were still too low.

She still needed a lot more points to become a Level Four cross-dimensional trader.

And once I'm a Level Five trader, Trovie can finally have a real body. That's going to be so awesome!
