Chapter 445

And now Paisley alone had killed it.

It was really dead!

"Paisley... she really killed that monster by herself?"

Ashley's voice shook. Her eyes were full of awe, and the fear from the serpent's pressure had given way to sheer shock.

Thomas watched Paisley's slender, upright figure, his throat working. He couldn't speak.

"Pais's fighting power is insane," he murmured in wonder.

Orion let out a long breath. The tension drained out of his back, leaving a trace of fear but mostly relief and wild joy.

They'd gone from the edge of wipeout to the monster's death in a few breaths, and his heart still pounded from the swing.

Everyone had expected to break out wounded, or die here. Nobody had thought Paisley would rewrite the ending alone.

Shock, joy, relief, awe. All of it mixed in their chests and washed away the pain and exhaustion, lighting a fresh fire in their spent bodies.

With the serpent mutant dead, its mind control vanished.

The lesser serpent mutants, the wall of zombies, and the low-level beasts closing in from every side stopped dead, like kites with cut strings.

The red, bloodthirsty look in their eyes faded. Their frenzy broke. Without a single command or target, they fell into chaos.

Some stood dazed. Some ran in every direction. Some crashed into each other. The tight ring that had crushed the squad fell apart.

They could finally breathe.

"The serpent's dead and the horde's out of control! Everyone, hit them!"

Elijah forced down the pain in his chest and gave the order in a low, hard voice, charging in first.

The others snapped out of it. The old despair was gone. Each of them pulled out the bottle of spirit water they carried, drained it, and refilled their Affinity. Their power came back at once, and they went on the attack.

Without the serpent's control, the zombies and mutant beasts were much weaker.

The cat and the fox held the flanks, ice blades sweeping and fire clearing the way, as if competing to see which could kill more.

Spencer's fire burned down the zombies running in every direction.

Orion's metal blades flew and cut down the lesser serpent mutants as they fled.

Thomas's vines bound and blocked their escape routes.

Ashley's rock walls sealed the roads and trapped the stragglers.

...

The battle turned completely.

Half an hour of slaughter.

The lesser serpent mutants, the zombies, and the mutant beasts were all wiped out.

The biggest threat in the ruins, together with all its minions, was gone for good.

When it ended, everyone breathed out. Wounds throbbing, Affinities drained to the limit, they sat down together to rest.

Then footsteps rang out beyond the broken walls.

Whoosh!

Over a dozen strangers closed in around them. All wore black tactical vests, with fierce, hard eyes and high-powered rifles loaded, the black muzzles aimed exactly at every member of the Falcon Squad. Killing intent hung in the air, and every way out was cut off.

They were roaming Affinity mercenaries.

The leader had a heavy face and cruel, greedy eyes. He looked down at the wounded, apparently exhausted squad, his tone bullying, openly planning to rob them.

"Hand over all your crystal cores and every bit of your supplies."

His gaze landed on the white cat and the golden fox beside Paisley, and the greed in his eyes grew.

"The big white cat and the fox stay too."

A henchman next to him rubbed his hands, his eyes roving over Paisley, Ashley, and Madison with filthy intent, grinning in a way that turned the stomach.

"Boss, these three beauties stay here to keep us company. A face like that is rare in the apocalypse. I've never seen anything this good..."

The vile, insulting words floated over the ruins like a taunt.

The Falcon Squad exchanged looks, all of them stunned, disgusted, and cold with anger.
