Chapter 157

Row after row of upright fridges and chillers still hummed along, holding steady temperatures.

Fresh fruit and leafy greens sat inside, plump and vivid, with none of the withered rot so common to the apocalypse. They looked like they'd come straight off a modern shelf.

Most surprising of all — unlike every other ruined store she'd seen — this one had a full seafood section, intact.

A massive chiller ran cold, packed with seafood far bigger than normal.

Fat lobsters, shells glossy red. Crabs with claws still raised. Scallops, oysters, fresh fish — none of it spoiled, all of it somehow still gleaming with freshness, the kind that made your mouth water on sight.

A flicker of delight crossed Paisley's eyes. A find like this didn't come twice.

Since she'd found it, she wasn't leaving it behind.

She cleared the last few zombies with a quick sweep, then let her will reach out, and everything vanished into her Storage Dimension at once.

Shelf after shelf of rice, oil, snacks, dry goods — cleared in an instant.

Cabinets of fresh produce, dairy, drinks — cabinet and contents both, swallowed whole.

The entire seafood section — lobster, crab, every last piece — gone the same way.

Take it. Take it. Take it.

She moved fast, fluid, every item within sight disappearing one after another.

To avoid raising suspicion, she left a few scattered items behind — ordinary goods, a handful of snacks — enough to make the place still look like an abandoned supermarket, nothing out of place.

In just minutes, most of the supermarket's stock had vanished into her Storage Dimension.

Trovie spun through the air in excitement, its chirpy little voice nonstop.

[Host! So much stuff! We're rich, we're rich!]

Kitty sat nearby, clutching a storage token in her teeth, wobbling over to rub against Paisley's ankle, head bobbing, clearly fishing for praise.

Paisley looked down at her, charmed, and reached down to ruffle her fluffy head, about to head for the second floor.

Just then, Trovie's voice piped up again, thrilled.

[Host! The basement's the warehouse! I smell gold! There's treasure down there!]

Paisley changed course immediately, heading down a hidden stairwell toward the basement.

The basement entrance was dark and damp, thick with mildew and dust. Somewhere in the silence, slow footsteps dragged closer.

A Level Four mutant zombie emerged — once a warehouse worker, now hulking and stiff, skin gray and rotted. It caught her scent and lunged with a shriek.

Paisley didn't flinch. One flick of the sword, and its head fell clean off.

Black blood sprayed. The body dropped.

She pulled a Fire Affinity crystal core from the corpse — full, warm, pure.

Past the body, the true scale of the basement opened up.

A vast space, packed floor to ceiling with neatly stacked crates — every kind of supply, stockpiled and waiting. A hidden treasure room, plain and simple.

Paisley didn't hesitate. She opened her storage and started pulling.

Take it! Crates of compressed rations, backup water — all gone.

Take it! Mounds of daily goods, emergency supplies — gone.

Take it! Tools, spare parts, everything — not a single piece left behind.

As the basement emptied fast, Trovie's excitement spiked.

[Host! Up ahead! There's an office in the back — gold! So much gold! I can smell the treasure!]

Paisley's gaze sharpened. She followed the signal to a private office tucked deep in the basement.

The closer she got, the heavier the dread in the air grew, pressing down like something solid.

Dozens of Level Four mutant zombies crowded the door, eyes glowing red, movements fast and coordinated — long past the mindless stage, showing real instinct and teamwork.

Inside the office sat something far worse. A hulking Level Five mutant wolf-man, fur thick, claws gleaming, an aura of violence rolling off it in waves.

The mission target. A Level Five mutant wolf-man.

Clearly, this thing ruled the mall's entire zombie population and had made the hidden room its own treasure vault.
