Chapter 157

Rows of upright fridges and refrigerated cases were still intact and running, keeping everything cold and fresh.

Fresh fruit and leafy greens sat inside, plump and vivid, with none of the withered rot so common in the apocalypse. They looked just as fresh as anything from before the world ended.

Best of all, unlike every other abandoned store, this supermarket actually had a whole fresh seafood section.

Its huge refrigerated display was still running cold, packed with all kinds of seafood far bigger than usual.

There were fat, glossy red lobsters and crabs with their claws raised, along with scallops, oysters, and fresh fish. None of it had spoiled, and it all looked so fresh and sweet that anyone's mouth would water.

Paisley's eyes lit up. Food like this was a once-in-a-lifetime find, and now that she'd come across it, she wasn't about to pass it up.

She quickly finished off the last few zombies, then waved her hand. With a single thought, she swept everything into her Storage Dimension.

Rows of flour, oats, cooking oil, instant food, snacks, seasonings, and dry goods vanished, leaving whole shelves empty in seconds.

Several big cases full of fresh fruit, vegetables, eggs, dairy, and drinks went in too, cases and all.

The fat lobsters, crabs, and every other kind of fresh seafood in the section went in as well.

Mine. Mine. All mine...

She moved fast and smoothly, sweeping up every supply she laid eyes on.

To keep things from looking too obvious, she deliberately left a few ordinary household items and some snacks scattered around. That way, the place looked like any other abandoned supermarket, and nobody would get suspicious.

In no time at all, most of the huge supermarket's stock had vanished into her Storage Dimension.

Trovie spun excitedly through the air, its sweet little voice chirping nonstop.

[Master! So much stuff! We're rich, we're rich!]

The little white cat sat nearby with a storage token in its mouth. It waddled over to rub against Paisley's ankle, its little head bobbing as it fished for praise.

Paisley looked down at the cute little thing and smiled softly. She reached down to ruffle its fluffy head and was about to head up to the second floor.

Just then, Trovie's sweet voice piped up again, full of excitement.

[Master! The basement is the mall's warehouse! I smell gold, and there's tons of treasure down there!]

Paisley changed course immediately, heading down a hidden stairwell toward the basement.

The basement entrance was dark and damp, thick with the smell of rot and dust. Somewhere in the silence, slow footsteps dragged closer.

A Level Four mutant zombie came at her. It had once been the mall's warehouse manager, and now it was huge and stiff, its skin gray and rotting. The second it sensed a living person, it let out a roar and lunged.

Paisley stayed calm. She swung her sword, and the cold blade sliced clean through its neck.

With a wet squelch, black blood sprayed out, and the zombie hit the floor.

She dug out its Level Four Fire crystal core. It was big and flawless, warm in her hand and full of pure energy.

Once she stepped past the body, she could finally see the whole basement.

It was huge and packed with neatly stacked crates of every kind of supply, including plenty of rare stock. It was a real hidden treasure trove.

Paisley didn't hesitate. With a thought, she opened her Storage Dimension and started sweeping everything in.

First, the crates of compressed rations and backup water disappeared. Next, the piles of household goods and emergency supplies went in. Then, every tool, piece of equipment, and spare part followed, down to the last one.

Trovie watched the basement storeroom empty out so fast and got even more excited.

[Master, the secret office in the very back has gold, so much gold! I can smell tons of treasure!]

Paisley's eyes narrowed, and she hurried toward the private office at the far end of the basement.

The closer she got to the secret room, the heavier the dark, savage presence in the air grew, until it was hard to breathe.

Dozens of Level Four mutant zombies crowded the door. Their eyes glowed red, and they moved fast. They were way past the dazed, clumsy stage of low-level zombies, and they'd started to think for themselves and work together.

A hulking Level Five mutant werewolf, the target of this mission, guarded the office door. It had thick fur and gleaming claws, and savage energy rolled off it in waves.

Clearly, the werewolf was the boss of this place. It ruled every mutant creature in the mall and guarded the treasure in the secret room.
