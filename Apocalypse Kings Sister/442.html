Chapter 442

"Crash!!"

The giant tail came down, and the rock shattered and collapsed in a spray of stone chips.

The layered barrier blew apart. It couldn't stop a peak strike.

The leftover force punched through and hit Elijah on the shoulder. His armor buckled and cracked, and he was thrown again. His injuries worsened, and his breath turned thin and unsteady.

Madison's eyes tightened. She tried to help, but the serpent's pressure caught her. She dropped to her knees, coughed up blood, and passed out.

Things were going badly for them.

Everyone finally understood the gap. After one night of evolving, the peak Level Seven serpent mutant was far stronger than yesterday's.

Ordinary methods couldn't touch it.

If this dragged on, the whole team would die here.

In the shadows, Paisley's eyes turned sharp. She held nothing back.

She unleashed all her Affinity and gathered her strongest psychic strike. The serpent's movements slowed.

The moment the Frostbloom Sword left its sheath, its cold light split the dawn. Its icy aura froze the air around it.

No flashy moves. She went straight for the core.

The serpent mutant sensed a deadly threat. It abandoned Elijah and locked its slit pupils on Paisley as she appeared in midair. Its icy snarl rang across the sky.

"You again, you insect! You ruined it for me again!"

"Today you die."

It clearly hated her more now, since she was the one who could hurt it.

Its human hand twisted into a black poison claw, cold and gleaming, and met the sword head-on. At the same moment its tail bent in an eerie arc and struck from below. The two attacks pinned her from above and below, sealing every path of retreat.

Clang, clang, clang!

Metal rang in rapid bursts, sparks flying.

Paisley dodged and wove with perfect footwork. Her blade struck the old wound again and forced through the surface scales.

Dark green blood sprayed out and ate small holes in the ground.

But the Level Seven peak's healing had evolved again.

The gash had barely opened when it squirmed shut, healing at an almost creepy speed.

And the serpent was ready this time. It deliberately tensed its muscles and stacked its hard scales over its vital spot. No matter how sharp her sword was, one cut wouldn't get through its defense and reach its core.

The psychic strike she'd launched earlier, aimed straight at its mind, had done real damage. A blow like that could cripple a high-level Affinity user. But it held the mutant for only five seconds.

After five seconds, its dazed eyes turned cold and sharp again. Its speed, agility, and burst power came back to their peak.

Its body twisted like an eel, supple and eerie. Even at thirty-three feet long it moved gracefully, flowing out of every attack. No matter how fast Paisley's sword was, or how tricky the angle, she couldn't find another opening to hurt it again.

Worse, it wanted to trap and kill the whole team in these ruins.

It threw back its head and let out a shrill, piercing, unnatural scream!

The roar shook the land, tore through broken walls, and carried a strange mind-controlling force that swept the whole zone.

A few seconds later, over a dozen lesser serpent mutants of different sizes burst from alleys, collapsed buildings, and tall grass on every side. Each had jet-black skin, poison, and a hunger for blood, with power far beyond ordinary Level Six zombies.

Right behind them, a flood of low-level zombies and mutant beasts poured out and blanketed the plains in black. Their eyes were red and wild, as if an invisible hand controlled them, and they surged toward the wounded Falcon Squad in a crushing ring.

The situation got worse again. Now they were truly trapped.

Everyone on the squad was hurt. Elijah's chest wound hadn't healed. Thomas, Orion, and Madison were all nursing backlash injuries, and their combat strength was down to less than a third.

And if a zombie or mutant beast bit or scratched them, the poison would flood their bodies at once, and they'd turn into zombies in no time.

Killing intent filled the sky, and they were on the edge of ruin.

At the last moment, Paisley's eyes went cold and steady.

With a thought, the little white cat appeared and grew into a giant white cat sixteen feet tall. Beside it, a golden fox suddenly swelled in size, its tails fanned out, its aura shimmering.

"Protect them," Paisley ordered.

Her cool voice was barely out when the two companions streaked forward like flashes of light and planted themselves in front of the squad.
