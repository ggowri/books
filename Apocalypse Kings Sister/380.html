Chapter 380

One of the elders spoke up, laying out the family's core plan.

"We have to do whatever it takes to win Pais back to the Lennox family, to repair her relationship with us."

"If she comes back to us, if her heart belongs to the Lennox family, we can ride her momentum and rise to become the most powerful faction at this base, crushing the Quinn family, outpacing every old established faction."

"Fortunately, her household registration is still with the Lennox family."

At the words, the patriarch nodded slowly, a trace of relief in his eyes.

"Fortunately, her registration's still with us. By name, she's still the Lennox family's rightful eldest daughter. That's never changed."

The statement settled everyone's nerves completely.

As long as the registration tie held, as long as her name was still on the books, they still had countless chances to make amends, to win her back.

Just then, a middle-aged relative beside him frowned slightly and spoke up.

"The plan's solid, but who's the right person to approach her?"

"Pais is cool and reserved, seemingly detached, but she's extremely strong-willed, neither soft nor hard tactics will move her. Sending someone in carelessly will only backfire."

Everyone fell into thought at that.

Paisley had already cut ties with the Lennox family; the distance between her and everyone here ran deep. Anyone they sent would come across as forced, calculated.

Someone else spoke up, right on cue.

"Did Pais's adoptive parents come today?"

"They raised her for over a decade. They're the ones in this world who know her best, the ones most likely to get her to let her guard down. Having them step forward would be the best move by far."

The words landed like a wake-up call.

Everyone's eyes lit up at once, nodding in agreement.

An outsider's flattery was transparent self-interest. The warmth of adoptive parents was a soft spot, having them step in beat any of these distant blood relatives forcing their way into her life, by a hundred times over.

After a brief discussion weighing the pros and cons, the Lennox family settled on their plan.

Paisley's adoptive parents would go in person, playing the emotional card, no talk of power or benefit, only blood ties and amends owed.

Inside the Lennox family's grand, opulent hall, the lights blazed bright, filling the room with splendor.

Paisley's adoptive father and mother stood side by side in the hall, tense, their brows creased with anxiety, utterly at a loss.

They knew, deep down, that this task looked simple but was nearly impossible.

All these years as her adoptive parents, they'd never once truly cared for or cherished Paisley, never been there for her growing up, never stood up for her.

They'd even silently allowed Rachel to bully her. From start to finish, they hadn't been decent parents to her, not by any measure.

Now that Paisley had risen so dramatically, so brilliantly, trying to pull her back with family sentiment felt utterly hopeless, they had no confidence at all that they could convince someone this strong-willed, this emotionally distant, to come back to the Lennox family.

At the head of the hall, the patriarch's gaze was grave as he looked at the two of them, visibly anxious, his tone solemn and weighty.

"The two of you need to come in with the lowest, most humble attitude possible."

"On this visit, don't rush her to come back to the family. Be sincere. Make amends slowly."

"Warm her heart first, then win her over. Meet sincerity with sincerity, given enough time, even the coldest heart will thaw."

Paisley's adoptive father and mother could only nod along, over and over.

Everything they had now came from the main family.

If they so much as said no, everything they currently had could be stripped away at any moment.

Right now, with Owen poisoned and still unconscious, Rachel without any Affinity, and Ryan an ordinary person with no powers at all.

If they wanted to keep living this comfortable life at the base, they had no choice but to do as they were told.

Paisley's adoptive mother thought of Owen, still unconscious after all this time.

"Family head, may I ask a favor?"

"My eldest son is poisoned and still hasn't woken up. I've heard Vivian is a Level Six Healing Affinity user, could she please save my son?"

Vivian sat to the lower right of the head seat, the second-highest position in the room, after the patriarch himself.

That alone showed how much the family valued her.

Vivian nodded.

"I can. I'll go with you in a bit."

Meanwhile, in a different, far warmer part of the base, a cozy home buzzed with the smells and warmth of a real kitchen, a world apart from the calculating atmosphere at the Lennox estate.

Night had fallen, the house glowing with soft, warm light, a table loaded with hearty home cooking, steam rising, washing away all the exhaustion of a day spent fighting in the apocalypse.

Paisley sat at the table, calmly enjoying dinner.

She'd changed into loungewear, shedding the battlefield's sharp edge entirely. Her features had relaxed in the warm light, taking on a softer, more girlish ease.

Suddenly, she sneezed several times in a row.
