Chapter 27

"Rachel's a pro at playing the victim. In front of everyone, she's all sweet and innocent, but behind my back, she's always setting me up and trashing my name.

"She made everyone think I was an ungrateful, spoiled brat who picked on her.

"Owen, Reid, and Ryan were even worse. They'd always say it was for my own good, or for the family, and then make me back down and do whatever they wanted.

"Just yesterday, Rachel set me up again so she could steal my pendant. I'd finally had enough, so I cut ties with all of them for good.

"But I never thought they'd keep chasing after me like this."

Paisley's face went cold, and her voice turned sarcastic. "If I had to guess, they were planning to hand me over to Jasper.

"Otherwise, he'd never have dared to look at me like that, like I was a piece of meat.

"They figured I had nobody, so they could push me around and hand me over like a gift."

She'd lived through all of it before, in her past life. But this time, she'd seen them for what they really were, and there was no way she'd let them push her around again.

The car went dead silent. Everyone was seething.

Orion went ice-cold. Every bit of warmth left his eyes, and the air in the car got so heavy it was scary.

He'd never imagined that Paisley had spent over a decade being pushed around, used, and lied about by a family that treated her like dirt.

"Those animals." His voice was rough and cold, and he sounded ready to kill.

"Don't worry, Pais. As long as I'm around, no one's ever going to hurt you again. I've got your back."

Spencer's fists were clenched so tight his veins popped. He wanted to turn the car around right then and there and beat the hell out of the Lennoxes.

Their sweet little Paisley had been living in hell all these years! Did those people really think the Thompsons would just sit back and let outsiders push their family around?

In the front seat, Madison's eyes had welled up. She reached back and gently took Paisley's hand.

"Don't be sad, Pais. You've got us now, and nobody's ever going to hurt you again. I'll always be here for you."

Paisley stayed calm in the face of all their concern, but her mind was made up.

"Thanks, everyone," she said coolly. "But I'm getting my revenge myself."

The Lennoxes owed her for a lot more than a decade of abuse and lies.

In her past life, they'd betrayed her, offered her up, hunted her down, and shoved her into a horde of zombies to die. That was a blood debt, and she'd make them pay it back a hundred times over.

Paisley stared out at the empty streets flying past, feeling nothing but cold.

This time, they didn't have her pendant or a doormat to take the blame and clean up their messes. She couldn't wait to see how far those selfish hypocrites would get. Would they still climb to the top as easily as they had in her past life?

Up front, Orion didn't say a word, but he was making plans of his own.

If Paisley wanted to handle her revenge herself, he'd respect that and stay out of it.

But beating the crap out of them to blow off some steam? That had to be fine. Otherwise, he'd never be able to let it go.

He kept a cold eye on the cars behind them in the rearview mirror. He was already planning exactly how hard he'd hit those spineless suck-ups.

Just then, the SUV ahead of them came to a stop.

Thomas got out first and walked over to them. "Orion, Paisley, Elijah says we're stopping here to rest and eat lunch before we head out.

"There's a big hospital just up ahead. It should have medical equipment and a pharmacy, so we can stock up on meds."

Nobody had a problem with that.

For Paisley, it was the perfect chance to absorb that Level Two plant core and level up her Plant Affinity.

She turned to Orion, sounding a little tired. "I'm kind of beat. Mind if I rest in the car for a bit?"
