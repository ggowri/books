Chapter 265

It should never have picked this fight.

All it wanted now was to run.

Paisley gave it no such chance. She shifted Elijah behind her, Ice-Water and Plant Affinities braiding together, cold blue light over deep green, and turned to face the zombie king head-on.

"Your turn."

Her voice stayed flat and even, but it carried the weight of a verdict already decided.

Vines burst up again, thicker and more savage than the ones before, weaving into a cage dense enough to catch the fleeing zombie king completely.

It roared in fury, throwing its own rotted vines against the net, trying to tear free.

But its power was nothing against her source-level Plant Affinity.

Fsssh—!

Where the vines clashed, its rotten strands withered and crumbled to black ash under hers.

Before it could struggle again, her Ice-Water Affinity crashed down.

Absolute cold froze the air itself, frost spreading along the vines until the zombie king was completely encased.

Crack. Crack.

Layer after layer of ice sealed the once-fearsome creature in place, frozen solid, suspended, helpless.

The cold didn't just hold it still. It shut down its Affinity entirely, cutting off any chance of fighting back or healing.

Panic and disbelief filled its eyes, a broken growl scraping out of its throat, unable to move even a finger.

How could one small, delicate human hold two top-tier Affinities and crush it this completely?

Paisley's eyes stayed flat as she stepped forward, gathering the purest thread of parasitic energy at her fingertips.

With something this cunning and intelligent, the only way was the direct one. Kill it, and be done.

The thread slipped through the ice, boring into its skull, straight toward its massive, pristine core.

"Parasitize. Extract."

Four flat words. Finished.

The zombie king convulsed once, then went still.

A soft thud, and the Level Six core came free.

Its body crumbled to nothing under the weight of ice and vine, disintegrating into the air.

In moments, a zombie strong enough to wipe out an entire team was simply gone.

The store fell silent again, nothing left but wreckage and broken vines.

Paisley drew her power back, the vines retreating, the cold fading to a faint chill in the air.

She picked up the core, then helped Elijah, pale and weak, over to a nearby couch.

He stared at her, stunned, unable to find words.

She'd crushed a Level Six completely out of her weight class, switching between two Affinities like it was nothing.

She'd been holding back this whole time. She's so much stronger than I ever realized.

If she hadn't come when she did, I'd be dead. Nothing left of me at all.

She'd saved his life again.

Something warm and bittersweet tangled together in his chest.

He opened his mouth to thank her, but suddenly stiffened, a cold wave of sickness washing through his body.

Dark, foul blood began seeping from the wounds on his back and shoulder, carrying the zombie virus's distinct taint.

Dizziness crashed over him, his limbs going slack, every ounce of strength draining out at once.

His vision went black, and he passed out.

Paisley didn't hesitate.

A soft green glow gathered at her fingertips, her Healing Affinity flowing into his damaged body.

The light spread through the wound, stitching it back together.
