Chapter 413

He was slouched on a sofa, bored, when his eyes drifted toward the door. The second he saw Paisley, he lit up, dropped his date, and hurried over, his face full of delight and eagerness to please.

The cocky swagger was gone. He was almost fawning.

"Paisley! What a coincidence, running into you here!"

Before she could speak, he brought up what he'd done, bursting with pride.

"Brenda was harassing people, throwing her weight around, and taking supplies. I had it handled personally."

"I didn't leave her an ounce of slack. She's locked up in the Security Office. Figured it'd get some payback for you and Aunt Diane."

He was bending over backward, hoping for her approval and a way to get closer.

Paisley lowered her eyes. A hint of a smile crossed her cool face.

"Good job."

She looked up at him. "Next time you run into something you can't handle, a mission that's too tough, I'll make an exception and help you once."

Those few words carried real weight.

With her strength now crushing high-level Affinity users, one favor from her was something countless people on the base would give anything for.

Jasper lit up. The effort of the last few days had paid off.

He had been a little nervous, afraid he'd done something wrong. Now, with her promise in hand, he felt light, and all the stiffness and worry fell away.

He dropped what was left of his lazy, spoiled act, straightened his back, and became even more respectful, his eyes shining.

"That's perfect! I've been waiting for you to say that."

"Whatever you need, no matter how much trouble, I'm there. No excuses."

He pushed harder, laying it on thick. "From now on, anyone who gives you a hard time or talks behind your back, I'll be the first to shut them down. I'll always have your back."

He meant every word. His eyes sparkled. He looked like a kid trying to impress someone, nothing like the careless Quinn heir he used to be.

His date, abandoned halfway through, stood stiff with a dress in her hand, her face red with fury.

From start to finish, Jasper hadn't looked at her. He had eyes for one person.

She had never seen him grovel like that.

Everything else had turned into background.

Why?

She was the girlfriend.

Who was this shameless woman, daring to steal him right in front of her?

But she couldn't offend Jasper Quinn. She had to swallow it.

Elijah hadn't said a word.

He stood quietly beside Paisley, his expression mild and unreadable, like he was watching from a distance, calm and untouched.

Only he knew the sourness already spreading inside him, quiet and thin, through every limb.

His gaze rested on Jasper's overly cheerful face, and something faintly cold passed through his dark eyes, too fast to notice.

Jasper's closeness with Paisley was grating.

When she promised to help Jasper once, the jealousy sharpened and settled heavy in his chest.

Everyone on the base knew her help was priceless, something big names would fight each other for. And she'd given it to someone else without a second thought.

Elijah's fingers curled slightly, his palm tightening as he held his feelings in.

He was steady by nature and never showed what he felt. Even jealous, he wouldn't sulk like some kid. It would just turn into a quiet possessiveness, marking where he stood.

He was unhappy.

If he'd known Jasper would be here, he wouldn't have come.

******

Jasper noticed nothing of the cold pressure beside him and kept pouring out his loyalty.

"Paisley, are you shopping too?"

"I know this mall like the back of my hand. Which stores have good stock, which ones are worth the price, I know it all."

"Want me to come with you? I'll show you around and carry your bags!"
