Chapter 450

Sparkling crystal cores of every level, brand-new rifles and ammunition, ration bars, clean water, all kinds of emergency supplies and medical gear, all of it covered the ground.

She quickly divided every crystal core, gun, scrap of food, and supply evenly.

Some of the team felt guilty and tried to refuse.

Paisley shut them up with one sentence.

"We're one team. We split it evenly."

"Or do you not see me as a teammate?"

Elijah said, "All right. Since Pais is giving it to us, everyone take it. Let's rest a bit and refill our strength, then leave here."

Everyone sat down, and Paisley took ten simple hot boxed lunches from her space and passed them around.

The little white cat and the fox got theirs too, and ate happily.

After the brutal fight, warm food settled in their stomachs, the exhaustion eased, and their strength slowly came back.

When lunch was done, the mood slowly turned heavy.

Orion's face grew more serious, and he broke the silence first.

"Those mercenaries were off."

He went back over their gear, tactics, and presence, and said with certainty, "They weren't ordinary roaming mercenaries. Matching equipment, well trained, working in sync, vicious, and very focused. They were professional hitmen. Someone hired them to wait for us here."

The mood in the room tightened.

Elijah looked down at the crystal core in his palm. The tenderness drained from his eyes and a heavy chill took its place. His voice was low and certain.

"They came for me."

Everyone looked up, startled.

"It has to be the Quinns."

He said it word by word, his voice icy, a flash of cold, murderous light crossing his eyes.

"The Quinn faction has always feared what the Blackwood family holds, the resources and the authority. They've tripped us up in secret more than once. This time they hired high-level killers to lie in wait in the Exclusion Zone, hoping to get rid of me."

"If I'm dead, we're no threat to them."

Orion's frown deepened, his face grim as he added, "If they'd use a professional hit team, there won't be just one wave. The Quinns have deep roots and ruthless methods. Every second we stay here is more danger."

He looked around and quickly set the plan.

"Everyone rest fast, then we all pull out of the ruins at once. We can't stay here. After this, there'll probably be ambush after ambush waiting for us."

A heavy mood settled over the team. They all knew how vicious the Quinns were. Follow-up ambushes would pile up, impossible to defend against, and heading back the way they came was very risky.

******

While everyone braced themselves, Paisley thought it over for a moment, then looked up and spoke calmly.

"If there are probably more ambushes on the way back, we shouldn't walk into their trap. Let's change course on purpose and take a detour through Larkspur."

She laid out the pros and cons, step by step.

"Larkspur isn't like these shattered ruins. It used to be a booming city, with huge supply reserves. After the apocalypse began, it likely still holds a lot of supplies nobody's looted. We can go around that way, collect plenty of supplies, restock the team, and then head back to the base."

The suggestion killed two birds with one stone. They'd avoid the ambush and restock, solving their problem completely.

Everyone's eyes lit up, and nobody objected.

After a string of near disasters, with the original road full of danger, a detour through Larkspur was clearly the safest choice.

"No problem, we'll follow Pais!"

"Going through Larkspur is way safer, and we can loot on the way. Two birds, one stone!"

Everyone nodded and agreed with the decision.

Elijah nodded too. "All right, we'll do as Pais says. Pack up, and we leave now. First stop, Larkspur."

With the captain's order, everyone got moving.

Fully geared up, they set out again.

The SUV's engine growled, breaking the stillness of the ruins. The wheels rolled over gravel and dust, turned around smoothly, and sped west toward Larkspur.

Outside the windows, the landscape raced by, broken walls slowly giving way to bare open land.
