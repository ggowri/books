Chapter 155

Paisley looked over the group, her face serious. "Everyone, stay safe. There are a lot of mutant zombies lurking in here, so pair up and don't take any crazy risks."

As she spoke, her fingers twitched, and several small, cool silver storage tokens appeared in her palm. They were round, simple, and unassuming, but each one held a hidden pocket of space.

She handed one to each of them. "These are storage tokens. Each one holds 100 square feet of private storage, so you can keep the supplies and crystal cores you collect today in them."

Everyone looked down, holding the tokens carefully in their palms, curious and stunned.

Ashley ran a finger over the cool metal, wide-eyed.

"Storage tokens are actually real?! I've only ever seen them in sci-fi novels, and I thought they were just made up!"

Spencer stared at the tiny thing in his palm, amazed. "A 100-square-foot storage space, packed into something the size of a button. That's insane."

One after another, they marveled at the tokens, buzzing with excitement.

Paisley cleared her throat, her cheeks a little warm, secretly relieved.

She only had a whole box of them because of that huge trade with the S-tier interstellar female last night. Otherwise, she'd never have been willing to hand out such rare Galactic treasures.

More importantly, the tokens were the perfect cover for her Storage Dimension. From now on, she'd have a good excuse whenever she stored or pulled out supplies, and she wouldn't have to worry about anyone finding out.

Paisley pushed those thoughts aside and carefully explained how they worked. "There are two ways to use them.

"First, you can just press the little button on the left side to open it and store or take things out. It's quick and easy, but it can't hold anything living, and nothing inside stays fresh, so food goes bad fast. Plus, someone could easily steal it from you.

"Second, you can bond it with your blood. Put a drop of blood from your fingertip on it, and it'll be locked to you, so nobody else can use it.

"Once it's bonded, storing and taking things out gets even easier, and it unlocks a preservation function. It still can't hold anything living, though."

By the time she finished, everyone was in total shock, their faces lit up with disbelief and pure joy.

Everyone understood exactly what this meant. In a world where carrying supplies was a constant struggle, a stable, private storage space was priceless.

Before, they'd had to haul supplies by hand or pile them in the cars, which was a pain, and a lot of it got lost or spoiled on the road. Now, these tokens solved every one of those problems. A hundred square feet was plenty of room for anyone's personal things and food.

Ashley clutched hers, visibly moved. "Paisley, you're so generous! You actually gave every one of us one of these rare treasures!"

Marcus looked down at the token in his palm, more certain than ever. Paisley had more resources and luck than anyone could imagine, and that only made him more determined to get close to her and protect her.

Thomas and Spencer were deeply touched. The tiny tokens felt heavy in their hands, full of Paisley's trust, and they admired her even more.

Madison and Mason held the cool little tokens in their palms, surprised and thrilled. Warmth flooded their chests, and both silently made up their minds.

If Paisley was willing to share something this rare, she clearly saw them as her own people. No matter what, they'd find a way to pay her back someday, and they'd never betray her trust.

Madison was the first to look up, sounding a little guilty but serious. "This is way too valuable, and we don't deserve it. How can we ever pay you back?"

After all, nothing came free in the apocalypse.

Mason quickly agreed, his face sincere. "Yeah, Ms. Lennox. We can't keep taking advantage of you without giving anything back."

Ashley, Spencer, and Thomas stopped smiling too and looked at Paisley seriously, awkward and guilty.

"Paisley, this is way too rare. We don't have anything worth trading you for it."

They all knew they still owed her 200 Level Two crystal cores for that super nourishing sacred fungus chicken soup last night. Taking a treasure like this on top of that made them feel ashamed.

They all looked anxious and guilty, yet they couldn't let go of their tokens. When Paisley saw that, she finally spoke up and set the rules.
