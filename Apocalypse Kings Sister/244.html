Chapter 244

She waved a hand, and a strawberry tree heavy with plump red fruit dropped onto the ground, its ripe Affinity scent spreading through the air.

"Everyone burned through a lot of Affinity and energy back there. Grab a strawberry. It'll help you recover fast."

She picked a plump one for herself first and took a small bite.

Kitty hugged one too, crouched on the ground, nibbling happily.

The sweetness melted on the tongue, juicy and full, never cloying. Just clean, bright fruit flavor.

******

Warmth followed close behind, a rich energy flowing down the throat and into every limb, refilling the strength the fight had drained.

What surprised her more was feeling her mental sea itself expand, just a little, nourished by the same energy.

These strawberries really were top-tier treasures in this ruined world.

No wonder a single one was worth thirty points, on par with a crystal core, valuable as gold.

Ashley finished hers first, eyes lighting up as she turned to Paisley.

"Paisley, these are amazing! They taste incredible, and they already refilled the Affinity I burned through. I feel so much lighter!"

Thomas, finishing his own, felt the same surge and spoke thoughtfully.

"This is a rare find, alright. Wonder if eating these regularly could help us break through, the way high-tier crystal cores do."

The others nodded along, curious and hopeful, all wondering the same thing. If these could help with leveling up, they'd be worth more than almost anything else out here.

Paisley, seeing their hopeful faces, laid it out plainly.

"I took the whole patch. Two hundred trees, almost five thousand ripe strawberries."

"There are nine of us. That's close to five hundred each."

She asked what they'd prefer.

"Do you want it all handed out now, or should I hold onto it and give you some every day?"

"My Storage Dimension keeps things fresh no matter how long they sit. They'll come out exactly like they are now, no energy lost."

Elijah, pushing down everything still churning in his head, spoke up.

"Paisley's right. Everyone should think this through before deciding."

Then he made his own choice first.

"I'll leave mine with you. One a day is plenty to keep my Affinity topped up."

"Out here, carrying supplies on me just means more ways to lose them or have them ruined. Better they stay with you."

With Elijah leading, the rest thought it over for a moment and made the same call.

Safe, fresh, and secure in Paisley's dimension. Far better than anything they could manage carrying it themselves.

Paisley nodded, easy about it.

"Works for me. I'll give everyone ten now, so you can try them out and see if eating more actually helps with leveling up."

"After that, one after lunch and one after dinner, to keep your Affinity topped off."

She waved her hand, and five more strawberry trees dropped neatly to the ground, branches swaying, fruit scenting the air.

"Go ahead and pick your own. Ten each. Keep them somewhere safe."

With that settled, she looked around at the group.

"Break's over. Let's get moving. We want somewhere safe before dark, before the mutant beast hordes start up."

They all agreed at once and gathered around the trees, picking in orderly turns.

Each strawberry, plump and red as a small apple, was picked with care and tucked away like treasure.

In no time, everyone was done.

With a small motion, Paisley pulled the five stripped trees back into her dimension.

A moment later, two olive-green SUVs materialized on the clearing.

Their metal frames caught the dappled light with a cold gleam.

By now they were used to her Storage Affinity, but it still took their breath away every time they saw it in action.
