Chapter 244

Paisley waved her hand lightly, and a strawberry plant loaded with plump red berries appeared out of thin air on the ground. The fruit was fresh and red, and its rich energy spread through the air at once.

"Everyone used up a lot of power and energy in that fight. Grab a strawberry to recover fast."

As soon as she finished, Paisley picked a big strawberry first and took small, slow bites.

The little white cat hugged a round, plump strawberry too and crouched on the ground, happily nibbling away.

The sweet, soft fruit melted on Paisley's tongue, juicy and full but not too sweet. Its pure, fresh flavor was so refreshing.

Then, a rich, warm energy flowed down her throat and spread through her whole body, and the power and strength she'd used up in the fight came rushing back.

What surprised Paisley even more was that she could clearly feel her psychic reserves quietly expanding a little, fed by that energy.

These mutant energy strawberries really were top-tier treasures in the apocalypse, and they were incredibly valuable.

No wonder a single one was worth 30 points, as much as a crystal core. They were worth their weight in gold.

Ashley finished hers first, and her face lit up as she looked at Paisley in delight. "Paisley, these strawberries are amazing!

"They taste super good and perk me right up, and they refilled all the power I used up. My body feels so much lighter!"

Thomas finished one too and felt the power filling him up, and he spoke thoughtfully. "These really are rare treasures.

"Could eating them regularly help us break through and level up, the way absorbing high-level crystal cores does?"

The others nodded along, curious and hopeful, all wondering the exact same thing. If these strawberries could help them level up, they'd be a precious chance to grow stronger in the apocalypse.

Paisley saw how hopeful everyone looked and explained openly. "I put the whole strawberry patch in my Storage Dimension, 200 strawberry plants and nearly 5,000 ripe strawberries.

"There are nine of us, so that's about 500 strawberries each."

Then, she asked what they wanted. "Do you want me to split them all up now, or should I keep them for you and hand them out every day?

"My Storage Dimension keeps things fresh. No matter how long they're stored, they'll come out as fresh as if they'd just been picked, without losing any energy at all."

When Elijah heard that, he pushed down all the thoughts swirling in his head and spoke up. "Paisley's right. Everyone should think it over carefully before deciding."

Then, he made his choice first. "I'll leave mine in your Storage Dimension. One a day is enough for me to keep my power topped up.

"Out in the wild, things get damaged or lost easily, and they're a pain to carry around, so it's better not to keep them on me."

With Elijah taking the lead, the others thought it over for a moment and all made the exact same choice.

Keeping them in Paisley's Storage Dimension was safe, kept them fresh, and was a hundred times more reliable than carrying them around.

Paisley nodded slightly and agreed right away. "Sure. For now, I'll give everyone 10 so you can try them out and see if eating more helps your powers break through and level up.

"From now on, eat one after lunch and one after dinner every day to make up for the power you use."

With that, she waved her hand, and five strawberry plants loaded with bright red fruit landed neatly on the ground. Their leaves swayed, and the sweet smell of fruit filled the air.

"Go ahead and pick them yourselves, 10 each, and put them somewhere safe when you're done."

Once she'd explained everything, she looked around at the group. "Break's over, and we need to get moving. Let's try to find somewhere safe to stay before dark and avoid the mutant beast hordes that come out at night."

Everyone answered right away and, without wasting any time, gathered around the strawberry plants to pick in an orderly way.

One by one, they carefully picked the plump, rosy mutant strawberries, each as big as an apple, and put them away like they'd found treasure.

In no time at all, everyone was done picking.

With a flick of her fingertips, Paisley put the five picked-clean strawberry plants back into her Storage Dimension.

A second later, two army-green SUVs appeared out of thin air on the clearing.

The cars' bodies gave off a cold, metallic gleam in the light and shadows of the woods.

Everyone was used to her space power by now, but they still couldn't help being amazed. Every time they saw it with their own eyes, it stunned them all over again.
