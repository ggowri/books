Chapter 84

"I used to be an idiot. I gave you everything I had, and all I got back was pain and betrayal.

"But I've woken up. Whatever mess you're in and however hurt you are, you brought it on yourselves. It has nothing to do with me.

"You want me to forget everything and humbly nurse you back to health?"

Paisley's eyes turned sharp, and her voice went cold. "Dream on.

"I'm not making you pay for everything you did in the past, and that's already more mercy than you deserve. Don't push your luck by coming after me again."

Her words were blunt, cold, and brutal. She didn't hold back one bit, and she crushed the brothers' smug sense of superiority completely.

"Besides, I already found a family that actually treats me right.

"I'm not some orphan you can push around and take back whenever you feel like it anymore."

Reid and Ryan's faces went white, then red. They were humiliated and furious, but they couldn't come up with a single word to say back.

They looked at Paisley, cold and distant and out of their reach. For the first time, it truly hit them. The naive girl who'd let them push her around and walk all over her was gone for good, and she was never coming back.

You brought this on yourselves. Don't worry, this is only the beginning.

She'd take her time making them pay for everything they'd done to her in her past life.

The tension was at its peak, and the two brothers were drowning in humiliation and regret. That was when Rachel forced down her jealousy and bitterness and hurried over, playing the sweet, kind peacemaker.

She was calculating her next move, but she put on an understanding, forgiving act and spoke in a soft, coaxing voice.

"Pais, please don't be so angry.

"Reid and Ryan really know they messed up. They've always cared about you deep down, so just forgive them this once.

"Say what you want, but you all grew up together. We're the closest family you've got.

"You can't just cut us off because you found your birth family.

"Don't worry. I'll take good care of all three of them. I just want you to come back so you can fix things with them."

Ashley had just finished making lunch, and she came walking over.

She rolled her eyes, clearly fed up, and stepped right in front of Paisley to shield her. Then, she looked Rachel dead in the eye and didn't bother being polite.

"God, you talk way too much. Can you just shut up? You're so loud that you're giving me a headache."

Once she'd shut down Rachel's fake act, Ashley's whole face changed. When she turned to Paisley, her eyes crinkled into a sweet, adorable smile, and her voice went soft.

"Paisley, just ignore them. Lunch is ready, so let's go eat."

Paisley nodded and gently ruffled Ashley's hair. It felt soft and fluffy under her fingers.

All the coldness in her eyes melted away, and only a soft warmth was left.

Paisley didn't even glance at the three Lennox siblings again. Those selfish hypocrites weren't worth her time. She turned and followed Ashley over to where Elijah and the others were resting.

They'd come fully prepared for the trip, with pots, pans, and every cooking tool they could need. Even out in the middle of nowhere, everyone could still sit down to a hot meal.

Lunch today was simple, just a side of greens and a big pot of egg soup. There wasn't any meat, but with supplies so tight in the apocalypse, a hot meal was already a luxury.

Rachel's group, on the other hand, hadn't brought a single pot or pan.

All they could do was dig some stale, rock-hard bread and cold water out of their bags and choke it down.

They watched the others sitting together not far away, digging into their hot food. They could only breathe in the smell, their mouths watering.

Jasper looked impatient and irritated, and he snapped at them coldly.

"Look at those guys over there. They brought everything, every pot and pan they could need, and they get a hot meal wherever they go. And then look at you."

His voice got even colder, dripping with disgust.

"How the hell did I get stuck dragging a bunch of idiots around? You're worse than useless.

"When we stop at the next city, every one of you better get pots, pans, and cooking supplies.

"I want a hot meal every day too. I'm sick of chewing on rock-hard rations and drinking cold water."

He glared at the four pale, beat-up Lennox siblings and warned them.

"And one more thing. From now on, nobody goes off on their own without my permission."
