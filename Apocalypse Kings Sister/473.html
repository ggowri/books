Chapter 473

"Thomas, grow vines first for the base framework and stabilize both ends. Orion, build the metal frame for the deck. Ashley, last, use your Earth Affinity to gather soil into stone, fill the gaps, and level the surface."

The three took their places at once, clear on their roles, and started.

Paisley stood behind them, looking at the bridge, her expression tight.

A bridge this solid doesn't just happen to collapse right now. It isn't an accident.

Someone doesn't want us getting back.

I knew the way home wouldn't be easy.

This bridge is probably only the start.

Thomas went first, and his Plant Affinity swept out.

Thick, tough dark green vines burst out of the ground, crossing and tangling, gripping the two broken ends and forming a dense net bridge. The collapse stabilized, and a solid base took shape.

Then Orion released his Metal Affinity.

Metal shavings from the road and wreckage rose and gathered. Heat shaped them and they stretched into thick, hard alloy beams. They snapped into the gap and built a neat, sturdy metal deck frame, layer by layer.

Last, Ashley spread out her Earth Affinity.

Rubble, fine soil, and concrete dust flew to her, tumbling and packing down.

Heavy layers of stone filled the gaps in the frame and hardened in an instant, leveling the uneven steel and vines into a smooth, solid surface.

The three worked together like water flowing. The repairs went fast, and the broken bridge was rebuilt piece by piece.

The thousand-yard span was almost done. Everyone was about to relax.

Then the river below exploded.

Boom!

A deafening splash tore the air.

A massive high-tier mutant shark, its body covered in gray-black scales and a dorsal fin sharp as a blade, burst out of the water in a spray of foul water.

Its eyes were blood red, its fury enormous. Its giant body, loaded with raw power, slammed into the half-finished new bridge.

Crack, crash!

The vine net snapped. The alloy beams twisted and burst. The deck, just hardened and leveled, collapsed into pieces.

Everything the three had burned their power on was ruined in an instant.

River water surged and debris fell. The scene was chaos.

Everyone's face changed. Their hearts sank.

Then, on the far bank, a few figures stepped into view, lazy, smug, mocking. They had clearly been waiting here a long time.

At the front was Grant, his right arm now a metal prosthetic. Chloe stood beside him, along with some teammates and several Quinn family core members who were clearly no pushovers.

They watched Falcon Squad stuck on the wrong side, and their eyes glittered with the cruel pleasure of a plan that had worked.

Grant slid his hands into his pockets and spoke lazily, with an insincere, teasing edge.

"Hey. What a coincidence, running into you here."

He glanced at the wrecked bridge, and his smile turned ice cold.

"The bridge is out like this, and a high-tier beast guards the river. No way you make it back to Aetheris Base today."

"Miss the deadline, and the mission is a failure."

His voice turned sharp, full of menace.

"And even if you could, while we're here, you're not setting one foot on that bridge."

Chloe took a step forward, eyes locked on Paisley, brimming with grudge, envy, and arrogance. A sharp, cruel smile curved her mouth.

She raised her voice on purpose, every word a taunt.

"Paisley Lennox, didn't see this coming, did you?"

"You used to be so full of yourself, remember? So strong. Always one step ahead of me. Always the star."

"When you used your connections to throw me and my mom in the Security Office, did you ever think the tables could turn?"

She was gleeful, vicious, drunk on revenge.

"You think you're so strong?"

"Let's see how you get across now. Let's see who protects you."

Her sneers carried on the wind, full of open malice, pressing down on the group.

Orion's face went cold. He stepped forward, Metal power stirring around him, and spoke with protective fury.
