Chapter 81

Reid tried to dodge in a panic, but the serpent's scales scraped his arm and sliced open a deep gash. Cold venom rushed into the wound, and his whole arm went numb and stiff until he couldn't feel it at all.

A Level Two ability and a couple of regular guns were nothing against a Level Four mutant beast. To that thing, they were just bugs, and there was no way they could get away.

Death was hanging right over their heads, and all they felt was pure terror.

Just as the giant serpent gathered all its strength to strike and swallow the three of them whole, a ruthless, selfish look flashed across Rachel's face.

Without a second's hesitation, she grabbed Reid and Ryan and yanked them in front of her as human shields!

Two agonized screams rang out at the same time.

The brothers never saw it coming. The serpent's fangs tore across their shoulders, and venom seeped into their flesh. Their wounds split wide open, and agony ripped through their bodies.

Rachel turned and ran without a backward glance. She didn't care at all whether the two of them lived or died, and she sprinted out of the forest as fast as she could.

Reid and Ryan lay on the ground, badly hurt, covered in cuts and blood, and unable to move. As the venom spread, their arms and legs slowly went stiff and numb. They were barely breathing and could die any second.

They watched Rachel run off without a shred of mercy, and they were consumed by despair and burning hatred.

When the serpent wasn't looking, they forced themselves to hold on. They fought through the pain, scraped together the last of their strength, and crawled and stumbled their way out.

All three of them made it out alive, but every one of them was hurt, some worse than others.

The next second, the air rippled slightly. Paisley slowly appeared out of nowhere with the white cat in her arms, perfectly calm and relaxed.

She looked up at the Level Four mutant serpent, still coiled in the old tree and still furious. Their eyes locked, and the air turned cold.

Before the angry serpent could even react, light flashed at Paisley's fingertips, and her pure, powerful Plant Affinity exploded out of her.

Countless thin, tough vines glowing with a faint emerald light burst out of the ground and shot through the air. Fast as lightning, they wrapped around the serpent's huge body, its neck, and the vital spot just behind its head, cutting off any chance to twist, strike back, or run.

In one move, the vines tightened all at once, and a blast of fierce plant energy drove straight through the serpent's weak spot.

The serpent's huge body went rigid. The poisonous mist around it cleared away, and the light in its blood-red eyes faded. It had no fight left in it.

Paisley didn't waste any time. She strolled over, and a sharp, gleaming knife appeared in her hand. Then, she bent down and carefully cut into the serpent's skull.

A moment later, a clear, deep green Level Four crystal core of perfect quality dropped right into her palm.

The giant serpent crashed to the ground. It twitched a few times, then went completely still.

[Master!] Trovie's voice suddenly rang out in her head, bursting with excitement.

[Every part of this Level Four mutant serpent is worth something! The scales, the fangs, the venom, the meat, it's all top-tier stuff, and you can trade all of it for tons of points in the shop! We just hit the jackpot!]

With a single thought, Paisley swept the whole serpent into her Storage Dimension.

Right after that, Trovie let out another excited squeal.

[Master! Look at the sacred fungi on the ground! They're top-tier treasures, and they're worth a fortune!]

Only then did Paisley look at the two fungi glowing with energy. [These aren't for sale. I'm keeping them for myself.]

Trovie sighed. Then, it suddenly sensed a faint pulse of energy deep in the treetop and cried out again.

[Master! There's something up in that old tree! I'm picking up an energy signal!]

Paisley looked up at the towering treetop. She pushed off lightly with her toes, leaped up in one smooth motion, and landed on a thick branch.

Tucked in the crook of the branches was a huge serpent's nest. Inside sat three eggs the size of basketballs. Their shells were smooth and glossy, with faint streaks of light swirling across them, and they were packed with rich, pure energy.

Paisley asked quietly, [Can you eat these?]

Trovie explained right away.

[Master, each egg has a whole living creature inside, and they're full of energy! You can eat them, sure. But if you hatch them, you'll get loyal pets of your own, and that's worth way more!]

Paisley smiled and made up her mind. [Then I'll leave these three eggs to you, Trovie. Hatch them for me.]

With a single thought, all three eggs disappeared into her Storage Dimension.
