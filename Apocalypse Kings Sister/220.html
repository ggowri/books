Chapter 220

[Secret main mission at the aquarium complete! Reward: 5,000 points, one premium lucky draw!]

[Current total points: 363,060!]

Paisley blinked, mildly surprised.

[That counts as finishing the mission?]

Trovie's voice came back, soft and sweet.

[Host, the core objective was locating the lab's classified files and precision equipment. Everything's already in your Storage Dimension, so the system registered it as a full completion.]

[Want to use the draw now, Host?]

Paisley thought she heard someone calling her name. She pushed down the urge to draw right then and answered, calm and steady.

[Later. Let me regroup with my brother first.]

The words had barely left her when Trovie's voice came back, suddenly serious.

[Host, completing this mission has triggered a hidden main quest: locate every secret research base in the country, recover all scattered experiment files and equipment, uncover the truth behind the apocalypse, and eliminate the root cause of the engineered mutation crisis.]

******

Trovie:

[Mission completion reward: 300,000 points, plus three treasure chests.]

Something tightened in Paisley's chest. She frowned, asking silently.

[Three hundred thousand points? That's not a normal difficulty level, is it? A standard aquarium mission only pays five thousand, and that comes with three chests on top?]

Trovie answered right away, obedient and honest.

[Mm-hm. This hidden main quest is rated 2S difficulty. Tasks at this tier run long, cover massive ground, and carry huge uncertainty. You might succeed, Host, or you might never finish it at all.]

Paisley: [...]

She had nothing to say to that. The weight of it settled in fast.

If a five-thousand-point mission had been this brutal, a 2S-rated quest worth three hundred thousand had to involve something far bigger, far darker, on an entirely different scale of danger.

Just the thought that this apocalypse might be a man-made disaster, not some natural catastrophe, left a heaviness sitting in her chest.

She shook off the thought, pushed everything down, turned, and walked quickly out of the vault, pulling the door shut behind her.

As she rounded the corner, she spotted Orion in the distance, brow furrowed, scanning the area for her.

The second he'd lost sight of her, his chest had gone tight.

This place was crawling with danger, the magnetic field was scrambled, signals were dead. Getting separated down here could get someone killed.

No matter how strong his little sister was, in his mind she was still just a girl.

He hadn't stopped to think about anything else. He'd just gone looking for her.

The moment he spotted her familiar, composed figure, the tension drained out of him. He closed the distance fast, his voice carrying a worry he couldn't quite hide.

"Paisley, where'd you go? I looked away for one second and you were gone. You scared me."

"It's too dangerous down here. Don't wander off."

Paisley's eyes curved into a faint smile.

"Got it, Orion. Whatever you say."

What exactly did her brother think her strength actually looked like?

Still, being cared for like this, every single moment, wasn't a bad feeling at all.

Orion had no reason to doubt her. He stepped in close, shielding her side, and murmured:

"Just don't wander off alone next time, okay? This place is too complicated, too dangerous."

Not far off, Elijah turned at the sound of their voices. Seeing Paisley back safe, he didn't press for details, just called out, voice firm:

"Everyone, regroup!"

The scattered search teams filed back in, all of them empty-handed, faces heavy with disappointment.

"Captain, we searched everywhere. No sign of the equipment or the files."

"Maybe they're on another floor."

Elijah's expression didn't change. He gave a slight nod.

"I'll report this up the chain. It's getting dark, and pushing deeper means running into more unknowns. Since we've swept the area, everyone prepare to pull out. We're heading back to base."

The group let out a collective breath of relief at that.

Every mutant in this place had been cleared out, the immediate danger gone.

But the whole tunnel still felt wrong, cold and heavy with something that hadn't fully lifted.

With night falling, no one could promise there wasn't something else waiting in the dark.

After a full day of danger, everyone was running on empty. Right now, all they wanted was to stay alive.

The elevator ride back to the surface went smoothly.
