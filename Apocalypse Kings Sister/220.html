Chapter 220

[Aquarium secret main quest complete! Rewards: 5,000 points and one premium lucky draw!

[Current total: 373,060 points.]

Paisley was a little stunned.

[That counts as finishing the mission?]

Trovie answered in its sweet, cute voice.

[Master, the main goal of this mission was finding the lab's classified files and precision equipment. They're all stored in your Storage Dimension now, so the system marked the mission as complete.

[Master, do you want to do the draw now?]

Paisley's eyes flickered. She thought she heard someone calling her, so she pushed down the urge to draw right away and answered calmly.

[Later. Let me meet up with Orion and the others first.]

The moment she finished, Trovie's voice rang out again, unusually serious.

[Master, mission completion detected. A hidden main quest has been activated: find every secret lab base in the country, recover all the scattered experiment files and precision equipment, uncover the truth behind the apocalypse, and wipe out the man-made mutation threat for good.

[Quest completion reward: 300,000 points, plus three treasure chests.]

Paisley's heart jolted, and she raised an eyebrow, asking silently.

[Three hundred thousand points? This quest must be ridiculously hard, right? A regular aquarium mission only gave 5,000 points, and this one comes with three treasure chests too?]

Trovie answered obediently and told her the truth.

[Mm-hm. This hidden main quest is rated 2S difficulty. Quests this hard take a really long time, cover a huge area, and have a ton of unknowns, so you might succeed, Master, or you might never finish it.]

Paisley was speechless for a moment, and then it all clicked.

A 5,000-point mission had already been this hard. A 2S main quest worth 300,000 points had to involve something much bigger and much harder, with a conspiracy and dangers on a whole different level.

Just thinking that the apocalypse might be man-made instead of a natural disaster made Paisley's heart feel heavy.

She quickly pushed down her racing thoughts, turned, and hurried out of the storeroom, closing the door behind her.

As soon as she came back around the corner, she spotted Orion in the distance. He was frowning and looking all around for her.

A minute ago, Paisley had vanished from sight, and Orion's heart had clenched.

Danger lurked everywhere underground, the magnetic field was a mess, and the signals were blocked. Getting separated down here was extremely dangerous.

No matter how strong his sister was, to him she was still just a girl.

He didn't care about anything else and searched everywhere for her.

When he finally spotted her familiar figure, the tension drained out of him, and he hurried over, his voice full of a worry and gentleness he couldn't quite hide. "Paisley, where did you go just now? You disappeared the second I looked away, and you scared me to death.

"It's still too dangerous down here, so don't wander off."

Paisley gave him a faint smile. "Okay, Orion. Whatever you say."

Did Orion have any idea how strong she really was?

Still, it felt pretty nice having someone care about her this much, all the time.

Orion didn't suspect a thing and stepped half a pace closer on instinct to shield her, warning her quietly. "Remember, don't go off alone next time. The layout down here's too complicated, and it isn't safe."

Not far away, Elijah turned at the sound. When he saw Paisley back safe and sound, he didn't ask any questions and just called out in a deep voice, "Everyone, regroup!"

The team members who'd scattered to search came back right away. Every one of them was empty-handed, and they all looked a little disappointed.

"Captain Blackwood, we searched everywhere and couldn't find the equipment or the files."

"Maybe they're on another floor."

Elijah's expression didn't change, and he gave a slight nod.

"I'll report this to the head of the base. It's already dark, and if we keep searching, we'll probably run into more unknown dangers. We've covered this area, so everyone get ready to pull out and head back to base."

When Elijah said that, everyone let out a sigh of relief.

Every mutant here had been killed, and the danger was gone for now. But the whole underground area was still eerie and freezing, and the sense of menace hadn't faded. On top of that, night was falling, and no one could promise there wasn't some other unknown danger out there tonight.

After a whole day of danger, everyone was exhausted, body and mind. Right now, all they wanted was to stay alive.

The elevator ride back up to the surface went especially smoothly.
