Chapter 99

"I had this dream once, and it felt really real.

"In the dream, Marcus creates a cure for the zombie virus three years from now. It saves millions of survivors and ends the whole apocalypse. He's humanity's only hope.

"Even if there's only a one-in-a-million chance that it's true, how could I let someone that important die? If I'd left him there, he never would've made it to Aetheris alive."

Elijah's hands froze on the wheel. All the bitterness and jealousy vanished from his face, replaced by pure shock.

His expression turned grave, and his mind reeled from the bombshell she'd just dropped.

She'd called it a dream.

But he knew her, and after everything he'd seen her do lately, he was sure it wasn't just a dream!

He stared at her. His voice shook slightly, and he was dead serious.

"That's the only reason you're set on bringing him?"

After a short pause, he pushed down the storm raging inside him and asked again, his voice low. Every word was heavy with disbelief.

"He's a medical researcher? He's really going to make a cure for the zombie virus?"

In that moment, all he felt was shock.

A cure.

In an endless apocalypse with zombies everywhere, that word meant the kind of salvation nobody even dared to hope for. It was a light that seemed impossibly far away.

He'd never imagined that Marcus, who looked so soft and delicate, was carrying something that huge on his shoulders.

Would humanity really get to see the light again someday?

So Paisley wasn't into Marcus at all.

She wasn't being softhearted either.

Everything Paisley did had a reason behind it.

Paisley nodded like it was obvious, the lollipop still in her mouth. "What else would it be?

"It's not like I'm some saint who saves every random person, right?"

Trovie suddenly piped up. [Master, he likes you!]

Paisley didn't believe it.

[You don't mean Elijah, do you? How could a clueless guy like him ever like me? Stop making stuff up.]

After all, in her past life, he'd never gotten married, right up until the day he died.

A big shot like him only cared about the greater good.

Trovie wouldn't let it go. [My instincts are never wrong. Trust the system's intuition!]

Before Paisley could say anything else, all the cars up ahead stopped.

A heavy, ominous feeling washed over them, and everyone's chest tightened for no reason.

Paisley's expression sharpened, and she sent out her psychic senses right away.

A second later, her face turned dead serious.

Up ahead, a huge, dark mass of figures filled the road, the wasteland, and the woods, completely blocking the way.

It was a zombie horde!

It was a dark sea of them, hundreds, maybe thousands, stretching so far that she couldn't see the end!

Behind the horde, three huge, high-level zombies stood on higher ground, looking down. They radiated brutal hostility, and dark mist swirled around their bodies. Their presence was terrifying.

They were three Level Four zombies!

The one in the middle was the biggest of the three, and its skull was covered in creepy greenish veins. It was the leader, a Level Four zombie with Psychic Affinity.

It had awakened the rare Psychic Affinity, and it had used its powerful mind control to gather every zombie for miles. That was how it had formed such a huge horde and blocked the road completely.

"Everyone out. Stay sharp."

Elijah's voice was low and cold as he switched straight into fight mode.

He pushed his door open and strode to the front.

Paisley followed right behind him with the little white cat in her arms.

She calmly took in the endless, dark horde.

Everyone else got out of the cars too and quickly gathered together, their faces grim.

At the back of the group, Jasper and Rachel climbed out as well. They stared at the endless mass of zombies snarling up ahead, shaking uncontrollably, with terror written all over their faces.

Both of their abilities were still stuck at Level Two, so they couldn't do much in a fight.

If a real fight broke out, they could die any second.

They were in full-blown panic, and their voices shook.

"There are way too many zombies. What... what do we do?"

Orion, Spencer, and the others, on the other hand, looked sharp and fierce. Their energy surged around them, ready to go, and they were prepared to fight to the death.

Someone spoke up, low and firm.

"We push straight through! This is the only road to Rivergate, and there's no way around it!"

As everyone stood ready, the Level Four Psychic Affinity zombie in the middle of the horde slowly raised its head. Black mist leaked from its empty eye sockets.
