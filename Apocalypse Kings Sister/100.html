Chapter 100

An invisible mental command quietly spread out.

The next second, the whole dark horde went wild!

Thousands of zombies threw their heads back and let out a deafening, raspy roar. Their arms and legs flailed as they charged at the group like a tidal wave!

The huge mass of zombies kept coming, wave after wave, rushing straight toward them.

There were so many of them that just fighting them all would wear anyone down until they dropped dead.

As the horde bore down on them like an unstoppable flood, Paisley's eyes turned sharp, and she called out to Trovie right away.

[Trovie, buy me a longsword made for killing zombies.]

Trovie's sweet little voice popped into her head right away.

[Master! Zombie-slaying longswords are Level Five shop items, and only Level Five traders can unlock them!

[But Trovie can sneak you in through the back door, just this once! It'll cost 5,000 points. Are you sure you want to buy it?]

Paisley didn't hesitate for a second. [Yes. Buy it now.]

A bright chime rang out. [Points deducted! Level Five divine weapon acquired: a frost sword with a living sword spirit!

[Remaining points: 51,900]

As soon as the chime faded, a bone-chilling ice-blue light burst out of Paisley's Storage Dimension.

A crystal-clear longsword, cold as solid ice, slowly floated out and hung silently in the air.

The blade was long and sleek with sharp, cold lines. It was forged from frost-crystal meteorite iron, and layers of pale blue frost patterns rippled across its clear surface like frozen clouds.

Every inch of it was wrapped in bone-deep cold and a faint, magical glow.

Best of all, the sword had a spirit of its own.

An invisible being lived inside the blade. It was gentle and pure, with no violence in it at all, only a fierce, righteous power and the force of extreme cold.

The sword spirit looked like a faint, see-through, ice-blue shadow curled up inside the blade. Its eyes were clear and bright, curious and alive, and it connected with Paisley's psychic power right away.

The spirit didn't need to be controlled. It could sense zombies on its own, and it naturally overpowered zombie poison and undead monsters, cutting down the undead, breaking through evil, and freezing vengeful spirits solid. This was a divine weapon made for slaying zombies.

The blade trembled slightly and let out a clear, ringing hum. Each note was pure and bright, and it scattered the foul zombie stench in the air.

Bone-chilling cold spread out from the sword, freezing, purifying, and wiping out the foul mist within ten feet of it.

Paisley reached out and gently gripped the hilt.

A cool, smooth feeling spread from her palm through her whole body. The hilt fit her hand perfectly.

The sword spirit inside gladly accepted her as its master. It bonded deeply with her energy and her psychic power until they were perfectly in sync.

A huge rush of pure, overwhelming power surged through her whole body. Paisley couldn't hide how thrilled she was, and her eyes sparkled.

Just then, Trovie's proud, smug little voice rang out in her head.

[It's called the Frostbloom Sword! Master, this is a top-tier divine weapon! It can cut down gods and demons alike, and it was made to destroy every evil spirit and zombie out there!]

Knowing how ridiculously powerful the Frostbloom Sword was made Paisley even happier. She tightened her grip on the hilt, thrilled to have such an incredible treasure.

In those few seconds, the massive horde had already crashed into them, and everyone jumped straight into the fight.

Brilliant, cold golden light blazed in both of Orion's hands as his Metal Affinity exploded at full power. His metal energy formed countless razor-sharp golden blades and a thick, solid metal wall, and they tore through the wave of zombies charging at him.

The sharp golden light could shred and shatter. It ripped through rotting flesh, crushed decayed bones, and burned away weak zombie poison. Whole rows of low-level zombies were swallowed by the flood of golden blades and sliced to pieces, leaving charred chunks scattered across the ground.

But using his Metal Affinity that hard drained it fast. After clearing out so many zombies, beads of sweat formed on Orion's forehead, and the golden light around him visibly dimmed as his energy ran low.

Purple-blue lightning crackled around Elijah's palm. His Lightning Affinity had huge burst power, and the damage it did was terrifying.

Every bolt he threw punched right through several zombies' heads and bodies at once and paralyzed whole groups of the horde around them. It was fast and brutal.

But Lightning Affinity drained mental energy faster than almost anything. Using it this hard for this long burned through his power quickly, and his strikes slowly lost their force and speed.
