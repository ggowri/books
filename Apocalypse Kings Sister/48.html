Chapter 48

The royal beauty eyed the frosty treat in Paisley's hand. [What is that in your hand? It's clear as crystal, and it's giving off a chill.]

Paisley smiled. [It's a popsicle, perfect for cooling off in the summer. Want to try one?]

The princess's eyes lit up, but then she drew herself up with royal dignity. [A princess never takes something for nothing. Would you accept the gold pin from my hair in exchange?]

Paisley was delighted. A pin worn by a princess had to be solid gold and worth a pretty penny.

Of course she said yes. She nodded and put two fresh popsicles into the trading slot, then waited for the princess to confirm the trade.

The princess took the popsicles and nibbled one. Her eyes went wide with delight, and she couldn't stop raving about it.

It was the middle of a sweltering summer, and she'd never tasted anything so cold and sweet.

She leaned in eagerly. [This is marvelous! Our summers are brutally hot, and ice is rare and terribly hard to come by, even in the palace.

[If you know a way to make ice, would you share it with me?]

Paisley glanced at the tablet on the table in the villa, and a plan clicked into place.

The Ancient Realm didn't have electricity or freezers. All they could do was store winter ice in cellars, which took a ton of work and didn't go far. They definitely didn't know how to make ice with saltpeter.

But with her tablet, she could look up step-by-step instructions in no time.

Better yet, the Ancient Realm had plenty of gold, and gold was exactly what she needed to level up. It was a win-win.

Paisley kept a straight face and looked at the princess. [Princess, I do have a way to make ice. You could make it anytime in summer and whip up popsicles by the batch, without storing ice all winter.

[It's quick and easy to learn. What would you be willing to give me for it?]

The princess practically trembled with excitement, her eyes shining with joy.

[Truly? The queen, my mother, suffers terribly every summer! If you'll give me this secret, I'll pay you a fortune in gold!]

Paisley was impressed. Royalty really was something else, and the princess had offered a fortune without even blinking.

She'd already figured out how trader levels worked. Each level took ten times the points of the one before it. Going from Level Two to Level Three would take 100,000 points, or about 11,000 pounds of gold.

Paisley nodded right away. [Deal. Give me a few minutes, Princess.]

Then, she grabbed her tablet and quickly pulled together the whole saltpeter ice-making process. It covered purifying the saltpeter, freezing water between two nested basins, flavoring and shaping popsicles, and storing ice in bulk. The steps were simple, and all the materials were easy to find in the Ancient Realm.

A few minutes later, she had the whole guide ready, and she uploaded it to the trading channel with one tap. The trade went through.

A second later, 1,100 pounds of gleaming gold appeared in her dimension, stacked in neat, dazzling piles.

Trovie bounced up and down, squealing. [Master, look at all this gold! You're amazing!]

Paisley looked at all that gold and grinned. [That princess is a real sweetheart.]

On the other side of the screen, the princess flipped through the instructions, getting more excited with every page. [This is brilliant, and so simple! I'll have my people gather the materials at once, and I'll be in touch soon!]

With that, she cut the connection, dying to rush back to the palace and get started.

Paisley stopped smiling and got down to business. [Trovie, convert all 1,100 pounds of that gold into points.]

Trovie was on it. [You got it, Master!]

A chime rang out again, and white light flashed across the display.

[Rare valuable resource detected: 1,100 pounds of gold.

[Calculating cross-dimensional exchange rate... Conversion successful!

[Points earned: 10,000! Total points: 21,730]

She still had a long way to go before she reached Level Three. That took 100,000 points, and she only had a little over 20,000.
