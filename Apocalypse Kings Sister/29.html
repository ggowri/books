Chapter 29

Paisley had really liked those luxury goods too.

And now, all of it had turned into 6,600 points. She still had a long way to go before she hit Level Two.

As a Level One trader, she couldn't do much. There wasn't a lot she could buy in the Trading Plaza, and she couldn't get any high-level items or rare ability boosters yet.

But she shook it off fast. This was still a big step forward.

It's fine. I just need to keep collecting.

One more haul of gold like this, and she'd make Level Two for sure.

Paisley stopped dwelling on it. [Trovie, open a cross-dimensional link. Connect me with the first trader.]

Trovie perked right up. [Searching for a channel... Match found! Connecting you to a Beast Realm trader!]

A second later, a pale blue holographic screen popped up in the middle of her dimension, flickering as it connected to a whole other world.

On the other side was a wild, rugged place, all endless jungle and rocky mountains. She could almost smell the earth and leaves through the screen.

The image wavered a moment longer before the connection fully stabilized.

A striking woman from the Beast Realm stood on the other side of the screen, with bold features and an air of total confidence.

She had sun-kissed skin and sharp, sexy eyes, and little bone charms were woven into her long, loose hair. She seemed laid-back but totally in charge, every inch a matriarch. When she spoke, her voice was bright and full of wild energy.

[A new realm? Are you a new trader?]

Paisley stayed calm and got straight to the point. [I'm from an apocalypse realm, and I can offer you nice dresses, trendy clothes, toiletries, lighters, and lots of everyday stuff. I've also got all kinds of food.]

As she spoke, she used her mind to display her stock on the trading screen, one item after another.

Soft, flowy dresses and sharp everyday outfits lined up in neat rows next to brand-new pocket tools and rows of lighters. After that, she showed vacuum-sealed meals, bags of snacks, fine white salt, all kinds of sauces and seasonings, and ration bars. The Beast Realm had never seen anything like it.

When she was done, Paisley asked, not really expecting much, [What can you give me in return?]

What could a primitive place like the Beast Realm possibly have that was worth trading for?

On the other side, the Matriarch was frozen in amazement. Then, a huge, delighted grin spread across her face.

In all her years, she'd never seen a single thing like what Paisley was showing her.

She couldn't take her eyes off the screen, burning with curiosity.

What was that little square thing? It made fire all on its own, no rubbing sticks together required. That was incredible!

And those neat little packages that smelled so good... were those food? Ready to eat, just like that?

Nobody had to roast or prep anything? They could just open the bag and eat? That blew her mind.

But what shocked her most was that pile of fine, snow-white powder. It was refined salt!

Salt in the Beast Realm was coarse, dirty, and bitter, and it was really hard to purify. Pure, fine salt was incredibly rare, and only the tribe's top leaders ever got a taste of it.

And this woman had a whole pile of it!

There were also cans of red sauce, plump dried fruit, and creamy white treats. She'd never seen any of it before, and it all looked amazing.

Then, she looked at Paisley's outfit. The fabric was light and soft, and it was so pretty, nothing like the thick, scratchy animal hides her people wore.

And Paisley's makeup was so clean and flawless. The Matriarch had never had anything like it.

Everything on the other side of that screen was exactly what she'd always wanted.

With clothes like these, she and the women of her tribe wouldn't have to sweat in heavy hides every day anymore.

With lighters and tools, her people wouldn't have to waste their strength rubbing sticks together or chipping stone tools by hand.

And with fine salt and ready-made food, the whole tribe would live so much better.

I have to make this deal happen! Whatever she wants, I'll give it to her!
