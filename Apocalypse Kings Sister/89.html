Chapter 89

Elijah had watched the whole thing in silence beside her. He'd already seen what the cat could do, so none of it surprised him.

He stepped forward and quickly tore down the barbed wire across the road. He ripped down the red sign with its obnoxious rules too and tossed it all into the weeds.

The road was clear.

"We can keep going," Elijah said, glancing at Paisley.

Paisley nodded. With the little white cat in her arms, she walked calmly beside him toward the abandoned gas station, through the lingering smell of blood.

Five hundred yards away, every light was on inside the abandoned motel. The air was thick with the smell of booze and meat.

The gang of thirty-some guys was inside, partying like there was no tomorrow.

They sat around together, knocking back drinks and passing bottles around. Drinking games and rowdy shouts filled the room.

Several women they'd kidnapped were serving them, shaking with fear. They carefully poured drinks, fed the men, and rubbed their shoulders. They were terrified and humiliated, but none of them dared to fight back.

This gang of vicious raiders had taken over the whole stretch of road. They'd gotten rich off insane tolls and the supplies they stole from passing convoys, and they'd gotten used to living it up like this.

And what they did was even filthier than that.

Instead of holding back, they got even bolder once the booze kicked in. They reached out and groped the women serving them, putting their hands all over them without a second thought.

The women they grabbed froze stiff. Their eyes turned red, and they bit their lips hard, too scared to cry out. They were trapped in terror and humiliation.

Instead of stopping it, the other men whistled and cheered, and crude laughter and hollering filled the whole room.

To them, these women were just loot, toys for their entertainment. What the women wanted and their dignity didn't matter one bit.

The men were so wrapped up in their drunken party that they'd let their guard down completely.

Not one of them had noticed that the four guards outside were already dead.

And they had no idea that danger was already right on top of them.

Through the broken window, Paisley saw the whole disgusting, perverted scene, and her stomach turned.

Rage built up over two lifetimes surged through her, and a bone-deep urge to kill burned in her eyes.

She was out of patience. Her lips pressed into a cold line, and her voice was pure ice.

"I'm done waiting."

The second the words left her mouth, green light flared around her, and her pure, overwhelming Plant Affinity energy exploded outward.

With a deep, thundering rumble, countless thick, tough vines oozing dark purple poison burst out of the ground. They tore straight through the walls and surged into the building with terrifying, unstoppable force!

The poison vines were sharp as knives and covered in tiny, needle-sharp thorns. Every inch of them carried enough venom to paralyze flesh and eat away at a person's insides.

The raiders inside were still laughing, drinking, and partying, with no idea what was coming.

Suddenly, one of them spotted a thick, thorny vine snaking in through the window and creeping slowly toward him.

His mind went blank. He fell on his ass in terror and screamed.

"What the hell is that?! We're under attack! It's an ability user!"

He'd barely gotten the words out before countless poison vines wrapped tightly around every last one of the men, coiling around their arms, legs, necks, and chests.

Just like that, they were trapped!

Level One, Level Two, Level Three, it didn't matter. Not one of them escaped.

The sharp thorns stabbed deep into their flesh, and searing pain ripped through their bodies. The paralyzing venom raced through their veins and shut down their abilities on the spot. Their strength drained away so fast that they couldn't even lift a hand to fight back.

A minute ago, those men had been groping the women and hollering like animals. Now, the poison vines had them hanging high in the air, their arms and legs twisted and dangling. They looked pathetic.

The dark purple venom kept spreading from their wounds, and their skin turned blue and black right before everyone's eyes. The agony was so bad that their bodies convulsed and their faces twisted. They didn't even have the strength left to beg for mercy.

In the blink of an eye, the rowdy party room had turned into a bloody hell on earth.
