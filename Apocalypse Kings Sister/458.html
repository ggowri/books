Chapter 458

He was out of danger, and he slipped quietly into sleep.

Paisley drew back her hand. The green light faded from her palm. She stayed calm and composed, as if what she'd just done were nothing at all.

At that moment, both the Lone Wolf Squad and the Falcon Squad who'd seen it before felt waves rise in their hearts.

The Lone Wolf members were the most shocked.

The woman who had collapsed in despair looked up in a daze at her unharmed teammate. Her voice trembled, full of awe.

"It's gone! The mutation lines are gone! The poison's totally cleared out!"

"That was lizard venom with mental contamination! We've been stuck here two days, tried everything, couldn't suppress it... and she cleared it out in a few minutes?!"

One teammate rubbed his eyes hard, still not believing it, and muttered to himself.

"I've only heard that Plant Affinity users might awaken healing and purifying. I've never seen healing this powerful... This isn't just treatment. It's rewriting fate!"

The Falcon Squad watched too, still stunned.

Ashley's eyes were bright with worship, and she whispered. "Paisley's amazing. No matter how bad the wound or how strange the poison, she heals it all."

Orion's gaze was deep. He marveled again at how much Paisley kept hidden. A trump card like this was a lifesaver for the whole squad.

Elijah watched the girl's slim back, his eyes warm, mixed with deep pride and a flutter in his chest.

She was always like this. Cool and distant on the outside, but gentle at heart, stepping in to save people at the worst moments and creating miracles in dead ends.

The rest of the Falcon Squad still hadn't come back from their shock. Their faces were stunned, their eyes full of wonder and awe.

The Lone Wolf members were churning inside. They looked at Paisley with deep gratitude and admiration, fully won over by her strength.

A friend they'd already given up on had been pulled back from death's door. They would remember that kindness for the rest of their lives.

Paisley lifted a hand and gently wiped the thin sweat from her forehead.

Continuous high-intensity purification and healing was draining. A faint trace of weariness showed between her brows.

She straightened up and spoke in a light, even tone.

"He's fine now. He's just sleeping. Let him rest, and he'll be fully recovered soon, with no aftereffects."

That one sentence settled everyone's hearts.

Next was working out the breakout.

Combining what both teams had learned from being trapped with what they'd seen on the ground, they settled on the best plan.

The black-scale lizard tide was dense and fierce, but their biology had a weakness. The second half of the night was when their overall strength was lowest and their guard weakest, and the nest's defense was thinnest.

By then everyone's wounds would be mostly healed. It was the best chance to break out.

With the plan set, everyone split up to rest and recover, quietly waiting for the second half of the night.

Paisley found a quiet sofa, leaned back, and closed her eyes to rest.

Not long after, footsteps approached.

Cole stood a short distance away. His usual calm, hard, decisive face showed a rare trace of awkwardness and shyness.

He looked at the girl resting peacefully in the corner, his eyes full of respect and affection. After a moment's hesitation, he gathered his courage and stepped up.

He spoke respectfully, sincerely, in a quiet voice.

"Ms. Lennox, thank you for saving my teammate today. I can't repay you. I'd like to officially get to know you, to make future missions go smoother. Could we swap contact info?"

His intent was pure, simple respect for a strong person and a wish to be on good terms. And he really did like her, which his voice couldn't hide.

Paisley opened her eyes. Her gaze was cool and even, her tone polite but distant.

"No need. Swap contacts with our captain. Anything about the team, talk to him."

She was simple and clear by nature, with no interest in extra entanglements. She turned him down cleanly.

The words had barely left her mouth when a steady, warm, but commanding male voice cut in.

Elijah had walked up without her noticing, a cup of water at just the right temperature in his hand. His stride was easy, his posture straight.

His eyes drifted lightly over Cole. He looked calm, but behind it was a trace of possessiveness and wariness he didn't show.
