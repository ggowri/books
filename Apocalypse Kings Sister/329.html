Chapter 329

"Get ready!"

Gabriel's voice cut through the chaos, his Level Six Metal Affinity flaring to full power, gold-edged blades sweeping the room as he charged the front line.

In an instant, fire, frost, metal, and vine collided in a storm of motion, and the full-scale fight broke loose.

Waves of fire rolled through the room, scorching the mutants surging forward.

Earth Affinity threw up solid barriers, holding the line against wave after wave.

Frost locked down the advance, freezing enemies mid-stride.

The team moved in perfect coordination, methodically cutting down the endless stream of mutants pouring in.

Screams, the clash of weapons, and the crackle of Affinity energy filled the chamber, echoing through the whole facility.

Wave after wave of mutants broke against the team's defense and failed to get through, crystal cores pulled one after another, bodies piling up in pools of dark blood.

As the fight wound down, the last of the mutants fell easily.

Right then, Paisley's eyes flickered. She slipped quietly out of the fight, her figure slipping into a hidden side lab deep in the facility.

The desk inside held stacks of yellowed, heavy research files, genetic modification reports, and breeding records for the mutants. Along the wall, a temperature-controlled storage cabinet held rows of sealed samples — rare source fluids, mutant genetic material, and crystallized deep toxin.

This was the core of everything the facility had been working toward — its most classified research.

Without hesitating, Paisley gathered every paper file and digital drive on the desk, then methodically emptied the storage cabinet — every sample, every specimen, every crystallized toxin — into her storage, leaving nothing behind.

In moments, the entire side lab had been cleared out completely.

With that done, Paisley's expression went calm and unreadable. She turned and walked back out, rejoining the team as they finished mopping up the last of the fight, slipping back in like she'd never left at all.

Right as she rejoined the group, Trovie's cheerful chime sounded in her mind.

[Ding! Host has successfully completed the hidden main questline — located the hidden domestic research facility, recovered all scattered research data, genetic samples, and precision equipment, and eliminated the threat posed by human experimentation at Stillwater.]

[Reward issued: 100,000 points, one premium rare lucky draw!]

[Deduction: seventeen Universal Antidote Pills exchanged, 1,700 points total.]

[Current host balance: 1,325,360 points!]

The sudden windfall brought a flicker of light to Paisley's eyes. Without hesitation, she gave the order.

[Trovie, activate the premium draw. Let's go.]

[Premium prize pool open! This round's prizes: Affinity Awakening Pill, Beauty Pill, Vitality Pill, Invisibility Talisman, Misfortune Talisman, Teleportation Talisman!]

The wheel spun fast, light flickering, and finally settled.

[Congratulations, Host — you've won: five Misfortune Talismans!]

Five dull gray talisman sheets sat quietly in her storage.

Paisley's brow twitched, a flat, unimpressed thought crossing her mind.

[Trovie, are you messing with me right now? That's what a premium draw gets me? Five Misfortune Talismans? What am I even supposed to do with these?]

Trovie launched into an explanation of their use.

[Don't knock it, Host! These are a limited premium item! Didn't you say you can't stand Rachel? Stick one of these on her quietly, and her luck tanks for the rest of her life, bad fortune, everything going wrong, all of it!]

[Stick one on whoever you want, Host — it locks in their luck for life, guarantees them nothing but misfortune and bad breaks. Sounds pretty satisfying, doesn't it?]

Paisley blinked, caught off guard for a second, then a faint, amused smile tugged at her lips, something playful flickering in her eyes.

[That's actually interesting. Fine, I'll let it slide this time. Better than nothing, I suppose. Call it a nice surprise.]

Suddenly, Trovie's voice spiked with excitement.

[Host! Detecting a massive energy signature up ahead — has to be serious wealth! Keep moving, there's a hidden vault at the very bottom of the facility!]

Paisley's eyes sharpened, and she turned toward a heavy, rust-covered alloy hatch buried deep in the facility, long sealed shut.

By now, the last of the mutants on the main battlefield had been cleared out. The team, exhausted from the fight, had started settling down to rest.

Off to the side, Chase and Rachel still lay unconscious, poisoned and limp, their faces a sickly blue-gray.
