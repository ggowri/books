Chapter 116

This Rat King wasn't just physically strong. It also had a rare psychic attack, and if this kept up, half the team would be dead by the end of the night.

"Kitty, go."

The second Paisley gave the cold order, the little white cat, which had been waiting quietly nearby, unleashed its full power!

A burst of white light exploded outward, and its tiny body stretched and transformed into a towering 33-foot cat.

Its fur was thick and fierce, wrapped in frost energy, and icy mist drifted off it. Its overwhelming beastly presence crashed down and crushed the Rat King's aura.

The next second, Paisley and the cat attacked together in perfect sync.

Paisley twisted her wrist, and the Frostbloom Sword flashed with bone-chilling light. Layer after layer of frost energy built up into a thick, icy blue wave that ripped through the air and slammed into the gaps in the Rat King's armor, aiming right at its weak spots.

The little white cat leaped into the air. Its huge white claws, wrapped in brutal frost, tore through the air with a deafening crack and slammed down on the Rat King's head.

The frost froze the cloud of rotting poison around the Rat King and smashed right through its toxic shield.

Under attack from both sides, the Rat King went into a frenzy. It kept blasting out psychic waves while swinging its thick claws and snapping its fangs in a wild counterattack.

But its psychic attacks did nothing to Paisley, and the little white cat blocked and shredded every one of its physical attacks.

In just a few rounds, the Frostbloom Sword had cracked through layer after layer of the Rat King's armor. It was covered in wounds and losing its fight, and its psychic power was draining fast. It couldn't keep up the pressure anymore.

Paisley seized the split-second opening. She darted forward, flipped through the air, and drove her sword straight through the crystal core in the Rat King's skull!

A surge of overwhelming plant energy exploded inside it and destroyed what was left of its defenses.

The Rat King let out one last, piercing squeal.

Its massive body crashed to the ground with a heavy thud. It twitched violently a few times, then went completely still. Its blood-red eyes dimmed, and all its fury and psychic pressure faded away.

The psychic pressure that had crushed the whole battlefield vanished, and the agonizing pain gripping everyone eased.

Everyone gasped for air. They clutched their throbbing heads and struggled to their feet, still pale with shock, and stared at the Rat King's huge corpse in disbelief and lingering fear.

Elijah leaned against the wall to steady himself. He still looked a little worn out, and his heart was still pounding.

He looked at the Rat King's 16-foot corpse, his eyes heavy with shock, and spoke in a low voice.

"A Level Four mutant beast's psychic attack is way too creepy and way too strong.

"For those few seconds, I couldn't even lift a hand to summon any lightning. If Paisley hadn't stepped in, we'd all be dead right now."

In the few days since the apocalypse began, this was the closest he'd ever come to dying. They'd come this close to losing everyone.

If it weren't for Paisley, none of them would've made it out alive.

He was still way too weak, and he was desperate to get stronger.

Elijah looked at Paisley with a whole mix of feelings. There was admiration, curiosity, and something that looked a lot like love.

Just how strong is she?

It felt like she was always the one protecting them.

Orion shook his throbbing head. His fingers were still trembling, and he couldn't believe what he'd just seen.

He was usually calm and steady no matter what, but even he couldn't hide how shaken he sounded.

He, Thomas, and Spencer traded looks, and they couldn't help gushing.

"That was way too close. The scariest thing about that Rat King wasn't how strong it was. It was that invisible psychic attack, and there was no way to fight it.

"If Paisley's mind weren't so much stronger than everyone else's, there's no way any of us would've survived. She's seriously amazing."

Paisley really was incredible!

How many more secrets was she hiding from them?

Ashley gazed at Paisley, her eyes shining.

"Paisley's so amazing! She's my hero!"

Madison, leaning against her, nodded.

"She really is incredible. We wouldn't have made it without her."

It took Kelly a long time to even stand up straight. Her face was still pale, and her chest felt tight and achy.

She stared at the huge corpse on the ground, torn between shock and jealousy.

"I never knew a Psychic Affinity could be this terrifying. I really thought I was going to die."
