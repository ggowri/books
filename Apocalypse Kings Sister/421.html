The Quinns and the Blackwoods already hate each other. When two tigers fight, both get hurt."

When both are weak, we step in and take what's left."

The senior leaders of the Lennox family lit up and nodded one after another, full of admiration.

"Vivian's plan is brilliant. Airtight!"

"We don't offend either the Quinns or the Blackwoods, and the worst case is we profit from the fight. We can't lose!"

"Let's go with Vivian's plan. We can step forward or back as we like. We'll be safe."

Agreement rose from all sides, and the family's strategy was set.

Harold looked at his composed, clever daughter with great satisfaction, nodded, and gave the final word.

"Good."

"Move now. Send someone to find Paisley's adoptive mother and persuade her."

"Pass the order. Everyone stays put and waits. No one is to get involved in the Quinn-Blackwood fight."

A secret game spanning three great families and shaking the base's top had been laid out.

A storm was coming, and the whole base was in the current.

******

At six in the evening, the dusk was soft, and the sunset spilled gold across the stone path of the villa district.

Elijah's black sedan stopped in front of the Thompson villa.

Time with her always went too fast.

He got out and held the door open for her like a gentleman. All day they'd spent together, and his eyes were still full of warmth.

His voice was low and gentle. "Get some rest. We leave tomorrow."

Paisley nodded, the cool lines of her face softening a bit. "You too."

Elijah looked at her deeply, hiding his reluctance, and didn't linger. He watched her walk through the gate, then drove off.

Inside the villa, the lights were warm and the smell of dinner filled the house.

Diane had made a rich dinner, with meat and vegetables and plenty of it. In the apocalypse it was a luxury.

The family sat around the table, warm and easy. After two days of intense Affinity training, everyone had made huge progress, and they couldn't hide their happiness.

Ashley's eyes shone, and she was the first to share her news, excited.

"I've got good news. After these two days of work, my Earth Affinity has reached mid Level Six!"

Thomas followed, calm but plainly proud. "Plant Affinity, mid Level Six."

"Fire Affinity, mid-level," Spencer said, looking up, his edge showing.

Orion added coolly, "Metal Affinity, mid-level. I'm several times steadier than before."

In two days, everyone had leaped forward. That speed would be astonishing anywhere on the base.

Gerald watched his children get stronger, reassured but weighed down by a heavy worry. He put his fork down and said gravely.

"Rest well tonight and get plenty of sleep.

Tomorrow's grain depot mission in the Exclusion Zone is dangerous and unpredictable. You'll face high-level mutant beasts and who knows what else. Be careful, and keep yourselves safe above all."

"Watch out for the Quinns' people and the Lennoxes. They may try to assassinate you."

"The situation is getting worse."

"So be extra careful on this mission."

At the mention of the mission, the mood tightened.

Diane frowned, worried sick, and couldn't hold back her unease.

"I heard today that Brenda and her family have been released from the Security Office."

The table went quiet.

She bit her lip and went on, anxious. "And Chloe's boyfriend, Grant, who was so badly hurt, has woken up. They say he's fully healed and even stronger than before."

"Will they come after us?"

Her heart was full of worry, and she couldn't settle.

Gerald patted her hand and said firmly, "Don't worry. I'm here. I won't let anything happen to this family."
