Chapter 472

Elijah sat in the front passenger seat, eyes fixed on the road ahead, his voice steady.

"We have to be back today. Tomorrow before dawn, the Great Freeze hits for real. Out here it's too dangerous. If we're caught outside, nobody can survive the temperature crash."

In the back seat, Paisley leaned quietly against the window and listened. Shadows crept over her clear eyes.

Everyone else only knew a blizzard was coming tomorrow. Nobody knew this freeze was only the start of the apocalypse's real disaster.

She remembered how it went in her last life.

Three days and nights of the heaviest snow, temperatures plunging to dozens of degrees below zero. The world turned to ice from horizon to horizon.

Every plant outside and every small beast froze dead. Rivers locked up and the ground split. Countless supplies left in the open were destroyed. One wrong step, and a survivor would freeze stiff.

That was only the first disaster.

After the Freeze ended and the ice melted, it was followed by three days and nights of crimson rain.

That rain carried deadly radiation and virus toxins. It washed over the land and pushed pollution to a whole new level.

The soil went bad, the water turned toxic, and plants mutated and rotted. The little edible food that remained was nearly gone. The whole world fell into famine.

The environment collapsed, resources dried up, and the zombies evolved again.

The mutated zombies were stronger, faster, and smarter. Worst of all, the cores they produced were tainted with toxin, cloudy and poisoned, and no longer usable.

Every core had to be purified by a specialist and stripped of toxin before it could be absorbed.

In the whole apocalypse, purifiers who could do that were few and far between.

Purifiers became the most sought-after talent at every base. Their status soared. They were worth more than gold.

First the Great Freeze, then the Crimson Rain, then mutated zombies, tainted cores, scarce purifiers, and no food.

The world for what remained of humanity would only get harsher.

People would fight bloodthirsty mutant zombies and fierce beasts. They would also fight harsh weather, dwindling resources, and poison everywhere.

No clothes, no food, no clean water, no safe supplies, nonstop disaster, beasts everywhere.

The apocalypse was entering its next phase of hell.

Paisley looked down at her slender, clean palm. Calm and clear thinking, far beyond her age, settled in her eyes.

Good thing I'm back, and I'm ahead of it all.

She had her Storage Dimension and the system. She had nearly two thousand acres of rich farmland, endless supplies, high-level Affinities across five elements, and the rarest of all, the power to purify.

In this life, she didn't just want to protect herself. She wanted to protect everyone she cared about, every teammate on Falcon Squad.

I hope we get back to Aetheris Base today, ahead of the Freeze, everyone safe, and dodge this disaster.

******

Half an hour later, the SUV slowed. The tires gave a faint, dragging squeal, and it came to a stop.

The road was gone.

The thousand-yard bridge over the wide river had snapped in the middle. Large sections of the deck had collapsed. Twisted rebar and chunks of concrete dangled in the air, and dark, muddy water rushed below.

The whole bridge was ruined and couldn't be crossed. It was the only road back to the base.

"The bridge is out," Mason said grimly.

Everyone got out and stood at the edge, looking at the wreck. Their faces turned serious.

River wind carried a cold dampness. Waves slammed the broken pylons with a dull roar.

Orion stepped forward and volunteered. "I'll fix it."

Before he finished, metal particles were already gathering around him, glittering under his Metal Affinity.

"I can help too!" Ashley strode forward, her Earth Affinity ready. "I can fix it too."

"Me too," Thomas said, his gaze steady, Plant power flowing. "My vines can anchor it and carry the load."

Elijah studied the broken bridge, a full thousand yards from end to end.

"This span is too long. One person fixing it would burn too much power. The three of you working together will be fastest and cost the least."
