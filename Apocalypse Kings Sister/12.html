Chapter 12

Orion gave a firm nod. "Exactly. That's why I wanted to tell you in private."

Elijah looked over at Paisley, who stood there quiet and sweet, and his stern face softened a little.

"Don't worry. I'll protect you too. Nobody's going to lay a finger on you.

"How big is your space right now? Can it fit these?"

With that, he turned and unlocked a storage room nearby, then pulled open a cabinet. Inside, two heavy black metal cases sat neatly side by side.

"Orion and I found these two cases of weapons in a warehouse. One's full of service pistols and ammo, and the other has folding combat knives and riot gear.

"We've been stressing about how to haul them on the road. All we could do was split them up and carry them ourselves."

Elijah looked at Paisley, his tone lighter. "Now that we've got your space, they can all go in there."

Paisley's eyes lit up at the sight of the two heavy cases.

He really is the best of the best.

While everyone else was still too scared to think, he'd already gotten his hands on gear like this. In the apocalypse, nothing kept a person safer than plenty of weapons and a steady flow of supplies.

With a wave of her hand, the two cases of military-grade weapons vanished into the dimension.

Elijah and Orion watched them disappear and breathed a sigh of relief. The biggest problem on their minds had been solved just like that.

The three of them headed back downstairs to join the others, who were already packed and ready to go.

Three black SUVs were parked in the lot outside the villa. All of them had been professionally modified to be crash-proof and blast-resistant, built to handle the apocalypse.

Elijah drove the first car himself. He had the steadiest hand and the sharpest instincts, so he'd take point and scout ahead. Ashley Thompson, Kelly Walker, and Thomas Thompson rode with him.

Orion drove the second car, with Madison Hughes in the passenger seat and Paisley and Spencer Thompson in the back.

Mason Coleman, who'd awakened a Wind Affinity, drove the third car alone. He'd bring up the rear and take out any stragglers so nothing could sneak up on them.

One by one, the engines roared to life. The three SUVs rolled out of the gated community in a line and sped toward the biggest mall downtown.

After the rain, the city was a wreck, with every trace of its old hustle and bustle gone. Dead branches, dust, and debris littered the roads, and abandoned cars lined both sides of the street.

There were way more zombies out than the day before, and the red rain had made a lot of them bigger and faster.

Every so often, a low-level mutant zombie streaked past at a terrifying speed. Things had gotten a lot more dangerous.

Outside the windows, everything was empty and dead silent. A cold wind howled through the streets, and the whole city felt suffocating.

Spencer stared out at the bleak streets, getting nervous. "There are so many zombies out there..."

Paisley stayed calm and gave him a small, reassuring nod. Her mind was crystal clear.

She knew this was only the real start of the apocalypse.

With the red rain over, the mutations were just getting started. From here on out, the zombies would only get stronger. After that would come mutant beasts and plants, brutal weather, and people scheming against each other, one thing after another.

But in this second life, she wasn't alone anymore.

She'd awakened an ability, found her brothers, and had a powerhouse like Elijah watching her back.

This time, she wasn't just going to survive. She was going to make them pay in blood for her last life, and the Lennoxes would suffer twice as much as she had.

The cars raced on, and 20 minutes later, they pulled up outside the biggest mall in Southspire.

The huge mall had lost every bit of its old glamour. Its front doors hung open and broken, and zombies wandered the empty streets around it, growling low. It was a chilling sight.

Elijah shoved his door open and climbed out first, a sharp machete in his hand.

His face was cold, and every move was quick and clean. When a few zombies lunged at him, a few precise strikes to the head dropped them for good. That alone showed how strong he was.

Orion was right behind him, already on high alert.

He turned back to Paisley and Madison in the car. "Stay put," he warned. "Don't get out until we've cleared out the zombies and it's safe."

Then, he turned to Spencer, his voice firm. "Spencer, you stay with the car. Keep them safe."

His words hit Paisley harder than she expected.

She couldn't help thinking back to her past life.
