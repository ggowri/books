Chapter 28

Orion smiled at her fondly and nodded. "Sure. Get some rest, and I'll come get you when lunch is ready."

As he spoke, he unclipped a small walkie-talkie from his belt and gently placed it in Paisley's palm.

"Keep this on. It reaches us anywhere within 150 feet.

"If you sense any danger at all, call me right away. I'm always listening."

Paisley closed her hand around the little radio, feeling warm inside, and nodded. "Okay, got it."

The car soon emptied out, leaving Paisley alone.

Once she was sure no one was around, her eyes sharpened. With a thought, she stepped straight into her dimension.

The Level Two plant core hovered in midair, clear and bright green, a soft light swirling across its surface. It gave off a rich, earthy energy, gentler and purer than a zombie core's.

Paisley reached out and closed her hand around the core. A cool, smooth feeling spread from her fingertips through her entire body.

She closed her eyes, focused, and called on her faint Plant Affinity to draw out the huge amount of energy stored in the core.

A second later, a flood of green energy surged up through her palm and poured into every part of her body.

It was gentle but incredibly powerful, and it slowly worked its way through her, strengthening her bones and muscles.

Until now, her Plant Affinity had been brand new and barely useful. All she could do was sense plants and help them grow a little.

But now, the Level Two core was powering it up.

Her thin, scattered power grew fast, building up layer by layer. Soon, it formed a swirling vortex of energy in the pit of her stomach, flowing in an endless loop.

The plant energy around her flowed in with every breath, and a faint green glow wrapped around her whole body.

Trovie was zipping around the dimension, chattering nonstop.

[Master, your energy's through the roof! Your Plant Affinity is leveling up fast!

[That core is so pure, you're skipping the slow buildup and jumping straight to the next level!]

When she'd absorbed the very last of the core's energy, the green light around Paisley flared.

Something inside her hummed and gave way, and the wall holding her ability back shattered.

Her Plant Affinity had broken through from a fresh awakening to early Level Two.

Now, she could grow all kinds of plants fast and sprout seeds, and she could use vines and branches to attack or defend. She could even use her Plant Affinity's life energy to heal herself, and other people too.

Plant abilities were super rare in the apocalypse. Not many people could fight, heal, and grow food all at once.

Paisley slowly opened her eyes. For a second, they glowed green, then faded back to normal.

Her whole body felt light and loose, she was buzzing with energy, and her senses were sharper than ever.

She could sense every blade of grass and every tree for dozens of yards around, down to the faintest flicker of life.

She lifted a finger, and a thin wisp of green light pulsed above it. It did exactly what she wanted, ready to attack or defend.

Paisley smiled a little.

So this is what it feels like to have an ability?

Just then, Trovie piped up again.

[Congratulations, Master! You've reached Plant Affinity Level Two!

[Do you want to open a cross-dimensional channel now and connect with your first trader?]

Paisley looked around and frowned. There wasn't any gold in sight.

[Where's all the gold I collected?

[Pull up the Trading Plaza panel. I want to see my points.]

She'd been wanting to check on everything she'd collected today anyway.

Trovie, who'd been chattering away a second ago, suddenly went dead quiet. That told Paisley everything.

Sure enough, a second later, a see-through Trading Plaza panel popped up in front of her.

[Total Points: 6,600]

Trovie had automatically converted every last ounce of gold and every luxury item she'd collected into points.

Trovie spoke up in a tiny, guilty voice. [Master... The Trading Plaza converts rare goods into points automatically. It's a default setting, so you'll have what you need to level up...]

Paisley stared at the number and sucked in a breath. It hurt a little.

Who didn't love the sight of gleaming gold?
