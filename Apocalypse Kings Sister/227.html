Chapter 227

There were also giant deep-sea monsters charging around wildly, and every time they rammed the wall, the sturdy barrier shook violently.

The front-line fighters held the defense line with everything they had, fighting through blood.

Fire users blasted roaring flames in midair, burning swarms of small mutant creatures as they charged. Lightning users brought down bolts of electricity, stunning giant beasts in mid-charge.

Earth users gritted their teeth and held up layer after layer of thick barriers, taking hit after devastating hit from the mutant sea beasts.

The battle was horrific. Roars, the blasts of powers, the crack of breaking bones, and the shaking of the wall all mixed together into a deafening noise.

Fighters kept running out of power or getting badly hurt by the beasts, and their teammates risked their lives to pull them back for treatment. But not one of them backed down or lost their nerve.

The heavy weapons had already opened fire, and the deafening boom of artillery rang out again and again.

Heavy shells slammed right into the middle of the dense mutant tide and exploded.

Scorching fireballs shot into the sky, and the violent shockwave swept out in all directions, wiping out a huge patch of ocean mutants at once. Torn limbs and broken shells flew through the air with splashing seawater, and the reek of blood filled the whole battlefield.

But there were way too many of them. As soon as one wave was wiped out, several more surged in, fearless and fighting back like crazy. Even when the cannons tore them apart, they kept coming with no sign of retreating.

The pressure on the defense line was still crushing, and the brutal fighting didn't let up.

Elijah climbed higher and quickly scanned the whole battlefield. "Everyone, be careful. Join the fight now."

Everyone took the order and rushed into battle.

At the very front of the high wall, Paisley stood quietly and watched.

A cold wind howled past, tossing her hair and making her clothes flap. A faint aura flowed around the Frostbloom Sword in her hand, giving it a sharp, menacing edge.

Beside her, the little white cat suddenly grew several times bigger, its fluffy white fur standing on end. Its usual sweetness was gone, and its cold, sharp eyes were locked on the surging horde below, its fierce aura swirling, ready to strike.

Paisley's eyes were calm as she looked down at the endless tide of mutants.

An organized, nonstop, crazy attack like this definitely wasn't simple. This was a carefully planned, man-made attack aimed right at them.

It reminded her of last night's strange zombie horde on the road, where countless zombies had moved like puppets on strings, ambushing them in perfect order.

Two identical controlled hordes had broken out back to back. It was obvious what the mastermind was after.

It was all because their group had gone deep into the underground lab. Whoever was behind this was afraid they'd found the core data from the top-secret experiment base.

They'd touched the enemy's deepest secret, so the enemy was willing to do anything, setting up traps overnight and sending zombies and ocean mutants to hunt them down again and again. It was all to wipe them out, erase everyone who knew the truth, and bury the crimes behind this man-made apocalypse.

The key to breaking this deadly trap at the base was finding the controller hiding in the shadows.

Paisley didn't hesitate. She released her massive, pure psychic power, and it spread like a huge invisible net over the whole sea, the mudflats, and the hidden reefs, searching every inch.

Just then, the commanding officer, who'd been one of the team members on yesterday's underground mission, saw Elijah. He straightened up right away and gave a respectful salute. "Colonel Blackwood."

The officer looked out at the endless waves of mutant sea beasts beyond the wall, the roars and explosions ringing in his ears, and his face was extremely grim. He couldn't hide how anxious he was.

"This is the first time since the base was built that we've faced an attack this huge from mutant sea beasts. If this keeps up, the defense line won't hold much longer, and the base will fall... Colonel Blackwood, do you have a way to stop this?"

Elijah narrowed his eyes and looked down at the battlefield, his gaze dark and cold.

Everywhere he looked, countless Affinity users were fighting for their lives, covered in blood. But people kept collapsing from exhaustion or running out of power, falling to the beasts' sharp claws and fangs. Warm blood kept staining the seawater on the mudflats red, and it was horrifying to see.

Meanwhile, the mutant sea beasts kept charging like crazy. They felt no pain and knew no fear. Like puppets pulled by strings, they kept ramming, chewing, and destroying, wave after wave, refusing to retreat.

"This looks a lot like last night's zombie horde, so they might be under someone's control too," Elijah said, frowning hard, his voice low and heavy. He was already preparing for the worst. "Maybe all we need to do is find whoever's controlling them."

The commanding officer remembered last night's zombie horde, and his face grew even grimmer.
