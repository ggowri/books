Chapter 42

Rachel had barely made it out alive, running for her life, and she'd made a total fool of herself.

Meanwhile, Paisley, the girl she'd always looked down on, had been hiding how strong she was this whole time. She'd one-shot a Level Two mutant, and everyone loved her for it.

It had been the same in her past life. Even without an ability, Paisley had always found her own way to kill zombies.

And now, she was still just an ordinary person, but she could take out a Level Two mutant in a heartbeat.

Rachel couldn't stand it.

Jealousy and the sting of falling so far behind were eating her alive. She was shaking all over, and her nails dug so deep into her palms that they bled, but she didn't even notice.

Hatred spread through her like poison ivy, wrapping itself tighter around her heart by the second.

Paisley has to die. I'll find a way to get rid of her.

Off to the side, Mason stood frozen with his machete half-raised, totally in awe.

Just how strong was this quiet, sweet-looking girl? He couldn't figure her out. No wonder even Elijah thought so highly of her.

Her skills spoke for themselves, and he had to respect that.

Orion slowly lowered his arm, and the tension drained out of him. His fear and worry gave way to pure pride.

Damn, that's my sister!

She'd never needed his protection. She was strong enough to take care of herself, and even the people around her.

Elijah stood still as the last sparks of lightning faded back into his body, and his intimidating aura disappeared.

His eyes were fixed on Paisley. A faint smile flickered across his face and was gone, but deep down, he was seriously impressed.

He'd been right about her. If anything, she was way more impressive and mysterious than he'd imagined.

She looked quiet and delicate, like someone who needed protecting.

But she was an incredible fighter, and she was cooler under pressure than anyone her age had any right to be. She kept her real strength hidden, but when it counted, she was ruthless. Every side of her kept catching him off guard.

Paisley didn't care one bit about the stares or what anyone was thinking.

She put the core away, her face as cool as ever. To her, one-shotting a Level Two mutant was about as impressive as squashing a bug.

Meanwhile, Orion and Mason quickly took out the rest of the ordinary zombies.

Moments later, there was nothing left outside the west building but carnage and the faint smell of blood.

Elijah held up a glossy, dark green Level Two crystal core, packed with energy. It had come from the mutant he'd killed earlier.

He walked over and held it out to Paisley. "As promised," he said in his low, cool voice. "This one's yours."

Paisley glanced at it, then took it without hesitating. They'd agreed on this, so there was no reason to turn it down.

When she took the core, Elijah's smile deepened a little. Then, he pulled himself together and walked over to Jasper, who was still on the ground.

"We're heading down to the basement storeroom next for supplies and equipment," he said, glancing at the two of them. "Can you two still move? Are you coming with us, or do you want to wait in the car?"

Jasper was just starting to come out of his shock. His back throbbed, his arms and legs felt like jelly, and he was scared to death of zombies now.

All the swagger he'd walked in with was gone.

He'd brought eight elite fighters with abilities, and now the only ones left were him and Rachel, who was useless in a fight.

When it came down to life or death, the mission didn't mean a thing. No mission or scheme was worth more than staying alive.

He took a shaky breath and gave up on going any deeper. "We're not going," he said, sounding exhausted and scared. "You guys go ahead, and we'll wait in the car."

Rachel started shaking all over again. "Yeah, we're not going," she said without even thinking.
