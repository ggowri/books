Chapter 216

Paisley took every transfer without a shred of hesitation or false modesty.

She smiled a little, not quite believing she'd become this rich this fast.

Worth every point.

Next time, she'd be stocking up on a few more of these guns.

No one objected. Not even close.

In a world like this, weapons that could flip a fight and crush high-tier beasts were priceless. Most bases couldn't have bought one even if they'd thrown every resource they had at it.

Selling it to them this cheap was already an enormous favor on her part.

Half an hour later, the group had finished resting.

Elijah pulled up the mission map, his voice low and steady:

"The core data and precision equipment are stored less than twenty yards from here.

Straight ahead. Keep moving."

Everyone snapped to attention and advanced carefully down the dim, damp tunnel.

Ten yards in, six grotesque humanoid shapes blocked their path.

All of them shared the same half-human, half-tendril form as the creature that had fled earlier, dread pressing down on the group in layers, sealing off the entire tunnel.

At the front of the six stood a stooped, white-haired old man, a mutant too, radiating the most hostility and the heaviest pressure of all.

Before anyone could strike, the old man lifted his head slowly. His clouded eyes shone with something close to madness, and he spoke, his voice rasping and strange with echo:

"Welcome to my world."

His gaze swept over them, lingering on the glow of their Affinities. Greed flickered in his eyes, and he let out a cold laugh.

"More test subjects, and all Affinity users this time. Strong bodies, plenty of power. Perfect material for an even better specimen."

The truth landed like a punch to the gut. Everyone's spine went cold.

Every mutant in this underground facility, every twisted, undying horror, hadn't come from the collapse of the world at all. Someone had made them.

Every monstrous experiment down here existed to chase stronger power, faster regeneration, maybe even the immortality humanity had always dreamed of.

The old man's voice had the fervor of a true believer, as if what he'd done counted as a kindness:

"Everything I've done is for the survival of our species. For evolution. It's for your own good, too."

Elijah's face went cold and hard, fury burning in his eyes as he fired back:

"You slaughter your own kind, experiment on living people, breed monsters that murder survivors, and you call lives you throw away nothing. You're not saving anyone, you're a murderer with blood on both hands."

"We're never going to become whatever these things are."

The old man let out a sharp laugh at that, dropping all pretense of goodwill. Hostility surged off him, murderous intent locking onto every person in the tunnel.

"Stubborn fools. If words won't reach you, let's see what strength has to say."

He threw up a hand and barked out an order:

"Take them! Tear them apart!"

The instant the command landed, all six half-human creatures erupted into motion, dark-teal tendrils thrashing and writhing, spreading across the walls and floor, poison dripping and hissing where it touched the ground. A wave of killing intent crushed the air out of everyone's lungs.

The old man hovered above them, eyes full of absolute control and contempt, his voice dripping with mockery:

"Give up. You're no match for them. Every struggle you make is wasted effort."

Elijah's eyes went hard. He lifted the energy gun with its freshly loaded crystal core and fired without hesitation.

A brilliant blue beam tore through the air, carrying blistering heat and force, slamming into the nearest creature with devastating power.

BANG—

The blast cracked through the tunnel, light flaring.

The creature at the front reacted fast, a thick tendril snapping up to catch the bolt barehanded.

The beam detonated on contact, the shockwave shredding the tendril instantly, flesh and poison spraying in every direction.

But even watching a hit strong enough to kill a high-tier beast, the old man at the front didn't flinch. He threw his head back and laughed, voice thick with scorn:

"Useless! My creations can't be killed!"

"You're just wasting your precious bullets!"

The words had barely left him when another sight, one that set every scalp crawling, began to unfold.
