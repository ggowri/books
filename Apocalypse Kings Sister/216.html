Chapter 216

Paisley didn't play coy or turn down any of the contribution points pouring in. She took every single one.

She smiled, a little amazed she'd gotten this rich so fast. That trade was so worth it. Next time, I'm getting more of these energy guns.

Everyone saw it, and nobody objected at all.

In the apocalypse, top-tier treasures like these, which could turn a battle around and crush high-level beasts, were almost impossible to buy at any price. An ordinary base could pour all its resources in and still not get a single one.

Paisley selling them this cheap was already a huge favor.

Half an hour later, everyone was done resting.

Elijah pulled up the mission map, his voice low. "The core files and precision equipment are less than 25 yards from here, right up ahead. Keep moving."

Everyone stayed on high alert and moved carefully down the dark, damp tunnel.

They'd only gone about 10 yards when six strange-looking mutants suddenly blocked their way.

They all had the same half-human, half-tentacle shape and came from the same source as the mutant that had escaped earlier. Their eerie aura pressed down on everyone, layer after layer, and sealed off the whole tunnel.

At the front of the six stood a hunched, white-haired old mutant. His hostility was the strongest, and his aura was the most terrifying of all.

Before anyone could make a move, the old man slowly raised his head. His murky eyes burned with madness and obsession, and to their shock, he spoke clearly in a dry, raspy voice that echoed strangely. "Welcome to my world."

He slowly looked them all over, and his gaze landed on the glow of power around them. Greed gleamed in his eyes, and he let out a cold laugh.

"I never expected so many new test subjects, and every one of you has powers. With bodies this strong and this much power, I can make even more perfect specimens."

Now that the truth was out, chills ran down everyone's spines.

All the mutants in this underground research lab, all the twisted, regenerating monsters that couldn't die, hadn't come from the apocalypse at all. Someone had made them in a lab!

Every cruel experiment down here was about chasing greater fighting power and healing, and even the eternal life people had always dreamed of.

The old man sounded obsessed and fanatical, like everything he'd done was a good deed. "Everything I've done is for the survival of humanity, for evolution, and for your own good too!"

Elijah's face went ice cold, and his eyes burned with anger as he snapped back. "You hurt your own kind, experiment on living people, and make monsters that slaughter survivors, like human lives mean nothing!

"This isn't about humanity at all. You're just a murderer with blood all over your hands!

"We'd never turn into monsters like you."

The old man scoffed, dropping his fake act of kindness for good. His hostility exploded, and he clearly wanted every one of them dead.

"Stubborn fools. If talking won't work, then we'll settle this with power."

He swung his hand sharply and barked an order. "Get them! Rip them to pieces!"

The moment he gave the order, the six half-human, half-tentacle mutants went wild. Countless dark blue-green tentacles thrashed, rolled, and squirmed, covering the walls and floor of the tunnel. Sticky poison kept dripping and ate into the floor with a hiss of white smoke, and their overwhelming savagery made everyone's breath catch.

The old man floated in midair behind them, looking down with total control and arrogance, and mocked them coldly. "Give up. You're no match for them, and all your struggling is useless."

Elijah's eyes turned cold. He raised the energy gun, now loaded with a fresh energy core, and pulled the trigger without hesitation.

A dazzling, powerful blue beam shot through the air, packed with intense heat and impact, and blasted into the front row of mutants with terrifying force.

With a deafening bang, the blast exploded, and flames rolled through the air.

The mutant in front reacted incredibly fast. It blocked with a thick, dark blue-green tentacle and caught the energy blast head-on.

The high-energy beam exploded on the spot, and the violent shockwave blew the whole tentacle to pieces. Bits of flesh and poison sprayed everywhere.

But even after seeing a hit strong enough to kill a high-level beast in one shot, the old man wasn't scared at all. He threw his head back and laughed wildly, full of contempt.

"It's useless! The creations I made with my own hands can't be killed! You're just wasting your precious bullets!"

The moment he finished, another hair-raising sight played out right in front of them.
