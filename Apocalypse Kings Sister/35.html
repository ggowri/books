Chapter 35

Paisley glanced at Rachel with total indifference, then turned to Jasper, her voice ice-cold. "You want my forgiveness? Fine.

"Have the woman next to you get down on her knees and apologize to me.

"This isn't the first time she's decided something for me, like she gets to forgive people on my behalf.

"I don't know her, and we're not friends. But she keeps butting in like she knows what I'm thinking. She has zero boundaries.

"Is this the kind of person you keep around, Mr. Quinn? She has no manners at all. Honestly, it's a joke."

The living room went dead silent.

It was like time had stopped. Every eye in the room snapped to Rachel, some curious, some amused, some mocking. Under all those stares, she froze, with nowhere to hide.

Her sweet, kind mask shattered. All the color drained from her face, leaving her white as a sheet.

She was burning with humiliation and resentment, and her nails nearly broke the skin of her palms.

Kneel? Me?

In front of everyone, she was supposed to get on her knees and apologize to Paisley, the worthless nobody she'd been walking all over for years?

How dare they?

This was worse than a slap in the face. It was the most humiliating thing she could imagine.

She bit down on her lip so hard it went white. She was seething inside, but she didn't dare let it show.

Who the hell did Paisley think she was?

But she knew Paisley held all the cards here.

If she didn't kneel and apologize, Paisley would never back down.

And she couldn't afford what would happen then. Jasper was arrogant and held grudges, so he'd take it all out on her.

It was either total humiliation or getting on the Quinns' bad side. She had no choice.

Jasper looked amused and didn't bother defending her. He lazily jerked his chin at her. "Do it," he said, and it wasn't a request.

To him, Rachel was just a handy pawn. If she could buy him a spot in this group, that was a great deal.

That was what pawns were for.

Rachel couldn't stop shaking. She was so furious she felt like her chest might explode, and every breath burned with humiliation.

She glared at Paisley, who stood there calm and collected with everyone protecting her. Jealousy and hate surged through Rachel until they nearly swallowed her whole.

How was this fair?

Paisley was a worthless nobody who couldn't do anything! Now, she got to stand there, all high and mighty, humiliating Rachel, while Rachel had to get down on her knees and apologize!

But with everyone staring and Jasper leaning on her, she had no way out.

In the end, in that dead-silent room, Rachel stiffly bent her knees and dropped onto the cold, hard floor with a heavy thud. Everyone heard her knees hit, and the sound was humiliating.

Her back was stiff as a board, and every inch of her screamed resentment and shame.

Her long hair fell forward and hid most of her face, leaving only her pale jaw showing. When she spoke, her voice came out dry and hoarse, every word forced through clenched teeth.

"I... I'm sorry. I was out of line. I had no right to decide anything for you, and I offended you."

Every word of that short apology hurt like hell to get out.

She knelt there, humiliated and pathetic, but something dark and bitter was already brewing inside her.

I'll never forget this, Paisley. I'll make you pay a thousand times over!

She'd take everything Paisley had and kill her with her own two hands. She'd drag Paisley through the mud until she knew exactly how it felt to be broken and humiliated, with nothing left.

The floor was ice-cold under her knees, and the chill crept up through her body, making the humiliation sting even more.

Paisley stood over her, looking down. This was only the beginning, and there was a lot more to come.

As long as Rachel was breathing, Paisley would make sure every day of her life was full of resentment and shame. Bit by bit, she'd crush Rachel's pride and every one of her delusions.

The more Rachel hated her, the harder she'd fight back. That would only make this revenge more fun.

Paisley let the coldness fade from her face. "Whatever, this is boring," she said lazily. "Fine, you can come."

Rachel pushed herself stiffly off the floor and slowly stood up.

She kept her head down, her messy hair hiding her pale, hateful face, and her shoulders wouldn't stop shaking.

She'd never forget what Paisley had done to her today.
