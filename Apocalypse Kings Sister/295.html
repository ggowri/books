Chapter 295

Paisley lifted her gaze, raised a hand, and let a soft, pure green glow flow from her palm, her Plant Affinity's purifying energy spreading out gently to envelop the entire mountain of crystals.

The clear green light seeped into every crystal's structure, carefully loosening and washing away the hidden impurities and dark contamination within.

Plant Affinity's purifying nature was pushed to its fullest extent, the stubborn black residue dissolving away piece by piece, fully cleansed.

The whole storage space shimmered with circulating spirit energy, green light swirling thick in the air.

Five minutes later, the entire mountain of crystals had been fully purified.

What had once looked dull and tainted now transformed completely, each crystal turning clear and radiant, catching the light in layered, dazzling brilliance, covering the ground like a glittering mountain of gemstones, breathtaking to look at.

A pure, untainted surge of energy filled the air.

Paisley crouched down, looking at the beautiful crystals before her, feeling an unexpected pang of reluctance.

But growing stronger, leveling up, mattered more than anything else.

Her resolve settled, firm and clear.

She sat down cross-legged at the center of the space.

She raised a hand, and the entire glittering mountain of crystals rose into the air at once, hovering around her, pouring dense, overwhelming energy into her body through every limb, gently yet relentlessly refining every channel and her core.

All four of her Affinities ran in sync, absorbing the crystals' pure energy together, grinding steadily toward the next breakthrough.

The higher the level, the thicker and more stubborn the bottleneck grew, the difficulty compounding with every step.

Even with this much energy backing her, progress came slowly, every sliver of advancement demanding vast amounts of energy to refine.

Time passed quietly, thick spirit energy wrapping around her, refining layer by layer, building her foundation stronger with every pass.

Eventually, the last of the floating crystal energy had been fully absorbed.

Paisley let out a long breath and settled her power, the glow of her Affinities drawing inward, her presence growing steadier and deeper.

With this batch of crystals fully absorbed, all four of her Affinities had made modest gains.

Her Psychic and Water Affinities rose from early Level Six to mid-Level Six.

Her Plant Affinity, already at mid-Level Six, advanced further, its vitality, purification, and control all climbing higher, its nourishing and cleansing effects stronger than before.

Her unique Level Seven mutant flame also progressed slightly, the fire growing more concentrated and commanding, its burning, dissolving, and corruption-breaking power significantly stronger.

These were small steps on paper, but each one came from a solid, deep foundation.

Every small advancement at this level marked a genuine transformation, bringing real gains in combat strength, precision, and energy reserves.

Now, her four Affinities worked in perfect harmony, her core brimming with dense energy, her mental range vast and refined, her physical strength and overall power climbing yet another level.

Her presence had grown increasingly unreadable, every trace of sharpness tucked carefully away.

Trovie's cheerful chime rang out.

Congratulations, Host, on the significant boost to your overall strength!

You still have two lucky draws left. Want to use them now?

This round's prize pool is excellent! Prizes include: Affinity Awakening Pills, Beauty Pills, Vitality Pills, Invisibility Talismans, Misfortune Talismans, Teleportation Talismans, and more rare items!

Paisley slowly opened her eyes, light settling deep and composed within them, and let out a slow breath of spirit energy.

Though she still felt a small pang of regret over turning an entire mountain of top-tier crystals to ash, the surge of power now coursing through her, far beyond anything she'd had before, swept that regret away completely.

There really was nothing like the feeling of growing stronger and taking control, step by step, in this world. That was real peace of mind.

Hearing about the prizes, a flicker of interest crossed Paisley's eyes.

Draw twice.

The wheel began to spin.

Ding! Premium draw activated! First draw complete! Congratulations, Host, you've won: one Affinity Awakening Pill!

Second draw complete! Congratulations, Host, you've won: five Invisibility Talismans!

The two rewards were announced one after another, an assortment of rare items materializing in front of her.

Paisley looked over the generous haul, pleased with her luck today.

Invisibility Talismans were valuable for survival, for sneak attacks, for quick escapes, able to turn the tide in a crisis or dodge a near-certain death.

But the most precious of all was that pearly white, spirit-energy-laced Affinity Awakening Pill.

This was a pill that could awaken any Affinity of her choosing.
