Chapter 148

Rows of cornfields stood lush and green, with thick stalks and plump ears.

Peanut vines spread across the fields, rooted deep in the rich soil.

Long rows of sweet potatoes grew thick and leafy, their roots swelling underground. The whole farm was bursting with life and green as far as the eye could see.

Paisley thought about what the princess and the interstellar trader needed and raised a finger. Warm, pure green Plant energy flowed out and gently covered the whole farm.

The pure power of growth seeped through every leaf, stem, and root into the rich soil. It was powerful yet gentle, and it pushed every crop to ripen at top speed.

Right before her eyes, green fruit turned red, shriveled fruit swelled up, and tender leaves unfurled. The wheat headed out and filled in, the corn fattened up, and the peanuts and sweet potatoes grew plump underground.

In just a few minutes, the whole huge farm was ready for harvest.

The sweet smell of fruit, the rich scent of ripe grain, and the fresh smell of vegetables mixed together and filled the whole dimension.

Heavy fruit bent the branches low, plump heads of wheat drooped, the corn was packed with kernels, and the root vegetables underground were thick and full. Everywhere she looked, it was a bumper crop, and it was amazing to see.

Paisley looked at the huge harvest in front of her and felt deeply satisfied. Her whole face relaxed into a happy smile.

Level Six, and I can pull this off on a whole farm? I love it.

Right then, Trovie's cute little chime went off on cue.

[Congratulations, Master! You've finished your first full-scale accelerated planting in the dimension and unlocked a farming achievement! Bonus: 5,000 points!

[Current total: 165,060 points!]

Paisley raised an eyebrow, surprised and happy. She hadn't expected to get bonus points just for farming her dimension.

She turned to Trovie, who was floating next to her, and gave it quiet instructions.

"Trovie, could you harvest and store all the ripe fruit, vegetables, and grain? Sort it all, then replant everything and keep the cycle going."

Then, she flicked her fingers, took out a bright, sweet fruit lollipop, and gently popped it into Trovie's little mouth.

With the sweet lollipop in its mouth, Trovie happily wiggled its whole body. It was all fired up now and answered loud and clear.

[No problem! Trovie promises to get it done!]

Once Trovie was taking care of the farm, Paisley reached out and gently stroked the swaying green willow branches beside her.

The willow had just helped speed up the whole farm, and its branches were full of energy, more flexible and greener than ever. Under her gentle touch, they slowly relaxed and drooped down obediently at her side.

Then, she walked into the villa's living room and sat down on the soft leather sofa.

In front of her were two very different piles of crystals.

On the left were the pure Beast Realm energy crystals. They were crystal clear, and their energy was clean, rich, and gentle, without any impurities. She could actually see lively energy swirling around them.

On the right was a huge pile of Galactic energy crystals. They were top-grade and packed with power, but each one was coated in a dull, gray layer of grime. Inside, they carried traces of radiation and wild, unstable energy left over from cosmic rays.

If a normal ability user absorbed contaminated crystals like these, the best case was that their ability would go haywire. The worst case was radiation damage, a ruined foundation, or a backlash that left them badly hurt and knocked their level back down.

Fully purifying them usually took several times the effort and complicated techniques. It took forever, and it hardly ever worked.

But now, this problem that had stumped countless high-level fighters was a piece of cake for Paisley, with her Plant Affinity at Level Six.

Paisley slowly closed her eyes, steadied her breathing, and focused. A faint green glow rose gently around her.

The pure, powerful life force of her Level Six Plant Affinity quietly spread out. It formed a soft, delicate veil of energy that covered the five huge crates of Galactic energy crystals in front of her.

Plant Affinity had always been about life and growth, and it could wash away corruption and filth. Now that she'd hit Level Six, her purifying power had taken a huge leap. It was powerful yet gentle, and it could purify almost anything.

Right before her eyes, the gray grime and chaotic radiation on the crystals melted away like snow in the sun. Wisps of black, murky energy were pulled out, broken down, and cleansed by the green glow.

The flawed Galactic crystals visibly shed their grime and turned clearer and brighter little by little. The wild energy inside them calmed down completely, and they became gentle, pure, top-grade crystals with no side effects at all.
