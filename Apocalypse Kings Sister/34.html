Chapter 34

The second they saw who was at the door, everyone tensed up.

It was Jasper, who'd been tailing them the whole way. He had four or five people with him, and Rachel was one of them.

Jasper leaned against the doorframe, looking bored. "My brother wants me to go to the hospital with you guys and help bring back the equipment," he said, like it was the last thing he wanted to do.

According to his brother, that piece of precision medical equipment was crucial. It might be the key to cracking the zombie virus and figuring out the mutations.

If there was any chance at all, Jasper had to get to it first and bring it back to the Quinns.

He'd had to pull a lot of strings to reach his brother in Aetheris. The Quinns only had a few contacts left, and he'd used them to get this top-secret intel.

Obviously, he wasn't about to tell these people any of that.

He had it all planned out. With a high-level Lightning fighter like Elijah clearing the way, he wouldn't have to risk his own neck.

He'd just tag along. Once they'd fought their way through and found the equipment, he'd make his move, kill whoever he had to, and take it. They'd do all the work, and he'd get the prize.

But if he didn't go with them, he'd worry the whole time that they wouldn't find it, and he'd let his brother down.

So he had to go and see it with his own eyes.

Inside, Elijah's face darkened as he took it all in.

He'd only just gotten the same top-secret mission himself, to retrieve the virus analysis equipment from the hospital. He hadn't expected the Quinns to hear about it so fast.

Things in Aetheris were clearly a lot murkier than he'd thought.

And he knew exactly what Jasper was up to.

Elijah glanced at Paisley beside him for a long moment, then made his position clear.

"You want to come with us? Fine. But only if my sister's okay with it.

"If you can get her to forgive you, you can come along."

Jasper blinked and raised an eyebrow. "Since when do you have a sister?"

Without missing a beat, Elijah gently pulled Paisley forward. "Her. You were way out of line with her this morning.

"Apologize to her properly, and promise you'll never try anything with her or bother her again.

"If she forgives you, we're fine with you coming along."

Orion stepped up to back him. "That's right. If Paisley's willing to forgive you, I'm fine with it too.

"Otherwise, you're on your own."

Paisley was a little caught off guard. She'd never expected Elijah to care this much about how she felt, or to put what she wanted first.

She looked past Jasper, straight at Rachel, who was scheming behind him, and her eyes went cold.

Come along? I'd love nothing more.

The road to the hospital would be crawling with zombies, which made it the perfect chance for revenge.

If they wanted to walk right into her hands, great. She could start making them pay, for the old grudges and the new ones.

Jasper didn't bat an eye. He dropped the swagger and turned to Paisley with a cheesy, sheepish grin. "Okay, okay, my bad.

"Sorry about this morning, gorgeous. I was way out of line.

"I promise I'll never disrespect you or try anything with you again. Come on, be the bigger person and let this one slide?"

Jasper knew when to back down. If it got him that equipment, an apology cost him nothing.

But behind him, Rachel was clenching her fists so hard her nails dug into her palms.

How the hell does she keep getting this lucky?

Since when did Paisley have powerful, well-connected people like Elijah and Orion in her corner?

Even an arrogant jerk like Jasper had to back down to them!

These people clearly had serious power, and nobody wanted to mess with them.

Rachel's greed surged.

Today, no matter what, she was going to find her chance and take that pendant off Paisley.

She stepped forward, putting on her sweetest, kindest face. "Paisley, Mr. Quinn already said he's sorry, and you didn't really get hurt. Can't you just let it go and forgive us this once?"
