Chapter 102

The three of them worked together perfectly and quickly took down the two Level Four zombies.

But right then, the Level Four Psychic zombie, which hadn't moved until now, suddenly went for the kill!

A silent psychic attack exploded outward, covering the whole area.

Terrifying psychic pressure blanketed the battlefield. It locked onto Elijah and Orion, forced its way into their minds, and tried to take control of them.

It wanted to force them to turn on Paisley, who was standing right next to them with her guard down!

After the long, brutal fight, both men's energy had already been running on empty, and now it was even lower.

That made their minds a lot easier to take over.

The Psychic zombie saw its chance and went for it. Elijah and Orion went rigid at the same time. Murky black fog clouded their eyes as their minds were dragged under its control.

Bone-deep psychic pain ripped through their bodies. Every muscle went tight, veins bulged on their necks and the backs of their hands, and their faces twisted as their minds were torn apart from the inside.

Invisible psychic chains wrapped tight around their minds, trying to force their bodies to attack Paisley, who was right there within arm's reach.

The Level Four Psychic zombie kept pushing harder, pouring pitch-black fog into their minds. It wanted to crush their will completely, take total control, and use them to kill Paisley.

But it had underestimated how strong their will was, and even more, how much Paisley meant to them.

Even in unbearable agony and under the zombie's control, part of them still knew they could never hurt her.

They could handle any amount of pain, and they'd hurt themselves before they'd ever lay a finger on Paisley.

They fought back with everything they had left, resisting the dark force clawing through their minds.

The two forces, one inside and one outside, ripped at each other, and the pain got even worse. It felt like a thousand needles were stabbing into their brains.

Thin trails of blood leaked from the corners of their mouths, and they were on the verge of breaking.

Paisley's face hardened. She called on her Psychic Affinity right away and threw up a mental barrier, forcing her way in to try to save them.

But the gap between their levels was huge. She was only a Level Three Psychic user, and the Level Four Psychic zombie was way stronger.

All Paisley could do was loosen the zombie's grip a little. She couldn't break its control completely.

The leader's black fog surged even harder. The two men struggled more and more desperately, and the pain kept getting worse.

Paisley could see they were about to break. At the last possible second, she whipped around and screamed for the little white cat.

"Kitty!"

The second she shouted, a blinding streak of white shot out from the side!

The tiny, fluffy little white cat leaped into the air and swelled into a giant 16-foot cat in a heartbeat.

Its fur was thick and fluffy as snow, and freezing energy swirled around it. Its power exploded across the whole battlefield.

The giant cat had the Frostbloom Sword clamped in its sharp teeth. It shot through the air like a white bolt of lightning, straight at the Psychic zombie leader!

The Frostbloom Sword already had its own frost spirit and the power to slay evil. Now, powered up by the cat's Ice Affinity, it was twice as strong.

The cat zeroed in on the crystal core inside the zombie's skull. It twisted in midair, and the sword's razor-sharp edge sliced the leader's skull wide open, ripping out the pitch-black Level Four Psychic core in one move!

With a loud crack, the skull shattered, and the crystal core dropped to the ground.

The mighty Level Four Psychic zombie leader that had been controlling the whole horde went rigid. The black fog around it vanished, and its huge body crashed to the ground and never moved again.

The second the leader died, the entire horde lost its orders.

The zombies had been organized and fearless, but now they fell into total chaos. They lost their targets and wandered off aimlessly in every direction.

The danger was over.

Everyone on the team stood frozen, staring at what had just happened. Their eyes were wide with shock and disbelief.

That Level Four Psychic zombie had just been killed in one shot by a little cat carrying a sword in its mouth!

Nobody could've guessed that the hero who turned the whole hopeless fight around and wiped out the massive horde would be the soft, spoiled little white cat that usually snuggled in Paisley's arms.

Paisley really was a freak of nature. Even her pet was on a whole different level.

Once Elijah and Orion were free of its control, they were both drenched in cold sweat.
