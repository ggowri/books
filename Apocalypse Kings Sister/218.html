Chapter 218

The tunnel went still, leaving nothing behind but ruined bodies and spilled venom.

The others finally snapped out of their daze, staring at the scene, too stunned for words.

Someone's throat worked, voice distant:

"They're all... dead?"

"They could regenerate from nothing. Even severed limbs grew back in seconds. And they just died like that?"

The rest of the squad murmured among themselves, their voices full of nothing but awe now.

"Ms. Lennox is unbelievable."

"This morning when she joined the team, I'll admit I had a problem with it. New girl, young, why's she getting special treatment, figured she'd just slow us down. Turns out she's the real deal, hiding in plain sight."

"She's on another level. Not just the healing, pulling people back from the edge of death, but her combat skill crushes everyone here. She saved all of us."

...

The praise kept rolling in, and every one of them meant it.

Elijah's gaze settled heavy on the girl standing tall and composed nearby, his admiration for her only deepening.

He understood it clearly now. Her foresight, her judgment, her raw combat power and killing instinct put her far ahead of everyone in this squad, himself included.

She'd never needed anyone's protection. She was the one strong enough to stand on her own, stronger than him, stronger than the whole team combined.

Something warm and bright flickered in his eyes. His feelings for her ran even deeper now.

Orion reached over and ruffled Paisley's hair, pride breaking into a grin he couldn't hold back.

This was his little sister. Calm, powerful, stepping up every single time things went sideways to shield everyone, to shield him.

He felt nothing but pure pride in her.

The group gathered themselves and pressed on.

They moved carefully through the dim tunnel for another five yards before the space opened up in front of them.

A massive underground lab came into view.

Even before they stepped inside, something cold and suffocating hung in the air, pressing down on their chests, chilling them to the bone.

The lab stretched out across roughly five thousand square feet, dark and menacing.

Rusted iron cages lined every wall, most of them with doors hanging corroded and broken, dried black-brown bloodstains smeared across more than a few.

The cages had once held all kinds of mutated creatures, kept alive purely for experimentation.

******

Rows of sealed containers lined a central lab table, each holding one of over thirty long-dead test subjects, grotesque fusions of human and beast, twisted into shapes that turned the stomach just to look at.

Along the tabletops and inside storage cabinets sat rows of strange chemical vials, crimson, dark green, deep violet, each liquid settled into layers, every one of them radiating a sense of danger.

Deep inside the lab, a small private office had been walled off.

Paisley spotted it first, stepping inside to find precision equipment, paper files, and digitized records, exactly what their mission had been sent to find.

Her gaze swept over the neat stacks of experiment files, fingers brushing the cold paper, her expression darkening by the second.

She turned to Elijah, who'd followed her in, and asked, voice tight with disbelief:

"Captain, you're telling me this is actually our mission target?"

"This is completely insane."

She tapped a line of text on one of the files, her voice dropping cold.

"This human-fusion program was already running in secret before the world even fell apart. And according to these records, there are more labs like this one scattered across the country."

Elijah's chest tightened. He took the file from her and flipped through it fast.

The deeper he read, the colder and more rigid his face became, the air around him dropping several degrees.

Every scattered clue clicked into place. The ugly truth was finally surfacing.

The situation was far worse, far more disturbing, than any of them had imagined.

He gripped the file tight and said, voice low:

"This is extremely dangerous. Until we understand exactly what we're dealing with, this stays between the two of us. No one else hears about it."

He already knew, deep down, that the truth was uglier than anyone wanted to admit.

This apocalypse hadn't been a natural disaster at all.

It was a war staged by people at the very top, people fighting to seize total control.
