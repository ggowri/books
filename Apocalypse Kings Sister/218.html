Chapter 218

The tunnel was finally calm again, with nothing left but mutant bodies and venom all over the floor.

Only then did the others come to, staring blankly at the scene. Words couldn't describe the shock they felt.

Someone swallowed hard and spoke in a daze. "They're all dead?"

"Those things could regenerate. Even blown-off limbs grew back in seconds, and they just died that easily?"

The other team members murmured to each other, nothing left in their voices but awe and admiration.

"Ms. Lennox is seriously amazing."

"When she joined the team this morning, I'll admit it bugged me, because she's new and just a girl. I figured she didn't deserve special treatment and would only slow us down, but now I get it. She's the real powerhouse, hiding in plain sight."

"Ms. Lennox is way too strong! Not only is she an amazing purifier and healer who pulls people back from certain death, but her fighting skills crush everyone here. She's fucking awesome, and she saved all of us!"

Praise rose from all around, and all they felt for Paisley now was genuine respect.

Elijah's eyes settled on Paisley, standing tall and cool, and he admired her more than ever.

Now, he saw it clearly. Paisley's vision, foresight, fighting power, and decisiveness were already way beyond everyone here, including him.

From start to finish, she'd never been a delicate flower who needed protecting. She was a true powerhouse, far stronger than him and the whole team, strong enough to handle anything on her own.

A bright light sparkled in his eyes, and his love for her grew much deeper.

Orion proudly patted Paisley's head, unable to stop grinning.

This was his little sister. She was calm and strong, and every time things got desperate, she stepped up again and again to protect everyone, including him.

He was so proud of her!

Everyone pulled themselves together and kept moving.

They moved carefully along the dark tunnel, and after about five yards, the space suddenly opened up in front of them.

A huge underground lab loomed ahead.

Before they even stepped inside, an eerie, oppressive, terrifying feeling hit them head-on. It squeezed the breath out of everyone and chilled them to the bone.

The whole lab was a good 5,000 square feet, dark and grim.

Countless iron cages were stacked all around the hall. Most of the cage doors were rusted and peeling, and quite a few cages still had dried, blackish-brown bloodstains inside.

The cages had held all kinds of mutant creatures, kept specifically for live experiments.

The central lab table held rows of sealed containers in all sizes. Inside were more than 30 test subjects that had died long ago, all deformed products of humans fused with beasts and monsters. They looked so twisted and horrifying that just seeing them sent chills down their spines.

The tabletops and storage cabinets were packed with all kinds of strange chemicals in crimson, dark green, and deep purple. The liquids had settled into layers, and every single one gave off an eerie sense of danger.

At the very back of the lab, a separate office had been walled off.

Paisley spotted it first and walked into the office. Precision equipment, paper files, and digital data archives were all gathered there, and they were exactly what the mission was after.

Her eyes swept over row after row of neatly stacked experiment files, and her fingertips brushed lightly across the cold paper as her expression darkened.

She turned to Elijah, who'd followed her in, and asked in disbelief, "Captain Blackwood, are you sure this is our mission target? This is beyond sick."

As she spoke, she pointed at a glaring line of text on the file, her voice cold and heavy. "It's obvious this brutal human-fusion experiment started in secret before the apocalypse even hit. And from these records, there are a lot more underground labs like this all over the country."

Elijah's heart lurched. He stepped forward right away, took the files, and flipped through them fast.

The more he read, the grimmer and colder his face got. The chill in his eyes deepened, and the air around him turned scarily tense.

All the scattered clues connected, and the brutal truth came to light.

The situation was far more complicated and disgusting than any of them had imagined.

He gripped the files tight and said in a low voice, "This is incredibly dangerous. Until we get to the bottom of it, only the two of us can know the truth about this place. It can't get out, no matter what."

Deep down, he knew the truth was brutal enough to send a chill down anyone's spine.

The so-called apocalypse wasn't a natural disaster at all.

It was a terrifying, sick game carefully planned by a group of people in high places who wanted total control over power and everything else.
