Chapter 136

She'd only come here to follow Elijah, to get close to him and stay by his side. Otherwise, she never would have bothered, let alone put up with a backwater like Rivergate.

And now, on top of all that, Paisley had slapped her in front of everyone.

It was the first time in her whole life anyone had ever slapped her in public and humiliated her like that!

Hatred consumed Kelly. She dug her nails into her palms and screamed silently.

I hate you, Paisley! I want you dead, and I'll make you pay a thousand times over!

Rachel was reeling too, full of shame, rage, and disbelief.

She was the Lennox family's true daughter. She'd always been on top, and she was used to pushing Paisley around.

In her mind, Paisley would always be that timid, lowly adopted daughter. She took whatever Rachel dished out and let herself be hit, yelled at, and bossed around without ever fighting back.

But now, the girl who used to do whatever Rachel said and put up with everything had actually hit her in public. She'd slapped Rachel without a shred of mercy!

Rachel pressed a hand to her burning cheek. She'd been completely humiliated, and shame, rage, and jealousy nearly swallowed her whole.

Paisley really had changed. She wasn't that lowly adopted daughter Rachel could push around, walk all over, and bully anymore.

In this new life, everything had been turned upside down.

Paisley was supposed to be ordinary and useless. Instead, she'd awakened a rare, powerful Plant Affinity, latched onto the top-tier Thompson family, and gotten her hands on tons of resources. She could crush Rachel without even trying.

And Rachel, the real daughter who was supposed to shine, kept falling behind her at every turn.

Rachel's eyes burned with almost crazy greed. She couldn't tear them away from the broken, gold-mended pendant on Paisley's chest.

She was sure of it.

It's the pendant. That's what changed everything!

Paisley's space, her affinity, her lucky breaks, and her crazy good fortune all came from that broken pendant!

If I can just get my hands on it, the space will still be mine, and all that luck will be mine too!

Jealousy and greed took over Rachel's mind. She threw caution to the wind and forgot the lesson she'd just learned.

If she couldn't steal it quietly, she'd just take it by force, right out in the open!

All it took was a drop of her blood to activate the space, and then it would belong to her.

After that, no matter how upset Paisley was, it wouldn't make any difference.

Her face twisted into something vicious. She gritted her teeth and lunged at Paisley with everything she had, reaching out to rip the pendant off her neck!

But she was still half a step away when several tough, sharp green willow branches whipped through the air and wrapped around her.

They moved so fast that she didn't stand a chance. They bound her arms, legs, and waist tight and held her up in midair.

Paisley's face was blank, and her eyes didn't even flicker. She gave a light flick of her fingers.

A burst of force exploded! The branches snapped tight and flung Rachel away with incredible strength.

Rachel flew several yards like a rag doll and slammed into the hard floor with a heavy thud.

The impact rocked her whole body. It felt like every bone in her had come apart, and the pain was unbearable. A coppery taste rose in her throat, and she coughed up a mouthful of blood.

Her back, elbows, and knees were scraped raw and bleeding, the skin torn open. She was a total mess.

Rachel lay sprawled on the floor, twitching all over. The pain was too much for her to even get up, her vision was going dark, and she could barely breathe.

Kelly stood there frozen, her eyes wide with shock and fear.

She'd thought Paisley was just teaching them a lesson. She'd never expected her to be this ruthless. Paisley didn't care about saving face for anyone, and she'd hit hard enough to do real damage.

When Kelly saw Rachel lying on the floor coughing up blood, fear swallowed most of her anger. She tensed up and stepped back without thinking, too scared to start anything again.

But under the fear, her jealousy burned even hotter.

Paisley's Plant Affinity was even scarier than Kelly had thought. With one casual strike, Paisley had badly hurt Rachel, a Level Three ability user.

It's not fair. Why does Paisley get all the perks and all the talent?

Fear, jealousy, and spite twisted together inside her, and her face kept flushing and paling. But she didn't dare mock Paisley like before. All she could do was glare at her and hold a grudge in silence.

On the floor, Rachel was shaking with pain. Blood kept trickling from the corner of her mouth, and her battered face was twisted with hatred.
