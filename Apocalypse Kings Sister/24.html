Chapter 24

With murder in his eyes, Jasper snapped his pistol up and aimed it right between Orion's eyes.

His face twisted with rage. "I don't give a shit who you are or who's got your back," he snarled. "You're not walking out of here alive!"

The barrel gleamed coldly, and the tension in the lobby shot through the roof.

Elijah's face darkened, and his Lightning Affinity crackled to life, ready to strike. Blue sparks flickered around him as he stepped silently to the front of the group. Just having him there made the air feel heavy.

The Thompsons closed ranks without a word, keeping Paisley safe in the middle. All of them were on high alert, ready to fight.

On the other side, Jasper's men tensed up too. Some raised their guns, and the ones with abilities started powering up, faint colored lights flickering around them.

The two sides stared each other down. One wrong move, and all hell would break loose.

That was when Rachel stepped forward, right on cue. She was still playing the sweet, innocent victim, but there was malice hidden behind her eyes.

She gave Jasper a timid look, then glanced at Paisley. "Mr. Quinn, I'm so sorry. This is all Paisley's fault.

"She's always been stubborn and impulsive. She just does whatever she wants and never thinks about anyone else.

"This whole mess is because of her. Please don't be mad. I'll make her apologize to you right now."

Then, she turned to Paisley, acting like she was only looking out for her. "Paisley, apologize to Mr. Quinn right now!

"You're the one who started this. If a fight breaks out and somebody gets hurt, that's on you. Can you really live with that?

"Just say you're sorry and end this before it gets any worse!"

The three Lennox brothers jumped in to back her up right away. They didn't care about the years they'd spent with Paisley. All they wanted was to suck up to Jasper and drag her down.

"She's right, Paisley! You're way out of line! Look at the mess you made!"

"Apologize to Mr. Quinn right now! Or you're gonna get us all killed!"

"Surviving the apocalypse is hard enough without you being so damn selfish! Get over there and apologize!"

The three of them took turns piling on, falling over each other to blame Paisley.

They knew exactly what was at stake.

If this turned into a real fight, the gunshots and the noise from the abilities would draw every zombie for miles.

None of the three brothers had awakened an ability yet. They couldn't fight, and they had no way to protect themselves. If a fight broke out, they were as good as dead.

To save their own skins and stay on Jasper's good side, they'd happily throw Paisley under the bus. They'd twist the truth and force her to apologize without thinking twice.

After all, they'd done this kind of thing plenty of times before.

To them, Paisley was just the adopted daughter. She had no one to back her up, so she was easy to push around and easy to throw away.

As far as they were concerned, she should be honored to sacrifice herself for them. That was the whole reason she existed.

So, like always, they shoved her out front to clean up the mess.

Ashley watched the Lennoxes' ugly, two-faced act and lost it. Small as she was, she planted herself in front of Paisley with her hands on her hips. She glared at Rachel and the others, her face red with anger.

"Wow, you people have some nerve! Why the hell should Paisley apologize?

"She hasn't said one word this whole time! What, is being pretty a crime now?

"That creep was the one looking at her like a piece of meat, and Orion just warned him! So who's really starting trouble here? Are you all blind?

"You're a bunch of spineless suck-ups. Now you want her to apologize just to save your own asses? How does that make any sense?

"You're all trash. How can anyone be this shameless?"

Ashley didn't hold back one bit, calling out the Lennoxes in front of everyone.
