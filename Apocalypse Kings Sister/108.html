Chapter 108

Every building, alley, and corner showed up in her mind at once, along with the level of every zombie hiding nearby.

In the whole area on the outskirts, the strongest zombie was only Level Four. That was nothing they needed to worry about.

She made a suggestion right away.

"Let's find somewhere clean and comfortable out here to stay tonight. While it's still light, we should focus on grabbing supplies.

"Check the docks and the riverside shops especially. See if we can find any boats that still work. Once it's light out tomorrow and we can see what we're dealing with, we'll cross the water into the city."

After the past two days, everyone on the team trusted Paisley completely.

Whatever she decided, they'd follow without question.

"Let's do what Pais says!"

"Let's go get supplies right now!"

Everyone split up fast and got to work. Elijah and Orion took the lead, clearing the way with lightning and metal. They wiped out the stray low-level zombies along the road and took care of any threats.

The group knew exactly where they were going and headed straight for the biggest shopping mall in the area.

The mall used to be the heart of Rivergate's shopping district. It had five floors packed with a supermarket, clothing stores, household goods, snacks, and everything else.

Most of the glass walls were shattered, and the front doors hung broken and wide open. Thick dust and junk were piled up at the entrance, and the wind howled through the empty halls. The whole place was bleak and lifeless.

But the building itself was still in one piece, with no collapsed sections. It was obvious that nobody had ever looted the place, and the supplies inside were still in great shape.

The group walked in together. The first floor was a huge supermarket.

The shelves were tilted and covered in thick dust, but they were hiding a massive stash of untouched supplies.

Flour, pasta, cooking oil, vacuum-sealed ready-to-eat meals, bottled water, candy, snacks, and compressed ration bars were stacked neatly in the corners of the shelves, under the counters, and deep in the stockrooms. There was a staggering amount of it.

The team split up and worked together. Spencer, Thomas, and the others spread out to clear the Level Two and Level Three zombies wandering the mall and guard every exit so nothing caught them by surprise.

Elijah stuck right by Paisley's side, protecting her every step of the way as she gathered supplies.

As Paisley walked through, everything useful she laid eyes on quietly disappeared into her Storage Dimension.

Gone! A whole shelf of flour, pasta, and cooking oil, cleared out completely.

Gone! Piles of vacuum-sealed meals, instant food, and bottled water, all of it.

Gone! Every kind of candy, snack, energy bar, and emergency supply.

In the blink of an eye, she'd cleaned out almost everything worth taking on the first floor. All that was left were empty shelves, garbage, and a few pieces of junk she'd left behind on purpose as decoys.

That was mostly to throw off Rachel. Paisley could hold her own in a fight now, but she didn't want anyone getting suspicious of her.

Next, the two of them headed straight to the household goods section on the second floor.

Rows of shelves filled the huge floor. There were brand-new toiletries, full bath sets, soft towels and bedding, storage bins, and cleaning supplies, and every bit of it was still in one piece.

Even after sitting there untouched through the apocalypse, everything was still dry, clean, and perfectly usable.

Paisley lifted her hand and gave it a light sweep. An invisible force from her Storage Dimension spread out and covered every shelf on the floor.

Everything useful in sight, from full sets of supplies to stockpiles of everyday items, vanished. The packed shelves were empty in a flash, and every essential item ended up in her Storage Dimension.

Then, Paisley and Elijah went up to the clothing and bedding section on the third floor.

This floor had everything. There were clothes for every season, windproof and heavy-duty jackets, waterproof workwear, warm hoodies, shoes, accessories, bedding, sheets, and pillowcases in every style and size.

Paisley took everything practical and tough enough for the apocalypse, from cold-weather gear and waterproof quick-dry clothes to non-slip, heavy-duty boots.

She was careful, too. She left behind a pile of outdated, bulky, useless clothes and broken accessories, scattering them across the shelves to make it look like someone had already picked through the place.

Next, they went up to the tools and equipment section on the fourth floor.

The shelves were neatly stocked with thick, heavy-duty rope, rustproof clips, full repair tool sets, waterproof sealing gear, portable lights, and emergency survival gear. Every single item was a must-have for camping out, long road trips, and handling emergencies.
