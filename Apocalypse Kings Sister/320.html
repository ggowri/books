Chapter 320

Reid's face went white, his grip on the bowl tightening until his knuckles went pale, jealousy and regret tearing through him in waves.

Ryan looked up, his eyes carrying a clear-eyed exhaustion and a regret that ran bone-deep. He spoke quietly.

"Brother, we need to stop going after Paisley."

"Everything changed the moment she cut ties with the family and we threw her out. Every single time we've tried to approach her since then, hoping for something, we've ended up hurt, humiliated, worse off than before."

"Paisley's not who she used to be."

"She's not that careful, deferential girl who used to tiptoe around us anymore, taking whatever we dished out, trying to earn our approval."

"She's untouchable now. Power, money, none of it's within our reach. We go after her again, and whatever happens, we won't be able to handle the fallout."

"I heard Rachel's back at the base this afternoon. Didn't she say she'd give us points once she got back?"

"Let's go find her first thing tomorrow. We can't keep waiting on this for Owen."

Reid just nodded, saying nothing.

The night wind cut through the gaps in the tattered tent, bitterly cold, the weak firelight flickering under the draft.

A threadbare room, bodies covered in wounds, a brother sick in bed, and regret with nowhere left to go.

The next morning.

Right after breakfast, an emergency alert dropped onto everyone's terminal at once.

A sudden mission. Full assembly required.

Per the base's latest assignment, the strongest three-person team for this high-risk mission had been finalized — Elijah, Paisley, and Orion.

They were ordered to report immediately to the command center for a briefing.

The three of them had already been ready to move at a moment's notice. They cleaned up quickly and headed straight to the command center.

The hall was vast and solemn, lit bright, the air tight with tension, like something was about to break.

The moment Paisley stepped inside, her gaze swept the room, and her brow tightened almost imperceptibly.

She hadn't expected to see Chase and Rachel here, let alone find them folded into this elite mission team.

The two of them stood among a fifteen-person elite squad, dressed in matching base combat gear, looking the part on the surface — but the pettiness and calculation in their eyes gave them away instantly.

The instant Rachel caught sight of Paisley, her expression froze, jealousy, fear, and resentment flashing across her face before she shrank back toward Chase, avoiding eye contact.

She'd learned her lesson, at least.

Paisley had changed. She wasn't the same person from before.

And every time Rachel went up against her, she came out the loser.

Chase looked even worse — a mix of fear and resentment on his face, clearly terrified they'd expose what he'd done back in Rivergate. Whatever arrogance he used to carry was long gone.

Nineteen hand-picked Affinity users filled the hall, every one of them vetted and elite, their presence sharp, each one locked in and ready.

Moments later, the base director walked in, Gabriel Sterling close behind him, composed and upright.

Gabriel wore a black combat uniform today, his posture sharp, shoulders square, no trace of yesterday's frailty left. His eyes were cold and clear, the full weight of his Level Six Metal Affinity pressing into the room.

The director stepped to the front, his gaze sweeping the room, his tone heavy and serious.

"This mission is rated 3S — high-risk, unpredictable, with major unknowns. Follow protocol and come back alive."

He stepped aside, gesturing to the man beside him with real weight.

"This is Gabriel Sterling, Stillwater's deputy director and our strongest asset. He's taking full command of this mission."

"He's already scouted the target location personally and knows the layout, the toxin, and the danger better than anyone. Regardless of rank or experience, everyone follows his orders without question."

Nobody objected. The whole room bowed their heads in agreement.

Gabriel stepped forward, standing tall, his gaze sweeping the room with cold composure, his voice low and steady.

"Hello, everyone. I'm Gabriel Sterling."

"This mission carries serious risk. Man-made mutants, unknown toxins, traps — expect all of it inside that facility. I need everyone's full cooperation, everyone doing their part, trusting each other. Let's get this done and get everyone home safe."
