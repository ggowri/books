Chapter 214

For the poisoned team member, Paisley was the only hope left.

With the last bit of strength in his body, he struggled to lift a finger. Out of pure survival instinct, he forced out a broken, faint whisper. "I... I agree... Please... save me..."

The moment he finished, he couldn't hold on anymore. His head lolled to the side, his eyes half closed, and he sank into a half-conscious state. Only a faint thread of breath was left, and it could stop any second.

After that, nobody dared hesitate anymore. They snapped out of it, carefully lifted him, and hurried him over to a clean, flat spot nearby, laying him down gently.

"Hurry! Please save him!"

"We trust you! Please, help him!"

The team members stepped back to give her enough room. Their eyes stayed locked on Paisley, and they were torn between dread and hope.

Everyone held their breath and didn't dare make a sound, afraid of distracting her.

Elijah and Orion stood on the outer edge, their backs to the treatment area. Without a word, they kept watch over the dark tunnel around them.

They blocked out every outside distraction and gave Paisley total quiet to work in.

Paisley didn't waste any more time and crouched down.

She reached out and pressed her fingertips lightly against the poisoned man's burning, blackened forehead.

A second later, a thread of warm, pure, pale green energy flowed slowly from her fingertips. It was gentle but piercing, and it seeped quickly into his body through the spot between his eyebrows.

Most healing powers could only fix outer injuries and wounds, but Paisley's healing was a whole different story. It carried the pure, original healing power of the Plant Affinity, made to cleanse every kind of contamination and poison and repair damaged organs.

The instant the green energy entered him, the cold, vicious poison raging through his body reacted like it had met its natural enemy. The black stream of poison that had been eating through his organs started disappearing as it was purified.

Everyone could clearly see the terrifying bluish black color on his face slowly fading, getting lighter right before their eyes.

Paisley's expression stayed calm. The energy kept flowing from her fingertips, gentle but unstoppable as it moved through his body, purifying the scattered poison bit by bit.

His twitching body slowly calmed down, and his stiff arms and legs relaxed. His weak, shallow breathing became long and steady, and the life that had almost slipped away was pulled back, little by little.

Three minutes later, Paisley pulled her hand back. The last thread of pure energy had wiped out the rest of the poison.

Wisps of black-gray toxic mist rose from his skin and quickly turned into a foul-smelling puff of smoke that vanished.

When the last bit of poison was gone, the team member gasped for a deep breath and slowly opened his eyes. The gray, deathly look in them was gone, the color had come back to his face, and there wasn't a single sign of poisoning left.

The poison was gone for good.

The team members who'd watched the whole thing were stunned, frozen in place. The shock kept hitting them wave after wave, and it wouldn't settle down.

"How is that possible... That's high-level mutant venom! There's no cure for it, and she actually did it."

"Exactly! Even the chief healer at the base could only barely hold it back, not wipe it out. She purified all of it in no time!"

"She's actually a high-level purifier!"

"This is unbelievable... How many more skills is she hiding?"

"Could she be that mysterious healer?"

The team member who'd just escaped death pushed himself up off the ground and slowly sat up. His body felt light and good, with no pain at all. His eyes went red, and he got to his feet and grabbed Paisley's hand, choking up as he thanked her.

"Ms. Lennox, thank you so much! You didn't just save my life, you saved my whole family, and I'll never forget it as long as I live!"

The other team members thanked her from the bottom of their hearts, full of awe and respect. "Thank you! Thank you for saving him and for saving all of us!"

Elijah and Orion both looked at Paisley, cool and gentle as ever. The shock in their eyes deepened until all that was left was pure admiration and a deep, protective fondness.

She could break a deadly trap that left everyone else helpless, and she could easily purify a poison no other healer could cure.

Paisley always found a way to pull off a miracle no one saw coming, even in the most hopeless situations!

Paisley glanced at the comm device on her wrist and saw it was already 1 p.m. Time had flown by.

"It's already past noon," she said. "Let's eat something and rest up first, then get back to the mission."
