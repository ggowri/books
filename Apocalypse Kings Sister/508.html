Chapter 508

That day, Paisley went into her storage dimension and took out the Awakening Pill from Trovie's reward.

The pill was pearly white and gave off a soft glow.

She swallowed it, and a vast, gentle force swept through her body. Every limb tingled.

She'd awakened Wind Affinity.

She ran the energy crystals from her trades through it, tempering it again and again until the new affinity reached early Level Six.

A light force wrapped around her. She could shoot forward in a blink, and her speed was many times what it had been before. She moved like a gust of wind.

The boost from the sacred fungus chicken soup had settled too.

Her Psychic, Water, and Lightning Affinities were all at peak Level Seven. Her Plant Affinity was stuck at peak Level Seven, unchanged.

The higher she went, the harder each barrier was to break. Every step needed huge amounts of crystal cores and a good opportunity.

For the next two days, she trained at the base's affinity training room with Orion, Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, and Elijah.

They sparred with each other, practiced techniques, and worked on teamwork, getting a firm grip on their new power.

Three days later, the storm suddenly stopped.

Lead-gray clouds didn't clear. The sky stayed heavy and oppressive. The first dark red raindrop splattered on a glass window.

The Crimson Rain had come.

Everyone received an emergency notice from the base. No one was to go outside. All personnel were to stay home.

The rain carried heavy contamination. If it touched bare skin, it could cause festering at best, or toxins could eat into the mind, causing mutation, and even push zombies and mutant beasts to evolve further.

Dark red rain fell steadily from the clouds, washing over the base walls, streets, and abandoned cars.

Where it hit the snow, the white turned blinding red. The snow slowly melted, soaked into the ground, and kept poisoning the land.

A faint, sweet, coppery smell hung in the air and made people's chests feel tight.

The base broadcast looped warnings, telling everyone to shut their doors and windows, avoid the rain, and stock up on drinking water.

Out in the wilds, the zombies and mutants soaked in the Crimson Rain began to warp. Their strength and resistance rose again, and a new disaster slowly took shape.

The Crimson Rain fell for three days straight.

When it stopped, dim gray light struggled through the thick dust and fell across the ravaged land.

Most of the surviving plants outside had withered and turned black. A few survivors mutated in strange ways.

Their branches twisted and swelled, their leaves turned dark purple-red, and their roots oozed a cloudy, sticky sap. Every one looked ominous.

The soil soaked by the rain was dark red. Streams and pools were murky, a thin layer of grayish-red scum floating on top.

After two days of rest, the base sent teams out on missions to collect supplies.

One team killed a few mutant beasts in the ruins, dug out the crystal cores, and brought them back to the base.

The members didn't think twice. As always, they gripped the cores and absorbed the energy.

Less than half an hour later, several of them began to have problems.

One felt a cold chill and weak, aching limbs. His powers stuttered and sharp pain pricked his meridians.

Another got dizzy and nauseous, a gray haze over his eyes. He turned irritable and angry and nearly attacked his own teammate.

The team rushed straight to the base's medical testing center.

The detector lit up with a harsh red warning. When the report came back, everyone's face fell.

A thin layer of gray toxin ran through the cores. It was contamination carried by the Crimson Rain.

[Contaminated crystal cores must not be absorbed directly. Forced absorption lets the toxin invade the body's meridians and eat away at the root of an affinity. In severe cases the victim gradually mutates and becomes a mindless monster.]

The news spread through the base and panic crept in.

That meant leveling up wouldn't be easy anymore. No more absorbing crystal cores directly.

The staff spot-checked everything collected from the wilds, and the results were bad.

Vegetables and fruit picked outside had trace contamination on the skin or deep in the flesh.

Untreated water outside the city had toxin levels far over the limit.

Even fruit that looked perfect was dirty, contaminated at the core.

From now on, every crystal core, fruit, vegetable, and water source from outside would need purification before use.

The amount of directly usable supplies plunged.

The base's reserves were being used up faster, and the pressure to survive shot up.

Tap water in particular could no longer be used directly.

This was the first problem the base had to solve.
