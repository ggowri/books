Chapter 187

As Paisley drank the cool, sweet spring water, warm, pure energy flooded her whole body, quickly refilling what she'd used up.

After a short rest, most of her energy was back. Without wasting any more time, she went to the next bed and started treating Elijah and the other badly injured, unconscious soldiers.

Her focus held steady, her expression calm.

Rich green light rose from her palm again. A steady stream of pure Plant Affinity poured into each patient, gentle yet unstoppable, carefully repairing every inch of flesh the toxin had damaged.

The clear, warm green light spread out, layer after layer.

Outside the treatment room, the senior staff, officers, and medical workers stood frozen, holding their breath, no one making a sound.

Everyone was on edge, torn between dread and hope as they waited for the outcome.

Every second felt painfully long.

Nobody dared to move.

The head of the base stood at the very front with his hands behind his back. His usually steady, commanding face was tense, and he couldn't stop frowning.

He kept glancing at the closed door, his feelings all over the place.

Right now, all his hopes rested on one young girl.

After all, the base's best doctors were at a total loss.

The senior officer whose wife Paisley had saved clenched his fists, his knuckles white.

Can she really save all five of them? Not even our best healers could do anything about this poison.

The medical staff stood with their hands at their sides, faces grave.

They knew better than anyone how critical these five were. All of them already had one foot in the grave, with the toxin deep in their bones and organs, and there was no known way to cure it.

Time crept by, 10 minutes, then 20, then a whole hour...

The room stayed dead quiet, without a single sound or movement, and the silence was nerve-racking.

Outside, the mood grew heavier and heavier. The hope in many people's eyes slowly faded, despair quietly crept in, and a few people began to whisper hopelessly.

"It's been this long and there's still nothing. You don't think... they're already..."

"Not even all the healers in the base could crack that poison. It's way too strong and weird. We never should've pinned our hopes on a young girl."

"It's over... it's over... Please don't tell me they're already gone..."

"It's been a whole hour, and there's still nothing. Let's just go in..."

The whispers kept coming, heavy with despair, hanging over the crowd.

Just as everyone's eyes were fixed on the closed door, a faint, soft green glow began to seep out through the gap, full of warm, vibrant life.

The whispering stopped at once, and everyone pressed closer, trying to see what was going on inside.

The next moment, the door opened from the inside.

Paisley stepped out, a faint tiredness in her eyes, a thin sheen of sweat at her temple, her steps unhurried, her gaze perfectly clear.

She looked up at the officials and medical staff staring at her and said something that stunned them all.

"The toxin's all gone, and the contamination's been fully purified. I've repaired the damage to their bodies and organs too, so they're all okay.

"They're out of danger now, and they could wake up at any moment."

She said it so casually, but those two sentences hit everyone like a bolt from the blue.

The whole place went dead silent.

Shock froze on every face, and they all stared at her in disbelief.

For several long seconds, nobody moved. Then, the silence broke all at once into a chorus of sharp, disbelieving breaths.

"They're a-alive?! All of them? Is this real, or am I dreaming?"

"Not even the base's top healer could do anything about that poison! She actually wiped it out? She's not lying to us, is she?"

Everyone rushed inside to check on the soldiers.

The five soldiers, whose faces had been blue-black and whose breathing had been so faint, now had their normal color back, and they were breathing deep and steady. They were still asleep, but they were out of danger, nothing like the dying men they'd been before.
