Chapter 96

A high-level Awakening Pill sat in her palm. It was completely clear and shimmered with shifting light. A faint white glow surrounded it, pure and flawless, and it held enough power to unlock a whole new ability.

Now, she was going to use it to awaken a second ability.

Paisley sat cross-legged with her back straight. She closed her eyes, cleared her mind completely, and slowly put the pill in her mouth.

It melted the second it touched her tongue and turned into a rush of pure, powerful warmth. It raced down her throat and flooded every inch of her body.

A faint hum echoed through her head. Suddenly, her mind turned crystal clear, and the solid wall inside it shattered and melted away.

A huge flood of pure energy poured into her mind. It gently but forcefully rewired her brain and the very core of her spirit, and her weak mental power grew, surged, and condensed at insane speed.

Countless bits of information, ripples of energy, and the faintest movements within 1,000 yards poured into her mind. Every tiny detail was crystal clear.

In that moment, her Psychic Affinity fully awakened!

Her invisible mental power formed a shimmering white veil of light that gently wrapped around her and quietly spread outward.

At the same time, the powerful energy left over from dinner's soup finally kicked in. It kept feeding her abilities and blended perfectly with the pill's power, each one making the other stronger.

With those two pure forces working together, her Psychic Affinity shot straight from Level One to Level Three.

Her green Plant Affinity glow and the new white shimmer of her psychic power twisted together around her. Both auras surged, condensed, and grew more solid, layer after layer.

The abilities inside her refined and evolved at incredible speed. Her mind grew wider and deeper, and her mental power became strong and steady.

Boosted by two top-tier lucky breaks at once, her Plant Affinity smashed through its limits and jumped straight from mid Level Five to late Level Five!

As soon as the breakthrough was done, Paisley slowly opened her eyes.

A faint white glow flickered in her eyes and quickly faded, and she looked as cool and clear-eyed as ever. But her whole presence had changed. It was deeper now, and more controlled.

She had two abilities now.

Plant Affinity could kill, heal, and regenerate endlessly. Psychic Affinity was all about controlling the battlefield and killing without leaving a trace.

Her sensing range had doubled. Within 1,000 yards, every breeze, every emotion, and every ripple of energy was under her control.

After leveling up, Paisley realized she felt grimy and gross.

She hurried to the bathroom, washed up, and soaked in a nice, relaxing rose-petal bath.

In the apocalypse, a rose-petal bath was an insane luxury.

Ever since the red rain, all the water had been contaminated, and bottled water was the only safe thing left to drink.

Any water people could use usually had to come from Water Affinity users.

So water was precious.

But Paisley had her own cheat code.

After her bath, she changed into a sporty jacket, tossed her old clothes into the washing machine, and put on a face mask.

When she came back out of her Storage Dimension, no time had passed at all.

The little white cat mewed softly.

Paisley flopped onto the bed and pulled the little white cat into her arms to sleep.

That was when she noticed the cat had leveled up too. Thanks to the soup, it had gone from late Level Four to early Level Five.

"Lucky you."

Going from Level Four to Level Five was a huge turning point.

For a magical beast, it was even harder.

But the little white cat had leveled up just from drinking one bowl of soup.

"Go to sleep."

The little white cat snuggled in her arms happily.

The next morning, Paisley stretched and headed down to the lobby.

She spotted the guy she'd rescued the night before.

He was awake now. Maybe it was because he'd had a bowl of soup too, but after one night, he looked a lot better.

He'd changed into clean clothes and cleaned himself up, and he actually turned out to be really good-looking.

He held out his hand to Paisley and shook hers gratefully. "Thank you for saving my life. I'm Marcus Whitfield."

With everyone watching, Paisley gave a sheepish little shrug. "I'm Paisley. It was nothing, and I'm just glad you're okay."

After all, humanity's future depended on him.

Marcus was dead serious.

"Maybe it was nothing to you, but you saved my life. I really thought I was going to die down there, and you got me out.

"From now on, my life is yours."

Paisley sighed. "What would I even do with your life? Just stay alive and well, and that's all the thanks I need."

Marcus asked another question.

"Paisley, where are you all headed? If it's not too much trouble, could I come with you?"
