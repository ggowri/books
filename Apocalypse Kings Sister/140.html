Chapter 140

The moment she said it, the room went dead silent.

All three brothers froze. The resentment and fear on their faces locked in place, and their eyes lit up with hope and greed.

Getting stronger was the only way out.

If they were strong enough, Paisley wouldn't even be on their radar.

These past few days, they'd watched everyone else wipe out zombies with their abilities, rack up crystal cores, and earn respect. Meanwhile, they'd had to hide at the back of the group just to stay alive, and a mountain of jealousy and resentment had piled up inside them.

Nobody wanted to be a useless nobody at the bottom of the food chain forever. Everyone wanted power and control over their own lives.

Owen frowned, forcing down his excitement, and tested the waters, pretending to be cautious.

"Rachel, are you serious? That pendant really holds the secret to awakening an affinity?

"If I absorb a few more crystal cores, my affinity will hit Level Four soon. I'm not super strong compared to them, but I'm not weak either.

"As long as Reid and Ryan get a shot at awakening their affinities, I'm in."

The three brothers were in this together.

If Reid and Ryan could awaken their affinities, the three of them could find their own way home.

Reid forgot all about his pain and leaned forward eagerly. "Rachel, you're not lying to us, right? Once we get the pendant, we'll have affinities too, and Paisley won't be able to keep us under her thumb anymore?"

Ryan was already sold. All his blame from before was gone, and all he wanted now was power.

As she watched all three of them get eaten up by greed, Rachel sneered to herself.

Just like I thought. All they care about is themselves, and their so-called family bond isn't worth a damn next to power.

What a bunch of idiots. Once I have the pendant, I don't care if they live or die.

On the outside, she kept up her sweet, sincere act. She nodded firmly, her voice steady.

"Absolutely. All of Paisley's luck comes from that pendant. Once we have it, we can take her luck for ourselves, and you'll all get to awaken your affinities and turn your lives around.

"And then the Lennox family will finally come out on top!"

The huge temptation wiped out the last of the brothers' common sense. With a lucky break almost in their hands, their fear of Paisley and the Thompsons slowly gave way to greed.

Owen's expression changed a few times before he finally gritted his teeth and made up his mind.

"Fine! I believe you. From now on, we'll do what you say and help you get that pendant!"

The other two nodded right away. Greed won out over common sense, and Rachel had them completely fooled. They were more than willing to be her pawns.

Rachel looked down to hide her dark satisfaction, and a faint, icy smirk crept onto her lips.

Same as always. They're still that stupid, and a few cheap tricks were all it took.

It's not my fault. They're just greedy, and greedy people are the easiest to play.

She looked up, wiped every trace of malice off her face, and started laying out the plan.

"From now on, we take turns keeping an eye on Paisley and tracking everywhere she goes.

"We lie low and wait for the right moment. The second she's on her own, we make our move."

Her face hardened, and she sounded dead certain.

"No matter how strong she is now, there are four of us. If we plan it carefully and catch her off guard, can't we take her down when she's alone?"

Then, she turned to Owen, the most level-headed of the three.

"Owen, tomorrow the four of us should go to the mission hall and take some hunting missions.

"We'll stock up on crystal cores and get stronger as fast as we can. We need to get both your affinity and mine up to Level Four.

"That'll give us a much better chance of getting the pendant."

Owen hadn't expected Rachel to be looking out for them every step of the way, and he was really touched.

He'd been right about her all along. She still cared about him, and he agreed without a second thought.

"Okay, we'll do it your way. First thing tomorrow, we'll take missions, farm crystal cores, and level up as fast as we can!"

Reid and Ryan quickly agreed too. Their heads were full of greed for affinities and luck, and they'd completely forgotten how scared they'd been when Paisley crushed them earlier.

They were already daydreaming about the day they'd turn the tables, rise to the top, and lord it over everyone else.
