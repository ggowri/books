Chapter 80

Right next to it was a second sacred fungus, just as perfect and full of life. The two of them grew side by side.

"Found it."

Paisley smiled faintly. But just as she reached out to pick both fungi, things took a turn.

The branches of the towering old tree overhead shook violently, and leaves rained down. A cold, foul-smelling wind rushed down from above.

A giant serpent, a good 65 feet long, was coiled at the top of the tree. Its whole body was covered in tiny black-purple scales, so hard and cold that they gleamed in the dim forest light.

The serpent's head was a vicious triangle, and its slitted blood-red eyes were locked on them. A long, thin tongue kept flicking in and out with a creepy hiss.

It was a Level Four mutant venomous serpent!

Paisley's eyes narrowed.

A treasure this rare always had a guardian beast. These two sacred fungi were priceless, and the serpent guarding them had mutated during the apocalypse into a vicious, high-level monster far more dangerous than any ordinary mutant.

The little white cat's back went rigid, and all its snow-white fur stood on end. It bared its sharp fangs and crouched low with its back arched, glaring up at the serpent and growling a low warning.

Just as the cat and the serpent were about to go at it, quick, hushed footsteps came from the forest behind them, along with three familiar voices.

It was Rachel, Reid, and Ryan!

Paisley had sharp senses.

When she overheard what they were saying, she couldn't believe it. Rachel had been reborn too!

That was how they'd found this place so easily.

They were obviously after the sacred fungi too.

The pain of her past life was carved into her bones.

In her past life, she'd fought her way into this deadly place alone and barely made it out alive. She'd come back to the brothers battered and bleeding, with the two sacred fungi in her hands, and gratitude was the last thing she got.

Reid and Ryan hadn't just snatched the fungi from her. They'd looked down their noses at her and mocked her mercilessly too.

They'd laughed at her for being useless and getting herself all cut up just to find some mushrooms.

Those two-faced, cold-hearted bastards had squeezed every last drop of use out of her and stomped all over her dignity.

What goes around comes around. This time, it was their turn to get a taste of that same desperate, near-death terror.

Paisley's face went cold, and a plan came together in her mind right away.

She gently held down the restless white cat and scooped it into her arms. With a single thought, she disappeared into her Storage Dimension.

The serpent's target had vanished into thin air, and it felt like it had been played.

The Level Four mutant serpent got even angrier. Its massive body twisted violently, its blood-red eyes blazed with murder, and cold, poisonous mist spread all around it. The rotten stench in the forest grew thicker and thicker.

Before long, Rachel, Reid, and Ryan walked right into its range.

The thorns on the vines had ripped their clothes to shreds, and they looked like a mess.

Rachel spotted the sacred fungus first.

"Reid, Ryan, look! There really is an age-old sacred fungus here. It's right over there..."

Greed was written all over her face.

Reid and Ryan looked where she was pointing, and their faces lit up.

All three of them were completely fixated on the fungus. It was almost in their hands.

Not one of them noticed the danger closing in from above.

"It really is the sacred fungus! We found it!"

Ryan's voice shook with excitement. He hurried forward and reached out to grab it.

Just as his fingertips were about to touch it, a loud hiss split the air!

The massive Level Four mutant serpent shot down from above with terrifying force, its tongue flicking, and suddenly it was right in front of them!

All the color drained from their faces. Pure terror flooded through them, and their blood practically froze.

In a panic, the three of them scrambled to fight back.

Rachel was the only one with an ability, a Level Two Water Affinity. She threw up a thin wall of water in front of her, but it was so flimsy that it couldn't have stopped a single hit.

Reid and Ryan were just regular guys who couldn't fight. All they could do was yank out their guns and pull the triggers like crazy.

Gunfire cracked over and over. But the bullets just bounced off the serpent's hard scales in a shower of sparks without leaving a single scratch. They were about as useful as a tickle, and they only made the furious serpent even angrier.

Murder flared in the serpent's blood-red eyes. Its huge head lunged down at them, its jaws gaping wide.

The three of them were terrified, and they turned to run.

Ryan wasn't fast enough. The serpent's tail whipped hard into his chest and sent him flying backward like a rag doll.

He slammed into the ground, and blood sprayed from his mouth. Every rib in his chest had snapped, and the pain was so bad that his vision went black and he almost passed out.
