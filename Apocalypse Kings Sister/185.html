Chapter 185

"I'm sorry, sir," the healer said. "I've done everything I can."

The attending doctor's face grew even grimmer.

"If even he can't do anything, then all these men can do is wait to die.

"With the healing resources we have at Rivergate Base, nobody can save them."

In just half an hour, every one of them had tried, and every one had failed.

Total despair fell over everyone.

After hearing the healers say there was nothing they could do, the heavyset head of the base turned dark as thunder. The air around him grew so tense that nobody dared go near him.

He paced back and forth with his hands behind his back, his steps heavy and quick, his face full of anxiety and panic. A crushing tension spread over the whole treatment area.

"There's really nothing you can do?" He stopped short and pressed them, his voice shaking despite himself. "What about some special high-level medicine or rare purification serum that could suppress or clear the toxin?

"Think of something! Try anything, no matter what it costs! They're the base's top elite fighters and our strongest line of defense against the beasts, so nothing can happen to them!"

His face was grim, and he sounded more and more anxious.

"They're supposed to head out on a high-risk mission at the aquarium the day after tomorrow. The safety of the whole base and our next push forward all depend on them!

"If they go down, nobody can finish that mission, and the base will lose all hope!"

The room went dead silent. No one answered.

Every doctor and healer there hung their heads.

In the end, the head of the base waved them away.

In that suffocating moment, the senior officer who'd just come back happened to glance over the crowd that was leaving. Suddenly, his eyes landed on a cool, slim figure, and they lit up.

It was Paisley.

In just a few hours, word of Paisley single-handedly crushing the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower had spread all over Rivergate Base.

Everyone knew she was actually an incredibly rare, high-level Plant Affinity user.

Plant Affinity came with healing and purification powers built in, and the higher the level, the more insanely effective its healing and purifying became.

She was a top-tier powerhouse who could kill a Level Six mutant carnivorous flower on her own, so her abilities had to be way beyond any healer in the base.

Right now, everyone else was out of options. Maybe... maybe she was the only one who could pull off a miracle.

Even if there was only the slightest chance, it was worth a shot at this point.

Like a drowning man clutching at his last straw, the senior officer pushed through the crowd and hurried over to Paisley, urgent and respectful, his voice full of hope.

"Thank God! Ms. Lennox, you're here!"

He dipped his head, as humble as he could be, and pleaded with her.

"Ms. Lennox, please, I'm begging you. Will you try to heal them?

"If you're willing to help, the base will agree to any terms or payment you want, no questions asked!"

Nearby, the head of the base looked up at that, his gaze settling heavily on Paisley as he sized her up in surprise.

He'd gotten a report the moment that Level Six high-risk mission was turned in, so he knew the base had a stunning new rising star with terrifying strength and limitless potential.

He just never imagined that the powerhouse who'd killed the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower would turn out to be such a young, slight girl.

There was no time to waste on surprise. He pushed it down, stepped forward, and named a huge reward.

"Ms. Lennox, if you can heal all five of them, I promise you 50,000 contribution points. These are official contribution points, good at every major base in the country."

The healers and everyone else not needed had already cleared out.

The huge treatment area was now empty except for the wounded, the medical staff, the head of the base, the senior officer, and Paisley.

Paisley had already been getting anxious when the healers couldn't help, and she'd made up her mind.

Orion and Elijah were lying there, their breathing faint and their lives hanging by a thread, and she just couldn't stand by and do nothing.

Hearing about a 50,000-point reward made Paisley's eyes light up.

What a nice surprise!

With 50,000 contribution points, she could trade for tons of rare medicine, high-level supplies, special access, and even scarce training resources at any major base in the country. They'd be useful in countless ways.
