Chapter 185

“I'm sorry, sir. I've done everything I can.”

The attending physician's face turned grimmer still.

“If even he can't do it, then there's nothing left but to wait.”

“No one at Rivergate has what it takes to save them.”

In half an hour, everyone had tried, and everyone had failed.

Despair settled over the room, total and complete.

******

Hearing the healers' verdict, the heavyset Director's face went black as pitch, the air around him dropping so low no one dared get close.

He paced, hands locked behind his back, steps heavy and fast, his brow twisted with panic. The oppressive weight of it rolled over the whole treatment area.

“There's really nothing?”

He stopped dead, pressing the question, his voice shaking despite himself.

“Is there no special high-tier medicine, no rare purification serum, that could suppress or clear this toxin?”

“Think of something! I don't care what it costs, just try! They're our best fighters, the ones holding the line against the beasts. We cannot lose them!”

His eyes were heavy with dread, his voice climbing with anxiety.

“They're supposed to head into the aquarium for a high-risk mission the day after tomorrow. The whole base's safety rests on them. If they go down, there's no one left to finish that mission. We lose everything.”

The room went dead silent. No one answered.

Every doctor and healer there dropped their eyes.

At last he waved them out.

In that suffocating moment, the senior officer who'd just returned let his eyes drift over the departing crowd—and stopped. A slight, composed figure. His eyes went wide.

Paisley.

Word of her solo kill of a Level Six mutant carnivorous flower had spread through all of Rivergate in a matter of hours.

Everyone knew by now that this young woman carried an exceedingly rare, high-tier Plant Affinity.

Plant Affinity came with healing and purification built in, and the higher the tier, the more absurd its power.

She'd killed a Level Six mutant alone. Whatever she could do had to outstrip every healer the base had.

Everyone else had failed. Maybe… she was the only one left who could pull off a miracle.

If there was even a sliver of a chance, it was worth grabbing.

Like a drowning man grabbing the last thing floating by, the officer broke from the crowd and hurried to her, his voice urgent, respectful, full of plea.

“Thank god—Ms. Lennox, you're here!”

He bowed slightly, lowering himself, his words pleading.

“Ms. Lennox, please, I'm begging you—will you try to heal them?”

“Whatever you ask, whatever it costs, the base will meet it. No questions.”

The Director looked up at that, his gaze settling on Paisley, equal parts scrutiny and surprise.

He'd already read the report the moment she'd turned in that SS-tier mission—a terrifying new talent, impossible potential, all in one recruit.

He just hadn't expected the one who'd killed a Level Six mutant to be this young, this slight a girl.

There was no time to waste on surprise. He pushed it down, stepped forward, and named his offer without hesitation.

“Ms. Lennox, heal all five of them and I'll give you fifty thousand contribution points, the official kind, good at any base in the country.”

The healers and everyone else not needed had already cleared out.

The wide treatment area now held only the wounded, the medical staff, the Director, the officer, and Paisley.

She'd already started to worry, already made up her mind, the moment the healers had given up.

Watching Orion and Elijah lying there, breath shallow, lives hanging by a thread, she simply couldn't stand by.

The offer of fifty thousand contribution points made her eyes light up.

An unexpected bonus, on top of everything.

Fifty thousand points could buy rare medicine, premium supplies, access privileges—even scarce cultivation resources—at any base in the country. The uses were endless.
