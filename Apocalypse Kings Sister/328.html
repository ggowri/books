Chapter 328

"If you die, how am I supposed to face the family?"

Beside him, Elijah struggled to hold onto the last thread of his consciousness, his body swaying, finally sinking to his knees. There was no fear in his eyes though, just a quiet murmur.

"Pais… if I have to go, at least I'm going with you. That's something."

Everyone else was down, bodies locked up, unable to move. Only Paisley remained unaffected — Total Poison Immunity kept her completely untouched.

But for now, she stayed still, playing dead, waiting for the right moment to strike and get everyone out in one clean move.

The mutant in the lab coat looked down at the room full of helpless bodies, something smug and satisfied flickering in its black eyes. It raised a hand lazily, ordering its remaining mutants to get to work.

They moved to lift the unconscious Gabriel, Elijah, and Orion, laying them out alongside the already-unconscious Chase, Rachel, and the rest of their team.

Convinced everyone was out cold and the threat neutralized, the mutant in the lab coat relaxed completely, turning its attention to mixing up some kind of solution.

Paisley reached out mentally, quietly opening her trading interface.

"Trovie, exchange for seventeen Universal Antidote Pills. Now."

A moment later, seventeen pale, luminous pills, radiant with clean, refined medicinal energy, appeared in her hand.

Still playing unconscious, she worked quickly, using her body to block the view, slipping a pill to each unconscious team member — everyone except Chase and Rachel.

The medicine worked fast, dissolving instantly, a cool, cleansing energy rushing through each person's system, stripping out the clotted poison from their channels.

Within five minutes, Orion was the first to recover, his Affinity returning.

His eyes snapped open, and he immediately turned to find Paisley beside him. She gave him a small wink, and the knot in his chest finally loosened.

He understood instantly. He stayed down, holding the act, waiting for the right moment to move.

Right after, Elijah, Gabriel, and the rest of the team came around one by one, exchanging a quick glance with each other — no words needed.

What had looked like certain death a moment ago had been completely flipped, thanks to Paisley's quiet maneuvering.

In the massive chamber now, aside from the team still feigning unconsciousness, only the mutant in the lab coat remained focused on its data readout, along with two hulking guard mutants standing silently at its side.

The time had come.

Paisley's closed eyes snapped open, sharp and gleaming, her presence suddenly unmistakable.

She released her Plant Affinity in an instant. Thick, pliant green vines tore up from cracks in the floor and the shadows along the walls, branches and roots twisting together with incredible speed, snapping tight around the limbs, torsos, and necks of the mutant in the lab coat and its two guards.

The vines cinched tight, locking down all three mutants completely.

By the time they realized what was happening and tried to break free, it was already too late.

"Move. Dig out their cores."

Paisley's voice came low and cold.

A brilliant cold light flared in her palm, and a pristine, crystalline blade formed, coated in a layer of frost, a bitter chill radiating off it, the edge razor-sharp. The Frostbloom Sword.

She closed the distance in a blink, appearing right in front of the mutant in the lab coat. Her wrist twisted cleanly, driving the sword straight into its forehead, splitting through flesh with precise force. A flick of her fingers, and a pulsing, dark, oily crystal core came free, whole.

The instant the core left its body, the mutant convulsed violently, the strange, controlling energy radiating off it collapsing in an instant. Its black eyes went dark, its body withering and stiffening fast before it crashed to the floor, lifeless.

At the same moment, Orion and Elijah both surged up, moving fast and clean, each cutting straight toward one of the vine-bound guard mutants.

Both strikes landed true, precise hits to the forehead, and in moments, two subordinate cores came free.

The two guard mutants dropped, fully dead.

The three of them had cleared the room's remaining threats in a matter of seconds, clean and efficient.

Just as everyone started to relax, the heavy alloy door to the chamber suddenly blew open with tremendous force.

A deep boom tore through the room.

A shrieking wave of noise and movement flooded in — countless altered test subjects and mutated undead, pouring through the opening, howling, packing the corridor wall to wall, surging toward the chamber.

It was the lab-coat mutant's final command, triggered at the moment of death — every dormant mutant in the facility, converging on them at once.
