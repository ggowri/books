Chapter 474

"Chloe, whatever grudge you have, take it out on me.

As long as I'm here, nobody touches my sister!"

"We're from the same family, blood relatives. Are you sure you want to tear it all apart and make enemies of us?"

"Family?"

Chloe let out a scoff, full of contempt.

Thomas's eyes were icy, his voice sharp with scorn.

"Chloe, I never thought you'd be this vicious. And I was so good to you. If you hurt Pais, I'll take you down with me, even if it kills us both."

Spencer stepped up, spine straight, planting himself between them and Paisley.

"Orion's right. You want to hurt Pais, you'll have to go through us first. As long as I'm here, nobody touches her."

Ashley's eyes were filled with disgust and anger, and she didn't hold back.

"I've never met such a petty piece of garbage. You think you can bark at Paisley?"

"As long as I'm here, you're not laying a finger on her!"

The siblings stood in a wall in front of Paisley, giving no ground.

Across the river, Chloe watched them stand together, and instead of backing off she smiled colder and crueler.

"Good. Very good. What a loving family."

"Perfect. You junior-branch siblings are all here. Today I'll settle every old score at once!"

She threw herself into Grant's arms, and her face flipped from vicious to soft and wronged, all pleading and sugar.

"Grant, they've been bullying me forever. Paisley used her power to throw me and my mom into the Security Office and humiliated us. Don't you dare go easy. You have to avenge me."

Grant stroked her hair and kissed her forehead gently, but murder boiled in his eyes.

His voice was low and brutal. "Relax. My strength's way up. I'm not the man I was."

"Today is Paisley Lennox's last day."

He pulled out a special black whistle, put it to his lips, and blew hard.

Shrill, piercing blasts split the air over the river, in a rhythm that could command beasts in the water.

Boom!

The dark river surged. Huge waves rolled up.

The mutant shark, over a hundred feet long, covered in gray-black armor, its dorsal fin like a blood-soaked blade, eyes red, burst out of the water in a cloud of stench and flew into the air.

Its body blocked the sky. With enormous force, it smashed into the unfinished bridge.

CRASH!

The impact was deafening. The remaining deck shook and split, cracks spreading over every stone and steel beam.

The bridge tilted and sank, with no time to brace.

"No!"

Paisley's pupils shrank. No time to think. She stored the SUV at once and shouted, "Everyone back! Retreat now!"

The collapse was faster than anyone could move!

Crack!

The shattered deck snapped and fell straight down.

Mason, Madison, Ashley, and Spencer stood too far out and couldn't get back in time. The floor vanished beneath their feet, and all four fell with the broken bridge toward the dark, rushing river far below!

River water churned and surged.

Below them, the mutant shark landed in the water, raised its huge head, and opened its horrible jaws.

Dense, sharp teeth gleamed pale and cold, and its mouth was foul and black. It was waiting for the four to hit the water so it could swallow them whole.

Life and death hung on a thread!

On the far bank, Grant and Chloe watched coldly, cruel smiles on their faces, waiting for them to die.

Right as the four fell toward the shark's jaws,

Paisley's eyes flashed with killing cold, her whole body erupting with power!

Her mid Level Seven Ice-Water Affinity burst out, and freezing power swept over the river!

"Deep Freeze!"

Two cold words, and the world dropped into winter.

The raging river locked. Every wave, every current, every flying spray froze in an instant.
