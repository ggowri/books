Chapter 201

He looked at her, gratitude filling his eyes.

“Thank you, Pais. Every point I earn on this mission, it's yours.”

Orion nodded right along, clearly of the same mind.

Paisley shook her head.

She looked up at them both, explaining.

“No need. And I should tell you how the guns work—they run on an energy core. Once that drains, you can't fire anymore.”

“I've got ten more of the same model. These two were free. The rest, if anyone else wants one, it's five hundred Level Three cores, or ten thousand contribution points each. No freebies.”

Then she added, almost as an afterthought.

“Same goes for the storage rings and the Poison Immunity Pills. Anyone else who wants one, they pay for it.”

Elijah nodded, agreeing.

“That's fair enough. I'll mention it in the team channel, see what people think.”

He paused, looking at her, a genuine sense of owing her something in his eyes.

“Pais, whatever I owe you on cores, I'll pay it off once the mission rewards come through. Every bit of it.”

Orion, hands steady on the wheel, agreed at once.

“Same here, Paisley. You've given me too much to let you come out behind every time.”

She gave in and nodded, unable to argue the point.

In the other car, Rachel had worked every angle she had—soft words, coy pleading, a carefully lowered posture—and had already reeled Chase in.

The car sat sealed, windows shut, the cramped space thick with something intimate.

The two of them had abandoned all pretense of restraint, wrapped around each other, kissing like they couldn't stop.

Up front, the base operative driving stared straight ahead, stiff with secondhand embarrassment, not daring to glance back at whatever was happening in the back seat.

Once things had settled, Rachel decided the moment was right.

She'd lived long enough to know exactly how to work someone, and she'd fed Chase just enough sweetness to leave him wanting more.

She curled into his chest, her voice soft, carrying just the right note of helplessness, tugging gently at his collar.

“Chase, if you really care about me, there's something I want so badly. Could you help me get it?”

“I think about it even in my dreams.”

She half-lifted her eyes, glistening, soft and deliberate, enough to keep him completely hooked.

Whatever humiliation he'd carried out of that office dissolved, most of it, under her careful affection.

Chase looked down at the pliant woman in his arms, and his old swagger started creeping back.

Rachel wasn't the stunning, cool beauty Paisley was, but she made up for it in obedience, in knowing exactly how to please him, how to flatter his ego with just the right amount of neediness.

Sour as his mood was right now, having someone this agreeable around to keep him company wasn't bad at all—something to amuse himself with, nothing more.

He ran a lazy hand through her hair, a careless, amused smile curling his mouth, his voice indulgent.

“Anything that catches your eye should be yours. Whatever it is, I'll get it for you.”

With that promise secured, she leaned in close to his ear, her breath warm against it, and laid out her whole scheme in a low, careful whisper.

By the time she finished, every trace of his careless smile was gone, replaced by something dark and calculating.

“That works perfectly for me.”

He let out a low laugh, humiliation and spite still churning beneath it.

That office, kneeling in front of everyone, his pride ground into the floor in full view of the whole team—there was no swallowing that.

This mission gave him the perfect cover to trip them up quietly, let them die out there.

And if the mission failed, none of it would come back on him.

He'd still be the untouchable top power at Rivergate, same as always.

Something cold and vicious flickered through his eyes, ugly thoughts taking shape underneath.

“Smart girl. I like that about you.”

“I promise you, I'll get that pendant for you.”
