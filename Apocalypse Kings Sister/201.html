Chapter 201

Elijah looked at Paisley with deep gratitude. "Thank you, Pais. Every contribution point from this mission is yours, all of it."

Orion nodded right away. He clearly felt the same.

Paisley shook her head and looked up at them both. "No need.

"But you should know how the energy guns work. They run on an energy core, and once the core's drained, they won't fire anymore.

"I've got 10 more just like them. The two you're holding are free, but anyone who wants one of the other 10 has to trade at least 500 Level Three cores or 10,000 contribution points per gun. No free rides."

Then, she added, "Same goes for the storage rings and the Poison Immunity Pills. Anyone else who wants one pays for it in resources."

Elijah nodded in agreement. "That's a fair price. Want me to post it in the team chat and see what everyone thinks?"

He paused and looked at Paisley, clearly feeling he owed her. "Pais, once this mission's over and the rewards come in, I'll pay back every core I owe you. Every last one."

Orion kept his hands steady on the wheel and backed him up at once. "Same here, Paisley. You've given me so many amazing things, and I'm not letting you lose out every single time."

Paisley couldn't talk them out of it, so she nodded.

In the other car, Rachel had sweet-talked, pouted, and played it as soft and submissive as she could, and it had worked. She'd already hooked Chase.

The doors were shut and the windows were rolled up, and the cramped space was thick with heat. The two of them had stopped holding back. They were all over each other, making out like they couldn't get enough.

Up front, the base operative behind the wheel stared stiffly straight ahead. He was so embarrassed he could barely sit still, and he didn't dare look back at the shameless show in the back seat.

Once they came up for air, Rachel decided the timing was perfect.

She'd lived two lifetimes, and she knew exactly how to play people. She'd given Chase just enough of a taste to leave him starving for more.

She snuggled into Chase's arms and tugged gently at his shirt, her voice soft and sweet with just the right touch of hurt and longing. "Chase, if you really care about me, there's something I want so badly. Will you help me get it?

"I want it so much. I even dream about having it." She glanced up at him with shining eyes, looking fragile and seductive all at once, and Chase was dying to give her whatever she wanted.

Paisley had crushed Chase and humiliated him in front of everyone, but most of that resentment melted away under all of Rachel's sweet talk and cuddling.

He looked down at Rachel, so sweet and obedient in his arms, and his old cockiness came right back.

Rachel wasn't as stunning as Paisley, or as cool and striking, but she was sweet and eager to please. She knew how to play helpless and how to flatter him, and that fed his ego like nothing else.

He was in a foul mood right now, so having an obedient girl around to cheer him up and do what she was told wasn't bad at all. He'd just play with her for a while to kill some time.

He ran a lazy hand through her hair, an amused smile spreading across his face, his voice indulgent. "If something catches your eye, then it should be yours. Don't worry, whatever it is, I'll get it for you."

Once he'd promised, Rachel leaned in close to his ear, her warm breath brushing against it, and quietly laid out her whole scheme, word by word.

By the time she finished, his lazy smile was gone, and a dark, calculating sneer had taken its place. "That's exactly what I had in mind."

He let out a low scoff, still seething with humiliation and spite.

Today in that office, Paisley, Elijah, and the rest of them had made him kneel in front of everyone. They'd stomped on his pride and turned him into a joke. How the hell was he supposed to let that go?

This high-risk aquarium mission was the perfect chance to sabotage them on the quiet and make sure they died in there.

Even if the mission went wrong and failed, none of the blame would fall on him.

And he'd still be the untouchable top fighter at Rivergate Base, the one nobody dared to cross.

Chase's face turned cold and vicious as an ugly idea took root. "Babe, you're a smart one. I promise you, I'll get that pendant for you."
