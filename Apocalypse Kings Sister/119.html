Chapter 119

Before Rachel could say a word, Ashley calmly tore her argument apart point by point. Every word hit hard.

"If it weren't for my sister, would you even be standing here right now?

"Sure, everyone helped clear out the low-level rats. But my sister's the one who came up with the plan and finished off every last rat in the end.

"Forget 600 crystal cores. Even if she took all of them, I'd say that was fair."

Then, she jabbed a finger right in Rachel's face and kept going.

"And what gives you the right to demand my sister's Level Four Psychic crystal core?

"When the Rat King hit us with that psychic attack, everyone's ability fell apart, and our heads felt like they were splitting open. Nobody could even lift a hand to fight back. We were all just sitting there, waiting to die.

"And what were you doing? You were just like everyone else, curled up in pain and unable to even protect yourself.

"From start to finish, Paisley was the only one who protected everyone and killed the Rat King."

Ashley's eyes turned sharp. She stared straight at Rachel, who'd gone pale, and her voice got even harder.

"Instead of thanking her for saving your life, you're blaming her for showing up late and not doing enough? If she hadn't shown up, none of us would still be breathing.

"All the rats we killed would've just been food for the Rat King, and none of us would've lived long enough to split these cores.

"Everyone helped clear out the low-level rats, but Paisley's the one who killed the mutant Rat King and saved our lives.

"A team has never split rewards based on who showed up first. It's about who contributed and who actually pulled their weight.

"She saved the whole team, so of course she gets the biggest share. That's as fair as it gets."

She paused for a second, and her voice turned cold and biting.

"If you thought it was so unfair, why didn't you step up and do something when the Rat King showed up and hit us with that psychic attack?"

Question after question, each one sharper than the last, laid bare how petty and selfish Rachel really was.

Rachel's face went white, then red. Her lips trembled, and she opened her mouth several times, but she couldn't come up with a single thing to say back.

All her jealousy and resentment were stuck in her chest with nowhere to go. She just stood there, frozen, humiliated, and speechless.

The rest of the team nodded along in agreement.

She was right. Without Paisley, nobody would've made it out of that rat swarm alive tonight, let alone gotten this many crystal cores.

Of course Paisley deserved the biggest share.

Orion spoke up first.

"Ashley's right. Paisley saved every single one of us by herself, and nobody else even comes close. Does anyone have a problem with that?"

Elijah nodded too and looked coolly around at the group.

"I always split resources based on who earned them. Tonight, Paisley's the reason any of us are still standing. Does anyone object?"

Everyone else chimed in. Nobody objected.

"He's right. Paisley saved us. She deserves extra."

"If it weren't for her, we'd all be goners. There wouldn't even be any cores to split."

Everyone was on Paisley's side, and it completely shut down Rachel's ridiculous complaint. She was more humiliated than ever.

Nobody was on her side. Rachel felt panic and rage rising inside her, and her face burned with shame.

Ever since she'd come back to the Lennoxes, she'd gotten used to being the center of attention. Right or wrong, her three brothers and her parents had always taken her side and backed her up, no matter what.

Now that she'd been humiliated in front of everyone, she instinctively turned to the three Lennox brothers for help.

She spun around with red eyes, putting on the same hurt, fragile look she always used, and stared at her brothers, pleading.

She pressed her lips together and silently begged them to step up and defend her like they always had, to put Paisley in her place and get her so-called justice back.

But this time, the support she'd been counting on never came.

The three Lennox brothers stood there in silence with complicated looks on their faces. Not one of them even looked at her.

After everything that had happened, after watching Paisley save them all by herself and thinking about how cruel and unfair they'd been to her, all they felt was regret and shame.

They knew better than anyone that Paisley had saved the day tonight, no question. Rachel's jealous tantrum was petty and pathetic.
