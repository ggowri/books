Chapter 407

The moment he strained, his chest seized. Blood surged upward and his vision went black.

His body went slack and he fell back, eyes closed, and passed out again.

"Patient's unconscious! Blood pressure dropping! Heart rate irregular!"

The room erupted. Nurses ran, alarms shrieked, and the little progress he'd made vanished. He was in critical condition again.

It had been a long night.

No one felt sorry for him. They were just annoyed that he kept causing trouble.

Did he really still think he was the golden boy he used to be?

At dawn the next day, sunlight poured through the windows of the junior branch's villa.

The Thompsons were up early, busy and out the door.

Only Paisley had nothing to do. She sat at the table in loose home clothes, her black hair falling around her shoulders, eating a beautiful breakfast without hurry.

There was warm oatmeal, soft rolls, and fresh fruit and vegetables. Quiet and comfortable, as if the world hadn't ended.

******

Meanwhile, in the base's top administrative tower, a high-level meeting began right on time.

The conference room was solemn and tense. Every major family and key power broker on the base was seated.

Elijah Blackwood sat at the right of the head seat, tall and cold, his presence steady and overpowering.

Nathaniel Blackwood, Russell Quinn, Damon Quinn, Harold Lennox, and a row of senior officials were all present. Every faction was in the room, and the tension was thick.

The fight for power never stopped.

In the end, the vote went the same way it always did. Nathaniel kept his seat as the base's top authority, with final control over command and deployment.

The Quinn family, with its deep roots, and the Lennox family, which had waited for years, both resented it.

But Falcon Squad, built by Elijah himself, was the strongest force on the base. They were ruthless, decorated, and feared, and they held the base's core strength.

The two families couldn't challenge them openly, so they swallowed their anger.

Having failed in the open, they turned to quiet schemes, digging pits and setting traps to weaken the Blackwoods and damage Falcon Squad's reputation.

After a pause, Damon spoke first, calm and calculating.

"Supplies on the base are running low. Our grain reserves will last two months at most. We can't guarantee everyone gets fed. This can't wait."

He looked at Elijah, who sat silently, and a hidden smile curved his lips as he applied the pressure.

"The largest surviving grain depot in Aetheris sits inside the Exclusion Zone, in an area crawling with zombies. It's too dangerous for an ordinary team."

"Since Falcon Squad is the strongest on the base, collecting that depot and bringing the grain back will be your assignment."

With one sentence, he had dumped the hardest, deadliest mission on Elijah and Falcon Squad.

If they succeeded, it was merely their duty and earned no reward. If they failed, their reputation would be empty, and the Blackwoods' standing would collapse.

The room went still. Everyone understood it was a deliberate move.

Elijah looked up, his dark eyes cold and unreadable. He saw straight through it, and he didn't refuse.

"From what I know, it's extremely dangerous. But the depot holds enough grain to feed the base for a year," he said evenly.

"Falcon Squad alone can't move that much. We'll need every Storage Dimension user on the base, plus every Affinity user at Level Five or above, to help escort it."

Damon frowned. "That's Falcon Squad's assignment. The base's other forces can't be pulled away."

"We have other missions to complete."

He leaned forward slightly, trying to bait him. "What, Captain Blackwood? Is Falcon Squad all name and no ability? Can't finish a mission on your own?"

Elijah's eyes flashed, but the bait didn't work. He kept bargaining.

"We'll take it. But there have to be rewards and consequences."
