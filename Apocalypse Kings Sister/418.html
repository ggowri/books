Chapter 418

Damon's eyes fixed on Grant.

"My team will fit and install this prosthetic. We'll clear the rest of the poison from your body and get you back to your peak. All your problems solved."

"And in return, from now on, your life, your strength, and your loyalty belong to me. You'll work for me and take my orders until you die."

The deal lay naked in front of him. On one side, freedom, the woman he loved, and a peaceful family. On the other, a lifelong chain, becoming a pawn, bound to someone else.

Grant stared at the cold, powerful prosthetic, his knuckles white, torn inside.

He had been proud all his life. He never imagined he'd become someone's puppet. But when he pictured Chloe suffering in that cell, and remembered that he was now crippled, all his pride and stubbornness couldn't stand against what he was facing.

He shut his eyes. When he opened them, only a dead, final resolve was left.

"I accept."

"I'll take the arm. My life is yours. But release Chloe and her family right now, as you promised."

"I want them to be taken care of."

A cold flash of success crossed Damon's face, and he nodded lightly. "Fine."

He never broke his word. After all, Grant would never escape his control anyway.

For the next two hours, the ICU became a makeshift operating room.

The Healing Affinity users poured their power into soothing and clearing Grant's damaged meridians. The research team connected bone and nerves with precision, carefully fitting, installing, and calibrating the advanced prosthetic.

Marcus watched from the side.

He was deeply shaken.

The Quinns were far stronger than he had known.

He thought of Paisley. He had to find a chance to tell her everything that had happened here.

When the metal limb locked into place, a faint current ran through Grant's body. The cold metal fused with his flesh and meridians with no awkwardness or stiffness at all.

He lifted the arm slightly. The metal arm turned smoothly. He clenched a fist, stretched, and pushed power through it. The motion was fluid, and explosive force surged out, far beyond what his real arm had been.

He could feel it clearly. The arm moved freely, and his power was greater than before.

Damon looked at the transformed man, fighting-ready again, and said flatly, "Get used to it. Your girlfriend's been brought here. Tomorrow I have a mission for you."

With that, he led his people out.

Soon the door opened again, and a slender, frail figure was led inside.

Chloe wore a faded, washed-out plain smock, nothing like her usual elegant clothes. A night in the Security Office had stripped away most of her pampered glow. Her hair hung loose at her temples, her face was a sickly pale, and fear and exhaustion clung to her.

She looked fragile and wrecked, and pitiful.

She kept her head bowed, her long lashes trembling, not daring to look around. She looked like a frightened, timid thing.

She stepped within reach of the bed, and her eyes met his without warning.

Grant sat straight and tall, spine like a pine. Even fresh from injury, he couldn't hide his cold, killing presence.

At his left shoulder was no longer warm flesh and blood, but a meteoric iron prosthetic.

The cold metal gave off a faint chill, intricate mechanical lines hid lethal power, and the Affinity chips in the joints flickered. It was hard, sharp, and icy.

It ground away the last of his gentle, boyish warmth and left him darker and more solitary, hard-edged from all he'd survived. He looked distant and frightening.

The instant their eyes met, Chloe's pupils shrank, and her heart lurched.

What surged up in her wasn't pity. It was a revulsion she couldn't control, a physical recoil.

She had always aimed high and craved strength, glamor, and perfection.

The old Grant had been a dazzling Level Six, proud and tall, the base's golden fighter that everyone envied, the best asset she had to show off.

Now he'd lost an arm, he was crippled, he wore a machine limb, and he looked savage and strange. He wasn't the flawless, brilliant young man he used to be.

Looking at that cold metal arm, a creeping layer of resistance and nausea spread through her. Even her breathing slowed.
