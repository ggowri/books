Chapter 104

Psychic Affinity really was powerful.

Even Orion and Elijah had almost been taken over by a Level Four Psychic zombie.

Ten minutes later, everyone stopped to rest at a spot about six miles away.

Orion walked over to Paisley and held out all his crystal cores. There were over a hundred of them, and every single one was Level Two or higher.

"Here, Pais. They're all yours."

Paisley was resting in the car with her eyes closed and a lollipop in her mouth.

When she heard Orion's voice, she opened her eyes and looked at him lazily.

"Keep your crystal cores, Orion. I've got my own."

Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley all came over too.

They each held out their crystal cores to her.

"Pais, you can have all of mine too."

"Mine too."

"Take all of mine too, Paisley. Without you, we'd probably all be dead by now, let alone have this many crystal cores."

Paisley stopped them. "Hold it.

"You all earned these yourselves.

"Keep them so you can level up. You can also use them to refill your energy whenever you need to.

"I've got plenty of my own.

"If you really feel bad about it, then let's do what we agreed on before. Everyone gives me 100 Level Two crystal cores, and we're even."

The second Paisley finished speaking, Elijah walked over and handed her 100 Level Two crystal cores.

"Here. We're still getting the better end of the deal."

This time, Paisley didn't hold back. She took them without a second thought.

The others stepped up one by one and handed her 100 Level Two crystal cores each.

Just like that, Paisley was loaded.

After such a brutal fight, everyone was too exhausted to move a muscle.

So Paisley went straight to the Cross-Dimensional Trading Plaza. She traded with the Ancient Realm princess for a whole table of lavish royal fare to reward the team.

There was delicate butter-poached shrimp, light and fresh, the meat snow-white and tender with a hint of herbs.

There were rich, slow-braised pork ribs, savory and so tender that the meat fell right off the bone.

For dessert, there was a delicate honey-glazed fruit cake and a warm rose-and-almond pudding, sweet but not too sweet, and easy on the stomach.

There was golden, glistening honey-glazed roast squab with crispy, reddish skin. Just looking at it was enough to make anyone's mouth water.

There was a pot of wild mushroom chicken broth, milky white and steaming, rich and comforting.

And there were big, glossy braised pork meatballs, juicy and coated in a thick, savory glaze.

Without being asked, the team brought over clean tables and set out one steaming dish after another.

Every dish was top-of-the-line royal food, beautifully plated and full of color, and every one of them smelled amazing.

It smelled so good.

In the middle of the apocalypse, a feast like this, as beautiful as it was delicious, was something nobody would've even dared to dream about.

The rich, mouthwatering smell quickly spread through the air.

Everyone's eyes went wide with shock and pure joy. They stared at the food, swallowing hard without even realizing it.

Of course, Rachel, Jasper, and the others didn't get to touch any of it.

They weren't part of this group, after all.

What confused Rachel was that she hadn't seen anybody cooking. So where had all that food come from?

Even in her past life, when she'd had her own Storage Dimension, she'd never been able to make food appear out of thin air.

So it never even crossed her mind that Paisley might be behind it.

All they could do was gnaw miserably on dry bread and rock-hard hardtack. It scraped their throats, and they had to wash it down with water.

As for Kelly, she'd chickened out of the fight earlier, and she was too embarrassed to come begging for food now.

All she could do was pretend to be asleep and hope everyone forgot she existed.

Marcus still hadn't fully recovered, so he was resting in Mason's car.

The delicious smell reached him too.

Before he could even get out of the car, Paisley walked over with a big plate piled high with food.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan were sitting in a car nearby. They hadn't eaten anything since the night before.

Their stomachs had been growling for ages.

Now that they could smell the food, they were even hungrier. It was almost unbearable.

Ryan sat up, leaned toward the window, and licked his lips.

"The food Paisley's carrying smells so good. If I could just have one bite, I'd die happy."

Reid looked over too, and right on cue, his stomach growled.

"If only we hadn't driven Paisley away. With her around, we'd never go hungry, at least.

"Owen, she always listened to you the most. Why don't you go ask her for some food?"
