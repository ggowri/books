Chapter 276

I had no idea your group was this strong.

Thank you all, truly, for saving us."

Elijah gave a small nod in response.

"No need. It wasn't much."

The commander, seeing the whole group was clearly made up of Affinity users with serious strength, made a sincere offer.

"Where are you all headed next? If you have no particular destination, you're welcome to join us at Stillwater Base.

With your skills, you'd easily rise to the top there. You'd make your mark in no time."

He added, patient and informative.

"Stillwater Base is the largest, best-equipped national base in the whole region. Secure, well-stocked, orderly.

If you're trying to find family or get in touch with anyone, going through the base is your fastest, safest option right now.

We're only half a day out from it."

Something flickered in Elijah's eyes. He pulled his officer's ID from his jacket and handed it over.

"This is my ID. Has your base opened a military air route yet?"

Maybe that would be the fastest way back to Aetheris.

Better to contact his family first.

The commander glanced down at the ID, and the moment he read the information, his expression sharpened. He snapped to a salute, his voice full of deference.

"Colonel Blackwood! My apologies, sir, I'd heard your name before but never imagined—"

He went on.

"Colonel, Stillwater Base just opened a military air route two days ago.

Are you heading back to Aetheris? Should I file the flight request for you?"

Elijah tucked the ID away.

"We have some personal matters to take care of first. We won't be traveling with you, but we'll report to Stillwater Base once we're done."

"Understood."

The commander didn't press, relief plain on his face.

This man's status was clearly not ordinary, and the pressure he carried wasn't something to trifle with — not to mention the rest of his group, especially that deceptively sweet-looking girl. None of them were people worth offending.

"I'll be waiting for you and your group at the base, then, Colonel. Be careful out there. The road ahead is dangerous."

The two groups exchanged brief farewells and went their separate ways.

The soldiers and survivors continued on toward Stillwater Base.

Watching them disappear into the distance, Elijah turned to Paisley and the others.

"Let's go. The armory."

They climbed back into the vehicles, engines humming to life, and turned toward the mountains.

The ride stayed quiet, the view outside growing more desolate — fewer buildings, more rolling dark forest.

Radiation had seeped into the land, twisting the trees and undergrowth into overgrown, mutated tangles, carrying none of a forest's usual life, only a grim, dead stillness.

Following the navigation, half an hour later the car rolled into the heart of the mountains.

The destination marker sat at the bottom of a valley — the Stillwater armory.

Twisted branches lined the road, vines bristling with sharp thorns sprawling everywhere, their dark green leaves giving off a faint, unnatural sheen. The wind stirred the brush into a dry rustle, like something crouched low and growling.

The whole mountain felt like a minefield, danger at every step.

A piercing shriek cut through the air.

A black cloud swept overhead — a flock of mutant crows!

Each one was three times the size of a normal crow, feathers black and hard as armor, eyes a bloody red, packed densely over the trees, fixed on the group trespassing into their territory.

Before anyone could react, the lead crow dove, throat swelling, and spat out a column of searing red flame.

Fire roared forward, heat rolling off it in waves, slamming straight toward the car and sealing off the road ahead in an instant.

"The crows breathe fire! Everyone out of the car, now!"

Orion barked the warning, already tense, shoving his door open and leaping clear.

Everyone followed, hitting the ground and scattering.

Paisley swept a hand through the air, and all three vehicles vanished into her storage.

The fire hadn't even died down before a new sound rose from the ground — heavy footsteps and snarling, closing in fast.

The brush on both sides of the road shook violently as dozens of mutant black wolves burst out, thick-coated and powerful, fangs gleaming, eyes a sickly green, cutting off every direction of retreat.

Fire from above, wolves from every side. The danger was absolute.

"Everyone, get ready to fight!"

Elijah's voice cut through, cold and commanding.

The group released their powers at once, bracing for the fight.
