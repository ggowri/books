Chapter 461

Elijah's face went grim. "Hold the formation! Don't panic!"

He made up his mind, eyes cold and sharp.

"Everyone keep pushing forward with the team. I'll deal with the leader. Kill it, and the horde loses its head and falls apart. Then breaking out is easy."

Cole stepped up without a second thought.

"Too dangerous. The Lizard King is a Level Seven beast. You can't take it alone. I'm coming with you."

Elijah gave a quick nod. "Fine. Orion, you're in command. Get everyone out of here."

With that settled, he turned. His eyes cut through the crowd and landed on Paisley at the back of the line. Worry and reluctance filled that one look.

It lasted a second. Then he turned away, and he and Cole sprinted toward the dark end of the corridor where the Lizard King lurked.

Elijah fired a shot at the beast and broke the other way.

The Level Seven mutant black-scale lizard in the shadows took the bait. Its furious roar shook the whole mall. It dropped the siege on the team and thundered after the two men.

With the Lizard King gone, the rest of the horde lost its leash. Their attacks slackened. The crushing weight on Falcon Squad and Lone Wolf Squad eased by more than half.

Orion didn't waste the opening. He ordered everyone to speed up and break out.

Teeth clenched, the group cut down the stragglers and pushed down the stairwell without stopping. A few minutes later they burst out of the mall and onto the open street.

They climbed into the vehicles and waited for Elijah and Cole.

Five minutes passed.

Wind howled outside. The street stayed dead silent, and there was no sign of either man. Everyone in the SUV felt their hearts climb into their throats.

"It's been five minutes. Why isn't Captain Blackwood out yet?"

Ashley's fists were clenched, her face pale. "That Lizard King is way too strong. There are only two of them. What if something went wrong?"

Thomas frowned hard. "They're probably pinned down in a fight. They can't break away."

The air in the car turned heavy. Worry pressed on everyone.

Then Paisley stood up. "I'll go check."

Orion's hand shot out and stopped her. "No. It's too risky. If anyone goes, it's me. Stay in the car. It's not safe out there."

He was already reaching for the door, ready to go back alone.

"Orion." Paisley's voice was soft. "I'm stronger than you, and I have more cards to play. You stay and hold the team together. Trust me. I'll come back, and I'll bring them with me."

Before anyone could argue, she gripped the Frostbloom Sword, its edge gleaming cold, and slipped out of the car. She ran back toward the dark mall without looking back.

******

On the fourth floor, the fight had already turned brutal.

Elijah was soaked in blood. His combat suit hung in shreds, and claw marks and poison welts covered his arms and shoulders. His Lightning Affinity was running dry, the sparks in his palm barely flickering.

Cole looked just as bad. His clothes were torn, his body battered, and both his strength and his power were gone. He was breathing in heavy gasps.

Together they had attacked with everything they had. They still couldn't get through the Lizard King's defenses.

The beast was covered in black scales as hard as tempered steel. Claws, a poisoned tail hook, and brutal charges came at them in an endless storm, and every blow could split stone.

They were at the end of their strength. All they could do was dodge and hold on.

"I can't take much more... We can't break this thing's defense!"

Cole panted, despair rising in his eyes.

Elijah gritted his teeth and tried to gather power for one more strike. A coppery taste flooded his throat, and black spots swam across his vision.
