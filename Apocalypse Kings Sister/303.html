Chapter 303

With the scene dealt with, the captain's expression smoothed back out. He turned to Elijah's group, all deference now.

"Colonel Blackwood, I'm sorry for what you went through. That's on us — we should've had better people at the gate."

"Our director's been waiting for word since dawn. The second you arrived, he wanted to see you personally. Right this way."

Elijah gave a small nod, unbothered, like the whole scene hadn't touched him at all.

Paisley held the little red fox steady against her chest, her expression unreadable, the edge from a minute ago already gone.

Orion, Thomas, and Ashley let their shoulders drop, the tension easing out of them.

They followed the captain through the crowd, past rows of watching, grateful faces, straight toward the base's central tower.

The main building was massive, built from reinforced concrete and steel, every line of it meant to say one thing: this is where the power lives.

They passed through corridor after corridor, checkpoint after checkpoint, until they reached a reception room on the top floor.

The room was bright and spacious, furnished simply but with weight to it, the air carrying a faint trace of tea.

A man in his fifties sat at the head of the room, his expression mild, a faint smile fixed at the corners of his mouth. He had the kind of face that put people at ease — which was exactly the point. Stillwater's people called him the smiling fox behind his back. Underneath the warmth was a man who calculated everything and struck without hesitation.

He rose the second they entered, stepping forward with both hands out, all warmth.

"Colonel Blackwood. Your reputation precedes you. It's an honor."

He shook each of their hands in turn, his smile easy and genuine.

"You're all exceptional. The kind of people who rise to the top in times like these, and with the character to match."

"We owe you everything for saving those twelve hundred people. Stillwater wouldn't exist as it is today without what you did."

"Our doors are always open to you. Stay as long as you like."

Elijah shook his hand, calm and unhurried, and got straight to it.

"I think you already know why we're here. We'd like to use a private military aircraft to get back to Aetheris."

The director's smile didn't waver, though something shifted behind his eyes.

"I can arrange a plane. I'll have you back in Aetheris."

"But I have one condition."

Elijah had expected as much. Nothing came free.

"What's the condition? If it's reasonable, we'll consider it."

The director paused, his tone turning serious.

"A week ago, this base took on an S-tier-plus mission. I've sent several elite teams after it. Every one either wiped out or came back comatose. We still don't know what happened down there."

"I need your help finishing it. Whether or not you succeed, I'll keep my word and fly you back to Aetheris."

Elijah's eyes narrowed slightly.

"What exactly is the mission?"

"The target is an underground research facility here in Stillwater's territory."

"This concerns the future of the human race."

The director's face went grave as he laid it out.

"We need a team to go in, recover every piece of research data, every mutant sample, every piece of equipment, and bring it all out intact."

Paisley and Elijah exchanged a glance, both of their expressions tightening at once.

The parallel to the Marisport facility hit them immediately. Two labs, nearly identical in design, had to be run by the same hidden hand.

Secret facilities like this kept turning up across the country, all of them experimenting on mutant samples and unknown Affinities. Maybe the apocalypse itself hadn't been an accident at all.

What was this hidden force really after?

Whatever had caused the collapse of the world, the truth behind it might be darker than anyone had guessed.

Right then, Trovie's chime sounded clear in her mind.

[Host, a hidden main questline has been activated: Locate every secret research facility in the country, recover all scattered research data and equipment, uncover the truth behind the apocalypse, and eliminate the root cause of man-made mutation.]

[Reward on completion: 100,000 points, one premium lucky draw!]

Seeing their expressions, the director spoke again, voice full of sincerity.
