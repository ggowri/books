Chapter 53

Jasper had never imagined this trip would go so wrong. They hadn't even finished the mission.

He'd barely made it out alive. Nine of them had rolled out in a big, cocky crew, and now he was the only one still breathing.

Bone-deep terror crept through his whole body. His back was soaked in cold sweat, and his legs shook so badly that he'd have fallen over if he weren't holding onto the car door.

That was fucking terrifying.

Even now, he was still rattled. He kept seeing the Level Three mutant humanoid wiping the floor with everyone, over and over.

The apocalypse is real. If you're naive or weak, you're dead.

In that moment, it finally clicked. In this new world, the only way to survive was to get stronger, no matter what it took.

He never wanted to come that close to dying again.

After a short rest, everyone piled into the car and headed back.

Mason drove, and Elijah sat in the passenger seat, his face calm. Nobody could tell what he was thinking.

Four crates of medical supplies were strapped to the roof.

For once, the car was quiet.

Paisley pulled three Level Two crystal cores out of her Storage Dimension, one to match each of the guys' affinities, and handed them out. "Find some time to absorb these and level up, fast."

Her face turned serious as she warned them. "There's going to be a massive storm for the next two days. Once it's over, every zombie around here will get stronger and evolve.

"Level One zombies don't have cores yet. But after the storm, they'll evolve fast and start growing white cores in their heads. They'll be way more dangerous, and staying alive will only get harder."

The second she finished, the mood in the car turned heavy.

Orion quickly waved her off, his voice earnest.

"Paisley, these cores are way too valuable. Keep them so you can level up yourself. You don't have to share them with us."

"He's right," Elijah said, turning her down too. "We can hunt our own cores later. You keep them."

But Paisley didn't pull her hand back. "We're a team. We don't need to keep score like that."

She turned to Orion and explained patiently. "Orion, you've got Metal Affinity, and this one's a Level Two Metal core. It's perfect for you.

"The higher you go, the harder it gets to level up."

Then, Paisley turned to Elijah.

"Elijah, think of this one as me paying you back. You gave me a core before, and I've ended up with most of the cores we've hunted as a team. I haven't forgotten that."

Finally, she smiled at Mason. "Mason, don't be polite with me either.

"Nobody makes it through the apocalypse alone. We have to stick together to survive. If all of us get stronger, our team will only get better, and we can all make it back to Aetheris in one piece."

She meant every word, and the three of them knew she was right. They stopped arguing and put the cores away, each of them feeling a whole mix of emotions.

Orion frowned, his face serious. "Paisley, are you sure there's going to be a huge storm for the next two days?"

Elijah looked over too, curious and a little skeptical. He clearly wanted to know if her information was legit.

Paisley nodded. "It starts tonight, as soon as it gets dark.

"It'll pour for two days straight, so there's no way we can go out. We'll have to stay in Southspire for at least two more days."

Then, she added, "Good timing, actually. My Storage Dimension just grew to 10,000 square feet. We can use these two days to stock up."

She was deliberately hiding the real size of her dimension. She always kept something in reserve and never showed all her cards.

Orion's jaw dropped. He stared at her, stunned.

"Your Storage Dimension leveled up again, Paisley? That's awesome! You're... you're unbelievable!"

Even Elijah, who never let his feelings show, couldn't help glancing at her in surprise. He looked genuinely happy.

But he quickly pulled himself together and started planning their next move.

"In that case, we can't waste any time. We need to stock up on as much as we can before the storm hits this evening.

"Since we have plenty of room, we can hit the city's emergency food reserve for food and water. We should also grab a ton of gasoline and fuel, so we can keep driving and run generators later."
