Chapter 405

"I don't care who you've been chasing or what connections you've made. Call Mr. Quinn right now and have him let your aunt out."

Paisley stayed still, her eyes flat. Dennis's face darkened, and he threw out a threat.

"I'm warning you. You'll handle this, or I won't recognize you as a Thompson. You'll have no place here, and you won't be able to hold your head up anywhere on the base."

Then he got greedy. "And as compensation, I'm taking all this food and every supply in this house. Think of it as making up for what my branch lost today."

The room went silent.

Brenda was the one who'd done wrong, and she was being punished as she should. Yet he wanted the junior branch to apologize, hand over supplies, and make Paisley beg.

It was shameless. He was twisting everything.

"Dennis, how can you be so unreasonable?" Gerald's chest heaved, and his face turned gray. "Your wife bullied mine for years. She brought this on herself."

"You come here at midnight, and instead of reflecting, you threaten my daughter and demand supplies. You've got some nerve."

Diane's eyes were red. Normally gentle, she snapped back to shield her daughter.

"We just want to live in peace. You won't recognize Pais? She doesn't need your recognition. You're selfish and small-minded."

Orion's expression went ice-cold. He rose half a step, angling himself in front of Paisley, and said in a low voice, "Uncle Dennis, that's enough. Don't use your age to bully people."

"We stopped being family with your branch a long time ago."

Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley were furious, and none of them hid their contempt anymore.

Dennis showed no shame. He pushed harder, sure that his seniority gave him the upper hand and that they wouldn't dare tear things apart.

Paisley sat where she was. Her face was calm, and her eyes held only a deep, cold indifference.

While Dennis ran his mouth, making threats and demanding supplies, she lifted a finger, tapped her phone, and dialed the Security Patrol hotline.

She didn't waste words. People this shameless only understood rules and the law.

A few minutes later, quick, even footsteps came up the path.

Several Security Patrol officers burst in and locked on to Dennis, who was still swaggering, and Chloe, who was still pretending to be fragile.

The patrol captain stepped forward. "We received a report of trespassing, harassment, threats, and attempted seizure of private property. You're both coming back to the Security Office for questioning."

Dennis's arrogance cracked. "You're arresting me? Do you know who I am?"

"Who called you? They're family. You can't arrest us."

"Family?"

Paisley finally looked up. Her cold eyes landed on him.

"Is this your idea of family? Bullying people, twisting the truth, showing up at night to make threats, trying to take what isn't yours?"

"You're an elder, and you let your family do evil. When they got caught, you came here to cause more trouble. You don't get to say 'family.'"

Her voice was cool and even, and it carried weight.

Chloe went pale. The warmth of her full stomach vanished, and she began to shake. She couldn't act anymore. She was terrified.

She never imagined that her father's visit would land her in the Security Office too.

"Uncle Gerald, we're sorry. Please let us go. We won't ever come back..."

She turned to her soft-hearted uncle.

Dennis panicked. He couldn't believe his brother would be this cold, this ruthless.
