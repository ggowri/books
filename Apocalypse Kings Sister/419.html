Chapter 419

But she buried it deep and didn't let a trace show.

Being locked up had stripped away all her pride.

With no one to back her and nothing but cold looks and humiliation, she had finally seen how brutal reality was.

******

Grant was still a Level Six Affinity user, even if damaged. He was her only backer right now, the one thing that could get her and her family out and let them stand on their own on the base.

She couldn't lose him. She couldn't offend him.

In an instant, all the disgust and contempt drained away, and her eyes filled with a perfectly timed haze of hurt and heartache, fragile and helpless.

"Grant..."

Her voice shook, thick with tears. Hot tears spilled over her lids and rolled down her pale cheeks without warning. She looked heartbroken and wronged, as if every tear were real.

Before her eyes rose the Grant of the past, the young man who had always stood in front of her and shielded her from every storm.

Next to him, the man in front of her was covered in scars, missing an arm, and hard-eyed. The contrast made her tears look more real, and they hid every dark thought.

On the bed, Grant looked at the girl crying so miserably, and his heart softened.

Days of agony after passing out, despair over his arm, humiliation at becoming a pawn, the bleak road ahead. All the sourness, guilt, and helplessness he had bottled up came flooding back, tangled in his chest.

He lifted the new metal arm. It was cool, the joints turned smoothly, and force lay hidden inside.

His movements were stiff and clumsy, awkward with unease.

He wanted to wipe the tears from her face and calm her. But his fingertips hung in the air, and he couldn't bring himself to touch her.

The metal was hard and cold with no warmth of flesh. He was afraid its chill would hurt her tender cheek, and afraid its mechanical look would scare the girl he'd fought so hard to protect.

In the end, that unstoppable weapon of an arm just hung there, then dropped.

Grant's throat bobbed, and his voice came out dry and rough, tired from surviving, and extremely gentle.

"Chloe, don't cry."

Even though he had just signed a contract for his life, and become Damon's pawn forever, even though he now had a damaged body, a harsh road ahead, and no freedom, and the rest of his life would be a chain.

If she was safe and happy, if she was fine and could smile like before, then this deal was worth it.

Chloe stepped forward half a pace, bent a little, and let the tears flow. Her eyes were red, her voice cracked as she leaned against the bed, full of fear and grievance.

"Grant, thank goodness you're awake. Thank goodness."

"While you were out, I was terrified. I was scared every single day. I was afraid you'd never wake up and I'd never see you again."

Her words were moving and sincere. Then her tone twisted, thick with resentment, every word an accusation.

"Grant, do you know what happened?

It was Paisley!

She targeted me on purpose. She put me and my family in the Security Office, and we suffered and were humiliated!"

She looked up at him through tears, gripped the hem of his shirt, and shook it lightly.

"You have to get revenge for me. She did this to me. She doesn't even take you seriously."

Looking at her wronged face, Grant's heart was fully stirred. All his tenderness turned into cold anger.

He pushed himself upright, back straight, the last of his weakness gone, and the fighter's aura came back around him.

He lifted his warm right hand and stroked her messy hair, as gently as he could.

As he looked down, the glint of the metal prosthetic caught his eye, and a freezing, dark chill passed through his gaze.

His voice was low and cold, resolute and protective.

"Don't worry, Chloe. I'm here."

"Anyone who's hurt you, I won't let off. Not one of them."

His strength has grown now. He might even be a match for her.

Leaning against him, Chloe lowered her eyes, and deep inside them a faint, icy smile curved.

There was no gratitude in her, only calculation and coldness.
