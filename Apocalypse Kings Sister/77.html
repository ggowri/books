Chapter 77

Ashley had been watching from her seat the whole time, completely in awe.

She couldn't help gasping out loud.

"Orion, that's incredible! These gold lilies are gorgeous. It looks way prettier than before!"

Orion handed the perfectly restored pendant to Paisley and looked at her fondly.

"Do you like it? If you're not into lilies, I can do other designs too."

Paisley took it and ran her fingers over the smooth stone and the raised gold lilies. It felt silky to the touch, the design was elegant and classy, and every last detail was beautifully done.

She'd actually planned to just throw it away, since it was useless now anyway.

But after Orion had gone to all that trouble, she couldn't bring herself to toss it.

In the end, she hung the pendant around her neck.

Her eyes crinkled into a warm, genuine smile. "It's beautiful. Thanks, Orion."

In the passenger seat, Madison nodded eagerly.

"It's so elegant! The lilies are classy and sophisticated. It's the perfect finishing touch!

"Orion's got serious skills."

Ashley knew exactly how Madison felt about Orion.

So she decided to tease her.

"Madison, if you ever need anything, you should totally ask Orion for help. I mean, considering how close you two are."

"Ashley's right," Orion said. "Anything you need, just ask."

He glanced at Madison and added shyly.

"If you like this kind of pendant too, I'll make you one once I find the materials."

Madison's cheeks turned bright red, and her ears burned. She ducked her head, so flustered that she couldn't say a word, but a sweet, warm feeling quietly spread through her.

Ashley looked back and forth between the two of them, both so obviously flustered, and gave a knowing smile. Something was definitely going on there.

Paisley was holding the little white cat, and they both had lollipops in their mouths.

With a single thought, she pulled another solid gold bar out of her Storage Dimension, along with a pendant just like the one she was wearing. She held them both out to Orion.

Then, she teased him.

"Orion, you can't just wait around when you're chasing a girl, can you? Here, take these."

The heavy gold bar and the beautiful pendant landed in his hands.

Ashley, Madison, and Elijah all turned to stare at Paisley in shock.

Just how many gold bars and pendants did Paisley have stashed away?

Talk about deep pockets!

Orion looked at what Paisley had given him, and warmth flooded his chest. He smiled at her fondly. "Thanks, Pais."

Without another moment's hesitation, he called on his Metal Affinity again.

Energy flowed out of him and gently wrapped around the new gold bar and the pendant. The soft liquid gold slowly spread across the surface, following the stone's natural grain as it took shape.

This time, instead of cool, elegant lilies, the gold formed layer after layer of full, raised roses in bloom.

The petals were lush and full, the lines soft and tender, and the flowers looked delicate and romantic. Wrapped around the smooth stone, the glowing gold was passionate and gentle at the same time, like a burning, heartfelt confession of love.

A few minutes later, a brand-new pendant covered in gold roses was finished. It was luxurious and romantic.

Orion held the warm pendant in his fingers and looked at Madison tenderly. Without hesitating, he turned and shyly held out the rose pendant, the symbol of how he felt.

"I hope you like it."

Madison stared at the impossibly beautiful pendant in her palm and the gold roses in full bloom. Her chest flooded with warmth, and a sweet, aching tenderness spread through her whole body.

Of course she knew what roses meant.

She'd always assumed Orion would never figure out how she felt, let alone tell her he felt the same way.

But somehow, the wish she'd held onto for so long had actually come true.

In that moment, her heart was overflowing.

Her throat went tight, and tears welled up in her eyes.

She looked up at Orion, took the rose pendant, and hung it around her neck, her eyes shining. "I love it!"

It wasn't just the pendant she loved. She loved Orion too.

Their eyes met, and a soft, lingering warmth filled the car. No words were needed.

In that one look, they both knew exactly how the other felt.

In the back seat, Paisley lounged lazily with the little white cat in her arms, grinning as she watched the two of them finally get together. She was enjoying it like it was her favorite rom-com.
