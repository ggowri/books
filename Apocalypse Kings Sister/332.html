Reid's whole body ached from the fall, and the humiliation burned worse than the bruises. Rage and resentment churned in his chest as he hauled himself up off the ground, fists clenched, eyes full of spite.

He knew he couldn't match her physically, and pushing further would only humiliate him more, but he refused to back down, grinding out a vicious parting shot.

With that, he shot one last glare at the door, turned, and limped off, bitter and seething, his retreat as pathetic as it was stubborn.

He wasn't going to accept walking away with nothing.

Paisley was the only shortcut left to him now.

Ashley watched him stagger off, the chill in her eyes slow to fade, and let out a cold laugh.

"Ruined? We'll see about that. We'll find out soon enough who actually ends up dropping a rock on their own foot."

She turned, pulled the door shut behind her, and let her edge fade as she went back inside.

Paisley had been awake the whole time, sitting calmly against her headboard. She'd heard every word of the shouting, the threats, the standoff outside.

Reid's guilt-tripping and threats hadn't ruffled her at all. All she felt for the Lennoxes now was irritation.

In her last life, he'd been an accomplice to her death.

This time around, they hadn't done anything unforgivable enough yet, which made it hard to justify striking first.

She had a Misfortune Talisman in hand, though. She could at least collect a little interest first.

Ashley knocked and came over, taking her hand, worry written all over her face.

"Paisley, did he wake you up?

That loser came to start trouble, but I ran him off.

I'm worried he'll actually go spread lies around the base. People will start gossiping, and it'll hurt your reputation."

Paisley turned her hand over and patted the back of Ashley's, her tone easy.

"Don't worry about it."

"That's their usual move. Twist the facts, smear someone else, get attention."

"You think I'm scared of tricks that cheap?"

"I haven't had the chance to deal with them properly yet. If they keep coming to me looking for trouble, I'll happily oblige."

Hearing how calm she sounded, Ashley's nerves melted away instantly. Her eyes lit back up, shining with pure admiration.

"You're amazing, Paisley! It's like there's nothing you can't handle!"

Paisley's mouth curved faintly. She wasn't losing any sleep over something this small.

The Lennoxes were nothing more than clowns. They couldn't stir up anything worth worrying about.

That evening, the doorbell rang. A base courier delivered a gold-embossed, elaborately sealed invitation — the base's highest-tier envelope, for its most exclusive auction.

Elijah Blackwood opened it, scanned the contents, and looked up at the others.

"It's an invitation to the base's top-tier auction. If anyone's interested, it could be worth seeing."

Ashley snatched the invitation right up, turning it over in her hands, eyes bright with curiosity. She tugged at Paisley's sleeve, practically bouncing.

"I wonder what kind of rare treasures they'd auction off after the apocalypse — it has to be interesting! Paisley, can we go too? Please?"

Paisley always kept the ledger even — one measure of kindness from someone, and she gave back ten.

Ashley and the rest of the Thompsons had looked out for her this whole way, thinking of nothing but her. That kind of unguarded devotion softened even someone as naturally reserved as Paisley.

She gave in with a small nod.

"Fine. I'll go with you."

Orion Thompson chimed in with a grin.

"Sure, nothing else going on tonight anyway. Let's all go check it out. I'm curious too — what kind of treasures show up when the whole apocalypse's scarce resources get auctioned off in one place."

At that, Elijah seemed to remember something. He pulled out his personal terminal and transferred ten thousand contribution points to Paisley.

"Your share of the mission payout.

If there's something you like at the auction, I can bid on it for you."

Paisley watched the ten thousand points land in her account. Total contribution: 191,690.

She thanked him quietly.

"Thank you."
