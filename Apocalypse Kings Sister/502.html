Chapter 502

Jasper's two teammates followed and climbed in.

Paisley didn't spare Rachel's group another glance. She started the engine.

The tires crunched through thick snow as the SUV turned around and pulled away from the mall.

The sound of the engine faded. The storm swallowed the empty lot again.

Rachel's group watched the car vanish, and the last thread of hope snapped.

Despair soaked into every one of them.

Were they really going to die here today?

The surviving snow rats kept screaming and lunging in turns.

Their powers were nearly gone, their wounds stiff with frost. They clenched their teeth and fought with the last of their strength, struggling to live in the ice and snow.

Behind the wheel, Paisley glanced into the rearview mirror at the group trapped in the rat swarm. A cold smile curved her lips, and a rush of satisfaction ran through her.

She'd been reborn. The reason she hadn't simply finished them off was that she wanted to take her time.

Death was too easy.

She wanted them stuck in endless torment, living in hell day after day, eaten alive by regret, pain, and suspicion. Unable to live, unable to die.

Traitors belong in hell.

******

The snow came down harder and harder. A white curtain shut out the view, and the world was blank white.

The SUV rolled steadily over the frozen road.

Half an hour later, it stopped outside the mission hall.

They got out and went inside with their mission cards to turn in the mission.

The clerk checked the details. When he saw it was Paisley and her group, his face broke into a happy grin.

He led them straight to the base's storage warehouse to inspect the cold-weather supplies they'd collected.

The warehouse was empty. Plenty of base members and support staff had heard and come to watch, all of them desperate for these supplies.

"Ms. Lennox, are the supplies ready?"

The inspector asked.

Paisley nodded, thought a command, and waved a hand lightly.

In the next instant, mountains of heavy down jackets, warm comforters, blankets, heaters, and diesel generators,

crate after crate of cold-weather supplies, materialized out of nowhere and covered the huge warehouse floor in neat rows. Most of the empty space filled in a heartbeat.

Beside her, Jasper's pupils shrank. He stared at the scene, mouth slightly open, completely dazed.

He'd seen Paisley store supplies at the mall. But watching so much come out at once still hit him with overwhelming force.

The warehouse staff's eyes lit up. They hurried forward to count, unable to hide their joy.

Only Ms. Lennox could bring back this many warm coats in one trip.

Plenty of people had taken missions today, but only she'd brought back this much.

Thank goodness for her. A lot fewer people would die at the base.

The onlookers erupted in cheers.

"So many coats and comforters! This fills a huge chunk of our shortage!"

"Great, with this many supplies, a lot of people won't freeze!"

"Ms. Lennox is incredible! She brought back this much in one trip!"

"Thank you! She's never let us down!"

…

Paisley split the rewards evenly among four. Each of them received 1,250 contribution points and 125 Level Three crystal cores.

Her panel read: total contribution points, 262,640.

At the same time, Trovie's voice chimed in her head.

[Side quest complete!]

[Reward: 10,000 points and one Affinity Awakening Pill. Issued.]

[Current total: 3,570,360 points! Affinity Awakening Pill x1]

With the Awakening Pill in hand, she could awaken a new affinity tonight.

With all the paperwork done, the group split up outside the mission hall.

Jasper turned to Paisley, sincere. "Thank you, Paisley. I hope you'll take me along again next time. I've never had a mission go so smoothly."

Paisley's face was calm. She answered without any particular feeling.
