Chapter 257

Trovie's voice turned urgent.

[Host, save them! There are people trapped next door!]

Paisley's eyes went cold. She planted her weight low and kicked, hard, straight into the wall.

CRASH—!

The wall caved in, dust and debris flying, leaving a gaping hole.

The filthy, ruined room behind it lay exposed.

Dim, fetid air, a stench that turned the stomach.

Five or six thin, battered girls huddled in the corner, clothes torn, faces hollow with fear and shock.

Two men in black stood in the center, arms crossed, watching with leering indifference.

On the bed, worst of all, a man was in the middle of assaulting one of the girls.

The sudden collapse of the wall snapped every eye in the room toward Paisley.

She stood in the settling dust, her white clothes untouched, looking like a full moon that had wandered into a swamp.

That same beauty made every man in the room underestimate her instantly, taking her for nothing more than a pretty, helpless girl.

The man on the bed looked her over shamelessly, lust flooding his eyes.

"Where'd a pretty thing like you come from? Lucky me, you're mine too now."

He didn't rate her as any kind of threat, not even with a stranger watching, and kept right on doing what he was doing.

The girls in the corner saw just a slight young woman, and whatever hope had flickered in them died, replaced by deeper despair.

One, braver than the rest, cried out through tears.

"Run! Get out of here! They're monsters, you can't save us, don't throw your life away too!"

Everyone assumed she'd end up just like them.

Then, without warning, Paisley's Level Five peak Psychic Affinity exploded outward.

An invisible storm of will swept the room, locking onto the man on the bed.

His smirk froze, then his mind erupted in unbearable, tearing agony.

"AAAH! My head! It HURTS!"

He couldn't withstand a high-tier Psychic Affinity at all, clutching his head, screaming, convulsing as he fell off the bed and onto his knees, unable even to stand.

A single moment, and he was finished.

The other man went pale, about to react—

Paisley's Psychic Affinity surged again, seizing control of his mind outright.

His eyes went blank, his body no longer his own.

Her voice came out flat, emotionless.

"Kill him."

The man obeyed instantly, picking up a blade and raising it high.

One swing. Clean.

A wet thud, and blood sprayed.

The man who'd been screaming on his knees went silent for good.

The whole thing took two seconds.

Silence fell, so complete you could hear a pin drop.

The girls in the corner stared, wide-eyed, unable to process what they'd just seen.

That slight, pretty girl was this strong.

She'd taken down two of their tormentors without even breaking stride.

The last man, still under her control, stood frozen, terror flooding what little mind he had left, unable to move a muscle.

Her gaze swept over the wreckage, over the battered girls, cold deepening in her eyes.

The worst thing in this ruined world was never the zombies. It was people like this.

She spoke, calm and even, her voice landing on the girls like salvation.

"It's okay now. You're safe."

"They can't move. If you want revenge, it's yours to take."

Her eyes moved over each of them, every word landing clear and direct.
