Chapter 252

"He's had this whole village under his thumb for ages! The real villagers are either dead or locked up as zombie food! Everyone left is one of his men!"

Gasping for air, the pressure choking him, he kept pouring out every secret he had.

"There's thirty-four of us with Affinities, all under Fat Wagner's orders! He was Level Four peak, and tonight he's going to hit Level Five!"

"The village's biggest secret... it's in the mansion's basement!"

******

His eyes flooded with fear as he spoke, his voice shaking.

"He's turned it into some kind of pen. Hundreds of zombies down there, plus a bunch of kidnapped survivors, mostly girls from nearby villages and travelers passing through."

"He feeds them to the zombies to level them up, then kills the zombies and takes their cores to level up his own Affinity!"

"The women, he does whatever he wants with. The men go straight to the basement. That's how he took over this whole area, no law, nothing stopping him!"

The others strung up beside him chimed in, desperate to back him up.

"It's all true! He's killed more people than anyone can count! There's nothing human left in him!"

"We didn't have a choice! If we didn't do what he said, we'd be down there too!"

"Please, we were forced into this, let us go!"

...

They all begged, all scared to die.

These three in front of them were clearly way out of their league.

There wasn't a flicker of anything in Paisley's eyes. Just cold, heavy stillness.

Surviving this world was hard for everyone. Some people banded together to make it through. Others turned on their own kind, using lives as stepping stones for their own greed. That was unforgivable.

People really were scarier than the apocalypse itself.

"Which house? Where's the basement entrance?"

she asked, flat and cold.

Thoroughly broken, the leader didn't dare hold anything back.

"Far end of the village! I can take you there! The basement's under a fake rockery in the back garden, hidden door! Someone's guarding the entrance round the clock!"

Elijah's expression darkened further, his eyes burning.

"We can't just walk away from this."

His voice was low, firm.

"If we leave these people here, who knows how many more they'll kill."

He turned to Paisley, already planning.

"You and the others stay here. I'll go scout it out first. If I'm not back by dawn, don't wait. Get out of Wexford."

Orion stepped up immediately, dead serious.

"What's that supposed to mean? Going in alone? You think I'm not your brother or something?"

"If we're going, we're going together. I'm coming."

He turned to the others, grave enough that it sounded like a will being read.

"They've got numbers and we don't know what else is down there. Everyone stay here. If the captain and I aren't back by dawn, don't wait. Leave, and don't look back."

"Thomas, Spencer, you're the oldest ones here. It's on you to get everyone home to Aetheris safe."

The weight of it settled heavy over everyone listening.

Paisley frowned and shut it down flat.

"No."

"I'm the one who found all this. It started with me. I'm coming."

She knew exactly how strong Elijah and Orion were.

Both of them had stood at the top of this ruined world in her last life, hardened by countless near-deaths. They weren't about to go down here.

Her gaze drifted over the bound men and landed on the weakest of them, the one who'd been shaking and begging the hardest.

"He's our guide. He's the most scared of dying."

The man flinched and nodded frantically, desperate to live, not daring to argue.

Ashley couldn't help stepping forward, her eyes pleading.
