Chapter 283

Paisley couldn't quite hide the excitement in her eyes.

She was so close to becoming a Level Four trader now.

Something occurred to her, and she let her psychic senses flare outward, sweeping through the deeper reaches of the armory.

Her mind spread out like a fine web, sweeping precisely through every corner.

A hidden storage room came into sharp focus in her awareness.

Inside, rows of fuel drums, diesel tanks, and pressurized gas canisters sat stacked neatly.

In a world where resources had run dry, fuel and gas were worth more than gold — the lifeblood of vehicles, mechs, generators, heaters. Money alone couldn't buy them anymore.

Pulling her senses back, Paisley turned to Elijah, her voice light.

"Captain, let's head that way."

Elijah's footsteps slowed, his dark eyes settling on her profile, warmth pooling in them as he spoke softly.

"Pais, now that we're fighting side by side as teammates, could we change how you address me?"

There was a hint of careful hope in his voice.

"You've always called me Captain. It feels distant. I'll call you Pais from now on. Would it be all right if you just used my name?"

Paisley blinked, mildly caught off guard, privately wondering what this powerhouse was up to.

She pressed her lips together, tried the word, and shook her head slightly.

"Elijah? No, I can't bring myself to say that."

After a moment's thought, she looked up at him, her voice soft and sweet.

"I'll call you Eli from now on."

At that, something lit up in Elijah's eyes, warmth filling his chest with a satisfaction he'd never felt before.

Eli.

A name that belonged to her and her alone, said that way by no one else. Special. Exclusive. It sounded better than anything he'd ever been called.

For some reason, hearing his own name spoken in her voice felt unbearably tender, settling deep in his chest, warming something that lingered.

"All right."

His voice came low and warm, unable to hide the smile in it.

"Whatever you want."

They turned and walked toward the storage room side by side.

The door had no complicated lock, just a simple alloy sliding panel, and it opened easily with a light push.

The instant the door swung open, a room full of energy supplies came into view.

Rows of metal fuel drums lined the storage room floor, gasoline and diesel neatly sorted and labeled, and nearby, reinforced crates held pressurized gas canisters, all perfectly intact, in staggering quantities.

Only the military could stockpile this much fuel.

Paisley's eyes brightened, and she couldn't help letting out a small delighted sound.

"Wow, didn't even have to work for this one. Take it, take it all, into storage!"

She focused her will, and her storage power surged out, pulling hundreds upon hundreds of fuel drums and gas canisters into the air, drawing them in neat order into her massive storage space. In moments the room was swept completely clean.

Elijah stood beside her, impressed.

"Pais, are you some kind of good-luck charm? Your luck is ridiculous. Finding this much gasoline, diesel, and gas canisters in one place.

This stuff is real currency in this world. Priceless."

Paisley stored away the last fuel drum.

"Eli, this energy haul, plus the armory supplies and grain from before — I want to keep half for myself and split the rest with you. Sound fair?"

She never hoarded credit. If they fought side by side, the rewards should be shared evenly.

Elijah laughed at that, his eyes going soft and warm.

"Whatever you say, Pais."

He went on, slow and sincere.

"If it weren't for you, we never would've found this hidden armory, and even if we had, nobody could've hauled away this much in one trip."

"The fact that you're willing to share half with us — I'm already grateful for that."

******

He glanced at the now-empty storage room, his voice earnest.

"I won't pretend I'm too proud to accept it, because our vehicles, equipment, and generators all need fuel badly, and the rest of the team will too eventually. Thank you for this, Pais."

Paisley looked at him. Powerhouse or not, he really was straightforward about things.

In a world where everyone looked out for themselves first, it was rare to find someone who could face this much wealth without greed, who understood gratitude.

"No need to thank me."

She shook her head lightly.

"We're fighting together. What's good for one of us is good for all of us."

In her storage, Trovie went wild with secondhand delight, chirping excitedly.
