Chapter 424

"My baby girl isn't someone you get to bully!"

Gordon and Helen refused to give up. They tried to step closer and press their case.

Paisley rose slowly. Her cool eyes passed over them, and the last of her patience was gone. Only a freezing chill remained.

"I cut ties with the Lennoxes a long time ago."

"Get out."

One word, and her presence turned to frost. A powerful wave of mental pressure spread out, pressing down on them until they couldn't breathe and went rigid.

They finally understood. The girl they'd mistreated for so long was no longer the weak, helpless kid who took whatever they dished out.

She had grown strong, and she had backers now. She wasn't afraid of their pressure.

She meant to cut them off for good.

The crushing pressure, along with the Thompsons glaring and closing in, was too much. Their faces drained of color and they panicked.

They didn't even care about looking dignified. They stumbled out of the villa gate in a rush.

The gate slammed shut and sealed everything out.

Quiet returned, but the sourness and heartache still hung over everyone.

Diane quickly took Paisley's hand, her eyes full of pain. "Sweetheart, don't think about those unhappy things anymore. I'm here now. Nobody will bully you again."

Gerald's eyes were red at the edges. He gripped his fork tight and said nothing.

Orion's fists were clenched. He wanted to tear the Lennoxes apart.

He had thought he knew how Paisley had lived. He hadn't. What he knew was only the tip of the iceberg.

The Lennoxes deserved to die. How dare they come back?

******

Ashley sniffled and hugged Paisley. "Paisley, from now on I'll protect you..."

Thomas and Spencer were furious, their feelings churning.

Paisley nodded gently, and the cold in her eyes faded, leaving a bit of warmth.

"Don't look like that. I stopped being sad a long time ago."

The Paisley who had once cared about the Lennoxes had already died once, in her last life.

She had long since seen what kind of people they were.

She no longer cared about them.

She knew exactly what they were like. They never came without a reason. Why had they come tonight?

Did they really just want her back?

But why?

Because she had grown strong, and now they regretted it?

She thought of Rachel, and of Owen, Reid, and Ryan.

Good. They were all alive. Now it was time to repay grudges and settle debts.

It was time to get justice for her last self.

******

At nine the next morning, bright sunlight cut through the clouds and spilled over the glass windows of the base's main tower.

The base's top-level operations room was grim and tense, the lights fully on.

The hall was wide and orderly. Huge maps of the Exclusion Zone hung on the walls, marking supply points and high-risk mutant beast zones. Dense red lines marked deadly dangers, and the whole room felt like war.

All of Falcon Squad was present.

The team members stood in tight formation in matching black combat gear, made of wear-resistant, scratch-proof fabric with Affinity protection layers inside. Each wore a gilded falcon insignia on the shoulder, gleaming under the cold light, a symbol of the base's greatest squad.

The usual ease was gone from their eyes. They were steady, sharp, and resolved.

They radiated a hard, iron presence.

Elijah sat at the head, upright, steady and cold, with the decisive, calm look of a commander.

Paisley wore a simple combat uniform that suited her lean, sharp frame, her cool eyes unmoved, seated in the very last chair.

The others stayed at their stations, wary out of habit.

Some had their eyes locked on the map, silently reviewing routes and plans.

Everyone looked grim. They all knew this mission wasn't just a supply run, and they had to be fully prepared, because it might well be a long-planned ambush.

The main door opened.

Nathaniel entered in a crisp army-green uniform, his shoulder insignia gleaming, his steps steady and commanding. He carried the weight of his position.

He slowly swept his gaze over Falcon Squad standing in formation, over each young, resolute face, and his eyes filled with approval and gravity.
