Chapter 294

The trader, seeing the sheer volume of goods, offered genuine thanks.

Pleasure doing business! Thank you so much!

If it weren't for your supplies, I'd never have climbed this high in interstellar society. Even the Federation's top enforcer values what I provide now, and has personally assigned people to gather gold and energy crystals for me. I expect we'll be trading again before long!

Paisley nodded, composed, as the deal wrapped up.

Ding!

Trovie's crisp chime rang out right on schedule.

Major interstellar trade completed! Reward: 10,000 points!

Current total points: 1,017,060

Host, the Beast Realm trader has been waiting a long time, requesting the connection repeatedly, desperate to trade!

A flicker of interest crossed Paisley's eyes, and she tapped the light screen.

Connect.

Time and space shifted again, and the channel switched over, revealing the bleak, frozen landscape of the Beast Realm.

A harsh wind howled across the wasteland, dead grass flattened under its force, the whole land gray and desolate.

Standing in the swirling snow, tall and composed, was the Beast Realm's Matriarch, her hair whipped by the biting wind, her thin clothing doing nothing against the brutal cold, her lips tinged blue, exhaustion and anxiety heavy on her face.

The moment she saw Paisley through the screen, her eyes flared with relief, barely containing her urgency as she spoke fast.

Good to see you! My side — temperatures have dropped sharply, a cold front's swept the whole territory, blizzards for days without end, and my people are starving and freezing! I desperately need warm supplies and food to get us through the winter!

Paisley stayed composed, holding to the same terms as always.

Fine. Gold and energy crystals, whatever you have, I'll take.

The Matriarch didn't hesitate, heading straight to a cave.

Inside, gold and crystals collected recently sat piled into two towering mounds.

A moment later, a steady stream of resources transferred over instantly, landing solidly in Paisley's storage.

Paisley looked over the haul, a flicker of satisfaction in her eyes.

With a thought, a large shipment of supplies transferred back at once.

Including thick, insulated down jackets and padded cotton coats in bulk.

Crate after crate of instant noodles, ration bars, oats, and fine flour appeared, along with various vacuum-sealed cooked goods and dried provisions, variety and quantity both substantial.

Watching the warm clothing and ample food materialize out of nothing, stacking into mounds, the Matriarch felt the tension that had gripped her for days finally release, the weight on her chest lifting at last.

She stepped forward quickly, fingers brushing over the thick, warm fabric, breathing in the solid, comforting scent of food, her eyes growing warm with gratitude.

For a Beast Realm tribe trapped in desperation like this, these supplies weren't just a trade. They were a genuine lifeline.

With warm clothing, the elders, children, and pregnant women in the tribe would no longer have to suffer the cold.

With full food stores, her people wouldn't have to brave the blizzards to hunt, wouldn't have to starve and struggle just to survive.

She bowed deeply to Paisley, her voice sincere and reverent.

Thank you so much! This shipment just saved hundreds of lives in my tribe! I'll gather every one of my people and comb the entire wasteland and forest for more beast crystals and natural gold deposits, and keep trading with you going forward!

Paisley answered evenly, calm and composed.

Anytime. I'm ready whenever you are.

Ding!

Trovie's cheerful chime rang out again.

Congratulations, Host, on completing the Beast Realm life-saving supply trade! Reward: 10,000 points!

Current total points: 1,027,060

With two successful cross-dimensional trades back to back, Paisley's storage was growing richer by the minute.

Mountains of pure gold, high-tier energy crystals and cores piled up around her.

Paisley's gaze settled, calm and composed, her presence drawing inward.

Trovie, convert all the gold into points.

Trovie answered, practically buzzing.

All two hundred twenty thousand pounds of gold converted! 200,000 points earned!

Current total points: 1,227,060

Paisley looked over the surging point total, a faint, satisfied smile touching her eyes, quietly reflecting.

Gold really was the most reliable route. Fastest, safest way to rack up points.

Settling her thoughts, her gaze shifted to the towering pile of interstellar energy crystals beside her.

******

This batch was high grade, the energy within dense and powerful, but every single crystal carried a faint layer of interstellar contamination. Absorbing it directly would destabilize her foundation and damage her body — not worth the risk.
