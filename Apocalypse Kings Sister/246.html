Chapter 246

"Elijah, for old times' sake, please take me with you," Kelly begged. "Get me out of here."

Elijah frowned hard, and his eyes went straight to Paisley in the passenger seat to check her reaction.

His fingers tapped the steering wheel slowly, one tap at a time, and his voice turned cold and flat, leaving no room for argument. "That won't work."

Kelly was the one who'd chosen to leave the team in the first place, and she had feelings for him. He couldn't let Paisley get the wrong idea.

Then again, Paisley probably didn't care much either way.

She froze for a second and glanced over at him in surprise.

She really hadn't expected Elijah to turn Kelly down so flatly, without a second's hesitation.

They'd grown up together, after all. On top of that, Kelly was in love with him.

Paisley truly didn't like Kelly. She'd seen the open contempt, hostility, and spite in Kelly's eyes before, and she remembered every bit of it.

But in the end, this was between Elijah and Kelly. It was none of her business.

Kelly stared at Elijah in shock. Her whole body went stiff, and every last bit of color drained from her face.

She'd never imagined Elijah would turn her down this coldly, and right in front of Paisley, of all people.

Out here in the middle of the wilderness, she had no way forward and mutant beasts behind her. This car and these people were her only shot at staying alive.

She bit her pale lip, swallowed the crushing humiliation, and lowered herself to beg again.

"Not even for the sake of our families? Your grandpa's always been so kind. If he found out you left me here to die, it would break his heart."

Bringing up his grandfather was the only card she had left to play.

But the moment she said it, Elijah's face only grew colder, and his eyes turned icy.

He didn't look at Kelly again. Instead, he turned straight to Paisley beside him and spoke calmly. "What do you think?"

The question came out of nowhere, and it put the decision right in Paisley's hands.

Paisley's expression stayed cool, and her tone was distant but fair. "Captain Blackwood, you're asking me?

"I don't care either way. This is between you two, so it's not my place to step in. She's not my responsibility."

She wouldn't help Kelly, and she wouldn't stand in her way either. She was simply staying out of it, open and detached.

Kelly's chest went tight, and her cheeks burned like she'd been slapped. Her face turned even paler.

It had never crossed her mind that one day, whether she lived or died would come down to a single word from Paisley.

Her hands balled into fists so tight her knuckles went white, and her whole body went stiff with humiliation.

She'd never been this pathetic or this humiliated in her whole life.

Endless regret and resentment churned inside her.

She regretted being so stubborn when she was younger, pouring her whole heart into Elijah and falling for such a cold, heartless man.

I'll never forget how they humiliated me.

Deep down, her bitterness and hatred kept building, and dark thoughts spread through her like wildfire.

Just wait. Someday, I'll make every one of them pay double for this.

But for now, she had to keep that hatred hidden.

Right now, the most important thing was staying alive and making it back to Aetheris.

Just as the standoff dragged on, the window of the car behind them rolled down. Madison leaned out and quietly spoke up for Kelly.

She and Kelly had known each other since high school, and some of that old friendship was still there. Madison was also kind and soft-hearted by nature, so she couldn't stand to watch Kelly wait out here to die.

"Captain Blackwood, Pais, she really has nowhere left to go," Madison said. "These woods are way too dangerous at night, and if we leave her here alone, she's dead for sure. Why don't we just take her with us for now?"

Since Madison had spoken up for her, nobody else objected.

In the end, they gave in and agreed to let Kelly come along for now.

"Fine, you can get in. Take the car in the back." Elijah's voice was flat and ice cold.

Kelly didn't dare say another word. She swallowed every feeling she had, kept her head down, and hurried to the back car, where she quietly got in.

The two SUVs started up again, engines roaring, and sped on toward the munitions depot.

The wilderness along the way was run-down and bleak. Every now and then, a few stray zombies staggered along the side of the road, growling and snarling.

Thomas, Spencer, and Mason used their powers from inside the car.

Wooden spikes, fire, and wind blades shot through the air with deadly precision and wiped out every stray zombie along the way.

The cars drove on fast and steady, and before they knew it, half an hour had gone by. It was already 5 p.m.

The weather had always been unpredictable since the apocalypse. It was only September, but it was already bitterly cold, and the evening wind blew in icy and sharp from the wilderness.
