Chapter 246

"Elijah. For old times' sake, take me with you."

Elijah's brow drew tight, his eyes flicking first to Paisley in the passenger seat, checking her reaction.

His fingers tapped the wheel, slow and deliberate, every bit of warmth gone from his voice.

"That's not going to work."

She'd been the one to walk away from the group. And she had feelings for him. He couldn't risk Paisley reading anything into this.

Though Paisley probably wouldn't care either way.

Paisley blinked, a little surprised, and glanced over at him.

She genuinely hadn't expected him to turn her down that flatly.

They'd grown up together. And more than that, Kelly actually liked him.

Paisley had never liked Kelly, not after the open contempt and hostility she'd seen in her eyes before.

But this was between Elijah and Kelly. Not her business.

Kelly stared at Elijah, stiffening, the color draining from her face.

She hadn't imagined he'd turn her down this coldly, and in front of Paisley of all people.

Stranded in this wasteland, trapped between the road ahead and whatever lurked behind, this car was her only chance.

She bit down on her bloodless lip, swallowed the humiliation, and kept pleading.

"Not even for our families' sake? Grandpa Blackwood's always been kind. He'd be heartbroken if he knew you left me here to die."

Invoking his grandfather was the only leverage she had left.

But it only made his expression colder, a chill rising in his eyes.

He didn't look at Kelly again. He turned to Paisley instead.

"What do you think?"

The question put the decision squarely in her hands.

Paisley's expression stayed even, her tone neutral.

"You're asking me? Doesn't matter to me either way. This is between you two. She's not my problem."

No for her, no against her. Just out of it entirely.

Kelly's chest tightened, her cheeks burning with humiliation, face going even paler.

She'd never imagined her life would hinge on a single word from Paisley Lennox.

Her hands clenched white-knuckled, her whole body rigid with shame.

She'd never been this low, this humiliated, in her entire life.

Regret and resentment churned together in her chest.

She regretted ever falling for him, wasting her feelings on someone this cold.

This humiliation. I won't forget it.

Bitterness and hatred buried themselves deep, dark thoughts taking root.

Someday, I'll pay every bit of this back, with interest.

But for now, the hatred had to stay buried.

Right now, all that mattered was surviving, and getting back to Aetheris.

Just as the standoff dragged on, the window of the second car rolled down. Madison leaned out, her voice gentle.

She and Kelly had known each other since high school, and Madison, soft-hearted by nature, couldn't stand watching her die out here.

"Captain. Pais. She's got nowhere else to go. Leaving her here overnight is a death sentence. Let's just bring her along."

With Madison speaking up, no one else objected.

In the end, they agreed to let Kelly come, for now.

"Get in the back car."

Elijah's voice carried not a trace of warmth.

Kelly didn't dare say another word. She swallowed everything down and hurried to the back car without another sound.

The engines started up again, and both cars sped off toward the armory.

The wasteland rolled past, bleak and broken, scattered zombies shambling along the roadside with hoarse, low growls.

Thomas, Spencer, and Mason fired off their Affinities from inside the car.

Wooden spikes, flame, and wind blades took down every stray zombie along the way.

The cars rolled on steadily, and half an hour slipped by. It was five in the afternoon.

The weather had gone strange since the apocalypse. It was only September, but the air already bit like winter, the evening wind carrying frost off the wasteland.
