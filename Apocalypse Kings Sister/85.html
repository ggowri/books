Chapter 85

"I'm not dragging three useless troublemakers along on this trip anymore."

The more he thought about it, the angrier he got. His eyes turned even colder as he laid into them.

"Go on, tell me. What good have any of you been this whole time? You're useless, so why the hell should I keep you around?"

After that chewing-out, Rachel, Reid, and Ryan turned red, then white. They clutched their stale bread, ashamed and mortified.

Before the apocalypse, they'd all been spoiled rich kids. When had they ever been treated like this?

But now, it was a dog-eat-dog world.

Until they got stronger, all they could do was swallow their pride for the time being.

Rachel kept glaring hatefully at Paisley the whole time.

She watched everyone crowd around Paisley and take care of her, and she swore to herself that sooner or later, she'd get rid of her.

Just like in her past life.

A useless loser like Paisley doesn't deserve to survive the apocalypse.

Not far away, Kelly caught the open hatred and viciousness in Rachel's eyes. She gave a faint, amused smile.

This game is getting more and more interesting.

Maybe she wouldn't even have to get her own hands dirty. Rachel was dying to get rid of Paisley, and she'd clear the way for Kelly all on her own.

If it came to that, Kelly wouldn't mind quietly giving Rachel a little help. Then, she could sit back and reap the rewards.

After a short break, everyone packed up their supplies and got ready to hit the road again.

Elijah spread out his detailed map and put his finger on a clearly marked city. He spoke to the group in a low voice.

"This is our next stop, Rivergate.

"Rivergate is built right on the water. It's a city with canals running through every part of it."

Then, he moved his finger to a key spot along the route.

"About four hours' drive from here, there's an abandoned gas station with a motel where we can get some sleep. That'll be the best place for us to stop tonight.

"Once we're past that area, we'll have to switch to boats and cross the river to get into Rivergate."

With that, he folded up the map and gave the order.

"Break's over. Everyone in the cars. We're moving out."

Jasper had heard the whole plan, and he frowned hard. He clearly didn't like it, and he challenged Elijah right away.

"Why do we have to take a detour to Rivergate?

"Isn't Aetheris where we're going? Going all the way around to Rivergate is a total waste of time.

"There's nothing in Rivergate for me. Can't we just change course and go straight to Aetheris?"

Elijah glanced at him coolly, his voice distant.

"You don't have to come. You can head to Aetheris on your own.

"I have my reasons for going to Rivergate, and I don't owe anyone an explanation.

"We were never one team to begin with. You're free to leave anytime."

That shut Jasper up. His face soured, but he didn't say another word.

Rachel's heart sank, and she started to panic.

They couldn't split off from Elijah's group.

In her past life, Elijah and Orion had both ended up at the very top of the food chain.

Besides, Elijah was strong and always thought things through. Sticking with him was the safe bet.

It was way safer than sticking with an idiot like Jasper.

She hurried over to smooth things over and talk Jasper down.

"Mr. Quinn, let's just stay with them."

Then, she leaned in and whispered in his ear.

"If Mr. Blackwood and his people go first and clear out the zombies and danger for us, we'll be way safer. It's really our best option."

But she had her own plan.

She remembered something from her past life. The doctor who'd eventually create the ultimate cure for the zombie virus, the cure that changed the whole apocalypse, had been badly hurt on the way to Rivergate.

The doctor had lost a leg and been disabled ever since.

Rachel couldn't remember the exact place, but going to Rivergate was definitely the right move.

If I get there first and save the doctor, I'll be their savior. That's my golden ticket.

Paisley was sick of Rachel's fake, scheming face.

She couldn't be bothered to listen to another word. She turned with the little white cat in her arms, climbed into the back seat of the SUV, and leaned against the window to rest with her eyes closed.
