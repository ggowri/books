Chapter 92

Paisley walked over and held her fingertip under his nose. His breath was so faint that she could barely feel it.

Her expression stayed cool, and her voice was flat.

"He's barely breathing. He's already got one foot in the grave. Is he even worth saving?"

Trovie's words came out fast and frantic.

[Master, take a closer look! He's young and good-looking, and most importantly, he's the doctor who's going to save the world and end the apocalypse!

[He can't die! If he dies, the apocalypse will never end, and everyone will be stuck in this hell forever!]

Paisley gave it a sideways look.

[What's it to you? Why are you so worked up about this? If I save him, do I get a reward?]

As soon as she said it, she studied his face more closely, and a buried memory came back to her.

In her past life, she'd seen news reports about this guy later in the apocalypse. This doctor had thrown himself into his research and finally created the ultimate cure for the zombie virus. He'd saved countless survivors and changed the course of the apocalypse.

So this was the key person Trovie had been talking about.

He really did need to be saved.

But Trovie was acting weird.

It answered quickly.

[Don't worry, Master. I just went and asked about a reward for you, and there is one! If you save him, the system will give you a bonus Awakening Pill.

[That's some seriously good stuff. It's a pill that can awaken any ability you want!]

Paisley had no idea Trovie could pull something like this. Had she been leaving money on the table this whole time?

[Deal.]

Paisley didn't hesitate anymore. With a single thought, she took a cup of spirit water out of her Storage Dimension. She propped up the limp, weak man and fed it to him a little at a time through his cracked, pale lips.

The warm, pure spirit water slid down his throat. It quickly brought life back to his drained body and slowly started healing him from the inside.

A moment later, a little life came back into the dying man. His heavy eyelids fluttered, and he struggled to open them just a crack.

The second he opened his eyes and saw Paisley, he thought he'd died and gone to heaven.

With the last of his strength, he whispered hoarsely, "Thank you... angel..."

Then, he couldn't hold on anymore. His head flopped to the side, and he passed out cold. But his breathing was steady now, and he was out of danger.

Paisley patted his cheek. "Hey, don't fall asleep on me! If you pass out, how am I supposed to get you out of here?"

She sighed, pulled out her gun, and shot the chains off. Then, she told the little white cat to grow to six and a half feet tall and carry him out.

Five minutes later, she met back up with the group.

Everyone stared curiously at the stranger draped over the cat's back.

The little white cat wasn't happy about having to carry this smelly guy around. Who knew how many days it had been since he'd showered?

Now, its fur was all dirty.

The second it reached the group, it dumped him on the ground.

Elijah frowned. He didn't like the look of this guy at all. "Who's he?"

Look at him. Some pretty boy who's obviously a total wimp and needs people to protect him.

She'd never go for a guy like that.

Ashley looked him up and down.

"Paisley, this guy's so hot! Who is he?

"Why's he hurt so bad? Did someone torture him?"

Orion immediately stepped in and pulled Paisley and Ashley back.

"You're young girls. Stay far away from smelly guys like him."

He couldn't have sounded more disgusted.

For once, Thomas and Spencer were completely on the same page.

"He's right. Guys out there are all up to no good, and you two are way too innocent.

"The better-looking a guy is, the bigger liar he is. Stay far away from players like that."

Elijah agreed with every word, and he wanted to give them a big thumbs-up.

Exactly. Every smelly guy out there is a liar, so never pick one up off the street.

But Paisley wasn't paying any attention to them.

With the sweet little white cat in her arms, she only had one thing on her mind: her reward, the Awakening Pill.

She wanted to use it right away.

That's it. Tonight, I'm using the Awakening Pill to awaken a second ability.

She thought carefully about which ability would suit her best.

Fire Affinity had huge explosive power and was brutal in a fight. It was perfect for head-on combat.

Earth Affinity had amazing defense, and it was great for staying alive.

Water Affinity didn't hit hard enough, and it wasn't cool enough.
