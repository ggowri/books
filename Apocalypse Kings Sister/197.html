Chapter 197

Paisley didn't let a shred of hostility show. There was only a faint chill in her eyes as she looked at Chase, and her cool, clear voice carried absolute authority.

"Level Five psychic power? That's it? You're that easy to beat..."

Those few casual words crushed every last bit of Chase's pride and swagger.

His mind throbbed with stabbing pain, his temples pounded, and cold sweat soaked the back of his shirt. He was so dizzy he could barely stay on his feet.

He stared at her in horror and kept backing away, like he'd seen something terrifying. How does she have psychic power this strong? No... it has to be a fluke.

Part of him refused to believe it, refused to accept that any of it had really happened.

Everyone else in the room stood frozen, eyes wide, unable to process it.

Nobody had expected Paisley, whom they'd all written off as dead weight, to have the strength to crush the base's top psychic.

Just how strong was this girl? She was terrifying, and she'd kept it hidden so well.

The pressure on Elijah vanished all at once, the pain in his mind fading with it.

He slowly steadied himself and turned to look at Paisley, who was standing calmly beside him, his feelings complicated. How many more surprises is she hiding? A Storage Dimension, a Plant Affinity, and now Psychic too?

Orion's long-lost sister never stopped surprising people, and Elijah didn't know what to do with himself. He wanted to get closer to her and know her better than ever.

Orion smiled knowingly, long used to Paisley surprising everyone. That's my sister. Who wouldn't love her?

Chase gritted his teeth, finally realizing she wasn't just a little stronger than him. He forced down the pain in his head, his face pale and twisted with shock and anger, but he didn't dare look down on her anymore.

He glared at Paisley, his voice trembling with fear and resentment he couldn't hide.

"H-how is your psychic power this strong?! You're just some nobody girl!"

Paisley gave a faint, cold smile, her voice distant.

"On base missions, strength is what talks. You're weaker than me in psychic power, and you're reckless and arrogant on top of it.

"You're not fit to be captain, and you definitely have no right to boss me around or hit on me. I say we stick with the original decision and keep Elijah as our captain for this mission."

She pulled back some of her psychic pressure but kept a thick, tight psychic hold locked around Chase's arms and legs.

Chase's body slowly bent against his will until his legs gave out and he dropped stiffly to his knees. Hunched over and straining with every muscle, he fought with everything he had but still couldn't get up.

Chase, who'd been so arrogant a minute ago, was now on his knees, pale and drenched in cold sweat. His dignity was in pieces, and he'd become a joke in front of everyone.

Every Rivergate team member in the room felt the same surge of satisfaction. Someone had finally settled a score they'd all been carrying.

For a long time, Chase had claimed the best of the base's resources on the strength of his rare psychic power alone, skipping missions, contributing next to nothing, and still sitting above every high-tier Affinity user in the place.

They'd risked their lives holding the line, yet they got far worse treatment than Chase, who did nothing. On top of that, he often bullied, mocked, and harassed them for no reason, and they'd all been too scared to say anything.

Today, the arrogant Chase had finally met his match, crushed by a girl who looked so delicate.

Every one of them was cheering on the inside.

At the front of the room, Elijah's eyes stayed on Paisley's slim, straight back, and a wave of emotion surged inside him that he could barely contain.

From crushing Chase to pushing for Elijah to be captain, everything she'd done was to stand up for him and back him up.

She'd stepped in to protect him from the psychic attack and to protect his pride too.

Pais really cares about me this much!

This sudden show of favoritism and protection sent Elijah's usually steady heart racing out of control. He looked at her with deep, burning emotion he could barely hold back, and his gaze lingered on her, heavier than ever.
