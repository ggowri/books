Chapter 197

No hostility leaked off her at all, just a thin, cool edge in her eyes as she looked at him, her voice clear and level, every word carrying absolute weight.

“Level Five psychic power? That's it? This weak?”

Just a few light words, and every scrap of his pride shattered.

His mind throbbed with pain, his temples pounding, cold sweat soaking through the back of his shirt. The room spun; he could barely keep his feet.

He stared at her in horror, backing away in small, fearful steps, like he'd just seen something monstrous.

Her. How could she possibly have power like that?

No… maybe it was just a fluke.

He refused to believe—refused to let himself believe—that any of it had really happened.

Everyone else in the room stood frozen, eyes wide, unable to process it.

Not one of them had expected the girl they'd written off as dead weight to carry power that could crush the base's top psychic without even trying.

Just how strong was this girl, really?

Terrifying. And she'd kept all of it hidden.

The pressure on Elijah vanished all at once, the pain in his mind fading with it.

He steadied himself and turned to look at the girl standing calmly beside him, a tangle of feeling in his eyes.

How many more surprises was she hiding from him?

A Storage Dimension, Plant Affinity, and now Psychic too?

The sister Orion had found was full of surprises, every single time.

What was he supposed to do with that?

He wanted to get closer to her, to know her, more than ever.

Something knowing flickered in Orion's eyes. He'd long since stopped being surprised that his sister always had another surprise in her.

His sister was amazing.

How could anyone not adore her?

Chase ground his teeth, finally understanding the gap wasn't small at all. He forced down the pain in his mind, his face cycling through white and green, equal parts fury and shock, not a shred of contempt left in him.

He stared at her, his voice shaking with a resentment he couldn't quite hide.

“How—how is your psychic power this strong?! You're supposed to be some nobody!”

The corner of Paisley's mouth curved, faint and cold, her voice distant.

“Missions go to whoever's actually strong enough.”

“In raw power, you're beneath me. In temperament, you're arrogant and reckless.”

“You don't get to lead this team, and you definitely don't get to order me around or talk to me like that.”

“I move we keep the original plan. Elijah stays team leader.”

She let her psychic force draw back slightly, though a fine, heavy binding stayed woven through his limbs, locking him in place.

Chase's body bent, out of his control, until his legs gave and he dropped to his knees, spine hunched, every muscle straining against it, unable to get back up no matter how hard he fought.

The man who'd been strutting a minute ago now knelt in a wreck, pale and drenched in sweat, every scrap of dignity gone, reduced to a joke in front of the whole room.

Every Rivergate team member in the room felt the same surge of satisfaction. Someone had finally settled a score they'd all been carrying.

For a long time, Chase had claimed the best of the base's resources on the strength of his rare psychic power alone, skipping missions, contributing next to nothing, and still sitting above every high-tier Affinity user in the place.

They'd risked their lives holding the line, every one of them, while he coasted on far better terms—and on top of it, they'd put up with his bullying and mockery, swallowing their anger because no one dared push back.

Today, the man who'd never respected anyone finally ran into something he couldn't handle, brought down by a girl who looked like she couldn't lift a sword.

Every one of them was cheering on the inside.

At the front of the room, Elijah's eyes rested on her slender, unbending back, something enormous breaking loose inside his chest.

She'd crushed him outright and pushed him back into the lead, start to finish, defending his standing the whole time.

******

Not just protecting him from the attack. Protecting his pride too.

Pais cared about him this much.

That sudden, fierce loyalty sent his usually steady heartbeat spinning out of rhythm, something hot and restrained building behind his eyes, fixed on her, growing heavier by the second.
