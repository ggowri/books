Chapter 335

warm yellow light spilled softly through the rooms, the whole place easy and relaxed.

Everyone had already changed and was ready to head out to the base auction.

Ashley stood by the front door with a small bag slung over her shoulder, practically vibrating with anticipation.

Orion, Spencer, and Thomas were ready off to the side, with Elijah standing closest to the door, all of them waiting on Paisley.

The moment they stepped outside, a squad of soldiers in uniform formation closed in, their faces hard and their stride sharp, blocking the path outright.

The one in front spoke first, his tone flat and rude, with no trace of courtesy.

"Which one of you is Paisley Lennox? You're coming with us."

The sudden ambush shattered the easy mood instantly.

Ashley's smile froze and vanished. She drifted closer to Paisley without thinking, every nerve on alert.

Elijah's gaze went cold in an instant, every trace of warmth gone. A killing edge rolled off him as he stepped forward without thinking, planting himself in front of Paisley, his voice low and hard.

"What unit are you with? What's your base duty number? What do you want with her?"

His status and record were well known enough that even in Stillwater, ordinary soldiers wouldn't dare act out of line in front of him.

But these soldiers clearly belonged to some private force under a powerful patron. They didn't recognize Elijah, looked him over with open contempt, and dismissed him as some junior Affinity user, their tone dripping arrogance.

"None of your business."

"My boss is one of the top men in Aetheris. Even the base commander treats him with deference."

"Do yourselves a favor and don't obstruct official business. Let Paisley come quietly. Crossing my boss won't do you any good."

Every word was a bald-faced threat, power thrown around like a club.

Orion's expression darkened instantly. He stepped forward, radiating pressure of his own.

"Base protocol requires written orders and a stated reason before summoning anyone. You're trying to drag her off with nothing but attitude. Who authorized this?"

The soldier only grew more contemptuous.

"The paperwork will come through later. Right now, she's coming with us."

Two soldiers stepped forward and reached for Paisley, grabbing hard, making no secret of hauling her off by force.

Paisley's eyes cooled.

She'd kept the ledger clean this time around, never picking fights she didn't need. She genuinely couldn't think who'd bother mounting an operation like this just for her.

But the thought passed quickly.

In her last life, she'd been an easy target, soft enough for anyone to squeeze.

This time, she had the strength and the backing to refuse that role outright.

Whoever wanted to push her around would have to earn the right.

Paisley didn't retreat an inch, her voice cold.

"I don't know you, and I haven't broken a single base regulation.

Whoever's behind you, without proper paperwork, I'm not taking a single step."

The lead soldier's face hardened, his patience gone, malice plain in his eyes.

"Fine — we'll do this the hard way."

"If you're going to keep resisting, let's find out just how tough you really are."

He raised his weapon without another word, leveling it at Paisley, and pulled the trigger without hesitation.

BANG—!

Muzzle flash lit the air, the bullet tearing straight toward Paisley's face.

In that split second, Orion's eyes went hard and his Level Six Metal Affinity erupted at full force.

Brilliant, razor-sharp gold light exploded outward, condensing into a swarm of solid blades that materialized from nothing and tore through the air, screaming across the yard.

Clang, clang, clang—!

Metal shrieked against metal as every rifle was knocked clean from its owner's hands, clattering to the ground.

At the same moment, the crushing pressure of a Level Six Affinity rolled out over them, pinning them in place, stealing their breath, locking their bodies rigid.

Orion's eyes were frost, his presence suffocating.

"Get out."
