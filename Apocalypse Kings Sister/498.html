Chapter 498

The mutant snow rats had a natural instinct for danger. They could sense the crushing aura of the truly strong.

Paisley's power was far beyond theirs. To the rats she was an untouchable predator.

They'd swarm Rachel's group without fear, but they shrank from Paisley and wouldn't come within a step of her.

Rachel's scheme collapsed. Her face turned green, then white.

She'd planned to beg pitifully, wait for her chance, and let the beasts finish off Paisley for her.

But the rats weren't cooperating.

Paisley looked at her coolly. Her voice cut clearly through the rats' shrieking and tore the wounded act off Rachel's malice.

"Don't waste your energy. You want to lure the rats onto us. Rachel, I'm glad to see you haven't changed. You're just as rotten as ever."

"That actually puts my mind at ease."

Rachel went stiff. The injured look cracked, panic flickered across her eyes, and she tried to defend herself.

"I'm not, Paisley, you've got it wrong. I just wanted you to help us…"

"Help?" Paisley arched a brow and scanned their battered group.

"When you set me up, when all of you went after me, nobody held back. Resources are limited in the apocalypse, and everyone takes their own risks. You took the mission, so you deal with the beasts."

Her gaze landed on Ryan, the one Rachel had shoved out as a shield. Her voice stayed flat.

"Besides, when things went bad you pushed each other into danger. So why should you expect me to save you?"

Ashley hooked her arm through Paisley's and glanced impatiently at the swarmed group. "Paisley, no point wasting words on them. There's more supplies waiting upstairs."

Jasper tightened his grip on his dagger, scanning the area, and nodded. "Let's go. No reason to hang around. It's not worth the risk."

Paisley didn't look at the faces of the Lennox family again. She turned and headed straight for the stairs to the third floor.

"Let's go upstairs and collect supplies."

Jasper gave Chase a complicated look before following her. It wasn't that he didn't want to save Chase. He just couldn't.

And he had to distance himself from Rachel now. What if Paisley got angry and stopped letting him tag along?

Nobody else came close to Paisley.

The others did the same, leaving the screaming and the rats' frantic shrieks behind.

Rachel watched Paisley's group walk off, frantic and furious. But she was pinned down by the rats and couldn't move. She gritted her teeth and kept pushing her vines forward.

Chase's face was grim. His Level Five mental blasts kept hitting the swarm, and his power was draining fast. His wounds hurt more and more in the cold.

Paisley's group climbed the icy stairs to the third floor.

It was a huge home-goods and clothing section, shelves packed. Nobody had come to collect from it.

When the weather was warm, hardly anyone had bothered taking this kind of stuff.

******

Jasper went ahead and swept the area, fingertips on the freezing shelves, sensing for any trace of beasts. Only when he was sure it was clear did he turn back.

"This is the coat and bedding section. Let's split up and find what we need."

Ashley hurried to a row of toppled shelves, pushed aside boxes covered in snow, and pulled out a thick, puffy long down coat. She squeezed the soft filling and her eyes lit up.

"This one's great. So soft. It'd be perfect for Paisley."

Paisley looked around. "Take what you need. I'll take the rest. Everyone move fast."

Jasper and his teammates had come out in thin jackets and spent the whole trip in minus-forty weather. They were nearly stiff from the cold.

At that, Jasper grabbed a thick padded jacket and pulled it on. The moment the zipper closed, warmth wrapped around him and he let out a long breath.

"Finally warm. Minus forty is just too much."

His breath fogged. He muttered, "How long is this nightmare weather going to last?"

A teammate beside him rummaged for scarves and gloves and chimed in.
