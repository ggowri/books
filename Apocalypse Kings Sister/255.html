Chapter 255

BOOM!

The ground heaved as thick, vicious vines tore through, spreading across the entire basement.

Venomous and fast as striking snakes, they snapped around every incoming attack, crushing them before they could land.

At the same moment, Elijah vanished and reappeared, lightning detonating.

A great bolt crashed down right in the center of the crowd.

The vines moved faster than the eye could follow, catching the front runners, thorns in deep, toxins spreading before they could even scream.

Several Level Two and Three men dropped where they stood, bodies smoking from the lightning, screaming filling the basement.

"AAAH!"

Orion, armored in metal, strode forward and swung.

Every punch landed with bone-shattering force, sending men flying, coughing blood, utterly unable to fight back.

In seconds, every one of Fat Wagner's men lay on the ground, moaning.

******

Watching his men get wiped out in an instant, Fat Wagner's face finally changed, arrogance replaced completely by fear.

That girl's Plant Affinity was on another level entirely. It wasn't even a fair fight.

His pupils shrank, every ounce of menace drained out of him, replaced by raw fear.

He never imagined three ordinary-looking young strangers could be this terrifyingly strong.

His whole body shook, every bit of his Level Five pride gone, bluffing his way through sheer terror.

"Who are you? Why are you targeting me? Everyone fends for themselves in this world, you stay out of my way, I stay out of yours! This is MY territory! I'm Level Five! Aren't you afraid of what happens if you cross me?"

He tried using his rank as leverage.

"Walk away right now, and I'll forget this ever happened. No hard feelings..."

Before he even finished bluffing, a golden blur was already closing the distance.

Orion didn't bother listening, his eyes utterly devoid of warmth.

He drew back his fist, Metal Affinity packed into every joint, and started swinging before Fat Wagner could react.

Each punch cracked through the air like a whip, bone-shattering force landing solidly on soft, fatty flesh.

THUD. THUD. THUD.

The impacts echoed through the whole basement.

His Level Five defenses crumbled like paper under the assault, his organs rattled, agony tearing through every limb.

"URGH—!"

A scream tore out of him as he staggered backward, his bulk nowhere near enough to withstand the beating.

Terrified now, survival instinct overriding everything, Fat Wagner tried pulling rank again.

"Don't push your luck! I've got backup! My guy's even stronger than me! You touch me, they'll never let this go!"

Gasping, he made one last desperate pitch.

"Let me go right now and leave, and I promise I'll let this whole thing slide!"

The threat only made Orion's eyes go colder.

The most pathetic thing about this world was that men like this, drowning in innocent blood, somehow always had friends.

"Your friend? I'm terrified."

His mouth curled in cold mockery, killing intent sharpening in his eyes, his next punch hitting harder than all the rest combined.

Another blow slammed square into Fat Wagner's face.

His nose shattered with a sharp crack, blood pouring down over his greasy, twisted face.

Agony swept through him, his legs buckling, and he collapsed to his knees on the cold, wet floor.

The arrogant warlord who'd ruled this village knelt there shaking, face covered in blood, every trace of his old swagger gone.
