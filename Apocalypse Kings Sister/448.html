Chapter 448

They hadn't known her Plant Affinity had broken through to mid Level Seven.

No wonder she could kill a peak Level Seven serpent mutant alone.

Across Aetheris Base and every survivor base around it, Level Six was already top-tier. Level Seven was practically legend, and high-level healing and purification was rarer still.

Ashley cracked first.

Her eyes went wide, her small mouth open, and she stood frozen, eyes full of worship and shock. Her voice shook.

"Level... Level Seven? Paisley's Plant Affinity can fight, can control, and can heal and purify too?!"

She ran forward, stared at Elijah's unmarked body, then spun to Paisley, her eyes shining with wild excitement.

"Paisley, you're incredible!"

"That serpent venom, those wounds, he was supposed to be dead, and you healed him in ten minutes?"

"I always thought Plant Affinity was just for binding and attacking. I never knew it could do this!"

"Healing and purifying is even rarer in the apocalypse."

"Paisley, how many surprises are you hiding from me?"

"If you hadn't been here today, we'd all be zombies by now!"

"Paisley, I worship you even more than yesterday!"

Orion was next to recover.

The usually calm man had lost his composure. His brows drew tight, shock filled his eyes, and his body went rigid.

He'd checked Elijah's injuries himself and knew how brutal and hopeless that double poisoning was. He'd braced for the worst.

Now, seeing the wounds closed and the poison gone with his own eyes, it still felt unreal. He muttered, voice full of awe.

"I've met other Plant Affinity users. Low-level healing, slow recovery, I've seen it. But I've never seen healing and purification like Pais's."

"Level Seven serpent venom with high-level mental contamination, a death sentence in the apocalypse, and Pais purified it just like that."

He looked up at her, his eyes complicated and shaken.

"Pais, you've overturned everything I knew again. How many more cards do you have? Mid Level Seven healing is one of a kind in the whole world."

Thomas, next in line, stood dazed and blank.

Clearly, he was stunned too.

He'd watched her grow along the way. He thought he knew her strength well. Today's fight had overturned all of it.

He scratched his head and sighed.

"I always thought your Plant Affinity was for control, binding, and heavy attacks. A pure combat Affinity. I never imagined it hid healing like this."

"And you jumped straight to mid Level Seven?! The whole team grinds our way to Level Six peak, and you left us a whole realm behind."

"No wonder you always turn things around... Pais, next to you I'm a total washout! We both have Plant Affinity. Why's the gap so huge?"

"Why didn't I awaken healing and purifying?"

He sounded mostly envious and a little regretful.

Spencer sucked in a breath, eyes wide, thrilled and stunned, and blurted it out.

"This is nuts! Pais can solo a peak Level Seven beast, and now she heals and purifies poison too?!"

"For other people, one high-level Affinity would be unbelievable. You've got Storage, Fire, Plant, and the craziest part is your Plant Affinity fights and heals, both at once, and it's at mid Level Seven. At this point, in fights, that's the ceiling, isn't it?"

******

Madison, always gentle and calm, finally lost her usual composure. Her eyes were full of shock and envy, and she clasped her hands tight. Her voice carried real admiration.

"Healing and purifying Affinities are the rarest in the apocalypse. High-level purification is something you can't get no matter what. The top bases pour resources into training them and still can't produce one."

"Pais's Plant Affinity isn't only Level Seven. It covers attack, defense, healing, and purification... She's a blessing from heaven for our whole team."

"With you here, no matter how bad we're hurt or how vicious the poison, we'll always have a way out."
