Chapter 120

They had no right to stick up for her, and they couldn't bring themselves to blindly take her side like they used to.

One by one, the brothers avoided Rachel's pleading eyes. They looked miserable, and they stayed silent.

In that moment, Rachel's heart sank, and a bone-deep chill washed over her.

She realized it with painful clarity. Everything had changed.

The three brothers who'd always stood behind her and taken her side no matter what weren't even backing her up anymore.

Ever since her rebirth, nothing had gone the way it was supposed to.

All Paisley had to do was stand there quietly, and everyone rushed to defend her.

Everyone's supposed to defend me, no matter what. I'm the one who's supposed to get the best of everything.

So how did it all get turned upside down?

She refused to accept it. She glared at Paisley one last time and stormed off with red eyes.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan wanted to find Paisley and make things right. Their wounds had only been patched up roughly and still hurt, and they dragged their exhausted, half-healed bodies along.

That evening, they'd come close to dying and seen what Paisley could really do. They thought back on everything they'd done to her, and regret ate at them.

They worked up the courage to go talk to her, hoping to make peace and win her back.

But when they looked over, Paisley was already surrounded by the whole Thompson family.

Ashley walked up to Paisley first. Without a second thought, she put every crystal core she'd just gotten down in front of her, her eyes shining with admiration.

"Paisley, you were so badass today! Nobody could touch that Level Four Rat King, but you took it out like it was nothing. That was incredible!

"It's official. As of today, you're my idol!

"I'm going to learn everything I can from you and train as hard as I can. I'm going to get as strong as you so I'm never dead weight again!"

Steady as ever, Orion stepped up right behind her. He set every crystal core he'd gotten on the table and slid the pile over to Paisley, beaming with pride.

"Pais, I'm so proud of you. If Mom and Dad could see how amazing you are, they'd be bursting with pride. These are all for you."

Thomas and Spencer stepped up too. Without hesitating, they pulled out every crystal core they had and stacked them on the pile, their voices full of gratitude.

"Paisley, take ours too. If it weren't for you tonight, none of us would be alive right now. Every last one of these is yours.

"And from now on, any crystal core we get goes to you."

Before long, the table was piled high with the crystal cores everyone had handed over.

Paisley looked at the family who truly cared about her, and her face softened.

"I appreciate it, really. But I don't need these, so keep them. You all need resources to get stronger."

Then, she casually took out the 600 Level One crystal cores she'd just been given and laid them out neatly on the table.

"Split these up among yourselves."

At Paisley's level, ordinary Level One crystal cores barely did anything for her.

The pure spirit water she drank kept her energy strong and constantly flowing, so she didn't need low-level cores to top it up.

She'd just killed the Rat King and tamed the mutant willow, and her Plant Affinity was about to break through to the next level. Low-level cores would hardly help with that.

They'd been willing to give up their share for her, so she would pay them back twice over.

The Thompsons were surprised and deeply touched.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan had been standing off to the side in silence for a while. They watched the whole thing, green with envy.

The brothers were in a tough spot.

Only Owen had awakened an affinity, and he was still just Level Three, which was nothing special.

Reid and Ryan were still ordinary people with no way to protect themselves at all.

They'd spent the whole fight tonight taking hits and hiding behind everyone else. Naturally, they'd ended up with a pitiful handful of crystal cores.

As they watched Paisley casually hand 600 crystal cores to other people, greed swelled up inside all three of them.

Owen took it the hardest.

As far as he was concerned, the Lennoxes had raised Paisley, so everything she had belonged to them. It belonged to him and his brothers.

Those 600 Level One cores would have pushed his affinity to mid Level Four at the very least.

What gives her the right? Why is she handing what should be ours to a bunch of outsiders like the Thompsons?
