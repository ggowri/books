Chapter 93

Metal Affinity was sharp and brutal, perfect for close combat.

But after comparing them all, Paisley decided Psychic Affinity was the coolest.

Out of every affinity, Psychic Affinity was definitely the flashiest and the most powerful.

Even early on, a Psychic Affinity user could move things with their mind, control anything nearby, and kill without making a sound.

As it leveled up, it could control zombies, and even take over people's minds.

More importantly, Psychic Affinity would hugely boost her perception and awareness, expanding her detection range and sharpening her senses. Combined with her Plant Affinity, her fighting power and her ability to see things coming would double.

Paisley had already made up her mind. Tonight, she'd awaken Psychic Affinity.

The good-looking guy in the lab coat was still out cold, and everyone already couldn't stand him.

Rachel was as shallow as they came. Everyone looked down on him, he was covered in wounds, and he obviously hadn't showered in days. He looked as filthy as a homeless guy, so she didn't recognize him as the doctor she'd been searching for, the one who'd create the cure for the zombie virus.

All she felt was disgust.

Rachel had just blown her one and only chance to win him over.

Night fell, and it was time for dinner.

Ashley was alone in the kitchen, calmly making dinner for the whole team.

Everyone was exhausted from traveling through the apocalypse nonstop. She'd quietly taken on all the little chores and cooked every meal without a single complaint.

Paisley walked into the kitchen with the little white cat in her arms and spotted Ashley working all by herself.

Then, she remembered the age-old sacred fungus and all the top-quality ingredients stashed in her Storage Dimension, and she walked over.

"Ashley, let me help."

Ashley turned around when she heard her. When she saw Paisley, she broke into a warm smile and quickly waved her off.

"You don't have to, Paisley. Just sit down and wait for dinner. I've got it."

Paisley deserves to be spoiled. How could I ever let her cook?

After two days together, Paisley knew for sure that Ashley was nothing like Rachel. Ashley genuinely cared about her.

Paisley smiled. "Ashley, I'm actually a pretty good cook. Want to try my cooking?"

Before Ashley could answer, Paisley pulled a spotless, fully prepped chicken straight out of her Storage Dimension with a single thought.

It was a spirit hen from the Beast Realm that Trovie had just butchered and cleaned for her. Its whole body was packed with pure energy.

"Let me show off a little tonight. Trust me, my chicken soup is amazing."

Paisley moved fast. She got the water boiling, blanched the chicken, and put it in the pot in one smooth flow.

She filled a big stockpot with clear, sweet spirit water and added the spirit hen. Then, she sliced off a few pieces of the priceless sacred fungus, dropped them in, and let it all simmer on low heat.

Right after that, she took out some fresh vegetables and quickly washed and chopped them.

She'd cleaned out several supermarkets earlier, after all.

Ever since her Storage Dimension had leveled up, time stood still inside it, so everything stayed perfectly fresh.

Spirit water, sacred fungus, and a chicken packed with pure energy made this soup something special from the very start.

It didn't need any extra seasoning. The rich, savory smell drifted up with the steam all on its own.

In no time, three fresh, tasty vegetable sides were ready, and the soup in the pot was done.

The rich, mouthwatering smell quickly filled the whole kitchen and drifted down the hall through the entire building.

Ashley stood next to her and took a deep breath of the delicious smell, her eyes wide. "Paisley, that soup smells incredible!"

Just the smell made her whole body relax and filled her with energy.

Elijah and Orion had just finished getting everyone settled, and the amazing smell made them look over too. When they saw Paisley cooking in the kitchen, they were stunned.

She could actually cook, and whatever she'd made smelled insanely good.

The rich smell spread everywhere and made everyone's stomachs growl even louder. They all crowded around the kitchen, their eyes glued to the steaming pot of soup.

Paisley calmly dished everything out. There were three plates of bright, fresh vegetables and a big pot of golden soup. Tender chicken and thin slices of sacred fungus floated in it, shimmering faintly with energy, and it was obviously no ordinary meal.

"Everyone, sit down and dig in.

"Where's Kelly? Isn't she eating?"

Madison answered for her.

"She said she's not hungry, so we don't have to wait for her. She's got her own instant ramen."
