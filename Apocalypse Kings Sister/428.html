Chapter 428

Her cool, sharp gaze cut through the dirty window to the dark depths of the steel mill.

In that instant, an extremely subtle, extremely deep sense of being watched locked onto her.

The gaze was hidden deep and steady. It carried no killing intent, no rage, only a silent, crushing pressure and a hunger to observe, clinging to them from far away.

Her brow twitched. Her alertness spiked.

Her mental power was vast, far beyond anyone's. Across the base and the surrounding Exclusion Zone, hardly anyone could match her.

But whoever was watching had mental power no weaker than hers, and their hiding skill was at the highest level.

She focused her senses but couldn't catch their position, breath, or movement at all. It was as if the gaze came from empty air, leaving no trace.

The unknown was the greatest danger.

A cold, heavy look crossed her eyes. Her presence pulled in, and she quietly raised every guard.

Before she could trace it, zombies suddenly surged out in huge numbers from the corners of the ruins, the depths of the weeds, and the gaps in the steel frames.

Dense packs of zombies closed in layer by layer.

Rotting bodies, hideous faces, stiff limbs, trailing a stench that filled the air, with howls rising everywhere and shaking the ruins, until the whole armored vehicle was surrounded and every route in or out was sealed.

There were a lot of them, a black mass stretching beyond sight.

Facing the endless horde, the squad inside didn't panic. They were eager, buzzing with fighting spirit.

Thomas rolled his shoulders and cracked his knuckles, his eyes full of excitement. "Perfect timing! Since breaking through to mid Level Six, I haven't had a real fight. These zombies are good practice."

Spencer's eyes glowed, his blood boiling. He rubbed his hands together. "My hands have been itching for ages!

If we clear that whole horde, the crystal cores and combat experience might be enough to push us to Level Seven!"

Both were young and eager, their heads full of breakthroughs and gains.

Orion was the calmest. He quickly scanned the distribution of the horde, checked the terrain, and cut off their excitement in a low, steady voice.

"Don't get cocky.

This is an unfamiliar ruined city. The terrain is complicated and there are surely lots of hidden dangers. No one knows whether there are high-level mutants in the dark, or people waiting to ambush us.

Nobody goes in alone. Watch for sneak attacks."

Elijah nodded slightly and quickly set the plan, giving orders cleanly.

"Pais and I will go into the warehouse and look for the key supplies. The rest of you stay on the outside and hold off this horde. Look after yourselves first. Safety comes first."

"Okay."

Paisley nodded, still wary of the unseen gaze in the dark.

Ashley looked worried and tugged Paisley's sleeve, her voice soft. "Paisley, you and the captain be very careful. Don't let your guard down."

"Don't worry."

Paisley's voice was calm and sure.

"You be careful too."

"Everyone, prepare for battle!"

At Elijah's order, they pushed open the doors.

The wind carried the stench of countless zombies, but it couldn't blow away their will to fight.

Six of them jumped out and formed up, positions spaced out, attack and defense clearly divided, perfectly in sync.

Every one of them held a top-tier new Affinity weapon from Paisley, a sharp combat knife at the waist, and a bag full of ammo and spirit water, fully geared up and confident.

"Fire!"

At the order, gunfire exploded and shook the silent ruined city.

The rapid, powerful shots rang out without a break. The top-tier weapons fired fast and hit hard, Affinity-enhanced bullets flying out and punching precisely through zombie skulls.

Blood sprayed, rotten flesh and shards flew, and the zombies in front fell in waves.

At the same time, Ashley spread her Earth Affinity, raising thick walls of rock and soil to block the zombie charge. Thomas used his Plant Affinity, and vines surged and wrapped tight around huge numbers of zombies to limit their movement. Spencer unleashed his Fire Affinity in a violent burst, flames sweeping across the packed horde.
