Chapter 64

Ever since her rebirth, Paisley had seen these three hypocrites for exactly what they were.

It had been that way her whole life.

The brothers loved climbing on their high horse and pulling the big-brother card to guilt her, control her, and boss her around. They always took Rachel's side, no matter what, and dumped every bit of blame on Paisley.

Sure, they talked a big game about how much they loved their sisters. But the second their own interests were on the line, all that love went right out the window.

Paisley's smile turned even colder as she ripped their masks off in front of everyone.

"What's the matter? Cat got your tongue?

"Weren't you just yelling at me and demanding I go find her? I told you exactly where she is. So why aren't you moving?

"Aren't you the ones who love her the most?

"This might be her only chance to survive, and not one of you is willing to go? You're her big brothers, the ones who supposedly love her more than anyone!

"So all that love and protectiveness was fake this whole time.

"At the end of the day, you're just a bunch of selfish, two-faced cowards. You love throwing your weight around with me, but the second there's real danger, you only care about saving your own skins."

Her words were sharp and brutally blunt. They tore right through the brothers' act and laid their selfishness and cowardice bare for everyone to see.

The three brothers' faces went red, then white.

Ashley planted herself in front of Paisley and glared at the Lennox brothers, furious and fiercely protective.

"Paisley's right! You three are total hypocrites, and you're selfish as hell!

"You use the big-brother card to bully my sister all the time, and you always take Rachel's side. Then, the second something goes wrong, you show up and blame her without even asking what happened!

"You're too chicken to go save Rachel yourselves, but you've got the nerve to come in here and blame someone else? Let me make this crystal clear. If you ever bully my sister or trash-talk her again, I swear I won't let you get away with it!"

Thomas calmly stepped up right after her, backing Paisley up.

"In the apocalypse, whether you live or die comes down to luck. Running into trouble out there is normal.

"When a group goes out and everybody makes it back, that's already a miracle. Nobody's obligated to risk their life to save somebody else or pay for their stupidity. Barging in here to point fingers without even knowing what happened is way out of line.

"If you ever blame Pais for something without a good reason again, I won't let it slide either.

"Paisley means the world to us. If you don't appreciate her, we sure as hell do.

"She's not someone you get to push around whenever you feel like it."

Spencer stepped forward too, his voice blunt and cutting.

"If you really cared about Rachel, you wouldn't be standing here grilling everybody else. You'd be out there searching for her yourselves.

"Instead, you're hiding in here running your mouths. You pick on people you think are weaker and guilt-trip them, and you're total hypocrites about it.

"You should be ashamed of yourselves."

Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer took turns tearing into them, standing firmly together to protect Paisley. They stripped the Lennox brothers' hypocrisy and cowardice bare.

Owen, Reid, and Ryan were completely humiliated, and they wished they could sink into the floor.

Paisley stood quietly in the middle of everyone protecting her. Some of the coldness left her face, and she felt a little warmer inside.

She looked up at the three brothers, who were red-faced and stuck with nowhere to go, and mocked them one last time as she kicked them out.

"You're not welcome here, so get lost. Who knows? Maybe Rachel will come running back on her own."

After all, Rachel was like a cockroach. Nothing could kill her.

The second Paisley finished speaking, Rachel actually showed up at the villa's front door. She stood there like a ghost, staring at them with a creepy look in her eyes.

She'd obviously heard every word.

The three brothers who supposedly loved her most had actually been willing to let her die.

She'd been abandoned all over again.

Her heart hurt so much that it had gone numb.

So that's all their love was ever worth.

From now on, she'd never count on anyone again.

Paisley watched it all play out, and it felt amazing.

This is way more fun than just killing them.

The three Lennox brothers' faces went sour.

They rushed over to Rachel in a panic, scrambling to explain themselves. Then, they followed her back to their own villa, still trying to justify what they'd done.

Owen went first.

"Rachel, you know none of us have awakened abilities yet. Going out in weather like that would've been way too dangerous. I didn't leave you out there on purpose.

"Thank God you made it back safe on your own."
