Chapter 490

The cheers rolled on and on and echoed over the whole base.

Everyone was cheering for Falcon Squad, for Paisley, for the miracle win that had come back from the edge.

Meanwhile,

Chloe, Grant, and the Quinn family core Affinity users who had ambushed the team and sabotaged the mission were taken by enforcement officers to cells at the Security Office to await judgment.

After their arrest, the base launched an investigation at once, tracing every family and network connected to the case, digging into every link, and leaving no traitor free who had plotted against the base.

The evidence was overwhelming. There was nothing to argue.

No matter how Grant, Chloe, and the rest cried, knelt, begged, or made excuses, it didn't help.

Nobody knew that at the start of the ambush, while everyone was shouting and the villains were making threats, Paisley had had the sharp little Trovie quietly start recording, capturing every one of their schemes, taunts, intentions to kill, and crimes.

The broken bridge, the burning bridge, the high-tier zombie trap, the blockade, the attempted murder, the sabotage of the base's mission. Every piece of evidence was clear and complete.

With proof on hand and the law firm, nobody dared cover for them and nobody could save them.

The Quinn faction dropped them completely.

The villains became criminals despised by the whole base, hated by all. They could only wait for their trial and sentence, and pay a heavy price for their ambition and malice.

This was only the beginning.

The Quinn faction took a heavy blow.

The Lennox faction watched from the sidelines, but seeing Falcon Squad come home safe with a win, which cemented General Blackwood's position, they grew anxious.

Falcon Squad's name kept growing.

Paisley went back to the villa early to rest.

The snow outside kept getting heavier.

By the next morning, the world outside was white.

Early the next day,

a night of heavy snow had covered Aetheris Base's outer walls, roads, and rooftops in endless white.

Wind carried fine snow across the street. But on the walk out for cold-weather supplies, the way everyone treated them was completely different from before.

Gerald and Diane Thompson, bundled in heavy coats, walked side by side along the packed snow road. They'd barely gotten out the door when residents began stopping to talk to them with smiles.

"Mr. Thompson, Mrs. Thompson, good morning!"

A middle-aged woman holding a blanket hurried over, her face warm and sincere, her eyes full of envy.

"I'm so jealous. Who knew the daughter you found would be a rare Storage Dimension user!

Five depots of grain came in, all thanks to your girl. Without those supplies, who knows how hard this freezing winter would have been for the base."

A man beside her nodded and chimed in. "And not just the supplies!

I heard the mission was brutal this time. The Quinns attacked them the whole way, and high-tier beasts blocked the road.

Falcon Squad got back with every member alive, and several times it was Paisley who turned things around. You raised a wonderful child."

Someone in the crowd lowered their voice and added, with a sigh of comparison.

"The Thompsons' junior branch has really done well.

Orion, Thomas, and Ashley are all on Falcon Squad, an elite squad, strong and upright.

And then there's Chloe from the senior branch. She teamed up with the Quinns and ambushed her own base's squad. Vicious. Nobody can say her name now without shaking their head. She's disgraced the whole senior branch."

Diane's cheeks warmed at the stream of praise, and she didn't know what to do with her hands.

In the past, everyone on this road had flattered the senior branch and made a fuss over the more famous Chloe.

Back then, when she and Gerald walked down the street, people gave polite, distant greetings. Few had ever been this warm and sincere with them.

Gerald patted Diane's hand gently and nodded to each of them, warm and courteous.

"Thank you all. Paisley only did her part."

"You're heading to the depot for cold-weather supplies, right? So are we. Let's walk together!"

They set off through the snow together, chatting and laughing all the way. The cold distance between them was gone.

Soon the group reached the supply depot. A fairly long line had formed at the door. The clerk handling sign-ups and distribution spotted Gerald and Diane from a distance and waved at once, smiling.
