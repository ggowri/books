Chapter 180

[Ten top-of-the-line golden talismans in one go! Nothing like this even exists in the apocalypse! Ordinary Teleportation Talismans have a distance limit, and you can't buy these at any price, but your golden ones have no flaws at all, Master!

[You can use them to protect yourself, break out of a siege, rush to help someone far away, escape in a pinch, or launch a surprise attack. They're good for so much!]

As she listened to the full explanation, Paisley's eyes lit up.

[Fighting in the apocalypse is so unpredictable, so these really are great. They're priceless. It's just a shame there are only 10.]

She gently ran her fingertips over the shimmering silver patterns on each talisman, then put all 10 into a special pouch in her Storage Dimension for safekeeping.

She smiled, bright and satisfied, and teased Trovie. [Looks like you're finally good for something.]

Trovie wiggled its little wings and puffed out its chest, sounding super smug. [Master, I've got plenty of other tricks up my sleeve, and you'll find out about them in time!]

Paisley laughed helplessly and stopped teasing it. Then, she turned serious and focused on the pile of crystal cores.

She had 1,765 Level Two crystal cores, 678 Level Three, 15 Level Four, five Level Five, and one Level Six Plant core.

With this many cores, she could finally level up.

She stood up, walked over to the soft couch in the lounge, and sat down with her back straight, closing her eyes.

A second later, with a single thought, Paisley released her affinity and called up the energy of every crystal core in her Storage Dimension.

Layer upon layer of crystal cores floated around her, their glowing lights of every color weaving together in a dazzling display, and rich, pure energy quickly filled the whole lounge.

Without the slightest hesitation, she started absorbing and refining the energy at full speed.

The pure energy from the huge pile of cores rushed into her like a raging river, flooding in through every pore and coursing through her whole body, washing over, nourishing, and tempering every inch of her.

Her Plant Affinity went first.

The precious Level Six Plant core burst with raw power. Since it was the same type as her affinity, it merged with her perfectly, and with the energy from countless lower-level cores backing it up, it shattered the barrier holding her back.

With a resonant hum, she felt a clear breakthrough ripple through her body. Her Plant Affinity shot up, broke through the early Level Six bottleneck, and kept climbing until it settled firmly at mid Level Six.

Lush, vibrant plant energy swirled around her, wrapping her in a faint green glow.

Her Psychic Affinity came next.

Her psychic power, which had been at mid Level Five, was honed, widened, and thickened as the flood of high-level core energy fed it.

Before, her mind had felt a little thin, but now it expanded fast and grew vast and stable. The reach, control, and intimidating force of her psychic power all skyrocketed.

Barrier after barrier shattered, and her Psychic Affinity steadily broke through, climbing from mid Level Five all the way to peak Level Five. She was only one step away from Level Six.

Her Water Affinity came last.

Clear, gentle water energy rushed through her body, and the sheer flood of power easily crushed and dissolved the peak Level Four barrier.

Her water energy grew deeper, smoother, and stronger, and in no time, she leveled up from peak Level Four straight to mid Level Five.

In less than an hour, she'd absorbed every last bit of energy from the cores.

The piles of cores floating around her turned into specks of light and faded away.

Paisley let out a long, slow breath, the three-colored glow of her affinities settling back into her body.

She slowly opened her eyes, which were clear and deep. She seemed even cooler and more powerful now, with a steady, weighty presence. Her overall strength had taken a huge leap forward, and her foundation was rock solid.

Trovie was amazed. [Master, you leveled up a ton again! You're amazing, and congratulations!

[Master, you're the hardest-working, most dedicated powerhouse I've ever seen!]

Paisley looked up with a faint smile. Her whole body felt loose and limber, every limb brimming with strength, and she felt better than ever.

Having three affinities really was amazing. She'd never felt this strong.

The only downside was that it burned through crystal cores like crazy.

In less than an hour, the huge pile of cores she'd worked so hard to save was all gone. She'd only just struck it rich, and now she was broke again.

Paisley shook her head helplessly. It looked like she'd have to trade some supplies for more crystal cores.

There were all the energy fruits and vegetables Trovie had grown in her Storage Dimension, the energy grapes she'd collected along the way, the huge stockpile of supplies she'd gathered, and everything she'd gotten from cross-dimensional trades. She could trade any of it.
