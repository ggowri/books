Chapter 180

[Ten top-tier gold talismans, all at once! Something this world basically doesn't have anymore! Regular talismans have distance caps. Host, your gold ones are flawless — zero limitations!]

[Great for emergency escapes, cross-region support runs, last-second survival, surprise strikes — endless uses.]

Hearing the full rundown, Paisley's eyes brightened.

[The apocalypse is unpredictable. This is genuinely priceless. Shame there's only ten.]

She ran her fingers over the silver-traced patterns glowing across each talisman, tucking all ten carefully into a dedicated pouch in her storage.

Her mouth curved into something bright and satisfied, teasing.

[Trovie, you're finally earning your keep.]

Trovie puffed up immediately, wings wiggling, voice dripping with mock pride.

[Host, I've got plenty more good stuff up my sleeve — you'll see!]

Paisley huffed a laugh and let it go, turning her attention to the pile of crystal cores in front of her.

1,765 Level Two crystal cores. 678 Level Three. 15 Level Four. 5 Level Five. 1 Level Six Plant Affinity.

Plenty to work with for a level-up.

******

She moved to the soft couch in her room and sat, posture straight, eyes closing.

A second later, her affinity surged, drawing on every crystal core stored in her space at once.

Dense layers of crystal cores floated around her, light from every affinity type weaving together, brilliant and blinding, flooding the whole room with raw, concentrated energy.

Without hesitation, she began absorbing and refining it all, full speed.

The condensed energy poured through her like a flood tide, surging through every pore, every meridian, washing through and tempering every inch of her body.

Plant Affinity went first.

The single precious Level Six core released its full force immediately, merging seamlessly with her plant meridians, the lower-tier cores filling in the gaps, shattering the barrier holding her back.

Hum —

A clear ripple of breakthrough rang through her body, Plant Affinity surging upward, breaking clean through the Level Six early-stage ceiling, climbing steadily, settling firmly at mid Level Six.

A wave of lush, vibrant plant energy rolled off her, wrapping her in a faint green glow.

Psychic Affinity came next.

Already at mid Level Five, it absorbed the flood of high-tier core energy, expanding and reinforcing itself layer by layer.

Her once slightly-thin mental sea stretched wide, growing dense and vast, her range, control, and raw force all surging at once.

Barrier after barrier cracked and fell, her Psychic Affinity climbing steadily from mid Level Five all the way to Level Five peak — one step shy of breaking into Level Six.

Last came Water Affinity.

Clean, cool water energy surged through her meridians, and the Level Four peak ceiling cracked, dissolved, and gave way under the sheer volume of power.

The current grew richer, more fluid and commanding, and her Water Affinity broke clean through, settling solidly at mid Level Five.

In just a few dozen minutes, every crystal core's energy had been fully absorbed, nothing left to spare.

The dense cloud of cores hovering around her dissolved into scattered light and vanished.

Paisley let out a long, slow breath, the three-colored glow of her affinities settling back into her body.

She opened her eyes, gaze clear and sharp, her presence settling into something colder, steadier, more powerful. A real leap forward, the foundation beneath it solid as bedrock.

Trovie marveled, impressed.

[Host just leveled up again, big time! You're incredible!]

[Host, you're the hardest-working, most dedicated cultivator I've ever seen!]

Paisley's eyes crinkled, a faint smile surfacing, her whole body loose and full of fresh strength, every limb humming with energy. She felt better than she had in a long time.

Three affinities working in sync — it really did feel good. Strong.

The one downside — it absolutely devoured crystal cores.

In just a few dozen minutes, the entire pile she'd worked so hard to collect was gone, completely drained. From freshly rich to broke again, just like that.

Paisley shook her head, resigned. Looks like it was time to trade some goods for more cores.

She thought of the energy fruits and vegetables growing in her space, the energy grapes she'd gathered along the way, all the supplies stockpiled from today and traded from the other realms — plenty there to convert into what she needed.
