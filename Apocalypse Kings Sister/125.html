Chapter 125

There was no point letting all that space go to waste. She might as well start farming on a huge scale.

Once she'd made up her mind, Paisley gave Trovie its orders right away.

"Trovie, time to get to work. Help me get this place in order.

"Turn all this open ground into farmland. Split it into an orchard, a vegetable patch, and a grain field. Then sort the seeds, plant them in batches, and water everything evenly with the spirit spring."

Trovie answered right away. [You got it, Master! Consider it done!]

With Trovie in charge of clearing, planting, and watering, Paisley didn't have to lift a finger. It saved her a ton of time and got the job done fast.

Once all that was taken care of, it finally hit Paisley how exhausted she was.

She'd spent the whole day fighting and on the move. Between gathering supplies, battling the rat swarm, and leveling up, she was covered in dust and sweat, and she felt gross.

She got up, went to the villa's private bathroom, and turned on the hot water for a quick shower.

The warm water washed away the dust and exhaustion, and she felt fresh and relaxed right away.

When she was done, she dried her hair and changed straight into the brand-new Galactic radiation-proof combat suit she'd just traded for.

The suit was sleek and form-fitting, and its light, breathable fabric adjusted to her body automatically. It didn't feel heavy or tight at all, but it had amazing protective power built in.

The suit could block the apocalypse's heavy radiation and soften the blow from both affinity attacks and physical hits. It was worth every point.

The fabric felt soft and comfortable against her skin, and a faint protective glow surrounded her. She felt completely safe in it.

Once she'd showered and changed, Paisley left her Storage Dimension, cuddled up with the little white cat, and closed her eyes to rest.

Early the next morning, after a good night's rest, everyone on the team had leveled up. Their overall strength had visibly improved.

Elijah and Orion had reached early Level Five, and Thomas and Spencer were now mid Level Four. Ashley and Madison had hit early Level Four. Kelly had made it to late Level Three.

Everyone looked thrilled.

They were just about to sit down for breakfast when Marcus showed up, tall and straight-backed.

He wore simple, clean clothes. His face was still a little pale from his illness, but he looked full of energy, and his injuries had mostly healed.

His eyes went straight to Paisley in the middle of the group as he crossed the living room.

He walked up to her and dipped his head respectfully.

"Paisley, thank you for saving my life. If it weren't for you, I'd probably be dead by now."

Paisley waved it off. "Don't mention it. Are you all better now?"

Marcus nodded and looked her straight in the eye, his voice earnest.

"I'm pretty much healed. You saved my life, so from now on, my life is yours, and I'll go wherever you go. I'd like to join your team and head to Aetheris with all of you."

Paisley hadn't expected him to be so straightforward.

Besides, sticking with Elijah and the others really was the safest thing he could do right now.

Nobody knew Marcus's value better than she did.

In the future, he'd be the only scientific genius who could crack the zombie virus and create a cure. He was humanity's future.

His decision to join them was huge news for the team and for every survivor out there. She couldn't have asked for more.

"Welcome aboard," Paisley said calmly.

Marcus let out a sigh of relief, and his face softened. Without thinking, he moved a little closer to Paisley, like he planned to stick right by her side from now on.

The mood in the room shifted, and things got a little tense.

Elijah was the most bothered of all. His face went dark, and a weird, nagging irritation welled up inside him.

This handsome, soft-spoken pretty boy was stuck to Paisley like glue, and Elijah's jaw clenched.

I want to drag this guy as far away from her as possible.

But then he remembered what Paisley had told him. Marcus was crucial to humanity's future, and they had to protect him no matter what.

So he had no choice but to swallow his resentment.

He pressed his lips together and kept telling himself to hold it in. His face gave nothing away, but inside, he was eaten up with jealousy.

Elijah could hold back, but the Thompson brothers couldn't take it anymore.

The three of them were ridiculously overprotective of Paisley. They'd only just gotten her back, and now some strange guy was sticking close to her. They all stepped up at once and started subtly squeezing him out.

Orion went first, casually slipping between the two of them. He was polite but cold.
