Chapter 167

It turned out the three Lennox brothers had been the stupid, clueless ones all along. Over and over, they'd hurt the one person who truly cared about them and treated them well, while blindly protecting and spoiling Rachel, who was rotten to the core.

Owen's chest heaved. The rage in his eyes slowly faded, replaced by a bone-deep chill and crushing disappointment.

He stared hard at Rachel's teary face, his gaze sharp as a knife, studying her features inch by inch, like he'd never really known his own sister.

The cramped, ruined car went dead silent. No one spoke.

On the surface, they were still sitting in the same car and traveling together. But the three Lennox brothers knew that something between them had already cracked.

The blind trust and favoritism they'd always shown Rachel was gone, replaced by cold suspicion and constant wariness.

Owen turned to look out at the ruined, desolate streets, his feelings tangled and complicated. But it was Paisley he was thinking about.

The next time he saw Paisley, he'd swallow his pride, apologize to her properly, and clear up every misunderstanding.

As long as he humbled himself and sincerely asked for forgiveness, she cared about him enough that she'd soften and come back to them. Then, they could all go back to the way things used to be.

Owen had finally seen Rachel for who she was, and he'd made up his mind.

Rachel was selfish and heartless. Whenever trouble hit, she sold out her friends and family to save herself, and she'd only ever drag everyone around her down.

But Paisley was different. She was open and genuine with people, and she'd always quietly done so much for them.

Now, her power had skyrocketed, and she had tons of resources to get even stronger. She was a hundred times more reliable than Rachel, someone who could actually help them get a foothold and grow stronger in this deadly apocalypse. He finally saw things clearly, and he'd never bet all their hopes on Rachel again.

If they kept spoiling and depending on a sister this vicious, sooner or later her selfish scheming would get them killed, and they wouldn't even see it coming.

Reid and Ryan sat in silence. After the danger they'd just been through, they were more determined than ever to get stronger.

After all, they had no idea when they'd get shoved out as a shield again.

Maybe Paisley really was their only option now.

Rachel thought their silence meant she'd smoothed things over, so she pulled out a map and started pointing out directions.

She'd never really cared how they felt, so of course she had no idea what was going through their heads right now.

******

Meanwhile, Paisley's group had already parked their SUVs outside the gates of the abandoned amusement park.

Once full of noise and laughter, the place had long since fallen into ruin.

Rusty rides stood silent in the dusty wind, and faded billboards hung by a thread. The ground was covered in broken branches, rubble, and dirt, and the air carried a strange, sickly-sweet smell mixed with rot. Everything about the place felt quietly deadly.

This was the site of the Level Six mission.

Everyone climbed out of the cars. The moment their feet hit the ground, they sensed an entirely different, oppressive presence, far darker and creepier than the zombies at the mall.

There were no roars and no sounds of movement. The whole park was so still that it made their scalps prickle and their blood run cold, and the dead silence was scarier than any noisy fight.

Paisley gazed into the depths of the overgrown, deserted park, her eyes cool and clear.

A second later, she spread out her psychic power like an invisible net. In a heartbeat, it covered the entire amusement park, scanning every corner.

A flood of information poured into her mind, crystal clear.

About 500 yards due south, in the flower field at the center of the park, a huge, sinister Level Six presence lurked, full of corrosive, paralyzing energy. It was their target, the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower.

Not far from the flower, she could also sense a few people, weak but alive. Those were the trapped hostages they had to rescue.

Paisley pulled back her senses and looked at everyone, her face serious. "Our mission is to kill the Level Six carnivorous flower and rescue the hostages. The target is 500 yards due south, and the hostages are trapped in a building under its roots, safe for now."

The words had barely left her mouth when a rustle of shuffling footsteps rose from every direction at once.
