Chapter 167

Turned out, they'd been the foolish ones all along — hurting the person who actually cared about them, over and over, while shielding the one with a snake coiled where her heart should be.

Owen's chest heaved, the fury in his eyes giving way to something colder — a deep, bone-level disappointment.

He stared at Rachel's tear-streaked face, gaze sharp as a blade, studying her features inch by inch, like he was seeing her for the first time.

The cramped, ruined car fell completely silent. No one spoke.

On the surface, they were still riding together, still a group. But the three brothers knew the truth — something had already cracked beyond repair.

The blind trust and favoritism they'd once given Rachel without question was gone, replaced by cold suspicion.

Owen turned to stare out at the ruined streets, his thoughts tangled and heavy — but it was Paisley on his mind.

Next time I see her, I'll drop all my pride and apologize properly. Clear up everything.

If I humble myself, really mean it — she cares enough about family that she'll soften eventually.

Then we can go back to how things were.

Having finally seen Rachel clearly, Owen had made up his mind.

Rachel was cold, self-serving, always willing to sacrifice friends and family to save herself. Cruel, selfish, nothing but a liability to anyone close to her.

Paisley was different. Open-hearted, genuine, always the one quietly giving.

And now her power had surged, and she had resources no one else had. Far more reliable than Rachel could ever be — someone who could actually help them survive, and grow stronger, in this brutal world.

He was done. Done pinning everyone's survival on a sister with a heart like that. Keep leaning on her, and sooner or later they'd all go down with her — and never even see it coming.

Reid and Ryan sat in silence, the danger they'd just survived only sharpening their resolve to get stronger.

Because they understood now — they had no idea when they'd be the next one thrown to the wolves.

Maybe Paisley really was their only option now.

Rachel, misreading their silence as acceptance, pulled out a map and started pointing out directions.

She'd never much cared how they felt. No reason to start wondering now.

Meanwhile —

Paisley's group had already parked outside the gates of the abandoned amusement park.

Once full of noise and laughter, the place had long since fallen into ruin.

Rusted rides stood frozen in the wind. Faded billboards swayed, ready to collapse. Broken branches and debris littered the ground, and the air carried a strange, sickly-sweet smell tangled with rot — quiet, and unmistakably dangerous.

This was the site of the Level Six mission.

******

Everyone climbed out, and the moment their feet hit the ground, they felt it — a pressure far heavier, far more unsettling than anything the mall had put off.

No roars, no movement. Just absolute stillness, the kind that crawled under the skin and made the silence itself feel like a threat.

Paisley looked out over the overgrown park, eyes calm and clear.

A second later, her Psychic Affinity spread wide, blanketing the entire park, sweeping every corner for details.

Information flooded in, sharp and clear.

Five hundred yards south, in the park's central flower garden, sat a massive presence — a Level Six affinity, thick with corrosive, paralytic energy. The mission target. A mutant carnivorous flower.

Nearby, a handful of faint but living human signatures. The hostages.

Confirmed — they were still alive.

"Our mission — kill the Level Six flower, rescue the hostages.

Target's five hundred yards south. The hostages are in a building under its root system, safe for now."

Paisley pulled her focus back, expression serious as she addressed the group.

The words had barely left her mouth when a rustle of shuffling footsteps rose from every direction at once.
