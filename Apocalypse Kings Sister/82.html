Chapter 82

Trovie was left completely speechless.

This trip had been more than worth it. She'd made out like a bandit.

She swung herself back onto the little white cat's broad, warm back, lay down against it, and headed back the way she'd come.

Wind rustled through the trees, and light and shadow danced across the ground. A faint smile played on Paisley's lips.

The best part of this trip had been watching the three Lennox siblings land in deadly danger, barely escape with their lives, and run away in total humiliation.

In her past life, they'd called her a useless loser.

In this life, she was paying them back for every bit of pain.

Now, they could feel the same terror and hurt she'd felt back then.

Before long, the little white cat carried her back to the group.

The fight was already over.

Everyone sat in small groups on the ground, catching their breath. Every one of them had at least a few scrapes and cuts.

The three Lennox siblings were in the worst shape by far.

Reid and Ryan were covered in cuts, deep and shallow. Thanks to the venom, the blood oozing out of them was a dark, dull red. Their wounds were numb and swollen, and they were weak, shaky, and struggling to breathe.

As for Owen, all anyone had given him for his burns was some anti-inflammatory medicine.

He'd been out cold ever since and still hadn't woken up.

When Reid and Ryan thought about their own injuries and Owen's, they knew Rachel was the one to blame for all of it. They were so furious that they couldn't hold it in.

A huge fight was bound to break out.

Reid clutched his numb, aching arm, his face twisted with disappointment and rage.

"Rachel, why the hell did you do that?

"We've always loved you and looked out for you, no matter what. And when it was life or death, you shoved us in front of that snake to save your own skin without even thinking twice!

"I never thought you could be like this! So all that sweet, kind, good-girl stuff was just an act this whole time!"

His chest heaved, and his voice was thick with regret and bitter disappointment.

"God, I regret it so much. Every time you and Paisley got into it, I always took your side first. I defended you and played favorites every single time!

"Every time there was a fight, you'd just cry and act like the victim, and we'd believe you and protect you, no questions asked!

"Now that I think about it, Paisley was always way better to us than you ever were. You're the real hypocrite. You're the most selfish, heartless person I know!"

Next to him, Ryan's face had gone deathly pale. He was fighting through the agony of the venom spreading through his body, and he looked sick with regret.

"I regret it too," Ryan said hoarsely, with a bitter laugh at his own expense.

"If it had been Paisley back there, there's no way she would've shoved us out to die. She would've fought with everything she had to protect us. She never would've abandoned us like that.

"If we'd known you were such an ungrateful snake, we never would've kept shutting Paisley out and pushing her away for your sake. She actually cared about us!

"She was a million times better to us than you. We were blind. We couldn't tell trash from treasure!

"We hurt Paisley because of you, and she was always so good to us."

They were covered in wounds, poisoned, and completely drained. Being betrayed by their own sister left them cold to the bone.

Rachel's true colors had been exposed in front of everyone, and the accusations just kept coming.

Her face went red, then white. She was completely mortified.

She knew she'd gone too far back there when it was life or death.

Why the hell didn't they just die?

Now that they'd said it all out loud, everyone knew exactly what she'd done.

No. I need to fix this.

They were her family, after all, and she still needed them to protect her in the apocalypse.

She couldn't afford to fall out with them yet.

With that, Rachel's eyes turned red, and tears streamed down her face. She put on her most fragile, helpless look, hanging her head and apologizing over and over in a soft, pleading voice.

"I'm so sorry, Reid, Ryan! I was wrong, okay? I was just so scared and panicked that I didn't even know what I was doing!

"I was so terrified, I didn't even realize I'd pushed you.

"Can you please forgive me? I have some serum on me that'll get the poison out and help your wounds heal. Please, just forgive me this once!"

Rachel cried her pretty little tears and pulled two vials of serum out of her bag, handing them over.

But inside, she was ice-cold. She didn't feel a shred of remorse.

If I had to do it all over again, I'd do the exact same thing.

It's the apocalypse. When it's life or death, who doesn't save their own skin first?
