Chapter 190

[Host, you're amazing, truly!]

[But this gold is way too hard! I can't even bite through it! Not tasty at all, and I think I chipped a tooth!]

Paisley watched it, charmed, and rubbed the soft little head affectionately, then pulled a fruit lollipop from her Storage Dimension and tossed it right in front of it.

[Trovie, convert all this gold into Trading System points.]

Trovie dropped the gold at once, tore open the wrapper happily, and crammed the lollipop in its mouth, mumbling an answer around it as it fired up the exchange program.

A crisp notification chimed.

[Large gold deposit detected. Beginning point conversion!] [Eleven thousand pounds of gold converted to one hundred thousand Trading System points. Conversion successful!] [Host's current total points: 289,060!] [First large-scale trade bonus: +10,000 points. New total: 299,060 points] [Reminder: reaching Level Four trader status requires one million cumulative points. Host's progress is nearly one-third there—keep it up!]

With that, the glittering mountain of gold vanished into thin air, not a trace left behind.

Paisley looked at the bare ground and let out a small sigh. This system eats money like it's nothing.

The gold hadn't even had time to warm in her hands before she was broke again.

She asked, resigned.

[Trovie, why is it so hard to level up this system?]

Trovie, still working on its lollipop, rubbed its round head sheepishly and offered what comfort it could.

[Host, you're already doing incredible! Almost three hundred thousand points in this short a time puts you way ahead of other new traders!]

[A few more trades like that and we'll hit Level Four trader status in no time, unlock more permissions and rare goods too!]

Paisley pressed a hand to her forehead, half laughing, half exasperated.

Galactic gold veins weren't exactly easy to find. A score like that was a once-in-a-blue-moon stroke of luck, not something she could count on twice.

But if she wanted to raise her trading rank and unlock rarer resources, there wasn't really a faster path. She'd just have to keep building it up, point by point.

Hearing movement in the room, Ashley and the others knocked at her door.

“Paisley… Paisley, are you up?”

It was Ashley's soft voice, testing the waters a little.

Paisley shook off her thoughts, smoothed her clothes, and opened the door.

The whole group stood outside, bright-eyed, clearly there just for her.

The moment the door opened, Ashley's eyes lit up. She hurried over, grabbed Paisley's arm, and leaned into her, her voice wheedling.

“Paisley, are you feeling okay? You slept forever, I was so worried!”

Then, pulling Paisley along, she said, unable to wait.

“The guys are awake! Come on, let's go see them!”

A faint smile crossed Paisley's eyes before she lowered them, playing innocent, her confusion perfectly timed.

“What happened to Orion and the others?”

Spencer jumped in, explaining earnestly.

“Orion and Captain Blackwood ran into some nasty mutant species out on a mission, something with serious toxin contamination. They got hurt bad—the whole base was in a panic yesterday.”

“But some mystery healer fixed them up, so it's fine now. They're all awake, back to normal. Nothing to worry about.”

Paisley nodded as if just catching up.

“Okay. Let's go see them, then.”

Ashley happily looped her arm through Paisley's and led the way, the rest of the group falling in behind as they headed for the hospital.

The whole way there, Ashley chattered on like an excited little bird, sharing every small thing on her mind.

“Paisley, I took on a bunch of Level Four missions with the squad this morning! I'm actually pretty strong around here now!”

“And we ran into a Level Five zombie today! It was brutal and fast, but I still took it down in the end. Pretty amazing, right?”

The girl's voice brimmed with energy and confidence, her whole face bright with an easy, unguarded smile.

They crossed the base's main corridor, and right at the hall's entrance, ran straight into Rachel's group.
