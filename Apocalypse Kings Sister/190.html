Chapter 190

[Master, you're amazing!

[But this gold is way too hard, and I can't even bite it! It doesn't taste good at all, and I almost broke my teeth!]

Paisley smiled warmly at how silly and cute it looked. She gently ruffled its soft little head, then pulled a fruit lollipop out of her Storage Dimension and tossed it right in front of Trovie.

[Trovie, convert all this gold into Trading System points.]

Trovie immediately dropped the gold, happily unwrapped the lollipop, and popped it into its mouth. It mumbled a reply around the candy and started the Trading System's exchange program right away, and a crisp chime rang out.

[Large amount of gold detected. Starting point conversion!

[11,000 pounds of gold converted into 100,000 Trading System points. Conversion successful!

[Current total: 289,060 points!

[First large trade completed! Bonus: 10,000 points. New total: 299,060 points!

[Reminder: Upgrading to Level Four cross-dimensional trader requires 1 million total points. You're almost a third of the way there, so keep it up!]

With that, the glittering mountain of gold vanished into thin air, not a trace left behind.

Paisley looked at the empty floor and sighed.

This system is a bottomless money pit.

She'd barely had that pile of gold for a minute, and now she was broke again.

She asked helplessly. [Trovie, why is it so hard to upgrade the system?]

Trovie, still working on its lollipop, rubbed its round head sheepishly and offered what comfort it could.

[Master, you're already doing amazing! You saved up almost 300,000 points in no time, and that's super fast for a new cross-dimensional trader!

[A few more trades like this, and you'll make Level Four trader in no time and unlock more permissions and rare goods!]

Paisley pressed a hand to her forehead, half laughing, half exasperated.

How easy did Trovie think it was to find a Galactic gold mine? A lucky break like that, loaded with valuable refined gold, came along once in a blue moon, so she couldn't count on it happening again.

But if she wanted to raise her trading rank and unlock rarer resources, there wasn't really a faster path. She'd just have to keep building it up, point by point.

Ashley and the others heard the noise in her room and knocked on her door.

"Paisley... Paisley, are you up?"

It was Ashley's sweet voice, a little hesitant.

Paisley shook off her thoughts, smoothed her clothes, and opened the door.

Everyone was standing outside, full of energy, obviously there to see her.

The moment the door opened, Ashley's eyes lit up. She hurried over, hugged Paisley's arm tight, and snuggled up to her, whining sweetly.

"Paisley, are you still feeling sick anywhere? You slept for so, so long, and I was worried sick!"

Then, she grabbed Paisley's hand and tugged her along eagerly. "Orion and the others are awake! Come on, let's go see them!"

Paisley smiled faintly, then lowered her eyes and played dumb, sounding just confused enough. "What happened to Orion and the others? Is something wrong?"

Spencer jumped in, explaining earnestly.

"Orion and Captain Blackwood ran into some crazy-strong mutant species on their mission. It had a really nasty toxin, and they got hurt badly. The whole base was freaking out yesterday.

"But luckily, some mysterious expert healed them, so they're fine now. Everyone's awake and back to normal, so don't worry."

Paisley nodded as if she'd only just heard. "Okay, let's go see them together."

Ashley happily looped her arm through Paisley's and led the way, the rest of the group falling in behind as they headed for the hospital.

The whole way there, Ashley chattered on like an excited little bird, sharing every small thing on her mind.

"Paisley, I took on a bunch of Level Four missions with the team this morning! I'm actually pretty strong around the base now!

"And we ran into a Level Five zombie today! It was super vicious and super fast, but I beat it in the end. Aren't I amazing?"

Ashley sounded bubbly, full of energy and confidence, her face lit up with a carefree smile.

As they crossed the base's long hallway and reached the corner by the hall, they ran right into Rachel's group.
