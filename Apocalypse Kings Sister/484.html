Chapter 484

But against six Level Seven zombies, all his advantages faded.

Lightning struck the Level Seven zombies and could only briefly stun them or scorch the surface. It couldn't kill.

Even when the bolts blackened and split their bodies, they kept coming, ignoring the pain and charging through the storm.

The level gap couldn't be crossed.

Elijah, alone against six, was pinned in the middle of the roof as the ring closed tighter. Waves of attacks pushed him back step by step.

Boom!

Another heavy punch landed. Elijah raised an arm and blocked it with lightning. The huge force shook through his whole body. His veins went numb, a coppery taste rose in his throat, and he stumbled back half a step.

Wind howled. His eyes were still sharp, but a strain showed in them.

Elijah split a lightning arc to drive back the zombies at his body, and fixed his eyes on the black figure on the roof. His voice was a little hoarse from the fight.

"Do we have history? Why are you wearing a mask?"

"You set this up here, with six Level Seven zombies. Is it just to kill me?"

"I don't get it. We've never met. What grudge could you have that makes you want me dead this badly?"

The masked man tilted his head slightly. The black mask hid every expression. A low, cold, flat, unfamiliar voice came out, each word edged with ice.

"When you're dead, you'll know everything."

His whistle shot higher and sharper!

The six Level Seven zombies surged, their attacks doubling in ferocity!

Claws tore the air, brute force smashed the floor, and poison mist swallowed every breath on the roof!

Elijah frowned hard. His Lightning Affinity was burning through reserves, and his face slowly lost color.

He was at the limit of what a human could reach, peak Level Six, but against the cliff between Level Six and Level Seven, he was still one step short.

Alone against six, he lost ground inch by inch. One slip and he'd be finished.

Suddenly two speed-type Level Seven zombies hit from the left and right blind spots, black claws aimed at his chest and the back of his neck. The power-type zombie smashed in from the front, and the poison mist closed over him completely!

Just as Elijah was about to be crushed,

a cold, resolute figure tore through the snow and landed on the roof!

Paisley's eyes were icy and sharp as frost. She looked at Elijah, trapped by the wall of high-tier zombies and cornered.

A freezing blast of power exploded from her palm. Her clear voice cut across the roof, calm and overwhelming.

"Deep Freeze!"

Hum!

Terrifying Ice-Water Affinity power swept over everything.

The howling wind stopped. Falling snow hung in the air. The corrosive dark green mist froze into fine ice grains and hovered.

White frost spread at breathtaking speed over the whole roof and the broken floor. It crawled through the air and quickly froze the six Level Seven zombies.

A second ago they were raging. Now every one of them stood locked in place!

Their bodies hung in mid-leap, claws stalled mid-swing, their muscles, rage, and corpse energy all sealed in.

Thick, clear layers of ice wrapped them and pinned all six in place, unable to move.

The tide turned in a heartbeat.

Paisley stood amid the freezing cold, slim and straight, her sleeves stirring in the icy wind, the frost around her undimmed.

She gripped the Frostbloom Sword. The clear blade reflected the snow light, its edge bright and cold.

She walked unhurriedly to the frozen Level Seven zombies and cut out their cores, crisp and clean, with no wasted motion.

The point drove through the ice, found the core location in each head, and pulled out the cores.

The hard bodies and thick ice were like paper under her exact control.

In moments, six full, warm Level Seven cores, packed with pure energy, were in her hands.

The frozen zombies went dead and stood stiff in the wind. They weren't a threat anymore.
