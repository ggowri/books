Chapter 362

On the raised platform at the front, several base officials sat in grave silence.

At the center, the base commander's sharp gaze swept over the crowd below, radiating authority.

Once the last few squads filed in and settled, the noise died down to near silence.

Nathaniel Blackwood rose, his deep voice carrying through the speakers across the entire hall.

"I've called this assembly for three reasons: to establish regular training and attendance protocols, to announce a new round of supply-gathering and clearing missions, and to formally re-register every squad's roster!"

At that, the crowd below held its collective breath, tension and anticipation flickering through plenty of faces.

In this world, base assignments decided resource allocation and combat ranking — every adjustment hit close to home for everyone there.

Elijah led Paisley and the others to a spot near the front, standing tall, his uniform only sharpening the imposing air around him.

Several nearby users glanced his way instinctively, every look a mix of awe and wariness.

No one in the whole base didn't know the Blackwood heir.

Even before the apocalypse, he'd been the brightest star in the top circles, the base chief's only son, with status, wealth, and resources beyond anyone's reach, countless stories trailing in his wake.

Since the outbreak, his own raw strength had cemented his reputation further — word of his exploits had already reached the base before he ever got back, making him the kind of untouchable figure most users could only dream of being.

Alongside the awe, curiosity ran even thicker through the crowd.

He'd always kept to himself, rarely traveling with anyone, and now here he was with a whole group of unfamiliar faces.

Especially the girl off to the side — cool, striking, standing quietly in the crowd and somehow impossible to ignore, drawing glances and whispered speculation about who she was.

The platform speeches wrapped up quickly, and squad re-registration began right after.

A staff member carrying an electronic tablet approached, eyes landing first on Elijah, the most commanding presence there, speaking with respect.

"Mr. Blackwood, may I have your squad's name and role assignments?"

******

The whole hall went quiet for a beat, every eye locking onto Elijah's group, curiosity, envy, and speculation tangled together in the air.

Elijah began providing the details.

Squad name: Falcon

Captain: Elijah Blackwood

Vice Captain: Orion Thompson

Members: Paisley Lennox, Spencer Thompson, Thomas Thompson, Ashley Thompson, Mason Coleman, Madison Hughes.

Eight members total.

The staff member confirmed everything was in order and filed it officially — Squad Falcon was now established.

Murmurs broke out across the hall at once.

"Elijah's leading this squad himself?! That lineup must be insane."

"No wonder they've got that kind of presence. With him at the head, Squad Falcon could end up top-tier before long."

"Never seen any of these other faces before. Must be people he handpicked himself — whoever he chooses can't be ordinary."

The chatter kept spreading, every bit of the room's attention swallowed up by Squad Falcon.

Not far off, two faces in another squad had already gone dark.

Chloe Thompson stood with her arm looped through her boyfriend Grant Quinn's, in the ranks of another elite squad, her pretty features drawn tight with resentment and anger.

As the daughter of the Thompson family's junior branch, her squad with Grant had always been the base's most celebrated — top of every mission ranking, the one everyone else looked up to.

But the second Elijah showed up with his squad, every bit of attention and praise got swept away from them.

Years of built-up reputation had just crumbled in an instant.

Chloe's mood soured completely.

On top of that, the Thompson family's senior and junior branches were already at odds, old grudges simmering, the rivalry never quite letting up.

Watching Orion and Ashley, from the senior branch, bask in all this glory alongside Elijah was more than Chloe could stomach, jealousy and resentment boiling over.

Beside her, Grant's expression was just as cold, hostility radiating off him without any attempt to hide it.
