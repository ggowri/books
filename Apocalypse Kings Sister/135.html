Chapter 135

Paisley looked down at the sweet little white cat in her arms, and her fingers stopped for a moment.

After being reborn, all she'd wanted at first was revenge. After that, she'd planned to live only for herself.

Being betrayed in her past life was what had turned her into the cold, distant person she was now.

But with people who truly cared about her, she couldn't bring herself to say no. Deep down, something in her stirred.

In the apocalypse, everyone was fighting just to survive. If there was a chance to break through the darkness and find a way to live, it was worth a shot.

Some things needed someone willing to carry the weight for everyone else.

Even in the apocalypse, Elijah and Orion still believed in doing the right thing. That was why, in her past life, they'd joined forces to build the Epoch Enclave and given humanity a fresh start!

If she could do her part too, she didn't have the heart to say no.

Paisley looked up, all the laziness gone from her cool eyes. Only calm was left. "Okay, I'm in.

"When do we go, and what's the plan? Just let me know when it's time."

When she agreed, the tension in Elijah's shoulders eased, and he could finally breathe easy.

He'd known all along that Paisley had a good heart and saw the bigger picture. She'd never back down just because something was dangerous.

If it weren't for her, who knows how many more close calls we'd have had on the way here? We might not have even made it.

He lowered his voice and quickly went over the plan.

"First, we'll rest for two days. We'll use that time to scope out the area around the aquarium's underground lab, how far the virus has spread, and what kind of mutants are down there.

"Two days from now, we'll move in together with every top ability user in Rivergate."

Then, he gave them another serious warning. "Remember, this stays top secret. Nobody but the three of us can know, not even the rest of our team."

Paisley calmly nodded, her face giving nothing away.

Elijah knew Paisley liked having her own space, so after the meeting, he made a point of getting her a comfortable single room with a private bathroom.

In the apocalypse, that was a pretty rare perk.

Paisley was very happy with it.

But that perk was bound to make some people jealous.

Kelly and Rachel were standing right outside her door, glaring at Paisley with sour, mocking looks.

Kelly sneered first, her voice dripping with snark.

"Must be nice. Some people haven't done a thing, and they still get a private room with their own bathroom in the middle of the apocalypse. What makes her so special?"

Rachel jumped right in. Her jealous eyes were locked on the pendant at Paisley's chest, and her voice was nasty.

"The four of us are crammed into one room, but she gets special treatment? Why? She's an ability user, but aren't we ability users too?

"This is so unfair..."

The two of them fed off each other, taking one dig after another.

They'd all gotten there on the same day. But the two of them were stuck in a cramped, run-down dorm while Paisley got special treatment and all the protection. The huge gap only made them more jealous.

Paisley stopped turning the key in the lock. She slowly walked over to them, her face cold and blank.

She's still eyeing my pendant? It's only been a few hours, and she's already forgotten my warning.

After days of being picked on and pestered over and over, Paisley was out of patience. She wasn't going to waste another word on them.

Before they could keep mocking her and stirring up trouble, she twitched her fingers. Two thin, tough green willow branches shot out of the ground, moving so fast they were just a blur.

Two loud smacks rang out at the same time. The springy willow branches hit both of them right across the face, hard, one slap each.

It was quick and clean, with no warning and no mercy.

Kelly and Rachel never saw it coming. Their heads snapped to the side, their cheeks burned bright red, their ears rang, and their minds went blank.

They'd never imagined that Paisley would actually hit them in front of everyone.

It was a total humiliation! Shame and rage flooded through both of them at once.

Kelly's chest heaved. Her pretty face twisted and flushed red, and her eyes blazed with fury and jealousy.

She was the pampered eldest daughter of the Walker family and had grown up spoiled rotten in Aetheris. Everyone had always fawned over her, and she was always the one doing the bullying. When had she ever been humiliated like this?
