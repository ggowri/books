Seating here was strictly stratified, the hierarchy obvious at a glance.

Ordinary team members and civilians without standing or combat records couldn't even get through the door.

Anyone with a little standing was packed into the back corners, their view blocked, their presence ignored.

The front three rows were reserved exclusively for the powerful — base leadership, and the heads of top families.
