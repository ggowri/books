Chapter 127

"Brace yourselves! Everyone, stay sharp!"

Elijah reacted in a split second. Level Five Lightning crackled to life around him in violet-blue arcs, and he grabbed the railing to steady himself as he barked out the order.

Everyone here had survived brushes with death before. They tensed up, ready to fight, and kept their eyes locked on the churning water.

Water sprayed everywhere as several gigantic black tentacles burst out of the river. Each one was as thick as a barrel and covered in rough suckers. They dripped with slime and reeked of rotten river water, and they whipped through the air and slammed down at the deck!

The air cracked wherever the tentacles struck. They hit so hard that one blow could break every bone in a person's body.

"It's a Level Five Water Affinity mutant sea beast! Everyone, be careful!" Paisley's face turned grim as she shouted the warning.

Who knows how many more of these things are hiding down there?

There was no time to think. Energy surged at her fingertips.

With a sharp whoosh, countless thick, tough vines covered in tiny barbs shot out of her hands. The dark green mass swept down toward the beast under the water!

Every vine was as hard as iron and coated with a mild paralytic venom. Their razor-sharp tips locked onto the tentacles below.

The Level Five beast hadn't seen the attack coming, and it flew into a rage.

The thick tentacles that had been sweeping across the deck lashed out at the vines!

Boom after boom thundered across the river as they collided, and spray and mist exploded into the air.

The brute force shook the whole river, but Paisley's vines kept piling on. They wrapped tighter and tighter, closing in fast with incredible binding power.

In moments, the giant beast thrashing below was tied up tight in a dense web of vines. Its huge body was pinned in the water, and it couldn't move.

Everyone had barely started to relax when they saw the trapped beast still writhing like crazy. It kept tearing at the vines with brute strength, and the paralytic venom wasn't affecting it at all.

"The paralytic venom isn't working on it!" someone shouted.

Paisley frowned. She'd spotted the problem right away.

High-level Water mutant beasts lived their whole lives in toxic water and soaked up foul mist, so they'd built up an incredible tolerance.

Normal poisons and paralytics did nothing to them. If this dragged on, the beast would rip the vines apart with sheer force sooner or later.

Orion and the others jumped into the fight too, but fire, earth, and metal blades couldn't hurt it at all.

"Elijah, try lightning!" Paisley shouted.

Elijah didn't need to be told twice. He unleashed his Level Five Lightning Affinity!

With a sizzling crackle, a storm of violet-blue lightning ripped through the mist and struck the water dead-on. Wave after wave of electricity shot through the river and hammered the beast's trapped body!

Lightning was a natural counter to Water, and the brutal current tore into the beast's flesh. It threw off the flow of the beast's energy and burned it from the inside.

Within minutes, the thick smell of burnt flesh hung over the water. The beast twitched and thrashed, letting out low, pained roars. Its massive body went limp, and it fought back less and less.

Paisley took her chance while the beast couldn't fight back. She pushed off the deck, landed steadily on the taut vines, and used them to spring onto the top of the beast's massive body.

She formed a blade of energy at her fingertips and sliced through the beast's tough hide. Then, she reached right into its core and pulled out a glistening, deep blue Level Five Water crystal core.

Trovie's voice rang out in her head. [Master, you're amazing!]

Inside her Storage Dimension, the little white cat let out an excited yowl. [Master, I want it!]

Paisley tossed the core straight into her dimension for the cat.

Then, Trovie piped up again. [Master, every part of this Level Five mutant sea beast is valuable! Its body, hide, and bones can all be traded in, for a total of 1,000 System points!]

With a thought, Paisley sent the beast's whole body into her Storage Dimension too.

Trovie reported back. [Conversion complete! Total points: 102,060.]

Well, that's a nice little bonus.

It all happened so fast that nobody noticed what Paisley had just done.

When everyone on deck saw that the mutant sea beast was dead, they let out a sigh of relief and broke into applause for Paisley.
