Chapter 193

Paisley had already promised Elijah she'd go to the aquarium with them to finish the mission.

It was already settled anyway. She hadn't expected one trip to get her 10,000 free base contribution points on top of system points and a lucky draw. What a sweet deal.

She agreed right away. "Okay, I'll join the mission."

The head of the base was finally able to relax, and he was overjoyed. He made the transfer on the spot.

"Thank you, Ms. Lennox! I'll send you the 10,000 contribution points right now! With you on board, everyone on this mission will be as safe as they can possibly be!"

With that settled, the two of them headed back to the ward.

The moment Paisley walked back in, everyone gathered around her.

Ashley got to her first and grabbed her hand, looking worried. "Paisley, is everything okay?"

Orion, Elijah, and the others were watching her with concern too, and they only relaxed once they saw she was fine.

Since they'd all fully recovered, they didn't need to stay for observation. After a quick word with the medical staff, they filled out their discharge paperwork and got ready to leave.

Just as they were ready to go, the attending doctor, who'd been standing off to the side the whole time, hurried over to Paisley. He looked at her with obvious admiration and eagerness and spoke sincerely.

"Excuse me, would it be okay if I added you as a contact?"

He'd been a doctor for years, so he knew better than anyone how bad those five had been yesterday, and how incredible Paisley's abilities were.

He looked up to Paisley so much, and he really wanted to get to know her and become friends.

That way, if he ever ran into a case he couldn't handle, he could ask her for advice.

But after that perfectly normal request, the mood in the room shifted right away.

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, Elijah, and Marcus all froze at the same moment, and a very subtle, telling look crossed their faces.

They looked at the doctor, calm on the surface, but a faint, invisible pressure quietly filled the air.

The air went quiet, just for half a second.

The doctor had no idea what was going on around him. His eyes were bright with respect and eagerness as he waited for Paisley's answer.

To him, this mysterious Affinity user who'd quietly saved the base's top fighters was a true master healer, way beyond every other Plant Affinity user in the base.

He just wanted a chance to learn from her.

While everyone held their breath, Paisley gave a small nod. "Sure," she said casually.

He was sincere and didn't mean any harm, so there was no reason to say no.

Besides, he was a doctor who saved lives.

The doctor's face lit up, and he couldn't hide how happy and excited he was. He quickly pulled out his terminal and hurried over to add Paisley as a contact.

When her profile popped up on his contact list, he was thrilled to finally get what he wanted, and he thanked her over and over.

"Thank you so much! If I ever run into a case I can't handle, I hope you'll help me out. I'll pay you, of course."

He had a Plant Affinity too, and he was the Plant Affinity healer the base had invested the most in, with every resource thrown his way. Even so, he was only at early Level Four.

Paisley was so young, yet he couldn't even begin to gauge how strong she was.

One thing he knew for certain: she was leagues above him.

When Orion and the others saw Paisley add some guy she barely knew just like that, they went quiet, and the mood around them visibly turned sour and gloomy.

Elijah was the first to go still.

The warm, polite smile on his face faded. His lips flattened, and the warmth in his eyes gave way to a cool distance.

He kept up his calm, polite front and stood perfectly straight, but when he looked at the doctor, his eyes held a quiet, chilly scrutiny.

He sighed to himself. Paisley was too softhearted and too nice to ever turn anyone down.

That won't do. What if he's a creep?

Any stranger who came near her with an agenda was bad news, and part of him wanted to rip that doctor to shreds.

I don't even have her contact yet. Who does he think he is?

The more he thought about it, the angrier he got.

The three Thompson brothers' sulking was even more obvious, and they didn't bother hiding it.
