Chapter 133

Paisley's sudden show of power rattled the Lennox brothers, and they stepped back without thinking. Then, their humiliation turned to anger.

Owen's face twisted, but he still talked down to her like he was doing her a favor.

"Paisley, how dare you? Aren't you scared we'll cut you off for good?

"You'd better think this through. We're the only family you've got.

"Hand everything over right now, and we'll forgive you and let you come back. Things can go back to the way they were, and we'll take you home with us."

Paisley was running out of patience.

It's like talking to a brick wall.

Her expression turned colder and colder. Just as she was about to make a move, quick, steady footsteps came from nearby.

Orion, Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley strode up, their faces ice-cold, and the air seemed to chill around them.

They went straight to Paisley and stepped in front of her, forming a solid wall between her and the Lennoxes.

Orion's eyes were sharp and cold as he looked the four of them over. Fury rolled off him, and his voice was dead serious.

"Paisley is the Thompsons' treasured daughter. She's my sister, and she's the apple of our whole family's eye.

"And who the hell do you think you are?

"Some small-time family from Southspire thinks it can push her around and scheme against her over and over? What makes the Lennoxes so special?"

His voice dripped with contempt, and he didn't hold back at all.

"If you know what's good for you, get the hell out of here right now.

"Since you raised her once, we'll let the past go and leave you alone today. But if you ever mouth off again, start trouble, or mess with Pais, don't say we didn't warn you!"

The moment he finished, a cold wave of Metal energy quietly spread out. The pressure of a Level Five ability user hit them head-on, an open threat and a clear warning.

Behind him, Thomas, Spencer, and Ashley's faces darkened too. Their abilities were charged and ready, and without a word, they made it clear they'd protect their own.

The Lennox brothers had been so cocky and pushy a moment ago. Now, their faces went white, and they looked absolutely sick.

Just moments before, they'd been throwing their weight around as her older brothers, pushing Paisley and demanding things. All that swagger was gone now.

Under the four Thompsons' fierce protectiveness and high-level pressure, they were almost afraid to breathe. They were scared and panicking, and they looked pathetic.

Paisley had actually found her real family.

And her family was the Thompsons, one of the most famous old-money families in Aetheris.

Everything they'd just said suddenly sounded ridiculous.

Paisley wasn't some unwanted orphan anymore.

She had people backing her now, and she was never coming back to the Lennox family.

Pale-faced, the three brothers watched Paisley, waiting to see what she'd say.

Would she speak up and argue with what they'd just said? Would she protect them like she always used to, without a second thought?

But Paisley didn't say a word. She didn't even look at them.

Their chests felt hollow and heavy, like they'd lost something precious forever.

Next to them, Rachel clenched her fists. The cut on her hand throbbed, her face was gray and ugly, and she was burning with jealousy, resentment, and humiliation.

She'd never dreamed that Paisley would stop being that pathetic loser anyone could push around.

In this life, Paisley had actually found her family.

And not just any family. The Thompsons were famous and powerful in Aetheris, the kind of backing most people could only dream of.

With the whole Thompson family protecting her, a Plant Affinity awakened, and maybe even the pendant's space figured out, Paisley had broken free of the Lennoxes long ago. Rachel couldn't push her around or scheme against her anymore.

What makes her so damn lucky? It's not fair!

The three brothers had mixed feelings too. All their scheming had gotten them nowhere.

They'd still hoped Paisley might remember the old days, soften up, and come back to them. But she'd ignored them the whole time and wouldn't even spare them a glance.

The sister who used to come running whenever they called, who always gave in and went along with whatever they said, was gone. They'd thrown her away themselves, and they'd never get her back.

A huge sense of loss swallowed their anger, but the greed in their bones hadn't gone anywhere.

Paisley didn't want to waste her energy figuring out what they were thinking. She was just sick of them.

She shifted the little white cat in her arms and said coldly, "I don't want to see them anymore. Let's go."
