Chapter 367

Both teams collected their gear in orderly fashion, checking their equipment and shouldering their packs with brisk efficiency.

The Scarlet Squad members packed up while throwing sidelong glances at Falcon Squad now and then, wariness and disdain mixed in their eyes.

Falcon Squad, in contrast, stayed calm and unhurried.

Everyone checked their weapons and ammunition with practiced ease, cross-referencing their supplies in smooth, orderly fashion, no trace of first-mission jitters, every move in perfect sync.

Once supplies were collected, the group moved to the base's dedicated vehicle lot.

Two matte-black armored vehicles sat parked at the center of the lot, their heavy, hard-edged frames a world apart from an ordinary SUV.

The entire vehicle was forged from specially reinforced alloy plating, blast-resistant, impact-resistant, tear-resistant, built to the absolute max. The shell was tough enough that an ordinary zombie's claws or a common mutant beast's bite couldn't so much as scratch it. Even a stray round would barely leave a mark.

Inside, the cabin was spacious, custom-fitted with ten seats, enough to comfortably hold an entire squad, with room left over for mission loot and rescue supplies, built for high-risk fieldwork, search-and-rescue, and resource gathering alike.

"Get in."

Elijah's voice was calm and cool. He pulled the door open first, stepping aside to wave the others in.

Orion took the front passenger seat.

Paisley climbed in right after him, followed by Ashley, Spencer, and Thomas, one by one.

Mason drove. The engine started.

Next to them, the Scarlet Squad boarded their own armored vehicle.

The window was half rolled down, and Grant's hard, arrogant profile was clearly visible. He tapped his knee, eyes brimming with smug certainty, a cold, dismissive curl at the corner of his mouth.

Chloe sat in the front passenger seat, turning back to murmur instructions to her teammates, every word dripping with absolute confidence in their coming victory.

A second later, both vehicles' engines roared to life almost in unison.

The deep, powerful growl of the engines filled the parking lot. The heavy vehicles pulled out smoothly, tires rolling over the pavement with an unstoppable momentum, driving out through the base gates one after the other.

Ahead lay the ruined, abandoned city, a treacherous dead zone crawling with zombies and mutant beasts, home to a Level Seven mutant trumpet flower.

Inside Falcon Squad's vehicle, the mood was relaxed and warm.

Ashley leaned against Paisley, cheeks puffed out, grumbling under her breath with righteous indignation.

"Chloe's always had it out for me, always picking on me. So of course I've never liked her either."

She pouted, eyes full of distaste.

"This is the first time I've ever met someone this unlikable."

Then she turned, pressing close to Paisley, her tone suddenly serious and earnest.

"Pais, stay away from her from now on. Chloe's calculating and vicious under all that. She's petty, and she holds grudges forever."

Paisley had a sweet lollipop tucked in her cheek. She tapped her fingers lazily and nodded along, her voice soft and easy.

"Whatever you say."

Seeing her sister so agreeable, Ashley's temper evaporated at once. She switched straight into pleading mode, eyes curving into a smile as she nuzzled against Paisley's arm.

"Pais, I wore myself out arguing with them just now. Don't I deserve some ice cream as a reward?"

Paisley's eyes softened, the corner of her mouth lifting. A thought crossed her mind, and a faint light flickered at her fingertips. An instant later, a cold, sweet scoop of cream ice cream appeared in her palm, held out to Ashley.

Ashley's eyes lit up instantly. Beaming, she took it, leaning in to plant a kiss on Paisley's cheek, her voice soft and adoring.

"Pais, you're amazing! You actually made ice cream appear out of nowhere!"

"Wow, my sister can do literally anything!"

Up front, Spencer and Thomas turned around at that, staring at the ice cream in Ashley's hand, swallowing hard, eyes practically begging.

Spencer spoke first, his tone carrying a hint of hope.

"Pais, I want an iced cola."

Thomas chimed in right after, equally hopeful.

"I want some spicy jerky."

Paisley didn't hold back at all. In an instant, a whole spread of snacks and drinks appeared in her hands, neatly distributed to every teammate in the car.

Last time, she'd traded supplies with a businesswoman from the modern timeline and stockpiled a massive stash of snacks, drinks, and rare goods, more than enough for her and everyone around her to enjoy for a long time.

In no time, the inside of Falcon Squad's vehicle filled with easy laughter and chatter. Everyone snacked and talked, completely relaxed, as if the wager they'd just made hadn't affected them at all.
