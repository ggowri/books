Chapter 468

Only the faint pallor on her forehead remained, the mark of overspent power. It hurt to look at.

Elijah's arms held her steady, one under her knees and one at her back. His chest was warm and sure. He carried her slowly toward the hotel.

The night breeze brushed the long hallway. Around them the others went about their rest, and the dark felt quiet and gentle.

The moment he stepped through the hotel doors, a bright, bouncy mechanical voice rang through Paisley's foggy mind.

[Master! High-density energy detected nearby! Large amounts of treasure and rare resources buried close by!]

The sudden notice dragged Paisley awake against her will.

Her lashes fluttered. She dragged her heavy eyelids open.

Through the haze she saw no car roof. She saw the sharp, tense line of Elijah's jaw. The clean cold-pine scent of him filled her nose, steady and safe.

A second later it hit her. He's carrying me.

Her mind went blank. Sleep vanished. Mortification flooded in.

Oh no.

Elijah is carrying me!!!

When did he start? Why don't I remember any of this?

The calm, reserved, keep-your-distance Captain Blackwood, since when is he this easy to deal with?

He picked me up himself, and he carried me all the way to the hotel?

She glanced around. Familiar faces everywhere.

Especially Ashley, who was trying very hard not to laugh.

Embarrassment crashed over her. Her whole body went stiff and she didn't know where to put her hands.

"Awake?"

Elijah's low voice came from above her head. The cold edge of the battlefield was gone. It was calm and warm and so gentle it nearly drowned her.

Her cheeks burned. She squirmed instinctively, trying to get down and stand on her own. "I... I can walk..."

"Don't move."

His arms tightened a little, steady and gentle, impossible to shake off. His voice stayed low and soft. "You overspent your power. You haven't recovered. We're almost at the room. Just lean back."

His arms were warm and solid. His voice sank in at her ear and settled something in her.

She stopped fighting and stayed quiet in his arms.

But her ears and the back of her neck turned pink, a flush spreading down into her throat. She couldn't hide how flustered she was.

Elijah caught every tiny reaction. The light in his eyes grew softer. He climbed the stairs without slowing and carried her up to a third-floor room.

He pushed open the clean, tidy door and walked in. Bending slowly, he laid Paisley down on the soft bed with great care.

Once she was settled, he straightened and looked down at her flushed face and shy eyes.

"Hungry? What do you want to eat? I've got ration bars, hot milk, and snacks."

"Anything else hurt? Are you feeling less drained?"

Every question was full of care.

But Paisley's mind was on Trovie's alert. There's a lot of treasure nearby. Her heart was already full of it. She had no interest in eating or resting.

Trovie's scans never missed. If it said there was treasure, then this hotel was packed with it.

She answered quickly.

"No, thanks. I'm not hungry. I just want to rest."

"Eli, you can go. If I need anything, I'll find you right away."

She looked up, a faint warmth in her eyes, and added softly, "Thank you, Eli."

That soft "Eli" landed in his ear.

His eyes deepened. Something in his chest eased. He tucked the blanket around her, careful with every touch.

"Rest well. I'll close the door for you. I'm just downstairs on the first floor, and someone's next door. Call me anytime."

He gave a last word of care, then stepped out quietly and closed the door behind him.

The room fell completely still.
