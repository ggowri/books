Chapter 495

Paisley tightened her scarf and walked steadily toward the mission hall.

******

She spotted a slight figure at the entrance from far away. Ashley was blowing on her cupped hands and rising onto her toes to look down the road, a thin layer of snow on her shoulders.

"Paisley!"

Ashley spotted her and jogged over, grinning, her nose bright red.

"Have you been waiting long?" Paisley slowed.

"Not too long. The wind is just brutal." Ashley rubbed her stiff hands together and hooked an arm through Paisley's. "Come on, let's go in. It's way warmer inside."

They walked in together.

The mission hall was noisy, the air thick with burning coal. Plenty of Affinity users were crowded around the huge mission board, talking quietly.

With the sudden cold, most short-range collection missions had been bumped up to higher danger ratings. The simple ones had been snatched up. What was left mostly meant going deep into dangerous areas outside the city.

A giant screen on the wall kept scrolling and refreshing.

[Mission: Collect warm clothing, blankets, space heaters, and other supplies from the mall outside the city. Danger rating: Level Six. A small number of mid-tier zombies and mutant beasts in the area. Rewards: 5,000 contribution points, 500 Level Three crystal cores.]

Ashley's gaze landed on it, and she turned to Paisley. "How about this one? It's not too far. We could be back today."

Paisley nodded. Trovie's side quest was about the base's shortage of warm supplies, so this mission could advance it too.

"Let's take it."

They went to the registration counter. The clerk looked up, saw Paisley, and his eyes lit up. He was suddenly warm and eager.

"Ms. Lennox, here to take a mission?"

"Yes. We'll take this Level Six."

He quickly pulled up the file, checked their IDs, logged Falcon Squad, and printed two hard mission cards. He handed them over with a worried frown.

"It's brutal out there, and the temperature keeps dropping. That mall is full of mid-tier zombies and cold-resistant mutant beasts. In this cold they get extra vicious, so be really careful."

"Thanks for the warning."

Ashley took the cards and lifted her chin, confident. "Don't worry. I'll protect my sister."

The clerk blinked and looked them over. "Just the two of you? This is a Level Six high-risk mission. It's not a small risk. Maybe team up with someone else? The more people, the safer."

Paisley was about to decline when a bright male voice cut in from beside her.

Jasper Quinn bounded over, eyes fixed on Paisley, open admiration all over his face. His grin was delighted.

"Paisley! What a coincidence, I can't believe I ran into you here!"

"Are you going out on a mission? We've got one too. Could we team up with you?"

Two teammates trailed behind him. When they saw Paisley they could barely contain themselves. They held their breath, wide-eyed and reverent.

Across the base, Paisley's name was known to everyone. Countless Affinity users saw her as a role model.

Just getting to talk to her, let alone go on a mission together, felt like an honor.

Paisley remembered how Jasper had stood up for her against Chloe not long ago. She owed him for that.

"Sure. We'll team up."

Jasper and his two friends lit up and grinned wide, thanking her over and over.

"Awesome! Thank you so much, Paisley! We won't slow you down!"

The clerk, relieved to see a team of five, went over field safety once more. He gave the group base-issue emergency rations, hand warmers, and flares.

They packed their gear, checked the mission cards, and walked out of the hall.

Howling snow whipped around them. The wind drove ice pellets into their faces. They pulled their winter gear tighter and headed for the parking lot, bound for the mall on the edge of the city to collect warm supplies.
