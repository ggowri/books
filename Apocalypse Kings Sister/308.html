Chapter 308

"Consider it allowance money from your big brother. No need to be polite about it. Buy whatever you want."

Ashley nodded along, enthusiastic.

"Just take it, Paisley! Elijah's loaded, this is nothing to him!"

"I've only got about a thousand points, so I can't transfer much — but I'm paying for whatever we buy this afternoon! Anything you want, it's on me!"

Warmth wrapped around every corner of Paisley's chest, driving out whatever darkness had been living there, something bittersweet and overwhelming spreading through her.

A small, helpless smile tugged at the corner of her mouth. She finally stopped pushing back against all this generosity.

"Alright. Nothing better to do this afternoon anyway. Let's go shopping."

Kelly watched how good Elijah was to Paisley and felt the old sting of jealousy flare up again.

But she'd seen what Paisley could actually do. That reality had settled in by now.

This was the apocalypse. She didn't have the luxury of being petty anymore.

If she wanted to keep breathing, crossing Paisley wasn't an option.

After lunch, Ashley took Paisley's arm and led her out of the villa to go shopping, Madison and Kelly trailing close behind.

The base's main street was clean, wide, and orderly. Shops lined both sides in neat rows, streetlights standing at even intervals, the pavement swept spotless, no trace of the ruin outside.

People moved up and down the street, busy but unhurried, none of the usual panic or numbness that hung over everywhere else.

The shops carried a surprising range — Affinity potion dealers, crystal core exchanges, grocers, craft stalls, specialty shops, even jewelry carts and tailors. The whole street hummed with life.

Vendors called out their wares, shoppers haggled, people chatted on the sidewalk — loud, but not chaotic, a rare kind of normal that the end of the world almost never allowed.

Ashley practically skipped along, tugging Paisley from stall to stall, eyes darting everywhere.

"Paisley, look! They've got dried fruit and cured meats here! This place has way more than Rivergate ever did!"

She dragged Paisley from one stall to the next, pointing out every little curiosity, chattering nonstop.

Madison browsed the specialty shops, picking out things that would keep well for the trip home as gifts for her family.

Kelly trailed behind them, pausing here and there to take in the sights, relaxed for once.

Paisley let herself be pulled along, a cool breeze brushing her hair, the sound of voices and street noise wrapping around her.

She hadn't felt this at ease since coming back.

The little fox curled against her chest, poking its head out now and then to watch the street before tucking itself back in, warm and content.

Ashley looked up at her, grinning.

"Pick something, Paisley! You never get to shop, go wild — I'm buying!"

Paisley's eyes crinkled with quiet amusement.

Her storage was already overflowing — food, potions, gear, she had everything she needed and wanted little else.

But watching the girl's eager face, she couldn't bring herself to dampen the mood.

Two lifetimes of being alone, and nobody had ever treated her like this before.

The Thompsons' love, unguarded and total, deserved something in return.

Might as well pick up a few gifts while she was out — a small gesture, but a real one.

"Let's check that place."

Paisley walked toward a sleek, understated storefront with a sign that simply read Fine Goods.

Inside, everything was arranged with care — none of the clutter of the usual supply shops. Display cases held designer watches, elegant cufflinks, delicate accessories. The kind of fine, considered pieces that barely existed anymore.

Paisley moved slowly through the shop, examining each piece, searching for something that would suit each of them.

Ashley trailed along beside her, and when she caught sight of the price tags, her jaw dropped. She leaned in and whispered.

"Paisley, this stuff's expensive. I don't think I have enough to cover any of this."

Paisley smiled, glancing over.
