Chapter 376

No one had expected this young, newly formed squad to pull off a Level Eight mission at this difficulty with zero casualties, flawlessly.

Mission reward: ten thousand contribution points per member, and a hundred Level Four crystal cores.

Promotion to elite squad status, with a dedicated training room and the freedom to choose their own missions going forward.

Within minutes, the news spread through the base's public display boards and internal communication channels, racing across all of Aetheris Base.

The entire base erupted.

Everyone was talking about this dark-horse squad that had come out of nowhere, debating how they'd managed to pull off a Level Eight mission so cleanly.

Falcon Squad cemented its legend in a single battle, making a name for itself overnight, rising to become the base's acknowledged elite squad, its status soaring past every veteran team.

And Paisley, the most dazzling, reality-shattering figure of the entire battle, became an overnight sensation.

A Level Six fighter, taking down a Level Seven mutant flower in a single strike, without even using her Affinity, dominating the entire fight with ease. It wasn't hard to imagine just how terrifyingly strong she really was.

By now, there wasn't a single person at the base who didn't know Paisley's name.

Every bit of the old gossip, the ridicule, the mockery, the whispers that she was nothing but a pretty face riding on someone else's coattails, vanished completely, replaced by endless awe, admiration, and reverence.

She'd outshone everyone, even eclipsing Falcon Squad's own captain, Elijah Blackwood.

Everyone understood now: it wasn't that Falcon Squad was riding on someone's coattails, it was that Falcon Squad was lucky enough to have a top-tier powerhouse like her, hiding in plain sight, among them.

After this battle, the entire power structure of the base's younger generation had been rewritten. Paisley had become the undisputed number one of the new generation, a figure countless Affinity users could only look up to, forever out of reach.

The Scarlet Squad, by contrast, met a grim end.

The wager had been made publicly, in black and white, lose, and the team disbands on the spot.

Every member except Ashley was unconscious, their bodies still wracked with the trumpet flower's lingering toxin, carried off to the base's medical ward for emergency treatment.

The once-glorious Scarlet Squad was officially disbanded.

From that day forward, the base's flagship squad roster no longer included the name Scarlet.

This squad had once brought the Quinn family enormous glory and military honor. Its collapse effectively severed the Quinn family's most important source of influence among the base's younger Affinity users.

When the news reached the upper echelons of the base, the reactions varied, but the Quinn faction's faces went dark as pitch. Losing such a critical asset left them suddenly at a disadvantage in the base's ongoing power struggles.

Standing outside the medical ward, Chloe watched the medical staff rush her former teammates inside, feeling not a shred of sympathy, only cold, calculating assessment.

The Scarlet Squad had collapsed. Grant had lost an arm. This team could no longer offer her any glory or resources.

The halo of favor and attention she'd once enjoyed was crumbling along with the squad's fall.

She clenched her fists, calculating silently. She needed to find a new patron, fast, she couldn't go down with a sinking ship.

Tucking away every trace of cold calculation, Chloe put on a show of fragile vulnerability and turned to head home alone.

It was a standalone villa in a prime location, the kind of place, in a resource-starved, overcrowded apocalypse base, that most people could only dream of.

She'd been able to live there, admired and envied by everyone, for two reasons.

First, she'd attached herself to Grant, a core member of the base's flagship Scarlet Squad, with all the privilege that came with it.

Second, she was a Level Five Plant Healing Affinity user, a rare, precious talent that every faction at the base was eager to court.

But now, with the flagship squad disbanded and Grant reduced to a cripple, most of what she'd once taken pride in had evaporated.

Level Five Plant Affinity alone wasn't enough to earn her this kind of life.

The thought of what was coming made her scalp prickle.

And especially, she had parents who loved nothing more than status and showing off.

She pushed open the villa's front door. The house, once bright and tidy, looked harsh and unbearable to her now.

Her mother, Brenda Thompson, was lounging in front of the TV. Seeing her daughter slouched and defeated, nothing like her usual flashy self, Brenda hurried over, grabbing her hand with concern.

"Chloe, honey, what happened? Did the mission go badly? Did someone bully you?"

Used to a lifetime of admiration and preferential treatment, Chloe, facing this sudden fall from grace, said nothing at all.
