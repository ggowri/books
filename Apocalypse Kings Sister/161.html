Chapter 161

Paisley took the full computer setups, gaming accessories, and office tech next, sorting them all neatly. The walkie-talkies, night-vision goggles, drones, and solar chargers went in too, along with every other piece of must-have tech for the apocalypse.

She moved quickly and smoothly. Everything she laid eyes on vanished, and the huge third floor was cleaned out in a matter of minutes.

Trovie's little wings fluttered nonstop as it watched, dazzled and excited.

[Master, you're such a good hoarder! Look at all these electronics! Can I have a phone too?]

Paisley agreed. [Sure. You can pick one for yourself.]

Trovie spun happily through the air, its little wings flapping. [Thank you, Master! You're the best!]

Paisley smiled faintly and was about to head upstairs when, out of the corner of her eye, she spotted a whole appliance section nobody had touched.

This section had a full range of products, with big appliances, small appliances, and electric scooters all neatly lined up and in perfect condition.

There were huge smart TVs, frost-free double-door fridges, energy-efficient standing AC units, and quiet electric fans. In the corner sat several brand-new electric scooters, fully charged and without a scratch on them.

These were all things people would need later on. Once the base's living areas were set up, the TVs would be great for relaxing, the fridges would keep food fresh, and the AC units and fans would make the hot and cold seasons bearable. The scooters would be perfect for short field trips and getting around the base.

It was better to take it all than leave it here to get wrecked.

Her Storage Dimension had half a million square feet of warehouse space anyway, so there was plenty of room left.

Paisley's eyes lit up, and she didn't hesitate for a second.

Every smart TV, brand-name fridge, and standing AC unit vanished, neatly stored in the living-supplies section of her Storage Dimension. The quiet fans, small household appliances, and spare parts followed without missing a single one. Finally, she stored the brand-new electric scooters, along with their chargers and repair tools.

In the blink of an eye, the huge appliance section was cleaned out.

Paisley didn't linger. Eager to see the treasure on the fourth floor, she said softly, "Upstairs."

She and the little white cat headed up to the fourth floor.

The moment they got there, dazzling golden light flooded over them, chasing away the floor's gloom and chill.

The entire fourth floor was a luxury section. Like the floors below, it was untouched, with no zombies anywhere. The werewolf must have kept them in line and claimed this place as its own private vault.

Rows of sparkling glass counters were filled with dazzling treasures that glittered under the light, the height of luxury.

Solid gold bars were stacked high in special safe counters, every one of them pure and heavy.

The cases beside them held gold bangles, chunky gold chains, filigree rings, and vintage-style pendants. Every piece was finely crafted, heavy, and eye-catching.

The jewelry counters next to them were even more stunning, with gemstones of every color sparkling under the lights.

There were clear, brilliant white diamonds, soft pink diamonds, and rare deep-blue diamonds set in platinum. They were all perfectly cut and threw sparks of light in every direction.

Rubies, sapphires, emerald pendants, and pearl bracelets were sorted by type, and every piece was a rare, top-tier luxury item.

Even after being shut away since the apocalypse began, all this gold and jewelry still shone brilliantly.

Paisley couldn't help being impressed.

A trading city really is loaded. The gold and gems here are way bigger than anything I saw back in Southspire.

Trovie's eyes went wide, and its tiny body froze in midair. Then, it shot around the whole floor like crazy, its little wings flapping so fast they blurred, practically drooling over all the gold and jewels.

[Whoa... gold, so much gold! Diamonds and jewelry, too! It's all treasure!

[Master! We're rich! This whole floor is solid currency!]

It was so excited it could barely talk straight. It circled the counters over and over, dying to scoop every treasure into its arms, its greedy little nature on full display.

Paisley stood still. For once, the usual coolness faded from her face, and her eyes lit up with a real smile. She stared at the cases full of gold and jewels, not even trying to hide how much she loved them.
