Chapter 161

Take it!

Computers, gaming peripherals, office tech — sorted and stored.

Take it!

Walkie-talkies, night-vision gear, drones, solar chargers — every piece of field-ready tech, gone.

She moved through it fast, and the third floor emptied just as quickly as the ones before.

Trovie watched, dazzled, wings fluttering.

[Host is a hoarding legend! So much electronics! Can I get a phone too?]

Paisley:

[Sure. Pick one.]

Trovie spun through the air, giddy.

[Thank you, Host! You're the best!]

Paisley's mouth curved, and she was about to head up when something caught her eye off to the side — a full appliance section, untouched.

Everything here was intact — big appliances, small ones, mobility scooters, all lined up neatly.

Oversized smart TVs, frost-free double-door fridges, energy-efficient AC units, quiet fans. In the corner, a row of brand-new electric scooters, fully charged, not a scratch on them.

All of it would matter later. Once the base's living quarters were set up — TV for downtime, a fridge to keep food fresh, AC and fans for the seasons, scooters for short runs around the base.

Better to take it than let it rot here.

******

She had fifty thousand square feet of space. Plenty of room left.

A flicker crossed her eyes. No hesitation.

Take it!

Every smart TV, every fridge, every AC unit — stored in her living-supplies section.

Take it!

Fans, small appliances, spare parts — all gone.

Take it!

The electric scooters, chargers, repair tools — all of it, stored.

In moments, the appliance section sat completely empty.

Paisley didn't linger, already thinking ahead to the fourth floor.

"Upstairs."

She and Kitty climbed to the fourth floor.

The second they arrived, a wash of gold light hit them, chasing off the floor's gloom.

The whole luxury section was untouched, same as the floors below — no sign of zombies, like the wolf-man had claimed this place as its personal vault.

Row after row of glass counters gleamed, packed with treasure, catching the light.

Solid gold bars sat stacked in display cases, heavy, pure, packed tight.

Nearby, gold bracelets, dragon-and-phoenix necklaces, filigree rings, antique pendants — fine craftsmanship, every piece substantial.

The jewelry counter beyond was even more stunning, gemstones catching fire under the light.

White diamonds, blush-pink diamonds, deep blue diamonds set in platinum, cut to perfection, throwing light in every direction.

Rubies, sapphires, jade pendants, pearl strings — sorted by type, every piece top-tier.

Even buried under years of apocalypse dust, the gold and gems still shone like new.

Guess a trading city really does have money. These stones were bigger than anything she'd seen back in Southspire.

Trovie's eyes went wide, its little body freezing mid-air before it shot off in a frantic loop around the floor, wings a blur, practically drooling at the sight of so much gold.

[Whoa... gold! So much gold! Diamonds! Jewelry! It's all treasure!]

[Host! We're rich! This whole floor is pure currency!]

It babbled, circling the counters, wanting to hug every single piece at once, greed fully on display.

Paisley stood still, and for once, the usual distance in her expression gave way to something brighter — her eyes locked on the gold and gems, unashamed of how much she wanted it.
