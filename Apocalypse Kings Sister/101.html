Chapter 101

Spencer, Thomas, and the rest of the team pushed their abilities to the limit and fought with everything they had, using their hard-earned battle experience to clear out the zombies around them.

But the horde kept coming, wave after wave, and it never seemed to end. Everyone's stamina and energy were draining fast. Every strike got harder, and holes started opening up in their defense.

Paisley, out in front of everyone, was a whole different story. She looked cold, sharp, and merciless.

Quick as a shadow, she pushed off the ground and shot straight into the middle of the surging horde.

Sword in hand, she faced thousands of charging zombies. She stood tall and calm, without a shred of panic.

She didn't use her ability at all, not even a little.

With nothing but her body, honed to its absolute peak, and the Frostbloom Sword that had already bonded with her, she started a one-sided slaughter.

Her strikes were fast, precise, and brutal. The blade's pale, cold light left thin trails of frost in the air, each one a streak of icy blue.

Every cut landed exactly where she wanted, and every swing was deadly. She didn't waste a single move.

The second a zombie stepped into her range, the Frostbloom Sword struck.

Tough high-level zombies and lightning-fast mutant ones alike were helpless against this top-tier weapon.

Its razor-sharp edge sliced through rotting skin and shattered thick bone with ease. Every swing ended the same way, with a clean beheading, and the headless body crashed down as blood sprayed everywhere.

The frost around the blade froze the spraying blood and drifting zombie poison on contact, so nothing could spread. Her moves stayed clean and sharp.

The huge swarm couldn't even get close to her. The second one lunged at her, it was already dead.

She kept moving forward, and everywhere she went, corpses piled up at her feet as the horde fell apart.

The little white cat trotted along behind her, working hard as her little crystal core collector.

It darted and hopped between the piles of bodies, picking out every crystal core with quick, practiced moves. It kept perfect pace with Paisley's killing rhythm.

In just a few minutes, most of the front line of the horde was completely wiped out.

The ground was covered in layers of zombie corpses. It was a jaw-dropping sight.

Behind her, the rest of the team froze mid-swing and stared in shock at the tall, lean figure in the middle of the horde. They couldn't believe their eyes.

Every one of them had been fighting with everything they had. They'd burned through their stamina and energy until they were running on fumes, and they could barely hold their own lines.

But Paisley hadn't used her ability once. With nothing but raw strength and a single sword, she'd crushed everything in her path.

She was clearing zombies faster than the rest of the team put together, and she'd already cut her way to the leader with ease.

Everyone knew that to really break this massive horde, they had to cut off the head of the snake.

That creepy Level Four Psychic zombie was the key. If they killed it, the horde would lose control and fall apart on its own.

But when Paisley finally stood in front of it, she realized it was way more dangerous than she'd thought.

Two vicious Level Four zombies stood perfectly still on either side of the vein-covered Psychic zombie. They guarded their leader like loyal bodyguards.

The one on the left gave off a faint golden shimmer, and its body was as hard as steel. It was a Level Four Metal Affinity zombie.

The one on the right was wrapped in blazing red flames and gave off insane heat. It was a Level Four Fire Affinity zombie.

The Psychic leader's invisible power drove the two high-level zombies to lock onto Paisley from both sides and attack at once.

The Metal zombie raised a hand and fired a volley of razor-sharp metal blades that sliced through the air straight at her.

The Fire zombie opened its mouth and blasted out a huge wall of fire, cutting off every way out.

The heat was so intense that it felt like the air itself might catch fire.

At the last second, two figures came racing in.

Purple-blue lightning crackled and roared around Elijah as he charged in. He planted himself next to Paisley and hurled a bolt of lightning that shattered the incoming blades.

Right behind him, golden light flared around Orion. He summoned a solid metal wall that blocked the wall of flames and shielded her from the deadly heat.

The two of them flanked her, and the three of them stood together, ready to take on the two Level Four zombies.

In the apocalypse, nobody survived on strength alone. No matter how powerful someone was, they still needed teammates by their side to win against impossible odds.
