Chapter 110

By now, Elijah trusted her completely.

"Okay. Whatever you say."

Elijah got on the walkie-talkie and let Orion know. Then, the two of them left through another exit and headed for the library.

They walked deep down the empty shopping street and ran into a few clusters of wandering zombies along the way.

Elijah and Paisley each took one side and cleared the way.

She didn't even slow down. With the Frostbloom Sword in her hand, she took them out without a second thought.

An icy blue light flared, and frost energy exploded from the blade. She slashed it sideways in one clean sweep.

The blade's ringing hum echoed down the street. A wide arc of freezing, evil-slaying energy spread out, and every zombie in its path dropped on the spot, their heads falling and their bodies collapsing. A whole wave of them went down in a single breath.

Fast, precise, and brutal. One swing, and a whole group was gone.

The little white cat followed behind, busy digging out crystal cores.

Once the path was clear, they kept going. Before long, a big, impressive standalone building came into view. It was the biggest library on the outskirts of Rivergate.

The whole building was in amazing shape. The outside was clean and neat, and the glass walls didn't have a single crack or bullet hole. There were no signs of fire, collapse, or any kind of fight.

The front steps were spotless except for a thin layer of old dust.

The plaza out front, once crowded with people, was deserted now. There was nothing but piles of leaves blown in by the wind. It was a strange sight in the dead, silent streets of the apocalypse.

She broke the lock on the front door with one swing of her sword.

Paisley gave the door a light push, and the inside came into view.

The whole library was bright and open, with a neat, organized layout. Several reading floors circled the space, divided into clear sections.

There was literature, history, engineering, medicine, mechanical repair, water engineering, geography, folklore, martial arts guides, and even research on abilities. It covered just about every kind of book that existed.

The collection was massive, with at least four million books. Shelves packed with books stood from the floor all the way up to the top level.

Rare out-of-print classics, lost handwritten journals, and the practical engineering, medical, water system, and repair manuals people needed most in the apocalypse were all here. It was a treasure trove of knowledge, completely untouched.

In the quiet sea of books, there were no zombies and no stench of blood or rot. There was only the faint smell of paper and ink, shutting out all the violence and ruin outside.

It was a rare clean, peaceful place in a ruined world.

Paisley waved her hand, and the whole collection vanished into her Storage Dimension.

Elijah stared at her in shock.

"Your dimension can really hold anything. This is a huge library with over four million books, and you just swept all of it in, shelves and all.

"On behalf of the whole country, thank you. If it weren't for you, who knows if any of these books would've survived?"

Once the apocalypse was over and people rebuilt the world, if the next generation grew up without all that knowledge, it would be on their generation.

Paisley stayed perfectly calm.

"I'm not as noble as you think. Come on, let's go to the next spot."

The truth was, Trovie had been squealing nonstop the whole time about gold in a building nearby.

Suddenly, Elijah's walkie-talkie crackled.

"We ran into a pack of mutant rats. We need backup."

Paisley recognized the voice right away. It was Kelly.

"I'll go check out what's up ahead. You go help them."

She'd never liked Kelly, and she definitely wasn't going to change her plans just to save her.

Elijah nodded and warned her seriously.

"Be careful. If you run into any trouble, contact everyone right away."

Paisley nodded.

"You still don't trust how strong I am? Besides, I've got the cat to protect me. Nothing's going to happen."

Elijah knew she was way stronger than most people, and he knew what the cat could do. He didn't say anything else. He just gave her a long look, then turned and headed back the way they'd come.

And just like that, they split up.

On her own now, Paisley followed Trovie's directions deeper into the district.

Before long, a tall, impressive office tower came into view. It was the tallest building in the whole area, obviously a major corporate headquarters before the apocalypse.

Any stray zombie that got close didn't even have time to snarl before Paisley drew her sword. The Frostbloom Sword flashed cold, and she took them down with one strike each.
