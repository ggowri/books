His eyes swept the four men, level and cold, and his voice stayed even. "Oh? Backing? Who's your backing?"

He jabbed a thumb at the dark hotel behind him. Faces crowded the windows, and the gleam of gun barrels flashed in the glass. The threat was in plain sight.

"Scared yet? Then be smart. Twenty Level Three crystal cores each, and you walk."

"Look at the hotel. We've got dozens of brothers in there. If it comes to a fight, your little group won't even be a snack."

The four men raised their rifles together and pointed them at Elijah.

Cole's pupils shrank, shock all over his face.

The Blackwood family of Aetheris? Mr. Blackwood?

In the whole Aetheris circle, only one man could be called Mr. Blackwood, a man with top-tier military power and a squad of elites. And he stood right in front of them.

These men were throwing Elijah's name around, shaking people down to his face, and didn't even know him by sight.

Elijah's lips curved, slow and thin and cold.

So that's how it is.

These thugs had been using his name to rob and bully survivors and wreck the Blackwood family's reputation.

It was absurd, laughable, and deserved a death sentence.

He raised his eyes, clear and sharp, and locked them on the leader. His voice carried a faint mockery and a cold edge.

"Mr. Blackwood's soldiers? Funny, I don't remember you."

"Do we know each other?"

Two short lines. The four swaggering men froze where they stood.

The leader blinked, then flared with anger. "You think you're funny? You calling Mr. Blackwood's men liars?"

Beside Elijah, Cole had recovered. He laughed under his breath, full of mockery.

"Throwing Captain Blackwood's name around while shaking down the man himself. Best joke I've heard since the apocalypse started."

The Falcon members were all out of the cars by now, on guard, staring coldly at the fakes.

Nobody knew better than they did that Elijah ran a tight ship. His people never bullied survivors. They sure as hell never held up travelers for crystal cores.

These men were rats, riding another man's name.

"Stop talking nonsense!"

The leader snarled, all bluster and no spine. "Last warning! Hand over the cores or we shoot!"

Elijah's last drop of patience ran out.

"Take them all down."

One order, and the fight began.

Spencer's Fire Affinity roared out, flames sweeping the road and sealing the four men in.

Orion's metal blades shot out in a dense wall and covered every angle they could shoot from.

Thomas's vines lashed out and bound two of the gunmen's wrists.

Madison fired clean, shooting two rifles out of their hands and ending any gun threat.

Mason charged in close, fists flying.

The Lone Wolf members saw the fight start and moved to flank.

The dozens hiding in the hotel burst out with their guns, but they were a rabble that bluffed its way on a stolen name. They couldn't match Falcon and Lone Wolf, two squads that had survived real fights.

Two minutes later, it was over.

Dozens of fakes lay on the ground, dead or hurt. The last seven or eight were surrounded, weapons dropped, kneeling and shaking. The arrogance was gone.

Covered in dust and cold sweat, they trembled on the ground in the night.

"Don't kill us! Please don't kill us!"
