Chapter 496

Snow howled around them, and the wind scraped ice grit across their cheeks until the skin ached.

The five of them tugged their hats and coats tighter and trudged through deep snow toward the lot. The modified SUV was buried in white, like a small hill from a distance.

Ashley walked close to Paisley, her arm hooked through hers, grinning.

"Paisley, once we're inside, I'll take the dangerous spots. My Earth Affinity can block beast attacks. I'll cover you."

"And when we find thick comforters, I'll pick the softest one for you!"

Jasper quickly stepped in on Paisley's other side, not about to be outdone.

"Paisley, I've got Speed Affinity. Scouting and clearing the way is on me. If there's a beast, I'll take it out first."

"When we find heaters, I'll carry them. Leave the heavy lifting to us. You don't need to waste energy."

Ashley shot him a look and sniffed. "I can scout and clear the way too. And my defense is stronger, so I can actually protect Paisley."

"Speed is how you spot danger early!" Jasper refused to back down. He turned to Paisley, eyes shining. "Don't worry, I won't hold you back."

They went back and forth, each trying to impress her.

Jasper's two teammates followed quietly. They glanced at each other and smiled. They had never seen him like this, and they didn't dare interrupt.

Paisley listened to the bickering, the corner of her mouth lifting. She said nothing and opened the Falcon Squad group chat on her phone.

[We took a Level Six mission and we're on our way to a nearby mall to collect warm supplies. Ashley, Jasper, and two of his teammates are with us.]

The chat blew up the moment she sent it.

Elijah had been in a meeting with the Blackwood faction. He read the message and his eyes locked on Jasper's name. His fingers tightened on his phone, and something sour twisted in his chest. He typed fast.

[Stay safe and stay in touch. Jasper's an outsider, after all. Call me right away if anything happens.]

The jealousy showed in every word.

Orion was resting at the training ground. He read it, nearly choked on his water, sighed, and typed.

[Tsk, that kid. Feels like my little sister's about to get stolen. I should've waited for Paisley and taken this mission with her instead of staying behind to practice shooting.]

Thomas had just finished clearing the main road. He read the messages and jumped in.

[Regret it. Wish I hadn't gone to shovel snow. I'd have gone with them.]

Spencer followed.

[+1. Same here. At least we could keep an eye on things. Pais's safety is our job.]

Mason sent a facepalm emoji.

Paisley smiled and slipped her phone back into her pocket.

They climbed into the SUV. The engine roared and the tires crunched through thick snow toward the nearby mall.

Snow pounded the windows. The world outside was a blur of white, and visibility got worse by the minute.

The drive normally took twenty minutes. In the snow it took nearly an hour before the car finally stopped in front of the mall's main entrance.

Long icicles hung from the exterior walls. Most of the display windows were shattered and clogged with old snow and frozen trash.

The building was dead still, and the only sound was wind whistling through broken openings.

They got out and readied their weapons.

Jasper called up his Speed Affinity and went first, shoving open the doors jammed with ice.

The heavy metal doors screeched wide, and a damp, bitter chill hit them in the face.

Half a foot of old snow covered the lobby. Shelves lay toppled, clothes shredded and scattered everywhere.

"We'll split up, two by two, and stay alert. Look for comforters, heavy coats, and heaters," Paisley said quietly.

******

Before she could finish, snow dropped from the ceiling tiles above.

With shrill shrieks, several mutant snow rats covered in thick gray-white fur leapt down from the second-floor railing.

They were the size of grown mutts, with sharp incisors gleaming. Their fur resisted the cold, and they were savage in this weather. Their icy red eyes locked on the intruders.

"Careful! Level Five frost-resistant mutant snow rats!"

Jasper shouted and burst forward, Speed Affinity blazing. His dagger slashed at the lead rat.
