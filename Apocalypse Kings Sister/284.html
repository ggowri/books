Chapter 284

Host's good karma is off the charts! Casually winning over an apocalypse powerhouse!

Paisley let out a helpless laugh and ignored the system's chatter.

Elijah watched her profile, the smile in his eyes deepening as he spoke softly.

"Supplies are all collected. Let's head back out and regroup with the others, find somewhere to rest for the night, and head for Stillwater Base first thing tomorrow."

Paisley nodded.

"Sounds good."

They walked out side by side, daylight spilling in through the exit and falling across their shoulders, the evening breeze soft, the mood quiet and warm.

Just as they were about to step out of the entrance, a sudden burst of footsteps echoed from deep within the passage behind them, growing louder, unmistakably clear.

"Someone's here."

Elijah's eyes sharpened instantly, and he pulled in every trace of his presence.

He pressed one hand lightly over Paisley's mouth, his other arm wrapping around her waist, pulling her into the dark corner of the passage, folding both of them into the shadows.

The sudden closeness left Paisley acutely aware of the warmth of his palm and the steady heat of his chest, his presence wrapping around her completely.

Too close.

Her body went rigid for a moment, and she held her breath without meaning to, every muscle tense, not daring to move, staying perfectly still against him.

The footsteps grew closer, and a loud, arrogant male voice carried through the empty passage.

"Can't believe this Level Seven mission was this easy. Came all the way from the base and didn't run into a single high-tier zombie the whole way. Practically free credit."

"How did nobody else manage to complete a mission this simple? And where's this big snake they mentioned?"

"Ha, guess the universe is on my side today. I really am destiny's chosen one."

"Once we haul all the weapons and food out of this armory back to base, that's twenty thousand contribution points, easy money."

Right behind him came a sweet, cloying female voice, dripping with flattery.

Rachel clung affectionately to Chase's arm, her tone soft and ingratiating.

"You really are something, Chase. Connections everywhere, strength to back it up, doors open wherever you go. Nobody else even comes close."

She leaned into a wheedling tone, angling for a favor.

"Chase, once this mission's done, think you could spare me ten thousand contribution points?

You know how it is, a girl needs nice clothes, makeup, bags, jewelry. My points just don't stretch far enough."

"It's for your sake too, really. As your girlfriend, I can't go around looking shabby. That would reflect badly on you."

Chase chuckled, his tone arrogant and self-satisfied, dripping with superiority.

"Don't worry, you'll get your share.

Now you know who's the most generous, most reliable person you can lean on, don't you."

The few Affinity users trailing behind the couple watched the exchange in silence, clearly annoyed, but none of them dared say a word.

Everyone knew mission rewards were split by effort. Contribution points were their pay for the work they'd done.

Rachel had stayed hidden behind Chase the entire time, never once using her Water Affinity, had done the least of anyone, and yet she expected to walk away with half the earnings. How was that fair?

But Chase was a Level Five psychic user, powerful and well-connected at the base, and none of them dared cross him lightly.

They exchanged glances, swallowing their resentment, and quietly pulled out their devices, recording the whole exchange of Rachel whining for points while contributing nothing, planning to report it once they got back to base.

In the shadows, Paisley heard every word clearly, a cold flicker of mockery crossing her eyes.

She hadn't expected the world to be this small, running into them here of all places.

Looks like they'd taken on a base mission and come all this way specifically for the armory's supplies.

Too bad. They were a step too late.

Every weapon, every scrap of food, every drop of fuel in this armory had already been cleared out, the whole place swept bare. They were about to come up empty-handed, mission failed.

She hadn't expected Rachel to have survived falling from that plane, let alone still be clinging to Chase.

Thriving, even.

Must be true love.

Where were her three brothers, then? They should've been together at all times.

Worried about being discovered, Paisley quickly wove a psychic shield around the two of them, blocking out any psychic probing, erasing every trace of their presence.
