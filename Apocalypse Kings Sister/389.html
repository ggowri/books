Chapter 389

Her daughter wasn't just powerful in her own right, she'd produced something as precious as spirit water and used it to help her, an ordinary person, awaken a rare Metal Affinity.

Diane's heart churned with a tangle of emotions. Her daughter, who seemed so quiet and unassuming on the surface, had clearly been hiding far more than anyone realized.

The bitterness and resentment in her chest faded away completely, replaced by pure pride and resolve.

Her daughter was this strong, this protective of her, as her mother, she couldn't afford to stay weak and become a burden.

Diane took a deep breath and nodded firmly, her eyes losing their usual mildness, replaced entirely by certainty.

"Okay. I'll go level up right now, get stronger as fast as I can!"

She needed the power to protect herself, to not be a burden to her daughter, to reclaim the dignity she'd been owed, with her own two hands, and live up to her daughter's faith in her.

Watching the resolve rekindle in her mother's eyes, Paisley's lips curved slightly as she offered a gentle reminder.

"No need to rush. Take it slow, building a solid foundation matters most for Metal Affinity. These low-tier cores have gentle energy, perfect for your early stage."

"I'll go pull the surveillance footage at the administration office. I'll be back soon."

With that, Paisley turned and headed out the door.

The staff at the base administration office had initially planned to stall, hoping to shield Brenda, her daughter was Chloe Thompson, Grant's girlfriend, backed by the Quinn family, not someone to casually offend.

But the moment they recognized the visitor as Paisley, and heard she wanted to pull the depot's surveillance footage, several staff members went rigid, not daring to show the slightest hesitation.

Who at Aetheris Base didn't know Paisley's name by now?

Young as she was, she held terrifying, overwhelming combat strength, reportedly surpassing even Elijah Blackwood, the base's acknowledged pinnacle of power.

Her future was limitless, her potential beyond anyone's ability to predict, even the base's upper leadership treated her with deference, going out of their way to stay on her good side.

A few ordinary clerks wouldn't dare offend someone at that level, not even with a hundred times the courage.

And besides, her request was entirely legitimate.

Paisley's expression stayed cool, her tone carrying no trace of emotion, her words crisp and direct.

"Pull all the public surveillance footage from the supply depot. Get me every clip of Brenda Thompson bullying people, cutting lines, and stealing supplies. I want a complete copy."

The staff didn't dare delay, immediately pulling up the backend recordings.

After a thorough check, they discovered that Brenda's bullying of Diane hadn't been a one-time incident at all, it had happened five or six times, front to back.

Every single time, Brenda had cut the line without cause, hurled abuse, shoved her around, repeatedly stealing the household supplies Diane had waited in line to collect, every incident a one-sided act of bullying.

The footage played out clip after clip, clear and unbroken, ironclad proof, impossible to deny.

Paisley looked down at the screen, watching her mother endure humiliation after humiliation, time and again. A bone-deep chill flickered through her eyes, and the corner of her mouth curved into a cold smile.

Good.

Bullying her mother, again and again.

Brenda definitely needed a proper reckoning.

With the full surveillance footage copied onto a drive, Paisley turned and left the administration office.

The moment she stepped out the door, a flashy, arrogant figure suddenly blocked her path.

Jasper Quinn stood there, one arm wrapped around a heavily made-up woman, a crowd of lackeys trailing behind him, the whole group moving with loud, showy swagger, the base's resident playboy, still fully himself even in the middle of an apocalypse.

She hadn't expected him to make it back to Aetheris Base safely after all the chaos.

The instant his eyes landed on Paisley, Jasper, usually arrogant to the point of looking down on everyone, dropped every trace of his usual pride at once. A fawning, obsequious smile spread across his face as he hurried over to greet her, his manner absurdly respectful.

"Hey, big shot! Fancy running into you here!"

Jasper had seen firsthand just how unfathomably powerful Paisley was, and over the past couple of days, word of her combat strength had spread through the base like wildfire, known to everyone by now.

He knew exactly where he stood, someone at her level wasn't someone he could afford to offend. He had to stay on her good side!

Paisley looked up, her cool gaze sweeping over him briefly, not a trace of warmth in her eyes.

The Quinn family had always been a nest of complicated motives and pure self-interest, she'd never had any good feelings toward them.

And Jasper himself was a reckless playboy with questionable conduct, he'd even made a pass at her before. In her book, he wasn't a good person.

Not worth getting close to.

"Something you need?"

Her tone was cold and distant, two clipped words carrying the unmistakable message to keep away.
