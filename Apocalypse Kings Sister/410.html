Chapter 410

His heartbeat sped up, hot and urgent, and it shook him.

Elijah turned and ran toward the Thompson villa.

An early-summer wind swept through the streets, warm with sunlight, carrying him toward her.

In the villa's courtyard, everything was peaceful and untouched by the world.

A new grapevine stood in the yard, planted that morning by Paisley herself. The tendrils were tender and green, curling up the trellis, the new leaves fresh and full of life.

Golden sunlight filtered through the thin vines in layers, throwing soft, shifting patches of light on the ground. A breeze stirred the leaves, and the shadows swayed gently.

Paisley lay back on a wicker lounge chair, relaxed, her guard completely down.

She wore soft, loose home clothes. Her long black hair spilled over her shoulders, and a few strands blew against her pale cheek.

She held a plump purple grape between her fingers, juicy and sweet, one she had taken from her Storage Dimension, a rare fruit in the apocalypse.

Her phone sat beside her, screen glowing, playing a drama series. Soft music drifted through the yard.

She rocked slowly in the chair, utterly at ease.

Power struggles, the base's troubles, a death-trap mission, none of it seemed to touch her.

The world outside was stirring, but she kept her own calm corner.

Elijah arrived at the gate and stopped. One look, and he walked straight into a scene so gentle it healed him.

Warm sun, soft breeze, swaying vines, a girl at rest.

The light traced the clean line of her profile and the soft curve of her jaw. Her lashes hung low, casting thin shadows, and her eyes were clear and clean.

She looked like a spirit of the woods who had wandered into the human world, calm and gentle and bright enough to soothe all his anger, exhaustion, and tension.

Every bit of rage, scheming, and strain he had carried from the meeting vanished the instant he saw her.

Only a hot, burning tenderness remained.

******

He walked up quietly, picked up a plain hand fan from beside her, and held it up to block the bits of sun slipping through the vines.

The fan swayed and cut the glare, leaving only a cool, gentle breeze for the girl in the chair.

Paisley's rocking paused. She pushed the tablet off her lap, looked up, and found herself face to face with his deep, tender eyes.

Her voice was sweet and lazy. "Eli, what are you doing here? Did you need something?"

Elijah looked into her clear eyes, and all the restlessness in him settled. He hid nothing. He told her everything, the meeting, the traps, the grain depot in the Exclusion Zone, the pledge he'd signed.

His voice was low and warm, stripped of the cold edge he used at work, and almost doting.

"I'm sorry. I took the mission without asking you or the others first."

"It's extremely dangerous. Damon set this up on purpose."

"But don't worry about the consequences. If we fail, I'll take all the blame and the punishment. It won't touch any of you."

"Still, I have to warn you. The Quinns and the Lennoxes won't let this go. There's a good chance someone will try to ambush us or kill us on the way."

"So we have to be careful every step of the way."

He leaned in half a step, serious and sincere.

"If it comes to the worst, I'll give everything I have to protect you. I won't let you get hurt."

Paisley stayed calm and nodded. "Okay. Got it."

Then she closed her eyes again and rocked back and forth, completely at ease. It was as if she'd just heard about something unimportant, not a mission with lives on the line.

There was no fear in her face. She was as relaxed as ever.
