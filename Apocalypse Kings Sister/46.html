Chapter 46

Paisley acted like taking down two Level Three monsters had been nothing.

She looked over at the other three, who were still in shock. "We're clear," she said calmly. "Let's go through the meds."

That finally snapped them out of it, and they hurried over to check what was in the storeroom.

It was just like Paisley had said. All the hospital's emergency meds, antibiotics, fever reducers, wound ointments, and disinfectant were stored down here, packed neatly into eight heavy crates.

Paisley raised a hand, and the four biggest crates vanished into her dimension.

She turned to the others. "My space can only fit these four. You guys will have to carry the rest."

Elijah nodded, catching on. "That actually works out. If we come back empty-handed, Jasper's going to get suspicious.

"If we carry these four crates out ourselves, it'll cover for the rest."

Just then, Trovie squealed in Paisley's head.

[Master, Master, I smell gold! Lots and lots of shiny gold!]

Paisley perked up. [Where?]

Her dimension was stuffed with all that equipment, records, and medicine, and she was running out of room.

She needed to level up badly, so this windfall couldn't have come at a better time.

Trovie answered right away. [Fourth floor of the west building, the director's office! There's so much gold!]

By now, Paisley had learned to trust Trovie's nose for treasure.

She looked up at Elijah and Orion a little sheepishly. "Elijah, Orion, I've got something personal to take care of on the fourth floor.

"It's safe down here now, so don't worry about me. You guys move the supplies, and I'll come find you as soon as I'm done."

They'd just seen how terrifyingly strong she was, so they knew she could handle herself.

Honestly, none of them could probably beat her in a fight. If she wanted to go alone, she had her reasons.

Elijah thought it over and nodded. "Okay, but get back fast. If anything happens, call us right away."

Orion nodded too, looking worried. "Be careful, Pais. If anything happens, call me," he said, pointing to the walkie-talkie he'd given her.

Paisley gave a little nod and headed straight for the fourth floor.

There were only a few stray Level One zombies in the stairwell, slow and weak. They were no threat to her now.

She took her time, and her dagger flashed as she cut down every zombie in her way without breaking stride.

Before long, she reached the director's office on the fourth floor of the west building.

Trovie was going crazy in her head. [This is it, Master! It totally reeks of gold in here!]

Paisley smiled to herself. Then, she reared back and kicked the office door as hard as she could.

With a loud crash, the heavy wooden door flew open.

The second it opened, something dark lunged straight at her!

It was a zombie in a bloodstained white coat, charging out of the dim office. Its face was rotting and twisted, an old director's badge was still pinned to its chest, and its eyes had a weird metallic shine.

It was a Level Two Metal-type mutant!

Its body was hard as iron, faint Metal energy swirled around it, and it was insanely strong. Its claws glinted like steel as it went straight for Paisley's face.

Paisley didn't even flinch. Her dagger flashed out, and she struck once, clean and without hesitation.

With a wet slice, the zombie's head hit the floor, and dark green blood sprayed everywhere.

She crouched down, dug out its crystal core like a pro, and tucked it away. Then, she looked around the room.

The office was way fancier than the rest of the beat-up hospital. Everything in it screamed money.

She quickly spotted a hidden combination safe under the desk.

She broke the lock with her bare fingers, no problem. The moment the safe swung open, a dazzling golden glow filled her eyes.
