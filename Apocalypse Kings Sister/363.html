Chapter 363

Arrogant and self-assured, he'd always considered himself one of the base's top young talents, never willing to concede anything to someone like Elijah, born into power.

If they'd started from the same place, he was certain he'd be the better man.

But this was the apocalypse now, and he'd already proven his own worth.

He'd built the strongest squad around.

Elijah. However good your pedigree, this new world runs on raw strength.

I'm waiting to watch you fall flat.

Watching him hog every bit of attention only deepened Grant's bitterness.

He stepped out of formation, eyes sharp as he turned toward Squad Falcon, his voice pitched just loud enough for the whole crowd to hear, dripping with mockery.

"Mr. Blackwood's barely back and already throwing his weight around with a whole new squad. Impressive show. Just curious — does this crew stand on actual skill, or just on his name?"

The jab landed precisely where it was meant to, implying the squad was all new blood, nothing but a name propping it up.

Chloe followed right up, looking at Ashley's group with a cold, mocking curl to her mouth, her tone sharp.

"Some people get lucky, I guess — think latching onto the right name gets you straight to the top.

But surviving in this base actually takes real skill.

Wouldn't want things to go badly when it actually counts.

Dragging the whole team down, wasting Mr. Blackwood's resources and slots for nothing."

Why do the senior branch's people get to stand next to him? Why does their showing up get everyone's attention?

If I'd known he'd come back safe, I never should've settled for someone else. Should've waited for him.

Whatever his strength was, Elijah was undeniably good-looking — a face like that was bound to have plenty of women falling over him.

She was no exception. She liked that face too, if she was honest.

And now his squad had her own cousin in it, found again out of nowhere, gorgeous on top of everything else — how unlucky could she get.

Chloe's resentment only deepened, mockery and hostility plain on her face.

The pointed jab landed hard enough to silence the surrounding chatter instantly, tension snapping taut across the hall as every eye locked onto the two squads, waiting for the showdown.

Ashley's face darkened at once. She stepped forward, standing ramrod straight despite her small frame, not the least bit intimidated.

Simple-hearted but fiercely protective, she wouldn't stand for anyone badmouthing her sister or her squad. She looked straight at the two of them, pride shining clear in her eyes.

Her voice rang out clear and sharp, pitched deliberately loud enough for everyone around to hear.

"Whether we got lucky, whether we're dead weight — that's not for you to decide.

You two are so busy looking down on everyone because of your family name — let me ask you something instead. Every single member of Squad Falcon is Level Six. What's your squad's level?"

The words hit like a thunderclap through the hushed hall.

The whole room went dead silent, every smirk and sneer freezing on people's faces, replaced by pure shock.

Level Six, every one of them?!

Barely twenty days into the outbreak, most of the base's users were still stuck at Level Three or Four, with only the rare prodigy scraping up to Level Five. Level Six was the top of the current ladder, vanishingly rare.

A single Level Six user was enough to make any squad elite — and here was a brand-new squad where every single member had hit that mark.

Shock rippled through the hall, gasps and murmurs breaking out in waves.

"All of them Level Six?! Did I hear that right? That's insane!"

"No wonder he recruited them himself. This isn't a rookie squad — this is an ace lineup!"

"I assumed they were just riding his coattails. Turns out they're the real deal all on their own."

Every bit of doubt and dismissal evaporated, every eye now fixed on Squad Falcon with pure astonishment and wariness.

Grant's arrogance froze on his face, his expression going livid, contempt replaced instantly by dark unease.

His own longtime elite squad had, at best, him alone at Level Six, with everyone else stuck at Four or Five — nothing close to a full roster of Sixes.

Chloe's face flushed between pale and livid, humiliation and jealousy threatening to boil over.
