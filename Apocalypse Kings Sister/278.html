Chapter 278

Paisley's eyes narrowed slightly.

Don't try to sweet-talk me. That Level Seven serpent in there has been feeding on military-grade metal and radiation for who knows how long. Its real combat strength is probably closer to Level Eight. This whole deal is lopsided in your favor, but fine, I'll take it.

Trovie went quiet for two seconds, then doubled down.

Bonus offer! Finish the mission and you get the 5,000 points plus one premium lucky draw! Guaranteed good stuff in that prize pool!

A small smile touched Paisley's eyes. The premium draw really did have good things in it.

Fine. Go on, then.

The deal was settled.

To cover more ground, the group split into two teams.

Paisley followed Elijah and Orion deeper into the underground armory.

The moment they stepped inside, all three of them froze, struck by what they saw.

It really was the largest armory in the Stillwater region. The underground complex stretched across a staggering two hundred thousand square feet.

The layout was clean and orderly, built from specialized alloys and blast-resistant materials, cold white lighting casting sharp, clean lines everywhere, every inch radiating advanced engineering.

The massive space was divided into clearly marked sections, rows of heavy military equipment lined up in perfect order as far as the eye could see.

The front staging area held pristine main battle tanks, their armor thick, cannons cold and imposing.

Along both sides, towering shelves held the latest automatic rifles, sniper rifles, submachine guns, and ammunition crates stacked neatly, the metal catching a cold gleam under the lights.

Beyond that sat stockpiles of bulletproof armor, tactical helmets, explosives, infrared sights, portable combat gear — an endless variety, all of it among the rarest, most valuable strategic resources left in this world.

Staring at the mountain of priceless military stock in front of them, all three of them couldn't hide the excitement in their eyes.

With this kind of supply backing them, surviving high-tier zombies and beast hordes would be a whole lot easier going forward.

Orion looked at the endless rows of heavy equipment and turned to Paisley, a note of worry in his voice.

"Pais, can your storage even hold all this?"

That was the real question.

Paisley's gaze swept over the tanks, guns, ammunition, and heavy machinery — far too much for her current storage capacity to hold.

She called out to her system directly.

Trovie, quit playing dead, get out here and fix this.

Don't forget, this is your mission. If I can't collect the supplies, the mission fails.

Trovie answered right away, eager.

Don't worry, Host! I'll request a permission boost right now!

[Trader Level 3.5 detected! Storage expansion privilege triggered!]

[Your current 100,000-square-foot storage space will now be upgraded to 5,000,000 square feet!]

The instant the message landed, a warm white light enveloped Paisley.

An invisible boundary stretched and expanded wildly, her once-limited space rapidly growing until a vast 5,000,000-square-foot storage unlocked, more than enough to hold virtually anything.

Paisley felt the shift clearly, the tension in her chest easing at once, a flicker of brightness crossing her eyes.

Five million square feet. This armory's entire stock wouldn't even come close to filling it. Ten more armories like it wouldn't either.

[Expansion complete! Host is clear to collect everything!]

Trovie's cheerful voice rang out again.

Paisley looked up at Orion and Elijah, both watching her expectantly, and spoke to reassure them.

"Don't worry, my storage got upgraded too. It's more than enough. We can take everything here."

Orion's face lit up with relief, the worry in his chest finally settling.

Elijah's gaze lingered on her, his voice low.

"Do you need help sorting through it?"

"No need." Paisley shook her head, brisk and efficient.

"You two keep watch. A high-tier mutant beast is holed up somewhere in this place. It won't stay quiet for long."

The two of them understood immediately, gripping their weapons, watching every direction.

A beat later, Paisley focused her will and laid a hand against the stacked supplies.

A deep rumble rolled through the room.

The neatly arranged tanks, heavy armor, rifles, ammunition crates, and precision equipment all vanished at once, as if pulled by some invisible force, swallowed into her storage space.

In barely a few dozen seconds, the entire first level of the armory had been emptied clean, not so much as an empty crate left behind.
