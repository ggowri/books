Chapter 446

They had just fought to the death and survived a peak Level Seven beast, battered and spent. And now a pack of nobody mercenaries had cornered them to rob them.

They hadn't taken the squad seriously at all. They were treating them like lambs waiting for slaughter after the battle.

If they hadn't used up their Affinities in the fight, these men wouldn't have been given the chance to talk.

Elijah was in the worst shape on the team.

His chest was badly hurt from taking the serpent's blows head-on, and the venom and mental contamination had drained him. He could only lean weakly against a broken wall, his face white as paper, his lips bruised blue-black, too weak even to lift a hand or speak. He gritted his teeth, his eyes full of dark, furious rage.

The situation tightened again.

Beside Paisley, the little white cat's fur bristled. Its clear eyes went icy, and its small body planted itself in front of her. It bared its sharp teeth at the mercenaries and let out a low growl.

The golden fox next to it fanned its tails, wind Affinity stirring, its spirit turning to killing intent, ready to leap at any moment to protect its master and fight to the death.

The cat and the fox stood on her left and right, battle-ready.

Paisley looked down, her fingers gently stroking the little white cat's head. She wasn't panicked at all.

She looked up. Her cool eyes were covered with a thin layer of frost, and a faint, cold smile curved her lips.

These idiots. They didn't see me kill a Level Seven alone, or they'd never dare say that, or go after my pets, or my people. Do they think I'm an easy mark?

They're asking to die.

At the same time, thoughts raced through her mind.

These mercenaries were well equipped and calm. Every one of them was a Level Five or Six Affinity user. They weren't ordinary scavenger bandits.

They looked like they'd come here on purpose, to move in while the squad was at its weakest, after the big fight. It was a planned strike aimed squarely at Elijah and the group.

Hiding in the dark, waiting to take the spoils. Vicious.

Elijah's face grew uglier. His bruised lips pressed together. The pain from the poison and mental damage made his body shake, but he still tried to get up and protect the team.

She watched these men get greedier and more shameless. She saw her whole team wounded, Elijah most of all, who looked about to collapse.

They all needed rest right now.

Paisley's last bit of patience was gone.

Her voice was cold, flat, and without a trace of warmth.

"Get lost. Or don't blame me for what happens."

She didn't want to waste another word on these parasites.

"Heh? Don't know what's good for you?"

The mercenary leader snorted, full of contempt, vicious light in his eyes.

"A little girl who can hardly stand, and she's mouthing off at us? Why not be our pretty pet? Boys, move! Take the goods, take the girls!"

Before he finished, killing intent broke loose.

They were about to fire when the whole space went still.

Paisley released a vast peak Level Six psychic force, invisible and colorless. The psychic pressure crashed over the field like a tsunami and slammed into the mind of every mercenary.

Aaaaah!

Screams rose from every side and tore apart the quiet of the ruins.

Over a dozen Level Five and Six mercenaries clutched their heads and curled on the ground, their minds feeling pierced by ten thousand needles. Their souls felt close to cracking.

They twitched, eyes bloodshot, every Affinity and every bit of fight sealed away. They couldn't even find the strength to pull a trigger.

The extreme mental agony wracked their bodies. They knelt, clutching their heads, unable to get up.

Paisley pulled out a base-issue pistol and aimed the black muzzle at each mercenary writhing on the ground.

Bang, bang, bang!

Gunshots rang out one after another, clean and without pause, with no mercy.

One bullet each, every one fatal.

In under five minutes.

Over a dozen Level Five and Six Affinity mercenaries, men strong enough to rule their own patch of the apocalypse, lay dead on the ground without a sound.

The field fell silent.
