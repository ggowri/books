Chapter 395

"Pais, where did all the supplies go? Don't tell me you've awakened a space Affinity too?"

Paisley held out a simple silver ring, resting it in Diane's palm.

"Mom, this is a storage ring. It holds about a hundred square feet of space inside."

"All the supplies are stored in there now. This is yours."

Diane cradled the small ring carefully, running her fingers over its cool metal surface, disbelieving.

"This ring can hold things?"

Paisley explained gently.

"Mom, try it and you'll see. Just wear it, and control what's inside with your mind."

"If you bond it with a drop of blood, it'll belong only to you from then on."

"Go ahead, try it."

Diane did as she said, pricking her fingertip and letting a drop of blood touch the ring's surface.

A faint flash of light, and the storage ring bonded with her instantly.

A thought crossed her mind, and a bag of rice and flour she'd just stored away appeared out of thin air, landing at her feet.

The crowd of onlookers gasped in unison, every eye locked onto that ring, eyes churning with intense curiosity and envy.

A storage item!

In a supply-starved apocalypse, this was a priceless treasure, something countless high-tier Affinity users could never get their hands on.

You needed real standing to acquire something like that.

The Thompson family's newly recovered daughter really was something!

And she'd just handed over something this precious to the second branch's wife, just like that.

She really was good to her mother.

Brenda's pupils shrank as she stared hard at the storage ring on Diane's hand, greed churning in her eyes.

If she hadn't spent all this time bullying and pushing around the junior branch, if she'd stayed on good terms with them instead, would this kind of fortune have landed on her, too?

Beside her, Grandpa Thompson's breath caught as well, his clouded eyes fixed on the ring, thick with regret.

He'd favored the senior branch his whole life, pushing down the junior branch at every turn, who could have guessed the branch he'd looked down on would end up holding a treasure this unbelievable?

Both of their gazes finally settled on Paisley.

She was the stroke of fortune that had landed on the junior branch.

If she were willing to give them a storage ring too, maybe they wouldn't mind welcoming the junior branch back into the fold after all.

Paisley took in the greed plain in both their eyes, but let on nothing at all, as if she hadn't understood their complicated motives.

She gently took her mother's arm, letting her gaze sweep once over the crowd without lingering, then turned and walked away from the villa entrance with Diane, without looking back.

This whole conflict had finally settled.

Seeing the two of them about to leave, the crowd set aside their hesitation and gathered around eagerly, offering sincere congratulations to Diane.

"Congratulations, sister-in-law! You actually awakened an Affinity! That's wonderful news!"

"And a rare Metal Affinity, too! That's a great one, so strong in a fight!"

"Sister-in-law, how exactly did you awaken it? Can you tell us?"

"Yeah! So many ordinary people at this base never manage to awaken in their whole lives, how did you pull it off?!"

"Reaching Level Four in just a few hours, your talent is off the charts!"

"Do you have any spare storage rings? Could I buy one off you?"

"My son's out gathering supplies all the time, something like that would be perfect for him!"

Everyone remembered, just that morning, Diane had been the helpless, defenseless woman Brenda had freely shoved and bullied.

In the span of just three or four hours, she'd transformed into a Level Four Metal Affinity user, a change so dramatic it was almost impossible to believe.

Every face in the crowd was full of envy and desperate hope.

In this brutal apocalypse, no one wanted to spend their life as a pushover, forever at the bottom, always the one being bullied, always the one sacrificed.

Affinity was the one thing everyone in the apocalypse dreamed of, the ultimate lifeline, the only real foothold in this world.

Who wouldn't want to awaken an Affinity, escape the fate of the powerless, stop having to watch everyone else's moods?

Watching the crowd's eager, envious faces, Diane kept her expression mild and said nothing at all.

She knew, deep down, that both the awakening and the storage ring were extraordinary strokes of fortune, gifts from her daughter.

A stroke of luck this unbelievable, if it ever got out, would draw endless coveting and no end of trouble.

So, facing the crowd's eager questions, she kept smiling and said nothing more.

But the more tight-lipped she stayed, the deeper the crowd's awe grew.

Everyone could see clearly, Diane had the unfathomable Paisley standing behind her.
