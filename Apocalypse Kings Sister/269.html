Paisley felt a flicker of satisfaction. That closed the gap considerably.

A moment later, pale blue light shimmered in the corner of the car, and a complete set of farming tools materialized, polished metal, sturdy wood, a thick bound set of blueprints with every detail marked.

[Shipment arrived at the dimensional gate. Free to retrieve.]

Paisley passed the word along.

The princess bowed, sincerely grateful.

[Thank you! With these, our whole kingdom's farming will double in output. No more collapsing from exhaustion every spring, no more fields left to waste. Our people can finally farm with ease and reap a real harvest.] [If there's ever more labor-saving tools or ways to boost yield, I'll trade for those too, same terms as always.]

Paisley nodded, easy about it.

[Works for me. Reach out anytime.]

The light faded, and the connection closed on its own.

The cabin went quiet again, the wind outside the only sound.

Elijah still had his eyes closed, breathing slow and even, the quiet between them still warm with something unspoken.

Paisley's eyes drifted to his face and lingered there a beat too long.

No wonder everyone's afraid of him. He really is something to look at.

Sharp features, hard lines, somehow still striking together, all that lethal edge gone soft now that he was resting.

Especially those lashes, thick and long, casting faint shadows under his eyes.

Those lashes look fake, honestly. Like tiny folded fans.

Her fingers itched to reach out and touch them.

She caught herself and pulled back.

No. Behave.

This is a top-tier warlord, deadly and intimidating. Not someone to just daydream about.

A pretty face really is a trap. Look too long and you're done for.

She pulled her eyes away and dug a lollipop out of her pocket, unwrapping it, letting the sweetness settle on her tongue.

She called Kitty out of her dimension and buried her hands in its soft fur, trying to get her mind off things.

Beside her, Elijah's mouth curved up, just barely.

His eyes were closed, but he'd been awake the whole time, every sense alert.

He'd felt her gaze on his face, steady, lingering.

He'd always known he was good-looking, heard it his whole life. But he'd never felt proud of it, not like this.

So she likes my face.

Whatever he'd been holding back loosened, just a little, something sweet creeping in.

Maybe... this isn't completely hopeless after all.

******

Maybe this feeling I've been hiding will get where it's going, eventually.

Just then, hurried footsteps approached outside.

A moment later, the others came rushing back, out of breath, sweat on their brows.

Tired as they looked, their eyes were bright with success.

Ashley got there first, leaning through the window, eyes shining.

"Paisley! I found so much stuff, it's all in my storage ring, I'll split it with you!"

Orion followed close behind, grinning.

"Paisley, didn't expect you two to finish up this fast."

"Got a good haul myself. Here, half's yours."

Spencer and Thomas came over as well, both grinning, both offering her half of what they'd found.

The second any of them found something, their first thought was to share it with her.

Only Kelly, at the back of the group, stood with her eyes down, clutching a stuffed canvas bag, bitterness churning through her.

After hours of searching as hard as she could, all she had to show for it was one small bag, barely enough to last a few days, even rationed carefully.
