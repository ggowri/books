Ashley lifted her chin and cut in.

She hooked her arm through Paisley's and walked off with her, leaving Jasper and his teammates standing there.

They got back to the villa by five in the afternoon.

Snow still fell outside and the wind still screamed, but inside it was quiet and warm.

Paisley wanted to cook a big dinner herself.

On a cold night like this, chicken soup would warm everyone up.

In the spirit fields of her storage dimension, a thick stand of sacred fungus grew strong, picked earlier and grown out again.

The spirit hens she'd traded from the Beast Realm trader lived in a special pen in there.

Paisley began prepping, steady and unhurried.

She cut up a plump hen, put the pieces into a Dutch oven, added aged sacred fungus and spirit water, and set it on low heat to simmer.

The broth began to bubble, and a rich, mellow fragrance rose and spread.

Next came the rest of the dishes.

Glossy braised ribs, tender pot roast, tangy fish chowder, red-glazed garlic butter shrimp, sautéed cabbage, and scrambled eggs with tomatoes.

She set a huge pot of mashed potatoes on to cook.

Before she started cooking, she'd texted Elijah to invite him and his parents to dinner at the villa.

As the dishes came off the stove one by one, the long dining table filled up. Aromas drifted through the house.

The soup in the Dutch oven was bubbling, steam curling up.

Just as the last dish was done, the sound of a car stopping drifted in from outside.

Gerald, Diane, Orion, Thomas, and Spencer tramped into the yard through the snow. They pushed open the front door, and the rich smell wrapped around all of them.

Thomas's nose was sharp. He took several deep breaths from far away, his eyes shining, and strode in, teasing.

"Smells amazing! Paisley, what did you make?"

"Or did you feel bad for me because I'd been shoveling snow all day and made dinner just for me?"

Ashley stood up from the table, smiling, and fired back.

"Keep dreaming! Smells great, right? Paisley's cooking is no joke. I was standing right beside her and I almost fainted from the smell. I'm practically drooling."

Gerald and Diane stood to one side, watching their kids bicker and smiling.

Right behind them, Elijah walked in with his parents.

His father was General Nathaniel Blackwood of Aetheris Base, always busy with military matters and respected by everyone.

The Thompsons straightened up a little at the sight of him and came over to greet him.

"General Blackwood!"

"Mr. Blackwood, Mrs. Blackwood!"

Paisley was a little surprised. She'd assumed the General was too busy for a family dinner. She hadn't expected him to come himself.

He was in plain, simple clothes, with no trace of the airs of a high-ranking officer. His expression was relaxed, and he looked at Paisley sincerely.

"Paisley, thank you for your hard work. Today's Level Six mission to collect winter supplies wasn't easy. You finished it perfectly and brought back a huge stock for the winter. You eased the base's emergency. On behalf of the base, thank you."

"You're too kind, Mr. Blackwood. It's just part of the job," Paisley said calmly.

Elijah's eyes went straight to Paisley, full of warmth. He took off his coat, dusted with snow, and hung it up.

"You must be tired."

Paisley invited everyone to sit.

They settled in around the long table, loaded with good food.

Glossy pot roast, red-glazed garlic butter shrimp, tangy fish chowder, braised ribs, sautéed cabbage, and scrambled eggs with tomatoes surrounded the table.

In the center, the sacred fungus chicken soup bubbled gently in the Dutch oven. A pale, milky broth with thin beads of fat on top.

The warm herbal scent mixed with chicken. It blended with the sweet smell of pot roast, the rich savor of the shrimp, and the sharp tang of the fish chowder, filling the whole living room.

Spencer stared at the table, swallowing hard, eyes bright.

"Oh my God, just the smell has me drooling.
