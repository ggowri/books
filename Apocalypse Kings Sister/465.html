Chapter 465

Moonlight spilled through the window onto Elijah's face. Tenderness softened his eyes, but watchful sharpness still lingered underneath.

The SUV started smoothly, engine low, and rolled forward. The Lone Wolf vehicle followed close behind, and the two cars headed toward the hotel.

The streets were empty and silent. Outside Larkspur, only the hum of tires on the road broke the quiet.

Ten minutes later, a harsh, man-made roadblock stood across the main road, sealing it completely.

Rusted iron frames, thick steel plates, and tangled chains had been welded into a crude barricade across every lane.

Four men in black stood behind it.

They stood straight and held themselves like trained soldiers, not like drifting survivors. Each carried a military-issue rifle, muzzle angled at the ground. Their eyes were arrogant and cold as they looked over the two approaching vehicles.

The SUV slowed and stopped.

The leader stepped forward, chin raised, his tone swaggering and flat.

"You want through here, you pay the toll."

He jabbed a finger at the barricade. "Read the rules. Twenty Level Three crystal cores each. One short, and nobody drives through today."

In the front seat, Mason scanned the four men, then looked toward the faint glow of the abandoned hotel in the distance. He lowered his voice and turned to Elijah.

"Captain, somebody's blocking the road. They've got guns. There are a lot of figures moving around in that hotel. There are plenty of them. This is going to be trouble."

Elijah's eyes cooled. His first glance went back to the rear seat.

Paisley was still asleep, breathing evenly.

The cold in his eyes eased. He adjusted her backrest gently once more, made sure she was comfortable, then stood and stepped out of the car.

In the car behind them, Cole saw him go and followed. He hurried to Elijah's side and spoke low, wary and resigned.

"Captain Blackwood, careful. These guys have been shaking down this stretch for a long time. They run this area."

"Last time my squad came through, they made every one of us hand over twenty Level Three cores. There was no bargaining."

He glanced at the rifles in the four men's hands, then at the hidden force in the distant hotel. "They're all armed. More are lying in wait. The ones in that hotel were probably regular soldiers before the apocalypse. Their training is serious. They'll be hard to beat."

"And nobody's stopped them from taxing the main road this openly. Someone up at the base lets them. They've got backing."

"If you ask me, we should pay and move on. Don't fight them. We'd lose time getting back, and people could die."

Twenty Level Three cores were nothing to a squad like Falcon.

Elijah's face stayed calm and still, but a thin layer of frost covered his eyes.

He walked up to the four men, his voice level but impossible to push around. "You collect protection money here?"

The leader snorted, full of smug confidence. He puffed out his chest.

"That's right. Rules are rules."

He paused on purpose, then dropped the name he thought would end the discussion. "Do yourselves a favor and cooperate. We're a squad directly under Aetheris Base. We've got backing."

The line carried real weight.

They belonged to the same base, yet they set up checkpoints to bleed other squads. They were bullies hiding behind a name.

Cole stiffened, his stomach dropping.

This is the worst kind. Same base, real backing, their own rules, and no reason in them.

Elijah stood before the roadblock, straight as a pine, face calm and unreadable. But the cold behind his eyes sank deeper with each second.
