Chapter 507

Russell frowned, anger barely held back.

"This is the first time you've ever failed on such a scale. After this, our family's position will only get harder. A lot of families that were sitting on the fence tipped toward the Blackwoods because of this."

His eyes flashed with cruelty.

"I know the turning point of this whole thing was Paisley. If it weren't for her, Falcon Squad would be buried outside the city and the mission would have failed."

Beside them, Jasper had a broad grin he couldn't hide.

Today he'd gone on a Level Six collection mission with Paisley. It had been thrilling, and the haul was huge. He'd gotten a pile of contribution points and crystal cores, and he was still riding a high. The smile stayed on his face.

Russell glanced over and saw his useless son beaming. His temper flared and he barked.

"What are you grinning at?"

Jasper's smile stiffened and faded slightly.

"If you were half as capable as your brother, I could retire sooner."

Russell shifted his eyes to Jasper and changed course.

"You know Paisley? How did you end up on a mission with her? Tell me, who is she, really? Are the rumors exaggerated? Is she actually that strong?"

Russell tapped a finger on the desk, weighing it.

"If we can, find a way to win her over to the Quinn side. If we can't… then we'll have to get rid of her."

"A person like that can't be allowed to keep growing."

Jasper's face changed. He stepped forward and quickly pleaded for her.

"Dad, no way! Paisley isn't someone you can just recruit."

"She's incredibly strong. She has multiple affinities, plus the rare Storage Affinity. She brought back a huge amount of winter supplies and saved countless ordinary people at the base. She's kind, and she's willing to give supplies to the base. Please don't kill her."

"And anyone who's fought her knows how deep her strength goes. If we strike carelessly, it'll bring disaster down on the Quinn family!"

Damon's eyes snapped up, killing intent glittering, and he cut Jasper off with a cold laugh.

"Jasper, you're naive."

"She's tied too deep to the Thompsons and Blackwoods. General Blackwood values her, Elijah is gone on her, and the Thompsons treat her like a jewel. Her camp will never lean toward ours."

Damon pushed himself upright on his wounded body. The gauze pulled painfully, but his eyes held only resolve.

"If she can't be useful to us, she has to be removed. If we leave Paisley alone, sooner or later she'll become a serious threat."

In the dim light, the head of the Quinn family stayed grim and silent. His gaze moved between his two sons, and he was already forming his own plan.

"What we've said tonight stays among us three."

"Leave Paisley alone for now. You two just focus on training. The weather's only going to get worse."

"Jasper, especially you. Keep your head down this period. Don't cause me any more trouble."

"If you can, get on good terms with Paisley. If she married you, that would be best of all."

******

Jasper blinked, then pointed at himself in disbelief.

"Dad, did you say that wrong? How would Paisley ever fall for me?"

After a moment of shock, he sighed softly.

"But I'll do my best to smooth things over. I don't want our family ending up as her mortal enemy."

"You two keep talking. I've got somewhere to be."

Without waiting for his father or brother to answer, Jasper turned and slipped out of the oppressive study.

The moment the door closed, the tension drained out of him.

The comm on his wrist buzzed. It was a message from his usual group of drinking buddies, inviting him to a party at a private villa.

A lazy grin curled his lips.

This is the life I want.

Scheming and killing were exhausting. He'd leave that to his big brother Damon.

He just wanted to have fun while he could and stay out of the dangerous whirlpool of plots.

The snow fell without stopping for three days.

Night settled over the Thompson villa, and the house was warm.
