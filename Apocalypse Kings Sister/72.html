Chapter 72

"Guess we won't be going anywhere together, then."

Rachel's smile froze. Every bit of warmth drained from her eyes, and all that was left was something dark and vicious.

She'd figured that now that she was a Level Two ability user and was offering to make peace, Paisley would know better than to say no.

She'd never expected Paisley to be this stubborn and shoot her down to her face.

Fine. If she won't hand it over, I'll just take it.

After all, Paisley was just a useless nobody with no ability.

Who would care about some regular person, anyway?

Rachel dropped the act completely.

The next second, she raised her hand, called on her Level Two Water Affinity, and grabbed for the pendant on Paisley's chest!

Cold mist swirled around her fingertips. She lunged hard and fast, making no secret that she was trying to rip it right off Paisley's neck.

"If you won't give it to me, I'll just take it myself!"

Paisley gave a cold little smile.

There she is. She's finally showing her true colors.

And this was the perfect chance to crush Rachel's hopes once and for all.

Paisley pretended to get knocked off her feet by the blast and fell to the ground.

The smooth, glossy pendant flew through the air in an arc and hit the ground hard. It broke into several pieces with a sharp crack, and shards scattered everywhere.

As Rachel stared at the broken pendant on the ground, she didn't look sorry at all. If anything, a sick sort of satisfaction flashed across her face.

Good! If I can't have it, neither can she!

Rachel even rubbed salt in the wound.

"Oops! Paisley, you really should be more careful. How'd your pendant end up on the ground like that?

"You're so clumsy."

Paisley started her act. She pretended to burst into tears, like her heart was breaking.

Elijah had just finished packing, and he came outside just in time to see it.

Paisley, who was always so cool and collected, had her head bowed. Her shoulders trembled, and silent tears ran down her face. She looked fragile and helpless.

It was the first time Elijah had ever seen her like this.

The Paisley he knew was always calm and in control. She kept her distance, and nothing in the world seemed to shake her. She was fiercely independent.

So even she had moments when she got this upset.

His chest suddenly went tight. An ache he couldn't explain spread through him, and he felt like he couldn't breathe.

His face darkened, and the air around him seemed to drop several degrees.

Elijah hurried over to Paisley and carefully, gently helped her up. His voice was low, and he looked worried sick. "What happened?"

Paisley didn't look up or say a word. She just let the tears keep falling, the picture of heartbreak.

But that was enough to set Elijah off completely.

He was usually a reasonable guy. Paisley was the one exception.

He turned a cold glare on Rachel, who stood off to the side looking way too pleased with herself. He didn't give her a single chance to explain.

Without a second's hesitation, Elijah slapped her hard across the face!

The crack of it rang out. The blow knocked Rachel straight to the ground, and a red handprint bloomed on her cheek as burning pain exploded across her face.

He'd obviously put everything he had into it.

The air went dead silent.

Elijah's expression was hard as stone.

"I don't hit women. But you're the exception.

"Anyone who messes with my people pays for it."

The slap left Rachel dizzy and dazed. Half her face burned and swelled up, and her ears wouldn't stop ringing. She was so stunned that she couldn't get up for a moment.

Humiliation and rage crashed over her. She gritted her teeth, her eyes burning with disbelief and resentment, and fury churned in her chest until it almost swallowed her whole.

He actually thinks of Paisley as one of his own? He's protecting her like this?

Only in the past two days had Rachel remembered exactly who Elijah was, and it had shaken her to the core.

Elijah was no ordinary apocalypse badass. He was the only heir of the Blackwood family, the most powerful of the Four Great Families of Aetheris. He had serious status and serious power.

What shocked her even more was that in her past life, the strongest man in the whole apocalypse had been Elijah. He'd built the mighty Epoch Enclave from the ground up and crushed every rival faction.

His strength, his vision, his influence, and his background put Jasper to shame. He was the undisputed king of the apocalypse.
