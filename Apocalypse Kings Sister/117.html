Chapter 117

She wished she had that kind of power herself.

Why did she have to be the one who saved me? Now, getting rid of her is going to be even harder.

Jasper rubbed his throbbing temples and stared at the Rat King's corpse, still shaken. A chill ran down his spine.

He'd been right in the middle of it, so he knew better than anyone how dangerous it had been. His voice was full of awe.

"That Rat King's psychic attack was terrifying. Even if we all fought it together, it could've crushed us without breaking a sweat.

"Without Paisley, we'd all be rat food by now. And she's even scarier than that thing. Just how strong is she?

"Thank God she was here today. Otherwise, we wouldn't even have known what killed us.

"I never would've guessed. She looks so delicate, but she's this freakishly strong?

"From now on, I'm keeping my distance and never crossing her. When I think about how I disrespected her before, I want to slap myself.

"If nothing else, I need to find a way to get on her good side."

A few of his men helped each other up. Their clothes were soaked with cold sweat, and they muttered to themselves, still shaken.

"For a second there, my mind went completely blank, and I couldn't even think, let alone fight back. It was terrifying. How did Paisley do it?"

"She's ridiculously strong!"

Paisley had completely won them over. Today, they'd seen what a real powerhouse looked like, someone way beyond ordinary people, with no limit in sight.

If it weren't for Paisley's powerful mind and her little white cat, none of them would've lasted even half an hour against a psychic attack that brutal.

Not far away, the three Lennox brothers had watched the whole thing. They were drowning in regret, their faces red and their expressions complicated. They were too ashamed to even step forward.

The brothers stared blankly at Paisley, who stood tall and calm. As memories of the past flooded back, it felt like a boulder was crushing their chests. Bitterness and guilt churned inside them, and regret tore them apart.

They finally understood how stupid and cruel they'd been.

Ever since Rachel had come back, they'd never treated Paisley with any real kindness.

Every time Rachel cried or threw a fit, they'd taken her side, no questions asked.

They'd hit Paisley, yelled at her, and taken all their frustration out on her. They'd stomped on her dignity every chance they got, all while spoiling selfish, two-faced Rachel.

After the apocalypse hit, they'd let Rachel's lies blind them even more. They'd tried to take Paisley's pendant for no reason and forced her to kneel and apologize.

In the end, they'd heartlessly kicked her out and cut all ties, leaving her to survive on her own in a brutal, ruined world.

They'd stupidly believed that Paisley was weak and awkward, nothing compared to charming, golden-girl Rachel. They'd thought getting rid of her meant getting rid of dead weight.

Sooner or later, they'd figured, Paisley would come crawling back, full of regret.

But now, they'd seen with their own eyes what Paisley could really do.

She'd taken down a Level Four mutant Rat King like it was nothing, saved everyone on her own, and become the whole team's savior.

And this amazing, powerful woman was the sister they'd grown up with, the one they'd pushed away and hurt with their own hands.

They'd thought Paisley would be the one to regret it. But in the end, they were the ones full of regret.

Paisley didn't need them. They were the ones who needed her.

Rachel was fake and cowardly, always hiding behind other people, while Paisley was brilliant and fearless. When the brothers compared the two of them, their regret hit an all-time high.

If they'd treated her well from the start, protected her, and trusted her, Paisley would never have abandoned them now. If only they hadn't taken sides so blindly, treated her so cruelly, or kicked her out so coldly.

She would've been the one person they could always count on.

And they never would've ended up like this, beaten and bloody.

But there was no undo button in life.

Every beating, every cruel word, every heartless rejection had turned into a razor-sharp thorn stuck in their hearts.

They watched everyone look at Paisley with such admiration and thought about everything they'd done. The three of them were so ashamed that they couldn't even look at her. All they felt was endless regret.

The only thing they could think about was finding a way to make Paisley forgive them.

Rachel was the only one frozen in place. She stared at the Rat King's huge corpse, then at Paisley, who looked calm and unbothered. Her feet felt nailed to the ground, her blood ran cold, and bitterness, jealousy, and resentment churned inside her.

She'd been reborn too.

She'd come back with all her memories of her past life. She knew about countless lucky breaks ahead of time and where all the high-level mutant beasts were and what their weaknesses were. She'd been sure this life would be the one where she crushed everyone and shined the brightest.
