Chapter 444

Crack!

The tough, paralyzing cage began to split and snap under the concentrated mutant flame. Thorny vines crumbled into withered scraps and drifted in the air, and the hold on it loosened at once.

In just three seconds most of the cage collapsed.

Dead vine scraps filled the sky as the cage dissolved.

The freed serpent mutant was covered in burns, its skin charred and curling, dark green venom dripping from its scales. But its slit pupils burned with a mad urge to destroy everything.

Its huge body, trailing foul poison mist, lunged at Paisley like a black tidal wave!

Its front claw was packed with concentrated, abyssal venom, the tips glinting with icy light. Behind it, the thirty-three-foot tail was coiled and tensed, with enough force to split mountains, cutting off her escape below.

Attacked from above and below, with no way out.

Against a kill strike that could tear a Level Six Affinity user to pieces in an instant, Paisley didn't back up a step. She stood straight as a pine, her eyes still calm.

She knew this artificial peak Level Seven serpent mutant had escaped every normal rule of life and death. Its body could heal endlessly. Its wounds closed in a heartbeat. Its blood could eat through anything.

As long as the core in its head stayed whole, it was immortal.

Ordinary wounds, fire, and Affinity attacks of every kind were all useless.

The only way was to take the core out.

In a flash, Paisley called up vines again.

Roots tore up from the ground once more, this time covered in poisonous thorns, and locked on the serpent's limbs and body joints.

The serpent roared with rage and shook with huge strength, trying to break free. But in that short hold, its body froze for an instant.

Now!

Paisley unleashed another psychic strike.

An invisible blade of mental force stabbed straight into the serpent's mind.

Five seconds of stillness.

The huge, leaping body locked in place. Its pupils dulled and its body went rigid, even the black mist around it freezing for a moment.

This was the last chance to kill it.

Everything Paisley had done until now had been waiting for this moment.

She kicked off the air and shot forward like a white blur, slipped past every claw and every blind spot of the tail, and landed on the mutant's head, right on its core.

The Frostbloom Sword was cold and bright, the blade packed with the last of her Affinity.

The serpent had burned through a lot of Affinity healing from the fire. Now it was badly drained.

It no longer had the full strength of its peak defense, and it couldn't block this lethal cut.

"Die!"

Squelch!

The blade pierced the charred scales and the thick flesh without resistance and drove into the deepest part of its skull.

Paisley's wrist twisted. She spun the blade, widened the wound, tore the flesh away, and clamped down on the Level Seven crystal core, swollen with black mist.

Five fingers closed. She yanked hard.

A pitch-black crystal core wrapped in violet poison veins was ripped out of the beast's body.

The moment it left, the serpent's crushing pressure vanished. The black venom mist collapsed. The wound that had been healing so fast stopped. The absurd regeneration was gone.

Its thirty-three-foot body convulsed violently. The savage, deadly pupils faded to nothing.

"No... I was going to evolve into perfection... how could I lose to a human..."

Its last bitter words broke apart on the wind.

Boom!

The huge body crashed into the dust of the ruins and went still.

The death-gray cloud that had choked the entire ruins drained away like a tide.

The oppressive killing atmosphere was gone. Even the cold wind felt better.

The battlefield fell silent for a moment.

The Falcon Squad, every one wounded and worn to the bone from fighting, froze. They stared at the huge corpse in the middle of the field, shock and disbelief in their eyes, their minds blank.

Paisley had killed a peak Level Seven serpent mutant alone, and it turned everything they knew upside down.

All of them together, fighting in rotation, couldn't beat a peak Level Seven artificial mutant.

It had been a beast that couldn't die, controlled beast hordes, and spread mental contamination. It had turned the whole ruins into a dead zone.
