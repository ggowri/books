Chapter 132

With a faint ripping sound, the claws broke skin. Three thin but deep scratches opened up across the back of Rachel's pale hand, and blood welled up and dripped slowly between her fingers.

"Ahh!" The sudden, sharp pain made Rachel scream. She jerked her hand back and stumbled half a step backward.

She stared at her bleeding hand in disbelief. Rage and humiliation flooded through her, and her sweet act fell apart completely, leaving nothing but twisted jealousy and spite.

"That damn animal! It scratched me again!"

The scratches it had left on her face before still hadn't fully healed.

Filthy little beast.

Rachel was in pain and furious. She glared at the little white cat in Paisley's arms. Then, she turned to the Lennox brothers, sounding hurt and angry.

"Owen, Reid, Ryan, look at this!

"Paisley let that feral cat attack me! She knew I just wanted to talk, and she let that thing hurt me on purpose!"

She raised her voice on purpose, and her eyes turned red on cue. She played the victim like always and dumped all the blame on Paisley.

Paisley's cold rejection had already humiliated the Lennox brothers and left them seething. Seeing Rachel hurt gave them the perfect excuse to go after Paisley.

Owen's face went dark with rage. He stepped forward, looming over Paisley, and glared at her as he snapped.

"Paisley! You've gone way too far!

"Rachel came over here to make up with you! Not only did you blow her off, you let your pet attack her?

"That's just a stupid animal, and it thinks it can act up in front of us!

"Hand that cat over right now, and make it up to us with all your crystal cores and treasures! Or we're not letting this go!"

He said it like he had every right. He'd always taken it for granted that Paisley would let them push her around and take whatever they wanted.

Reid was right behind him, his face dark. He got in her face, and his words were as nasty as they come.

"Too good for us now, huh? Just because you're cozying up to the Thompsons and Captain Blackwood, you think you can turn your back on your own family?

"You really think they care about some orphan like you? They're just using you because you're useful right now.

"Without the Lennox family, you'd be nothing! Everything you have, we gave you.

"We ask for a little in return, just some help awakening our affinities, and you make excuses. Now you're letting some animal hurt your own sister! How can you be so cruel?"

Ryan gave her a cold, selfish look, clearly taking Rachel's side.

"Kill that troublemaking stray right now. Hand over all your resources and tell us how you really awakened your affinity, or we'll make you talk."

The three of them played off each other, twisting everything around.

They didn't care who was actually at fault. Like always, they put all the blame on Paisley.

They'd been like this since they were kids. Right or wrong, they always took Rachel's side, and no matter what happened, Paisley was always the one to blame.

When she gave in, they expected it. When she gave things up, they acted like she owed them. And when she said no, they treated it like a crime.

The warm sun was still shining, but the last bit of warmth in Paisley's eyes was gone.

She looked up slightly at the four hateful faces in front of her and gave a faint, icy smile.

"My cat never attacks anyone for no reason.

"She came at me with bad intentions and tried to sneak in and grab the pendant right off my neck. Getting scratched was her own fault. She had it coming."

Her voice wasn't loud, but every word was clear and ice-cold. There was no emotion in it, only total control and distance.

"And another thing."

Paisley slowly stood up with the little white cat in her arms. She wasn't big, but she stood straight and tall, and the pressure of a Level Five ability user rolled off her. The people in front of her could barely breathe.

"Before, I let things slide because of whatever history we had. But you kept pushing your luck. You're greedy, and you twist everything around.

"The moment you chose to abandon me, take Rachel's side, and scheme against me again and again, I was done with the Lennox family.

"I'll say it one more time. I have nothing to do with any of you anymore, and you're not touching a single thing that's mine.

"As for the secret to my luck, keep dreaming."
