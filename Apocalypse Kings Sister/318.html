Chapter 318

Paired with a plate of simple, crisp greens to balance it all out, the whole spread covered the table, colorful and fragrant.

Right at six, as dusk settled in and the evening air cooled, the rest of the group trickled back to the villa.

They'd barely reached the gate when the smell hit them, thick and mouthwatering, instantly setting off a wave of hunger.

They hurried inside, and the second they saw the table loaded with a top-tier seafood feast, everyone froze, shock and delight washing over their faces at once.

"Whoa! Seafood!"

Ashley's eyes lit up like fireworks. All her tiredness vanished instantly as she rushed forward, taking an exaggerated, deep breath, practically vibrating with excitement.

"This smells incredible!"

"I can't believe we're eating a full seafood spread in the middle of all this! Paisley, you're amazing! My sister can do literally anything!"

She couldn't help herself, grabbing a piece of white lobster meat with her fingers despite the heat, blowing on it twice before popping it straight into her mouth.

The sweet, tender meat melted on her tongue, pure freshness flooding her mouth, not a hint of anything off, just rich, clean flavor.

Ashley's eyes went soft with satisfaction.

"This is so good! So fresh! What kind of magic is this?"

"Best seafood I've ever had in my life."

Thomas and Spencer walked up next, taking in the spread with wide-eyed appreciation.

Even ordinary meat had become a luxury since the world fell apart. Fresh seafood was practically a myth. Getting to eat like this felt almost obscene.

"Pais is really spoiling us."

Spencer shook his head, smiling, warmth all over his face.

"I'd bet money nobody else in Stillwater's eating like this tonight."

Thomas smiled and stepped up to help set the table, laying out plates and serving rice.

Elijah came in last, his gaze sweeping over the table of steaming, beautifully prepared seafood before settling on Paisley, who was drying her hands off at the sink. Something soft crossed his expression.

Marcus, who'd already finished tidying the kitchen, watched the lively scene with a rare warmth settling behind his usually calm eyes.

Everyone took their seats, no formalities, and dug in.

The garlic scallops were tender and full of flavor, every bite juicy. The stir-fried crab had soaked up the sauce perfectly, savory and sweet all at once. The steamed lobster kept its natural texture, springy, firm, with a sweet finish.

Everyone ate with real enthusiasm, praise flying around the table.

Ashley talked with her mouth full, cheeks stuffed, not slowing down for a second.

"This is the best seafood I've ever had in my life. Nothing before the apocalypse even comes close."

Orion ate at an easy pace, watching everyone's happiness with a soft look.

"My sister really can do anything. This is amazing. Thank you."

Marcus, who rarely went for seconds, couldn't help reaching for a few more pieces, something stirring quietly in his chest. Traveling with Paisley's group meant moments like this — peace and comfort most people could only dream of in a world like this.

If not for her, he wasn't sure he'd even still be alive.

He was glad he'd found her.

Elijah ate with his usual poise, every movement measured, but each bite deliberate. Every so often, his gaze would drift toward Paisley, eating quietly beside him, and something deep and soft settled in his expression.

The whole room filled with warmth and laughter, the rich smell of the meal hanging in the air.

Outside, the night stretched barren and dangerous, full of unseen threats. Inside, warm light, a full table, and good company made for a rare kind of peace.

Everyone ate until they were full and content, the exhaustion and tension of the past few days melted away by one perfect meal.

Paisley ate slowly, savoring the sweetness of the shrimp, a deep calm settling over her.

The small want that had gone unmet for so long, back in the Lennox house, had finally been fulfilled.

No more waiting on anyone's permission, no more holding back. Good food, people who cared about her, a safe place to live, and the security to never worry again.
