Chapter 224

One of the older directors pressed a hand to his brow, voice tight with regret.

"If I'd known this mission was this dangerous, I never would've sent Elijah Blackwood and Orion Thompson out there."

"If something happened to them because of us, none of us are walking away from the fallout."

Another official, face drawn tight, pressed further.

"Are you certain no one made it back at all?"

No one answered. Silence filled the room.

The man slammed a hand on the desk and stood, urgency in his voice.

"That's not acceptable. Pull together a team and send support immediately.

If there's even a chance someone's still alive down there, we can't just give up on them."

The rest of the room echoed him, worry thick in their voices.

"He's right. We need proof either way, dead or alive.

If they really are gone, we'll never be able to explain this to the people above us."

The senior director turned to Chase and Rachel, voice firm.

"Chase, Rachel, you two know that aquarium's layout better than anyone. Lead a rescue team back there now. There's a real chance survivors are still alive."

Chase's stomach dropped. He had to stop this. What if they really had survived and were waiting on rescue?

Where did that leave him?

He was the only one who knew exactly what he'd done.

"Sir, it's too late. They're all dead.

Even if we go back now, all we'll find are bodies."

He faked a note of caution.

"The lab's buried right under the aquarium. Whatever's down there is strong enough to wipe out an elite squad. Sending more people in is a suicide mission. There's no point."

The directors' faces only grew more grim at that.

"If an entire elite squad really was wiped out, how are we supposed to explain this up the chain..."

Most people in the base had no idea what this really meant, but the senior leadership understood exactly how bad this was.

Elijah Blackwood and Orion Thompson weren't ordinary squad members. They were the direct heirs of the Blackwood and Thompson families, two of Aetheris's most powerful bloodlines, groomed from birth to take control of their respective houses.

If those two had died out here, in a backwater base like this, it wouldn't stop at the base leadership's heads rolling. The whole region would feel the full fury of two of the most powerful families in the country. No one wanted to imagine where that would end.

The office sat choked with tension, the air itself feeling hard to breathe.

Just then, a calm, cold voice cut through the room from the doorway, shattering the gloom instantly.

"No need to trouble yourselves with a rescue."

Elijah stepped through the door first, posture straight as a blade, soaked in blood but radiating authority, his presence filling the room the instant he entered.

His gaze swept over Chase and Rachel, both of them gone pale, cold as ice.

"We're back."

Right behind him came Orion, Paisley, and the rest of the squad, filing in one by one.

Every one of them intact, a few minor wounds between them, spirits clearly fine.

Their return shattered every word Chase and Rachel had spun.

Chase's carefully constructed weakness slipped. He stared at the group, cursing silently.

These people were impossible to kill. His whole plan was in ruins.

His plan had gone up in smoke. All he could do now was improvise.

Rachel's chest felt just as tight, jealousy and bitterness clawing at her as she watched Paisley walk in without a scratch.

Paisley's luck really was something else, surviving all this.

The two of them knew their lie was done for. Neither dared push for credit anymore. They scrambled for an excuse to leave.

Chase spoke first.

"Sir, I've got something to take care of. I'll head back and rest."

Rachel followed right behind him, voice hurried.

"I'm exhausted too. I should rest as well."

Both of them were desperate to get out of the room before they slipped up and gave themselves away entirely.

The senior staff had no attention left to spare for the two of them.

Seeing Elijah, Orion, and the whole squad walk back in safe, every tense nerve in the room finally eased, a wave of relief washing through everyone present.

As long as these two were safe, nothing else mattered.

The lead director gathered himself and spoke.

"Elijah, Orion, stay a moment. The rest of you have had a long day. Get food in the mess hall, then go rest."

At ten that night,

Paisley's group headed to the mess hall as instructed. Dinner service had technically ended hours ago,

but the base's leadership had already alerted the kitchen staff, who'd rushed back in specifically to prepare a late meal for them.
