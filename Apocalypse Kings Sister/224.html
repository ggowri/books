Chapter 224

One of the older leaders frowned hard, full of regret. "If we'd known this mission was this dangerous, we never should've sent Elijah Blackwood and Orion Thompson out there.

"With who they are, if something happens to them here, every one of us is going down for it."

Another official, his face tense, pressed in a low voice, "Are you saying none of them are coming back?"

No one answered, and the room went dead silent.

He slammed his hand on the desk and stood up, anxious. "No. Pull a team together and send backup right now!

"If there's any chance someone survived, we can't give up on them!"

The other leaders looked worried and agreed. "That's right. Dead or alive, we have to bring them back.

"If they really are dead, there's no way we can explain this to the people upstairs!"

The head of the base turned to Chase and Rachel and gave the order in a deep voice. "Chase, Rachel, you two know the aquarium best, so lead the way right now and get to the survivors as fast as you can. They're definitely still alive!"

Chase panicked inside. He had to stop this, because what if they really weren't dead and were waiting to be rescued?

And where would that leave him?

Only he knew what he'd really done.

"Sir, it's too late, they're all dead!" Chase blurted. "Even if we head over now, all we'll find are their cold bodies!"

He pretended to be scared. "The lab was built right under the aquarium, and the mutants down there are terrifying. Even an elite team couldn't beat them, so taking more people down there is suicide, and there's no point!"

The leaders' faces darkened even more. "If the whole elite team really got wiped out, how are we supposed to explain this to the higher-ups..."

Everyone else had no idea what was really going on, but the few leaders in high positions knew exactly how bad this was.

Elijah and Orion weren't ordinary team members at all. They were the direct heirs of the Blackwood and Thompson families of Aetheris, the future leaders those two top families had carefully raised.

If those two VIPs had an accident and died in a small place like Rivergate Base, it wouldn't just be the base's top brass who couldn't handle the fury of those two great families. Even all of Rivergate couldn't. None of them dared to imagine what would happen to them.

The mood in the office was unbearably tense, so heavy it was hard to breathe.

Just then, a calm, cold male voice suddenly rang out clearly from outside the door and broke through the gloom in the room.

"There's no need for any of you to send a rescue team."

Elijah was the first to walk into the office. He stood tall and straight, and even though he reeked of blood, he carried himself with commanding authority. His natural presence filled the whole office at once.

His icy gaze swept over Chase and Rachel, who had both gone white. "We're back."

Orion, Paisley, and the rest of the team walked in one after another right behind him.

All of them were healthy, with only a few minor injuries, and they seemed to be in decent shape.

Their return ripped Chase and Rachel's lies to shreds.

Chase couldn't keep up his fake weakness anymore. He stared at the group and cursed under his breath.

These fuckers were impossible to kill. They'd survived even this and ruined his whole plan, so now all he could do was play it by ear.

Rachel felt just as sick to her stomach. When she saw that Paisley had actually come back without a scratch, jealousy and bitterness ran wild inside her, and her eyes went dark with resentment.

Paisley sure had nine lives. Even this hadn't killed her.

The two of them knew their lie had been exposed, so they didn't dare try to claim credit anymore and hurried to make excuses to leave.

Chase spoke first. "Sir, I've got something to take care of, so I'll head back and rest."

Rachel quickly followed, sounding rushed. "I'm tired too. I'll go rest."

They were both in a total panic, terrified that if they stayed one more second they'd slip up, and they practically ran out of the office.

The leaders didn't have the energy to notice anything strange about them right now.

When they saw Elijah, Orion, and the whole elite team back safe with their own eyes, everyone could finally breathe again. They let out a huge sigh of relief, like a weight had been lifted.

As long as those two VIPs were okay, everything was fine!

The head of the base quickly pulled himself together and spoke. "Elijah, Orion, you two stay here. The rest of you have been at it all day, so go grab something to eat in the cafeteria and get a good night's sleep."

******

At 10 p.m., Paisley and the others headed to the base cafeteria as arranged. Dinner time was long over, but the base leaders had called ahead, and the cafeteria staff had rushed back to cook a meal just for the team.
