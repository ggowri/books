Chapter 233

After a quick wash, with night settling deep over the base and most of its lights dimmed to a few scattered glimmers, something caught her attention.

The screen on her wrist-mounted personal terminal lit up suddenly, an urgent private message arriving from the base's top leadership.

The message was brief and direct.

[Urgent task: five patients suffering severe psychic contamination are currently under base care. Standard treatment has had no effect. Requesting your intervention. Reward: 10,000 contribution points.]

Paisley's gaze stayed level as she scanned the message. Without hesitation, she accepted the task.

She grabbed a plain mask off her desk and pulled it on, covering most of her face, leaving only her clear, cool eyes visible. Dressed in simple black, she headed out the door toward the hospital's treatment ward.

******

The five severely contaminated patients were all fighters who'd fought on the wall that day,

their minds infiltrated by the dark, corrupted mist carried by the sea mutants, leaving them disoriented and violent. Standard treatment couldn't touch the corruption rooted deep in their minds, and left unchecked, they risked losing themselves entirely, turning into something no longer human.

Paisley stayed calm and composed, drawing on her Plant Affinity's healing power, letting gentle threads of energy sink into each of the five minds, precisely isolating and purifying the corrupted darkness lodged there.

Within minutes, all five unconscious patients had stabilized and returned to normal.

Compared to the last time, this round was effortless for her, barely a strain at all.

The base director said,

"Thank you, Ms. Lennox. I genuinely don't know how we'd repay this."

He transferred ten thousand contribution points to her without hesitation.

The personal data panel on her screen refreshed automatically, revealing a new total.

[Total contribution points: 125,690]

Staring at the six-figure sum glowing on her screen, Paisley's dark eyes gleamed brighter still.

She glanced toward a nearby security camera.

"A fair trade. I trust you'll keep your word and keep this strictly confidential."

The director glanced at the camera too, his voice carrying genuine respect as he assured her.

"Of course. Lewis, escort Ms. Lennox back to her quarters right away."

Looking past her own strength, and with the backing she clearly had behind her now, no one in their right mind would dare cross this little powerhouse.

That would be suicide.

A guard stationed nearby stepped forward immediately, ready to lead the way.

Paisley waved him off casually.

"No need. The base is safe. I can get back on my own."

With that, she turned and walked off, composed and unhurried, disappearing into the night.

A night of rest passed, and dawn broke the next morning, sunlight cutting through the clouds and casting warm light over everything, clearing away the lingering chill of yesterday's smoke.

The group ate breakfast early and gathered their belongings, arriving right on time at the base's newly completed temporary airstrip.

A large military transport plane sat waiting on the open, flat stretch of tarmac.

But as they approached, their steps all stopped at once.

Chase Sullivan, Jasper Quinn, and Rachel Lennox, along with Owen, Reid, and Ryan Lennox, stood quietly off to one side of the airfield, clearly preparing to board as well.

Paisley's brow tightened slightly.

They stuck like glue, showing up wherever she went.

Were these people really tagging along all the way to Aetheris too?

Elijah caught her displeasure right away and turned to the base officer who'd come to see them off, his tone cooling.

"What's going on here? Why are they here?"

The officer's chest tightened at the question. He quietly wiped a bead of sweat off his forehead, privately marveling at how much presence this young leader carried, even asking a calm question felt heavy. No wonder people avoided provoking him.

He explained quickly, voice deferential.

"My apologies, Colonel. This came down from above this morning, an emergency authorization letting them travel with your group. It's a last-minute addition, and we're just following protocol."

Once it came from the top, there was nothing anyone lower could do about it.

Orion's face went cold immediately, frost settling over his expression, his voice carrying an open warning, loud enough for Rachel's group to hear clearly.

"If you're tagging along, keep your heads down. Don't test us. It's a long trip, and we wouldn't mind leaving someone behind halfway there."

"And if something happens along the way, that's not exactly something we'd be able to control."
