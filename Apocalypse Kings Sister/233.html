Chapter 233

After Paisley washed up, it was already late at night. The whole base had gone quiet, with only a few scattered lights still flickering.

Just then, the screen of the base terminal on her wrist suddenly lit up, and an urgent private message from the base's top leadership popped up.

The message was short and to the point.

[Urgent task: The base has taken in five patients with severe psychic contamination, and scans show standard treatment isn't working. We're asking you to step in and treat them. Reward: 10,000 contribution points.]

Paisley glanced over the message calmly and accepted the task without any hesitation.

She grabbed a plain face mask off the table and put it on, covering most of her face so only her clear, cool eyes showed. Dressed in a black casual outfit, she pushed the door open and hurried to the hospital's treatment area.

The five badly contaminated patients were all team members who'd fought hard defending the base that day. The sinister black mist left behind by the mutant sea beasts had invaded their minds, leaving them confused and violently out of control. Ordinary treatment couldn't remove the darkness rooted in their minds, and they could be fully infected at any moment, turning into monsters with no sense left at all.

Paisley stayed calm and composed. She used the healing power of her Plant Affinity, letting gentle threads of energy sink into the five patients' minds and precisely stripping away and purifying the sinister contamination inside.

In just a little while, the five unconscious team members slowly returned to normal.

Compared to the last time she'd healed someone, this time was easy, and she didn't break a sweat.

The head of the base was deeply grateful. "Ms. Lennox, thank you. I honestly don't know how to repay you."

He transferred 10,000 contribution points to her without a second thought.

The personal data panel at the top of the screen refreshed automatically and showed her new total.

[Total contribution points: 125,690]

Paisley stared at the six-figure balance on the screen, and her eyes lit up even more.

She looked up toward the security camera in the distance. "It's a fair trade. I hope you'll keep your end of the deal and keep this strictly confidential."

The head of the base glanced at the camera and promised her with deep respect. "Of course. Lewis, escort Ms. Lennox back to rest right away."

As far as the head of the base was concerned, her skills alone were enough, and she had her background on top of that. Who would dare mess with this little princess? That would be suicide.

A guard on standby nearby stepped forward right away to lead the way.

Paisley waved her hand casually. "No need. The base is safe, and I can get back on my own."

With that, she turned and left, walking calmly until she disappeared into the night.

******

After a night's rest, dawn broke. The morning sun pierced the clouds and spread warm light everywhere, chasing away yesterday's smoke and chill.

Everyone ate breakfast early, packed up, and got to the base's newly built temporary airfield right on time.

A large military transport plane sat quietly on the open, flat airfield.

But as they got closer and looked at the people waiting up ahead, they all stopped in their tracks.

Chase, Jasper, and Rachel, along with Owen, Reid, and Ryan, were waiting quietly on one side of the airfield. They were obviously getting ready to fly back too.

Paisley frowned slightly.

They were like gum stuck to her shoe, turning up everywhere she went. Were they going to follow them to Aetheris too?

Elijah noticed right away that Paisley was annoyed and turned to the base captain who'd come to see them off, his expression guarded. "What's going on? Why are they here?"

The officer's heart skipped a beat. He quietly wiped the light sweat from his forehead, thinking to himself how intimidating this young big shot was. Even a calm question carried so much pressure, so no wonder nobody dared to look him in the eye when he was angry.

He quickly explained, very respectfully. "Sorry, Colonel Blackwood. This came down from above this morning as a last-minute approval, giving them special permission to fly back with your team, so we're just following the rules."

Since it was a decision from the base's top leaders, nobody else had the right to change it.

Orion's face went cold, his expression icy, and he made his warning loud and clear on purpose so Rachel's group could hear every word. "If you're coming along, behave yourselves. Don't come looking for trouble, or it's a long trip, and we wouldn't mind tossing you out halfway there.

"If some accident happens then, well, that won't be something we can control."
