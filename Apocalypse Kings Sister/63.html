Chapter 63

Mason frowned and opened the door. Three furious figures stormed into the living room, bringing the pouring rain in with them.

It was the three Lennox brothers, Owen, Reid, and Ryan.

All three of them looked dark and hostile, and the air around them felt heavy. The second they walked in, they zeroed in on Paisley.

They'd just seen Jasper come back alone, and Rachel was nowhere to be found.

No matter how many times they asked, Jasper just dodged the question and kept his mouth shut.

Nine people had gone out together, and only Jasper had come back.

Panic and rage hit all three brothers at once.

As far as they were concerned, Rachel was sweet, innocent, and lovable, and it was Paisley's job to protect her every second of the day.

They marched right up to Paisley and surrounded her, looming over her. They were arrogant and pushy, clearly there to tear into her.

Owen spoke first. He sounded arrogant and entitled, and he didn't bother hiding the accusation in his voice.

"Paisley, where's Rachel? Didn't she go out with you guys?

"Everybody else made it back safe, and she's the only one missing? Did you ditch her on purpose?"

Reid jumped in right after him with a cruel smirk. He looked at her with open contempt, and every word was meant to cut her down.

"You really think you're hot shit now? You think you can just cut ties with the Lennoxes and do whatever the hell you want?

"Looking after Rachel has always been your job! You're her sister!

"She's sweet and innocent, and she never causes trouble. If she's missing, it's a hundred percent because you went after her on purpose!

"So let me ask you something. What kind of sister are you? How can you be so damn heartless?"

Ryan spoke last. His voice was cold and hard, like he was giving an order nobody could argue with, and every word was a threat.

"I don't care what happened out there. Go back out right now and find Rachel.

"If she's missing so much as a hair on her head, you'll pay for it with your life, and that still won't be enough!"

The three of them fed off each other, climbing up on their high horse to pile on the pressure. Like always, they dumped all the blame on Paisley and tried to push her around.

The tension in the room shot up.

Elijah, Orion, and Mason turned cold at once and pulled Paisley behind them. They glared at the three brothers, ready for anything.

Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer came over to stand beside Paisley too, their eyes burning with anger.

But Paisley stayed perfectly calm in the face of their bullying and threats. All she showed was cold indifference and mockery.

She looked up lazily and glanced at the three smug faces in front of her. Then, with a cold little smile, she spoke lightly, but every word cut deep.

"Oh, Rachel? She's probably still down by the river near the food reserve outside the city, all alone and stuck in the storm."

She paused, deliberately slowing down, and her voice dripped with sarcasm.

"Aren't you guys the ones who love her and protect her the most? Haven't you been going on and on about how worried you are?

"Better hurry, then. If you're lucky, she might still be alive when you get there. If you're too late, well, who knows?

"After all, it's way out in the middle of nowhere, and there are zombies everywhere..."

The second she finished, the living room went eerily, dead silent.

The anger and swagger froze on Owen, Reid, and Ryan's faces. The hostility drained out of their eyes fast, replaced by hesitation and fear.

Outside, the rain was pouring down in sheets, thunder was booming, and the night was pitch black. Zombies roamed everywhere, and high-level mutant beasts could be lurking around any corner. Going out to look for Rachel would mean risking their lives, and they'd be lucky to make it back.

A minute ago, they'd stormed in demanding to save Rachel and make Paisley pay with her life. Now, not one of them dared to take a single step outside.

All their love and protectiveness had only ever been talk. They were only brave enough to yell at Paisley, guilt-trip her, and look down on her.

The second it meant actually risking their own necks, all that love disappeared.

All that was left was selfish, hypocritical cowardice.

Paisley saw the cowardice and calculation in their eyes. The last ridiculous shred of hope she'd held onto disappeared, leaving only a bone-deep chill and contempt.
