Chapter 411

Watching her stay so calm, Elijah let out a breath.

He spoke softly, with a gentle invitation. "I've got some free time. Want to go for a walk? The base looks different since the rebuild. You've barely seen it since you got back."

"We leave tomorrow. We can pick up a few things we'll need for the mission too."

Paisley had been bored at home for days. At that she opened her eyes, a small smile in them.

"Sure. Let's go."

Elijah smiled, warmth rising in his eyes, and he helped her up carefully.

They got ready, walked out of the villa side by side, and strolled down the clean streets.

The rebuilt base had wide, smooth streets, rows of shops, and busy crowds. Affinity users and ordinary residents moved through it all. It was lively, and the bleakness of the apocalypse had faded into something steadier and almost prosperous.

Elijah slowed to match her pace and pointed out shops, base rules, and landmarks as they went, patient and thorough.

He was tall and slim in a simple black outfit, and his cold, noble air stood out even in a crowd. He drew every eye.

Soon two young women who were shopping together recognized him.

They stopped. Their eyes lit up, and they whispered excitedly.

"Oh my God, that's Elijah Blackwood! The captain of Falcon Squad. The Blackwood heir!"

"It's really him! I've never seen him this close. He's so good-looking, and he's got such a presence."

One of them was braver. She smoothed her clothes, took a deep breath, and hurried over, cheeks flushed, voice nervous.

"Captain Blackwood! I'm a big fan, I really admire you! Could I get your number? Someday I'd love to fight alongside you."

Elijah didn't slow. His eyes passed over her, and he turned her down without a pause.

"Not a good idea."

The air around him cooled. He was distant and unmistakably firm.

His eyes never left Paisley. He had no attention for anyone but the person next to him.

The woman nodded awkwardly, embarrassed, and backed away disappointed. She didn't dare bother him again.

Passersby watched and murmured.

Everyone knew Elijah was cold and aloof. He never gave special treatment to anyone and never got tangled up with women.

And now, following his gaze, everyone finally looked at the girl at his side.

She was slim and graceful, her features cool and delicate, her air clean and unworldly. Even in plain clothes, her presence was impossible to hide.

She walked calmly through the noisy crowd, unhurried, with a quiet confidence that stood out from everyone else.

Someone studied her for a moment, then suddenly caught his breath and blurted out, loud enough to cut through the murmurs.

"That's Paisley! The one who just came back to the Thompson family!"

His words tumbled out, full of awe. "She's the one who took down a Level Seven mutant trumpet flower by herself, outclassed all the veteran Affinity users, and even got Jasper Quinn, the most arrogant guy around, to back her!"

The street erupted.

People who had only glanced over stopped, and the crowd thickened fast. For a moment the busy street went quiet, and then the chatter surged even louder.

"So that's her! I'd only heard the rumors. I never saw her in person!"
