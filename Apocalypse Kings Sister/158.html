Chapter 158

A wave of lethal danger crashed down. Paisley didn't waste a second — she went all out, instantly.

Level Six Plant Affinity surged through the ground. Thick, barbed, poison-laced vines tore up through the floor like a swarm of serpents, lashing out at the zombies in a single coordinated strike.

They never had a chance to react before the vines locked them in midair.

Paisley gripped the Frostbloom Sword and moved, weaving through the crowd.

Every strike landed exactly where it needed to — the crystal core, right at the base of the skull. One zombie after another dropped, cores pulled clean, no wasted motion.

Within seconds, dozens of Level Four zombies lay dead on the ground.

******

The Level Five wolf-man watched its entire pack get wiped out in a blink. Its eyes flashed with terror, then fury, and a low growl rumbled through the sealed room.

Smarter than any ordinary mutant beast, it sensed the danger and didn't linger — it twisted its massive body, gathering strength to break through the vines and flee.

Too late.

The vines surged, faster now, locking around its legs and ankles. Thorns bit into its flesh, pumping paralytic poison straight into its bloodstream, freezing it in place.

Cornered, the wolf-man let its power loose all at once — a sharp, violent roar tearing from deep in its throat.

Brutal cold burst out from its body, and Ice Affinity swept the whole basement.

Mist rolled through the room. Frost raced across every surface — floor, walls, debris — all of it locking under a thick layer of ice. The warehouse turned into a frozen cell in seconds.

The cold hit without warning. Paisley froze mid-step, encased. Kitty, too slow to dodge, got caught in a thin layer of ice, unable to move.

The vines shattered under the frost, and the wolf-man, free again, let out a breath of relief. It raised its massive head, red eyes gleaming with triumph.

As far as it was concerned, its Ice Affinity could freeze anything. The human and her little cat were already as good as dead. One kick to crack her skull, and this would be over.

That confidence lasted less than ten seconds.

Crack —

A sharp sound rang out.

Fine cracks spread across the ice around Paisley, and the next second it shattered completely, shards scattering in a burst of spiritual energy.

She stood there, untouched, not a trace of frost on her clothes.

No one understood how she'd done it.

The moment the ice broke, she pushed off the ground, closing the distance in a blink. The Frostbloom Sword flashed, and one clean strike took the wolf-man's massive head clean off.

Hot blood sprayed. The head hit the floor, the triumph in its eyes going dark instantly.

Paisley crouched and pulled a flawless Level Five Ice Affinity crystal core, cold to the touch, pure blue, from the skull.

Kitty shook off the ice clinging to her fur and bounded over, meowing softly, looking up at the core in Paisley's hand with open hope.

Paisley's mouth curved. She flicked her wrist and tossed the rare core to the cat.

Kitty leapt and caught it cleanly, hugging it to her chest, gnawing at it happily.

Same affinity. Perfect fit for her next level-up.

With that done, the basement fell quiet, nothing left but zombie remains and the fading chill of the dead cold.

Paisley didn't linger. She walked toward the private office at the far end.

Unlike the chaos around it, this room looked untouched — solid door, clean walls, no sign of any zombie ever getting close. It had the feel of something deliberately sealed off.

She gripped the cold handle, pushed, and the old lock cracked and gave way. The heavy door swung open.

Inside was simple — a dusty desk, two old chairs, and in the corner, a tall, solid black safe, alloy plating thick with anti-theft etching. The source of everything Trovie had sensed.
