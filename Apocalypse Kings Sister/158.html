Chapter 158

Deadly danger closed in around her. Paisley didn't dare let her guard down, so she unleashed her full power at once.

Her Level Six Plant Affinity surged out and raced across the floor. Countless thick, thorny, poisonous vines burst up from the ground like giant snakes and whipped toward the zombies.

Before the zombies could even move, the vines had them all trapped in midair.

Paisley gripped the Frostbloom Sword and darted through the horde, her movements quick and hard to follow.

Her blade was precise and deadly. Every strike went straight for the crystal core in a zombie's skull, and one Level Four zombie after another went down as she dug out their cores in one smooth motion.

Moments later, dozens of Level Four mutant zombies lay dead on the floor.

The Level Five werewolf had watched its whole pack get wiped out in seconds. Its savage eyes filled with fear and fury, and a deep growl boomed through the closed-off basement.

It was far smarter than an ordinary mutant beast. The moment it sensed danger, it didn't stick around to fight. It twisted its massive body, gathering its strength to break through the vines and escape.

It was too late. The poisonous vines spreading across the floor shot up even faster and wrapped tight around its legs and ankles. Their sharp thorns stabbed deep into its flesh, pumping paralyzing poison into its blood until it couldn't move an inch.

With nowhere left to go, the werewolf suddenly unleashed its power, and a vicious roar tore from deep in its throat.

Bitter cold exploded out of its body, and a wave of Ice Affinity swept through the entire basement.

Icy mist billowed everywhere, and freezing ice spread fast. The floor, the walls, and all the junk lying around were sealed under a thick, solid layer of ice, and the whole warehouse turned into a frozen hell in seconds.

The sudden freeze caught them off guard. Ice swallowed Paisley where she stood, and even the little white cat, too slow to dodge, was trapped in a thin layer of ice, unable to move.

The frozen vines snapped and shattered. Free again, the werewolf relaxed and raised its huge head high, its red eyes gleaming with smug arrogance.

As far as it was concerned, its top-grade Level Five Ice Affinity could freeze anything. The human and the little beast in front of it were sitting ducks, and all it had to do now was kick her head in to end this.

But its smugness lasted less than 10 seconds. Suddenly, a loud, sharp crack split the air.

Fine cracks raced across the ice encasing Paisley. A second later, it exploded, and tiny shards of ice scattered in a gust of energy.

She stood tall without a speck of frost on her clothes, as if the ice had never touched her.

Nobody knew how she'd pulled it off.

The instant the ice broke, Paisley pushed off the floor and shot forward. The Frostbloom Sword gleamed with cold light, and with one clean stroke, she cut off the werewolf's massive head.

Hot blood gushed out as the huge wolf head rolled across the floor, and the smug light in its eyes went dark for good.

Paisley crouched and deftly pulled a flawless Level Five Ice crystal core from the wolf's skull. It was a clear, glowing blue and freezing cold to the touch.

The little white cat shook off the ice clinging to its fur and bounded lightly to her feet. It gave two soft meows and tilted its little head, staring hopefully at the blue core in her palm.

Paisley smiled and flicked her wrist, tossing the rare Level Five Ice core right to it.

The cat sprang up and caught it neatly in its mouth. It wobbled a little, then happily hugged the core to its chest and started gnawing on it to absorb the energy.

Since the cat had an Ice Affinity too, this core was perfect for leveling it up.

With that, the basement fell silent. All that was left were zombie corpses and a fading, sinister chill.

Paisley didn't linger. She headed for the private office at the far end of the basement.

Unlike the mess all around it, this office looked untouched. The door was thick and solid, the walls were clean, and there wasn't a single sign of zombies. The whole room had a secretive, heavy feel to it.

She gripped the cold handle and pushed. With a sharp crack, the old lock broke, and the heavy wooden door slowly swung open.

The office was simple, with a dusty desk and two old chairs. In the corner stood a tall, heavy black safe made of rock-hard alloy and covered in anti-theft grooves. That was where the treasure Trovie had sensed was coming from.
