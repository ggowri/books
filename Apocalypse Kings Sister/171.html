Chapter 171

All along the way, Paisley had led them out of hopeless situations and turned the tables more times than they could count.

Right now, she was their only hope.

Ashley's eyes were red, and her voice shook with panic.

"Paisley, what do we do? It's about to attack again, so let's run! Staying alive is all that matters, and we can't beat this thing!"

"She's right, Paisley! Fighting this thing head-on is suicide!" Spencer agreed in a low voice, looking worn out and helpless.

"It's way too strong, and we're no match for it. We need to fall back and save ourselves!"

Thomas, Mason, Marcus, and Madison all nodded. Right now, all they wanted was to run and stay alive.

But just then, an eerie rustling came from behind them.

Everyone spun around, and their hearts sank even deeper into despair.

They were surrounded by a sea of flowers. Swarms of lesser mutant carnivorous flowers had already cut off their way back.

Rows of waist-high mutant blooms opened their petals, fangs bared, venom dripping, closing in tighter with every second.

These lesser carnivorous flowers were just as poisonous and vicious. Their vines tangled together into an airtight cage of poison flowers, trapping the group right in the middle.

They couldn't go forward or back, and there was no escape.

A fresh wave of poison seeds tore through the air, so thick it blocked their view. Deadly purple-black mist surged toward them with a strange, sweet, rotten smell, filling the air all around them.

Death was no longer some distant threat. Bone-deep terror spread through every limb and squeezed every heart.

A moment ago, they'd still had a way out. Now, that last chance was gone, and every one of them broke down.

Ashley was shaking like a leaf. She pressed tight against Paisley's side, gripping so hard her knuckles turned white, her teeth chattering. It's over, and we're really going to die here...

Spencer, usually so steady, had sweat soaking through the back of his shirt. His chest heaved, but he didn't dare take a deep breath, terrified of breathing in even a trace of the poison mist.

The determination in his eyes was gone, leaving only helplessness and a cold, sinking feeling. There's no way out, and we're dead for sure.

Thomas was usually sharp and careful, but now his whole body went stiff and cold, and his mind went blank.

He'd never felt this hopeless before. Are we really going to die?

Was there any hope left at all? He'd never get to bring Paisley home to meet their grandparents and parents now. He'd broken his promise.

Even if he wanted to protect Paisley and get her out, he just didn't have the strength.

Marcus, Madison, and Mason sank into total despair. They couldn't hold themselves together anymore, so they squeezed their eyes shut and stopped fighting.

Marcus, always the one to find a way, took one last long look at Paisley and accepted what was coming.

Madison couldn't see any future ahead. Tears slid down her face, and she regretted that she hadn't ended up with Orion.

Mason, who'd never been afraid to die, gave up for the first time in his life. His arms and legs went stiff and numb as he quietly waited for the poison to eat away at him and end it.

They could barely breathe, and their hands and feet were ice-cold. The heavy shadow of death hung over all of them, and every second of waiting was pure torture.

But in that life-or-death moment, Paisley stood her ground, still calm.

She knew she couldn't panic now. If she did, they'd all die here.

She hadn't expected this mission to be so tough either.

They were trapped here because of her.

None of them had ever blamed her, but she was responsible for getting everyone out safely.

Paisley asked urgently in her head. [Trovie, how many points for an all-purpose ward?]

Trovie's sweet voice answered right away. [Master, it costs 5,000 points.]

Paisley frowned, speechless for a moment, and shot back coldly. [Trovie, are you seriously price-gouging me in the middle of a crisis? Why is it so expensive?]

Trovie explained earnestly. [But this all-purpose ward lasts forever, Master!

[It's expensive for a reason. You only buy it once, and it blocks all poison, physical attacks, and affinity damage!]

The piercing whistle of the poison seeds grew closer and closer. The storm of seeds and thick poison mist were about to swallow them all, and their lives hung by a thread.

Paisley couldn't take the gamble, not with everyone's lives on the line. It hurt to spend the points, but she had no choice.

She gritted her teeth and gave the order without hesitation.
