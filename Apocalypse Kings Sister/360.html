Chapter 360

A faint, disbelieving light stirred in his hollow eyes, his weak frame trembling slightly. Even through the pain, something in him caught fire with the desperate hope of a man clinging to life.

Not just him — Mrs. Blackwood, Elijah, and her mother all held their breath too, eyes locked on Paisley's hands, not daring to make a sound.

Only Ashley stood calm, pure trust in her eyes.

Before anyone could react, Paisley had already begun.

She called on the healing power of her Plant Affinity.

A faint green glow spilled from her palm, pure Plant energy carrying the cleanest purifying force, flowing gently but insistently into Nathaniel's meridians, pressing deeper with every passing second.

It began to cleanse the poison lodged deep in his organs.

The green light traveled along his veins, clearing every blocked passage it passed, purifying the cold, rotten poison on contact.

The poison had no defense against energy this pure, breaking apart almost instantly.

At the same time, agony surged through his whole body.

Cleansing the poison out felt like fire searing every meridian, needles driving through every inch, tearing pain spreading through every limb.

His whole body convulsed, cold sweat breaking out across his forehead, his already pale face going translucent, teeth clenched against a strangled groan.

His fists clenched so hard his knuckles went white, muscles twitching uncontrollably.

He'd remembered Paisley's warning, but hadn't expected it to hurt quite this much.

He gritted his teeth and endured it anyway.

Mrs. Blackwood's chest clenched watching it, hands wringing together, knuckles white, aching with worry, but she didn't dare interrupt, only stood by watching the two of them on the couch.

Elijah's jaw was tight, his body leaning in slightly, worry plain on his face, though he kept silent throughout.

He knew exactly how much it hurt. He'd been through this himself before.

Like having your bones and sinew rebuilt from scratch.

The faint green light kept flowing steadily around him, its soft glow slowly driving back the poison soaking through his body.

Paisley's focus never wavered, her eyes calm and clear, her touch steady as she kept channeling the purifying force, bit by bit.

As the minutes passed, the sickly dark pallor faded from his face, color slowly returning to his skin, his ragged breathing gradually steadying out.

The poison that had defeated every expert in the base for over three weeks was visibly, unmistakably being wiped out.

The last traces buried deep in his marrow dissolved into fine black wisps under the pure Plant energy, drifting out through his pores.

The bone-deep agony faded fast, replaced by a gentle warmth that spread through his whole body, repairing the damage the poison had left behind.

His rigid body finally began to loosen, his clenched fists unfurling, his shoulders relaxing fully at last.

He let out a long breath, days of exhaustion and gloom clearing from his eyes, his chest opening up, breath coming easy and long — like climbing out of an ice-cold lake, reborn.

"I think... I'm better. My body feels so light. Is it really done?"

He looked at Paisley gratefully, asking.

A moment later, Paisley drew the faint green glow back into her palm, her own aura settling.

A faint trace of fatigue crossed her eyes from the sustained effort, but her tone stayed calm.

"The poison's completely gone, root and all. All that's left now is rest — your body will recover on its own from here."

He opened his eyes fully, the old clarity and steadiness back in them, nothing left of that clouded weakness.

He looked at the girl in front of him, awe, gratitude, and respect all plain on his face, struggling to rise and thank her properly.

"Thank you, Pais... you saved my life. What you've done for me, for this whole family — I owe you more than I can ever repay."

Paisley wiped the sweat from her brow.

"Don't mention it, sir."

Only now did everyone else, who'd held their breath the whole time, let themselves breathe again.

******

Mrs. Blackwood pressed a hand to her chest, eyes burning red, weeks of despair, fear, and torment finally collapsing all at once, grief and joy tangled together inside her.
