Chapter 44

Elijah's eyes went cold. He aimed for the thickest cluster of vines and unleashed a torrent of lightning straight into it!

With a loud crack, blinding blue light filled the dark storeroom, and wild currents of electricity raced through the vines. The thrashing vines went stiff and convulsed, and their attack stalled as their leaves curled up and sizzled.

"Mason, cover our flanks and our way out! Orion, you're with me!" Elijah barked.

While the vines were stunned, he charged forward, lightning coiling around him as he blasted a path through the tangle.

Orion was right behind him, his machete flashing as he hacked through the stiff vines at their base. Every vine he cut hit the floor and melted into a foul green sludge that smoked.

But the Level Three pothos was insanely hard to kill. Whether the vines got fried by lightning or chopped off, fresh shoots grew back within seconds and came at them even harder.

And it wasn't just the plant. All the noise had woken up something else, and a hunched, vicious Level Three zombie slowly stepped out from the shadows behind the shelves!

With poison vines on one side and a zombie on the other, the four of them were pinned at the storeroom door. They couldn't go forward or back, and things had just gotten really dangerous.

They were stuck in a sealed basement, fighting a plant and a zombie at once. There was nowhere left to go.

The Level Three Venom-Devouring Pothos was way scarier than any ordinary mutant zombie.

It had been mutating under the hospital for days, and it was fully transformed now.

Its thick main stem was rooted in the middle of the room. Countless wrist-thick vines crisscrossed the walls, floor, and ceiling, weaving an airtight web of death.

The vines were coated in a sticky, dark green layer of venom, and every one bristled with sharp thorns. A drop of corrosive poison glistened on the tip of each thorn.

When the venom dripped onto the floor, it hissed and sent up black smoke. If it touched skin, it wouldn't just eat through flesh. It would paralyze the nerves on contact.

Worst of all, it could regenerate like crazy.

Blades and lightning couldn't kill it. As long as there was a tiny scrap of vine or root left, it would sprout new growth within seconds and attack again. It never ended.

At the same time, the Level Three zombie lumbered out of the shadows.

It was tall and hunched over, and its whole body was covered in a hard, rotten crust like armor. Ordinary blades and bullets couldn't get through it.

Its sharp black claws gleamed, and every step sent ripples through the water on the floor. It reeked of rot, and it lunged straight at the group's flank.

They were in serious trouble.

Orion swung his machete and hacked through several vines coming at him. Then, he caught the Level Three zombie charging in out of the corner of his eye. His stomach knotted, and he had no choice but to turn and take it head-on.

Beside him, Mason kept chopping at the vines, but they just kept growing back.

But no one noticed a thin, poisonous vine hiding in the shadows on the ceiling. It shot down without a sound!

The vine was as thin as a finger and blended right into the shadows. It streaked like a blur straight at the middle of Mason's back, loaded with deadly paralyzing venom!

"Mason, look out!"

Mason heard a faint whistle by his ear, but it was already too late to dodge.

The high-level vine's crushing power washed over him. His eyes went wide, and a deathly chill flooded his whole body.

His arms and legs went stiff and numb, and he couldn't move. He couldn't even lift his machete to defend himself. All he could do was watch the deadly vine close in!

In that split second, a brilliant wave of green light exploded and lit up the whole dark storeroom!

Paisley suddenly seemed like a different person.

She finally unleashed the Plant Affinity she'd been hiding. Pure, powerful life energy exploded out of her, laced with the will to kill, and it flooded the entire basement in an instant.

It was the overwhelming power of someone who could command every plant in the world.

Just like that, everything turned around.

The vicious Level Three zombie suddenly froze. Its bloodthirsty, cloudy eyes glazed over, all its killer instinct drained away, and its limbs started to tremble.

Countless thin green vines burst up out of the mud under its feet and wrapped around its arms, legs, body, and neck. They squeezed down so hard that it didn't have a prayer of breaking free.
