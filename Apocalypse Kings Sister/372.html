Chapter 372

Grant, especially.

He'd just watched with his own eyes as Paisley killed the flower in one strike, and the fear that had gripped him moments ago vanished instantly, leaving only a wild, burning mix of shame and resentment.

He figured the mutant trumpet flower's reputation outstripped its actual threat. If a Level Six like Paisley could kill it so easily, then surely he, the base's top Level Six talent and captain of the Scarlet Squad, could do the same.

The humiliation of being forced toward death by Chloe moments ago vanished under a wave of arrogance. Desperate to save face and outdo Paisley, determined to win the wager, he acted.

"That's all it takes? I can do that too."

Grant let out a cold snort, shaking off the last trace of hesitation. Ignoring his teammates' warnings, he shot forward, straight into the thick grass beside the silo, locking onto another hidden Level Seven mutant trumpet flower.

This particular flower was tucked away in the densest, most toxic pocket of the field, its petals half-closed, mist churning silently, a hidden danger that Grant, desperate to prove himself, completely overlooked.

He raised his hand and unleashed his Wind Affinity at full force, a sharp gust of wind-blades screaming out, slashing toward the flower's stem, his posture arrogant, certain of victory.

But the instant his wind-blade touched the petals, a thick cloud of purple mist erupted violently, engulfing him completely.

Unlike Paisley's frigid blade, which had frozen and overpowered the toxin outright, Grant's attack didn't just fail to hurt the plant, it enraged it.

The flower's massive petals snapped open and shut in a blur, too fast to track, lunging toward him with horrifying, devouring force.

Grant's pupils shrank. His mind went hazy in an instant, his head spinning as the paralytic toxin took hold.

His whole body locked up, and he watched, helpless, as the massive flower disk came at him, unable to dodge in time.

In that split second, pure combat instinct, honed over years of fighting, forced him to twist aside, just barely avoiding being swallowed whole.

But the next moment, an agonized scream tore through the battlefield.

"AAAHHH!"

The massive petals clamped down and tore clean through, ripping his entire right arm off at the shoulder.

Blood sprayed out in an instant, soaking the ground beneath him. Searing pain and paralytic numbness swept through his entire body.

Grant staggered and collapsed, his face bone-white, drenched in cold sweat, the mangled, bloody stump of his arm a horrifying sight.

Half a second slower, and he would have been swallowed whole, nothing left of him at all.

Moments ago, he'd been arrogant, vowing to kill the mutant flower himself. Now, the star captain lay there, gravely wounded and utterly defeated.

The field fell dead silent.

Every member of the Scarlet Squad froze, the last flicker of false hope inside them shattering. Fear washed over every face, a chill sinking into their bones.

They finally understood.

It wasn't that the mutant trumpet flower was easy to kill. It was that Paisley was just that strong.

"Help... someone help me..."

The searing pain sent convulsions through Grant's body, his face nearly translucent with pallor, his voice a hoarse, shaking whisper.

Hearing their captain's wretched cry for help, the Scarlet Squad rushed forward without hesitation.

But in their rush to reach him, they forgot entirely that the whole area was blanketed in invisible toxin.

The instant they stepped into the trumpet flower's miasma, they all went down, dizzy, limbs weak, a fine numbness spreading across their bodies, their combat strength cut in half in an instant.

Fighting through the nausea and dizziness, they gritted their teeth and hauled the convulsing, agonized Grant up between them, dragging him toward the vehicle without stopping, stumbling the whole way.

The rough, jolting retreat only made his wound bleed worse, his face growing paler by the second, the pain nearly enough to knock him out.

They finally managed to drag him into the vehicle and slam the door shut, sealing out the toxic mist and the chaos of battle outside, but the cabin inside was just as grim and silent.

Standing off to one side, Chloe looked down coldly at Grant, mangled and in agony, feeling not a shred of sympathy, only deep, thick disgust.

Pathetic.

She used to think Grant was the pinnacle of the younger generation at the base, strong, steady, reliable, worth attaching herself to, worth standing behind.

But after watching Falcon Squad's full strength today, especially the terrifying power behind Paisley's single strike that had crushed a Level Seven mutant flower, she finally saw clearly.

You didn't know what a real gap looked like until you had something to compare it to.

Grant was weak, barely worth mentioning. The so-called star captain, the so-called top prodigy, nothing but a frog at the bottom of a well, all reputation and no substance.

This level of weakness wouldn't just fail to help her, it would drag her down, hold back her future, indefinitely.

As the base's strongest Affinity user, she deserved the best treatment, a place on the best team.
