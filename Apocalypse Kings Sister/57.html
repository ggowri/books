Chapter 57

"There's a big bottled water plant five minutes' drive from here."

He looked at Paisley beside him. "You still have room? Water's a must, and it'd be a shame to miss out."

Paisley kept her eyes on the fluffy little white cat in her arms, gently petting it.

"My dimension's only a third full. After the storm, every water source is going to get contaminated or dry up, and nothing can replace bottled water. Let's go."

Orion and Mason were all for it, of course.

The four of them hopped in the car, and a few minutes later, they pulled up at the plant's front gate.

As soon as they got close, they heard loud cracks like thunder, along with the raspy, savage snarls of a zombie.

The plant's iron gate was locked tight. They stood on the roof of the car and used binoculars to see what was going on inside.

A few survivors in ragged clothes were huddled in a storage room at the very back of the building, pushing hard against the door. Their faces were white, and they were shaking all over. They looked like they'd been stuck there a long time and were about to lose all hope.

Outside the storage room's iron door stood a lean, agile mutant zombie, crackling with tiny bolts of electricity.

It was a Level Three Lightning Affinity zombie!

Electric arcs sizzled and popped all over its body, blue sparks kept shooting across the floor, and the air reeked of burning sulfur. Every hit it threw came down like a lightning strike, and the damage was brutal.

This lightning zombie was blocking the only exit, and it had the survivors trapped inside.

When the people inside heard the car, they started screaming for help. "Is anyone out there? Please, help us..."

Mason couldn't stand it. "Don't worry! We're getting you out of there!" he called back.

Then, he jumped down from the car, ready to fight.

Paisley loosened her arms and gave the little white cat a gentle nudge. "Let the cat handle this."

The kitten got the message right away. Its soft, cuddly act vanished, and a freezing mist rose around its body.

In the blink of an eye, the snow-white kitten shot up into a 16-foot frost giant. Its icy fur bristled, its slitted ice-blue eyes turned sharp and cold, and waves of freezing air rolled off it as its power exploded outward.

The Level Three lightning zombie sensed the overwhelming pressure and flew into a rage. Lightning flared all around it, and it raised a hand and hurled a massive bolt that split the air with terrifying force!

But the evolved ice cat could crush anything at its level, and it was insanely fast.

Before the bolt even hit the ground, the giant cat leaped forward in a blast of freezing wind. It slammed one paw right down on the lightning zombie's head.

Its extreme cold snuffed out the electricity around the zombie. The overwhelming ice energy crushed every last trace of its lightning power!

It only took one hit. The Level Three lightning zombie, so vicious and tough a second ago, froze stiff and dropped. Its crystal core had been ripped cleanly out of its head, and it was dead for good.

It was a total beatdown!

With the threat gone, the giant cat shrank back down in the blink of an eye. In no time, it was a tiny snow-white kitten again, trotting back to Paisley's feet with the crystal core in its mouth.

It kept rubbing its little head against her pant leg and gazing up at her with big, shiny ice-blue eyes, begging to be praised. Its soft little mews were adorable.

Paisley bent down, scooped it up, and took the Level Three Lightning core. She gently ruffled its fluffy fur and smiled.

Orion, Mason, and Elijah were speechless all over again.

Today had been one eye-opener after another.

A Level Four Ice Affinity cat that could grow or shrink at will, with power like that? It had one-shot a high-tier Level Three zombie, then dug out the core and begged for praise like a pet. How was that even possible?

With a mutant like this on the team, it was like having an insanely powerful ace up their sleeve!

Mason hurried over to the iron door. "The Level Three zombie's dead. You can come out now."

The trapped survivors carefully opened the door and peeked out. When they saw what had happened, their faces lit up with shock and pure joy.

They'd been trapped in there for three whole days, scared out of their minds. They'd given up hope and were sure they were going to die. None of them had ever thought anyone would come to save them.

They rushed out, grabbed the rescuers' hands, and shook them hard. "Thank you so much for saving us! Thank you!"

Paisley gave a small nod and didn't say much.

The survivors looked at them with awe and gratitude. One of the older men stepped forward and eagerly invited them.

"Our town is just up the road. You saved all our lives, and we can never repay you for that. Please, come back with us and be our guests, and at least let us show you how grateful we are!"
