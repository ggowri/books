Chapter 388

Bullying her family, stealing from her home, hurting her mother, this debt, she was keeping track.

But she sensed her mother's mood wasn't quite lifted yet.

Maybe...

"Mom, do you want to awaken an Affinity?"

"Do you want to get your own revenge, in person?"

Diane looked at Paisley, startled.

"Pais, I'm just an ordinary person. How could I possibly awaken an Affinity?"

"Only one in a hundred people ever awakens."

Paisley pulled a bottle of spirit water from her Storage Dimension and handed it to Diane.

"Mom, drink this. It'll be good for you."

Diane stared, stunned, at the clear glass bottle Paisley held out, the water inside pure and glowing faintly, a soft shimmer clinging to it. Just being near it brought a wave of soothing, refreshing vitality.

She hesitantly reached out and took it, and the moment her fingers touched the bottle, a warm sensation spread slowly through her whole body.

"This... what is this water?"

Diane asked quietly.

"Spirit water. Drinking it might let you awaken an Affinity."

Paisley's gaze stayed gentle as she watched her. "Mom, don't be afraid."

Diane asked nothing more, though the grievance and resentment still lingered in her chest.

She took a deep breath, unscrewed the cap, and drank the entire bottle of spirit water in one go.

The water tasted clean and sweet going down, sliding into her stomach before transforming into a hot, gentle current that swept through every meridian and vessel in her body in an instant.

At first, it was just a warm, pleasant sensation. But moments later, tremors began rippling through her body, her bones and flesh felt as though they were being reshaped by some outside force.

Diane's whole body trembled faintly, her fingertips going numb, a faint metallic sheen slowly spreading across her palm.

Paisley stayed quietly by her side, silently channeling her Plant Affinity life force to protect Diane's body, helping to steady the energy surging within her and prevent the awakening process from doing any harm.

Diane felt a thunderous roar inside her head, as though some barrier that had sealed her for years had just shattered.

A dense, concentrated metallic force surged up from deep within her, flowing through her meridians and spreading through her entire body. Her fingers twitched slightly, and a faint golden metallic light appeared in her palm, hard and sharp, carrying the distinct, keen edge unique to Metal Affinity.

Her eyes went wide with disbelief as she stared down at her own glowing golden palm, her voice trembling.

"Pais... I, I awakened an Affinity! It looks like Metal!"

Metal Affinity was both offensive and defensive, capable of forging, breaking through armor, and backed by overwhelming raw strength.

The corner of Paisley's mouth curved into a faint smile.

"Congratulations, Mom. Metal Affinity, of all things."

"Mom, you're amazing!"

Diane clenched her fist, a flash of golden metallic light sparking at her palm, a faint metallic hum rippling through the air.

The grievance of being shoved and humiliated at the supply depot, of having their supplies stolen, in this moment all crystallized into pure resolve.

She'd only been able to endure and back down before. But now, she was no longer just an ordinary person with nothing to defend herself with.

"I... I can actually go get justice myself now."

The timidity faded from Diane's eyes, replaced by a glint of determination.

Paisley nodded, pulling a stash of low-tier crystal cores, all Level Two and Three, from her Storage Dimension, ones she'd tossed in there without much thought before.

Added together, there were at least five or six hundred of them.

"Mom, let's level you up first."

"Just find somewhere quiet, hold the crystals, and absorb the energy inside. That'll boost your level."

"I'll go to the base administration office to pull the depot's surveillance footage and get proof. Once we have evidence, the base's rules are on our side. Then we go to your uncle's house."

"This time, we're not just getting our stolen supplies back, Brenda's going to pay for putting her hands on you."

Diane's jaw dropped at the sight of the crystal cores piled in front of her, eyes wide with disbelief.

She'd lived at the base long enough, done the shopping often enough, to know exactly how precious crystal cores were.

Resources were scarce in the apocalypse, prices sky-high, a single ordinary Level Two crystal core could be traded for a bit over two pounds of well-marbled pork, or a whole chicken, or five and a half pounds of rationed rice, enough to feed an ordinary family for several days.

A typical Affinity user, risking their life hunting zombies all day, might come home with three to five low-tier cores and consider it a lucky day.

But her daughter, without even trying, had just handed over five or six hundred of them, piled up and gleaming, that many!

And beyond that, awakening an Affinity at all was unbelievably rare.

Across the entire base's population of tens of thousands, only a handful had ever successfully awakened. That rarity was exactly why Affinity users held such high status, so revered, even a Level Three Water user like Brenda could throw her weight around, bully ordinary people, and get away with it.
