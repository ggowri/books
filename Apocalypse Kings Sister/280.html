Chapter 280

Madison snapped back to focus and threw up her Water Affinity, a thick barrier of water forming behind the group, thinning the drifting poison and buying them precious time.

"Ashley, Marcus, seal the path!"

Orion barked the order, one hand still gripping Paisley protectively, sprinting flat out for the exit.

The two of them threw up their Earth Affinity at once.

Heavy slabs of rock and soil shot up one after another, sealing off the passage behind them, trying to buy a moment's delay against the serpent's pursuit.

The next second, a thunderous crash ripped through the air. The serpent's tail swept through with brutal force, and the solid wall shattered instantly, unable to slow it at all.

Mason pushed his Wind Affinity to the limit, a gust lifting the group and boosting their speed while hurling a storm of grit straight into the serpent's eyes to blind it.

Thomas's Plant Affinity surged wildly, vines tearing up in a frenzy to wrap and bind the serpent's body, trying to pin down its massive form.

But the creature, closing in on Level Eight, was simply too powerful. One violent shudder snapped every vine taut around it, debris scattering everywhere, nothing able to hold it back.

Just as the group neared the exit, the serpent's eyes flared crimson. It tore free of the vines and the lightning net, launching forward like an arrow from a bow, its massive jaws snapping open, fangs gleaming cold, aimed straight at Elijah at the back of the line.

In that split second, a cool, composed figure wheeled around and planted her feet.

"Go on ahead!"

Paisley wrenched free of Orion's grip, her eyes turning to ice, Ice-Water Affinity exploding to life around her.

A wave of brutal cold swept the entire passage. Countless razor-edged ice shards formed out of nothing, raining down in a dense volley, each one aimed precisely at the serpent's weak points — its seventh vertebra, its eye sockets.

At the same time, green energy pulsed in her palm, her Plant Affinity's parasitic ability slipping quietly into motion, threads of vine-energy finer than hair slipping through the gaps in the serpent's scales.

The serpent screamed in pain and fury, rearing back, cracks spreading across its armored scales where the ice had struck, dark blood seeping out.

Elijah seized the opening to fall back, his Lightning Affinity flaring again, striking hard into the crack, deepening the wound.

"Pais!"

Orion's chest tightened. His Metal Affinity surged to full power, a swarm of razor-edged metal fragments hovering around him before launching straight at the serpent's seventh vertebra.

But Paisley had already timed her move. Her voice came out cold and clear.

"Keep this up and the whole place is coming down. Get outside, it'll be easier to fight it in the open!"

The group understood at once and gave up ground, sprinting out of the underground armory without looking back.

They broke through the sealed passage one after another, emerging into open ground.

A cold mountain wind swept through, clearing away the lingering stench of poison and rot.

But a massive crash followed right behind them.

The Level Seven serpent, covered in wounds of all sizes, had gone fully berserk, crimson slit eyes burning with murder as its massive body smashed through the entrance, scales grinding against stone, sending up a storm of dust and rubble.

The group formed up into a tight formation, ready to fight.

"Everyone, stay sharp. Let's finish this!"

******

Standing at the front, Orion bellowed the order, golden light surging around him, a layer upon layer of hovering blades locking onto the serpent's weak points before launching.

Elijah unleashed his Lightning Affinity, silver-blue bolts cracking across the sky, thick pillars of lightning striking down with precision into the serpent's open wounds, electricity crawling through its veins, deepening every crack in its armor.

Thomas's Plant Affinity spread across the entire mountainside, the vegetation surging wildly, countless thick, tough vines rooting into the stone and coiling tight, layer after layer binding the serpent's massive body.

Spencer hurled blasts of fire into the air, scorching the serpent's flesh wherever they landed, countering its cold, poisonous nature.

Mason's Wind Affinity swept the battlefield, hurling sharpened debris at the creature.

Ashley and Marcus threw up heavy stone barriers around the group, blocking the venom and debris flying off the serpent, keeping everyone safe.

Madison condensed sharp blades of water, striking repeatedly into the serpent's wounds, deepening the damage.

Everyone worked in perfect sync, pinning the near-Level-Eight serpent down in the valley.

The serpent roared in agony, thrashing wildly, its scales cracking further under the combined assault of lightning, metal, fire, and vines, black blood pouring out and staining the rocky ground red.
