Chapter 169

A light swipe of her paw and the frozen bodies cracked open, each one revealing a perfectly intact core.

A quick flick of her tongue, and the core was gone, tucked away.

A few cracking sounds, and the ice-frozen corpses crumbled apart, scattering frost and remains across the ground.

Nothing that tried to block their path survived.

One controlled the field, one wielded the blade — perfect sync.

Mist drifted along the garden path, the cold replacing the once-oppressive dread with something clean and sharp.

Every low-tier zombie lurking nearby cowered back under the weight of that icy pressure, too scared to risk coming closer.

Behind them, Spencer and the others stayed close, watching the flanks and rear, clearing out any stray threat that tried to slip in.

With cat and swordswoman clearing the way, they moved fast, unopposed.

Before long, the ruined brush thinned out, and a massive, unsettling field of flowers came into view.

******

At the same time, a pressure far heavier than anything before crashed down over the whole area, thick with the smell of rot and something sickly-sweet.

At the center of the field sat the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower, its size genuinely monstrous.

Thick, twisted dark green roots sank deep into the ground, spreading across the entire field, coated in a sticky green venom.

Massive petals peeled open, lined with rows of sharp teeth, the flower's center dripping corrosive venom that sizzled and smoked wherever it landed.

Every plant in the field had been warped by the thing's poison. The air itself hung still and cold, a fine purple mist drifting through it, every breath carrying the threat of death, locked squarely on the intruders.

The pressure alone made it hard to breathe.

Ashley's face went white. She ducked behind Paisley on instinct, fingers digging into her sister's sleeve, trembling, voice thick with tears.

"Paisley, I'm scared..."

She wasn't alone. Spencer, Thomas, and Mason all went rigid, fear plain on every face.

"God, what is that thing?"

Mason's throat bobbed as he forced down a swallow, voice shaking.

The Level Six flower towered over them, rivaling the height of the ruined Ferris wheel nearby, its roots sprawling across the entire field, its gaping, fanged center a yawning void of menace.

"It's as tall as the Ferris wheel... this is insane."

Thomas's eyes went wide, disbelief plain on his face.

"Can we actually kill that?"

Everything they'd fought until now — the zombies, the Level Five wolf-man — suddenly felt small next to this. Just the pressure rolling off it was nearly too much to stand against.

And it was loaded with poison, head to root.

Floating overhead, even Trovie looked stunned, wings frozen mid-air, its little mouth hanging open in shock.

[Host, this thing is way too big... is this really just Level Six? It's completely off the charts!]

Paisley stared down the massive, venomous horror, her usual composure finally cracking. She muttered a complaint to Trovie in her head.

[I'm starting to think we got a bad deal on this one.]

[This thing's a nightmare. Poison spores, poison sap, poison seeds — one mistake and it's game over. This difficulty's way past what the listing said.]

Trovie rushed to reassure her.

[Host, don't panic! I'm putting in a request for extra compensation right now! Finish this mission and I'll bump the reward by five thousand points — fifteen thousand total, plus a bonus premium lucky draw!]

[That's more like it.]

Paisley, satisfied, let her focus sharpen.

[Trovie, tell me — how do we actually kill this thing? Regular attacks and affinities are probably useless against it.]
