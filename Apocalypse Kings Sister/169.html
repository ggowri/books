Chapter 169

With one light swipe of its paw, it cracked the frozen bodies open, revealing round, flawless crystal cores.

It expertly flicked its tongue and scooped up each core.

With a few sharp cracks, all the frozen zombies shattered, scattering chunks of ice and body parts across the ground.

Every zombie that dared to jump out and block their way was killed on the spot. The cat controlled the field, Paisley handled the blade, and the two of them worked in perfect sync.

Wisps of icy mist drifted with the wind along the path through the flower field, and the creepy, deathly stillness gave way to a clean, biting cold.

Every low-tier zombie lurking nearby cowered back under the weight of that icy pressure, too scared to risk coming closer.

Spencer and the others followed close behind, covering the blind spots on both sides and at the back, and picking off the occasional straggler that tried to follow. With Paisley and the cat clearing the way, they moved fast and met no resistance.

Before long, the ruined brush thinned out, and a massive, unsettling field of flowers came into view.

At the same time, a presence far more terrifying than any beast before it crashed down over the whole area. The cold, damp smell of rotting plants mixed with a sickly-sweet, poisonous scent hit them in the face, making it hard to breathe and shaking them to the core.

The Level Six mutant carnivorous flower loomed in the middle of the field, so enormous it was terrifying.

Thick, twisted dark green roots sank deep into the ground, spreading across the entire field, coated in a sticky green venom.

Its huge petals spread open layer upon layer, lined with rows of sharp fangs. Murky, highly corrosive venom dripped from its center, hissing and smoking wherever it hit the ground.

Every plant in the field had been twisted by its poison. The air was still and cold, filled with a fine, pale purple poison dust drifting silently everywhere. The whole place radiated deadly intent aimed straight at the intruders, enough to make anyone's skin crawl.

The crushing pressure kept building, and an ordinary person would've struggled just to breathe here.

Ashley's face went pale. She ducked behind Paisley without thinking, clutching the hem of her shirt and trembling uncontrollably. "Paisley, I'm scared..." she whimpered.

She wasn't the only one. Spencer, Thomas, and Mason all turned grim, fear filling their faces as their bodies tensed and shook.

"Oh my God, what kind of monster is that?" Mason swallowed hard, his voice shaking.

The Level Six carnivorous flower towered over them, as tall as the giant abandoned Ferris wheel nearby, so huge it blotted out the sky. Its thick, twisted roots covered the whole field, and its hideous petals gaped open like giant jaws.

"It's as tall as the Ferris wheel... th-this is terrifying!" Thomas stared in disbelief. "Can we really kill that thing?"

The zombies and the Level Five werewolf they'd fought earlier were nothing compared to this giant. Just the pressure leaking off it was almost too much for them to handle.

On top of that, its whole body was full of deadly poison.

Even Trovie looked stunned as it floated in the air, its little wings frozen midair and its round little mouth hanging open in shock.

[Master, this thing is way too big... Is this really Level Six? It's way off the charts!]

Paisley stared at the huge, poisonous flower, and her cool expression turned grim. She grumbled to Trovie in her head.

[I'm starting to think we got a bad deal on this one.]

[This thing is way too creepy, and it's poisonous all over. Its poison dust, venom, and seeds are all deadly, so one slip and I'm dead. This mission is way harder than advertised.]

Trovie rushed to calm her down.

[Master, don't worry! I'm asking my higher-ups for a bonus right now! If you finish this super hard mission, you'll get 5,000 extra points for a total of 15,000, plus a premium lucky draw!]

Satisfied, Paisley let it go. [That's more like it.]

Her eyes glinted. [Trovie, so tell me, how do we kill this Level Six mutant carnivorous flower? Normal affinities and physical attacks probably won't work on it.]
