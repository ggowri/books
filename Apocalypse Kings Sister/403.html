Chapter 403

To Dennis, Paisley was just a pretty face who had climbed up by flirting. Jasper was acting on a whim. Once the senior branch lowered itself and came to the door, the junior branch would have to help, out of family loyalty if nothing else.

Father and daughter set out in silence. It was a twenty-minute walk.

Inside Gerald's villa, the lights were warm and the air smelled like dinner. It was nothing like the cold quiet of Dennis's house.

Ever since Paisley had come home, life for the junior branch had gotten better every day. The tight, scraping days were gone, and the food and supplies had visibly improved.

Tonight's dinner was especially generous. The table was covered with stewed chicken, braised pork, pan-seared fish fillets, and sautéed vegetables, all of it rare in the apocalypse.

Most of the ingredients were supplies recovered from the senior branch that afternoon, enough to feed the family for a month.

Diane was in the best mood she'd had in years. The grievances she'd swallowed for so long had cleared in a single day. She had awakened the rare Metal Affinity, and she had publicly crushed Brenda, who had bullied her for years.

Her face was soft and glowing, with a quiet new strength in it.

A bottle of red wine had been opened, and the deep red caught the light in the glasses.

Diane raised hers, smiling. "Today's a good day. It's the day our family came back together. Everyone, cheers!"

The whole family sat around the table, warm and easy. Paisley sat among them, straight-backed and calm. Today she had helped her mother win back her dignity and her supplies.

Gerald smiled, deeply relieved. Since the apocalypse began, his worry had never let go. Now it finally had.

Paisley, Gerald, Orion, Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer all raised their glasses. The clink rang through the warm living room, and everyone laughed.

Then a sharp, heavy knocking broke the peace.

Bang, bang, bang.

It was loud and pushy, an uninvited kind of knock. The laughter faded.

Who would come at this hour?

Paisley's eyes cooled and dimmed.

Gerald frowned and set down his glass. "I'll get it."

He pulled open the door.

Dennis stood outside in a formal jacket dusted with night dew, his face dark and calculating. Beside him, Chloe looked wrecked, her clothes stained with wine, her hair messy, her eyes red.

The night wind blew in and scattered the smell of dinner.

Dennis showed no sign of a man begging a favor. He held his head high, playing the stern elder.

He walked in before Gerald could say a word. "Gerald, sorry for the late visit, but I had no choice. I brought Chloe to ask you to step in and let your sister-in-law go."

"Brenda is still locked up. And look at Chloe. This is what you've done to her."

Every word was arrogant, and he didn't apologize for any of it. He spoke as if the junior branch owed them.

Chloe took her cue right away.

She lowered her eyes. Her lashes shook, hiding the envy and humiliation underneath. Her eyes filled with tears, and she looked like a poor, fragile thing who had been wronged.
