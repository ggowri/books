Chapter 383

Feeling the surging, heavy Affinity coursing through her, Paisley's lips curved into a faint, almost imperceptible smile. She was more than satisfied.

The next moment, Trovie's soft, bouncing voice rang out.

[Congratulations, Host! You got stronger again! You're so amazing, Host!]

Trovie floated in the middle of the system space, spirit light flickering all around its round little body as it bobbed up and down, gazing at Paisley with pure adoration.

Paisley, in a relaxed mood, tapped her finger lightly and held out a crystal-clear fruit-flavored lollipop to the floating little guy.

Trovie caught it happily, peeled off the wrapper, and popped it into its mouth, sweetness washing through its whole body instantly.

It wiggled its round little body, its tone carrying a note of pouting hope.

[Host, look at my body, it's still half see-through. I still can't become solid.]

It tilted its small face up, speaking earnestly.

[System rules say I can only fully materialize once you reach Level Five trader. Then I can really stand beside you, stay with you all the time, instead of being stuck in this space! Host, you've got to work harder! Trovie really, really wants to stay by your side!]

Looking at the little creature's eager, innocent expression, Paisley's gaze softened. She pulled up the system's points panel.

A cold white light screen appeared, a line of clear numbers displayed in front of her.

[Current total points: 2,425,360!]

Reaching Level Five trader required five million points, still a long way off.

Paisley sighed.

[Trovie, this is going to take a while. Not even halfway there yet.]

Hearing this, the little guy, so full of energy moments ago, drooped its head instantly, ears sagging, eyes rimmed red, letting out small, soft, pitiful sobs, dripping with disappointment.

Just then, a new connection notification popped up at the top of the system interface.

[Modern-timeline trader requesting cross-dimensional connection. Accept?]

Paisley's gaze paused briefly. She gathered herself, tapped the screen without hesitation, and accepted.

The dimensional channel connected instantly, and an eager, fast-talking woman's voice came through at once, barely containing her excitement.

[Hello! Pais, you're amazing!]

[Thank you so much! I've been waiting for you to come online!]

Paisley's tone stayed easy.

[Hello!]

The modern trader on the other end was clearly thrilled, rushing to share her progress, her voice sincere.

[Ever since I drank the spirit water you traded me, my whole body's changed so much! My constitution's several times stronger, I'm full of energy all the time, and those chronic issues I've had for years, the neck pain, the insomnia, the headaches, the constant fatigue, they're all completely gone!]

[This spirit water isn't some regular supplement. The effects are unbelievable, totally worth it! I'd love to trade with you long-term!]

The camera shifted slightly, and through the dimensional screen, Paisley caught sight of three massive modern warehouses lined up neatly behind the woman, doors wide open, packed to the brim with supplies, rice, flour, cooking oil, daily necessities, emergency goods, all stocked in staggering quantities.

Clearly, this woman had poured everything she had into gathering a mountain of trade goods in a very short time, full of genuine effort.

Paisley felt a flicker of surprise. She hadn't expected the woman's turnaround to be this fast.

It had only been four days.

Still, it was a shame, this woman lived in a modern world with no Affinity at all. Even drinking the spirit water, she couldn't break the dimensional rules and awaken an Affinity.

But even without awakening, the spirit water's effect on the body, refining the flesh, curing chronic conditions, boosting overall health, was enough to drive ordinary people wild with desire. No wonder she was this eager to trade again.

Paisley responded evenly, then laid out her next round of terms.

[Good to hear it's working.]

[Since you're serious about continuing to trade, these nine bottles of spirit water are the final payment.]

With that, Paisley opened the trade channel and sent over nine bottles of clear, shimmering spirit water, faint traces of spiritual energy curling off them.

The dimensional trade completed instantly, the goods arriving without a hitch.

The modern trader caught the bottles right away, unscrewing one eagerly and tipping her head back for a sip.

Cool, sweet spiritual energy slid down her throat and spread through her entire body, instantly washing away the exhaustion of days spent gathering supplies, her whole body feeling light and refreshed, energy surging through her.

Her eyes lit up, her tone turning even more fervent and earnest.

[This feels amazing! Even better than last time! I'll take as much of this spirit water as you've got! Whatever supplies you need, I don't care how much, I'll get it for you! What do you need this time? I'll make sure you get it!]
