Chapter 79

If someone who'd already awakened an ability ate one, it would quickly refill the energy they'd burned in a fight and get them back in shape.

If they ate a few more, there was even a chance they'd break through a bottleneck and level up their ability.

Now that she'd stumbled onto a jackpot like this, there was no way she was letting it slip away.

The whole grapevine, every last mutant grape, and the age-old sacred fungus hidden deep in the forest were all going into her Storage Dimension.

Paisley looked around at everyone in the car.

"I need to take care of something, so I'll be gone for a bit.

"These Level Two mutant grapevines are thorny and annoying, but they won't kill you. Just be careful out here, okay?"

Before anyone could react, she gave the little white cat a light pat on the head.

The cat got it right away and hopped out of the car. A flash of light rippled around its body, and just like that, it grew into a huge, powerful cat six and a half feet tall. Its snow-white fur was thick and fluffy, its legs were strong and agile, and its sharp eyes gave it a commanding presence.

Paisley swung herself up lightly and settled onto the cat's broad back.

The giant cat's paws barely touched the ground as it carried Paisley off into the vine-choked depths of the forest.

Ashley was still in the car, staring with her mouth hanging open, green with envy.

"I can't believe the little white cat can get that big! That's so cool. I want a magical pet like that too!"

She couldn't take her eyes off the giant cat as it disappeared, and she kept gushing about it.

"It's so soft and cute when it's little, but it looks so majestic and gorgeous when it's big! It's such a badass!"

But the second she said it, she frowned with worry.

"Is Paisley going to be okay in there all by herself? What if something happens to her?"

Orion watched Paisley calmly disappear into the distance, and his face turned serious too.

"We have to trust her. Pais never does anything unless she's sure about it.

"Come on, let's go help. Everyone's cars up ahead are tangled up in those mutant vines, and they're getting squeezed tighter by the second. If we wait any longer, they'll all be totaled."

They hurried forward to help.

At the same time, Rachel saw the mutant grapes, and her past life came rushing back to her too.

This is it! This is the place!

Last time around, Paisley had found those two age-old sacred fungi deep in this forest of mutant vines!

Paisley had risked her life to find them right here, and after Reid and Ryan ate them, they'd both awakened abilities.

Greed blazed in Rachel's eyes, and her heart started racing.

The age-old sacred fungus was a priceless treasure!

If she ate it herself, she might even awaken a rarer, stronger, higher-level ability!

And even if it couldn't give her a new ability, something that powerful was bound to be good for her body.

A treasure this rare deserved to be hers. Why should anyone else get it?

Last time, Paisley had gotten her hands on it and then stupidly handed it over to someone else.

What an idiot.

But Rachel was different! She'd never be as dumb as Paisley.

This time, both of those sacred fungi are mine!

She forced down her excitement and greed, then quickly turned toward the car and lowered her voice to talk to Reid and Ryan.

"There's an age-old sacred fungus hidden deep in the forest. It can awaken an ability and completely transform you.

"Everyone's busy fighting up front, and nobody's paying attention to us. Let's hurry in and find it."

Reid and Ryan traded a look, their eyes burning with hunger for abilities of their own.

If they had a shot at becoming ability users, who'd want to stay ordinary?

Owen, meanwhile, was covered in burns. He lay unconscious in the back seat, barely breathing.

None of the three gave Owen a second thought. While everyone else was fighting the vines, they crouched low, slipped quietly past the tangle, and snuck off deeper into the forest.

Meanwhile, the little white cat carried Paisley steadily deeper into the forest.

The mutant cat had a faint barrier around it. Whenever the wildly thrashing mutant vines got close, an invisible force knocked them away.

The cat also gave off a powerful aura of its own. Mutant plants and animals steered clear of it long before it got anywhere near them.

Nothing got in their way the whole time.

The forest grew thicker and thicker.

A little while later, they found a palm-sized age-old sacred fungus growing by the roots of a huge tree. It was in perfect condition and practically bursting with life.

The fungus had a thick, round cap with neat rings and a warm, deep red color. A faint, silvery glow like moonlight surrounded it, and wisps of pure energy kept drifting off it. It was obviously a top-tier treasure, a thousand years old and at the peak of its power.
