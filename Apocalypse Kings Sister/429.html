Chapter 429

Orion shaped his Metal Affinity into sharp golden blades and finished off the rest with his rifle, clean and precise.

Madison's Water Affinity supported the team, steadied everyone, and covered gaps right away.

With the whole squad at mid Level Six, their power erupted. Affinity and guns worked together perfectly, with long-range suppression, close-range cutting down, and defense and control all covered.

The charge of the dense horde was stopped in an instant. The terrifying encirclement was firmly held in place by the squad's top-tier strength and teamwork, and the zombies collapsed in waves.

Smoke rose and flames flew.

Elijah took the lead, dodging a spray of rotten flesh, and said in a low voice, "Pais, let's go."

Paisley's eyes were dark, her cool gaze holding a wariness she couldn't shake.

The high-level gaze from deep in the steel mill hadn't gone away. It had only hidden itself deeper, like a hunter lying in wait, watching their every move.

She moved light and fast and caught up with Elijah. Together they left the battlefield and sprinted deeper into the abandoned warehouse.

As they got closer, the stench of rotting zombies grew weaker. In its place was a cold, sticky, strange smell, with a faint trace of chemicals, that clung to the nose and made their hearts race.

The warehouse walls were rusted and peeling, with large patches of plaster missing to show the dark reinforced concrete beneath. Cracks crisscrossed it like a spiderweb.

The thick iron door hung half open, its edges twisted. The black doorway was like the mouth of some lurking beast, deep and dark, swallowing all the light and radiating a deadly silence.

Elijah pressed his hand to the warped door and pushed. A harsh metal screech cut through the quiet.

The door slowly opened. Inside, the warehouse was huge and empty, stacked with abandoned shelves and broken machines, layers of shadow blocking their view so they couldn't see the whole depth.

They moved without a sound, their steps nearly silent, and scanned the area in the faint light from the entrance.

The floor was strangely clean. There was no dust, no zombie remains, and hardly any weeds. It was too clean, as if someone had cleaned it on purpose.

"Something's wrong," Elijah said quietly, gripping his weapon, his fingers tense, fully alert.

"The horde outside is huge, but the warehouse is spotless. That's no accident."

Paisley nodded. Her vast mental power spread out and covered the whole warehouse in an instant.

But the high-level presence that had locked onto her before stayed hidden, as if it had vanished into thin air, yet it was everywhere. The pressure stacked up and made it hard to breathe.

Just as they moved steadily toward the core of the supply stacks...

[Hisss.]

A long, hoarse, bone-chilling hiss suddenly exploded from deep in the darkness at the back of the warehouse!

The sound was sharp and piercing, carrying a powerful mental shock that made their ears ring and their minds shake.

The abandoned sheets of iron and equipment around them trembled, and dust fell in soft streams.

A moment later, a huge dark shape slowly coiled out and showed itself, filling most of the warehouse. The visual shock hit them hard. Even these two, who'd seen the worst of the apocalypse, felt a jolt of shock cross their eyes.

It was a strange high-level mutant that had never been seen before!

Its upper half was a perfect male human body, pale and firm, with sharp lines and a regular face that had no human warmth. Its eyes were narrow slits, a dark black, cold and dead, showing no emotion, only an inhuman indifference and cruelty.

But from the waist down, it had no human legs. In their place was a thick, long, enormous serpent tail!

The serpent body stretched thirty-three feet, thick and terrifying, covered in layers of black scales, each one hard as iron.

They glittered with a cold metal shine in the faint light, and dark venom oozed from the gaps between them, hissing and eating tiny holes in the floor with white smoke. The poison was deadly.

Its huge tail coiled and stacked and locked every exit of the warehouse, cutting off both their ways out.

Human head, snake body, human and serpent fused together, twisted and strange, utterly unlike any ordinary zombie or mutant beast!

[Level Seven mutant!]

Paisley and Elijah both sank inside at once, instantly judging its level.
