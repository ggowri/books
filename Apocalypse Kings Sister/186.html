Chapter 186

It was the first time Paisley had ever felt like contribution points came this easily.

She didn't hesitate any longer. She looked the head of the base straight in the eye, calm and self-assured. "Okay, I can try to heal them."

Then, her tone shifted, and she firmly laid out her conditions.

"But I have two conditions. First, everyone leaves right now. Nobody else can be in the room while I'm treating them.

"Second, what I do here today stays strictly confidential. I don't want anyone else to know, because I'm not interested in that kind of attention."

Everyone in the room froze, staring at her in surprise.

Most people turned nervous and careful around the head of the base, not even daring to speak up.

But Paisley stood her ground in front of him, calm and unbothered, laying out her terms without a trace of hesitation or fear.

Not many people had that kind of nerve.

The head of the base looked surprised for a second, then understood.

An ordinary kid would've been bowled over by 50,000 contribution points and done whatever he said.

But she'd calmly accepted the reward and still had the nerve to set terms and take charge.

Only someone extraordinary could stay that steady and confident.

She clearly had real skills, and that was where all her confidence and pride came from.

"Agreed." The head of the base didn't hesitate at all. He gave a firm nod and waved his hand, issuing orders right away.

"Everyone, clear the treatment area right now, no lingering! What happens here today is top secret, and no one is allowed to talk about it or spread it around!

"Anyone who does will be punished under military law."

The medical staff and officers all answered and filed out.

In no time, the huge treatment room was empty, and the doors were shut tight, blocking out all prying eyes and noise from outside.

The room was so quiet a pin drop would've echoed. Only the five badly poisoned, barely breathing soldiers and Paisley remained.

Paisley's expression turned serious and focused.

She hurried to the first bed, where Orion lay. His skin had turned the darkest shade of all of them, and his breathing was the weakest.

His face, usually so calm and sharp, was twisted tight. His lips were cracked and bluish-black, and dark veins of poison crawled beneath his skin. What little life he had left was so faint it could slip away at any second.

She immediately pressed her fingertips to his wrist, and right away, she sensed the strange toxin raging through his body.

The toxin was cold and vicious. It clung to him like a parasite, wrapping around his veins and steadily eating away at his organs, and it devoured any power used against it. No wonder all the base's healing methods had failed.

Luckily, her Plant Affinity could counter every kind of dark, evil toxin and contamination in the world.

Paisley calmed her breathing and closed her eyes, slowly releasing her pure mid Level Six Plant Affinity.

A warm, clear green light spread out from her palm. Unlike the thin, faint glow of ordinary healing affinities, hers was rich and full of life, carrying incredibly strong purifying and healing power, and it slowly wrapped around Orion's whole body.

The green light slowly seeped into his body through every pore, gentle yet incredibly powerful, hunting down the black toxin deep in his blood.

Strand by strand, the dark, foul energy was wrapped up, stripped away, and slowly forced out of his body.

The contamination that had run rampant through him dissolved, broke apart, and faded away under her purification.

Five minutes later, Orion's faint breathing slowly grew steady and deep, and the heavy blue-black tinge on his face faded right before her eyes.

His skin returned to its normal color, and his cracked, blue-tinged lips slowly took on a faint, living pink.

The vicious toxin had been wiped out, and her healing Plant Affinity mended everything it had corroded, from his veins to his worn-out organs.

Only after she was sure Orion was out of danger and fully healed did she slowly pull back her power.

She wiped a thin sheen of sweat from her brow, a faint weariness settling over her features from the energy she'd spent.

High-level purification and healing took way more energy than attacking with an affinity.

If her Plant Affinity hadn't already reached Level Six, giving her deep reserves of energy, she never could've purified the toxin so thoroughly.

She pulled a stool over and sat down to rest. With a thought, she took a cup of spirit water out of her Storage Dimension and drank it.
