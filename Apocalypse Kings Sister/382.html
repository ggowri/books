Chapter 382

The thing that struck her hardest was the fully formed, godlike regeneration ability.

Level Six Plant Affinity could only mend flesh wounds and knit broken bone, standard healing. But Level Seven Plant Affinity carried a vastness of life force that could achieve something truly beyond belief: regrowing an entire severed limb.

Even if a body was torn apart, bones shattered, organs ruined, as long as some remnant of the body survived, as long as a single thread of life remained, it could be saved.

An endless stream of Plant Affinity life force could rapidly regenerate flesh, reconnect meridians, and rebuild bone and muscle, pulling a dying body back from the very edge.

Next came the boost to her purification ability.

Her Level Six purification range had been limited, only able to target and remove localized toxins, miasma, and necrotic poison. Now, that range had expanded dozens of times over.

Whether it was the deadly miasma of high-tier mutant creatures, the corrosive malice left behind by zombies, or the faint radiation contamination plaguing the base, everything in her path could now be purified.

But the thing that excited her most was the entirely new kill move her Level Seven Plant Affinity had unlocked.

Paisley curled her fingers slightly, cupping her palm. A seed, fine as dust and nearly transparent, appeared quietly, floating weightless in her hand, so subtle it was almost impossible to spot with the naked eye.

This parasitic seed was pure condensed Plant Affinity, light and intangible, with extraordinary penetrating power. It could bypass most Affinity defenses and physical barriers, silently infiltrating an enemy's body and rooting itself precisely in the depths of their mind.

Once it took hold, the seed would run wild, greedily drawing on the enemy's flesh and Affinity essence, rapidly sprouting and spreading roots and branches through their skull, before finally blooming into an eerie, monstrous flower.

The moment it bloomed was the moment of death.

The victim's head would burst apart under the violent growth of branches, blood and flesh scattering like some grotesque, beautiful firework, nothing left of the body, the death gruesome, inescapable, with no cure.

She liked this way of killing.

Having mastered her Plant Affinity's new abilities, Paisley's gaze steadied, and she moved on to her next upgrade.

The hundred Level Four crystal cores and two hundred assorted mixed-level cores still remaining in her Storage Dimension shimmered with light.

Paisley closed her eyes again, steadying her focus, and turned her attention to the dormant Psychic Affinity within her.

Compared to the gentle, life-giving energy of Plant Affinity, the invisible, colorless power of Psychic Affinity was far more overwhelming and concentrated, hidden deep in the mind, refining the soul, amplifying perception, and commanding all things.

The massive stockpile of cores released a wash of dazzling light, their pure Affinity energy stripped of impurities, refined layer by layer, all converted into the purest psychic force suited for tempering the mind, flooding into her consciousness like a surging tide.

Her Psychic Affinity, which had been sitting at a fully saturated Level Five, began rapidly iterating and expanding under the ceaseless flood of energy.

Her once-cramped mental space was scoured, widened, and thickened by the pure psychic energy, the once-fragile threads of her mind rapidly solidifying, thickening, stretching out, becoming tough and razor-sharp.

The barrier of her Level Five psychic power loosened, crumbled, and shattered layer by layer, without the slightest hitch.

Crack!

The invisible barrier of her realm shattered completely. Her Psychic Affinity broke through, stepping into early Level Six!

The flood of energy kept coming, endless, continuing to nourish and refine her mind and soul without pause.

Her psychic power climbed rapidly again, steadying and solidifying, advancing further still, finally settling at mid Level Six.

The instant she broke through, a vast, overwhelming wave of perception swept through her entire being.

Her psychic range expanded exponentially, its clarity and precision surging many times over.

Every blade of grass, every tree, every person's energy signature, every flicker of Affinity, even the faintest heartbeat within three miles, all of it registered clearly in her mind, nothing hidden from her anymore.

Her soul grew steadily more unshakable, ordinary psychic interference, illusions, and mental suppression no longer had any effect on her at all.

At the same time, her psychic assault, mental restraint, and mind-piercing abilities underwent a massive transformation. A single thought could now invade another's consciousness, shatter their mind, kill without a trace.

And beyond that, anyone with weaker psychic power than her could now be controlled, more than twenty of them at once.

Made to do whatever she wanted.

Mid Level Seven Plant Affinity, with self-healing, purification, and the Parasitize kill move all combined, offense, defense, and support, fully maxed out; mid Level Six Psychic Affinity, with expanded perception and silent lethal mental attacks, plus the ability to control over twenty people at once.

Paisley slowly opened her eyes, her gaze calm and clear.

By now, her strength had long since surpassed every Affinity user of her generation at the base, even against other high-tier Affinity users, she had absolute, overwhelming confidence.
