Chapter 263

On the second floor, three Level Fives roared in response, claws tearing the air, heavy footsteps closing in fast.

The dead-quiet supermarket turned into a cage of killing intent in an instant.

Elijah's face went hard, lightning crackling furiously around him as he turned to face the Level Six head-on.

A massive shadow crashed through the third-floor ceiling, dropping straight to the ground level.

A rare Level Six Plant Affinity zombie, wrapped in rotting black vines, its skin marked with strange dark patterns, its eyes holding something far too human, something calculating.

It had awakened true intelligence, cunning and vicious, far beyond ordinary zombies.

A hoarse, mocking voice scraped out of its throat.

"Living prey... and a mutant Lightning Affinity, no less. Delicious."

Before it finished speaking, the three Level Fives landed in formation around Elijah, sealing off every retreat. Four against one.

******

Elijah, at Level Five peak, was already outmatched facing a Level Six Plant-type alone. Fighting four at once tipped the odds hard against him.

Lightning tore the sky apart, every strike meant to kill, every hit thrown with everything he had.

But the Level Six was too cunning to take a hit head-on, dodging and weaving, grinding down his Affinity bit by bit.

One against four, thirty exchanges and counting.

Thunder, explosions, tearing vines filled the building. Elijah held his ground through sheer skill, but his Affinity was draining fast, and the fight was slipping away from him.

While he was pinned down fighting the three Level Fives, the Level Six's vines lashed out, wrapping around his limbs and waist.

Zzzt—

The instant the vines touched skin, paralytic venom surged into his veins.

His body locked up, numbness spreading through every limb, his lightning stuttering, losing power fast.

Worse still, the vines were feeding off him, draining his leaking Affinity energy and converting it into power for themselves.

His breathing grew ragged, cold sweat beading on his forehead, the lightning around him dimming by the second.

The three Level Fives seized the opening, striking together, claws aimed for every vital point.

Vines binding him. Venom paralyzing him. His Affinity drained. Four zombies closing in.

His limbs had gone numb, his lightning sputtering out, barely enough strength left to raise a hand.

He'd never felt death this close before, a cold suffocation pressing in from every side.

He'd never felt this hopeless.

And the first thought that crossed his mind was relief.

Thank god Paisley isn't here.

A flicker of resolve, red and final, crossed his eyes. He'd made his decision.

If death was coming anyway, he'd detonate his own core and take all four zombies down with him.

One last thing I can do for her. My life, for her safety.

Regret churned through him, endless and heavy.

Such a waste. Right up to the end, I never found the courage to tell her how I feel. Never said the word I'd been holding onto.

No going back to Aetheris. No seeing my family again. No chance to say a proper goodbye.

No more walking behind her through whatever's left of this world, keeping her safe.

Too many regrets.

He closed his eyes slowly, what lightning he had left swelling, building toward detonation.

Then, in that final instant, a cold, sharp figure tore out of the storeroom behind them.

Paisley, having finished clearing the treasure room, had felt his danger and come at once.

Seeing what had happened to him, frost settled over her face, her voice edged with killing intent.
