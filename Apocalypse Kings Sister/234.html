Chapter 234

The words landed cold and sharp, carrying real menace, not an ounce of courtesy in them.

Rachel's group went pale instantly, hands clenching at their sides. Resentment burned under the surface, but none of them dared talk back, not with tempers this close to the edge.

Ashley stepped up and looped her arm through Paisley's, her small face set in open, protective defiance as she followed Orion onto the military plane.

Spencer and Thomas fell into step on either side, half a pace behind, flanking the two girls in a quiet show of protection.

The group boarded without issue, calm and orderly.

Only once everyone else was seated did Rachel's group dare step cautiously onto the plane.

Rachel took a seat next to Chase, her gaze locked the whole time on Paisley, who'd settled by the window and drifted off to sleep.

The girl sat relaxed and at ease, surrounded by the Thompsons, every one of them fussing over her with care.

The daughter they'd once dismissed and mistreated without a thought now sat cradled like something precious.

And she was the one left scraping by, just trying to survive.

The gap between them burned. Jealousy, bitterness, resentment churned violently through Rachel's chest.

Her fingers curled tight at her side, her dark gaze fixed on the pendant hanging at Paisley's throat, her obsession with it deepening by the second.

The pendant. She had to find a way to take it.

Everything Paisley had, the honors, the strength, the luck, Rachel would eventually take all of it back.

Especially that storage pendant. It should have been hers.

She was determined to have it, no matter what.

This military transport had plenty of room, more than thirty seats in total.

The second she'd boarded, Paisley had leaned against the window and closed her eyes. Yesterday's brutal fighting, then healing five people in the middle of the night, had left her running on no sleep at all. She drifted off almost instantly,

completely unaware of Rachel's venomous, envious stare fixed on her.

Rachel couldn't tear her eyes away, bitterness nearly spilling over.

That was when Ashley's brow furrowed, her round eyes narrowing in Rachel's direction, small frame drawing up straight, radiating a ferocity completely at odds with her size.

"What are you staring at? She's my sister, and I'm the only sister she's got. Keep looking at her with that look on your face, and you won't need those eyes much longer."

Rachel's expression froze, her whole body going rigid, a flicker of panic breaking through.

She hadn't expected to get caught staring, let alone called out on the spot.

Bitterness surged through her, absurd and furious all at once.

Sister?

She'd called Paisley "sister" for ten years.

The old Paisley had given in to her every whim, put her first in everything, every time, without fail.

But this life around, everything had flipped completely.

Paisley's luck really was something else, finding a family who genuinely loved her, who cherished her like this.

And she'd been reduced to groveling just to scrape by in this world.

Why had it turned out this way?

Regret gnawed at her, vicious and relentless. She ground her teeth, thinking she should have played her part more convincingly back then, should never have given Paisley the chance to slip free.

Beside her, Chase watched the whole exchange, his face cooling as he leaned in, voice low and warning.

"Don't stare. Keep whatever you're thinking to yourself, or I won't be able to cover for you if things go wrong."

Rachel's chest tightened. She lowered her eyes, bitterness simmering unspoken beneath the surface.

The cabin settled back into quiet, the plane cutting steady through the clouds. Three hours passed.

Then a sharp, blaring alarm tore through the cabin, shattering the calm completely.

"Beep—beep—beep—"

The alarm wouldn't stop, the plane shaking violently, lurching hard from side to side.

Paisley, deep in sleep, jolted awake at the sound, mind still foggy, Ashley's panicked voice ringing in her ears, small hands shaking her arm hard.

"Paisley, wake up! Please wake up!"

******

Paisley's eyes snapped open, fully alert in an instant, her voice still rough with sleep.

"What's happening?"

She looked out the window, and her stomach dropped.

Outside, a dense black cloud of mutant crows wheeled and dove, each one massive, just over three feet across, black wings sharp as blades, red eyes glinting with bloodlust, slamming and pecking at the plane's hull in a frenzy.

These crows had gone fully feral, savage and fearless, throwing themselves at the aircraft's shell one after another without pause.
