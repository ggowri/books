Chapter 234

Orion's icy words carried real menace and didn't spare anyone's feelings.

Rachel and the others went pale and clenched their fists without thinking. As bitter as they were, they didn't dare talk back right then and make Orion and the others angry.

Ashley stepped up and hooked her arm through Paisley's, looking fiercely protective, and followed Orion onto the military plane.

Thomas and Spencer walked half a step behind them, one on each side, protecting the two girls.

Their group boarded the plane calmly and in order.

Only after all of them had boarded did Rachel's group dare to carefully step into the cabin.

Rachel sat down next to Chase, her eyes locked the whole time on Paisley, who'd taken a window seat and fallen fast asleep.

Paisley looked relaxed and at ease, surrounded and carefully looked after by the Thompsons.

The adopted daughter the Lennoxes had once looked down on and treated so badly was now being treasured like this.

And Rachel could only scrape by, just trying to survive.

The huge gap made her even more jealous. Resentment and bitterness raged inside her and stuck in her chest.

The fingers at her side curled into tight fists, and her dark gaze landed on the pendant on Paisley's chest. Her obsession and greed grew deeper and deeper.

She had to find a chance to get that pendant. Sooner or later, she'd take everything Paisley had, her honors, her strength, and her luck.

That space pendant should've been hers most of all, and she was going to get it, no matter what.

The military transport plane was roomy, with more than 30 seats in all.

The moment Paisley got on the plane, she leaned against the window and closed her eyes. Yesterday's brutal fighting and healing five people in the middle of the night had left her exhausted with no rest, so she was fast asleep in no time. She didn't even notice the venomous stare Rachel was giving her, full of jealousy and greed.

Rachel refused to look away for a long time, her bitterness almost spilling out of her eyes.

Just then, Ashley frowned and glared at Rachel with her big round eyes. Small as she was, she sat up straight, cute but fierce, and her warning was sharp.

"What are you looking at? She's my sister, and I'm the only little sister she's got! Keep staring at her like that, and you can kiss your eyes goodbye!"

Rachel's face froze, her whole body went stiff, and a hint of panic showed on her face.

She never expected to get caught spying on Paisley, especially not right there in front of everyone.

Bitterness welled up inside her, and it all felt absurd and unfair.

Sister? I'm the one who called her my sister for 10 years!

Back then, Paisley had gone along with whatever Rachel wanted. She'd given Rachel all her kindness and love and always put her first.

But in this second life, everything had changed.

Paisley sure was lucky. She'd actually found her real family, the Thompsons, who truly cared about her, loved her best, and treasured her.

And Rachel had ended up having to suck up to other people just to survive the apocalypse.

Why had it turned out like this?

Regret gnawed at her like crazy, and she clenched her teeth. If she'd known it would end up like this, she would've played her part better back then and never given Paisley the chance to get away.

Chase saw all of it from beside her, and his face went cold as he lowered his voice to warn her. "Stop staring. Keep your little schemes to yourself, or if something goes wrong later, I won't be able to protect you."

Rachel's heart sank. She looked away, frustrated, and hid her bitterness.

The cabin went quiet again, and the plane flew smoothly through the clouds. Three hours passed.

Suddenly, a shrill, urgent alarm blared and ripped the calm inside the plane apart. It kept beeping over and over.

The alarm wouldn't stop, and the plane shook violently, swaying hard from side to side.

Paisley was jolted awake from a deep sleep, and while she was still groggy, she heard Ashley calling her anxiously, her little hands shaking Paisley's arm hard. "Paisley, wake up! Come on, wake up!"

Her eyes snapped open, and she was wide awake, her voice a little hoarse from sleep. "What's going on?"

She looked out the window, and her heart sank.

Outside the window, a dark mass of mutant crows was circling and swooping. Every one of them was huge, over three feet across, with pitch-black wings as sharp as blades and blood-red eyes gleaming with bloodlust. They were ramming and pecking at the plane like crazy.

These crows had fully mutated. They were vicious and fearless, and they kept slamming into the plane's hull one after another.
