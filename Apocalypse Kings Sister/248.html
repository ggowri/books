Chapter 248

She closed her eyes, focused, and guided the energy from the cores into her own body.

Warm, dense energy flowed through her meridians, nourishing her Affinities and wearing down the barriers inside.

Core after core dissolved, pure energy pouring in without pause, pushing her Affinity levels steadily upward.

Some time later, the last of the bear's core was fully absorbed.

With a silent crack, the barrier inside her broke open, and her spirit energy surged.

Her Plant Affinity broke through from Level Six mid-stage straight to Level Six peak, one step shy of Level Seven. Her vines grew denser and more lethal, the toxin stronger than before, and a new ability surfaced: Parasitize.

Her Psychic Affinity climbed to Level Five peak as well, her range and precision both sharpening. Her Water Affinity hit Level Five peak too, her control over water growing far stronger.

All three Affinities leveled up at once. Her presence deepened, harder to read, harder to measure.

Night settled fully over the village, and the villa's windows glowed with light.

Kelly and Madison worked the kitchen together, and soon had a hot, home-style spread on the table.

Kelly, curious, wondered aloud where all this fresh food had come from.

She hadn't seen anyone carrying supplies in the car.

When she asked Madison, Madison just said she'd had it the whole time.

Maybe she was imagining things.

She pressed a hand to her empty stomach. At least tonight she'd eat properly.

After dinner, Paisley stepped outside to walk off the meal, looking up at the scattered stars.

The same sky as before the apocalypse. The stars hadn't changed. But the world beneath them had turned upside down, order gone, ordinary life now a luxury out of reach.

The night wind stirred her hair, and something quiet and wistful settled in her eyes.

Steady footsteps approached. Elijah came to stand beside her, following her gaze up to the stars.

"What are you looking at?"

Paisley pulled her eyes back down, toward the heart of the silent village.

"Doesn't it feel wrong to you? How quiet this place is?"

"Since we'd arrived, there'd been no sound, no sign of a single person. Just dead silence, and something dangerous underneath it."

Elijah's gaze darkened slightly. He'd noticed it too.

He kept his voice calm.

"I'll stay alert tonight. Don't worry too much. We'll be out of here first thing tomorrow."

"I'll handle it."

Deep in the village, in a lavish standalone villa, a different kind of scene was playing out.

Under dim yellow light, a fat, greasy middle-aged man lounged in a chair, picking his teeth without a care, jowls hanging heavy.

A henchman bent low beside him, fawning, lighting his cigarette. Through the smoke, he leaned in to report, his eyes shifty.

"Boss, ten strangers showed up in the village. I scoped them out quiet. Four women, and one of them's a real looker. Skin, figure, bearing, all top-shelf. Raised rich, no question."

His eyes gleamed with greed as he rubbed his hands together.

"A catch like that beats anything from the nearby villages. Perfect for the boss."

The fat man spat on the floor and stood up, excited.

"Good work."

"I'm bored of the girls upstairs. Nothing new about them."

He smirked, cruel and greedy.

"Once we bring in these four new women, the rest of you can have your pick."

His face turned serious, a feverish gleam in his eye.

"Don't forget to feed the basement zombies on schedule. I'm leveling up tonight. This time I'll hit Level Five for sure."

"Rest of you, don't sit around. Eat up and get to work."

He slammed a fist on the table, his face twisting vicious.

"Go round up those outsiders now! Every man gets thrown to the zombies, helps me build my strength. Every woman stays. They're mine."

"Yes, Boss!"

A dozen or so thugs answered at once, grabbing pipes and machetes, the steel glinting cold in the dim light.
