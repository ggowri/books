Chapter 248

She closed her eyes, focused, and calmly guided the power inside her, slowly drawing in the pure energy from the crystal cores.

Wave after wave of warm, rich energy flowed through her veins and out to every part of her body. It kept feeding the roots of her powers and wearing down the barriers that held them back.

The crystal cores melted away one by one, and pure energy kept pouring into her body. The huge flood of energy fed her powers, and they steadily broke through and climbed higher and higher.

She had no idea how much time had passed when she finally absorbed the last core, the one from the Level Six bear.

Something inside her gave way with a roar as the barrier burst open, and her power surged!

Her Plant Affinity broke through from Level Six mid-stage and settled firmly at Level Six peak, just one step away from Level Seven. Her vines carried denser, fiercer power, their poison paralyzed even better than before, and they'd gained a brand-new skill, Parasitize.

And that wasn't all. Her Psychic Affinity rose to Level Five peak, so she could scan farther and sense things in sharper detail. Her Water Affinity also broke through to Level Five peak, and her water reserves grew a lot bigger.

All three of her powers had leveled up, and her strength had grown by leaps and bounds. Her whole presence felt deeper and harder to read, and it was more commanding than ever.

By now, night had settled over the whole village, and every light in the mansion was on.

Kelly and Madison cooked dinner together, and soon they'd put a hot, home-cooked meal on the table, with a good mix of meat and veggies.

While they cooked, Kelly wondered how the team had so much fresh food.

She hadn't seen anyone bring food in the car earlier.

When she asked Madison, Madison actually said they'd had it with them the whole time.

Am I just overthinking this?

She rubbed her empty stomach. At least she'd finally get a full meal tonight.

After dinner, Paisley went out into the yard to walk off her meal and looked up at the stars twinkling here and there in the night sky.

It was the same sky as before the apocalypse, and the stars still hung high above. But the world under them had been turned upside down, order had collapsed, and an ordinary, peaceful life had become a dream that was far out of reach.

The cool night wind blew through her hair, and she felt a little wistful.

Steady footsteps slowly came closer. Elijah walked up beside her, followed her gaze up to the night sky, and asked quietly, "What are you looking at?"

Paisley looked away from the sky and toward the dead-silent heart of the village. "Doesn't this village seem way too quiet to you? It's creepy."

From the moment they'd driven in until now, they hadn't heard a single other sound or seen a single person. The whole place was dead silent, and danger seemed to be hiding everywhere.

Elijah's eyes darkened a little. He'd already sensed that something was off.

He spoke softly to reassure her. "I'll stay on high alert tonight, so don't worry too much. First thing tomorrow, we're leaving this village, and I'll take care of everything."

Meanwhile, deep in the village, a scene of pure excess was playing out inside a big, flashy standalone mansion.

Under the dim yellow light, a greasy middle-aged man with a huge beer belly lounged in a chair. His face was fat and mean, and he was picking his teeth without a care in the world.

One of his men bowed and scraped beside him, all smiles, and hurried to light his cigarette. Through the curling smoke, the henchman reported in a low voice, a sleazy look on his face.

"Boss, 10 strangers just showed up in the village. I checked them out on the sly, and four of them are hot chicks.

"One of them's way hotter than the rest. Her face, her body, her skin, the way she carries herself, all of it's top-notch. You can tell she's some spoiled rich girl."

He rubbed his hands together greedily, trying to please his boss. "A piece of ass like that beats any woman from the villages around here. We grab her, and she's all yours to fuck, Boss!"

The gang leader hawked up a thick wad of phlegm and spat it out, then stood up, all fired up. "Nice work.

"I got sick of fucking those girls upstairs a long time ago. We grabbed them from around here, and there's nothing new about them anymore."

He raised an eyebrow and sneered, sounding cruel and greedy. "Once we get these four new girls in here, you guys can have the old ones."

Then, he turned serious, a crazed look on his face. "Don't forget to feed the zombies in the basement on schedule. I'm leveling up my power later, and this time I'll hit Level Five for sure.

"The rest of you, don't just sit around. Eat up, drink up, and get to work."

He slammed his hand down on the table, his face twisting with cold, vicious rage. "Go bring that whole group of outsiders back here right now!

"Throw every man into the basement to feed the zombies and help me build up my power. Keep all the women here for me to fuck!"

"Yes, Boss!" More than a dozen thugs answered at once. Every one of them was holding a steel pipe or a machete, and the metal glinted cold in the dim light.
