Chapter 146

Then, Paisley pulled up one item after another from the huge stockpile in her dimension.

Heavy-duty flashlights, a full set of stainless steel pots and pans, folding tables and chairs, a sturdy wooden bed frame, thick, warm bedding, and complete sets of kitchen and bathroom supplies all lined up neatly in front of the screen. Every single thing was super practical.

[I can trade you any of this everyday stuff and winter gear.

[As long as you bring enough energy crystals, mutant beast meat, or gold, there's no limit on how much I'll trade.]

The Matriarch stared at the neat, handy things she'd never seen before. Her face lit up, and she trembled with excitement and hope.

[Thank you so much!] She couldn't hide how excited she was.

[Every winter in the Beast Realm, the cold cuts right to the bone, and blizzards trap us in the mountains. The weak elders and the cubs always freeze to death or get frostbite, and we lose more people every year.

[With warm bedding, supplies, and lights like these, we could save so many lives!]

She memorized what the gold bar looked like and made a promise right away.

[I've seen this hard, shiny yellow stuff before! I'll have my mates lead the whole tribe into the mountains to look for it, and we'll gather as much as we can for you!]

They hit it off and settled on the trade list fast. Huge loads of goods passed through the realm channel in both directions.

Warm clothes and household supplies poured into the Beast Realm, while piles of energy crystals and mutant cattle, sheep, and chickens landed in Paisley's storage and were neatly sorted.

[Beast Realm trade complete! Bonus: 2,000 points!

[Current total: 155,060 points!]

As the cheerful announcement ended, Paisley watched her points climb. She looked at the new piles of high-energy crystals and prime mutant livestock in her dimension and felt thrilled.

These super-pure Beast Realm energy crystals were way gentler and cleaner than the crystal cores in the apocalypse. She didn't need to purify them, so she could use them right away to strengthen her body and abilities. They'd make her a lot stronger.

Paisley smiled, and she already had a plan.

From now on, whenever I've got free time, I need to stock up on more supplies and trade as much as I can.

Racking up points and becoming a higher-level trader as fast as possible was the only way to unlock more of the System's hidden perks and access.

[Master, do you want to connect to the last trader in line, the one from the Galactic Realm?]

"Put her through."

[Galactic Realm connection successful!]

The screen flickered, and a breathtaking figure slowly appeared.

It was the rare S-tier interstellar female. Their last trade had been rushed, so Paisley hadn't gotten a good look at her. Now that she could see her up close, she realized how stunning the trader was.

Her long, shimmering sapphire-blue hair fell over her shoulders, and her eyes were the same clear, crystal blue. She looked elegant and refined.

Her skin had a flawless, radiant glow. She wore a light, flowing gown covered in tiny diamonds that sparkled like starlight, and every move she made looked effortlessly glamorous.

She gave off the air of someone who'd only ever had the best of everything. She'd clearly been pampered all her life in the Galactic Realm and had never wanted for anything.

Before Paisley could say anything, the trader spoke first. Her voice was gentle and straightforward, and she sounded completely sincere.

[Last time, you traded us natural fruit seeds, high-yield vegetable seeds, natural compressed rations, and clean ingredients that stay fresh. The galaxy is desperate for those. Do you still have a lot in stock?]

Paisley scanned her storage with her mind. Fruit and vegetable seeds, natural ingredients, and preserved food were piled up like mountains, more than enough to keep the supply going.

On top of that, she'd already planted a lot of it in her dimension. With her Plant Affinity, she could grow it all with no problem, so she'd never run short.

She answered calmly. [We can trade, and you can have as much as you want. What are you offering in return?]

The S-tier trader didn't hesitate. She tapped the storage ring on her finger, and a huge pile of rare goods filled the trading channel. It was five times as much as last time.

[Is this enough for the first round?]

She looked up, sounding humble and easygoing. [If there's anything else you need, as long as we have it in the galaxy, I'll do my best to get it for you.]
