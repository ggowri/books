Chapter 292

With that arranged, Paisley stepped into the center of the spacious main hall.

She raised a hand, and the plump, gleaming Level Five psychic crystal core, along with every other high-tier core she'd collected through the fighting, rose up in front of her, hovering, light shifting across their surfaces.

Paisley settled into a cross-legged seat.

The little red fox curled up quietly beside her, its red eyes soft and lowered, standing silent guard over its master the entire time, never once making a sound.

She cleared her mind, focused entirely, and took the Level Five psychic core into her hand.

An instant later, vast, overwhelming psychic energy poured in through her palm, flowing steadily through her channels, washing through and refining her mind over and over.

Her once somewhat limited psychic range expanded instantly, the long-standing bottleneck in her mental power finally giving way, cracking open.

Countless fine threads of psychic pattern regrew and interwove deep within her mind, her already formidable awareness surging and evolving at a visible pace, growing sharper and more refined.

Wave after wave of gentle yet overwhelming energy swept through her body, thoroughly refining her channels, strengthening her physical form in step, her presence climbing steadily with each breakthrough.

[Psychic power breakthrough! Level rising to early Level Six!]

[Mental scan range doubled! Psychic resistance and control significantly enhanced!]

Paisley didn't pause, continuing to draw on the remaining core energy.

With the boost from the abundant cores, her Water Affinity broke through its ceiling first, rising to early Level Six, with her Plant Affinity following close behind, reaching mid-Level Six, her combat strength climbing sharply.

Time passed unnoticed, and eventually the last traces of core energy in the space were fully absorbed, the dense spirit energy around her settling back into quiet calm.

By now, her Psychic, Water, Plant, and mutant Fire Affinities complemented and reinforced one another, forming a stable, powerful energy loop within her core, each ability's strength significantly amplified.

She now stood at Psychic Level Six, Water early Level Six, Plant mid-Level Six, and Level Seven mutant flame — four Affinities working in harmony, her psychic power alone far outstripping any peer at her level, her overall strength firmly among the apocalypse's top tier.

Paisley slowly opened her eyes, light flickering deep within them, her presence settling into something steady, powerful, and effortless, her whole bearing having undergone a complete transformation — cool, sharp, and increasingly unreadable.

Just then, Trovie came online.

Congratulations, Host, on becoming a Level Four trader!

Current total points: 1,007,060! Bonus: one premium lucky draw! That brings your total to two premium draws.

Points needed to reach Level Five trader: 5,000,000. Keep going, Host!

Shall we connect the trade channel to the 21st-century modern-era merchant now?

A flicker of brightness crossed Paisley's eyes, and she nodded.

Agreed. Connect now.

The moment the words landed, a soft, translucent light screen unfolded before her, images and timelines shifting and crossing, instantly bridging the gap between the apocalypse and the modern world.

On the other side of the screen sat a tidy, minimalist modern office. A woman in sharp, professional office wear sat behind the desk, eyeing the strange light screen that had just appeared before her with evident surprise, her voice coming through.

Hello there!

Paisley answered calmly.

Hello.

The woman's eyes were a mix of curiosity and caution as she spoke up.

I'm from the 21st century, modern era. Judging by your surroundings, you must be from some kind of apocalypse. I've got plenty of supplies on hand. Anything I can help you with?

Paisley nodded without hesitation, getting straight to the point.

I need a large quantity of supplies — grain, fresh vegetables, meat, poultry, fish, everyday goods, household items. As much as possible, no limit.

In this world, paper currency was the least valuable thing around. Nobody bothered picking it up off the ground.

But in the modern world, it was still legal tender, perfect for bulk-purchasing exactly what she needed.

She'd already amassed a mountain of cash from raiding wealthy estates and strongholds, and now it was finally going to good use.

The woman's eyes lit up at the offer, and she agreed on the spot.

No problem! I've got plenty of stock. Whatever budget you want to work with, I can make it happen.

Paisley's tone stayed even.

As much as possible. Give yourself three days to gather everything you can. Money's the one thing I'm not short on.

With that, she turned and walked toward a nearby room.

The massive room was packed floor to ceiling with stacks of crisp, new bills, piled high, hundred-denomination notes covering the floor and walls in dense, towering rows.
