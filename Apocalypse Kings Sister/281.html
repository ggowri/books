Chapter 281

Paisley's gaze turned cold and sharp, Ice-Water Affinity swirling around her.

She shot into the air, the Frostbloom Sword wrapped in a layer of pure, frigid energy, the blade glinting with icy light, cold enough to cut to the bone.

At the same time, her Plant Affinity's parasitic ability slipped into motion again, threads of vine-energy finer than hair sliding through the cracks in the serpent's scales, sinking deep into its body, closing in on the Level Seven crystal core pulsing at its center.

Sensing the fatal threat, the serpent let out a final, desperate roar, throwing every last bit of strength into one final lunge, jaws spread wide, fangs bared, intent on taking its killer down with it.

Paisley's expression didn't change. Calm, unbothered, she curled her fingers in one sharp, decisive motion.

"Parasitize. Strip."

The serpent's massive body convulsed violently, all its life force drained away in an instant. Its crimson slit eyes dimmed and went blank, and its huge body crashed down onto the rocks, never to move again.

A moment later, a gleaming, dense Level Seven crystal core, rich with energy, rose out of its body on a bed of vines and hovered in the air, pristine and flawless.

The deafening roars had gone silent, and the valley settled back into peace, nothing left but a faint breeze through the trees.

Everyone released their powers, staring at the massive serpent corpse on the ground, struck speechless.

This apex predator, closing in on Level Eight strength, had finally fallen — brought down by their combined teamwork and Paisley's decisive final strike.

In her mind, Trovie's cheerful chime rang out right on cue.

[Congratulations, Host, on completing the temporary mission! All armory supplies collected, and the Level Seven mutant serpent eliminated! Mission reward: 5,000 points plus one premium lucky draw, automatically credited!]

[Large stockpile of survival supplies detected! Bonus 5,000 points awarded!]

[Current total points: 520,060!]

The tension that had held her for so long finally eased. Paisley let out a long breath, feeling the weight lift from her shoulders.

Before she could even catch her breath, Trovie's chime sounded again.

Host, don't forget! There's still a ton of treasure in the armory nobody's grabbed yet!

Also, every part of this serpent is valuable — scales, bones, gallbladder, all high-tier materials you can trade for serious points!

Paisley's eyes lit up. On impulse, she pulled the serpent's entire massive body into her storage.

Exchange it right now.

She gave the order silently.

Trovie answered instantly.

Successfully exchanged the Level Seven serpent's body! Reward: 1,000 points!

Current total points: 521,060!

The rest of the group had long stopped questioning Paisley's odd behavior. What had once shocked them had become routine.

There were too many strange things about her to keep track of.

Only Kelly, standing off to the side, was still reeling.

She'd spent the entire fight cowering in the back, too afraid to even draw her weapon.

Watching Paisley take down a beast closing in on Level Eight with such ease had left her shaking down to her bones, cold sweat beading on her skin.

Whatever rivalry or resentment she'd carried before had evaporated completely.

She would never dare provoke Paisley again.

Who would have guessed that the thin, cold-looking girl who seemed so delicate held this much power.

The rest of the team, by contrast, looked completely unbothered, clearly used to Paisley's habit of crushing enemies far above her level.

Paisley glanced around at the exhausted group.

"I still need to go back in and search for more supplies. Do you want to wait outside, or come with me?"

After that brutal fight, everyone's Affinity reserves were running dry, bodies worn past the point of exhaustion.

None of them could even hold themselves upright anymore, half-collapsed on the warm rock, breath shallow, too weak to so much as lift a hand.

Only Elijah looked slightly better off.

He sat upright, spine straight, though his Affinity was spent too. He held a crystal core between his fingers, drawing on its energy to recover.

At Paisley's words, he looked up, pushing himself to his feet despite how drained he was.

"I'll come with you."

He wasn't about to let her walk into a trap alone.

Paisley gave a small nod.

"Fine. The rest of you rest here. We'll be quick."

Orion and the others could barely manage to open their eyes, let alone speak, and just nodded weakly.

Paisley waved a hand, tossing a bottle of spirit water to each of them.

"Drink up."
