Chapter 130

The only difference was that this new city had its own rules for trading.

With the apocalypse, paper money, gold, and silver had become worthless. Crystal cores were the new hard currency.

Everything in the city was paid for in crystal cores, from food, housing, clothes, and supplies to gear, ability training resources, and ammo.

A bulletin board next to the lounge spelled out all the rules, and right beside it was a separate hall just for picking up missions.

For survivors, the only way to stay alive and earn enough to level up was to take official missions. They could kill mutant beasts, clear out zombies, patrol the defense line, or haul supplies, and they'd earn crystal cores and contribution points in return.

Contribution points could be traded in for entry passes, housing rights, high-level resources, and even special backing to help them level up.

Everyone had a job, and everyone got paid for the work they did.

It was pretty much how the safe bases would run in the later years of the apocalypse.

Paisley calmly looked over the neat buildings, the orderly crowds, and the tight security, and she couldn't help being impressed.

The apocalypse had broken out only about two weeks ago, but Rivergate's leaders had moved fast and made tough calls.

In just over ten days, they'd brought survivors together, set up military order, and built a whole trading and mission system. Out of total chaos, they'd made a city that was safe, orderly, and rock-solid.

That was huge.

Whoever's running Rivergate really knows what they're doing!

Everyone took a short break in the lounge.

The others huddled together, talking quietly about getting stronger and how Rivergate was run. Only Paisley got up and went out alone to look around.

The base had everything it needed. Most of the shops along the streets had stayed open or reopened, and there was even a remodeled milkshake and snack shop.

For once, it felt like real life.

Since the whole city ran on crystal cores, regular people saved every single one they could. They spent them all on leveling up and getting stronger.

Nobody could bear to waste any on treats.

Paisley didn't care one bit. She walked right up, took out a Level Two crystal core, and bought two sweet milkshakes, a burger, and half a crispy roast chicken.

She carried the food to a seat in the sun. The warm sunlight chased away the chill of all those days of fighting.

The little white cat curled up in her arms. The two of them sat in the sun without a care in the world, slowly sipping milkshakes and squinting happily.

Not far away, Rachel saw it all, and it cut her like a knife.

Her Water Affinity was stuck at mid Level Three, and she didn't have any crystal cores to level it up.

Paisley was clearly strong, and she'd just blown a Level Two core on milkshakes like it was nothing. Rachel's jealousy and hatred burned even hotter.

Food was incredibly scarce in the apocalypse. She'd almost forgotten what milkshakes and fried chicken tasted like.

Day after day, she'd been filling up on rock-hard hardtack and bread.

And Paisley was actually giving the other milkshake and the chicken to that damn cat!

Rachel clenched her fists so hard her nails dug into her palms. She hated Paisley so much her teeth ached.

Unbelievable. People out there are starving and freezing, and she's wasting food on a cat!

Her throat kept bobbing as she swallowed again and again. Greed burned in her eyes, and she wanted to rush over and grab all that food for herself.

The longer she watched, the more she wanted Paisley dead.

She was more sure than ever that Paisley had a secret.

The space has to still be there. Wait, the pendant!

Sure enough, the pendant was still hanging around Paisley's neck.

Breaking it obviously hadn't changed a thing.

Paisley had been making a fool of her all along.

She had to get that pendant back and find out for sure.

Paisley was alone right now. It was the perfect chance to test her.

She turned to Owen beside her. Just as she'd thought, he was staring at Paisley too as she relaxed nearby, and he looked torn.

A plan quickly took shape in Rachel's mind. A nasty little smile crept across her face, and she leaned in to whisper to him.

"Owen, Paisley's alone right now. This is our best chance to make up with her.

"She acts all cold and heartless, but deep down, she's the softest of all of us. Ever since we were kids, you've been the one she looked up to most. She always listened to you more than anyone.

"Paisley's gotten so strong. She must have tons of crystal cores, or some valuable stuff we don't even know about.

"If we can just get back on her good side, we won't even need Jasper. We can make it back to Southspire safely."

Then, she turned to Reid and Ryan.
