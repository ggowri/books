Chapter 481

Everyone nodded. The mood in the car grew heavier.

Paisley looked out the window. The dark, bleak sky had started to scatter fine white flakes.

Small bits of snow drifted down, drifting over the ruined walls and laying a thin layer of white on the dead world.

The temperature was plunging, visibly. A bone-deep chill seeped through the window cracks.

"It's snowing?"

Ashley hunched her shoulders and wrapped her arms around herself, shivering. "It's so cold. The temperature's dropping so fast."

Paisley's eyes darkened. She knew this was only the prelude to the Great Freeze.

She made a thought. A flicker of light from her Storage Dimension, and a thick, fluffy, very warm women's down jacket landed in her hand. She passed it to Ashley.

"It's already snowing, and it's only going to get colder. Put this on and stay warm."

Ashley took it and put it on right away. The soft, puffy fabric shut out the biting cold, and warmth wrapped her whole body. Her eyes crinkled with sweet happiness, and her voice came out soft and bright.

"Paisley's the best to me! It's so warm! Thank you, Paisley!"

Beside her, Spencer was green with envy. He leaned in, put on a pitiful face, and looked at Paisley with shining eyes.

"Pais, I'm cold too. I want one."

Thomas chimed in right behind him. "Me too. I'm cold."

Only Orion sat quietly, watching his younger siblings take turns begging for jackets. He smiled helplessly.

He didn't ask. He just cast Paisley a sad little look, full of wounded feelings, and let her think about it.

The sight of them made Paisley's lips curl. She made another thought, and a neat row of down jackets in different styles and colors appeared in front of her.

"There's plenty. Pick whichever you like."

Everyone came forward to choose, and the car warmed up.

Elijah took his turn last and picked a blue down jacket. It fit him well and looked sharp. He looked up at Paisley, his voice low and gentle.

"Thank you. It fits perfectly."

She must have picked this one just for me, or it wouldn't fit this well, and the color's my favorite too.

Dressed, he turned serious again and watched the snow thicken outside.

"It's started. The real Freeze is about to hit. Everyone stay alert. Nobody relaxes."

The loosened mood in the car turned tense again.

Everyone knew this first snow was only the opening. The real Great Freeze, the cold that would lock up the world and kill everything in it, was about to sweep the sky.

It was warm and cozy in the car, but on the roof it was a completely different kind of hell.

Chloe, Grant, and the rest of the captives were tied to the roof, with nowhere to hide from the snow and wind.

Icy flakes slammed into skin, and freezing wind swept over them again and again. Cold seeped through their pores into their limbs and froze them stiff, teeth chattering.

Chloe had been playing dead since she was tied up, trying to keep a low profile and avoid trouble.

But the snow was coming harder and the temperature kept dropping. The cold turned her face blue and white and made her shake. She couldn't keep it up.

She couldn't hold the act. Her eyes flew open, snow covering her, and she shouted at the car, bossy and entitled.

"It's freezing! I'm not staying on the roof! Paisley, get me a down jacket too!"

The sunroof popped open, and cold wind and snow rushed into the car.

Ashley stood and leaned out through the opening. She looked down at the miserable Chloe with mockery and cold fury in her eyes. She wasn't going to give her anything.

Ashley shot back without mercy.

"You've got some nerve! You're a prisoner and you're making demands?"

"One more word and I'll throw you off the car and let the zombies have you in the snow!"

She tugged the front of her own puffy purple down jacket on purpose and flashed a smile.

"See this? It's gorgeous and warm. Paisley got it just for me. One of a kind."

"Having a sister who loves you and a family behind you, a petty, selfish person like you will never know what that feels like."
