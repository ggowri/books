Chapter 195

In her past life, Paisley's balance had never even hit five digits, not even when she died.

In this brutal apocalypse, getting stronger herself really was the only way to go.

Soon, the door to the private room opened, and the cafeteria staff wheeled in a cart.

The boiling stewpot came first. Its rich, spicy broth bubbled away, sending up thick, fragrant steam that filled the room, and the hearty smell chased away the chill and deathly gloom of the apocalypse.

Plate after plate of fresh ingredients was set out neatly, from tender meat, mushrooms, and greens to meatballs and baby potatoes. Everything was fresh and colorful, the height of luxury in the apocalypse.

The steam curled around every face in the room, warm and soft, chasing off days of mission fatigue, and the whole room came alive.

Ashley, the liveliest of them all, grabbed her fork first, her eyes sparkling.

"Let's eat, let's eat! This stew smells so good. Thanks, Paisley, we never could've had a feast like this without you!"

The mood caught fire. Everyone dug in, laughter weaving through the warmth of the room.

They acted like they were just eating, but their eyes kept drifting to Paisley, and they were quietly competing with each other.

Orion sat down quietly and dropped the tenderest slices of meat into the pot. Once they were cooked, he fished them out and put them right on Paisley's plate, gentle and natural about it.

Elijah calmly served everyone, all while cooking the vegetables Paisley liked best, unable to hide how much he adored her. "Slow down," he said softly. "Don't burn yourself."

Spencer and Thomas weren't about to lose either, taking turns piling food on her plate, one adding meat while the other cooked veggies. Whenever she took a bite, they raced to win her over, childish but genuine.

Marcus said the least, sitting apart with his usual reserve, but he kept track of her all the same, dropping in her favorite ingredients whenever the pot came to a boil.

The whole room was warm and steamy, full of nonstop laughter and the cozy feel of a real home.

Paisley's plate was piled high from everyone feeding her. Her cheeks were a soft pink from the steam, and warmth filled her heart.

In this bleak, cold apocalypse, this one small room was filled with the rarest kind of warmth.

******

It was early the next morning, the day of the high-risk aquarium mission. Everyone was geared up and ready to head deep into the contaminated zone to retrieve the sealed classified research data and precision equipment.

As usual, before they set out, everyone on the mission gathered in the top-floor command office for a briefing to settle assignments, routes, and backup plans.

When Paisley, Elijah, and Orion arrived, the office was already full of the base's elite Affinity users, and the mood was tense and serious.

Near the head seat at the front, a flashy guy lounged lazily against the table, wildly arrogant, every inch of him screaming entitlement.

This was Chase Sullivan, an early Level Five Psychic Affinity user the head of the base had personally invited to help with the mission.

His psychic power was extremely special. He could sense danger over a wide area, detect movement, and intimidate mutant beasts, which made him irreplaceable on high-risk missions.

Because of that one-of-a-kind ability, he had a special status at Rivergate Base and countless privileges.

He never bothered with any of the base's missions, big or small.

But he got the best leveling resources and the best treatment in the base. He was lazy and arrogant, but nobody dared to say a word about it.

Over time, he'd only grown more arrogant and full of himself, and most people at the base had gotten used to it. They just put up with him and stayed out of his way.

If the aquarium mission hadn't been so dangerous that they couldn't do it without him, the higher-ups never would've asked him to step in.

After all, getting him to do anything always came at a steep price.

Chase glanced lazily at everyone coming in, and his first words left no room for argument.

"I'm going to be the captain on this mission.

"What I say goes, period. Otherwise, I'll walk away right now, and you can all go in there and get yourselves killed."

He was bossy and domineering, with zero interest in teamwork. He was just using his special skill as leverage, spoiled by all the special treatment.

The veteran team members and high-level Affinity users in the room all stayed quiet. Nobody dared to argue, and they just put up with him.

That was when Paisley's group walked in.

When Chase saw Paisley in the group, slim and pretty, contempt and amusement crossed his face, and he got even cockier.

He let out a short laugh and leaned in, his tone light and mocking, pitched just loud enough for everyone to hear.
