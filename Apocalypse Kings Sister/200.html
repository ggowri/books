Chapter 200

Something vast and wordless surged through Orion, his chest full to bursting.

A storage ring was priceless in this world, something no amount of money could buy—and his sister had one, more than one, and had given it to him without holding anything back.

Warmth spread through his cool eyes, and he settled on something, quiet and certain.

He had to be the one she loved most.

Of course he was. He was the one who mattered most to her.

He looked at the cool ring between his fingers, unable to hide how much he treasured it.

“Paisley, I love this. Once the mission's done, I'll gather every crystal core I can and give them all back to you.”

Elijah nodded along at once.

“Same here. Every one, to Pais.”

Before they'd finished speaking, light flickered in Paisley's palm, and two silver energy guns appeared, sleek and sharp-edged, pale blue light rippling along their bodies—nothing like the crude weapons this world had left to offer.

She leaned forward and placed one in each of their hands, her voice serious.

“This mission is going to be brutal. The mutants in there are strange and strong, and ordinary weapons won't do much against them. These are energy guns—far stronger than anything we've been using, the kill power several times over.”

They were a premium resource she'd traded for from the interstellar merchant a while back, sitting unused in her Storage Dimension until now.

This seemed as good a time as any to put them to the test.

The Galactic Realm's technology had developed around enemies far tougher than anything in this world—star-beasts with bodies that could shrug off damage that would kill ordinary mutants outright.

A gun built to hunt those was overkill, by any measure, against this world's zombies and mutants.

Orion and Elijah took the guns, their fingers finding the cool, smooth metal, eyes tracing the rippling blue light along the barrels, awe rising in them all over again.

Heat rushed through them both, hands tightening slightly around the grips, wonder filling every thought.

There seemed to be no end to what she was hiding.

Whatever she'd seen, whatever she was sitting on, it had long since outstripped anything they'd known to expect.

Amid the roar of the engine, a dark shape suddenly tore out from the overgrown brush along the road.

It moved fast, on all fours, its body twisted and blackened with rot, blood-red eyes locked on the moving car. A vicious Level Four mutant zombie.

“Watch out!”

Orion's face set. His hands held steady on the wheel, the car adjusting its line with a small, precise turn, no panic in him at all.

Beside him, Elijah reacted fast, raising the energy gun, aiming, and squeezing the trigger without hesitation.

A thin streak of blue light tore through the air, moving so fast it left only an afterimage.

No thundering gunshot, just a low, muffled crack as the light punched clean through the zombie's skull.

Its stiff body jerked once, head blown apart, and it collapsed, dead for good.

A clean Level Four crystal core dropped onto the road, rolled a couple of turns, and settled.

Something flickered in Paisley's eyes. She raised a finger, and a thin green vine shot up from the ground, wrapping neatly around the core and drawing it back into her palm.

The car never slowed, still racing steadily toward its destination.

Elijah looked down at the gun in his hands, awe and admiration plain on his face.

“This thing is unbelievable.”

“An ordinary gun would've just wounded a Level Four zombie—we'd still need Affinity to finish it off, and that costs both time and energy.”

He'd just one-shot it, without the slightest effort.

Orion kept his eyes on the road, though his whole mind stayed on the gun, his grip tightening slightly on the wheel, something urgent and hopeful rising in his eyes, his voice unable to hide the excitement.

“If we could mass-produce weapons this powerful, it wouldn't just change the base's fighting strength. It'd change how everyone in this world survives.”

He went quiet for a moment, pushing down his own excitement, then glanced back at her.

“Paisley, where'd this gun even come from? Do you have the schematics, how it works? How many more do you have on hand?”

Elijah turned to her too, his tone serious.

“With the guns Pais gave us, I'd put our odds at ninety percent for this mission.”

“Last time we came out here, every step nearly got us killed. If we'd had these then, it never would've gone the way it did.”
