Chapter 200

Orion was overwhelmed with emotion he couldn't put into words, his chest so full it ached.

Storage rings were incredibly precious, treasures money couldn't buy in the apocalypse. Paisley actually had something this rare, and she'd given it to him without holding back.

A soft light warmed his cool eyes, and he felt sure of one thing. Paisley definitely likes me best! I'm the most special, most important person to her.

He looked at the cool ring between his fingers, unable to hide how thrilled he was or how much he treasured it.

"Paisley, I love this gift! When this mission's over, I'll collect even more crystal cores and give them all to you to pay you back."

Elijah nodded right away. "Same here. They're all going to Pais."

The second they finished, light flashed in Paisley's palm, and two silver energy guns appeared. They were sleek and sharp-lined, with a pale blue glow rippling across them, high-tech and nothing like the crude weapons people used in the apocalypse.

She leaned forward and placed one in each of their hands, her voice serious.

"This aquarium mission is extremely dangerous. The mutants in there are weird and powerful, and ordinary weapons won't do much. These energy guns are way more powerful than anything we normally use, several times deadlier."

They were high-end gear she'd gotten in an earlier trade with the interstellar trader. They'd been sitting in her Storage Dimension ever since, and she'd never had a chance to use them.

This was the perfect chance to see what they could do.

The Galactic Realm had advanced technology, but its star beasts were incredibly tough and hard to kill, several times harder than the mutants in the apocalypse.

Energy guns designed to kill star beasts would wipe out mutant zombies and other mutants with ease.

Orion and Elijah took the guns, their fingers finding the cool, smooth metal, eyes tracing the rippling blue light along the barrels, awe rising in them all over again.

Their blood surged with excitement. They tightened their grips on the guns, amazed.

Paisley seemed to have endless secrets and surprises hidden away. Her vision and the resources she had were way beyond anything they could've imagined.

Amid the roar of the engine, a dark shape suddenly shot out of the overgrown median on the side of the road.

It was incredibly fast, running on all fours, its body twisted, rotting, and black. Its blood-red eyes were locked onto their car, a vicious Level Four mutant zombie.

"Watch out!" Orion's face hardened. He held the wheel steady and made a slight adjustment, calm and composed, without a hint of panic.

In the passenger seat, Elijah reacted fast. He raised the energy gun, aimed, and pulled the trigger without hesitation.

A thin streak of blue light tore through the air, moving so fast it left only an afterimage.

There was no thundering gunshot, just a low, muffled crack as the light punched clean through the zombie's skull.

In the next instant, the Level Four zombie's stiff body jerked, its head blown apart, and it crashed to the ground, dead.

A clean Level Four crystal core dropped onto the road, rolled a couple of turns, and settled.

Paisley's eyes flickered. She lifted a finger, and a thin green vine shot out of the ground, wrapped around the core, and pulled it right into her palm.

The car never slowed, still racing steadily toward its destination.

Elijah looked at the energy gun in his hands, amazed and impressed. "I can't believe how powerful this thing is.

"A regular gun could only wound a Level Four zombie at best. We'd still have to finish it off with an affinity, which wastes time and energy."

He'd just one-shot a Level Four mutant zombie without breaking a sweat.

Orion kept his eyes on the road, but his mind was all on the energy gun. His fingers tightened slightly on the steering wheel, and he couldn't hide the excitement and hope in his voice.

"If we could mass-produce weapons this powerful, it wouldn't just change the base's fighting strength. It'd change how everyone survives in the apocalypse."

He paused for a second, pushed down the shock, and glanced back at Paisley.

"Paisley, where did you get these guns? Do you have the blueprints or know how they work? How many more do you have right now?"

Elijah turned to her too, his tone serious. "With the guns you gave us, Pais, I'd say we've got at least a 90 percent shot at pulling this mission off.

"Last time our team came out to scout the aquarium, every step was a nightmare, and we barely made it out. If we'd had guns like these back then, it wouldn't have been nearly that hard."
