Chapter 334

"I'm Chase's cousin. Mr. Quinn. I heard he was badly hurt on a mission and came to see for myself."

His gaze drifted back to Reid, his tone even but heavy with authority.

"You're a friend of his? Thank you for looking after him.

What have the doctors said? When will he wake up?"

Reid had always had a knack for reading a room and clinging to anyone with power, and he knew instantly this Mr. Quinn was someone important — maybe his one chance to turn things around.

He swallowed every stray thought and answered eagerly, working to close the distance between them.

"Mr. Quinn, Chase and I are close friends. This is my sister — she and Chase are actually dating, so of course I'd want to look after him."

"They're both in bad shape right now."

He let out a drawn-out sigh for effect, his face twisted with manufactured frustration, laying it on thick.

"The doctors are stumped. They've both been hit with some rare, powerful poison. Nothing normal works — they won't wake up unless we find the actual cure."

******

Damon's eyes darkened slightly, a frown creasing his brow.

"How did they get poisoned in the first place? What have the doctors tried? Have you brought in a healer?"

Reid wasted no time twisting the story further, laying every ounce of blame on Paisley.

"Paisley has a universal cure. But she's selfish and cold-blooded — she picked and chose who to save. She healed the rest of the team and just left my sister and Chase to rot."

He said it with such conviction that Paisley sounded like a genuine monster — cruel, self-serving, willing to let her own teammates suffer.

A deeper chill crept into Damon's eyes, his voice cooling further.

"So you're telling me she has the ability and the cure to wake them both, and she's refusing on purpose?"

"Yes!"

Reid nodded hard, his hand drifting to the bruises still fresh on his face, a flash of spite in his eyes as he piled on more lies.

"I went to beg her myself this afternoon. I humbled myself, asked as nicely as I could, just for her to help. She wouldn't budge — and then she beat me and threw me out.

These bruises? All her doing!"

He made sure every word painted Paisley as arrogant and cruel, conveniently leaving out that he'd shown up to pick a fight, throw threats around, and guilt-trip her into submission.

His hands clenched at his sides as he finished, a cold sneer curling inside him, hatred churning.

Paisley. This time I've got Mr. Quinn backing me. Let's see you wriggle out of this one. Someone was always going to be able to put you in your place.

Damon fell silent for a moment, his gaze settling heavily on the two lifeless figures on the beds. The air around him turned colder, sharp enough that the whole room seemed to drop a good ten degrees.

"The base trains Affinity users so we can fight side by side and keep each other safe — not so one person can hoard a cure and let people die out of spite."

His tone was flat, but there was no room in it for argument.

"If she has the cure and the ability to save them, then she has the responsibility to do it."

Reid could barely contain his glee at that, and pressed his advantage immediately.

"Exactly right, Mr. Quinn! That's exactly it! Please, see justice done for my sister and Chase!"

Damon said nothing more, his gaze cutting toward the door.

"Get in here."

An aide waiting outside pushed the door open at once, standing ramrod straight, head bowed, awaiting orders.

"Take a squad to the villa district. Bring Paisley Lennox here."

Damon's voice was hard, without a shred of mercy.

"I don't care how you do it. Just get her here."

"Yes, sir!"

The aide acknowledged the order and left at once.

Reid's hands balled into fists at his sides, triumph surging through him so hard he nearly laughed out loud.

It worked!

Mr. Quinn was clearly someone with deep roots in Aetheris's upper echelons — nothing like some ordinary squad leader.

With him applying pressure, Paisley was about to be in serious trouble.

No matter how strong she was, no matter how much her team trusted her, she had no hope of fighting raw power like this.

Go on, Paisley, be arrogant. Refuse to help.

Let's see who can protect you this time!

Vicious satisfaction churned in Reid's eyes as he settled back to wait for Paisley to be dragged in, forced to admit fault and bow her head.
