Chapter 402

"Begging me?"

"Who do you think you are? What gives you the right?"

"Your little Healing Affinity? Or that useless boyfriend of yours, who's nothing in front of me?"

His tone was lazy, but every word cut, and it crushed what was left of her pride.

******

Her door swung open and Dennis hurried in with a cup of warm water. He took in his daughter's wine-stained clothes, tangled hair, and gray face, and worry flooded his eyes.

"Sweetheart, what happened? Why did you drink so much? Look at you."

He glanced around the empty room, and his stomach tightened. "Where's your mother? Why isn't she here? What's going on with you two?"

Chloe took the cup. The warmth in her fingers loosened her nerves for the first time all night.

She sipped. The water ran down her throat, but it couldn't thaw the cold in her chest.

Everything she had swallowed all day came up at once. Her eyes went red, and her voice cracked as she told him all of it, from begging in the middle of the night to being humiliated in front of everyone.

"Dad, Mom's still locked up in the Security Office. I can't get her out."

"I went to Jasper Quinn myself. He's the one pushing the case, and he won't give an inch."

"Nobody on the base will stand up for us. This isn't going to be easy to fix."

Dennis's face darkened. A heavy shadow settled between his brows, and the air around him turned cold.

"Outrageous!" he snapped. "The Thompsons may not be the top family in Aetheris, but we're nobody's doormat."

"Jasper Quinn? That layabout who does whatever he wants? Why would he go after your mother?"

Chloe lowered her eyes, lashes trembling. "Because of Uncle Gerald's new daughter, Paisley. Jasper's backing her. He's doing this on purpose."

She looked up at him, lost and scared. "Dad, what do we do now?"

Dennis scowled. "Grant still hasn't woken up? Useless. When it matters, he's no help at all."

"If he's really lost that arm, just break up with him."

His voice turned cold and practical. "He's a side-branch Quinn. No real weight, no foundation. He can't protect you."

"If you'd landed Damon Quinn or Elijah Blackwood, none of this would've happened."

Then he reined himself in. "But what's done is done. First, we get your mother out. Otherwise the whole base will be laughing at us tomorrow."

Chloe's eyes flickered with something cold. "Grant's probably finished anyway. I'll use this to end things with him."

She had wanted to dump Grant for a while now, and this time he was most likely done for.

"Okay, Dad. Whatever you say."

"That's my girl. You always see things clearly." Dennis nodded, pleased. "With your looks and your Affinity, you can find a better perch."

Then he added, "The root of all this is the daughter Gerald's branch just found."

"Come on. Don't change. Come as you are. We're going to Uncle Gerald's to beg."

His voice dripped with sour envy. "The junior branch sat quiet for years, and suddenly they find a daughter who has Jasper Quinn backing her. Lucky them."

"But Jasper's just a spoiled brat with no real roots. If Gerald speaks up for us, this goes away."
