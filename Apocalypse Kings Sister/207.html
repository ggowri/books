Chapter 207

If those were hers, she'd be at Level Six in no time, right up there with the base's best.

And Paisley had just turned them down.

What a joke.

Just then, the black, standing water deep in the passage suddenly churned, boiling up in violent bubbles.

A high-tier presence far more savage, far colder than anything before erupted outward, blanketing the whole area.

The roar of churning water pounded at their eardrums, the murky black surface heaving as a massive shadow surged beneath it.

The next moment, several thick, slick, ink-black tentacles studded with suckers burst from the surface.

They swept through the air in every direction, slamming into the ground, water exploding outward with a wave of rotten stench and crushing pressure.

A Level Six mutant octopus, hidden all this time in the darkest corner of the water.

It had been lying low the entire time—the true ruler of this whole stretch of the building.

Its body was massive, tentacles tough as iron, skin slicked with venomous black ink that ate through anything it touched, smoking on contact. Wherever it passed, the concrete floor pitted and dissolved.

Worse than anything they'd fought yet, by far.

“It's bad—a Level Six mutant octopus! Way worse than that swarm earlier!”

The team, barely steady from one close call, fell apart all over again at the sight of it.

Chase's Affinity was spent dry. There was no barrier left in him to raise.

Pure terror seized Rachel whole, her body ice-cold and shaking, pupils blown wide, nothing left in her but fear.

No. She couldn't die here.

Not after everything it took to come back. Not before she'd made it to the top. Not in some cold, filthy pit like this.

Watching the water churn with tentacle after tentacle, slick and sucker-studded, slamming against the opening—the sheer mass of writhing limbs was enough to turn anyone's stomach.

She swallowed her terror and grabbed hold of Chase, pressing her mouth to his ear, her whisper fast and urgent, pitched low enough for only him to hear.

“Chase, there's no killing this thing. We need to slip out, now, quietly…”

“Stay here and we're both dead!”

******

She'd seen those tentacles and that corrosive black venom with her own eyes, and this thing was worse than anything the old reports had described. She had no intention of dying alongside anyone here.

Chase, already running on empty and wanting out, made up his mind the second she said it—he'd slip away the first chance he got.

Up ahead, Elijah and Orion raised their guns and fired on the octopus at once, hoping the guns' firepower would be enough to put it down.

But when their fingers pulled the triggers, nothing happened—the cores were spent.

Both their faces darkened. They holstered the guns and called up their own Affinity instead.

Purple lightning cracked from Elijah's palm, sharp and biting, striking the octopus's tentacles dead on.

Flame leapt from Orion's fingers, a blazing wall of fire scorching the ink flying through the air.

Against the octopus's thick, toxin-armored hide, lightning and fire only managed to scorch the surface, numb it for a moment—nothing that amounted to real damage.

“What now? Are we really meant to die here today?”

Watching the venomous ink splash and eat into the floor, watching those massive tentacles sweep and crash, everyone shrank back into the corners, too afraid of being corroded to even think about joining the fight.

The whole fight had gone hopeless.

As panic gripped the whole team, Paisley cut through it, her order firm.

“Everyone, cover your nose and mouth. Hold your breath, now!”

With a flick of her fingers, she pulled out a massive bag of absurdly hot chili powder, and before anyone could even process it, flung it out over the mutant with a snap of her wrist.

A fine red cloud drifted down, settling over the octopus's whole body, into every opening, into the water itself.

The specially-bred powder spread fast in the damp air, a sharp, blistering scent flooding the whole corridor—devastating to a mutant whose senses ran this sharp.

The octopus seized up at once, its massive body thrashing, tentacles flailing wild, abandoning the attack entirely just to beat at the water in agony, trying to escape the burning in the air.
