Chapter 207

If Rachel had those cores, she'd hit Level Six in no time and become one of the top fighters at the base. And Paisley had actually turned them down. What a joke.

Just then, the pitch-black pool of standing water deep in the passage suddenly began to churn, bubbling and boiling like crazy.

A high-level aura, far more savage and sinister than the last, exploded outward and smothered the whole area.

The thundering roar of water made their eardrums throb. The murky black seawater churned wildly as a gigantic shadow spread and moved fast beneath the surface.

A second later, several thick, slimy, ink-black tentacles covered in creepy suckers burst out of the water.

The giant tentacles swept in every direction and slammed into the ground. The water exploded, and the spray carried a thick, rotten stench and a crushing pressure that hit them head-on.

It was a Level Six mutant octopus, a high-level beast that had been hiding in a blind spot of the deep water! It had been lying low the whole time, hiding its presence, and it was the true ruler of this place.

The mutant octopus was enormous, and its tentacles were tough and hard. Its skin was coated in a slimy layer of inky black venom, and anything it touched started corroding and smoking right away. Everywhere it went, the concrete floor quickly melted into tiny pits.

This was way more dangerous than before!

"Oh no! It's a Level Six mutant octopus! It's way harder to deal with than that swarm of sea beasts!"

The team members who'd just barely survived went white, and the calm they'd fought so hard to get back fell apart all over again.

Chase had used up all his power and couldn't put up a psychic barrier anymore.

Rachel was gripped by an overwhelming fear of death. She went ice cold and shook all over, her eyes wide with terror.

No, I can't die here! I finally got a second life, and I'm not dying in this cold, filthy hole before I make it to the top!

She watched tentacle after tentacle rise from the churning water, long and slimy and covered in suckers, reeking as they slammed against the opening. The sight of all those holes and writhing limbs made her skin crawl and her stomach turn.

Rachel forced down her fear and grabbed Chase. She pressed her face to his ear and whispered fast, so quietly only he could hear.

"Chase, there's no way we can kill this thing! When we get the chance, let's sneak out of here... If we stay, we're dead for sure!"

She'd seen the swarm of disgusting tentacles and that super-corrosive black venom with her own eyes. This Level Six mutant octopus was even scarier than the news reports from her past life had said, and she had no intention of staying behind to die with them.

Chase had already been thinking about backing out since his power was drained. When he heard her, he made up his mind to run the first chance he got.

Meanwhile, up front, Elijah and Orion raised their energy guns right away and fired at the Level Six mutant octopus again, hoping the guns would be strong enough to kill it.

But when they pulled the triggers, nothing happened. The energy was gone, and the guns wouldn't fire.

The two of them frowned, put the energy guns away, and called on their powers instead.

Lightning exploded in Elijah's palm, and sharp, icy bolts of purple lightning split the air and struck the octopus's tentacles dead-on.

Fire danced at Orion's fingertips, and a roaring blaze swept out, burning away the black venom splashing through the air.

But the Level Six mutant octopus had thick skin and a coat of venom protecting it. The lightning and fire could only scorch its surface and stun it for a moment. They couldn't do any real damage.

"What do we do? Are we really doomed to die here today?"

Venomous ink splashed everywhere, eating into the floor, and the thick tentacles kept sweeping and smashing down. Everyone was terrified of getting corroded, so they huddled in the farthest corners of the passage, shaking. Nobody had the courage to step up and help fight.

There seemed to be no way out.

Just as panic was spreading through the whole team, Paisley gave a firm order. "Everyone, cover your nose and mouth and hold your breath, now!"

With a flick of her fingers, she pulled out a huge bag of insanely hot chili powder. Before anyone could react, she snapped her wrist and flung it into the air above the mutant beast.

A thick cloud of fine red powder drifted down, covering the octopus's body, getting into every hole, and settling into the water.

The special chili powder spread fast in the damp air, and the sharp, burning smell filled the whole passage within seconds. It worked wonders against mutant sea beasts, since their sense of smell was so sharp.

The Level Six mutant octopus in the water reacted right away. Its huge body rolled and thrashed like crazy, and its thick tentacles flailed wildly. It forgot all about attacking and just slapped at the water in pain, trying to get away from the burning smell.
