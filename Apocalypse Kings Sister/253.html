Chapter 253

"Paisley, take me too. I want to help."

Madison stepped forward as well, clearly wanting in on it too.

Paisley shook her head, her tone calm but final, shutting down the idea before it spread.

"A night raid works best small. More people just means more chances to get spotted."

"Stay here. I'll bring them both back safe. I promise."

Something about her steadiness just made people believe her.

Ashley pressed her lips together, still wanting to go, but nodded anyway and let it go.

Elijah and Orion exchanged a look. With Pais along, calm and lethal as she was, their odds just went up considerably.

No time to waste. Paisley freed the weakest one and tightened the vines on the rest, sealing off any chance of escape.

"Lead the way."

At her cold words, the man scurried ahead, too terrified to think of anything but obeying.

Night lay thick as ink over the whole uncanny village.

Paisley, Elijah, and Orion followed close behind, hidden in the dark, presence suppressed.

Ten minutes later.

A grand, lavish villa came into view.

Unlike the dead silence everywhere else, this place blazed with light, noise spilling out in layers.

Mahjong tiles clacking, lewd laughter, drunken revelry, all tangled together with faint, desperate sobbing and cries for help.

Luxury and despair, side by side, laid the rot of this place bare.

Paisley's eyes went cold. A slender vine curled silently around the guide's neck and knocked him out cold.

He crumpled to the ground, unconscious.

The three of them slipped through the shadows along the wall, moved into the courtyard, and found the hidden door beneath the rockery in the back garden.

The door sat ajar, darkness below, the stench of death and rot hitting them full force.

They held their breath and descended into the dark.

The basement was cold and damp, lit by sickly white light, rows of cages holding zombies and shaking, terrified people.

In the open center, Fat Wagner sat on a couch, his gut spilling over, barking orders at his men.

"Keep going! Dig out another core... don't stop!"

His men hacked at Level Five zombies penned in the cages, one after another.

The zombies couldn't even move, limbs hacked off, flesh rotted and turned inside out, grotesque beyond description.

******

Their method was monstrous. Capture people alive, maim them deliberately, then infect and pen them to grow into zombies slowly, for harvesting.

Once a zombie leveled up enough, they'd kill it and take the core for a quick power boost.

Cruelty beyond words. Crimes too many to count.

After five Level Five cores in a row, Fat Wagner absorbed them all at once, his Affinity surging.

"HAHAHA! I broke through!"

He threw his head back laughing, eyes manic, voice raw with triumph.

"I just hit Level Five! Early stage, baby!"

"I'm a genius! Give it time, I'll hit Six, Seven, even Eight!"

"I'm going to be the strongest Affinity user in this whole apocalypse! I'll run the entire world!"

His men bowed and groveled, the praise pouring out.

"Boss is unstoppable! Nobody can touch him!"

"Boss is a genius, finding a way to level up this easy."

"Boss is amazing! There's nothing he can't do!"

One Level Three underling rubbed his hands together, fawning.

"Boss, I'm only Level Three. Please, help me break through to Level Four! Then I can serve you even better!"

Riding high, Fat Wagner waved a careless hand.
