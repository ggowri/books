Chapter 109

Paisley kept going, taking everything that would help them travel, fight, or survive and putting it in her Storage Dimension.

She figured the fourth floor was the top of the mall. But just as she was about to leave, she spotted a hidden staircase in a corner that led straight up to a fifth floor.

The fifth floor was the mall's high-end jewelry and gold section, with rows and rows of glass display cases.

They were packed with gold and silver jewelry, fancy ornaments, collectible gold bars, and commemorative gold coins. All that pricey luxury stuff was just sitting there in the ruins, waiting for someone to take it.

Paisley's eyes lit up.

[Master! There's a ton of gold here! You can trade it for so many shop points!]

Trovie's sweet little voice piped up, bursting with excitement. It switched straight into treasure-hunting mode.

Tiny specks of light darted through the air, pinpointing every bit of gold hidden deep in the display cases, behind the counters, and in secret compartments.

[So much gold! We're rich!]

As the glittering treasure came into view, several Level Three zombies that had been lying in wait burst out of the dark corners behind the display cases. A Level Four Water Affinity zombie that had been hiding for a long time lunged at them too.

Paisley didn't even have to lift a finger. Elijah struck first. Purple-blue lightning cracked through the air and tore through several zombies at once, paralyzing them and ripping them apart in one clean move.

Next to him, the little white cat shot up to 16 feet tall and leaped into the air. Its claws, wrapped in frost, smacked the Level Four Water zombie aside.

Then, with one powerful swipe, it smashed the zombie's skull and took it out in the blink of an eye, grabbing its crystal core.

Paisley wasn't just standing around either. With Trovie guiding her, she swept every gold bar, gold ornament, piece of fine jewelry, and collectible coin into her Storage Dimension until the cases were completely empty.

She took it, took it, and took it some more. All of it was gone!

Even after she'd cleaned everything out, she still felt like she wanted more.

Just as she turned to leave, Trovie's alert chimed again, and it sounded thrilled.

[Master! There's more! There's a secret vault in there, a gold vault packed with all kinds of hidden treasures!]

Paisley's eyes brightened. Trovie pointed her to the decorative wall at the very back of the showroom. She reached out and pushed a loose panel, and a hidden entrance appeared right away.

The second the vault door swung open, a blinding wave of golden light poured out, so bright that she had to blink.

This was the mall's vault, built just to store high-end gold collections and reserve gold.

Inside the huge room, rows of pure gold bars were stacked perfectly, gleaming with a rich, heavy shine. They looked solid and seriously valuable.

Collectible gold statues, ornate gold jewelry, gold-plated dishes, and rare limited-edition coins filled the whole vault. Storage boxes in the corners held even more, including countless chunks of raw precious metal and high-end jewelry that had never even been sold.

The whole room shimmered with metal. Even under a thin layer of dust, the treasures still looked rich and heavy, and they stood out even more against the dead silence of the apocalypse.

[We're rich, we're rich! With all this gold, you can trade for a crazy amount of shop points! Master, you might actually make Level Three trader now!]

Trovie spun happily through the air, its light flickering and bouncing. Its system voice was practically bursting with joy.

Paisley smiled too. She was in a great mood.

Who wouldn't be happy seeing this much shiny gold?

Supplies were precious in the apocalypse, but this gold could be traded in the Trading Plaza for rare items and high-level resources that were worth even more.

With a single thought, Paisley waved her hand.

The huge stacks of gold bars, the dazzling piles of gold and jewelry, and every collectible treasure and chunk of raw metal disappeared into her Storage Dimension.

In just a few moments, the whole secret vault was picked clean. She didn't even leave the tiniest gold coin in the corner or the plainest little charm behind. Nothing was left.

When she was done, Paisley finally walked out of the vault and found herself face-to-face with Elijah, who'd just finished the fight.

Paisley cleared her throat awkwardly.

"That's everything here. Let's go check out somewhere else."

Elijah nodded and pulled up a map of Rivergate's outskirts.

"How much room do you have left in your dimension? How much more can it hold?"

Paisley checked her space. She still had more than half left, so she pointed at a big library on the map.

"Let's go there. Books are going to be really valuable later, and I've got plenty of room, so don't worry."

If those books got destroyed, they'd be gone forever.

Later on, the base they'd build would have missions just for finding books, and they'd be worth a lot of contribution points.
