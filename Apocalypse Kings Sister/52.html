Chapter 52

"How do you even have the nerve to ask me for help? Don't you know how much everybody hates you?

"Not kicking you while you're down is the nicest thing I'll ever do for you. And now you want me to save you? Dream on."

Telling Rachel off felt amazing.

In her past life, Rachel had schemed against her, crushed her, and framed her at every turn, until Paisley died a horrible death.

Now Rachel was finally getting what she deserved. Paisley wasn't the one dishing out the payback, but she was happy to watch, and she didn't feel an ounce of pity.

That threw Rachel for a loop.

Since when did Paisley get so smart?

Before, all Rachel had to do was bring up how the Lennoxes raised Paisley or play the victim, and Paisley would always cave. But today, nothing got through to her. She was ice-cold and wouldn't give an inch.

Paisley really had stopped caring about the Lennoxes. When she'd cut ties with them yesterday, she'd meant it.

When did she start changing?

In her past life, Paisley hadn't seen through any of it until the day she died.

Rachel gave up on Paisley completely and stopped pinning any hope on her. She whipped around to Elijah instead and screamed at him through her tears.

"Captain Blackwood! You're a soldier, aren't you? Protecting survivors and civilians is your job!

"Are you seriously going to stand there and let that freak drag me away? You'd really just let me die?"

Rachel acted like guilt-tripping Elijah was her God-given right. Paisley just sighed, bored, and shot right back.

"Have some shame, Rachel. Stop being so naive. This is the apocalypse, not the good old days when you could play the victim and get babied.

"Mr. Blackwood doesn't owe you a damn thing. Why should he pay for your stupidity? Who the hell are you to him that he'd risk his life for you?

"Every single day, people die by the thousands, and even more are begging to be saved. There are only so many supplies and only so much strength to go around. So why the hell should any of it go to you?"

With that, she didn't even bother looking at Rachel's pathetic face anymore. Instead, she looked up at the Level Three mutant humanoid.

"Well, big guy, what are you waiting for? The pretty lady's all yours. Grab her and go already.

"If you don't leave soon, somebody else might show up."

The second she said it, everyone went dead silent.

The Level Three mutant humanoid was smart. It tensed up all over and let out a low growl.

From the very start, the one it had feared most was Paisley, lazily munching her popcorn.

She was keeping her Level Four power hidden, but it still bore down on the creature and pinned it in place. It didn't dare make a careless move.

It knew that the moment Paisley stepped in, it would die right here, today.

Now that she clearly wasn't going to interfere, the mutant hesitated for a moment, like it was weighing its options. Then, it lowered its head and dropped two smooth, gleaming, top-quality Level Two crystal cores from its claws.

The cores hit the ground and rolled to a stop at Paisley's feet.

It was a thank-you gift, and a way to get on her good side.

With its business done, the mutant clutched the screaming, struggling Rachel tight and leaped away. In a few bounds, it vanished into the shadows of the stairwell.

Nobody dared to stop it.

Paisley bent down and picked up the two warm Level Two crystal cores. She ran her fingers over their smooth, full surfaces and smiled faintly, more than happy to accept them.

One was a Level Two Lightning Affinity core, and the other was a Level Two Wind Affinity core.

Along with the Level Two Metal Affinity core she already had, that was exactly enough for Elijah, Orion, and Mason to level up.

Smart beast. It knows how this works.

Around her, everyone had a different reaction.

Orion was long used to Paisley doing things her own way. With her, it felt like anything could happen, and he looked at her with pure affection.

Rachel didn't deserve an ounce of sympathy. Anyone who'd ever bullied Paisley was unforgivable.

Paisley knew exactly who deserved kindness and who didn't. She was no bleeding-heart saint, and Orion wouldn't have it any other way.

Elijah smiled as he watched Paisley calmly pocket the cores. The more he saw of her, the cuter she got.

And the way she'd stood up for him just now was even cuter.

She was cool-headed and sharp, with no fake saintliness and no drama. She did whatever she felt like, but when it counted, she was ruthless and decisive. He was finding it harder and harder to look away.

I'm starting to care about her way too much.

Mason felt nothing but deep respect for Paisley. As far as he was concerned, his idol could do no wrong.

Jasper had finally gotten over his terror, and now he just stood there, stunned.

This woman isn't even human. She can talk to that thing?

Deep down, she was colder, clearer-headed, and more ruthless than anyone he'd ever met.

Rachel had it coming. That's what she gets for messing with Paisley.
