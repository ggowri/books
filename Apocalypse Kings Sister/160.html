Chapter 160

The whole floor had brand-name clothing, home textiles, shoes, and accessories. Everything was neatly displayed, and the shelves were intact, with no sign of burning or ripping.

The mall must have been sealed up tight, keeping out the weather and the rampaging zombies, which was why everything here had survived in such good shape.

Rows of brand-new clothes for every season hung neatly on the racks. There were thick down jackets, soft sweaters, windproof shell jackets, and tough work clothes in every size and style, all clean, without a speck of dust or mildew.

The bedding section next to it was even better stocked.

There were stacks of sealed cotton bedding sets, thick fleece comforters, warm blankets, light summer quilts, and soft pillows, all fluffy to the touch.

There were also waterproof mattress covers, blackout curtains, house slippers, cotton pajamas, and every other home essential anyone could want.

Ever since the apocalypse, clean, warm clothes and bedding had been as good as cash. The winters were brutal, and cheap bedding couldn't keep out the bitter cold. Countless Affinity users had survived bloody battles, only to die in the dead of winter because they didn't have enough to keep warm.

Complete sets of clean, warm bedding like this were far rarer and more precious than ordinary supplies.

She wasn't going to leave anything useful behind.

Paisley's eyes swept the floor, and with a thought, she opened her Storage Dimension without hesitation.

In seconds, every neatly hung piece of clothing, every shell jacket, and every pair of shoes was gone, leaving whole racks bare. Next, she took the stacks of sealed bedding, blankets, sheet sets, and pillows, sorting them neatly into the living-supplies section of her Storage Dimension. After that, the curtains, mattress covers, small home goods, and warm accessories disappeared too.

She worked fast, and anything her eyes landed on vanished. The huge second floor emptied out right in front of her.

She thought of the others, though, so she left some for them.

In just a few minutes, everything on the second floor except what she'd set aside was in her Storage Dimension.

Trovie circled through the air, amazed, chattering nonstop in its sweet little voice.

[Master, you're amazing! Look at all these clothes!]

Paisley looked around once more and decided she'd gotten most of it. "Next floor."

With that, she turned toward the stairs to the third floor, ready to grab the electronics next.

With the power and internet down, electronics seemed useless for now. But Paisley knew order would be restored someday. Sooner or later, the base would be fully built, the power grid and networks would come back, and then all this high-tech equipment would be usable again.

The third floor opened into a clean electronics section, just as untouched as the ones below.

Only a few low-level zombies shuffled between the counters. They were slow to hear and slow to react, probably weakened from being stuck so long in this sealed-off area.

Paisley didn't bother with her sword. A flick of her fingers sent thin vines snaking out, wrapping around their necks and draining the life out of them, quick and clean.

Once they were gone, she could see the whole third floor.

The entire floor was lined with brand-name electronics counters, their display cases bright and their glass spotless.

There were brand-new smartphones, tablets, wireless earbuds, and smartwatches lined up in every model and style, without a single scratch or speck of dust.

The counters next to them were stocked with high-performance gaming laptops, slim work laptops, and full desktop setups, along with mechanical keyboards, gaming mice, and HD monitors.

On top of that, there was a ton of must-have outdoor tech, like high-capacity power banks, solar chargers, portable projectors, walkie-talkies, night-vision goggles, and HD camera drones. It was exactly the kind of gear field teams and base guards needed most in the apocalypse.

The walkie-talkies and night-vision goggles were especially valuable. With no cell signal and danger lurking everywhere at night, they were rare gear money couldn't buy, perfect for team missions, night watch, and long-distance contact.

Paisley looked over the floor full of high-tech gear, satisfied.

The base was just starting to take shape. Once it expanded and started setting up security systems, surveillance cameras, and field communication teams, this gear would come in very handy.

Without wasting another second, she opened her Storage Dimension and swept it all in.

Every brand-new phone, tablet, and wearable vanished, leaving the display cases empty.
