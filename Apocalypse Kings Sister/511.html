Chapter 511

"This willow has mutated, and it's at least Level Seven. It's a mutant carnivorous willow!

It lives off this underground water source and changed after the Crimson Rain. This entire cave is its hunting ground, and the fruit is bait!"

Thomas shouted to warn everyone.

Lightning cracked from Elijah's fingers, blinding silver arcs running along the ground and slamming into the thick mass of vines.

The lightning hit and dark green sap sizzled and smoked. Struck vines curled up and twitched, but more kept stretching from the trunk without end.

Spencer raised a hand and mutant flames rolled out, burning the web of vines coming for them.

The fire licked the branches, and a burnt smell filled the underground cavern. But the willow's roots went deep, and its life force was fierce. Burning alone wouldn't seriously hurt it.

Paisley's eyes were cold. Wind Affinity surging, she shot to Ashley's side, drew the Frostbloom Sword, and in a flash of light cut through several poisonous vines in a row.

She spread her psychic sense and instantly locked onto the main trunk buried deep underground.

"Its real body is deep under the ground behind the fruit trees. Attacking the branches only holds it back. We have to damage the roots to hurt it!"

Paisley raised a hand and released a pure, gentle purifying force.

It fell on the vines. The contaminated branches drooped and blackened at once, and their growth slowed visibly.

******

Ashley steadied herself and poured all of her Earth Affinity into the ground. The earth rumbled, thick layers of soil squeezed in on the main trunk, and spikes drove from every side toward the roots below.

"Orion, help me!"

Orion forged metal chains right away. Chain after chain flew out and bound the thick main vines, locking them down so they couldn't strike again.

The mutant willow shuddered violently. The whole orchard shook, and ripe fruit thudded to the ground.

More vines burst out from every direction and swept toward the team in a storm. The roots churned the soil, and the ground heaved and sank.

Paisley's eyes narrowed. She unleashed her full peak Level Seven Plant Affinity and raised a mass of tough vines that wrapped and held the willow's main trunk.

"Now!"

Elijah understood. Lightning crashed down onto the willow's trunk.

A huge explosion echoed through the cave. The trunk jerked violently and dark green contaminated sap sprayed everywhere.

The thick trunk shuddered. Countless branches lost their strength and dropped limp, no longer attacking.

The massive mutant willow slowly toppled.

Everyone let out a breath.

Thomas stepped up to check the withered trunk.

"The mutant plants made by the Crimson Rain hide really well. Good thing we reacted quickly."

"It feels like it had some intelligence. Plants seem to have gotten way smarter."

Paisley thought of the mutant willow in her storage dimension. Wouldn't this be perfect nutrition for it?

"Can I have this willow?"

Nobody objected.

She took it straight into her storage dimension and let the mutant willow slowly absorb its energy.

Meanwhile, Orion took out a tester. He took a river water sample first, then picked up a fallen apple and tested that.

Numbers jumped on the screen. After a moment, a green light came on.

"The water and fruit are clean! This underground water source was sealed off from the toxin in the Crimson Rain."

Ashley patted her chest, still a little shaken, and looked at the laden trees with shining eyes.

"That scared me. I almost walked right up and became fertilizer. But this is great, we found clean water and edible fruit!"

Elijah took out his own tester and ran the samples again. The clean numbers held steady and a green light came on, confirming the water was untouched by the contamination from the Crimson Rain.

He let out a long breath, a trace of delight in his eyes.

"I didn't expect today's mission to go this smoothly."

"The fruit trees and fruit are easy to carry out. But this living river, what do we do about that?"

Thomas turned to Paisley with a suggestion.

"Pais, do you have any big barrels in your storage dimension? We could carry some water back to the base for everyone to use. Then we can come back and haul water every day."

Orion looked at Paisley too and spoke thoughtfully.
