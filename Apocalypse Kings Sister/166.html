Chapter 166

Reid and Ryan glared at Rachel too, annoyed and angry, and they didn't hide their resentment.

"Last time you used us as shields too, and now you dragged Owen down with you. How can you be so selfish, Rachel? Do your brothers even mean anything to you?"

"If Owen hadn't dodged so fast, that zombie would've hit him head-on, and he'd be dead right now!"

Kelly was still shaken too. When she looked at her scrapes and saw how wrecked she was, she couldn't help complaining.

"If I'd known teaming up with you would land me in this kind of deadly mess, I never would've done it. Our plan didn't even work, and I almost got myself killed!"

For a moment, the car filled with blame and complaints, and their shaky alliance was close to falling apart.

Rachel saw how furious everyone was and put on a hurt, helpless look. Her eyes went red, and tears rolled down her cheeks as her voice shook with sobs.

She made herself look small and lost, glancing at her three angry brothers, then at Kelly, who looked fed up. She kept shaking her head and frantically tried to explain.

"I'm sorry, I'm so sorry, everyone. It's all my fault, but I swear I didn't mean it!

"When that zombie lunged, I froze and my mind went blank, so it was pure instinct. I never meant to hurt Owen, and I'd never drag you all into getting killed!"

Rachel wiped away her tears in a hurry, her shoulders trembling, looking as pitiful and wronged as she could.

"I know better than anyone how hard it is for us to stick together and survive in the apocalypse. Why would I ever try to hurt you guys on purpose?

"What would I get out of that? I was scared half to death too, and my hands are still shaking."

With that, she turned to Owen, whose face was still dark with resentment. Her voice grew even softer, and she looked humble and sorry as she apologized through her tears.

"Owen, I know I really messed up this time. I panicked and dragged you down with me, putting you in danger and nearly getting you hurt.

"You can blame me or yell at me all you want, and I won't argue.

"But we're family, blood family. I never wanted to hurt you, not even for a second, so please forgive me for panicking, okay?"

Then, as if all the hurt she'd been holding in for so long finally broke loose, she pushed back softly, her voice thick with tears.

"Every time we had a fight before, I always said it wasn't Paisley's fault, that it was an accident or that I was just being clumsy.

"But every time, you all insisted it was her fault... And now that there's been an accident, you're going to pin this on me too?"

Her words sounded helpless, but they hit the three Lennox brothers hard.

All three froze at the same time, a hollow feeling in their chests as memories of the past came flooding back.

Every time Rachel and Paisley had clashed, Rachel had always looked just like this, her eyes red, hurt and holding back, on the verge of tears.

She'd never come right out and said it was Paisley's fault. She'd only make soft, vague excuses, and it was always the same line.

"It's not Paisley's fault. I was just being clumsy."

Back then, all they ever saw were the tears in Rachel's eyes and how fragile she looked. They'd jumped to the conclusion that Paisley was a bossy, vicious bully.

They'd believed Rachel was the one being wronged, just too kind and softhearted to call Paisley out.

Every single time, without even asking what happened, they'd torn into Paisley and yelled at her. They'd called her petty, an ungrateful wretch, someone who didn't care about her own family and bullied Rachel whenever she felt like it.

But now, thinking back over every little detail, they couldn't help wondering. What if... what if they'd been wrong about Paisley from the very start?

Had every fight, every argument, every rift really been Paisley being pushy? Or had it been Rachel all along, playing weak on purpose and stirring things up behind the scenes?

All those years, Paisley had quietly put up with all their misunderstandings, harsh words, and cold looks. She'd carried every false accusation and every hurt all by herself.

The real cause of all those misunderstandings had never been quiet, reserved Paisley. It was Rachel, the fragile, innocent, pitiful-looking girl sitting right in front of them.

The sheer absurdity of it and the guilt crashed down on the three brothers, squeezing their hearts until they could barely breathe.
