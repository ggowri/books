Chapter 98

She didn't stop there. She turned and walked over to the huge fuel storage tank at the edge of the lot and pressed her palm flat against the cold, heavy metal.

The next second, the whole tank, along with all the fuel inside, disappeared into her Storage Dimension.

A minute ago, the gas station had been packed with fuel. Now, it was completely empty.

"Let's go," Elijah said. "Orion says Marcus can ride with him. You're riding with me from now on."

Paisley nodded and climbed into the passenger seat.

They pulled out behind the other two cars.

It was calm and quiet in the car. The road ahead was bleak and empty, and outside the window, the world was in ruins. But inside, it felt rare and peaceful.

The second Paisley sat down, she found herself holding a cat and a lollipop.

The little white cat curled up on her lap and kept rubbing its head against her, clingy and sweet. It stared longingly at the lollipop in her hand and let out a soft, begging mew.

Paisley sighed and ruffled its fluffy head, scolding it gently.

"How can a cat have such a sweet tooth?"

She sounded annoyed, but her hands were nothing but gentle. She quickly peeled off the colorful wrapper and slipped the candy into the cat's soft little mouth.

With the sweet candy in its mouth, the little white cat was in heaven. Its sapphire-blue eyes curved into two little crescents as it snuggled in her arms, licking the candy and letting out two soft mews.

Behind the wheel, Elijah caught the whole thing out of the corner of his eye, and his heart melted a little.

Sunlight slipped in through the window and fell across Paisley's gorgeous profile, softening the coldness in her face.

The sweet little kitten in her arms licked its candy happily. It was a perfect, peaceful moment.

Paisley, warm sunlight, and an adorable cat. It was such a simple picture, but it was enough to soothe every wound the apocalypse had left behind.

The road ahead was full of unknowns. Zombies and evil people were everywhere, and countless dangers were waiting for them.

But as long as she was by his side, Elijah felt more at peace than he ever had.

The cat was super smart and insanely powerful, and it never warmed up to anyone else. Paisley was the only one who could tame it.

Why did she love picking up strays so much?

First a cat, and now a guy.

Elijah's fingers tightened slightly on the wheel.

When will she look at me like that? When will I be the only one she pays attention to?

Just then, Paisley looked over at him. She pulled two more lollipops out of her Storage Dimension and held one out to him. "Want one?"

Elijah glanced at the bright, sugary candy out of the corner of his eye, and something stirred inside him.

He'd been cold and serious ever since he was a kid, and he'd never been into sweet little snacks like this. But if she was the one offering, he figured he could give it a try.

He pushed down the flutter in his chest, and his voice came out a little rough. "I'm driving. I don't have a free hand."

Paisley didn't think twice. She treated him exactly like she treated the cat, quickly unwrapping the candy and leaning over to carefully hold it out to him.

She did it like it was the most natural thing in the world. "Open up..."

Elijah's whole body tensed. He parted his lips slightly and took the lollipop into his mouth.

A sweet, fruity flavor burst across his tongue, and the sweetness spread through his whole body, all the way down to his heart.

The tips of Elijah's ears turned bright red. The warmth of her touch and the sweet taste mixed together, scrambling his thoughts and making his heart race.

He got so flustered that he choked a little on the candy and coughed twice.

Paisley turned to him right away, worried. "Elijah, what's wrong? Are you okay?"

Her warm breath brushed past his ear, and his ears burned even redder. He panicked, terrified she'd figure out how he really felt. He forced the flutter down and answered stiffly.

"I'm fine."

God, this is so embarrassing.

Elijah silently kicked himself.

To change the subject, he spoke up first. There was a faint hint of jealousy in his voice.

"Do you know this Marcus guy? You saved him, so why are you so set on keeping him on the team and bringing him with us?

"When you rescued those women, you didn't worry about what happened to them after. So why is he different?"

It had been bugging him the whole time, and he couldn't let it go.

Paisley looked at him in surprise. She hadn't expected him to bring it up himself, but that was fine. It was the perfect chance to explain everything.
