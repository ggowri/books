Chapter 279

The instant the last of the supplies disappeared, the whole underground armory shuddered violently.

From somewhere deep below, a low, vicious roar rumbled up, and a thick, suffocating malice came pouring through the ventilation shafts and cracks in the floor.

In the darkness at the back of the chamber, a pair of slit-pupiled, poison-green eyes slowly opened.

The Level Seven mutant giant serpent had woken.

The Level Seven mutant giant serpent had woken.

A deep, vicious roar rolled up from somewhere far below, shaking the armory's metal walls hard enough to rattle in their frames.

A thick, bitter malice came pouring through the vents and cracks in the floor, carrying the stench of rot and rusted metal, pressing down on all three of them until the air itself felt impossible to breathe.

In the endless dark at the back of the chamber, two points of crimson light flickered open, like a pair of demon's eyes waking in the night, cold and bloodthirsty, carrying nothing resembling life.

A heavy, grinding sound rose up as the enormous creature slid out from the depths of the tunnel below, and the Level Seven mutant serpent revealed itself in full.

Its body was massive, thick as a grown man's torso, covered head to tail in layered black scales, each edge razor-sharp, each surface gleaming with a cold metallic sheen — plating forged from a lifetime of devouring military-grade metal, hard enough to turn aside blade and bullet alike.

Its long body coiled and stretched, easily more than thirty feet, muscle bunched tight beneath the scales, radiating the crushing presence of an apex predator with every inch of its length.

Most unsettling of all were its eyes — slit pupils the color of fresh blood, narrow and sharp, shot through with veins, locked onto the three intruders with unmistakable, manic bloodlust.

Its forked tongue, deep red and impossibly long, flicked constantly in and out, trailing bursts of bone-chilling cold. Its fangs gleamed pale and sharp, and the venom dripping from them ate small, hissing craters into the ground.

This Level Seven serpent had fused metal and radiation mutations into something far beyond an ordinary Level Seven beast, its malice thick and heavy, its strength closing in on Level Eight. Just looking at it was enough to chill the blood.

All three of them froze, every hair standing on end, facing down the pressure of an apex predator. There was no pretending they weren't afraid.

Even Elijah, usually unshakable, felt the arcs of lightning around him go taut, his expression deadly serious.

Orion didn't hesitate. He grabbed Paisley's wrist and shouted, low and urgent.

"Run!"

The sudden pull caught Paisley off guard, and before she could even react, Orion was hauling her into a dead sprint, fleeing for their lives.

A cool gust of air hit her face as she stumbled to keep pace with him, calling out on instinct.

"Orion?"

"Don't look back! This thing's not something we can fight head-on right now!"

Orion's words came fast, his voice grimmer than she'd ever heard it, his grip on her wrist tightening further.

Elijah fell in behind them, running too.

Enraged, the Level Seven serpent let out another low roar, loud enough to make their ears ache.

The massive body surged after them, scales scraping the floor with a piercing screech, its thick tail lashing out and smashing through an entire row of alloy shelves, sending metal fragments exploding everywhere.

Ashley and Kelly came into view ahead, and the sight before them froze both girls stiff with shock.

Elijah reacted fast, throwing up a net of lightning to slow the serpent's charge, his voice booming.

"Everyone fall back! Get out of the armory!"

No one hesitated. They fell in behind Orion and Paisley, running for their lives.

The flight for survival had begun.

The serpent, briefly caught in the lightning net, grew only more furious.

Crackling sparks flew where the lightning struck its scales, but the bolts barely scorched the surface, unable to pierce armor forged from a lifetime of devouring metal. It only slowed the creature down, nothing more.

In the next instant, the serpent's head snapped up and spat out a thick cloud of dark green toxic mist.

The mist spread fast, hissing white smoke rising wherever it touched the ground, a foul, poisonous stench rolling out to seal off most of the escape route.

"Toxic gas! Hold your breath!"
