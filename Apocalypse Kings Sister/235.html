Chapter 235

Flashes of fire sparked faintly outside the cabin, the plane's hull torn and dented in several places, dark smoke billowing out through ragged holes. Heat and freezing high-altitude wind poured into the cabin through the gaps.

Wind tore in through the shattered side window, whipping everyone's hair and clothes into chaos, the whole cabin descending into panic, danger closing in from every side.

Elijah, Orion, and the others were already on their feet at the front of the cabin, hands moving fast through seals, channeling their Affinities, trying to drive back the crows and keep the plane steady.

But the mutant crows came in numbers too massive to count, blotting out the sky, impossible to kill fast enough, impossible to hold back at all.

After several rounds of fighting them off, the plane's damage kept mounting, the turbulence growing more violent by the second. It wouldn't be long before they lost control entirely.

Elijah's face had gone grim, cold dread settling over his features as he swept his gaze across the cabin.

"The plane's too badly damaged, it won't hold much longer, we could lose control any second. Everyone get ready to jump. Evacuate now!"

The situation left no room to wait.

Orion's voice carried loud and steady over the alarm and the roar of wind, cutting through the chaos.

"Everyone stay calm! Don't panic! Grab your gear from under your seats and suit up now, we're jumping!"

Freezing wind tore through the broken window, knocking people off balance, the weightlessness and sheer terror of the altitude gripping everyone's nerves at once.

More than a few fighters had gone pale, fear and panic plain on their faces, hands white-knuckled around their parachute packs, bodies shaking uncontrollably.

"What do we do? I'm scared..."

"Are we actually going to die up here today? I've never jumped before."

"Those crows are still attacking out there, are we going to get eaten alive?"

"I just want to go home. I miss my parents. I hate this world."

.........

Ashley, the youngest among them, had never faced anything this dangerous before. Her face had gone white, and she clung tight to Paisley's arm, trembling, her voice thick with tears and fear.

"Paisley, I'm scared..."

Paisley reached over and covered her cold, shaking hand with her own, letting the warmth of her touch settle something in the girl.

Her mind raced, thoughts spinning fast.

She had over a dozen hovercars stored away in her Storage Dimension, more than enough to get everyone down safely and sidestep this disaster entirely.

But with Rachel, the three Lennox brothers, and everyone else with questionable motives right here in this cabin, pulling out a hovercar now would draw far too much attention and expose her secret, with consequences she couldn't predict.

Besides, a standard high-altitude jump, while risky, wasn't fatal for an Affinity user.

Weighing it all, Paisley pushed down the urge to reveal her hidden resources and spoke gently to reassure Ashley instead.

"Don't worry. Jump with me, and you'll be fine."

Her voice came out certain, carrying a quiet strength that settled the girl instantly.

Across the cabin, Rachel and the three Lennox brothers had completely lost their composure, faces drained of color, bodies trembling uncontrollably.

The three of them had never been particularly strong to begin with, surviving mostly by sticking together. Reid and Ryan hadn't even awakened an Affinity yet; they were still ordinary people.

Thousands of feet up, wind howling, the plane seconds from going down, the situation had completely shattered their nerves.

Reid's hands and legs had gone weak, fingers digging into the edge of his seat, voice shaking apart with terror.

"I never awakened an Affinity. If I jump from this height, am I going to die?!"

Ryan looked even more terrified, eyes red-rimmed, clutching at Owen's sleeve, voice breaking as he begged.

"I haven't awakened either! Please, you have to protect me!"

Owen took a deep breath, forcing down his own fear, fighting to stay composed as he tried to steady the other two.

"Don't worry. I've got you both. Nothing's going to happen."

The words had barely left him when he turned to Rachel, who was fumbling frantically with her own parachute gear, his tone turning pleading.

"Rachel, I can only protect one person while jumping. I can't cover both Reid and Ryan. Can you take one of them? Pick whichever one."

The question made Rachel's hands still for a second. Nothing in her eyes carried any trace of sibling warmth, only pure, cold selfishness.

She didn't hesitate for a second, her refusal sharp and dismissive, dripping with irritation and scorn.

"I can't even guarantee my own safety, and you want me hauling dead weight along with me?

You can't be serious."

When it came down to life or death, she could only look out for herself.

Hearing that, the three Lennox brothers went pale, speechless, a mix of cold despair and heartbreak settling over them.
