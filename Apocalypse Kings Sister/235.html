Chapter 235

Outside the cabin, flames flickered faintly. The plane's hull had been pecked open and smashed in several places, and thick black smoke poured out of the broken holes. Hot air mixed with the freezing high-altitude wind kept rushing into the cabin.

A fierce wind blew in through the shattered side window, whipping everyone's hair around and making their clothes flap wildly. The whole cabin was in chaos, with danger on every side.

Elijah, Orion, and the others were already on their feet at the front of the cabin. Their hands moved fast as they called up their powers, driving back the crows that kept ramming the plane, trying to scare off the flock and keep the plane steady.

But there were way too many mutant crows. They filled the whole sky, and no matter how many they killed or drove off, more kept coming. There was no way to stop them.

After several rounds of fighting, the plane was more and more damaged, and it shook harder and harder. Before long, it would lose control and crash.

Elijah looked extremely grim, his face icy, as he scanned everyone.

"The plane's badly damaged, and it won't hold up much longer. It could lose control and crash at any moment. Everyone, get ready to parachute out now!"

There wasn't a second to lose.

Orion immediately shouted instructions to everyone, his voice steady and strong over the alarm and the wind.

"Everyone, stay calm! Don't panic! Grab the emergency gear from under your seat cushions, put it on fast, and get ready to jump!"

The freezing wind blowing in through the broken window made it hard to stand. The weightlessness and danger of being so high up swept through the cabin, and fear gripped everyone.

Plenty of team members had gone pale, panic and fear in their eyes. They clutched their parachute packs tight, and their bodies couldn't stop shaking.

"What do we do? I'm scared..."

"Are we going to die here today? I've never parachuted before."

"The crows out there are still attacking. Are they going to eat us?"

"I want to go home so bad. I miss my mom and dad. I hate the apocalypse."

Ashley, the youngest, had never been through anything this dangerous. Her little face had gone white, and she hugged Paisley's arm tight, trembling. "Paisley, I'm scared..."

Paisley reached over and gently covered Ashley's cold, shaking hand, and the steady warmth of her fingers calmed her.

Her mind was racing.

Her Storage Dimension held more than a dozen hovercars she'd traded for, enough to get everyone safely to the ground and avoid this whole crisis.

But Rachel, the three Lennox brothers, and other people with shady motives were all right here in the cabin. Pulling out a hovercar now would be way too flashy and would only expose her secret, and the trouble that followed would never end.

Besides, a normal high-altitude parachute jump was risky for Affinity users, but it definitely wasn't deadly.

After weighing the pros and cons, Paisley pushed down the idea of using her trump card. Her eyes stayed calm as she gently reassured Ashley.

"Don't worry. Jump with me, and you'll be fine." Her confident voice had a calming strength to it, and it settled Ashley right away.

But on the other side of the cabin, Rachel and the three Lennox brothers were falling apart. Their faces were deathly pale, and they couldn't stop shaking.

The three brothers had never been very talented, and they'd only survived this long by sticking together. Reid and Ryan still hadn't awakened any powers, so they were just ordinary people.

Thousands of feet up, with the wind howling and the plane about to crash at any moment, this hopeless situation shattered what nerve they had left.

Reid's arms and legs went weak, and he dug his fingers into the edge of his seat, his voice shaking terribly. "I never awakened a power! If I jump from this high up, am I gonna die?!"

Ryan was so scared his eyes went red, and he clung to Owen's sleeve, begging close to tears. "I haven't awakened one either! Owen, you have to protect me!"

Owen took a deep breath and forced down his own fear. He tried hard to stay calm and reassured the two of them in a steady voice.

"Don't worry. I'm here, and nothing's going to happen."

As soon as he finished, he turned to Rachel, who was frantically struggling into her parachute pack, and spoke almost pleadingly. "Rachel, I can only protect one person when I jump, so I can't look after both Reid and Ryan. Will you take Reid or Ryan?"

The moment he said it, Rachel, who'd been hurriedly adjusting her gear, froze. There wasn't a trace of sibling love in her eyes, only pure selfishness and coldness.

She didn't hesitate at all and turned him down coldly, sounding impatient and mocking. "I can't even keep myself safe, and you want me to drag dead weight along too? You've got to be kidding me."

When it came down to life or death, she could only look out for herself.

When the three Lennox brothers heard that, they went white and couldn't say a word. They felt cold and hopeless, their feelings all mixed up.
