Chapter 330

Gabriel and Elijah each took a group to search the facility for more intel.

While everyone was occupied, Paisley slipped away alone toward the hidden alloy hatch.

The lock on the door was simple — just an old mechanical mechanism. A faint pulse of her Affinity, and the tumblers gave way easily.

Click.

The heavy alloy door swung inward, releasing a wave of old, dust-laden air mixed with the cold gleam of metal.

The hidden vault opened up before her, vast and empty of people, stacked wall to wall with neat rows of gold bars, their dull gold shine catching the dim light — an almost absurd quantity, more than she'd ever seen in one place.

Along both sides of the vault, ornate wooden display shelves held an array of rare antiques — fine jade carvings, vintage artwork, gilded relics, every piece in pristine condition, the kind of thing that had all but vanished from the world.

Beyond that, crates stacked in the corner held untouched precision equipment, rare medical supplies, and several cases of old-world cash, an amount that was hard to even estimate.

The sheer volume of wealth packed into this room was staggering, a genuine treasury hiding underground.

Trovie bounced with excitement in the back of her mind, voice pitched high.

[Host! We hit the jackpot! That's got to be at least 8,800 pounds of gold!]

A flicker of delight crossed Paisley's eyes, and she didn't hesitate for a second.

"Take it. All of it. Leave nothing behind."

The moment she said it, every gold bar, every antique, every piece of equipment and medicine, and every stack of cash in the vault dissolved into light and flowed into her storage.

In seconds, the massive vault stood completely empty, not a single item left behind.

Paisley gave the order.

[Trovie, convert all the gold directly into system points.]

Trovie quickly tallied up the total value.

[Conversion complete! 8,800 pounds of gold converted into 80,000 points!]

[Current host balance: 1,405,360 points!]

Watching her balance spike again, Paisley's mood lifted considerably.

She tucked away her satisfaction, settling her expression back into neutral calm as she returned to the group.

By then, the battlefield outside had been fully cleared, the team resting and recovering. Elijah and Gabriel had already returned with their search parties, having come up empty-handed.

Of course they had. Every piece of research data, every sample, every instrument — Paisley had already claimed it all.

After an unsuccessful search, with no other options left, the team gathered up the bodies of the mutants scattered across the battlefield to bring back as proof the mission had been completed. A reasonable enough outcome for a 3S high-risk mission.

Once everyone had regrouped, every eye in the group drifted, almost involuntarily, toward Chase and Rachel, still unconscious in the corner, expressions complicated.

The rest of them had fought through hell to clear out every mutant in this place.

Chase and Rachel had simply slept through the whole thing.

Both of them were poisoned badly enough that without a cure, they'd likely stay unconscious forever, reduced to nothing.

Not that it was anyone else's problem.

Whatever life they had left, they owed entirely to Paisley.

Noticing the weight of everyone's attention, Paisley spoke up, calm and matter-of-fact.

"Don't look at me. I already handed out every Antidote Pill I had. There's nothing left."

Gabriel's eyes stayed steady, understanding settling in without needing to be said.

He knew exactly what Paisley's healing was capable of. She could have saved Chase and Rachel if she'd wanted to.

But he wasn't going to push her into it. He didn't say a word of persuasion.

Instead, he gave a quiet order to two of the sturdier fighters.

"Get them on the plane. We're heading back."

The fighters moved immediately, lifting the unconscious Chase and Rachel, and the team began the return trip.

Half an hour later, the aircraft touched down safely back at the base.

Paisley returned to the villa.

Ashley, who'd been waiting anxiously the whole time, rushed over the second she saw Paisley, checking her over carefully for any injuries. Once she confirmed Paisley was unhurt, the tight knot in her chest finally loosened, and her face broke into a relieved smile.

"Paisley! Did the mission go okay?"

"Does this mean we can head back to Aetheris now?"

Paisley gave a small nod, her tone easy.

"It's done, more or less."

"We'll head out once they give us the word."

"Then you must be exhausted, and starving! Go rest, I'll bring you lunch right now."

Ashley's eyes softened with concern as she hurried off to get the food.

After a simple, warm meal, the exhaustion of the mission finally caught up with Paisley. She went straight to her room, lay down, and fell into a deep, dreamless sleep.
