Chapter 32

Paisley wasn't surprised. Ever since they'd first met, she'd sensed that Kelly didn't like her for some reason.

Not that she was a big fan of Kelly either. So hearing Kelly trash-talk her behind her back didn't shock her, and it just left her cold.

A moment later, Kelly reached the car door.

In a flash, every trace of resentment disappeared from her face, replaced by a warm, friendly smile. "Paisley, sweetie, lunch is ready," she said sweetly.

Looking at that smile, no one would ever guess she'd been talking trash a second ago.

Paisley just gave her a cool little nod, pushed the door open, and calmly followed her.

But inside, her mind was made up. She wasn't the same pushover she'd been in her past life, the one who swallowed every insult and let people walk all over her.

Kelly had better not mess with her. Anyone who tried to hurt her or scheme against her would pay for it.

On the way over, Paisley could clearly hear Rachel and the Lennox brothers scheming in low voices in the villa next door. She could even hear a zombie 100 yards away, shuffling along and scratching at a wall.

The two of them walked into the villa's living room, one after the other.

Elijah, Orion, and the others were sitting together, quietly going over the plan.

Elijah looked around at everyone and gave the order. "After lunch, we head straight to the hospital nearby for equipment and medicine.

"It's going to be dangerous. I'll lead the group. Orion, Mason, and Paisley are coming with me, and everyone else stays here at the villa until we get back."

Most of them nodded, but Kelly's face darkened. Her resentment and jealousy were about to boil over.

She'd had it out for Paisley from the start.

If she weren't into Elijah and dying to be close to him, she never would've come all the way from Aetheris to Southspire.

And she definitely wouldn't have gotten caught in the apocalypse and gone through all this hell.

Out of the four women in the group, she was the only one who'd awakened an ability. By all rights, that should've made her the most valuable person on the team, but Elijah had never even looked twice at her.

Then there was Paisley. She hadn't awakened anything, and she never lifted a finger to help. And yet, Elijah treated her like she was special.

Why her?

Kelly swallowed her anger and spoke up, sounding proud and defiant.

"Elijah, Paisley doesn't have an ability. She'll just slow you down and get in the way. It's too dangerous.

"Why don't I go instead? I've already awakened a Water Affinity, so I can actually help. I won't drag anyone down."

She sounded smug.

Ashley caught the dig at Paisley right away. She couldn't stand anyone putting Paisley down, so she stepped forward and pushed back.

"Paisley would never drag anyone down! Sure, she hasn't awakened an ability, but she kills zombies like it's nothing. She's an amazing fighter, and she's sharper and knows more than all of us put together.

"You might have an ability, but when it comes to real fighting and thinking on your feet, you're not even in her league. Besides, Orion will be there the whole time to protect her, so who asked you to nitpick?

"If you ask me, Paisley's the right one to go. She's the best! You can't even compare to her."

The second Paisley walked in, Orion noticed something different about her. She seemed so much more powerful than before. Had her ability leveled up?

He also knew exactly why Elijah wanted Paisley along. Elijah knew how strong she really was, and he knew about her Storage Dimension.

Without her, there'd be no way to haul all that equipment out.

He just hadn't expected Kelly to trash-talk Paisley right in front of everyone.

The warmth drained from Orion's face, and he looked Kelly dead in the eye.

"Kelly, you're my friend, but I'm not letting anyone look down on Pais or talk trash about her.

"Nobody could ever take her place. Nobody even comes close.

"This is the first and last time you talk about her like that. Do it again, and we're done being friends."

Kelly froze, and all the color drained from her face. She'd only made one little comment, and she'd never dreamed Orion would humiliate her like this in front of everyone.
