Chapter 94

Paisley ladled the first bowl of soup for Ashley.

"You work the hardest making dinner, so you get the first bowl. Try my cooking."

Ashley's stomach had been growling for a while from the smell alone.

She happily took the bowl and took a small, eager sip.

The warm, rich soup slid down her throat, smooth and not greasy at all. An incredible, savory sweetness burst across her tongue, and as it went down, a pure, gentle warmth spread through her whole body.

"Paisley, this soup is amazing! It's the best chicken soup I've ever had in my life!

"I've never had soup this good. I feel all warm inside, and I've got so much energy right now!"

She drained the whole bowl in one go, and her voice shook with disbelief.

"My ability... I think my ability's breaking through..."

The second she said it, the air around her surged, and an earthy golden glow of Earth Affinity energy burst out of her!

That one bowl of soup set her Level Two Earth Affinity off. It surged wildly and broke through its limits, and waves of energy rippled out from her body.

The energy grew thicker and thicker as it swirled around her. Her thin Earth Affinity energy condensed and surged until it smashed right through the wall to Level Three.

Everyone nearby turned to stare in shock.

Nobody could believe that drinking one bowl of soup could make someone level up.

It didn't stop until she hit mid Level Three.

Energy was pumping through Ashley's whole body. She threw her arms around Paisley, bursting with excitement.

"Paisley, I leveled up! I'm a Level Three Earth Affinity user now!

"Your chicken soup is magic! Thank you so much!"

Everyone else at the table was stunned.

None of it made any sense.

They'd never heard of anyone leveling up from a bowl of soup.

They had to see for themselves, so one after another, they ladled out their own bowls and started sipping.

The warm, rich soup went down smooth, and the same savory sweetness burst across their tongues. A pure, gentle warmth spread through their bodies.

Days of travel and fighting had left them exhausted, sore, and mentally drained. But as the energy sank in, all of it melted away.

The heavy exhaustion vanished, and they felt light and loose. Days of tension drained out of their nerves, and they all felt refreshed and clear-headed.

Then, something even more shocking started happening to every one of them.

The warm energy flowed through them and kept feeding and strengthening the source of their abilities. Their powers surged, grew purer, and swelled fast. Their thin, unstable energy turned several times richer and stronger in no time.

One after another, faint cracks of breakthroughs sounded inside their bodies.

Every single person there leveled up, no exceptions!

Metal Affinity energy surged around Orion, and his power got sharper and more refined. A fierce edge came into his eyes as he jumped straight from early Level Three to mid Level Four.

Elijah's Lightning Affinity rose steadily, and his presence grew deeper and more intimidating. His power kept climbing until it went from mid Level Three all the way to late Level Four.

Even sweet, gentle Ashley felt her power grow fuller and purer, going from mid Level Two to mid Level Three.

Madison's ability climbed to mid Level Three too.

Thomas and Spencer went from mid Level Two to late Level Three.

They could all feel their bodies changing in huge ways, and they were stunned and overjoyed.

One simple dinner had wiped out every bit of their exhaustion, healed the wear and tear of days on the road, and pushed the whole team to the next level.

Everyone turned to stare at Paisley.

She'd made dinner, so this had to have something to do with her.

No wonder her chicken soup smelled so amazing.

Only now did they understand. The ingredients Paisley had casually pulled out weren't ordinary food at all. That soup was a priceless, once-in-a-lifetime superfood that could strengthen the body and level up abilities.

Elijah looked up at Paisley, who stood there calmly, and once again, he could hardly believe it.

The lucky breaks she stumbled into were always beyond anything anyone could imagine.

In that moment, all anyone felt toward Paisley was deep respect and trust. Getting to travel with her was the luckiest thing that had ever happened to them.
