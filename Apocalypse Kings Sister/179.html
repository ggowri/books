Trovie bounced around the space in her mind, its little wings flapping nonstop, beside itself with excitement.

[And on top of that, there's still a premium lucky draw! Master, today was an epic haul!]

Its round little body fluttered as it asked eagerly. [Master, do you want to do the draw now?]

Paisley's eyes flickered, and she asked casually in her head. [What's in the premium draw?]

There was a hint of warning in her voice.

[Trovie, don't try to con me again.]

Trovie quickly straightened up and listed every prize, its little voice dead serious.

[Master, every prize in the premium draw is top-notch! There's the Affinity Awakening Pill, the Beauty Pill, the Vitality Pill, the Invisibility Talisman, the Misfortune Talisman, and the Teleportation Talisman!

[Master, no matter which one you get, you can't lose!]

Paisley's interest stirred. Each of these items had its uses, whether for training, getting stronger, saving her life in a fight, or keeping enemies in check. They were all rare, top-tier goods in the apocalypse.

She stopped hesitating and spoke softly. [Then let's start the draw.]

Trovie was even more excited than she was. [Got it! The premium lucky draw is starting now!]

The moment it finished, a dazzling golden light lit up the space in her mind.

A giant prize wheel appeared in midair, divided into six equal sections. Each section held a shimmering image of its prize, glowing with swirling light and energy.

A gleaming golden pointer spun at the wheel's center, moving so fast it blurred into a single streak of light, trailing energy.

Trovie held its tiny body perfectly still, staring at the wheel, nervous and excited.

[Spin, spin, spin! Give us a jackpot! Master's luck is off the charts!]

The spinning pointer gradually slowed, the streaks of light faded, and the spot where it would land slowly came into view.

Finally, the golden pointer came to a stop on a prize glowing with soft, pure light.

With a resonant hum, a dazzling burst of golden light exploded outward, and pure, powerful spatial energy flooded the whole space in her mind, almost blinding.

[Congratulations, Master! You hit the jackpot on the premium draw! You've won a top-tier treasure, 10 Golden Teleportation Talismans!]

Trovie burst into wild cheers, flapping its little wings like crazy. Its whole tiny body sparkled gold as it practically vibrated with excitement.

[Master, those are super rare Golden Teleportation Talismans! You might not get one in a hundred thousand draws! Your luck is insane!]

Paisley lifted her hand slightly, and 10 gleaming golden talismans covered in deep, ornate patterns floated down neatly into her palm.

Thick golden spatial energy flowed across each one, giving off a noble, otherworldly aura. Every talisman held incredible spatial power, a hundred times more advanced than an ordinary Teleportation Talisman.

She glanced up and asked in her head. [What exactly do these Teleportation Talismans do? What are they good for?]

Trovie stopped bouncing and explained, dead serious and wildly excited.

[Master! These are top-grade Golden Teleportation Talismans, way beyond ordinary ones! You don't have to cast anything, and they ignore all affinity locks and terrain blocks!

[And here's the amazing part. There's no distance limit! Whether it's 100 yards, 10,000 yards, across an entire ruined city, or even across regions, you'll get there with a single thought and land exactly where you want!]
