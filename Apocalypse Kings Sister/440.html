Pais, how weak do you think I am?

But I have to admit, being tucked safely behind you, being the one someone watches over, is addictive.

You're clear-headed, strong, gentle, and ruthless when you need to be. Everything good in the world is in you.

You're so good, and I like you more every day.

The night passed, and dawn broke.

A strip of gray light tore through the thick haze over the ruins, just enough to light the battered land. The dead plains lost the cold of night but kept their grim, hanging menace.

After a quick breakfast, the team geared up.

As they set out, they ran into the Southspire team again. The five were hurrying into an old SUV, ready to leave.

The two teams met face to face and exchanged polite greetings.

The Southspire leader took in the Falcon Squad's battle-ready look. He guessed they were headed deeper into the ruins, and his eyes filled with respect and worried warning.

"We're heading back. This place is too creepy, with a high-level beast lurking. There's still plenty of loot out here, but next to your life, it's nothing. Be careful, and stay safe!"

"You too. Safe travels," Elijah said with a nod.

After a short goodbye, the Southspire team drove off toward the outer roads, away from the core, wanting only to get out safely.

The Falcon Squad's armored vehicle turned the other way and headed deeper, straight for the line where the grain depots met the steel plant.

The road raced past, weeds swaying, broken walls streaking by. The core zone was eerily quiet.

The low-level zombies that normally swarmed everywhere were gone. It was as if the high-level beast's pressure had driven them off. All that was left was a heavy, suffocating silence.

At the edge of the target zone, Elijah raised a hand. "Stop the car."

The engine cut. The team got out in order.

They took position as planned the night before, working together smoothly without needing to talk.

Orion and Spencer moved fast, using the ruined steel frames and broken walls as cover, and hid on high ground to the west.

Thomas slipped into the weeds and ruins to the east and quietly set up an invisible binding net, waiting for the prey to walk in.

Ashley held the rear middle, ready to raise a thick defensive barrier at any moment.

Madison stayed at the very back, her gentle Affinity flowing around her, watching the whole area.

Elijah turned to Paisley. "I'm going. You all stay here. Don't follow."

Paisley nodded. Her eyes were cool and sharp, and her presence vanished completely. Her psychic force spread across the area and locked onto every dark corner.

With a small movement, she sent a thread of it into the shadow of a broken wall behind Elijah, following him without a sound.

All in place.

Elijah walked alone into the open grain depot plaza, deliberately looking alone and not yet recovered.

He paced back and forth at the place where the serpent mutant had fled yesterday, baiting it on purpose.

One second, two, three... five minutes passed.

The silence kept spreading. The wind died, the grass went still, and the ruins fell so quiet that even the breeze was gone. Killing intent piled up layer by layer until it was hard to breathe.

Then a faint, icy snake smell drifted in on the wind.

It was coming.

Hidden in the dark, Paisley's eyes narrowed. Her psychic sense caught that familiar, overwhelming pressure and the sinister aura behind it.

A moment later, a soft, grating rasp of scales on the ground came from the dark alley behind the depot, growing louder as it got closer.
