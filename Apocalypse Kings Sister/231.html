Chapter 231

From the very start, they'd never meant to let him give up anything at all.

With the hooded man thoroughly dead, the black mist surrounding him dissolved completely, and every invisible thread controlling the endless tide of sea mutants snapped and dissolved along with it.

Out on the battlefield, still locked in brutal combat, the shift hit in an instant.

The once-fearless, mindlessly charging horde went suddenly sluggish, movements slowing, eyes going vacant.

Elijah, watching the battle closely from the wall, caught the change immediately, his gaze sharpening as he called out, voice carrying.

"Their controller's been cut off! The horde's losing cohesion! Now's our chance to push back, everyone, let's finish this!"

Hearing that, the base's fighters surged with renewed energy, throwing everything they had left into clearing the stranded mutants from the shoreline.

...

Without a controller, the horde fell apart fast, offering nothing but easy kills now.

Morale surged. The squad kept pushing, and before long, the mutants had either fled back to the depths or fallen where they stood.

The artillery fire gradually quieted, the reek of smoke and blood slowly clearing. The deadly sea-mutant crisis was finally over.

The setting sun broke through the thick smoke overhead, spilling warm light across the wreckage of the battlefield.

Mutant corpses and blood-soaked seawater covered every inch of the shore, the lingering stench marking everything the fight had cost.

Six hours of cleanup later, the entire battlefield had finally gone quiet.

The scattered sea mutants had retreated to the deep, the last few stragglers cleared out one by one, and the base's shaky defense line had finally stabilized. The brutal fight had ended.

Because of the sea-mutant assault, the squad's planned departure got pushed back a full day.

Night fell slowly over the base.

The base's grand hall blazed with light that evening, warm white glow filling the wide, orderly space, chairs lined up in neat rows, the atmosphere heavy with solemn ceremony.

Every fighter, every support staffer, every member of leadership who'd taken part in defending the base had turned out, not a single absence, gathered for a ceremony honoring the fallen and recognizing those who'd earned it, a chance to settle the grief and tension of the day.

Up on the stage, the base director stood in a dark, pressed combat uniform, his voice carried steady and clear through the sound system, filling every corner of the hall.

"Today's sea-mutant assault was the single most dangerous siege this base has ever faced.

Every one of you held the line, fought through blood and exhaustion, and in doing so, secured the survival of this base and tens of thousands of people."

His tone shifted, heavier now, carrying unmistakable respect.

"And the single greatest contributor to today's victory was Paisley Lennox."

******

The moment he said it, every eye in the room turned to the girl standing off to one side.

Paisley stood in a clean, simple combat uniform, hair pulled back neat, posture straight and unbothered, meeting the full weight of the room's attention without a hint of pride or discomfort.

"It was her who uncovered the truth behind the controlled horde, who went alone behind enemy lines to take out the mastermind, completely breaking the assault and winning this fight for all of us."

The director made the commendation official in front of the whole hall.

"Effective immediately, Paisley Lennox is awarded a First-Class Combat Commendation, along with ten thousand contribution points."

Ten thousand points ranked among the highest honors the base gave out, enough to buy high-tier Affinity resources, rare cultivation serums, and more supplies than most fighters could scrape together in years of combat.

Applause rolled through the hall, loud and sustained, every person in the room genuinely moved, genuinely grateful. If not for Paisley breaking the deadlock alone, the whole base would already have fallen.

Rachel sat quietly in the middle of the crowd, a thin, forced smile on her face, dark envy and resentment churning behind her eyes, her nails digging into her palms until it hurt.

She'd been on this same mission today, right alongside Chase.

They'd both been out there with the squad, so why did Paisley get to walk away with the top honor, the applause, the public praise from the colonel himself?

Meanwhile, she'd stayed in the background, picking up injuries with nothing to show for it.

First-class honors, ten thousand points, the full attention of the room, all of it should have been hers. Instead, it had all landed on Paisley.

Why did Paisley get to walk away with honors most people could only dream of, without even trying?

If only that ten thousand points had been hers.

It had to be that pendant. The storage one.

That had to be why her luck had turned, why she'd gotten this strong.
