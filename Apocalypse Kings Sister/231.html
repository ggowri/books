Chapter 231

Whoever was behind the hooded man had never given Paisley a chance to pry the secret loose.

Once the hooded man was stone-cold dead, the black mist around him vanished. The invisible psychic threads that had been controlling the thousands of sea beasts snapped inch by inch and disappeared for good.

On the battlefield in the distance, where the fight was still raging, everything changed in a flash.

The huge horde of mutant sea beasts that had been charging mindlessly and fearlessly suddenly froze up. Their movements slowed, and their eyes went blank.

Without the psychic control, they sensed danger, and their instincts came back. They panicked, scattered every which way, and turned to flee back into the deep sea.

Up on the high wall, Elijah, who'd been watching the battle closely, spotted the change right away. His eyes sharpened, and he shouted.

"Their controller's been cut off, and the horde's falling apart! Now's our chance to fight back and drive them off. Let's go, everyone!"

When they heard that, the base fighters got their second wind and threw everything they had into clearing out the mutant sea beasts still stranded on the mudflats.

Gradually, the cannon fire died down, the stench of blood slowly settled, and the deadly beast horde crisis was finally over.

The setting sun broke through the thick clouds of smoke and spread a deep, warm glow over the wrecked battlefield on the mudflats.

Beast corpses and bloodstained seawater covered the ground, marks left by the brutal fight. The lingering stench of blood in the wind was proof of how intense the battle had been.

After six hours of cleanup, the whole battlefield was finally quiet.

The mutant sea beasts that had scattered all retreated into the deep sea, and the team members cleared out the last stragglers one by one. The base's shaky defense line held, and the brutal battle had come to an end.

Because of the surprise beast horde attack, the team's plan to head home had to be put on hold, and their departure was pushed back a day.

******

Night slowly fell.

The base's huge auditorium was brightly lit. Warm white light filled the wide, neat hall, the tables and chairs were lined up in rows, and the mood was solemn.

Every team member, support worker, and base manager who'd fought to defend the base that day showed up, and not a single person was missing. The awards ceremony was held to honor their fallen comrades, reward the heroes, and help ease the tension and grief of the day's fighting.

Up on the stage, the head of the base stood in a neat, dark combat uniform and gave a speech. His voice came through the sound system, clear and steady, and filled the whole auditorium.

"Today's beast horde attack was the most dangerous siege since this base was built.

"Every one of you held the line and fought through blood, and you protected this whole base and the lives of tens of thousands of survivors."

He paused, and his voice grew heavier, full of clear praise and respect. "And the biggest hero of this battle is Paisley Lennox."

The moment he said it, every eye in the hall turned to Paisley, who was off to one side below the stage.

Paisley wore a clean, simple combat uniform, her hair neatly done. She stood tall and calm, and even with everyone staring at her, she stayed cool and composed, without a hint of pride.

"She was the one who saw that someone was controlling the beast horde," the head of the base said. "She went around behind them alone, killed the mastermind, broke the horde's attack for good, and won us this victory."

Then, he announced the award in front of everyone. "Paisley Lennox is hereby awarded First-Class Combat Merit and 10,000 contribution points."

Ten thousand contribution points was one of the top honors the base could give. It could buy high-level power resources, rare training serums, and plenty of supplies. Countless team members couldn't save that much even after years of risking their lives.

Thunderous applause broke out across the auditorium, and everyone was truly impressed. If Paisley hadn't broken the deadlock on her own today, the whole base would've already become a living hell, and no one would've made it out.

Rachel sat quietly in the middle of the crowd with a forced little smile. But dark resentment churned in her eyes, and her nails dug into her palms until they hurt.

She'd fought today too, right alongside Chase.

They'd been out there with the team just like everyone else, so why did Paisley get to become famous overnight, win the top honor, and be praised in front of the whole base?

Meanwhile, nobody had noticed Rachel at all, and she'd gotten hurt plenty.

First-Class Merit, 10,000 contribution points, every eye on her, the whole hall cheering... All the glory that should've been Rachel's had landed on Paisley instead.

Why did Paisley get to win honors other people could only dream of, so easily?

If only those 10,000 contribution points were hers.

It had to be because of that space pendant, didn't it? Was that why Paisley's luck had gotten so good and why she'd become so strong?
