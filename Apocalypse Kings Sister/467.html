Chapter 467

"We were wrong! We'll never do it again! Please, mercy!"

"Please let us go, I'm just an accomplice! I never killed anyone!"

...

The begging rose and fell across the night, pitiful and ragged.

Elijah walked up slowly, boots crunching over scattered weapons. He looked down at the kneeling men, his eyes empty of any warmth.

"Tell me who's behind you."

"Who told you to use the Blackwood name to block roads, rob people, and hurt survivors?"

He had a feeling this wasn't simple.

The men flinched. How does he know?

With their lives on the line, there was no point hiding it. The leader babbled, kowtowing frantically.

"I'll talk! I'll tell you everything!"

"We're not Blackwood men at all! We're a minor branch of the Quinn family!"

"The Quinns fear the Blackwoods' standing, and they can't take you head on. So they sent us out to make trouble."

"They had us use the Blackwood name and Mr. Blackwood's name to rob and bully every group that passed. It's to ruin your reputation, so survivors and outside factions hate your family!"

"And it's not just here. There are checkpoints like this all over."

"We're just pawns they pushed out front! Everything we did, the Quinns told us to! Please, spare us!"

The truth was out.

Elijah's pupils tightened. He understood.

It was the Quinn family, all along.

They couldn't win head on, so they worked in the dark. They stole his name to do evil, smeared the Blackwoods, and stirred up trouble between factions. The scheme was vicious.

The Quinn family.

A neat trick, poisoning a name to cut someone down.

Elijah looked at the men on the ground without a flicker of feeling. Only deep cold remained.

After a two-second pause, he spoke, voice sharp as ice.

"Settle accounts with every last one of them. Leave no one behind."

Mercy wasn't kindness. It only fed criminals and hurt everyone around them.

Men who openly worked this stretch of road, who held guns on travelers and bled survivors dry, had to have blood on their hands. Their crimes were piled high. They had earned this.

Their words rang in his head, every line a knife.

They had checkpoints all over, all using the Blackwood name to make money. The aim was vicious.

They wanted to ruin the Blackwood family's reputation, make survivors and outside bases resent the Blackwoods and Falcon Squad, and stir up division so the Quinns could pick up the pieces.

He wasn't going to let it stand.

This was only one checkpoint. More allies were hiding out there.

To cut out the root, he had to follow the thread and tear down every fake checkpoint.

What chilled him most was that the Quinn family was behind it.

He had always known the Quinns feared Falcon Squad's power. They hid their hostility and clashed with him at every turn. But he had never imagined they'd be this low, this vicious. They couldn't face him directly, so they hid and smeared and plotted.

The Quinns' reach was long.

Elijah's eyes darkened. A deep wariness settled in.

If they could set up here, on this exact road, and hold it this long, do they already know Falcon's travel routes?

The beast horde at the mall, two days of siege, a death trap that came out of nowhere. It all felt wrong, like it had been arranged.

Was that horde just beasts running wild? Or did the Quinns push it?

Thread after thread of suspicion wound through his thoughts and darkened his eyes.

Nothing like this is a coincidence.

But it was the dead of night, two in the morning, the wind biting, everyone worn out.

Elijah pushed down the anger rolling in his chest.

"It's two in the morning. Everyone head into the hotel and rest. Recover. We leave at first light."

The others nodded, cleaned up the battlefield, cleared the roadblock, and filed toward the hotel.

Elijah turned back to the SUV.

He opened the door, leaned in, and carefully lifted the sleeping Paisley from the seat.

She was slight and light in his arms, soft and warm, resting quietly against him. Her breathing was even and long. Her face looked gentle, the battlefield sharpness gone, like a sleeping child.
