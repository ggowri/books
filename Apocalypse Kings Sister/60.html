Chapter 60

"Help... please... help me..."

Everyone in the car looked up at the filthy figure in front of the hood.

Paisley had sensed Rachel coming a while ago.

And pass up a perfect chance to humiliate her? No way.

Paisley was the first to push her door open. She hopped down lightly and looked down at Rachel, who was covered head to toe in filth. Then, with a mocking smirk, she took her sweet time speaking.

"Well, well. Isn't this Rachel, the Lennoxes' precious little princess? We split up just a few hours ago, so how the hell did you end up like this?"

Paisley made a show of wrinkling her nose and fanning the air in front of her face, taking a half-step back in disgust. "Stay away from us. You smell like mud and rotting fish, and it's disgusting."

The moment their eyes met, a wave of humiliation and rage crashed over Rachel. She was shaking with fury, and her nails dug deep into her palms.

She used to be the center of attention, the glamorous rich girl everyone fawned over. When had she ever been humiliated like this in front of other people?

Would I even be in this mess if she hadn't left me to die?

But underneath all that shame and rage was a bone-deep fear and despair.

She was alone in the wilderness in the middle of the apocalypse, and these people were her only way out. No matter how much they mocked her, she had to take it.

To stay alive, she'd have to swallow every last scrap of her pride and dignity.

Rachel forced down her anger and squeezed out a smile. Her voice came out meek and pleading.

"Paisley, I'm so lucky I ran into you out here. I know I treated you badly and did a lot of awful things.

"I've already been punished for it. Please, can't you be the bigger person and take me back with you? I swear I'll never go against you again."

Paisley watched her grovel like this and couldn't help feeling a grudging respect.

That was Rachel for you. To save her own skin, she'd swallow any amount of humiliation. She was more shameless and tougher than most people could ever be.

How did I ever think she was a helpless little lamb who needed protecting?

In her past life, Paisley had died at Rachel's hands in the end.

Thinking about it brought the hatred flooding back.

She pinched her nose, her face twisted in disgust, and spoke in a light, mocking tone that cut deep.

"You're filthy. Even if I agreed to take you with us, go ahead and ask them. Who'd want you in their car?

"Honestly, Rachel, you really need to work on your hygiene."

Paisley tilted her head and faked a sympathetic look. "Sure, you grew up out in the sticks, but that's no excuse to be this gross, right?

"If all those devoted admirers of yours saw you like this, covered in mud and looking pathetic, would any of them still want to simp for you?"

Then, she turned to Orion beside her with a sly smile. "Right, Orion?"

Orion smirked and nodded, playing along. He sounded thoroughly grossed out.

"Yeah, she reeks. If she hadn't said anything, I'd have taken one look at her from a distance and figured she was a zombie.

"She could've just been a normal person, but she had to go and make herself filthier than a zombie. What a freak."

Every single word was a brutal insult.

Rachel's face burned. Shame, humiliation, and hatred tangled together and nearly swallowed her whole. But she gritted her teeth and kept her head down, taking every insult without daring to say a word back.

Mason was the most oblivious guy in the group. He missed the tension completely and just thought Rachel was disgustingly dirty, so he pointed at a river nearby and spoke bluntly.

"There's a river right over there. Go get yourself cleaned up! I've never seen a girl this dirty and smelly in my whole life!"

The whole time, Elijah sat silently in the car. He watched Rachel coldly, his face unreadable.

Every insult hit Rachel like a slap in the face.

She knew she had no choice. If she did what they said, there was at least a chance they'd take her back with them.

She was seething with hatred and bitterness, but she had to act obedient. She dragged her exhausted, aching, cut-up legs and limped and stumbled toward the river nearby.
