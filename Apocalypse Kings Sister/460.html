Chapter 460

"Understood!"

Both teams answered in low voices.

Cole's gaze drifted to Paisley in the corner, and his heart finally settled.

With her on their side, this dangerous breakout had a real chance.

Paisley's eyes held nothing but cool sharpness.

She raised her hand and called out the little white cat and the fox, stroking their heads as she spoke quietly.

"Time to work."

The cat and the fox let out soft cries, grew larger, and got ready to fight.

The Lone Wolf members stared at the sight in surprise, but silently agreed not to say anything.

It was the apocalypse. Anything could happen.

"Go," Elijah ordered.

The iron door of the fifth-floor fire stairs flew open!

Hum!

Half a night's worth of stinking cold wind blasted out. In the pitch-black stairwell, countless red slit pupils lit up like scattered stars, layer upon layer, dense and horrifying.

The beast tide, sluggish in the dead of night, looked weaker, but the numbers were still terrifying. They blocked the whole stairwell from the first floor up to the fourth, a black mass of gleaming scales.

"Charge!"

Spencer unleashed his Fire Affinity first. Scorching flame swept the stairwell, its light tearing through the dark and burning the front rows of black-scale lizards, forcing the pack back with frantic shrieks.

Right after him, Elijah's palm crackled, and a pale blue mutant lightning burst out.

Bolts as thick as arms crashed through the narrow stairwell. Where they touched a lizard's scales they spread and exploded, numbing the beasts' nerves.

The lizards hit by lightning went rigid and twitched, their lunge stuck halfway. Foul drool dripped from their fangs.

"Metal, rise!"

Orion's low shout rang out as his Metal Affinity spread.

On both sides of the stairwell, metal scraps and steel rebar tore out of the broken walls like countless sharp short blades and shot out at his command.

Cold light flashed again and again. The metal spikes drove between the lizards' scales, through flesh, and pinned several of the biggest to the steps.

Thomas seized the moment. His vines surged and wrapped the lizards that had been paralyzed by lightning and wounded by blades, tying them down and dragging them away, clearing a small gap to push through.

Madison steadied her rifle, aimed by the firelight at the lizards that had dodged the first wave, and bullets screamed out one after another, finishing them cleanly.

...

The Lone Wolf Squad moved at the same time. Their wind Affinity kicked up gravel to blind the beasts. Their earth Affinity built a rough barrier on the steps. On both flanks, they covered the squad and cleared the stray lizards sneaking in from the sides, working together without a gap.

Two teams together doubled their strength, and they actually pushed the sluggish tide back, step by step.

But after a few seconds, a sharp, piercing shriek rang from deep in the stairwell!

The retreating lizards suddenly turned wild and frantic.

"Something's wrong! They've been woken up!"

Cole's face changed, and he shouted urgently.

"A high-level leader is putting pressure on them, forcing the tide awake!"

He'd barely finished when a gigantic black-scale beast, far bigger than the others, crashed out of the shadows at the stairwell corner!

It was nearly twenty-six feet long, with jet-black scales that gleamed and were as hard as steel, jagged bone spikes on its head, and a venomous barb on its tail. Its red slit eyes were full of savage cunning. It was a Level Seven Lizard King!

With one angry shriek, the hundred ordinary lizards around it went berserk. They ignored the fire and the vines and surged forward in layers, ramming and piling up until they sealed the stairwell.

Controlled by the Level Seven leader, the black-scale lizard pack charged without fear of death.

Black scales scraped with a grating hiss. A foul wind blew in their faces, and the dense mass of beasts blocked every inch of space they might have advanced.

The pressure on both teams spiked.

The front line was forced back by the endless wave of beasts. Everyone's Affinity drained wildly, their breathing ragged, and their tight formation swayed and almost broke.

"At this rate, we'll all die here!"

Cole snarled through clenched teeth, his eyes heavy with grim urgency.

He stared at the endless tide, seeing it clearly.

"That king is clever. It won't fight us head-on. It's just back there controlling the pack to wear us down! It wants to work us to death!"

Elijah held his rifle in one hand. The barrel was hot, the veins on his arm stood out, and his palm throbbed numb from the recoil.
