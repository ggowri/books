Chapter 45

This was Paisley's Plant Affinity at full strength, and nothing could stand against it.

The zombie was Level Three too, but against her power, it didn't stand a chance. The vines had it locked down tight, and all its strength was useless. It was nothing but a puppet now, completely at her mercy.

One slash of her dagger, and the Level Three zombie's crystal core was in her hand.

With the zombie taken care of, Paisley's eyes went cold, and she turned to the Venom-Devouring Pothos in the middle of the room.

No matter how many times they cut it down, the Level Three plant just kept growing back. But Paisley stayed perfectly calm, and power flared at her fingertips again.

All the vines that had been thrashing and spraying venom everywhere froze.

Her power went straight to the source and took control of the plant's life force. Plants were her domain, and this one had no choice but to bow to her.

The pothos depended on its life force and venom to survive, but next to Paisley's pure, deep Plant Affinity, they were nothing.

A steady stream of green energy flowed backward through the vines and into the pothos, spreading through every root and stem.

The vines that had kept growing back stopped. Right before their eyes, they withered, turned black, and shriveled up, and the huge poisonous leaves curled and wilted.

In a matter of seconds, Paisley cut out the pothos's crystal core and tucked it into her dimension.

Trovie squealed. [Master, those vines are full of energy! They're worth a lot!]

With one thought, Paisley made the whole 30-foot pothos disappear. If it could be sold, it was worth taking.

The storeroom went still, and nobody said a word.

Orion was still frozen mid-swing, his machete hanging in the air. His guard was completely down now, and his mouth hung open in shock.

It was the first time he'd ever seen Paisley use her Plant Affinity.

He'd always figured she was just observant and a better fighter than most. He'd thought she kept a low profile and survived on speed and good instincts.

It had never crossed his mind that her ability could be this terrifying.

She could control zombies, kill mutant plants, attack, defend, and crush high-level mutants like it was nothing!

She'd never been a fragile little girl who needed him to protect her. She'd just been hiding how strong she really was.

She didn't need anyone. All by herself, she could take down the toughest high-level threats out there without breaking a sweat.

Elijah's lightning faded away. He was always calm and never let anything show, but for the first time, he looked truly shaken.

He couldn't take his eyes off Paisley, and he couldn't hide how stunned he was.

He'd always known Paisley wasn't ordinary. Her senses were too sharp, her calls were too accurate, and she fought better than anyone. She was hiding secrets nobody could guess.

But he'd never imagined she was this ridiculously strong.

A Level Three plant and a Level Three zombie attacking together? Even he wouldn't have stood a chance. He could've burned through every ounce of his ability and still lost.

But Paisley had wiped out both of them on her own in a matter of seconds.

Now, he finally got it. She'd always dodged danger so easily without using her ability because she'd never needed to.

She was way beyond everyone else, and ordinary threats just weren't worth her time.

Her calm had never been an act. It came from knowing she was stronger than anything she faced.

Off to the side, Mason stood there speechless, his chest heaving. He was still in shock.

That sneaky poisonous vine had almost killed him.

He'd been through a lot of fights, and he was always on guard, but he still hadn't seen that ambush coming.

The second the venom got into him, his arms and legs had locked up, and he could feel the life draining out of him. He'd been ready to get badly hurt, or even die.

For the first time, he'd known what it was like to see death coming and be helpless to stop it.

That high-level pothos was sneaky and impossible to defend against, way scarier than any zombie coming at him head-on.

And Paisley had turned it all around in an instant with her overwhelming power. She'd quietly saved his life and protected everyone else too.

Mason looked at her standing there, calm and steady, and felt a deep, fierce admiration.

From then on, Paisley was his hero, and he respected her with his whole heart.

The green energy around Paisley slowly faded back into her body. The killer look left her eyes, and she was back to looking sweet and harmless.
