Chapter 369

"It just keeps putting out that purple mist. That's got to be the toxic miasma everyone talks about, right?"

"Even with masks on and antidote pills in our systems, are we really safe from it?"

The words had barely left his mouth when Spencer, beside him, had already clenched his Affinity tight, his expression turning sharp as he warned the others in a low voice.

"Everyone, stay alert. Get ready."

"It's about to attack."

Paisley's expression suddenly turned grave.

Her eyes narrowed slightly, and her vast, finely tuned psychic senses surged outward all at once, sweeping over the entire silo and reservoir area like a tide, probing every corner in meticulous detail.

A few seconds later, she slowly opened her eyes and spoke, her tone serious.

"It's not just the one at the top."

"There are several more of the same level hiding in the grass around here, behind the silo, and in the reservoir's blind spots. Everyone needs to be extra careful."

The moment the warning left her lips, Paisley's wrist turned.

The Frostbloom Sword flashed free of its sheath, a frigid light glinting along its blade, its edge sharp enough to cut the air itself. She lunged straight into the surging wave of zombies.

Her sword flowed like water, every strike clean, every stroke precise, shattering zombie skulls one after another. In moments, she'd cleared an entire swath of the field.

Seeing this, Falcon Squad moved as one, Affinities bursting to life in a seamless, coordinated assault.

Elijah's Lightning Affinity cracked through the air in violent purple bolts, strike after strike scorching zombies black and tearing their bodies apart.

Orion channeled his Metal Affinity, sharp alloy blades condensing at his fingertips and shooting out with devastating force, punching clean through skull after skull.

Thomas's Plant Affinity unfurled wildly, vines shooting out to bind zombie torsos, sharp thorns piercing and crushing, locking down the enemy's movement.

Spencer's Fire Affinity erupted with overwhelming force, flames roaring from his palms, waves of fire sweeping through and incinerating zombies by the dozen.

Ashley commanded her heavy Earth Affinity, raising thick stone walls from the ground to block the zombie charge, hurling boulders down to pin the battle line in place.

Mason's Wind Affinity cut through the air, swirling gusts tearing nearby zombies apart, sweeping up the stragglers.

Madison channeled her Water Affinity, shaping water blades and arrows that struck with precision, while softening the flow to shield her teammates, clearing out any zombies that slipped in close.

Before long, they'd cleared a huge stretch of the field.

The battle raged on, fierce and relentless, while not far off, in the shadow of the trees, the Scarlet Squad's vehicle sat quietly, watching from across the water.

Chloe, Grant, and the rest stayed inside the vehicle, watching the fierce fighting ahead through the tinted windows with cold, calculating eyes, not moving an inch to help.

"We'll just wait here."

Grant leaned back against his seat, posture lazy and arrogant, his expression venomous, gaze locked on the battlefield as he watched Falcon Squad fight.

A cold, sinister smile curled at Chloe's lips, eyes full of certainty.

"With this many zombies, plus the mutant trumpet flower's toxic mist everywhere, that's more than enough to burn through their Affinity."

"We just have to wait for the right moment. Once their Affinity runs dry and their stamina's spent, we strike and finish them off. That's only fair."

Every stir of movement across that vast stretch of land fell within the reach of Paisley's psychic senses.

Her range was immense, her perception razor-fine. The closer something was, the more clearly she could pick up its movements, its mood, even its conversation.

From the moment they'd hidden in the trees to watch from across the water and plot in secret, Paisley had already known everything.

A faint chill passed through her eyes, though her sword never slowed.

They'd shown up, and now they wanted to hide in the back and reap the rewards while doing nothing. That was a joke.

The next instant, the dozens of zombies that had been charging mindlessly at Falcon Squad suddenly froze.

Their hollow eyes snapped toward a new direction. Their stiff bodies spun toward the armored vehicle hidden in the trees' shadow, throats rasping out low, hoarse growls as they surged forward in a frenzy, slamming straight into the Scarlet Squad's vehicle.

Thud, thud, thud!

The heavy impacts rang out one after another. Zombies piled against the vehicle's body, and the heavy frame shook violently, windows rattling, the once-calm cabin suddenly pitching and lurching.

Inside, the Scarlet Squad members, who'd been settling in to watch the show, went pale in an instant, snapping from relaxed to rigid with tension.

"What's going on?! Why are these zombies suddenly coming at us?!"
