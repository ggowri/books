Chapter 182

"These are amazing!" the staffer blurted. "Zero-contamination, high-grade energy fruit!"

He'd worked this job ever since the apocalypse began and had handled more trades than he could count. Almost all the fruits, vegetables, and energy crops out there carried at least some radiation and toxins, and they had to be purified again and again before anyone could eat them.

Fresh fruit like this, a hundred percent pure, zero contamination, and full of energy, was extremely rare in the apocalypse. Nobody could buy it, no matter how much they offered.

The crowd that had been quietly watching went wild when they heard the results, every face full of shock.

"Zero contamination?! Where can you even find radiation-free fruit in the apocalypse these days?"

"And it's got 35% energy content!"

"She's the real deal!"

"Now I know what legendary luck looks like. We're chewing on ration bars and fighting over low-level supplies, and she just casually pulls out treasure like this!"

The young Affinity users stared at the sparkling bunch of energy grapes on the counter, insanely jealous and unable to tear their eyes away.

They'd already admired her for her monstrous combat strength. Now, they envied her luck too, and the rare resources that seemed to just find their way to her.

The staffer swallowed down his shock and looked up, his tone respectful.

"Ms. Lennox, energy grapes this high-quality never show up on the market. At the base's top exchange rate, I can give you eight Level Three crystal cores for them! Would you like to go ahead with the trade?"

That price set off another wave of gasps.

An ordinary bunch of wild mutant fruit could get a few Level Two cores at most. But Paisley's perfectly purified energy grapes crushed everything else on the market and were worth as much as high-level mutant beast materials.

Paisley kept a straight face, but she was a little surprised. She hadn't expected these energy grapes to actually be worth this much.

She gave a small nod and answered simply. "Go ahead."

As soon as she said it, she took five more bunches of energy grapes out of her bag and set them neatly on the counter. Each grape was plump and glossy, glowing with fresh energy, and every bunch was exactly as top-quality as the first.

"Could you trade these in too, please?"

The shock the staffer had just pushed down came flooding back, and he froze.

He quickly grabbed the scanner and checked each bunch one by one, and every reading came back exactly the same.

[Item scanned: fresh energy grapes

[Contamination: 0 (fully purified, no apocalypse toxins left)

[Energy content: 35%]

The staffer could hardly believe it.

Six identical bunches of top-grade, zero-contamination energy grapes in one go? She's unreal...

The crowd went absolutely wild. Gasps and exclamations rang out one after another, and everyone there was stunned.

"Oh my God! She's got five more bunches of energy fruit just as good?!"

"Six bunches of top-grade energy grapes, and that's 48 Level Three cores! She's loaded!"

"When that girl makes a move, she goes big."

"I work myself to death all day and can't even get one Level Two core. Comparing yourself to people like her will drive you crazy."

"I wonder what those premium energy grapes taste like. Are they good?"

"What I really want to know is how she even got grapes like these in the middle of the apocalypse."

"Who would've thought anyone could grow zero-contamination, high-grade energy fruit in bulk in the apocalypse? Whatever she's got going for her is terrifying!"

Everyone stared at the grapes and swallowed hard. These days, all they had was hardtack and cold water, and they couldn't remember the last time they'd had any fruit, let alone grapes this good.

Some of them wished they could get on Paisley's good side and tag along. Was she looking for any more followers?

The staffer's hands were shaking slightly. He forced down his shock and excitement, pulled himself together, and spoke respectfully, a little nervous.

"M-Ms. Lennox, all six bunches have been checked, and they're all premium quality. That comes to 48 Level Three crystal cores in total!"

As soon as he finished, he quickly counted out the cores, put them in a special sealed pouch, and handed it to Paisley with great care.

"Ms. Lennox, please come trade with us again!"

Paisley took the pouch and tucked it into her bag, as calm and composed as ever.

These cores should last her a while, so she wouldn't need to come back and trade anytime soon.

For some reason, everyone seemed to know who she was, so it was better to keep a low profile.

Paisley gave a slight nod, then turned and left with light steps, paying no attention to anyone's stares.
