Chapter 151

She'd already pulled fresh ingredients from her Storage Dimension and tucked them into the room's fridge, just in case.

"No rush."

She kept her voice soft.

"Sit tight for a few minutes. Eat something hot first, then we'll head out for the mission."

She turned and opened the fridge, pulling out what she'd prepped earlier.

Rich, fragrant soy milk. Soft, plump pork buns. Golden, crackling fried dough sticks. All of it there, waiting.

The room's little kitchen made quick work of reheating everything.

Warm steam rolled through the space in minutes, chasing off the morning chill and leaving the place smelling like an actual home.

"Dig in."

Paisley laid the food out, piece by piece, and waved the three of them toward the table.

Ashley didn't wait. She grabbed a fried dough stick and bit straight into it.

Crisp shell, soft center, the wheaty warmth spreading through her mouth.

Her eyes lit up. She mumbled around a full mouth, utterly satisfied.

"This is so good! You can't get real fried dough like this anywhere in the apocalypse. Crispy, sweet — better than anything I've ever had."

Thomas picked up a cup of warm soy milk and took a slow sip.

Rich, smooth, sweet without being cloying. The warmth slid down his throat and spread through his whole body, easing the hollow ache of an empty stomach.

His face relaxed. He let out a breath.

"I never thought soy milk could taste this good. Sweet, rich — one sip and my whole body feels better. Miles ahead of those dry ration drinks."

Spencer picked up a pork bun and took a bite.

Soft bread wrapped around juicy, well-seasoned filling, the fat and lean meat in perfect balance.

He chewed fast, nodding over and over.

"This bun is unreal. The filling's tight and flavorful, the bread's soft and sweet — every bite's packed. Top-tier apocalypse food, no contest.

With Paisley around, we're the lucky ones."

The three of them ate like they hadn't eaten in days, completely content.

A simple hot breakfast. In a world stripped bare of food, it felt like luxury.

Kitty crouched by the table too, nibbling at the bun and soy milk Paisley fed her, cheeks puffed out, looking thoroughly pleased with herself. Her white fur seemed to shine a little brighter with every bite.

A few minutes later, everyone was full, warm, and running on energy again.

They cleared the table themselves and tidied up before leaving.

Paisley stretched, lazy, cat in her arms.

"Let's go. Mission hall."

"Yeah!"

The three answered together, each grabbing a trash bag on the way out, eyes bright with purpose.

Morning at Rivergate base was already in full swing. Affinity users moved briskly in every direction — some heading out to hunt zombies for crystal cores, others lining up to turn in missions or trade for supplies. The whole place hummed with the particular tension of the end times.

A short walk brought them to the wide, bustling mission hall.

******

The bulletin board was crammed with hunting, rescue, and supply-gathering missions, ranked from Level One to Level Six, each tier tied to its own crystal core payout and contribution points.

Elijah and Orion had already arrived, standing in front of the Level Five high-risk board, checking the mission details. Both stood straight-backed, expressions calm and serious.

Across the hall, the four Lennox siblings and Kelly Walker were waiting too.

A night of rest and medicine had eased the worst of the Lennox brothers' injuries, but their faces were still drawn and bruised, and their eyes still carried yesterday's humiliation — and no small amount of fear.

Only Rachel looked composed. Calm on the surface. Underneath, her calculations stayed buried deep.

The moment she caught sight of Paisley's group approaching, the corner of her mouth curved up, satisfied, greed and envy tangled together underneath.

Today, Paisley was going to die.

Beside her, Kelly's acting was flawless — nothing on her face betrayed last night's ugly scheme with Rachel.

Only she knew her own fingers were clenched white inside her sleeves, resentment and spite growing wild underneath.

She watched Paisley calmly, watched the way Ashley and the others crowded close around her, watched Elijah walk over and greet her first. Jealousy burned through her like a flame.

Why?

Why did Paisley get everyone's favor, while she got nothing but cold shoulders?
