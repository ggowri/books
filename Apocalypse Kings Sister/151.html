Chapter 151

Paisley had already pulled fresh ingredients from her Storage Dimension and tucked them into the room's fridge, just in case.

"No rush," she said softly. "Sit tight for a few minutes. Eat something hot first, then we'll head out and pick up a mission."

She turned and opened the fridge, pulling out what she'd prepped earlier.

She had it all, from rich, creamy milk to soft, plump sausage biscuits and golden, crispy doughnuts.

The room's little kitchen made quick work of reheating everything.

Warm steam rolled through the space in minutes, chasing off the morning chill and leaving the place smelling like an actual home.

"Dig in." Paisley laid the food out, piece by piece, and waved the three of them toward the table.

Ashley didn't wait. She grabbed a golden doughnut and bit straight into it.

The outside was crisp, the inside was soft, and the warm, toasty flavor spread through her mouth.

Her eyes lit up, and she looked utterly satisfied.

"This is so good! You can't get doughnuts like this anywhere in the apocalypse. They're crispy and sweet, better than any I've ever had!"

Thomas picked up a cup of warm milk and took a slow sip.

It was rich, smooth, and sweet without being cloying. The warmth slid down his throat and spread through his whole body, easing the hollow ache of an empty stomach.

His face relaxed, and he let out a long sigh.

"I never thought milk could taste this good. It's sweet and rich, and one sip makes my whole body feel better. It's miles ahead of those dry ration drinks."

Spencer picked up a plump sausage biscuit and took a bite.

The soft, fluffy biscuit was stuffed with juicy, well-seasoned sausage.

He chewed hard, nodding over and over.

"This biscuit is unreal! The sausage is hearty but not greasy, and the biscuit's soft and sweet, with filling in every bite. It's top-tier apocalypse food!

With Paisley around, we're the lucky ones."

The three of them wolfed down their food, savoring every bite.

Even a simple hot breakfast felt like luxury in a world stripped bare of food.

The little white cat crouched by the table too, nibbling the biscuit and lapping up the milk Paisley gave it, its cheeks puffed out. It looked thoroughly pleased with itself, and its snowy fur seemed to shine a little brighter with every bite.

A few minutes later, everyone was full, warm, and running on energy again.

They cleared the table themselves and tidied up before leaving.

Paisley looked lazy and relaxed, the little white cat in her arms. "Let's go to the mission hall."

"You got it!" the three of them answered together. Each grabbed a trash bag on the way out, fired up and ready to go.

Morning at Rivergate Base was already in full swing. Affinity users hurried in every direction, some heading out to hunt zombies for crystal cores, others lining up to turn in missions or trade for supplies. The whole place hummed with the tension of the end times.

A short walk brought them to the wide, bustling mission hall.

The bulletin board was crammed with hunting, rescue, and supply-gathering missions, ranked from Level One to Level Six, each tier tied to its own crystal core payout and contribution points.

Elijah and Orion had already arrived and were going over the details at the Level Five high-risk board. Both stood tall, their faces calm and serious.

Across the hall, the four Lennox siblings and Kelly Walker were waiting too.

A night of rest and medicine had eased the worst of the Lennox brothers' injuries, but their faces were still drawn and bruised. They couldn't hide how humiliated they felt after yesterday's beating, or how wary they'd become.

Only Rachel seemed calm. She kept her eyes lowered, hiding every bit of her scheming.

The moment she spotted Paisley's group walking over, she gave a small, satisfied smile, greed and envy churning inside her.

Today, she was going to make sure Paisley died.

Beside her, Kelly's acting was even better. Nothing on her face gave away the ugly scheme she and Rachel had cooked up last night.

But only she knew how hard her fingers were clenched inside her sleeves, her knuckles white, while resentment and spite grew wild inside her.

She calmly watched Paisley, who outshone everyone in the hall. She watched Ashley and the other two crowd close around her, and she watched Elijah, of all people, come over to say hi first. The jealousy nearly burned her alive.

Why? Why does Paisley get all the love, while nobody even notices me?
