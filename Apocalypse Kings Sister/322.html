Chapter 322

The room went still at that.

Stillwater's top asset, the mission commander himself, choosing to join Paisley's team.

Rachel's smug expression cracked instantly, her face twisting with barely contained jealousy.

Chase's face darkened further, his fists clenching tight.

Gabriel ignored the ripple of reaction and gave his final orders.

"Everyone report to supply immediately for standard gear — rifles, ammunition, emergency med kits, clean water, and rations."

"Assemble at the front doors in ten minutes. We leave on time."

With the orders set, the tension in the room eased slightly as everyone filed toward supply to collect their gear.

A few minutes later, everyone had their equipment and regrouped at the entrance, lined up in formation.

Gabriel stood at the front, eyes sweeping the orderly ranks, his voice cutting through the evening air, cold and precise.

"Everyone's in position. Move out."

A sleek, silver-gray military transport sat waiting on the tarmac, its hull sharp-edged and gleaming with cold metal — the base's top-of-the-line field aircraft.

Everyone boarded in turn.

The hatch sealed shut, the engine roared to life, and the aircraft lifted off smoothly, cutting through the evening wind as it climbed toward their target.

Paisley leaned against the cabin wall, genuinely surprised.

She hadn't expected Stillwater to go this far. Most field missions were vehicle transport only — deploying a military aircraft meant this mission's priority and danger level went far beyond the usual.

The cabin stayed quiet and tense. Fifteen minutes later, the aircraft hovered steady in the air, about ten yards off the ground.

The hatch slid open, cold evening wind rushing in, snapping everyone's uniforms. The barren terrain stretched out below.

Gabriel stood at the open hatch, scanning the team, his voice level.

"Who's going first?"

The cabin went silent.

The team exchanged uncertain looks.

Ten yards wasn't enormous, but it wasn't nothing either. Jumping without any kind of cushion meant risking a sprain, a break, or worse. Nobody wanted to be the one to try it.

Then, from Chase's team, someone stepped forward — a tall young fighter.

"I'm Level Five Plant Affinity. I'll go first."

Green light surged from his fingertips, his Affinity pouring out in a dense wave.

On the ground below, saplings burst up through the dirt, growing explosively, stretching and thickening in seconds into a sturdy branch over three yards tall, bridging the gap below the hatch.

The fighter leapt, landing cleanly in the center of the branch, the wood flexing slightly before holding steady.

With the precedent set, the rest of Chase's team followed one by one, using the same cushion, landing cleanly, no injuries.

Once on the ground, Rachel looked up at the hatch, pride flashing in her eyes, and called up to Gabriel, voice loud and self-satisfied.

"Commander, want to join our team instead?"

Gabriel's expression didn't shift at all, his tone flat and cool.

"No."

Her moment of smugness deflated fast. She turned instead toward the remaining team still in the cabin, her voice shifting into something more persuasive.

"You guys want to come over to our side? That's a serious drop with no cushion — one wrong landing and you could end up crippled. Are you really not scared?"

The comment landed. Two of the remaining fighters, already nervous, exchanged uncertain looks, clearly tempted.

The captain from Paisley's team spoke up, calm and easy.

"No pressure either way. Go if you want to. Doesn't matter which team, as long as the mission gets done."

That settled it. The two fighters didn't hesitate any longer, jumping down and landing on the branch below, joining Rachel's team.

Only six remained in the cabin — Paisley, Gabriel, Elijah, and the others.

Gabriel walked to the open hatch, his voice steady.

"I'll go first."

Elijah stepped forward immediately, his gaze softening as he looked at Paisley.

"I'll jump first and catch you below. Don't be scared."
