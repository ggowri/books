Chapter 78

Next to her, Ashley rested her chin in her hands, her eyes sparkling. She was secretly thrilled for Orion and Madison.

The little white cat seemed to sort of get what was going on. It nuzzled Paisley's neck and watched the couple with its big round eyes, snuggled up in her arms.

Just then, the walkie-talkie in the car crackled with static.

Elijah's low, cool voice came through.

"Orion, where are you guys? It's been ten minutes, and there's still no sign of you. What are you doing?"

As soon as he finished, they could clearly hear chaotic snarling and the sharp clang of blades over the radio. It was the frantic, violent noise of a brutal zombie fight.

His voice dropped, and he sounded worried. "Did you run into trouble? Do you need backup?"

Everyone in the car heard the fighting on the other end, and their faces turned serious.

Orion grabbed the walkie-talkie and answered right away. "Elijah, we're good. We're on our way."

Orion put the walkie-talkie down and turned to everyone in the car.

"Sounds like they ran into zombies. We can't waste any more time. Let's go meet up with them right now."

Then, he floored it and sped toward the rest of the group.

The SUV tore down the road, and the sights along the way were horrifying.

Both sides of the road were in ruins, with collapsed walls and rubble everywhere. Zombie corpses were strewn across the asphalt, and their dark, stinking blood had soaked into the road and dried a dull blackish-brown. The air reeked of blood and rot.

A few leftover zombies wandered around aimlessly. At the roar of the engine, they turned stiffly and lunged at the car, snarling, but they were nothing but mangled limbs and ruined bodies. They couldn't stop the speeding car.

The closer they got to the meeting point, the creepier and heavier the air felt.

Up ahead, the empty, barren road was completely blocked off by a thick wall of deep purple vines.

It was a mutant grapevine that had sprung up after the storm.

The vines were thick and tough, twisted and knotted layer after layer, and covered in sharp, bone-white thorns. Heavy bunches of black-purple mutant grapes hung between the leaves. Their glossy, see-through skins gave off a faint, eerie glow.

The cars up ahead were trapped in the tangle of mutant vines.

The vines kept growing like crazy. Their flexible branches whipped and coiled like countless poisonous snakes, cutting off every way forward and trapping the whole group.

The four of them in the car saw the danger right away.

Orion stopped the car immediately.

When Paisley saw it, her expression darkened as memories of her past life came flooding back.

She remembered this place perfectly. Deep inside this forest of mutant grapevines was something incredibly rare, an age-old sacred fungus.

If a regular person ate even one, they were guaranteed to awaken an ability.

Even someone weak with zero talent could use it to completely transform and become an ability user.

In her past life, she'd fought her way through this deadly tangle all alone. She'd dodged the mutant vines and slipped past high-level zombies, and by the time she got out with two sacred fungi, she was covered in wounds and had almost died.

But the second Reid and Ryan saw the fungi in her hands, they'd snatched them away without a word.

They hadn't left her a single one. They each took one and ate it on the spot.

That was how they'd both awakened abilities, and it had given them a solid footing in the apocalypse. From then on, life had been smooth sailing for them.

And Paisley, the one who'd risked her life for that treasure and come out exhausted and covered in scars, had ended up with nothing.

She hadn't even gotten one honest thank-you out of it.

Back then, she'd been naive and stupid. She'd treated everyone with nothing but sincerity, thinking kindness would be paid back with kindness. Instead, she'd handed everyone else a win on a silver platter, only to be betrayed and die a miserable death.

Now, when she thought about it, Paisley just gave a faint, cold smirk. She didn't feel a thing anymore. Her mind was perfectly clear.

All her stupid sacrifices in her past life had only been repaid with betrayal.

This time, without me doing all the work, let's see if those Lennox boys can still awaken abilities.

She looked up at the thick tangle of mutant grapevines ahead again, and an idea started to take shape.

The fruit these mutant vines produced had hidden powers too, and it was useful for all kinds of things.

If a regular person with no ability ate one, it would make their body way stronger, boosting their physical strength far beyond normal. It would give them a fighting chance to protect themselves in the deadly apocalypse.
