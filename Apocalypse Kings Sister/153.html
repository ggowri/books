Chapter 153

Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer didn't hesitate for a second. They stood behind Paisley, backs straight, eyes steady, trusting her without condition.

In their minds, Paisley never did anything she couldn't handle.

Trust, no questions asked.

Across the hall, the four Lennox siblings' faces lit with poorly hidden glee.

Rachel's mouth curved into something cold. She laughed inwardly.

Even better. No need to set anything up ourselves — Paisley's about to get herself killed.

Rivergate's one and only Level Six mutant carnivorous flower was vicious, powerful, and loaded with poison.

Every team that had gone after it was gone. This time, Paisley would be too.

And once she's dead, that pendant is mine.

The three Lennox brothers traded glances, the sting of yesterday's humiliation melting into something close to pleasure. They waited, eager, for Paisley to die without even a body left behind.

Beside them, Kelly's mind was already working. She stepped back, publicly cutting ties, her tone cold and deliberate.

"If you want to die, leave me out of it. I'm not going down with you. This Level Six thing is suicide — I'll take a normal mission with another team."

Rachel jumped on the opening.

"Come with us, then. We're short a water-type anyway. You'd fit right in."

Paisley let her gaze slide over the two of them, unbothered.

She turned to Elijah.

"She's leaving on her own. Not my problem."

Then, glancing up, she gave a light nudge.

"You and Orion heading out or what?"

Elijah gave Kelly one cool look.

"Your call. Don't come crying about it later."

Not one wasted word.

Kelly's hands clenched. The resentment burned deeper than ever.

Elijah's always like this.

We grew up together. Do I really mean so little to him?

If none of them care, then fine. One day, they'll regret it.

"Fine. I've decided."

With that, the line between them was drawn.

Elijah didn't look at her again. He glanced down at the two mission tokens in Paisley's hand, brow creasing with worry, and tried once more.

"You sure you don't want to think this over?"

"That Level Six flower's loaded with corrosive poison. It's dangerous — "

His face stayed grim.

They'd never gone up against a Level Six mutant plant before.

Mutant plants hid, camouflaged, struck with venom from odd angles. Far nastier than any zombie or beast.

Orion stepped up too, brow furrowed.

"Paisley, why not take the Level Six tomorrow? I'll come with you."

Aside from Paisley, he was the strongest of them all. And today, of all days, he wouldn't be around.

Paisley stayed easy, unbothered by his concern.

"Don't worry about me. Go on."

Who knew if their own mission would even leave time tomorrow.

Besides — after last night, her power had jumped. She was confident she could handle this.

She reached into her bag and pulled out two bottles of spirit water, one for Elijah, one for Orion.

"This restores energy fast. Drink it when you're worn down."

They both knew exactly what it could do — they'd leaned on it before, during that brutal zombie siege. A treasure money couldn't buy in this world.

Orion took his bottle, fingers brushing the cool glass, warmth spreading through his chest. He nodded, solemn.

"We'll head out first. Be careful. Don't push yourselves."

Elijah gripped his own bottle, held Paisley's gaze a moment, then turned and left with Orion for their own assignment.

Behind them, Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer stared at the spirit water with open envy, lips pressed together, clearly hoping for some of their own.

Paisley cleared her throat.
