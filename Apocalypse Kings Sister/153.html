Chapter 153

But Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer didn't hesitate for a second. They stood tall behind Paisley with steady eyes, ready to back any call she made.

As far as they were concerned, Paisley never made a move she wasn't sure of. They trusted her, no questions asked.

Meanwhile, across the hall, the four Lennox siblings could barely hide their glee or the malice behind it.

Rachel gave a faint, icy smile and laughed to herself.

Perfect. We don't even have to lift a finger, because Paisley's about to get herself killed.

Rivergate's one and only Level Six mutant carnivorous flower was vicious, powerful, and loaded with poison.

Every team before had been wiped out. This time, Paisley and her friends were dead for sure.

And once she's dead, that pendant is mine.

The three Lennox brothers traded glances, looking downright gleeful. All the frustration of getting crushed these past few days was gone, and they couldn't wait to watch Paisley get torn to pieces.

Kelly, always quick on her feet, took a step back to publicly distance herself from Paisley. She put on a wary but calm act and spoke up loudly, her voice cold.

"If you want to get yourself killed, leave me out of it. I'm not going down with you. This Level Six mission is way too dangerous, so I'll take a normal one and team up with someone else."

Rachel jumped right on it. "Then come with us. We're short a Water Affinity user, so you'd be perfect."

Paisley gave the two of them a cool glance, not bothered in the slightest, and turned to Elijah. "She's the one who wants to leave. That's got nothing to do with me."

Then, she looked up at him and gently hurried him along. "Why haven't you and Orion left yet?"

Elijah glanced at Kelly, his face cold. "It's your call, so don't come crying about it later." He didn't say another word.

Kelly clenched her fists, so bitter she could barely stand it.

Elijah was always like this.

We grew up together. Do I really mean that little to him?

If none of them cared about her, then fine. One day, she'd make them all regret it.

"Yeah, I've made up my mind," Kelly said.

With that, they officially parted ways.

Elijah didn't look at her again. He glanced down at the two mission tokens in Paisley's hand, brow creasing with worry, and tried once more.

"You sure you don't want to think this over? That Level Six flower is loaded with corrosive poison, and it's really dangerous..."

His face was grim. After all, they'd never killed a Level Six mutant plant before.

Mutant plants hid in the shadows and were masters of camouflage. Their poisons were sneaky and their attacks were unpredictable, which made them far nastier than mutant zombies or mutant beasts.

Orion stepped up too, frowning with worry. "Paisley, why don't you take the Level Six mission tomorrow instead? I'll go with you."

Aside from Paisley, he was the strongest of them all. And today, of all days, he wouldn't be around.

Orion was clearly worried, but Paisley stayed calm and relaxed. "Don't worry about me. You guys go on."

Who knew if they'd even finish their own mission today, or have any time tomorrow?

On top of that, her power had jumped last night, and she was sure she could handle the mission.

With that, she reached into her bag, pulled out the spirit water she'd prepared, and handed a bottle each to Elijah and Orion. "This restores your energy and stamina fast. Take a sip whenever you're worn out."

Back when they'd fought off that zombie horde and nearly run out of strength, this spirit water was what had kept them going. They knew better than anyone how well it worked, and that it was a treasure no amount of money could buy in the apocalypse.

Orion quickly took his bottle. The glass felt cool in his hand, but his chest filled with warmth, and he gave a serious nod.

"We'll head out first, then. You guys be really careful, and don't try to be heroes."

Elijah gripped his bottle and gave Paisley a long look. He pushed down his worry, then turned and left with Orion for their own mission.

Off to the side, Ashley, Thomas, and Spencer stared at the spirit water with open envy, lips pressed together, clearly hoping for some of their own.

Paisley cleared her throat.
