The instant the words left her, a clear, golden-tinted barrier surged up from beneath everyone's feet, spreading wide, sealing them all inside.

The round shield shimmered, warm and steady, sealing out every bit of danger from outside.

The storm of poison seeds slammed into it and shattered on contact, dissolving into harmless black mist.

The surging toxic fog hit the barrier and dissolved instantly, purified clean, unable to get within an inch of them.

Whether it was the Level Six flower's main poison or the lesser blooms' corrosive mist, the ward blocked it all, completely.

Those who'd already closed their eyes, bracing for pain and death, felt nothing but clean, fresh air instead, the suffocating weight gone.

Everyone's eyes flew open at once. When they saw the golden barrier surrounding them, the poison and seeds shut out completely, they froze, stunned beyond words.

What is this thing?

It's blocking everything. Even the poison.

Relief and disbelief crashed over them in waves, and every eye turned to Paisley.

She'd done it again.

Saved them. Again.

Just then, a small voice — a young boy's — rose in Paisley's mind.

It was the mutant willow, bonded to her through the life-bond pact.

This was the first time it had ever spoken.

"Master. Let me out."

"I can handle the lesser flowers."

Paisley blinked, startled. First time they'd ever actually communicated — she hadn't known the willow could talk at all.

This world really has gone insane.

But it volunteering to fight was a welcome surprise.

Without hesitation, her will moved, and she released it.

Hum —

******

A surge of clean, vibrant plant energy rippled outward, and a massive mutant willow burst from the ground, its branches thick and strong, standing tall amid the poisoned field.

Thousands of soft willow fronds unfurled in the wind, radiating such powerful vitality that it naturally suppressed the toxic plants around it.

The lesser mutant flowers ringing the field, the ones that had sealed off every escape, were nothing in front of a fully awakened mutant willow.

Paisley's gaze sharpened, cutting past the cleared perimeter straight to the center of the field — the towering, horrifying Level Six carnivorous flower boss.

Her mind flicked to the space, and she pulled out a bag of ghost-pepper-hot spicy jerky.

Hope Trovie's right about this being its weakness.

Her eyes narrowed, and she channeled Plant Affinity, feeding power into the willow.

The mutant willow grew larger, green light pulsing along its thick trunk, a dense Plant Affinity sigil surfacing at its core — intricate, ancient-looking, glowing green, pouring out endless vitality and vine strength.

A deep hum rolled through the air, and countless branches, thick and thin, shot upward in a sudden surge of growth.

The once-soft, drooping fronds hardened and stretched, losing their flexibility, transforming into countless thick green limbs, rising like a swarm of dragons breaking from the earth, stacking and interlocking mid-air.

Layer after layer of branches wove into a solid wooden platform, lifting Paisley steadily upward, carrying her dozens of yards into the air.

Wind caught her clothes as she stood, cold and composed, now level with the massive flower's gaping, fanged center.

Below, the others craned their necks, watching her rise, awe and admiration plain on every face, breath held.

Everyone willed her to come back victorious.

Paisley tore open the bag of ghost-pepper spicy jerky, the seal splitting with a sharp rip, and a wave of overpowering, nose-searing heat burst into the air.

Below, the Level Six flower seemed to sense the movement overhead. Its massive petals shuddered, its grotesque center opening and closing, venom dripping and hissing against the ground, purple mist surging up again as its huge body stirred, gathering for another wave of poison.

Now.

Paisley flicked her wrist, and the bag of jerky sailed through the air, landing dead center through the flower's gaping, open mouth — straight into its deepest, most vulnerable core.
