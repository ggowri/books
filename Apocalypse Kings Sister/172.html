Chapter 172

[Buy it!]

The moment she said it, a clear, pale gold barrier shot up from beneath their feet, spreading out to cover all of them.

The round shield glowed softly, shutting out every danger outside.

The storm of poison seeds slammed into it and shattered on contact, dissolving into harmless black mist.

The moment the churning purple-black poison mist touched the barrier, it was purified and scattered, unable to get within a step of them.

Whether it was the Level Six flower's deadly poison or the lesser flowers' corrosive mist, the all-purpose ward blocked all of it perfectly.

The ones who'd shut their eyes to wait for death never felt the pain they'd braced for. Instead, the air around them felt clean and fresh, and even the suffocating pressure was gone.

Everyone's eyes flew open in shock. When they saw the golden barrier around them and the poison mist and seeds shut outside, they froze, stunned and unable to believe it.

What was this thing? It actually blocked every kind of damage, even poison.

They'd survived by a hair, and they were flooded with shock and joy. Every one of them looked at Paisley. She'd pulled off another miracle and saved them all again.

Just then, a little boy's voice suddenly rang out in Paisley's head.

It was the mutant willow she'd bonded with through a life-bond pact.

[Master, let me out. I can take care of those lesser mutant carnivorous flowers.]

Paisley was a little stunned. This was the first time she'd ever communicated with the mutant willow, and she'd had no idea it could even talk.

This world really has gone insane.

Still, it was a nice surprise that it had volunteered to fight.

Without another moment's hesitation, she released it with a single thought.

With a deep hum, a wave of fresh green energy rippled outward, and a giant mutant willow with a thick, sturdy trunk and lush leaves burst out of the ground. It stood firm in the middle of the poisonous flower field.

Thousands of soft willow branches swayed in the wind, full of vitality, which happened to be the perfect counter to the poisonous mutant plants all around.

The lesser mutant flowers ringing the field, the ones that had sealed off every escape, were nothing in front of a fully awakened mutant willow.

Paisley's eyes turned sharp. Her gaze went past the poisonous flowers on the outer edge, which the willow had already cleared away, and locked onto the terrifying Level Six mutant carnivorous flower towering at the center of the field, the boss of this place.

With a thought, she pulled a big bag of insanely hot spicy jerky out of her Storage Dimension.

I hope Trovie's right and this insanely spicy jerky really is its weakness.

Her eyes narrowed as she released her Plant Affinity to make the willow grow.

The mutant willow grew bigger and bigger as green light flowed through its thick branches. A powerful Plant Affinity rune slowly appeared in the center of its trunk, glowing bright green with intricate, ancient-looking patterns, pumping out endless life and vine power.

A deep hum rolled through the air, and countless branches, thick and thin, shot upward in a sudden surge of growth.

The thousands of soft, drooping willow branches hardened and stretched out, turning into long, sturdy green limbs. They shot into the sky like giant serpents bursting from the earth, stacking and crisscrossing in midair.

Layer after layer of branches wove into a solid wooden platform, lifting Paisley steadily upward, carrying her dozens of yards into the air.

The breeze tugged at her clothes as she stood high in the air, cool and composed, right across from the huge, hideous mouth at the flower's center.

Down below, everyone stared up at Paisley standing high in the air, awed and full of admiration, unconsciously holding their breath.

They were all counting on her to win and come back to them.

Paisley tore open the bag of insanely hot spicy jerky with a sharp rip. A wave of fierce, eye-watering heat exploded into the air, burning straight up the nose.

Down in the center of the field, the Level Six mutant carnivorous flower seemed to sense the movement overhead. Its huge petals shook violently, and its hideous center kept opening and closing, dripping venom that sizzled on the ground. Purple poison dust swirled up again as its huge body stirred, getting ready for another poison attack.

This was her chance. Paisley flicked her wrist, and the bag of spicy jerky flew through the air, straight into the flower's gaping mouth and down into the dark depths of its core, its weakest spot.
