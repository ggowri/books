Chapter 131

"Reid, Ryan, are you really just going to give up? Are you okay being ordinary people with no affinity for the rest of your lives?

"I heard Paisley had something to do with Thomas, Spencer, Ashley, and Madison awakening their affinities!"

Every word was meant to drive a wedge. She was pumping up their resentment and greed on purpose, and she hit the three brothers right where it hurt most.

The Lennox brothers already held a grudge against Paisley.

Maybe they felt a little regret, but not much.

With Rachel egging them on, their greed completely took over.

The three of them marched up to Paisley and stood over her. There wasn't a shred of apology on their faces, just the same arrogant, bossy attitude as always.

In their minds, Paisley was a daughter the Lennoxes had raised. Every lucky break, every resource, and every bit of strength she had belonged to the family, period.

As far as they were concerned, letting her be useful to them now was them doing her a huge favor.

Owen took the lead. He looked down at Paisley, who was half lying in her chair, and his tone was hard and entitled, like there was nothing to discuss.

"Paisley, hand over every crystal core you've got."

His eyes swept over the half-eaten burger, milkshake, and roast chicken next to her, and he looked impatient and greedy.

"And buy each of us the same thing. If you've got supplies and good stuff, family gets them first."

Before Paisley could say a word, Reid jumped in, sharp and pushy.

"And that's not all. Every treasure, every piece of ability gear, every resource you've saved up, hand it all over.

"A girl like you doesn't need to be that strong anyway. Just give it all to us."

Then, Ryan laid all his greed out in the open. He stared hard at Paisley, prying and pushing.

"We're dying to know. In less than half a month, you went from a total nobody to someone this powerful, with resources to spare. How'd you even get everyone around you to awaken their affinities?

"What did you do to awaken your affinity? And how did you get your hands on all these treasures and resources?

"Tell us everything! Help me awaken my affinity right now!"

The three of them took turns, bossy and pushy. Every word was a demand, and they acted like it was their right.

To them, Paisley owed them everything. Her lucky breaks should be split up, her resources taken, and her abilities used to make their dreams come true.

Paisley lay back lazily in her chair under the warm sun, the little white cat curled up in her arms. At their words, she slowly looked up.

Her expression was calm. She wasn't angry, just bone-deep cold and completely indifferent.

She looked at the three greedy, selfish Lennox brothers, and her voice was cold and distant.

"My crystal cores, my food, my treasures, my luck, none of it has ever had anything to do with you.

"I earned everything I have on my own. I don't owe you a single thing, and it's not my job to help you awaken an affinity or get stronger."

Her flat refusal shattered their fantasy of getting something for nothing.

All three brothers' faces darkened at once, and their arrogance turned into anger.

Rachel stood half a step behind them, but her eyes were glued to the pendant around Paisley's neck.

The pendant had shattered days ago, but every crack had been carefully filled with gold. It looked even more beautiful and precious than before.

Even if it's broken, the space has to still be in there!

Once I get that pendant and the space, everything Paisley has will be mine!

While Paisley was busy dealing with the Lennox brothers, Rachel saw her chance. She gathered energy in her fingertips and lunged for the pendant around Paisley's neck!

Just as her fingers were about to touch it, the little white cat looked up from the chicken leg it was chewing on. Its blue eyes flashed ice-cold.

Quick as lightning, its fluffy paw shot out, sharp claws gleaming, and it slashed hard across the back of Rachel's outstretched hand!

The claws whipped through the air so fast that no one had time to react.
