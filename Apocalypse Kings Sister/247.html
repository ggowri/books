Chapter 247

The sky got dark early too. Gray clouds hung lower and lower, covering the whole wilderness in gloom.

Traveling at night was a bad idea. If they kept going, they'd only run into more unknown beasts and danger.

Just then, a cluster of houses came into view at the far edge of the wilderness.

It was a village.

An old, weathered stone marker stood at the entrance, its lettering worn almost smooth by wind and rain. They could barely make out the name Wexford.

The whole village was dead silent, with every door and window shut tight and the streets empty. There were no voices, no roosters crowing, no dogs barking, and even the wind in the grass and trees was so faint it had almost died away. It was way too quiet, and something about it felt eerie in a way none of them could put into words.

Out here in the empty hills, the deserted old village was so silent that it put everyone on edge.

Elijah studied the whole village through the car window, his face grim and wary, and spoke to everyone in a low voice. "We'll pick an empty house on the very edge of the village and make do there for the night.

"Keep a low profile, and don't do anything that'll draw attention."

He paused, and his voice turned colder and more serious. "In the apocalypse, people are scarier than any disaster. Everyone, be careful."

They'd all been through plenty of close calls by now, so everyone nodded and agreed.

Paisley frowned slightly and quietly sent out her psychic power to scan the whole area.

Her scan spread across 500 yards in every direction. On the surface, the village looked calm, but the silence felt wrong everywhere, and a strange, heavy energy hung in the air. Something dangerous was lurking underneath, and it was way off.

Wexford was definitely hiding something.

But night had fully fallen, and traveling through the wilderness at night was full of danger. The unknown threats out there were far deadlier than this creepy village, so stopping here for the night was their only safe choice.

The cars slowly pulled up outside the village, and soon they spotted a large standalone mansion on the very edge of it. The land around it was empty, and the place looked like it had been abandoned for a long time.

Elijah and Orion got out first and carefully checked the mansion floor by floor, inside and out, including every corner and dark spot. Once they were sure there were no zombies and the whole place was safe, they waved everyone in.

"It's safe," Elijah said. "We'll stay here."

Everyone gathered in the mansion's living room, and Elijah laid out the plan. "Pick whatever rooms you want. We'll spend the night here and head out again at first light tomorrow."

Paisley put the cars away in her Storage Dimension, walked in, and spoke up. "I'll stay by myself. Just save me a room on the second floor."

Ashley pouted right away and had to settle for hooking her arm through Madison's, whining. "Madison, then I guess I'm bunking with you again tonight."

Madison smiled warmly and nodded. "Sure thing."

Kelly, who'd been quiet the whole ride, made herself sound humble. "Any room is fine for me."

Then, she went out of her way to win everyone over and offered on her own. "I'll make dinner. I can't just keep freeloading."

Thomas let out a soft scoff, a little mocking. "At least you know your place."

Elijah looked around at everyone. "We'll take the night watch in two shifts. I'll take the first half of the night."

Orion jumped in right away. "Then I'll take the second half."

Ashley beamed at them, full of gratitude. "Orion, Elijah, thanks so much. I can actually get some sleep because of you two."

While no one was paying attention, Paisley went into the kitchen alone.

Her fingers twitched, a faint glow from her Storage Dimension flickered, and a big pile of fresh ingredients appeared out of nowhere on the kitchen table.

There were all kinds of crisp seasonal vegetables, fresh meat that had already been cleaned and trimmed, and bags of whole oats, all lined up neatly. There was plenty of everything, and it all looked fresh.

Her eyes narrowed a little, and she quietly kept her guard up.

Now that Kelly was with them, Paisley couldn't be sure what anyone was really up to, so she had to stay careful. It wasn't smart to show off too much of her supplies or her powers.

Better keep Kelly from seeing any of this.

When she was done, Paisley turned and headed straight upstairs. "I'm gonna go rest for a bit. Call me when it's time to eat."

Upstairs, she picked the cleanest room on the second floor and gently closed the door, shutting out every sound from outside.

Paisley entered her Storage Dimension and sat cross-legged on the couch. She opened her palm, and one clear, glittering high-level crystal core after another quietly appeared.

She'd collected all of them over the past two days from mutant beasts and zombies. The purest and most powerful one was the core from the Level Six mutant bear that afternoon, and it was packed with energy.
