Chapter 256

Pure terror and pain shattered whatever resolve he had left.

He didn't dare talk back anymore, pressing his forehead hard against the freezing floor.

THUD. THUD.

The dull, heavy sound of him bowing echoed clearly through the cold, silent room.

"I was wrong! I was so wrong!"

"Please, spare me! I was blind, I had no idea who I was dealing with! I've done terrible things, hurt innocent people, please, don't kill me!"

"I'll never do it again! Take everything, all my supplies, all my cores! Just let me live!"

His forehead split open from the bowing, blood seeping out, every trace of the man who'd once ruled this village gone.

Orion's face stayed hard. Nothing about this man deserved mercy.

Before anyone could say more, Elijah's eyes went glacial, a flash of silver-blue lightning gathering around him.

Crackle—!

A single thread of lightning, condensed and precise, shot out and punched clean through Fat Wagner's skull.

His body locked up mid-bow, eyes wide, every trace of fear and greed draining out at once.

The next instant, he toppled over, dead.

The warlord of Wexford was finished.

Paisley stood by quietly, watching the corpse without a trace of feeling.

In this world, people got what they deserved. He'd earned every bit of it.

She turned to Elijah and Orion.

"Handle the rest however you want. I'm going to look around."

The moment she said it, Trovie's bright voice chimed in.

[Host! There's a ton of treasure stashed on the second floor of the mansion! Gold, supplies, high-tier crystal cores, all of it!]

A faint gleam crossed her eyes.

Made sense. A man like Fat Wagner, ruling this village through theft and murder, would have plenty hidden away.

She didn't linger, heading up through the damp dark basement toward the second floor.

The hallway was decked out in lavish decor, nothing like the squalor below, every detail a testament to how these men had lived.

The door at the end of the second-floor hall was shut tight.

A thin vine slipped through the crack beneath it, mapping the room instantly.

The master bedroom at the far end had a heavy combination lock and reinforced panel. This was it.

She pushed, and the vine wound around the lock, giving it a slight turn.

With a click, the heavy door swung open.

Inside, supplies were stacked floor to ceiling.

An entire room, dedicated to hoarding.

Crates of gold bars, at least a few thousand pounds' worth, stacked neat and tight.

Jewelry, cash, trinkets.

Medicine, ration bars, bottled water piled against the walls.

Wooden boxes and silk pouches held crystal cores of every rank, even a few rare Level Six and Seven ones.

Probably too high-tier for them to absorb, which was why they'd been left untouched.

On top of that, high-tier weapon parts, reinforced armor, and upgrade materials, everything worth a fortune out here.

Trovie's voice rang out again, giddy.

[Host's rich! All this is the scumbag's life savings, and it's all yours now!]

Paisley relaxed slightly and raised a hand.

A faint glow swept the room.

******

In an instant, every scrap of gold, every core, every weapon, vanished into her dimension, the room stripped bare.

"Help..."

A faint, broken cry bled through the wall from the room next door.

"Help... someone, please..."

The voice was hoarse, shaking, right at the edge of giving up.
