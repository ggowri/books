Chapter 470

A flash of light, and crate after crate of cores, stacks of gold, bundles of cash, and rare supplies disappeared into her Storage Dimension, neat and complete. Not a scrap was left behind.

The basement was empty.

A sweet smile stayed on her lips, and her heart felt light.

Trovie, nice work. Convert all the gold into points.

[Okay, Master!]

[Gold converted automatically. 50,000 points received!]

[Congratulations, Master! Large-scale hoarding bonus triggered! 5,000 points awarded!]

[Current total, 3,495,360 points!]

She looked at the big number on the system panel, and her mouth curved up, a glint of light in her eyes.

She didn't linger. She turned and went back up the hidden stairs, stepped out of the passage, and put the tile back so neatly nobody could tell it had moved.

She remembered Trovie's note that the hotel hid more supplies. She turned and headed for the empty room next door.

The moment she pushed the door open,

she saw a room someone had converted into a storeroom. Bags of oats, flour, and grain were stacked against the walls and floor, along with piles of vacuum-sealed dried pasta and instant meals. They reached halfway up the wall. The stock was huge.

Food was the scarcest thing in the apocalypse. A stockpile this size had dropped right into her hands.

A thought, and the whole room of oats, flour, oil, and instant food vanished into her Storage Dimension, leaving the room clean.

She crossed to the spare storeroom on the other side.

The door opened on rows and rows of war supplies.

Bundles of dry firewood were stacked tight and wrapped in waterproof tarps, perfectly preserved. Rows of sealed metal drums stood nearby, all full of high-grade gasoline, rare fuel in the apocalypse.

For campfires, vehicle range, or fire barriers in battle, it was essential.

With the Great Freeze coming, everything here was exactly what they needed.

Her eyes brightened even more, and she took it all.

Firewood, gasoline, spare fuel of every kind went into storage, sorted and stacked.

A few minutes later, both hidden rooms were empty, every rare supply inside her Storage Dimension.

Satisfied, Paisley climbed back to the third-floor room.

She went in and locked the door behind her.

Then she slipped into her Storage Dimension.

She sat on the soft sofa there and looked at the mountain of crystal cores. Eyes closed, she settled her mind.

She steadied her breathing and began her upgrade.

A soft glow rose on the cores. Pure power poured through her pores like a river, circulating through every limb.

Hum!

Her Plant Affinity settled first. Green life energy filled her core and kept expanding, and her veins widened and strengthened. Healing, parasitizing, and growth powers climbed higher and higher, and finally locked in at peak Level Seven.

Life and decay answered to a single thought. Mass healing and purification, or the bloom-and-burst parasite kill, would all hit harder than before.

Her Psychic Affinity came next.

Invisible psychic power surged through her mind. Her inner world widened, and her spirit grew denser and stronger.

The wall at peak Level Six cracked under the flood of energy. It loosened, split, and fell apart.

Warm, vast psychic energy washed through her mind. Her mental domain grew, stretched, and steadied. The sea of her thoughts turned clearer and wider, and rose higher.

Every bit of damage from her earlier psychic attacks healed and refined, leaving her mind sharper and purer.

Peak Level Six, early Level Seven, mid Level Seven.

The barrier shattered with no resistance.

Finally, her Psychic Affinity transformed, broke through, and took root. It was now mid Level Seven.

Her mighty psychic pressure spread, then pulled back and hid inside her. With a thought, she could control or attack at least three hundred zombies at once.
