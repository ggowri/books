Chapter 485

Not far from them, the masked man, who had been blowing the whistle the whole time, stopped dead.

The cold, heavy aura around him sank to the bottom. The air on the roof seemed to freeze.

He raised his head slowly. Through the slits in the black mask, he locked onto the girl in the snow who had flipped the whole fight. His eyes held disbelief, deep wariness, and a trace of dark rage.

This can't be right.

Six Level Seven zombies.

I don't even know how much I spent raising them.

They were my carefully laid trap, the card I'd built to kill Elijah, a sure win.

The gap between Level Six and Level Seven was a cliff. Even a peak Level Six like Elijah could only hold on and lose ground. And a young girl had frozen and killed all six in an instant.

She had top-tier Plant Affinity, and also the terrifying Deep Freeze of her Ice-Water Affinity!

Hatred and resentment rolled in the masked man's chest.

Paisley again!

She wrecked my plan again!

Since they'd gotten back to the base, she had broken his plans every single time and thrown off his schemes.

Without her, Aetheris Base would have been mine long ago.

If she didn't keep showing up and turning things around, Elijah would have died in my trap long ago. He wouldn't be alive now.

This Paisley is too strange, too hard to handle.

Killing intent weighed on the masked man's eyes. He ground his teeth.

With her here, I can't kill Elijah today.

If this drags on, I'll end up on the back foot and expose myself. It's not worth it.

He made up his mind. He had no more heart for the fight. He turned, raised a hand, tapped the hidden comm at his ear, and gave a low order to retreat.

The next second, a black armed helicopter burst out from beneath the clouds, its roar shaking the wilderness. It hovered over the roof of the gas station, and the cabin door slid open.

The masked man flashed into motion, leapt, and dropped into the cabin.

"Stop! Who are you?!"

Elijah snapped back to himself. Ignoring the drain on his power, he made lightning arc again and sprinted after him.

He was one step too late.

The cabin door sealed. The engine roared louder, the helicopter swung around at full speed and fled toward the far clouds, about to vanish from sight.

Everyone assumed the ambush would end with the enemy's escape.

Nobody expected a twist.

Just as the helicopter was about to disappear, Paisley raised her eyes. There wasn't a trace of emotion in them.

She made a thought, and a high-precision modified sniper rifle dropped into her hands.

She settled low, raised the rifle one-handed, aimed, and pulled the trigger, all in one smooth motion, with astonishing accuracy.

Bang!

A clear, sharp gunshot ripped through the snow and the roar!

A custom explosive round streaked across the air, faster than sound, and hit the exact core of the helicopter's tail rotor!

Crack, boom!

The solid alloy rotor blew apart. The helicopter lost balance, tilted up, then dropped hard!

Black smoke billowed out of the fuselage, fire flashing inside. The huge machine spun out of control, shuddering and roaring, and fell straight toward the empty field in the distance!

Nobody had expected Paisley to shoot down a fleeing armed helicopter with a single shot!

On the roof, the wind and snow went quiet. The whole place was silent.

Elijah stood still, watching the helicopter fall in a column of black smoke, and felt his heart give a hard jolt.

He turned slowly and looked at the calm, cool girl beside him. Shock filled his eyes, and the feeling buried deep inside him layered and grew heavier.

He'd never imagined her aim could be this sharp and deadly.

In a world run by Affinities, where strength was everything, most people relied on their powers to fight and let their body and weapon skills go. But her marksmanship was trickier and deadlier than most dedicated special forces.

At that moment, he was certain. Even if every Affinity she had were stripped away, even if she were an ordinary person, Paisley could stand firm in this brutal apocalypse and thrive.

Her strength never came only from her Affinities. It was in her bones, in her toughness and effort.
