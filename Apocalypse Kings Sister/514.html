Chapter 514

Chase's expression shifted several times as he looked from Elijah to the others.

The entire Falcon Squad was here. They weren't a team he could afford to cross.

And clearly they meant to step into this.

Taking it by force would mean a heavy penalty from the base, maybe even an instant fight, and his team might not walk away unharmed.

He gritted his teeth, shot a last look at the supplies he'd had to give up, and growled to Rachel.

"Let's go!"

Rachel's face was ugly, and she resented every step. She wanted to say more, but Chase grabbed her arm.

"Enough talk. We're leaving!"

Owen and Reid didn't dare say anything and just followed.

Before they left, Rachel shot Paisley a venomous glare. The group turned and slunk off down the wild trail, looking back with every step, full of resentment.

Only after Chase's group had gone out of sight behind the rocks did the tension drain from the ordinary team.

The team leader let out a long breath, led his teammates to Paisley, and thanked her, his face full of gratitude.

He gave a slight bow. His voice carried the relief of someone who'd just cheated death.

"Thank you! If you hadn't stepped in, we'd have lost the clean food we finally found.

When we heard her say she was your sister, we got really nervous. We thought…"

He trailed off, embarrassed.

Paisley's face was calm. She explained evenly.

"We just used to live in the same house. We cut ties with them a long time ago and we're nothing to each other now.

She has no right to use my name to throw her weight around.

What you found belongs to you."

"If you ever see her out there using my name to scam people, please help set the record straight."

Ashley nodded and added.

"And if she ever uses Paisley's name for something bad again, report her to the base."

The ordinary team finally understood, and the weight on their hearts lifted. They thanked her again.

"So that's how it is!

Thank you all so much!

We won't forget this!"

"Don't worry, we'll keep an eye out. If they do it again, I'll make sure everyone knows how things really are."

The team leader pulled a bright red wild fruit from the basket and held it out.

"Just a small token. Please take it."

Paisley shook her head.

"No, keep it. Clean food is hard to find after the Crimson Rain.

It's getting late. Head back to base quickly, and be careful on the way."

Elijah added a warning.

"There are a lot of beasts around here. Travel in a group. Don't go alone."

The team agreed and thanked her again. They shouldered their supplies and hurried off toward the base.

Thomas watched them go and said quietly.

"Supplies are scarce after the Crimson Rain, and people's ugly sides are coming out."

"I never thought they'd be that low. Openly robbing someone else's supplies."

Orion nodded.

"The tighter resources get, the more people grab and fight.

Paisley, this gave us a warning. We should put out an announcement at the base saying you cut ties with the Lennox family a long time ago and have nothing to do with them.

They've got some nerve. They tore everything apart ages ago, and they still use your name to rob people."

Paisley nodded.

"Yeah, we should take care of that soon."

Elijah looked at Paisley, his gaze gentle.

"Leave it to me."

"Let's head back and turn in the mission."

"The base is probably out of water by now."

Paisley looked up at the gray sky, her eyes cold and clear.

Rachel was using her name outside to throw her weight around. That debt, she would settle sooner or later.

They didn't linger. They went back to the SUV and climbed in.

The engine roared, the tires rolled through the dark red mud, and the car turned toward Aetheris Base.

The road was clear. In a little while the SUV drove into the base, passed through layer after layer of security, and headed straight for the mission hall.

The hall was still packed. Red high-risk missions scrolled on the screen, and low voices filled the room. Everyone was worried about clean water and food.

Today's rations had been cut by twenty percent.

Many people looked tired and anxious. The pressure of shortages after the Crimson Rain weighed on everyone's hearts.
