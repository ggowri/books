Chapter 223

These zombies' minds were already locked tight by an even more domineering, more powerful psychic chain. Just like the old man underground had controlled his mutants, someone had trapped them, and Paisley couldn't break through.

There was no other way to break up the horde for good. Only killing the one controlling them would break that hold.

Paisley held her breath, focused, and sent out her psychic senses again at full power. Countless tiny signals quickly came together, and she pinpointed exactly where the controller was hiding.

"Kitty, get big."

The little white cat leaped up on command, and its body grew several times bigger in a flash. Its snow-white fur was fluffy and dazzling, and it looked strong and fierce.

Paisley swung herself onto its back and lay low against it.

The cat released its power, and freezing cold air froze the swarms of zombies charging down the road ahead, clearing the way.

In the dark wilderness, the Frostbloom Sword glowed with a pearly white light. Like a lamp in the night, it cut through the heavy darkness and lit the way.

Paisley and the cat raced off, breaking through wave after wave of zombies, and in just three minutes, they covered about 1,000 yards and reached the top of a hill.

The night wind howled on the hilltop, and the view was wide open. An ordinary human, a man dressed in black and wearing a strange mask, stood there quietly.

He was gripping an old bronze bell, and he was obviously using it to control all the zombies in the wilderness from a distance.

When the masked man in black suddenly saw Paisley and the giant white cat racing toward him, his eyes went wide in disbelief. "This... How is this possible... How did you find me here?!"

He looked shocked and scared, and he didn't even get to finish.

Paisley's eyes stayed cold and calm. She raised her sword and swung, and a cold gleam flashed past.

With one quick, clean slash, she cut right through his neck, and warm blood gushed out.

She killed zombies all the time, but this was the first time she'd killed a person with her own hands since the apocalypse began.

The warm, sticky feel of it hit her, and the thick smell of blood rushed up her nose. It made her feel uneasy and strangely sick.

She pushed down the strange feeling, bent over, and quickly searched his pockets. She found a pouch full of crystal cores and a token with a special symbol on it.

These might lead to whoever he'd been working with, so she kept everything.

Then, she picked up the old bronze bell and gave it two gentle shakes.

The bell's clear ring spread in every direction. Down the hill, the thousands of zombies that had been so orderly and fearless lost all their restraints and commands at once. They fell into total chaos and scattered everywhere.

The team members fighting hard below noticed the change right away. Their terror finally eased, and their shattered confidence came rushing back.

"Ms. Lennox did it! She took out whoever was controlling them!"

"Nobody's controlling the zombies anymore! Every kill counts!"

Everyone's spirits soared. They pushed through their exhaustion and started mowing down the out-of-control zombies.

Without anyone controlling it, the horde fell apart and didn't stand a chance.

Morale was high, and the team kept clearing them out. Before long, the zombies had either run off or died.

As the smoke slowly cleared, everyone leaned on their knees, gasping for air. Their clothes were soaked with blood, they were covered in wounds, and their strength and powers were nearly drained, but they were all so relieved.

They'd survived after all.

After a short rest, they patched up their wounds and set out for the base again.

******

Back at the base, Rachel and Chase were in the leaders' office. The others still hadn't returned, so the two of them were sure the team was gone for good.

That made it easy for them to play the heroes.

They'd made themselves look as beat up as possible. They'd faked a bunch of wounds, some deep and some shallow, and with their pale faces and weak breathing, they looked like they'd just barely escaped death.

Chase knocked weakly and opened the door to the leaders' office. "Sir, the aquarium mission failed. The whole team got surrounded by high-level mutants, and everyone else... they're all dead."

Rachel stood next to him with red eyes, faking a choked sob as she chimed in right on cue. "The two of us only made it because my foresight warned us ahead of time and Chase's strong psychic power got us out. We barely made it back to base alive.

"The rest of the team will be stuck in that aquarium lab forever. They're never coming back."

The two of them played off each other perfectly, painting themselves as heroes who'd fought their way back on their own. Once this was over, they'd definitely get praise and rewards from the leaders.

The senior leaders of the base looked regretful and grim when they heard that, and the mood in the office was incredibly heavy.
