Chapter 447

The whole Falcon Squad stood frozen, hearts pounding, shock rolling through them and not fading.

Those were Affinity users, a dozen of them, not mere mooks.

At any base they'd be core fighters. And Paisley had wiped them out in five minutes.

Just how strong was she, really?

They couldn't even tell how she'd made all of them fall to the ground in agony.

It looked a bit like a Psychic Affinity's mind control!

Impossible. Had Paisley awakened Psychic Affinity too?

No... they were probably overthinking. She was simply that strong.

******

Before anyone could recover, the little white cat and the golden fox flashed over to the bodies. They quickly and neatly stripped every one of them of their storage pouches, crystal cores, ammunition, and supplies, leaving nothing behind.

The danger had passed.

Orion had no time to marvel. He hurried to Elijah and crouched to check on him. His fingers met cold skin and blue-black veins, and his face turned grim as stone. He spoke, deeply worried.

"There's serious serpent venom built up in him, and the mutant's mental contamination is eating at his mind."

"A double attack like this is vicious. Without the right antidote and purification fast, the worst case is his mind shatters and he dies. Even if he survives, the toxin will warp him into a mindless serpent puppet."

Ashley, Spencer, and the others went pale, their hearts clenching.

"But we're not at the base. We're deep in the ruins. Where would we find the right antidote?"

They were anxious and helpless. None of them knew how to break the venom and the mental contamination.

As far as anyone knew, a Level Seven beast's venom and mental poison like this had no cure.

Even back at the base there'd be nothing that could save Elijah.

In other words, Elijah was as good as dead!

Right as panic set in, Paisley, who had been drinking spirit water to refill her Affinity, walked up to Elijah. Her gaze was calm and steady.

"Don't worry, everyone. I can save him."

She didn't hesitate. She laid her hand over the wound on his chest and gut.

Pure, warm, living green Plant Affinity flowed from her palm. It wasn't the sharp vines of attack and defense now, but soft wisps of light, carrying gentle healing and purifying power that settled over Elijah's whole body.

It was high-level healing and purifying power, mid Level Seven.

Refined life force raced through his damaged veins and soaked into every part of him.

Like spring wind sweeping away dead leaves, it stripped the savage serpent venom from his body bit by bit, gently soothed the cruel mental contamination in his mind, and quickly repaired his torn organs and mended his gaping wounds.

The higher the level, the more terrifying the healing and purifying speed.

For ten full minutes the field was silent.

The squad held their breath, eyes fixed on Paisley and Elijah, hearts tight, even their breathing soft.

Ten minutes passed.

A miracle happened before their eyes.

The blue-black on Elijah's skin faded back to its normal pale color. The venom and mental contamination in his body were completely purified.

His chaotic, drained breathing grew steady and long. The savage, murky gloom in his eyes was gone.

The chest and gut wounds that had been ghastly, bone-deep, had closed without even a faint scar. Even his internal injuries were healed.

The unsolvable Level Seven venom that had plagued them was gone. The deadly mental contamination was fully purified. Every injury he had was healed!

Elijah opened his eyes. They were clear and steady, full of life. Nothing remained of the weak, dying, nearly warped look he'd had before. He was back at full strength.

The sight turned everyone's worldview upside down.

They stood stunned, pupils shaking, minds blank, shock washing over them like waves.

They'd never known that Paisley's Plant Affinity had awakened such an absurd healing and purifying power!
