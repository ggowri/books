Chapter 156

"If you really feel bad about it, just give me 200 Level Three crystal cores for each token," Paisley said. "You can owe me for now and pay me back whenever you have them."

If she didn't name a price, they'd keep feeling like they owed her and wouldn't feel right using the tokens.

Everyone let out a long breath of relief, and the heavy guilt lifted from their shoulders. At that price, the tokens were an absolute steal.

They all knew that anywhere in this apocalypse, a portable Galactic storage item that kept food fresh was priceless. No amount of crystal cores could buy one.

A measly 200 Level Three cores didn't come close to what a storage token was really worth.

They all knew Paisley had set the price that low to give them a break, because she saw them as her own people.

For anyone else, she wouldn't have offered this at all.

Without a second's hesitation, everyone nodded.

"Totally worth it!" Spencer blurted out, thrilled.

"A permanent storage token for 200 Level Three cores? That's not a bargain, we're practically stealing from you! Thanks, Paisley!"

Ashley gripped her token tight, her eyes shining. "I'm in! You're the best, Paisley!

"I love this thing so much! The second this mission's over, I'll pay you back every core!"

Madison and Mason nodded along, grateful.

With 100 square feet each, missions, zombie hunts, and supply runs would be so much easier. They could store supplies, crystal cores, and gear without hauling things back and forth or losing anything along the way. Right now, it was exactly what they needed most.

Marcus lowered his gaze to the cool metal token, and his eyes grew brighter.

Paisley seemed cool and distant, but she was actually thoughtful and genuinely kind to people.

How could he not fall for someone like that?

"No problem," Marcus said, his voice serious and sincere. "I'll pay you back as soon as I can."

Every one of them felt grateful to Paisley, and more curious about her than ever.

Paisley was always so mysterious, and she never stopped surprising them.

But no matter how mysterious she was, she was their teammate, and they'd keep her secrets forever.

Paisley checked the time. It was already 9 a.m., so she quickly gave them their instructions.

"All right, it's up to you whether to blood-bond your tokens. Once you're done, pair up and get moving. Stay safe, and if you run into any danger, page me right away."

Without another word, she pushed open the mall's rusted, weathered door.

Cold air thick with the stench of rot hit her in the face. Paisley calmly stepped into the dark, deathly quiet mall first, where danger lurked everywhere.

Once they were through the door, the space opened up, and low zombie growls rose from every direction.

Packs of Level Two and Three mutant zombies shuffled across the open lobby, their flesh rotting and their movements stiff.

They caught the scent of the living and surged forward all at once, jamming the path ahead.

Paisley didn't even flinch. Cold light gleamed, and the Frostbloom Sword appeared in her hand.

Her Level Six Plant Affinity wrapped around the blade, giving it a bone-chilling edge.

As the low-level zombies swarmed her, she moved lightly and easily, slipping in and out, and every swing of her sword was clean and efficient.

With one wet slice after another, zombie heads tumbled to the floor, splattering black blood everywhere.

Now that Paisley's power had skyrocketed, these ordinary Level Two and Three mutant zombies didn't stand a chance. She mowed them down in no time.

The little white cat trailed obediently behind her, its small white body hopping nimbly along.

Every time a zombie dropped, it darted in, split the skull open with one precise swipe of its little pink paw, and dug out the sparkling crystal core. Then, it carefully tucked the core into a storage token, all business.

From the outside, the mall looked rusted and run-down. But inside, the building was in great shape, and the fixtures, shelves, and merchandise had somehow been kept safe, with no sign of damage or looting.

The mall was huge. The first floor was a massive supermarket packed with household goods, snacks, instant meals, flour, oats, and cooking oil, all neatly stocked in an incredible variety.
