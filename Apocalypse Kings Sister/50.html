Chapter 50

Paisley narrowed her eyes, quietly working out her next move.

To keep getting stronger and keep leveling up, she'd need a ton of high-energy gemstones and high-level mutant crystal cores.

Trovie's happy voice chimed in. [Master, you're amazing! You got even stronger!]

Then, its voice turned serious. [Master, the pothos's body is still in one piece! It's loaded with deadly poison, but it's also holding a huge amount of pure energy.

[Try absorbing it and fusing with it. Its energy is the same kind as yours, so there's a good chance you'll end up with an amazing new ability!]

Paisley perked up and nodded right away.

With a single thought, she lifted the pothos's remains into the air. Countless green vines shot from her hands and latched onto it, slowly draining every drop of power and venom left inside.

Right then, something incredible happened!

Her pure, gentle plant energy suddenly took on a deep, dark green sheen of poison. Healing life force and deadly venom fused perfectly. Instead of clashing, each one made the other stronger.

Wild, concentrated toxic energy rushed back up the vines and into her body. It surged through her again and again, refining and rebuilding every last bit of her power.

Something inside her gave a sharp crack as another barrier shattered!

In just a few seconds, her power shot up again, jumping straight from early Level Four to mid Level Four!

This was more than a simple level-up. Her Plant Affinity had completely evolved.

Paisley could feel the change in every inch of her body.

Her Plant Affinity used to lean toward healing, support, and restraint. Now, it had picked up the Level Three Venom-Devouring Pothos's signature power, Venom Vines.

Every vine and plant she grew could still bind, trap, heal, and attack. But now, they also came coated in a powerful paralyzing venom.

If her vines so much as touched, scratched, or wrapped around an enemy, the toxin would hit instantly. Their nerves would go numb, their bodies would lock up, and their abilities would shut down. Meanwhile, her vines would suck out their blood and energy, draining them until they had no strength left to break free.

On top of that, she'd also inherited the pothos's most unbeatable trait, its crazy-fast regeneration.

As long as there were plants and energy around, her vines could grow back endlessly. She could keep fighting at almost no cost, and she'd only get stronger the longer the fight went on.

She'd also unlocked another ability, Total Poison Immunity. No poison, venom attack, or toxic cloud in the world could hurt her anymore. Instead, her Plant Affinity would swallow it up and turn it into energy for leveling up.

She now had six powers in one: raw strength, restraint, deadly poison, life drain, regeneration, and poison immunity. Her Plant Affinity was a top-tier powerhouse now, as deadly on offense as on defense. Nobody at her level stood a chance.

And it wasn't just her ability that had evolved. Her body had gotten a full upgrade, too.

Pure toxic energy and gentle life force had washed through her bones and muscles over and over, flushing every last impurity out of her body.

She felt lighter on her feet and stood taller. She looked lean and toned, but she packed serious explosive power.

Every move she made was smooth and sharp, without a single wasted motion. She moved like the wind, and she could charge in or pull back whenever she wanted.

Her senses got way sharper, and her detection range shot up, too. Before, she could only sense things within 100 yards. Now, her range stretched out to 300 yards.

Within 300 yards, every blade of grass and every tree showed up clearly in her mind. So did every flicker of energy and every trace of a zombie.

Every plant around her had become her eyes and ears.

Paisley raised a hand and slowly made a fist. A swirl of green and black energy curled in her palm, gentle life force hiding a deadly poison underneath. She could unleash it or rein it in whenever she wanted.

She couldn't help smiling.

It was only day four of the apocalypse, and she'd already climbed higher than she ever had in her past life, even by the day she died.

Not bad. Not bad at all.

In a flash, she left the dimension and strolled downstairs. As soon as she reached the meeting point, she spotted Orion, Elijah, Jasper, and Mason teaming up against a rare Level Three mutant humanoid.

The mutant looked twisted and terrifying. It had a vicious dog's head on a strong human body, with coarse, bristly fur and bulging muscles that screamed savage strength.

Unlike a mindless, bloodthirsty zombie, this Level Three mutant humanoid was smart. It moved fast and fought dirty, knew exactly when to strike and when to back off, and clearly had a mind of its own.

In all the time since her rebirth, Paisley had never seen a mutant like this one.
