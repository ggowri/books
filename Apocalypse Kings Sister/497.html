Chapter 497

A thick earth wall rose from the floor in front of Paisley, courtesy of Ashley. Earth spikes erupted one after another and drove into the next two rats lunging at them.

The spikes pierced the rats' thick gray fur. Jasper moved like a streak of shadow, his dagger sweeping across and opening the throats of the remaining two.

In moments, the snow rats lay in the snow, twitching and then still.

Ashley dropped her Earth Affinity and caught her breath. She frowned at the dog-sized corpses.

"Please tell me we didn't just poke a rat nest."

"Rats the size of dogs. The apocalypse really does have everything."

Jasper held his dagger, scanning every direction and listening. When he was sure there were no more lurking, he turned back.

"I think the third floor is clothes and bedding. Let's go up and look."

Paisley nodded. She'd meant to use this mission to bring out her supplies openly anyway.

She'd traded with the modern-world trader several times and had stockpiled heavy down jackets, warm comforters, and heating equipment. Even if they found nothing today, she could hand over enough to finish Trovie's side quest.

They stepped over ice and old snow and walked up toward the second floor.

They had just reached the landing when a bloodcurdling scream burst from the left corridor and echoed through the dead mall.

Jasper stopped and tilted his head to place the sound. "Someone's calling for help over there."

Ashley hesitated. "Should we check?"

"Whatever," Paisley said.

They went around the corner. The scene at the end of the corridor came into view.

Rachel, Chase, Owen, Reid, and Ryan were pinned down by a dense swarm of mutant snow rats.

Every one of them was wounded, their clothes torn and bleeding. In the deadly cold, their wounds had frozen purple.

Chase was the strongest on the team, a Level Five Psychic. His mental blasts kept sweeping the rats back and holding the line.

Rachel had reawakened with Chase's backing over the past weeks. She was now a Level Four Plant Affinity user, vines stretching out to block the swarm.

Reid and Owen were both Level Five Affinity users and kept pushing back anything that got close.

With help from Rachel and Chase, Ryan had awakened the rare Storage Affinity.

They weren't a top team, but they weren't weak either.

But today their luck was terrible. They'd run into a huge pack of Level Five snow rats and had no answer for it. Their line was about to crack.

Ryan was hurt the worst.

Moments ago, in the middle of the chaos, Rachel had shoved him out without a second thought to take a rat's full attack for her. That agonized scream had been Ryan's.

Paisley stood a little way off and took it all in.

The corner of her mouth curled faintly. She watched with interest. She had no plan to step in.

Ashley, Jasper, and the others knew the history between Paisley and this group. They recognized them on sight and had no desire to help.

Ashley made a face and murmured to Paisley, "What rotten luck. If I'd known it was them, I wouldn't have come."

"Let's go, Paisley. Let them fend for themselves."

Jasper tightened his grip on his dagger and nodded. "Exactly. Let them fend for themselves."

Rachel's eyes darted over and caught Paisley turning away. Her eyes lit up. She raised her voice and put on a pitiful, helpless face.

"Paisley, don't go! Please help us!"

She begged on the surface. Underneath, she had a cruel plan. She'd use the plea to lure the swarm toward Paisley's group.

Owen clutched his bleeding arm and chimed in, trying a guilt trip.

"Paisley, can you really watch us get eaten alive?"

"Whatever happened, the Lennox family raised you. You can't just be this heartless and let us die."

Ryan's face was bone white and his wounds were numb with cold. He shook all over and lifted a hand, begging Paisley with his eyes.

Reid joined in, shouting for help.

"Help us, Paisley. We grew up together. We're your brothers."

But something strange happened.

No matter how they screamed or how hard they tried to draw the snow rats toward Paisley's group, the swarm never moved.
