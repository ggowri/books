Chapter 393

She finally, truly understood, the woman standing in front of her now was nowhere near the pushover she'd once bullied freely.

Diane's Affinity was steady and powerful, far beyond her own, more than enough to crush her Level Three Water Affinity with ease.

Paisley watched coldly from the side, then raised a hand at just the right moment, calling up a floating holographic screen, projecting a series of clear surveillance clips into the air.

The footage showed, in detail, scenes from the supply depot over the past month.

Clip after clip played out clearly: Brenda cutting in line, shoving people around, slashing Diane with a water-blade, publicly hurling abuse, stealing household supplies, ironclad proof, impossible to deny.

"Abuse of power. Deliberate assault. Theft of allocated public resources."

Paisley's voice was cool and detached, carrying an unmistakable weight.

"Any one of these is enough to get you formally disciplined by the base, enough to strip you of your Affinity-holder privileges."

"And you, old man." She turned to Grandpa Thompson, his face already ashen. "You keep saying we're family, that we should be generous."

"My mother has forgiven her, again and again, and it's only gotten worse. There's a limit to favoritism. Enabling bad behavior isn't protecting family, it's aiding and abetting."

Grandpa Thompson looked at the damning footage, then at Diane, utterly transformed, radiating strength and authority, and found himself at a complete loss for words, every trace of his bias and bluster collapsing on the spot.

Brenda was completely unnerved now, fear flooding her eyes, her bravado collapsing entirely, her last shred of confidence shattered.

Diane stepped forward, her presence radiating outward, her Metal Affinity energy trembling faintly, pressure building with every step.

"Return everything you've stolen from me. All of it."

"Apologize, publicly, right now."

"And from this day forward, if you ever bully me again, I can strip your Affinity shield today. Tomorrow, I can end you completely."

Every word rang out firm and sharp.

The once-meek, timid second daughter-in-law had completely transformed.

Brenda was thoroughly shaken, her whole body rigid, watching the mother and daughter standing before her, radiating strength and command, she no longer dared talk back.

She clenched her jaw, her face pale, humiliation written across every feature, resentment festering deep beneath the surface, but crushed by overwhelming strength, she had no way to fight back, no shred of defiance left.

Today's public humiliation, she filed away silently in her heart, not daring to act on it now, though the seed of malice had already been planted.

The commotion from Paisley's public display of the surveillance footage had drawn no small amount of attention. Neighbors had gathered to watch, witnessing every bit of the bullying footage clearly.

The moment the truth came out in full, the crowd of onlookers erupted, a wave of murmurs rippling through, every word landing squarely in Brenda's ears.

"I can't believe it. Brenda's always put on such airs, acting high and mighty, looking down on everyone, turns out she's been nothing but a bully behind closed doors!"

"No wonder she's been living so well lately, never short on rice or vegetables, always looking well-fed, turns out she's been stealing from her sister-in-law's household the whole time!"

"Serves her right! Thought she could throw her weight around just because she awakened Level Three Water Affinity, bullying an honest, decent person, now she's finally hit a wall!"

"She and her daughter used to look down their noses at everyone, always putting on airs, acting superior, karma's finally caught up with them!"

"You're telling me! She used to bully the meek second-branch sister-in-law all the time, who could've guessed the second-branch sister-in-law would not only awaken an Affinity, but come out a full level ahead of her at peak Level Four Metal!

And don't forget, the biological daughter they just got back has insane strength, one of the base's top powerhouses! The second branch is about to take off!"

"And look at the senior branch now, their daughter's team disbanded, her fiancé's still unconscious."

"That old man's had this coming too, always playing favorites with the senior branch. Wonder if he regrets it now?"

"If he'd been fair from the start, the two branches wouldn't have ended up like this."

"Karma's real, isn't it? It's not that it doesn't come, it just takes its time. Bit late for regrets now, I'd say."

Each comment landed like a slap across Brenda's face, stripping away every last shred of her dignity.

She'd always valued appearances above everything else, loved being feared and admired, and now she'd become the whole neighborhood's laughingstock, mortified beyond words, wishing she could disappear into the ground.

Beside her, Grandpa Thompson's face had gone ashen, his knuckles white around his cane, every trace of his usual favoritism and bluster wiped away, replaced by nothing but shame.
