Chapter 191

When the two groups came face to face, the contrast was striking.

Rachel and the others all looked exhausted and drained. Their clothes were torn and filthy, and every one of them had fresh injuries, from bleeding scrapes on their arms to bruised, swollen shoulders. They looked like a total mess.

Each of them was holding a lump of stale, dark hardtack.

Paisley's group, on the other hand, was neat and clean, every one of them bright-eyed and full of energy. The difference couldn't have been clearer.

Paisley's expression didn't change at all. She walked right past Rachel and the others without a glance, and as they passed each other, she could even hear their stomachs growling.

They'd brought all of this on themselves.

After being ignored like that, Rachel's group stood frozen, their faces red with humiliation and frustration. But they'd learned their lesson, and none of them dared to walk up and say a word.

Still, they couldn't hide the resentment, humiliation, and hatred in their eyes.

"Paisley, they look like a total mess," Ashley gloated.

Spencer chimed in, his voice cold. "They had it coming."

Last night, he and Thomas had snuck in while they were asleep and taught them a lesson.

"Trash like them should just disappear from this world for good," Spencer added.

"Exactly," Thomas agreed. "If you ask me, they're not suffering enough, and it's only going to get worse for them. We should find a way to get them kicked out of the base."

Mason shared some news he'd heard that day.

"I heard they've split with Dominic Harrington.

"Actually, it was Dominic who dumped them. He thought they were too weak and too much trouble, so he just cut them loose."

"So when we leave, we won't have to take them with us at all? It should've been this way from the start. That's what they get for picking on Paisley," Ashley said with a laugh.

"We won't even have to lift a finger. As weak as they are, they'll die out there on their own."

Paisley's expression stayed calm and indifferent.

Revenge is only satisfying when I get it myself. I'm not some weakling anymore.

But everyone seemed to have forgotten that. Still, the way they stood up for her no matter what warmed her heart.

As they talked, the group arrived at the base hospital.

In the ward, Orion, Elijah, and the other soldiers were all awake.

After a night of rest, plus the healing from Paisley's Affinity, all five of them had been purged of the toxin and were back to normal.

The five of them were Rivergate's top core fighters, about as strong as anyone the base had.

More importantly, Orion and Elijah came from the top elite families in Aetheris. They were well-known, well-connected heirs in those circles, and their status carried enormous weight.

Elijah in particular had come to Rivergate Base under orders on an important mission. Their safety mattered to the whole base and even the higher-ups, and it was tied to humanity's future.

Word that all five had woken up fully healed reached the head of the base right away.

He'd been on edge all night, and the relief hit him hard. Without wasting a moment, he gathered the senior staff and medical team and went to the ward himself.

They moved quickly but respectfully, quieting their steps the moment they reached the door.

The head of the base walked into the ward first. When he saw the five of them looking healthy, steady, and fully recovered, the tension and worry finally left his face.

His eyes settled on Orion and Elijah, and he spoke to them with great respect and humility.

"Mr. Blackwood, Mr. Thompson, I heard you've all woken up and recovered. How are you feeling? Is anything still bothering you?"

He knew perfectly well that these two were VIPs from Aetheris's inner circle, with status that put them far above him. If anything had happened to them at Rivergate Base on his watch, a small-time base leader like him could never have handled the fallout.

The weight on his chest finally lifted now that he'd seen them whole, and he let out a long breath.

Elijah gave a small nod, calm and courteous.

"We're fine now. Thank you for worrying about us."

Orion gave a slight nod too, his voice cool and steady. "Thanks for your concern. We've fully recovered."

The two of them sat up straight in their hospital beds, cool and refined. Even fresh off a near-fatal injury, they still carried themselves like heirs of Aetheris's top families, steady and commanding, which made the officials in the room even more respectful.
