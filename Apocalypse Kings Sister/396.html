Chapter 396

With a top-tier powerhouse like her backing things up, no matter how envious or curious the crowd got, no one dared to push too hard or pry. They could only swallow their envy and step back in quiet deference.

Among the crowd, no one felt more bitter than Brenda.

She kept her head down, forcing back the resentment and malice in her eyes, quietly consoling herself.

So what if she'd lost a bit of face and had to return some supplies.

Sooner or later, she'd get Chloe to even the score.

As far as this incident went, it seemed over.

Paisley and her mother had already turned and left. The crowd would murmur a bit more and then disperse, and before long, today's embarrassment would fade from memory.

She was still the senior branch's matriarch, a respectable Affinity-holding lady.

This whole mess, she figured, ended right here.

But she never imagined this was only the beginning.

The moment mother and daughter's figures disappeared around the corner, a crisp, synchronized sound of footsteps suddenly approached from a distance.

Several base Security Patrol officers, uniformed and grim-faced, pushed straight through the crowd and marched into the villa's courtyard, their manner firm, leaving no room for negotiation.

"Brenda Thompson, you're suspected of abusing your Affinity to bully others, assault, misappropriate public rationed supplies, and disrupt base order. Come with us for investigation."

With that, the officers produced the full surveillance footage as evidence, clear, unmistakable recordings of her repeated bullying, theft, and assault.

Brenda froze completely, her mind going blank, utterly disbelieving.

She'd assumed this was nothing more than a spat between sisters-in-law, a petty family dispute, at worst, a bit of embarrassment. She'd never imagined it would draw the attention of the Security Patrol, let alone that she'd be arrested in public.

She'd thrown her weight around at this base for ages, relying on her daughter, her own Affinity, her connections, always acting without restraint. Even when she occasionally bullied someone or took more than her share, people might grumble, but no one had ever actually reported her to the Security Patrol.

Now, suddenly facing arrest by law enforcement right at her own door, overwhelming panic swept through her.

Before she could even protest, several officers had already moved in, grabbing hold of her without the slightest regard for her dignity, ready to take her into custody.

Brenda panicked completely, thrashing and resisting, her eyes full of humiliation and fury.

She'd lived a pampered, entitled life her whole way through, and ever since awakening her Affinity, she'd grown even more used to being looked up to, fawned over, when had she ever endured this kind of public, dignity-stripping arrest?

In a panic, she immediately fell back on her biggest leverage, shouting furiously.

"Let go of me! Don't you dare touch me!"

"My daughter is Chloe Thompson! She has a rare Healing Affinity, she's the squad's darling!

She's Grant Quinn's girlfriend, a core member of the flagship squad!

You're arresting me? I'm calling my daughter, you cannot take me away!"

"You'll regret this more than you can handle."

She was certain that between her daughter's status, the rarity of her Healing Affinity, and Grant's standing and connections, the Security Patrol would surely back down out of courtesy, maybe even apologize to her.

In the past, just invoking her daughter's name and Grant's had been enough to make the whole base think twice before crossing her, every single time, getting her out of trouble without fail.

Under normal circumstances, hearing the names of Grant and the flagship squad, they probably would have hesitated, given her some leeway.

But this particular case had been handed down by Mr. Quinn himself.

If they angered him, they'd have far more to answer for.

Grant and Jasper Quinn weren't even remotely in the same league.

Jasper's standing at Aetheris Base carried far more weight than Grant's ever would.

Too bad for her, she'd happened to cross the one person she really shouldn't have: the base's resident playboy, Jasper Quinn.

No one knew that Jasper, who'd run into Paisley at the base gates just earlier that day, was sharp enough to see an opportunity.

Eager to win Paisley's favor, to find a way onto her good side, he'd rushed straight to the Security Office himself and personally insisted they handle this matter immediately.

He'd made the call in person, applying pressure with absolute resolve, making it clear he wouldn't tolerate this kind of bullying, this disruption of base order, under any circumstances.

Every officer in the Security Patrol understood immediately: Mr. Quinn was dead set on getting justice for Paisley.

Anyone who dared show favoritism or let Brenda off easy would be crossing Jasper Quinn, and by extension, the terrifyingly powerful Paisley.

Weighing the two sides, there was nothing to hesitate over.

Forget being merely Grant's girlfriend, even if she'd been his wife, it wouldn't have made a difference today!

The officer stopped wasting words, firmly gripping her arm, his voice flat.
