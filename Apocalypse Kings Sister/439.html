Chapter 439

"Guys, sorry to bother you. We've been scavenging on the outskirts for a while now, eating cold hardtack every day. No fat, nothing. Our mouths taste like cardboard, and we're desperate."

"Could we... trade crystal cores for some of your food?"

The other four nodded hard, eyes eager and hopeful.

After days of rough rations, they'd lost their appetites. Now, smelling real hot food, they couldn't hold back.

Paisley glanced at them calmly.

With a thought, five boxed lunches appeared on the table, steaming hot, meat and vegetables, the smell rich and tempting.

"Thirty Level Three crystal cores each," she said flatly.

For a field team, the price wasn't cheap. But next to a hot meal you couldn't get anywhere and the comfort of a full stomach, it was worth it.

The five of them lit up and grinned.

"Deal! No problem! Thank you so much!"

They didn't hesitate. They pulled the cores from their storage pouches, counted them out, and handed them over in a neat stack, afraid she'd change her mind.

With the trade done, the five men carried their warm boxes carefully. The heat against their fingers filled them with gratitude. They thanked the Falcon Squad again and again before heading happily to the next room to rest.

******

After the stew, everyone sat around the table to work out how to take down the serpent mutant.

Orion spoke first, his face serious, laying it out clearly.

"Based on what we've seen, and what the Southspire team told us, we can be sure this thing has been living in the Ridgemont ruins for a long time, hunting survivors. It's cunning and patient. It's as smart as a person. It avoids a head-on fight and strikes when we're off guard. When it can't win, it hides or runs."

"The worst part is the healing. As long as the core is intact, it recovers from any wound in an instant. Sieges, brute force, wearing it down, none of it works. We'd just waste our strength and our Affinity."

Thomas dropped his usual grin and added, serious now. "And it goes after the weak. It finds the gaps. This afternoon, if it hadn't gone straight for Captain Blackwood, Pais's sword would have killed it on the spot. It never would've gotten away."

Thinking of that thirty-three-foot black tail, the venom, and the mental contamination, Ashley still felt a chill. She asked in a small voice, "It hides in the dark. We'll never find its den, and we don't even know how many there are. Searching for it is too dangerous. We could walk right into an ambush."

Elijah tapped his knee, his eyes deep, working through the key points of the fight.

"Pais hurt it badly. Its body can heal fast, but a high-level beast can't recover its lost strength that quickly. It's still somewhere in the ruins, nursing itself. It won't run far, and it won't show itself again easily."

"It's smart and patient, so we'll flip it around and set a trap. It hunts humans. It's bloodthirsty, and it holds grudges. We'll use that. Set bait and force it out."

Everyone looked at him and waited.

His gaze swept the group. "It wants me most. It remembers me, and it'll want revenge. I can be the bait. I'll go into the outer area, show myself, and act like I'm alone to draw it out."

"It's arrogant and spiteful. It won't pass up the chance to kill me. The moment it shows, you close in, seal off the battlefield, and block every escape. No giving it a chance to run and hide."

The plan was bold and dangerous, and it hit the serpent's weak spot exactly.

Spencer frowned and objected right away. "No way! Too risky! That's a Level Seven with venom and mental contamination. Last time Captain nearly died going toe to toe with it. If the net slips even a little, we'll never get to you in time!"

Everyone chimed in, worried.

Paisley listened in silence for a moment, her cool eyes flickering, then gave her verdict.

"The plan works, but it means putting Captain at risk. I'll do everything I can to protect him."

"The moment it shows up, I'll trap it first and seal off its attack routes."
