Chapter 73

No wonder Jasper, who was usually so cocky and pushy, groveled like a whipped dog around Elijah.

Once Rachel knew who Elijah really was, she'd started scheming right away.

Her plan had been to seduce Elijah and land the richest, most powerful man around as her meal ticket.

And before she could even make her move, the very man she'd been dying to sink her claws into had slapped her across the face in front of everyone!

Pain and humiliation twisted together, and jealousy chewed away at her heart.

Why? How did Paisley get so damn lucky?

In this life, Paisley had found her real family, and on top of that, she'd landed someone as powerful as Elijah. He was protecting her out in the open, like nothing else in the world mattered.

What gave a useless nobody like Paisley the right to any of this?

Rachel refused to accept it.

Meanwhile, Paisley stood there with her head bowed, crying. Out of the corner of her eye, she took in Rachel's twisted, hateful face and the bright red handprint on her cheek. She was in such a good mood that she almost burst out laughing.

Serves her right! She brought this on herself.

Just then, Owen heard the commotion and came storming outside.

The second he saw Rachel slumped on the ground with a red handprint on her cheek, looking pathetic and hurt, he got furious. Without even thinking, he did what he always did and blamed Paisley for everything.

His eyes narrowed, and his voice was cutting.

"Paisley, why can't you ever change? You're picking on Rachel again!

"How can you be so damn cruel? You go after Rachel over every little thing. What the hell is your problem?"

At the sudden accusation, Paisley's eyes welled up even more and turned redder. She kept her head down and spoke in a soft, hurt voice, choking back a sob.

"I didn't... It wasn't me.

"I was just standing here. Is that a crime now?"

Seeing her so fragile and helpless made Elijah's chest ache even worse.

Before Owen could say another word, Elijah raised his hand and slapped him hard across the face, too.

The blow was so brutal that half of Owen's face swelled up red on the spot, and he staggered back several steps.

Elijah's voice dripped with contempt. "Who the hell do you think you are, talking to her like that?"

Owen had been spoiled his whole life, and nobody had ever humiliated him like this.

On top of that, he had no clue who Elijah was. Getting slapped for no reason made his blood boil.

He glared at Elijah with hostility and defiance, and he balled up his fists, ready to hit back.

Just as he was about to lunge, a sharp gunshot cracked through the air!

The bullet grazed Owen's arm, sending blood spraying, and a searing pain shot through him.

Jasper stepped out of the shadows with a gun in his hand. His eyes were cold and flat as he warned Owen.

"If you want to die, fine, but don't fucking drag me down with you.

"Do you even know who he is? And you were going to swing at him? You're way out of your league."

Owen's arm was bleeding and throbbing. He was scared and furious at the same time, but he froze where he was.

He'd never imagined some stranger could be this intimidating, scary enough that even Jasper didn't dare cross him. All Owen could do was grit his teeth, fight through the pain, and swallow the humiliation, seething with nowhere to vent it.

With Owen taken care of, Jasper's hostility vanished. He hurried over to Elijah, suddenly all humble and respectful, with a big, fake smile plastered on his face.

"Mr. Blackwood, don't waste your time on a guy like him. He's not worth it.

"Are you all heading back to Aetheris? Mind if we tag along?"

Elijah didn't even look up. His voice was cold, firm, and left no room for argument, and he made it clear whose side he was on.

"Same rule as always. If my sister's okay with it, I don't have a problem.

"But if she's not happy about it, you can get the hell out of here."

The message was crystal clear. If they crossed her, did they really think they'd still get to come along?

Not a chance. They could get lost and stay lost.

After everything that had happened, Jasper knew perfectly well that the "sister" Elijah was talking about was Paisley.

And now, these two idiots had gone and pissed her off again.

Why the hell do these morons keep messing with Her Royal Highness?

Even Jasper didn't dare cross her.
