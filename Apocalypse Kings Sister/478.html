He knew her freeze was powerful, but fire melts ice. Melt the bridge, and crossing wouldn't be easy for them.

At the order, more than twenty Affinity users on the far bank flared with power. Wind, Earth, Metal, and Water Affinity all charged up, killing intent thick, ready.

Blazing fire power locked onto the ice bridge. Hot waves rolled across the river and slammed into the freezing cold.

Over the river, freezing mist and blazing fire stared each other down. Ice and fire clashed. Killing intent filled the sky, and a fight was about to break out.

The Fire users released their power at the same moment, and the flames roared!

Several thick red pillars of fire tore through the air, hot waves rolling, and slammed into the thousand-yard ice bridge.

Fire and scorching wind hit the near end. The ice there hissed, white steam billowing. The surface melted fast, dripping, and small chips of ice boiled away in the heat.

Grant stood in front of his people and watched the steam rise, his face grim.

Ice and fire, that's how it works.

I don't believe ice made by a person can hold out against so many high-tier Fire users.

Once the bridge breaks, Paisley and her people are stuck in the middle with no way forward or back.

A sick smile finally crept over Chloe's tense face. She stared at the ice and waited to see Paisley and the others fall and fail.

But the next second, at the start of the bridge, Paisley raised her eyes. They were cool and calm.

In the face of the flames and the heat wave, she pushed forward first.

"Don't be afraid, everyone! Keep your balance and skate full speed!"

Before she finished, her mid Level Seven Ice-Water Affinity surged out again, with nothing held back!

Hum!

Freezing power raced down the ice under her feet and out along the whole bridge. The ice that had been melting thickened, hardened, and solidified before their eyes.

You burn an inch, I freeze three feet!

The sea of fire fell onto the ice. It couldn't break through. It was crushed, frozen, and wiped out by the freeze.

Hot flame crashed against freezing mist, and clouds of white steam burst out. It was dramatic, but it couldn't touch the bridge.

Paisley kicked off lightly. Her skates touched the ice, and she shot forward like a cold streak of light.

The ice was perfectly smooth. She glided across with straight, easy posture, so fast it was terrifying. Wind roared past her ears.

She burst out through the ring of fire.

"Follow me!"

Elijah gave a low shout, his eyes locked on her figure leading the charge. Tenderness and fighting spirit mixed in his gaze as he pushed off behind her.

Orion, Thomas, Ashley, and Spencer followed. Everyone raced over the ice in a line, fast, fierce, and full of spirit.

They charged through the flames across the thousand-yard bridge in swirling white steam, nothing stopping them.

Grant's pupils shrank. He stared, unable to believe it.

He had never seen water control like this.

She'd frozen a whole river in a flash, and she could keep re-freezing it, nonstop. With one Affinity she was holding off the combined fire of several high-tier Fire users and breaking every rule about ice and fire.

If they weren't enemies, he'd have applauded.

But they were enemies. One of them had to die.

I don't believe her power can last forever.

"Keep burning! Don't stop! Everything you've got!"

Grant gritted his teeth and screamed.

Every Fire user pushed harder and poured out their power. Thicker, wilder waves of fire roared at the bridge.

Red flames filled the sky and nearly covered the river, scorching the air, but the ice bridge stood solid and didn't move.

Paisley's eyes were icy as she glided. She flicked a finger, and her power poured out!
