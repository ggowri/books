Chapter 412

"She's gorgeous! I thought she'd be some tough, badass fighter, but she's cold and soft at the same time. She's beautiful!"

"Oh my God, Blackwood and Paisley walking side by side. This is unreal."

"One is the strongest fighter on the base, cold and noble and untouchable. The other came out of nowhere with ridiculous talent, looks, and power. They look perfect together!"

"They're made for each other. Looks, presence, power, a perfect match."

......

Praise rose from every side, and countless eyes turned toward them, dazzled, admiring, and hungry for gossip.

Suddenly surrounded and talked about, Paisley felt awkward for the first time in a long while.

The tips of her ears went faintly pink. She wanted to disappear.

Of course. Everyone loves gossip, no matter the time.

She wanted to explain, to say they were only acquaintances, but one look at the crowd's eager faces and she didn't know where to start. She gave up.

She glanced sideways at Elijah to see how he was taking it.

He stood tall as a pine, his face cold and sharp, his eyes deep. Even surrounded, he was calm and untouched.

No wonder he was the center of attention wherever he went. No one could match that face and that presence.

She wondered whether he could hear the chatter around them.

If she had known the base was this obsessed with gossip, she would have worn a mask and a hat before leaving the house.

Elijah, though, caught only one line out of all that noise. The one that said they were made for each other.

Those cold, calculating eyes, always so free of feeling, softened. A faint warmth spread through them, like ice melting into spring.

His sharp features eased, the chill around him vanished, and only tenderness remained.

He turned his head slightly and looked down at her flustered profile, his gaze soft and steady.

They are made for each other.

The noise of the world, the crowds and the lights and the bustle, all of it faded. Nothing could compare to the person beside him.

In the crowd, someone quietly pulled out a phone, snapped a clear photo of the two standing together, and posted it on the base's public forum.

The post blew up. The views skyrocketed, the comments flooded in, and residents rushed to see it.

"Caught Blackwood and Paisley out shopping! Even our cold idol has a human side. What a treat!"

"Paisley's aura is unreal. The coolest beauty on the base, and she's strong too. Who wouldn't love her?"

"I'm shipping them so hard. This vibe is perfect. I'm 100 percent sure they're a couple."

"Two of the strongest fighters, looks and power at the top. What a dream pairing."

"My idol Paisley is actually here? I have to go meet her right now."

......

The buzz kept growing. More and more people gathered in person, curious and admiring, drifting toward the two of them, and the crowd got closer and noisier.

Paisley sensed the shift and lost all interest in walking around.

She tugged Elijah's sleeve, sounding resigned. "Too many people. Let's hide."

Elijah nodded and let her lead him. They turned away from the crowd and walked quickly out of the busy street into a high-end shopping mall nearby, where the foot traffic was thinner.

The mall was clean and softly lit, lined with shops selling clothes, accessories, and supplies. It was one of the few places on the base to relax.

Away from the noise, they finally had peace and wandered slowly past the stores.

But just as they reached the door of an upscale women's boutique, a familiar figure appeared.

Jasper Quinn, in loose, flashy casual clothes, was inside with a nicely dressed date, helping her pick out new outfits.
