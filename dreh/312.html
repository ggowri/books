Chapter 313

Charles and Caroline were still walking toward the house when they suddenly slowed down at the same time. Something felt wrong. 

The moment they stepped through the gate, both of them froze.

Everyone was there.

Every single member of the Quill family stood inside the compound, gathered in front of the main building as if they had been waiting for something. But what shocked them the most was not the crowd.

It was Jeremy.

He was standing in the middle of the yard like he owned the place, his hands behind his back, his posture relaxed, his face full of arrogance. Around him, several of the family members were trembling, their heads lowered as if they didn’t even dare to look at him.

One of them was on his knees, wiping Jeremy’s shoes with his bare hands.

Charles’ eyes narrowed instantly.

Caroline gasped.

“What… what is this…?”

Then her gaze moved forward, and she saw her father.

Mr. Quill was on his knees.

His clothes were messy, and there was a clear bruise on the side of his face. His lips were slightly swollen, and there was a faint cut near his eyebrow. He looked humiliated, but he didn’t dare to raise his head.

Caroline’s eyes widened in shock.

“Dad!”

She ran forward without thinking, pushing past the others until she reached him. She dropped to her knees in front of him and grabbed his shoulders.

“Father! Father, are you okay?!” she demanded, her voice shaking with anger and fear.

Mr. Quill slowly lifted his head. His eyes met hers for a brief moment, and then he gave a small nod, trying to act like nothing was wrong.

“I… I’m fine,” he said quietly.

Caroline clenched her teeth. Her hands tightened on his arms.

“Why are you doing this?!” she shouted, turning toward Jeremy. “Why are you making him kneel?!”

Before Jeremy could answer, another voice yelled from the side.

“This is nothing compared to what your stupid boyfriend did to me!”

Charles turned his head.

The man he had beaten earlier stepped forward, his whole body covered in bandages. His arm was wrapped, his forehead was taped, and one of his eyes was half swollen shut. He looked miserable, He pointed at Charles with a shaking finger.

“You think you’re strong?!” he shouted. “You almost killed me! This is nothing compared to that!”

Charles didn’t even look at him for long.

His eyes went back to Jeremy.

Slowly, he started walking forward.

The moment Jeremy saw him, his face broke into a wide smile, as if he had just seen an old friend.

“Well, well… look who finally came,” Jeremy said, spreading his arms slightly. “Hey, pal. It's been a long time.”

Charles stopped a few steps away from him. His face was completely expressionless, cold like stone.

“I should have killed you that time,” Charles said calmly.

Jeremy laughed.

“Well… you didn’t. And I should thank you for that.”

He leaned slightly closer, lowering his voice but keeping the smile on his face.

“And thanks for the Kyoul Stone you helped us obtain,” he added. “Because of that, I’m now the most powerful man in the world.”

He threw his head back and laughed loudly.

Several of the family members trembled even more when they heard that.

Charles’ eyes became sharper. He now understand that the stone was only inside Jeremy.

“You think killing the cultivator leaders is the best you can do with the Kyoul Stone?” he asked coldly.

Jeremy tilted his head, amused.

“You see… you still don’t understand how power works in the cultivator world,” he said. “This is our moment. The world is changing, and we must seize it.”

He raised his chin proudly.

“We will become powerful… just like Godfather.”

Charles didn’t respond.

He just stared at him silently.

Jeremy smirked again, then suddenly asked,

“Oh, by the way… how is Lizzypie? Is she doing well?”

Charles didn’t answer.

Jeremy chuckled.

“I can’t wait to come and get her,” he said. “This time, there’s nothing you can do. And if you dare try to stop me… I will kill you.”

Still no response.

Jeremy’s smile grew darker.

“In fact, I heard you have more beauties around you now,” he continued. “I’m going to take all of them… and fuck them all”

Charles stepped forward.

“What about you shut up,” he said coldly, his eyes full of killing intent,

“and let us settle things now.”

Hearing those words, the entire compound went silent for a second. Everyone stared at Charles as if they had just heard the most ridiculous thing in the world.

Then suddenly, laughter exploded.

Jeremy men burst out laughing, some in disbelief, others in fear, as if they thought Charles had just signed his own death sentence.

Jeremy laughed the loudest.

He bent slightly forward, holding his stomach as he laughed, then wiped the corner of his eye as if tears almost came out.

“You?” he said, pointing at Charles. “You… are challenging me?”

Charles didn’t react. His face stayed cold, his eyes steady.

“Yes,” he said.

Jeremy straightened up slowly, the smile still on his face, but his eyes turned sharp.

“Maybe I should remind you,” he said, tapping his chest lightly, “I have the stone right now. The Kyoul Stone. The strongest power in the world.”

He spread his arms proudly.

“I am the most powerful man alive at this moment,” he continued. “And you… you think you can challenge me?”

Charles tilted his head slightly.

“Yes,” he said again. “Who knows… maybe it’s all bluffing.”

The laughter around them stopped.

Jeremy’s smile faded a little.

Charles continued calmly,

“Maybe the Kyoul Stone isn’t as great as you think. Maybe you’re just talking big.”

For a moment, the air became heavy.

Jeremy stared at him, then slowly nodded, a dangerous smile forming on his face.

“I always knew you were brave,” he said quietly. “But today… I will beat that bravery out of you.”

His eyes moved to Caroline.

“And I will humiliate you in front of your woman.”

Caroline clenched her fists, her face pale but angry.

Jeremy looked back at Charles and cracked his neck.

“Since you want a spar,” he said, stepping forward, “then I shall give you one.”

Charles let out a slow sigh and stepped forward as well, his expression calm, his gaze steady, as if he was walking into nothing more than an ordinary fight.
