Chapter 297

Collins’ expression tightened the moment Charles said that.

A thousand thoughts began racing through his mind at once.

Has he found out?

Impossible!!!.

There was no way Charles could have evidence.

Still, Collins felt his throat go dry and he gulped unconsciously.

“Stop joking with us,” he snapped, trying to sound confident.

The lead officer frowned impatiently.

“You have already wasted enough of our time,” he said to Charles. “If you truly have proof, now is the time to show it.”

Collins quickly added, trying to pressure the situation.

“You don’t even have to listen to him, officer. He’s just playing you.”

Charles ignored him.

He calmly took out his phone.

The entire hall fell silent again as everyone waited.

After all, the girls had what looked like perfect evidence already. People wondered what Charles could possibly show that would counter it.

Then Charles tapped the screen and played a video.

The footage appeared on the large display screen connected to the hall’s projector.

Inside the video, the scene was clear.

Charles was lying asleep on the couch.

Completely unconscious.

A few moments later the two girls quietly approached him.

They looked around nervously before sneaking closer. One of them slowly unbuttoned Charles’ shirt down to his chest while the other positioned the camera.

Then they leaned beside him and began taking pictures of themselves.

Charles never moved.

He was clearly asleep the entire time.

The room erupted with murmurs.

Seeing the truth unfold, Lizzypie let out a relieved sigh.

Sapphire crossed her arms calmly.

Gabriella smiled slightly.

“I knew it,” Gabriella said confidently. “He’s always careful. There’s no way he would fall into something like this.”

Natasha nodded with a small grin.

“I knew it too.”

Guests began whispering among themselves again.

“Wait… what is happening here?”

“Did the girls frame him?”

“And is Collins behind it?”

Tracy felt a huge wave of relief wash over her chest seeing that Charles was innocent.

But almost immediately another worry replaced it.

If Charles was innocent…Then Collins might be the one behind all of this.

The girls quickly tried to defend themselves.

“This proves nothing!” one of them argued loudly.

“We already finished earlier. He was resting and we just took pictures.”

Collins immediately jumped in to support them.

“Yes! Exactly,” he said quickly. “That makes perfect sense.”

He pointed at Charles.

“He planned everything ahead. He’s like some kind of psychopath.”

Several people nodded uncertainly.

“Hmm… that explanation could make sense.”

The officer rubbed his chin thoughtfully.

“I think everyone involved will need to come with us to the station,” he said finally. “This video alone doesn’t actually prove anything.”

Charles nodded calmly.

“Well,” he said, “that was just one piece of evidence.”

He raised his phone slightly.

“I have many.”

The room immediately fell silent again.

“Oh?”

The officer frowned.

“What else do you have?”

Charles swiped to the next video.

The moment it started playing, the entire hall gasped.

Because the footage clearly showed Collins meeting privately with the two girls.

The moment the video played, the hall exploded with gasps.

“Wow!”

“That’s Collins!”

“He’s meeting the girls!”

“For what?”

“Oh no… did he really frame someone?”

People leaned forward, staring at the screen in disbelief as the footage clearly showed Collins sitting with the two girls in a quiet corner of a lounge. They were talking closely, and at one point Collins even handed something across the table.

The whispers quickly turned into accusations.

“This is bad.”

“Collins set him up!”

“I can’t believe it.”

The two girls standing beside the officers suddenly went pale. One of them swallowed nervously as the police officer turned toward them.

“Is this true?” the officer asked sternly. “Did you meet him?”

The girls immediately shook their heads.

“We… we can’t even say if that video is real,” one of them said quickly. “Maybe it’s fake.”

The other added hurriedly, “And even if it’s real, maybe we just coincidentally met Mr. Collins somewhere. That doesn’t mean anything.”

Charles scoffed softly.

“How convenient,” he said. “You coincidentally met the same person who might benefit from framing me.”

The atmosphere in the hall shifted again.

People could now clearly feel the direction things were turning.

Across the room, Collins felt his stomach tighten.

Charles had caught him.

He quickly forced a defensive tone.

“Officer, don’t believe him,” Collins said sharply. “That video could easily be fake.”

He hesitated slightly.

“Or… or…”

Charles stepped closer.

“Or what?” he asked calmly. “Explain.”

Collins suddenly found himself unable to finish the sentence.

Because the truth was simple.

Charles had known from the beginning.

From the moment the girls appeared, Charles had already recognized them and understood their purpose. He knew Collins had sent them, and even earlier at the lounge he had already realized something was suspicious when the girls tried to approach him.

But instead of exposing them immediately, Charles chose to play along.

Even when the two girls were being beaten earlier by the big man in the lounge, Charles understood the entire act. He pretended not to know anything and allowed the situation to unfold.

He was waiting.

Waiting for Collins to walk straight into the trap.

And now…He had.

Right in front of everyone.
