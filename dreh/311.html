Chapter 312

Serena stood frozen after hearing the words. Her eyes stayed on Lizzypie, but it was clear she was no longer really seeing her. Slowly, she shook her head, then took a step back and sat down again, her breathing becoming uneven.

“No… no. This is impossible,” she muttered. “What the heck is that supposed to mean? Me and Charles are siblings?”

Lizzypie raised both hands slightly, trying to calm her.

“I said might,” she replied. “That’s why we want to do the DNA test. Nothing is confirmed yet.”

Serena pressed her fingers against her forehead as if trying to stop her head from spinning.

“This doesn’t make sense at all,” she said. “Charles being my brother? That would be messed up. So messed up. Oh God…”

She stood up again and started pacing around the room, her thoughts clearly in chaos.

Lizzypie sighed.

“Hey. Don’t conclude anything yet,” she said. “Let’s do the DNA test first, then we talk.”

Serena stopped walking and looked at her for a moment, then nodded quickly.

“Yes. Yes, we do the test now. Right now.”

She grabbed her bag without even thinking twice.

“We’re going to the hospital.”

Not long after, the two of them arrived at the hospital. Serena walked straight to the front desk without slowing down.

“We want to do a sibling DNA test,” she said immediately.

The doctor on duty looked at them, then nodded calmly.

“Alright. We can do the test, but the result will be ready tomorrow.”

Serena frowned.

“Tomorrow? Why can’t you fast-track it? I will pay more.”

The doctor shook his head.

“We can’t. We are currently busy with a government task, so all lab work is scheduled. You will have to wait until tomorrow.”

Serena let out a long breath, clearly frustrated.

This was the most reliable hospital she could use, so she knew there was no point arguing.

Lizzypie touched her arm lightly.

“It’s fine,” she said. “Tomorrow is okay. We’ll wait.”

Serena nodded slowly, then ran her hand through her hair in stress.

“Oh no… this is messed up,” she muttered. “This is really messed up.”

Lizzypie looked at her seriously.

“Calm down. Nothing is certain yet.”

Serena kept nodding to herself, as if trying to convince her own mind.

“It won’t be like that… it can’t be like that,” she whispered. “Please don’t let it be like that…”

At that moment, Lizzypie’s phone rang.

She took it out and saw the name on the screen.

Natasha.

She answered immediately.

“Hello?”

On the other side, Natasha’s voice came out fast and panicked.

“Lizzy, we need to meet right now. You have to see this.”

Lizzypie frowned.

“What happened?”

“Just come,” Natasha said. “You need to see it yourself.”

“…Alright. I’m on my way.”

She hung up and turned to Serena.

“I have to go,” she said. “Natasha needs me. I wil call you later, okay? Just try to relax.”

Serena nodded absent-mindedly, still lost in her thoughts.

“Yeah… okay…”

Lizzypie left the hospital and went straight to Natasha’s store.

When she arrived, Natasha was already waiting near the door. The moment she saw her, her face lit up with excitement.

“Wow. You came faster than I expected.”

Lizzypie looked at her carefully.

“You sounded anxious on the phone,” she said. “What did you want to show me?”

Natasha didn’t answer immediately. Instead, she quickly locked the door of the store, then pulled the curtain slightly and looked outside to make sure no one was watching.

Only after that did she walk behind the counter.

“Come here,” she said quietly.

She opened one of the drawers and took out a small cloth bundle. When she unfolded it, eight small stones appeared inside, each one faintly shining.

“I found these in my store,” Natasha said in a low voice. “I think this is the reason Raphael wanted to buy my store.”

Lizzypie frowned.

Natasha continued, her voice full of excitement.

“Last night, these stones started blinking and glowing. They were radiating energy. I swear, the whole drawer was shaking.”

She pushed them closer.

“Do you know what this is?”

Lizzypie picked one up and examined it carefully. The moment her fingers touched the surface, her expression changed.

She looked at Natasha seriously.

“…This is a Rip Stone”.

Natasha blinked in surprise.

“Rip Stone?” she repeated. “What is that?”

Lizzypie was still staring at the stone in her hand, turning it slowly under the light as if she could not believe what she was seeing.

“I swear… it is,” she said quietly. “This is really a Rip Stone.”

She looked back at the other stones on the table, then let out a short breath.

“Wow… this stone is extremely rare. The chance of getting one is almost nothing. Not even one percent. And you have eight of them in your store?”

Natasha nodded quickly.

“I didn’t even know they were there until last night.”

Lizzypie placed the stone back carefully.

“Raphael must have seen the energy one day,” she said. “He probably noticed the radiation coming from them and realized what they were. That’s why he wanted to buy your store so badly.”

Natasha leaned closer, curiosity written all over her face.

“So what are they for?”

Lizzypie folded her arms and thought for a moment before answering.

“It’s a very powerful stone,” she said. “But it’s consumable. Each stone can only be used once.”

Natasha frowned.

“Used for what?”

Lizzypie picked one up again and held it between her fingers.

“This stone can create an impenetrable shield,” she said slowly. “Once activated, nothing can break it. I mean nothing. No matter how strong the attack is, it won’t go through.”

Natasha’s eyes widened.

“Seriously?”

Lizzypie nodded.

“And that’s not all. It can also be used as an absolute attack. Once the power is released, the opponent cannot stop it. No matter how strong they are, the attack will hit.”

Natasha stared at the stones in shock.

“That sounds insane…”

Lizzypie continued,

“But each stone disappears after one use. If you use it for a shield, the shield will only last about three minutes. After that, the stone is gone. Same with the attack. One use, then it’s finished.”

Natasha slowly nodded, still amazed.

“Wow… that sounds really cool.”

Lizzypie looked down at the stones again, then muttered under her breath,

“…Charles.”

Natasha looked at her.

“What?”

Lizzypie picked up one of the stones and held it tightly.

“This could help him a lot.”
