Chapter 303

Charles and Serena both stared at the small recorder lying on the table. For a moment neither of them touched it. Then they slowly looked at each other.

Serena frowned.

“I didn’t open to this page before,” she said quietly. “I swear I never saw that thing. What is this about?”

Charles reached forward and picked up the recorder. He turned it in his hand, checking the sides, then pressed the only button on it.

A faint click sounded, and a voice came from the device.

Both of them froze the moment they heard it.

It was clearly Mr. Moretti’s voice.

They leaned closer without saying a word.

The recording started with a low static sound, then his voice spoke slowly.

“I know you are curious about something if you come across this recorder. First of all… don’t seek the truth about whatever you find in those documents. Never. Don’t dig into it. Don’t press further. Please…”

Charles and Serena exchanged a quick look, both confused and tense, but neither of them stopped the recording.

The voice continued.

“I’m sorry for not telling you about your father. I found out late… and I… I didn’t want to hurt your feelings. You were always my favorite, and I didn’t want to see you get involved in things you shouldn’t. I’m so sorry.”

Serena’s hand slowly covered her mouth, her eyes already turning red.

The recorder crackled again, then Mr. Moretti spoke one more time.

“If you really want to know who your father is… check my secret safe in my office wardrobe. The password is 76734. I didn’t want you to find out like this, but maybe you deserve to know the truth now. I’m sorry for everything… I love you, Serena.”

The recording ended with a soft click.

Silence filled the room.

Serena’s breathing became uneven. She swallowed hard, staring at the recorder like she couldn’t believe what she just heard.

Her fingers trembled slightly on the table.

“I… I can’t believe this,” she said, her voice shaking. “What is all this about? Why would he hide something like this… and what does he mean by not seeking the truth?”

She looked at Charles, her eyes full of confusion and fear.

“…And my father… What secret safe?”.

Charles let out a slow sigh after the recording stopped and leaned back on the chair. He rubbed his forehead for a moment before looking at Serena.

“I don’t have any idea either,” he said quietly. “But one thing is clear. Your father was involved in some kind of organization, and whatever it is, he didn’t want you digging into it.”

Serena was still staring at the recorder, her fingers trembling slightly.

“And about your real father,” Charles continued, “I think the truth is exactly where he said it is. In that safe in his office.”

Serena swallowed and nodded slowly.

“What the heck was my father involved in…” she whispered. “He lived with me all those years and I never noticed anything strange. Not even once.”

Charles stood up and picked up the document stack again.

“Thinking about it won’t help. We should check the safe first. That’s the only way we’ll know.”

Serena hesitated, then nodded again.

“…Okay. Let’s go.”

They left the house not long after and drove straight to the late Mr. Moretti’s office building, which was now officially under Serena’s control. The place was quiet when they arrived, the morning staff barely paying attention as Serena walked in with Charles beside her.

Once inside the office, Serena closed the door behind them and looked around slowly.

“This is his office… I’ve been here many times since he died,” she said. “But I never saw any safe.”

Charles glanced toward the wardrobe at the side of the room.

“He said wardrobe. Did you check behind it before?”

Serena shook her head.

“No… I never thought there would be anything there.”

They walked toward it together. Charles opened the wardrobe doors and moved the clothes aside, then pushed the back panel slightly.

Behind the panel was a small metal safe hidden inside the wall. It was placed in a way that nobody would notice unless they were looking for it.

“If we weren’t told, we would never find this,” Charles muttered.

He reached in, loosened the holder, and carefully carried the small safe out, placing it on the table.

Serena’s hands tightened nervously.

Her breathing became heavier as she stared at it.

“I… I’m scared,” she said honestly. “I don’t even know if I want to open this.”

Charles looked at her calmly.

“It’s your choice. We can stop right here. We can destroy the safe and forget everything. Or… you can open it and know the truth.”

Serena closed her eyes for a second, then took a deep breath.

“…No. I have to know,” she said. “I must check it.”

Charles nodded.

He turned the safe toward himself and slowly entered the password.

7… 6… 7… 3… 4…

Both of them held their breath.

A soft click sounded.

The lock opened.

Serena gulped loudly as Charles slowly pulled the door open.

They both leaned closer.

Inside, there was no money, no weapon, nothing dangerous. Just a bunch of old photos and a few folded papers.

Charles frowned and took the stack out, placing it on the table.

Serena moved closer, her hands slightly shaking as they started looking through them one by one.

The first few photos showed Mr. Moretti when he was much younger, standing together with several other men in suits. They looked serious, like they were part of something important.

Behind them, on the wall, was the same symbol they had seen in the documents.

Charles’ eyes narrowed.

“It’s the same logo…”

Serena nodded slowly, her heart beating faster.

They kept going through the pictures until one photo slipped out from the middle of the stack.

Something was covering part of it.

A small paper was placed over the face of one of the men in the photo, as if someone didn’t want the person to be seen immediately.

Serena frowned and picked it up carefully.

There was writing on the paper.

It said:

Serena’s biological father.

She gulped and gently opened it, as soon as she did. Charles frowned at first, gasped!. Then he checked the photo well.

Because, the man… inside… was none other than his own father!
