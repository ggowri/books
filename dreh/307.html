Chapter 308

Meanwhile, Charles arrived in Texas late in the evening. The moment the plane landed, he did not waste a second. He went straight out of the airport, took the fastest ride he could find, and headed directly toward the Quill family residence.

The Quill house stood far from the city, surrounded by a wide stretch of land filled with herbs, greenhouses, and tall fences. The place looked peaceful from the outside, but Charles could feel the tension even before the car stopped.

As soon as he stepped out, he saw someone already waiting at the front gate.

Caroline.

She was standing there as if she had been waiting for a long time, her hands tightly clasped together. The moment she saw him, her eyes widened with relief. She did not even try to act calm.

“Charles!”

She rushed toward him without hesitation and threw her arms around him.

The hug was sudden and tight, so tight that he could feel her body trembling. 

Charles froze for half a second, then slowly wrapped his arms around her back.

“It’s okay,” he said quietly. “Calm down. Everything will be fine.”

Caroline sniffled against his shoulder, trying to hold herself together. After a moment, she pulled back, wiping her eyes quickly as if she did not want anyone to see.

“Thank you… for coming as soon as I called,” she said.

Charles looked at her face for a moment.

Her features were still the same as before. Beautiful, sharp, confident. Just as attractive as the last time he saw her, but now there was fear hiding behind her eyes.

He scoffed lightly and looked away.

“It’s nothing,” he said.

Caroline let out a small breath, then nodded.

“Come in. My father is waiting for you.”

She turned and led him through the gate. Guards were standing around the yard, all alert, all tense. It was obvious the whole family was preparing for trouble.

They entered the main house, and as soon as Charles stepped inside, he saw that the entire Quill family was already gathered in the living hall.

Uncles, elders, guards, and several younger members were all there. The moment Charles walked in, every single one of them stood up.

Some bowed slightly. Some lowered their heads in respect.

Charles did not react much. He simply nodded once, then walked forward and gave a small respectful bow toward the man sitting at the center.

Mr. Quill.

Mr. Quill immediately stood up.

“I don’t know how to thank you enough,” he said. “Thank you so much for coming.”

Charles shook his head.

“It’s nothing.”

Mr. Quill sighed, then gestured for him to sit.

“I believe Caroline already told you what happened,” he said. “But let me explain more.”

Charles sat down slowly, his expression already serious.

“The Hierem family wanted to kill me,” Mr. Quill continued. “They have been killing many empire leaders one by one. I don’t know how they got their hands on something so powerful, but whatever it is, they are using it to wipe out anyone who stands in their way.”

His face tightened.

“And now… it is my turn.”

Caroline stepped forward slightly.

“They gave Father an option,” she said.

Charles looked at her.

“What option?”

Caroline clenched her hands.

“They said he must step down as the leader of the Quill family… and hand over all the herbs, antidotes, and cures we have.”

Charles’ eyes narrowed slightly.

Mr. Quill nodded slowly.

“You see… because of the herbs and cures we produce, many empire leaders respect the Quill family,” he said. “We supply medicine, antidotes, and rare ingredients. Because of that, we have influence in many territories.”

He looked straight at Charles.

“The Hierem family knows this. If they control the Quill family, they will gain power over another entire section of the empire.”

He paused, then his voice became firm.

“I cannot do that. This is my legacy. I built this family with my own hands. I cannot just give it away.”

He leaned forward slightly.

“So… we need your help.”

Mr. Quill looked at Charles with a serious expression, his hands resting on his knees as if he was trying to hold himself steady.

“Please,” he said slowly. “I want you to stop the Hierem family. Protect us.”

The whole hall became quiet after those words. Everyone’s eyes were on Charles, waiting for his answer.

Charles did not reply immediately. He just stared at them one by one, his face calm but his eyes sharp.

“You are asking for protection…” he finally said. “You just said they are powerful enough to kill empire leaders one after another. Why do you think I’m any different?”

The room fell silent again.

Mr. Quill took a deep breath before speaking.

“I heard about what happened during the Raphael incident,” he said. “You fought Raphael when he was known as the most powerful youth cultivator of his generation… and you won.”

Several people in the hall nodded quietly.

“People talk about you everywhere now,” Mr. Quill continued. “They say your strength is abnormal”

He looked straight into Charles’ eyes.

“You are my only hope. If anyone can stop the Hierem family… it’s you. Please.”

There was a short pause, then Mr. Quill added, “If you are able to stop them… I will give you a reward.”

Charles raised one eyebrow slightly.

“What reward?”

Mr. Quill did not hesitate.

“I will give you my daughter, Caroline, in marriage,” he said. “And not only that. Once you become part of the Quill family, you will automatically become one of the executives. You will receive one hundred million dollars every month, along with access to all our resources.”

Some of the people in the hall gasped quietly, but no one objected.

Caroline lowered her head slightly, her face turning red, but she did not speak.

Charles scoffed softly.

The offer sounded huge, but to him it meant nothing. Money, status, marriage… none of that mattered right now. In fact, the whole situation almost amused him.

Because no matter what, this was already his mission.

He had to stop the Hierem family anyway.

But he did not say that out loud.

Instead, he leaned back slightly and nodded once.

“Okay,” he said. “Deal.”

Mr. Quill’s eyes widened in surprise, then his face lit up with relief.

“You agree?!” he said quickly. “Oh, thank you… thank you so much!”

Caroline also looked at Charles, her eyes full of gratitude.

“Thank you, Charles… really.”

Charles just gave a small nod, but inside, he swallowed slowly.

Someone using the Kyoul Stone as energy…

He wondered how exactly he was supposed to fight that.
