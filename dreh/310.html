Chapter 311

The leader kept screaming on the floor, his voice breaking into hoarse cries.

“YAAAA—!! No! No! Fuck—my hand! My hand!!”

He rolled on his side, clutching his twisted arm, his face pale and covered in sweat. His broken leg trembled every time he tried to move, and the pain made him gasp like he couldn’t breathe.

Charles looked down at him without any emotion.

“If you and the others don’t get out of here right now,” he said coldly, “I will do something much worse.”

The words were quiet, but the threat in them made everyone in the bar go still.

The leader froze for a second, then forced himself to push up from the floor. His body shook as he stood, one arm hanging uselessly, his leg barely able to support his weight. He sniffled in pain, wiping blood from the corner of his mouth.

“I swear…” he said, breathing hard. “You… you will regret this.”

Charles didn’t reply. He just stared at him.

The leader clenched his teeth and pointed at him with his good hand.

“I will report this to Boss Jeremy,” he said. “And he will come here himself. When he does… consider yourself dead. I swear… consider yourself dead!”

He staggered backward, almost falling again, then turned and rushed toward the exit.

The other men quickly got up, some limping, some holding their ribs, some still coughing blood. None of them dared look at Charles as they followed their leader out of the bar.

The door slammed shut behind them.

For a moment, the entire place was silent.

Then whispers started.

“Did you see that…?”

“I can’t believe it…”

“There’s actually someone who can put them in their place…”

“Wow… I’m satisfied just watching that…”

People looked at Charles with admiration, shock, and fear mixed together. Some even smiled as if they had just watched something they had wanted to see for a long time.

But the bar owner didn’t look happy at all.

He hurried out from behind the counter, his face full of worry.

“Please… please leave now,” he said in a low voice. “You don’t understand. If the boss really comes back, it won’t be good. This place will be finished… we will all be finished.”

Charles looked at him for a moment, then nodded slightly. He understood what the man meant.

He turned to Caroline and nudged his head toward the door.

“Let’s go.”

Caroline nodded quickly, still a little shaken, and followed him outside.

They walked a few steps before Caroline spoke.

“With this… Jeremy will definitely come here today,” she said quietly.

Charles glanced at her.

“He’s the boss?”

Caroline nodded.

“I heard he’s the one carrying out the killings himself,” she said. “People say he enjoys it.”

Charles scoffed softly.

He remembered the last time he fought him, the way he fell like a child in front of him.

“Doesn’t matter,” he said. “He can come.”

Far away in the city, Lizzypie stood in front of the Moretti building, looking up at the tall glass walls. She crossed her arms and let out a small sigh.

She was here to get Serena’s hair for the DNA Charles had requested.

“…Let’s just finish this,” she muttered.

She walked inside.

When she entered the house, Serena froze the moment she saw her.

“Lizzypie?!”

She rushed forward in surprise.

“Lizzypie!”

“Serena,” Lizzypie said, smiling, and hugged her.

For a moment, Lizzypie thought about taking a strand of her hair right there, but Serena’s hair was already packed and tied tightly behind her head, making it difficult.

After they pulled away, Serena looked at her curiously.

“I hope nothing is wrong… I’m so surprised to see you here.”

Lizzypie shook her head.

“No. I just wanted to tell you something.”

Serena smiled and stepped aside.

“Come in.”

She motioned her inside, and for better privacy, they went straight to her room.

Serena turned to face Lizzypie after they entered the room and smiled lightly.

“Before we start, let me get you something to drink,” she said. 

Before Lizzypie could answer, Serena had already turned and walked out of the room.

The moment the door closed, Lizzypie let out a slow breath and looked around.

“This is my chance…”

Her eyes moved across the room carefully. The dressing table stood near the wall, filled with bottles, creams, and hair products. Serena was taking care of her hair . Long dark strands were everywhere, especially near the comb lying on the table.

Lizzypie’s eyes lit up.

“Perfect…”

She walked over quickly but quietly and picked up the comb. Several strands of hair were tangled between the teeth. Exactly what she needed.

She reached into her pocket with one hand, ready to pull the small packet Charles had given her.

Just as she started to pull the hair free—

The door suddenly opened.

“What flavor do you—”

Serena stopped mid-sentence.

Her eyes locked on Lizzypie.

Lizzypie froze with the comb in one hand and strands of hair in the other. For a second, neither of them moved.

“Oh… no,” Lizzypie muttered under her breath.

Serena’s expression slowly changed. Her smile disappeared, and her brows tightened as she stepped closer.

“…Is that my hair?” she asked quietly.

Lizzypie forced a small smile.

“Oh… yeah. Actually… I was just admiring it. It looks really tender… and long.”

Serena didn’t smile back.

She kept walking toward her, her eyes fixed on the hair in Lizzypie’s hand.

“Lizzypie,” she said, her voice low, “don’t lie to me.”

Lizzypie’s fingers tightened slightly.

“What are you doing with my hair?”

Lizzypie bit her lip. Charles had told her clearly to do it without Serena knowing.

She clicked her tongue softly and looked away.

“What do you think? Admiring it, of course.”

Serena stared at her for a long moment, then shook her head.

“You never visit me,” she said. “And today you come out of nowhere… just to admire my hair? Come on, Lizzy. I know Charles put you up to this.”

Lizzypie looked back at her. Serena stepped closer.

“What’s happening?” she asked quietly. “Please tell me.”

Lizzypie sighed heavily.

For a moment she said nothing, then she rubbed her forehead in frustration.

“…Fine. I wanted to take it for a DNA test.”

Serena frowned and slowly sat down on the edge of the bed.

“…DNA?” she repeated. “For what? to compare with who?”

Lizzypie hesitated for a second, then answered.

“With Charles.”

Serena blinked.

“…What?”

Lizzypie swallowed.

“For a sibling test,” she said.

The words hung in the air.

Serena’s face went blank.

“…Siblings?” she whispered. “Me… and Charles?”

Lizzypie nodded slowly.

“…Yeah.”

She took a breath before continuing.

“Actually… the man in the photo you found as your father…was also his father.”

Serena’s vision blurred.
