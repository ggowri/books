Tracy’s vision blurred as the officer’s words sank in. She stared at Collins in disbelief.

“So… all this time…” her voice trembled, “you were trying to dupe me?”

Her heart began beating faster.

If this was true… everything would have been over for her.

She had been preparing to sign her entire company to him as part of the so-called partnership deal.

If Collins had succeeded, he would have sold the company and disappeared. Her entire life would have been ruined.

Tracy felt a chill run through her body.

She looked at him desperately.

“Is… is that true?” she asked.

Collins gulped.

His face was pale and his lips trembled slightly.

“Wait… listen to me…” he stammered. “It’s… it’s not like that…”

The police officer cut him off immediately.

“Don’t even try to deny it,” he said sternly. “We already know everything.”

But no words came out.

Tracy’s entire body shook.

“You really did it?” she whispered.

Then suddenly she screamed.

“You did? You did! Why?” she cried. “Why would you do that?!”

“You were the one who invested in my company!” she shouted. “You were the one who helped me when everything was collapsing!”

Her voice broke.

“You turned my life around when I was about to lose everything. How could you do this to me?!”

“Get away from me, bitch!” he snapped.

Tracy stumbled backward in shock.

“What?” Tracy whispered.

“You were the one throwing yourself at me just because I’m the son of the Moretti family. I’m sure you’re lying about that money too,” Collins added dismissively.

“Hey… Collins,” she said weakly. “You saved me. Remember? 150 million…”

“You!” he shouted angrily. “You are so senseless.”

People looked at him in disbelief.

“That’s enough,” he said firmly.

He nodded to the other officers.

“Arrest him.”

Two policemen immediately grabbed Collins.

“You are under arrest for fraud, fabrication of evidence, and involving minors in criminal activity.”

They began dragging him toward the exit.
