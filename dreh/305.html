Chapter 306

Charles picked up the photo and turned toward the door. His jaw was still tight, his mind still burning with anger. He had taken only a few steps when Godfather’s voice came from behind him.

“Alright,” Mr. Vincent said calmly. “I will tell you everything… after this mission.”

Charles stopped in his tracks.

For a moment he didn’t move, then he slowly turned around.

“What did you just say?” he asked.

Godfather looked at him without any change in expression.

“I said I will tell you everything. About your father. About the organization. About all of it. After you finish this mission. I was planning to tell you anyway.”

Charles stared at him for a long second, then scoffed.

“I’m not letting myself get played again,” he said. “Forget it. I told you already. I’m not doing any mission for you anymore.”

Mr. Vincent crossed his legs slowly.

“This mission exists because of you,” he said.

Charles frowned.

“What’s that supposed to mean?”

Godfather looked straight at him.

“You remember the Hierem family.”

Charles hesitated, then nodded slowly.

“…Yeah. I remember.”

“You,” Godfather continued, “gave them the Kyoul Stone from the Hole Realm.”

Charles’ expression changed instantly.

The anger on his face faded, replaced by tension. He slowly walked back and sat down without saying a word.

Godfather continued speaking.

“They used the stone,” he said. “They gave its power to their heir. Jeremy.”

Charles’ eyes narrowed.

“And now,” Godfather said, “he has become the strongest man in the Cultivator world.”

Charles looked up quickly.

“You mean… stronger than you?”

Godfather nodded once.

“Yes. Stronger than me.”

The room became completely silent.

“The Kyoul Stone is not just powerful,” Godfather continued. “It multiplies the potential of whoever controls it. Jeremy didn’t just gain strength. He gained influence. Authority. Fear.”

Charles swallowed slowly.

“And now he’s using that power,” Godfather said, “to eliminate the other empire leaders. One by one. So he can annex the entire Cultivator world and rule it alone.”

Charles leaned forward slightly.

“He already killed three empire leaders,” Godfather added. “The others have started bowing to him out of fear. If this continues, my position as Godfather will become meaningless. They will have all the power.”

Charles looked down at the floor, his mind racing.

Godfather’s voice became colder.

“And this all started because you gave them the Kyoul Stone.”

Charles clenched his fist.

“I did it to save Loxzypie,” he said quietly.

Godfather nodded.

“And now the consequences have come back to you. Every step of this problem leads to you, Charles. All the way.”

Charles let out a long breath and leaned back in the chair, staring at the ceiling for a moment.

The anger was still there, but now it was mixed with something heavier.

Responsibility. After a few seconds, he looked back at Godfather.

“…So,” he said slowly, “what exactly do I have to do?”

Godfather leaned back in his chair and folded his hands.

“You just have to stop him,” he said calmly. “If necessary… kill him.”

Charles blinked in disbelief.

“But you just said he’s stronger than you now,” he replied. “If he’s even more powerful than you, then how the hell am I supposed to stop him?”

Godfather shrugged slightly.

“You’ll have to figure that out yourself.”

“Fuck…” Charles muttered under his breath. He rubbed his face and let out a long breath, trying to think.

After a moment he said, “Fine. I’ll try to meet him first. I’ll talk to him properly. Maybe there’s another way to settle this without fighting.”

Godfather gave a faint smirk.

“That won’t work,” he said. “But do whatever you want. Just make sure the problem disappears.”

Charles stood up slowly.

“You better keep your promise,” he said, pointing at him. “When this mission is done, you tell me everything. About my father. About the organization. About all of it.”

Godfather nodded once.

“Of course.”

Charles stared at him for a second longer, then turned and walked out of the room. The men outside bowed as he passed, but he didn’t even notice them this time.

His head was still spinning.

Nothing made sense anymore.

When he reached the lobby, he walked straight out of the hotel without looking around.

Outside, Serena was still nearby, standing beside her car where she could see the entrance without being obvious.

The moment she saw him come out, she straightened slightly.

“I wonder who he came to meet…” she muttered.

Charles walked past without noticing her at all. He raised his hand, flagged down a taxi, and got inside.

Serena watched the taxi drive away, her eyes full of suspicion.

Meanwhile, Charles reached the hotel where they were staying. When he entered the room, only Lizzypie was there. The others had already left for their work.

Lizzypie looked up the moment Charles walked in and immediately noticed his face.

“What is it this time?” he asked. “You look like the world just ended.”

Charles didn’t answer at first. He sat down heavily on the chair and stayed quiet for a few seconds, then finally spoke.

“You won’t believe what just happened,” he said.

Lizzypie frowned.

“Try me.”

Charles then told him everything. From the moment they found out that Moretti wasn’t Serena’s real father, to the photo in the safe, to the note saying who her real father was… and how that man turned out to be his own father and how he confronted Godfather

Lizzypie’s eyes widened.

“What the hell… You mean you and Serena might be siblings?”

Charles nodded slowly.

“That’s what it looks like… but I still can’t believe it. It sounds insane.”

“And you went to confront Mr. Vincent about it?” Lizzypie asked.

“Yeah,” Charles replied. “I went straight to him.”

“What did he say?”

Charles shook his head.

“He didn’t give me a straight answer. Just the usual. Talking around things like always.”

There was a moment of silence.

Then Lizzypie spoke seriously.

“We have to confirm it first. Before anything else. I think we should do a DNA test. You and Serena. That’s the only way to know if you’re really siblings.”

Charles froze for a second, then nodded quickly.

“Yeah… yeah, you’re right. I should have done that first instead of running to Godfather like an idiot. I was too emotional.”

Lizzypie crossed his arms.

“If you two are really siblings… then this is bad. Really bad.”

“I know,” Charles said quietly.
