Chapter 307

After a moment, Charles also told him about the mission Godfather gave him. How the hierem family is misusing The stone

Lizzypie suddenly stood up.

“Fuck… I caused all this,” he said. “If you didn’t help me back then, none of this would have happened.”

Charles shook his head.

“No. It’s not your fault. The Hierem family chose to be wicked. They’re the ones who started this. And we’ll put them in their place.”

Lizzypie nodded slowly.

“Yeah… we will.”

Just then, Charles’ phone suddenly rang.

Charles picked up the phone and looked at the screen. He frowned slightly when he saw the name, then pressed the answer button and raised it to his ear.

“Hello?”

A familiar female voice came from the other side.

“Hello… it’s me. Caroline.”

Charles’ eyes narrowed for a second as he tried to remember, then his expression changed.

“Caroline… from the Quill family?” he said.

“Yes,” she replied softly.

Charles immediately recalled her. The Quill family… that was where he obtained the Red Moon Herb during the last mission. He remembered the trouble that happened there, and how Caroline helped him at the time.

“Ohh… long time,” he said. “How are things?”

“I’m okay,” Caroline said. “I’m doing fine… how about you?”

“Fine. I’m fine,” Charles replied, though his voice sounded tired.

There was a short silence on the line, then Caroline spoke again, this time her voice shaking slightly.

“Please… I need your help.”

Charles’ expression changed immediately.

“What happened?”

“Please save my father,” she said quickly. “Someone is about to kill him.”

Charles sat up straight.

“Your father?” he asked. “Who would want to kill him?”

Caroline hesitated for a moment, then answered.

“Jeremy… from the Hierem family.”

Charles froze.

His eyes slowly widened, then narrowed again.

“The Hierem family…” he muttered.

Lizzypie looked at him, confused, but Charles raised his hand slightly to signal him to wait.

On the phone, Caroline continued.

“He came here with his men,” she said. “They said my father has to hand over everything… our territory, our resources, our herbs, everything. If he refuses, they will kill him.”

Charles clenched his jaw.

So Jeremy had already started moving that crazy. And the Quill family was the next target.

He remembered what Godfather said.

Jeremy was killing empire leaders one by one.

Charles spoke again, his voice now serious.

“Where are you right now?”

“At our main house,” Caroline replied quickly. “Please hurry. I don’t know how long we can hold them.”

Charles stood up from the chair.

“Alright,” he said. “Don’t panic. I’m on my way right now. I will be there.”

There was a small pause, then Caroline spoke again, her voice full of relief.

“…Thank you. And… sorry for disturbing you. I just… I couldn’t think of anyone else who could help.”

Charles shook his head even though she couldn’t see him.

“It’s okay,” he said. “You did the right thing by calling me.”

He glanced at Lizzypie, his face already turning serious again.

“I’m on my way now,” he added into the phone.

“Thanks,” Caroline said quietly.

With that, the call ended.

Charles lowered the phone slowly and stared at it for a second.

Lizzypie immediately stepped closer.

“What happened?” he asked.

Charles took a deep breath.

“The Hierem family already made their move,” he said. “They are attacking the Quill family. He is the next target”

Lizzypie’s eyes widened.

“That fast?!”

Charles nodded once.

“I have to go”.

Charles stood up immediately .

He grabbed his bag from the chair and started putting things inside without wasting time.

Lizzypie stood up too.

“I’m coming with you,” she said.

Charles shook his head at once.

“No. You shouldn’t.”

She frowned. “Why not? This is dangerous for you to do alone, Jeremy is now very powerful!”

Charles stopped packing for a moment, then reached to his head and pulled out a few strands of his hair. He held them out to her.

“I know, I know… I can do it, instead… I need you to do something for me,” he said.

She looked at the hair in his hand, then back at him.

“The DNA test,” he continued. “You can do it, right?”

Lizzypie slowly took the strands.

“…Yeah. I can.”

Charles nodded.

“Good. You can get Serena’s sample without her noticing, right?”

A small smile appeared on her face.

“That part will be easy,” she said. “Don’t worry about that.”

She then looked at him again, more serious this time.

“But are you sure you’ll be okay by yourself?”

Charles zipped his bag and picked it up.

“I’ll be fine,” he said. “I just need to go see what Jeremy is doing. If anything happens, I’ll call you. Just… please. Help me with the DNA first.”

Lizzypie nodded slowly.

“Okay,” she said. “Be careful.”

“You too.”

Charles slung the backpack over his shoulder and walked out of the room without looking back.

Not long after, he arrived at the airport. He bought the first ticket available and boarded to Texas.

During the flight, his mind kept moving from one problem to another.

Serena… the DNA test… Jeremy… the Kyoul Stone…

Nothing was simple anymore.

Meanwhile, Natasha was in her room, fast asleep.

She had fallen into a deep sleep after a long day, her phone lying beside her on the bed.

Suddenly, a strange sound woke her up.

A faint humming noise.

Her eyes opened slowly.

“…What…?”

She sat up, still half asleep, then froze.

A bright light was shining inside the room.

From the wardrobe.

The light flickered again, glowing stronger, then weaker, then stronger again. It made a soft shimmering sound that filled the room.

Natasha’s heart started beating faster.

“What the heck is that…?”

She slowly moved back on the bed, staring at the wardrobe. The light was so bright it reflected on the walls, making the whole room look strange.

She swallowed and grabbed her phone from the bed.

“Should I call Charles…?”

Her fingers trembled slightly as she held the phone. Just then, the light suddenly became weaker.

The humming sound slowed down.

Natasha stopped moving and stared.

After a few seconds, the light became softer, almost like it was waiting.

She stayed there for a moment, then slowly stood up.

Step by step, she walked toward the wardrobe. Her heart was pounding.

When she reached it, she hesitated, then slowly opened the door.

Inside, something was glowing.

Her eyes widened.

It was the small stone she had found in her store. The same stone Raphael once tried to buy her store for.

Now it was floating slightly above the shelf, shining with a slow, pulsing light.
