Chapter 310

Caroline’s face tightened the moment the man touched her. She leaned back slightly and tried to push his hand away, but he didn’t move. Instead, he smiled wider and started playing with a strand of her hair as if they were close friends.

“Stop…” she said under her breath, clearly uncomfortable. “Don’t touch me.”

The man ignored her and kept smiling.

Charles’ hand slowly stopped moving. The glass in his fingers froze halfway to the table. His eyes darkened, and the relaxed look on his face disappeared.

He turned his head slowly toward the man.

“You heard her,” Charles said coldly. “Leave her alone. Right now.”

The leader paused for a second, then burst into laughter.

“Hahaha! Look at this one!” he said loudly. “Trying to act like a man in front of me!”

Two of the other men behind him stepped forward, grinning as if they were waiting for a show.

“Do you even know who we are?” the leader asked.

Charles stood up slowly.

“Look,” he said, his voice low but sharp. “I don’t fucking care who you are. Now take your hand off… my woman.”

The words made the whole bar go silent.

The leader blinked once, then started laughing again.

“Your woman?” he repeated. “Your woman?!”

He turned to his men and laughed even louder.

“You hear this? He said his woman!”

Then he looked back at Charles, his smile turning cold.

“You? Who are you even supposed to be, acting like this in front of me?”

Charles stepped closer, his eyes locked on him.

“Someone who can make you a cripple,” he said, “just like you did to that man last week.”

The smile on the leader’s face disappeared instantly.

His eyes narrowed.

“…Are you challenging me right now?” he asked quietly.

Charles did not hesitate.

“Yeah,” he said. “I am.”

For a moment, the two of them just stared at each other. Then Charles' eyes glimpsed blue. 

Then the leader leaned closer and lowered his voice.

“You must be from the cultivator world… right?”

Charles said nothing. The leader smirked.

“I thought so,” he whispered. “We are from Lord Hierem’s family. I recently got power from the Kyoul Stone myself.”

He tapped his chest proudly.

“You think you can handle me with that?”

Charles shrugged slightly. Kyoul stone power. Maybe this was the time to check how powerful it was. 

“Well… who knows,” he said. “Just leave my woman. That’s all.”

The leader’s smile turned ugly.

“Your woman?…”

He suddenly reached behind Caroline and smacked her hard on the backside.

“What about this?” he said with a grin.

The moment the man slapped Caroline, something inside Charles snapped.

His eyes turned cold instantly.

Before the leader could even pull his hand back, Charles grabbed his wrist.

Fast.So fast the man didn’t even see it.

“What the—”

CRACK! Charles twisted his arm without hesitation.

He expected resistance. He expected the man to release some kind of energy, to block, to counter, to use the power of the Kyoul Stone.

Nothing came.

The arm bent the wrong way.

Bone twisted under the skin with a sickening sound.

“YAAAAA—!!”

The leader screamed and dropped to his knees, clutching his arm.

“My hand! My hand! What the fuck! My hand!!”

The entire bar froze.

The other men stared in shock for a second, not believing what just happened.

Then the leader shouted in pain,

“What are you waiting for?! Kill him!!”

The others rushed forward at once.

One of them threw a punch straight at Charles’ face.

Charles didn’t move until the last second.

Then he stepped sideways and drove his elbow straight into the man’s ribs.

CRACK!

The sound was loud enough to make people flinch. The man flew backward and smashed into a table, coughing blood.

Another one charged from behind.

Charles turned and kicked him in the knee.

SNAP!

The leg bent sideways.The man collapsed screaming, holding his shattered joint.

A third man tried to grab Charles from the side.

Charles caught his collar, pulled him forward, and slammed his face into the counter.

THUD!

The wood cracked. Blood splashed across the surface.

The man slid down, unconscious.

The whole bar was silent except for the sound of groaning.

Caroline stood frozen near the counter, her eyes wide. She had seen fights before, but nothing like this.

The leader was still on the floor, holding his twisted arm, staring in disbelief.

“…How… how are you this strong…”

He forced himself to stand, his arm hanging uselessly.

“You think…  this enough to scare me?”

He clenched his teeth and released his energy.

A faint aura burst around him, shaking the bottles behind the counter.

Charles watched him calmly.

The leader roared and rushed forward, even with his broken arm.

Charles didn’t move.

Not until the man was right in front of him.

Then—

BAM!

Charles punched him straight in the stomach.

The air exploded out of the man’s mouth.

His eyes rolled back, but Charles didn’t stop.

He grabbed his shoulder and slammed him down onto the floor.

THUD!

The ground shook. The man tried to get up, but Charles stepped on his chest and pressed him down.

“You like touching women?” Charles said coldly.

The leader tried to speak, but blood came out instead.

Charles grabbed his leg.

The man’s eyes widened.

“W–wait—”

CRACK!

Charles twisted.

The leg broke instantly.

The scream that came out of the man’s mouth filled the whole bar.

“AAAAAAHHHH!! MY LEG!!”

Charles let go and stepped back.

All the other men were already on the floor, broken, bleeding, or unconscious. The leader lay there shaking, one arm twisted, one leg broken, his face full of terror.

Charles looked down at him without emotion.

“You crippled someone last week,” he said.

He leaned slightly closer.

“Now you know how it feels.”
