Chapter 296

Collins laughed harshly and pointed at the women standing beside Charles.

“So now you are defending him?” he said with open contempt. “You are pedophile apologists. That’s what you are. Just as disgusting as him.”

Lizzypie stepped forward immediately, her eyes cold.

“Asking for a proper investigation makes us apologists?” she replied calmly. “Interesting logic. I’m actually more curious about why you seem so desperate to push this narrative before anyone even investigates.”

Collins scoffed loudly.

“We already have evidence,” he said, gesturing toward the girls’ phone. “What more do you need?”

Before the argument could escalate further, the entrance doors opened again.

Two police officers walked into the hall, followed by three more behind them.

The room quieted instantly.

The team leader, a tall officer with a serious expression, stepped forward and looked around the hall.

“We received a call from this location,” he said firmly. “Who reported the incident? What happened here?”

Collins immediately stepped forward like a man eager to deliver justice.

“Officer, thank goodness you’re here,” he said loudly. “This man is right here.”

He pointed dramatically at Charles.

“This dirty, stinky pedophile. He hooked up with underage girls. That’s already a crime. And after abusing them, he even refused to pay.”

A loud wave of murmurs spread across the room again.

The officer’s face darkened.

“What?” he said sharply.

Then he turned toward Charles.

“Is that true?”

Charles met his gaze calmly.

“It’s a lie,” he said evenly. “Straight from the pit of hell.”

The two girls immediately stepped forward.

“No, we are not lying!” one of them cried out. “Officer, he refused to pay us.”

The second girl nodded quickly.

“Yes! Just collect our money from him.”

Then she raised the phone again.

“We even have proof.”

The officer took the phone and studied the picture carefully.

For a few seconds the room remained silent as he examined it.

Finally he sighed and turned back to Charles.

“Sir,” he said more gently now, “you are under arrest for suspected involvement with minors and exploitation.”

He reached for his handcuffs.

Collins immediately burst into a satisfied grin.

“You see?” he said loudly. “You thought the police would side with you after committing such a disgusting act?”

Several people in the crowd nodded angrily.

“Just arrest him already.”

“I can’t stand looking at him.”

The officer stepped forward to cuff Charles, his tone becoming firmer as he grabbed Charles’ wrist.

But Charles calmly raised his other hand.

“Wait a moment.”

Collins frowned immediately.

“What the hell do you still have to say?” he snapped. “Officer, arrest him already.”

Charles ignored him and turned his attention to the two girls.

His voice remained calm but serious.

“Look,” he said quietly, “you still have time to tell everyone who sent you to frame me.”

The girls froze for a split second.

“Or else,” Charles continued, “things will become much worse from here.”

One of the girls laughed nervously.

“What are you even saying?” she replied. “We have no idea what you’re talking about. You’re the one who duped us. No one framed you.”

Charles nodded slowly.

“Oh… someone did,” he said. “And I know it.”

Across the room, Collins felt his stomach tighten slightly.

Why is he saying that?

“What nonsense is he talking about?” Collins said impatiently. “Officer, why are you even letting him talk? Just take him away already.”

The girls glanced briefly at each other.

Then one of them forced a smile.

“Mr. Man,” she said nervously, “we really don’t know what you are talking about.”

Collins immediately stepped forward again, his voice sharp with irritation.

“Officer, you’re disappointing everyone here by letting this man keep talking,” he said loudly. “Why are we even entertaining this nonsense? Just arrest him already.”

Charles turned his eyes toward Collins calmly.

“You don’t need to be this nervous,” he said quietly. “Because I know the one behind all this… is you.”

Collins stiffened instantly.

Then he burst out in disbelief.

“What the hell!” he shouted. “You are accusing me of what you did? They even have proof!”

The crowd began whispering again.

“What is he saying now?”

“He’s just trying to shift the blame.”

“This guy keeps getting worse.”

Collins noticed people were still skeptical of Charles, and his confidence returned. He smirked and folded his arms.

“I know you’re jealous of me,” he said coldly. “You are just trying to drag someone else down with you. Your words hold no value.”

Tracy frowned slightly and looked at Charles.

“Do you have any evidence for what you’re saying?” she asked.

Collins laughed immediately.

“He doesn’t,” he said mockingly. “Because he’s lying.”

Charles smiled calmly.

“Who told you I don’t?”
