Chapter 304

Charles was shocked and just gulped as he stared at the picture in Serena’s hand. His eyes stayed fixed on the man in the photo and his mind went completely blank for a moment. He wasn’t wrong. He knew that face too well. The man in the picture was his father. There was no doubt about it. The same sharp jaw, the same cold eyes, the same expression he had seen his whole life.

But how was that possible?

The note clearly said that this man was Serena’s biological father.

Charles felt a sudden heat run through his body and he started sweating without even noticing it. His fingers tightened slightly on the edge of the table as he tried to keep his face normal. His heart was beating so fast he was afraid Serena might hear it.

Meanwhile, Serena didn’t notice his reaction at first. She kept staring at the photo, slowly touching the corner of it with her fingers as if the man could somehow feel it.

“…This is my father?” she said quietly. “I wonder who he really is… and where he is now.”

Charles quickly reached forward and took the picture from her hand, pretending to look at it more carefully, but in reality he just wanted to make sure he wasn’t seeing things wrong.

He checked the face again.

It was still the same.

His father.

That was when Serena noticed how quiet he had become.

She looked at him closely.

“Charles… do you know him?” she asked. “Or have you seen him somewhere before?”

Charles froze for half a second, then shook his head a little too fast.

“No… no, I don’t,” he said quickly. “I don’t think I’ve ever seen him before.”

Serena kept looking at him for a moment longer. Something about his voice felt strange, but she couldn’t tell exactly what it was. His face looked normal, yet there was a tension in his eyes that she had never seen before.

She narrowed her eyes slightly, studying him, but then she looked back at the picture again.

“I really want to find out who this man is,” she said. “I’ll investigate it myself. There must be records somewhere.”

Charles reacted immediately.

“No,” he said, a little too fast, then forced his voice to calm down. “No… don’t worry about it. I will do it. I’ll look into it myself. It might be connected to those documents, so it’s better if I handle it.”

Serena looked at him again, suspicious this time, but after a second she slowly nodded.

“…Alright. If you say so.”

They continued checking the rest of the photos and papers in the safe, but Charles barely paid attention anymore. His mind was somewhere else.

When Serena wasn’t looking, he quietly slipped a few of the pictures into his folder and closed it as if nothing happened.

Not long after that, they left the office and drove back together.

During the drive, Serena talked a little about the photos and the strange organization symbol, but Charles hardly replied. He just kept staring through the windshield, his thoughts spinning faster and faster.

If Serena’s father was his own father…

Then that meant…They were brother and sister.

Charles swallowed hard and gripped the steering wheel tighter.

Oh no…This is messed up.

He gulped.

Serena looked at him again as they drove, her eyes narrowing slightly. She could no longer ignore the strange tension on his face.

“You know you are acting strange now,” she said. “Can you please tell me what’s going on?”

Charles forced a smile and turned to her.

“It’s nothing,” he said. “Just something stuck in my mind.… that’s all.”

Serena didn’t look convinced, but she didn’t push further.

A few seconds later, Charles spoke again.

“Can you pull over for a moment?”

Serena frowned but slowed the car and stopped at the side of the road.

Charles unfastened his seatbelt.

“I need to go somewhere,” he said.

Serena turned toward him, confused. “Now? What happened?”

He opened the door, then paused and looked back at her.

“I’m sorry for the abrupt stop. I’ll be fine. I just need to check something. I will call you if necessary.”

Serena studied his face carefully, trying to read what he was hiding.

“…Will you be okay?” she asked.

Charles nodded.

“I will.”

He stepped out and closed the door.

Serena forced a small smile as she watched him walk away, but the moment he turned the corner, her expression changed.

Something was wrong.

Very wrong.

She waited a few seconds, then quietly started the car again.

“…You are not getting away that easily, i must know what is going on with you” she muttered.

Keeping a distance, she slowly followed him.

Meanwhile, Charles raised his hand and flagged down a taxi.

“Elite Hotel,” he said as he got inside.

The ride felt longer than usual. His head felt like it was vibrating, his thoughts crashing into each other.

Godfather was the one who pushed him to get engaged to Serena.

And now…She might be his sister.

His real sister. Blood.

His jaw tightened.

Did Godfather know this all along?

The anger inside him kept rising until his hands started shaking.

“I need answers,” he muttered.

The taxi stopped in front of the Elite Hotel. Charles threw some money at the driver and stepped out without waiting.

He walked straight inside.

“I’m going to the 1000th floor,” he said to the receptionist.

The woman looked at him calmly.

“…Sir, we don’t have that floor here.”

Charles leaned closer, his eyes cold.

“Eyes of Satan,” he said quietly. “Mr. Vincent.”

The receptionist’s expression changed instantly.She picked up the phone and called directly.

“Yes… someone named Charles is requesting access to the 1000th floor,” she muttered.

There was a pause.

Then she nodded slowly.

“…Understood.”

She hung up and looked at him again.

“You may go.”

She pressed the elevator button, then entered a code and pressed the number ten ten times.

The elevator doors opened.

Charles stepped inside without saying another word. When the doors opened again, he was on the hidden floor.

The hallway was silent.

Several men standing there immediately bowed as he walked past, but Charles didn’t even look at them.

He went straight to the large door at the end and pushed it open.

Godfather was inside.

Charles walked forward, took the photo from his folder, and slammed it on the table in front of him.

“Explain this!” he shouted.

His voice echoed in the room.

“How the hell does Serena’s biological father become mine?!”
