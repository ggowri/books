Chapter 300

The ladies walked out of the hall with Charles, the cold night air outside feeling much calmer after the chaos inside. None of them spoke at first. The sound of their heels on the pavement echoed softly as they moved toward the parking area.

Lixxypie finally let out a long breath and shook her head.

“I still can’t believe Collins would go that far,” she said. “Involving minors? That’s beyond disgusting.”

Sapphire crossed her arms, her expression still tense.

“Good thing you noticed in time,” she said to Charles. “If that went on any longer, this could have landed you in prison with no way out. Those charges were serious.”

Serena looked at him quietly for a moment before speaking.

“Please… just be more careful next time,” she said softly.

Charles smiled faintly and nodded.

“Sure,” he said. “I will.”

Natasha suddenly clicked her tongue in annoyance.

“I’m just angry about one thing,” she said. “Why did you even help her? After everything she did to you? She humiliated you, compared you to Collins all the time, treated you like trash… and you still stepped in to save her. I wouldn’t have done that.”

The others looked at Charles, clearly waiting for his answer.

He was quiet for a second before speaking.

“Actually… I didn’t want to,” he said calmly. “Not at first.”

They frowned slightly.

“But she stood up for me,” he continued. “When the accusation started earlier, she didn’t stay silent. She spoke for me. She didn’t have to… but she did.”

He shrugged lightly.

“She deserved some help for that.”

The girls looked at each other, then slowly nodded.

“…Fair enough,” Sapphire said.

Serena gave a small smile.

“That sounds like you.”

Just then, Charles’ phone vibrated in his pocket. He took it out casually, but the moment he saw the name on the screen, his expression changed slightly.

Tracy.

He frowned.

He opened the message.

[Have you left? Please come back. Let’s talk.]

Charles stared at the screen for a second without replying.

He was about to put the phone away when it vibrated again.

Another message.

He opened it.

[If you don’t come back… I might kill myself]

Charles shut his eyes for a moment and exhaled sharply.

“She always does this…” he muttered under his breath. Calling him whenever she was down  

The girls noticed his expression.

“What happened?” Serena asked.

Charles hesitated for a moment, then told them he had to go back and meet Tracy.

Natasha’s face darkened immediately.

“You’re not going back,” she said firmly. “Don’t even think about it.”

Sapphire nodded.

“She’s manipulating you again.”

Lixxypie frowned.

“She only calls you when she’s in trouble.”

Charles rubbed his forehead.

“I know,” he said quietly. “I know all of that.”

He looked at the message again.

“…But I can’t just ignore this,” he added. “If she really does something stupid, I will blame myself for it.”

Natasha scoffed.

Serena finally spoke, her voice calm but slightly cold.

“If you’re going, then go,” she said. “Just don’t stay long.”

Charles nodded.

“I won’t.”

He looked at Serena specifically.

“We will meet tomorrow morning,” he said.

She nodded once.“Alright.”

Charles turned as if to leave, but suddenly stopped.

“…Gabriella,” he called.

She blinked in surprise and walked closer.

“Yes?”

Charles looked around briefly to make sure the others were a little farther away, then lowered his voice.

“Can I ask you something…?”

Gabriella looked confused but nodded.

“Sure… what is it?”

Charles hesitated for a second before speaking.

“You were… a virgin, right? When we had sex.”

Gabriella’s eyes widened slightly, then she nodded quickly.

“Y-Yeah. Of course. Why are you asking that ?”

Charles frowned.

“That’s strange…”

“What is?” she asked.

He crossed his arms, thinking.

“Normally, I should have developed a new power by now,” he said quietly. “Raphael said you have a special ability… and whoever takes your virginity should gain something very powerful from it.”

Gabriella’s expression changed with surprise.

“You haven’t seen it yet?” she asked.

He shook his head.

“No.”

He exhaled slowly.

“…But it’s fine. Maybe it just needs time.”

Gabriella nodded slowly but she felt somewhat disappointed ,  She smiled lightly.

“Okay.”

Gabriella turned and ran back to join the others, and they all left together.

Charles pushed the door open and stepped back into the hall.

The place was almost empty now. The bright lights were still on.

A few glasses and half-empty bottles were scattered across the tables, and at the center of the hall Tracy was sitting alone, surrounded by alcohol. Several bottles were already open in front of her, and her face was red from drinking. Her movements were slow, unsteady, and when she heard the door, she turned her head with difficulty.

Her eyes struggled to focus, then suddenly widened when she recognized him.

She raised her glass toward him and laughed weakly.
“Well… look who came back… here comes my knight on a dark shining horse.”

Charles stopped a few steps away and let out a tired sigh as he looked at the mess on the table.

“You’re drunk,” he said calmly.

Tracy ignored the tone and reached for the bottle again, trying to pour another full cup, but before she could lift it, Charles stepped forward and took the glass from her hand.

“What the hell are you doing?” he asked, his voice low but firm.

Tracy stared at her empty hand for a moment, then looked up at him with blurry eyes and gave a crooked smile.
“What do you think I’m doing? I’m drinking myself to death,” she said, her voice trembling before turning bitter. “After tonight… after everything that almost happened… I just want to die.”

Charles frowned and put the glass down on the table.
“You called me here for this?”
