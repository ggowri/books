Chapter 309

After the agreement was made, the tense atmosphere inside the hall finally eased a little. Mr. Quill immediately ordered the servants to prepare a proper welcome for Charles, treating him like an honored guest instead of just a helper.

Charles was led upstairs to one of the best rooms in the mansion. The room was large, clean, and filled with expensive furniture. 

Caroline was the one guiding him around.

“This will be your room,” she said. “If you need anything, just tell the servants… or tell me.”

Charles looked around once, then dropped his bag near the bed.

“You people really know how to welcome someone,” he said.

Caroline smiled a little. “You deserve it. After all… you came to save us.”

They stayed silent for a moment after the servants left the room. The atmosphere suddenly felt more personal now that they were alone.

Charles leaned against the table and crossed his arms, then looked at her.

“What your father said earlier… about your hand in marriage,” he said calmly. “I don’t want that. So you don’t need to worry about it.”

Caroline blinked in surprise.

“…Why don’t you want it?” she asked.

Charles did not answer immediately.

She stepped a little closer and looked at him carefully.

“Do you already have a woman in your life…?”

Her voice was casual, but it was obvious what she meant. She really want Charles.

Charles stared at her for a second.

He didn’t have a woman. He had women.

But he did not say that.

Instead, he just shrugged slightly.

Caroline realized she might have asked too much and quickly looked away.

“Sorry,” she said. “I shouldn’t ask that.”

Then she forced a small smile.

“…Why don’t we go out for a bit? You just arrived. Staying inside the house with everyone looking nervous is not very fun.”

Charles looked at her for a moment, then smiled faintly.

“Why not.”

A few minutes later, the two of them left the mansion and headed toward the nearest town. The place was not very big, but it had a few bars and restaurants that were still open.

They entered one of the bars and sat near the counter.

Caroline let out a long breath after sitting down.

“I’m really grateful you came,” she said. “You have no idea how much this means to us.”

Charles waved his hand lightly.

“Let’s just drink first,” he said. “We can talk later.”

She laughed a little.

“Alright. Drink first.”

They ordered a few bottles and started drinking slowly, talking about small things, trying to forget the problem for a moment. The atmosphere became lighter, and for a while, it almost felt like nothing bad was happening.

They were still enjoying the moment when suddenly—

THUD!!!

The door of the bar burst open so hard that everyone inside turned at once.

Several men rushed in.

They were big, rough, and full of killing aura. Their clothes were messy, but their presence alone made the whole bar go silent.

Caroline’s face changed instantly.

“Fuck…” she muttered under her breath.

Everyone inside lowered their heads or moved away, as if they already knew who these people were.

Charles frowned slightly and looked at them.

“Who are they?” he asked.

Caroline clenched her jaw.

“…Some Hierem men,” she said quietly. “They’ve been terrorizing this town since they arrived.”

Her eyes stayed on them.

“They act like they own the place.”

Charles frowned slightly as he watched the men walk in.

“Hierem men?” he said quietly. “Then why haven’t the police arrested them yet?”

Caroline let out a dry laugh, but there was no humor in it.

“I told you already,” she said. “Lord Hierem has gained popularity everywhere. Even people who don’t know anything about the cultivator world respect him now.”

Charles glanced at her but did not speak.

“They already annexed several big companies,” she continued. “He has influence in the normal world too. Police, officials, businessmen… many of them are on his side.”

She lowered her voice.

“And those men are strong. A few days ago, one of them crippled someone here just for talking back. Nobody dared to report it.”

Charles scoffed softly and took another sip from his glass.

Just then, one of the men in front stretched his neck and looked around the bar.

“Mmm… so everyone is enjoying themselves without us?” the man said loudly.

He laughed, and the others laughed with him.

They started moving through the bar, pushing chairs aside, kicking tables, slapping some customers on the shoulder as if they were joking, but no one laughed with them.

People lowered their heads and stayed silent.

The leader slowly walked toward the counter.

Caroline’s face turned stiff the moment she recognized him. She stopped moving completely.

Charles, however, remained seated, calmly drinking as if nothing was happening.

The leader noticed that immediately.

His eyes narrowed when he saw Charles sitting there without fear.

He walked closer.

“Uhn… who is this man sitting with our lady?” he said with a smirk.

Charles did not answer. He just finished his drink and placed the glass on the counter.

The man’s smile widened.

“Ohh… my lady, Miss Caroline,” he said. “You didn’t tell me you are already engaged.”

Caroline swallowed but did not speak.

“What’s your fiancé’s name?” the man continued. “Who is he?”

Caroline’s lips parted slightly, but no words came out.

Charles calmly poured another drink.

The man’s expression darkened a little, then he suddenly reached out and touched Caroline’s face with the back of his fingers.

“Have you told him already?” he said with a grin.

His voice lowered.

“That I already confessed my love to you.”
