Chapter 295

.

The girls stood their ground.

One of them pointed at Charles again.

“Don’t you dare deny it,” she snapped. “Don’t you!”

Collins immediately seized the moment.

A smug grin spread across his face as he folded his arms.

“Charles,” he said mockingly, “I knew one day you would go too far.”

He shook his head in fake disappointment.

“You are a huge womanizer. A man who would sleep with anything that moves. You simply can’t control yourself.”

He glanced at the girls with disgust.

“Girls, for God’s sake… I feel like vomiting.”

Whispers began spreading through the hall like wildfire.

“What kind of man is this?”

“He hires girls and refuses to pay them?”

“That’s disgusting.”

Someone muttered harshly, “That’s borderline predatory behavior.”

Another voice whispered, “This is shameful.”

The murmurs grew louder as people started judging him openly.

But Charles remained calm.

His expression didn’t change.

He simply looked at the two girls.

Then he spoke quietly.

“Who sent you to do this? Because this is a lie”

The question made the girls hesitate for a split second.

But they quickly recovered.

“You see?” one of them said loudly. “I knew you would say that. I knew you would deny it.”

She lifted her chin confidently.

“But we have proof.”

Charles nodded slightly.

“Well,” he said calmly, “show us.”

The girl smirked.

“Gladly.”

She pulled out her phone, tapped the screen a few times, and turned it toward the crowd.

The image appeared clearly.

It was a picture of Charles.

He was lying shirtless on a couch, clearly asleep. And the two girls were curled up beside him.

The moment the picture appeared on the screen, the hall exploded with noise.

“That’s the proof!”

“The big proof!”

“He really did it!”

“Wow… this is unbelievable.”

People leaned forward, staring at the image again and again. The photo clearly showed Charles shirtless on the couch with the two girls lying close beside him.

The whispers turned ugly.

“So he really picked them up.”

“And refused to pay them?”

“What kind of man is this?”

The ladies beside Charles were stunned.

Sapphire and Lizzypie exchanged quick glances.

They all knew Charles. There was no way he had done this. Someone had clearly set him up. But the evidence was powerful, and the crowd was already turning against him.

Tracy stepped forward slowly.

Her expression had turned cold.

“Charles…” she said.

She pointed at the phone.

“Is this true?”. Her voice carried disbelief and anger.

“How could you be like this? Do you understand this is a huge criminal act?”

The girls crossed their arms confidently.

“Anyway,” one of them said, “just give us our balance.”

Before Charles could respond, Collins stepped forward with a loud laugh.

“Give them their balance?” he mocked.

He looked at Charles from head to toe with disdain. “You can’t give them anything because you are broke. You don’t even have a dime.”

He shook his head dramatically.

“And now it’s even worse. You messed with young girls and refused to pay them.”

The murmurs turned darker.

“That’s sick.”

“What kind of man does that?”

Then Collins suddenly shouted.

“What are we all doing just standing here?”

His voice echoed across the hall.

“Someone should call the police and arrest this pedophile!”

A few guests immediately pulled out their phones.

One man nodded.

“I already called.”

The tension in the hall thickened instantly.

Charles finally turned to face the two girls again.

His voice remained calm.

“You should tell the truth now,” he said quietly. “If the police arrive and you continue lying, there will be consequences.”

The girls didn’t back down.

One of them scoffed.

“And you keep gaslighting us.”

She pointed at him angrily.

“You are the one who should be afraid. You’re the one going to jail.”

Collins stepped closer to Charles with a cold smile.

“In fact,” he said loudly, “we should beat him before the police even arrive.”

A few people in the crowd murmured in agreement. But Charles only looked at him calmly.

“I’m curious,” he said.

“You are taking this very personally.”

He folded his arms.

“Let the police arrive. And let them do their job.”

Collins shook his head with exaggerated disgust as he looked at Charles standing there so calmly, his expression barely changing despite the chaos growing around him.

“Look at him,” Collins said loudly to the crowd. “See how calm he is. Not even a little remorseful for what he did.”

A few people nodded, their anger building.

“I know you might try to twist things when the police arrive,” Collins continued with a smug smile, raising his voice so everyone could hear him clearly. “But we won’t let that happen. Everyone here has seen the evidence.”

Immediately several guests began shouting.

“Yes!”

“He must be locked up!”

“This kind of man shouldn’t be walking free!”

Amid the noise, Tracy slowly walked toward Charles. Her face showed worry more than anger.

“Please tell me you didn’t do that,” she said quietly. “I know you. You can’t be that kind of person.”

Charles met her eyes calmly.

“I didn’t.”

Tracy nodded once. “Then I believe you.”

Collins scoffed loudly. “Don’t tell me you ladies are still siding with this pedophile.”

Sapphire crossed her arms. “We’re not concluding anything until the police arrive.”
