Chapter 305

Meanwhile, outside the building, Serena’s car slowly stopped in front of the Elite Hotel only a few seconds after Charles had gone in. She leaned forward slightly, watching through the windshield so she wouldn’t be noticed.

She saw him walk straight to the receptionist, say something, and then he was allowed to go to the elevator without any delay.

Her eyes narrowed.

“That was fast…,” she muttered.

She waited until the elevator doors closed, then stepped out of the car and walked inside as if she had every right to be there.

The receptionist looked up as she approached.

Serena gave a polite smile.

“The man that just left… where did he go?” she asked.

The receptionist’s expression immediately became serious.

“I’m sorry, ma’am. We can’t disclose our clients’ privacy.”

Serena kept smiling, trying to sound casual.

“Actually, we were supposed to arrive together, but we got separated on the way. It’s kind of important. Can you just tell me which floor he went to?”

The receptionist shook her head.

“I’m sorry. I can’t.”

Serena’s smile faded slightly.

On a normal day, that lie would have worked easily. She was used to people believing her without question.

But this time, the woman didn’t even hesitate.

Her face looked too strict.

Too careful.

Serena felt a small chill.

Something was definitely wrong.

She glanced toward the elevator, then back at the receptionist again, but the woman had already lowered her eyes to the desk as if the conversation was over.

Serena clenched her jaw slightly.

“…Fine,” she said quietly, then turned away.

As she walked toward the exit, her mind kept racing.

What was Charles doing here?

×××××

Meanwhile, on the hidden floor, inside the wide and luxurious room, Mr. Vincent sat calmly on a large chair, slowly sipping his coffee as if nothing in the world could disturb him. Rain stood beside him in silence, his hands behind his back.

The door burst open as Charles walked in.

He didn’t greet anyone.

He walked straight to the table and slammed the photo down in front of Mr. Vincent.

“Explain this!” he shouted.

His voice shook with anger.

“Tell me I’m wrong… Godfather. Tell me this isn’t what I think it is. Is she really my sister?!”

Mr. Vincent slowly raised his eyes and looked at the photo, then at Charles.

His face showed no surprise.

“I didn’t send you on that mission to ask questions,” he said calmly. “Just do the mission I gave you.”

Charles let out a sharp scoff.

“Newsflash,” he said angrily. “I’m not doing anything again, you hear me? Because of your damn mission, I might have slept with my own sister!”

Rain’s eyes moved slightly, but he said nothing.

Mr. Vincent took another sip of coffee.

“I told you to get engaged,” he said. “I didn’t tell you to sleep with her.”

Charles laughed bitterly.

“Oh really? That’s your excuse?”

Mr. Vincent placed the cup down slowly.

“Why are you rushing here like this?” he asked. “You made your own decisions.”

Charles stared at him in disbelief.

“My decision?” he said. “You must be kidding me.”

He pointed at the photo on the table.

“So you knew all along… didn’t you?”

His voice dropped, but the anger inside it became even heavier.

“You know Serena is my sister.”

Godfather looked at the photo for a moment, then a slow smirk appeared on his face.

“I didn’t see any reason to tell you,” he said calmly.

Charles froze for a second, then suddenly exploded.

“Fuck…! Fuck!” he shouted, pacing in front of the table. His hands ran through his hair as if he was trying to pull the thoughts out of his head. His breathing became heavy, almost shaking.

He turned back sharply.

“You knew!” he said. “You knew and you still sent me to her!”

Mr. Vincent didn’t move. He simply leaned back in his chair.

“This doesn’t change anything,” he said. “Just continue the mission.”

That sentence made Charles snap completely.

His eyes widened in disbelief, then filled with anger.

“Continue?” he repeated. “Continue?!”

He hit the table with his palm.

“Listen to me carefully,” he said, his voice trembling with rage. “Fuck all your wealth. I don’t need it anymore. You can take everything you ever gave me. Every house, every car, every damn favor. I’m done.”

Rain shifted slightly at the side, surprised, but stayed silent.

Mr. Vincent’s eyes narrowed a little, though his face still looked calm.

“You really mean that?” he asked.

“Fuck yeah, I mean that!” Charles shouted. “All this time I thought you cared about me. But you don’t. You’ve just been using me like a tool. You send me on missions, you tell me what to do, and you don’t even care if my life gets ruined.”

His voice cracked with anger.

“I just made the biggest taboo of my life, and you’re sitting there drinking coffee like nothing happened!”

Mr. Vincent’s fingers stopped moving on the cup.

Charles leaned closer, his eyes burning.

“I’m not doing any mission again. Not one. Not for you, not for anyone. You hear me?”

Silence filled the room for a moment.

Then Charles spoke again, slower this time, but more serious.

“And now… you’re going to tell me the truth. About my father. About that organization. About everything.”

Mr. Vincent let out a short scoff and looked away.

“And you really think I will tell you?” he said.

Charles stared at him for a few seconds, then gave a cold laugh.

“…Fine.”

He grabbed the photo from the table.

“Then I will find out for myself.”
