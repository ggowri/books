Chapter 55 Power Unleashed

Logan stared in shock as the woman in tactical gear lifted one corner of the armored vehicle. She planted her feet and pushed hard, and with a deafening crunch of metal, she actually pulled the whole thing up.

Holy shit! She really just lifted it!

Right then, the Level-2 zombie burst out of the smoke again, its entire body still engulfed in flames. It let out a guttural, inhuman roar.

Iris finished lifting one side of the vehicle and jumped to the other. She needed more time.

Logan went full throttle to cover her, and flames poured from the cannon straight onto the Level-2 zombie. But this time something unexpected happened. The zombie wasn't afraid of the fire anymore.

The flames only slowed it down temporarily, and it still charged at them at full speed. In the blink of an eye, it reached Iris' position.

Iris had just grabbed the tire on the other side when she saw the flaming zombie charging straight at her. She stood right at the edge of the overpass and didn't dodge. Instead, she spread her arms wide like a matador, welcoming its attack.

Logan watched from inside the vehicle, completely stunned. He knew exactly what Iris was doing. She was trying to lure it off the overpass.

This might work, but she was putting herself in massive danger at the same time. Logan's scalp prickled with fear. One wrong move and she'd fall with it.

Why was she putting herself at such risk? She could've let him handle the dangerous part. Hell, she could've just abandoned this whole thing and run. But instead, she was putting herself in the most dangerous position possible to give them all a chance to survive.

"Don't!" Logan threw open the window.

The moment he did, thick smoke and a wave of scorching heat slammed into his face. A massive fireball blazing with intense heat surged directly in front of him.

The zombie roared again, a bone-chilling sound that echoed across the overpass.

Logan's eyes narrowed sharply. The fireball shot past him, raising the temperature to over 120 degrees and incinerating everything in its path. When the flames cleared, there was nothing left. Just empty space.

His heart dropped into his stomach. They'd only known each other for such a short time. Everyone said she was cold and ruthless. But she would risk her life to protect her people.

Just when panic threatened to overwhelm him completely, a slender figure leaped up from below the overpass. Logan saw her, and relief flooded through him so intensely his eyes stung.

At that moment, it felt like his heartbeat came back to life.

Behind Iris, another massive ball of flames appeared, and the zombie's roar echoed once more.

A massive torrent of water exploded from Logan's palm, completely out of his control. The force shot straight at the level-two zombie's head and blasted right through its skull.

When Iris landed, the Level-two mutant creature that had been chasing her got slammed back down with a thunderous crash. This time it actually stayed down for good.

Iris turned around and watched the zombie fall completely, water still hanging in the air before it scattered into rain that pattered down around them. She stared at Logan in amazement as he controlled the water ability he'd just awakened.

Not bad at all! His power had only awakened recently, and he could already unleash this much force with his water manipulation.

Iris had been planning to finish the creature off herself once she got up here, but Logan had already handled it.

"Hell yeah, nice work!" She walked over and clapped him hard on the shoulder.

Logan felt the force of her hand and looked at Iris, safe and sound beside him. A faint smile crept across his normally cold expression.

He got out of the truck and helped Iris lift their vehicle back up, though honestly it was mostly Iris doing the heavy lifting. His strength really couldn't match hers at all.

After fighting through this battle together, they'd basically risked their lives for each other. Trust was something that deepened through experiences like this, one encounter at a time.

Iris had always trusted Logan, but since this was technically a fresh start, they needed to rebuild that bond from scratch. After today though, Logan clearly felt closer to her than before.

"Iris, I honestly don't know what's going on with this."

"I noticed about 10 days ago that I could sense water, just a tiny bit of it. I've mostly been using it to provide drinking water for the special ops team. I have no idea how I just did that back there."

Logan told her everything about what had been happening to him, explaining every single detail of the changes he'd been experiencing.

"That's your superpower."

Iris drove the truck down from the overpass with the tanker still attached behind them.

"A superpower." Logan repeated the word, his brow furrowing slightly.
