Chapter 106 Chaotic

They reached the entrance.

Beep.

Derek honked at the main gate.

The heavily armed guards lifted the barrier the second they saw his vehicle.

One guard stepped over, greeted Derek politely, then scanned everyone in the car head-to-toe with an infrared device before waving them through.

No waiting needed.

The car drove straight into the tunnel as the refugees outside stared enviously.

Once inside, Derek glanced at Iris proudly. "Captain Flint is now the base's vice administrator."

Iris's eyes lit up.

"Actually," he lowered his voice, "Captain Flint's grandfather is a marshal here. After we got back last time, they went to see Louisa's father. Half an hour later, Louisa's dad stepped down—Captain Flint took his place.

"Now he only handles daily production and living. Everything else is under Captain Flint."

Iris froze for a second, then almost laughed out loud.

Perfect.

She never expected saving Damon would completely upend Base K1's power structure.

In the past life, Damon and his team must have died in Louisa's trap.

The military lost its grip, Louisa returned with the equipment, and her father held full control. The military couldn't interfere even if they wanted.

But this time, Louisa was dead. Damon came back with the equipment.

Between old scores and new grudges, Louisa's father didn't stand a chance. He'd been totally sidelined.

Lost his daughter and his power in one blow... At this rate, he wouldn't even keep his title for long.

Damon's faction would be the real rulers of Base K1.

Iris had accidentally ruined the Bartons.

She was fighting back a mischievous grin when the car exited the tunnel.

Bright lights shone ahead, with a large iron gate marked Base K1. Beyond it, the noise of a huge crowd drifted over.

"Iris, we need one more security check. Let's get out for a minute."

"Okay."

Iris and Logan slung on their backpacks and stepped out.

Guards sprayed disinfectant on the car while they went through full-body black X-ray scanners one by one.

Dual checks ensured everyone entering was safe and uninfected.

They also had a supply rule: anyone entering Base K1 had to hand over half their stock. Unreported items found in a search were seized entirely.

"Open your packs! Surrender half voluntarily! Hidden supplies will be confiscated!"

"These are Captain Flint's personal guests, not regular residents," Derek said, showing an authorization letter.

The staff saluted at once and let Iris and Logan pass with their bags untouched, only finishing the X-ray scan.

Once cleared of any suspicious wounds, they officially entered Base K1.

What greeted them was row after row of shabby shanties, packed tightly together. There were no real doors—just hanging cloth curtains.

It was dinnertime. People sat on the ground in small groups, like nomads, gnawing on dry bread while watching one another warily.

In the western clearing, laborers—either finished eating or with nothing to eat—kept working, sweat pouring as they threw up more shacks and crude barriers.

Voices carried everywhere. People drifted through the narrow paths, shouting over one another. Fights broke out in the shadows between the shanties, yet no one reacted, as if they had long since grown used to it.

"Iris, let's go further in. It's messy out here," Derek said.

"Mm."

Iris got back in the car.

As they drove down the main road, people stepped aside automatically, not daring to provoke them. Everyone stared at the car with envy and longing.

Iris had seen that look too many times.

Don't mistake it for kindness. If this had been a regular resident's car, those same eyes would turn greedy and predatory—ready to pounce and tear her apart.

Only strength earned respect. The weak got crushed.

This was the extreme of survival of the fittest. Large bases were especially brutal.

"Iris, these are new survivors. It's chaotic now, but nicer inside," Derek explained.

Iris nodded.

The shanties slowly turned into proper houses and small buildings, with much tighter security.

These were for the base's earliest members—their quality of life was leagues better than the outer refugees.

When Derek turned onto a side road, the whole scenery changed.
