Chapter 85 A Fatal Liability

Iris's gaze darkened.

Defied all common sense, child zombies were far more dangerous than adult ones.

When children turned, they retained the flexibility and agility of their small bodies. They were crueler, more aggressive, and their mutation levels far outstripped those of ordinary zombies. Nearly all of them gained enhanced strength and speed.

Thud! Thud! Thud-thud!

Sensing living humans, the child zombies inside the room threw themselves excitedly against the glass walls.

Each impact left spiderweb cracks spreading across the reinforced glass—supposedly far sturdier than the panes in the other rooms.

A shrill scream suddenly exploded from within. "Ahhhh—"

It was Louisa.

The little zombies that had been clawing at the glass immediately darted toward the sound.

Gunshots rang out.

Derek and the others charged in without a second thought for their own safety.

Watching the chaos unfold, a jolt went through Iris—a memory snapped loose in her mind, sharp and sudden.

She remembered something critical.

In her past life, she had found Logan around this same time, on the outskirts of Aster City.

Back then, Logan had been a complete wreck—disheveled, broken, collapsed by the roadside like a stray dog with no will to live. If Iris hadn't saved him, he wouldn't have lived through the night.

After she took him in, he refused to speak about his past. It wasn't until they were on a life-or-death mission together that he finally opened up and saved her life.

Only then did Logan reveal he was from a military district—a soldier. He never shared any other details.

Iris occasionally heard him mention old teammates, but she never saw him make any attempt to find them.

So... this was his final mission from the previous life? Had everyone here died on this very operation?

Just then, Iris watched the group that had rushed in suddenly come sprinting back toward her.

They hurled small energy orbs and fired their remaining ammo, covering Damon and Louisa as they fled the center of the chaos.

Through the crowd, Damon spotted Iris, his eyes lighting up with gratitude.

His expression said it all—thank you. For the warning.

They had all made it out alive.

Damon owed Iris his life. Without her, he never would have survived today.

This was an SSS‑level mission for Base K1—the highest classification there was. His objective was to recover research data and equipment from the United Pharmaceutical R&D Factory, dating back to before the zombie virus outbreak.

That data might hold the key to unlocking the secret of extracting energy from zombie crystal cores.

Before the virus spread, the government had detected traces of this new latent virus in multiple locations.

At first, they'd dismissed it as just another flu strain. But when researchers tested it on lab mice, they realized it was an entirely new virus, unlike anything humanity had ever seen.

They were stunned to discover the virus contained the power to alter cells beyond their normal biological limits, twisting them into something akin to immortal energy.

Cracking its secrets would have meant a monumental leap in human civilization.

Unfortunately, disaster struck before any real breakthroughs could be made.

The "immortality virus" rewrote human biology into something else entirely—zombies.

Even with government preparations, nothing could stop the zombie virus, a dimensional catastrophe beyond containment. Humanity had unleashed something it could not control.

In an instant, the whole world fell.

Thankfully, some foundational research had been completed.

Louisa's father, Wyatt Barton, had designated this facility as the central virus research hub, where massive volumes of data had been stored.

With that data alone, Damon could earn himself a management position at Base K1, the heavily fortified military stronghold.

This mission had been arranged specifically to escort Louisa here personally to retrieve the files and equipment. No one could have predicted she would turn out to be such a fatal liability.

Every piece of her intelligence had been wrong.

She'd claimed the factory was completely clear of personnel and zombies, yet the first basement level was swarming with infected lab workers.

Even on B2, Louisa had insisted with absolute certainty that it was zombie‑free and entirely safe. Then she'd led everyone straight into a death trap.

Even someone as capable as Damon could not compensate for such dead weight.

Thank God he'd run into Iris today. Without her warning before entering, he would have been done for in that room, all because of Louisa.

He'd stayed as alert and cautious as possible while helping Louisa locate the materials. But the second they stepped inside, that fool flipped on the lights.

And when she spotted the zombies, she screamed—nearly forgetting to use her power entirely.

Fortunately, Damon was a true professional. And with Iris's warning still fresh in his mind, he'd maintained peak special-forces readiness the entire time.

He took down several child zombies in quick succession, buying Louisa enough time to grab what she needed and escape.

The moment he stepped out of that room, he knew—none of this would have been possible without Iris.

He had no idea how she'd known so many zombies were waiting inside. But without her, he truly would have died in there today.
