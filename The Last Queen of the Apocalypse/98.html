Chapter 98 Completely Abnormal

Iris's car didn't slow for a second.

The armored vehicle plowed straight through the scattered zombies in its path, crushing them under the wheels until they reached their target: Development Zone Mall.

A horde of zombies crowded the entrance.

Most wore ordinary clothes, but quite a few were bundled up in thick gear—clearly locals who had tried to loot the place after the outbreak. It just hadn't ended well for them.

If ten people went out for supplies, getting even one back alive was considered lucky.

The second Iris's car arrived, the roaming undead swarmed toward them.

Iris gestured to Logan. He flipped a switch inside, and a turret popped up from the roof, unleashing brutal, efficient fire on the horde below.

Kael watched the zombies erupt in bursts and yelled, "Whoa! Captain Beauty, this ride is insane! Where'd you get it?!"

Iris replied coolly, "Questions later. You're going out to scout first. Most of the zombies are down."

Kael raised an eyebrow. "Sure thing."

He stepped out unarmed to check the area.

The remaining scattered zombies completely ignored him. He walked straight to the entrance and waved back at Iris, signaling it was clear.

Logan couldn't help asking quietly, "Iris... what exactly is he?"

Iris smirked. "Special physique. Zombies don't attack him."

Logan's eyes lit up at once, his gaze shifting as he studied Kael.

No wonder Iris had recruited him. The guy really did have a useful ability.

Still, he clearly looked like trouble.

Iris pushed the door open. "Let's move."

"Yeah."

Logan grabbed his weapons and exited with the dogs.

The hounds worked in pairs to take down straggler zombies, while Logan finished off the rest.

Iris strolled through the cleared path like she owned the place, reaching the entrance.

She stood at the doorway, peering inside.

Complete darkness.

"How is it?" she asked.

Kael grinned and pulled out a blank white card. "Captain Beauty, door's locked, but... I got a way in."

He crouched and fiddled with the lock using the card. Within moments, the sealed entrance clicked open.

"Pretty handy, right?" Kael beamed, clearly fishing for compliments.

Iris kicked him straight through the doorway. "Good job. Now get in and scout ahead."

She'd noticed something weird about this guy—his loyalty was nearly impossible to raise, but it never dropped either. No matter how roughly she treated him, his pathetically low loyalty didn't sink lower.

Strangely enough, Kael actually seemed to enjoy her harsh attitude. Whenever she was nice to him, he pushed his luck. But when she used her fists, he followed orders perfectly.

Iris couldn't figure out what went on in his head. He was completely abnormal.

Then again, if he'd been normal, he wouldn't have pulled all that messed-up stuff in the past life.

"On my way."

Kael vanished quickly into the dark building.

Iris left two dogs at the entrance as usual, then took the rest and Logan to slowly check the first floor of the huge building.

The mall had three levels. Besides the supermarket, clothing stores and snack shops were spread throughout.

Right in front of Iris was an open clothing retail area.

With the power out, even though it was bright morning outside, the inside was dim. Only green emergency exit lights glowed faintly along the paths.

Rows of clothing racks held discounted off-season winter coats. Huge red promotional signs stood next to them, bold letters reading: $30 Clearance!

But no customers would ever come for those deals again.

Thick dust covered everything, from floor to ceiling.

Several clothing racks on the right had toppled over, with human-like limbs, torsos, and heads scattered across the ground.

Logan moved closer with the dogs and realized they were just mannequin parts.

"Iris, just dummies. No bodies, no blood."

Iris nodded. "Got it."

They didn't wait long before Kael came running back. "Captain Beauty, I checked the whole floor. It's clear, totally safe. The supermarket entrance is just ahead, and there's tons of supplies inside."

Iris studied his one eye and nodded. "Nice work."

Kael immediately started giving orders. "Logan, Captain Beauty and I are gonna head into the supermarket to collect supplies. You gather everything out here, and we'll come back for it after we're done."

After all, he'd told Logan he was a spatial-type metahuman.

Logan looked to Iris for confirmation.

Iris nodded and spoke softly. "Logan, sort the supplies and take the first load to the car."

Her armored vehicle could hold about a ton of materials.

"Yes, ma'am!"

With Iris's approval, Logan got to work at once.

Kael turned to Iris. "Captain Beauty, ready to go in? There's a mountain of stuff waiting in there."
