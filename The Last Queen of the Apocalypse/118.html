Chapter 118 Pay Him Back Tenfold

Everyone within earshot practically felt like coughing up blood.

Did Damon have to be this blatant about favoring her? He might as well have tattooed "I'm covering for them" right on his forehead.

It was shamelessly obvious.

But as much as they disliked Iris, they dared not disrespect Damon.

If not for him and Base K1 turning the tide as the main fighting force the night before, they would all be dead. Not to mention he had helped them handle their injured and bury their dead afterward.

They truly owed Damon their respect. His word carried real weight.

Damon glanced around at the group and continued, "Let this matter drop. I brought them here, so Base K1 will cover any contribution owed by Midsummer Base."

That only made everyone feel even more bitter.

Now it was crystal clear: Midsummer Base was just a pair of freeloaders leeching off Damon.

No ability, no effort—just connections.

"I think Captain Flint makes a good point," Chloe stepped forward to defend Iris.

The second she spoke, Jace immediately tugged her arm and shot her a warning look.

Chloe glanced at Iris, remembering those eyes from the night before, and spoke firmly. "Either way, I think it's fine if Base K1 covers their part."

With Chloe backing the idea, everyone took the out she'd handed them.

Whatever. Damon was already shielding them; there was nothing more they could say. Objecting now would just make them look petty.

Just then, a cold voice cut through the crowd from behind.

"I don't think this should be brushed aside so easily."

Zane's words made everyone turn.

He walked over slowly.

His aura was different today—cold, domineering, radiating an unshakable sense of superiority.

Under that crushing presence, the leaders of the smaller bases felt a stabbing pressure in their minds, forcing them to lower their heads in discomfort.

Only Damon and Iris remained unaffected.

Iris watched him saunter over lazily, sensing streams of mental power spreading outward from a six-foot radius around Zane, filled with clear probing intent.

Psionic power was dominant within its domain, but it couldn't distinguish specific superpower types—only whether someone was empowered at all, unlike Iris, who could pinpoint exactly what powers others held.

And Iris's mental power far outstripped his. As long as she didn't actively release her power, he would never detect her.

Zane spread his domain, trying to locate the powerful psychic power user in the crowd.

He scanned everyone and found nothing.

Damon, however, stared at him with obvious displeasure. "Captain Foster, what do you think you're doing?"

Damon's lack of reaction came from his own immense power, not insensitivity. He could feel Zane's invasive energy sweeping over everyone.

What was this man playing at?

At Damon's words, Zane's gaze lingered on him suspiciously.

A moment later, he retracted his domain, his eyes dark and frigid. "Captain Flint, I've been considering your proposal.

"I don't think it's acceptable that simply because you fight for them, they get to skip contributing entirely."

Zane turned to look directly at Iris.

His icy eyes held no trace of humanity. "This mission distributes resources based on merit. No contribution means no share—no matter who you came with."

The area fell completely silent.

Damon stepped in front of Iris. "Enough. Midsummer Base will be attached to Base K1, and we will share our supplies with them."

He blocked Zane's gaze entirely.

Zane's cold expression darkened. "If that's how you wish it, I have nothing more to add."

He turned and left with his team.

The other groups found the arrangement reasonable enough and slowly dispersed.

Once they were alone, Damon turned around. "Iris, don't be upset. Everyone's just..."

"No need to explain." Iris patted his shoulder casually, grinning. "You brought me here to freeload anyway. Why would I be mad?"

She wasn't some naive girl who thought Damon owed her everything. He had invited her without demanding she lift a finger, letting her tag along for free.

Damon had already more than repaid whatever old favor existed. Getting angry at him would be idiotic.

But the others, especially Zane... He had successfully gotten on her bad side.

If she didn't pay him back tenfold for today, she wouldn't be able to live with herself.

*****

Once the morning commotion settled, everyone organized their teams and prepared to move out.

The previous night's battle had been brutal, but it had also wiped out the main zombie horde along the route.

With Base K1 leading the way, the convoy worked together to clear scattered undead and wrecked cars, successfully opening the main road.

By the afternoon of the second day, they passed through the most dangerous stretch and smoothly entered the Eastern Grain Reserve.
