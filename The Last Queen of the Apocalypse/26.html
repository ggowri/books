Chapter 26 Trapped

They were driven forward like two frightened lambs, herded from behind, panicked and at a complete loss.

Iris had fought her way through the apocalypse long enough to have seen scenes like this more times than she could count.

After the collapse, women, because of the brutal gap in strength and sheer psychological pressure, were often reduced to accessories for men.

In an environment like this, the ugliness of human nature came out in full force.

There was no such thing as a moral floor, only depths people kept sinking to.

She just had not expected it to happen this fast.

The apocalypse had barely been half a month old, and people were already trampling over every last boundary, treating women as less than human.

The "ever-righteous" Iris found it hard to stomach.

She usually considered her own moral compass to be bottom of the barrel, but these guys were subterranean.

They weren't even human anymore.

And since they weren't human, Iris took her finger off the crossbow trigger.

She swapped out the standard bolt and rigged a little something extra. It was time to send them a parting gift.

"Keep moving, or I'll leave you here as zombie bait!"

Jack shoved the two women ahead. When he saw them step fully into the firing range without being shot, his eyes lit up.

"Stop right there! Stay put!"

Now he was sure of it. The person inside still had some sense of decency. She would not kill innocent women.

That meant they finally had leverage.

"Jack, what now?"

"Tow the car back, swap the tires, and then we're using these two to lead the way!"

He had his men quickly pull the truck into a safer spot, swap on the spare tires from the supplies, and then tie the two women to the front of the vehicle.

The message was crystal clear.

If she made a move, the women would die first.

If she chose to ignore their fate, the men could still escape immediately in the truck.

But if their plan worked, they would kill her next.

In the apocalypse, morality was a luxury no one bothered pretending to keep.

They finished fixing the tires and tied the hostages down on the roof. Then they jumped back into the truck. Staring at the road Iris had blocked with wrecked cars, they floored the gas and smashed straight through with a roaring engine.

This time, the charge went smoothly.

There was no shot.

The men were just starting to celebrate—

"Whoosh!"

A short bolt wrapped with a pouch of fresh chicken blood slammed into the driver's side door.

The bolt pierced in at an angle. Half of it lodged deep in the body of the truck, the other half pinning the door shut.

The men inside the vehicle were shocked.

Before they could even process it, bolts struck both sides of the truck almost simultaneously.

"Bang!"

The tires blew out for the second time. The truck spun out and screeched to a halt.

Bright red blood coated the vehicle, and the heavy stench of gore acted like a dinner bell for every zombie in the area.

All the remaining undead along the road began converging on them.

The gang was trapped inside the truck.

"Holy shit!"

"That bitch played us! She led us down the garden path!"

"Can we still get out?"

"The doors won't budge!"

Watching the zombies swarm in, Jack didn't hesitate. He lowered down the window and started scrambling out.

But he wasn't going alone. He grabbed one of the women to use as a human shield, keeping her between himself and danger the entire way.

Just then, a half-bodied zombie latched onto his leg. Jack shoved the woman forward right away to break free.

"Whoosh!"

A bolt shot straight at his back.

Jack dodged it by instinct, the shaft grazing past him.

He realized right then that he couldn't let go of his hostage. He had to keep her glued to his front and charge.

He grabbed the woman again, shoved her in front of him as a shield, and sprinted toward the bunker.

After running about half a mile down the narrow path, he finally spotted it hidden deep in the woods.

It was ringed with high wire fences, bristling with security cameras, and sealed shut by a massive, heavy iron gate.

Beyond the fence, he could vaguely make out rows of vegetables and chickens and ducks being raised inside.

He stared in shock at the fortifications and the fully equipped setup beyond them.

It was clear someone had claimed this spot early.

Someone had bought it and turned it into a full-fledged base fortress.

Had someone predicted the apocalypse?

But how was that even possible?

But just as confusion swirled in his head, the woman he was holding suddenly lunged and sank her teeth into his hand.

Watching through her crosshairs, Iris nearly burst out laughing.

She had spotted the bite on the girl ages ago.

That was the only reason she'd let Jack charge all the way to her front door.

She'd waited patiently for the girl to turn and give Jack a taste of his own medicine.

There was nothing more satisfying than watching someone dig their own grave.

It was poetic justice at its finest.

Smiling to herself, Iris kept her crosshairs trained on the rest. If any of them tried to run, she would kindly send an arrow to help them along.

By "them", she meant the zombies.

Iris had never claimed to be a good person.

She was a vengeful soul through and through.

Anyone who crossed her would pay it back a hundredfold.

From atop the tower, she kept the situation firmly under control. Once the group was completely wiped out, her gaze shifted to the truck.

That agent was still inside, hiding like a rat.
