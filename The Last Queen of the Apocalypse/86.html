Chapter 86 Go Die

His gaze toward Iris had shifted entirely.

"Fall back! Now!" Damon shouted.

Iris watched as the survivors poured out, and only then did she let out the breath she'd been holding.

They'd made it.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Behind them, the group opened fire on the child zombies.

At that moment, Louisa spun toward the door. "Seal it! Activate the self-destruct sequence!"

She lunged straight for the control panel beside the door. Without waiting for everyone still inside to escape, she began slamming the door-close button like a madwoman.

Iris's patience for this idiot finally snapped.

She kicked Louisa flying. "Are you insane? You fucking moron!"

"Three seconds!

Iris planted herself by the door, controlling its closing speed, and roared at those still inside.

"Three!"

Hordes of small zombies poured out of the doorway, while even more slammed themselves against the glass panels.

"Two!"

Crack! Spiderweb cracks spread rapidly across the glass as the child zombies inside threw their heads full-force into it. Each impact flattened their skulls a little more, their faces twisting into something even more grotesque.

"One!"

Shatter! Glass shards exploded in all directions.

At that exact moment, the last special forces soldier burst through the doorway.

SLAM!

The door crashed shut with a thunderous noise.

"Thank you!"

Damon gasped for breath, staring at Iris. Even his usually cold expression softened.

The others looked at her with unbridled gratitude too.

"We owe you our lives!"

"What's this self-destruct she was talking about?" Iris asked.

"The factory has a fail-safe. Once we're clear, the whole place gets destroyed," Damon explained.

Just then, they heard the mechanical whir of glass doors sliding open ahead.

Immediately afterward, the lights on the second basement level flashed once... then went dark entirely.

Only the elevator area at the far end remained illuminated.

Louisa clutched her stomach, a smug, triumphant grin spreading across her face.

"My dad said if I get the chance, I should let you all die! Perfect! You're all trapped here now! Burn in hell together! Hahaha!"

Her sabotage had been intentional all along. That was why she'd kept letting Iris tag along.

She swiped her card hard against the door panel in front of her.

Alarms blared throughout B2.

"Beep— Self-destruct sequence initiated. T-minus 30 seconds."

Every door on the second basement floor slid open at once. All the previously sealed rooms holding zombies were now unlocked.

Louisa sneered at them all, sealed the basement door shut, and sprinted for the elevator.

As she glanced back one last time, she locked eyes with Iris, flipped her off, and mouthed a silent curse.

How dare she defy her? Go die!

Feeling utterly pleased with herself as she watched the group get surrounded by the newly freed undead, Louisa turned to make her escape in the elevator.

But a figure stepped directly in front of the elevator doors.

Iris's voice was icy. "Logan. Kill her."

"Ahhhh—"

Above them, red warning lights flashed the countdown. Below, zombies poured out of the unlocked offices.

They lurched and clawed, heads lolling like frenzied demons, lunging straight for the group of living, breathing prey at the end of the hall.

The zombies were loose.

"Twenty-nine seconds remaining!"

Groaaaarrrrr

Beneath the flashing red alarms, a zombie in a hospital gown opened its gaping maw and charged.

Bang!

Before it could reach them, a bullet drilled straight through its forehead. A small, charred hole marked the entry wound, while the back of its skull blew apart into a bloody, crater-sized mess.

Dark red blood and white brain matter erupted outward. The soft tissue looked like pudding, churned apart by the force of the shot. Flesh and bone fragments scattered across the floor.

One shot, one kill.

As the headshot zombie collapsed to the ground, Damon— the one who'd fired —shouted, "Break through!"

The second his order landed, all the special forces soldiers surged forward at full speed.

Bullets sprayed wildly, a desperate, all-or-nothing assault.

The intense gunfire carved a path through the horde blocking their way.

The soldiers spontaneously moved to shield Iris, keeping her at the center of their formation.

Derek and Brett flanked her left and right, while the others formed a protective circle around her out of gratitude.

They blocked the zombies, cleared a path, and escorted her toward safety.

In that moment, none of them remembered just how capable Iris really was.

All they saw was an unarmed civilian—someone who had never revealed any abilities in front of them—and so, without question, they treated her as someone who needed protection.
