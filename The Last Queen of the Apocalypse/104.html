Chapter 104 Cooperation

And she had to keep the base hidden while developing it.

Until Midsummer Base was strong enough to stand on its own, she couldn't let anyone find it.

To celebrate the prosperity upgrade, Iris handed out a pound of meat to every resident as welfare. It sent morale soaring.

She returned to her bunker to cheers and praise.

"Ms. Hale is the best!"

"We're so lucky to be in Midsummer Base!"

"We get meat today! Long live Ms. Hale!"

"Let's work harder! She said there'll be more rewards if we do well!"

Iris felt thoroughly pleased.

As soon as she stepped in, she heard movement from the second floor.

"My dear master! You're back!"

Iris headed up. "What is it, Irie?"

"You have a new message. Shall I play it?"

Iris walked over to the main computer. "Someone's looking for me? How did they find this frequency?"

The virtual screen flickered, and Irie's avatar appeared.

"It came through a radio intercept, originating from Base K1."

Iris immediately thought of Damon. Last time he'd asked for her radio frequency. He'd actually come looking for her.

"My dear master, I answered automatically for you. Want to hear the recording?"

Iris snapped her fingers. "Play it."

The speakers began to play.

"Is this Iris? If so, respond on this frequency. This is Damon. I have urgent business."

"Gentleman, you've reached the right place! This is the channel of the greatest, most beautiful, most amazing Iris! How may I help you?"

Iris's mouth twitched.

Damon's hesitant voice came through. "Are you... Iris?"

"Nope! I'm Irie!"

"Who are you?"

"I'm my master's little secretary! Just tell me what you need. Don't be shy!"

"Alright... Irie, tell your master that Base K1 has detected a massive cold front coming at the end of the month. I don't know if you have enough supplies, but if not, I'd like to invite your master on a mission—raiding the Eastern Grain Reserve. Is she interested?"

Irie replied cheerfully, "Okay, I'll pass that along, dear Mr. Damon!"

Damon went quiet for a moment. "...Um, goodbye, Irie."

Irie immediately corrected him. "No. You have to say, 'Goodbye, dear Irie!'"

Damon's voice came out awkwardly. "Goodbye."

Irie shot back, "If you don't say it, I won't help you connect next time!"

After a long, reluctant pause, the final response came. "Goodbye, dear Irie."

Iris stared in disbelief.

Irie twirled on the screen. "My dear master, that's all! How was my reception?"

Iris smiled stiffly. "Thanks, my dear Irie. Great job."

"You're welcome, my dear master." Irie spun in a circle on screen, heart-eyes looking at Iris. "It's my honor to serve you. Do you want me to reply now?"

Iris nodded. "Yeah. Ask him what supplies they're targeting, how resources will be split, and how dangerous the mission is."

"Understood. One moment."

Irie immediately transmitted the questions back to Base K1.

Damon's reply came quickly.

"Iris, the Eastern Grain Reserve holds over ten thousand tons of grain—rice, corn, wheat—plus a large oil depot. Danger level is low. Very few staff, and the surrounding area is sparsely populated.

"The only thing is, this isn't just between you and me. Hope Base and Base K1 are leading a joint operation. A few smaller bases can join, and I want to bring you in."

Iris understood at once. Damon owed her a favor and was handing her free benefits.

With multiple major bases leading the charge, she wouldn't get a huge share. But she wouldn't reject it.

Besides, she'd like to see where the national grain reserve was even located.

"Irie, tell dear Damon I'm in."

Irie blinked and relayed the message.

A moment later, Damon's voice came through, slightly amused. "Good. I'll send someone to pick you up shortly... Dear Irie and ... Ms. Hale."

Iris froze.

"Wait. How did you respond to Damon?"

Irie's electronic eyes sparkled proudly. "I switched it to live transfer! He can hear everything!"

Iris facepalmed.

Shortly after, Irie alerted her that a military pickup had entered her territory.

Through the drone feed, she recognized the license plate. Damon's men were fast.

Ten minutes later, the truck stopped right outside her bunker.

They clearly knew exactly where to find her.

Derek stepped out. "Iris! I'm here!"
