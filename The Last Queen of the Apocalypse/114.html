Chapter 114 Pure Chaos

Across the vast grassland, pairs of pale gray eyes flickered to life in the dark.

Countless orbs of light emerged from every direction, all locked onto the convoy, closing in with terrifying, single-minded hunger.

In an instant, Iris's psychic sense picked up gray energy signatures popping up at its edge—one after another, endless waves.

A massive horde of zombies swarmed toward them from all sides, like ants swarming a nest.

It was a nightmarish parade of the dead.

Iris flung open the car door and shouted toward the tent outside. "Logan! Get up! Now!"

Logan was out of his tent in three seconds flat. By the fourth, Iris had yanked him bodily into the car.

The moment they were safely inside, a scream split the night.

"Zombies! We've got zombies!"

"We're under attack!"

The shouts jolted everyone awake. But by the time people grabbed their weapons to fight back, the horde was already on top of the convoy.

Thud!

A zombie lunged straight at Logan's empty tent. Finding nothing inside, it let out a guttural snarl and slammed into Iris's car.

A bloodless, withered, twisted face pressed against the window, jaws gaping as it tried to bite through the glass.

It was a short, elderly zombie.

Through the tattered clothes, the remains of a once-nice shirt were still visible.

Most of its head was bald—a classic comb-over gone to rot, hairline receded all the way to the back of its skull.

Even in death, it was clear the man had once been dignified.

The few strands left were still neatly combed into a tiny patch on the back of his head, like an overgrown shrub. And a gleaming gold tooth shone in its front row.

Screech—

The gold tooth scraped against the glass in front of Iris, the sharp, grating sound sending a chill down her spine.

Thick, dark blood splattered across the window as the zombie clawed, bit, and slammed itself against the car in a frenzy.

On the third impact—Bang!

A bullet tore through its right temple. The force wrenched its head sideways at a ninety-degree angle, and with a wet, sickening thud, its neck snapped clean.

Footsteps approached as a squad of Damon's guards rushed over. They checked the car's occupants, flashed a quick hand signal, then fanned out to take down other zombies.

Logan tensed, ready to jump out and help.

Iris held him back. "Don't."

She calmly settled into a good viewing spot, pulled a pack of crackers from her pocket, and tore them open. "They've got it covered."

Logan could only stare helplessly.

Outside, the zombie horde kept surging forward in unending waves.

All the convoy vehicles had huddled together for defense. Aside from Iris and the Hope Base group, everyone else was out fighting for their lives.

Fire and blood painted the chaos outside.

Iris noticed that only two of the four smaller bases had firearms, and very few at that.

Gun control had been extremely strict before the apocalypse, and weapons in civilian hands remained scarce even now.

The real firepower came from Base K1. All of Damon's men carried heavy gear, every soldier charging the front lines as gunfire roared in a relentless barrage.

The other small bases lacked that kind of strength, but they threw everything they had into the fight anyway.

Everyone with superpowers unleashed their abilities—fireballs, water orbs, and shards of metal streaking through the air.

Strength-type metahumans wielded machetes, baseball bats, and any cold weapon they could find, turning into berserkers in close combat.

Even those with no powers at all stayed within the defensive line, fighting with whatever they could grab.

Everyone was fighting for their lives.

Through the chaos, Iris spotted Chloe.

She stood behind her brother Jace, a strength-type metahuman.

He gripped a pitchfork, swinging it with brutal force, sending four or five zombies flying with each hit.

After he battered the undead back, two fireballs cut through the air from behind him, slamming into the fallen zombies.

The tips of Chloe's short buzz cut glowed faint red. Her hands moved quickly, face set in a dark, fierce scowl as she molded fireball after fireball, coordinating perfectly with her brother.

But as time dragged on, her power began to fail her.

She was only a Level 1 metahuman; she could barely manage two fireballs at once. Each use drained her further, her face growing paler by the second.

Still, the zombies came—wave after endless wave.

Chloe shouted, "Jace! Why are there so many? We saw nothing during the day!"

Jace roared back while fighting. "They must hide when the sun's up!"

At night, zombies were several times stronger, their senses sharpened to an insane degree.
