Chapter 99 Kael's Family?

Iris stepped forward. "Let's go."

She didn't trust Kael one bit, so she brought Spike with her, leaving the rest of the dogs with Logan.

With someone like him, the real danger wasn't being near him—it was leaving him alone. If he planned to betray someone, he'd do it when their back was turned.

Keeping most of the dogs with Logan also kept him safe, blocking any dirty tricks Kael might try.

Iris was taking zero chances.

They reached the mall's central area, and Kael spoke up. "Captain Beauty, you really don't trust me, do you?"

Iris shot back coldly. "If that's what you think, maybe you're the one who doesn't trust me."

Kael chuckled softly.

By then, they'd reached the supermarket section.

The metal rolling shutter was pulled all the way down. Before Iris could say a word, Kael pulled out a small tool and fiddled with the lock.

With a sharp clatter, the door slid open.

In that moment, Iris was sure of one thing: this guy was a professional at breaking and entering.

She teased, "Impressive. Was this your old job?"

"How dare you? I'm a law-abiding citizen," Kael said smugly, tucking his tool away. "But you don't exactly look ordinary either, Captain Beauty. So decisive and ruthless. You weren't in a gang, were you?"

Iris matched his tone. "Please. I'm the most upstanding, law-abiding person you'll ever meet."

Kael fell silent.

Yeah. Sure.

Iris followed Kael into the supermarket.

It was even darker inside. Without lights, they could barely see.

Iris pulled out two high-powered flashlights, handing one to Kael, and they moved slowly inward.

It was eerily quiet. And empty.

Every checkout counter was thick with dust.

Iris immediately spread her mental power.

Her current range was about seventy feet—enough to cover a quarter of the large supermarket.

There were no signs of living people or zombies.

Iris, Kael, and Spike moved deeper, clearing the items near the registers.

Kael spoke up. "By the way, Captain Beauty, I only ever see you with Logan. Where's your family?"

"All dead," Iris replied casually.

The Hales stopped being her family a long time ago. Even if they'd somehow escaped the hospital, they wouldn't last long without her to leech off.

They'd never find another Iris to exploit in this lifetime.

"What about yours?" she asked.

"Also dead," Kael smiled faintly, a flicker of coldness in his voice.

As Iris finished collecting gum and candy from the checkout area, she noticed all the chocolate and high-calorie snack boxes were empty.

At that moment, Spike began barking violently toward the dark back of the store.

"Woof! Woof! Woof!"

Iris immediately shone her flashlight in that direction.

She caught a quick glimpse of a figure darting away.

Even though her mental power couldn't reach that far, she could tell it was human.

Because zombies didn't hide. They charged.

"Someone's here," Kael said.

Iris pulled her AK straight out of her space. Since Kael already knew about her ability, there was no point hiding it anymore.

She raised the rifle and walked toward the back with Kael and Spike.

Along the way, she noticed the food shelves had been almost completely cleaned out.

Someone was definitely living here.

When Iris reached the rear half of the supermarket, her mental power picked up the break room ahead.

"Seven people. Men, women, old, young. Not a single metahuman."

As soon as she finished speaking, a group stepped out of the employee lounge.

"Are you here to rescue us?!"

Two twin boys around thirteen or fourteen, three gray-haired elders, a pregnant young woman, and one middle-aged man appeared.

The man was probably the only able-bodied adult in the group, yet he didn't look like the leader. He stood quietly behind them, like a simple laborer.

Iris found the group strange. In the apocalypse, surviving this long with so many elderly and children was almost unheard of.

The oldest woman in the group saw Iris's gun and immediately assumed she was military.

She snapped in a sharp, demanding tone. "What took you so long! Hurry up and get us out of here!"

Her voice was rude and entitled, like they were her servants or something.

Before Iris could even retort, another voice exploded.

"Grandma! Look who's with her!" One of the boys shouted at the top of his lungs. "It's that bastard!"

"It's definitely him! How's he still alive? What's he doing here?!" the other twin echoed.

"Grandma, chase him away! I hate looking at him! Having that bastard in front of me is disgusting!"

In an instant, every eye locked onto Kael, standing behind Iris in the shadows.

Kael lowered his head and smiled.
