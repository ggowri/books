Chapter 92 A Strange Group

With the base no longer needing her constant oversight, Iris could finally focus on what mattered most.

The global zombie virus had wiped out 99.9% of humanity, dealing a catastrophic blow to civilization. Areas with poor terrain and dense populations had been completely overrun.

Zombies had split the world into isolated pockets, where small groups of survivors huddled and formed makeshift bases.

Even then, they faced endless hardships: food shortages, scarce resources, constant zombie hordes, and raids from other humans.

In her past life, Iris had been trapped in a tiny base, scraping by for ten years.

In that decade alone, she had lived through eight massive zombie waves. Several times, they had only narrowly avoided total annihilation. The final, apocalyptic wave had erased her base completely.

The worst part was not death itself—it was having nowhere left to run.

Her base was gone, and so were all the other small settlements. In the entire eastern region, only the mighty K1 Base still stood, and it was barely holding on.

Faced with the endless sea of undead, humanity had only one question left: how much longer could they survive?

It was not a matter of waiting for things to get better. There would be no better.

This was extinction. The end of civilization. Absolute, inescapable despair.

After yesterday's events, a realization hit Iris. Fate could be changed.

She had saved Damon's team and wiped out what would have grown into a future nightmare: the zombie king.

That meant she could do far more in these early apocalypse days. Especially now, when threats were still weak and her own power was already formidable.

If she cleared out more dangers ahead of time, while quietly and rapidly building her base—establishing sustainable food production, powerful defenses, and a stable population—then when future disasters struck, she would have the strength to face them.

At the very least, Midsummer Base had to survive that devastating wave ten years from now.

While the early apocalypse remained relatively calm, she intended to use every second to grow stronger and develop her base. Each day of progress meant another layer of defense against the coming storms.

With her goal set, Iris headed out with Logan.

First, she would eliminate every high-risk zone near her territory.

She drove toward a livestock farm in the western suburbs.

She remembered this place well—six months after the outbreak, it had become ground zero for a massive animal plague.

The zombie virus did not infect animals, but without human care, all the chickens, ducks, and sheep had starved or died of disease. As the corpses rotted, they spawned a horrific plague.

In her past life, Iris had joined a supply run here. What they found was a field of death, littered with rotting animal carcasses.

Swarming flies had formed dark clouds over the stinking pigs, cows, and sheep. White maggots wriggled through the remains, and putrid, pus-filled flesh stuck to everything.

The small furry animals were the worst. Their fur decayed slowly, leaving moldy-looking patches of pelt clinging to the ground while their bodies melted into the soil.

The scene was nauseating beyond words. One glance was enough to give someone nightmares for months.

Worse still, the rotting corpses carried countless bacteria and viruses. After her team returned, an animal plague swept through their base, making their already desperate situation even worse.

Later, it spread to other bases too, wiping out several smaller settlements entirely.

Iris's first major move was to use her past-life knowledge to get there first and take control of the place.

She followed her memory to the location, only to hear gunshots as soon as she arrived.

Bang!

Bullets sprayed toward the livestock farm ahead.

A small group of seven or eight people was attacking the entrance.

Someone had beaten her to it.

But this group looked completely amateur. They only had two handguns between them, and their aim was terrible—several shots fired, none hit.

Iris extended her mental power to scan the area.

Five men. Two women.

Only one fire-type metahuman. Two strength-types—and the handguns were in their hands.

The rest were ordinary survivors, clutching clubs, machetes, and whatever melee weapons they could find. They kept close behind the three metahumans, faces tight with fear.

But Iris noticed something odd about the last man in the group, the one wearing a baseball cap.

He showed no power fluctuations, yet she sensed a strange energy unlike that of ordinary humans.

He was not normal, but he was not a metahuman either.

After a few more useless shots, the group clearly realized they couldn't break through. That was when they noticed Iris's vehicle approaching.

"Stuart, over there! A car!"
