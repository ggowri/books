Chapter 100 He's a Zombie!

"Captain Beauty, why don't you head upstairs and gather supplies? I know these people. I need to settle something here."

Iris studied Kael for a moment, then nodded in agreement.

As she turned to leave, Margaret Cross immediately stepped in her way. "Where do you think you're going? You can't leave! Is he your teammate? How could you team up with trash like him? Dump him right now!

"I'm telling you, the Cross family owns the biggest real estate in Aster City. My youngest son is the vice mayor of Briarfield. Get us out of here, help us find him, and you'll be rewarded handsomely."

The twin boys kept whining loudly.

"Grandma, I wanna get out! I want fried chicken! I don't want to stay in this awful place!"

"Me too! I wanna play games! I haven't been online forever—make her charge my phone first!"

"Alright, alright," Margaret soothed her grandsons, then glared at Iris. "You heard them. Make arrangements now. Don't let my boys suffer.

"And first, get rid of this garbage! The Cross family would never claim some disgusting vermin like him."

Kael listened silently, pulling his baseball cap lower and lower, a cruel smile spreading across his face.

Then he heard the sharp click of a gun being cocked.

In the dim light, Kael stared in shock as Iris raised her weapon and pressed the barrel to Margaret's mouth.

Her lazy, cold voice cut through the air. "Who exactly did you call garbage? My people aren't yours to insult."

Iris was always ruthless, selfish, and unbound by rules. But she protected anyone she claimed as hers.

Even if she didn't fully trust Kael yet, she would never let anyone talk to him like that.

She pressed the icy metal harder against the old woman's lips.

Behind her, Kael's single eye slowly lit up.

The whole group froze in terror.

The pregnant woman, Joan Byrd, hid in the arms of the man Kenelm Byrd, scrambling backward.

But the two boys still shouted arrogantly.

"He is trash! His mom was a homewrecker who crawled into our dad's bed. They've been hanging around our house for years, scamming money. He was sent to juvie at thirteen—stealing, fraud, you name it! Our family doesn't have trash like that!"

"If you lay a hand on us today, our uncle will kill you! You're just another bitch!"

Bang!

One of the boys went flying from a brutal kick. Kael had struck him.

After sending the kid sprawling, Kael turned to Iris with a mocking grin. "Captain Beauty, leave this to me. Don't trouble yourself."

Iris said nothing, flipped the safety back on, and walked away.

By the time she reached the second floor to collect daily supplies, sharp screams erupted from below.

"Ahhh! Grandma! He's a zombie!"

"That bastard's a zombie!"

"Kill him! Don't let him bite me!"

"Grandma, you got bit! Stay away!"

"Grandma's turning into a zombie! We have to kill her!"

Iris raised an eyebrow.

Kael didn't carry the active virus. He was a half-zombie with antibodies, but he couldn't infect people.

He was just terrorizing them on purpose.

Psychological torture was worse than killing them outright.

Kael laughed maniacally, watching the so-called loving family turn on each other in savage panic.

This world deserves to be destroyed. Let it all burn!

Iris imagined this was how he'd laughed when he destroyed bases in his past life. He was truly anti-human.

She finished clearing the second and third floors. They were full of daily necessities and clothes.

These things were useless to the Cross group, who were too scared to leave the food section anyway. Almost everything was untouched.

But Iris desperately needed these supplies right now.

Food wasn't urgent—her base was starting to produce its own.

But her residents wanted shampoo, toilet paper, sanitary pads, clothes, shoes, kids' garments, and even books. All the "useless" things no one else bothered with.

What others discarded was exactly what she craved. No competition at all.

She happily looted both floors completely.

Once done, she didn't go down immediately, but waited by a second-floor window.

Through the glass, she saw Joan and Kenelm scrambling out a side door.

"They were my grandma's maid and driver."

A voice came from behind her.

Iris turned.

Kael was covered in blood, walking toward her step by step, backlit by the faint light.
