Chapter 95 Give Me Your Heart

Back when he'd first wandered into her old small base, Iris hadn't paid him much mind.

It was only long after he left that she heard his name on the radio—and learned exactly what kind of man he was.

After that, she'd occasionally heard wild rumors about his reckless lifestyle in the base's tiny tavern.

But his ending had been ugly.

In the eighth year of the apocalypse, he'd snuck into Hope Base, another major stronghold, planning to stir conflict between them and K1 Base. Hope Base caught him.

Word was they dragged him to their lab and cut him open for research, hoping to unlock the virus's secrets from his half-infected body.

She never heard from him again.

She never expected to run into this legendary troublemaker in this lifetime.

"Hey there, beautiful," he grinned, "I'll be straight with you. I was infected with the zombie virus, but I didn't turn. I can walk right through hordes, and they won't touch me.

"So what do you say? Someone with my talent... can I join your team?"

Kael looked at Iris with his mismatched eyes—one human, one corpse-gray—his pupil glinting.

His gaze seemed so sincere that, if Iris hadn't lived through ten years of hell, she might've been fooled by his honest act and rare ability.

How could she not recruit someone like him?

But Iris knew exactly who she was dealing with.

She stared at the man surrendering to her, expression torn.

Kill him, or keep him?

This guy was utterly disloyal, a walking time bomb waiting to go off. She could never trust him.

But his ability...

Everyone she recruited had their special traits copied to her. His half-zombie physique...

She had to have it.

"Fine. I'll let you join."

"Wait!" Kael cut in. "Beautiful, I'm not done. If I join, what kind of treatment do I get?"

Iris smiled coldly. "I'll give you the best treatment. Stick with me, and you'll eat meat every meal."

Kael grew excited. "Got any girls at your base? Uh, I mean, how many people are there total?"

Iris pressed her lips together.

Just like the rumors said.

"Just me and Logan—the man outside."

"Only two of you?" Kael stroked his chin, a strange light flickering in his human eye.

Then he grinned. "He's not your boyfriend, right?"

Iris held back an eye-roll. "...No."

Kael agreed at once. "Perfect. I'm in."

Immediately, a voice chimed in Iris's mind.

[Ding. Base Guard (Fake) +1]

[Reward: 100 points. New guard's loyalty: 59. Unable to unlock special attributes.]

Iris stared in shock at the first-ever (Fake) tag, pulling up Kael's status panel.

White numbers burned clearly: Loyalty: 59(Initial: 19 + System boost: 40)

Holy shit. His starting loyalty was only 19? Even with the system's 40-point boost, he still couldn't hit 60.

This guy really was a natural-born traitor.

Logan, by comparison, was already at 100 loyalty—deep purple rank.

When Logan first joined, he'd started at 70. The system added 40, pushing him over 100.

Once loyalty broke 100, it was divided into three color tiers: red, purple, and gold.

Red meant blazing loyalty, purple meant never betraying, and gold meant eternal devotion.

And this guy? Only 59. Not even enough to unlock his attributes.

Iris was so annoyed that she wanted to shoot this backstabbing bastard on the spot.

Who in their right mind would recruit someone like him?

But he was so close to the threshold...

No way. She had to get that half-zombie body.

She smiled sweetly. "Kael, what do you like? Since you're joining, I should give you a welcoming gift."

She needed to bump his loyalty up.

"Not much, really," Kael grinned. "But if you insist, Captain Beauty... how about giving me your heart? I fell for you at first sight. I mean it."

BAM!

Iris couldn't take it anymore and kicked him flying.

"Get to work! Round up all the live pigs. Move too slow, and I'll rip your heart out and feed it to them!"

Kael sprawled on the ground, watching Iris move to the next pen. A strange glint flashed in his mismatched eyes, and his lips curled into a faint smile.

*****

Half an hour later, Iris had stored all the live pigs into her space.

For the dead ones, she had Kael drag them into a deep pit.

His half-zombie body seemed enhanced in strength and speed too. He worked fast.

They buried the dead swine together, doused the pit with gasoline, and set it ablaze.

These pigs had been dead for days—their rotting flesh was inedible, and leaving them would only spawn bacteria and plague.
