Chapter 127 The Tough-guy Act

He was, and always would be, the one Iris defeated.

*****

On the open highway, a single car led the convoy.

The trip back was smooth and uneventful.

Zombies and roadblocks had already been cleared on the way there, so Logan could drive straight through without a single hold-up.

As they passed the highway rally point from two days prior, Iris heard honking behind her.

Glancing in the rearview mirror, she spotted a small cargo truck tailing them. The vehicle looked familiar—it belonged to Spark Base.

Iris told Logan to slow down.

As their speed dropped, the truck caught up and pulled alongside.

The window rolled down, revealing a soot-streaked face.

Iris lowered her window and rested an arm on the frame.

She was mildly surprised to see Jace driving, then noticed Chloe leaning over from the passenger seat to wave.

"Captain Hale!"

Iris raised an eyebrow. "What's up?"

Chloe nudged Jace's arm.

Jace looked awkward, turned his head, and said stiffly, "Thanks."

Iris blinked, caught off guard.

"Jace!"

Chloe wasn't satisfied with his half-hearted thanks.

She looked straight at Iris and spoke openly. "Captain Hale, we came to thank you—for yesterday, and for saving us before!"

Iris's lips curved into a faint smile. "Got it."

She'd saved everyone, but only these two had bothered to thank her. These siblings weren't half bad.

Chloe breathed a sigh of relief, a weight lifting off her chest.

But neither she nor Jace was talkative, and now that their thanks were out, they didn't know what else to say.

Then Iris spoke. "Hey, girl. Wanna come with me?"

Chloe blinked. "Captain Hale... me?"

"How many other girls are in your team?" Iris leaned against the window. "Come with me. I can give you a good life. Meat with every meal, pretty much whatever you want. Most importantly, no one at my base will look down on you."

Chloe's eyes lit up, and she quickly glanced at Jace.

Iris went on. "You can bring your brother too. But let's be clear—if you come to my place, both of you follow my rules. The benefits at my base are pretty good, though."

Chloe was completely tempted. "Jace, let's go together!"

Jace thought for three full seconds, then answered. "Alright, Chloe. You go. I'm not coming."

Chloe poked him. "Jace!"

Jace shook his head. "Spark Base still needs me. I can't leave. But you should go with Captain Hale. I'll worry less knowing you're with her. Take care of yourself, and radio me when you can."

Spark Base was full of elderly people. If he left, no one else would look after them.

But Chloe could start over.

He'd seen the supplies Iris had laid out for Base K1 yesterday. He knew if Chloe went with her, she'd never go hungry again.

And Iris was so powerful—following her would be the best chance Chloe could get.

As much as it hurt to send her away, he had to think about her future.

He forced a smile. "Captain Hale, she's my only sister. I'm entrusting her to you. If you keep her safe, I, Jace Reed, will owe you my life."

Jace hit the brakes and pulled over to the side.

Iris had Logan stop as well.

But once they parked, Chloe refused to get out.

"Jace, I'm not going. Let's just go back."

Jace frowned. "Don't be ridiculous. Don't waste Captain Hale's time. Get over here!"

Chloe clung to her seat, refusing to move. "I'm not going unless you come too!"

Jace felt a headache coming on.

This was an amazing opportunity. What if Iris changed her mind?

He faked anger. "Get lost! I finally found someone willing to put up with a brat like you, and you're still clinging to me? I'm your brother, not your dad! I'm sick of you being dead weight!"

Chloe's eyes welled up. "Jace..."

Jace snapped. "Just go! Get out! Stop being a burden!"

Chloe pouted stubbornly, glaring at him. "I'm not leaving! No way!"

Jace tried to drag her out. "You think I'm playing? Go!"

Just then, a voice cut in from behind. "Idiot."

Jace turned and met a pair of bright, sharp eyes that saw straight through him, shattering his act completely.

Iris sighed. "It's the apocalypse. Drop the tough-guy act. You're her brother, not her enemy. If you care about your sister, just say it. No shame in that."

A piece of paper fluttered down from above.

"This is my base's radio frequency. Contact me when you've made up your mind. I can take up to thirty people from your base."
