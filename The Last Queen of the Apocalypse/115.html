Chapter 115 Useless

This was the main road leading to the grain reserve—and one of Briarfield City's major thoroughfares.

Even if they cleared the path by day, they could never wipe out every zombie hiding in the surrounding area.

With so many survivors gathered in one spot, they had lured the undead from miles around.

Damon could tell this horde was far worse than he'd expected. Without hesitation, he barked, "Everyone, hold on for five minutes!"

The second he spoke, he led the entire K1 Base force through a gap in the rear and pulled out.

The four smaller bases tensed up instantly.

Five minutes?! Was K1 Base really abandoning them?

They dared not retreat, though—they gritted their teeth and tightened their defense around the convoy.

Five minutes. They had to hold on for just five minutes.

The zombie wave crashed against them again and again. All four bases fought desperately, while Hope Base stayed completely still, not a single person stepping out of their vehicles.

By the three-minute mark, the metahumans from the smaller bases were already at their limit.

Those who'd burned through their powers were exhausted; those who couldn't keep pace were falling behind. Every empowered fighter switched to cold weapons just to keep going.

Shovels, hammers, wooden clubs, machetes... they wielded whatever they could get their hands on.

Everyone swung with everything they had, slashing at the undead.

Still, their defensive circle shrank inward.

But they had to hold.

At four and a half minutes, their perimeter had been pushed all the way back to their convoy.

No more room to retreat. One step back, and they would be overrun.

Chloe was shielded by Jace, backing slowly toward their truck.

"Chloe, get in the car!" he shouted.

Chloe refused right away. "I'm not leaving!"

Jace snapped louder. "Your power's gone! What use is a useless woman? Move!"

Chloe strained to summon one last spark of flame, but the fire in her palm fizzled out completely.

At that instant, a long-haired zombie lunged at her from nowhere, jaws open to bite.

It happened too fast. By the time Chloe reacted, the festering, rotting face was inches away. Its mouth gaped open like a bottomless pit, a half-torn tongue writhing with stinking maggots.

In that moment, Chloe forgot to fight. She almost wanted to just close her eyes.

But just as despair fully took hold, the zombie never bit down.

It froze completely, mere inches from her neck, as if some invisible, overwhelming force had locked it in place.

Bang!

An axe pierced straight through its skull and sent the body flying.

"Chloe!!" Jace stared at her, panic and relief warring on his face.

But Chloe was staring past him, at a pair of eyes.

A beautiful woman sat calmly inside the car, watching the whole thing, smiling faintly at her.

Her eyes were far too bright—bright enough to seize the soul, brimming with indescribable power.

Whoosh!

A fireball shot past Jace's head.

He stared at the fresh flame flickering in Chloe's palm. The heat made his heart race, and behind him, a zombie's head exploded in a burst of fire.

Thick black blood splattered across half his face.

At that moment, a wall of fire roared to life around their entire position.

Then—rat-a-tat-tat! Heavy gunfire resumed.

Base K1 had returned!

They'd doused the outer perimeter with gasoline and set a blazing barrier, successfully cutting off the oncoming horde. They rejoined the convoy and used heavy firepower to quickly clear the remaining zombies inside the circle.

Chloe gasped, "Jace! Let's keep fighting!"

Jace nodded fiercely. "Let's go!"

Hope surged the second they saw Base K1 back. Everyone rallied and helped clear the safe zone.

Ten minutes later, everyone was utterly spent.

All zombies inside the safe perimeter were dead.

People collapsed to the ground, relieved just to be alive.

Chloe looked at Jace, panting. "Jace... was I useful?"

Jace smiled and ruffled her buzzcut roughly, the short hair prickling his palm. "Of course. Stronger than me, even!"

Chloe's pale, chapped lips curved into a small laugh.

As she smiled, her gaze drifted back to the car that had never moved an inch.

The woman inside was no longer in the front seats. The car was quiet, as if they'd gone back to sleep.

Chloe's eyes darkened.

Had that been an illusion?

Why did the zombie stop attacking her?

Had Iris done that?

Inside the car, Iris yawned and rubbed her throbbing head—achey from overusing her mental power—then drifted into deep sleep.

The defense hadn't been without casualties.

Among the four smaller bases, five people were dead. One had been killed by friendly fire.

Four had been bitten. Three threw themselves straight into the horde to fight to the end, buying their bases precious time.

The last one tried to hide his wound. But once Damon's squad returned and secured the perimeter, his mutation began.

He begged desperately, "Don't kill me. Please... don't.

"My wife... she's still home, waiting for me. Don't kill me. If I die... they'll bully her... Ugh... agh—"
