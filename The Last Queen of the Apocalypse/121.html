Chapter 121 A Legend

Everyone was packed tight as sardines.

Front, back, left, right, above, below... There was nowhere to run.

Just as all of them accepted death, a wall of water erupted around them.

It was unlike any water barrier they had ever seen—so overwhelming that for a second, they felt the moisture in their own bodies being pulled away, leaving the air parched.

BANG!

Everyone stared in disbelief as countless droplets shot through the air like high-pressure bullets, piercing straight through the zombie skin that normal bullets couldn't even scratch.

Iris smirked. "From now on, I'm in charge of food distribution."

The mist exploded outward, blasting the lunging zombies back with brutal force.

A member from Future Base, mid-rescue, gaped as the zombie lunging for his chest froze. Its razor-sharp claws had torn through his tactical vest and stopped barely an inch from his heart, blocked by a wall of crystallized water.

Before his eyes, the wall shattered into fine particles.

Those ultra-fine droplets moved at blistering speed, turning into water blades that sliced clean through the zombie's arm. Each bead struck like a bullet, targeting weak points and piercing their steel-hard bones without effort.

This power was... terrifying!

The giant zombie in front was sent flying several feet, too.

Its bloated frame, after Iris's assault, resembled a waterlogged loaf of bread: riddled with beehive-like holes, blood and murky fluid oozing from every puncture.

It staggered, swaying unsteadily before righting itself, dripping with crimson water as if dredged from a vat of blood.

Then it hacked up a spray of foamy blood, spitting the gruesome mixture toward the group.

Iris spoke calmly. "Logan, cover me."

"On it!"

Before everyone's eyes, Iris drew the katana from her back, planted one foot on the walkway rail, and launched herself high into the air.

She crashed down onto the massive zombie.

This bloated undead was fast and unnaturally strong. But Iris was faster. Stronger.

The silver katana flashed under the red flare light.

Iris moved like a ghost, her blade leaving afterimages, her explosive strength chilling. Every strike landed with a sonic boom, brutal and efficient.

No fancy moves—every slash was meant to kill.

Iris wielded combat techniques far beyond this era. One cut wasn't enough? Then she'd cut again.

Graceful, lightning-fast, lethal.

CLANG!

With one final, earth-shaking strike, Iris drove her katana deep into the metal wall, pinning the zombie in place.

She breathed slightly heavy, gripped the hilt, and pulled the blade free with a sharp ring.

The moment the sword left its body, the giant zombie collapsed like overmixed cream, sliding down the wall in a gory heap.

A crystal core the size of a newborn's fist tumbled from the tip.

Just then, two skeletal, roach-like zombies burst from the shadows, lunging straight for Iris.

Before anyone could shout a warning, two arm-thick jets of high-pressure water blasted outward.

Only then did everyone realize the truth.

The quiet man who always followed Iris—Logan—was also a metahuman. And his power was just as monstrous.

While everyone else struggled to form fist-sized orbs, he casually unleashed water jets thick as a limb.

Derek and his team were especially stunned.

This was their first time seeing Logan use his superpower in combat. When they'd separated, his power had been about the same level as theirs.

And water-type power was mostly just useful for drinking water in the early stages.

How, in just a few months, had Logan's power grown this strong? It was at least ten times more powerful than theirs.

BANG!

The two water jets flanked Iris perfectly, blowing the zombies away as she swept her katana horizontally.

With Logan's flawless support, she decapitated both in a single stroke.

Behind them, the zombies she'd blasted earlier began crawling back up.

Once more, Iris acted—this time, everyone watched clearly.

Water surged around her like a tidal wave, instantly forming a protective dome over the entire group.

If Logan's jets were shocking, Iris's power was on another level entirely.

Everyone stared at Iris, completely speechless.

So she wasn't some useless plaything Damon was carrying. She was a walking weapon of mass destruction.

No wonder Damon treated her so well, catered to her every whim, spoiled her. She was a one-man army.

He'd brought a legend along.

The members of the four smaller bases watched as Iris cut through the horde like a hot knife through butter, their faces burning with a mix of shock and humiliating shame.
