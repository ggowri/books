Chapter 97 A Liar

Logan didn't ask any more questions. He followed Iris into the dormitory, and they each picked a clean room.

The six dogs stuck with Logan.

They'd barely settled in when Kael's voice called from outside. "Captain Beauty!"

Iris replied, "What?"

"Are we gonna eat or what?"

Iris turned to Logan. "Right, time for food."

She told him to grab the emergency rations from the car.

*****

Kael stared at their small pile of supplies. "This is all we got?"

"What were you expecting?" Iris raised an eyebrow.

"Alright, at least we have food." Kael grinned. "But I know a spot nearby loaded with supplies."

Iris looked at him. "Where?"

"About three miles east. There's a three-story shopping mall."

Iris nodded. "Good. We'll hit it tomorrow."

She always needed more resources. Since they were already out here, they might as well clear the area.

Kael watched her agree, his smile widening.

He started chatting up both of them, trying to worm out information.

"So, bro, you ex-military? You've got that look. How'd you meet Captain Beauty?"

Unfortunately for him, Logan was about as talkative as a brick.

He only answered Iris. Everyone else got silence.

By the end of the meal, Kael had gotten nothing out of either of them—except maybe a tail wag from the dogs.

He didn't seem bothered, though. He just kept rambling like a wandering storyteller.

Once they finished, he suddenly looked at Iris. "Captain Beauty, didn't you say you had something for me today?"

Iris turned to him. "What do you want?"

But the second she met his gleaming single eye, she punched him lightly. "Don't stare like that."

Kael clutched his eye in fake pain.

"Captain Beauty, can't you be a little softer? I never had parents, just a gambling grandma who beat me whenever she felt like it. And I've never had a single friend my whole life.

"Now with the world ended...I really wanna stick with you guys. Be comrades who'd die for each other. Can you accept someone like me?"

Iris snapped, "Spit it out!"

Kael switched back to smooth talk. "I want supplies. I don't feel safe without them."

Logan stared in disbelief at how shameless he was.

Iris already saw through his act. She smiled faintly. "Fine. Take whatever's left in the car. Tomorrow, you get a third of whatever we loot."

Kael cheered. "Captain Beauty, you're an angel! Gorgeous and kind! I'll follow you anywhere!"

If Iris couldn't see his loyalty meter still stuck flat, she might've bought the performance.

This guy was tough. Without real, solid benefits, he'd never warm up.

Satisfied, Kael happily went to raid the car.

After he left, Logan spoke at once. "Iris, do we..."

Iris shook her head. "Let him have it."

Her eyes were thoughtful.

*****

The next morning, everyone was up early.

All their supplies from yesterday had gone to Kael, so there was no breakfast.

Iris secretly gave Logan food for himself and the dogs. She wasn't gonna let her own people starve.

When Kael came downstairs, she acted like she hadn't eaten.

"Kael, I gave you everything yesterday. I haven't had breakfast. We need to find supplies now."

Kael immediately pulled a bag of bread from his pack—the exact emergency ration from her car.

"Captain Beauty, can't let you go hungry. Here, have mine."

Iris didn't refuse. It was hers anyway.

"Let's go. If we don't find something, we'll all starve."

Her gaze lingered on his half-empty bag for a second. She tucked the bread away and headed for the car.

Kael and Logan followed.

Ten minutes later, they reached the Development Zone Mall on the east side.

This area sat on the edge of Aster City.

Rapid expansion had turned a few small villas into clusters of new residential blocks, most still barely occupied.

Some projects had even been abandoned halfway through, left as empty concrete shells after major scandals.

After the apocalypse, the place felt even more desolate. The wind carried distant howls through the hollow buildings.

Every so often, those empty structures would spit out gaunt, ghost-like zombies, shrieking the moment they sensed the living.

A few wandering the streets spotted Iris's car and rushed straight toward it.
