Chapter 116 Psionic Power

The man was from Victory Base.

He knelt before the group, begging for mercy.

As he pleaded, his body contorted into horrifying, joint-snapping positions. The veins beneath his skin darkened and bulged. In the blink of an eye, all color drained from his flesh.

He curled into a tortured ball on the ground, still pleading.

The survivors from Victory Base fell silent. None could bring themselves to act.

This was one of their own.

After the apocalypse, they had fought side by side too many times to count. They knew each other's lives inside out.

This man had married his wife not long before the outbreak. They had endured years of long distance, finally tying the knot just as the world fell apart.

When he left for this mission, she had cried and begged him not to go. He had promised her that after this trip, they would have enough supplies to stay home for a long, long time.

He sobbed brokenly. "Please... please spare me. Lily... I love you so much..."

Bang!

A cold gunshot cut through the air.

The man dropped lifelessly to the ground.

Every member of Victory Base fixed a furious glare on the shooter.

It was Zane.

He stared coldly at the body, smoke still curling from the barrel of his gun.

The Hope Base team had finally stepped out of their vehicles now that the area was secure.

Mason, leader of Victory Base, charged forward in a rage. "Are you even human?! He wasn't a zombie yet! Not yet!"

Before he could reach Zane, several gun muzzles pressed against his forehead.

The men behind Zane watched him with frigid, unyielding eyes.

One more step, and Victory Base might not leave this mission alive.

"But he was still human!" Mason's fists clenched so tight his knuckles whitened.

He froze in place, bloodshot eyes burning into Zane. "Why did you kill him?!"

Zane's gaze remained utterly emotionless—inhumanly indifferent.

"Once he turned, we would all be at risk. I do not tolerate unnecessary danger."

Mason's jaw tightened.

Before he could speak, Zane continued. "Teams with casualties will receive extra supplies, proportional to the lives lost."

With that, he turned and walked away.

The rest of Hope Base followed.

The entire group watched them leave in silence.

Damon stepped over to Mason. "Need assistance?"

Mason managed a bitter smile. "No, thank you, Captain Flint. Risk comes with every mission. You all did what you could."

...

Each team dug graves for their fallen.

Damon sent his men over with medical supplies and digging tools. Everyone thanked him gratefully.

After the night's battle, only Base K1, Hope Base, and Midsummer Base had emerged without casualties—Midsummer because they had stayed hidden the entire time.

Once the other bases were settled and guards posted around the safe zone, Damon headed toward Iris's car.

Logan stepped out of his tent the moment he approached. "Captain Flint!"

Damon nodded. "Logan."

Logan had once been his soldier, after all.

Damon looked around. "Where is Iris?"

Logan smiled awkwardly. "Still asleep..."

Damon's mouth twitched faintly.

Typical. But at least she was unharmed.

He had told her not to fight, anyway. This whole trip was just to get her supplies and repay an old debt.

He nodded. "Get some rest too. We move out at first light."

Logan saluted as Damon left. "Yes, Captain Flint!"

*****

The next day.

Sunrise thinned the zombie hordes significantly. The massive swarm from the night before had been completely wiped out.

Damon's forces had worked tirelessly through the night, but they had gained plenty in return—over a thousand crystal cores.

That was pure profit for every base. All the teams that had fought went out to collect the cores.

Iris had slept soundly.

To her pleasant surprise, after fully draining her psychic power, one night's rest had actually strengthened it.

She stretched her mental senses outward, scanning everyone and everything nearby.

Her range had expanded by roughly four feet.

She could also perceive the superpowers within other metahumans far more clearly.

Before, she had only sensed Damon's lightning—his dominant attribute. Now she detected a faint undercurrent of ice energy alongside it.

Multi-power metahumans had tiers; not all abilities were equally strong.

Damon's lightning overpowered his other traits, but Iris could now pick up those subtle traces.

She scanned every member of every base.

Most carried the same ordinary powers she had sensed the previous day—until she reached Hope Base's convoy.

Hope Base had seven people in total. Four were spatial-type metahumans, one fire-type, one strength-type, and one more...

Iris probed deeper, only to hit a sharp, defensive barrier.

That was—psionic power! A rare variant of psychic power.
