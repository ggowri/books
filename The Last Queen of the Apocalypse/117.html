Chapter 117 Confrontation

Metahumans with psionic power wielded absolute control over anyone and anything within their domain.

This was nothing like Iris's standard radar-style psychic power.

Psionic power was born for dominance—absolute, unchallenged rule.

Everything within their mental range obeyed their will. Telekinesis, hypnosis, mental barriers—all were second nature.

They were the undisputed rulers of their own territory.

Statistically, only one in a thousand metahumans had psychic power. And only one in a thousand psychics awakened psionic power.

It was an exceedingly rare, king-tier power.

But it had one critical flaw: psionic power only worked within its fixed domain.

Step outside that zone, and the user was effectively powerless.

They could not expand their range endlessly like Iris, let alone launch remote attacks.

The ability was confined to a small, fixed area. Expanding the domain was nearly impossible—they could only strengthen control within it.

Powerful, yes. But deeply limited.

The needle-sharp mental force detected the probe and lashed out instantly, attempting to trace its source. But its mental power was no match for Iris's.

The second she recognized psionic energy, she pulled her consciousness back completely.

If the opponent couldn't catch her inside their domain, they would never sense her anywhere else.

After that brief clash, Iris rubbed her temples.

She had basically laid Hope Base bare. So they were hiding a psionic power user.

Just then, the door of Hope Base's vehicle opened.

Zane stepped out in his white uniform, face dark, scanning the area with heavy, suspicious eyes.

Iris immediately knew who the psionic power user was.

Zane was seething.

He had clearly sensed someone probing them with psychic power, yet couldn't catch even a trace of the intruder.

This was an extremely high-level psychic power user.

The person had entered his domain without him noticing at first. He only picked up on them when they probed deeper—and the second he reacted, they vanished. He couldn't even pin them down inside his own territory.

Who was it? How was their mental power this strong?

They'd been fully exposed, and he couldn't find a single clue.

Damn it.

He stood outside, scanning the crowd for any sign, when a voice cut through the air.

"You didn't lift a finger against the zombies yesterday, did you?"

A man from Victory Base spotted Iris and marched over, furious.

His voice drew a crowd, and everyone quickly realized the truth.

Iris and Logan had stayed hidden the entire night.

"I was sleeping," Iris said lazily, facing the gathering mob.

The man nearly exploded. "Sleeping?! Do you have any idea what we went through? We lost teammates! And you were curled up in there, asleep! How could you?"

"What else was I supposed to do?" Iris tilted her head.

The man trembled with rage, pointing at her. "How can you be so shameless! Don't you feel guilty at all?!"

Iris chuckled. "Got anything else?"

Now he was shaking so hard he could barely speak. "Don't you walk away! You owe us all an explanation! Apologize to everyone!"

"An apology?" Iris stretched casually and looked him up and down.

She patted his chest lightly. "Sure. Tell me which part upset you, and I'll say it again."

The man froze.

His rage was so strong that he wanted to cry.

How could someone like this even exist?

Derek and the others who'd just arrived could only exchange glances.

This felt way too familiar. He'd almost been reduced to tears by her once too.

People from the other bases were growing incensed as well.

When it came to riling up a crowd with just a few words, Iris, the troublemaker, did it effortlessly like breathing.

"Midsummer Base is so full of themselves!" someone from Victory Base snapped.

"You only sent two people, and neither even fought! That's too much!" someone from Future Base shouted.

"You owe us an explanation today!" Stellar Base demanded.

Just then, a voice cut through the noise from behind them.

"What's happening here?"

Damon strode over with his men.

The smaller bases immediately began talking over each other.

"Captain Flint, Midsummer Base didn't send anyone out last night!"

"They're completely arrogant! They only have two people, and neither showed up to fight!"

"We cut them slack for being small, but listen to her! During that life-or-death defense, she was sleeping!"

Damon stared at Iris, massaging his temples, and scrambled to cover for her. "Captain Hale must have been sleeping soundly and simply didn't hear anything."
