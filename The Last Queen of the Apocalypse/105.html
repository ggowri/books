Chapter 105 Visit Base K1

"Woof! Woof! Woof!"

"Happy, Lucky, Red, White, Summer! Long time no see!"

Derek grinned as the pack of dogs bounded over, greeting each one cheerfully.

Logan, who'd been working in the fields, heard the noise and came over.

"Logan!" Derek waved.

Logan's eyes widened. "Derek?! What are you doing here?"

"I'm here to pick you two up!" Derek said excitedly. "We get to go on a mission together again!"

Logan looked confused.

Just then, Iris stepped out. "Logan, pack your things. We're going with them."

Half an hour later, they were ready.

Iris didn't bring the dogs this time—they'd stay behind to guard the base.

It was a long-distance trip with multiple teams. She couldn't watch them properly, and too many dogs would draw too much attention. Better leave them home.

Besides, this was just a side mission for her. She didn't need to be the main force, just tag along and grab supplies when she could. The dogs weren't necessary.

She was even riding in Damon's car.

Using their vehicle, their gas... what a reliable ally.

She did pack her own food, though: a case of ready-to-eat meals, instant noodles, marinated eggs, bread, and snacks, all stuffed into Derek's trunk.

"Iris, we're leaving tomorrow. Captain Flint asked me to bring you to Base K1 early so you can stay the night."

Iris smiled. "Sounds good."

"You'll get to see our base!" Derek said proudly. "It's not as fancy as yours, but it's big and packed with people. Real lively! Just the two of you must get lonely sometimes. Humans are meant to stick together."

Iris smiled but didn't reply. So far, no one knew Midsummer Base already had nineteen residents.

"Sure, I'd love to see how lively Base K1 really is."

Derek drove them toward Base K1.

Bases were survivor settlements—groups trapped by zombie hordes banding together to live, big and small.

From her past-life memories, the entire eastern region had twenty to thirty bases. Most were small and fragile.

But over ten years, they were swallowed up one by one.

By the final zombie wave, only three major bases remained: Base K1, Hope Base, and the farther Ember Base, near the central region.

Base K1 had always been famous because it was military-run, with strong firepower that drew tons of survivors early on.

But that was also its biggest flaw. It couldn't support that many people, not like the other two.

Hope Base only took capable survivors and recruited top scientists early on. Fewer people, higher quality—they managed well.

Ember Base was always short on supplies, but its leader was brutally ruthless—top of the list of the most dangerous men in the east.

He killed zombies outside and his own people inside. When supplies ran too low, he culled the population to keep numbers sustainable.

Brutal, but effective. Ember Base never fell to riots or infighting.

In the apocalypse, humans were often more dangerous than zombies. When food ran out, people turned into beasts.

Base K1 had endless internal conflicts. Only its military foundation kept it from collapsing.

Worse, the leadership let troublemakers run wild, using them to balance the military's power so they could stay in charge.

"What happened to you after that mission?" Iris asked from the passenger seat.

"Completed it successfully!" Derek sighed in relief.

Iris raised an eyebrow. "But that Barton girl died. Isn't her dad one of the base administrators?"

Just then, the car turned onto a main road leading to a huge tunnel. Barbed-wire fences surrounded the entrance, with armed guards standing watch.

Dozens of vehicles lined both sides of the mountain, and refugees crowded in temporary tents, waiting in long lines.

Iris knew exactly what this was—quarantine screening for bites. Anyone showing signs of mutation would be shot on sight.

Every survivor had to wait a full 24 hours before getting a number and entering Base K1.
