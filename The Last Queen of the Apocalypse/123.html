Chapter 123 Unfair Distribution

Iris couldn't take on the world's big problems. But she could definitely settle her own little scores.

Once everyone finished cleaning up, Iris was ready to settle accounts.

She called out, "Everyone, gather round! I'm redividing the grain. Any objections?"

Iris stood outside the granary and summoned all the teams.

The four small bases didn't dare argue and hurried over. Base K1 followed naturally.

Hope Base group seemed to be having a private discussion, but when they heard Iris, Zane glanced over and led his people over last.

Once there, Zane kept his usual authoritative tone. "Captain Hale, your contribution today was enormous. Everyone saw it. You deserve a larger share.

"How about this—we restructure the split. Hope Base, Base K1, and Midsummer Base each take thirty percent. The remaining ten goes to the other bases, divided equally."

When he finished, members from the other four bases stiffened, their displeasure obvious. But no one dared speak.

They had seen Iris turn the tide on her own. If she didn't get a significant share, a monster like her wouldn't hesitate to crush them.

So they had no choice but to swallow it.

Iris turned to him. "You're Zane?"

Zane softened his usual coldness. Though his eyes still lacked warmth, he held out a hand.

"Yes, Captain Hale. Nice to meet you. I'm Zane Foster, from Hope Base."

This was the first time he'd formally introduced himself. Not even Damon had received this courtesy.

Iris ignored his friendly gesture and shut him down cold. "Zane, save the flattery. I'm the most unfair person around. If people think shaking my hand gets them extra grain, they'll be lining up to grip my fingers all day."

Zane's hand froze mid-air.

She went on. "But your plan sounds decent. You all heard him, right? Zane just coldly offered you only ten percent. You okay with that?"

Zane's face darkened, but he said nothing.

The four base leaders exchanged looks, then gritted their teeth. "We accept."

Ten percent totaled a thousand tons. Two hundred and fifty tons each.

Although it was half of their original share, it was still a massive amount.

Each base had two or three hundred people. 250 tons could feed them for nearly ten years.

In reality, grain wouldn't last that long, but it would carry them through the worst years easily.

This was more than enough.

Besides, who were they to argue?

They couldn't afford to offend Zane, and they definitely couldn't offend Iris.

Iris nodded. "Alright, we'll go with Zane's idea then. You four split the ten percent equally. Good?"

They nodded. "Agreed."

Iris called, "Logan, release their grain!"

Logan obeyed at once and began distribution.

The granary still had power, fully automated. A few system commands, and the overhead pipes began dispensing grain directly into the trucks.

The bases lined up their heavy haulers. Each truck carried over twenty tons, with several maxed-out fifty-five-ton behemoths.

One by one, the vehicles filled to capacity until each base had secured its allotted 250 tons.

Watching the trucks roll forward in steady succession, relief finally settled over the crowd.

The trip had been worth it. Their most pressing food shortage was over.

After the teams finished, Iris turned to Damon. "Captain Flint, Zane said thirty percent for you. You accept?"

Damon's eyes flickered, then he said simply, "It's fine."

One percent less was nothing—especially since it went to Iris.

Iris smiled. "Alright. Logan, keep going. Thirty percent for Captain Flint. And set aside two hundred tons—he's giving those to me personally."

Damon smiled faintly.

She had a pretty good memory.

Then Zane stepped forward. "Captain Hale, it's our turn now, right?"

Iris looked at him and smirked. "Zane, I don't think your split works. I did tell you I'm unfair, remember?"

Zane's brow tightened. "What are you suggesting?"

Iris shrugged. "Everything left is mine."

Zane's face turned icy, radiating cold fury. "Captain Hale, are you joking?"

Iris smiled sweetly. "Zane, you're still too young to handle this. Mad already?"

Zane's temples throbbed. "What do you want, Captain Hale?"

Iris laid it out plainly. "Fine, I'll teach you a lesson. I'll calculate by your rules, so you can't complain.

"You said distribution is based on contribution, right? So what exactly did Hope Base do? Did you fight in last night's horde? Or did you kill even one zombie today?"
