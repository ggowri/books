Chapter 89 Zombie King

Iris fell deep in thought, her gaze fixed sharply on Damon.

A terrible, thrilling suspicion was creeping over her.

"You have three superpowers?" she blurted out.

"Yeah," Damon answered plainly.

Iris studied him, and her heart jolted.

No way... it can't be... Did she just save the Zombie King from her past life?

The more she thought, the more it made sense.

First, in her previous life, she had found Logan around this exact time.

All his old comrades were dead, and if Damon had still been alive, Logan would have searched for him without hesitation—he knew exactly where their base was.

Second, Damon had three superpowers.

The Zombie King from her old life had also come from this region... and had wielded three superpowers.

"Thank you. I owe you twice now."

Iris snapped back to reality at Damon's voice.

Her face instantly lit up into a bright, money-minded grin. "Don't forget to pay me back later. Especially those crystal cores—you still owe me 22."

Zombie King or not, she was collecting that debt. Those cores were as good as hers.

"Deal."

Damon caught her bright smile, and something faintly flickered in his cold eyes. He looked away just as the elevator doors began to close.

But in that last split second, he saw Louisa's corpse outside... and the conspicuously empty space where her supplies had been.

Where did everything go?

Ding.

The elevator reached the ground floor.

The second they stepped out, a deep, thunderous rumble roared up from below—the underground levels collapsing.

A soft alert chimed inside Iris's head.

[Congratulations on completing the conquest mission: United Pharmaceutical R&D Factory!]

[Reward: Super Electronic Butler!]

Iris froze.

Could she get any luckier?

*****

"Iris!"

"Iris!"

"My queen!"

The moment she stepped outside, Derek and the others swarmed her.

Every one of them stared at her like starstruck fans.

Iris had revealed her power today, and it had completely blown their minds.

None of them had guessed she was a metahuman—let alone one this absurdly strong.

While everyone else struggled to conjure tiny fireballs and water orbs, she'd waved a hand and summoned a full-on water wall.

How was she that powerful?

After today, they knew one thing for certain: Iris was the boss. They were just side characters.

"Everyone all right?" Iris asked lazily.

"We're fine!"

Though they looked battered, their gear torn and bloodstained, miraculously, not a single person had been bitten.

They'd lost a few comrades on B1, but on the far deadlier B2, no one had died. They could barely believe their luck.

And that only made their admiration for Iris burn hotter. Even the men who'd originally followed Louisa now looked at her with utter reverence.

No wonder the special forces team was so close to her, so respectful. She wasn't just strong; she was on another level entirely.

"By the way, Iris, what did you come here for?" Derek asked.

"Yeah, need us to help with anything?"

"Nah." Iris scanned the group until she spotted Logan. "Logan, put up our sign now."

Logan replied quickly, "On it!"

He grabbed a sign from the truck, took down the tattered original at the entrance, and hung the new one in its place.

Everyone watched in silent disbelief.

Holy shit. They'd fought through hordes, risked their lives, just so she could claim another territory?

They stared at the newly marked land, too stunned to form words.

She really had come here to take over the place.

None of them could understand the mind of someone at her level. Iris seemed to have some weird obsession with expanding her territory.

They couldn't fathom why anyone would want so much empty, zombie-infested wasteland. Especially now that civilization had fallen—any unclaimed land was free for the taking.

No one else was fighting over it. Not them. Not any other faction.

What they didn't know was that in the future, these lands would generate massive wealth and form the foundation of a powerful kingdom.

No matter the era, land was the core of all growth.

Later, when bases fought to expand and resources ran dry, they would realize every usable piece of land already belonged to Iris.

*****

"Goodbye, Iris!"

"Bye, my queen!"

The sun began to set, and after securing the factory, Iris parted ways with Damon's team as they returned to their own bases.

Before driving off, Damon stopped his vehicle and asked, "Do you have a satellite phone?"

Iris didn't even hesitate. "No."

Damon continued, "A radio?"

Iris nodded. "I have one."

With the city's power grid dead, all old signal towers were useless. The only working communication left was satellite.
