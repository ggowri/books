Chapter 96 Just the Three of Us

Kael stayed quiet the whole time, his one eye glinting with amusement as he watched Iris work.

Once the flames crackled to life inside the pit, Logan and the dogs outside started making a fuss.

"Woof! Woof! Woof!"

"Iris! You okay in there?!"

Iris finished clearing the pig barn, pushed the door open, and stepped out. "I'm fine."

Logan looked at her anxiously. "What happened?"

Iris shrugged. "Nothing big."

His gaze then fell on the man behind her—baseball cap, eye patch, a strange vibe all over.

"Who are you?"

"Logan, this is my new subordinate, Kael Cross," Iris introduced.

Kael grinned and held out a hand. "Hey, buddy! Kael Cross. Nice to meet you."

Logan didn't like him at all. One eye hidden, smile warm but eyes sharp and scheming... it made his skin crawl.

He frowned and ignored the outstretched hand entirely.

"Iris, what about the pigs inside? Want me to clean up?"

Before Iris could answer, Kael cut in smoothly. "No need! I'm a spatial-type metahuman. Already stored all the live ones away."

Logan stared at him in surprise. "You're a spatial-type metahuman?"

"Yep!" Kael crossed his arms. "With me around, we'll never run out of storage!"

He shot Iris a cheeky grin.

When Logan wasn't looking, he leaned in and murmured quietly. "Captain Beauty, our little secret."

Iris studied this sharp-witted man and smiled faintly. "Alright."

If she hadn't known his true nature from her past life, she might've actually been impressed by how quickly he caught on.

Noticing she'd sent Logan away earlier, he covered for her without being told.

That kind of cunning was exactly how he'd wreaked havoc across so many bases back then. Truly a slippery fox.

"Let's go," she said.

"On my way, Captain Beauty."

Kael followed behind her, arms folded, watching her back with a burning glint in his eye.

The neighboring chicken farm was beyond saving. All the chicks were dead.

Luckily, Iris already had plenty of chickens and ducks stockpiled in her bunker. She had Kael and Logan handle the remains and burn the whole place down.

The hidden plague risk of this entire breeding zone was completely eliminated. The animal epidemic that would've ravaged bases in the months to come was nipped in the bud.

On the drive back, Kael sat in the armored vehicle, gawking at everything inside excitedly.

"Captain Beauty, where'd you get this beast?!"

"Ask what you should. Don't ask what you shouldn't," Iris replied coldly.

The colder she was, the more enthusiastic he got.

"Captain Beauty, is our base really just the three of us?"

Logan glanced at Iris from the driver's seat.

She answered firmly, "Yes."

"So where's our base, exactly?"

"Grain Processing Factory," Iris said flatly.

Logan's eyes widened.

"Logan, drive back to the base."

Logan didn't dare ask further. "Okay."

He steered toward the huge factory they'd cleared earlier.

Kael stared at the massive complex, his eyes lighting up.

Taking over a place this size meant real power.

Last time they'd left, they'd dug out all the crystal cores and burned the zombie corpses in a centralized pile. The area was fairly clean now.

Iris had Logan park by the dorm building.

"Pick any room, any floor. If you run into zombies... well, you aren't scared of them anyway."

Kael chuckled. "Sounds perfect, Captain Beauty. I'll go pick a nice spot."

Once he was gone, Logan frowned deeply. "Iris, that guy's bad news."

As an ex-soldier, he could smell the treachery on him from a mile away.

"I know," Iris said, stepping out of the car. "That's why I brought him here."

Kael was scum—but so was Iris, in her own way. She'd never bring a walking time bomb like him to her real base.

She'd keep him here and grind his loyalty up.

Daring to join her with only 19 starting loyalty, already planning to betray her? Not a chance.

Logan nodded, finally understanding. "What about the real base, then? Should we head back?"

Iris waved a hand. "Irie's got it covered."

Irie could run the entire base alone, even feeding the dogs with drones. Staying out here a few days wouldn't hurt at all.
