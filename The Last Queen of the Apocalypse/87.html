Chapter 87 I'll Hold Them Off

Protecting the innocent is a soldier's duty. Even in this broken world, a soldier's spirit could not be easily erased.

Especially not when Iris had saved them when they needed it most.

Without hesitation, they formed a protective circle around her, shielding her as they charged ahead.

This left Iris, who'd been ready to yank out her AK and go on a rampage in the dark, completely stuck.

Tucked safely in the center, she couldn't exactly lead the slaughter. But she could still make good use of her Desert Eagle.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Derek and Brett fought aggressively to clear the zombies on both flanks, but the endless horde was overwhelming.

Iris remained calm in the middle, using the men around her as a living shield. Any zombie that broke through their line became easy target practice for her pistol.

Under the crushing pressure of the undead and relentless gunfire, they pushed toward the exit.

But in less than ten seconds, their ammunition was gone.

The countdown blared on.

"Twenty-one!"

"Twenty!"

They'd left most of their heavy firepower on the upper basement level, and what little they'd brought was nowhere near enough.

They still had over a hundred meters to cover—with only twenty seconds left, and zombies closing in from all sides.

"Ahhh—"

The second their firepower weakened, one of Louisa's men at the front was tackled by two zombies at once. A piece of debris had struck his leg, slowing him just long enough.

A zombie latched onto the side of his helmet and bit down hard.

Then brilliant lightning flashed across everyone's vision. A bolt of electricity struck the zombies attacking the fallen soldier.

Damon hauled the shaken man to safety, revealing deep bite marks indented into his thick helmet. He slung him over his back and kept running.

The man whispered, "Captain Flint, I'm not even part of your unit. Just leave me and go!"

"Anyone who follows me is my responsibility," Damon said coldly.

Iris finally understood why Derek and the others would never abandon him. The man might be cold as ice, but he had a core of unshakable honor.

Crackle, crackle, crackle.

Lightning continued to strike as the other soldiers discarded their empty weapons and unleashed their abilities.

Egg-sized water orbs, fireballs, and metal shards sliced through the air.

Their powers were not strong, but in this moment, they poured everything they had into the fight.

Those without superpowers—metahumans with far superior speed and strength compared to normal people—drew military bayonets and relied on their mutated physiques for close combat.

Bleeding and sweating, they fought their way forward.

"Fifteen!"

Seventy meters remaining.

"Twelve!"

Fifty meters.

"Ten!"

Forty meters.

Just as they were about to break free of the horde, the soldiers leading the charge stumbled.

The energy orbs in their hands shrank smaller and smaller until they vanished completely.

Their powers were exhausted. Their abilities were too weak to maintain such intense output.

Everyone's pace slowed abruptly. They shifted from attacking to defending, barely holding each other up.

They were fading fast... while the zombies never tired.

Humans fatigue. Zombies do not.

Near Iris, Derek's fireball fizzled out mid-air. A zombie lunged at him with a snarl.

There was no room to dodge.

Bang!

Iris yanked Derek out of the way and kicked the zombie, sending it flying.

But before the pale Derek could thank her, Brett was seized by three or four zombies. Blake tried to pull him free and was swarmed in turn.

One by one, the men protecting Iris ran out of strength, falling left and right.

"Iris! Run!" Derek shoved her aside and threw himself back into the horde to save his teammates. "This is as far as we can go! Run!"

They could only take her this far. Whoever survived from here would have to save themselves.

Derek stayed behind to cover her escape.

Suddenly, multiple bolts of lightning crashed down in a fury, wiping out the zombies surrounding Derek.

Damon stood motionless, his eyes glowing pure silver.

He looked deeply at Iris, electricity crackling across his body.

"Iris, save whoever you can. I'll hold them off."

To Iris's shock, a terrifyingly powerful aura erupted from him.

And then, alongside his lightning-type power, he unleashed ice-type power and light-type power too!

Iris's mind jolted—a realization struck her like a bell.

Could it be...?

"Everyone run!" Damon roared.

In an instant, every zombie turned toward him, charging as one.

"Captain Flint!"

"Captain Flint!"

Just as the horde converged on Damon and despair set in, a water barrier a hundred times more powerful than anything they'd ever seen slammed into place.

A lazy, calm female voice echoed in their ears. "What are you waiting for? Move!"
