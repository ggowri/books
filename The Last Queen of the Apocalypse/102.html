Chapter 102 This Grudge Wasn't Over

Kael headed for the stairs, then paused at the corner and looked back.

"Promise me you won't leave. Wait here for me, okay?"

"Okay," Iris replied.

He broke into a wide grin and hurried down.

Iris watched him vanish into the dark below, but her brow only furrowed tighter.

She'd just heard this demon's whole life story. They'd talked honestly, shared their pasts.

So why... had his loyalty not moved even an inch? Still stuck at 59.

What a cold-hearted bastard.

Just then, Logan's voice boomed from downstairs. "Iris!"

Iris replied quickly, "I'm up here!"

Then, a wire snapped in her head.

Wait, did Kael...

She bolted downstairs like a gust of wind.

On the first floor, Logan came panting over, looking frantic. "Iris, thank God you're safe!"

"Where's Kael?" she asked immediately.

Logan scratched his head. "He said you were in trouble and told me to hurry up and help."

She knew it!

Iris sprinted for the entrance, chasing after him.

When she burst outside, a convertible sports car zoomed past with a roar.

Kael waved wildly at her from the driver's seat, grinning like a fool.

"Captain Beauty, I'm outta here! Thanks for the supplies! You're so cute! Next time, I'm definitely gonna make you mine!"

Iris stared back at the empty first floor. It hit her like a truck.

Kael really was a spatial-type metahuman.

She'd been played—completely, utterly fooled.

Iris was so furious she could barely stand, stamping her feet in rage.

"Fuck you! Just you wait! Next time I see you, I'll break both your legs! You're dead meat!"

Her chest ached with anger.

She'd always been the one conning others. This was the first time someone had conned her. And she'd actually believed his sad story!

Kael had lied from the very start. He only joined to steal supplies.

"Logan, remember this!" she snarled. "From today on, Kael Cross is our number one enemy!

"Don't believe a single word he says. If you see him, catch him! Break his legs! I'll torture him to death!"

"...Got it," Logan nodded.

Iris angrily checked what was left.

Kael had only taken food from the first-floor supermarket, completely ignoring the daily necessities on the second and third floors. Aside from the pile of clothes Logan had sorted, he hadn't touched anything else.

Good thing she'd left two dogs guarding the car. Otherwise, he might have taken that too. And Logan had already moved a batch of supplies into the armored vehicle earlier.

In the end, he'd only gotten part of the first-floor food—much of which was already eaten or spoiled anyway. Less than a quarter of the total haul.

Iris tried to comfort herself by considering it the one-third cut she'd promised him.

But she was still fuming.

The more she thought about it, the angrier she got.

When she got back to base, she made a wanted notice and had it play on every screen 24/7, complete with a sketch she drew of Kael.

[Midsummer Base Public Enemy #1: Kael Cross. Wanted dead or alive. 100 points reward for capture!]

This grudge wasn't over.

*****

After getting scammed by Kael, Iris stayed gloomy for a long time.

She didn't even feel like going out to expand territory.

Instead, she focused on base affairs, developing the settlement, and spending time with her dogs—the only ones who'd never betray her.

Dogs were way more reliable.

Before they knew it, more than three months had passed.

Midsummer Base finally reached stable self-production.

The residents started with fast-growing crops: radishes, small carrots, and cucumbers. Now they were harvesting medium-cycle high-yield plants like potatoes and sweet potatoes.

The cleared farmland was full of corn, wheat, and all kinds of vegetables. Tomatoes, cabbage, spinach, peppers... They even grew delicate fruits like strawberries and blueberries.

Iris had planted almost every seed she owned.

Nathan, with his plant ability, took charge of early crop nurturing.

Kevin and the other metahumans expanded the fields.

Everyone else worked together on planting and care.

Plus, Iris's territory had system boosts. Everything grew faster and yielded more than normal.

Her territory had finally achieved real, sustainable food production.
