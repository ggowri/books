Chapter 108 The Hale Family

Stacks of cash lay spread on the ground.

Clearly, some rich boss had tried to flee with his fortune, only to discover that money had become the most worthless thing in the apocalypse.

Iris wandered down the busy street with fresh curiosity, taking in the bizarre sights.

Suddenly, a familiar voice cut through the noise.

"Food! I need food! You think you can just take it for free? Everyone, look at this bastard!"

A middle-aged man's shout echoed ahead.

Iris watched as a burly man stumbled out of a small shack, zipping up his pants.

The second he stepped outside, a limping middle-aged man in his fifties and a ragged old woman grabbed him.

Right behind them, a haggard woman in her forties rushed out of the shack.

All three began yelling at the gate.

"You think you can just leave without paying?!"

"Help! This guy's trying to skip out!"

The man cursed. "Bullshit! You dared charge me? You said your wife was young and tender, so I agreed. Then you throw this old hag at me and expect payment?"

The limp man protested. "I gave you options!"

"Options? Your mother's seventy-eight!" the man spat.

The limp man clung desperately to his leg. "I don't care! You're not leaving without paying!"

The old woman shrieked, drawing stares from the whole street. "Right! You have to give us something! It's not my fault you didn't like me! You came in, so you pay!"

The man spotted base soldiers approaching in the distance. Grimacing with disgust, he pulled a pack of instant noodles from his pocket.

"Fuck my luck!"

He threw the noodles on the ground, shoved the trio aside, stomped the packet flat, and stormed off.

The three immediately pounced on the crushed noodles like starved dogs.

"Get off! This is mine! Don't you dare steal it!"

"Honey, you can earn more later. Let Mom and me eat first—we haven't had anything all day."

"Yeah, yeah. I'm starving."

"Fuck off!" A younger man stumbled out of the shack, unshaven and gaunt, but in far better shape than the others.

He snatched the noodles away at once.

The limp man begged. "Ryan, stop being selfish. You already ate today. You took my portion, and your grandma's too!"

The old woman sobbed. "Yeah. Ryan, leave us some. Grandma's dying of hunger."

Ryan kicked them away. "Useless pieces of shit don't deserve to eat!"

Then he turned to the woman. "Mom, you shouldn't stay with these two losers. They're dead weight!

"One's a wrinkled hag who should've died already, the other's a cripple who can't even do base missions. Why are you still with them?"

"You're right!" The woman nodded fiercely, staring hungrily at the noodles. "We're the real family!"

Ryan declared, "From now on, it's just you and me. No more of these two wastes."

"Absolutely! Now that you're better, we can dump them!"

The middle-aged woman followed Ryan as he walked away.

The limp man lunged desperately. "I'm your father! How can you abandon me? You forgot I carried you all the way here and nursed you back to health? You can't just dump your dad the second you're better!"

The old woman wailed. "Ryan! I'm your grandma! I loved you more than anything! How can you leave me? Take me with you!"

"Fuck off! You two garbage pieces can just die!" Ryan kicked them both hard.

Iris sneered.

Of course it was the Hale family.

Ryan had gotten absurdly lucky. After Iris had beaten him half to death in the hospital and taken his phone and ID, his devoted parents had somehow found him.

When the outbreak hit, Benjamin had slung the unconscious Ryan over his shoulder and fled with Adeline and Amanda.

Their luck was unbelievable. They hid in the hospital's medicine storage room, locked the door, and refused to open it for anyone. Somehow, they survived the early days of the apocalypse.

Even stranger, living on nothing but pills had actually healed Ryan, who had nearly been reduced to a vegetable.

They missed the first rescue wave, but later hitched a ride with a supply team and eventually made it to Base K1.

God really did watch over trash.

Without Iris's protection, though, they suffered badly.

In those early days, they survived on bitter medicine alone. For water, they collected rain through a small window. Not once did they have a proper meal after the world fell.

During the escape, with no one to shield them, everyone was injured.

Benjamin's leg was broken, leaving him completely crippled.
